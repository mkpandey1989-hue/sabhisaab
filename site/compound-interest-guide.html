Compound Interest Kya Hai? Paisa Tezi Se Kaise Badhta Hai

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Kaha jaata hai ki compound interest (chakravriddhi byaj) duniya ka aathva ajooba hai — aur ye baat sach ke bahut kareeb hai. Yahi wo taakat hai jiske chalte chhoti-chhoti bachat lambe samay me bada fund ban jaati hai. Is guide me hum simple aur compound interest ka farak, aur compounding ka jaadu bilkul aasaan bhasha me samjhenge. Neeche free calculators bhi hain.

➤ Interest Calculators kholein

Simple Interest kya hai?

Simple interest me byaj sirf asli rakam (principal) par lagta hai — har saal utna hi. Jaise ₹1,00,000 par 10% simple interest se har saal ₹10,000 milega, chahe kitne bhi saal ho jayein. 3 saal me ₹30,000 byaj.

Compound Interest kya hai?

Compound interest me byaj sirf principal par nahi, balki pehle kamaye gaye byaj par bhi lagta hai. Yani "byaj par byaj". Isi se paisa tezi se badhta hai.

Farak dekho — ₹1,00,000, 10%, 3 saal:
• Simple interest: ₹30,000 byaj → total ₹1,30,000
• Compound interest: ₹33,100 byaj → total ₹1,33,100

Sirf 3 saal me ₹3,100 ka farak. Jitna lamba samay, utna bada farak — 20-30 saal me ye farak laakhon me pahunch jaata hai.

Compounding ko taakatwar banane wali 3 cheezein

  • Samay: Sabse zaroori. Jitna jaldi shuru karenge, utna zyada fayda. 25 saal ki umar me shuru karna 35 se kahin behtar.
  • Return dar: Thodi zyada dar bhi lambe samay me bada farak laati hai.
  • Frequency: Byaj jitni baar judta hai (saalana, quarterly, monthly), utna thoda zyada fayda.

Compounding asli zindagi me kahan?

Ye sirf theory nahi — SIP, PPF, EPF, FD, NPS — sab compounding par hi kaam karte hain. Isi liye financial planning me kaha jaata hai: jaldi shuru karo aur lambe samay tak jame raho. Yahi compounding ko poora mauka deta hai.

Ulta pehlu: Compounding loan/credit card me aapke khilaaf bhi kaam karta hai. Credit card ka bakaya na chukane par byaj par byaj tezi se badhta hai — isliye card ka bill hamesha poora chukana chahiye.

Calculator kaise use karein?

  1. Principal (rakam), byaj dar aur saal daalein.
  2. Simple aur compound dono me result dekh kar farak samajhein.

FAQ

Kaunsa behtar — simple ya compound?

Nivesh ke liye compound behtar (paisa zyada badhta). Loan lene wale ke liye simple sasta padta. Isliye invest me compound dhoondhein, udhaar me kam byaj.

SIP me kaunsa interest lagta?

SIP aur mutual funds me returns compounding jaise hi badhte hain (units par returns phir returns kamate). Isi liye lamba SIP itna faydemand hota.

Compounding kitni baar hoti hai, isse farak padta hai?

Haan. Ek hi dar par saalana, chhamahi, timahi aur maasik compounding me natija alag aata hai — jitni baar zyada, utna zyada. FD ka vigyapan aksar saalana dar batata hai par jodta timahi par hai.

Rule of 72 kya hai?

Paisa kitne saal me dugna hoga, iska mota anuman. 72 ko byaj dar se bhaag dijiye — 12% par lagbhag 6 saal. Ye 25-30% se upar ki dar par sahi jawab nahi deta.

Samay bade se bada khilaadi hai

Compounding me log sochte hain ki sabse zyada farak byaj dar se padta hai. Sach ye hai ki sabse zyada farak samay se padta hai.

Do log dekhiye. Pehla 25 saal ki umar me ₹1 lakh lagata hai aur 55 tak chhodta hai — 30 saal. Doosra 35 me wahi ₹1 lakh lagata hai aur 55 tak chhodta hai — 20 saal. Dono ne barabar paisa lagaya, dono ko barabar return mila. Par pehle wale ka paisa doosre se lagbhag dugna ban jaata hai. Sirf 10 saal ke farak se.

Isi liye kehte hain: nivesh me sabse achha din "aaj" hota hai. Perfect waqt ka intezaar karne me jo saal nikal jaate hain, wo wapas nahi aate.

Aakhri saal sabse bade hote hain

Compounding ki growth seedhi rekha me nahi chalti — wo shuru me dheemi aur baad me bahut tez hoti hai. 30 saal ke nivesh me aakhri 5 saal me utni badhotri aa sakti hai jitni pehle 20 saal me aayi thi.

Yahi wajah hai ki log beech me nikal kar sabse bada nuksan karte hain. Wo asli fayda aane se theek pehle bahar nikal jaate hain.

Compounding kitni baar — iska bhi farak

Ek hi dar par bhi, jitni zyada baar compounding hoti hai utna zyada milta hai:

  • Saalana — sabse kam (kai sarkari yojanaein)
  • Quarterly — FD aur PPF me aam
  • Monthly — kuch scheme aur loan me
  • Daily — savings account me

Isi liye "7% saalana" aur "7% quarterly compounding" me farak hota hai. Doosre me asli return (effective yield) thoda zyada nikalta hai.

Ye ulta bhi chalta hai — karza

Compounding ki taakat aapke khilaaf bhi utni hi kaam karti hai. Credit card ka bakaya, personal loan, ya koi bhi mehnga karza — wahan yahi compounding rakam ko tezi se badhati hai.

Credit card par sirf "minimum due" bharna sabse bada jaal hai. Late fee to nahi lagti, par baaki poori rakam par bhaari byaj chalta rehta hai, aur wo byaj agle mahine ke bakaye me jud jaata hai. Isi tarah chhota sa bakaya saalon me kai guna ho jaata hai.

Byaj kitni baar juda — utna hi farak

Ek hi dar par bhi, byaj jitni baar jodta hai utna zyada banta hai. Rs 1 lakh, 10%, 1 saal:

Isi wajah se do FD ki "dar" ek jaisi hone par bhi paisa alag banta hai. Tulna karte waqt likhi hui dar nahi, asli saalana dar (effective rate) poochhiye — bank use "annualised yield" kehte hain. Aur yahi baat ulti taraf bhi lagti hai: credit card ka byaj roz jodta hai, isliye "3.5% mahina" sunne me chhota lagta hai par saal bhar me 50% se upar pad jaata hai. Jama karte waqt zyada baar jodna faayde ka hai, karza lete waqt nuksan ka.
Simple niyam: nivesh me compounding aapki dost, karze me dushman. Isi liye pehle mehnga karza chukayein, phir nivesh badhayein.

Poora fayda uthane ke 4 niyam

  1. Jaldi shuru karein — chhoti rakam se bhi chalega, par aaj se
  2. Beech me haath mat lagayein — chain tootne se sabse bada nuksan hota hai
  3. Byaj/dividend wapas nivesh me jaane dein — kharch kiya to compounding hi khatam
  4. Har saal rakam thodi badhayein — salary badhe to nivesh bhi badhe
