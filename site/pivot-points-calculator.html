Pivot, R1-R2 aur S1-S2 nikaalna

Pivot (P) = (High + Low + Close) ÷ 3
R1 = 2P − Low  |  S1 = 2P − High
R2 = P + (High − Low)  |  S2 = P − (High − Low)

Ye classic (standard) pivot ka tarika hai, jo sabse zyada istemaal hota hai.

Kya dekhna hai:

  • Bhaav pivot ke upar — din ka rukh aam taur par tezi ka maana jaata hai
  • Bhaav pivot ke neeche — mandi ka
  • R1, R2 — jahan bhaav ruk sakta hai upar jaate hue
  • S1, S2 — jahan sahara mil sakta hai neeche aate hue

Level nikaalne ke liye pichhle poore din ka data lijiye — high, low aur closing.

Ye level kaam kyun karte hain

Isme koi jaadu nahi hai. Wajah saada hai — bahut saare log yahi level dekh rahe hote hain.

Pivot points dashakon se istemaal ho rahe hain, aur lagbhag har trading software me mil jaate hain. Jab hazaaron trader ek hi level par nazar rakhte hain, to us level par kharid-bikri badh jaati hai — aur bhaav sach me wahan ruk jaata hai.

Isi liye ye level bade aur zyada trade hone wale share aur index me behtar kaam karte hain, jahan bahut log dekh rahe hote hain. Chhote share me, jahan kam log hain, ye utne bharosemand nahi rehte.

Aur ek zaroori baat — ye anuman hain, waada nahi. Koi badi khabar, result, ya bazaar ka jhatka in sab level ko ek jhatke me tod deta hai. Us din pivot ka koi matlab nahi rehta.

Istemaal ka practical tarika

Anubhavi trader inhe akele istemaal nahi karte. Kuch tarike jo chalte hain:

  • Stop-loss lagane ki jagah — S1 ke thoda neeche stop-loss lagana, sirf "2% neeche" se behtar hai, kyunki wo chart par ek asli jagah hai.
  • Target tay karne me — agar entry pivot ke paas hai to R1 ek waajib target hai.
  • Din ka rukh samajhne me — subah bhaav pivot ke upar khula ya neeche, ye din ka mood bata deta hai.
  • Doosre sanket ke saath — agar R1 ke paas ek bada moving average bhi hai, to wo level aur mazboot ho jaata hai.
Sabse aam galti — sirf isliye sauda kar dena ki bhaav S1 par aa gaya. Level chhoona ek sanket hai, sauda ka aadesh nahi. Bhaav wahan ruka ya nahi — ye dekh kar hi kadam uthaiye.

Isse zyada zaroori kya hai

Chart ke level seekhna aasaan hissa hai. Jo cheezein sach me farak laati hain wo ye hain:

  • Position size — ek sauda me 1-2% se zyada jokhim nahi. Isse zyada kuch nahi bachata. Position Size Calculator
  • Risk-reward — bina 1:2 ke sauda hi mat kijiye. R:R Calculator
  • Charges ka hisaab — chhote target wale saude charges me hi doob jaate hain. Brokerage Calculator
  • Nuksan ka record — apne saudon ka hisaab likhiye. Zyadatar log ye nahi karte, isliye unhe pata hi nahi hota ki wo sach me kama rahe hain ya nahi.
Ek imaandaar baat: SEBI ke aankdon ke hisaab se F&O me trading karne wale zyadatar log ghaate me rehte hain. Isliye trading ko kamai ka pakka zariya maankar poonji mat lagaiye. Sirf wahi paisa lagaiye jo doob jane par bhi zindagi na bigde — aur loan ya credit card se to bilkul nahi.
