CGPA Se Percentage Kaise Nikalein? Poori Guide

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Marksheet par CGPA likha hota hai, par job form, admission aur videsh ki university percentage maangte hain. In dono ke beech conversion samajhna zaroori hai — warna galat number likhne se admission ya application me dikkat ho sakti hai. Ye guide poora tarika aur har board ka formula samjhaati hai.

CGPA aur GPA kya hai?

GPA (Grade Point Average) ek semester ke grade points ka औसत hota hai. CGPA (Cumulative GPA) saare semester ka ausat — yaani poore course ka. Grade points aksar 10-point ya 4-point scale par diye jaate hain.

Bharat me zyadatar board aur university 10-point scale use karte hain. Videsh me aksar 4.0 scale chalta hai. Isliye conversion karte waqt sahi scale samajhna zaroori hai — 10-point ka 8 aur 4-point ka 8 bilkul alag cheezein hain.

CBSE ka formula (9.5)

CBSE ne khud ek aasaan formula diya hai:

CGPA se percentage — alag-alag board ka niyam

Sabse badi uljhan yahi hai ki har jagah formula alag hai.

KahanFormula (aam taur par)
CBSECGPA × 9.5
Kai vishwavidyalay (10 point)(CGPA − 0.5) × 10
Anna University jaisi(CGPA − 0.5) × 10
Kuch autonomous collegeCGPA × 10
4-point GPA (videsh)Alag chart, seedha guna nahi
Naukri ke form me galat formula bharna aage dikkat karta hai. Kai company aur sarkari aavedan me percentage maangi jaati hai, aur baad me marksheet se milaan hota hai. Agar antar nikla to aavedan rad ho sakta hai. Isliye apne college ya board ki marksheet ke peeche dekhiye — zyadatar sansthan wahin apna conversion formula chhaapte hain. Wo na mile to registrar ya exam cell se likhit me poochh lijiye. Kisi doosre college ka formula apne upar mat lagaiye.
Percentage = CGPA × 9.5
Misaal: 8.2 CGPA = 8.2 × 9.5 = 77.9%

Ye 9.5 ka number CBSE ne pichhle saalon ke topper data se nikala hua औसत hai. Ye sirf CBSE ke liye hai — kisi doosri university ke CGPA par 9.5 lagana galat natija de sakta hai. Isliye pehle dekhein ki aapka board/university kaunsa hai.

Engineering aur doosri university ka formula

Har university ka apna formula hota hai — 9.5 sirf CBSE ka hai:

  • 10-point scale: Percentage = CGPA × 10 (kai engineering college).
  • Anna University type: Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10.
  • Kuch VTU ya other: Apna alag formula ya conversion table.

Isliye hamesha apni marksheet ke peeche ya university ki website par diya formula dekhein. 2-4% ka farak admission cutoff me bada mayne rakhta hai — kabhi-kabhi isi se seat milti ya chhootti hai.

Conversion certificate kab chahiye?

Kai jagah sirf formula kaafi nahi hota. Higher studies (khaaskar videsh), kuch government jobs aur competitive exams university ka official conversion certificate maangte hain.

Aise me aap formula se apna anuman laga sakte hain, par final application me university ka certificate ya WES jaisi official evaluation hi maanya hoti hai. Time rahte apne college se ye certificate le lein — aakhri waqt me isme der ho sakti hai jo aapki deadline bigaad de.

Aam galtiyan

Log yahan galti karte hain:

  • Har jagah 9.5 lagana — ye sirf CBSE ka hai, engineering/doosri univ ka nahi.
  • SGPA (ek semester) ko CGPA (poora course) samajh lena.
  • Round-off ki galti — 77.9% ko 78% likhna kai form me galat maana jaata hai.
  • Backlog clear karne ke baad purana CGPA lena — hamesha updated marksheet ka CGPA lein.

Sahi tarika: apni university ka formula lein, poora (final) CGPA use karein, aur zaroorat par official certificate lein. Chhoti si galti application reject karwa sakti hai.

Videsh me padhai ke liye conversion

Agar aap videsh (USA, Canada, Europe) me padhna chahte hain, to yahan ki CGPA ko wahan ke system me badalna aur bhi zaroori ho jaata hai. Zyadatar videshi university WES (World Education Services) ya isi tarah ki official evaluation maangti hain — sirf aapka formula nahi chalta.

USA me aksar 4.0 GPA scale hota hai, jahan aapke 10-point CGPA ko convert kiya jaata hai. Har university ka apna tareeka ho sakta hai — kuch percentage maangti hain, kuch seedhe marksheet. Isliye application se pehle us university ki website par "international credentials" ka section zaroor padhein. Time se WES evaluation karwa lein kyunki isme kuch hafte lag jaate hain. Galat ya adhoora conversion aapki poori application ko atka sakta hai, isliye is step ko halke me na lein.

Placement aur job applications

Job aur campus placement me percentage ka apna alag mahatva hai. Kai company cutoff rakhti hain — jaise "60% throughout" (10th, 12th aur graduation teenon me). Isliye apni sahi percentage jaanna aur sahi formula se nikaalna zaroori hai.

Kuch zaroori baatein: application form me hamesha apni official percentage bharein, andaza nahi. Agar company CGPA maangti hai to CGPA dein, percentage maangti hai to conversion certificate ke hisaab se. Kabhi-kabhi form me "aggregate percentage" maanga jaata hai — ye saare semester ka औसत hota hai, sirf final saal ka nahi. Galat number bharne se application aage jaakar reject ho sakti hai, ya interview me dikkat aa sakti hai. Isliye ek baar apni saari marksheet nikaal kar sahi figure note kar lein, taaki har application me same aur sahi number jaaye. Choti si laaparavahi acchi opportunity chhudwa sakti hai.

Saath me ye bhi kaam aayega — CGPA to Percentage aur CBSE Best of 5 zaroor dekh lein — hisaab aur samajh dono ek jagah mil jaate hain.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

CGPA ko percentage me kaise badlein?

Aam taur par CBSE me CGPA &times; 9.5. Doosri university ka formula alag ho sakta hai.

9.5 se hi kyun guna karte hain?

Ye CBSE ka official multiplier hai, pichhle saalon ke data se nikala gaya औसत.

SGPA aur CGPA me farak?

SGPA ek semester ka, CGPA sabhi semester ka औसत.

Conversion certificate kab chahiye?

Higher studies, videsh aur kuch jobs me university ka official certificate zaroori hota hai.

10 CGPA ka kitna percent?

CBSE formula se 95%. 10-point scale me 100%.
