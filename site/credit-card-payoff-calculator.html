Number kaise nikalta hai

Har mahine bache hue bakaya par byaj judta hai, phir aapki bhari hui rakam katti hai. Agar aap sirf "minimum due" bharte hain to us rakam ka zyadatar hissa byaj me hi chala jaata hai aur mool lagbhag wahi rehta hai. Isi liye minimum due bharte rehne se bakaya saalon tak khatam nahi hota.

Formula: Har mahine: bakaya = (bakaya × (1 + mahine ka byaj)) − bhari rakam
mahine ka byaj = saalana% ÷ 12 ÷ 100

Asli zindagi ki teen misaal

Misaal 1 — 24% dar. ₹1,25,000 bakaya, 24% saalana, ₹5,000 mahina. Bakaya lagbhag 36 mahine me khatam hota hai, kul ₹1,80,000 bharna padta hai — yaani ₹55,000 extra byaj.

Misaal 2 — rakam badhane ka asar. Wahi ₹1,25,000 par ₹10,000 mahina bharein to lagbhag 15 mahine me khatam, aur byaj kareeb ₹22,000. Sirf dugni kist se byaj aadhe se bhi kam.

Misaal 3 — minimum due ka jaal. ₹1 lakh bakaya par 5% minimum due yaani ₹5,000 se shuruaat. Har mahine minimum hi bharte rahein to 7-8 saal lag jaate hain aur byaj mool se zyada ho jaata hai.

Ye galtiyan mat kijiye

  1. Sirf minimum due bharna. Isse late fee to nahi lagti, par byaj poore bakaya par chalta rehta hai. Ye sabse mehngi aadat hai.
  2. Card se cash nikalna. Cash withdrawal par interest-free period milta hi nahi — byaj nikalne ke din se shuru ho jaata hai, saath me alag fee.
  3. Naya card lekar purana bharna. Isse bakaya ghumta rehta hai aur badhta jaata hai. Jad tak kharch na roka jaaye, samasya khatam nahi hoti.
  4. Due date bhool jaana. Ek late payment par fee, byaj aur credit score — teeno ka nuksan hota hai. Auto-debit laga dein.
  5. EMI me badalne ka kharcha na dekhna. Bade kharch ko EMI me badalna theek hai, par processing fee aur GST milakar asli dar dekhein.

Bharatiya sthiti me khaas baatein

  • Byaj dar: Zyadatar card 3-3.5% mahina lete hain, yaani saal ka 36-42%.
  • Interest-free period: Poora bill due date tak bharein to kharch par byaj nahi lagta. Ek rupaya bhi bakaya chhoda to poore bill par byaj shuru.
  • Balance transfer: Doosre bank me kam dar par transfer karke kuch mahine ki raahat mil sakti hai — fee zaroor dekhein.
  • Credit score: Limit ka 30% se zyada istemaal score girata hai.
  • Bank se baat: Bakaya bahut zyada ho gaya ho to bank se EMI plan ya settlement ki baat karein — chhipne se haalat bigadti hai.

Sirf minimum due bharne par kitna samay lagta hai

Minimum due aksar bakaya raqam ka 5% hota hai. Wo bharte rehne se card band nahi hota, par karza lagbhag khatam bhi nahi hota.

Sirf minimum (5%)lagbhag 9 saal+₹1.3 lakh se zyada
₹5,000 har mahine (fix)lagbhag 30 mahine~₹48,000
₹10,000 har mahine (fix)lagbhag 12 mahine~₹20,000
₹20,000 har mahine (fix)lagbhag 6 mahine~₹9,000
Wo baat jo bank nahi bataata: jis mahine aap poora bill nahi bharte, us mahine se aapka interest-free period khatam ho jaata hai. Uske baad har nayi kharidari par byaj usi din se lagta hai — 50 din ki chhoot nahi milti. Isliye minimum bharne ka sabse bada nuksan byaj ki dar nahi, ye niyam hai. Ek hi rasta seedha kaam karta hai: jab tak bakaya poora khatam na ho, us card se nayi kharidari band kar dein.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Credit card ka byaj kitna hota hai?

Aam taur par 3-3.5% mahina, yaani saal ka 36-42%.

Minimum due bharna kaafi hai?

Nahi. Isse sirf late fee bachti hai, byaj poore bakaya par chalta rehta hai.

Bakaya jaldi kaise khatam karein?

Har mahine ki rakam badhayein, card ka naya istemaal rokein, aur zaroorat ho to kam dar wale loan me shift karein.

Kya card ka byaj tax me chhoot deta hai?

Nahi, nijee kharch par koi chhoot nahi milti.

Late payment se score girta hai?

Haan, aur uska asar kai mahine tak rehta hai.

Credit card settlement percentage kya hota hai?

Settlement matlab bank se kam rakam par maamla khatam karna — aksar bakaya ka 40-70%. Par isse credit report par 'settled' likha jaata hai, jo kai saal tak naya loan mushkil kar deta hai. Ye aakhri raasta hai, pehla nahi.

Credit card repayment ka hisaab kaise lagta hai?

Bakaya rakam, byaj dar aur aap har mahine kitna de rahe hain — teeno se pata chalta hai kitne mahine lagenge aur kul kitna byaj jayega. Sirf minimum due dene par ye samay saalon me chala jaata hai.
