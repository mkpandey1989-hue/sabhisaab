Overtime Pay Calculator

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~4 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Nirdhaarit ghante se zyada kaam kiya to overtime milta hai — aksar normal se 1.5x ya 2x rate par. Apna hourly rate (ya monthly salary se), overtime ghante aur multiplier daaliye. Extra kamai aur total pay turant dekh lijiye. Ye tool shift aur hourly workers ke liye khaas kaam ka hai, jinki kamai ka ek bada hissa overtime se aa sakta hai.

Number kaise nikalta hai

Overtime pay = overtime ghante × hourly rate × multiplier. Bharat me Factories Act ke tahat aksar overtime 2x (double) rate par milta hai, par kai jagah 1.5x ya company policy ke hisaab se hota hai.

Formula:
Overtime pay = OT ghante × hourly rate × multiplier
Misaal: 12 × ₹150 × 2 = ₹3,600

Agar aapko sirf monthly salary pata hai, to hourly rate = monthly ÷ (kaam ke din × roz ke ghante). Ye tool overtime ka extra hissa aur uski keemat saaf dikha deta hai.

Overtime ka hisaab saaf rakhna dono taraf ke liye achha hai — aapko sahi paisa milta hai aur company ka record theek rehta hai. Isliye apne overtime ghante ka apna alag record (date aur ghante) rakhein, taaki salary slip se milaan kar sakein. Dhyan rahe ki overtime ki rakam aapki kul income me judti hai, isliye ye tax ke daayre me aa sakti hai. Kuch jagah overtime cash me diya jaata hai aur slip me nahi dikhta — aise mamlon me likhit record aur bhi zaroori ho jaata hai taaki baad me koi vivad na ho.

Bharat me shram kanoon (labour law) kai workers ko overtime ka kanooni haq deta hai, khaaskar factory aur daily-wage kaam me. Par kai baar workers ko apna sahi haq pata hi nahi hota, ya wo maang nahi paate. Apna hourly rate aur overtime ghante jaan kar aap apni asli kamai samajh sakte hain aur zaroorat par sahi baat rakh sakte hain. Overtime record aur salary slip milaana isi liye zaroori hai.

Asli zindagi ki teen misaal

Misaal 1 — factory (2x). ₹150/ghanta, 12 ghante OT, 2x. Overtime pay = 12 × 150 × 2 = ₹3,600 extra.

Misaal 2 — 1.5x policy. Same par 1.5x. Overtime = 12 × 150 × 1.5 = ₹2,700.

Misaal 3 — monthly se. ₹30,000/mah, 26 din, 8 ghante = hourly ~₹144. 10 ghante OT (2x) = ₹2,880 extra.

Ye galtiyan mat kijiye

  1. Multiplier bhoolna. Overtime aksar 1.5x ya 2x hota hai — normal rate se galat kam nikalta hai.
  2. Hourly rate galat. Monthly se hourly nikaalte waqt asli kaam ke din aur ghante lein.
  3. Legal rate na jaanna. Factories Act me kuch workers ko double overtime ka haq hota hai — niyam dekhein.
  4. Tax bhoolna. Overtime bhi income me judta hai — tax lag sakta hai.
  5. Verbal promise par bharosa. Overtime rate likhit policy me hona chahiye — warna vivad ho sakta hai.

Bharatiya sthiti me khaas baatein

  • Factories Act: Kuch workers ke liye overtime kanooni taur par double rate par dena zaroori hai.
  • White vs blue collar: Manager/salaried ko aksar overtime nahi milta; hourly/shift workers ko milta hai.
  • Company policy: Har company ka apna OT niyam aur multiplier hota hai — HR se confirm karein.
  • Record rakhein: Overtime ghante ka apna record rakhein — claim me kaam aata hai.

Thodi aur gehraai se

Overtime ki ganana har jagah ek jaisi nahi hoti. Kai company nirdhaarit ghante se upar ke kaam ka 1.5x ya 2x rate deti hain, jabki factory aur labour law me alag niyam hote hain. Isi liye log "overtime kaise calculate karein", "OT rate kya hota hai", "overtime pay calculator" search karte hain. Sabse pehle apna per-hour rate nikaalna hota hai (monthly salary ÷ mahine ke kaam ke ghante), phir overtime ghante ko OT multiplier se guna.

Ek zaroori baat — overtime pay taxable income me judta hai, isliye zyada OT karne par tax slab bhi upar ja sakta hai. Aur har company ki OT policy alag hoti hai; kuch fixed allowance dete hain, kuch actual ghante ke hisaab se. Apni in-hand salary samajhni ho to hamara CTC to In-Hand, aur ghante ke hisaab se kamai jaanni ho to Salary Per Hour tool dekh lein — dono OT ke saath kaam aate hain.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Overtime pay kaise nikalti hai?

Overtime ghante &times; hourly rate &times; multiplier (aksar 1.5x ya 2x).

Bharat me overtime rate kya hai?

Factories Act me aksar double (2x); company policy ke hisaab se 1.5x bhi ho sakta hai.

Monthly salary se hourly kaise?

Monthly &divide; (kaam ke din &times; roz ke ghante).

Kya overtime par tax lagta hai?

Haan, ye income me judta hai aur tax slab ke hisaab se tax lag sakta hai.

Salaried ko overtime milta hai?

Aksar nahi — ye zyada tar hourly/shift workers ko milta hai. Policy dekhein.

Overtime ka paisa kaise banta hai

Kaarkhana kanoon me overtime aam taur par dugni dar par milta hai. Rs 20,000 mahine ki tankhwah par lagbhag hisaab:

KyaHisaabRaqam
Ek din ki tankhwah20,000 ÷ 26Rs 769
Ek ghante ki dar769 ÷ 8Rs 96
Overtime ki dar (dugni)96 × 2Rs 192
20 ghante overtime192 × 20Rs 3,840
Yahan sabse aam galti 26 aur 30 ki hai: ek din ki tankhwah nikaalte waqt mahine ko 26 din maana jaata hai, 30 nahi — kyunki saptahik chhutti tankhwah me pehle se shaamil hoti hai. 30 se bhaag dene par har ghante ki dar lagbhag 13% kam nikal aati hai. Niyam alag-alag rajya aur udyog me thode alag hote hain, aur management wale padon par overtime ka niyam lagu nahi hota. Apni company ke niyam aur salary slip se milaan zaroor kar lijiye.
Zaroori baat: Overtime ke niyam aur rate kanoon (Factories Act) aur company policy par nirbhar karte hain. Apne rozgaar anubandh aur HR se sahi rate confirm karein.
