Zakat Kaise Nikalein? Poori Aasaan Guide

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Zakat Islam ke paanch stambhon me se ek hai — apni bachi hui sampatti ka ek hissa zaroortmand ko dena. Par sawaal aksar yahi hota hai: "kitni sampatti par lagti hai, kya-kya ginte hain, aur kitni deni hoti hai?" Is guide me hum ise bilkul aasaan bhasha me samjhenge, step by step.

Zakat kya hai?

Zakat ek nishchit hissa (aam taur par 2.5%) hai jo har saal apni us sampatti par diya jaata hai jo ek nyoontam seema (nisab) se upar ho aur poore chandra varsh aapke paas rahi ho.

Ye sirf ek khairaat nahi, balki ek deeni zimmedari maani jaati hai. Iska maqsad daulat ka behtar bantwara aur zaroortmand logon ki madad hai. Maana jaata hai ki Zakat dene se sampatti "shuddh" hoti hai — isi liye ise ada karna zaroori maana gaya hai.

Nisab kya hota hai?

Nisab wo nyoontam seema hai — agar aapki zakat-yogya sampatti isse kam hai, to Zakat nahi banti. Ise do tarah se aankte hain:

Nisab:
Sona: 87.48 gram ki keemat
Chaandi: 612.36 gram ki keemat

Aam taur par chaandi wala nisab kam hota hai, isliye zyadatar log usse compare karte hain — taaki zyada log Zakat ke daayre me aayein aur zaroortmand ko fayda pahunche. Aaj ke bhaav se sona/chaandi ki keemat le kar nisab nikaalein, kyunki bhaav har waqt badalta rehta hai.

Kis cheez par Zakat lagti hai?

Zakat aapki bachat aur nivesh par lagti hai, roz istemaal ki cheezon par nahi:

  • Lagti hai: Cash, bank balance, sona, chaandi, share aur mutual fund, business ka bikne wala maal, aur wapas aane wala udhaar.
  • Nahi lagti: Rehne ka ghar, personal gaadi, roz ke kapde aur saman, kaam ke auzaar.

Aasaan tarika samajhne ka: jo aapki "badhti daulat" hai us par Zakat hai, aur jo "roz istemaal ki cheez" hai us par nahi. Isliye ghar aur gaadi jaisi zaroori cheezein chhoot jaati hain.

Zakat ka hisaab kaise karein?

Teen aasaan step hain:

  1. Jodo: Saara cash, sona, chaandi, nivesh aur wapas aane wala udhaar jodein.
  2. Ghatao: Turant dena wala karz ghata dein.
  3. 2.5% nikaalo: Agar bachi rakam nisab se upar hai, to uska 2.5% Zakat hai.

Nisab — kitne paise par zakat wajib hoti hai

Nisab do dhaton se naapa jaata hai. Jo bhi hisaab lagu ho, uske hisaab se seema badalti rehti hai.

Chaandi ka nisab sone se kaafi kam paisa banta hai, isliye us hisaab se zyada log zakat ke daayre me aa jaate hain — aur kai vidwaan garibon ke faayde ke liye wahi paimana pasand karte hain. Jahan nakad, sona aur vyapaar ka maal mila-jula ho, wahan aam taur par chaandi wala nisab dekha jaata hai. Bhaav roz badalta hai, isliye har saal hisaab ke din ka bhaav lena zaroori hai. Ye tool sirf ganit ke liye hai — nisab, tareekh aur mas'ale ke liye apne aalim ya mufti se hi poochhiye. Hum deeni raay nahi dete.
Misaal: Cash ₹2 lakh + sona ₹1.5 lakh + nivesh ₹50k = ₹4 lakh. Turant dena karz ₹30k ghata = ₹3.7 lakh. Zakat = 2.5% = ₹9,250.

Agar bachi rakam nisab se neeche hai, to us saal Zakat nahi banti.

Zakat kab aur kaise dein?

Zakat tab banti hai jab aapki sampatti poore chandra (Hijri) varsh nisab se upar rahi ho. Chandra saal solar saal se ~11 din chhota hota hai, isliye har saal Zakat ki tareekh thodi aage aati jaati hai.

Bahut log Ramzan me Zakat dena pasand karte hain kyunki us mahine sawab zyada maana jaata hai, par aap saal poora hone par kisi bhi samay de sakte hain. Ek tareekh (jaise har saal Ramzan ki koi fix taareekh) tay kar lena sabse aasaan rehta hai — isse hisaab yaad rakhna simple ho jaata hai.

Zakat kisko di ja sakti hai?

Zakat sirf kuch nishchit logon ko hi di ja sakti hai. Quran me aath shreni batayi gayi hain — jinme mukhya hain: gareeb aur zaroortmand, wo log jinke paas nisab se kam ho, karz me dabe log, aur musafir jo pardes me phans gaye hon.

Zakat aksar apne najdeeki zaroortmand rishtedaar (jo aapke seedhe zimpledar nahi, jaise maa-baap ya bachche nahi) ko dena zyada sawab wala maana jaata hai — isse rishta bhi judta hai aur madad bhi hoti hai. Kai log vishwasneey sanstha (jaise madrasa, orphanage, ya registered charity) ke zariye bhi Zakat baantte hain, taaki wo sahi haath tak pahunche. Zakat dete waqt niyat saaf rakhein aur dikhawe se bachein. Agar aap khud kisi zaroortmand ko jaante hain, to seedhe madad karna sabse accha rehta hai.

Aam galtiyan jo log karte hain

Zakat nikalte waqt kuch aam galtiyan hoti hain jinse bachna chahiye. Pehli — nisab check na karna. Log seedhe 2.5% nikaal dete hain bina ye dekhe ki unki sampatti nisab se upar hai ya nahi. Doosri — sona ka purana bhaav lena; hamesha aaj ke bhaav se keemat lagayein.

Teesri galti — roz istemaal ki cheezon (ghar, gaadi) ko sampatti me jod dena, jo galat hai. Chauthi — chandra varsh ko bhool jaana; Zakat solar saal par nahi, Hijri saal par banti hai jo ~11 din chhota hota hai. Aur paanchvi — diya gaya udhaar (jo wapas aayega) ko na ginna, jabki wo bhi aapki sampatti hai. In galtiyon se bachne ka aasaan tarika: har saal ek hi tareekh par baith kar theek se hisaab karein, aur shak ho to kisi jaankaar se poochein. Sahi Zakat dena hi iska asli maqsad poora karta hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Zakat kitni deni hoti hai?

Zakat-yogya sampatti ka 2.5%, agar wo nisab se upar ho.

Nisab kya hai?

Nyoontam seema — 87.48g sona ya 612.36g chaandi ki keemat. Aam taur par chaandi wala kam hota hai.

Kya ghar-gaadi par Zakat lagti hai?

Nahi, rehne ka ghar aur personal use ki cheezein Zakat me nahi ginti.

Karz ghata sakte hain?

Haan, turant dena wala karz zakat-yogya rakam se ghata sakte hain.

Zakat kab deni chahiye?

Jab sampatti poore chandra varsh nisab se upar rahe. Bahut log Ramzan chunte hain.
