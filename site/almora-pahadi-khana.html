Almora ke Mashhoor Mukhya Vyanjan

Kafuli, Phaanu, Chainsoo, Thechwani, Bhatt ki Churkani, Dubuk, Ras-Bhaat, Gahat ki Dal & Jholi

Seedha jawab: Almora aur Kumaon ke mukhya pahadi vyanjan hain: Kafuli (palak-methi ki goondh kar bani garam sabzi), Chainsoo (bhuni urad dal ki dal), Bhatt ki Churkani, Dubuk, Phaanu, Thechwani, Gahat ki Dal, Ras-Bhaat aur Jholi. Inme lohe ki kadhai aur kam masale ka prayog hota hai.

Pahadi khana swaad se zyada sehat aur mausam ka khana hai. Sardi me garam taseer wali dalen, garmi me halka jholi-bhaat. Aur sabse khaas baat: zyadatar vyanjan lohe ki kadhai me dheemi aanch par pakte hain, jisse unme ek alag soundh aati hai.

KafuliPalak aur methi ko ubaal kar peese hue chawal ke aate ke saath pakai gayi hari, goondh jaisi sabziLohe ki kadhai me bani, bhaat ke saath

Kafuli banane ka tareeka (4 logon ke liye):

Samagri: 500 gram palak, 150 gram methi (taazi), 3 chamach chawal ka aata (ya besan se kaam chalta hai), 1 chamach sarson tel, 1 chamach ghee, 1 tsp jakhiya/jeera, 2 hari mirch, 1 tsp namak, 2 lahsun.

Vidhi: Palak aur methi ko dho kar ubal lein aur mota pees lein. Lohe ki kadhai me tel garam kar jakhiya ka tadka dein. Lahsun aur mirch daalein. Peesi hui hari sabzi daalein. Chawal ke aate ko paani me ghol kar dheere-dheere milate hue paka lein, jab tak gadhi sabzi ban jaye. Upar se ghee daalein. Garam bhaat ke saath parosein.

Chainsoo banane ka tareeka (4 logon ke liye):

Samagri: 1 cup sabut kaali urad dal, 2 chamach sarson tel, 1 tsp jakhiya, 2 sukhi lal mirch, 1 tsp haldi, 1 tsp namak, 3 cup paani.

Vidhi: Lohe ki kadhai me urad dal ko bina tel ke halki aanch par bhunein jab tak sugandh na aaye (jale nahi). Thanda karke mixer me mota pees lein. Kadhai me tel garam kar jakhiya aur lal mirch ka tadka dein. Peesi dal, haldi, namak aur paani daal kar dheemi aanch par 25-30 minute pakayein, jab tak gaadhi ho jaye. Bhaat ke saath khayein.

Note: Matra aur paani ka anupaat gaon-gaon ke hisaab se badalta hai. Hum yahan sabse aasan ghar ka version de rahe hain.

Almora: Paramparik Roti aur Chawal ke Pakwan

Mandua ki Roti, Gahat ke Parathe, Chhoi/Lesu Roti, Jhangore ki Bhaat

Seedha jawab: Almora me sardi ki sabse khaas roti Mandua (Ragi) ki roti hai, jo ghee ya gur ke saath khayi jati hai. Gahat ke parathe, lesu roti (mandua ya gehu ki bhari roti), Jhangore ka bhaat aur Gahat ras-bhaat pahadi rasoi ke mukhya rooti-chawal vyanjan hain.

VyanjanKya haiKab / Kaise
Mandua ki RotiRagi ke aate ki kali-bhuri rotiSardi, ghee/gur/bhangjeera chutney ke saath
Gahat ke ParatheGahat dal bhar kar bane paratheNaashte ya subah ke khane me
Lesu RotiGehu ki roti jisme mandua/dal ki bharvan hoTyoharon me
Jhangore ka BhaatBarnyard millet ka bhaatVrat aur roz ke khane me
Lal ChawalPahadi lal dhan ka bhaatDal-rayta ke saath

Mandua ki roti banane ka tareeka (2 logon ke liye):

Samagri: 1 cup mandua ka aata, 1/4 cup gehu ka aata (jisse roti na toote), garam paani, namak ek chutki, ghee.

Vidhi: Mandua aur gehu ka aata mila kar garam paani se dheere-dheere goondh lein, jab tak narm aur chikna dough na bane. Gola banakar haath se dabaayein ya patli plastic par belein. Garam tawe par dono taraf sekein aur seedhe aanch par phulayein. Garam-garam ghee ke saath khayein.

Tip: Agar roti toot rahi ho, to aate me thoda gehu ka aata aur garam paani badhayein. Mandua ka aata thanda paani se nahi goondha jata.

Almora: Chutney, Raita, Achaar aur Pisyun Loon

Bhangjeera Chutney, Bhang ki Chutney, Pahadi Raita, Darim Chutney, Timur Chutney & Mooli Achar

Seedha jawab: Pahadi khane ki jaan chutney aur raita me hai. Bhangjeera (Perilla beej) ki chutney, bhune hue bhang ke dane (hemp seed) ki chutney, kheere ka pahadi raita, darim (jangli anar) ki chutney aur timur ki chutney Kumaon ki pehchan hain. Yeh chhote swaad ghar ke khane ko khaas bana dete hain.

Kanooni note: Bhang ke dane (hemp seeds) ki chutney Uttarakhand ki paramparagat khane ki cheez hai, jisme sirf bhune hue beej istemal hote hain, nasha dene wala patta ya phool nahi. Beej ke alawa bhang ke paudhe ke kisi bhi hisse ka sevan ya bikri kanoonan pratibandhit ho sakti hai.

Chutney / RaitaMukhya samagriKisi ke saath
Bhangjeera chutneyBhune bhangjeera ke dane, nimbu, hari mirch, namakMandua ki roti, bhaat
Pahadi Kheere ka RaitaPahadi kheera, dahi, bhuni raiPoore khane ke saath
Darim chutneyJangli anar ke dane, namak, mirchDal-bhaat
Timur chutneyTimur (Zanthoxylum), lahsun, namakRoti, naashta
Mooli ka AcharMooli, sarson tel, masaleSardi me khane ke saath

Pahadi Kheere ka Raita (rai ke teekhepan wala): Pahadi kheera bahut bada hota hai. Kheere ko kaddukas kar paani nichod lein. Dahi me bhuni peesi rai, haldi, namak aur zara sa jeera daal kar mila lein. Rai ki nasal me chadhne wali teekhi jhaal is raita ki pehchan hai. Iska swaad ek baar chakhne par yaad rehta hai.

Bhangjeera ki chutney banane ka tareeka: Bhangjeera ke dane ko bina tel ke halki aanch par bhun lein jab tak sugandh na aaye. Thanda karke silbatte par peesein. Nimbu ka ras, hari mirch, namak aur zarurat ke hisaab se paani daal kar chutney banayein. Silbatte par pisne se swaad behtar aata hai.

Pisyun Loon (Haath se Pisa Swaad Wala Namak)

Seedha jawab: Pisyun Loon pahadi namak ka khaas masala hai, jise silbatte par saadha namak, lahsun ke patte, hari mirch, dhaniya, jeera aur timur ke saath pees kar banate hain. Isko roti, kheere, mooli ya fal par chhidak kar khaya jata hai.

Pisyun Loon ki samagri (1 chhote dibbe ke liye): 4 chamach pahadi/sendha namak, 1 chamach bhuna jeera, 1 chamach dhaniya beej, 10-12 timur ke dane, 2 hari mirch, lahsun ke patte ya 3 kaliyan, thoda sa pudina.

Vidhi: Sabhi cheezein silbatte par dheere-dheere ragad kar pees lein. Agar silbatta na ho to kharal-musal ka upyog karein. Hawa band dibbe me rakhein aur 15 din ke andar upyog karein (hara lahsun patta ho to jaldi bigad jata hai).

Parampara: Pahadi parivaar me gaon ki mahilayein ek-dusre ke saath baith kar pisyun loon banati thi, jo ek sanjhi parampara bhi thi.

Almora: Jungli Khane Layak Cheezein aur Mausami Saag

Bicchu Ghas (Sisunak) Saag, Linguda, Kankoda, Ghingaru, Kaphal, Hisalu, Kilmora, Bedu & Buransh

Seedha jawab: Almora ke jungle aur khet ki mendon par mausami jangli saag aur phal milte hain: Bicchu Ghas (Sisunak/Nettle) ki saag, Linguda (jangli fern), Kankoda, Ghingaru, Kaphal, Hisalu, Kilmora aur Bedu (jangli anjeer). Buransh ke phool ka sharbat garmiyon ki khaas pehchan hai.

Suraksha ki chetavani: Jangal ki koi bhi cheez kisi anubhavi gaon ke buzurg ya vishesagya ki pehchan ke bina na khayein. Kai jangli paudhe aur mushroom zehrile hote hain. Is jankari ko khud se jangal me khane ki salah na samjhein.

Bicchu Ghas (Sisunak)Barsaat se pehle aur baadUbaal kar saag (ubalne se chubhan khatam ho jati hai)
Linguda (fern)BarsaatAchhi tarah pakakar sabzi aur achar
KankodaBarsaatSabzi
KaphalMay-JuneKachha phal, namak-mirch ke saath
Hisalu (jangli raspberry)GarmiSeedha khane ke liye
KilmoraGarmiKhatta-meetha phal

Bicchu Ghas ki saag banane ka tareeka: Dastane pehen kar komal patte todein (bina dastane ke chubhan hoti hai). Paani me ubaal lein, jisse chubhan khatam ho jati hai. Paani nichod kar kaat lein. Lohe ki kadhai me tel, jakhiya aur lahsun ka tadka daal kar bhunein. Namak daalein aur garam bhaat ya mandua ki roti ke saath khayein. Iron aur poshan se bharpoor hone ke kaaran sardi aur barsaat me iska prayog hota hai.

Kaphal: “Kaphal pakko, maine nahi chakho” (Kaphal paka, maine nahi chakha) Kumaoni lok-geet ki prasiddh pankti hai. Kaphal ki yeh kahani ek bhavuk lok-katha se judi hai, jise Almora: Lok-Kathayein: Achhari, Veer Yoddha aur Rahasyamay Kisse aur Almora: Lok-Sangeet, Lok-Gaathayein aur Gayan Parampara me aap padhenge.

Buransh ka sharbat: Buransh ke phool ko saaf paani se dhokar, ubaal kar aur cheeni ke saath sharbat banaya jata hai. Sirf Rhododendron arboreum (Buransh) ke phool upyog hote hain. Doosre Rhododendron ki kai prajatiyan zehrili hoti hain, isliye kisi anubhavi vyakti se pehchan karwayein. Bazaar me taiyar buransh sharbat milta hai.

Almora: Paramparik Mithai, Tyohar ke Pakwan aur Prasad

Bal Mithai, Singodi, Chocolate, Arsa, Rote, Puwa, Singal, Jhangore ki Kheer & Gulgule

Seedha jawab: Almora ki sabse mashhur mithai Bal Mithai hai, jo bhune hue khoye aur cheeni ke chhote dano se bani bhuri chocolate jaisi barfi hoti hai. Singodi (khoye ko Malu ke patte me lapetkar banai jati hai) bhi Almora ki khaas pehchan hai. Iske alawa Arsa, Rote, Puwa, Singal aur Gulgule tyoharon ki mithaiyan hain.

Bal Mithai ka itihaas: Prachlit manyata ke anusar Bal Mithai ki shuruaat 20vi sadi ke shuru me Almora ke ek halwai ne ki thi (aam taur par Lala Joga Ram Shah ka naam liya jata hai). Aaj yeh mithai Almora ki sabse badi pehchan ban chuki hai, aur Bal Mithai ko Geographical Indication (GI) jaisi pehchan dilane ke prayas hote rahe hain.

Bal Mithai ghar par banane ka tareeka (aasan version):

Samagri: 250 gram khoya, 150 gram cheeni (peesi hui), 1 chamach ghee, 100 gram cheeni ke dane (saaf, chhote dane) ya poppy-seed (khaskhas) ki cheeni-lapet.

Vidhi: Kadhai me khoye ko dheemi aanch par bhunein, jab tak woh gehra bhura aur sugandhit na ho jaye. Peesi cheeni mila kar sath me hilayein. Ghee lagi thali me jama dein. Jab halka thanda ho jaye to barfi ke tukde kaat lein. Har tukde ko cheeni ke dano me lapet dein. Hawa band dibbe me 7 din tak rakh sakte hain.

Arsa (Garhwal-Kumaon ki mithai): Arsa chawal ko bhigo kar sukhaya, pees kar aur gur ki chashni me goondh kar sarson tel me tala jata hai. Isme tel ki sugandh aur gur ki mithaas milkar alag swaad dete hain. Shaadi aur tyoharon me ise parosa jata hai.

Almora: Paramparik Peya aur Khameer Wale Pey

Chhyang/Jaan, Buransh Squash, Malta Juice & Gud-Adrak ki Chai

Seedha jawab: Pahadi peya me garmi ka Buransh ka sharbat, sardi ki gud-adrak ki chai aur sthaniya phalon ka juice (jaise malta) shamil hain. Chhyang/Jaan jaise paramparik fermented peya mukhya roop se uchch Himalayi kshetron ke Bhotia aur janjaati samudayon ki sanskriti ka hissa hain.

Dhyan dein: Chhyang/Jaan ek paramparik, kam alcohol wala fermented peya hai, jo mukhya roop se Pithoragarh aur Chamoli ke uchch Himalayi gaon me bana karta tha. Almora ki pehchan nahi hai. Yeh jankari sirf sanskritik hai.

PeyaKab piya jata haiKhaas baat
Buransh ka sharbat/squashGarmiHriday aur garmi ke liye thanda, lal rang
Gud-Adrak ki ChaiSardiGur, adrak aur chai ka garam mel
Malta/Narangi juiceSardiTaaza pahadi phal ka ras
Chaach/MathaGarmiPahadi dahi ka chhach
Haldi-doodhSardiGharelu parampara

Gud-Adrak ki chai (2 logon ke liye): 2 cup paani, 1 inch adrak (kuchla hua), 1 chamach chai patti, 1 chhota tukda gur, 1 cup doodh. Pehle paani me adrak aur chai patti ubaalein. Doodh daalein aur ek ubaal aane par gur daal kar chhan lein. (Gur ko ant me daalein, nahi to doodh phat sakta hai.)

Almora: Pahadi Kitchen Architecture & Utensils

Loh ki Kadhai, Silbatta, Kansi Thali, Bhaddu, Parat & Mud Chulha with Dhumala

Seedha jawab: Pahadi rasoi ke mukhya bartan aur saaman hain: Lohe ki kadhai, Silbatta (peesne ka patthar), Kansi ki thali, Bhaddu (bhaari tambe-peetal ka bartan), Parat aur mitti ka chulha. Chulhe ke upar Dhumala (dhuein se sukhane ki laddi ka mancha) hota hai.

SaamanKaamKhaas baat
Lohe ki KadhaiDal, saag aur sabzi paknaIron ki matra badhta hai, dheemi pakaai
SilbattaChutney aur masale peesnaHaath se pisne ka alag swaad
Kansi ki ThaliKhana khane ke liyeParamparik dhaatu ka bartan
BhadduDal ko dheemi aanch par pakane ke liyeBhaari tambe-peetal ka bartan
ParatAata goondhna, bhaat dhoknaBadi thali
Mitti ka ChulhaLakdi par khanaDhuyen ki sugandh khane me
DhumalaChulhe ke upar laddi ka manchaBeej, lakdi aur lahsun sukhane ke liye

Almora ke Tamta aur tambe ke bartan: Almora ka Tamta samudaay tambe (copper) ki dastkari ke liye jana jata hai. Tambe ke bartan, gagar aur gharelu saaman pahadi rasoi ka hissa rahe hain. Is kala par aur uske bazaar par alag se Almora: Hathkargha, GI-Tag Utpad aur Sthaniya Hastshilp me vistar se aayega.

Lohe ki kadhai ke fayde aur dhyan: Lohe ki kadhai me pakane se khane me thodi iron milti hai. Isse saaf rakhne ke liye use pani se dhokar turant sukha lein aur halka tel laga kar rakhein, taki jung na lage. Khatti cheezein (imli, tamatar) zyada der lohe me na pakayein.

Dhumala aur pahadi ghar ka vigyan: Chulhe ka dhuaan upar uthkar dhumala ki laddi, beej aur lahsun me samata hai. Isse beej me keede nahi lagte aur laddi pakki aur sukhi rehti hai. Yeh pahadi ghar ki chhoti si engineering hai, jise Almora: Anaj Store Karne ki Purani Engineering me aur samjhaya jayega.
