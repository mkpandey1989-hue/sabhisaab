Peeche ka ganit

Bharat me ginti ka tareeka duniya se alag hai. Yahan lakh aur crore chalte hain, jabki angrezi mein million aur billion. Isliye 1,25,777 ko "One Lakh Twenty Five Thousand Seven Hundred Seventy Seven" likha jaata hai, "One Hundred Twenty Five Thousand" nahi. Comma bhi Indian tareeke se lagta hai — 12,34,567.

Formula: Indian format: Crore → Lakh → Thousand → Hundred → Tens
Cheque line: "Rupees <shabd> Only"

Kuch misaal jo saaf kar dein

Misaal 1 — cheque ki rakam. 1,25,777 → One Lakh Twenty Five Thousand Seven Hundred Seventy Seven Rupees. Cheque par: "Rupees One Lakh Twenty Five Thousand Seven Hundred Seventy Seven Only".

Misaal 2 — round rakam. 50,000 → Fifty Thousand Rupees. 5,00,000 → Five Lakh Rupees.

Misaal 3 — crore. 2,50,00,000 → Two Crore Fifty Lakh Rupees. Property ke kaagaz me yahi format chalta hai.

Bharatiya ginti me har do ank ke baad comma lagta hai — 1,25,777 — jabki angrezi tareeke me teen ke baad, jaise 125,777. Cheque bharte waqt bharatiya tareeka hi istemaal karein, kyunki bank ka poora system isi par bana hai.

Ek chhota niyam yaad rakhein: 100 se neeche ke ank angrezi me hyphen ke saath aate hain, jaise Twenty-Five. Cheque par hyphen lagana zaroori nahi, par likhne me galti kam hoti hai. Aur "and" ka istemaal Bharat me rupaye aur paise ke beech hota hai — "One Thousand Rupees and Fifty Paise Only".

Jahan log atakte hain

  1. "Only" chhod dena. Cheque me rakam ke baad "Only" likhna zaroori hai — isse koi aage kuch jod nahi sakta.
  2. Khaali jagah chhod dena. Shabd ke baad line khaali chhodne par usme kuch aur likha ja sakta hai. Line kheench dein.
  3. Lakh ki jagah thousand likhna. International format ki aadat se 1,25,000 ko "One Hundred Twenty Five Thousand" likh dena aam galti hai — Indian cheque me ye galat hai.
  4. Ank aur shabd me farak. Dono jagah rakam ek jaisi honi chahiye. Farak hone par bank cheque lauta deta hai.
  5. Paise ka hissa bhool jaana. ₹1,250.50 ho to "One Thousand Two Hundred Fifty Rupees and Fifty Paise Only" likhna hota hai.

Bharat ke hisaab se dhyaan dein

  • Cheque ki validity: Cheque aam taur par 3 mahine tak hi maanya hota hai.
  • Kaat-chhaat: Cheque me kaat-peet karne par bank lauta sakta hai — nayi cheque bharna behtar hai.
  • Account payee: Do samanantar lakeerein aur "A/c Payee" likhne se cheque sirf khaate me hi jama hota hai.
  • Invoice: GST bill me bhi rakam shabdon me likhna aam practice hai.
  • Rent agreement: Kiraya aur security dono shabdon me likhna jhagde se bachaata hai.

Cheque aur form me likhne ka sahi tarika

Bank aur sarkari kagaz me raqam shabdon me likhne ka apna dhang hai. Isi jagah cheque wapas aata hai.

RaqamCheque par
45,000Forty Five Thousand Only
1,25,500One Lakh Twenty Five Thousand Five Hundred Only
7,50,000Seven Lakh Fifty Thousand Only
2,00,00,000Two Crore Only
Char aadatein jo cheque bounce hone se bachati hain: ek — aakhir me "Only" likhna zaroori hai, isse baad me kuch joda nahi ja sakta. Do — shabd khatam hone ke baad khaali jagah par ek lambi line kheench dein. Teen — ank aur shabd ki raqam bilkul ek honi chahiye; alag hone par bank aksar shabd wali raqam maanta hai, aur kai baar cheque hi lauta deta hai. Chaar — lakh aur crore Bharat ke bank me maanya hain, par videsh bhejne wale cheque ya invoice me "million" likhna behtar rehta hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Cheque par rakam kaise likhein?

"Rupees" se shuru karein, phir Indian format me shabd, aur ant me "Only".

Lakh aur million me kya farak hai?

1 lakh = 1,00,000 aur 1 million = 10,00,000 yaani 10 lakh.

Paise kaise likhein?

"... Rupees and Fifty Paise Only" is tarah.

Kya ye tool crore tak kaam karta hai?

Haan, crore aur usse aage tak.

Comma kahan lagta hai?

Indian format me — 12,34,567 (pehle 3 ank, phir 2-2 ke jode).
