CTC to In-Hand Salary Calculator

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Free · Bina login · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Offer letter par bada package likha hota hai, par account me utna nahi aata. Wajah — PF, tax aur doosre deductions. Ye calculator batata hai ki aapke CTC me se har mahine haath me kitna aane ki ummeed hai.

CTC me kya-kya juda hota hai

CTC ka matlab hai "Cost to Company" — yaani company aap par kul kitna kharch karti hai. Ismein wo cheezein bhi hoti hain jo kabhi aapke account me nahi aatin:

  • Basic salary — sabse zaroori hissa, isi par PF, HRA aur gratuity ka hisaab lagta hai
  • HRA — kiraye ka bhatta
  • Company ka PF hissa — ye aapke PF khaate me jaata hai, haath me nahi
  • Gratuity — 5 saal baad milegi, abhi nahi
  • Bonus / variable pay — performance par milta hai, pakka nahi
  • Insurance premium — company deti hai, aapko cash nahi milta
Isi liye: ₹12 lakh ka CTC ka matlab har mahine ₹1 lakh haath me aana bilkul nahi hai. Aam taur par in-hand CTC ka kareeb 70-80% hota hai.

Salary se kya-kya katta hai

  • Aapka PF — basic ka 12%
  • TDS — aapki income aur regime ke hisaab se
  • Professional tax — kuch rajyon me lagta hai (chhoti rakam)
  • Insurance/canteen — company ke apne niyam

Offer letter dekhte waqt 3 baatein

  1. Basic kitna hai? Zyada basic matlab zyada PF aur zyada gratuity — lambe samay me achha, par in-hand thoda kam.
  2. Variable kitna hai? Agar CTC ka bada hissa variable/bonus hai, to wo pakka nahi hota. Fixed part hi asli salary hai.
  3. Kya-kya "cost" me juda hai? Kuch companies insurance, canteen aur training ka kharch bhi CTC me jod deti hain.

In-hand badhane ka tarika

Agar aap Old Regime me hain to HRA, 80C nivesh aur doosri chhoot se TDS kam karwaya ja sakta hai — isse har mahine ka kata hua tax ghatta hai. New Regime me ye chhoot nahi milti, par rate kam hote hain. Dono ki tulna karke apne liye behtar chunein. Ye calculator ek anuman deta hai; asli figure aapke offer letter ke breakup se hi pakka hoga.

Ek asli misaal — poora hisaab

CTC ₹10,00,000 saalana. Aam dhaanche me:

  • Basic (CTC ka ~40%) = ₹4,00,000
  • HRA (basic ka 50%) = ₹2,00,000
  • Employer PF (basic ka 12%) = ₹48,000 — ye aapke haath me nahi aata
  • Gratuity provision = ~₹19,000 — ye bhi haath me nahi aata
  • Special allowance = bacha hua hissa

Haath me aane se pehle katta hai: employee PF ₹48,000, professional tax, aur income tax (TDS). Nateeja — ₹10 lakh CTC par in-hand aam taur par ₹66,000-72,000 mahina ke aas-paas rehta hai, ₹83,000 nahi.

CTC company ka kul kharcha hota hai, aapki tankhwah nahi. Ek aam dhaanche par hisaab:

Isme se aur do cheezein katti hain: aapka apna PF hissa (basic ka 12%, lagbhag Rs 57,600) aur income tax. Sab kaatne ke baad Rs 12 lakh CTC par haath me aksar Rs 78,000 se 82,000 mahina aata hai — yaani CTC ka lagbhag 79%. Offer letter me "12 LPA" dekhkar 1 lakh mahina maan lena sabse aam galti hai. Naya offer tulte waqt hamesha in-hand poochhiye, CTC nahi.
Isi liye chaunk lagta hai: CTC ko 12 se bhaag dena galat tareeka hai. CTC me wo cheezein bhi juddi hoti hain jo aapko mahine me milti hi nahi.

Log yahan galti kya karte hain

  1. CTC ÷ 12 = salary maan lena. Ye sabse aam galti hai. Asli in-hand aam taur par CTC ka 70-80% hi hota hai.
  2. Offer letter ka breakup na dekhna. Sirf CTC ka number poochna kaafi nahi. Basic kitna hai, PF kaise kata hai, gratuity shaamil hai ya nahi — ye poochein.
  3. Basic ka hissa na dekhna. Basic kam hone se PF aur gratuity dono kam bante hain. In-hand thoda badh jaata hai par lambe samay ka fayda ghat jaata hai.
  4. Variable pay ko pakka maan lena. Bonus aur variable CTC me juda hota hai par performance par milta hai — kabhi poora, kabhi aadha, kabhi bilkul nahi.
  5. Job compare karte waqt sirf CTC dekhna. Do offer me CTC barabar par in-hand alag ho sakta hai, kyunki dhaancha alag hota hai.

Bharat me CTC ki khaas baatein

  • Employer PF: kai company ise CTC me jodti hain. Ye aapka paisa hai, par mahine me haath me nahi aata — retirement par milta hai.
  • Gratuity provision: CTC me dikhti hai par 5 saal poore hone par hi milti hai.
  • Professional tax: kuch rajyon me lagta hai, kuch me nahi — isliye rajya badalne se in-hand thoda badal jaata hai.
  • Salary structure ka tax par asar: HRA, LTA aur kuch allowance par chhoot mil sakti hai (Old Regime me). Isi liye do barabar CTC walon ka tax alag ho sakta hai.
  • Notice period aur joining bonus: ye CTC me nahi dikhte par asli paise par asar daalte hain — offer letter me alag se dekh lein.

CTC se in-hand — asli antar kahan jaata hai

Offer letter me bada number dikhta hai aur khaate me chhota aata hai. Wo antar kahin chori nahi hota — in jagah jaata hai:

  • PF (aapka hissa) — Basic ka 12%. Ye aapka hi paisa hai par abhi haath me nahi aata.
  • PF (company ka hissa) — CTC me joda jaata hai par salary me kabhi nahi aata.
  • Gratuity — CTC me dikhta hai, milta 5 saal poore hone par hai.
  • Income tax (TDS) — har mahine katta hai.
  • Professional tax — rajya ke hisaab se, chhoti rakam.
  • Health insurance premium — kai company ise CTC me jodti hai.
  • Variable ya bonus — CTC me poora gina jaata hai, milta lakshya poora hone par hai.
Mota anuman: in-hand aam taur par CTC ka 70-80% baithta hai. Yaani ₹12 lakh CTC par lagbhag ₹70,000-80,000 mahina. Aur CTC jitni badi, percentage utna kam — kyunki tax slab upar chala jaata hai. 1.5 crore package par in-hand aam taur par 55-60% hi rehta hai.

Salary ka PF kitna percent katta hai

Aapki salary se Basic + DA ka 12% katta hai, aur utna hi company apni taraf se deti hai. Company ke 12% me se ek hissa pension (EPS) me jaata hai aur baaki aapke PF khaate me. Poora hisaab EPF Calculator me hai.

Do offer ki tulna kar rahe hain to CTC mat dekhiye — in-hand aur kaam ke ghante dekhiye. Salary Per Hour Calculator me wo antar saaf dikh jaata hai, aur kai baar chhoti CTC wali naukri asal me behtar nikalti hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

CTC aur in-hand me kitna farak hota hai?

Aam taur par in-hand CTC ka kareeb 70-80% hota hai. PF, tax aur doosre deductions ki wajah se farak aata hai.

Company ka PF hissa haath me aata hai?

Nahi. Wo seedha aapke PF khaate me jaata hai. CTC me judta hai par salary me nahi aata.

Basic salary zyada hona achha hai ya kam?

Zyada basic se PF aur gratuity zyada bante hain — lambe samay me achha. Par in-hand thoda kam ho jaata hai.

Variable pay pakka milta hai?

Nahi. Wo performance aur company ke nateeje par depend karta hai. Offer dekhte waqt fixed part par dhyan dein.

In-hand salary kaise badha sakte hain?

Old Regime me HRA aur 80C jaisi chhoot claim karke TDS kam karwaya ja sakta hai. Dono regime ki tulna karke chunein.

1.5 cr package me in hand salary kitni hogi?

Us star par in-hand aam taur par CTC ka 55-60% rehta hai — tax, PF, gratuity aur variable ghatane ke baad. Exact number aapke salary structure aur chune hue tax regime par nirbhar karta hai.

Salary ka PF kitna percent katta hai?

Basic + DA ka 12% aapki salary se katta hai, aur utna hi company apni taraf se deti hai. Company ke hisse ka kuch bhag pension (EPS) me jaata hai.
