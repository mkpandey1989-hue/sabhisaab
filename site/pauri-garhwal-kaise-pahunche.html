Pauri Garhwal: Hawai aur Helicopter Seva (UDAN aur Charter)

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ka sabse nazdeeki hawai adda Jolly Grant Airport (Dehradun) hai, jo Pauri se lagbhag 131 se 155 km door hai (raste ke hisab se). Zile me koi regular passenger airport nahi hai. Helipad (Pauri, Srinagar aadi) mukhya roop se aapatkaal, VIP aur charter ke liye hain.

Nazdeeki Airport, Helipad, UDAN Heli-Seva aur Charter Booking

Seedha jawab: Hawai yatra ke liye Delhi se Dehradun (Jolly Grant) ki flight lein, phir sadak se Pauri, Srinagar ya Lansdowne jaayein. Charter helicopter Uttarakhand ke registered operators se book hota hai.

Charter helicopter book karne ka tareeka (saamanya):

  • Registered operator chunein: Rajya ke Civil Aviation (UCADA) ke jaanch-parakhe operator ka naam poochhein.
  • Route aur samay batayein: Kahan se kahan, kitne yatri, kab.
  • Vajan ki limit: Helicopter me yatri aur saamaan ka vajan seemit hota hai.
  • Mausam ki shart: Kharab mausam me udaan radd ho sakti hai.
  • Likhit confirmation: Rate, cancellation aur refund likhit me lein.

Salah: Sirf online ya WhatsApp par mile “sasta heli-package” par bharosa na karein. Operator ka registration number aur office ka pata pushti karein.

Jolly Grant se Pauri tak kaise jaayein?
Taxi ya bus se Rishikesh, phir Devprayag-Srinagar ya seedhe Devprayag marg se Pauri. Pauri ke zila prashasan ki jaankari ke anusaar Devprayag me Alaknanda-Bhagirathi sangam par naye pul ke baad Rishikesh se Pauri ka raasta lagbhag 18 km chhota hua.

Pauri Garhwal: Railway Station aur Aane Wali Rail Pariyojanayein

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ke nazdeeki railhead Kotdwar (Pauri se lagbhag 101-108 km) aur Rishikesh (Pauri se lagbhag 117 km) hain. Haridwar aur Ramnagar bhi entry-points hain. Rishikesh-Karnaprayag rail line ke station Pauri Garhwal ke kuch hisson me banne hain, par ETV Bharat ki 1 November 2026 ki report ke anusaar poori line 2029 tak aani hai.

Nazdeeki Railway Station, Delhi-Lucknow-Howrah se Train aur Nayi Rail Line

Seedha jawab: Pauri ke liye sabse upyogi railhead Kotdwar (Garhwal ka pravesh dwar) hai, jahan Delhi aur aas-paas ke shaharon se train aati hai. Rishikesh (Yog Nagari) aur Haridwar doosre vikalp hain. Kathgodam aur Tanakpur Kumaon ke railhead hain, Pauri ke liye upyogi nahi.

Pauri Garhwal: Sadak, Highway, Pul aur Seema Sadak (BRO)

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ke mukhya raaste hain NH-7 (Rishikesh-Devprayag-Srinagar-Rudraprayag), NH-534 (Najibabad-Kotdwar-Satpuli-Bubakhal) aur Pauri ko Kotdwar, Lansdowne aur Srinagar se jodne wala rajya raajmarg. Char Dham All-Weather Road ka hissa NH-7 par Pauri Garhwal ke Devprayag-Srinagar kshetra se guzarta hai. Pauri Garhwal me BRO ke seema-raste nahi hain.

Rashtriya Rajmarg, Char Dham Sadak, Jaam ki Jagah aur BRO ki Sadkein

Seedha jawab: Rishikesh se Srinagar tak NH-7 par chalna padta hai, phir Srinagar se Pauri ka sampark raajmarg se hai. Kotdwar se Pauri ka raasta Satpuli-Lansdowne kshetra ke raaste hota hai. Barish me landslide aur sadak band hone ka khatra Pauri Garhwal: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh me bataye gaye karan se bana rehta hai.

Dhyan dein: Ye doori ke andaze hain. Raste (via Devprayag ya via Srinagar) aur nirman ke hisab se alag ho sakte hain. Google Maps ya zila prashasan ki “How to Reach” jaankari se confirm karein.

Pahadi sadak par 8 surakshit-yatra niyam:

  • Subah jaldi niklein: Shaam ke baad ghati ke mod khatarnaak hote hain.
  • Barish me yatra na karein (khaaskar July-September) agar zaroori na ho.
  • Oonchi gaadi/SUV ko tarjeeh dein agar kaccha ya tooti sadak ho.
  • Brake aur tyres safar se pehle jaanch lein.
  • Overtake karne se bachein mod par.
  • Pahadi sadak par oopar chadhne wali gaadi ko pehle raasta dein.
  • Landslide-prone zone (jaise kuch NH-7 ke hisse) me rukne se bachein.
  • Sthaniya taxi driver ke paas raste ki taaza jaankari hoti hai, unse poochhein.

BRO ke baare me: Border Roads Organisation ke strategic seema-raste (jaise Shivalik, Hirak project) Chamoli, Uttarkashi aur Pithoragarh jaise seema-ghire zilon me hain. Pauri Garhwal ki seema Tibet/Nepal se nahi lagti, isliye BRO ka yahan bahut seemit kaam hai.

Pauri Garhwal: Bus, Shared Taxi (Maxx/Bolero) aur Sthaniya Aavagaman

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal me Uttarakhand Parivahan Nigam (UTC), GMOU (Garhwal Motor Owners’ Union Ltd), KMOU aur private bus chalti hain. Delhi ISBT Kashmiri Gate se Pauri aur Kotdwar ke liye bus milti hai. Chhote raaston par shared taxi (Bolero/Maxx) aam hain, jo seat ke hisab se bhara kiraya lete hain.

Roadways (UTC) Bus ka Samay, KMOU/GMOU/TGMO Bus aur Shared Taxi (Subah ki Pehli Jeep, Prati Seat Kiraya)

Seedha jawab: Roadways aur GMOU ki bus lambi doori ke liye bharosemand hain. Shared taxi sabse tez par seat bharne par chalti hai, isliye subah ki pehli gaadi ka samay roz thoda badal sakta hai. Kiraya season, doori aur taxi union ke hisab se badalta hai.

Bus ke samay aur kiraya Roadways/union tay karti hai aur ye mausam, sadak ki sthiti aur season ke hisab se badalte hain. Galat jaankari dene se yatri ka samay barbaad hota hai, isliye hum yahan sirf tareeka dete hain:

  • UTC ki bus: Uttarakhand Parivahan Nigam ki official website/app par booking aur route dekhein.
  • Bus-stand par poochhein: Pauri, Srinagar aur Kotdwar bus stand par enquiry counter hai.
  • Taxi union se: Pauri, Srinagar aur Kotdwar ke taxi stand par union ka board aur rate-list lagi hoti hai, uski photo le lein.
  • Subah jaldi niklein: Pehli gaadi aksar subah 5-7 baje ke beech hoti hai.

Safety tip: Raat me akele ya anjaan taxi me safar karne se bachein. Parivaar ya mahila yatri ke liye Pauri Garhwal: Akeli Mahila Yatri: Suraksha aur Madad dekhein.

Pauri Garhwal: Petrol Pump, EV Charging aur Mechanic

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal me petrol pump Kotdwar, Satpuli, Lansdowne, Pauri aur Srinagar jaise bade kasbon me milte hain. Oonche aur door ke raaston (Khirsu, Doodhatoli, Binsar) par pump kam hain, isliye tank pehle se bhar lein. EV charging ke liye bhi pehle se plan karein, kyunki pahadi kshetra me charger seemit hain.

Faisla ka seedha niyam hai: Khirsu, Binsar, Doodhatoli ya kisi door gaon jaane se pehle aakhri bade kasbe (Pauri/Satpuli/Srinagar/Lansdowne) me tank full kar lein.

EV (Electric Vehicle) yatra ke 6 niyam:

  • Route pehle check karein: PlugShare ya Statiq jaise app par charger ki jagah dekhein (taaza sthiti app me hi milti hai).
  • Range ka 30% buffer: Pahadi chadhai me battery jaldi khatam hoti hai.
  • Charger ki guarantee nahi: Door ke kasbo me charger kharab ya band ho sakta hai.
  • Kotdwar/Rishikesh/Srinagar: Bade kasbon me charger milne ke chance zyada.
  • Downhill me regeneration: Utarte samay battery thodi wapas charge hoti hai.
  • Ghar ka charger: Homestay se pehle poochh lein ki wahan 15A ya 16A socket hai ya nahi.

Tyre puncture aur chhoti kharabi: Pahadi raaste me tyre aur suspension jaldi kharab hote hain. Spare tyre, jack aur puncture-repair kit saath rakhein. Kotdwar, Srinagar aur Pauri me mechanic milte hain, par door ke gaon me nahi.

Pauri Garhwal: Rukne ki Jagah: GMVN/KMVN, Hotel aur Resort

Booking GMVN ki official website (gmvnonline.com) par hoti hai. Kumaon ke KMVN ke property Pauri me nahi hain.

Sarkari Guest House (GMVN/KMVN), PWD Dak Bungalow, Van Vishram Grih, Resort aur Sasti Lodge

Pauri Garhwal: Mobile Network aur Internet: Asli Sthiti

Seedha jawab: Pauri, Srinagar, Kotdwar aur Lansdowne jaise kasbon me Jio, Airtel, Vi aur BSNL ka 4G aam taur par chalta hai. Gahri ghati, ghane jungle aur door ke gaon me network kamzor ya nahi hota. Kai jagah Jio ya Airtel me se ek hi achha chalta hai, isliye do SIM rakhna surakshit hai.

Kahan Chalta Hai Jio, Airtel, Vi aur BSNL; Bina Network Wale Ilake

Aam niyam: shahar/kasba me achha, sadak par theek, gahri ghati aur jungle-trail par kamzor. 5G abhi mukhya roop se bade shaharon me aa raha hai, Pauri Garhwal ke har gaon me nahi.

Network-sambandhit 6 tips:

  • Do SIM (Jio + Airtel) rakhein, ek na chale to doosra.
  • Offline map download karein (Google Maps offline).
  • Trek par jaane se pehle ghar par apna route share karein.
  • Emergency number (112) kai baar bina network ke bhi connect ho jaata hai (kisi bhi network se).
  • BSNL kuch door ke ilake me chalta hai jahan private network nahi.
  • Wi-Fi calling aur offline music/maps safar me madad karte hain.

Signal booster ke baare me: Mobile signal booster ka upyog India me licensed/anumodit upkaran tak seemit hai. Bina anumati ke booster ka upyog kanoon ke khilaaf ho sakta hai. Network provider se poochh kar hi lein.

Workation ki asli salah: Pahadi workation me sabse zaroori teen cheezein hain: (1) achhi internet speed, (2) bijli ka backup, (3) paani ki suvidha. Inhe booking se pehle poochhein.

Pauri Garhwal: Bijli, Solar aur Sardi mein Paani ki Sthiti

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal me bijli UPCL (Uttarakhand Power Corporation Ltd) deta hai. Barish aur sardi me ped girne ya line kharab hone se bijli kat sakti hai. Isliye inverter ya solar backup aur pure sine-wave wali battery rakhna samajhdari hai. Sardi me oonche ilake me paani ki pipeline jam sakti hai.

Bijli (UPCL), Sardi mein Katauti, Inverter-Solar aur Jame Pipe ka Upay

Seedha jawab: Pahadi ilake me bijli ki aapoorti aam taur par chalti hai par barish, tez hawa aur ped girne se rukawat aa sakti hai. Pure sine-wave inverter aur tubular battery laptop aur electronics ke liye surakshit hain. Sardi me khule paani ke pipe ko moti kapde ya insulation se dhakein.

Pauri Garhwal: Inner Line Permit (ILP), Van Anumati aur Suraksha Niyam

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal me Inner Line Permit (ILP) ki zaroorat nahi hoti, kyunki ye zila Tibet ya Nepal ki seema par nahi hai. ILP jaise permit Chamoli (Niti, Mana), Uttarkashi (Nelong) aur Pithoragarh (Darma, Byans, Chaudans) ke seema-kshetron ke liye lagte hain. Pauri me Corbett (Dhikala-Kalagarh) aur Rajaji jaise sanrakshit kshetra me Van Vibhag ki anumati/booking zaroori hai.

Inner Line Permit (SDM/Online), Seema par Drone Rok aur AMS se Bachav

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ki oonchai (zyadatar 3,100 m se neeche) me Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) ka khatra bahut kam hai, jabki Niti, Mana jaise kshetron me ye aam hai. Drone udane se pehle sthaniya prashasan ki anumati zaroori hai, khaaskar sainik kshetra (Lansdowne) aur sanrakshit jungle me.

Safety ke 10 niyam (Pauri Garhwal ke liye):

  • Corbett/Rajaji: Sirf pramanit safari aur anumati-praapt route par jaayein. Bina anumati ka entry kanooni apradh hai.
  • Gulbaag (leopard) ka khatra: Shaam ke baad akele paidal na chalein, khaaskar gaon ke bahar aur jungle ke paas.
  • Bandar aur suar: Khaana khule me na chhodein, bandar se door rahein.
  • Nadi aur gadhere: Barish me achanak paani badhta hai. Gadhere ya nadi ke bahav me na utarein.
  • Landslide zone: Barish me raste ke kinare gaadi na rokein.
  • Mandir aur gram-Devta sthal: Niyam aur parampara ka sammaan karein.
  • Jungli phal/phool: Anumati ke bina na todein.
  • Plastic aur kachra: Wapas laayein, jungle me na phenkein.
  • Emergency: 112 (poore desh me), DDMA control room (Pauri Garhwal: Aapda Prabandhan aur Emergency Vyavastha).
  • Sainik kshetra: Lansdowne chhavni me photography aur ghoomne ke niyam ka palan karein.

AMS ke baare me: Agar aap Pauri se aage Chamoli ya Uttarkashi ke oonche kshetra (Mana, Niti, Gaumukh aadi) jaa rahe hain, to AMS ke lakshan (sar dard, ulti, chakkar) pehchaanein aur unchai dheere-dheere badhayein. Gambhir sthiti me turant neeche utarein aur doctor se milein. Ye jaankari medical salah nahi hai.

Lakshya December 2029, pehli section ka trial lakshya June 2028 (November 2026 ki report ke anusaar).

NH-7 (Rishikesh-Srinagar-Rudraprayag), NH-534 (Najibabad-Kotdwar-Satpuli-Bubakhal).

Lagbhag 151 km.
