Bijli Ka Bill Kaise Banta Hai Aur Kaise Kam Karein

“Unit” kya hoti hai

Bijli ki ek unit ka matlab hai 1 kilowatt-hour (kWh) — yaani 1000 watt ka upkaran ek ghante chalane par jitni bijli lagti hai.

Unit = (Watt × Ghante) ÷ 1000

Misaal: 100 watt ka pankha roz 10 ghante chale, to mahine me:

(100 × 10 × 30) ÷ 1000 = 30 unit

Isi tarah 1500 watt ka geyser sirf 1 ghanta roz chale to mahine me 45 unit — pankhe se zyada, jabki chala sirf 1 ghanta. Yahi baat samajhna sabse zaroori hai: watt zyada mayne rakhta hai, ghante nahi.

Slab system — bill achanak kyun badh jaata hai

Zyadatar rajyon me bijli ka rate flat nahi hota. Unit jitni badhti hain, rate bhi badhta jaata hai. Ek aam dhaancha (rate har rajya me alag hai):

UnitRate (misaal)
0 – 100₹3.50 / unit
101 – 200₹4.90 / unit
201 – 400₹6.50 / unit
400 se upar₹7.50 / unit

Zaroori baat: ye slab tax slab jaisa kaam karta hai. 250 unit par poori 250 unit ₹6.50 me nahi jaati — pehli 100 ₹3.50 par, agli 100 ₹4.90 par, aur baaki 50 ₹6.50 par.

Par asar phir bhi tez hota hai. 190 unit ka bill lagbhag ₹791 (energy charge), aur 210 unit ka lagbhag ₹905. Sirf 20 unit zyada, par ₹114 zyada — kyunki wo 20 unit sabse mehnge slab me gayi.

Bill me aur kya-kya juda hota hai

  • Fixed charge — aapke connection ke load (kW) par lagta hai. Bijli bilkul na jalayein tab bhi ye lagta hai.
  • Electricity duty — rajya ka tax, bill ke percentage me.
  • Fuel surcharge / FPPCA — coal ke daam badhne par jodha jaane wala charge. Ye har mahine badal sakta hai.
  • Meter rent — chhoti si maasik rakam.
  • Subsidy — kai rajyon me kuch unit tak chhoot milti hai, jo bill me minus me dikhti hai.

Isiliye "unit × rate" karke bill milaane par number kabhi nahi milta.

Number saaf baat kehte hain. Log bulb band karne me lage rehte hain, jabki AC aur heater mil kar bill ka 60-70% bana rahe hote hain. Pahadi ilaakon me sardi ka room heater woh cheez hai jo december-january ka bill dugna kar deti hai.

Bill kam karne ke asli tarike

1. AC 24-26°C par rakhein. Har ek degree neeche karne par lagbhag 6% zyada bijli lagti hai. 20°C aur 25°C ke beech ka farak mahine ke saikdon rupaye ka hota hai.

2. Geyser ka istemaal ginti me. Geyser paani garam karke rakhta bhi hai — yaani baar-baar chalu hota rehta hai. 15 minute pehle chalu karke nahane ke baad band, ye aadat hi kaafi hai.

3. Purana fridge sabse chupa hua kharcha hai. 10 saal purana fridge naye 5-star ke muqable do se teen guna bijli le sakta hai, aur wo 24 ghante chalta hai. Ye ek badlav aksar khud ka kharcha nikaal deta hai.

4. Star rating par dhyaan dijiye. 5-star AC 3-star se lagbhag 15-20% kam bijli leta hai. Kharidte waqt ₹5,000 zyada lagte hain, par 8-10 saal me wo nikal jaate hain.

5. Load sahi rakhein. Fixed charge aapke connection ke sanctioned load par hai. Zaroorat se zyada load liya hua hai to har mahine faltu paisa ja raha hai. Bijli daftar me application dekar ise kam karwaya ja sakta hai.

6. Standby power. TV, set-top box aur charger plug me lage rehne par thodi bijli leti hai. Ye chhota number hai — mahine me 5-10 unit — par muft ki bachat hai.

Meter theek hai ya nahi, khud check kijiye

Ye 30 minute ka test hai:

  1. Ghar ke saare upkaran band kar dijiye, sirf ek 1000 watt ka heater ya geyser chalu rakhiye
  2. Meter ka reading note kijiye
  3. Theek 1 ghante baad phir reading note kijiye
  4. Farak lagbhag 1 unit aana chahiye

Agar farak kaafi zyada aa raha hai to meter ki jaanch ke liye bijli vibhag me likhit shikayat dijiye. Zyadatar rajyon me meter testing ki suvidha hoti hai.

Aakhri baat

Har rajya ke slab, fixed charge aur subsidy alag hain, aur har saal badalte hain. Yahan diye number samajhne ke liye misaal hain, aapke rajya ka asli rate nahi. Apna sahi rate apne bijli vitaran company (DISCOM) ke tariff order me milta hai, jo unki website par hota hai.

Sardi ka bill: pahadi ilaakon ki khaas dikkat

Uttarakhand, Himachal aur aise thande ilaakon me december se february ka bill baaki mahinon se dugna ya tigna aa jaata hai. Wajah ek hi hai — room heater aur geyser.

Do cheezein kaafi farak laati hain. Pehli, oil-filled radiator lijiye normal coil heater ki jagah. Dono lagbhag 2000 watt ke hote hain, par radiator garmi rok kar rakhta hai, isliye thermostat baar-baar band ho jaata hai aur kul khapat kam hoti hai. Doosri, kamre ki darwaze-khidki ki jhiriyan band kar dijiye — ₹200 ka rubber seal mahine ke saikdon rupaye bachaata hai, kyunki heater ko baar-baar chalu hi nahi hona padta.

Aur ek aam galti: poore ghar ko garam karne ki koshish. Ek kamra garam karke usi me rehna kaafi sasta padta hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

1 unit bijli me kitni der AC chalta hai?

1.5 ton ka AC lagbhag 1500 watt leta hai, yaani 1 unit me lagbhag 40 minute. Inverter AC me ye zyada ho sakta hai kyunki wo compressor ki speed ghata deta hai.

Fixed charge kyun lagta hai jab bijli jalaayi hi nahi?

Ye connection banaye rakhne, line aur meter ka kharcha hai. Isliye zero unit par bhi lagta hai. Ye aapke sanctioned load par nirbhar karta hai.

Inverter AC sach me sasta padta hai?

Haan, agar roz kai ghante chalta ho. Ye compressor band karne ke bajay uski speed ghata deta hai, jisse lagbhag 20-30% bijli bachti hai. Kam istemaal par farak kam padega.

Bill zyada aaye to kya karein?

Pehle meter reading khud milaayein, phir ek-do mahine ka bill compare karein. Farak samajh na aaye to DISCOM me likhit shikayat dijiye — zyadatar rajyon me online shikayat aur meter testing dono ki suvidha hai.

Solar lagana faayde ka hai?

Agar aapki mahine ki khapat zyada hai (250+ unit) aur chhat par dhoop aati hai, to aam taur par 4-6 saal me kharcha nikal jaata hai. Subsidy aur net metering ke niyam rajya ke hisaab se alag hain, apne DISCOM se confirm karein.
