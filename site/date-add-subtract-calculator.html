"90 din ke andar jama karein" — to aakhri tareekh kaunsi hui? Neeche tareekh aur din daaliye. Aage ke liye plus, peeche ke liye minus.

Kaise chalta hai

Ye us tareekh me utne din jod deta hai ya ghata deta hai. Mahine ke alag-alag din (28, 30, 31) aur leap year — sab apne aap sambhal jaate hain.

  • Positive number (jaise 90) — us tareekh ke baad ki tareekh
  • Negative number (jaise −30) — us tareekh se pehle ki tareekh
Ek baat jo bahut kaam ki hai — ulta hisaab. Agar kisi kaam ki aakhri tareekh pata hai aur aapko taiyaari ka samay chahiye, to minus me din daal dijiye. "Exam 15 March ko hai, 60 din pehle tayaari shuru karni hai" — minus 60 daal kar shuru karne ki tareekh mil jaayegi.

Sarkari aur kanooni kaamon me sabse zyada zaroori

Sarkari kaagaz me samay seema bahut aam hai, aur usse chookna mehnga padta hai:

  • Notice ka jawab — "15 din ke andar", "30 din ke andar"
  • Appeal ki samay seema — kai maamlon me 30, 60 ya 90 din
  • Insurance claim — ghatna ke baad tay dinon me soochna deni hoti hai
  • Cheque ki validity — aam taur par 3 mahine
  • Warranty aur return — 7 din, 30 din
  • Property registration ke baad mutation
Do zaroori baatein: (1) ginti aksar kaagaz par likhi tareekh se hoti hai, aapke haath me aane ki tareekh se nahi. (2) Kuch jagah "din" ka matlab kaam ke din hota hai (chhutti chhodkar) aur kuch jagah saare din. Agar samay seema tang hai to ye zaroor confirm kar lijiye — dono me kai din ka farak aa jaata hai.

Aur ek practical salah — aakhri din ka intezaar kabhi mat kijiye. Portal band, server dheema, ya koi kaagaz kam — ye sab aakhri din hi hote hain. Apne liye 3-4 din pehle ka lakshya rakhiye.

Rozana ki zindagi me

  • EMI ya bill ki agli tareekh — "30 din baad" wale bill me
  • Dawa ka course — "10 din khaani hai" to aakhri din kaunsa
  • FD ki maturity — jama karne ke din se kitne din baad
  • Chhutti ka hisaab — kis din se kis din tak
  • Shaadi ya aayojan ki tayaari — kaam ke liye ulta samay nikaalna
  • Garbhavastha ki anumanit tareekh — aakhri period se lagbhag 280 din. Ye sirf anuman hota hai; asli tareekh doctor hi batate hain
  • Notice period — naukri chhodne ki tareekh se aakhri din
Ek chhota tarika jo bahut kaam aata hai — jo bhi zaroori tareekh nikale, use turant phone ke calendar me daal dijiye, aur 3 din pehle ka reminder laga dijiye. Hisaab laga lena aadha kaam hai; yaad rehna poora kaam hai.

Tareekh ke hisaab me hone wali aam galtiyan

  • Mahine ke din maan lena. "3 mahine baad" aur "90 din baad" ek nahi hote. February wale mahine me antar 2-3 din tak aa jaata hai. Jahan kanooni maamla ho, wahan wahi ginti kijiye jo kaagaz par likhi hai.
  • Shuru ka din ginna ya na ginna. Kuch niyamon me jis din kaagaz mila wo din bhi ginta hai, kuch me agle din se ginti shuru hoti hai. Tang samay seema me ye ek din bhaari pad sakta hai.
  • Chhutti bhool jaana. Aakhri din agar Sunday ya rajpatrit chhutti ho, to kai jagah agla kaam wala din maanya hota hai — par ye har jagah nahi chalta. Bharosa mat kijiye, pehle nipta lijiye.
  • Leap year. Ye calculator apne aap sambhal leta hai, par haath se ginte waqt log chook jaate hain.

Kanooni ya sarkari samay seema ka pakka jawab sambandhit vibhaag ya vakeel hi de sakte hain — hum vakeel nahi hain, aur ye tool sirf ganit karta hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

90 din baad ki tareekh kaise nikaalein?

Apni tareekh chuniye aur 90 daal dijiye. Peeche ki tareekh chahiye to minus me daaliye, jaise −90.

3 mahine aur 90 din ek hi hote hain?

Nahi. Mahine 28 se 31 din ke hote hain, isliye 2-3 din ka antar aa jaata hai. Kanooni maamle me wahi ginti kijiye jo kaagaz par likhi hai.

Sarkari samay seema kis din se ginti hai?

Aam taur par kaagaz ya notice par likhi tareekh se. Aur kuch jagah 'din' ka matlab kaam ke din hota hai — tang samay seema me ye confirm kar lena zaroori hai.

Leap year ka hisaab sahi rehta hai?

Haan, ye apne aap sambhal jaata hai. Haath se ginte waqt hi log isme chookte hain.
