Almora: Puranon ka Bhugol: Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand

Skanda Purana (Manaskhand), Mahabharata & Kalidasa ke Granthon me Almora ka Kshetra

Seedha jawab: Skanda Purana ke “Manaskhand” me Kumaon kshetra ka varnan milta hai, jabki Garhwal ke liye “Kedarkhand” naam prachlit hai. Almora Kumaon me aata hai, isliye uska pauranik sandarbh Manaskhand se judta hai. Kumaon naam ko bhi “Kurmanchal” (Kurma avatar ki bhoomi) se joda jata hai.

Uttarakhand ko pauranik granthon me do bade hisson me dekha gaya hai. Garhwal ka kshetra Kedarkhand aur Kumaon ka kshetra Manaskhand kehlata hai. Dono naam Skanda Purana se jude mane jate hain. Almora ka sambandh Manaskhand se isliye hai ki yeh Kumaon ki dharti hai.

Kai website bina granth dekhe aise numbers likh deti hain jo galat nikalte hain. Agar aapko Manaskhand ke adhyay-shlok ka hawala dena hai to Skanda Purana ka pramanik sanskaran dekh kar hi jodein.

Mahabharata aur Kalidasa: Mahabharata ke Van Parva me tirthon ki yatra aur Mahaprasthanika Parva me Pandavon ke Himalaya jane ka zikr aata hai. Kalidasa ke Meghaduta me Himalaya aur Kailash ke marg ka kavyik varnan hai aur Kumarasambhava me Himalaya Parvati ke pita ke roop me aate hain. Itna kehna theek hai ki yeh poora Himalayi kshetra Bharatiya sanskriti me pavitra mana gaya hai.

Kailash-Mansarovar marg ka sandarbh: Kumaon se Kailash-Mansarovar yatra ka marg Kali (Sharda) nadi ke saath Lipulekh darre tak jata hai. Yeh marg Pithoragarh zile se guzarta hai, Almora se nahi. Almora se yatri aam taur par Kathgodam ya Tanakpur ki taraf se is marg tak pahunchte hain.

Almora: Pramukh Mandir, Dham, Gurudwara aur Math

Bhawan Shaili, Kapaat Khulne-Band Hone ki Tithi aur Pooja ka Samay

Seedha jawab: Almora zile ke pramukh mandir Jageshwar Dham, Katarmal ka Surya Mandir, Chitai Golu Devta, Kasar Devi, Nanda Devi (Almora shahar) aur Dwarahat ke mandir samuh hain. Char Dham ki tarah in mandiron ke kapat saal me band nahi hote, aam taur par yeh poore saal khule rehte hain. Pooja ka samay mandir ke hisaab se badalta hai.

Jageshwar ka rochak sach: Jageshwar ko log aksar “Aadi Shankaracharya ne banwaya” kehte hain. Par Wikipedia ke anusar iske liye koi likhit ya shilalekh praman nahi hai. Kuch mandiron ki shaili Shankaracharya se bhi pehle (7vi sadi) ki lagti hai. Kuch vidwan ise Pashupata Shaiv sampraday aur Lakulisha parampara se jodte hain.

Jageshwar me dhyan dene wale mandir: Jageshwar mandir (mandir sankhya 37, lagbhag 12vi se 13vi sadi), Mrityunjaya Mahadev (mandir 76, lagbhag 850 se 950 CE), Dandeshwar samuh, Kuber mandir, Navgrah mandir aur Navdurga ya Nanda Devi mandir. Yahan ke pandit aur sthaniya sansthayein mela aur puja ki taareekhein har saal tay karti hain.

Gurudwara, Church aur anya sthal: Almora me Church aur Mission ki imaratein angrezi kaal ki hain (Almora: Chhavni Kasbe, Angrezi Kabristan aur Girjaghar me vistaar). Ramakrishna Mission se jude sthal aur Vivekananda smritiyan Kasar Devi aur Kakrighat ke aas-paas milti hain.

Pooja timing aur darshan ki salah: - Subah jaldi (suryoday ke aas-paas) aur shaam ki aarti ke samay mandir me sabse shaant mahaul milta hai. - Shravan aur Maha Shivratri par Jageshwar me bheed bahut badh jati hai, isliye subah jaldi pahunchna behtar hota hai. - Mandir ka exact samay aur pooja ki vyavastha badalti rehti hai. Jane se pehle mandir samiti ya sthaniya pujari se confirm kar lein.

Almora: Isht Devta, Gram Devta aur Jagar mein Devta Avtaran

Golu Devta aur Kumaon ke Lok Devta

Seedha jawab: Golu Devta ko Kumaon me “nyay ke devta” mana jata hai. Almora ke paas Chitai me unka sabse prasiddh mandir hai, jahan bhakt stamp paper ya saade kagaz par arji likhkar mandir me bandhte hain aur manokamna poori hone par pital ki ghanti chadhate hain.

Golu Devta ki lok-katha (sthaniya parampara ke anusar): Kaha jata hai ki Champawat ke raja Jhalrai (kuch jagah “Halrai” ya “Jalrai” bhi likha milta hai) ki aathvi rani Kalinka ka putra Golu tha. Sauteli rani’on ne navjat shishu ko badal kar uski jagah silbatta (patthar ka silauta aur musal) rakh diya aur shishu ko Gori nadi me bahaa diya. Bhana naam ke machhuare ko shishu mila aur usne use paal kar bada kiya. Bada hokar Golu ne apni maa ka sammaan lauta aur yogi bankar Chitai me siddhi paayi. Yahi katha Kumaon me alag-alag roop me suni jati hai.

Chitai ka mandir kaise juda: Ek manyata ke anusar jab Chand rajvansh ki rajdhani Champawat se Almora aayi to ek Pant Brahman ko sapne me Golu Devta ne Chitai me mandir banwane ko kaha. Champawat ka mandir moolsthan mana jata hai. Bhimtal ke paas Ghorakhal ka mandir bhi bahut prasiddh hai.

Arji aur ghanti ki parampara: - Bhakt apni arji stamp paper ya saade kagaz par likhkar laal dhaage se mandir ke lohe ki rodon par bandhte hain. - Manokamna poori hone par ek tay vajan ki pital ki ghanti chadhane ka sankalp liya jata hai. - Mandir ke aas-paas hazaron ghantiyan latakti dikhti hain. Kuch ghantiyan bahut bhaari bhi hain.

Dhyan dein: Gram devta ki parampara har gaon ki apni hoti hai.

Jagar, Jagariya, Dangariya aur Devta Khel

Seedha jawab: Jagar Kumaon-Garhwal ki wo parampara hai jisme Jagariya (gayak) hudke ya dhol ki thaap par devta ki katha gata hai aur Dangariya (madhyam) par devta aate hain. Dangariya ka hilna-dulna “jhoolna” kehlata hai. Jagar aam taur par raat bhar chalta hai aur gaon ya parivaar ki manokamna ya samasya ke liye hota hai.

Jagar me teen kirdar: 1. Jagariya: Mukhya gayak, jo devta ki gatha gata hai aur hudka ya thali bajata hai. 2. Dangariya: Wo vyakti jis par devta ka avtaran mana jata hai. 3. Sau-sau (shrota): Gaon ke log aur parivaar jo bhakti bhav se baithte hain.

Is parampara ko samman ke saath samjhna chahiye. Yeh logon ki gehri aastha hai, tamasha nahi. Agar aap bahari vyakti hain aur Jagar dekhna chahte hain to gaon walon se anumati lein aur video ya photo se pehle zaroor puchhein.

Almora: Pragaitihasik Kaal, Shila-Chitra aur Shailashray

Lakhudiyar aur Almora ke Prachin Shailashray

Seedha jawab: Almora zile ka sabse prasiddh pragaitihasik sthal Lakhudiyar hai, jo Barechhina gaon me Suyal nadi ke kinare Almora se lagbhag 19 km door hai. Yahan ki chattanon par kala, laal aur safed rang se ungli se bane chitra milte hain. In chitron ki khoj 1968 me Dr. M. P. Joshi ne ki, aisa Wikipedia me likha hai.

“Lakhudiyar” ka sthaniya arth “ek lakh gufayein” (Lakh Udyar) bataya jata hai, jo ek lok-vyutpatti hai. Yeh shailashray (rock shelters) Suyal nadi ke tat par hain. Yahan ke chitron me insaan, janwar, hathiyar, kapde, paltu pashu aur samuhik nritya ke drishya dikhte hain. Ek chitra me ek taraf lagbhag 34 aur doosri taraf lagbhag 28 aakritiyan nritya karti dikhayi gayi hain (Wikipedia ke anusar). Shikari aur sangrahak jeevan ke drishya bhi dikhte hain.

Umar ke baare me saavdhani: Wikipedia in chitron ki exact umar nahi batata. Kuch itihaskar inhe pragaitihasik kaal ka mante hain.

Doosre shailashray ke baare me: Kimni aur Dungri jaise shailashray Chamoli zile me hain, Almora me nahi. Almora kshetra me kuch aur shailashray (jaise Lwethap, Phaldakot, Fadkanauli) ka naam sandarbhon me milta hai, par unki pushti abhi baaki hai.

Cup-marks (Okhali chinh) aur Megalith: Kumaon ke kai ilakon me chattanon par katori jaise gol gadhde (cup-marks) milte hain, jinhe sthaniya log “okhali” kehte hain. Almora kshetra me inke hone ka zikr kuch pustakon me aata hai, par gaon ke naam aur gina nahi diye ja rahe jab tak pakka sandarbh na ho.

Tamba nidhi (Copper hoard): Bankot ka tamba nidhi Pithoragarh zile me milta hai, Almora me nahi. Is tarah ki galti log aksar karte hain, isliye ise saaf likhna zaroori hai.

Almora: Kuninda aur Katyuri Raajvansh ka Daur

Kuninda Sikke, Katyuri Rajdhani, Tamrapatra aur Pathar ke Mandir

Seedha jawab: Kuninda aur Katyuri, Kumaon-Garhwal ke sabse prachin rajvanshon me gine jate hain. Katarmal ka Surya Mandir (9vi sadi) Katyuri raja Katarmalla se joda jata hai. Dwarahat me Katyuri shakha ka mahal Chandragiri (Chanchari) pahadi par bataya jata hai, aur wahan 7vi se 11vi sadi ke beech mandir bane mane jate hain.

Kuninda: Kuninda ek prachin ganrajya tha jiske sikke Himalaya ke talhati kshetron me milte hain. Sikkon par “Amoghabhuti” naam ka ullekh milta hai. Itna kehna theek hai ki Kuninda sikke Kumaon-Garhwal ke itihas ke sabse prachin mudra praman me gine jate hain.

Katyuri rajvansh: Katyuri rajya ki rajdhani Kartikeyapura (aaj ka Baijnath, Bageshwar zila) mani jati hai. Bageshwar pehle Almora zile ka hissa tha, isliye Almora ke itihas me Baijnath ka naam aata hai. Katyuri kaal me mandir nirman aur pathar ke shilpa ki shaili vikasit hui.

Katyuri se jude Almora ke sthan: - Katarmal: Surya Mandir, lagbhag 2,116 m par, 44 chhote mandiron se ghira. Mool murti ki chori ke baad lakdi ke dwar aur panel National Museum, Delhi me rakhe gaye. - Dwarahat: Katyuri shakha ka nivas, kai mandir (Ratandev, Kachahri Deval, Gujardev, Maha Mrityunjaya aadi). - Jageshwar: Purani maanyata ise Katyuri se jodti hai, par Wikipedia ke anusar aise kisi likhit ya shilalekh praman ki kami hai. Yahan Pashupata Shaiv parampara ke praman milte hain.

Tamrapatra aur shilalekh: Kumaon ke prachin tamrapatra aur shilalekh Bageshwar, Pandukeshwar (Chamoli) jaise sthanon se mile hain. Jageshwar me 25 se zyada shilalekh (7vi se 10vi sadi, zyadatar Sanskrit aur Brahmi) hone ki jaankari Wikipedia me hai.

Almora: Panwar (Garhwal) & Chand (Kumaon) Kingdoms

Chand Rajvansh, Almora ki Rajdhani aur Kumaon ke Parganas

Seedha jawab: Almora Chand rajvansh ki rajdhani rahi. Chand raja Kalyan Chand ne rajdhani Champawat se Almora layi. 1790 me Gorkhon ke akraman se Chand shasan ka ant hua.

Garhwal ke “52 garh” Panwar (Parmar) vansh se jude hain, Almora se nahi. Almora ka sambandh Kumaon ke Chand rajvansh se hai.

Almora ke Chand-kaleen sthal: - Malla Mahal (Upar ka darbar): Jahan aaj Collectorate hai. - Talla Mahal (Neeche ka darbar): Jahan aaj Zila Chikitsalaya (District Hospital) hai. - Lal Mandi Qila (Fort Moira): Angrezon ne is purane qile ka naam Fort Moira rakha. - Nanda Devi, Tripur Sundari aur Udyot Chandeshwar mandir: Udyot Chand ke samay ke.

Parganas aur prashasan: Ain-e-Akbari me “Sarkar Kumaon” ko 21 mahals ke saath ginaya gaya hai. Ratan Chand ke samay jagiren Panch-purviya Rajput kulon ko dene ka zikr bhi milta hai. Almora zile ke kaun se parganas the (jaise Katyur, Dwarahat, Pali Pachhaon, Sor aadi), unki pooree list Almora ke Purane Pargana aur Patti ki Poori Suchi me di jayegi.

Goonth zameen: Kumaon me “goonth” us zameen ko kehte the jo mandir ya Brahman ko kar-mukt dan me di jati thi. Is ke liye puraane Kumaon Gazetteer aur tehsil ke record dekhna zaroori hai.

Almora: Gorkha Raaj (Gorkhyani) aur Angrez-Nepal Yuddh

Gorkha Shasan (1790 se 1815), Almora ka Yuddh aur Sugauli Sandhi

Seedha jawab: Gorkha sena ne 1790 (kuch sandarbhon me 1791) me Almora par adhikaar kiya aur lagbhag 24 saal shasan kiya. Angrezo-Gorkha yuddh me British sena ne April 1815 me Almora ko ghera. Gorkha governor Bam Shah ne 27 April 1815 ko aatmasamarpan kiya (kuch sandarbh 26 April likhte hain). Sugauli ki sandhi (4 March 1816) ke baad Kumaon British ke paas chala gaya.

Gorkhyani kya thi: Kumaon me Gorkha shasan ko “Gorkhyani” kaha jata tha. Itihaskaron ke anusar is daur me bhari kar-vasooli aur kathor shasan ki kahaniyan sthaniya smriti me gehri baithi hain. Sthaniya bolchal me “Gorkhyani” shabd aaj bhi atyachar ya afratafri ke arth me aata hai.

Almora ka yuddh: tathya ek nazar me (Wikipedia ke Bam Shah page ke anusar): - Bam Shah 1814 se 1815 tak Kumaon ke Gorkha governor the. - British ne pehle Almora ke aas-paas ki chowkiyan ek-ek kar jeet li. Captain Hearsey ki tukdi purvi Kumaon se ghoom kar aayi par haar gayi. - Bam Shah ke bhai Hasti Dal Shah lagbhag 500 sainikon ke saath Almora ki madad ko aa rahe the, par Almora ki uttari line par rok liye gaye aur yuddh me maare gaye. - Colonel Nicolls lagbhag 2,000 sainikon ke saath Almora par chadhe. Gorkha paksh me lagbhag 1,000 sainik bataye jate hain. British toppon ne qile ke dwar ke bahut paas se deewar tod di. - Antatah Bam Shah ne Almora saupa aur Amar Singh Thapa ko patra likha.

Sugauli ki sandhi: Is sandhi ke baad Kumaon aur Garhwal ka bada hissa British ke adhikaar me aaya (Almora: British Garhwal / Kumaon Commissionery & Tehri Riyasat me iska asar). Sandhi ki sahi tithi par kuch sources me antar hai (paripushti 4 March 1816).

Gorkha kaal ke chinh: Gorkha kile aur chowkiyon ke avshesh kai jagah milte hain.

Almora: British Garhwal / Kumaon Commissionery & Tehri Riyasat

Traill, Batten, Beckett & Pauw ki Zameen Vyavastha (1815 se 1896)

Seedha jawab: 1815 me E. Gardiner Kumaon ke pehle Commissioner bane (8 May 1815). Uske baad G. W. Traill ne 1817 me doosra rajasv bandobast kiya. British shasan me Kumaon alag prashasanik ikai rahi, jahan zameen, jangal aur kar ki naye tarike se vyavastha hui. Ek hi shaili ki dohrayi gayi vyavastha ko Almora: Pahadi Land Measurement: Nali, Mutthi & Bigha Calculator ke Nali-Mutthi aur zameen shreniyon me bhi dekha ja sakta hai.

Zameen bandobast ke naam: Traill, Batten, Beckett aur Pauw se jude settlements ka kram aam taur par 1815 se 1896 tak bataya jata hai. Har settlement me kar ki dar, zameen ki naap aur Patwari vyavastha me kya badlav aaye, yeh vistaar Almora ke Purane Pargana aur Patti ki Poori Suchi ke Patti aur Pargana wale hisse me aur Kumaon Gazetteer ke sandarbh me diya jayega.

Almora Jail aur Nehru: Wikipedia ke anusar Pt. Jawaharlal Nehru ko swatantrata andolan ke dauran Almora jail me kuch samay ke liye rakha gaya tha. Sahi tithi ke liye pustakon ka sandarbh dekhna chahiye.

Uday Shankar aur Almora: Uday Shankar ne 1930 ke dashak ke ant me Almora me apna nritya kendra sthapit kiya (Wikipedia ke anusar “late 1930s”). Is par Almora: Cinema, Kitabein, Media aur Digital Creators me alag se likha jayega.

Almora: Azaadi ki Ladai, Kuli Begar aur Rajya Andolan

Kumaon Parishad, Coolie Begar, Salt aur Rajya Andolan

Seedha jawab: Almora Kumaon ke swatantrata andolan ka kendra raha. Hargovind Pant (Almora me janm, 19 May 1885) ne Kumaon Parishad ki sthapna ki (Wikipedia ke anusar 1915). 14 January 1921 ko Bageshwar me Coolie Begar ke khilaf andolan hua, jisme Almora ke Badri Datt Pandey (“Kumaon Kesari”) ki mukhya bhoomika thi. Gandhi ne ise “raktheen kranti” kaha.

Hargovind Pant: Hargovind Pant ka janm Almora me hua. 1905 me unhone Almora ke Government College se intermediate kiya. 1910 me Ranikhet me vakalat shuru ki aur sthaniya rashtravadi akhbaron ko aarthik madad di. Baad me ve Constituent Assembly ke sadasya bane aur 4 January 1951 ko Uttar Pradesh vidhanmandal ke upadhyaksh chune gaye.

Govind Ballabh Pant: Wikipedia ke Almora page ke anusar unka janm 10 September 1887 ko Almora zile me hua (aam taur par Khoont gaon bataya jata hai). Ve Uttar Pradesh ke pehle Mukhyamantri bane. Unka naam Kumaon aur Almora ki rajneeti me bahut sammaan se liya jata hai.

Salt satyagraha aur anya andolan: 1930 ke namak satyagraha ke samay Almora zile me Salt tehsil kshetra se jude swatantrata senani ka zikr sthaniya sandarbhon me milta hai. Isi tarah Kumaon ke jangal andolan (1921 ke aas-paas) ka sambandh bhi Almora kshetra se jodna chahiye, par exact gaon aur ghatna ka sandarbh jaanchna hoga.

Chipko andolan (1974, Chamoli ki Gaura Devi) mukhya roop se Chamoli se juda hai. Almora ke log is andolan ke prabhav me aaye, par Almora ko Chipko ki janmbhoomi nahi kaha ja sakta.

Almora: Lok-Kathayein: Achhari, Veer Yoddha aur Rahasyamay Kisse

Lok-kathayein, Veer Gaathayein aur Rahasyamay Kisse

Seedha jawab: Almora kshetra ki lok-kathayon me Golu Devta ka nyay, Rajula-Malushahi ki prem gatha, Haru-Sain jaise devta aur Kasar Devi se jude rahasyamay kisse prasiddh hain. Yeh sab sthaniya manyatayein hain. Inhe itihas ya vigyan ke roop me nahi, lok-sanskriti ke roop me padhna chahiye.

Rajula-Malushahi: Kumaon ki sabse prasiddh prem-gaathaon me se ek Rajula aur Malushahi ki hai. Kai lok-kathaon me Rajula ko Munsiyari ke Shauka vyapari Sunpati ki beti aur Malushahi ko Bairath (Dwarahat kshetra) ka raja bataya jata hai. Alag-alag gayak alag roop me yeh gaatha gate hain, isliye sthaan aur naam me antar milta hai.

Rami Baurani: Kumaon ki ek aur lok-gaatha Rami Baurani ki hai, jise Katyuri kaal se joda jata hai. Katha ke ant aur kirdaron ke naam alag-alag gayakon me alag milte hain.

Achhari (Aanchri) ki manyata: Kumaon aur Garhwal ke ooche chaaragahon (bugyal) me Achhari ya Aanchri naam ki pariyon ki manyata hai. Almora ke 1,600 se 2,400 m ke aas-paas ke ilake me bugyal nahi hote (Almora: Bugyal / Payar), isliye Almora me yeh manyata mukhya roop se ooche pahadi sthanon ya doosre zilon se aati hai.

Kasar Devi ka “magnetic anomaly”: Kasar Devi pahadi ko log “Van Allen belt ke sabse nazdeek ka bhoo-bhaag” ya “chumbakiya shakti ka kendra” batate hain. Itna sach hai ki Kasar Devi Swami Vivekananda, Lama Anagarika Govinda aur Danish sant Alfred Sorensen (Sunyata Baba) jaise naamon se jodi gayi hai. 1960 ke dashak me yahan Western yatriyon ki aamad se ise “Hippie trail” ka hissa bhi kaha gaya. Timothy Leary ka naam bhi is sandarbh me liya jata hai.

Swami Vivekananda aur Almora: Wikipedia ke anusar Swami Vivekananda teen baar Almora aaye (aam taur par 1890, 1897 aur 1898 bataye jate hain; tithi par sroton mein matbhed ho sakta hai). Kakrighat aur Kasar Devi ka unse gehra sambandh bataya jata hai.

Syalde Pokhar ki bagwali: Dwarahat ke Syalde Pokhar par har Baisakh me Syalde Rajput kul “bagwali” (patthar aur gulel se nakli yuddh) khelte the, jo gambhir chotein lagne ke karan roka gaya. Yeh parampara Kumaon ke Bagwal mele (Almora: Mausami Tyohar) se milti-julti hai.

Dunagiri aur Sanjivani ki katha: Dwarahat ke Dunagiri parvat ke baare me kaha jata hai ki Hanuman Sanjivani buti lane ke liye Drona parvat ka ek hissa yahan laye. Isi tarah ka dawa Chamoli ke Dronagiri ke liye bhi kiya jata hai. Isliye dono sthaniya manyatayein hain, aur page par dono ka zikr rakha gaya hai.

Ek aur dhyan dene wali baat: Lok-kathayein samay ke saath badalti hain.

Almora ka sabse prasiddh mandir kaun sa hai?
Jageshwar Dham, Katarmal Surya Mandir, Chitai Golu Devta aur Kasar Devi sabse prasiddh hain.
Jageshwar me kitne mandir hain?
Wikipedia ke anusar Jageshwar samuh me 124 aur Dandeshwar samuh me 15 mandir hain.
Jageshwar Almora se kitna door hai?
Lagbhag 35 se 36 km.
Kya Jageshwar ko Aadi Shankaracharya ne banwaya?
Yeh parampara hai, par Wikipedia ke anusar iska koi likhit ya shilalekh praman nahi hai.
Katarmal Surya Mandir kab bana?
Maanyata hai ki 9vi sadi me Katyuri raja Katarmalla ne banwaya.
Katarmal ke dwar ab kahan hain?
Lakdi ke dwar aur panel National Museum, Delhi me rakhe gaye hain.
Chitai Golu Devta me arji kaise likhte hain?
Stamp paper ya saade kagaz par likhkar laal dhaage se mandir ki rodon par bandhte hain.
Golu Devta kaun hain?
Kumaon ke nyay ke devta, jinhe Champawat ke raja Jhalrai aur rani Kalinka ka putra mana jata hai.
Lakhudiyar kahan hai?
Barechhina gaon, Suyal nadi ke kinare, Almora se lagbhag 19 km.
Lakhudiyar ki khoj kisne ki?
Wikipedia ke anusar 1968 me Dr. M. P. Joshi ne.
Almora ko rajdhani kisne banaya?
Chand raja Kalyan Chand ne (1563 ya 1568, sandarbh ke anusar).
Almora par Gorkhon ne kab adhikaar kiya?
1790 (kuch sandarbhon me 1791).
Angrezon ne Almora kab jeeta?
April 1815 me; Bam Shah ne 26 ya 27 April 1815 ko aatmasamarpan kiya.
Coolie Begar andolan kab hua?
14 January 1921 ko Bageshwar me, jo us samay Almora zile ka hissa tha.
Kumaon Kesari kise kehte hain?
Badri Datt Pandey ko, jinhone Almora Akhbar ke zariye andolan chalaya.
