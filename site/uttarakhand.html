Uttarakhand Ek Nazar Me: Devbhoomi Snapshot

Uttarakhand kahan hai aur kyun khaas hai?

Uttarakhand bharat ke uttar me Himalaya ki god me basa rajya hai, jahan Ganga aur Yamuna dono nadiyon ka udgam hota hai. Ise Devbhoomi isliye kehte hain kyunki yahan Char Dham, Panch Kedar, Panch Prayag aur hazaron prachin mandir hain.

Rajya ka ek taraf Tibet (China) se aur dusri taraf Nepal se antarrashtriya sima judti hai. Dakshin me Terai-Bhabar ke maidan hain, jahan Haridwar aur Udham Singh Nagar jaise ghane base zile hain. Uske upar Shivalik pahadiyan, phir Lesser Himalaya (madhya Himalaya), phir Great Himalaya ki barf-dhaki chotiyan aur sabse upar Trans-Himalaya ka thanda registani ilaka aata hai. Itni kam doori me itni alag-alag jalvayu bahut kam jagah milti hai, aur yahi Uttarakhand ki sabse badi pehchan hai.

Uttarakhand ka naam: Uttaranchal se Uttarakhand tak

Rajya ka naam shuru me Uttaranchal rakha gaya tha. 1 January 2007 se ise badalkar Uttarakhand kar diya gaya.

“Uttarakhand” shabd bahut purana hai. Puraanon me is kshetra ka zikr Kedarkhand (Garhwal kshetra) aur Manaskhand (Kumaon kshetra) ke roop me milta hai. Skanda Purana me in dono khando ka varnan hai. Isi liye aaj bhi Garhwal ko Kedarkhand aur Kumaon ko Manaskhand kehne ki parampara hai. Is page ke Itihas aur hastiyan me purane itihas ki puri kahani di gayi hai.

Rajdhani ka sawaal: Dehradun aur Gairsain

Dehradun rajya ki antarim (sheetkalin) rajdhani hai aur Gairsain (Bhararisain), zila Chamoli ko grishmakalin rajdhani ghoshit kiya gaya hai.

Gairsain Garhwal aur Kumaon dono ke beech me sthit hai, isi liye isse rajya andolan ki bhavna ke anusar sthayi rajdhani banane ki maang purani hai. Vidhan Sabha ke kuch satra Gairsain me hote hain. Aaj ke roz kaam-kaaj ka bada hissa Dehradun se hi chalta hai. Rajdhani se judi sthiti badalti rehti hai, isliye taza jaankari ke liye Uttarakhand Vidhan Sabha aur sarkar ke official portal dekhein.

Rajya ke pratik (State Symbols)

Uttarakhand ke rajya pratik hain: Kasturi mrig (rajya pashu), Himalayan Monal (rajya pakshi), Brahma Kamal (rajya phool), Buransh/Rhododendron (rajya vriksh) aur Common Peacock (rajya titli).

  • Kasturi mrig (Alpine Musk Deer): Himalaya ke unche jungalon me milta hai aur sanrakshit prajati hai.
  • Himalayan Monal: rang-birange paankhon wala ye pakshi pahadi pehchan hai.
  • Brahma Kamal: unchai par, aksar barish ke mausam me khilne wala durlabh phool, jise Nanda Devi aur Kedarnath se jodkar dekha jata hai.
  • Buransh (Rhododendron arboreum): Chait-Baisakh me lal phool khilte hain, aur iska sharbat pahadon ki pehchan hai.
  • Dhol-Damau: rajya ka sabse prasiddh lok-vadya jod hai, jo har sanskar, jagar aur tyohar me bajta hai.

Uttarakhand ke naam ki alag-alag spelling

Log Uttarakhand ko Uttrakhand, Uttarakand, Uttrakand aur purane naam Uttaranchal se bhi search karte hain. Isi tarah Garhwal ko Garwal aur Kumaon ko Kumaun, Kumau ya Kumayun likha jata hai. Sahi spelling Uttarakhand, Garhwal aur Kumaon hai.

Kai jagah naam ki spelling alag likhi milti hai: Kedarnath (Kedarnaath), Badrinath (Badrinaath), Gangotri (Gangotri), Yamunotri (Jamnotri), Haridwar (Hardwar), Rishikesh (Hrishikesh), Dehradun (Dehra Dun), Almora (Almora), Pithoragarh (Pithoragarh), Nainital (Naini Tal), Mussoorie (Mussourie), Jim Corbett (Jim Corbett). Is page me sahi aur aam taur par maanya spelling istemal ki gayi hai, taaki padhne wale ko bhram na ho.

Ek line me Uttarakhand ki taqat

  • Adhyatm: Char Dham, Panch Kedar, Panch Badri, Panch Prayag, Haridwar-Rishikesh.
  • Prakriti: 6 National Parks, 2 Tiger Reserves (Corbett, Rajaji), unche bugyal (alpine meadows), glacier aur kund.
  • Sanskriti: Kumaoni aur Garhwali lok-geet, Chholiya, Pandav Nritya, Aipan, Jagar.
  • Seva aur suraksha: bharatiya sena ki Garhwal Rifles aur Kumaon Regiment ka bada yogdan; Dehradun me Indian Military Academy.
  • Shiksha aur vigyan: IIT Roorkee, AIIMS Rishikesh, GB Pant University Pantnagar, Forest Research Institute aur Wildlife Institute of India Dehradun me.
  • Udyog: SIDCUL ke audyogik kshetra Haridwar, Pantnagar, Sitarganj aur Dehradun me.

Garhwal vs Kumaon: Dono Mandal Ka Farak

Garhwal aur Kumaon me kitne zile hain?

Garhwal mandal me 7 zile hain: Uttarkashi, Chamoli, Rudraprayag, Tehri Garhwal, Dehradun, Pauri Garhwal aur Haridwar. Kumaon mandal me 6 zile hain: Nainital, Almora, Pithoragarh, Bageshwar, Champawat aur Udham Singh Nagar.

Ye vibhajan Garhwal aur Kumaon ke purane rajneetik aur prashasanik itihas se chala aa raha hai (Garhwal me Panwar vansh aur Tehri riyasat, Kumaon me Chand vansh aur British Kumaon Commissionery). Dono mandal ek-dusre ke sath Chamoli-Bageshwar-Almora ki simaon par judte hain, aur Nanda Devi Raj Jat jaisi yatra dono kshetron ko ek dhaage me bandhti hai.

Boli aur sanskriti: kitna milta, kitna alag?

Garhwali aur Kumaoni dono Central Pahadi bhasha samooh ki boliyan hain. Dono me shabd, geet aur tyohar kaafi milte-julte hain, par uchcharan aur kuch rozmarra ke shabd alag hain.

Kumaon me Kumaoni ki kai upaboliyan hain, jaise Majh-Kumaiya, Soriyali, Johari aur Danpuriya. Garhwal me bhi kshetra ke hisaab se boliyan badalti hain. Bhotia samuday ki boliyan sima-kshetron me alag chhaap chhodti hain. “Mait” (mayka), “Khuded” (yaad ka dard), “Gham” (dhoop) jaise shabd dono taraf samajhe jate hain. Pahadi virasat aur tyohar me aise shabd aur unka matlab vistaar se diye gaye hain.

Ek ghati, do mandal, ek bhavna

Rajya andolan ke samay Garhwal aur Kumaon ek saath khade hue. Mahatvapurn ye hai ki Chipko andolan (Chamoli, Garhwal) aur Coolie Begar prtha ka ant (Bageshwar, Kumaon, 1921) dono ne pure rajya ki pehchan banayi. Yahi sanjhi virasat Uttarakhand ki sabse badi taqat hai.

13 Districts Ke Mini Profile: Har Zile Ki Poori Pehchan

Har zile ka vistrit (200-heading) page alag se banega. Yahan har zile ka ek mazboot, search-friendly profile diya gaya hai, taaki hub page se hi zile ka asli parichay mil jaye.

Almora zila

Almora Kumaon mandal ka zila hai, jiska mukhyalay Almora shahar hai. Ye zila Jageshwar Dham, Katarmal Surya mandir, Binsar, Kasar Devi, Ranikhet aur Bal Mithai ke liye jaana jata hai.

  • Mukhyalay aur mandal: Almora, Kumaon.
  • Kshetrafal aur aabadi (Census 2011, lagbhag): 3,689 sq km; 6.2 lakh.
  • Pramukh nadiyan: Kosi aur Suyal (kshetra ki pramukh pahadi nadiyan).
  • Pramukh mandir: Jageshwar, Katarmal, Chitai Golu Devta, Dunagiri, Kasar Devi.
  • Pramukh sthal: Ranikhet (Kumaon Regiment ka kendra), Binsar, Dwarahat, Chaukhutia.
  • Khaas khana aur saugaat: Bal Mithai, Singori, Kumaoni raita aur Bhatt ki Churkani.
  • Pramukh sanskritik pehchan: Almora ko Kumaon ki sanskritik rajdhani kaha jata hai; Nanda Devi mela, Holi (Baithaki Holi) aur Dussehra ke putle isi kshetra ki pehchan hain.
  • Ghoomne ka samay: saal bhar; March se June aur September se November sabse achcha.
  • Pahunch: Kathgodam/Haldwani se sadak marg; nikatam rail station Kathgodam; nikatam hawai adda Pantnagar.

Bageshwar zila

Bageshwar Kumaon ka chhota par sanskritik roop se bahut mahatvapurn zila hai. Yahan Saryu aur Gomti nadi ka sangam hai aur Uttarayani mela ke liye ye shahar prasiddh hai.

  • Mukhyalay aur mandal: Bageshwar, Kumaon.
  • Kshetrafal aur aabadi (lagbhag): 2,302 sq km; 2.6 lakh.
  • Pramukh nadiyan: Saryu, Gomti, Pindar (Pindar ghati ka kuch hissa).
  • Pramukh mandir: Bagnath (Bageshwar), Baijnath.
  • Pramukh sthal: Kausani, Garur, Kapkot, Song, Khati.
  • Trek: Pindari Glacier, Kafni Glacier, Sunderdhunga.
  • Itihas: 1921 ke Uttarayani mela me Coolie Begar pratha ke virodh me sabha.
  • Ghoomne ka samay: garmi aur sharad ritu; trek ke liye April se June, September-October.

Chamoli zila

Chamoli Garhwal mandal ka sabse prasiddh zilon me se ek hai. Badrinath, Hemkund Sahib, Valley of Flowers, Auli, Roopkund aur Gairsain (grishmakalin rajdhani) yahin hain.

  • Mukhyalay aur mandal: Gopeshwar, Garhwal.
  • Kshetrafal aur aabadi (lagbhag): 7,520 sq km; 3.9 lakh.
  • Pramukh nadiyan: Alaknanda, Pindar, Nandakini, Dhauliganga, Birahi Ganga.
  • Pramukh sangam: Vishnuprayag, Nandprayag, Karnaprayag.
  • Pramukh mandir: Badrinath, Yogdhyan Badri, Bhavishya Badri, Vriddha Badri, Rudranath, Kalpeshwar.
  • Prakritik dharohar: Nanda Devi aur Valley of Flowers National Parks (UNESCO).
  • Trek: Valley of Flowers, Hemkund Sahib, Roopkund, Bedni Bugyal, Brahmatal.
  • Itihas: Chipko andolan (Reni gaon, 1974) aur Ramman utsav (UNESCO).
  • Dhyan rakhein: Monsoon me landslide aur sadak band hone ka jokhim.

Champawat zila

Champawat Kumaon ka sabse chhota zila (kshetrafal me) hai aur Chand rajvansh ki pehli rajdhani rahi hai. Purnagiri, Lohaghat, Mayawati aur Abbott Mount iski pehchan hain.

  • Mukhyalay aur mandal: Champawat, Kumaon.
  • Kshetrafal aur aabadi (lagbhag): 1,781 sq km; 2.6 lakh.
  • Pramukh nadiyan: Kali (Sharda), Lohawati.
  • Pramukh mandir: Purnagiri, Baleshwar, Barahi Devi (Devidhura).
  • Pramukh sthal: Lohaghat, Tanakpur, Banbasa, Abbott Mount, Mayawati.
  • Sima: Nepal ke saath antarrashtriya sima Kali nadi par.
  • Sanskritik pehchan: Devidhura ka Bagwal mela.
  • Pahunch: Tanakpur rail station se sadak marg.

Dehradun zila

Dehradun Uttarakhand ki antarim rajdhani aur sabse bade shahari kendron me se ek hai. Mussoorie, Rishikesh (shahar), Chakrata, IMA, FRI aur Doon ghati yahin hain.

  • Mukhyalay aur mandal: Dehradun, Garhwal.
  • Kshetrafal aur aabadi (lagbhag): 3,088 sq km; 17 lakh.
  • Pramukh nadiyan: Ganga, Yamuna, Tons, Song, Asan.
  • Sanstha: Indian Military Academy, Forest Research Institute, Wildlife Institute of India, Doon School.
  • Pramukh sthal: Mussoorie, Landour, Sahastradhara, Robber’s Cave, Malsi, Kalsi, Chakrata, Rishikesh.
  • Pramukh khana: Basmati chawal ki prasiddhi, Garhwali vyanjan aur sthaniya mithai.
  • Pahunch: Jolly Grant hawai adda; Dehradun rail station; Delhi se sadak marg.
  • Dhyan rakhein: Mussoorie aur Rishikesh me week-end par bheed.

Haridwar zila

Haridwar Uttarakhand ka vah zila hai jahan Ganga pahadon se maidanon me utarti hain. Har Ki Pauri, Kumbh mela, Roorkee aur SIDCUL audyogik kshetra iski pehchan hain.

  • Mukhyalay aur mandal: Haridwar, Garhwal.
  • Kshetrafal aur aabadi (lagbhag): 2,360 sq km; 18.9 lakh (rajya me sabse zyada).
  • Pramukh nadiyan: Ganga, Solani.
  • Pramukh mandir: Mansa Devi, Chandi Devi, Maya Devi, Daksha Mahadev.
  • Sanstha: IIT Roorkee, Gurukul Kangri, BHEL.
  • Mela: Kumbh (har 12 saal), Somvati Amavasya aur Kanwar yatra ke samay badi bheed.
  • Dhyan rakhein: Mele ke samay hotel advance book karein aur yatayat niyam janein.

Nainital zila

Nainital Kumaon ka sabse prasiddh paryatan zila hai. Naini Taal, Bhimtal, Naukuchiyatal, Mukteshwar, Kainchi Dham aur Jim Corbett National Park iski pehchan hain. Rajya ka Uchch Nyayalaya bhi yahin hai.

  • Mukhyalay aur mandal: Nainital, Kumaon (Kumaon mandal mukhyalay bhi).
  • Kshetrafal aur aabadi (lagbhag): 3,422 sq km; 9.5 lakh.
  • Pramukh nadiyan: Kosi, Gaula, Ramganga (Corbett kshetra).
  • Pramukh taal: Naini, Bhimtal, Naukuchiyatal, Sattal, Khurpatal.
  • Pramukh sthal: Haldwani, Kathgodam, Ramnagar, Kaladhungi, Mukteshwar, Ramgarh, Bhowali.
  • Dharmik: Kainchi Dham, Naina Devi, Garjia Devi, Hanuman Garhi.
  • Wildlife: Jim Corbett National Park (Ramnagar kshetra).
  • Dhyan rakhein: Weekend aur garmi ki chhuttiyon me Nainital me yatayat jam aur parking ki dikkat.

Pauri Garhwal zila

Pauri Garhwal zila Garhwal mandal ka mukhyalay hai. Lansdowne, Khirsu, Srinagar, Kotdwar aur Neelkanth Mahadev iski pehchan hain, aur Garhwal Rifles ka kendra yahin hai.

  • Mukhyalay aur mandal: Pauri, Garhwal (Garhwal mandal mukhyalay bhi).
  • Kshetrafal aur aabadi (lagbhag): 5,230 sq km; 6.9 lakh.
  • Pramukh nadiyan: Alaknanda, Nayar (Nayar ghati), Malini, Ramganga (kuch hissa).
  • Pramukh sthal: Lansdowne, Khirsu, Srinagar, Kotdwar, Satpuli, Dhari Devi.
  • Dharmik: Neelkanth Mahadev, Tarkeshwar Mahadev, Sidhbali, Kanvashram, Binsar Mahadev.
  • Sanstha: HNB Garhwal University (Srinagar), Garhwal Rifles Regimental Centre (Lansdowne).
  • Pahunch: Kotdwar rail station; Dehradun/Haridwar se sadak.

Pithoragarh zila

Pithoragarh Uttarakhand ka vah seema zila hai jahan Tibet (China) aur Nepal dono se sima judti hai. Munsiyari, Adi Kailash, Om Parvat, Patal Bhuvaneshwar aur Panchachuli yahin hain.

  • Mukhyalay aur mandal: Pithoragarh, Kumaon.
  • Kshetrafal aur aabadi (lagbhag): 7,090 sq km; 4.8 lakh.
  • Pramukh nadiyan: Kali, Gori Ganga, Ramganga (Eastern), Dhauli Ganga (Eastern).
  • Pramukh sthal: Munsiyari, Dharchula, Didihat, Gangolihat, Berinag, Askot.
  • Dharmik: Patal Bhuvaneshwar, Hat Kalika, Thal Kedar, Adi Kailash, Om Parvat.
  • Trek: Milam, Ralam, Namik glacier, Panchachuli base, Adi Kailash route.
  • Sima: Kailash Mansarovar Yatra ka Lipulekh marg.
  • Permit: seema kshetron ke liye anumati zaroori (Yatra planner dekhein).

Rudraprayag zila

Rudraprayag Garhwal ka chhota par dharmik roop se bahut mahatvapurn zila hai, jahan Kedarnath, Tungnath, Madhyamaheshwar, Triyuginarayan aur Chopta hain.

  • Mukhyalay aur mandal: Rudraprayag, Garhwal.
  • Kshetrafal aur aabadi (lagbhag): 1,984 sq km; 2.4 lakh (rajya me sabse kam).
  • Pramukh nadiyan: Alaknanda, Mandakini.
  • Pramukh sangam: Rudraprayag (Alaknanda-Mandakini).
  • Dharmik: Kedarnath, Tungnath, Madhyamaheshwar, Kalimath, Triyuginarayan, Guptkashi Vishwanath, Ukhimath.
  • Prakritik: Chopta, Chandrashila, Deoria Tal.
  • Itihas ka sandarbh: Rudraprayag ka aadmkhor tendua (Jim Corbett ki kitab ka sandarbh) is zile se judta hai.
  • Dhyan rakhein: Kedarnath marg par monsoon me landslide aur flash flood ka jokhim.

Tehri Garhwal zila

Tehri Garhwal me Tehri Dam aur Tehri Lake hai. Devprayag (Ganga ka sangam), Dhanaulti, Chamba, Surkanda Devi aur Kunjapuri Devi iski pehchan hain. Purane Tehri shahar ko Tehri Dam ki jheel ne dubo diya.

  • Mukhyalay aur mandal: New Tehri, Garhwal.
  • Kshetrafal aur aabadi (lagbhag): 3,796 sq km; 6.2 lakh.
  • Pramukh nadiyan: Bhagirathi, Bhilangana, Alaknanda (Devprayag).
  • Pramukh sangam: Devprayag.
  • Siddh peeth: Surkanda Devi, Kunjapuri Devi, Chandrabadni.
  • Sthal: Dhanaulti, Chamba, Narendranagar, Muni Ki Reti, Shivpuri, Kaudiyala.
  • Trek: Sahastra Tal, Khatling Glacier, Panwali Kantha.
  • Itihas: Tehri riyasat 1949 me bharat me mili.

Udham Singh Nagar zila

Udham Singh Nagar Kumaon ka maidani (Terai) zila hai, jahan audyogik kshetra SIDCUL, GB Pant University (Pantnagar), Kashipur, Rudrapur aur Nanakmatta Sahib hain.

  • Mukhyalay aur mandal: Rudrapur, Kumaon.
  • Kshetrafal aur aabadi (lagbhag): lagbhag 2,900 sq km; 16.5 lakh.
  • Pramukh nadiyan: Sharda, Kosi, Dhela, Nihal.
  • Pramukh sthal: Rudrapur, Kashipur, Sitarganj, Kichha, Khatima, Gadarpur, Bazpur, Jaspur.
  • Dharmik: Nanakmatta Sahib, Chamunda Devi (Kashipur).
  • Sanskritik pehchan: Tharu aur Buksa janjatiyon ka kshetra.
  • Sanstha: GB Pant University, Pantnagar airport.
  • Sima: Nepal sima ke saath.

Uttarkashi zila

Uttarkashi kshetrafal me Uttarakhand ka sabse bada zila hai. Gangotri, Yamunotri, Harsil, Dodital, Har Ki Dun aur Kedarkantha yahin hain. Nehru Institute of Mountaineering bhi Uttarkashi me hai.

  • Mukhyalay aur mandal: Uttarkashi, Garhwal.
  • Kshetrafal aur aabadi (lagbhag): 8,016 sq km; 3.3 lakh.
  • Pramukh nadiyan: Bhagirathi, Yamuna, Tons, Asi Ganga, Kamal.
  • Dharmik: Gangotri, Yamunotri, Vishwanath mandir (Uttarkashi), Shakti mandir.
  • Trek: Har Ki Dun, Kedarkantha, Dodital, Dayara Bugyal, Gaumukh-Tapovan, Nelong.
  • Sanskritik pehchan: Rawain, Jaunsar-Bawar aur Har Ki Dun ghati ki sanskriti.
  • Dhyan rakhein: Upari kshetron me AMS aur barf; kai trek ke liye permit.

Kshetrafal aur aabadi ke aankde lagbhag hain (Census 2011). Zila-wise pages me exact aankde official source ke saath diye jayenge.

Har Zile ka Himachal Jodida (Kaun Si Jagah Kiske Jaisi)

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand ke har zile ke page ki Heading 201 me Himachal se tulna di gayi hai. Ek nazar me sabse lokpriya jodiyan: Nainital-Shimla, Mussoorie-Shimla, Kausani-Khajjiar, Auli-Solang, Chopta-Kheerganga, Lansdowne-Kasauli, Munsiyari-Kalpa aur Tehri Dam-Bhakra Dam.
