Jungli paudhe aur mushroom ke bare me chetavani har jagah dohrai gayi hai, kyunki galat pehchan khatarnak ho sakti hai.

Chamoli Ki Prasiddh Mukhya Dishein

Pahadi Khane Ki Pehchan

Pahadi khana mausam aur oonchai ke hisab se banta hai. Sardi me garam aur takat dene wali dal aur bhune anaj, garmi me halki sabziyan aur chhachh. Lohe ki kadhai me dheemi aanch par pakna, sil-batte par peesna, aur sarson ya ghee ka istemal inki khaas pehchan hai. Garhwal ki ye rasoi sirf Chamoli ki nahi, poore Garhwal ki saajhi viraasat hai.

GI Tag Wale Pahadi Utpad (December 2023)

2 December 2023 ko Uttarakhand ke 18 utpadon ko ek hi din me GI (Geographical Indication) tag ke certificate diye gaye. Isse rajya me kul GI tag wale utpad 27 ho gaye. Inme se jo khane se judte hain:

  • Uttarakhand Mandua (finger millet)
  • Uttarakhand Jhangora (barnyard millet)
  • Uttarakhand Gahat (horse gram)
  • Uttarakhand Kala Bhatt (kala soyabean)
  • Uttarakhand Lal Chawal (red rice)
  • Uttarakhand Chaulai (Ramdana)
  • Uttarakhand Pahari Toor (toor dal)
  • Uttarakhand Malta
  • Buransh Sharbat
  • Almora Lakhori Mirchi, Berinag Tea, Ramnagar Litchi, Ramgarh Aadu aur kuch hastshilp bhi isi suchi me the.

Is suchi me Chamoli ka lakdi ka Ramman mask bhi shamil tha, jo Chamoli ki sanskriti ki ek khaas pehchan hai (Chamoli: Lok-Kala: Aipan, Jyunti, Mask Aur Lakdi Ki Kalakari aur Chamoli: Mausmi Tyohar me iska vistar aata hai). Iske alawa pehle se Tejpat, Basmati, Aipan, Munsiyari ki safed rajma, Ringal craft, Thulma, Bhotia Dann, Chyura tel aur taamra (copper) utpad ko GI tag mil chuka tha. Dhyan dein: GI tag ka matlab hai ki naam ek khaas kshetra se jura hai. Ye vyanjan ka swaad ya swasthya laabh ka praman nahi hai.

Kafuli (Kaphuli)

Kya hai: Palak aur methi jaise hare patton se bana gaadha, hara saag.

Kab banta hai: Sardi aur basant me, jab hare patte aam hote hain.

Ek prachalit vidhi: Palak aur methi ko dho kar kaat lein aur ubaal lein. Phir sil-batte ya mixer me motta peesein. Lohe ki kadhai me tel ya ghee me jeera, lahsun aur sookhi lal mirch ka tadka lagayein. Peesa hua saag daalein aur chawal ke aate ka ghol milakar dheemi aanch par gadha hone tak pakayein. Garam chawal ya mandua ki roti ke saath parosein.

Kisse jodkar khayein: Chawal, mandua ki roti.

Kai jagah ise “Kaphuli” ya “Kaphli” bhi likha jata hai.

Phaanu

Kya hai: Bhigoi hui dal ko sil-batte par peesakar, lohe ki kadhai me dheemi aanch par gadha pakayi gayi dish.

Kis dal se: Gahat, toor, urad ya masoor jaisi dal se, ghar ke chalan ke hisab se.

Ek prachalit vidhi: Dal ko raat bhar bhigoyein. Peese hue paste ko tel ya ghee me jeera, jakhiya ya jambu ke tadke ke saath pakayein. Namak aur halka masala milakar gadha hone tak hilate rahein.

Kisse jodkar khayein: Garam chawal.

Dal ko bhigoyein aur peeskar pakane ki ye vidhi dal ko pachane me halka banati hai, aisa kai log mante hain, par is par koi vaigyanik dava na karein.

Chainsoo

Kya hai: Bhuni hui kali urad (ya gahat) dal ko peeskar banayi gayi dish.

Ek prachalit vidhi: Dal ko lohe ki kadhai me bina tel ke sukha bhoon lein, jab tak khushboo na aaye. Thanda karke motta peesein. Phir tel ya ghee me jeera ya jakhiya ka tadka dekar, peese hue dal ko paani ke saath pakayein aur gadha karein. Garam chawal ke saath parosein.

Khaas baat: Chainsoo mukhya roop se Garhwal ki dish mani jati hai. Kumaon me iske jaise vyanjan alag naam se milte hain.

Thechwani

Kya hai: Mooli (ya aloo) ko kootkar bani sabzi.

Naam ka arth: “Thechna” ka matlab kootna ya kuchalna hota hai.

Ek prachalit vidhi: Mooli ko chhilkar patthar ya sil-batte se halka kootein. Tel me jakhiya ya jeera ka tadka dekar, kuti hui mooli, namak, haldi aur mirch daalein aur dhak kar pakayein. Roti ya chawal ke saath parosein.

Bhatt Ki Churkani

Kya hai: Kale bhatt (kali soyabean) ko bhoonkar aur peeskar banayi gayi patli-gaadhi sabzi.

Ek prachalit vidhi: Bhatt ko lohe ki kadhai me bhoonkar peesein. Tel me jeera, lahsun aur masale ka tadka dekar peesa hua bhatt aur paani daalein aur dheemi aanch par pakayein. Garam chawal ya roti ke saath khayein.

Kala Bhatt ko GI tag mila hai (upar dekhein).

Dubuk (Dubka)

Kya hai: Dal ke paste se bani gadhi dish, Phaanu se milti-julti.

Fark: Dubuk aam taur par bhatt ya gahat ke gaadhe paste se bana mana jata hai. Gaon-gaon me naam aur vidhi me bhed hota hai.

Ras-Bhaat, Gahat Ki Dal, Jholi Aur Kaphli

  • Gahat ki dal: Gahat (horse gram) ko ubaal kar bani dal. Sardi me khaas pasand ki jati hai. Gahat ko GI tag mila hai.
  • Ras-bhaat: Gahat ke rase (shorbe) aur chawal ka mel.
  • Jholi: Garhwali kadhi, jo chhachh aur besan ya chawal ke aate se bani patli kadhi hoti hai.
  • Kaphli: Is naam se Kumaon aur Garhwal me hare patton ki sabzi ke jaisi dish milti hai (upar Kafuli dekhein).

Chamoli: Paramparik Roti Aur Chawal Ki Tayyariyan

Mandua Ki Roti

Mandua (finger millet, ragi) ka aata gunde jaane par garm paani ki zaroorat padti hai kyunki isme gluten nahi hota. Aate ko garm paani se gundhkar hathon se gol karke tawe par ya seedhe aanch par sekte hain. Ise ghee, makhan, gud ya sabzi ke saath khate hain. Mandua ko GI tag mila hai aur ye rajya ke Garhwal aur Kumaon dono ke bhojan ka hissa hai.

Bedu Roti Aur Bhari Hui Rotiyan

Garhwal me ek mashhoor roti “Bedu roti” ke naam se jaani jati hai, jisme gehun ki roti me urad ya anya dal ka bhara hua paste bhara jata hai. Kai jagah gahat ya mandua ke paste se bhari hui rotiyan bhi banti hain. Naam aur bharne ki saamagri gaon-gaon me alag hoti hai, isliye “kuch jagah” likhna theek hai.

Jhangora Ka Bhaat Aur Kheer

Jhangora (barnyard millet) ko chawal ki jagah ubaal kar bhaat ki tarah khaya jata hai. Isse kheer bhi banti hai, jo tyohar par banti hai. Jhangora ko GI tag mila hai. Ek news report me jhangora ko iron, calcium aur vitamin se bharpoor bataya gaya hai aur kuch jagah madhumeh ke liye achha bataya gaya hai. Madhumeh ya kisi bhi bimari ke liye isse ilaj ka dava na karein. “Poshan ke liye achha mana jata hai” likhna kaafi hai.

Lal Chawal (Red Rice) Aur Anya Chawal

Uttarakhand lal chawal ko GI tag mila hai. Kuch news reports me ise Uttarkashi ke Purola kshetra me ugne wala bataya gaya hai. Chamoli me bhi paramparik dhan ki kismein ugayi jaati hain.

Gehun Ki Roti, Makke Ki Roti Aur Anya

Sardi me makke ki roti, jau ki roti aur gehun ki roti bhi banti hain. Kuttu (buckwheat) ke aate ki puri vrat me khayi jati hai. Ogal aur phaphar jaise kuttu ke prakar Chamoli ke oonche kshetra me ugte hain (District Industrial Profile ke anusar).

Sawal-Jawab: Roti Aur Chawal

Mandua ki roti kaise banti hai?
Aate ko garm paani se gundh kar haath se bel kar seka jata hai.
Jhangora kya hota hai?
Barnyard millet, chawal ka pahadi vikalp.
Bedu roti kya hoti hai?
Dal bhari hui Garhwali roti.

Chamoli: Chutney, Raita, Achar Aur Pisyun Loon

Bhangjeera Ki Chutney

Bhangjeera (perilla) ke bhune hue beej ko namak, mirch aur nimbu ya imli ke saath sil-batte par peesa jata hai. Isse khatti-teekhi chutney banti hai jo roti aur bhaat ke saath khayi jati hai. Beej ko bhoonne se uski khushboo badhti hai.

Bhang Ke Beej Ki Chutney (Zaroori Savdhani Ke Saath)

Garhwal aur Kumaon me bhang (hemp) ke beej ki chutney prasiddh hai. Ye beej nashe wale nahi hote aur paramparik roop se khaaye jaate hain. Par bhang (cannabis) ke paudhe aur patte ke upyog par Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act ke niyam hain.

  • Sirf beej ki chutney ki baat karein.
  • Kanooni sthiti ke liye prashasan ya vakil se salah lein.

Pahadi Kheere Ka Raita

Pahadi kheera (motta, peele rang ka) ko kaddukas karke dahi me milaya jata hai. Isme peesi hui rai (sarson ke dane) ka tikhapan daala jata hai, jo naak me chadhne wala jhanjhahat deta hai. Ye Garhwal aur Kumaon dono me garmi ka ek lokpriya raita hai.

Darim (Anardana) Ki Chutney

Darim (jangli anaar ke sukhe beej) ki chutney khatti hoti hai aur roti ke saath khayi jati hai.

Timur Ki Chutney

Timur ek pahadi masala hai, jisse chutney me ek alag zayka aata hai. Timur ki chutney aur pisyun loon me iska istemal aam hai.

Linguda Aur Mooli Ka Achar

Linguda (jungli fern ki naram nokein) ka achar sardi aur barsaat ke mausam me banta hai. Mooli ka achar sardi me banta hai. Linguda ko khud todne se pehle sahi pehchan zaroori hai (Chamoli: Jungli Khaane Ki Cheezein Aur Mausmi Saag dekhein).

Pisyun Loon

Pisyun loon (jise “pahadi namak” bhi kehte hain) sil-batte par pisa hua namak hai jisme lahsun ke patte ya lahsun, hari mirch, dhaniya, jeera aur timur milaye jaate hain. Kai jagah ise nimbu ya santre ke saath khaya jata hai. Aaj ye “Pahadi Namak” ke naam se online bhi bikta hai.

Sawal-Jawab: Chutney Aur Achar

Bhangjeera kya hai?
Pahadi perilla beej.
Pisyun loon kya hota hai?
Sil-batte par pisa masala namak.
Timur kya hai?
Pahadi masala, jiska swaad thoda jhanjhahat wala hota hai.
Darim kya hai?
Jangli anaar ke sukhe beej.

Chamoli: Jungli Khaane Ki Cheezein Aur Mausmi Saag

Sabse pehle suraksha: Jungli paudhe, phal aur mushroom ko bina sahi pehchan ke kabhi na khayein. Kai zehreele paudhe khane wale paudhon se milte-julte hote hain. Sanrakshit kshetra (jaise national park) me kisi bhi paudhe ya phal ko todna kanooni apradh ho sakta hai.

Jungli Saag Aur Sabziyan

  • Kandali / Bichhu ghas (Stinging nettle): Chubhne wala paudha, jisse pakane par chubhan khatam ho jati hai. Iska saag lokpriya hai. Iske rishon se bana kapda “Bichhu Buti” ko December 2023 me GI tag mila, aur ye rishe mukhya roop se Almora aur Chamoli ke kshetron me paye jaate hain.
  • Lingda (Linguda): Jungli fern ki naram nokein. Isse sabzi aur achar banta hai. Fern ki kuch prajatiyan zehreeli hoti hain, isliye sahi pehchan zaroori hai. Zyadatar kachcha na khayein, achhi tarah pakakar hi khayein.
  • Kankoda (Kantola): Kaante wali choti sabzi jo barsaat me milti hai.
  • Jungli pyaz aur lahsun: Oonche kshetra me jambu (faran) jaise masale ugte hain.

Jungli Mushroom Ki Chetavani

Barsaat me kai jungli mushroom ug aate hain, aur unme se kuch jaanleva ho sakte hain.

Buransh Ka Sharbat

Buransh ke phool se banne wala sharbat Uttarakhand ki ek jaani-maani pehchan hai, aur ise December 2023 me GI tag mila. Garmiyon me ise thanda karke piya jata hai. Buransh ke phool todne par kuch jagah pabandi ya niyam ho sakte hain (khaaskar sanrakshit kshetra me), isliye sthaniya niyam dekh lein.

Jungli Khane Ka Arthik Pahlu

Jungli phal aur saag gaon ke log apne upyog ke liye todte hain, aur kuch ko bazaar me bhi bechte hain. Is par aap gaon ki mahilaon ki kahaniyan jodkar page ko manviya bana sakte hain. Isse gaon ki parampara bhi darj hoti hai.

Chamoli: Paramparik Mithai, Tyohar Ke Pakwaan Aur Prasad

Garhwal Ke Pakwaan

  • Arsa (Arse): Chawal ke aate aur gud se bana, tel ya ghee me tala hua meetha pakwaan. Shaadi aur tyohar ke avsar par banta hai.
  • Puwa: Gehun ke aate aur gud se bana meetha, tala hua pakwaan.
  • Gulgule: Gud aur aate se bane meethe pakode.
  • Rote (Roth): Mota, meetha, gehun ke aate aur gud se bana roth, jo kai jagah devta ko chadhaya jata hai.
  • Jhangore ki kheer: Jhangora (barnyard millet) aur doodh se bani kheer.
  • Chaulai (Ramdana) ke ladoo: Chaulai ko GI tag mila hai.

Kumaon Ki Mithai

Singori (khoya se bani aur maalu ke patte me lipti mithai), Bal Mithai (bhoone hue khoye aur cheeni ke daanon se bani) aur Singal (Kumaoni pakwaan) mukhya roop se Kumaon, khaaskar Almora, se jude hain.

Badrinath Ka Prasad

Badrinath mandir me aam taur par van tulsi ki mala, kachchi chana dal, mishri aur gola (sukha nariyal) aur sookha mewa prasad ke roop me diya jata hai. 2006 me HESCO sanstha ki madad se Chaulai (Ramdana) ke ladoo ko bhi Badrinath me prasad ke roop me shuru kiya gaya, jisse sthaniya logon ko rozgar mila. Prasad ke roop me kya diya jata hai, ye mandir samiti ke niyam se badal sakta hai.

Tyohar Aur Pakwaan Ka Rishta

Har tyohar ke saath ek pakwaan juda hota hai: jaise Makar Sankranti par Kumaon me aate aur gud se bane “Ghughutiya” jaise pakwaan banaye jaate hain, aur Garhwal me us din khichdi ka mahatva hota hai.

Sawal-Jawab: Mithai Aur Prasad

Arsa kya hai?
Chawal ke aate aur gud ka meetha pakwaan.
Badrinath me kya prasad milta hai?
Aam taur par van tulsi ki mala, chana dal, mishri aur gola.
Singori kahan ki mithai hai?
Kumaon (Almora) ki.

Chamoli: Paramparik Peya Aur Khameeri Pey

Chhyang: Sanskritik Jaankari

Chhyang (ya chhang) Bhotia aur Tibetan kshetra ki paramparik khameeri pey hai, jo jau ya anya anaj se banta hai. Ye oonche seema ke kshetra (jaise Niti aur Mana) ki sanskriti ke kuch hisson me raha hai. Sharab ka utpadan aur bikri Uttarakhand Excise Act ke niyam ke adhin hai.

Buransh Ka Sharbat (Paramparik Peya Aur Khameeri Pey)

Buransh ka sharbat (ya squash) Chamoli ke oonche kshetra ki ek mashhoor garmi ki peya hai. GI tag ke baad ise bahar ke bazaar me bhi pahchan mili.

Malta Aur Anya Fal Ke Ras

Malta (citrus ka ek prakar) ko December 2023 me GI tag mila hai. Sardi me malta ka ras aur garmi me kaccha aam panna jaisi peya prachalit hain.

Gud-Adrak Ki Chai Aur Van Tulsi Ki Chai

Sardi me gud aur adrak ki chai bahut peeyi jati hai. Mana gaon me “Bharat ki antim chai ki dukaan” ke naam se mashhoor ek chai ki dukaan hai, jahan van tulsi ki chai milti hai (kai yatra blogs me iska zikr hai).

Chhachh, Lassi Aur Doodh

Chhachh (matha) aur doodh pahadi ghar ke roz ke peya hain, khaaskar garmiyon me.

Sawal-Jawab: Peya

Buransh ka sharbat kya hai?
Buransh ke phool se bana sharbat, jise GI tag mila hai.
Mana me kya khaas chai milti hai?
Van tulsi ki chai (yatra blogs ke anusar).
Chhyang kya hai?
Bhotia kshetra ki paramparik khameeri pey (sirf sanskritik jaankari).

Chamoli: Pahadi Rasoi Ki Banawat Aur Bartan

Paramparik Rasoi Ka Dhancha

Pahadi ghar me rasoi aam taur par ghar ke andar ek kone me hoti thi, jahan mitti ka chulha hota tha. Chulhe ke upar lakdi ka ek mach (kai jagah “dhumala” jaisa naam) banaya jata tha, jahan anaj, beej aur lakdi chulhe ke dhuyein se sukhayi jaati thi. Is jaankari me gaon-gaon me naam badal sakte hain, isliye apne gaon ke naam jodein.

Lohe Ki Kadhai Ka Ghar Me Mahatva

Lohe ke bartan me khana banane se khane me thodi matra me iron aa sakta hai, aisa kai adhyayan batate hain. Par isse kisi bhi anemia (khoon ki kami) ke ilaj ka dava na karein. Saaf karne ke baad sukhakar thoda tel lagane se lohe ke bartan me jung nahi lagti.

Aadhunik Rasoi Me Badlav

Ab bahut se gharon me gas ka chulha, pressure cooker aur steel ke bartan aa gaye hain. Dhuyein se hone wali sehat ki samasya (jaise aankh aur saans ki takleef) ki wajah se sarkar ne Ujjwala jaisi yojanayein chalayi hain. Paramparik rasoi ki baat karte hue is baat ka dhyan rakhein ki dhuyein wali rasoi me kaam karne wali mahilaon ki sehat par bura asar padta hai.

Rasoi Ki Tasveer Aur Kahani Ka Section

Neeche ek sawal rakhein: “Aapke gaon me is bartan ko kya kehte hain?” Isse sthaniya logon ki bhagidari badhegi.

Sawal-Jawab: Rasoi
