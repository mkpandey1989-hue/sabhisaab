Kumaoni khana sirf pet bharne ka tareeka nahi, pahad ki kheti, mausam aur rishton ki kahani hai. Is hisse mein wo sab hai jo Nainital aur Kumaon ki thali me milta hai: dal, saag, roti, chutney, jangli phal, mithai, peene ki cheezein aur wo bartan jisme ye sab pakta hai.

Naam ke baare me ek baat: Kumaon aur Garhwal ke kai pakwaano ke naam ilaake ke hisaab se badal jate hain (jaise Garhwal ki “Kafuli” Kumaon me “Kapa” ya “Kaphli” bhi kehlati hai). Apne gaon ya parivar ka naam alag ho to wo bhi sahi hai.

Nainital ke Mashhoor Mukhya Vyanjan

Kafuli, Phaanu, Chainsoo, Thechwani, Bhatt ki Churkani, Dubuk, Ras-Bhaat, Gahat ki Dal & Jholi/Kaphli

Kumaoni thali ka dil dal aur saag hai: Bhatt ki Churkani, Gahat ki Dal, Dubuk, Kapa/Kafuli, Jhoi (kadhi) aur Ras-Bhaat. Ye zyadatar loh ki kadhai me dheemi aanch par pakte hain aur bhaat ya mandua ki roti ke saath khaye jate hain.

Loh ki kadhai kyun?
Pahadi pakwaan aksar loh (iron) ki kadhai me dheemi aanch par pakte hain. Isse khane me ek alag swaad aata hai aur kuch loha khane me bhi aa jata hai (ye aam jaankari hai, kisi bimari ka ilaaj nahi).

Ek saral recipe: Bhatt ki Churkani (4 logon ke liye)

  • Samagri: Kaali bhatt 1 cup, sarson ka tel 2 chammach, jeera aur 1 sookhi lal mirch, haldi aadha chammach, namak swaad anusar, hari mirch aur dhaniya.
  • Bhatt bhunna: Bhatt ko bina tel ke kadhai me dheemi aanch par chatakne tak bhoon lein.
  • Pesna: Bhune hue bhatt ko mixer ya silbatta me mota pees lein.
  • Tadka: Kadhai me tel garam karke jeera aur lal mirch daalein, phir haldi.
  • Pakana: Pisa hua bhatt aur 3 cup paani daalkar 25 se 30 minute dheemi aanch par pakayein. Beech-beech me hilate rahein taaki neeche na lage.
  • Parosna: Bhaat ke saath garam parosein; upar se hara dhaniya.

Aalu ke Gutke (aasan): Chhote aalu ubal kar chhil lein. Garam sarson ke tel me jakhiya (na ho to jeera) aur sookhi lal mirch ka tadka daalein, aalu, haldi, namak aur hari dhaniya daal kar halka bhoon lein. Garam poori ya roti ke saath khayein.

Nainital: Paramparik Roti aur Chawal ke Pakwan

Mandua ki Roti, Gahat ke Parathe, Chhoi/Lesu Roti, Jhangore ki Bhaat & Gahat Ras-Bhaat

Pahad ki mukhya rotiyan Mandua ki roti (ragi), Gahat ke parathe aur lesu roti hain. Jhangora ka bhaat chaawal ka halka vikalp hai aur Gahat Ras-Bhaat sardiyon ka garam khaana.

  • Mandua ki roti: Mandua (ragi) ke aate ko garam paani se ghol kar gundhte hain aur haath se thapkar tave par sekte hain. Isko ghee, gud ya hare saag ke saath khaya jata hai. Mandua ka aata sakht hota hai, isliye iska aata garam paani me gundhna zaroori hai.
  • Gahat ke Parathe (Bedoo / Bhari roti): Gahat ki pithi bharkar bani roti, jise ghee ya makhan ke saath khate hain.
  • Lesu Roti / Chhoi: Gehun ki roti ke andar masaledar mandua ki pithi bharkar ki gayi roti. Kuch jagah ise gaon ke tyohar aur bhoj me banate hain.
  • Jhangore ka Bhaat: Jhangora (barnyard millet) ko chaawal ki tarah pakaya jata hai. Ye halka aur jaldi pakne wala hota hai.
  • Gahat Ras-Bhaat: Gahat ka ras bhaat ke upar daalkar, sardiyon me khaya jane wala garam khana.

Mandua ki roti banane ke 4 asaan nuskhe

  • 1 cup mandua ke aate me thoda sa gehun ka aata milayein, roti phatne ka darr kam hota hai.
  • Paani ubalta hua ho; aate me daalkar chammach se jaldi milayein, phir haath se masalein.
  • Gole banakar haath se dabayein ya patle plastic/ kapde par bel kar tave par dono taraf sekein.
  • Ghee ya gud ke saath garam parosein.

Note: Mandua ya anya anaj ko kisi bimari ke ilaaj ke roop me na samjhein. Bimari ke mareez ya garbhwati mahila apne doctor ya dietician se khane ki salah lein.

Nainital: Chutney, Raita, Achaar aur Pisyun Loon

Bhangjeera Chutney, Bhang ki Chutney, Pahadi Kheere ka Raita, Darim Chutney, Timur Chutney & Linguda/Mooli Achar

Pahadi chutney me Bhangjeera (perilla beej) ki chutney, Timur ki chutney aur Pahadi kheere ka raita sabse mashhoor hain. Pahadi raita me sarson ka teekhapan khaas hota hai, aur Mooli ya Linguda ka achar sardi me banta hai.

  • Bhangjeera chutney: Bhangjeera (perilla ke beej) ko bhunkar nimbu, hari mirch aur namak ke saath pissa jata hai. Swad me halka khatta aur dhuaan sa.
  • Bhang (Hemp) ke beej ki chutney: Pahadi gaon me bhang ke beej (hemp seeds) ki chutney parampara se bante hain. Ye sirf beej hai; ye nasha nahi hai. Bhang ke patte, phool aur sookha maal ka istemal gair-kanooni ho sakta hai. Beej ki chutney ke bahane kisi bhi tarah ki bhang ki kheti ya nasha na karein.
  • Pahadi kheere ka raita: Pahadi kheere (peele-hare, mote) ko kas kar dahi me milaya jata hai aur peesi hui rai (sarson) ke teekhapan ke saath tadka lagta hai.
  • Darim (jangli anaar) ki chutney: Pahadi jangli anaar ke sookhe daane (anardana) ki khatti chutney.
  • Timur ki chutney: Timur (Zanthoxylum) ke beej ke halke sunn-pan wale swaad se bani chutney.
  • Mooli ya Linguda ka achar: Sardi ki mooli, nimbu aur masalon se bana pahadi achar.

Timur kya hai? Timur ek pahadi masala hai jise munh me rakhne par halki sunn-pan (jeebh par jhunjhunahat) mehsoos hoti hai. Ye Nainital: Jadi-Booti aur Paramparik Paudha-Gyan me bhi aaya hai.

Pisyun Loon: Silbatte par Pisa Pahadi Namak

Pisyun Loon (pisa hua namak) pahadi swaad ka sabse khaas masaledar namak hai. Ise silbatta par pehle rock namak, lehsun ke patte, hari mirch, dhaniya, jeera aur timur ko ek saath pees kar banate hain, aur phal, kheere ya roti ke saath khate hain.

Ghar par banane ka tareeka (1 chhoti katori):

  • Samagri: Moti rock namak 2 chammach, hare lehsun ke patte (ya 3 kali lehsun) 1 mutthi, hari mirch 2, dhaniya patta, bhuna jeera aadha chammach, timur 5 se 6 daane.
  • Pissna: Silbatta par pehle namak aur jeera ko halka kootein, phir baaki cheezein jodkar bareek pees lein.
  • Parosna: Mooli, kheera, matte, nimbu ya aadoo ke saath.

Zyada namak ka istemal sehat ke liye theek nahi hota, isliye ise sirf swaad ke liye chimti bhar khayein. Blood pressure ya gurde ke mareez doctor ki salah se hi namak ka istemal karein.

Nainital: Jungli Khane Layak Cheezein aur Mausami Saag

Jungli Saag-Phal: Bichhu Ghaas, Linguda, Kankoda, Ghingaru, Kafal, Hisalu, Kilmora, Bedu aur Buransh Ras

Pahad ke jungle aur khet ki mendon par mausam ke hisaab se kai khane layak sabziyan aur phal milte hain: Bicchu ghas, Linguda, Kankoda, Kaphal, Hisalu, Ghingaru, Kilmora aur Bedu. Inhe jaankari ke bina na todein, kyunki kuch paudhe zehrile ya galat pehchane ja sakte hain.

Kaphal ki kahani: Kumaon ka mashhoor lok-geet “Kafal Pako Maine Nahi Chakho” is phal ke mausam me gaya jata hai. Kaphal pakne ka mausam dhup aur garmi ka hota hai.

Zaroori savdhaniyan (zaroor padhein):

  • Pehchan: Jungle ki koi bhi cheez bina sahi pehchan ke na khayein. Galat paudha pet kharab kar sakta hai ya zehrila ho sakta hai.
  • Pakane ka niyam: Bicchu ghas aur Linguda ko hamesha acchi tarah pakakar hi khayein. Kaccha ya adhpaka na khayein.
  • Bachche aur garbhwati mahila: Naye jangli khane ko ghar me pehli baar aajmane se pehle doctor se poochh lein.
  • Jungle ke niyam: Corbett, Reserve Forest aur Sanrakshit kshetra me jungle se kuch todna, uthana ya bechna Van Vibhag ke niyam ke virudh ho sakta hai. Gaon ke apne van panchayat ya khet ke kinare ki jaankari ke saath hi ye cheezein lein (Nainital: Van Panchayat aur Samudayik Van Adhikar).
  • Jangli janwaron ke liye chhodein: Sab kuch na todein; chidiya aur bhaaloo jaise jaanwar bhi inhi phalon par nirbhar hain.

Nainital: Paramparik Mithai, Tyohar ke Pakwan aur Prasad

Arsa, Singori, Bal Mithai, Chocolate, Jhangore ki Kheer, Rote, Puwa, Singal, Lagad & Gulgule

Kumaon ki mashhoor mithaiyan Bal Mithai, Singori, Chocolate (khoya wali) aur Singal hain, jinka ghar Almora kshetra ko maana jata hai lekin Nainital me bhi ye mil jati hain. Tyoharon par Gulgule, Puwa, Arsa aur Rote jaise ghareloo pakwaan bante hain.

Gulgule banane ki saral vidhi (4 log):

  • Samagri: 1 cup gehun ka aata, aadha cup gud (garam paani me ghola hua), 1 chhoti chammach saunf, chutki bhar baking soda ya thoda sa dahi, tel taliye ke liye.
  • Ghol: Gud ka paani chhan kar aate me daalein, saunf milayein aur dheere-dheere gaadha ghol banayein (na zyada patla, na zyada gaadha).
  • Aaram: 10 minute dhak kar rakh dein.
  • Talna: Garam tel me chammach se chhote gole daalkar sunehra hone tak dheemi aanch par talein.

Tyohar se jodkar samjhein:

  • Makar Sankranti (Uttarayani): Ghughuti aur Gulgule, jinhe bachche subah “Kale Kauva” kehkar kauvon ko khilate hain (kumaoni parampara).
  • Pooja ka prasad: Rote aur Puwa mandir me devtaon ko chadhaye jate hain.
  • Shaadi aur bhoj: Singal aur Arsa jaise pakwaan.

Note: Kisi bhi mithai me chini ya gud zyada hota hai. Madhumeh (diabetes) ke mareez apni khurak doctor ya dietician ki salah se tay karein.

Nainital: Paramparik Peya aur Khameer Wale Pey

Chhyang / Jaan / Kacchi (Traditional Tribal Millet/Barley Fermented Brew in High Border Valleys using ‘Balma’ Herbal Starter Cake), Buransh Squash, Malta Juice & Gud-Adrak ki Chai

Nainital ki sabse mashhoor pahadi pe-yein Buransh ka squash, Malta ka juice aur gud-adrak ki chai hain. Chhyang jaisi parampragat kiddi hui (fermented) peya sarhadi unche ghati kshetron ke samuday ki parampara hai aur Nainital zile ki pehchan nahi hai.

  • Buransh squash: Buransh (Rhododendron arboreum) ke laal phoolon se bana meetha sharbat. Garmi me kai log ise pasand karte hain, aur Nainital, Mukteshwar aur Kumaon ki dukanon me milta hai. Savdhani: Buransh ki sirf ek hi prajati (Rhododendron arboreum) khaane layak maani jati hai; Rhododendron ki kai doosri prajatiyan zehrili hoti hain. Isliye ghar par jungle ke phool todkar squash na banayein, sirf FSSAI-licensed ya bharosemand banane walon ka squash kharidein. Ye koi dawa ya ilaaj nahi hai.
  • Malta juice: Kumaon ke Malta (meethe-khatte santre jaisa phal) ka taaza ras. Sardiyon me aam, aur phal mandi me sasta milta hai (Nainital: Bagwani, Phalon ke Kshetra aur Nakdi Fasalein).
  • Gud-Adrak ki chai: Chai me chini ki jagah gud aur kuchli hui adrak; sardi ki thandi subah ki sabse pasandida chai. Gud ko doodh me daalne par doodh phat sakta hai, isliye gud ko thoda thanda karke ya paani me ghol kar daalein.
  • Nimbu Saana: Pahadi galgal (bada pahadi nimbu) ko dahi, gud aur masalon ke saath mila kar bana pahadi nimbu ka sanana; sardiyon ki dhoop me khaya jata hai.
  • Chhyang / Jaan: Ye Himalaya ke sarhadi unche kshetron me anaj (jau/ mandua) se banne wali parampragat fermented peya hai, jise banane me ek jadi-booti ki starter tikiya (aksar “balma” kaha jata hai) lagti hai. Ye sanskritik jaankari hai. Nainital zile me ye mukhya paramparik peya nahi hai, aur sharab se jude kisi bhi vyapar, banane ya bechne ke liye Uttarakhand ki Excise niyamavali ke tahat license zaroori hai. Kam umr ke logon ke liye sharab ka sevan kanooni roop se aur sehat ke hisaab se uchit nahi.

Nainital: Pahadi Kitchen Architecture & Utensils

Loh ki Kadhai (Iron Wok Science), Silbatta (Grinding Stone), Kansi Thali, Bhaddu, Parat, Mud Chulha & Dhumala (Smoke-drying Shelf)

Pahadi rasoi me loh ki kadhai, silbatta (pisne ka patthar), kansi (kansa) ki thali, bhaari pital ka bartan (bhaddu), parat aur mitti ka chulha khaas jagah rakhte hain. Ye bartan dheemi aanch par pakne aur swaad ke saath-saath saamagri ko lambe samay tak surakshit rakhne ke kaam aate the.

Bartan ki dekhbhaal ke aasan tareeke:

  • Loh ki kadhai: Pehli baar kharidne par sarson ke tel se “season” karein; paani me der tak na chhodein, warna zang lagta hai. Khatti cheezein (imli, tamatar) zyada der pakane se kadhai ka rang aur swaad badal sakta hai.
  • Kansa / pital / tamba: Kalai (tin coating) purani ya ghisi ho to khatti cheezein (dahi, nimbu, imli) un bartanon me pakane ya rakhne se bachein. Kalai karwane ke baad hi istemal karein.
  • Silbatta: Istemal ke baad dhokar sukhayein; kabhi-kabhi chhainni se tarasha jaata hai taaki pisna theek chalta rahe.

Chulhe ka dhuaan aur sehat: Band rasoi me lakdi ya upalon ka dhuaan saans ki bimariyon ka kaaran ban sakta hai, khaaskar mahilaon aur bachchon ke liye. Is liye hawadar rasoi, chimney ya LPG jaisi saaf eendhan sahulat ka istemal behtar hai. Ujjwala jaisi yojanaon ki jaankari apne gram pradhan ya najdeeki gas agency se lein (E-District / Apuni Sarkar: Nainital me Sarkari Praman Patra).

Nainital me pahadi swaad kahan mile?
Nainital ki Mall Road aur Tallital ke aas-paas kai restaurants me pahadi thali (bhatt ki churkani, gahat ki dal, aalu ke gutke) milti hai. Nainital-Almora highway par Garampani ke pakode aur raita ghoomne wale logon ke beech jaane-pehchaane hain. Haldwani me bhi sthaniya dhaabon par Kumaoni khana milta hai. Daam, samay aur menu badalte rehte hain, isliye jaane se pehle taaza review ya sthaniya logon se pooch lein.

Note: Khane ki jaankari sanskritik aur aam ghareloo jaankari par aadharit hai. Kisi bimari, allergy, garbhawastha ya vishesh diet ke liye apne doctor ya dietician se salah lein. Jangli phal, saag aur jadi-booti bina sahi pehchan ke na khayein.

Nainital: Pahadi khana aur mithai: Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Nainital ka mashhoor khana kya hai?
Bhatt ki churkani, aalu ke gutke, kapa-kafuli, mandua roti, aur Garampani ke pakode.
Kumaoni thali mein mukhya roop se kya hota hai?
Dal aur saag: Bhatt ki Churkani, Gahat ki Dal, Dubuk, Kapa/Kafuli, Jhoi aur Ras-Bhaat, jo bhaat ya mandua ki roti ke saath khaye jaate hain.
Pisyun Loon kya hai?
Silbatta par rock namak, lehsun ke patte, hari mirch, dhaniya, jeera aur timur ko ek saath pees kar bana masaledar namak, jo phal, kheere ya roti ke saath khaya jata hai.
Kumaon ki mashhoor mithaiyan kaun si hain?
Bal Mithai, Singori, Chocolate (khoya wali) aur Singal. Inka ghar Almora kshetra maana jata hai, par Nainital mein bhi milti hain.
Jungle ki sabziyan todte samay kya dhyan rakhein?
Bicchu ghas, Linguda, Kankoda, Kaphal jaisi cheezein jaankari ke bina na todein, kyonki kuch paudhe zehrile ya galat pehchane ja sakte hain.
