Is hisse mein: Tehri Garhwal ke Puranik sandarbh, shaktipeeth aur mandir, gram devta aur jagar, Garhwal ka Panwar vansh, Gorkha shasan, Tehri Riyasat, Tehri ki azaadi ki ladaiyan, Chipko, aur madhyakaleen veer-gathayein. "Tehri Garhwal ka itihas", "Tehri riyasat ke raja", "Surkanda Devi ki kahani" jaise sawal yahan seedhe poochhe jate hain.

Jahan farak hai wahan lagaya hai. Prakashit karne se pehle Atkinson ka Himalayan Gazetteer, Harikrishna Raturi ka "Garhwal ka Itihas" aur Shiv Prasad Dabral ke "Uttarakhand ka Itihas" jaise sandarbhon se ek baar match karein (Tehri Garhwal par likhi mukhya Kitaben aur Shodh).

Tehri Garhwal: Puranon ka Bhugol: Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand

Seedha jawab: Skanda Puran ke do bhag Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand ke naam se jaane jate hain. Kedarkhand Garhwal kshetra ka aur Manaskhand Kumaon kshetra ka Puranik bhugol batata hai. Tehri Garhwal Kedarkhand ke antargat aata hai, jahan Bhagirathi, Devprayag aur Tapovan jaise sthano ka zikr hota hai.

Skanda Puran, Mahabharat & Kalidas ke Sandarbh

  • Kedarkhand: Garhwal ke tirtho (Kedarnath, Badrinath, Gangotri, Yamunotri aur prayag) ka varnan.
  • Mahabharat: Pandavo ki Mahaprasthan yatra Himalaya ki taraf maani jati hai. Garhwal ki kai jagah (Kedar kshetra) is katha se judi hain.
  • Brahma ki tapasya: Tehri naam ka ek sthaniya mool Brahma ji ki tapasya se bhi joda jata hai (Etymology: Jile ke Naam ka Itihasik Mool).
  • Devprayag: Kedarkhand me Devprayag ko mahatvapurn tirth bataya gaya hai.

Tehri Garhwal: Pramukh Mandir, Dham aur Math

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal ke mukhya mandir hain Surkanda Devi, Kunjapuri Devi, Chandrabadni Devi (teeno Shaktipeeth ke roop me manye), Raghunath Mandir (Devprayag), Sem Mukhem Nagraja Mandir aur Koteshwar Mahadev (Tehri Dam ke paas).

Zaroori: Teeno Shaktipeeth ki shatik kathayein (Sati ke kaunse ang kahan gire) ki alag-alag manyata hain.

Kapat (dwar) aur samay: In mandiro ke dwar saal bhar khule rehte hain (Char Dham ki tarah band nahi hote). Aarti aur darshan ke samay badalte rehte hain, isliye mandir samiti se confirm karein.

Dhyan dene wali baat: Devprayag ki Nakshatra Vedhshala (khagol-vedhshala) bhi is kshetra ka ek anokha sthal hai.

Budha Kedar: Bhilangana Ghati ka Prachin Shiv Dham

Seedha jawab: Budha Kedar Tehri Garhwal ki Balganga ghati (Ghansali kshetra) me sthit Bhagwan Shiv ka prachin mandir hai. Lok-manyata ke anusar Pandav Kedarnath jaate samay yahan Shiv se "budhe" (vriddh) roop me mile the, isliye ise Budha Kedar kehte hain. Yahan Balganga aur Dharmganga ka sangam bhi mana jata hai (pushti karein: sahi sthan, doori aur katha sthaniya mandir samiti se confirm karein).

Tehri Garhwal: Isht Devta, Gram Devta aur Jagar mein Devta Avtaran

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal me gram devta ki puja har gaon ki alag parampara hai. Prasiddh devtaon me Nagraja, Bhairav (Bhairon), Narsingh, Ghandiyal, Bhumiyal (kshetrpal) aur Jaunpur kshetra me Mahasu Devta shamil hain. Jagar me devta kisi vyakti par aata hai.

Dhyan dein: Golu Devta (nyay ke devta) mukhya roop se Kumaon ke hain, Tehri ke nahi.

Jagar, Jagariya aur Dangariya: Kaise Hota Hai

  • Jagariya: Gayak jo dhol-damau ya hudke ke saath devta ki gatha gata hai.
  • Dangariya / pasva: Wo vyakti jis par devta "avtarit" hota hai.
  • Jagar ka uddeshya: Devta ko jagana, kisi samasya (bimari, parivarik kalesh) ka karan poochna aur shanti ke upay batana.
  • Aadhunik drishtikon: Ek taraf ye aastha ki parampara hai, dusri taraf kuch log ise andhvishwas bhi kehte hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Pragaitihasik Kaal, Shila-Chitra aur Shailashray

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand ke prasiddh pragaitihasik sthal jaise Lakhu Udyar (Almora) aur Dungri, Kimni (Chamoli) Kumaon-Garhwal ke anya jilo me hain. Tehri Garhwal me badi painted rock-shelter ya megalith ke prasiddh sthal kam jaane jate hain.

Chitrit Gufayein, Pashan Yug ke Auzaar aur Okhali Nishan

  • Sthiti: Tehri me puratatvik khoj ki jaankari seemit hai. Aap Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) ya Uttarakhand Archaeology Department se pooch kar jaankari jod sakte hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Kuninda aur Katyuri Raajvansh ka Daur

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand me prachin kal me Kuninda (lagbhag doosri sadi ISA purv se teesri sadi ISvi tak) aur Katyuri (lagbhag 7vi se 11vi sadi) rajvansh ka shasan raha. Katyuri rajao ki rajdhani Joshimath (Kartikeyapura) aur baad me Baijnath me rahi.

Kuninda Sikke, Katyuri Rajdhaniyan aur Tamrapatra

  • Kuninda: Himalaya ke is kshetra ke prachin shasak, jinke sikke (Amoghbhuti ke) milte hain.
  • Katyuri: Garhwal-Kumaon ke bade bhag par shasan, patthar ke shikhar wale mandir banwaye.
  • Tamrapatra: Katyuri aur Panwar kaal ke tamrapatra (copper plates) us kaal ki prashasan aur zameen-daan ki jankari dete hain.
  • Tehri se sambandh: Garhwal ke is kal ka prabhav Tehri kshetra tak maana jata hai. Tehri-vishesh khoj ke liye Garhwal Vishwavidyalaya ke itihas vibhag ke shodh dekhein.

Tehri Garhwal: Panwar (Garhwal) & Chand (Kumaon) Kingdoms

Seedha jawab: Garhwal me Panwar (Parmar) vansh ka shasan lagbhag 888 ISvi se shuru hua maana jata hai, jab Kanakpal ne gaddi sambhali. Baad ke raja Ajaypal ne 52 chhote garhon (kilo) ko ek kar ke Garhwal rajya banaya. Panwar vansh ka shasan lagbhag 915 saal chala aur 1803 me Gorkha hamle se bhang hua.

52 Garh, Panwar Raja aur Zameen ke Daan

  • 52 garh: Pahad ke har kshetra me ek chhota raja aur uska garh (kila) hota tha. Ajaypal ne inhe ek karke Garhwal rajya banaya (lagbhag 14vi sadi).
  • Rajdhani: Chandpurgarh se Devalgarh aur baad me Srinagar (Garhwal).
  • Prasiddh raja: Mahipat Shah (jinke senapati Madho Singh Bhandari aur Rikhola Lodi prasiddh hue), Pradyumna Shah (antim swatantra raja).
  • Rani Karnavati: "Nak-kati rani" ke naam se prasiddh Garhwal ki rani, jinhone Mughal sena ko haraya.
  • Goonth land: Mandir ko raja dwara di gayi kar-mukt zameen.

Dhyan dein: 52 garh ki exact list Tehri se kitni judti hai, iske liye Harikrishna Raturi ka "Garhwal ka Itihas" dekhein.

Tehri Garhwal: Gorkha Raaj (Gorkhyani) aur Angrez-Nepal Yuddh

Seedha jawab: 1803 me Gorkha sena ne Garhwal par hamla kiya. Khurbura ke yuddh (Dehradun) me Garhwal ke raja Pradyumna Shah veergati ko prapt hue (1803 ya 1804, source ke hisab se). Gorkha shasan lagbhag 1815 tak chala, jisse yahan ke log ise "Gorkhyani" ke naam se bure daur ke roop me yaad karte hain.

Gorkha Raaj, Kile, Kar aur Sugauli Sandhi

  • Shasan kaal: Garhwal me lagbhag 1803 se 1815 tak.
  • Kathor kar vyavastha: Bhari karo aur begar (muft majdoori) se logo ka jeevan kathin hua.
  • Anglo-Nepalese yuddh (1814 se 1816): Angrezo aur Nepal ke beech yuddh hua. Is yuddh me Angrezo ko jeet mili.
  • Sugauli ki sandhi: 2 December 1815 ko hastakshar hue aur 4 March 1816 ko pushti hui. Is se Kumaon aur Garhwal ka purvi bhag Angrezo ke paas gaya, aur pashchimi Garhwal Raja Sudarshan Shah ko mila, jisse Tehri Riyasat bani.

British Garhwal / Kumaon Commissionery & Tehri Riyasat

Seedha jawab: 1815 ke baad Garhwal do bhago me bat gaya. Purvi hissa British Garhwal (Pauri, Chamoli, Rudraprayag ke aas-paas) Angrezo ke seedhe shasan me gaya, jabki pashchimi hissa Tehri Riyasat raja ke paas raha. Tehri Riyasat 1815 se 1949 tak Panwar (Shah) vansh ke raja ke adhin rahi.

Tithiyan alag-alag sources me kuch saal ke farak se milti hain. Ek hi source (jaise Raturi ya Dabral) se tithiyan jodein.

  • Raj-vyavastha: Tehri Riyasat angrezo ki Punjab Hill States Agency ka hissa thi. Raja ko salami (gun salute) ka sammaan prapt tha.
  • Zameen vyavastha: British Garhwal me alag "bandobast" (land settlements) kiye gaye, jo Tehri Riyasat me alag tareeke se chale. Traill, Batten, Beckett aur Pauw ke bandobast mukhya roop se British Garhwal aur Kumaon se jude hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Azaadi ki Ladai, Begar aur Rajya Andolan

Seedha jawab: Tehri ki azaadi ki ladai angrezo ke virudh nahi, balki Tehri Riyasat ke raja ke virudh thi. Sridev Suman (1944 me 84 din ke anshan ke baad shahadat), Rawain Dhandak (1930), Kirtinagar aandolan (1948), Chipko aandolan aur Tehri baandh virodhi aandolan is jile ke mukhya aandolan hain.

  • Sridev Suman: Tehri ke Jaulgaon me janme. Raja ke virudh jan-adhikaro ki ladai ladi. Unke balidan ki yaad me har saal 25 July ko Sridev Suman Balidan Diwas manaya jata hai.
  • Sunderlal Bahuguna (1927 se 2021): Chipko aandolan ke pramukh netao me, paryavaran ke sainik. Tehri Dam virodh me lambe samay tak sakriya rahe. Unka nidhan 21 May 2021 ko hua.
  • Chipko ka Tehri adhyay: Chipko ka sabse prasiddh chitra Chamoli (Reni, 1974, Gaura Devi) ka hai. Tehri ke Henwalghati me bhi 1977 me ped bachane ke liye mahilao aur gramino ne aandolan kiya.

Tehri Garhwal: Lok-Kathayein: Achhari, Veer Yoddha aur Rahasyamay Kisse

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal ki sabse mashhoor veer-gatha Madho Singh Bhandari ki hai, jinke gaon Malethha (Tehri Garhwal) me aaj bhi Malethha ki kul (nahar) unki yaad dilati hai. Iske alawa Achhari / Aanchri (pahadi apsara), Jeetu Bagdwal ki bansuri ki katha aur Old Tehri ki ghanta-ghar ki kahaniyan prasiddh hain.

Madho Singh Bhandari, Achhari & Jeetu Bagdwal

  • Madho Singh Bhandari (Malethha): 17vi sadi ke Garhwal ke senapati, jinhone Tibbat ke yuddh me bahaduri dikhayi. Lok-katha ke anusar unhone apne gaon ke liye Chandrabhaga nadi se kul (nahar) khodkar paani laya, jise Malethha ki kul kehte hain. Is nahar ko laane ke liye apne putra ka balidan dene ki katha prachalit hai. Ye katha aaj bhi pahadi pawade (veer gatha) me gayi jati hai.
  • Achhari / Aanchri: Oonche bugyal me rehne wali pahadi apsara-sadrish shakti, jinke bare me lok-manyata hai ki ve chamkile laal-peele kapde pehne logo ko lubha leti hain. Isliye bugyal par chatak rang pehnne aur shor karne ko mana kiya jata tha (Tehri Garhwal: Buzurgon ki Chalti-Phirti Salah).
  • Jeetu Bagdwal: Garhwal ki lok-gatha ke ek prasiddh patra, jinki bansuri aur prem-katha jagar aur lok-geeto me gayi jati hai.
  • Teelu Rauteli: "Garhwal ki Jhansi" kehlayi jaati hain, par ve Pauri Garhwal ki hain, Tehri ki nahi.
  • Purane Tehri ki ghanta-ghar ki kahani: Tehri Dam ke jalashay me doobe Old Tehri ka ghanta-ghar kabhi-kabhi jab jheel ka jalstar bahut neeche aata hai to dikh jaane ki charcha hoti hai.

Tehri Garhwal: Itihas, mandir aur lok-katha: Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Surkanda Devi mandir tak kaise jaate hain?
Kaddukhal (Dhanaulti ke paas) se ropeway (502 meter lambi, lagbhag 5 se 10 minute me mandir tak) chalti hai; 2022 me shuru hui. Ropeway band ho (rakhrakhav ya mausam) to paidal chadhai (lagbhag 2 ghante tak) ka vikalp hai. Mandir lagbhag 2,750 meter par hai. Ropeway ke samay aur shulk ke liye taaza jaankari lein, kyunki rakhrakhav ke liye kuch dino ke liye band ho jaati hai.
Chipko aandolan se Tehri Garhwal ke kaun se vyakti jude the?
Sundarlal Bahuguna (Maroda gaon, Tehri) pramukh netao me the. Henwalghati kshetra me bhi Chipko ke charan hue.
Tehri Garhwal ke teen prasiddh Shaktipeeth kaun se hain?
Surkanda Devi, Kunjapuri Devi aur Chandrabadni Devi.
Tehri Riyasat kab tak rahi?
1815 se 1949 tak, Panwar (Shah) vansh ke raja ke adhin; 1815 ke baad Garhwal ka pashchimi hissa riyasat bana.
Sridev Suman kaun the?
Tehri riyasat ke virudh nagrik adhikaron ke andolan ke neta, jinka 1944 mein 84 din ke anshan ke baad nidhan hua.
Madho Singh Bhandari ki katha kahan se judi hai?
Tehri Garhwal ke Malethha gaon se, jahan ki kul (nahar) aaj bhi unki yaad dilati hai.
