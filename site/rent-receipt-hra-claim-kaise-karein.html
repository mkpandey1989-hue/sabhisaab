Rent Receipt Se HRA Claim Kaise Karein — Poora Tarika

HRA hai kya

HRA (House Rent Allowance) aapki salary ka ek hissa hai jo makaan ka kiraya dene ke liye milta hai. Iski khaas baat — agar aap sach me kiraye par rehte hain, to HRA ka ek hissa tax-free ho jaata hai, jisse aapka tax kam ho jaata hai. Par ye fayda seedha nahi milta, claim karna padta hai.

Kitna HRA tax-free hota hai

Tax-free HRA in teen me se jo sabse kam ho, wo hota hai:

1. Asli HRA jo salary me mila
2. Metro me basic ka 50%, non-metro me 40%
3. Actual kiraya − basic ka 10%

Teeno nikaal kar sabse chhoti rakam tax-free hoti hai, baaki par tax lagta hai. Ye hisaab thoda uljha lagta hai, isliye HRA Calculator me apne number daal kar seedha jawab le lein.

Ek misaal

Basic salary ₹30,000/mahina, HRA ₹15,000/mahina, kiraya ₹18,000/mahina, non-metro sheher. Saal ke hisaab se: asli HRA ₹1,80,000; basic ka 40% = ₹1,44,000; kiraya − 10% basic = ₹2,16,000 − ₹36,000 = ₹1,80,000. Teeno me sabse kam ₹1,44,000 — yaani itna tax-free. Baaki HRA par tax lagega.

Kaunse kaagaz chahiye

  • Rent receipt — landlord ke dastakhat ke saath, har mahine ya har quarter ki.
  • Rent agreement — makaan malik ke saath ka likhit samjhauta (achha rehta hai).
  • Landlord ka PAN — agar saalana kiraya ₹1 lakh se zyada hai to zaroori.
  • Payment ka proof — bank transfer behtar hai (cash se zyada bharosemand).

Landlord ka PAN kab zaroori

Agar aapka saalana kiraya ₹1 lakh se zyada (yaani ~₹8,300/mahina se upar) hai, to HRA claim ke liye landlord ka PAN dena zaroori hai. PAN na ho to landlord se ek declaration lena padta hai. Bina PAN ke bade kiraye ka HRA claim atak sakta hai, isliye pehle se maang lein.

Zaroori shartein

  • Sach me kiraye par rehna zaroori — apne hi ghar me reh kar HRA claim nahi kar sakte.
  • Maa-baap ko kiraya — de sakte hain aur claim kar sakte hain, par wo apni ITR me use income dikhaayein (warna problem).
  • Sirf old regime — new tax regime me HRA ki chhoot nahi milti. Kaunsa regime theek, wo yahan dekhein.
  • Jhoothi receipt mat banayein — pakde jaane par penalty aur notice.

HRA na mile to kya

Agar aapki salary me HRA nahi hai (jaise business ya kuch naukriyon me), par aap kiraya dete hain, to Section 80GG ke tahat thodi chhoot mil sakti hai — kuch sharton aur seema ke saath. Ye HRA se kam hoti hai. Poora tulnaatmak vivaran tax regime guide me hai. Aur salary slip me HRA kahan hai, ye salary slip wale lekh me samajh sakte hain.

Zaroori: ye lekh aam jaankari ke liye hai, tax salah nahi. Niyam har budget me badalte hain. Apne case ke liye CA se ya incometax.gov.in se confirm karein.

Rent receipt me kya-kya hona chahiye

Ek sahi rent receipt me ye sab honi chahiye taaki tax office ko koi shak na ho:

  • Kirayedar ka naam (aap) aur makaan malik ka naam
  • Kitna kiraya aur kis mahine/avdhi ka
  • Makaan ka pata
  • Kiraya kaise diya (bank transfer / cash)
  • Makaan malik ke dastakhat
  • Landlord ka PAN (agar saalana kiraya ₹1 lakh se zyada)

Ek chhoti salah — jitna ho sake, kiraya bank se dein (UPI/transfer). Cash se diya kiraya proof karna mushkil hota hai, aur bade amount par tax office sawaal kar sakta hai. Bank statement khud ek pakka proof ban jaata hai.

Aur receipt har mahine ki rakhein ya kam se kam har quarter ki. Saal ke ant me ek saath banwaana theek nahi lagta, aur agar kabhi jaanch hui to mahine-dar-mahine ka record hi bharosa deta hai. Thoda dhyaan pehle se rakhne se baad me HRA claim bina dikkat nikal jaata hai.

Aam galtiyan jo claim rok deti hain

HRA claim aksar chhoti galtiyon ki wajah se atak jaata hai. In se bachein:

  • Landlord ka PAN na dena — jab kiraya ₹1 lakh saalana se zyada ho. Ye sabse aam wajah hai claim rukne ki.
  • Sirf saal ke ant me receipt banwaana — mahine-dar-mahine record na hone par shak hota hai.
  • Apne hi ghar ka kiraya dikhaana — ye galat hai, sirf sach me kiraye par rehne par HRA milta hai.
  • Cash me bada kiraya bina proof — bank transfer na hone par bade amount par sawaal uth sakta hai.
  • New regime chun kar HRA maangna — new regime me HRA milta hi nahi. Pehle regime dekh lein.

Agar aap ye chhoti baatein pehle se dhyaan me rakhte hain, to HRA claim saal ke ant me bina kisi jhanjhat ke nikal jaata hai aur aapka achha-khaasa tax bach jaata hai. Poora hisaab pehle se HRA Calculator me laga kar rakh lein taaki pata rahe kitna tax-free hoga.

HRA claim ke liye kya-kya dena padta hai

Kitna kiraya, uske hisaab se kagaz badalte hain.

Saalana kirayaKya chahiye
Rs 1 lakh takSirf receipt (aam taur par)
Rs 1 lakh se uparReceipt + makaan maalik ka PAN
Makaan maalik ke paas PAN nahiUska likhit declaration
Badi raqam ka kirayaTDS bhi kaatna pad sakta hai
Do sthitiyaan jinme log galti karte hain: pehli — maa-baap ko kiraya dena kanoonan theek hai, par tabhi jab ghar sach me unke naam ho, paisa bank se jaaye, aur wo apne ITR me wo kiraya dikhayein. Sirf receipt bana lena jaanch me nahi tikta. Doosri — apne hi ghar me rehte hue HRA nahi liya ja sakta. Aur sabse zaroori: HRA ki chhoot sirf purani tax regime me milti hai; nayi regime chuni to receipt ka koi faayda nahi. Company ko samay par kagaz na dein to wo poora TDS kaat leti hai — phir ITR me refund maangna padta hai. Hum CA nahi hain.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

HRA claim ke liye rent receipt zaroori hai?

Haan, rent receipt HRA claim ka mukhya proof hai. Rent agreement aur bank se payment ka proof bhi rakhna behtar hai.

Landlord ka PAN kab chahiye?

Jab saalana kiraya ₹1 lakh se zyada ho. PAN na ho to landlord se declaration lena padta hai.

New regime me HRA milta hai?

Nahi. HRA ki tax chhoot sirf old regime me milti hai. New regime chunne par ye fayda nahi milta.

Maa-baap ko kiraya dekar HRA claim kar sakte hain?

Haan, agar aap sach me unke ghar me kiraye par rehte hain aur wo apni ITR me us kiraye ko income dikhaayein.

Kitna HRA tax-free hota hai?

Teen me se sabse kam: asli HRA, metro me basic ka 50% (non-metro 40%), ya kiraya minus basic ka 10%. HRA calculator me seedha nikal jaata hai.
