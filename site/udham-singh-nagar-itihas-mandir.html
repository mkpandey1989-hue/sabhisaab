Udham Singh Nagar: Puranic Geography: Terai ka Pauranik Sambandh

Kedarkhand, Manaskhand aur Udham Singh Nagar ki Pauranik Manyatayen

Seedha jawab: Skand Puran ke Kedarkhand (Garhwal) aur Manaskhand (Kumaon) ka sidha sambandh pahadi ilakon se joda jata hai. Udham Singh Nagar Terai ka maidani hissa hai, isliye iska Pauranik sambandh zyadatar Mahabharat se jude sthaniya vishwaason se milta hai, jaise Kashipur ka Dronasagar aur Moteshwar Mahadev.

Yahan ek zaruri baat dhyan me rakhein. Niche likhi baatein "manyata" yaani logon ki shraddha aur parampara hain. Inme se kai batein sthaniya mandir-samitiyon aur lok-kathaon se aati hain, aur inka sidha praman shilalekh ya granth se milna zaruri nahi.

Girital (Kashipur)Pandavon se jodi jaane wali sthaniya manyata
NanakmattaGuru Nanak Dev ji ki Udasi (yatra) se juda Sikh itihas

Dhyan dein: Dakini, Bheemashankar aur Bhim-Hidimba jaisi kathaon ko lekar vidwanon me matbhed hai. Jyotirling ki ginti ko lekar bhi alag-alag manyatayen hain.

Ek zaruri antar: Pahadi zilon ke Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand wale shlok yahan seedhe lagoo nahi hote. Udham Singh Nagar ko uske Terai-Bhabar ke bhugol aur Chand rajvansh ke itihas se samajhna zyada sahi hai.

Udham Singh Nagar: Pramukh Mandir, Dham, Gurudwara aur Math

Zile ke Mukhya Dharmik Sthal

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar ke sabse prasiddh dharmik sthal Gurudwara Nanakmatta Sahib, Moteshwar Mahadev mandir (Kashipur), Maa Bal Sundari (Chaiti Devi) mandir (Kashipur), Atariya Devi mandir (Rudrapur) aur Dronasagar hain. Yeh sthal Sikh aur Hindu dono samajon ki shraddha ke kendra hain.

Moteshwar Mahadev ke bare me jaankari (sthaniya sthal-jankari ke anusar):

  • Mandir Kashipur ke Ujjanak kshetra me hai aur Kashipur bus station se lagbhag 3 km door bataya jata hai.
  • Shivling bahut mota hai, isi se naam "Moteshwar" pada, aisa maana jata hai.
  • Parisar me Bhairav Nath mandir, Shiv Ganga Kund aur Shiv Manokamna Kund hain. Paschim me Bhagwati Bal Sundari mandir aur Dronasagar hain.
  • Mandir ke darshan ka samay lagbhag subah 6 baje se dopahar 12 baje tak aur shaam 4 baje se raat 8 baje tak bataya gaya hai. Aarti ka samay mausam ke hisab se badalta hai, isliye jaane se pehle mandir samiti se confirm karein.
  • Mandir ki sthapana ka samay lagbhag 302 AD bataya jata hai, par yeh anuman aur manyata par aadharit hai.
  • Maha Shivratri, Chaiti Mela aur Shravan me mele lagte hain.

Gurudwara Nanakmatta Sahib: Sikh itihas ka pavitra pada: Nanakmatta me Guru Nanak Dev ji ki Udasi (yatra) ke dauran yahan ke Siddh-Jogiyon se milne ki kahani juda hai. Gurudwara jaane ke samanya niyam:

  • Sar dhak kar jaayein, jootein bahar utarein aur haath-pair dho kar andar jaayein.
  • Tambaku, sharab ya nasha lekar andar na jaayein.
  • Langar ka prasad shraddha ke saath grahan karein.

Mandir jaane ke samanya niyam:

  • Mandir parisar me saaf-safai ka dhyan rakhein.
  • Bheed ke samay (Navratri, Shivratri, Shravan) subah jaldi jaana behtar hota hai.
  • Pooja ka saman kharidte samay maanak-bhaav ka dhyan rakhein.

Udham Singh Nagar: Isht Devta, Gram Devta aur Lok-Dharm

Zile ke alag-alag Samajon ki Shraddha

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar me ek hi devta ki jagah kai samajon ke alag-alag devta aur pooja-paddhatiyan hain. Sikh samaj Guru Granth Sahib aur das Gurus ko maanta hai, Hindu parivar Shiv, Devi aur Ram-Krishna ko, Pahadi parivar Golu devta aur Nanda devi jaise sthaniya devtaon ko, Tharu aur Buksa prakriti aur pitron ki pooja karte hain, aur Muslim samaj Allah ki ibadat karta hai.

Pahadi parivaron ke devta yahan kaise aaye?
Jab Almora, Pithoragarh, Champawat ya Garhwal ke parivar yahan bas gaye, to apne saath apne kuldevta aur gram devta ki manyata bhi laaye. Ghar ke mandir me Golu Devta ya Nanda Devi ki tasveer aur samay-samay par Jagar (devta ko bulane ka gayan) ka aayojan yahan bhi dikhta hai.

Jagar kya hai? Pahadi parampara me Jagar ek aisa anushthan hai jisme jagariya (gayak) devta ki gatha gata hai aur ek madhyam (dangariya) par devta ka avtaran maana jata hai. Yeh Pahadi samaj ki manyata hai, isliye Udham Singh Nagar me yeh kewal Pahadi parivaron me hota hai.

Tharu aur Buksa ki shraddha: Uttarakhand Tribal Welfare ke anusar Tharu samaj me prakriti-pooja aur pitron ki pooja ka mahatva hai, aur Buksa samaj me sthaniya devta, atma aur prakriti ki shraddha lok-roop Hindu parampara ke saath mili hai. Kisi ek parivar ka riti-riwaj puri janjati ka niyam nahi hota.

Sant-Parampara: Gorakhnath, Udasi aur Guru Nanak

Seedha jawab: Nanakmatta ka purana naam "Gorakhmata" bataya jata hai. Sikh itihas ke anusar Guru Nanak Dev ji ne yahan Gorakhnath panth ke Siddh-Jogiyon se samvad kiya, aur tab se yeh sthan "Nanakmatta" ke naam se jaana gaya. Baad me yeh Udasi sampraday ka bhi ek kendra raha.

Udham Singh Nagar: Prag-Itihas, Mahapashan, Gufa-Chitra aur Prachin Bastiyan

Terai ka Prachin Itihas: Ghane Jungle se Govishan tak

Seedha jawab: Himalayan zilon ki tarah Udham Singh Nagar me Lakhudiyar jaise painted rock shelters ya megalith nahi milte. Is Terai ka bada hissa prachin kaal me ghane jungle, daldal, bimari aur kam aavagaman ke karan baste-baste kam basa tha. Iska sabse purana praman Kashipur ka prachin nagar Govishan hai.

KaalIs Terai ki sthiti

Govishan (Govisana): Kashipur ka prachin naam: Samrat Harsha ke kaal (606-647 AD) me Kashipur ka naam Govishan (Govisana) tha. Chini yatri Hiuen Tsang (Xuanzang) ne 7vi shatabdi me yahan ki yatra ki aur is nagar ka vivaran diya. Unke anusar nagar ki paridhi badi thi aur yeh bagichon, talabon aur machhli ke talabon se ghira tha. Kashipur ke paas us purane nagar ke khandhar aaj bhi dikhte hain.

Dhyan dein: Kashipur ke sthapana ka samay itihaskaron me vivadit hai. Bishop Heber ne ise lagbhag 5,000 saal purana bataya, Alexander Cunningham ne lagbhag 1718 AD, aur Badri Datt Pandey ne 1639. Aadhunik nagar ke sansthapak Kashinath Adhikari maane jaate hain.

Khandhar dekhne se pehle: Govishan ke khandhar dekhne jaane se pehle Archaeological Survey of India ya zila prashasan se jaankari lein ki wahan prvesh ki anumati aur suraksha ke kya niyam hain.

Udham Singh Nagar: Chand Dynasty & Terai ka Shasan

Gyan Chand se Rudra Chand aur Kashipur ke Adhikari tak

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar ka madhya-kaleen itihas Kumaon ke Chand rajvansh se juda hai. Chand shasakon ne Bhabar-Terai ko apne rajya me milaya. Rudra Chand ne 16vi shatabdi me Rudrapur basaya, aur baad me Kashipur Terai ke adhikari (governor) ka mukhyalay bana.

Adhikari ki satta aur sangharsh: 18vi shatabdi ke ant me Kashipur ke adhikari Nand Ram ne Rudrapur ke governor Manorath Joshi ki hatya karwa kar khud ko Terai ka swatantra jaisa shasak ghoshit kiya. Kashipur ke baad ke rajyon me Raja Guman Singh ko Kashipur ka pehla raja bataya jata hai.

Udham Singh Nagar: British Terai: Zila, Jungle aur Basavat

Tarai & Bhawar Government Act (1864-65), Kheti aur Nagar Palika

Seedha jawab: British kaal me Terai ka prashasan alag dhang se chalaya gaya. 1864-65 ke "Tarai and Bhawar Government Act" ke tahat Terai-Bhabar kshetra seedhe British Crown ke niyantran me aaya. 1837 me Kashipur Moradabad zile me shamil hua, 1872 me Kashipur ki nagar palika bani aur 1891 me Rudrapur Nainital zile me rakha gaya.

Kashipur ka naya darja: Kashipur 26 January 2013 ko Nagar Nigam bana. Rudrapur ko 28 February 2013 ko Nagar Nigam ka darja mila.

Vibhajan ke baad basavat (1947-1960 ke dashak): Desh ke batware ke baad Terai ke is jungle wale ilake me purnavas ka bada kaam hua. Uttar Pradesh sarkar ki "Up Nivesh Yojana" ke tahat 1948 ke ant se Punjab aur Purvi Bengal se aaye visthapit parivaron ko yahan zameen dekar basaya gaya. Pehla jattha December 1948 me aaya bataya jata hai. In parivaron ne jungle saaf karke ganne, dhan aur gehun ke khet banaye aur Terai ko "Anna-Bhandar" banane me bada yogdan diya.

Dhyan dein: Kuch websites par vishesh vyakti ya parivar ko "pehla basne wala" bataya gaya hai.

G.B. Pant University ka mahatva: Pantnagar ke is vishwavidyalay ne Hari Kranti ke dauran kheti ke naye beej aur tareeke vikasit karne me bada kaam kiya. Udham Singh Nagar: Shiksha: University, College aur Sansthan me iske bare me aur likha gaya hai.

Udham Singh Nagar: Azaadi ki Ladai, Rajya Andolan aur Shaheed

Shaheed Udham Singh aur Zile ka Naam

Seedha jawab: Zile ka naam shaheed Udham Singh ke naam par rakha gaya hai. Unhone 13 March 1940 ko London me Michael O'Dwyer ko goli maari thi, jise Jallianwala Bagh hatyakand (1919) ka badla maana jata hai. Zila 29 September 1995 ko bana aur unke naam se pehchana gaya.

Dhyan dein: Udham Singh ka janm Punjab ke Sunam me hua tha, wo Udham Singh Nagar zile se nahi aaye the. Zile ka naam unke balidan ke sammaan me rakha gaya.

17 October 1991 ko Rudrapur me Ramlila ke aayojan ke dauran do bam dhamake hue the, jisme 41 logon ki jaan gayi thi. Yeh zile ke itihas ki ek dukhad ghatna hai. Is ghatna ko sammaan ke saath yaad karna zaruri hai.

Udham Singh Nagar: Local Folklore: Dronasagar, Moteshwar aur Nanakmatta ki Kathayen

Nanakmatta ki Chaar Kathayen (Sikh Itihas ke anusar)

Seedha jawab: Nanakmatta se juda sabse prasiddh kissa Guru Nanak Dev ji ka Siddh-Jogiyon se samvad hai. Sikh itihas ki kathaon ke anusar yahan sookhe pipal ke ped ka hara hona, Mardana ke liye aag ka jal uthna, Jogiyon dwara ped ko ukhadne ki koshish aur bachche ka "Nanak Mata" kehna jaisi char ghatnayen judi hain.

1. Sookha pipal hara hua: Guru Nanak Dev ji ek sookhe pipal ke ped ke neeche baithe aur ped phir hara ho gaya, aisa bataya jata hai.

2. Aag ka chamatkar: Bhai Mardana ne thand me Siddhon se aag maangi to unhone mana kar diya aur mazaak udaya. Guru ji ne paas rakhi lakdi ke dher ki taraf dekha aur wo jal uthi. Phir achanak barish ne Jogiyon ki aag bujha di, par Guru ji ki aag nahi bujhi.

3. Ped ukhadne ki koshish: Jogiyon ne apni shakti se ped ukhadna chaha. Ped kuch phit oopar uth gaya, par Guru ji ke haath rakhne par ruk gaya. Yeh ped aaj bhi Gurudwara parisar me dikhaya jata hai.

4. "Nanak Mata" ka naam: Jogiyon ne ek bachche ko gaddhe me chhupakar kaha ki jab poochha jaye ki yeh zameen kiski hai to "Sidhan di" (Siddhon ki) kehna. Bachche ne Guru ji ke poochne par "Nanak Mata" kaha, aur isi se sthan ka naam pada.

Baad ka itihas: Sikh itihas ke anusar Baba Almast ne is sthan ki dekhbhal ki. Baad me Gorakh-panthiyon ne is par adhikar kar ke ise "Gorakhmat" kaha aur ped ko jala diya. Baba Almast ne Guru Hargobind Sahib ji ko sandesh bheja. Guru Hargobind Sahib ji ne aakar ped par jal chhidka aur wo phir hara ho gaya. Ped ke kuch hisson me jalne ke nishaan aaj bhi dikhte hain, aisa maana jata hai.

Dhyan dein: Yeh kathayen Sikh dharm ki shraddha aur sampraday ki parampara hain. Kuch granthon me ghatnayon ke vivaran me thode antar milte hain.

Dhyan rakhne ki baat: Gurudwara Nanakmatta Sahib Uttarakhand ke Udham Singh Nagar zile me Sitarganj ke paas hai. Rudrapur se iski doori kuch sources me alag batayi gayi hai (lagbhag 45 se 56 km), isliye rasta aur samay google maps se confirm karein.

Moteshwar Mahadev ki Lok-Kathayen

Seedha jawab: Moteshwar Mahadev ke bare me lok-manyata hai ki shivling lagatar badh raha hai aur itna mota hai ki do haathon me nahi sama sakta. Ek kahani ke anusar Guru Dronacharya ne Bheemsen ko mandir ke punarnirman ke liye kaha tha, aur isi se yeh "Bheemashankar" kehlaya.

  • Badhta shivling: Sthaniya manyata ke anusar shivling dheere-dheere badh raha hai aur dusri manzil tak pahunch gaya hai.
  • Naam ki kahani: "Mota" (mota/moti paridhi wala) shivling ke karan "Moteshwar" naam pada, aisa maana jata hai.
  • Kila aur Dronacharya: Mandir ke paas "Kila" naam ke sthaan par Dronacharya dwara Kaurav aur Pandavon ko shiksha dene ki lok-manyata hai.
  • Shravan Kumar: Ek sthaniya manyata ke anusar Shravan Kumar ne yahan vishram kiya tha.

Dronasagar aur Girital

Seedha jawab: Dronasagar Kashipur ka ek sarovar-sthal hai jise Mahabharat ke Dronacharya ki guru-dakshina ki lok-katha se joda jata hai. Girital ko bhi Pandavon se jodkar dekha jata hai. Yeh sab sthaniya manyatayen hain, aur inka sambandh itihas se zyada shraddha se hai.

Kahaniyon ko kaise padhein?
Lok-kathaon ka mahatva is baat me hai ki ve logon ki pehchan aur shraddha ka hissa hain. Inhe itihas ke saboot ke roop me nahi, balki sanskriti ke hisse ke roop me samjhein.
