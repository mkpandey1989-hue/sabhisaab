Market Girne Par SIP Band Karun Ya Nahi?

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
Chhota jawabSeedha jawab: market girne par SIP band karna aam taur par sabse badi galti hoti hai. Girte market me aapki SIP ka wahi paisa zyada units khareedta hai (sasta), jisse aage market chadhne par zyada fayda hota hai. Ghabrahat me band karne se aap sirf apna nuksan pakka kar lete hain.

Girte market me SIP ka jaadu

SIP ki sabse badi taakat tab kaam karti hai jab market girta hai — par log ise ulta samajhte hain. Jab bhaav gire hue hain, aapki har mahine ki SIP ka wahi paisa zyada units khareedta hai. Jab market wapas chadhta hai, ye saste units bada fayda dete hain. Isse rupee cost averaging kehte hain.

Bhaav zyada → kam units milte hain
Bhaav kam (girte market) → zyada units milte hain
Lambe samay me aapki average keemat kam ho jaati hai

Ek misaal

Aap har mahine ₹5,000 SIP karte hain. Jab NAV ₹100 hai, ₹5,000 me 50 units milte hain. Market gira, NAV ₹80 ho gaya — ab ₹5,000 me 62.5 units milte hain. Yaani girte market me aapko zyada units mile. Jab NAV wapas ₹100+ hota hai, ye extra units seedha fayda dete hain. Isliye girte market me SIP chalana faayde ka sauda hai. Apna SIP hisaab SIP Calculator me dekh sakte hain.

Ghabrahat me hone wali galtiyan

  • SIP band kar dena — sabse aam aur mehngi galti. Aap saste units khareedne ka mauka kho dete hain.
  • Sab kuch bech dena — girte market me bechna nuksan ko pakka kar leta hai. Kagaz ka nuksan asli nuksan ban jaata hai.
  • Doosron ki dekha-dekhi — "sab bech rahe hain" dekh kar bechna. Bheed aksar galat samay par ghabraati hai.
  • Khabar dekh kar faisla — roz ki news dekh kar SIP chalu-band karna. Lambe nivesh me roz ki khabar mayne nahi rakhti.

SIP kab band karni chahiye

SIP band karne ki sahi wajah market ka girna nahi hai. Sahi wajah ye ho sakti hain:

  • Aapka goal aa gaya — jis kaam ke liye SIP thi (ghar, padhai) wo samay aa gaya.
  • Paise ki asli zaroorat — naukri chali gayi ya emergency, aur koi emergency fund nahi.
  • Fund lagataar kharab — 3-5 saal se apne jaise doosre funds se bahut peeche (sirf 6 mahine ka nuksan nahi).

Girte market me kya karna behtar

Agar aapke paas extra paisa hai aur aap lambe samay ke liye nivesh kar rahe hain, to girta market ghabraane ka nahi, balki anushasan se SIP chalate rehne ka samay hai. Kuch log to girte market me thoda extra bhi daalte hain (par sirf tab jab emergency fund aur baaki zaroorat poori ho). Sabse zaroori — apni SIP ko goal se jodein, market se nahi.

Asli sabak

Share bazaar lambe samay me upar jaata hai, par beech me kai baar girta hai — ye normal hai. Jo log har giravat me ghabra kar bechte hain, wo aksar kam kamaate hain. Jo anushasan se SIP chalate rehte hain, wo girte market ka fayda uthate hain. SIP banai hi isliye jaati hai taaki aapko market ka samay (timing) na dekhna pade — bas chalate raho. SIP vs ekmusht ya FD ki tulna yahan aur poori SIP samajh SIP guide me hai.

Zaroori: ye lekh aam jaankari ke liye hai, nivesh salah nahi. Hum SEBI-registered advisor nahi hain. Mutual fund me jokhim hota hai. Apne faisle se pehle SEBI-registered advisor se salah lein. SEBI Investor par aur jaankari hai.

Itihaas kya kehta hai

Share bazaar ne pichhle kai dashakon me kai badi giravat dekhi — par har baar, samay ke saath, wo wapas upar aaya aur naye oonchai tak pahuncha. Jo log giravat me ghabra kar nikal gaye, unhone aksar wo wapasi ka fayda kho diya. Jo ruke rahe ya SIP chalate rahe, unhe lambe samay me achha return mila.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki "market kabhi nahi girega" — girega, aur beech me nuksan bhi dikhega. Par SIP ka poora design hi isliye hai ki aapko market ka samay na dekhna pade. Aap har mahine tay paisa lagate hain — kabhi mehnga, kabhi sasta — aur lambe samay me ye average ho jaata hai.

Ek aakhri baat — apne aap se poochein

Jab market gire aur SIP band karne ka man kare, khud se ye teen sawaal poochein:

  • Kya mera goal (ghar, retirement, padhai) badla hai? Nahi, to SIP kyun badlein.
  • Kya mujhe ye paisa abhi chahiye? Nahi, to bechna sirf nuksan pakka karega.
  • Kya main 5-10 saal ke liye nivesh kar raha hoon? Haan, to 6 mahine ki giravat mayne nahi rakhti.

Teeno ka jawab aksar yahi hota hai ki SIP chalate rehna sahi hai. Sabse bada return anushasan se milta hai, hoshiyari se market ka samay pakadne se nahi — jo lagbhag koi nahi kar pata. Ghabrahat me liya faisla hamesha mehnga padta hai.

Market girne par teen raste — teeno ka natija

Maan lijiye 10 saal se Rs 5,000 ki SIP chal rahi hai aur bazaar 30% gir gaya. Aage 10 saal me lagbhag:

Aapne kya kiyaAage 10 saal baad
SIP band kar di, paisa nikaal liyaNuksan pakka ho gaya
SIP band, par paisa laga rehne diyaTheek — par nayi kist ka sasta bhaav chhoot gaya
SIP chalti rahiSabse behtar — girte bhaav par zyada unit mile
SIP badha diSabse zyada faayda, par himmat chahiye
SIP ka poora ganit hi giravat par tika hai. Bhaav girne par usi Rs 5,000 me zyada unit aate hain — yahi "rupee cost averaging" hai. Isliye girta bazaar SIP wale ke liye nuksan nahi, sasti khareed hai. Nuksan sirf ek hi tarah se pakka hota hai: girte bazaar me bech dena. Ek imaandaar baat — ye tabhi kaam karta hai jab paisa 7-10 saal tak na chahiye. Agla saal hi paisa chahiye to wo paisa equity me hona hi nahi chahiye tha. Hum SEBI-registered advisor nahi hain.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Market girne par SIP band karni chahiye?

Aam taur par nahi. Girte market me aapki SIP zyada (saste) units khareedti hai, jo aage fayda deti hai. Band karna aksar nuksan pakka kar leta hai.

Rupee cost averaging kya hai?

Har mahine tay paisa lagane se bhaav kam hone par zyada units aur bhaav zyada hone par kam units milte hain. Lambe samay me average keemat kam ho jaati hai.

Girte market me SIP ka paisa nikaal lena chahiye?

Nahi, agar goal door hai aur paise ki asli zaroorat nahi. Girte market me bechna kagaz ke nuksan ko asli nuksan bana deta hai.

SIP kab band karni chahiye?

Jab goal aa jaaye, asli emergency ho, ya fund 3-5 saal se lagataar bahut kharab ho. Sirf market girne par nahi.

Girte market me extra paisa lagana chahiye?

Sirf tab jab emergency fund aur baaki zaroorat poori ho, aur lamba nazariya ho. Warna anushasan se normal SIP chalana hi kaafi hai.
