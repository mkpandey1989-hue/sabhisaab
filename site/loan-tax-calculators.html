Loan lene se pehle char number nikaal lijiye

1. Kul byaj, sirf EMI nahi. ₹40 lakh ka 20 saal ka home loan 8.5% par lijiye to EMI lagbhag ₹34,700 aati hai — sunne me sambhal jaane layak. Par poore 20 saal me byaj lagbhag ₹43 lakh jaata hai, yaani ghar ki keemat lagbhag dugni. Ye number dekhne ke baad log tenure aur prepayment dono ke baare me alag tarah se sochte hain.

2. EMI aapki income ka kitna hissa hai. Aam samajh ye hai ki saare loan ki EMI milakar in-hand salary ke 40% se upar nahi jaani chahiye. Bank iski limit se zyada bhi de deta hai, kyunki uska hisaab aapke ghar ke kharche ko nahi jaanta.

3. Down payment ke baad kya bachega. Log poori jama-punji down payment me laga dete hain aur emergency fund zero ho jaata hai. Phir pehli hi museebat me credit card nikalta hai, aur 8.5% ke loan ke saath 45% ka karza shuru ho jaata hai.

4. Registry ke upar ke kharche. Stamp duty, registration, brokerage, processing fee — ye milakar ghar ki keemat ka 7-10% tak ho sakte hain, aur ye loan me nahi aate. Ye nakad chahiye hota hai.

Tax ka hisaab: kahan galti hoti hai

Regime chunne me jaldbaazi. New regime ki slab kam hai par exemption nahi milti; old regime ulta hai. Jinka HRA bada hai, home loan chal raha hai, ya 80C poora bhara hua hai — unke liye kabhi-kabhi old regime abhi bhi behtar baithta hai. Dono ka hisaab lagaye bina chunna hi galti hai. Tulna yahan hai.

Investment proof samay par na dena. Company saal ki shuruaat me declaration leti hai. Proof na diya to poora TDS January-March me kat jaata hai, aur teen mahine ki salary aadhi aati hai. Paisa baad me refund me wapas aata hai, par tab tak fasa rehta hai.

Form 26AS na milaana. ITR bharne se pehle apna kata hua TDS portal par dikhne wale record se milaayein. Kabhi-kabhi company kaat kar jama nahi karti, aur ye yahin pakda jaata hai.

GST ka rate andaze se lagana. Har cheez par ek jaisa GST nahi hai, aur rate badalte rehte hain. CBIC ke rate finder se confirm kar lena behtar hai.

Karza chukane ka sahi kram

Ek se zyada loan chal rahe hain to saara extra paisa sabse zyada byaj wale par daaliye, baaki par sirf minimum. Aam kram ye hota hai:

  1. Credit card ka revolving balance (40-50% saalana)
  2. Personal loan (11-18%)
  3. Car loan (9-12%)
  4. Gold loan (9-12%)
  5. Home loan (8-9%) — sabse sasta, sabse aakhir me

Log ulta karte hain — home loan jaldi khatam karna chahte hain kyunki wo sabse bada dikhta hai, jabki card ka chhota balance chupchap sabse zyada kha raha hota hai.
