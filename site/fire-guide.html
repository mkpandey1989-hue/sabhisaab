FIRE Kya Hai? Jaldi Retire Ke Liye Kitna Paisa?

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

FIRE ka matlab hai Financial Independence, Retire Early — yaani itna paisa jama kar lena ki phir naukri karna majboori na rahe, aap 40-45 ki umar me bhi "retire" ho sakein aur aapka kharch aapke nivesh se chalta rahe. Ye vichaar aajkal kaafi popular hai. Is guide me hum samjhenge ki iske liye kitna paisa chahiye, aur neeche free calculator se aap apna FIRE number nikaal sakte hain.

Kitna paisa chahiye? (4% rule)

FIRE ka sabse mashhoor niyam hai 4% rule. Iske hisaab se, agar aapke paas itna corpus ho ki aap har saal uska sirf 4% nikaal kar apna kharch chala sakein, to aapka paisa (theory me) kabhi khatam nahi hoga. Formula seedha hai:

FIRE number = saalana kharch × 25

Misaal: agar aapka saalana kharch ₹6 lakh hai, to FIRE number = 6,00,000 × 25 = ₹1.5 crore. Itna corpus ho to 4% (₹6 lakh) har saal nikaal kar kharch chal sakta hai.

FIRE ke prakaar

  • Lean FIRE: kam kharch, chhota corpus — simple zindagi.
  • Fat FIRE: zyada kharch, bada corpus — aaram ki zindagi.
  • Coast FIRE: itna jama kar lena ki aage bina daale bhi retirement tak badh jaye.

FIRE tak kaise pahunchein?

  1. Savings rate badhao — jitna zyada bachao, utni jaldi. 50%+ savings rate wale sabse jaldi pahunchte.
  2. Jaldi shuru karo — compounding ko time do (compound interest ka jaadu).
  3. Equity/SIP me nivesh — lambe samay me inflation se aage rehne ke liye.
  4. Kharch control — jitna kam kharch, utna chhota FIRE number.
Dhyan: 4% rule ek anuman hai, guarantee nahi. Inflation, market girawat, aur health kharche ise bdl sakte hain. Bharat me kuch log 3-3.5% zyada surakshit maante hain (yaani thoda bada corpus).

FAQ

Kya FIRE India me possible hai?

Haan, par ismein high savings rate aur anushasit nivesh chahiye. Chhote shahar/gaon me kharch kam hone se thoda aasaan ho sakta.

Inflation ka kya?

Isi liye corpus equity/SIP me hona chahiye taaki wo inflation se aage badhe. Sirf FD me rakhne se real return kam ho jaata.

Retire hone ke baad paisa kahan rakhein?

Aam taur par ek mix — kuch safe (FD, debt) aur kuch equity. Har saal 4% nikaalte hue baaki badhta rahe. Personal plan ke liye advisor se salah lein.

FIRE ke liye kitna paisa chahiye?

Mota niyam — saalana kharche ka 25 guna. Aaj ₹6 lakh saal me chal raha hai to lagbhag ₹1.5 crore, par usme mehngai ka asar bhi jodna zaroori hai.

4% rule Bharat me chalta hai?

Ye anuman videshi aankdon se aaya hai. Yahan mehngai zyada rehti hai, isliye kai log 3-3.5% ka zyada savdhaan paimana rakhte hain. Ye waada nahi, ek shuruaati anuman hai.

Bharat me FIRE — kya alag hai

FIRE ka vichaar paschim se aaya hai, jahan sarkari pension, sasta health insurance aur bacchon ki padhai ka dhaancha alag hai. Bharat me kuch cheezein FIRE ko mushkil banati hain aur kuch aasaan:

  • Mushkil: mehngai zyada, medical kharcha khud uthana padta hai, buzurg maa-baap ki zimmedari, bacchon ki padhai bahut mehngi
  • Aasaan: rehne-khaane ka kharcha paschim se kam, apna ghar hone par kiraya bachta hai, pariwar ka sahara

Isi liye bharat me FIRE ka number banate waqt health insurance aur medical fund alag se jodna zaroori hai. Isse chhod dena sabse badi bhool hoti hai.

4% rule aur uski seema

FIRE ka aam niyam kehta hai ki agar aapke paas saalana kharche ka 25 guna corpus hai, to aap har saal uska 4% nikaal kar guzara kar sakte hain. Ye niyam amreeki bazaar ke purane aankdon par bana hai.

Kaunsa raasta aapke liye theek hai

Sab log ek hi tarah ki aazadi nahi chahte. Aam taur par teen tarah maane jaate hain:

NaamMatlabCorpus (kharche ka)
Lean FIRESaada zindagi, kam kharcha~20-25 guna
Regular FIREAbhi jaisi zindagi~25-30 guna
Fat FIREAaram aur ghoomna bhi~33-40 guna
Coast FIREAb nivesh band, purana paisa apne aap badhegaKam — par naukri jaari
Barista FIREAadhi kamai, aadha corpusBeech ka
Coast FIRE sabse kam charchit aur sabse vyavaharik hai. Ismein aap itna paisa jama kar lete hain ki bina naya nivesh kiye bhi wo retirement tak apne aap kaafi ho jaayega — uske baad aap kam tankhwah wali, par pasand ki naukri kar sakte hain. Poori FIRE ke muqable ye bahut jaldi mil jaata hai. Aur ek chetavni — FIRE ka poora ganit health insurance ke bina adhoora hai. Naukri chhodte hi company ka cover khatam hota hai, aur 45 ke baad apna premium tez badhta hai.
Bharat ke liye savdhaani: yahan mehngai aam taur par zyada rehti hai, isliye kai log 4% ki jagah 3-3.5% ka hisaab rakhna zyada surakshit maante hain. Iska matlab corpus 25 guna nahi, 28-33 guna chahiye.

FIRE ke alag roop

  • Lean FIRE — bahut saada jeevan, kam kharch, chhota corpus
  • Fat FIRE — aaram ka jeevan, bada corpus
  • Coast FIRE — itna jama ho gaya ki bina naya paisa daale wo retirement tak khud badh jaayega; ab aap kam tanav wali naukri kar sakte hain
  • Barista FIRE — poora kaam chhoda nahi, part-time kaam se rozana kharcha nikalta hai

Zyadatar bharatiya logon ke liye Coast FIRE sabse practical hai — poori tarah kaam chhodna zaroori nahi, bas paise ka dabav khatam ho jaata hai.

Number se pehle ye 4 cheezein

  1. Emergency fund — 6-12 mahine ka kharcha alag rakha ho
  2. Health insurance — apna aur parivaar ka, achhi rakam ka. Naukri chhodne par company wala cover khatam ho jaata hai
  3. Term insurance — agar koi aap par nirbhar hai
  4. Karza mukt — FIRE se pehle mehnge karze khatam hone chahiye

Imaandar baat

FIRE har kisi ke liye nahi hai, aur ye zaroori bhi nahi. Bahut se log 20 saal tak kanjoosi karke jaldi retire hone ki koshish me wo saal hi kho dete hain. Isse behtar aksar ye hota hai ki aap aisa kaam dhoondein jo aapko pasand ho, aur saath-saath achhi bachat karte rahein. Paise ki azadi ka maqsad kaam se bhaagna nahi, balki chunne ki azadi paana hai.
