Formula aur uska matlab

NEET me har sahi answer par +4 aur har galat par −1 (negative marking) hota hai. Chhode gaye (un-attempted) question par kuch nahi. Total marks 720 me se hote hain (180 question × 4).

Formula:
Score = (sahi × 4) − (galat × 1)
Chhode gaye question ka koi asar nahi
Adhiktam = 180 × 4 = 720

Isliye tukka (guess) tabhi faydemand jab aap 2 option hata chuke ho — warna negative marking score gira deti hai.

Teen misaal, asli number ke saath

Misaal 1 — accha attempt. 150 sahi, 20 galat. Score = 600 − 20 = 580 / 720. 10 question chhode.

Misaal 2 — tukka mehnga pada. 170 sahi par 10 galat. Score = 680 − 10 = 670. Agar wo 10 na attempt karte to 680 hota... par sahi ho sakte the — isliye soch-samajh kar.

Misaal 3 — safe khela. 120 sahi, 5 galat, 55 chhode. Score = 480 − 5 = 475. Kam risk, par kam attempt.

Sabse aam galtiyan

  1. Negative marking bhoolna. Har galat par −1 lagta hai — ise ginna zaroori, warna score zyada dikhega.
  2. Chhode gaye ko galat ginna. Un-attempted par kuch nahi katta — unhe galat me mat jodein.
  3. Total galat lena. NEET me aksar 180 question ginte hain (200 me se 180 attempt). Apne pattern ka total lein.
  4. Score ko rank samajhna. Same score ka rank har saal alag hota hai (difficulty par). Score ≠ rank.
  5. Bina answer key anuman. Sahi/galat official answer key se milaayein — yaadaasht se galti hoti hai.

Bharat me kya alag hai

  • Pattern: NEET me aam taur par 200 question aate hain, jinme se 180 attempt karne hote hain.
  • Cutoff: Category ke hisaab se qualifying cutoff alag hota hai — har saal badalta hai.
  • Rank: Same marks par rank saal-dar-saal badalta hai, kitne log aaye aur paper kitna mushkil tha.
  • Answer key: NTA official provisional answer key deti hai — usse milaakar hi sahi score aata hai.

Aur thoda samajhte hain

NEET me negative marking hi wo cheez hai jo score ko sabse zyada prabhaavit karti hai. Har galat answer par −1, yaani ek sahi (+4) ki bachat ek galat (−1) mita nahi sakta, par 4 galat ek sahi ka fayda kha jaate hain. Isi liye "tukka" (random guess) mehnga pad sakta hai. Hukum ka niyam ye hai — agar aap 2 option pakke taur par hata chuke hain, to bache 2 me se guess karna ganit ke hisaab se faydemand hai; par bilkul andhera me guess karna score gira deta hai.

Ek zaroori baat — score aur rank do alag cheezein hain. Same 650 marks ka rank ek saal 5,000 ho sakta hai aur agle saal 8,000, kyunki ye is baat par nirbhar karta hai ki kitne log baithe aur paper kitna mushkil tha. Isi liye apne score ko "rank" maan kar college ka andaza mat lagaiye — pichhle saal ki rank-vs-marks list dekhein. Aur ye score sirf tabhi sahi hai jab aap NTA ki official answer key se apne sahi-galat milaayein; yaadaasht se ginne me aksar 20-30 marks ka farak aa jaata hai.

NEET ki marking — galat jawab ki asli keemat

NEET me ek galat jawab sirf ek number ka nuksan nahi hai. Sahi par +4 aur galat par -1 hone ke chalte ek galti asal me 5 number ka fark paida karti hai.

Isliye "tukka" ka ganit samajhna zaroori hai. Chaar option me se bilkul andhera me tukka lagaayein to ausatan faayda shoonya ke aas-paas rehta hai — 4 me se 1 baar +4 aur 3 baar −1. Par agar aap do option pakke kaat chuke hain, to bache do me se ek chunna faayde ka sauda ban jaata hai. Isi wajah se topper "sab bhar do" ya "shaq ho to chhod do" — dono ki jagah teesra tarika apnaate hain: pehle option kaato, phir tay karo. Aur cut-off ke saath percentile bhi dekhein, kyunki seat percentile aur category se milti hai, sirf marks se nahi.

Chhote sawaal, chhote jawab

NEET me marking kya hai?

Har sahi par +4, har galat par &minus;1. Chhode gaye par kuch nahi.

Total marks kitne hote hain?

720 (180 question &times; 4).

Chhode gaye question ka asar?

Koi nahi — un-attempted par na marks milte, na katte.

Score se rank pata chalega?

Nahi. Same score ka rank har saal alag hota hai.

Sahi score kaise nikaalein?

Official answer key se sahi/galat gin kar is tool me daalein.

Zaroori baat: Ye anumaanit score hai, official result nahi. Sahi score ke liye NTA ki official answer key se milaayein. Cutoff aur rank har saal badalte hain.
