Pahad me ek hi dish ka naam, banane ka tareeka aur swad gaon-gaon me badal jata hai, isliye yahan “aam taur par” wala tareeka diya hai. Aapke ghar ya gaon ka tareeka alag ho to wo bhi sahi hai.

Pithoragarh ke Mashhoor Mukhya Vyanjan

Kafuli, Phaanu, Chainsoo, Thechwani, Bhatt ki Churkani & Aloo ke Gutke

Pithoragarh aur Kumaon ki sabse prasiddh dishes Kafuli, Phaanu, Chainsoo, Thechwani, Bhatt ki Churkani, Dubuk aur Aloo ke Gutke hain. Zyadatar dishes loha ki kadhai me, kam masale aur sarson ke tel ya ghee me banti hain.

DishKis cheez se banti haiSwad aur khaasiyat
KafuliPalak, methi ya aur patte wali sabzi, chawal ya atte ke ghol meHari patti wali garam sabzi, loha kadhai me
Phaanu (Phanu)Bhigi hui dal peesi hui, pakayi huiGaadhi, protein bharpoor, roti ya chawal ke saath
ChainsooBhuna hua kaala bhatt ya gahat, peesa huaSmoky, thoda kurkura, protein ka khazana
ThechwaniKuti hui mooli ya aloo, masale ke saathMote mote tukdo wali sabzi
Bhatt ki ChurkaniKaala bhatt, pani me ubal karPatla, garam shorba jaisa, sardi ke liye
DubukBhatt ya gahat ki peesi hui dalGadhi, chawal ke saath
Aloo ke GutkeUbal kar bhune hue chhote aloo, jakhiya aur dhaniya ke tadke ke saathNaashte ya sabzi dono ke roop me

Kafuli ek line me: Kafuli palak ya methi ko ubal kar, usme chawal ka ghol milakar loha kadhai me bhuna jata hai. (Kai gharon me chawal ki jagah atta ya besan ka ghol bhi use hota hai.)

Aam sabzi ki shaan: Pahadi ghar me roz ki rasoi me Aloo ke Gutke, Pindalu (arbi) ke patte ki Gaderi, Kaddu ki sabzi, Gahat ki dal aur Mooli ka saag bahut aam hai.

Sabhisaab ki rai: Agar aap pehli baar Pithoragarh aate hain to Bhatt ki Churkani, Phaanu aur Aloo ke Gutke zaroor khayein. Ye teen swad aapko poore Kumaon ka paricay de dete hain.

Pithoragarh: Paramparik Roti aur Chawal ke Pakwan

Mandua ki Roti, Gahat ke Parathe, Jhangore ka Bhaat & Anya

Pahad ki rotiyan mote anaj se banti hain, jisme Mandua (ragi) ki roti sabse mashhoor hai, jo sardi me pet ko garam rakhti hai.

Roti / BhaatKis cheez seKaise khayein
Mandua ki rotiMandua ka attaGhee, gur, ya Bhatt ki churkani ke saath
Gahat ke paratheAtte me gahat ki peesi dal bhar karChutney ya dahi ke saath
Chhoi / Lesu rotiAtte ki roti me masala ya mandua ki bharwanChutney ke saath
Jhangore ka bhaatJhangora (sanwa)Dal aur sabzi ke saath, chawal jaisa
Kauni / Cheena ka bhaatChhote mote anajSardi me ya vrat me

Ek fayda ki baat: Mandua aur Jhangora me fibre aur mineral ache maane jate hain, aur ye maida ya safed chawal ki tulna me dheere hazam hote hain. (Kisi bimari ke liye khana tay karne se pehle doctor ya dietician se salah lein.)

Seemant ki roti: Munsiari aur Dharchula ke unche ilake me Phaphar (buckwheat) ki roti ya Ogal ke roti-parathe bhi bante hain. Shauka samaj me ye khaas mana jata hai.

Pithoragarh: Chutney, Raita, Achaar aur Pisyun Loon

Bhangjeera, Bhang ke Beej ki Chutney, Pahadi Raita, Pisyun Loon & Achar

Pahadi chutney sirf swad nahi, balki khane ka “tadka” hai: Bhangjeera chutney, Bhang ke beej ki chutney, Pahadi raita aur Pisyun loon, har ek ki apni alag pehchan hai.

CheezKya hai
Pisyun LoonSil-batte par peesa hua masaledar namak, lehsun, mirch, dhaniya aur jakhiya ke saath, kai baar jadi-bootiyon ke saath
Bhangjeera chutneyBhangjeera ke beej ki khatti-masaledar chutney
Bhang ke beej ki chutneyBhang (hemp) ke bhune beej ki chutney, jo beej se banti hai, nashe ka paudha nahi mana jata
Pahadi Kheere ka raitaPahadi kheera, dahi, rai ke saath, rai ki tez tajgi
Pahadi nimbu (Galgal) ka acharGalgal, mooli, aadu, nimbu ya hari mirch ka achar

Zaroori spashtikaran: Bhang ke beej ka khane me istemal sthaniya paramparik hai, par nasha ya bhang ki patti ka istemal ek kanooni aur alag mudda hai. (Kanoon aur niyam ke liye NDPS Act aur rajya ke niyam alag se dekhein.)

Pisyun loon ka baazaar: Pisyun loon aaj kal kai jagah “Pahadi Namak” ke naam se bechne wali chhoti dukanon aur self-help groups ke zariye bhi milta hai.

Pithoragarh: Jungli Khane Layak Cheezein aur Mausami Saag

Bicchu Ghas (Nettle), Linguda, Kafal, Hisalu, Kilmora, Bedu

Pahad ke jungle me barsat aur sardi ke hisab se kai khane layak sabziyan aur phal milte hain, jinme Bicchu Ghas (Sisunak) ka saag, Linguda, Kafal, Hisalu, Kilmora aur Bedu mukhya hain.

NaamKya haiKab milta hai (aam taur par)
Bicchu Ghas / SisunakChubhne wali ghaas, ubal kar saag, chutney aur chaiBarsat aur uske baad
LingudaJungle ki fiddlehead fern (sabzi)Barsat ka mausam
KafalLaal, khatta-meetha jungli phalApril se June
HisaluPeela-narangi, khatta jungli phalMay se June
KilmoraChhota laal-kala jungli phalGarmi aur barsat
BeduHimalayan anjeer jaise jungli phalGarmi aur barsat

Ek zaroori chetavni: Kisi bhi jungli paudhe ya mushroom ko bina sthaniya jaankar ya anubhavi insaan se pehchane na khayein. Kuch jungli paudhe ya mushroom zehrile ho sakte hain.

Kafal ki khaasiyat: Kafal ka mausam April se June ke aas-paas hota hai, aur Kumaoni geeton me iska zikr bahut aata hai.

Pithoragarh: Paramparik Mithai, Tyohar ke Pakwan aur Prasad

Singhal, Singori, Bal Mithai, Arsa, Jhangore ki Kheer & Rote

Pahad ki paramparik mithai tyohar aur puja ke samay banti hai, jisme Singhal, Singori, Bal Mithai, Rote aur Jhangore ki Kheer mukhya hain.

Mithai / SnackAam taur parDhyan dene wali baat
SingoriKhoya ko Maalu ke patte me lapet karMukhya roop se Kumaon ki mithai
Bal MithaiBhune khoya aur cheeni ki chhoti goliyanAlmora-Kumaon se prasiddh, Pithoragarh me bhi milti hai
SinghalAtte ka mulayam, meetha tala hua nashtaTyohar ke samay
Rote / RothAtte, gur aur ghee ki meethi rotiPuja ya vrat me prasad
Jhangore ki kheerJhangora aur doodhSardi ya tyohar me
ArsaChawal ke atte aur gur ka meethaMukhya roop se Garhwal ka, Kumaon me alag naam se

Ghughuti (Ghughutiya) ka sambandh: Ghughutiya tyohar par bacchon ke liye banai jane wali ghughute ke aakar ki meethi tali hui mithai hai, jo mukhya roop se Makar Sankranti (Uttarayani) par banti hai. (Is par Pithoragarh: Sanskriti, tyohar aur mele me tyohar ke saath vistar se.)

Pithoragarh: Paramparik Peya aur Khameer Wale Pey

Buransh Sharbat, Chhaas, Pahadi Chai aur Chhang

Pahad ka sabse mashhoor pey Buransh (rhododendron) ka sharbat hai, jo garmi me thanda aur swadisht hota hai, aur kai log ise dil ke liye achha bhi maante hain. (Swasthya labh ke daave vaigyanik shodh se pushti karein.)

PeyKhaasiyat
Buransh ka sharbatLaal burans ke phoolon se, thanda aur khatta-meetha
Chhaas / MaathaDahi se bani chhaas, kai jagah jeera aur pisyun loon ke saath
Pahadi chaiDoodh wali chai, kai jagah gud ya adrak ke saath
Jhangore ki chhaasGaon me tyohar ke samay
Chhang / JaanJohar-Darma-Vyans ki Shauka paramparik anaj-adharit, khameeri pey (sthaniya sanskriti)

Chhang ke bare me spashtikaran: Chhang ya jaan seemant ki Shauka samaj ki paramparik sanskriti ka hissa maana jata hai. Sharab ka kanoon, license aur niyam rajya ke Aabkari niyam ke anusar hain.

Pithoragarh: Pahadi Kitchen Architecture & Utensils

Loh ki Kadhai, Silbatta, Kansi Thali, Bhaddu, Parat & Mitti ka Chulha

Pahadi rasoi ki pehchan loha ki kadhai, sil-batta, kansi ki thali, bhaddu (bhaari pital ka bartan) aur mitti ka chulha hai.

Bartan / CheezKaam
Loh ki kadhaiKafuli, saag aur dalon ke liye, khane me loha ka swad aur poshan
Sil-battaMasale aur chutney peesne ke liye
Kansi ki thali / katoriKhane ka paramparik bartan
BhadduDal dheemi aanch par pakane ka bhaari bartan
ParatAtta gundhne ya anaj saaf karne ka chaura bartan
Mitti ka chulhaLakdi ki aag par khana, kuch ghar me ab bhi

Rasoi ka ghar ke andar sthaan: Pahad ke purane ghar me rasoi aksar ghar ke andar alag, saaf aur pavitra jagah maani jati hai, jahan juta-chappal le jana mana hota hai. (Ye ghar-ghar ki parampara hai.)

Aaj ki rasoi: Gas ka chulha aur mixer ne kaam asaan kiya hai, par kai pariwar tyohar ke samay purane tareeke se khana banate hain, kyunki unka maanna hai ki swad alag aata hai.

Pithoragarh: Pahadi khana aur mithai: Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Pahadi daal gahat kya hai?
Gahat (kulath, horse gram) ek pahadi daal hai, jo sardi me khaayi jati hai.
Bhatt kya hai?
Kaala soyabean, jisse churkani aur dubuk jaisi dishes banti hain.
Singori kis cheez ki mithai hai?
Khoya ko maalu ke patte me lapet kar banayi jane wali Kumaon ki mithai.
Pithoragarh ki sabse prasiddh dishes kaun si hain?
Kafuli, Phaanu, Chainsoo, Thechwani, Bhatt ki Churkani, Dubuk aur Aloo ke Gutke, jo zyadatar loha ki kadhai mein bante hain.
Pahad ki sabse mashhoor roti kaun si hai?
Mandua (ragi) ki roti, jo ghee, gur ya Bhatt ki churkani ke saath khayi jaati hai.
Bhang ke beej ki chutney mein nasha hota hai?
Nahi, ye bhune hue beej se banti hai, nashe wale paudhe ke hisse se nahi.
