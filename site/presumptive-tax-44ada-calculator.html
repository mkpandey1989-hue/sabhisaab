44ADA Calculator — Freelancer Aur Professional Ka Tax

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Section 44ADA un professionals ke liye hai jo apna kaam khud karte hain — doctor, vakeel, engineer, architect, accountant, technical consultant, interior designer aur aise hi kaam. Isme aadhi kamai ko hi aay maan liya jaata hai, aur baaki aadhi ko kharcha.

Neeche saal ki kul receipt aur apni doosri aay daaliye. Tax kitna banega, ye turant dikh jayega.

44ADA ka poora matlab

Saadharan tarike me aapko har kharch ka hisaab rakhna padta hai — laptop, internet, office ka kiraya, software, safar. Har bill sambhalna padta hai aur books banani padti hain.

44ADA me ye poora jhanjhat khatam ho jaata hai. Sarkar maan leti hai ki aapki kamai ka 50% kharch me gaya hoga, aur baaki 50% par tax lagta hai. Bill sambhalne ki zaroorat nahi, books banane ki zaroorat nahi, audit ki zaroorat nahi.

Yahi wajah hai ki chhote freelancer aur consultant ke liye ye rasta itna aaram deta hai. Saal me ek baar ITR bhariye, kaam khatam.

Seema — 50 lakh, aur 75 lakh ki shart

44ADA sabke liye nahi hai. Saal ki kul receipt ek seema me honi chahiye.

Aam seema 50 lakh hai. Par agar aapki nakad me mili receipt kul ka 5% se zyada nahi hai, to seema badhkar 75 lakh ho jaati hai. Yaani jo poora paisa bank, UPI ya cheque se leta hai, use zyada chhoot milti hai. Aaj ke daur me zyadatar freelancer isi shart me aa jaate hain.

Doosri shart ye hai ki aapka kaam nirdhaarit professional list me aana chahiye. Doctor, vakeel, engineer, architect, accountant, technical consultant, interior designer, film ke kuch kaam — ye ismein aate hain. Aam vyapaar ismein nahi aata; uske liye alag section hai jisme 8% ya 6% ka niyam chalta hai.

Agar receipt seema paar kar jaye, to us saal saadharan tarika apnana hota hai — poora hisaab, books aur zaroorat par audit.

Kab 44ADA faayde ka hai, kab nahi

Naya kaam, ghaata huaSaadharan tarikaGhaata aage le jaa sakte hain

Ek writer, designer ya coder jo ghar se kaam karta hai, uska asli kharch aksar 15-20% hi hota hai. Aise vyakti ke liye 44ADA seedha faayda hai — asli munafa 80% hone ke bawajood tax sirf 50% par lagta hai.

Ek zaroori niyam: 44ADA chunne ke baad agar aap beech me saadharan tarike par jaate hain, to kuch saalon tak wapas 44ADA me aane par rok lag jaati hai. Isliye ye faisla saal-dar-saal badalne wali cheez nahi hai. Ek baar sochkar tay kijiye.

Advance tax — sirf ek kist

Saadharan tarike me advance tax chaar kist me bharni padti hai. 44ADA me sirf ek kist hai — poora advance tax saal ki aakhri tay tarikh tak.

Ye bhi ek badi suvidha hai jo kam charcha me aati hai. Par iska matlab ye nahi ki aakhir tak bhool jayein. Ek hi baar me badi rakam jama karni padti hai, isliye saal bhar thoda-thoda alag rakhna samajhdaari hai.

Der hone par byaj lagta hai, jo har mahine ke hisaab se judta hai. Apni kist ka hisaab Advance Tax Calculator me dekh lijiye.

Ek aur baat — 44ADA aur GST do alag cheezein hain. 44ADA income tax ka rasta hai. GST ka registration alag seema par zaroori hota hai, aur videsh ke client ke liye alag niyam lagte hain. Dono ko ek maan lena aam galti hai.

Freelancer ke liye chhoti si vyavastha

Chaar cheezein jo saal bhar aaram deti hain.

  • Alag bank khaata — kaam ka paisa alag khaate me lijiye. Saal ke aakhir me receipt ka hisaab ek statement se nikal aata hai.
  • Nakad se bachiye — 5% wali shart poori rehti hai aur 75 lakh ki badi seema chalu rehti hai.
  • Har invoice ka number — client se paisa aane par milaan aasan rehta hai, aur notice aane par saboot bhi.
  • 26AS aur AIS dekhte rahiye — client ne aapke naam par TDS kaata hai to wahan dikhta hai. Bahut freelancer ye credit lena bhool jaate hain.

Ye page samajhne ke liye hai, tax salah nahi. Hum CA nahi hain. Receipt badi ho ya videshi client hon, to ek baar CA se baat kar lena sabse suraksit hai.

Apna kul tax dekhna ho to Income Tax Calculator aur invoice ka hisaab Invoice Total Calculator me dekh lijiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

44ADA kaun le sakta hai?

Nirdhaarit professional — jaise doctor, vakeel, engineer, architect, accountant, technical consultant aur interior designer. Aam vyapaar iske andar nahi aata.

Seema 50 lakh hai ya 75 lakh?

Aam seema 50 lakh hai. Agar nakad me mili receipt kul ka 5% se zyada nahi hai, to seema 75 lakh ho jaati hai.

Asli kharch 50% se zyada ho to kya karein?

Aisi sthiti me saadharan tarika behtar hai, kyunki wahan asli kharch poora ghatta hai. 44ADA tabhi faayde ka hai jab asli kharch 50% se kam ho.

44ADA me books banani padti hain?

Nahi. Yahi iska sabse bada aaram hai — na books, na audit, na har bill sambhalna. Sirf receipt ka hisaab saaf hona chahiye.

Advance tax kitni kist me bharni hoti hai?

Sirf ek kist me, saal ki aakhri tay tarikh tak. Saadharan tarike me ye chaar kist me bharni padti hai.
