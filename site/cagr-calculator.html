Peeche ka ganit

CAGR ka matlab hai wo ek dar jispar aapka paisa har saal badhta to wahi aakhri value banti. Ye upar-neeche ke utaar-chadhav ko chikna kar ke ek seedha number de deta hai. Isi liye 3 saal me 40% aur 7 saal me 90% badhne wale do nivesh ki tulna CAGR se hi theek se ho paati hai.

Formula: CAGR = [(Aakhri value / Shuru value)1/n − 1] × 100
n = saal

Kuch misaal jo saaf kar dein

Misaal 1 — lamba nivesh. ₹12,000 se ₹2,00,000, 25 saal me. CAGR lagbhag 11.91% nikalta hai, jabki total growth 1,566.7% dikhta hai. Bada percent dekh kar dhokha na khaayein — saal ka return 12% ke aaspaas hi hai.

Misaal 2 — dogna hone ka samay. ₹1 lakh 6 saal me ₹2 lakh hua. CAGR = 12.25%. Yahi "Rule of 72" ka aadhaar hai — 72 ÷ 12 = 6 saal.

Misaal 3 — zameen ki tulna. Zameen ₹10 lakh se 12 saal me ₹30 lakh hui — CAGR lagbhag 9.6%. Sunne me teen guna bada lagta hai, par saalana dar FD se thodi hi zyada hai.

Jahan log atakte hain

  1. Total return ko saalana samajh lena. 5 saal me 60% badha, iska matlab saal ka 12% nahi hota — CAGR lagbhag 9.9% banta hai.
  2. Beech me daale gaye paise ko na ginna. Agar aapne beech me aur paisa daala hai to CAGR sahi jawab nahi dega. Aise me XIRR chahiye hota hai.
  3. Mehngai ko chhod dena. 12% CAGR par mehngai 6% hai to asli badhotri lagbhag 5.7% hi hai.
  4. Bahut chhote samay par CAGR nikalna. 6 mahine ke data ko saalana banane se number bhramit karta hai.
  5. Kharch aur tax na ghatana. Brokerage, expense ratio aur tax ke baad haath me aane wala CAGR hamesha kam hota hai.

Bharat ke hisaab se dhyaan dein

  • Mutual fund: Fact sheet me 1/3/5 saal ka CAGR diya hota hai. 5 saal se kam ka number bahut bharosemand nahi hota.
  • SIP ke liye: SIP me har kist ki tareekh alag hai, isliye CAGR ki jagah XIRR sahi maap hai.
  • Property: Zameen ya makaan ka CAGR nikalte waqt registry, stamp duty aur maintenance bhi jodein.
  • Gold: Lambe samay me gold ka CAGR aam taur par 8-10% ke aaspaas raha hai, par beech ke saal bahut alag rahe hain.
  • Tulna: Do nivesh ki tulna hamesha ek hi avadhi par karein.

CAGR ka full form aur matlab — saaf bhasha me

CAGR ka full form hai Compound Annual Growth Rate. Hindi me ise chakravriddhi vaarshik vriddhi dar keh sakte hain — yaani har saal ki ausat badhotari, chakravriddhi ke hisaab se.

Saral bhasha me: agar aapka paisa 5 saal me ₹1 lakh se ₹2 lakh ho gaya, to CAGR batayega ki har saal ausatan kitne percent badha — is case me lagbhag 14.87%.

CAGR aur kul return me farak

Kul returnSamayCAGR
100%3 saal26%
100%5 saal14.87%
100%10 saal7.18%
50%5 saal8.45%

Yahi wajah hai ki koi kahe "maine dugna kar liya" to poochna chahiye — kitne saal me? 10 saal me dugna karna sirf 7.18% saalana hai, jo FD ke aas-paas hai.

CAGR ki ek kami zaroor jaan lijiye — ye beech ka utaar-chadhav chhupa deta hai. Ek fund jo har saal 12% de aur ek jo pehle saal 60% chadhe phir gire — dono ka CAGR ek ho sakta hai, par risk bilkul alag. Isliye CAGR ke saath ye bhi dekhiye ki safar kaisa raha.

SIP jaise nivesh me, jahan paisa har mahine lagta hai, CAGR sahi jawab nahi deta — wahan XIRR ka hisaab chahiye. Kul return ke liye ROI Calculator aur mehngai ke baad asli badhotari ke liye Real Return Calculator dekhiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

CAGR ka matlab kya hai?

Compound Annual Growth Rate — wo saalana dar jispar paisa badha maana jaata hai.

CAGR aur absolute return me kya farak hai?

Absolute return kul badhotri batata hai, CAGR use saal ke hisaab se baant deta hai.

Kya CAGR negative ho sakta hai?

Haan. Agar aakhri value shuru se kam hai to CAGR negative aayega.

SIP ke liye CAGR sahi hai?

Nahi, SIP ke liye XIRR behtar hota hai kyunki har kist ka samay alag hota hai.

Achha CAGR kitna maana jaata hai?

Equity me lambe samay me 10-12% aam maana jaata hai. Isse bahut zyada ka daava shak ki nazar se dekhein.

SIP par CAGR laga sakte hain?

Theek se nahi. SIP me paisa har mahine lagta hai, isliye wahan XIRR ka hisaab sahi jawab deta hai.

CAGR ki kami kya hai?

Ye beech ka utaar-chadhav chhupa deta hai. Do bilkul alag risk wale nivesh ka CAGR ek jaisa ho sakta hai.

CAGR ka full form kya hai?

Ye "ausat return" se alag hai. Ausat me har saal ka return jodkar bhaag de dete hain; CAGR me pichhle saal ka munafa bhi agle saal kamai karta hai — isi liye dono ka jawab alag aata hai.
