Haridwar ke liye khaas note: Haridwar ka khana "Pahadi" kam aur "Tirth-nagri ka satvik, shuddh shakahari khana" zyada hai: aloo-puri, kachori-sabzi, chole-bhature, rabri, lassi aur jalebi. Pahadi dishes (Kafuli, Chainsoo, Mandua roti) Haridwar me bhi mil jati hain, kyunki ye Garhwal ka pravesh-dwar hai, par ye yahan ki mool pehchan nahi hain.

Haridwar ke Mashhoor Mukhya Vyanjan

Haridwar ka Asli Khana: Aloo-Puri, Kachori-Sabzi, Chole-Bhature, Thali & Sattvic Bhojan

Seedha jawab: Haridwar ka sabse prasiddh nashta Aloo-Puri aur Kachori-Sabzi hai, jo Har Ki Pauri ke aas-paas ki dukanon me subah milta hai. Iske saath chole-bhature, thali, rabri, lassi aur jalebi bhi mashhoor hain. Ye sab shuddh shakahari hota hai.

Haridwar ek tirth-nagri hai, isliye yahan ke khane me shuddh shakahari (pure veg) parampara hai. Kai ashram aur dharamshala me bhojan satvik (bina pyaaz-lehsun) bhi banta hai. Subah Ganga snan ya Aarti ke baad garam aloo-puri aur kachori khana yahan ki sabse purani aadat hai.

Haridwar ke 8 pramukh vyanjan:

VyanjanKya haiKahan prasiddh (Har Ki Pauri aur aas-paas)
Aloo-PuriGaram puri ke saath masaledar aloo ki sabzi; aksar kaddu (peethe) ki meethi-khatti sabzi aur imli ki chutney ke saathMohan Ji Puri Wale, Hoshiyar Puri
Kachori-SabziKhasta kachori aur tikhi aloo ki sabziKashyap Kachori Bhandar, Chotiwala, Pehalwan ji ki kachori (Kankhal) jaisi dukane
Chole-BhatureChhole aur tala hua bhaturaBhagwati Chole Bhature (Ranipur More aur Har Ki Pauri ke aas-paas)
ThaliNorth Indian aur kabhi-kabhi Garhwali vyanjanChotiwala, Hoshiyar Puri
JalebiGaram, kai jagah desi ghee me baniHar Ki Pauri aur Upper Road ke halwai
Rabri aur LassiGadhi malai wali rabri aur kulhad ki lassiMathura Walon ki Pracheen Dukan jaisi purani dukane
PedaDoodh ke khoye ka mithaiMathura Walon ki dukane, Bada Bazar
Chaat, Aloo TikkiShaam ke nashteHar Ki Pauri, Upper Road

Naam likhne ki sahi tareeka: Upar ke dukano ke naam samachar/travel sources me bar-bar aate hain, par dukan ka sthan, naam aur khulne ka samay badal sakta hai. Page par "log aksar... ka naam lete hain" shaili me likhein, aur paid promotion na ho to kisi ko "number 1" na kahein.

Haridwar me mans, anda aur sharab ka niyam: Haridwar ke pavitra kshetra me nagar nigam aur dharmik parampara ke tahat mans-machhli, anda aur sharab par pratibandh ka niyam prachalit hai (2024 me nagar nigam ne bhi is disha me kadam uthane ki khabar aayi thi). Poore jile (Roorkee, Bahadrabad aadi) ke liye niyam alag ho sakte hain; sahi sthiti ke liye Nagar Nigam ya Abkari Vibhag se pushti karein.

Ek din ka Haridwar Food Trail (suggested plan):

  • Subah (6-8 baje): Har Ki Pauri par snan aur Ganga Aarti ke baad kulhad ki chai.
  • Nashta (8-10 baje): Aloo-puri ya kachori-sabzi aur jalebi.
  • Dopahar: Thali ya chole-bhature.
  • Shaam: Chaat, lassi aur Mathura-wale peda.
  • Raat: Aarti ke baad rabri ya garam doodh.

Pahadi Main Course Dishes (Haridwar Gateway se Garhwal ka Swad): Kafuli, Phaanu, Chainsoo, Thechwani, Bhatt ki Churkani, Dubuk, Ras-Bhaat, Gahat ki Dal & Jholi/Kaphli

Seedha jawab: Kafuli (palak-methi ki mooti sabzi), Chainsoo (bhuni urad dal), Phaanu (bhigi dal ki sabzi), Bhatt ki Churkani (kale soyabean ka gravy), Thechwani (kuti mooli-aloo) aur Gahat ki dal Garhwal-Kumaon ke mashhoor vyanjan hain jo lohe ki kadhai me bante hain. Haridwar me inka swad kuch restaurants aur Garhwali thali me milta hai.

Pahadi vyanjan ek nazar me (saadharan jankari):

VyanjanMukhya samagriKhaas baat
KafuliPalak, methi, chawal ka aata ya pasteLohe ki kadhai me dheemi aanch par; chawal ke saath
PhaanuBhigi aur pisi hui dal (gahat, urad, masoor ya mishrit)Mote pare tadke ke saath; Garhwal ka prasiddh vyanjan
ChainsooBhuni aur pisi hui urad dalBhunne se khushboo aur swad bahut alag aata hai

Lohe ki kadhai me khana kyun?
Pahadi rasoi me lohe ki kadhai, silbatta aur chulhe ka istemal vyanjano ko alag swad deta hai. Lohe ke bartan me khana banane se thodi iron ki matra khane me aa sakti hai, par isse jude swasthya dave ke liye dietitian ki salah lein.
Haridwar me ye kahan milta hai?
Kuch restaurants aur Garhwali thali dene wale dhabon me; sthan badalte rehte hain, isliye google maps par "Garhwali thali Haridwar" search karein.

Palak aur methi se bani Garhwali mooti sabzi.
Chainsoo kya hai?
Bhuni hui urad dal ka Garhwali vyanjan.
Phaanu kaise banta hai?
Bhigi dal pees kar lohe ki kadhai me paka kar.
Gahat ki dal sardi me kyun khayi jati hai?
Paramparik maanyata ke anusar garam taseer wali maani jati hai (chikitsakiya salah nahi).

Kale soyabean ka gaadha Pahadi gravy.

Haridwar: Paramparik Roti aur Chawal ke Pakwan

Mandua ki Roti, Bedu Roti (Dal-Bhari Roti), Chhoi/Lesu Roti, Jhangore ka Bhaat & Gahat Ras-Bhaat

Seedha jawab: Haridwar me roz ki roti gehun ki hoti hai (kyunki yahan gehun ugta hai), aur nashte me puri, kachori aur paratha. Mandua ki roti, Bedu roti (dal-bhari roti), Jhangora ka bhaat jaise Pahadi vyanjan Haridwar ke bazaar aur restaurants me Garhwal se aakar milte hain.

Haridwar me bread ki mukhya shreniyan:

  • Puri aur Bhatura: Maide ya gehun ke aate ki tali hui roti.
  • Khasta kachori: Dal ya aloo ki stuffing wali tali kachori.
  • Tawa paratha: Aloo, gobhi, mooli aadi ke paranthe (kai dhabon me).
  • Tandoori roti: Dhabon aur highway ke restaurants me.

Pahadi roti aur chawal (Garhwal-Kumaon se):

Roti / ChawalKaisi hoti hai
Mandua ki RotiRagi (finger millet) ke aate ki gehri bhuri roti; ghee ya makhan ke saath
Bedu RotiPisi hui urad dal ki stuffing wali gehun ki roti (Garhwal)
Chhoi/Lesu RotiGehun ke aate me masaledar mandua ki bharwan wali roti
Jhangora ka BhaatJhangora (sanwa) ke daane se bana "chawal"
Gahat Ras-BhaatGahat ke ras ke saath chawal

Tip: Mandua ka aata roti me bandhta kam hai; pehle halka garm paani milakar achhi tarah gundhein aur patla belein.

Chhote Sawal (Quick FAQ)

Mandua ki roti kaise banti hai?
Mandua ke aate ko garam paani me gundh kar tawe par sekte hain.
Bedu roti kya hai?
Dal ki stuffing wali Garhwali roti.
Jhangora kya hai?
Barnyard millet, jisse chawal jaisa bhaat banta hai.

Haridwar: Chutney, Raita, Achaar aur Pisyun Loon

Haridwar ki Chutney, Aam-Amla ka Achar aur Pahadi Chutney (Bhangjeera, Bhang ke Beej, Pahadi Kheera Raita, Darim, Timur, Mooli Achar)

Seedha jawab: Haridwar me kachori-puri ke saath imli-saunth ki khatti-meethi chutney aur hari chutney di jati hai. Pahadi chutneyon me Bhangjeera (perilla beej), Bhang ke beej (hemp seed), Darim (anardana) aur Timur (Pahadi mirch jaisa masala) ki chutney prasiddh hain.

Haridwar ki chutney aur achar:

  • Imli-saunth chutney: Kachori, chaat aur puri ke saath.
  • Hari chutney: Dhaniya-pudina-mirch.
  • Kaddu (peetha) ki khatti-meethi sabzi: Aloo-puri ke saath ek khaas pehchan.
  • Aam, nimbu, mirch ka achar: Gharelu achar parampara (Western UP aur Haridwar me aam).
  • Amla ka murabba/achar: Sardiyon me Amla ki fasal ke saath.

Pahadi chutney (jinka swad Haridwar ke Pahadi restaurants aur gifts me milta hai):

Chutney / AcharMukhya samagri
Bhangjeera chutneyBhangjeera (perilla) ke bhune beej, namak, mirch
Bhang ke beej ki chutneyHemp ke beej (nashe wala nahi)
Pahadi kheere ka raitaPahadi peele-bade kheere, dahi, pisi hui rai
Darim chutneyJangli anaar (darim) ke daane
Timur chutneyTimur (Zanthoxylum) ka tikha-khatta swad
Mooli / Linguda acharPahadi mooli, hari sabzi

Kanooni dhyan: Hemp (bhang) ke beej ka upyog pahadi parampara me hota hai, par ped/patte ka upyog aur kheti kanooni niyamon ke antargat hai. Isliye "Bhang ki chutney" likhte waqt saaf likhein ki ye sirf beej ki chutney hai aur patte ya nashe wale hisse ka koi sambandh nahi.

Chhote Sawal (Quick FAQ)

Bhangjeera kya hai?
Perilla ke beej, jinhe bhunkar chutney banti hai.
Timur kya hai?
Himalayan Pahadi masala, thoda jhanjhanahat wala swad.
Pahadi chutney kahan se kharidein?
Haridwar ki Pahadi dukano aur cooperative stores se.

Pisyun Loon: Silbatte par Pisa Pahadi Namak

Seedha jawab: Pisyun Loon Pahadi swadisht namak hai jise silbatta (pathar ki chakki) par lehsun ke patte, hari mirch, dhaniya, jeera aur timur ke saath pees kar banate hain. Isse roti, sabzi aur kheere ka swad badh jata hai.

Tareeka (gharelu, aasan):

  • Silbatta par moti namak ke saath hari mirch, lehsun ke patte, hara dhaniya aur bhuna jeera daalein.
  • Chahein to thoda timur mila dein.
  • Dheere-dheere rag-rag kar mota-mota pees lein.
  • Kheere, mooli, roti ya sabzi ke saath parosein.

Chhote Sawal (Quick FAQ)

Pisyun loon kya hai?
Pahadi masaledar pisa hua namak.
Ise kaise banate hain?
Silbatta par namak, hari mirch aur patton ko pees kar.

Haridwar: Jungli Khane Layak Cheezein aur Mausami Saag

Mausami Saag-Phal aur Jungli Khane Layak: Sisunak, Linguda, Kafal, Hisalu

Seedha jawab: Haridwar ki mausami hari sabziyon me sarson, bathua, methi aur palak, aur phalon me aam, jamun, bel aur amla prasiddh hain. Bicchu ghas (Kandali/Sisunak), Linguda, Kafal, Hisalu, Kilmora aur Buransh jaise jangli khadya pahadi (1,000 meter se upar) kshetron me milte hain.

Haridwar ke mausami khadya:

Bel (Bilva) ka sharbat: Haridwar ki Mansa Devi pahadi "Bilwa Parvat" kehlati hai aur garmi me bel ka sharbat yahan lokpriya hai.

Pahadi jangli khadya (Haridwar ke aas-paas ke pahadon me milne wale):

Suraksha chetavani: Jangli paudhe aur phal ko bina visheshagya ki pehchan ke na khayein; kai jangli paudhe zehreele hote hain. Rajaji ya kisi sanrakshit jungle se kuch todna kanoonan apradh hai.

Haridwar ke Pey (Kulhad Chai, Lassi, Thandai, Ganne ka Ras, Bel Sharbat) aur Pahadi Pey (Buransh Squash, Malta Juice, Gud-Adrak ki Chai) & Pahadi Paramparik Brew ka Sanskritik Parichay (Chhyang/Jaan)

Seedha jawab: Haridwar me kulhad ki masala chai, mitti ke kulhad ki lassi, thandai, ganne ka ras aur bel ka sharbat sabse lokpriya pey hain. Pahadi pey me Buransh squash, Malta juice aur gud-adrak ki chai prasiddh hain.
