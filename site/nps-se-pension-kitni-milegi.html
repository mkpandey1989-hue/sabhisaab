NPS Se Retirement Par Pension Kitni Milegi?

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~7 minute

Pension kitni milegi ye aapke kul corpus aur annuity ki dar par nirbhar karta hai. Jitna jaldi shuru karenge, utni badi pension.

NPS kaam kaise karta hai

NPS (National Pension System) ek retirement scheme hai jahan aap naukri ke saalon me har mahine thoda-thoda jama karte hain. Ye paisa share bazaar aur bond me lagta hai, isliye lambe samay me achha badhta hai. 60 saal ki umar par ye paisa (corpus) do hisson me kaam aata hai.

60 saal par: 60% paisa ekmusht (tax-free) + 40% se pension (annuity) khareedni zaroori

Pension kaise banti hai

Retirement par aapke kul corpus ka kam se kam 40% se ek "annuity" khareedni hoti hai — ye ek bima company ki scheme hai jo aapko marte dam tak (ya tay saalon tak) har mahine pension deti hai. Baaki 60% aap ekmusht le sakte hain, jo tax-free hota hai.

Pension kitni milegi ye do cheez par nirbhar: (1) aapka kul corpus kitna bada hai, (2) annuity ki dar us waqt kya hai (aam taur par 6-7% saalana).

Ek misaal — number ke saath

Maan lijiye 30 saal ka vyakti har mahine ₹5,000 NPS me daalta hai, 30 saal tak, aur paisa ausatan 10% badhta hai. Retirement par corpus lagbhag ₹1 crore ban sakta hai.

Ab 60 saal par: 60% yaani ~₹60 lakh ekmusht (tax-free). Baaki 40% yaani ~₹40 lakh se annuity. 6.5% annuity dar par, ye ~₹2,600 mahina pension deti hai. Ye number seedha corpus par nirbhar karta hai — isliye jitna jaldi aur jitna zyada jama karenge, pension utni badi. Apna hisaab NPS Calculator me laga kar dekhein.

Jaldi shuru karne ka jaadu

NPS me sabse badi cheez hai samay. 25 saal ki umar me shuru karne wala aur 35 me shuru karne wala — dono ₹5,000 daalein, par 25 wale ka corpus retirement par lagbhag dugna ho sakta hai. Wajah — compounding ko jitne zyada saal milenge, utna paisa badhega. Ye asar compound interest me samajh sakte hain.

Tax me fayda

NPS me teen tarah ke tax fayde hain (purane regime me):

  • 80C ke andar ₹1.5 lakh tak (baaki nivesh ke saath)
  • 80CCD(1B) me extra ₹50,000 — ye sirf NPS ko milta hai, iska koi aur vikalp nahi
  • Naukri wale ke liye employer ke contribution par bhi alag chhoot

Ye extra ₹50,000 wali chhoot NPS ki sabse khaas baat hai. New regime me ye 80C/80CCD(1B) fayde nahi milte — kaunsa regime theek hai, wo yahan dekhein.

NPS ke fayde aur kamiyan

Fayde: sasta (charges bahut kam), share bazaar ka fayda, tax me alag chhoot, sarkari nigraani. Lambe samay ke retirement goal ke liye achha.

Kamiyan: 60 saal se pehle poora paisa nahi nikalta (kuch sharton ke saath thoda nikal sakta hai). 40% se annuity lena zaroori hai, jispar aapka poora control nahi. Aur annuity ki pension par tax lagta hai.

Kiske liye NPS sahi hai

NPS un logon ke liye achha hai jo retirement ke liye anushasan se, lambe samay tak, tax bachate hue paisa banana chahte hain — khaaskar wo jinhe 80C ke upar extra ₹50,000 ki chhoot chahiye. Par ye poori retirement planning nahi hai. Iske saath SIP aur PPF jaise vikalp bhi rakhein taaki paisa ek hi jagah band na ho aur zaroorat par nikal sake.

Zaroori: annuity dar aur niyam badalte rehte hain, aur ye YMYL vishay hai. Bade faisle se pehle ek SEBI-registered advisor se salah lein. Current niyam NPS Trust par milte hain.

NPS ke andar paisa kahan lagta hai

NPS me aapka paisa teen tarah ke fund me baant kar lagaya jaata hai, aur aap chun sakte hain ki kis me kitna:

  • Equity (E): share bazaar me. Zyada badhta hai par upar-neeche bhi hota hai. Jawaani me isme zyada rakhna theek.
  • Corporate bond (C): company ke bond me. Madhyam jokhim, madhyam return.
  • Government bond (G): sarkari bond me. Sabse surakshit, par kam badhta.

Do tareeke hain: Active (aap khud tay karein kitna E/C/G me), ya Auto (umar ke hisaab se apne aap badalta — jawaani me zyada equity, retirement paas aane par kam). Jinhe khud manage nahi karna, unke liye Auto aasan hai. Jaise-jaise umar badhti hai, equity ka hissa apne aap kam hota jaata hai taaki retirement ke paas paisa surakshit rahe.

Ye lachak NPS ko FD/PPF se alag banati hai — lambe samay me equity ka fayda milta hai, par retirement ke paas surakshaa bhi. Isi wajah se 30 saal jaise lambe safar ke liye NPS ka corpus PPF se bada ban sakta hai, chahe dono me barabar paisa daalein.

Corpus se pension — annuity ka hisaab

Retirement par 40% se annuity lena zaroori hai. 6% annuity dar par lagbhag:

Kul corpus40% se annuityMahine ki pension60% ek saath
Rs 25 lakhRs 10 lakh~Rs 5,000Rs 15 lakh
Rs 50 lakhRs 20 lakh~Rs 10,000Rs 30 lakh
Rs 1 croreRs 40 lakh~Rs 20,000Rs 60 lakh
Rs 2 croreRs 80 lakh~Rs 40,000Rs 1.2 crore
Yahi wo number hai jo log dekhkar chaunk jaate hain. Rs 1 crore ka corpus sunne me bada lagta hai, par usse mahine ki pension lagbhag Rs 20,000 banti hai — aur wo poori taxable hai. Upar se zyadatar annuity me raqam har saal badhti nahi, jabki mehngai badhti rehti hai. 20 saal baad wahi Rs 20,000 aadhe se kam ki lagegi. Isliye NPS ko akela sahara maan lena theek nahi. Annuity ki dar bima company aur vikalp par badalti hai; "wapas milne wali" annuity me pension kam hoti hai par mool nominee ko mil jaata hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

NPS se kitni pension milti hai?

Ye aapke kul corpus aur annuity dar par nirbhar karta hai. Corpus ka 40% se annuity khareedni hoti hai, jo 6-7% ke aas-paas pension deti hai. Bada corpus = badi pension.

NPS ka poora paisa nikal sakte hain?

60 saal par 60% ekmusht tax-free nikal sakte hain. Baaki 40% se annuity (pension) lena zaroori hai. 60 se pehle sirf kuch sharton me thoda nikalta hai.

NPS me extra tax chhoot kya hai?

80CCD(1B) me ₹50,000 ki extra chhoot, jo sirf NPS ko milti hai — 80C ki ₹1.5 lakh limit ke upar. Ye purane regime me milti hai.

NPS jaldi shuru karna kyun zaroori?

Jitne zyada saal, utna compounding ka fayda. 10 saal jaldi shuru karne se corpus lagbhag dugna ho sakta hai.

NPS ki pension par tax lagta hai?

Ekmusht 60% tax-free hai. Par annuity se milne wali maasik pension aapki income me judti hai aur slab ke hisaab se tax lagta hai.
