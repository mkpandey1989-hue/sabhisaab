Haridwar: Army Bharti (Agniveer) aur Physical Training

Agniveer Bharti ki Prakriya, Physical Test aur Training ke Surakshit Tareeke (Haridwar ke Yuvaon ke liye)

Seedha jawab: Army bharti (Agniveer) ke liye aavedan Join Indian Army ke official portal (joinindianarmy.nic.in) par hota hai: online registration, Common Entrance Exam (CEE), phir physical aur medical test. Haridwar-Roorkee ke ummeedwar apne ARO zone ke anusar bulaye jate hain. Bharti poori tarah muft hai; paise maangne wale dalal se bachein.

Bharti ke aam chaar charan:

  • Online registration (official portal).
  • Common Entrance Exam (CEE) (computer based).
  • Physical Fitness Test aur Physical Measurement Test (rally/centre par).
  • Medical test aur final merit.

Physical test ke aam hisse: 1.6 km ki daud, pull-ups (beam), zig-zag balance aur 9 feet ki khai (ditch) jaise test hote hain; marks aur samay-seema ke group alag ho sakte hain (taaza notification dekhein).

Agniveer scheme ke baare me (jankari ke liye): Seva-avadhi, umra aur yogyata ke niyam samay ke saath badal sakte hain; hamesha taaza notification dekhein.

Haridwar-Roorkee me training ke surakshit tareeke:

  • Subah/sham ko khule maidan, stadium ya surakshit nahar-kinare ki patri par daud; highway par na daudein (durghatna ka khatra).
  • Pehle halki warm-up aur baad me stretching; dheere-dheere stamina badhayein.
  • Paani aur santulit aahar; chot lage to aaram aur doctor.
  • Steroid, "supplement" ya koi bhi pratibandhit padarth na lein; ye medical me pakde jate hain aur swasthya ke liye khatarnak hain.
  • Roorkee me Bengal Engineer Group ke karan sainik mahaul hai, par seva me chayan sirf mehnat aur merit se hota hai.

Savdhani: Bharti me paisa dene ya "setting" ka vada karne wale ke jhaanse me na aayein. Shikayat ke liye 112 ya cyber crime helpline 1930.

Police/Forest Guard bharti: Uttarakhand Police, UKSSSC (Forest Guard, Patwari/Lekhpal, VPDO) ki sooch official portal par aati hai; aavedan sirf wahin se karein.

Chhote Sawal (Quick FAQ)

joinindianarmy.nic.in par.

Haridwar: Scholarship, Hostel aur Coaching Yojana

Scholarship (NSP aur Rajya), Samaj Kalyan Hostel, Mukhyamantri Abhyudaya aur Kanya Yojanayein

Seedha jawab: Students ke liye rashtriya scholarship National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) par milti hain, aur rajya ki Samaj Kalyan Vibhag ki scholarship alag portal/process se. Uttarakhand me competitive exam ke liye "Mukhyamantri Abhyudaya" jaisi coaching yojana aur kanya ke liye Gaura Devi/Nanda Gaura jaisi yojanayein chali hain; shartein badalti hain, isliye official portal dekhein.

Scholarship ke aam steps:

  • National Scholarship Portal (NSP) par registration (Aadhaar, mobile, bank khata).
  • Jati/aay/niwas praman patra taiyar rakhein (Haridwar: E-District / Apuni Sarkar Sevayein).
  • Sahi scholarship chunein (SC/ST/OBC/Minority/Merit-cum-means aadi).
  • Antim tithi se pehle aavedan; sansthan aur jila star par verification.
  • Rakam seedhe DBT (bank khata) me.

Haridwar: Rozgar Karyalay (Sewayojan) aur Career Mela

Jila Sewayojan Karyalay me Panjikaran, National Career Service aur Career Mela

Seedha jawab: Rozgar ke liye Uttarakhand ka Sewayojan Vibhag (Jila Sewayojan Karyalay) aur rashtriya portal National Career Service (ncs.gov.in) uplabdh hain. Panjikaran ke liye Aadhaar, shiksha ke praman aur mool niwas lagte hain. Career mela aur campus drive me SIDCUL jaise audyogik kshetra ki companies aati hain. Naukri dilane ke naam par paise maangna dhokha hai.

Panjikaran ke aam kadam:

  • Sewayojan portal/Jila Sewayojan Karyalay par panjikaran (official portal ka link sthaniya sarkari site se).
  • National Career Service (NCS) par profile banayein aur naukri khojein.
  • Zaroori kagaz: Aadhaar, shiksha ke praman, mool niwas/jati (jahan lagu), photo.
  • Career mela / Rozgar mela ki sooch jila prashasan aur sewayojan vibhag se.

Haridwar me rozgar ke sanket: SIDCUL aur BHEL ke ancillary units, hotel-paryatan, shiksha, health aur sarkari seva (Haridwar: Arthvyavastha: GDP, Per Capita Aay aur Rozgar).

Fraud se bachaav: Sarkari naukri ke liye koi "fees" ya "setting" nahi hoti. "Visa/videsh me naukri" ke naam par bhi thagi hoti hai. Shikayat ke liye cyber crime helpline 1930 aur 112.

National Career Service (ncs.gov.in).

Nahi, ye dhokha hota hai.
