Nainital ki aastha, devta aur itihas: Naina Devi Shakti Peeth se lekar Golu Devta ke ghanton tak, Chand aur Gorkha raaj se lekar angrezon aur azaadi ke andolan tak. Jahan koi katha ya itihas sirf lok-manyata par tika hai, wahan saaf likha hai.

Nainital: Puranon ka Bhugol: Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand

Skanda Puran (Kedarkhand/Manaskhand), Mahabharat aur Kalidas mein Zikr

Skanda Purana ke Manaskhand ko Kumaon ke liye aur Kedarkhand ko Garhwal ke liye jod kar dekha jata hai. Manaskhand me Naini jheel ka “Trishi Sarovar” (Atri, Pulastya, Pulah) ke roop me ullekh maana jata hai.

  • Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand: Puranik parampara me Himalaya ke is kshetra ko do bhaagon me bata gaya hai: Garhwal ke liye Kedarkhand aur Kumaon ke liye Manaskhand (naam Mansarovar ke aas-paas ke kshetra se judta hai).
  • Kurmanchal: Kumaon ka prachin naam “Kurmanchal” maana jata hai. Kaha jata hai ki Vishnu ne Kurma (kachhua) avtar isi kshetra ke ek parvat par liya tha, jise Kurmanchal kaha gaya.
  • Trishi Sarovar: Manaskhand ke anusar teen rishi (Atri, Pulastya aur Pulah) ne paani na milne par yahan sarovar ka nirman kiya (dekhein Nainital Naam Kaise Pada: Naam ka Itihas). Naini jheel ko isi se jod kar dekha jata hai.
  • Naina Devi aur Sati ki katha: Shakti Peeth ki katha Devi Bhagwat aur anya granthon me milti hai, aur Nainital ko 51 Shakti Peethon me ginne ki lok-aastha hai.
  • Mahabharat se jude naam: Pandavon ke vanvas aur Himalaya yatra ki kathayein Uttarakhand ke kai sthanon se jodi jati hain. Bhimtal ka naam Bhima se jodkar dekha jata hai, aur Bhimeshwar Mahadev Mandir isi parampara ka hissa hai (Nainital: Pramukh Mandir, Dham, Gurudwara aur Math). Ye lok-manyata hai, itihas ka pramaan nahi.
  • Kalidas: Kalidas ke Meghdoot aur Kumarasambhava jaise kavya me Himalaya ka varnan milta hai. Unme Nainital naam ka seedha ullekh nahi hai; Himalaya ke kshetra ka saamanya varnan hai.
Shlok aur adhyay kahan dekhein?
Sahi sandarbh ke liye Manaskhand ke pramanik sanskaran, ya Kumaon University / Vedic shodh sansthan ke vidwaanon se salah lein.

Nainital: Pramukh Mandir, Dham, Gurudwara aur Math

Bhawan Shaili, Kapaat Khulne-Band Hone ki Tithi aur Pooja ka Samay

Nainital zile ke mukhya mandir Naina Devi (Nainital), Golu Devta (Ghorakhal), Kainchi Dham (Neem Karoli Baba), Bhimeshwar Mahadev (Bhimtal), Garjiya Devi (Ramnagar) aur Mukteshwar Dham hain. Ye saal bhar khule rehte hain; Char Dham jaisi kapat band hone ki parampara yahan nahi hai.

Mandir ki banawat: Kumaon ke kai purane mandir nagar shaili ke patthar ke shikhar wale hain, jinke upar lakdi ya pathar ka chhatra hota hai. Nainital ke Naina Devi jaise mandir aadhunik ruup me dobara bane hain, isliye unka rup paramparik shikhar shaili se thoda alag hai.

Kapat aur samay: Char Dham ki tarah yahan mandir ke kapat mausam ke hisaab se band nahi hote. Mandiron me subah aur shaam ki aarti ke samay alag hote hain. Peak season aur Navratri me bheed zyada hoti hai. Aarti aur darshan ka sahi samay mandir samiti ya sthaniya prashasan se pooch lein; samay badal sakte hain.

Anya dharmik sthal: Nainital ki Mall Road ke aas-paas Gurudwara, masjid aur church (St. John in the Wilderness jaisa angrezi kaal ka church, jo 1840 ke dashak me bana) saath-saath maujood hain. Ye Nainital ki sahishnu pehchan ka hissa hain. Haldwani me bhi sabhi dharmon ke dharmik sthal hain. Kaun sa sthal kab khulta hai, is ke liye sambandhit samiti se jaankari lein.

Darshan ke samay shishtachaar: Chamda ya chamde ki cheezein na le jayein; sir dhank kar (jahan zaroori ho) jayein; photo lene se pehle puchh lein; aur parisar ko saaf rakhein.

Nainital: Isht Devta, Gram Devta aur Jagar mein Devta Avtaran

Golu Devta, Nanda-Sunanda, Latu Devta, Mahasu Devta, Pokhu Devta, Someshwar, Bhumiyal, Airi, Kalbisht, Haru Heet, Chaumu & Gangnath

Kumaon ke sabse jaane-pehchane lok devta Golu Devta hain, jinhe “Nyay ke devta” kehte hain. Log apni arzi stamp paper ya saade kagaz par likhkar mandir me tangte hain aur manokamna poori hone par ghanti chadhate hain.

Note: Lok devta ke baare me alag-alag ilake me alag kathayein aur naam milte hain. Is table me di gayi jaankari saamanya prachlit manyata par aadharit hai, aur kisi ek katha ko antim satya nahi mana jana chahiye.

Golu Devta ke baare me: Alag-alag kathayein milti hain. Ek katha ke anusar wo Chand raja Baz Bahadur (1638-78) ki sena ke ek veer senapati the, jinki yaad me mandir bana. Doosri manyata unhe Katyuri raja ka putra batati hai. Lok-aastha me wo Shiv ke ek roop (Gaur Bhairav) bhi maane jate hain. Ghorakhal ka mandir Sainik School ke paas ek unchi pahadi par hai, aur wahan ghanto ki bhaari sankhya dikhti hai. Tarikh aur nirmaata ke baare me sahi pramaan nahi hai.

Ghorakhal Golu Devta Mandir kaise jayein?
Nainital se lagbhag 12 se 15 km, Bhowali se kuch km. Kathgodam railway station lagbhag 35 se 40 km door hai. Mandir tak thodi chadhai (kuch sau sidhiyan) chadhni padti hai. Yatra se pehle taaza sadak aur mausam ki jaankari lein.

Jagar, Jagariya, Dangariya aur Devta Khel: Kaise Hota Hai

Jagar ek aisi dharmik prakriya hai jisme gayak (Jagariya) hurka aur thali bajakar devta ki gaatha gaata hai, aur maana jata hai ki devta ek vyakti (Dangariya) ke madhyam se “avtarit” hokar bhakton ki baat sunte hain.

  • Jagariya: Gayak jo devta ki katha geet roop me sunata hai, hurka (damru jaisa vadya) aur thali ke saath. Jagar kai raaton tak chal sakta hai.
  • Dangariya (Dangri): Wo vyakti jis par bhakt maanyata ke anusar devta “aate” hain. Wo devta ke roop me bolta hai aur bhakton ke sawaalon ka jawab deta hai.
  • Devta khel / avtaran: Jagar ke beech wo kshan jab devta ka avtaran maana jata hai. Bhakt apni samasya rakhte hain.
  • Kab hota hai: Parivar me shubh kaam ya kasht ke samay, ya gaon ki samriddhi ke liye.

Samajh se dekhein: Jagar ek gehri aastha aur lok-sanskriti ki parampara hai. Kuch log ise manovaigyanik drishti se bhi dekhte hain. Hum sirf yeh batate hain ki parampara kya hai. Agar koi aadmi gambhir bimari ya maansik pareshani me ho, to jagar ke saath-saath doctor ya manochikitsak ki salah bhi leni chahiye.

Savdhani: Jagar ya kisi bhi dharmik vidhi ke naam par paise ya ghatiya ilaaj ki maang karne wale logon se savdhaan rahein.

Nainital: Kuninda aur Katyuri Raajvansh ka Daur

Kuninda Sikke, Katyuri Rajdhaniyan (Kartikeyapur/Joshimath aur Baijnath), Tamrapatra aur Pathar ke Mandir

Kumaon ka prachin itihas Kuninda (lagbhag doosri sadi isa-purv se teesri sadi isvi) aur Katyuri (lagbhag 7vi se 11vi sadi) shasan se jura hai. Wikipedia jaise sroton ke anusar Katyuri raajaon ka prabhav Kumaon ke Bhabar kshetra tak bhi tha.

  • Kuninda: Kumaon-Garhwal ke prachin janapad. Inke sikke (jinme Amoghbhuti naam ke raja ke sikke mashhoor hain) Uttarakhand aur aas-paas ke kshetron me mile hain.
  • Katyuri: Pehle Joshimath (Chamoli) aur baad me Baijnath (Bageshwar, prachin naam Kartikeyapura) se shasan karne wale. Inhone Kumaon-Garhwal ke bade bhoo-bhag par shasan kiya.
  • Tamrapatra (taambe ke abhilekh): Pandukeshwar (Chamoli) aur Bageshwar jaise sthano se mile taambe ke patra Katyuri kaal ki jaankari dete hain.
  • Mandir nirman: Jageshwar (Almora) aur Baijnath ke patthar ke mandir Katyuri-pashchat ke kaal ki mandir kala ke udaharan maane jate hain.
  • Panchal aur Kushan: Wikipedia ke anusar Terai ka dakshini bhaag Vedic kaal me Panchal kshetra tha, aur pehli sadi isvi se kuch samay ke liye Kushan samrajya ka hissa raha. Guptakaal me Samudragupta ke vijay ka bhi ullekh milta hai.

Nainital ke sandarbh me: Nainital shahar ka apna prachin itihas lambe samay tak sthaniya Kumaoni parampara (jaise Trishi Sarovar aur Naina Devi ki katha) me simit raha; Katyuri ya Kuninda ke seedhe pramaan Nainital shahar se jude nahi dikhte. Nainital zile ke Bhabar-Terai ke bhaag (Kaladhungi, Haldwani, Ramnagar) Chand kaal se pehle ki shasan paramparaon se bhi jude the.

Nainital: Panwar (Garhwal) & Chand (Kumaon) Kingdoms

Pargana, Garhwal-Kumaon ke Yuddh aur Mandiron ki Goonth Zameen

Kumaon par 10vi sadi ke aas-paas se Champawat ke Chand raajaon ka rajya raha, jo 1790 me Gorkhaon ke aane tak chala. Chand raajaon ki rajdhani pehle Champawat aur baad me Almora (1568 ke aas-paas) rahi. Garhwal ke Panwar raajaon se unke kai yuddh hue.

  • Chand kaal: Chand raajaon ne Kumaon ko ek majboot raajya banaya. Wikipedia ke anusar Chand raja aam taur par apne dakshin ke samrajyon se dosti rakhte the, par kabhi seedhe unke adheen nahi rahe.
  • Baz Bahadur Chand (1638 se 1678): Chand vansh ke prabhavshali raja. Unhone 1655 ke aas-paas Mughal badshah Shahjahan ke saath milkar Garhwal par chadhai ki aur Terai kshetra par bhi apna adhikar badhaya. Kai sthaniya manyatayein Bhimeshwar Mahadev (Bhimtal) aur Golu Devta (Ghorakhal) ke nirman ya jeernodhhar ko unse jodti hain; ye sthaniya parampara hai.
  • Pargana: Chand aur baad ke kaal me Kumaon pargana me banta tha. Nainital aur Bhimtal ka kshetra aam taur par Chhakhata pargana se jodkar dekha jata hai (is naam ki vistrit pushti ke liye Atkinson ke Himalayan Gazetteer ya zila gazetteer dekhein). Kota (Kotabagh) aur Rau-Chaubhainsi jaise naam bhi purane kshetriya naam rahe hain.
  • Garhwal ke 52 garh: “52 garh” Garhwal ke chhote-chhote kile hain; Nainital zile me inki baat nahi hoti. Kumaon me purane kile aur kot (jaise Almora ka Khagmara kila) Chand kaal ke pratik hain.
  • Gunth bhumi: Raajaon aur chhote shaasakon dwara mandiron aur brahmanon ko diya gaya kar-mukt zameen ka dan “gunth” kehlata tha. Aaj bhi kai mandiron ke naam par aisi zameen ke record revenue vibhag me mil sakte hain.

Note: Chand kaal ke tithi-vaar aur naam alag-alag itihaskaron me thode alag milte hain. Hum yahan sarvajanik sroton se mel khate hue pramukh tathya hi dete hain.

Nainital: Gorkha Raaj (Gorkhyani) aur Angrez-Nepal Yuddh

Gorkha Raaj (1790-1815), Bhaari Kar, Yuddh aur Sugauli Sandhi

Nepal ke Gorkhaon ne 1790 me Kumaon (Nainital zile samet) par kabza kiya aur lagbhag 24 saal tak shasan kiya. 1814-16 ke Anglo-Nepalese yuddh ke baad Kumaon British shasan me aaya, aur Sugauli ki sandhi (1815-16) ne is vyavastha ko pakka kiya.

  • Gorkha shasan (Gorkhyani, 1790 se 1815): Kumaon me ise Gorkhyani kaha jata hai. Is kaal ko kasht aur bhaari kar ke roop me yaad kiya jata hai. Sthaniya smriti me is shasan ko “Gorkhyani” ke naam se kathor shasan mana jata hai.
  • Anglo-Nepalese yuddh: 1814 me British aur Nepal ke beech yuddh chhida. 1815 me Kumaon British ke haath aaya, aur Treaty of Sugauli (December 1815, march 1816 me manjoori) ke baad Nepal ne Kumaon aur Garhwal British ko saunp diye.
  • Nainital zile par asar: Nainital zila (tab Kumaon ka hissa) British nirantran me aaya. Aage ke saalon me Terai-Bhabar ke ilake, Haldwani ki bazaar aur pahadi marg British prashasan ke adheen vikasit hue (Angrezi Kumaon Commissionery aur Hill Station Nainital).
  • Yuddh sthal: Is yuddh ke mukhya sthal Almora kshetra aur uske aas-paas ke qile the.
  • Shesh asar: Gorkhaon ke samay ki bhasha ke kuch shabd aur kuch jagahon ke naam aaj bhi Kumaoni boli me milte hain, aur kai parivaron ki kahaniyan us daur ke palayan se judi hain.

Note: Is kaal par kai itihaskaron me matbhed hai, khaaskar Gorkha shasan ki kathorta ki matra par.

Angrezi Kumaon Commissionery aur Hill Station Nainital

Traill, Batten, Beckett aur Pauw ke Bandobast (1815-1896), Van Kanoon, Dak Bungalow aur Ghode ke Raaste

1815 ke baad Kumaon ko British ne ek alag Commissionery ke roop me chalaya. Nainital 1841 ke baad angrezon ka pasandida hill station bana, aur 1882 me Kathgodam tak sadak aur 1884 me Bareilly-Kathgodam railway ne is kshetra ko maidan se jod diya.

  • Nainital ek hill station ke roop me: Raj Bhawan (Government House) ki bhavya Gothic imarat 1900 ke aas-paas bani. St. John in the Wilderness church, Mall Road aur clubs angrezi kaal ki yaad dilate hain.
  • Rail aur sadak: Haldwani ki bazaar aur Kathgodam ka railhead isi kaal me bade huye. Kathgodam aaj bhi Kumaon ke pahadi zilon ka mukhya railhead hai.
  • Dak bungalow aur paidal raste: Angrezi kaal ke Dak Bungalow aur Forest Rest House jungle aur pahadi raston par yatriyon aur adhikariyon ke thaharne ke liye bane the. Purane paidal raste aaj bhi gaon aur trek ke liye istemal hote hain (Nainital: Pahadi Darre).

Nainital: Azaadi ki Ladai, Kuli Begar aur Rajya Andolan

Kuli Begar Ant (1921), Van Satyagrah, Peshawar Kand, Chipko aur 1994 ka Uttarakhand Andolan

Nainital zile ne 1857 me Haldwani par rohilkhand ke vidrohiyon ke kabze, Kumaon Parishad (1916), Coolie Begar virodh (1921) aur 1990 ke dashak ke Uttarakhand andolan, sabhi ki yaad dekhi hai. Chipko, Tilari aur Peshawar Kand mukhya roop se Garhwal ki ghatnayein hain.

Rohilla vidrohiyon ki saza: Wikipedia ke anusar 1857 me Haldwani par hamle ke aaropi Rohillaon ko British ne Nainital ke “Phansi Gadhera” me phansi di thi. Is naam ki jagah aaj bhi Nainital me jaani jati hai.

Govind Ballabh Pant: Almora ke Khoont gaon me janme Pant ne Kumaon Parishad ke gathan me bhumika nibhayi aur baad me Uttar Pradesh ke pehle Mukhyamantri bane. Unhe 1957 me Bharat Ratna mila. Unke naam par Pantnagar vishwavidyalaya (Udham Singh Nagar) jaana jata hai.

Zile ke shaheed aur andolankari: Nainital zile se jude 1994 ke andolan ke shaheedon aur swatantrata senaniyon ki poori aur pramanik soochi Zila Prashasan, Uttarakhand Rajya Andolankari Samiti ya sarvajanik abhilekhon se hi di ja sakti hai.

Nainital: Lok-Kathayein: Achhari, Veer Yoddha aur Rahasyamay Kisse

Achhari-Matri, Veer Yoddha aur Rahasyamay Lok-Kathayein

Kumaon aur Garhwal ki lok-kathayein Achhari (pahadi pariyan), Malushahi-Rajula ki prem katha, Madho Singh Bhandari ke balidan aur Tilu Rauteli jaisi veeranganaon se bhari hain. Nainital kshetra ki apni sabse jaani-maani kahani Jim Corbett ke aadmkhor sheron ki hai.

  • Achhari / Aanchri: Lok-manyata me unche pahadi jungle aur charagahon ki pariyan, jo sundar striyon ke roop me maani jati hain aur kabhi-kabhi laal-peele kapde pehenne walon ko apni taraf kheenchti hain. Ye lok-katha hai; isko khatre ya andhvishwas ke roop me prachaar karna theek nahi.
  • Malushahi-Rajula: Kumaon ke Johar (Shauka) kshetra ki prem katha, jo lambi gayan parampara me gaayi jati hai (Nainital: Lok-Sangeet, Lok-Gaathayein aur Gayan Parampara). Ye Pithoragarh kshetra se juri hai.
  • Madho Singh Bhandari: 17vi sadi ke Garhwal ke veer, jinke naam par Maletha ki nahar (guhl) ki katha jaani jati hai. Ye Garhwal ki katha hai, Nainital ki nahi.
  • Tilu Rauteli: Garhwal ki veerangana, jinki saat yuddh ki kahani lok-geeton me gaayi jati hai. Garhwal ki katha.
  • Rami Baurani: Kumaon-Garhwal ki lok-katha jisme ek rani ki dukh-bhari kahani sunayi jati hai. Alag-alag ilake me iska roop badal jata hai.

Nainital ki apni kahani: Jim Corbett aur Kumaon ke aadmkhor

  • Corbett aur Kumaon: Jim Corbett (1875 se 1955) ne Kumaon ke Champawat, Rudraprayag aur Kumaon ke aas-paas ke aadmkhor sheron aur teendue ka shikaar kiya. Unki 1944 ki kitaab “Man-Eaters of Kumaon” ne unhe vishwa me mashhoor kiya. Sarvajanik sroton ke anusar Champawat ki bagh ko 436 logon ki maut ke liye zimmedar maana gaya.
  • Gurney House, Nainital: Corbett ka grishm ka ghar, jo Nainital me hai. Sardiyon me wo Kaladhungi ke paas Chhoti Haldwani me rehte the, jahan aaj Corbett Museum hai.
  • Sanrakshak ke roop me Corbett: Baad me Corbett shikaar chhodkar vanyajeevon ke sanrakshan ke samarthak bane. Isi liye Bharat ke sabse purane rashtriya udyan ko unke naam par Jim Corbett National Park kaha gaya (Nainital: National Park, Wildlife Sanctuary aur Sanrakshit Kshetra).

Nainital se jude anya lok-vishwaas: Naini jheel ki utpatti (Trishi Sarovar aur Sati ki aankh ki katha), Naina Devi ke chamatkar aur Golu Devta ke nyay ki kathayein yahan ki aastha ka hissa hain.

Note: Dharmik aastha aur lok-kathayein alag-alag parivaron aur ilakon me alag hoti hain. Ye page sarvajanik sroton par aadharit hai. Itihas ke dawe ki pushti ke liye mool sroton (Gazetteer, ASI, Zila Prashasan) ko dekhein.
