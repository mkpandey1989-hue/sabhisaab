EPF yaani Employee Provident Fund, naukri karne walon ki retirement bachat ki sabse badi yojana hai. Har mahine aapki salary se thoda paisa katta hai, utna hi (lagbhag) company daalti hai, aur us par sarkar byaj deti hai. Ye paisa retirement tak jama hoke bada fund ban jaata hai. Contribution kaise hota

Aam taur par employee apni basic salary ka 12% EPF me daalta hai, aur company bhi utna hi daalti hai (kuch hissa pension me jaata). Dono milke har mahine account me jama hote hain, aur us par saalana byaj (jaise ~8.15%) milta hai.

Compounding ka fayda

EPF ki sabse badi taakat compounding hai — byaj har saal judta hai aur agle saal bade amount par phir byaj milta. 20-30 saal ki naukri me ye chhota-chhota paisa bahut bada fund ban jaata hai.

Misaal: Agar har mahine (dono taraf milake) ₹3,600, 20 saal, ~8.15% par jama ho, to corpus lagbhag ₹21-22 lakh tak ho sakta (anuman — salary badhne pe aur zyada).

EPF ke fayde

  • Retirement ke liye majboot, surakshit bachat
  • Tax me chhoot (80C)
  • Byaj + maturity aam taur par tax-free (shart ke saath)
  • Zaroorat par kuch nikaasi (ghar, shaadi, ilaaj) shart ke saath

FAQ

EPF aur PPF me farak?

EPF naukri walon ke liye (salary se katta), PPF koi bhi khol sakta. Dono retirement bachat.

Job badalne pe EPF ka kya?

UAN number se EPF transfer ho jaata — naya nahi kholna padta.

Aapka 12% aur company ka 12% — poora sach

Salary slip me dikhta hai ki basic + DA ka 12% aapse kata aur 12% company ne diya. Par company wala poora 12% aapke PF me nahi jaata. Uska ek bada hissa EPS (Employee Pension Scheme) me chala jaata hai, jo retirement ke baad pension banta hai. Baaki hissa hi PF khaate me judta hai.

Isi wajah se log passbook dekh kar chaunk jaate hain — "mera to zyada katta tha, itna hi kyun hai?" Ab wajah samajh aa gayi hogi.

UAN — sabse zaroori number

UAN (Universal Account Number) aapka sthayi PF number hai. Company badalne par ye nahi badalta. Naye employer ko yahi UAN dena hai — tabhi purana paisa naye khaate se jud paayega.

EPF ka paisa kahan-kahan jaata hai

Basic ka 12% aap dete hain aur 12% company. Par company wala hissa poora EPF me nahi jaata:

Kaun deta haiKitnaKahan jaata hai
AapBasic ka 12%Poora EPF me
CompanyBasic ka 3.67%EPF me
CompanyBasic ka 8.33%Pension (EPS) me
Yahi wo baat hai jo passbook dekhkar log uljh jaate hain — company ka poora hissa EPF me nahi dikhta. Us 8.33% se aage jaakar pension banti hai, aur uski apni seema hoti hai. Do aur cheezein jaan lijiye: naukri badalte waqt PF transfer kijiye, nikaaliye mat — 5 saal se pehle nikaalne par tax lagta hai aur pension ke saal bhi toot jaate hain. Aur UAN ko Aadhaar aur bank se jodkar rakhiye; zyadatar claim isi ek kami se atakte hain. Dar saal me ek baar tay hoti hai, epfindia.gov.in par dekh lijiye.
Aaj hi kar lein: UAN activate hai ya nahi check karein, aur KYC (Aadhaar, PAN, bank khata) poora karwa lein. KYC adhoora hai to claim atak jaata hai — aur ye baat aksar tab pata chalti hai jab paise ki zaroorat sabse zyada hoti hai.

Job badalne par sabse badi galti

Naukri badalne par bahut se log PF nikal lete hain. Ye teen tarah se nuksan karta hai:

  • Compounding toot jaati hai — 30 saal me ye lakho ka farak banta hai
  • 5 saal poore hone se pehle nikala to us par tax lag sakta hai
  • Pension ke saal ginti se nikal jaate hain — EPS pension ke liye 10 saal ki service chahiye hoti hai

Sahi tarika hai transfer. EPFO portal par online form 13 se ye ho jaata hai, aur aajkal kai baar apne aap bhi ho jaata hai agar UAN aur KYC theek hai.

Paisa kab nikal sakte hain

  • Poora: retirement par, ya 2 mahine se zyada berozgaar rehne par
  • Ghar kharidna/banana: kuch saal ki service ke baad, tay limit tak
  • Shaadi ya padhai: apni, bhai-behen ya bachho ki — 7 saal ki service ke baad
  • Ilaaj: apna ya parivaar ka — service ki koi shart nahi

Claim ab poori tarah online hota hai. UAN se login karke form bharein; paisa aam taur par kuch dinon me bank khaate me aa jaata hai.

Balance kaise dekhein

Teen aasaan tarike — epfindia.gov.in par UAN se login, UMANG app, ya registered number se EPFO ke number par missed call. Saal me ek baar passbook zaroor dekh lein aur milaayein ki company ne har mahine paisa jama kiya hai ya nahi. Kabhi-kabhi company kaat kar bhi jama nahi karti — aisa dikhe to turant EPFO me shikayat darj karein.

Naukri badalne par PF ka kya karein

Ye wo jagah hai jahan sabse zyada paisa bekaar me kharaab hota hai. Naukri chhodne par teen raaste hote hain, aur zyadatar log sabse mehnga wala chun lete hain.

1. Transfer karwana (sabse behtar). UAN wahi rehta hai, isliye naye employer ke saath account jodna aasan hai. EPFO portal par online transfer claim (Form 13) daal sakte hain. Isse aapki continuous service bani rehti hai — jo pension (EPS) aur tax dono ke liye zaroori hai.

2. Nikaal lena (sabse mehnga). Chhoti si rakam lagti hai, par wahi rakam 25 saal me kai guna ban sakti thi. Aur agar 5 saal ki continuous service poori nahi hui, to nikaalne par TDS bhi kat jaata hai.

3. Chhod dena. Account chalu rehta hai aur byaj bhi milta rehta hai, par agar aap 3 saal tak kuch nahi karte to account inoperative ho jaata hai. Baad me claim karne me kaagazi jhanjhat badh jaati hai.

Do aam galtiyan

  • UAN ek se zyada hona. Har naye employer ne alag UAN bana diya — aisa kaafi logon ke saath hua hai. EPFO portal par ise merge karwa lein, warna transfer atak jaata hai aur pension ka saal kam gina jaata hai.
  • Nominee update na karna. Shaadi ya bachcha hone ke baad e-nomination update karna zaroori hai. Ye 10 minute ka kaam hai aur parivaar ko baad me mahinon ki dauda-daudi se bachaata hai.

Apna balance dekhne ke liye UMANG app, EPFO member portal, ya missed call/SMS — teenon chalte hain. Saal me ek baar passbook zaroor kholein aur dekhein ki employer ne har mahine ka hissa jama kiya hai ya nahi. Kai baar yahin gadbad milti hai, aur jitni jaldi pakdi jaaye utna aasan hota hai.

Salary ka PF kitna percent katta hai?

Poori salary ka nahi — Basic + DA ka 12%. Utna hi company deti hai, par uska bada hissa pension (EPS) me chala jaata hai.

Naukri badalne par PF ka kya karein?

UAN se transfer karwa lijiye, nikaaliye mat. Nikaalne par compounding tootti hai aur pension ke saal bhi gin-ti me nahi aate. Ye sabse aam aur sabse mehngi galti hai.

EPF me 12% se zyada daal sakte hain?

Haan, use VPF kehte hain aur byaj wahi milta hai jo EPF par. HR ko batana hota hai; koi naya khaata nahi khulta.
