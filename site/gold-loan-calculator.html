Gold loan me kitna milta hai

Bank aapke sone ki poori keemat nahi deta — ek hissa deta hai, jise LTV (Loan to Value) kehte hain. Ye anupaat RBI tay karta hai aur samay-samay par badalta hai. Baaki hissa isliye chhoda jaata hai taaki sone ka daam gire to bhi bank surakshit rahe.

Loan = Sone ki shudh value × LTV%
Shudh value = weight × (carat÷24) × 24k bhaav

Ek misaal

20 gram 22-carat sona, 24k bhaav ₹7,000/gram, LTV 75%. Shudh value = 20 × (22÷24) × 7000 = ~₹1,28,000. Loan = 1,28,000 × 75% = ~₹96,000. Yaani 20 gram sone par lagbhag ₹96,000 tak loan mil sakta hai.

Gold loan ke fayde aur risk

  • Sasta byaj — personal loan se kam (aksar 9-12%), kyunki sona surakshaa me hai.
  • Jaldi milta hai — usi din, kuch ghanton me.
  • Credit score zyada mayne nahi rakhta — sona girvi hai.
  • Risk: EMI na chukane par sona neelaam ho sakta hai — isliye samay par chukana zaroori hai.

Repayment ke tareeke

Gold loan me aksar do vikalp hote hain: normal EMI (har mahine mool+byaj), ya bullet repayment (poora paisa ant me ek saath, beech me sirf byaj). Bullet tab theek hai jab aapko pata ho ki paisa kab aayega (jaise fasal bikne par). Gold aur personal loan me kaunsa sasta, wo yahan dekhein.

Bank, NBFC ya sunar — teeno ka farak

Ek hi gehne par teen jagah teen alag jawab milte hain. Farak sirf byaj ka nahi hota:

  • Bank — byaj sabse kam, aam taur par sabse sasta rasta. Kaagzi kaarwai zyada, paisa milne me ek-do din lag sakte hain. Khaata pehle se ho to kaam aasaan.
  • NBFC (gold loan company) — paisa usi din, kabhi ek ghante me. Byaj bank se zyada. Chhoti rakam aur turant zaroorat ke liye theek.
  • Sthaaniya sunar ya sahukar — koi kaagaz nahi, koi niyam nahi. Byaj sabse zyada, aur agar aap chook gaye to gehna kab bik gaya pata bhi nahi chalta. Isse bachiye — yahi wo jagah hai jahan log gehna hamesha ke liye kho dete hain.

Ek cheez jo teeno jagah alag hoti hai wo hai processing fee aur valuation charge. Byaj dar dekhkar khush ho jaana galti hai — 1% processing fee dedh saal ke chhote loan par lagbhag 0.7% byaj ke barabar padti hai. Poochne layak sawaal ek hi hai: "sab milakar wapsi me kitna dena hoga?"

Sone ka bhaav girne par kya hota hai

Ye wo baat hai jo loan lete waqt koi nahi bataata. Bank aapke gehne ki value par ek tay hissa (LTV) hi loan deta hai. Agar loan ke beech me sone ka bhaav kaafi gir gaya, to aapke loan aur gehne ki value ka anupaat bigad jaata hai — aur bank aapse ya to kuch rakam bharne ko keh sakta hai, ya aur gehna jama karne ko. Isse margin call kehte hain.

Yahi wajah hai ki poori LTV limit tak loan lena samajhdaari nahi hai. Agar 75% tak mil sakta hai, to 60-65% par ruk jaana bhaav girne ki sthiti me aapko saans lene ki jagah deta hai.

Doosri taraf, agar loan chukane me chook ho jaye to bank notice ke baad gehna neelam kar sakta hai. Ye niyam ke andar hi hota hai. Isliye gold loan hamesha usi kaam ke liye lena chahiye jiska wapsi ka rasta pehle se dikh raha ho — fasal katne par, salary aane par, kisi payment ke aane par.

Bahut se log gold loan lekar aur poora byaj bharkar bhi khush rehte hain, kyunki gehna ghar wapas aa jaata hai. Gehna bech dene par wo hamesha ke liye jaata hai, aur usse kahin zyada mehnga padta hai jab dobara banwana pade — making charge ka nuksan alag se. Is nazariye se dekha jaye to gold loan aksar gehna bechne se behtar sauda hai, agar wapsi ki niyat aur rasta dono saaf ho.

10 gram sone par kitna loan — LTV ka poora hisaab

RBI ki seema tay karti hai ki sone ki keemat ka kitna hissa loan me mil sakta hai. Rs 60,000 ke 10 gram par:

LTVLoan milegaBhaav kitna gire to margin call
60%Rs 36,000~33% girne par
65%Rs 39,000~27% girne par
70%Rs 42,000~22% girne par
75% (seema ke paas)Rs 45,000~17% girne par
Poori LTV limit tak loan lena sabse bada khatra hai: sone ka bhaav 15-20% girna aam baat hai. Agar aapne 75% par loan liya hai to bank margin call karta hai — yaani turant paisa jama karo ya aur sona do. Na kar paaye to bank sona neelam kar deta hai. Isliye zaroorat 45,000 ki ho tab bhi 60-65% par hi rukna samajhdari hai. Aur gold loan aksar 3-12 mahine ka hota hai; renew karte waqt processing fee dobara lagti hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Sone par kitna loan milta hai?

Sone ki shudh value ka ek hissa (LTV), jo aam taur par 75% ke aas-paas hota hai. RBI ise tay karta hai aur ye badalta rehta hai.

Gold loan ka byaj kitna hota hai?

Aam taur par 9-12%, personal loan se kam, kyunki sona girvi hota hai. Bank aur scheme se alag hota hai.

EMI na di to sona chala jayega?

Baar-baar EMI chhootne aur notice ke baad bank sona neelaam kar sakta hai. Isliye samay par chukana zaroori hai.

Kitne carat sona chalega?

Aam taur par 18-22 carat gehne. Purity jitni zyada, value aur loan utna zyada. Bank khud purity jaanchta hai.

Sone ka bhaav gir gaya to kya bank aur paisa maangega?

Ho sakta hai. Bank loan aur gehne ki value ka anupaat dekhta hai. Bhaav kaafi girne par kuch rakam bharne ya aur gehna jama karne ko kaha ja sakta hai — isi liye poori limit tak loan na lena behtar hai.

Gold loan lena behtar hai ya gehna bech dena?

Zaroorat kuch mahine ki ho to gold loan aam taur par behtar hai, kyunki gehna wapas aa jaata hai. Bechne par wo hamesha ke liye jaata hai aur dobara banwane par making charge dobara lagta hai.
