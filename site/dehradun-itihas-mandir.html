Dehradun ke bahar ki cheezein (Golu Devta, Nanda Raj Jat, Tilari Kand, Chipko, Katyuri rajdhani, Lakhudiyar rock art) Dehradun ki nahi hain. Unhe Dehradun ka likh kar nahi dikhaya gaya.

Dehradun: Puranon ka Bhugol: Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand

Doon ghati ko Garhwal ke Kedarkhand kshetra se joda jata hai. Lok-manyata me Dehradun ka naata Dronacharya (Dronnagari, Tapkeshwar), Pandavon (Lakhamandal ka Lakshagriha) aur Rishikesh ke Puranik naam se jura hai. Manaskhand Kumaon ke liye hai, Dehradun ke liye nahi.

Dehradun ka Skanda Puran, Mahabharat aur Lok-Manyata mein Zikr

Skanda Purana ke Kedarkhand ko Garhwal ka Puranik bhugol mana jata hai. Dehradun kshetra ke kuch sthan, jaise Rishikesh (jise Puranik paramparaon me “Kubjamraka” kehte hain) aur Dronashram, isi parampara se jode jate hain. Exact shlokon ke bina yeh sirf maanyata ke roop me likhi ja sakti hai.

Jaunsar ke log kaun hain (maanyata): Jaunsar-Bawar ke log khud ko Pandavon se aur Bawar ke log Kauravon se jodte hain (Indigenous Communities: Jaunsari, Buksa, Bhotia me bhi). Yeh lok-manyata hai, aitihasik vanshavali ka praman nahi.

Dehradun ka Dronacharya se kya sambandh hai?
Lok-manyata hai ki Dronacharya ne Doon ki ghati me tap kiya. Tapkeshwar mandir isse jora jata hai. Itihas ka praman nahi.
Lakhamandal ka Mahabharata se kya sambandh?
Sthaniya manyata ke anusar Lakshagriha prasang se juda. Lakshagriha prasang se jodi sthaniya manyata.
Rishikesh ka Puranik naam kya hai?
Parampara me “Kubjamraka” ka zikr hota hai.
Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand me kya fark?
Kedarkhand Garhwal ke liye, Manaskhand Kumaon ke liye.

Dehradun: Pramukh Mandir, Dham, Gurudwara aur Math

Dehradun zile ke pramukh dharmik sthal: Tapkeshwar Mahadev, Santala Devi, Guru Ram Rai Darbar, Mindrolling Monastery, Mahasu Devta (Hanol, Thaina), Lakhamandal Shiva mandir, aur Rishikesh ke Triveni Ghat, Bharat Mandir aur Parmarth Niketan. Char Dham ke kapat Dehradun me nahi, Uttarkashi aur Chamoli me khulte hain.

Dehradun Zile ke Mandir, Gurudwara aur Math (Shaili, Samay aur Yatri Note)

Dehradun ke mandiron me do prakar ke hain: (1) Doon ghati ke shehri mandir (Tapkeshwar, Santala Devi, Daat Kali), aur (2) Jaunsar-Bawar ke prachin Kath-Kuni/Pahadi mandir (Hanol, Thaina, Lakhamandal). Doosre prakar ke kuch mandir ASI ke sanrakshit smarak hain.

Ek bahut zaroori saaf baat (Neelkanth Mahadev): Neelkanth Mahadev mandir Rishikesh ke paas ka lokpriya yatra sthal hai, lekin yeh Pauri Garhwal zile me hai, Dehradun me nahi. Isi tarah Lakshman Jhula aur kuch Rishikesh ke ashram ki seema Tehri/Pauri se judi hai (Rishikesh ke bhoogolik bantwara ki wajah se). Yatri ke liye sab “Rishikesh” hai, par sarkari seema ke liye zila prashasan se confirm karein.

Dehradun in yatra ka pravesh-dwar (transit hub) hai. Tithi ke liye Badrinath-Kedarnath Temple Committee ya Uttarakhand Tourism ki official site dekhein. Dehradun ke mandir aam taur par saal bhar khule rehte hain; darshan ke samay ke liye mandir samiti ya official site se hi confirm karein.

Dehradun: Isht Devta, Gram Devta aur Jagar mein Devta Avtaran

Dehradun ke Jaunsar-Bawar ke mukhya devta Mahasu Devta hain, jo chaar bhaiyon (Botha, Pavasi, Vasik, Chalda) ke roop me poojey jate hain. Golu Devta, Nanda-Sunanda, Latu aur Pokhu jaise devta mukhya roop se Kumaon aur anya Garhwal ilakon ke hain, Dehradun ke Jaunsar-Bawar ke nahi.

Mahasu Devta: Chaar Bhai aur Nyay ke Devta

Mahasu Devta ko “nyay ke devta” mana jata hai. Sthaniya lok-katha ke anusar kirmir naam ke rakshas ke atyachar se logon ko bachane ke liye chaar bhai prakat hue: Botha, Pavasi, Vasik aur Chalda. Ye chaaron milkar “Mahasu Devta” kehlate hain. Mahasu ka matlab “Mahashiva” bhi batate hain (maanyata).

Huna Bhatt ki katha (lok-kathaon me): Lok-katha ke anusar Kashmir ke Huna Bhatt ne Shiva ki aradhana ki, aur khet me jotne se chaar devta prakat hue. Kirmir rakshas ka vadh Chalda Devta ne kiya, aur khanda parvat ki ek chattan par talwar ke nishan dikhaye jane ki baat kahi jati hai.

Mahasu ke mandir:

  • Hanol (Dehradun zila, Tyuni-Mori road par), Thaina, Lakhwar, Lakhsiyar aur Bisoi ke naye mandir ke naam sources me milte hain (Wikipedia/Jaunsar lekh).
  • Lok-manyata: Mahasu ke mandir se jude sone ko mandir ke kshetra se bahar le jana apshagun mana jata hai (Wikipedia ke ek lekh me aisa zikr hai).
  • Do gol patthar: Hanol me do gol patthar jinhe “pavitra mann wala vyakti hi utha sakta hai” aisi maanyata hai.
  • Trophies: Mandir ki deewaron par latki hui “trophies” ka zikr bhi sources me hai, jinke baare me detail ke liye mandir samiti se baat karein.

Mahasu aur nyay: Jaunsar-Bawar me Mahasu ko nyay ka devta maanne se jude kai sthaniya vishwas hain. Kanoon ke hisaab se nyay ka adhikar adalat ke paas hai, aur kisi bhi vivad ke liye adalat ya prashasan ke paas jana chahiye.

Anya devta: Golu Devta (Kumaon ke nyay ke devta), Nanda-Sunanda, Latu Devta (Wan, Chamoli), Pokhu Devta, Someshwar, Bhumiyal, Airi, Haru Heet aadi Dehradun ke Jaunsar-Bawar ke gram-devta nahi hain. Pravasi Kumaoni aur anya Garhwali parivar Dehradun me inhe maante ho sakte hain, par yeh Dehradun ki mool parampara nahi hai.

Jaunsar-Bawar mein Jagra Kaise Hota Hai

Jagra (Jagda) Mahasu Devta ke jagran ka mahotsav hai, jo Hanol aur Thaina jaise mandiron me hota hai. Isme dhol-damau, devta ka avahan aur shraddhaluon ka jamavda hota hai. Hazaron log aate hain.

  • Jagra ka samay: Maanyata hai ki Bhadon (August-September) me; 2026 ki ek media report me Thaina Mahasu mandir ke Jagra utsav me hazaron shraddhaluon ke aane ka zikr hai.
  • Mandir ki visheshta: Thaina ke mandir ke baare me media report ne ise ek vishesh nirman ke roop me likha hai (headline me “Designed by Ants” jaise rochak shabd). Is par pakka vaigyanik/aitihasik praman nahi mila, isliye hum ise sirf “media ka rochak shabd” kehte hain.
  • Jagar aur Dangariya (spirit-medium): Garhwal-Kumaon me Jagar ka matlab devta ya purvaj ki atma ko jagana hota hai, jisme Jagariya (gayak) devta ki gatha gata hai aur Dangariya (madhyam) ke upar devta ke aane ki maanyata hoti hai. Dehradun ke Jaunsar-Bawar ke Jagra me iski bhumika ke vistar ke liye sthaniya jaankar se baat karein.

Yeh log aastha se jura vishay hai. Kisi bhi upchar ya bimari ko jhaad-phoonk ya “devta khel” ke bharose na chhodein aur zaroorat par doctor ke paas jayein.

Jagra kya hai?
Mahasu Devta ke jagran ka mahotsav.
Jagariya kaun hota hai?
Jagar me devta ki gatha gane wala gayak.

Dangariya kaun hota hai? Wo madhyam jisme devta ke aane ki maanyata hai.

Dehradun: Pragaitihasik Kaal, Shila-Chitra aur Shailashray

Dehradun zile ke pramukh pracheen puratatva sthal: Kalsi (Ashoka ka shila-lekh, 3vi sadi BCE) aur Jagatgram-Badhwala (3vi sadi CE ke Ashwamedh yagya ki vediyan). Lakhudiyar, Dungri, Kimni jaise rock shelter Dehradun me nahi hain.

Dehradun ke Prachin Sthal, Pashan Yug ke Auzaar aur Ashwamedh Vedi

Dehradun me prehistory ke koi mashhoor painted rock shelter (jaise Almora ka Lakhudiyar) nahi hain. Is zile ka sabse purana aitihasik praman Kalsi ka Ashoka-kaleen shila-lekh aur Jagatgram ki Ashwamedh vedi hain, jinhe ASI ne sanrakshit kiya hai. Doon ke Siwalik me fossil milte hain, par Dehradun ke liye pakka rock-art/cupule record nahi mila.

Kalsi ka Ashoka shila-lekh (poori jaankari):

  • Kahan: Kalsi, Yamuna aur Tons ke sangam ke paas; Kalsi se lagbhag 3 km; Dehradun se lagbhag 40-50 km (Chakrata marg par).
  • Kisne khoja: Maanyata hai ki 1860 me British forest adhikari Forrest ne khoja; patthar par kai/kachchi parat ki wajah se likhavat dikhti nahi thi, saaf karne par dikhi.
  • Kya hai: Ek bada quartz/quartzite ka patthar, lagbhag 10 ft ooncha, 10 ft lamba aur 8 ft chaudaa. Isme Ashoka ke saare 14 Major Rock Edicts hain. Yeh uttar Bharat ki wahi jagah hai jahan 14 edicts ek saath mile hain.
  • Lipi aur bhasha: Brahmi lipi, Prakrit bhasha.
  • Hathi ki aakriti: Patthar ke ek hisse par ek haathi ki rekha-aakriti bani hai, jiske paas “gajatame” (gajottama, sabse shreshth hathi) likha hai. Vidwan ise Buddha ke prateek se jodte hain.
  • Edict 13: Isme Greek shaasakon (jaise Antiochus, Ptolemy, Antigonus, Magas, Alexander) ke naam hain, jinhe Ashoka ne apne dharm-sandesh ke prapt-karta bataya.
  • Sanrakshan: ASI Dehradun Circle ke anusar notification 23-11-1909 ka hai.

Jagatgram (Badhwala) ka Ashwamedh sthal:

  • Kya mila: ASI ki 1952-54 ki khudai me teen agni-vedi aur anya samagri mili.
  • Eent par likhavat: Teesri sadi ke ant ke Brahmi akshar me Sanskrit likhavat ke anusar Raja Silavarman (alias Pona), jo Yugasaila ke Vrishagana gotra ke the, ne yahan chaar Ashwamedh yagya kiye. Isse yeh sanket milta hai ki 3vi sadi me is pashchimi Madhya Himalaya ko Yugasaila kaha jata tha.

Sthal (Lakhudiyar, Dungri, Kimni, Bankot copper hoard): Ye Almora, Chamoli jaise zilon ke hain. Dehradun ke liye ye nahi hain.

Dehradun: Kuninda aur Katyuri Raajvansh ka Daur

Dehradun ke Kalsi kshetra ko Kuninda (Kulinda) janapad se jodne ki charcha hai. Panini ke ullekh ke aadhar par ek lekh Kalsi ko Kulind janapad ki rajdhani “Kalkut” se jodta hai. Katyuri vansh ka kendra Baijnath aur Joshimath tha, Dehradun nahi.

Doon-Yamuna Ghati mein Kuninda, Yugashail aur Varman Kaal

Kuninda (Kulinda) janapad Yamuna-Satluj ke beech ke pahadi kshetra me tha. Kalsi aur Jagatgram ke praman ise Himalaya ke is pashchimi hisse ki prachin raajneeti se jodte hain. Kuninda ke sikke (Amoghabhuti) Garhwal-Himachal ke kai sthanon par mile hain.

  • Kulinda aur Kalkut: Ek aitihasik lekh ke anusar Panini ke mutabik Kulind janapad ki rajdhani Kalkut thi, jise aaj ka Kalsi mana jata hai. Yeh vidwanon ke beech ka mat hai, siddh tathya nahi.
  • Yugasaila aur Silavarman: Jagatgram ki eenton par Raja Silavarman (Pona) ka naam, jo Vrishagana gotra ka tha, aur uske chaar Ashwamedh yagya ka zikr ek aitihasik sanket hai ki 3vi sadi me is kshetra ka alag raajneetik astitva tha.
  • 12vi se 17vi sadi: Garhwalpost ke ek lekh ke anusar Kalsi 12vi sadi se 17vi sadi ke beech kuch samay raajneetik roop se charcha me rahi.

Katyuri kaal: Katyuri rajya ke kendra Baijnath (Bageshwar) aur Joshimath (Kartikeyapura) the.

Kuninda kaun the?
Yamuna-Satluj ke pahadi kshetra ke prachin janapad.
Kalsi ko aur kya naam se jante the?
Ek mat ke anusar “Kalkut”.
Yugasaila kya tha?
Jagatgram ki eenton me ullekhit kshetra ka prachin naam.

Dehradun: Panwar (Garhwal) & Chand (Kumaon) Kingdoms

Dehradun ki Doon ghati madhyakal me Garhwal ke Panwar rajya ka hissa thi. Chakrata-Jaunsar-Bawar ka kshetra aitihasik roop se Garhwal-Sirmaur ke beech ka seema-kshetra raha. Chand rajya Kumaon ka tha, Dehradun ka nahi.

Garhwal Raajaon ke Samay Doon aur Ram Rai ko Mili Zameen

Maanyata hai ki Garhwal ke Raja (Fateh Shah ke kaal me) ne Guru Ram Rai ko Doon ke kuch gaon daan me diye, jinse Dehradun ki basti ka vikas shuru hua. 1675-76 ke aas-paas ki is ghatna ko Doon ke shehri itihas ki shuruaat mana jata hai.

Dehradun: Gorkha Raaj (Gorkhyani) aur Angrez-Nepal Yuddh

1804 me Khurbura ke yuddh me Garhwal ke Raja Pradyumna Shah ki maut ke baad Doon par Gorkhaon ka kabza hua. 31 October se 30 November 1814 tak Nalapani (Khalanga) ke kile par Anglo-Nepalese yuddh ka pehla badi ladai lada gaya, jisme British General Gillespie maare gaye.

Khurbura (1804), Nalapani-Khalanga (1814) aur Sugauli Sandhi

Gorkha shasan Uttarakhand me lagbhag 1790 se 1815 tak raha, jise “Gorkhyani” ya “Khukri Raj” kaha jata hai. Dehradun ke liye do ladai mahatvapurn hain: 1804 ka Khurbura ka yuddh aur 1814 ka Nalapani-Khalanga ka yuddh.

Nalapani (Khalanga)31 Oct - 30 Nov 1814Khalanga pahadi, Dehradun (Sahastradhara road ke paas)Anglo-Nepalese yuddh ki pehli badi ladai; Gorkha commander Captain Balbhadra Kunwar (Balbhadra Singh Thapa) ne lagbhag 600 sainikon ke saath British ke 3,000+ sainikon ko ek mahine tak roke rakha; British General Rollo Gillespie pehle hi din maare gaye; Gorkha Khalanga kila chhodkar chale gaye; British ki jeet

Nalapani ki ladai ka vivaran:

  • British ki sena Mohand aur Timli pass se Doon me aayi aur 24 October 1814 ko Dehradun me ikatthi hui.
  • 31 October 1814 ko Gillespie ke netritva me British ne Khalanga kile par hamla kiya, aur Gillespie sniper ki goli se pehle hi din maare gaye.
  • Ek mahine ke gher ke baad (30 November 1814) Gorkha kila chhodkar nikal gaye. Kai sources me Balbhadra ke lagbhag 90 sainikon ke saath bach nikalne ka zikr hai.
  • Yuddh me nuksan ke aankde alag-alag sources me alag hain (kuch me British taraf se 690 se zyada maare gaye aur 2100+ ghayal likha hai; doosre me 750 sainik aur 31 afsar).
  • Baad me Balbhadra Thapa Maharaja Ranjit Singh ki sena me shamil hue aur 1823 ki Sikh-Afghan ladai me maare gaye (sources ke anusar).

Khalanga War Memorial: British ne Khalanga me do stambh (obelisk) banwaye: ek Gillespie aur uske sainikon ke liye aur doosra Balbhadra aur Gorkha sainikon ki veerta ke liye. Yeh smarak ab ASI ke sanrakshan me hai, Tibbanala Pani, Sahastradhara Road par. Har saal November ke aakhri hafte me yahan Khalanga Mela lagta hai, jisme Gorkha itihas aur sanskriti ka utsav manaya jata hai (media reports ke anusar yeh 40 se zyada saal purana mela hai) (mela ki tithi aur ginti).

Sugauli ki sandhi: Anglo-Nepalese yuddh ke baad Sugauli ki sandhi (December 1815 me hastakshar, March 1816 me lagu) hui. Isse Nepal ko Garhwal-Kumaon ka kshetra chhodna pada aur Uttarakhand British adheen aaya (Angrezi Daur ka Dehradun: Bandobast, Hill Station, Forest School aur…). Sandhi ke tareekh ke liye ek aur pakka itihas-source dekhein.

Gorkha ka Dehradun se naata: Kai sources ke anusar Amar Singh Thapa ki parajit sena se hi British Gorkha regiments ke pehle battalion banaye gaye (24 April 1816). Is par pakka source dekhne ke baad hi daava karein. Aaj bhi Dehradun me Gorkha parivar aur sena ke Gorkha regiments ka bada kendra hai.

1815 ke baad Doon British shasan me aaya. British kaal me Dehradun ek bade shiksha-vanani aur sainik kendra ke roop me vikasit hua: Mussoorie hill station, Chakrata cantonment, Imperial Forest Research Institute (FRI), Survey of India aur Indian Military Academy yahin bane.

Angrezi Daur ka Dehradun: Bandobast, Hill Station, Forest School aur Sansthan

Dehradun ki British kaaleen pehchan teen cheezon se bani: sainik (cantonment, IMA), vaigyanik (Survey of India, FRI) aur hill-station (Mussoorie, Landour, Chakrata). 1871 se yeh alag zila bana (Dehradun Zila Kab aur Kaise Bana).

Dehradun: Azaadi ki Ladai, Kuli Begar aur Rajya Andolan

Dehradun jail me Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru ko British ne kai baar kaid rakha. 1994 ke Uttarakhand rajya andolan me Mussoorie (2 September) aur Dehradun (3 October) me goli-kand hue. Coolie Begar, Tilari Kand aur Chipko Dehradun ki ghatnayen nahi hain.

Dehradun Jail mein Nehru aur 1994 ka Rajya Andolan

Jawaharlal Nehru ko British ne Dehradun jail me chaar baar rakha (sources ke anusar): 6 June 1932 se 23 August 1933, 8 May se 11 August 1934, 17 November 1940 se 28 February 1941, aur 19 April se 3 December 1941. Purani jail ka “Nehru Ward” ab smarak (memorial) hai; nayi jail Selaqui me hai.

Purani jail ka Nehru Ward ab “Nehru Heritage Centre” ke roop me bhi jana jata hai aur uski dekhbhal ki sthiti par samay-samay par report aati rahi hain.

1994 ka Uttarakhand Rajya Andolan (Dehradun-Mussoorie):

  • 2 September 1994, Mussoorie: Mussoorie goli-kand me kai andolankari shaheed hue (sources me 6 shaheed aur ek police adhikari ki maut ka zikr hai). Mussoorie ke Jhulaghar me Shaheed Smarak Bhawan hai jisme andolan ke shaheedon ki murtiyan hain.
  • 2 October 1994, Rampur Tiraha (Muzaffarnagar): Dilli rally ke liye jaa rahe andolankariyon par goli-kand hua. Dehradun ke Girish Bhadri aur Rishikesh ke Muni ki Reti ke Suryaprakash Thapliyal jaise naam is ghatna ke shaheedon me ginaye jate hain.
  • 3 October 1994, Dehradun: Rampur Tiraha ke virodh me Dehradun me aakrosh hua, aur police goli-kand me kai logon ki maut hui. Ek source ke anusar Dehradun goli-kand me teen log maare gaye (Balwant Singh Sajwan, Rajesh Rawat, Deepak Walia jaise naam diye jate hain).
  • 7 October 1994: Dehradun me ek mahila andolankari ki police hamle me maut ki baat ek source me hai.
  • 15 October 1994: Dehradun me curfew lagaya gaya aur ek andolankari ki maut hui (source ke anusar).
  • Shaheed Sthal, Dehradun: Dehradun ke kachehri ilake me andolan ke shaheedon ki yaad me Shaheed Sthal hai, jahan har saal 2 September aur 2 October ko shraddhanjali di jati hai. 2026 me bhi 2 September ko Mussoorie goli-kand ke shaheedon ko Shaheed Sthal par shraddhanjali di gayi (media report).

Dhyan rakhein: Shaheedon ke naam aur sankhya ke liye hamesha sarkari ya shaheed-suchi ka source dekhein. Hum yahan sirf wahi naam likh rahe hain jo kisi source me aaye hain, aur unhe flag ke saath rakha gaya hai, kyunki alag-alag suchiyon me naam aur sankhya alag milti hain.
