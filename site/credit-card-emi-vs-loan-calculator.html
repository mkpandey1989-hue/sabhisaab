Credit Card EMI Vs Personal Loan — Kaunsa Sasta Padta Hai

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Fridge kharab ho gaya, naya 62 hazaar ka aa gaya, aur card se le liya. Bill aane par app me ek button dikhta hai — EMI me badlein. Ek tap me kaam ho jaata hai. Wahi ek tap sabse mehnga bhi ho sakta hai.

Seedha jawab: chhoti rakam aur 3-6 mahine ke liye card EMI theek hai. Badi rakam aur saal-do saal ke liye personal loan aksar sasta padta hai — kyunki card ke byaj par bhi GST lagta hai, loan ke byaj par nahi.

Neeche rakam, dono ke rate, samay aur fee daaliye. Kaunsa sasta pad raha hai, wo saaf dikh jayega.

Card EMI ka chhupa hua kharch — GST

Ye wo baat hai jo statement dekhne par hi pata chalti hai. Card EMI ke byaj par bhi GST lagta hai, aur processing fee par bhi.

1.5 lakh ki EMI 12 mahine ke liye 16% par — byaj lagbhag 13,300 banta hai. Uspar 18% GST yaani lagbhag 2,400 aur. Ye rakam kisi calculator me nahi dikhti, seedha aapke statement me har mahine judti rehti hai.

Personal loan ke byaj par GST nahi lagta — sirf processing fee par lagta hai. Isi ek antar se kai baar thoda ooncha dikhne wala loan bhi card EMI se sasta nikal aata hai.

Upar wala calculator ye GST ginta hai. Isi wajah se uska jawab card company ke batae number se thoda alag aa sakta hai.

Card EMI kab behtar hai

Card EMI hamesha buri nahi hai. Kuch sthitiyon me wo saaf jeet jaati hai.

  • Rakam chhoti hai — 30-40 hazaar par personal loan ki fee aur mehnat faayde se zyada pad jaati hai.
  • Samay chhota hai — 3 se 6 mahine me chuka dena hai, to byaj waise hi kam banta hai.
  • Brand ka apna offer hai — jahan sach me kam rate ya seedha discount milta hai.
  • Turant chahiye — card EMI ek click me ho jaati hai, loan me kagaz aur din lagte hain.

Iske ulat, badi rakam aur lambe samay par personal loan aksar sasta baithta hai — kyunki rate kam hota hai aur byaj par GST nahi lagta.

Sabse mehnga rasta — revolving credit

Teesra rasta bhi hai, aur wahi sabse mehnga hai — minimum due bharkar baaki chhod dena.

RastaLagbhag saalana bojh
Personal loan11% se 16%
Card EMI13% se 20%, upar se GST
Minimum due bharna36% se 45%, upar se GST
Minimum due bharne par ek aur cheez hoti hai jo aur mehngi hai: aapka interest-free period khatam ho jaata hai. Uske baad har nayi kharidari par pehle din se byaj lagna shuru ho jaata hai. Yaani agle mahine ka doodh aur petrol bhi byaj par chadh jaata hai.

Isliye agar poora bill nahi bhar sakte, to EMI me badal dena revolving se kahin behtar hai. Poori kahani Credit card minimum due ka jaal me likhi hai.

EMI banane se pehle chaar cheezein

Card EMI me kuch chhoti sharten hoti hain jo baad me chubhti hain.

  • Limit block ho jaati hai. EMI ki poori rakam aapki credit limit se ghat jaati hai, jo dheere-dheere hi khaali hoti hai.
  • Pehle band karne par foreclosure charge lagta hai, aur uspar GST bhi.
  • Reward point aksar nahi milte EMI me badle gaye kharch par.
  • Ek EMI chhootne par late fee, byaj aur credit score — teeno par asar padta hai.

Pehla point sabse zyada dikkat deta hai. 1.5 lakh ki EMI banayi aur aapki limit 2 lakh hai, to ab sirf 50,000 bacha. Kisi emergency me card kaam nahi aayega.

Faisla lene ka seedha tarika

Teen sawaal, aur jawab saaf ho jaata hai.

  • Rakam kitni hai? 50,000 se kam — card EMI theek. Isse zyada — dono ka number nikaal kar dekhiye.
  • Kitne samay me chukayenge? 6 mahine tak — card EMI. Saal ya usse zyada — personal loan aksar aage rehta hai.
  • Card ki limit kitni khaali rehni chahiye? Emergency ke liye jagah chhodni ho to loan behtar hai, kyunki wo limit nahi rokta.

Aur ek imaandaar baat — sabse sasta rasta wo hai jisme koi EMI banti hi nahi. Kharch ko ek-do mahine taal kar bachat se karna hamesha dono se aage rehta hai. Ye kehna aasan hai aur karna mushkil, par ginti yahi kehti hai.

Poora card bakaya kab tak khatam hoga, ye Credit Card Payoff Calculator me dekh lijiye.

Teen raste, teen bahut alag bojh

Ek hi 1.5 lakh ka bakaya, teen tarike se chukane par saalana bojh kitna baithta hai — lagbhag.

Teesra stambh sirf ooncha nahi hai — wo ek jaal hai. Minimum due bharte hi aapka interest-free period khatam ho jaata hai, aur uske baad har nayi kharidari par pehle din se byaj lagne lagta hai. Agle mahine ka petrol aur doodh bhi byaj par chadh jaata hai.

Jo galtiyan sabse zyada dikhti hain

Card EMI me dikkat byaj se nahi, in chhoti sharton se aati hai.

  • GST ko ginti me na lena. Byaj par 18% GST alag se judta hai. 12 mahine ki EMI par ye aksar 2,000-2,500 baith jaata hai, aur kisi calculator me nahi dikhta.
  • Limit block hone ka andaza na hona. EMI ki poori rakam credit limit se ghat jaati hai. 2 lakh ki limit par 1.5 lakh ki EMI banayi, to emergency ke liye sirf 50,000 bacha.
  • Reward point ki ummeed. EMI me badle gaye kharch par aksar point milte hi nahi.
  • Jaldi band karne ka mansooba. Foreclosure charge lagta hai, aur uspar bhi GST.
  • Do-teen EMI ek saath chalu rakhna. Har ek chhoti lagti hai, par milkar wo salary ka bada hissa kha jaati hain aur ek mahine ki der sab bigaad deti hai.
Sabse sasta rasta wo hai jisme EMI banti hi nahi. Bada kharch ek-do mahine taal kar bachat se karna dono vikalpon se aage rehta hai. Kehna aasan hai, karna mushkil — par ginti yahi kehti hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Card EMI ke byaj par GST lagta hai?

Haan, byaj aur processing fee dono par. Personal loan ke byaj par GST nahi lagta, sirf fee par lagta hai. Yahi antar aksar faisla badal deta hai.

Chhoti rakam ke liye kya behtar hai?

Aam taur par card EMI, kyunki personal loan ki processing fee aur kagaz ka jhanjhat chhoti rakam par faayde se zyada pad jaata hai.

Card EMI banane par credit limit ghat jaati hai?

Haan. EMI ki poori rakam limit se block ho jaati hai aur EMI bharne ke saath dheere-dheere khaali hoti hai.

Card EMI pehle band kar sakte hain?

Haan, par foreclosure charge lagta hai aur uspar GST bhi. Band karne se pehle ye rakam poochh lena chahiye.

Minimum due bharna EMI se sasta hai?

Nahi, wo sabse mehnga rasta hai. Uspar bojh kaafi ooncha rehta hai aur interest-free period bhi khatam ho jaata hai.
