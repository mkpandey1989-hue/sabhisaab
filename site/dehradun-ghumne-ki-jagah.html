Is hisse mein: Dehradun zile ki Top 20 jagahein (doori, unchai, kitna samay), Mussoorie-Landour, Chakrata-Deoban-Mundali, Kalsi-Lakhamandal, Rishikesh ke rafting stretch (grade ke saath), trek (Dehradun zile ke aur Dehradun se shuru hone wale par doosre zilon ke), winter sports, camping, photography points, 3-5-7-10 din ke itinerary aur 12 mahine ka travel guide.

Kisi bhi travel guide ke liye 5 saaf niyam jo yahan lagu hain: Doori aur samay anumanit hain. Sadak, mausam aur traffic ke hisaab se badalte hain. Chalne se pehle Google Maps par live check karein. Ye har season badalte hain. Jahan ek sources me rate dikha hai wahan “anumanit, sirf andaza” likha gaya hai. Nag Tibba (Tehri), Dhanaulti (Tehri), Kanatal (Tehri), Dodital, Kedarkantha, Har Ki Dun (Uttarkashi) Dehradun se shuru hote hain par Dehradun zile me nahi aate. Rishikesh ke rafting stretch ka bada hissa Tehri Garhwal me hai. Brahmpuri, Shivpuri, Marine Drive, Kaudiyala Rishikesh se upar Tehri Garhwal ki seema me hain, aur raft Rishikesh ke ghat par aakar rukte hain. Yatri ke liye sab “Rishikesh rafting” hai, par sarkari seema ke liye zila prashasan se confirm karein. Chakrata cantonment access-restricted hai. Bharatiya nagrik ke liye Indian ID zaroori hai. Videshi nagrikon ke liye pehle se anumati ki baat kai travel sources me aati hai.

Dehradun: Hill Station aur Weekend par Ghumne ki Jagah

Dehradun zile me hill station ke roop me Mussoorie (aur Landour) aur Chakrata hain. Mussoorie bheed wala “popular” hill station hai, Chakrata shant aur cantonment wala. Dhanaulti aur Kanatal bhi Dehradun se jude jate hain, par Tehri Garhwal me hain.

Mussoorie, Landour ya Chakrata (Bheed ya Shaanti)

Mussoorie sabse zyada bheed wala hai (Mall Road, ropeway, Kempty Falls). Landour Mussoorie ke upar ka shant, purana aur cantonment-wala ilaka hai. Chakrata sabse shant, kam hotel wala aur cantonment ke karan ilaka seemit hai.

Dehradun: Paani ke Khel: Rafting, Kayaking aur Machhli Pakadna

Rishikesh me Ganga par river rafting ke 4 mukhya stretch hain: Brahmpuri (lagbhag 9 km, Grade I-II), Shivpuri (lagbhag 16 km, Grade II-III), Marine Drive (lagbhag 24-26 km, Grade III+) aur Kaudiyala (lagbhag 34-36 km, Grade III-IV). Season mukhya roop se mid-September se June tak; monsoon me rafting band rehti hai.

Rafting ke Hisse, Grade, Mausam aur Suraksha

Rafting ke stretch ki starting points Rishikesh se upar Tehri Garhwal ki seema me padti hain, aur raft Rishikesh ke ghat (Ram Jhula/Nim Beach) par ya uske paas rukti hai. Neeche ki table kai travel/operators sources par aadharit anumanit data hai.

Season (kai sources ke anusar):

  • Rafting season: Mukhya roop se 15 September se 30 June ke beech.
  • Monsoon band: July se mid-September tak (kuch sources me mid-June se). Taaza adhisuchna Uttarakhand Tourism/zila prashasan se dekhein.
  • Sabse lokpriya: September-October (monsoon ke turant baad) aur March-June.
  • December-January: Paani bahut thanda hone se kam log karte hain.

Rate (sirf andaza, 2025-26 ki kuch reports ke anusar): Brahmpuri lagbhag Rs. 600-800, Shivpuri lagbhag Rs. 800-1,200, Marine Drive lagbhag Rs. 1,200-2,000, Kaudiyala lagbhag Rs. 2,000-3,400 prati vyakti. Alag-alag operator aur weekend ke hisaab se bahut farak hota hai, isliye “yahi rate hai” na maanein.

Safety (zaroori):

  • Sirf registered/licensed operator se hi book karein; bahut sasta rate dekh kar na jayein.
  • Life jacket aur helmet pehnna zaroori hai.
  • Tairak na hone par Brahmpuri/Shivpuri jaise chhote stretch chunein.
  • Sharaab ya nashe me rafting na karein.
  • Barish ya paani ka star badhne par rafting na karein; operator ki chetavani maanein.
  • Bacchon aur buzurgon ki umr-seema operator ke niyam ke hisaab se alag hoti hai (kuch operators 14-65 saal ki baat karte hain).

Kayaking aur cliff jumping: Rishikesh me kayaking aur cliff jumping ki sevayen bhi di jati hain (kai sources ke anusar). Yeh sab Tehri ki seema me zyada hote hain. Operator ka registration aur safety record check karein.

Angling (Mahseer fishing): Golden Mahseer aur Snow Trout ke angling ke liye Uttarakhand me permit ki vyavastha hai. Asan Barrage ek Ramsar wetland hai, jahan fishing aur gatividhiyon par niyam hain. Angling ke liye Uttarakhand Fisheries/Forest Dept. se pehle permit lein.

Boating: Mussoorie Lake me pedal boating aur Asan Barrage par seasonal boating ka zikr aata hai (Dehradun: Lakes, Kunds, Tals & Tarns). Taaza sthiti ke liye.

Rishikesh rafting kab hoti hai?
Mid-September se June ke beech; monsoon me band. Mid-Sept se June.

4 mukhya: Brahmpuri, Shivpuri, Marine Drive, Kaudiyala.
Beginner ke liye kaunsa stretch?
Brahmpuri ya Shivpuri.

Rapid ki kathinai (Grade I sabse halka, Grade IV-V kathin).
Kya Rishikesh Dehradun zile me hai?
Haan, shehar Dehradun zile me hai; rafting ki starting points zyadatar Tehri Garhwal me.

Photography ke niyam: Sainik kshetra (Chakrata cantonment, Landour cantonment, IMA, Garhi Cantt) me photography par pratibandh ho sakta hai. Mandir ke andar photography ke niyam alag hote hain. Drone ke liye DGCA ke niyam aur sthaniya anumati zaroori hai.

Shehar me roshni zyada hai; door ke pahadi ilakon me behtar, par official astro-site ka record nahi mila.
