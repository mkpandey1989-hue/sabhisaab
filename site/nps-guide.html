NPS Kya Hai? Retirement, Pension Aur Tax Bachat

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026

Ye sirf jaankari hai — nivesh/tax salah nahi.

NPS yaani National Pension System, sarkar dwara chalayi gayi ek retirement bachat yojana hai. Ismein aap naukri ke dauraan thoda-thoda paisa jama karte hain, jo market-linked funds me lagta hai aur retirement (60 saal) tak ek bada corpus ban jaata hai. Us corpus ka ek hissa aap ekmusht le sakte hain aur baaki se aapko har mahine pension milti hai. Neeche free NPS calculator bhi hai.

NPS kaise kaam karta hai?

Jab aap NPS me har mahine paisa daalte hain, to wo equity aur debt funds ke mix me lagta hai. Lambe samay me compounding ke chalte ye chhoti rakam bada fund ban jaati hai. 60 saal ki umar par:

  • 60% corpus aap ekmusht (lumpsum) nikal sakte hain — ye tax-free hota hai.
  • 40% corpus se ek "annuity" khareedni padti hai, jisse aapko jeevan bhar pension milti hai.
Misaal: Agar aap 30 saal ki umar se har mahine ₹5,000, 60 saal tak, ~10% return par lagate hain, to corpus lagbhag ₹1 crore ke aas-paas ho sakta hai (anuman). Ismein se 60% cash aur 40% se pension banti hai.

NPS me tax ki chhoot

NPS ki sabse badi khaasiyat iski tax bachat hai:

  • Section 80CCD(1): 80C ke andar (₹1.5 lakh limit ka hissa).
  • Section 80CCD(1B): ₹50,000 ki extra chhoot — sirf NPS me milti hai, ye 80C ke ₹1.5 lakh se alag hai.

Yani NPS me nivesh karke aap normal 80C se ₹50,000 zyada tax bacha sakte hain. (Dhyan rahe: ye chhoot mukhya roop se old tax regime me milti hai — apne regime ke hisaab se confirm karein.)

NPS ke fayde aur dhyan dene wali baatein

  • Fayda: Lamba compounding, extra tax chhoot, kam kharch (charges), sarkari regulation.
  • Dhyan: Paisa 60 saal tak lock rehta (kuch shart par partial withdrawal). Return market par depend, guaranteed nahi. Pension par tax lag sakta hai.

Calculator kaise use karein?

  1. Har mahine ki rakam daalein.
  2. Anumanit return %, apni abhi ki umar, aur annuity return daalein.
  3. Result me 60 saal par corpus, lumpsum aur anumanit monthly pension dikhega.

FAQ

NPS aur PPF me kya farak?

PPF poori tarah safe aur fixed byaj deta, tax-free maturity. NPS market-linked hai (zyada return ki sambhavna par risk bhi), aur ismein pension ka element hai. Dono ka apna role — kai log dono rakhte hain.

Kya NPS ka paisa 60 saal se pehle nikal sakte?

Aam taur par nahi, par kuch shart (ghar, ilaaj, bachche ki padhai/shaadi) par partial withdrawal milta hai. Poori nikaasi ki apni shartein hain.

NPS me kaun khol sakta hai?

18 se 70 saal ka koi bhi Bharatiya nagrik NPS account khol sakta hai.

NPS ka paisa kab nikal sakte hain?

Aam taur par 60 saal par. Us waqt ek hissa ek saath milta hai aur bache hue se annuity leni hoti hai. Beech me kuch khaas zarooraton ke liye aanshik nikaasi ki ijaazat hai.

NPS ki pension par tax lagta hai?

Annuity se milne wali pension aapki aay me judti hai aur slab ke hisaab se tax lagta hai. Maturity par milne wale ek-musht hisse ke niyam alag hain — CA se confirm kar lijiye.

Tier 1 aur Tier 2 me farak

NPS ke do khaate hote hain aur log inme confuse ho jaate hain:

  • Tier 1 — asli pension khaata. Ismein lock-in hota hai (aam taur par 60 saal tak), aur isi par tax chhoot milti hai.
  • Tier 2 — ek tarah ka nivesh khaata. Jab chahein paisa nikaal sakte hain, par aam logon ke liye ismein tax chhoot nahi milti.

Tier 2 khulwane ke liye Tier 1 hona zaroori hai.

Paisa kahan lagta hai — aapka control

NPS me aap chun sakte hain ki paisa kis anupat me equity, corporate bond aur government bond me jaaye. Do vikalp hote hain:

  • Active choice — aap khud percentage tay karte hain (equity ki ek adhiktam seema hoti hai)
  • Auto choice — umar ke saath apne aap equity ghatti jaati hai aur surakshit hissa badhta hai

Jo log nivesh me zyada samay nahi de sakte, unke liye auto choice aasaan aur samajhdaari bhara vikalp hai.

60 saal par kya hota hai — sabse zaroori niyam

Ye baat bahut log baad me jaan kar chaunk jaate hain: 60 saal par aap poora paisa nikal nahi sakte.

Kul rakam ka ek hissa (aam taur par 60% tak) ekmusht mil jaata hai, aur bacha hua hissa (kam se kam 40%) annuity me lagana zaroori hota hai — yaani us se aapko jeevan bhar mahine ki pension milegi. Annuity se milne wali pension par tax lagta hai.

Iska matlab NPS bura nahi hai — bas ye samajh lena chahiye ki ye ek pension product hai, ek aam nivesh nahi. Agar aapko 60 ke baad poora paisa apne haath me chahiye, to NPS akela vikalp nahi hona chahiye.

Tax ka fayda

Old Regime me NPS par 80C ke alawa ek atirikt chhoot bhi milti hai, jo ise tax bachane ke liye lokpriya banati hai. Kai company employer contribution bhi deti hai, jispar alag chhoot milti hai. New Regime me vyaktigat chhoot to nahi milti, par employer contribution wali chhoot aam taur par bani rehti hai. Apni sthiti CA se confirm kar lein, kyunki niyam badalte rehte hain.

Kis ke liye theek hai

  • ✅ Jinhe retirement ke liye ek anushasit, kam kharche wala vikalp chahiye
  • ✅ Jo tax bachana chahte hain aur lock-in se pareshaan nahi
  • ✅ Jinke paas EPF hai aur wo uske upar aur jodna chahte hain
  • ❌ Jinhe beech me paise ki zaroorat pad sakti hai
  • ❌ Jo 60 ke baad poora paisa ekmusht chahte hain

NPS ka kharcha (fund management fees) bahut kam hai — ye iska sabse bada, aur sabse kam charcha me rehne wala fayda hai.
