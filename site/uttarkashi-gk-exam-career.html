PYQ Kahan Se Lein

Pichhle saalon ke UKPSC aur UKSSSC ke asli paper in sansthaon ki official websites par milte hain: UKPSC ke liye psc.uk.gov.in aur UKSSSC ke liye sssc.uk.gov.in.

Iske bajaye hum aapko sahi tareeke se PYQ padhna sikha rahe hain:

1.Official paper download karein: Aayog ki website par "Previous Papers" ya "Question Papers" section dekhein.

2.Sawal ko patterns me dekhein: Kaun se vishay (geography, itihas, arthvyavastha, culture) se kitne sawal aate hain.

3.Uttarkashi ke tathya ko pehle Uttarakhand-level se jodein: Exam me aksar poore Uttarakhand par sawal aate hain, aur kisi ek zile se kam. Isliye zile ke tathya ko rajya ke dhaanche me jodkar padhein.

4.Sirf kitab ya coaching ke "guess paper" par nirbhar na rahein.

Paper Leak Ka Sandarbh (Taaki Aap Satark Rahein)

Uttarakhand ki bharti pariksha pichhle saalon me paper leak ke vivadon se guzri hai. Reports ke anusaar 21 September 2025 ko UKSSSC ki snatak-stareeya pariksha (jisme Patwari, Lekhpal aur anya pad the, kai reports me 416 pad ka zikr) ek paper leak ke aarop ke baad aayog ne radd ki, aur pariksha dobara karne ka vaada kiya. Baad me is maamle me jaanch aur aarop-patra ki khabrein aayi. Isse pehle 2022-23 me bhi kuch bharti pariksha ke paper leak ke maamle samne aaye the.

Is ka aapke liye matlab:

1.Taaza sthiti sirf official website se dekhein: Re-exam ki tareekh aur nayi bharti ki jaankari sssc.uk.gov.in aur psc.uk.gov.in par aati hai.

2.Kisi se "paper" ya "seat" ka sauda na karein: Ye apradh hai aur dhokha bhi.

3.Afwah par yakeen na karein: Telegram ya WhatsApp group ke "leaked paper" ke daavon se bachein.

Hum sirf is liye likh rahe hain ki aap ki padhai surakshit raaste par rahe.

Uttarkashi: Agniveer Aur Fauj Ki Taiyari

Army Bharti Ke Liye Taiyari

Pahad ke bahut se yuva Army aur ardh-sainik bal me jaane ka sapna dekhte hain. Agniveer jaise bharti ke liye official jaankari Indian Army ki website joinindianarmy.nic.in par milti hai.

Aap joinindianarmy.nic.in par apna zila chunkar apna ARO aur rally ki taareekh dekhein. Rally aur online registration ke niyam har saal badalte hain.

Taiyari ke liye aam salah (surakshit tareeke se):

1.Dheere-dheere badhayein: Daud ke liye ek din me zyada mehnat ki jagah dheere-dheere doori aur gati badhayein, taaki chot na lage.

2.Pahad ka fayda: Pahadi dhalaan par chalna aur daudna sehat ke liye achha hota hai, par dhalaan par girne aur chot ka dhyan rakhein.

3.Halka stretching daud se pehle aur baad me karein.

4.Khaana aur neend: Poshtik khana, paani aur 7-8 ghante ki neend zaroori hai.

5.Sadak par daudne me savdhani: Pahadi sadak par gaadiyon ka dhyan rakhein, subah ke andhere me reflective kapde pehnein, aur pahad ki sadak ke kinare khadi dhalaan se door rahein.

6.Medical aur padhai ka dhyan: Bharti me likhit pariksha aur medical bhi hota hai. Official notification me di gayi sharten dhyan se padhein.

Rozgar ke liye aam salah:

1.Sarkari naukri ke alawa nijee kshetra, paryatan (guide, homestay, trek operator, driver), kheti-bagwani aur swarozgar ke vikalp bhi dekhein (Uttarkashi: Sarkari Yojnayein, ODOP Aur Startup , Uttarkashi: Homestay Registration Aur Paryatan Subsidy).

2.Swarozgar ke liye Mukhyamantri Swarozgar Yojana (rajya) jaisi yojnayein hain. Ek third-party suchi ke anusaar isme 25% subsidy ka zikr aaya hai (jisme adhikatam seema hai). Taaza jaankari msy.uk.gov.in par dekhein.

3.Fake naukri ke vigyapan se bachein: Koi bhi sachchi bharti aap se "registration fee" ya "seat ke paise" nahi maangti.

4.Online CV / profile banana seekhein. CSC kendra isme madad kar sakte hain.
