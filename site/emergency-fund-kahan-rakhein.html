Emergency Fund Kahan Rakhein Ki Turant Mile?

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
Chhota jawabSeedha jawab: emergency fund ko aisi jagah rakhein jahan se turant nikal sake aur nuksan na ho — thoda savings account me, baaki liquid fund ya short FD me. Ise SIP/share bazaar me mat rakhein (waha giravat me phas jaata hai) aur poora cash ghar par bhi mat rakhein.

Emergency fund hota kya hai

Emergency fund wo paisa hai jo aap sirf musibat ke liye alag rakhte hain — jaise naukri chali jaye, koi bimari, ya achanak bada kharcha. Iska ek hi kaam hai: zaroorat par turant milna, taaki aapko loan lena ya nivesh todna na pade.

Emergency fund = 3 se 6 mahine ke kharch jitna paisa
(EMI, kiraya, khaana, bill sab jodkar)

Kitna rakhna chahiye

Aam salah — apne 3 se 6 mahine ke kul kharch jitna. Agar aapki naukri sthir hai to 3 mahine kaafi; agar income ऊपर-neeche hoti hai (business, freelance) to 6 mahine ya zyada. Ismein EMI, kiraya, ration, bill, school fees — sab jodein. Apna kharcha nikalne ke liye 50/30/20 budget kaam aata hai.

Kahan rakhein — sahi jagah

Emergency fund ko ek jagah nahi, do-teen hisson me baant kar rakhna sabse achha:

  • Ek hissa bank savings account me — turant nikal sake, ATM/UPI se. 1-2 mahine ka kharch itna.
  • Ek hissa liquid mutual fund me — 1-2 din me paisa aa jaata hai, aur savings se thoda zyada byaj milta hai.
  • Ek hissa short-term FD me — thoda zyada byaj, zaroorat par tod sakte hain (chhoti penalty ke saath).

Is tarah paisa turant bhi milta hai aur thoda byaj bhi kamaata hai — dono fayde. FD ka hisaab FD Calculator me dekh sakte hain.

Kahan NAHI rakhein

  • Share bazaar / equity SIP me — yahan emergency ke waqt agar market gira ho to nuksan me bechna padega. Emergency fund ka kaam badhna nahi, surakshit rehna hai.
  • Poora cash ghar par — chori/kho jaane ka risk, aur mehngai se keemat ghatti hai. Thoda cash (kuch hazaar) theek hai, poora nahi.
  • Lambe lock-in wali jagah — PPF, lambi FD, insurance — jahan se turant nahi nikalta.
  • Kisi ko udhaar dekar — "zaroorat par le lunga" bharosa emergency me kaam nahi aata.

Emergency fund kaise banayein

Agar abhi fund nahi hai to ghabrayein mat — thoda-thoda karke banayein. Har mahine salary ka ek chhota hissa (jaise 10%) alag account me daal dein, jab tak 3-6 mahine ka na ho jaye. Ise "kharch ke baad jo bache" par mat chhodein — pehle nikaal kar alag rakhein, phir baaki kharch karein. Ye aadat hi fund bana deti hai.

Emergency fund aur bima alag hain

Emergency fund chhoti-badi zaroorton ke liye hai. Par bade jhatke — jaise hospital ka bada bill ya kamane wale ki mrityu — ke liye bima chahiye, emergency fund nahi. Health insurance aur term plan emergency fund ki jagah nahi lete, balki uske saath chahiye. Term vs endowment ka farak yahan hai.

Ek baar emergency fund ban gaya, to aap baaki paisa bina dar ke lambe samay ke nivesh (SIP, PPF) me laga sakte hain — kyunki aapko pata hai musibat me ye fund hai. Isi liye emergency fund har achhi financial planning ka pehla kadam hai, aakhri nahi.

Zaroori: ye lekh aam jaankari ke liye hai. Apni sthiti ke hisaab se faisla karein, aur zaroorat ho to advisor se baat karein.

Emergency fund ka istemaal kab karein

Emergency fund banane jitna zaroori hai use sahi jagah istemaal karna. Ye sirf asli emergency ke liye hai — naukri chali gayi, hospital ka bill, ghar ki koi zaroori marammat, ya achanak koi bada zaroori kharch. Ye "sale lagi hai", "naya phone", ya "ghoomne jaana hai" ke liye nahi hai.

Aur jab kabhi emergency fund se paisa nikaalein, to use dobara bharna sabse zaroori hai. Emergency khatam hone ke baad, agle kuch mahine thoda-thoda daal kar fund ko wapas poora karein. Warna agli musibat me aap phir se bina surakshaa ke ho jaayenge.

Ek aur baat — emergency fund ko samay-samay par badhaayein. Jaise-jaise aapka kharcha badhta hai (nayi EMI, badhta parivaar), waise-waise 3-6 mahine ka hisaab bhi badhta hai. Saal me ek baar dekh lein ki aapka fund abhi bhi 3-6 mahine ke kharch jitna hai ya nahi. Ye chhoti si aadat aapko bade karzon aur tanaav se bachati hai.

Kitne hisse me baantein — ek saral tareeka

Maan lijiye aapko 6 mahine ke kharch jitna fund chahiye. Use aise baant sakte hain: 1 mahine ka bank savings account me (ekdam turant, ATM/UPI se), 2-3 mahine ka liquid fund me (1-2 din me aa jaata hai, thoda byaj), aur baaki 2-3 mahine ka short-term FD me (thoda zyada byaj, zaroorat par tod sakte hain). Is tarah aapka paisa alag-alag jagah surakshit rehta hai aur zaroorat ke hisaab se turant ya 1-2 din me mil jaata hai.

Ye baantna isliye zaroori hai kyunki har emergency ek jaisi nahi hoti. Kuch me turant cash chahiye (gaadi kharaab, chhoti medical), kuch me 1-2 din ka time hota hai (bada bill). Alag-alag jagah rakhne se aap poora paisa ek jagah "phasa" kar nahi rakhte, aur thoda byaj bhi kama lete hain — surakshaa aur samajhdaari dono.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Emergency fund kitna rakhna chahiye?

Aam taur par 3-6 mahine ke kul kharch jitna. Sthir naukri me 3, utaar-chadhav wali income me 6 ya zyada.

Emergency fund kahan rakhein?

Turant nikalne wali jagah — thoda savings account me, baaki liquid fund ya short FD me. Taaki zaroorat par mile aur thoda byaj bhi ho.

Kya emergency fund SIP me rakh sakte hain?

Nahi. Share bazaar/equity me giravat ke waqt nuksan me bechna pad sakta hai. Emergency fund ka kaam surakshit rehna hai, badhna nahi.

Emergency fund aur bima me farak?

Emergency fund chhoti-badi zaroorton ke liye. Bade jhatke (hospital, mrityu) ke liye bima chahiye. Dono alag aur dono zaroori.

Emergency fund kaise banayein?

Har mahine salary ka chhota hissa (jaise 10%) pehle alag rakhein, phir baaki kharch karein. 3-6 mahine ka hone tak jaari rakhein.
