Nali–Mutthi Converter (Uttarakhand)

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~4 minute

Uttarakhand ke pahadi ilaakon me zameen nali aur mutthi me naapi jaati hai. Par registry, loan ya bechne me square feet ya square meter chahiye hota hai. Ye converter nali/mutthi ko turant square feet, square meter aur bigha me badal deta hai.

Nali aur mutthi kya hain

Nali aur mutthi Uttarakhand (Kumaon aur Garhwal) me zameen naapne ki paramparik ikaiyan hain. Ek nali aam taur par lagbhag 2,160 square feet (240 gaj) ke barabar maani jaati hai, aur ek nali me lagbhag 16 mutthi hoti hain. Yaani mutthi nali ka chhota hissa hai — jaise rupaye me paise.

1 Nali ≈ 2,160 sq ft ≈ 240 gaj ≈ 200 m²
1 Nali ≈ 16 Mutthi (kuch ilaakon me 20)

Maap ilaake se badalta hai

Ye sabse zaroori baat hai: nali aur mutthi ka theek maap har ilaake me thoda alag hota hai. Kahin nali 2,160 sq ft hai, kahin thodi kam-zyada. Isliye ye converter ek anumaan deta hai. Registry, bikri ya loan ke liye patwari ya registry ka asli aankda hi antim maanein.

Ye converter kab kaam aata hai

  • Zameen bechte/khareedte waqt — kharidaar aksar square feet ya gaj me poochhta hai.
  • Loan ke liye — bank ko square meter/feet me maap chahiye hota hai.
  • Registry ke kaagaz samajhne me — jab document me square meter likha ho.
  • Tulna karne me — do zameenon ka maap ek ikai me laakar.

Zameen ke kaagaz zaroor dekh lein

Zameen ka maap sahi jaanne ke saath-saath uske kaagaz bhi zaroori hain. Khasra, khatauni aur registry — ye sab kharidne se pehle patwari se milaayein. Poori list zameen ke kaagaz wale lekh me hai. Uttarakhand ke kisan Farmer ID (UKFR) bhi bana lein.

Garhwal aur Kumaon me naap ka farak

Nali-mutthi Uttarakhand ki apni maap ikai hai, aur ye baaki desh me kahin nahi chalti. Isi liye bahar se aaye kharidaar ya bank wale ko samjhaana mushkil ho jaata hai.

Do baatein jaanna zaroori hai:

  • Mutthi, nali ka chhota hissa hai. Aam taur par 16 mutthi milakar ek nali banti hai. Chhote khet aur seedhi dhaar wali zameen mutthi me hi boli jaati hai.
  • Ilaake ke hisaab se farak aata hai. Garhwal aur Kumaon me, balki alag-alag pattiyon me bhi, nali ka naap thoda upar-neeche milta hai. Isliye is converter ka jawab ek achha anuman hai, adaalat ka pramaan nahi.
Jab bhi sauda, batwara, ya bank ka kaam ho — patwari ke record me likha rakba hi maanya hai. Nali-mutthi ka hisaab baat samajhne ke liye hai, kaagaz ke liye khatauni hi chalegi.

Pahadi khet aksar seedhi dhaar me kayi tukdon me hote hain — ek parivaar ki zameen 8-10 alag jagah bikhri ho sakti hai. Aise me har tukde ko alag naapkar jodna hi sahi tareeka hai, ek saath andaza lagane se hisaab bigadta hai.

Nali ka hisaab kahan-kahan kaam aata hai

Sirf zameen bechne-kharidne me nahi — nali ka hisaab kai rozana ke kaamon me lagta hai:

  • Batwara — bhaiyon me zameen baantte waqt "kitni nali kiske hisse" ka hisaab sabse pehla sawaal hota hai.
  • Beej aur khaad — kitni nali me kitna beej lagega, ye poore kharche ka aadhaar hai.
  • Sarkari yojana aur muaawza — kai yojanaon me rakba ke hisaab se laabh milta hai, par form hectare ya square metre maangta hai. Yahi converter us waqt kaam aata hai.
  • Farmer ID aur AgriStack — Uttarakhand Farmer Registry me apni zameen jodte waqt rakba kaagaz wali ikai me bharna hota hai.

Kheti ka laagat-munafa nikaalna ho to Fasal Munafa Calculator me rakba nali me bhar kar seedha hisaab dekh sakte hain. Aur agar square feet me samajhna ho to nali-mutthi se square feet wala poora lekh isi site par hai.

Ek aakhri sujhaav — apni zameen ka rakba nali, hectare aur square metre — teeno me ek baar likh kar rakh lijiye. Jab bhi koi form, bank ya yojana ka kaam aayega, dobara hisaab nahi lagana padega.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

1 nali kitne square feet ki hoti hai?

Aam taur par lagbhag 2,160 square feet (240 gaj). Par ilaake ke hisaab se thoda alag ho sakta hai — patwari ka aankda antim maanein.

1 nali me kitni mutthi hoti hai?

Aam taur par 16 mutthi, kuch ilaakon me 20. Mutthi nali ka chhota hissa hai.

Nali ko bigha me kaise badlein?

Ye ilaake par nirbhar karta hai kyunki pahadi bigha alag hota hai. Registry ka aankda dekhna sabse sahi rehta hai.

Registry me kaunsa maap chalega?

Registry me jo maap (aksar square meter) likha hai wahi kanooni roop se maany hai. Converter sirf samajhne ke liye hai.

Sarkari kaam me nali ka hisaab chalta hai?

Nahi. Sarkari kaagaz aur form hectare ya square metre maangte hain. Nali-mutthi baat-cheet ke liye hai — kaagaz me patwari ke record ka rakba hi maanya hota hai.
