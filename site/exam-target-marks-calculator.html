Exam Target Marks Calculator — Kitne Marks Chahiye

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~5 minute

Chaar paper ho gaye, do bache hain — ab 75% ke liye kitne marks laane honge? Neeche ab tak ke marks, ab tak ka total aur target percentage daaliye. Saaf jawab mil jayega.

Hisaab aur ek misaal

Zaroori kul marks = (Target% ÷ 100) × Poore paper ka total
Aage chahiye = Zaroori kul − Ab tak mile

Misaal: 6 paper, har ek 100 ka. Chaar ho gaye, unme 290 mile. 75% chahiye.

  • Poora total: 600
  • 75% ke liye chahiye: 450
  • Ab tak mile: 290
  • Bache 2 paper me chahiye: 160 (yaani 200 me se)
  • Yaani har paper me lagbhag 80

Ab lakshya saaf hai. "Achha karna hai" ki jagah "80 laane hain" — ye bahut zyada kaam ka lakshya hai, aur padhai ki disha bhi tay kar deta hai.

Jawab mumkin hai ya nahi — ye pehle dekhiye

Kabhi-kabhi hisaab aisa number deta hai jo mumkin hi nahi. Jaise 2 paper (200 marks) me 210 chahiye. Aisa ho to samajh lijiye ki wo target ab nahi ban sakta.

Aisi sthiti me ghabraane ke bajay agla mumkin target nikaaliye. Agar 75% nahi ban raha, to dekhiye ki 70% ke liye kitna chahiye, ya 65% ke liye. Ek mumkin lakshya, ek asambhav lakshya se hazaar guna behtar hai — kyunki uske peeche mehnat karne ka mann banta hai.

Aur ulta bhi dekhiye — kai baar jawab bahut chhota aata hai (jaise 200 me se sirf 90 chahiye). Tab do baatein:

  • Aaram mat kar lijiye. Target percentage sirf ek number hai; zyada marks hamesha kaam aate hain — admission, scholarship aur agli class dono me.
  • Par tanaav bhi mat lijiye. Jaanna ki aap surakshit hain, exam ke dinon me bahut raahat deta hai.

Bache hue dinon me kya kariye

Number nikal gaya, ab uske hisaab se yojana banaiye:

  • Jahan sabse zyada faayda hai wahan samay dijiye. Jis subject me 40 se 70 tak ja sakte hain, wahan mehnat ka phal zyada milega us subject se jahan 85 se 90 karna hai.
  • Purane paper hal kariye. Board exam me sabse zyada asar isi ka hota hai — sawaal ka tarika samajh aa jaata hai aur samay ka abhyaas ho jaata hai.
  • Aasaan number pehle pakdiye — jo definition, formula, diagram aur seedhe sawaal hain. Wo pakke number hain aur kam samay maangte hain.
  • Poora paper likhiye. Chhoda hua sawaal pakka zero hai. Adhoora likha hua bhi kuch number de deta hai.
  • Neend mat chhodiye. Raat bhar jaag kar padhne se agle din paper me dimaag kaam nahi karta — jitna padha wo bhi yaad nahi aata.
Aur ek baat jo bahut zaroori hai — ye ek number hai, aapki keemat nahi. Marks se aage ki rah tay hoti hai, aapka mol nahi. Agar padhai ka tanaav bardaasht se bahar lag raha ho, to ghar me kisi bade se, teacher se, ya kisi bharosemand insaan se baat zaroor kijiye. Akele mat jhelenge.

Aur kaam ke hisaab

  • Best of five — CBSE me percentage aksar sabse achhe 5 subject se banta hai. Uska tarika Marks Percentage Calculator me hai.
  • Attendance — kai jagah 75% haaziri zaroori hoti hai warna exam me baithne nahi milta. Uska hisaab Attendance Calculator me.
  • CGPA se percentage — form bharte waqt ye lagta hai. CGPA to Percentage Calculator dekhiye.
  • NEET aur competitive exam — wahan negative marking hoti hai, isliye hisaab alag hota hai. NEET Marks Calculator me wo alag se hai.
Ek chhoti aadat jo bade kaam ki hai — har paper ke baad apne anumanit marks likh lijiye. Poora natija aane se pehle hi aapko pata rahega ki kahan khade hain, aur agle paper ki yojana usi hisaab se ban jaayegi.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Bache hue paper me kitne marks chahiye, ye kaise nikalta hai?

(Target% ÷ 100) × poore paper ka total, usme se ab tak mile marks ghata dijiye. Jo bache, wo aage laane hain.

Jawab mumkin hi nahi aa raha to?

Iska matlab wo target ab nahi ban sakta. Agla mumkin target nikaaliye — ek mumkin lakshya, asambhav lakshya se kahin behtar hai.

Kis subject par zyada dhyaan dein?

Jahan sudhaar ki sabse zyada gunjaish hai. 40 se 70 tak jaana, 85 se 90 karne se kahin zyada number deta hai.

Best of five ka hisaab isse alag hai?

Haan. Usme sabse kam wala ek subject hata kar 5 se percentage banta hai. Uska hisaab Marks Percentage Calculator me hai.
