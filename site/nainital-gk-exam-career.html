Nainital zile ke students ke liye: pariksha ke liye zile ke 100 pakke tathya, taiyari ka tareeka, bharti ki tayari, skill aur school, scholarship aur rozgar ke sarkari raaste. Har jagah sirf official source ka naam diya gaya hai, taaki purani ya galat jaankari par bharosa na karna pade.

Nainital: UKPSC, PCS aur UKSSSC ke liye 100 Ek-Line GK Tathya

Nainital Zile ke 100 Tathya

Jahan sroton me antar hai wahan “lagbhag” ya “sroton ke anusar” likha hai. Pariksha se pehle official/ NCERT-state books se milaan zaroor karein.

A. Prashasan aur janganana

  • Nainital zile ka mukhyalay Nainital shahar hai (DM ka camp karyalay Haldwani me bhi).
  • Nainital Uttarakhand ke Kumaon mandal me aata hai.
  • Zile ka sabse bada shahar Haldwani (Haldwani-Kathgodam Nagar Nigam) hai, jo “Gateway of Kumaon” kehlata hai.
  • Nainital ko “Lake District of India” kaha jata hai.
  • Census 2011 me zile ki aabadi 9,54,605 thi.
  • Census 2011 me sex ratio 934 tha.
  • Census 2011 me saksharta dar lagbhag 83.9% thi.
  • 2001 se 2011 ke beech zile ki aabadi vriddhi lagbhag 25.1% rahi.
  • Census 2011 me aabadi ghanatva 225 vyakti prati sq. km tha.
  • Zile me SC aabadi 20.02% aur ST aabadi 0.79% thi (Census 2011).
  • Census 2011 me Hindu 84.82%, Muslim 12.65% aur Sikh 1.82% the.
  • Zile me sabse zyada boli jane wali bhasha Kumaoni hai (lagbhag 48.5%), uske baad Hindi.
  • Zile me 8 Vikas Khand hain: Haldwani, Ramnagar, Kotabag, Okhalkanda, Dhari, Ramgarh, Betalghat aur Bhimtal.
  • Zile me tehsil ki sankhya 10 batayi jati hai (kuch sroton me 13 tak).
  • Block-wise jod ke anusar zile me lagbhag 475 Gram Panchayat aur 1,080 gaon hain.
  • Zile me 6 Vidhan Sabha seatein hain: Lalkuan, Bhimtal, Nainital, Haldwani, Kaladhungi aur Ramnagar.
  • Nainital Vidhan Sabha seat SC ke liye aarakshit hai.
  • Nainital zile ki 5 Vidhan Sabha seatein Nainital-Udham Singh Nagar Lok Sabha seat me aati hain; Ramnagar seat Garhwal Lok Sabha seat me.
  • Nainital-Udham Singh Nagar seat se Ajay Bhatt (BJP) 2019 aur 2024 me sansad chune gaye.
  • Haldwani RTO ka code UK-04 aur Ramnagar ARTO ka code UK-19 hai.

  • Naini jheel ko 1841 me P. Barron ne bahari duniya ke saamne rakha.
  • Naina Devi ki murti 1842 me Moti Ram Shah ne sthapit ki (sarvajanik sroton ke anusar).
  • 18 September 1880 ko Alma Hill ke bhuskhalan me 151 log mare gaye.
  • Naina Devi mandir 1880 me nasht hokar 1883 me dobara bana.
  • Nainital zila lagbhag 1891 me prashasnik roop se gathit hua.
  • 30 September 1995 ko Udham Singh Nagar zila Nainital se alag hua.
  • Uttarakhand rajya ka gathan 9 November 2000 ko hua.
  • Uttarakhand High Court Nainital me sthit hai.
  • 1882 me Bareilly se Nainital tak sadak aur 1884 me Kathgodam tak railway ki line aayi.
  • Sir Henry Ramsay 1856 se 1884 tak Kumaon ke Commissioner rahe.
  • 1857 me rohilkhand ke vidrohiyon ne Haldwani ko kuch samay ke liye apne kabze me liya.
  • Rohilla vidrohiyon ko Nainital ke “Phansi Gadhera” me phansi dene ka zikr milta hai.
  • Kumaon Parishad ka gathan 1916 me hua.
  • Coolie Begar ka ant 1921 me Uttarayani mela (Bageshwar) par hua.
  • Nainital Bank ki sthapna 1922 me hui.
  • Van Panchayat ke niyam 1931 me Kumaon me aaye.
  • Chand raja Baz Bahadur ne 1638 se 1678 tak shasan kiya.
  • Gorkhaon ne 1790 se 1815 tak Kumaon par shasan kiya (Gorkhyani).
  • Sugauli ki sandhi December 1815 me hui aur March 1816 me manzoor hui.

  • Naina Devi ko 51 Shakti Peethon me se ek maana jata hai.
  • Golu Devta ko “nyay ke devta” kaha jata hai; Ghorakhal me unka mashhoor mandir hai.
  • Kainchi Dham (Neem Karoli Baba) me har saal 15 June ko mela lagta hai.
  • Garjiya Devi mandir Kosi nadi ke beech chattan par hai; Kartik Purnima par mela lagta hai.
  • Bhimeshwar Mahadev mandir Bhimtal me hai.
  • Aipan kala geru (laal mitti) aur biswar (chaawal ke ghol) se banti hai.
  • Rangwali Pichhora Kumaon ka shubh peele-narangi rang ka dupatta hai.
  • Phool Dei Chaitra Sankranti par, Harela Sawan Sankranti par aur Ghughutiya Makar Sankranti par manaya jata hai.
  • Bhitauli ki parampara me bhai apni behen-beti ko uphar deta hai.

  • Dhol-Damau bajane ki paramparik zimmedari Bajgi (Auji) samuday ki rahi hai.

  • Khurpatal, Sariyatal, Harishtal aur Lokhamtal zile ke anya taal hain.
  • Zile ki mukhya nadiyan Kosi, Gaula, Dabka, Nandhaur aur Baur hain.
  • Kosi aur Gaula barf se nahi, mukhya roop se jharno aur barish se chalti hain.
  • Gaula nadi Kathgodam aur Haldwani se guzarti hai; Chitrashila Ghat uske kinare hai.
  • Garjiya Devi mandir Kosi nadi par hai.
  • Patli Dun aur Kota Dun zile ki dun bhoo-rachnayein hain.
  • Zile me Terai, Bhabar, Shivalik aur Lesser Himalaya ki paatiyan milti hain.
  • Nainital zile me koi glacier nahi hai.
  • Nainital zile me koi bugyal (alpine meadow) nahi hai.
  • Nainital kshetra ko aam taur par bhukamp zone IV me ginte aaye hain.
  • Dakshin-purvi seema par Udham Singh Nagar aur dakshin-pashchim me Uttar Pradesh ka Bijnor zila hai.
  • Nainital zile ki koi antarrashtriya seema nahi hai.

  • Jim Corbett National Park 1936 me Hailey National Park ke roop me sthapit hua, Bharat ka sabse purana rashtriya udyan.

  • Dhikala zone Corbett ka mukhya core zone hai.

  • Jim Corbett ka janm 1875 me Nainital me hua; unki kitaab “Man-Eaters of Kumaon” 1944 ki hai.
  • Corbett ka grishm ka ghar Gurney House Nainital me hai, aur Corbett Museum Kaladhungi (Chhoti Haldwani) me.

Nainital: Coaching, Sarkari Pustakalay aur Reading Room

Self-Study ke Saadhan aur Coaching Chunne ke Niyam

Nainital zile me Haldwani aur Nainital me kai coaching kendra aur library hain. Kumaun University ki library aur sarkari janta pustakalaya padhai ke liye achhe vikalp ho sakte hain.

Coaching chunne ke 8 niyam:

  • Pehle demo class: Poori fees dene se pehle demo class aur teacher ka anubhav dekhein.
  • “100% selection” jaise vaade se bachein: Koi bhi coaching selection ki guarantee nahi de sakti.
  • Fees aur refund: Fees ka poora byora aur refund niti likhit me lein.
  • Purane students se pooch lein: Wahan padhe huye chhatron se baat karein.
  • Batch ka size: Bahut bheed wali class me dhyan nahi milta.
  • Study material: Sahi aur taaza ho; aayog ki official syllabus se mel khata ho.
  • Cost vs vikalp: Aaj muft online saadhan (aayog ki site, sarkari free coaching yojana) bhi hain.
  • Mansik swasthya: Taiyari me tanaav ho to dost, parivar ya Tele-MANAS 14416 se baat karein (Nainital: Puchhyari, Gantua aur Deva-Prashna Parampara).

Library aur self-study:

  • Kumaun University library: Nainital me; vishwavidyalaya ke students ke liye, baahari padhne walon ke liye niyam alag ho sakte hain.
  • Sarkari janta pustakalaya: Kai kasbon me; shulk aur samay sthaniya library se pooch lein.
  • Bookstores aur sasti kitaabein: Haldwani aur Nainital ke bazaar me; purani kitaaben bhi kai baar madadgar hoti hain.
  • Online: Aayog ki website, syllabus aur previous papers sabse bharosemand saadhan hain.

Nainital: Fauj Bharti (Agniveer ARO) aur Physical Training

Army Bharti aur Subah ki Taiyari

Army ki bharti Agnipath yojana (2022 se) ke tahat Agniveer ke roop me hoti hai. Nainital zile ke yuva aam taur par Army Recruiting Office (ARO) Almora jaise nazdeeki karyalay se jude hote hain. Sahi ARO aur bharti ki tareekh ke liye Bharatiya Sena ki official bharti website (joinindianarmy.nic.in) dekhein.

  • Agniveer yojana: Sarvajanik jaankari ke anusar ye 4 saal ki seva ki yojana hai, jisme likhit pariksha, physical test aur medical hota hai. Shartein (umar, shiksha) aur seva ki sharten badal sakti hain; official website par taaza dekhein.
  • Kumaon Regiment aur Kumaoni yuva: Kumaon Regiment ka Regimental Centre Ranikhet me hai (Fauj ki Regiment , ITBP aur SSB Camp). Zile ke bahut se yuva sena aur ardh-sainik balon me jaate hain.
  • Rally ground: Bharti rally ka sthan har saal ARO tay karta hai. Pehle se sthan ki jaankari official notification se lein.

Subah ki daud aur taiyari: surakshit rehne ke 7 niyam (Nainital ke pahadi aur jungle ilake ke liye khaas):

  • Jungle aur sunsan sadak par akele na daudein: Subah ke andhere me guldar jaise janwar sakriya ho sakte hain (Nainital: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh). Toli me daudein, aur raasta bheed-bhaad wala ho.
  • Traffic ka dhyan: Subah ke andhere me sadak par gaadi se chot lagne ka khatra hota hai. Reflective kapde aur torch istemal karein.
  • Garam hone ke baad hi daudein: Pahad ki thand me muscle jam sakte hain; halka warm-up zaroor karein.
  • Dheere-dheere badhayein: Ek hi din me zyada doori na daudein.
  • Khana aur paani: Sahi khana, paani aur neend taiyari ki neev hain.
  • Chot lagne par: Aaram karein aur zaroorat par doctor ko dikhayein.
  • Nashe aur shortcut se bachein: Bharti me nashe ya dawaiyon ka istemal karne wale kai yuva phaste hain. Kisi dalal ke “seat dilane” ke vaade par bharosa na karein; Army ki bharti poori tarah merit par hoti hai.

Nainital: Polytechnic, ITI, Nursing College aur Kaushal Vikas Kendra

Technical Courses aur Admission

Nainital zile me sarkari aur nijee ITI, polytechnic, nursing aur skill kendra hain. Seat aur admission ki prakriya har saal badalti hai. Sahi jaankari Uttarakhand ke Prashikshan aur Technical Shiksha vibhag ki official website par milti hai.

  • ITI (Industrial Training Institute): 1 se 2 saal ke trade course (jaise electrician, fitter, COPA). Admission aam taur par 8vi, 10vi ya 12vi ke baad, merit ya counselling se hota hai. Zile ke sarkari ITI ki suchi aur seat ke liye Uttarakhand ke Prashikshan Vibhag aur DGT ki website dekhein.
  • Polytechnic: 3 saal ke diploma course. Admission rajya ke polytechnic entrance ya counselling ke madhyam se hota hai. Uttarakhand Board of Technical Education (UBTE) ya rajya ke technical shiksha vibhag se jaankari lein.
  • Nursing aur swasthya course: GNM, ANM aur B.Sc Nursing jaise course. Government Medical College Haldwani se jude college aur nijee nursing college hain. Nursing council se manyata zaroor dekhein.
  • Skill Development: Skill India, PMKVY aur NAPS (apprenticeship) jaisi yojanayein hain. Skill India Digital aur Apprenticeship India portal par course aur apprenticeship ke avsar dekhein.
  • Savdhani: Kisi nijee sansthaa ki manyata (AICTE, UBTE, nursing council) aur placement ke daavon ki jaanch karein. “100% placement” jaise vaade par bharosa na karein.

Nainital: Navodaya (JNV), Kendriya Vidyalaya (KV) aur Aawasiya School

Sarkari Awasiya aur Day School

Har zile me ek Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya (JNV) hota hai, jisme kaksha 6 me pravesh ek rashtriya pariksha (JNVST) ke madhyam se hota hai, aur shiksha, rehna aur khana muft hota hai. Nainital zile ke JNV ka pata Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti ki website par dekhein. Sainik School Ghorakhal aur Sherwood jaise sansthaan zile me alag awasiya vikalp hain.

  • JNV: Ye sarkari awasiya schools hain jo gramin pratibhaon ke liye bane hain. Pravesh kaksha 5 ke baad JNVST ke zariye hota hai. Sahi tareekh aur form ki prakriya navodaya.gov.in par dekhein.
  • Kendriya Vidyalaya (KV): Kendra sarkar ke schools; Nainital zile me KV ki list aur pravesh prakriya KVS ki website par milti hai.
  • Sainik School Ghorakhal: Nainital zile me; pravesh sainik school pravesh pariksha (AISSEE) se hota hai (Nainital: Shiksha: University, College, Boarding School aur Sansthan).
  • Sherwood, St. Joseph’s, All Saints’, Birla Vidya Mandir: Nijee awasiya schools; fees aur pravesh sthaniya school ki website par.
  • Sarkari schools: Zile ke kai gaon me sarkari prathmik aur madhyamik school hain; Uttarakhand sarkar ke “Atal Utkrisht Rajkiya Vidyalaya” jaise protsahan yojana ka zikr bhi milta hai. Sahi jaankari ke liye Zila Shiksha Adhikari se pooch lein.

Shiksha ki gunvatta school, teacher aur bachche ki zaroorat par nirbhar karti hai; school ki official jaankari aur result dekhkar faisla karein.

Nainital: Chhatravritti, Hostel aur Coaching Yojanayein

Chhatravritti aur Muft Coaching ke Saadhan

Nainital zile ke students National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) par kendra aur rajya ki kai chhatravritti ke liye aavedan kar sakte hain. Uttarakhand sarkar ki Mukhyamantri Abhyuday jaisi yojana me competitive exams ke liye muft coaching ka praavdhan sarvajanik sroton me bataya gaya hai.

  • National Scholarship Portal (NSP): Kendra aur rajya ki alag-alag chhatravritti (SC, ST, OBC, alpsankhyak aur anya) ke liye ek hi portal par aavedan hota hai. Pehle apne school ya college me pooch lein ki kaunsi chhatravritti lagu hai.
  • Samaj Kalyan Vibhag: SC, ST aur OBC ke liye rajya ki chhatravritti aur hostel yojana. Zile ke Samaj Kalyan Adhikari se jaankari lein.
  • Hostel: Zile aur nazdeeki shehron me sarkari chhatrawas ki suvidha hoti hai. Seat aur prakriya sambandhit vibhag se pooch lein.
  • Muft coaching: Uttarakhand sarkar ki Mukhyamantri Abhyuday jaisi yojana me NEET, JEE, UPSC jaisi pariksha ke liye muft coaching ka praavdhan bataya gaya hai. Eligibility aur chayan ki prakriya sarkari website par dekhein.
  • Savdhani: Chhatravritti ya coaching dilane ke naam par paise maangne wale dalalon se bachein; aavedan hamesha sarkari portal par karein.
  • Aavedan ke tips: Aadhaar-linked bank khata, aay aur jaati praman patra (E-District / Apuni Sarkar: Nainital me Sarkari Praman Patra) pehle se taiyar rakhein; antim tareekh se pehle aavedan karein.

Nainital: Rojgar Karyalay aur Rojgar Mele

Sewayojan Portal par Registration aur Rojgar Mela

Uttarakhand sarkar ke Sewayojan portal par nivasi apna naam panjikrit kar sakte hain aur sarkari ya nijee naukri ki jaankari pa sakte hain. Zile me Zila Sewayojan Karyalay aur kai baar rojgar mela bhi lagte hain. Registration ka sahi tareeka sarkari portal par dekhein.

Registration ke aam step:

  • Uttarakhand Sewayojan portal par jaakar naya panjikaran chunein (portal ka sahi pata Uttarakhand sarkar ki website se pakka karein).
  • Aadhaar-linked mobile number aur email se account banayein.
  • Shiksha, anubhav, kaushal aur pasand ke kshetra ki jaankari bharein.
  • Zaroori dastavez (shaikshanik praman patra, niwas praman patra, photo) ki scan copy taiyar rakhein.
  • Panjikaran ke baad naukri ki soochna aur rojgar mela ki jaankari milti rehti hai.

Rojgar mela aur apprenticeship:

  • Rojgar mela: Zile me kai baar sarkari aur nijee company ek jagah bharti karti hain. Tareekh aur sthan Sewayojan portal, Zila Prashasan aur akhbaar se jaankar lein.
  • Apprenticeship: National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) aur Apprenticeship India portal par prashikshuon (apprentice) ke liye avsar milte hain.
  • Skill: Skill India Digital portal par muft ya sasti skill training ke course milte hain.
  • Nainital ka rozgar ka sach: Zile me paryatan, hotel, homestay, vyapar, sarkari naukri aur Lalkuan-Haldwani ke udyog mukhya rozgar hain (Nainital: Zile ki Arthvyavastha, Aamdani aur Rozi-Roti). Pahadi gaon ke yuvaon ke liye homestay, kheti aur paryatan ke kaam bhi vikalp ban rahe hain (Nainital: Homestay Panjikaran aur Paryatan Subsidy).

Dhokhadhadi se savdhan: “Naukri dilane” ke naam par paise ya security deposit maangne wale dalalon se bachein. Sarkari naukri me selection sirf aayog ya vibhag ki official prakriya se hota hai. Kisi ko paise dene se pehle sarkari website par bharti ki jaankari ki pushti kar lein.

Note: Pariksha, bharti, scholarship aur seat ki jaankari badalti rehti hai. Ye page aam jaankari hai; hamesha sambandhit official website par taaza notification dekhein.
