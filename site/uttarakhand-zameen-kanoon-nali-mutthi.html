Uttarakhand mein Zameen: Nali-Mutthi ka Naap aur Bhoo Kanoon 2025

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: 23 November 2026 · Padhne me ~9 minute

Uttarakhand mein zameen ke naap (nali, mutthi, bisi), khasra-khatauni aur bhoo-kanoon 2025 ke niyam: bahari log kya kharid sakte hain aur kya nahi.

Nali-Mutthi, Zameen Ke Naap Aur Uttarakhand Land Laws

1 Nali kitne square feet ka hota hai?

1 Nali = 2,160 square feet = 240 gaj (square yard) = lagbhag 200.67 square meter. 1 Nali me 16 Mutthi hoti hain, yani 1 Mutthi = 135 square feet = 15 gaj.

Nali Uttarakhand ke pahadi zilon me kheti ki zameen naapne ki sabse prachalit ikai hai. Maana jata hai ki “nali” us zameen ka naap tha jisme ek nali (lagbhag 2 kg ke barabar paramparik anaaj-naap) beej boya ja sake. Isi tarah “mutthi” us zameen ka naap tha jisme ek mutthi beej boya ja sake. Isliye ye ikaiyan sirf lambai-chaudai ka hisaab nahi, balki kheti ki purani soch ko dikhati hain.

Apni zameen ka hisaab turant karne ke liye Sab Hisaab ka Nali-Mutthi Converter aur vistrit guide Nali-Mutthi Kya Hoti Hai (square feet me) dekhein.

Bigha ka naap: ek saavdhani

Bigha ka maan alag-alag rajyon aur yahan tak ki alag-alag kshetron me alag hota hai. Isliye “1 bigha = itne nali” jaisi ek hi sankhya sab jagah sahi nahi hoti.

Uttarakhand ke maidani aur pahadi kshetron me bigha-biswa ka prayog ek jaisa nahi hai, aur purane records me “kachcha” aur “pakka” bigha ka bhi bhed milta hai. Isliye kisi bhi lain-dein se pehle apne tehsil ke lekhpal/revenue record se bigha ka sthaniya maan pushti kar lein. Rajya-wise bigha ki jaankari ke liye Bigha Kitna Hota Hai: Har Rajya aur Bigha/Gaj/Acre Converter dekhein. Bade khet ke naap ke liye Hectare-Bigha-Acre Converter upyogi hai.

Pahadi zameen ke prakar aur revenue shabdavali

Pahadi gaon me zameen ke naam uski sinchai aur dhalaan ke hisaab se alag hote hain. Ye shabd record aur baatcheet dono me milte hain, par har gaon me iska prayog ek jaisa nahi hota.

Khatauni kaise dekhein (Bhulekh)

Uttarakhand me zameen ka record online dekhne ke liye sarkar ka Bhulekh portal (bhulekh.uk.gov.in) istemal hota hai. Wahan zila, tehsil, gaon chunkar khasra number ya khatedar ke naam se khatauni khoji ja sakti hai.

  • Official Uttarakhand Bhulekh portal kholein.
  • Zila, tehsil aur gaon chunein.
  • Khasra/khata number ya khatedar ka naam bharein.
  • Khatauni ya naqal dekhein, aur zaroorat par official channel se pramanit prati lein.

Portal ka design badalta rehta hai, isliye jo bhi naye option dikhein unhe sarkari aadesh ke anusar hi istemal karein. Kisi dalal ya anjaan website par bharosa na karein.

Baahri logon ke liye zameen kharidne ke niyam (2025 ke baad)

Uttarakhand sarkar ne 2025 me bhoomi kanoon me badlav kiya hai. Reports ke anusar rajya ke baahar ke log Haridwar aur Udham Singh Nagar ko chhodkar baaki 11 zilon me kheti aur bagwani (agriculture/horticulture) ki zameen nahi kharid sakte.

Sarkari ghoshna aur samachar reports ke mutabik is badlav ke mukhya bindu ye hain:

  • Kheti/bagwani zameen: Haridwar aur Udham Singh Nagar ke alawa 11 zilon me rajya ke baahar ke logon par kharidne ki rok.
  • Awasiya (residential) zameen: baahri log seemit maatra me (reports me 250 square meter prati parivar ki seema ka zikr hai) aur ghoshna-patra (affidavit) ke saath hi khareed sakte hain.
  • Anumati ki vyavastha: reports ke anusar ab anumati zila magistrate ke bajaye sarkar ke star par di jati hai.
  • Bade projects: paryatan, udyog, shiksha jaise karyon ke liye badi zameen par seema (reports me 12.5 acre ke seema ka zikr) wapas lagu ki gayi hai.
  • Digital portal: kharid-farokht ki prakriya ke liye online portal aur nigrani ki vyavastha ka zikr hai.

Mahatvapurn: Is kanoon ki dhaaraon, notification aur adhikari niyam badal sakte hain, aur ye jaankari samachar reports par adharit saar hai. Ye kanooni salah nahi hai. Zameen kharidne se pehle Uttarakhand sarkar ke Revenue vibhag ki official adhisuchna aur ek sthaniya vakil se jaanch zaroor karwayein.

Section 143: kheti ki zameen ka gair-kheti upyog

Uttar Pradesh Zamindari Vinash aur Bhoomi Vyavastha Adhiniyam, 1950 (Uttarakhand me lagu roop) ki dhara 143 ke antargat kheti ki zameen ko gair-kheti (jaise awas) ke roop me badalne ki prakriya hoti hai. Ye badlav kar (revenue) aur zila prashasan ke niyamon ke adheen hai, aur yahan bhi 2025 ke sanshodhan ka asar pad sakta hai. Pahadi zameen ke case me tehsil aur SDM karyalay se sthaniya prakriya jaroor pata karein.

Zameen kharidne se pehle 12 jaanch

  • Khatauni aur khasra ka milan, aur khatedar ka naam.
  • Ek hi khate me kitne hissedar hain (Gol Khata me vivad ka jokhim).
  • Zameen ka prakar: kheti ya awasiya, aur uska reeta purana upyog.
  • Dakhil-kharij (mutation) ka record.
  • Sadak se seedhi pahunch aur raste ka adhikar.
  • Paani ka strot aur bijli ka connection.
  • Dhalaan, bhoo-skhalan (landslide) aur nadi se doori.
  • Bhawan nirman ke niyam aur map manzoori ki sambhavna.
  • Van vibhag ya sarkari zameen se seema ka vivad.
  • Stamp duty aur registry ka kharcha, jise Stamp Duty & Registry Calculator se andaza lagayein.
  • Baahri kharidar hone par rajya ke nai niyamon ke anusar patrata.
  • Sthaniya vakil se title (swamitva) ki jaanch.

Is vishay par vistrit lekh: Zameen kharidne se pehle kaun se kaagaz. Property ka daam andaza lagane ke liye Property Price Calculator aur Sq Ft to Sq Meter Converter bhi istemal karein.

Van Panchayat: gaon ke jungal ka apna prabandh

Uttarakhand me Van Panchayat vyavastha 1931 se chali aa rahi hai, jisme gaon ke log milkar gaon ke jungal ka prabandh karte hain, jaise ghaas, lakdi aur chara ke haq. Ye vyavastha duniya ki sabse puraani samuday-adharit van prabandhan vyavasthaon me ginti hai. Sarpanch aur panchayat ke niyam ke anusar ghaas kaatne ya jungal kholne ki tithiyan tay hoti hain.

Uttarakhand mein Zameen: Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

1 Nali me kitne square feet hote hain?
1 Nali = 2,160 square feet = 240 gaj = lagbhag 200.67 square meter. Ek Nali me 16 Mutthi hoti hain.
1 Mutthi kitne square feet ki hoti hai?
1 Mutthi = 135 square feet = 15 gaj (1 Nali ka 1/16 hissa) = lagbhag 12.5 square meter.
1 acre me kitni Nali hoti hain?
1 acre (43,560 sq ft) me lagbhag 20.17 Nali hoti hain. 1 hectare me lagbhag 49.83 Nali hoti hain.
Kya Uttarakhand me baahar ke log zameen kharid sakte hain?
2025 ke sanshodhan ke baad reports ke anusar rajya ke baahar ke log Haridwar aur Udham Singh Nagar ke alawa 11 zilon me kheti aur bagwani ki zameen nahi kharid sakte, aur awasiya zameen par bhi seema aur shartein hain. Kharidne se pehle sarkari adhisuchna aur vakil se jaanch karein.
Uttarakhand me khatauni online kaise dekhein?
Uttarakhand sarkar ke Bhulekh portal (bhulekh.uk.gov.in) par zila, tehsil aur gaon chunkar khasra ya khatedar ke naam se khatauni dekhi ja sakti hai. Pramanit prati ke liye official channel istemal karein.
Gol Khata kya hota hai?
Gol Khata ek sanyukt (sajha) khata hota hai jisme kai parivar ke sadasya hissedar hote hain. Isme hissa-batwara aur vivad ka jokhim rehta hai, isliye kharidne se pehle hissedaron ki jaankari zaroori hai.
Bigha aur Nali me kya sambandh hai?
Bigha ka maan alag-alag kshetron me alag hota hai, isliye ek sankhya sab jagah sahi nahi. Apne tehsil ke record se bigha ka sthaniya maan pushti karein, aur Sab Hisaab ke converter se hisaab jaanch lein.
Pahadon me ghar banane ke liye kya dhyan rakhein?
Bhukamp-rodhi tarike, dhalaan ka adhyayan, nadi se suraksha-doori, paani ka strot, sadak ki pahunch aur map manzoori ka dhyan rakhein. Ghar banane ke hisaab ke liye cement, eent, sariya ke calculator upyogi hain.
Section 143 kya hai?
Zamindari Vinash aur Bhoomi Vyavastha Adhiniyam ki dhara 143 ke antargat kheti ki zameen ko gair-kheti upyog me badalne ki prakriya hoti hai. Ye prakriya sarkari niyamon ke adheen hai aur 2025 ke sanshodhan ka asar pad sakta hai.
Uttarakhand me UCC kya hai?
UCC (Samaan Nagrik Samhita) ke antargat Uttarakhand me vivah, talaaq aur sahjeevan jaise vishayon par registration ke niyam hain. Registration ke liye sarkari UCC portal hai. Vistrit niyam sarkari adhisuchna me dekhein.
Uttarakhand me property registry ke liye kya kagaz chahiye?
Aam taur par khatauni, pehchan patra, sale deed, stamp duty ki pushti aur gawahon ki jaankari lagti hai. Sthaniya registry karyalay aur vakil se sahi suchi ki pushti karein.
Stamp duty kaise nikalein?
Stamp duty zameen ke prakar aur sarkari dar par nirbhar karti hai. Andaza lagane ke liye Sab Hisaab ka Stamp Duty Calculator istemal karein.
Pahadon me ghar banane ka kharcha kaise nikalein?
Nirman saamagri (cement, eent, sariya), majdoori aur dhalaan ke hisaab se kharcha badalta hai. Andaza ke liye Sab Hisaab ke cement, eent aur sariya calculator dekhein.
5 Nali zameen ka kshetrafal kitna hota hai?
5 Nali = 10,800 square feet = 1,200 gaj = lagbhag 1,003 square meter.
10 Nali zameen kitni hoti hai?
10 Nali = 21,600 square feet = 2,400 gaj = lagbhag 2,007 square meter.
