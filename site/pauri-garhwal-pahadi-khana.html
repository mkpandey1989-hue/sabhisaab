Is hisse ka maksad: Pauri Garhwal ka khana sirf swaad nahi, ek poori “jeevan-vigyan” hai. Yahan har dish ke peeche loha, mausam, oonchai aur buzurgon ka tajurba chhupa hai. Is hisse mein hum Garhwali rasoi ki sabhi mukhya dishes, rotiyan, chutney, jungli saag, meetha, peya aur rasoi ke bartanon ko step-by-step samjhenge.

Zaroori note (Imaandari ki baat): Garhwali rasoi ki bahut si dishes poore Garhwal Mandal me ek jaisi banti hain, aur kuch (jaise Singori, Bal Mithai, Chhyang) mool roop se Kumaon ya seemavarti ilakon ki hain.

Pauri Garhwal ke Mashhoor Mukhya Vyanjan

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ki pehchan wali mukhya dishes hain Kafuli (palak-methi ka gaadha saag), Phaanu (bhigi dalon ki lai), Chainsoo (bhuni urad dal ka dish), Thechwani (kuti mooli/aaloo ki sabzi), Bhatt ki Churkani, Gahat ki Dal aur Ras-Bhaat. Sab kuch paramparik taur par loha ki kadhai me dheemi aanch par pakta hai.

Kafuli (Spinach/Fenugreek thick curry in iron wok), Phaanu, Chainsoo (Roasted Urad dal protein dish), Thechwani (Crushed Radish/Potato), Bhatt ki Churkani, Dubuk, Ras-Bhaat, Gahat ki Dal & Jholi/Kaphli

Seedha jawab: Kafuli = palak + methi + chawal ka aata, Phaanu = bhigi dal ko pees kar pakayi hui sabzi, Chainsoo = bhuni urad dal ko kut kar pakayi hui dal, Thechwani = okhal/silbatte me kuti mooli ya aaloo ki sabzi, Churkani = bhatt (kali soyabean) ki gaadhi sabzi, Ras-Bhaat = dal ke ras ke saath chawal.

Garhwali khane ki sabse badi khoobi ye hai ki yahan kam masale, zyada swaad ka niyam chalta hai. Pauri Garhwal ke gaon me aaj bhi buzurg kehte hain: “Loha ki kadhai me bana khana pet se pehle khoon ko khush karta hai.” (Loha ki kadhai me khana pakne se khane me thoda loha ghulta hai, ye ek jaana-maana tathya hai; isi liye anemia se bachav ke liye iron cookware ki salah aam hai.)

Ek line me “Garhwali Thali”: Bhaat + Gahat ki dal + Kafuli ya Phaanu + Thechwani + Bhangjeera ki chutney + Mandua ki roti + ghee. Pauri aur Srinagar ke kai homestay is thali ko pre-booking par parosate hain, isliye yatra se pehle ek phone zaroor kar lein.

Kafuli kaise banti hai? (Step-by-Step Recipe)

  • Taiyari: 500 gram palak aur 100 gram methi ke patte achhe se dhokar bareek kaat lein.
  • Ubalna: Loha ki kadhai me thoda paani garam karke saag ko 8 se 10 minute dheemi aanch par pakayein.
  • Chawal ka ghol: 2 badi chammach chawal ka aata aadha cup thande paani me ghol lein (gaanth na rahe).
  • Pakana: Saag me ghol ko dheere-dheere daalte hue lagatar chalate rahein, 10 minute tak pakayein.
  • Tadka: Alag kadhai me ghee garam karein, usme jakhiya (ya jeera), hing aur sookhi lal mirch ka tadka lagakar saag par daal dein.
  • Parosna: Garam bhaat aur ghee ke saath serve karein.

Chainsoo kaise banta hai?

  • Urad dal (kali, chilke wali) ko loha ki kadhai me bina tel ke dheemi aanch par sunehra hone tak bhuniye.
  • Thanda hone par silbatte ya mixer me dara (coarse, poori bareek nahi) pees lein.
  • Kadhai me ghee garam karke jakhiya ka tadka dein, phir dal ka dara daalein.
  • Garam paani milakar 20 se 25 minute dheemi aanch par pakayein, beech-beech me chalate rahein.
  • Namak aur halki si pisi hui lal mirch dalkar bhaat ke saath parosein.

Isliye har dish ka naam aur uska seedha jawab alag hisse me diya gaya hai.

Pauri Garhwal ke khane ke teen bade niyam:

  • Mausam ke hisab se khana: Garmi me jholi/raita, sardi me gahat, bhatt aur mandua.
  • Mitti ke bartan ya loha: Dheemi aanch par pakne se swaad aur poshan dono bante hain.
  • Zero-waste rasoi: Sabzi ke chhilke aur dalon ka paani bhi jholi ya rasa banane me kaam aata tha.

Pauri Garhwal: Paramparik Roti aur Chawal ke Pakwan

Seedha jawab: Garhwal me mukhya roti Mandua (finger millet) ki hoti hai, jise ghee ya gud ke saath khaya jata hai. Gahat ke parathe, Siddu (bhara hua khameeri roti), Jhangore ka bhaat aur Gahat ras-bhaat bhi aam hain. Mandua garm taseer ka mana jata hai, isliye ye mukhyatah sardiyon ka anaj hai.

Mandua ki Roti, Gahat ke Parathe (Bedoo/Bhari Roti), Chhoi/Lesu Roti (Wheat dough stuffed with spiced Mandua paste), Jhangore ki Bhaat & Gahat Ras-Bhaat

Seedha jawab: Mandua ka aata gunde jane par chipakta nahi, isliye ise garm paani se gundh kar haath se thap kar sekte hain. Gahat ke parathe me gahat ki pisi dal bhari jaati hai. Jhangora (Madira) ka bhaat chawal jaisa hota hai par thoda chipchipa aur khushbudaar. Gahat ras-bhaat sardiyon ka sabse prasiddh bhojan hai.

Mandua ki roti banane ka tareeka (Step-by-Step):

  • 2 cup mandua ka aata + 1 chammach gehun ka aata mila lein (isse roti tootegi nahi).
  • Garm paani thoda-thoda milate hue narm aata gundhein.
  • Chhoti loi lekar haath se ya belan se mote chakle me thapein (patla nahi, aadha centimetre kaafi).
  • Garm tawe par dono taraf sekein, phir seedhi aanch par bhi phulaayein.
  • Garm roti par ghee ya makkhan lagayein.

Poshan ke bare me: Mandua me prakritik taur par calcium aur fibre ka ullekh milta hai aur isliye ise “pahadi superfood” kaha jata hai. Magar ye koi dawa nahi hai; kisi bimari (jaise diabetes ya kidney ki samasya) me khane ki matra doctor se poochkar hi tay karein.

Jhangore ka bhaat kaise banta hai?
1 cup jhangora ko achhe se dho lein, 2.5 cup paani me 15 se 20 minute dheemi aanch par pakayein. Paani sookh jaye to dhak kar 5 minute chhod dein. Garm dal ya Kafuli ke saath parosein.

Pauri ke gaon me roti ka rishta: Purane samay me Mandua ki roti sirf khana nahi thi. Ghar ki bahu ka haath ka swaad isi roti se pehchana jata tha. Mandua gundhna aur garm-garm thapna ek kala maani jaati thi, jo ab kam hoti ja rahi hai. Isi liye is kala ko zinda rakhna Sabhisaab.com ka bhi maksad hai.

Pauri Garhwal: Chutney, Raita, Achaar aur Pisyun Loon

Seedha jawab: Pahadi chutney aam chutney se alag hoti hai kyunki isme bhangjeera (perilla ke beej), timur, jakhiya aur silbatte ka pisa hua loon hota hai. Pisyun Loon (pisa hua masaledar namak) Garhwal ki sabse khaas “swaad-ki-chaabi” hai. Ye roti, bhaat aur kheere ke saath khaya jata hai.

Bhangjeera Chutney, Hemp Seed (Bhang ki) Chutney, Pahadi Kheere ka Raita (with crushed Rai/Mustard sharpness), Darim (Wild Pomegranate) Chutney, Timur Chutney & Linguda/Mooli Achar

Seedha jawab: Bhangjeera chutney bhune bhangjeera (perilla) ke beej, hari mirch, lahsun aur nimbu se banti hai. Bhang ke beej (hemp seeds) ki chutney me sirf beej use hote hain, jinme nasha nahi hota (par poudhe ki kheti par kanoon alag hai). Pahadi raita me kaddukas kiya hua kheera aur pisi hui rai milti hai.

Pahadi kheere ka raita (Step-by-Step):

  • 1 mote pahadi kheere ko chheel kar kaddukas kar lein, paani nichod dein.
  • 2 cup taza dahi phent lein.
  • 1 chhoti chammach rai ko silbatte par bareek pees kar dahi me milayein.
  • Namak, thoda bhuna jeera aur hari mirch daalein.
  • Ye raita ghante bhar thanda hone par aur swaad deta hai.

Pisyun Loon: Silbatte par Pisa Pahadi Namak

Seedha jawab: Pisyun Loon silbatte par haath se pisa hua masaledar namak hai jisme lahsun ke patte, hari mirch, dhaniya, jeera aur timur milaye jaate hain. Ye Garhwal ke har ghar ki “khaas dawat” hai. Mooli, kheere, aur roti ke saath ye sabse zyada khaya jata hai.

Pisyun Loon banane ka tareeka:

  • 2 chammach pahadi namak ya sendha namak silbatte par rakhein.
  • Isme 4 se 5 lahsun ke patte (ya 3 kali), 2 hari mirch, thoda dhaniya aur 1 chammach jeera daal dein.
  • Timur ke 8 se 10 daane daalein (zyada na lein).
  • Pathar ki laat se tab tak pisein jab tak sab aapas me mil na jaye.
  • Ise kaanch ki shishi me bharkar 1 hafte tak thanda jagah par rakhein.

Tip: Pisyun Loon ke saath ek taza kata hua mooli ya kheera ka tukda khayein. Ye ek tarah ka “pahadi sandwich” hi hai.

Kyun mashhoor hai ye “pisyun loon”: Gaon ki auratein khet ke kaam ke beech me dopahar ko chhaon me baith kar mooli par pisyun loon lagakar khati thi. Ye unka “fast food” tha. Aaj Khirsu, Pauri aur Srinagar ke kai cafe aur homestay ise “Pahadi Salt” ke naam se bechte hain.

Pauri Garhwal: Jungli Khane Layak Cheezein aur Mausami Saag

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ke jungal aur khet ki mendon se milne wale jungli khadya hain Kandali/Bichhu ghas (stinging nettle) ka saag, Linguda (fern), Kaphal, Hisalu, Kilmora, Bedu (Himalayan fig) aur Buransh ka rasa. Ye mausmi hote hain aur zyadatar garmi me milte hain.

Jungli Saag-Phal: Bichhu Ghaas, Linguda, Kankoda, Ghingaru, Kafal, Hisalu, Kilmora, Bedu aur Buransh Ras

Seedha jawab: Bichhu ghas (Kandali) ko ubal kar uska chhuan-wala janlewa dank khatam hota hai, phir uska saag ban jaata hai. Kaphal (Myrica) garmi me (April-June) paka laal phal hai. Hisalu aur Kilmora jungli beri hain. Bedu (jungli anjeer) garmi me milta hai. Buransh ke phool ka juice Pauri Garhwal ke oonche ilako me (jahan buransh hota hai) banta hai.

Kandali (Bichhu Ghas) ka saag banane ka tareeka:

  • Mote dastane pehen kar taaze komal patte todein.
  • Ubalte paani me 5 minute dalein taaki dank khatam ho jaye.
  • Paani nichod kar patte bareek kaat lein.
  • Ghee ya sarson tel me jakhiya aur hing ka tadka dein, patte daalkar 10 minute pakayein.
  • Namak aur halki si lal mirch dalkar bhaat ya roti ke saath khayein.

Safety Rule (Zaroori): Jungli phal, patte aur phool tab hi khayein jab aapko unki sahi pehchan ho. Kuch jungli phool aur beej zehreele ho sakte hain. Agar aap yatri hain, to kisi sthaniya gaon wale ya gaon ke jaankar se poochh kar hi khayein. Buransh ke phool ke sharbat ki matra bhi seemit rakhein; ye bhi alag-alag logon par alag asar kar sakta hai.

Gaon me “mausam ka kalendar” (Jungli khane ka): Chaitra-Baisakh me linguda aur kaphal ki shuruaat, Jeth me hisalu aur kilmora, Asadh-Saun me kandali ka naya saag. Sardi me gahat, bhatt aur mandua ki hukumat.

Pauri Garhwal: Paramparik Mithai, Tyohar ke Pakwan aur Prasad

Seedha jawab: Garhwal ki prasiddh mithai hain Arsa (chawal-gud ka teliya pakwan), Jhangore ki Kheer, Rote (gud-gehun ka mota prasad), Puwa, Singal aur Gulgule. Singori aur Bal Mithai mool roop se Kumaon ki mithaiyan hain, par aaj Pauri ke bazaaron me bhi milti hain.

Arsa (Jaggery & Rice sweet cooked in mustard oil), Singori (Khoya wrapped in Malu leaf), Bal Mithai, Chocolate, Jhangore ki Kheer, Rote (Thick wheat-jaggery offering for Devtas), Puwa, Singal, Lagad & Gulgule

Seedha jawab: Arsa chawal ke aate aur gud ke ghol ko tel ya ghee me talkar banta hai. Rote gehun ke aate aur gud se banne wali bhari roti hai jo Devtaon ko chadhai jati hai. Jhangore ki kheer doodh me pake jhangora aur gud/cheeni se banti hai. Gulgule gehun ke aate, gud aur saunf ke golden fried pakode hain.

Arsa banane ka tareeka (Step-by-Step):

  • 2 cup bhigoye aur sukhaye chawal ko pees kar aata banayein.
  • 1 cup gud ko thode paani me ubal kar chaasni banayein.
  • Chaasni me chawal ka aata milakar ghol (batter) banayein; thoda saunf bhi daalein.
  • Sarson ya refined tel garam karke chhoti poori jaisi golai ko dheemi aanch par sunehra talein.
  • Thanda hone par rakhein, ye 1 hafte tak chal jata hai.

Jhangore ki kheer (Step-by-Step):

  • 1/2 cup jhangora dho kar 1 ghanta bhigoyein.
  • 1 litre doodh me jhangora daalkar dheemi aanch par 25 se 30 minute pakayein (beech-beech me chalate rahein).
  • Gud ya cheeni, elaichi aur kaju-kishmish milayein.
  • 5 minute aur pakayein, garm ya thanda parosein.

Pauri Garhwal: Paramparik Peya aur Khameer Wale Pey

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ki mukhya peya hain Buransh ka sharbat (squash), Malta aur kinnow ka juice, Gud-Adrak ki chai, Chhaachh (matha) aur sardiyon me garm gud ka paani. Chhyang jaise paramparik fermented drinks mukhya roop se Jaunsari, Bhotia aur seemavarti kshetron ki paramparayen hain, Pauri me ye aam nahi hain.

Chhyang / Jaan / Kacchi (Traditional Tribal Millet/Barley Fermented Brew in High Border Valleys using ‘Balma’ herbal starter cake), Buransh Squash, Malta Juice & Gud-Adrak ki Chai

Seedha jawab: Chhyang jau (barley) ya mandua se banne wala halka fermented peya hai jise Bhotia aur Jaunsari samudayon me use kiya jaata hai. Pauri Garhwal me iski paramparik upasthiti kam hai. Buransh squash, Malta juice aur Gud-Adrak ki chai Pauri ke bazaaron aur homestay me aasani se milti hain.

Kanooni aur samajik dhyan: Sharab aur fermented peya ke utpadan aur bikri par Uttarakhand ka Aabkari kanoon lagu hota hai. Is page ka maksad kisi alcohol ko badhava dena nahi, balki Uttarakhand ki sanskritik jaankari dena hai. Buransh ka sharbat (Squash) kaise banta hai (ghar par)?

  • Taaze buransh ke laal phool todein (sirf wahi jagah jahan sthaniya log anumati dete hain aur van vibhag ki rok nahi hai).
  • Phoolon ki pankhudiyan alag karke dho lein.
  • Paani me ubal kar chhan lein, cheeni ki chaasni milayein.
  • Thanda hone par nimbu ka ras daalein aur kaanch ki botal me bharein.
  • Pani me milakar peeyein.

Dhyan dein: Buransh Uttarakhand ka rajya vriksha hai. Van kshetron me iske phool todne par van vibhag ki pabandi ho sakti hai. Sirf anumati wale ya gaon ke apne ped se hi phool lein.

Pauri Garhwal: Pahadi Kitchen Architecture & Utensils

Seedha jawab: Paramparik Garhwali rasoi me loha ki kadhai, silbatta, kansi ki thali, bhaddu (bhari pital/taambe ki degchi), parat aur mitti ka chulha hote hain. Chulhe ke upar Dhumala (dhuein se sukhane ka machan) hota hai jisme gosht, lakdi, beej aur lahsun sukhaye jaate the.

Loh ki Kadhai (Iron Wok Science), Silbatta (Grinding Stone), Kansi Thali, Bhaddu (Heavy brass pot for slow-cooking dal), Parat & Mud Chulha with Dhumala (Overhead smoke-drying shelf for meat/wood/seeds)

Seedha jawab: Loha ki kadhai garmi ko barabar failati hai aur khane me thoda loha deti hai. Silbatta masale ko haath se pisne ke liye pathar ka aadhar hai. Kansa (bronze/kansi) ki thali pahadi samay me bhojan ka mukhya bartan thi. Bhaddu me dal ghanton dheemi aanch par pakti thi. Dhumala chulhe ke upar ka machan hai jahan beej aur lakdi dhuein se sukhaye jaate the.

Chulhe ka vigyan: Mitti ka chulha dheemi aur lambi aanch deta hai, jisse mandua ki roti aur dal ek saath pak jaati hai.

Aaj ki rasoi me paramparik tareeke kaise apnayein?

  • Loha ki kadhai kharidein par sirf tabhi jab aap usko achhi tarah se season karna jaante hon (tel laga kar garm karna).
  • Silbatta ki jagah mixer chal sakta hai, par swaad me fark aata hai.
  • Kansi ki thali me khatti cheezein (jaise nimbu ya imli) rakhne se bachein.

Pauri Garhwal ke ghar me rasoi ka darja: Rasoi (chulha wali jagah) ko “pavitra” mana jata hai. Juta pehen kar rasoi me jaana varjit hota hai. Ye sirf andhvishwas nahi, safai ka bhi saral niyam tha. Mehmaan se bhi aamtaur par iske palan ki ummeed rakhi jaati hai.

Pauri Garhwal: Chhote Sawal-Jawab

Kafuli kya hai?
Palak aur methi ka gaadha pahadi saag jisme chawal ka aata milta hai.
Phaanu kya hota hai?
Bhigi hui dalon ko pees kar loha ki kadhai me pakaya gaya dish.
Chainsoo kis dal se banta hai?
Kali urad dal ko bhun kar, dara pees kar.
Thechwani kis cheez se banti hai?
Kuti hui mooli ya aaloo se.
Bhatt kya hota hai?
Kali soyabean, Uttarakhand ki prasiddh dal.
Gahat ki dal kya hoti hai?
Kulath (horse gram) ki dal jo sardi me khai jaati hai.
Dubuk kya hai?
Bhatt ya dal ki pisi hui gaadhi sabzi.
Mandua ki roti kaise banti hai?
Mandua ke aate ko garm paani se gundh kar haath se thap kar.
Siddu kya hai?
Khameeri aate ki bhari hui bhaap me pakai hui roti.
Jhangora kya hota hai?
Barnyard millet, pahad ka chawal jaisa anaj.
Bhangjeera kya hai?
Perilla ke beej, jinki chutney banti hai.
Pisyun Loon kya hai?
Silbatte par pisa hua masaledar namak.
Timur kya hota hai?
Pahadi masala, Sichuan pepper jaisa, zubaan par jhunjhuni dene wala.
Jakhiya kya hai?
Jungli sarson ke chhote beej jinse pahadi tadka lagta hai.
Kandali / Bichhu ghas kya hai?
Stinging nettle, jisko ubal kar saag banta hai.
Kaphal kab milta hai?
April se June ke beech.
Hisalu kya hai?
Pahadi jungli peeli raspberry.
Kilmora kya hai?
Jungli berberry (khatti-meethi beri).
Arsa kis cheez se banta hai?
Chawal ke aate aur gud se.
Rote kya hai?
Gehun-gud ka mota prasad.
Jhangore ki kheer kaise banti hai?
Jhangora ko doodh me dheemi aanch par pakakar gud/cheeni ke saath.
Singori kahan ki mithai hai?
Mool roop se Kumaon (Almora) ki.
Chhyang kya hai?
Jau ya mandua se banne wala paramparik fermented peya, Jaunsari/Bhotia kshetra me.
Buransh ka sharbat kab banta hai?
March-April ke beech jab phool aate hain.
Dhumala kya hota hai?
Chulhe ke upar ka machan jahan lakdi, beej aur lahsun sukhaye jaate the.
Garhwali thali me kya-kya hota hai?
Bhaat, gahat ki dal, kafuli ya phaanu, thechwani, chutney, mandua ki roti aur ghee.
Kya Pauri me Garhwali khana restaurant me milta hai?
Zyadatar homestay aur kuch dhabon me advance booking par.
Pahadi khana sehat ke liye kaisa hai?
Zyada anaj, dal aur saag hone se santulit, par kisi bimari me doctor se poochhein.
