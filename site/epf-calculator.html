EPF Calculator

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Free · Bina login · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Har mahine salary se PF katta hai aur utna hi company daalti hai. Saalon me ye chhoti rakam ek bada corpus ban jaati hai. Ye calculator batata hai ki aapke mahine ke contribution se retirement tak kitna paisa jama ho sakta hai.

EPF me paisa kahan se aata hai

Do taraf se — aapki salary se aur company ki taraf se. Dono ka contribution basic salary + DA ka 12%-12% hota hai. Ek zaroori baat: company wale 12% me se ek bada hissa EPS (pension) me chala jaata hai, poora PF me nahi aata. Isliye aapke PF passbook me dikhne wali rakam kabhi-kabhi ummeed se kam lagti hai.

Byaj: EPFO har saal dar tay karta hai (aam taur par 8% ke aaspaas). Byaj saal ke aakhir me khaate me judta hai aur agle saal us par bhi byaj milta hai — yahi compounding EPF ko itna taakatwar banati hai.

EPF ka sabse bada fayda

EPF ka byaj FD se behtar hota hai, aur ek tay shart poori hone par tax-free bhi. Sabse badi baat — ye paisa apne aap kat jaata hai. Aapko har mahine "bachat karni hai" ye sochna hi nahi padta. Isi liye zyadatar naukri-peshaa logon ka sabse bada retirement fund EPF hi hota hai.

Job badalte waqt sabse badi galti

Naukri badalne par PF nikalna nahi chahiye — use naye employer me transfer karna chahiye. UAN ek hi rehta hai, isliye transfer online ho jaata hai. Har baar nikal lene se compounding toot jaati hai aur retirement tak lakho ka farak pad jaata hai. Aur 5 saal se pehle nikalne par tax bhi lag sakta hai.

Nikasi kab ho sakti hai

  • Poora paisa: retirement par, ya 2 mahine se zyada berozgaar rehne par
  • Aanshik nikasi: ghar kharidna/banana, shaadi, padhai, ilaaj — har ek ki apni shart aur limit
  • Online claim: UAN active aur KYC (Aadhaar, PAN, bank) poora hona chahiye

Ye calculator ek anuman deta hai. Asli rakam salary badhne, byaj dar badalne aur EPS ke batware se alag ho sakti hai. Apna asli balance epfindia.gov.in ya UMANG app par dekh sakte hain.

Teen asli misaal — number ke saath

Misaal 1 — nayi naukri. Basic salary ₹25,000, umar 25 saal, retirement 58 par. Salary me har saal 8% badhotri aur 8.25% byaj maankar, retirement par corpus lagbhag ₹1.5 crore tak pahunch sakta hai. Ye sirf EPF ka number hai — koi alag nivesh nahi.

Misaal 2 — 10 saal ki deri. Wahi basic, wahi sab kuch, par shuruaat 35 saal ki umar me. Corpus ghat kar lagbhag ₹60-65 lakh reh jaata hai. Der ka nuksan yahan bhi utna hi bada hai.

Misaal 3 — beech me nikaal lena. Agar 30 saal ki umar me naukri badalte waqt aapne ₹4 lakh ka PF nikaal liya, to 28 saal baad wo rakam lagbhag ₹35-38 lakh ban sakti thi. Ek baar nikaalne ka asli daam yahi hai.

Log yahan galti kya karte hain

  1. Naukri badalte waqt PF nikaal lena. Teen nuksan ek saath — compounding tootti hai, 5 saal se pehle nikaalne par tax lag sakta hai, aur pension ke saal ginti se nikal jaate hain. Sahi tarika transfer hai, withdrawal nahi.
  2. UAN activate na karwana ya KYC adhoora chhodna. KYC adhoora hone par claim atak jaata hai — aur ye baat aksar tab pata chalti hai jab paise ki sabse zyada zaroorat hoti hai.
  3. Company ka poora 12% PF me maan lena. Company wale hisse ka ek bada bhaag EPS (pension) me jaata hai, PF me nahi. Isliye passbook me rakam kam dikhti hai.
  4. Nomination na bharna. Join karte waqt ye form khaali chhod dena bahut aam hai, aur baad me parivaar ko badi pareshaani hoti hai.
  5. Passbook kabhi na dekhna. Kabhi-kabhi company salary se kaat kar bhi jama nahi karti. Saal me ek baar milaan zaroor karein.

Bharat me EPF ki khaas baatein

  • Byaj dar: EPFO har saal tay karta hai — ye aam taur par PPF aur FD se behtar rehti hai, aur poori tarah tax-free hoti hai (kuch oonchi limit ke upar ke yogdaan ko chhod kar).
  • VPF: aap apni marzi se apna yogdaan badha sakte hain — usi dar par byaj milta hai. Ye ek bahut kam istemaal hone wala achha vikalp hai.
  • Nikasi ke niyam: ghar kharidna/banana, shaadi ya padhai (7 saal ki service ke baad), ilaaj (koi shart nahi), ya 2 mahine se zyada berozgaar rehne par poora.
  • EPS pension: pension ke liye kam se kam 10 saal ki service chahiye. Isi liye beech me PF nikaal kar service ka silsila todna mehnga padta hai.
  • Balance dekhne ke 3 tarike: epfindia.gov.in par UAN se login, UMANG app, ya registered number se missed call.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

EPF me kitna paisa katta hai?

Katauti poori salary par nahi hoti — sirf Basic + DA par. Isi liye jinki salary me allowance zyada aur basic kam hota hai, unka PF apne aap kam katta hai.

EPF ka byaj kitna milta hai?

EPFO har saal dar tay karta hai, aam taur par 8% ke aaspaas. Byaj saal ke aakhir me khaate me judta hai.

Job badalne par PF nikaal lena chahiye?

Nahi. Transfer karna chahiye. Nikal lene se compounding tootti hai aur 5 saal se pehle nikalne par tax bhi lag sakta hai.

PF ka paisa kab nikal sakte hain?

Retirement par ya 2 mahine se zyada berozgaar rehne par poora. Ghar, shaadi, padhai, ilaaj ke liye aanshik nikasi ho sakti hai.

Apna PF balance kaise dekhein?

epfindia.gov.in par UAN se login karke, ya UMANG app se. SMS aur missed call se bhi balance pata chal jaata hai.

Salary ka PF kitna percent katta hai?

Poori salary ka nahi — Basic + DA ka 12% aapki taraf se katta hai, aur utna hi company deti hai. Company ke hisse ka bada bhaag pension (EPS) me jaata hai.

PF poori salary par katta hai?

Nahi, sirf Basic + DA par. Isi liye jinki salary me Basic kam aur allowance zyada hota hai, unka PF kam katta hai.
