Puranic Bhugol: Manaskhand, Kurmanchal aur Champawat

Skand Puran ka Manaskhand aur “Kurmanchal” Naam

Seedha jawab: Kumaon ko puranon me “Manaskhand” kaha gaya hai (Skand Puran ka ek khand), aur “Kurmanchal” naam Vishnu ke Kurma (kachhua) avatar se jorkar bataya jata hai. Champawat ke paas Kandev (Kranteshwar Mahadev ki pahadi) ko Kurmavatar se joda jata hai.

Uttarakhand ke puranic bhugol me do bade khand mane jate hain:

  • Kedarkhand: Garhwal kshetra (Skand Puran ka ek hissa).
  • Manaskhand: Kumaon kshetra, jisme Kailash-Mansarovar ki yatra ka marg bhi shamil mana jata hai.
Kurmanchal naam kaise pada?
Lok-manyata ke anusar Vishnu ne yahin ke ek parvat par kachhua (Kurma) avatar liya, isliye ise “Kurmanchal” kaha gaya aur samay ke saath “Kumaon” ban gaya. Uttarakhand paryatan ki sarkari website ke anusar Champawat ke purv me pahadi par sthit Shiv mandir ko “Kurmapad” ya “Kandev” bhi kaha jata hai. Iska zila-prashasan ke sarkari vivaran me Kranteshwar Mahadev naam se zikr milta hai.

Vayu Purana me Champavati: Wikipedia ke anusar Vayu Purana me Champavati ko “Puri” Naga rajvansh ke nau rajaon ki rajdhani bataya gaya hai. Nagnath mandir ki parampara ko is Naga-sanskriti se jodkar dekha jata hai, par yeh jod mukhya roop se lok-manyata hai.

Agar aap chahein to Skand Puran (Manaskhand), Mahabharat ke Van Parva aur Kalidas ki rachnaon ke mool pathon se sahi sandarbh bharke is box me jod sakte hain.

Mandir ki vaastukala: Baleshwar jaise mandir Kumaon ke “Nagara” shaili ke pathar ke mandir hain, jinme shikhar (ooncha gumbad), nakkashidar patthar aur parisar me anya chhote mandir hote hain. Samay ke saath isme marammat bhi hui hai.

Mandir kab khulte hain?
Zile ke zyadatar mandir saal bhar khule rehte hain. Purnagiri par Chaitra Navratri me bheed sabse zyada hoti hai. Kapat kholne-band karne ki koi alag mausmi tithi nahi hoti, jaisi Garhwal ke Char Dham me hoti hai.

Mandir ke darshan ka samay, aarti ka waqt aur pooja ka shulk badalta rehta hai. Yatra se pehle mandir samiti ya Champawat zila prashasan ki official website se confirm karein.

Hingla Devi Mandir: Champawat Ki Sabse Oonchi Choti Par Devi

Champawat bazaar se yahan tak lagbhag 7 km ki sadak hai, aur shehar se lagbhag 5 km ka paidal raasta bhi bataya jata hai. Champawat: Ishta Devta, Gram Devta aur Jagar Parampara

Golu Devta, Haru Heet aur Champawat Ke Lok-Devta

Seedha jawab: Golu (Gwal / Goril) Devta Kumaon ke nyay ke devta hain, aur Champawat ko unki katha ka mool sthan mana jata hai. Lok-katha ke anusar woh raja Jhal Rai aur rani Kalinka ke putra the, aur Haru Heet (Harish Chand) unke mama maane jate hain.

Golu Devta kaun hain?
Kumaon me Golu Devta ko nyay ka devta aur Shiv ke Gaur Bhairav roop ka avatar mana jata hai. Champawat ka Gwarel (Gorill) Chaur unke sabse purane mandiron me ginta jata hai. Unke prasiddh mandir Chitai (Almora), Ghorakhal (Nainital) aur Champawat me hain.

Golu Devta ki katha ke teen version (jaisa sources me milta hai): 1. Raja Jhal Rai ka putra: Champawat ke raja Jhal Rai aur rani Kalinka ka putra, jisse ek lokpriya katha ke anusar sauteli ranion ki jalan me bachpan me hi dur kar diya gaya tha. Baad me woh Champawat laute aur nyayapriya raja bane. 2. Katyuri raja ka senapati: Kuch sources unhe Katyuri raja ka senapati batate hain. 3. Baz Bahadur Chand ka senapati: Ek aur manyata unhe Chand raja Baz Bahadur (1638-1678) ke samay ka veer senapati batati hai jo yuddh me shahid hue.

Itihaaskaaron me in teeno version par ek raay nahi hai. Isliye hum ise lok-katha aur aitihasik vivaran ka mishran maante hain. Is par aage Champawat: Golu Devta Me Arzi: Stamp Paper Wali Parampara me “Golu Devta ko chitthi-arzi” ki parampara aur Champawat: Lok-Kathayein Aur Kimvadantiyan me poori katha hai.

Jagar Kaise Hota Hai: Jagariya, Dangariya aur Devta Khel

Seedha jawab: Jagar ek raat-bhar chalne wala anushthan hai jisme Jagariya (gayak) hudka aur thali ki thaap par devta ki gatha gata hai, aur Dangariya (devta ka madhyam) par devta avatarit hote hain. Is anushthan me samasya ya “dosh” ka karan aur niwaran poochha jata hai.

Jagar ke mukhya patra: - Jagariya: Gatha gaane wala, aam taur par Hudkiya ya Bajgi samudaay se. - Dangariya: Woh vyakti jis par devta ka avatran maana jata hai. - Sankalp-karta (Sevak): Wah parivar jiske ghar Jagar ho raha hota hai. - Hudka, Thali, Dhol: Anushthan ke vadya.

Jagar ka samay aur sthan: Aam taur par raat ko ghar ke aangan ya mandir me hota hai aur kai ghante chalta hai. Isme kul-devta ya gram-devta ko jaga kar parivar ki samasya ka samadhan poochha jata hai.

Ek Zaroori Baat: Jagar aur Devta Khel lok-aastha ka vishay hain. Iska ullekh yahan sanskritik jaankari ke roop me hai, kisi bimari ya kanooni samasya ke ilaaj ke roop me nahi. Medical ya kanooni samasya hone par doctor aur vakil se hi salah lein.

Champawat: Prag-Itihas, Pathar Yug aur Prachin Avshesh

Champawat Ke Prag-Aitihasik Praman: Sahi Tasveer

Seedha jawab: Champawat zile me koi bahut prasiddh aur well-documented “painted rock shelter” ya megalithic site abhi sources me nahi mila. Kumaon ka sabse prasiddh rock-art sthal Lakhudiyar (Almora) hai, jo Champawat ke paas ke zile me aata hai.

Lakhudiyar, Dungri aur Kimni jaise sthalon ka naam hai, jo Kumaon-Garhwal ke alag-alag zilon me hain.

  • Lakhudiyar: Almora zile me Suyal nadi ke paas, Kumaon ke sabse prasiddh prag-aitihasik chitra-sthal me se ek.
  • Champawat ke liye: Sarkari ya shodh-granthon me abhi koi pakka vivaran nahi mila, isliye hum yeh khana “khali” rakhte hain.
  • Cup-marks (okhli jaise nishan) aur Megalith: Kumaon ke kai hisson me inka ullekh milta hai.

Agar aapke paas Champawat ke kisi gaon me purane patthar ke chinh, okhli jaise nishan ya chitra ki jaankari ho to hume jaroor bhejein. Hum pehle Archaeological Survey of India (Dehradun Circle) ya Kumaon University ke itihaas vibhag se pushti karenge.

Champawat: Kuninda aur Katyuri Kaal

Champawat Se Judi Prachin Rajsatta: Naga, Kuninda aur Katyuri

Seedha jawab: Champawat ke baare me sabse purana likhit sandarbh Vayu Purana me Champavati ko Naga rajaon ki rajdhani batata hai. Baad me Katyuri rajya tootkar kai chhoti riyasaton me bant gaya, jinme “Sui (Kali Kumaon)” bhi shamil thi, jahan baad me Chand rajvansh aaya.

Kuninda: Kuninda ek prachin Himalayi gan-rajya tha jiske sikke Kumaon-Garhwal ke pahadi ilakon me milte hain. Champawat zile ke liye kisi khaas sthan ka verified praman nahi mila.

Katyuri: Katyuri shasak Baijnath (Bageshwar) ko apni rajdhani (Kartikeyapura) bana kar lagbhag poore Kumaon-Garhwal par shasan karte the. Samay ke saath rajya tootkar kai chhoti riyasaton me baant gaya, jinme Baijnath-Katyuri, Dwarahat, Doti (aaj ka Nepal), Baramandal, Askot, Sira, Sora aur Sui (Kali Kumaon) shamil the. Is Sui kshetra me hi Champawat aata hai.

Champawati aur Som Chand ki katha (NIC ke anusar): Zila prashasan ki website ke anusar zila ka naam Raja Arjun Deo ki putri Champawati ke naam par pada. Chandravanshi Rajput Som Chand ne yahan apna kila banaya aur raja ki putri se vivah kiya. Kila 15 acre zameen par banaya gaya aur pehle ise “Rajbunga” kaha gaya, jo baad me “Champawat” ho gaya. Yeh vivaran parampara aur lok-itihas par aadharit hai.

Lok-manyata: Kuch lok-manyata ke anusar Banbasa ke paas ke “Brahmdev” naam ka sambandh Katyuri raja Brahmdev se hai, par is baat ka koi pakka aitihasik praman humare paas nahi hai.

Chand Rajvansh: Champawat Se Almora Tak

Chand Rajvansh Ki Pehli Rajdhani Champawat

Seedha jawab: Chand rajvansh ki pehli rajdhani Champawat (Kali Kumaon) thi. Parampara ke anusar Som Chand ne yahan Rajbunga kila banaya. Chand rajdhani baad me, lagbhag 1563 me, Almora le jayi gayi.

Chand rajvansh kya hai?
Chand rajvansh ne Kumaon par sadiyon tak raj kiya. Unka rajya “Kurmanchal” kehlata tha. Parampara ke anusar rajvansh ki sthapna Som Chand ne ki, jinke samay ko lekar itihaaskaaron me matbhed hai, kyunki Wikipedia hi unhe “legendary” (parampara me mane jane wale) shasak likhta hai aur alag-alag sources 7vi se 10vi shatabdi ke beech batate hain.

Rajbunga Kila: Chand Kaal Ka Kila Aur Aaj Ka Tehsil

Seedha jawab: Champawat ka purana kila, jise Rajbunga kaha jata hai, ab Champawat tehsil ke karyalay ke roop me istemal hota hai (Uttarapedia ke anusar). Wikipedia par 1815 ke is kile ka ek purana chitra bhi hai.

Is kile ke baare me do baatein dhyan rakhne layak hain: - Aaj ka upyog: Prashasan ke karyalay ke roop me, isliye andar jaane ke liye sarkari samay aur anumati zaroori ho sakti hai. - Kila kitna purana hai: Parampara ke anusar Som Chand ne ise banaya. Kisi nirdharit tithi ka sarkari praman nahi mila.

ADB ka dastavez (Champawat: Patti, gaon aur bazaar dekhein): ADB ke ek pariyojana-dastavez ke anusar Rajbunga aur Banasur, dono kile tehsil karyalay ke roop me istemal hote hain aur unke restoration aur adaptive reuse ka prastav hai. Golu Devta mandir Rajbunga se lagbhag 3 km bataya gaya hai.

Kile ke purane nishan kitne bache hain, isko lekar hume kisi bharose layak survey report ya ASI ke vivaran ki zarurat hai. Jab tak wo nahi milti, hum is box ko khuli jaankari ke roop me rakhenge.

Champawat: Gorkha Shasan (1790-1815)

Gorkha Aakraman Kali Kumaon Se Shuru Hua

Seedha jawab: Nepal ki Gorkha sena ne 1790 me Kali nadi paar karke Kumaon par kabza kiya. Wikipedia ke anusar ek sena toli Kali Kumaon se Sor ki taraf gayi, aur Amar Singh Thapa ki sena Sor-Gangoli hote hue Almora pahunchi. Gorkha raj lagbhag 24-25 saal chala.

Champawat Nepal seema aur Kali nadi par sthit hai, isliye Kumaon me Gorkha aakraman ka sabse pehla rasta isi ilaake se hokar jata tha.

Anglo-Nepal Yuddh Aur Sugauli Ki Sandhi

Seedha jawab: 1815 me British aur Kumaoni sahayakon ne Gorkhaon ko Kumaon se hataya. Almora par April 1815 ke ant me kabza hua, aur Sugauli ki sandhi 4 March 1816 ko pakki hui, jiske baad Kali nadi ke paschim ke ilake (Kumaon-Garhwal) British ko mile.

Ghatnakram (sources ke anusar): 1. April 1815: British senayein Kashipur se Kumaon me ghusin aur Almora par kabza kiya (26 ya 27 April, sources me ek din ka fark hai). 2. 30 April 1815: Gorkha jhulaghat ke raste Doti (Nepal) laut gaye. 3. 3 May 1815: Gardner ko Kumaon ka Commissioner banaya gaya. 4. Dec 1815: Sugauli ki sandhi ka masauda Nepal ko bheja gaya. 5. 4 March 1816: Nepal ne sandhi ko pakka kiya, jiske baad Kali nadi Kumaon aur Nepal ke beech seema bani.

Harakh Dev Joshi ki bhumika: Nainital Tourism ke anusar Chand raja ke ek sardar Harakh Dev Joshi ke saath lagbhag 4,000 Kumaoni British ke saath mile. Itihaaskaar is sankhya aur Joshi ki bhumika ko lekar ek raay nahi rakhte, isliye ise “ek source ke anusar” maanein.

Champawat ke liye mahatva: Kali nadi aaj bhi Champawat zile aur Nepal ke beech ki seema hai. Isi karan Champawat ka sabse bada seema-vishayak itihas 1815-16 ki is sandhi se jura hai. Banbasa-Mahendranagar ke paas aaj ki antarrashtriya seema ke baare me Champawat: Bhugol, nadiyan aur pahad (bhugol) dekhein.

Champawat: British Kaal (1815-1947)

Kumaon Ka Prashasan: Gardner, Traill, Batten aur Ramsay

Seedha jawab: British ne Kumaon ko lagbhag 70 saal “non-regulation system” me chalaya, jise teen prashaskon Traill, J. H. Batten aur Sir Henry Ramsay ne sambhala. Gaon ke star par “Patwari Halka” ki vyavastha banayi gayi, jo aaj bhi Uttarakhand ki revenue police ka aadhar hai.

Champawat ka sthan: British kaal me Champawat Kumaon zile ka hissa tha. 1997 me zila banne se pehle yeh Pithoragarh zile ka hissa raha. Zile ke aitihasik prashasnik badlav ka poora vivaran Champawat: Tehsil, block aur prashasan me hai.

British kaal ki chhap aaj bhi dikhti hai: - Abbott Mount: Lohaghat ke paas ka British-kaleen hill-station, jahan purani kothiyan aur church hain (poora vivaran Champawat: Ghumne ki jagah aur trek). - Mayavati Advaita Ashram: Ramakrishna Mission ka ashram, jise lagbhag 1899 me sthapit hua mana jata hai (Champawat: Ghumne ki jagah aur trek). - Champawat ki aadamkhor sherni: Jim Corbett ki pehli kitab “Man-Eaters of Kumaon” ka ek mashhoor prasang Champawat se jura hai (iska poora vivaran Champawat: Mausam, jungle aur jeev-jantu me hai). - Zameen ke bandobast (land settlements): Kumaon me British kaal me kai baar zameen ka bandobast hua, jinme Traill, Batten aur Beckett ke naam prasiddh hain. Iska aaj ki zameen-sambandhi samasyaon par asar Champawat: Zameen, nali-mutthi aur kheti (zameen ke niyam) me samjhaya gaya hai.

Pauw Garhwal ka prasang hai. Hum Champawat ke liye sirf wo naam likh rahe hain jinka Kumaon ke sandarbh me bharosemand ullekh mila hai.

Champawat: Swatantrata Sangram Aur Veer Parampara

Kalu Singh Mahara: Kumaon Ke Pehle Bade Swatantrata Senani

Seedha jawab: Kalu Singh Mahara Lohaghat ke paas Bisung Patti (aaj ka Karnakarayat kshetra), Champawat ke Thakur the, jinhe 1857 ke vidroh me Kumaon ka pehla bada swatantrata senani mana jata hai. Unhone “Krantiveer” naam ka sangathan banaya aur Lohaghat ki British barracks jalane ka ullekh milta hai.

Kalu Mahara ki kahani (sources ke anusar): - Kaun the: Bisung Patti, Lohaghat ke paas, Champawat ke Thakur. - Vidroh ka karan: Awadh se mile ek gupt patra ke baad unhone vidroh me hissa liya. Patra ke anusar jeet ke baad Kumaon ka pahadi hissa unhe aur saathiyon ko, tatha terai ka hissa Awadh ko diya jana tha. - Saathi: Anand Singh Phartyal (Fartyal) aur Bishan Singh Kharayat (Karayat). - Sangathan: “Krantiveer”, jo Kali Kumaon ke yuvaon ka sangathan tha. - Kya hua: Commissioner Ramsay ne Tanakpur aur Lohaghat se sena bheji. Bastia me ladai ke baad Kalu Mahara ko peeche hatna pada. - Ant: Is baare me sources me alag-alag vivaran hain. Kuch sources ke anusar Kalu Mahara ko giraftar karke fansi di gayi, jabki kuch ke anusar saathi Anand Singh aur Bishan Singh ko goli maari gayi aur Kalu Mahara kai jailon me rakhe gaye.

Aaj unhe kaise yaad kiya jata hai: - Lohaghat ke ek chauraha ka naam 2010 me unke naam par rakha gaya (ek source ke anusar). - 14 October 2021 ko Uttarakhand dakhvibhag ne unke samman me vishesh lifafa (special cover) jari kiya. - Rajya me unhe Kumaon ka pehla swatantrata senani ke roop me mana jata hai.

Kuch sources unke janm-tithi, mrityu aur bhai-saathiyon ke vivaran me ek-doosre se alag hain. Hum jaankari ko “Uttarakhand sarkar ke gazetteer aur sodh-granthon” se milakar hi update karenge.

Baad Ke Aandolan, Sainik Parampara Aur Rajya Andolan

Seedha jawab: 1921 ke Kuli Begar andolan me poore Kumaon ke log shamil hue, jiski mukhya ghatna Bageshwar me hui. Kumaon Regiment ka mukhyalaya Ranikhet me hai, aur Champawat jaise zilon se bade paimane par yuva sena me jate hain. Rajya andolan ki ek saaf sthaniya jaankari abhi sources me nahi mili.

  • Kuli Begar andolan (1921): Saryu nadi ke kinare Bageshwar me bade paimane par hui is ghatna ke baad pahadon me begar pratha khatam hui. Champawat ka khaas yogdan ya naam nahi mila.
  • Sainik parampara: Kumaon Regiment ka mukhyalaya Ranikhet me hai. Champawat ke bahut se parivaron me sena me jana ek purani parampara hai. Is par aage Champawat: Zile Ki Arthvyavastha, Per Capita Income Aur Rozgar aur Champawat Ke 100 One-Liner GK Facts me vistar hai.
  • Rajya andolan (1994): Mukhya ghatnayein Khatima, Mussoorie aur Muzaffarnagar ki hain. Champawat ke sthaniya shaheedon ki verified suchi humare paas nahi hai.

Jin sthaniya naamon ka praman nahi mila, unka naam jodna bina dastavez ke ek galat jaankari phailane jaisa hoga.

Champawat: Lok-Kathayein Aur Kimvadantiyan

Golu Devta Aur Rani Kalinka Ki Katha

Seedha jawab: Sabse prachalit katha ke anusar Champawat ke raja Jhal Rai ki rani Kalinka ke bete ko dusri ranion ne pathar se badal kar nadi me bahva diya. Bada hokar woh ek lakdi ke ghode par lautkar aaya aur apni sachchai raja ke saamne sabit ki. Baad me woh nyay ke devta bane.

Yeh katha ek lok-katha hai. Sahapedia aur kai anya sources me iske alag-alag roop milte hain, par kuch baatein sabhi me samaan hain:

  • Raja aur rani: Champawat ke raja Jhal Rai ki satvi rani Kalinka thi, jo do lok-devtaon Harishchand Devjyu aur Sem Devjyu ki behen mani jati hai.
  • Jalan aur saazish: Baaki ranion ne Kalinka ke bachche ke badle ek pathar rakh diya. Sahapedia ke anusar raja ne bina jaanch ke ranion ki baat maan li aur Kalinka ko pagal samajh kar ek jagah band kar diya.
  • Bachche ka bachna: Bachche ko alag-alag roop me nadi ya gaushala me rakhne ki baat milti hai, jahan se use ek parivar ne pala. Yeh hissa sources me alag-alag hai.
  • Wapsi aur nyay: Bada hokar woh lakdi ke ghode par sawar hokar lauta aur raja ko dikhaya ki jaise lakdi ka ghoda paani nahi peeta, waise hi ek stri pathar ko janm nahi de sakti. Is tarah sachchai saamne aayi.
  • Nyay ke devta: Baad me woh “Golu” yani Gwal Devta ke roop me puje gaye, aur log unhe apni arzi bhejne lage.

Chitai ka mandir aur “chitthi-arzi”: Almora ke Chitai ka mandir sabse prasiddh hai, jahan log ghanti chadhate hain aur mannat ki chitthiyan bandhte hain. Champawat ka Gwarel Chaur mandir isi katha ka mool sthan mana jata hai.

Golu Devta ki katha ke aitihasik aadhar (kaun sa raja, kab) par itihaaskaar ek raay nahi rakhte. Jagar me gayi jane wali asli “Golu Devta ki Gatha” ke baare me Champawat: Golu Devta Me Arzi: Stamp Paper Wali Parampara me alag se likha jayega.

Ek Hathiya Naula: Ek Haath Wale Shilpkar Ki Ek Raat

Seedha jawab: Lok-katha ke anusar ek haath wale shilpkar ne ek hi raat me Champawat ke paas yeh naula banaya. Yeh Champawat se lagbhag 5 km door hai aur Chand kaal ki pathar-kala ka namuna mana jata hai.

Is katha ke kai roop hain. Sabse prachalit roop yeh hai ki shilpkar ko ek raat me naula banane ki shart di gayi, aur usne ek hi haath se is kaam ko pura kiya. Is bhavya naule ki pathar ki khudayi isliye lok-kathaon ko jagah deti hai.

Banasur Ka Kila: Pauranik Daitya Aur Lohaghat Ka Kila

Seedha jawab: Lok-manyata ke anusar Banasur, jise Shiv se 100 bhujaon ka vardan mila tha, ka kila yahin tha, jahan Shri Krishna ne use maara. Yeh kila Lohaghat se lagbhag 7 km aur Champawat se lagbhag 20 km door, 1,859 meter ki oonchai par hai.

  • Kila kab ka hai: Nativeplanet jaise sources ise madhyakalin (Middle Age) ka batate hain, jabki lok-katha ise Banasur se jodti hai.
  • Kahan kaise jayein: Lohaghat se Karnakarayat tak bus aur wahan se lagbhag 1 km ki paidal chadhai (Uttarapedia ke anusar).
  • Dhyan rakhein: Banasur-Usha-Aniruddh ki katha ko bharat me kai alag-alag sthanon se joda jata hai, isliye yahan ki manyata ek sthaniya manyata ke roop me hi likhi gayi hai.

Barahi Dham Aur Bagwal Ki Kimvadanti

Seedha jawab: PTI ki report ke anusar lok-manyata hai ki chaar Kham (Walig, Lamgadiya, Gahadwal, Chamyal) ne Maa Barahi ki sharan li aur ek manushya ke khoon ke badle patthar-yuddh dwara devi ko prasann kiya. Aaj yeh pratha fal-phool ki barsaat me badal chuki hai.

Is katha ke baare me samajhne layak baatein: - Yeh lok-manyata hai: Kisi aitihasik dastavez ke bina, ek kimvadanti jo paimane par peedhi dar peedhi chali aa rahi hai. - Sanskritik mahatva: Is katha se samajh aata hai ki Bagwal purani bali-parampara ki jagah ab ek pratikatmak, samudayik utsav ban chuka hai. - Aaj ka roop: Ab fal-phool se hone wale is mele ko dekhne har saal badi bheed jutati hai (poora vivaran Champawat: Prasiddh Mele aur Doli Yatrayein me).

Purnagiri Mata Ki Manyata

Seedha jawab: Manyata ke anusar Purnagiri me Maa Sati ki nabhi (navel) giri thi, isliye ise 108 Siddhpeethon me ginte hain. Mandir Tanakpur ke paas Kali nadi ke kinare Annapurna parvat par hai.

Shaktipeeth ki yeh katha poore Bharat me prachalit hai: Daksh ke yagya me Sati ne deh tyag di, aur Shiv unka shaav lekar ghoomte rahe, jiske kuch ang alag-alag jagahon par gire. Purnagiri ke liye yeh manyata hai ki yahan nabhi giri thi. Isliye yeh sthal Shakti ke upasakon ke liye kendra bana. Is yatra ki poori jaankari (raasta, safar ka samay, suraksha) Champawat: Ghumne ki jagah aur trek aur Champawat: Kaise pahunchein, kahan rukein me aayegi.

Yeh mukhya roop se Garhwal ki kathayein hain.

Champawat: Sabse Zyada Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQ)

Champawat ka itihas kya hai?
Champawat Chand rajvansh ki pehli rajdhani thi (Wikipedia ke anusar lagbhag 1200 se 1563 tak). Baad me Gorkha (1790-1815) aur British (1815 se) ka shasan raha.
Champawat ka naam kaise pada?
NIC ke anusar Raja Arjun Deo ki putri Champawati ke naam par. Parampara ke anusar Som Chand ne yahan Rajbunga kila banaya.
Champawat ki rajdhani Almora kab gayi?
Wikipedia ke anusar lagbhag 1563 me.
Baleshwar mandir kisne banwaya aur kab?
Chand rajvansh ne, zyadatar sources ke anusar lagbhag 10vi se 12vi shatabdi me. Kuch sources me tithi alag-alag milti hai.
Ek Hathiya Naula kya hai aur kahan hai?
Champawat se lagbhag 5 km door ek pathar ka naula, jise lok-katha ke anusar ek haath wale shilpkar ne ek raat me banaya.
Banasur ka Kila kahan hai?
Lohaghat ke paas, Lohaghat se lagbhag 7 km aur Champawat se lagbhag 20 km, 1,859 meter ki oonchai par.
Golu Devta ka mool sthan kahan mana jata hai?
Champawat ko, jahan Gwarel (Gorill) Chaur me unka mandir hai. Sabse prasiddh mandir Chitai (Almora) me hai.
Golu Devta ko “nyay ke devta” kyun kehte hain?
Kyunki lok-manyata ke anusar woh nyay dilate hain aur log mannat ki chitthiyan aur ghantiyan chadhate hain.
Kalu Singh Mahara kaun the?
Lohaghat ke paas Bisung Patti, Champawat ke Thakur, jinhe 1857 ke vidroh me Kumaon ka pehla bada swatantrata senani mana jata hai.
Gorkha raj Kumaon me kab tak raha?
1790 se 1815 tak, lagbhag 24-25 saal.
Sugauli ki sandhi kab hui?
Masauda December 1815 me bheja gaya, aur 4 March 1816 ko pakki hui. Isse Kali nadi Kumaon-Nepal seema bani.
Kumaon ka British prashasan kaun-kaun se prashasakon ne chalaya?
Lagbhag 70 saal Traill, J. H. Batten aur Sir Henry Ramsay ne (Gardner pehle Commissioner the).
Purnagiri mandir kyun prasiddh hai?
Yeh 108 Siddhpeethon me ginta jata hai, aur manyata hai ki yahan Maa Sati ki nabhi giri thi.
Kya Champawat me koi purani rock-art ya prag-aitihasik jagah hai?
Abhi koi well-documented site sources me nahi mila. Sabse prasiddh Kumaon sthal Lakhudiyar (Almora) hai.
Champawat ki aadamkhor sherni kaun si kitab me aati hai?
Jim Corbett ki pehli kitab “Man-Eaters of Kumaon” me.
