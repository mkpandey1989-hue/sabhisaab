Har category ke tools — ek nazar me

Nivesh (Investment): SIP, Step-Up SIP, Lumpsum, SWP, PPF, NPS, FD, RD, Sukanya, EPF, NSC, KVP, SCSS aur Post Office MIS jaise calculators se aap apne paise ka future value, maturity aur pension ka anuman laga sakte hain. Compounding ke chalte jitna jaldi shuru karenge, utna zyada fayda — isi liye SIP aur PPF long-term wealth ke liye popular hain. Emergency Fund aur 50/30/20 Budget tools se planning ki shuruaat karein.

Loan aur Tax: EMI Calculator se loan ki monthly kisht aur total byaj pata chalta hai, jabki Loan Prepayment aur Home Loan Eligibility tools bade faisle me madad karte hain. Income Tax (New Regime) aur Old vs New Regime comparison se aap dono tareeko ka tax dekh kar kam wala chun sakte hain. GST, HRA aur Credit Card Payoff calculators roz kaam aate hain. Tax slab aur charges samay-samay par badalte hain — hamesha official website se latest confirm karein.

Trading tools: Intraday P&L, Brokerage & Charges, Position Size, Risk-Reward, Options Break-even aur Pivot Points — ye traders ke liye hain. Sabse zaroori baat: gross profit nahi, charges (brokerage, STT, GST, stamp duty) ke baad ka net profit dekhein, aur ek trade me capital ka sirf 1-2% risk karein. Ye tools sirf jaankari ke liye hain, koi nivesh salah nahi.

Sehat aur Converter: BMI, Calorie (BMR), Ideal Weight, Body Fat aur Water Intake se sehat ka andaaza milta hai (doctor ki salah ki jagah nahi). Area (Bigha), Length/Weight, Temperature, Speed aur Data converters roz ki zaroorat poori karte hain — khaaskar Bigha, jo har rajya me alag hota hai.
