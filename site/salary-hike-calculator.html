Formula aur uska matlab

Hike ka hisaab hamesha purani salary par lagta hai. Formula seedha hai: badhotri ko purani salary se bhaag dein aur 100 se guna karein. Dhyan rahe ki company hike CTC par batati hai, par haath me aane wala paisa tax aur PF ke baad kam badhta hai.

Formula: Hike% = [(Nayi − Purani) ÷ Purani] × 100
Badhotri = Nayi − Purani

Teen misaal, asli number ke saath

Misaal 1 — aam hike. Purani ₹31,000, nayi ₹35,500. Badhotri ₹4,500 aur hike 14.52%.

Misaal 2 — job change. ₹6 lakh se ₹9 lakh CTC. Hike 50% — job badalne par aam taur par hike zyada hoti hai.

Misaal 3 — mehngai ke baad. 8% hike mili aur mehngai 6% hai to asli badhotri sirf 2% ke aaspaas hai.

Ye table ek nazar me batata hai ki alag-alag salary par hike kaisa dikhta hai. Dhyan dein ki bade number par chhota percent bhi acchi rakam ban jaata hai — ₹60,000 par 10% ka matlab ₹6,000 hai, jabki ₹25,000 par 10% sirf ₹2,500.

Appraisal ke waqt sirf percent par mat atkein. Ye bhi dekhein ki fixed kitna badha aur variable kitna, kyunki variable har saal poora nahi milta. Aur agar hike ke saath designation ya kaam badal raha hai to wo aage ke liye percent se zyada keemti hai.

Sabse aam galtiyan

  1. Nayi salary par percent nikalna. Hike hamesha purani salary par ginte hain, nayi par nahi.
  2. CTC ko in-hand samajhna. CTC me PF, gratuity aur kai bhatte hote hain jo har mahine haath me nahi aate.
  3. Variable pay ko pakka maan lena. Bonus aur variable performance par nirbhar hote hain — poora milega ye zaroori nahi.
  4. Tax slab na dekhna. Badi hike se slab badal sakta hai, jisse haath me badhotri thodi kam lagti hai.
  5. Mehngai bhool jaana. Mehngai se kam hike ka matlab hai kharidne ki taakat kam ho gayi.

Bharat me kya alag hai

  • Aam hike: Bharat me saalana hike aam taur par 8-12% ke beech rehti hai, industry par nirbhar.
  • Job change: Company badalne par 25-50% tak hike aam hai.
  • Promotion: Promotion ke saath hike alag hoti hai aur aam taur par zyada.
  • Form 12BB: Nayi salary ke baad HR ko investment declaration dobara dena padta hai.
  • EPF: Basic badhne par PF ka contribution bhi badhta hai — wo bhi aapka hi paisa hai.

Hike ka asli faayda — percentage se nahi, in-hand se naapein

10% hike ka matlab 10% zyada paisa haath me aana nahi hota. Katouti aur tax slab ke chalte asli badhotri kam nikalti hai. Aur mehngai ke baad ka hisaab alag hai.

Isi table se ek saaf natija nikalta hai: jis saal hike mehngai se kam mile, us saal aap sach me peeche gaye — salary badhi dikhti hai par khareedne ki taakat ghat jaati hai. Doosri baat, naukri badalne par milne wale bade hike ke saath aksar chhupi laagat hoti hai — nayi jagah notice period, gratuity ka dobara 5 saal ka intezaar, aur variable pay ka anupaat badal jaana. Offer ki tulna karte waqt hike ka percentage nahi, haath me aane wali mahine ki raqam saath rakh kar dekhein.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Salary hike percentage kaise nikaalein?

(Nayi − Purani) ÷ Purani × 100.

Achhi hike kitni maani jaati hai?

Bharat me 10% se oopar aam taur par achhi maani jaati hai.

CTC hike aur in-hand hike alag kyun hote hain?

Kyunki CTC me PF, gratuity aur variable bhi jude hote hain.

Hike ke baad tax badhta hai?

Slab badalne par haan. Nayi in-hand dekhne ke liye CTC calculator istemaal karein.

Mehngai ke baad asli hike kya hai?

Hike% me se mehngai% ghata dein — jo bacha wahi asli badhotri hai.
