Uttarkashi ki sanskriti ek jagah ki nahi, kai ghatiyon ki milijuli pehchan hai. Bhagirathi ghati me Gangadi boli aur Gangotri-Harsil ki parampara hai, Yamuna ghati (Rajgarhi-Barkot) me Koti Banal jaise purane makaan hain, Mori-Purola-Naitwar me Mahasu aur Pokhu jaisi devta-parampara aur Parvati boli hai, aur oonchi seema ki ghati me Jad Bhotia samuday ka jeevan hai. Shahar ke beech Barahat ka Magh Mela, Raithal ka Anduri (Makkhan) Utsav, Raso aur Tandi nritya, Dhol-Damau ki taal aur gaon-gaon ke devta ki doli yahan ki sanskriti ko jeevit rakhte hain.

Inme se kai parampara Kumaon ki hain, Uttarkashi ki nahi. Hum yahan har cheez ke saath saaf likh rahe hain ki wo Uttarkashi ki hai, Garhwal ki hai, ya Kumaon ki. Isse aapko sahi aur bharosemand jaankari milegi.

Uttarkashi: Aabadi, Jaatiyan Aur Janjatiyan (Demographics)

Census Ke Aankde: Aabadi, Sex Ratio, Sakshrata Aur Gaon-Shehar Ka Vibhajan

Census 2011 ke anusaar Uttarkashi ki aabadi 3,30,086 thi, jisme 1,68,597 purush aur 1,61,489 mahilayein shaamil thi. Zile me 67,602 parivar (households) the, aur aabadi ghanatva lagbhag 41 vyakti prati varg km tha.

Zile ki aabadi ka bahut bada hissa gaon me rehta hai, aur shehar (Uttarkashi, aur kuch kasbe) me aabadi bahut kam hai. Pichhli janganana ke aankde ka hi hum yahan istemal kar rahe hain.

Ek aur dhyan dene wali baat: Zile ki ek sarkari yojna ke dastavez me aabadi ka figure 3,48,624 likha hua hai, jo Census 2011 se alag hai. Hum Census wala figure (3,30,086) hi istemal kar rahe hain, kyonki wo ek sthayi, sarkari aur sabse zyada upyog hone wala aankda hai.

Sex ratio zile me lagbhag 958 hai, jo desh ke average se zyada hai.

Janjatiyan: Bhotia (Jad), Jaunsari Aur Anya

Census 2011 ke anusaar Uttarkashi me Anusuchit Janjati ki aabadi lagbhag 3,500 (kul aabadi ka lagbhag 1.06%) thi. Inme sabse zyada Bhotia (lagbhag 2,061), phir Jaunsari (lagbhag 1,089) aur Buksa (lagbhag 75) the.

Ye samuday mukhya roop se Kumaon aur Chamoli ke hain. Uttarkashi me Bhotia ke roop me mukhya roop se Jad Bhotia hain.

Jad Bhotia samuday: Ye samuday Jadh Ganga ghati (Nelong, Jadung) ke roop me jaane jaate hain, aur 1962 ke yuddh ke baad ghati khali karwane ke baad unka bahut hissa Dunda aur Bagori ke aas-paas basne ke roop me bataya jata hai. Ye jaankari kai sources me alag-alag roop me milti hai.

Uttarkashi: Samajik Sanrachna, Upnaam Aur Gotra

Pramukh Upnaam (Surnames)

Uttarkashi me Rajput, Brahmin, Shilpkar (Anusuchit Jaati) aur janjatiyon ke kai upnaam sunai dete hain. Ye upnaam Garhwal ke baaki hisson se milte-julte hain, par har ghati me alag-alag upnaam zyada milte hain.

Neeche sirf wo naam diye gaye hain jo Garhwal aur Uttarkashi ke kshetra me aam taur par sune jaate hain.

Upnaam aur gotra ke bare me ek saaf baat: Kisi bhi samuday ki ginti ya suchi se hamara uddeshya kisi ko upar ya neeche dikhana nahi hai. Ye sirf jaankari ke liye hai.

Mool Niwas (Thaat) Aur Pahchan

Pahad me "mool niwas" ya "thaat" ka matlab hai parivar ka puraana gaon, jahan unke puraane ghar aur kul-devta hote hain. Palayan ke baad bhi kai parivar apne mool gaon se jude rehte hain.

Mool niwas ki pehchan aaj bhi kai kaam me zaroori hoti hai: kul-devta ki pooja, shaadi-byaah me, aur zameen ke record me. 2025 ke naye land law me bhi "mool niwasi" ki pehchan ka sawal sankhya me aaya hai (Uttarkashi: Zameen Kharidne Ke Niyam Aur Section 143 dekhein).

Shaadi ke niyam (jaise ek gotra me vivah ka nishedh) gaon, jaati aur parivar ke hisaab se alag hote hain.

Uttarkashi: Bhasha, Boliyan Aur Aam Shabd

Uttarkashi Me Bolee Jaane Wali Bhashayein Aur Boliyan

Uttarkashi me sabse zyada bolee jaane wali bhasha Garhwali hai, aur sampark bhasha ke roop me Hindi ka istemal bahut hota hai. Garhwali ki kuch boliyan, jaise Gangadi (Bhagirathi ghati me) aur Parvati (Mori block me), is zile se judi batayi jaati hain.

Garhwali ko UNESCO ki "Atlas of the World's Languages in Danger" me "vulnerable" shreni me rakha gaya hai, yaani is bhasha ko bachane ke liye lagataar koshish zaroori hai.

Uttarkashi: Pahadi Vastukala: Pahadi Ghar Aur Koti Banal

Koti Banal: Uttarkashi Ki Bhukamp-Rodhak Pahadi Shaili

Koti Banal Uttarkashi ke Rajgarhi (Yamuna ghati) kshetra ki lakdi aur patthar ki ek purani vastukala shaili hai, jise bhukamp ke liye bahut surakshit mana jata hai. Shodh ke anusaar ye shaili lagbhag 900 se 1,000 saal puraani maani jati hai.

Koti Banal ka naam Koti Banal gaon se pada, jo Barkot ke uttar me Yamuna ke paar sthit hai. Is shaili ki khaas baatein:

Bhukamp me pradarshan: World Housing Encyclopedia ki report ke anusaar 1991 ke Uttarkashi bhukamp (tivrata mb 6.6) me kai nayi imaaratein dhah gayi, jabki Koti Banal imaaraton ko nuksan nahi hua, aisi prattyaksha-darshiyon ki jaankari bhi mili hai. Is shaili ke bare me shodh karne wale vaigyanikon ne ise bhukamp-surakshit nirman ki ek bahut mahatvapurn mishaal mana hai.

Chintajanak baat: Kai Koti Banal imaaraton ki sthiti kamzor ho rahi hai, kyonki unki dekhbhal, sanrakshan aur jaagrukta ki kami hai. Shodh me kaha gaya hai ki aaspaas ke naye nirman aur puraani imaarton ko todkar saamagri nikalne se bhi unko nuksan hota hai.

Aap kya kar sakte hain: Agar aap Rajgarhi-Barkot ke aas-paas ke kshetra me jaate hain, to in imaaraton ko aadar ke saath dekhein, unhe nuksan na pahunchayein, aur local gaonwalon se unke bare me poochein.

Aam Pahadi Ghar Ka Naksha Aur Uske Hisse

Pahadi ghar me aksar neeche ki manzil me pashu rehte hain (goth ya obra) aur upar ki manzil me parivar. Isse pashuon ki garmi se upar ki manzil bhi garm rehti hai.

Ye shabd Garhwal me aam taur par sune jaate hain, par har ghati me inka naam aur roop thoda alag ho sakta hai. Isliye is table ko "aam samajh" ke roop me hi dekhein.

Aaj ki sthiti: Aaj kal pahad me RCC ke ghar bahut banaye jaa rahe hain, par kai jagah bhukamp-rodhak design aur sahi neev (foundation) ka dhyan nahi rakha jaata. Isliye ghar banane se pehle engineer se salah lein (Uttarkashi: Geology, Soil Types Aur Seismic Profile aur Uttarkashi: Makaan Ka Naksha, Seismic Niyam Aur Nirman dekhein).

Uttarkashi: Paramparik Pehnawa Aur Vastra

Mahilaon Ka Pehnawa

Garhwal ki mahilayein paramparik roop se angra (choli), ghagri ya dhoti, aur dhatu (sir ka dupatta) pehanti thi. Tyohar aur shaadi par rang-birangi pichhoda aur gehne pehne jaate the.

Uttarkashi me alag-alag samuday ka pehnawa alag hai. Jaunsari-Rawain kshetra ki mahilayein Jhagga (lambi frock) aur Dhantu jaise vastra pehanti hain, aur oonchi ghatiyon ke Bhotia samuday me oonn ke garam kapde.

Purushon Ka Pehnawa

Garhwal ke purush paramparik roop se mirzai, sadri (jacket), kurta-pajama, oonni coat aur pahadi topi pehante the. Sardi me oonni kapde aur "dora" jaisi topi bhi pasand ki jaati hai.

Aaj kal gaon me bhi shirt-pant aur jeans aam hain, aur paramparik vastra zyadatar tyohar, mele aur shaadi me dikhte hain. Pahadi topi aur oonni shawl aaj bhi Uttarkashi ke bazaar me milti hain, aur Bhotia samuday ke oonni utpad (Bhotia Dann jaise) me unki parampara dikhti hai (Uttarkashi: Pashupalan, Ghumantu Pashupalan Aur Dairy).

Uttarkashi: Paramparik Pahadi Gehne

Gale, Naak, Kaan Aur Haath Ke Gehne

Garhwal ki paramparik gehnon me Nathuli (bada naak ka nath), Galobandh (gale ka gehna), Hansuli (chaandi ka mota haar), Pahunchi (kalai ka gehna), Bulak (naak ke beech ka gehna), Murkhi (kaan ka gehna) aur Bichhua (pair ke gehne) shaamil hain.

Aaj kal sone ke daam aur sunar ki kala ke badalne se paramparik gehnon ki jagah aadhunik gehne zyada pehne jaate hain.

Uttarkashi: Lok-Kala: Chitrakala Aur Lakdi Ka Kaam

Aipan, Jyunti Aur Garhwal Ki Samanya Chitrakala

Aipan Kumaon ki paramparik rangoli kala hai, jisme geru (laal mitti) aur chawal ke ghol se pooja ke sthan par aakritiyan banayi jaati hain. Ye Uttarkashi ki mukhya kala nahi hai, par Garhwal me bhi ghar ki pooja ke sthan par chowk aur aakritiyan banane ki parampara hai.

Uttarkashi ke gaon me lakdi ki naqqashi (darwaze, khidki aur Tibari par) aur Bhotia samuday ki oonni kala zyada pehchaan rakhti hain.

Lakdi ka kaam: Koti Banal jaise purane makaanon aur mandir ke darwaze, chaukhat aur balconies par lakdi ki naqqashi dikhti hai. Zile me ringal (pahadi baans) ke utpad aur oonni galeeche bhi paramparik shilp ka hissa hain (Uttarkashi: Phool, Mushroom, Madhumakkhi-Palan Aur Chai aur Uttarkashi: Pashupalan, Ghumantu Pashupalan Aur Dairy).

Uttarkashi: Paramparik Lok-Nritya

Raso, Tandi, Pandav Nritya Aur Doli Nritya

Uttarkashi me Raso aur Tandi nritya Magh Mela aur Raithal ke Anduri Utsav jaise avsar par dekhe jaate hain. Pandav Nritya (Pandav Lila) aur devta ki Doli ka nritya bhi yahan ki khaas pehchan hain.

Chholiya, Barada Nati aur Harul ke bare me: Chholiya (talwar-dhaal ka nritya) mukhya roop se Kumaon ka hai. Barada Nati aur Harul Jaunsar-Bawar (Dehradun zile) ki parampara me aate hain.

Uttarkashi: Paramparik Vadya Aur Bajgi/Auji Samuday

Dhol-Damau, Ransingha, Bhankora Aur Anya Vadya

Pahad ke tyohar, mele aur anushthan me Dhol-Damau sabse aham vadya hai. Inke saath Ransingha (S-aakar ka tambe ka narsingha), Bhankora aur Masakbeen (bagpipe) bhi baje jaate hain.

Dhol Sagar: Maana jata hai ki Dhol Sagar ek paramparik shastra hai, jisme dhol ke alag-alag taal (rhythm) ka vivaran hota hai, jaise shaadi, devta ki pooja aur utsav ke liye alag taal. Taal ki sankhya (jaise 84) ke bare me alag-alag sources alag baat kehte hain.

Bajgi aur Auji samuday: Pahadi dhol vadya parampara ko jeevit rakhne me Bajgi/Auji samuday ki bahut badi bhumika rahi hai. Ye samuday gaon ke har utsav, shaadi aur devta ki pooja ka ek zaroori hissa raha hai, par samajik-aarthik drishti se unhe kai baar bahut kam samman aur sahayata mili. Hum is par ek saaf baat likh rahe hain: ye kala aur kalakaar samman ke haqdaar hain.

Uttarkashi: Jeevan-Chakra Ke Sanskar Aur Vivah

Namkaran, Janeu, Vivah Aur Antim Sanskar

Pahad me bhi baaki Hindu parivaron ki tarah janm se mrityu tak ke sanskar kiye jaate hain, jaise namkaran, anna-prashan, mundan, janeu aur vivah. Har ghati aur parivar me inke tareeke thode alag ho sakte hain.

Vivah ki aam roop-rekha (Garhwal ki aam parampara ke anusaar, har parivar me alag ho sakti hai):

1.Rishta aur tilak: Dono parivaron ki baat pakki hone par.

2.Ganesh pooja aur haldi: Vivah se pehle ki rasmein.

3.Baraat ka aagman: Dhol-damau ke saath baraat vadhu ke ghar aati hai.

4.Dhuli-arghya aur kanyadan: Vivah ke mukhya sanskar.

5.Phere aur sindoor: Vivah ko poora karne ki rasmein.

6.Vidai: Vadhu ka apne maayke se vida hona.

Rasmon ke naam aur kram (jaise "Suwal Pathai" aur "Anchali") ilaake ke hisaab se badalte hain.

Antim sanskar: Garhwal me aam taur par antim sanskar nadi ke kinare kiya jaata hai. Uttarkashi me Bhagirathi ke kinare ke ghat par aisa hota hai. Teharvi (13ve din) tak ke anushthan parivar ki parampara ke anusaar kiye jaate hain.

Zaroori note: Ye jaankari aam parichay ke liye hai. Kisi bhi sanskar ke liye apne parivar ke buzurgon aur purohit ki salah hi sabse bharosemand hai.

Uttarkashi: Mausami Tyohar

Uttarkashi Ke Mukhya Tyohar Aur Mele

Uttarkashi ke mukhya tyohar hain: Makar Sankranti (Magh Mela), Akshaya Tritiya (Gangotri aur Yamunotri ke kapat khulna), Anduri Utsav (Dayara Bugyal), Bhai Dooj (Yamunotri ke kapat band hona) aur Diwali (Gangotri ke kapat band hona).

Phool Sankranti (Phool Dei) Kumaon me bahut mashhoor hai, aur Garhwal ke kuch hisso me bhi manayi jaati hai.

Budhi Diwali / Egaas: Garhwal aur Jaunsar ke kai kshetron me Diwali ke kuch din baad ek alag Diwali manayi jaati hai.

Uttarkashi: Aitihasik Mele, Jatra Aur Doli Yatra

Barahat Ka Magh Mela, Anduri Utsav Aur Doli Yatra

Uttarkashi ka sabse mashhoor aitihasik mela Magh Mela hai, jise sthaniya bhasha me "Barahat Ku Thaulu" kehte hain. Isme Kandar Devta, Hari Maharaj aur anya gaon ke devta ki palkiyan Uttarkashi aati hain.

Magh Mela (Barahat Ku Thaulu) ki roop-rekha:

1.Makar Sankranti par mela shuru hota hai.

2.Aas-paas ke gaon se devta ki palkiyan (doli) dhol-damau ke saath Uttarkashi aati hain.

3.Kandar Devta ko Barahat patti ke mukhya devta ke roop me jaana jaata hai.

4.Log Bhagirathi me snaan karte hain.

5.Palkiyan Barahat ke "Chamala ki Chaunri" me jaati hain aur wahan Doli Nritya aur Raso-Tandi nritya hota hai.

6.Ramleela maidan par mela ka aayojan hota hai.

Is mele ki avadhi alag-alag sources me alag likhi milti hai, isliye hum sirf "Makar Sankranti se kuch din" likh rahe hain.

Anduri Utsav (Makkhan Mela), Raithal aur Dayara Bugyal: Ye mela Raithal gaon ke log Dayara Bugyal par manate hain. Isme makkhan aur chhaachh se Holi ki tarah khelte hain. Maana jata hai ki ye pashuon ki raksha ke liye devta ke prati aabhar prakat karne ka utsav hai. Raithal me Someshwar Devta paanch gaon ke saanjhe devta maane jaate hain, aur utsav me Raso aur Pandav Nritya hote hain.

Doli Yatra ki mahatta: Pahad me gaon ke devta ki palki (doli) dharmik aur samajik ekta ka pratik hai. Doli ke saath dhol-damau aur ransingha ki dhun hoti hai. Doli ko laane-le jaane ki ek paramparik vyavastha hoti hai, aur kai jagah ye saal me nishchit samay par hi nikalti hai.

Gangotri aur Yamunotri ki Doli: Akshaya Tritiya ke aas-paas Gangotri ki Doli Mukhba se aur Yamunotri ki Doli Kharsali se dham ki taraf aati hai (Uttarkashi: Micro-Climatic Zones Aur 12-Month Weather Calendar aur Uttarkashi: Itihas, mandir aur lok-katha dekhein).

Kumaon aur Chamoli ke mele: Uttarayani, Jauljibi, Thal, Bageshwar, Nanda Devi, Syalde-Bikhoti, Gindi aur Nanda Raj Jat Uttarkashi ke mele nahi hain. Nanda Raj Jat Chamoli zile se judi 12-saal wali yatra hai.

Mele me jaane ke tips:

1.Mele ki taareekh pehle se confirm karein.

2.Bheed me bachchon aur buzurgon ka dhyan rakhein.

3.Devta ki palki aur pooja sthal par samman se pesh aayein, tasveer lene se pehle poochein.

4.Mausam ke hisaab se garam kapde saath rakhein.

Selku, Losar aur Gendua: Uttarkashi ke Teen Anokhe Mele

Seedha jawab: Selku upari Bhagirathi ghati ka phoolon ko vidaai dene wala utsav hai — "Selku" ka arth lok-boli me "soyega kaun" bataya jata hai, kyunki ye raat bhar chalta hai. Losar Jad Bhotia samuday ka naya saal hai, jo Phalgun Shukla Pratipada ko teen din manaya jata hai. Gendua Mela Mori kshetra ka mela hai, jisme Purola ki Singtur aur Panchgai patti ke lagbhag 24 gaon ke log bhaag lete hain.

Uttarkashi: Chhote Sawal-Jawab

Uttarkashi me kaun si bhasha bolee jaati hai?
Garhwali sabse zyada bolee jaati hai, aur Hindi sampark bhasha ke roop me istemal hoti hai.
Gangadi boli kahan bolee jaati hai?
Gangadi Garhwali ki ek boli hai, jo Uttarkashi me Bhagirathi nadi ki ghati me bolee jaati hai.

Parvati boli kahan bolee jaati hai? Parvati boli Uttarkashi ke Mori block me bolee jaati hai.

Jad Bhotia kaun hain?
Jad Bhotia Uttarkashi ke oonche seema kshetra (Jadh Ganga/Nelong ghati) ka ek Bhotia samuday hai, jisme Tibeto-Burman bhasha Jad bolee jaati hai.
Uttarkashi me janjati ki aabadi kitni hai?
Census 2011 ke anusaar Uttarkashi me Anusuchit Janjati ki aabadi lagbhag 3,500 (lagbhag 1.06%) thi.

Uttarkashi me anusuchit jaati ki aabadi kitni hai? Census 2011 ke anusaar lagbhag 24.41%.

Koti Banal kya hai?
Koti Banal Uttarkashi ke Rajgarhi (Yamuna ghati) kshetra ki lakdi aur patthar ki bhukamp-rodhak purani vastukala shaili hai.
Koti Banal gaon kahan hai?
Koti Banal gaon Uttarkashi me Barkot ke uttar me Yamuna ke paar sthit bataya jaata hai.
Koti Banal imaarat kitni purani hai?
Shodh ke anusaar ye shaili lagbhag 900 se 1,000 saal puraani maani jaati hai.
Koti Banal imaarat kitni manzil ki hoti hai?
Shodh me aksar paanch manzil ki imaaratein batayi gayi hain.
Kya Koti Banal imaaraton ko 1991 ke bhukamp me nuksan hua tha?
World Housing Encyclopedia ke anusaar prattyaksha-darshiyon ke vivaran me Koti Banal imaaraton ko nuksan na hone ki baat kahi gayi hai, jabki kai nayi imaaratein dhah gayi thi.
Goth ya Obra kya hota hai?
Goth ya Obra pahadi ghar ki neeche ki manzil hai, jahan pashu rakhe jaate hain.
Tibari kya hota hai?
Tibari lakdi ki naqqashi wala teen-mehrabi baithne ka sthan hai.
Pathal chhat kya hoti hai?
Pathal (slate) ki chhat patthar ki patliyon se bani chhat hoti hai.
Magh Mela Uttarkashi me kab hota hai?
Magh Mela Makar Sankranti (lagbhag 14 January) se shuru hota hai.
Barahat Ku Thaulu kya hai?
Barahat Ku Thaulu Uttarkashi ke Magh Mela ka sthaniya naam hai.
Kandar Devta kaun hain?
Kandar Devta Barahat patti ke mukhya devta maane jaate hain, jinki doli Magh Mela me aati hai.
Barahat kya hai?
Barahat Uttarkashi shahar ka puraana naam aur puraana kshetra hai.
Anduri Utsav kya hai?
Anduri Utsav (Makkhan Mela) Dayara Bugyal aur Raithal me manaya jaane wala utsav hai, jisme makkhan aur chhaachh se Holi ki tarah khelte hain.

Anduri Utsav kab hota hai? Ye utsav aam taur par August me hota hai (Incredible India ke anusaar 2026 me 16 aur 17 August).

Raithal gaon kahan hai?
Raithal gaon Uttarkashi me Dayara Bugyal ke raaste ka ek gaon hai.
Someshwar Devta kaun hain?
Someshwar Devta Raithal jaise paanch gaon ke saanjhe devta maane jaate hain.
Raso nritya kya hai?
Raso Garhwal ka samuhik loknritya aur geet hai, jo Uttarkashi ke Magh Mela aur Anduri Utsav me dekha jaata hai.
Tandi nritya kya hai?
Tandi bhi ek samuhik loknritya aur geet hai, jo Uttarkashi ke mele me dikhta hai.

Pandav Nritya kya hai? Pandav Nritya Mahabharat ke Pandavon se jude paatron ka nritya-natya hai, jo dhol-damau ke saath hota hai.

Dhol-Damau kya hai?
Dhol aur Damau pahad ke tyohar, mele aur shaadi ke sabse mukhya vadya hain.
Ransingha kya hai?
Ransingha tambe ka S-aakar ka narsingha vadya hai.
Masakbeen kya hai?
Masakbeen pahad ka bagpipe hai.
Bajgi samuday kaun hain?
Bajgi ya Auji samuday pahadi dhol vadya parampara ko jeevit rakhne wala samuday hai.
Jagar kya hota hai?
Jagar gayan ke saath devta ya shakti ko jagane ka anushthan hai.
Khuded geet kya hote hain?
Khuded geet virah aur ghar ki yaad ke lok-geet hain.
Kya Aipan Uttarkashi ki kala hai?
Nahi, Aipan mukhya roop se Kumaon ki paramparik kala hai.
Kya Harela aur Bhitauli Uttarkashi ke tyohar hain?
Nahi, Harela, Bhitauli aur Ghughutiya mukhya roop se Kumaon ke tyohar hain.
Nanda Raj Jat kahan hoti hai?
Nanda Raj Jat Chamoli zile se judi 12-saal wali yatra hai, ye Uttarkashi ki yatra nahi hai.
Gangotri ki Doli kahan se aati hai?
Akshaya Tritiya ke aas-paas Gangotri ki Doli Mukhba se aati hai.

Yamunotri ki Doli kahan se aati hai? Akshaya Tritiya ke aas-paas Yamunotri ki Doli Kharsali se aati hai.

Yamunotri ke kapat kab band hote hain?
Yamunotri ke kapat Bhai Dooj (Yam Dwitiya) ke aas-paas band hote hain.
Garhwali bhasha ki sthiti kya hai?
Garhwali ko UNESCO ke Atlas of the World's Languages in Danger me "vulnerable" shreni me rakha gaya hai.
