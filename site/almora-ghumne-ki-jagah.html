Almora ki 20 Sabse Zaroori Ghumne ki Jagah

Har Jagah ki Unchai, Mukhyalaya se Doori, Entry Fee, Parking aur Samay

Seedha jawab: Almora zile ki sabse prasiddh jagahein hain Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary, Kasar Devi, Jageshwar Dham, Katarmal Surya Mandir, Chitai Golu Devta, Nanda Devi Mandir, Ranikhet, Dwarahat aur Dunagiri. Almora shahar se inki doori 500 meter se lekar lagbhag 78 km tak hai. Entry fee aur samay badalte rehte hain, isliye jane se pehle sambandhit karyalay se confirm karna behtar hai.

Almora ghoomne wale aksar do galti karte hain. Pehli, wo sab kuch ek din me nipta dena chahte hain. Dusri, wo Kausani aur Munsiyari ko Almora zile ka hissa maan lete hain. Kausani Bageshwar zile me aur Munsiyari Pithoragarh zile me hai. Is page ki list me sirf wahi jagahein hain jo Almora zile me aati hain.

Entry fee, parking aur samay: Zyadatar mandiron me koi pravesh shulk nahi hota. Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary me pravesh ke liye van vibhag ka shulk lagta hai, jiski dar badalti rehti hai. Parking ke liye Almora shahar me jagah kam hai. Mall Road aur Lala Bazar ke aas-paas gaadi chalana mushkil hota hai, isliye bahar gaadi khadi kar ke paidal ghoomna behtar rehta hai.

Almora: Hill Station aur Weekend par Ghumne ki Jagah

Lokpriya Kasbe aur Shaant Pahadi Gaon (Himalaya ke Nazaron Wale)

Seedha jawab: Almora zile ke popular hill station hain Almora shahar, Ranikhet aur Binsar. Shaant aur kam bheed wale thikane hain Kasar Devi, Majkhali, Shitlakhet aur Jawalbanj jaise ridge-top ilake. Ranikhet Almora se lagbhag 50 km aur Kathgodam se lagbhag 68 km door hai.

Almora zila “ek jaisa pahadi shahar” nahi hai. Yahan teen tarah ke thikane milte hain: purani sanskritik nagri (Almora), faujee shaanti ka kasba (Ranikhet) aur jungle ke beech ki ooche ridge wali jagahein (Binsar aur Kasar Devi). Aapki yatra ka mood kaisa hai, us hisab se jagah chunein.

Binsar ke Himalaya nazaare: Wikipedia ke anusar Binsar ke Zero Point se Kedarnath, Chaukhamba, Shivling, Trishul, Nanda Devi, Nanda Kot aur Panchachuli ke nazaare dikhte hain. Yeh nazaara mausam saaf hone par hi milta hai. Barsaat me aur dhund wali subah me choti dikhna mushkil hota hai, isliye subah jaldi pahunchna behtar hota hai.

Ranikhet ki khaas baatein: - Chhavni 1869 me bani maani jati hai, aur yahan Kumaon Regiment aur Naga Regiment ke kendra hain. - Chaubatia me seb, khubani, aadu, chestnut aur badam ke bagh hain. - Ranikhet ka golf course Upat Kalika me hai, jo shahar se lagbhag 5 km door hai. - 2011 ki janganana me Ranikhet ki jansankhya lagbhag 18,886 thi.

Kab kahan jayein: Garmiyon me Almora aur Ranikhet sukhad rehte hain. Barf dekhne ki ichha ho to sardi me Binsar aur ooche ridge ka mausam dekh kar nikalein (Almora: Mahine-dar-Mahine Yatra Guide me mahine-wise guide hai).

Almora: Kam Jaane-Pehchane aur Chhupe Gaon

Sadak Wale aur Paidal Wale Gaon: Slow Travel, Kalakar aur Birdwatcher ke Liye

Seedha jawab: Almora zile me kai chhote gaon aur ilake aise hain jahan bheed kam hai aur gaon ki zindagi dekhi ja sakti hai. Page ke sources me Majkhali, Tarikhet, Syalikhet, Baburkhola, Jawalbanj, Kalimath, Barechhina (Lakhudiyar) aur Kakrighat jaise naam milte hain. Inme se kuch sadak par hain aur kuch paidal pahunchne wale hain.

Almora: Khaali Gaon (Palayan) aur Virasat ki Sair

Khali Hote Gaon aur Almora ki Heritage Walk

Seedha jawab: Almora zile ki jansankhya 2001 se 2011 ke beech ghati thi, jo palayan ka sanket hai. Is wajah se kai pahadi gaon khali ya adhe-khali ho gaye hain. Kin gaon me kitne ghar band hain, iska pakka aankda Rural Development & Migration Commission ya Census se milta hai.

Palayan aur uski kahani: Palayan ka matlab hai gaon se rozgar, shiksha aur sehat ki talash me shahar chale jana. Is par Almora mein Palayan Vapasi ki Safal Kahaniyan me “Palyan Vapasi” ki kahaniyan aur Almora: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh me jungli janwaron se khet ka nuksan vistaar se likha gaya hai. Yahan sirf itna samajhna zaroori hai ki “khali gaon” ek dukhad sach bhi hai aur tourism ke liye ek avsar bhi.

Khali gaon ko kaise dekhein: - Khali gaon kisi ki nijee sampatti hote hain. Bina anumati ke kisi ghar me na ghusein aur kuch na uthayein. - Jahan aaj bhi parivar rehte hain, wahan ke logon ki kahani sunna zyada arthpoorn hota hai. - Khali gaon ko “bhootiya gaon” ya “horror tourism” ki tarah dikhane se bachein. Yeh logon ki yaadein aur virasat hain.

Almora: Trekking ki Poori Suchi (Aasan, Madhyam aur Kathin)

Ek Din ki Hike, Weekend Trek, Lambe Trek aur Oonche Darre

Seedha jawab: Almora zile me bade barf-wale trek ya high-altitude pass nahi hain. Yahan ke trek jungle, mandir aur ridge walk wale hain. Sabse prasiddh route hai Kasar Devi se Katarmal, Binsar aur Jageshwar tak ka 4 din ka trek, jiske liye March se May aur September se November sabse achha samay mana jata hai.

Is zile ko “Himalaya ke trek ka base” na samjhein. Almora se aap Himalaya ko dekhte hain, par Pindari, Kafni ya Milam jaise bade trek Bageshwar aur Pithoragarh se shuru hote hain. Almora ke trek chhote, shaant aur sanskriti se bhare hote hain.

Ek 4 din ka sample route (trekking site ke anusar): - Din 1: Kathgodam se Almora, phir Kasar Devi tak trek aur wahan camp. - Din 2: Kasar Devi se Katarmal ke darshan aur wapas Kasar Devi. - Din 3: Kasar Devi se Binsar, phir Jawalbanj tak 3 km walk (Sapteshwar Mahadev mandir ke raste se). - Din 4: Sunrise Point, phir Jageshwar aur wapasi.

Trek ke sunehre niyam: 1. Pahadi trek me paani sabse bada mudda hai. Hamesha kam se kam 2 litre paani saath rakhein. 2. Sanctuary ya jungle me camping ke liye van vibhag ki anumati zaroori hai. 3. Garmi me ek hi din me 8 se 10 km se zyada chadhna na rakhein, agar aap shuruwat kar rahe hain. 4. Mausam vibhag ka alert dekh kar hi nikalein. 5. Kisi bhi paidal raste par akele na jayein.

Local guide se baat kar ke hi tay karein.

Almora: Parvatarohan, Chattan Chadhai aur Bouldering

Kya Almora mein Barf Wali Badi Chotiyan Hain?

Seedha jawab: Almora zile me koi IMF (Indian Mountaineering Foundation) dwara listed barf wali choti nahi hai, kyunki yeh Lesser Himalaya ka zila hai. Nanda Devi, Trishul aur Panchachuli jaisi chotiyan Almora se dikhti hain, lekin unka base camp aur chadhai Chamoli, Bageshwar ya Pithoragarh se hoti hai.

Is baat ko seedhe samjhein: Mountaineering ke liye barf wali, 5,000 m se oonchi chotiyan chahiye hoti hain. Almora zile ki sabse ooche ilake lagbhag 2,400 se 2,500 m tak hi pahunchte hain (Binsar ke Zero Point ke aas-paas). Isliye yahan mountaineering expedition nahi hoti.

Almora me kya mil sakta hai: - Chhoti pahadiyon par rock climbing aur rappelling ke liye sthaniya adventure operators se baat karein. - Mountaineering ka asli shauk rakhne wale Pithoragarh, Chamoli ya Bageshwar se jaayein. Un zilon ke page par isi Almora: Parvatarohan, Chattan Chadhai aur Bouldering me poori jaankari milegi.

Permit aur porter dar par saavdhani: HAP (High Altitude Porter) dar aur permit fees sarkari aur IMF ke notification ke anusar badalte hain.

Almora: Sardi ke Khel: Skiing, Snowboarding aur Barf mein Trek

Kya Almora mein Ski Slope Hai?

Seedha jawab: Almora zile me koi sthayi ski slope ya ski lift nahi hai. Sardiyon me kabhi-kabhi Binsar aur Kasar Devi jaise ooche ilake me halki barf girti hai, par yeh pakki nahi hoti. Skiing ke liye Uttarakhand me Auli (Chamoli) jana padta hai.

Aksar log “Almora me snowfall dekhne” ki ummeed me jaate hain aur ghar laut aate hain bina barf dekhe. Isliye saaf samajh lena zaroori hai ki Almora shahar me barf girna kam hota hai, jabki Binsar jaise ooche ilake me kabhi-kabhi girti hai. Almora: Mausam ke Zone aur 12 Mahine ka Mausam me mausam ka poora table hai.

Agar barf dekhni ho: 1. Sardi (December se February) me mausam ki jaankari dekh kar hi plan banayein. 2. Barf girne par sadak band hone ka khatra hota hai. Raasta dekh kar nikalein. 3. Garam kapde, achhe joote aur gaadi me chain rakhein. 4. Barf ka pakka intezam chahiye to Auli, Munsiyari ya Chopta jaisi jagahein dekhein (yeh Almora ke bahar ke zilon me hain).

Winter courses: NIM (Nehru Institute of Mountaineering) aur GMVN ke winter courses Uttarkashi aur Auli jaise sthanon par hote hain.

Almora: Paani ke Khel: Rafting, Kayaking aur Mahseer Machhli Pakadna

Rafting ke Hisse, Grade aur Machhli Pakadne ka Permit

Seedha jawab: Almora zile me vyavsayik river rafting ya kayaking ka koi sthapit stretch nahi hai. Kosi, Suyal aur Ramganga jaise nadiyan yahan behti hain, par yeh chhoti pahadi nadiyan hain. Rafting ke liye log Rishikesh (Ganga) ya Pithoragarh aur Champawat ke Kali ya Sharda ka rukh karte hain.

Machhli pakadna (angling): Pahadi nadiyon me machhli pakadne ke liye aam taur par sambandhit vibhag (Matsya ya van vibhag) ki anumati lagti hai. Golden Mahseer ek sanrakshit prajati ki taraf ishara karti hai aur uska shikar kai jagah par pratibandhit ya sirf catch-and-release ke saath anumat hai.

Pani ke kinare ki suraksha: Barsaat me Kosi, Suyal aur gadhere achanak bhar sakte hain. Nadi me nahane ya photo khinchne ke liye utarna khatarnak ho sakta hai (Almora: Aapda Prabandhan aur Emergency Vyavastha aur Almora: Purani Aapdayein, Bhukamp aur Sambhalne ki Kahani me aapda ka itihas).

Almora: Hawai Khel, Paragliding, Mountain Biking aur Camping

Paragliding ki Jagah, MTB Raaste aur Manzoor Camping

Naukuchiatal (Nainital) jaisi jagahein paragliding ke liye jaani jati hain, jo Almora zile ke bahar hain. Almora me camping aur cycling ka maza liya ja sakta hai, par anumati aur suraksha ke saath.

Camping: Almora ke aas-paas Kasar Devi, Binsar ke bahar ke ilake aur kai gaon me private camping site milti hain. Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary ke andar camping ke niyam sakht hote hain, isliye van vibhag se likhit anumati ke bina camp na lagayein.

Camping ke niyam: 1. Aag sirf tay jagah par jalayein. Chir ke jungle me aag lagna sabse bada khatra hai (Almora: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh). 2. Shor na karein, khaaskar raat ko. 3. Plastic aur kachra wapas le jayein. 4. Jungli janwar ko khana na dalein.

Mountain biking (MTB): Almora, Ranikhet, Majkhali aur Binsar ki pahadi sadkein cycling ke shaukeen logon ko aakarshit karti hain. Almora mein Cycling aur Daud ke Raaste me cycling aur marathon ke routes alag se likhe jayenge. Cycling karte samay helmet, light aur sadak ke mod par savdhani zaroori hai.

Almora: Taare Dekhna, Astro-Tourism aur Photography ki Jagah

Andhera Aasman, Milky Way aur Suryoday-Suryast ki Chotiyan

Seedha jawab: Almora ke ooche ridge aur jungle wale ilake (jaise Binsar aur Kasar Devi ke aas-paas) shahar ki roshni se door hone ki wajah se tare dekhne ke liye achhe mane jate hain. Photography ke liye Binsar ka Zero Point, Bright End Corner aur Majkhali prasiddh hain.

Bortle scale aasman ki andhere ki darja batata hai. Kisi jagah ko “Bortle 1 ya 2” kehne ke liye maap-tol ya vaigyanik survey chahiye hota hai.

Almora: Taiyar Yatra Yojana (3, 5, 7 aur 10 Din)

Yatra Yojana: Weekend, Parivar-Mandir, Backpacker aur Poora Zila

Seedha jawab: Almora ghoomne ke liye 3 din me shahar, Kasar Devi aur Binsar dekhe ja sakte hain. 5 din me Jageshwar aur Katarmal jud jate hain. 7 din me Ranikhet aur Dwarahat aate hain. 10 din me poora zila, trek aur gaon ke anubhav ke saath dekha ja sakta hai.

Kathgodam (nearest railway station) se Almora ki doori lagbhag 90 km ke aas-paas hoti hai aur sadak se 3 se 4 ghante lagte hain (doori aur samay mausam aur traffic par nirbhar karte hain). Ranikhet Kathgodam se lagbhag 68 km hai.

Almora: Mahine-dar-Mahine Yatra Guide (January se December)

Mahine ke Hisaab se: Kya Khula, Sadak, Phool-Barf ka Mausam aur Saman

Seedha jawab: Almora ghoomne ka sabse achha samay March se May aur September se November mana jata hai. March-April me buransh khilta hai. Barsaat (June se August) me bhuskhalan ka khatra hota hai, aur sardi (December se February) me thand aur kabhi-kabhi barf hoti hai.

Kab bachein: Tourist season (May-June) me Almora aur Ranikhet me bheed aur parking ki dikkat hoti hai. Barsaat me pahadi sadkon par savdhani rakhein.

Sabse achha mahina kaun sa: Agar aapko Himalaya ke nazaare chahiye to October-November. Agar buransh chahiye to March-April. Agar bheed se bachna hai to September ke aas-paas jaayein (Nanda Devi mele ki bheed ke alawa).

Packing checklist: - Aadhaar ya koi photo ID - Garam kapde (layers me), raincoat ya chhata - Anti-slip joote, torch, power bank - Zaroori dawaiyan, paani ki bottle - Nakad paisa (kai gaon me ATM aur network kam hote hain, Almora: Mobile Network aur Internet: Asli Sthiti aur Almora: Bank, ATM, Dakghar aur Mandi Samiti)

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal: Almora: Tourism

Almora me ghoomne ki sabse achhi jagah kaun si hai?
Binsar, Kasar Devi, Jageshwar Dham, Katarmal aur Chitai Golu Devta sabse prasiddh hain.
Kya Kausani Almora me hai?
Nahi, Kausani Bageshwar zile me hai.
Kya Munsiyari Almora me hai?
Nahi, Munsiyari Pithoragarh zile me hai.
Almora ghoomne ka sabse achha samay kaun sa hai?
March se May aur September se November.
Binsar me kaun si Himalaya ki chotiyan dikhti hain?
Kedarnath, Chaukhamba, Shivling, Trishul, Nanda Devi, Nanda Kot aur Panchachuli (mausam saaf hone par).
Binsar Almora se kitna door hai?
Lagbhag 26 se 30 km.

Jageshwar Almora se kitna door hai? Lagbhag 35 se 37 km.

Ranikhet Almora se kitna door hai?
Lagbhag 50 km.

Kasar Devi Almora se kitna door hai? Lagbhag 8 se 10 km.

Almora me barf kab girti hai?
Sardiyon me (December se February) ooche ilake me kabhi-kabhi, par pakki nahi hoti.
Kya Almora me skiing hoti hai?
Nahi, skiing ke liye Auli (Chamoli) jana padta hai.
Kya Almora me river rafting hoti hai?
Almora me sthapit rafting stretch nahi hai; rafting ke liye Rishikesh jaisi jagahein dekhein.
Ranikhet me kya dekhne layak hai?
Chaubatia ke bagh, Jhula Devi mandir, Haidakhan mandir, Mankameshwar mandir, KRC museum aur golf course.
Kathgodam se Almora kitna door hai?
Lagbhag 90 km, sadak se 3 se 4 ghante (mausam aur traffic par nirbhar).
Almora me kitne din kaafi hain?
3 din me mukhya jagahein, 5 se 7 din me aaraam se poora zila.

बिनसर अल्मोड़ा से कितनी दूर है?
लगभग 26 से 30 किमी।
बिनसर से हिमालय की कौन सी चोटियां दिखती हैं?
मौसम साफ हो तो केदारनाथ, चौखंबा, शिवलिंग, त्रिशूल, नंदा देवी, नंदा कोट और पंचाचूली।
रानीखेत अल्मोड़ा से कितनी दूर है?
लगभग 50 किमी।
काठगोदाम से अल्मोड़ा कितनी दूर है?
लगभग 90 किमी, सड़क से 3 से 4 घंटे (मौसम और ट्रैफिक पर निर्भर)।
अल्मोड़ा घूमने के लिए कितने दिन काफी हैं?
3 दिन में मुख्य जगहें, 5 से 7 दिन में आराम से पूरा जिला।
