Ye demo kyun?

Bahut log puchhte hain ki banne ke baad cheez kaisi dikhegi. Isliye har aam kaam ka ek chhota, chalta hua namoona yahan hai. Aapka asli app isi tarah ka hoga, par aapke dhandhe ke hisaab se: aapka naam, logo, rang, aapke saman ki list, aapke tax ke rate, aapke customer ka data aur aapki zaroorat ke button. Demo mein jo aap bharte hain wo sirf aapke phone/browser mein rehta hai — hum use kahin save nahi karte.

Asli app mein aur kya jud sakta hai: kai log (staff) ka login, roz ki report WhatsApp ya Telegram par, Google Sheet mein backup, PDF bill customer ko seedha bhejna, barcode, aur mahine ka hisaab. Jo aapko chahiye wahi banta hai — zaroorat se zyada kuch nahi, taaki kharcha kam rahe aur chalana aasaan ho.

Rate "se" likha hai. Aapka kaam dekh kar likhit quote milta hai jisme version number hota hai — sirf wahi pakka hai. Advance ke baad kaam shuru hota hai, pehle demo dikhaya jaata hai, 2 badlav shaamil hain, aur final code, file aur account aapke naam par milta hai. Poori shartein Terms ke section 8 mein hain, aur saare kaam ki suchi rate list par.

Kaise shuru karein

Jo demo aapke kaam ke paas ho, uska "Apne naam se banwao" button dabaiye. Form mein bas apna naam, WhatsApp number aur do-teen line mein apni zaroorat likhiye - jaise "meri kapde ki dukaan hai, 3 staff hain, roz 40 bill bante hain, udhaar wale customer bhi hain". Hum padh kar likhit quote bhejte hain. Pasand aaye to advance dijiye, hum pehle ek demo aapke naam se banakar dikhate hain, phir aapke kehne par badlav karte hain. Sab theek lage tab baaki payment, aur poora app aapke account par chalu. Koi chhoti cheez baad mein badalni ho to care plan le sakte hain, jo kabhi bhi band ho sakta hai.
