FD me paisa rakhne se pehle jaan lein ki maturity par kitna milega. Ye calculator rakam, byaj dar aur saal ke hisaab se aapki FD ki poori value nikaal deta hai — compounding ke saath, jaise bank karta hai.

FD ka byaj kaise banta hai

Zyadatar bank FD par quarterly compounding karte hain — yaani har teen mahine ka byaj mool rakam me jud jaata hai aur agle quarter me us badhi hui rakam par byaj lagta hai. Isi liye FD ka asli return (effective yield) bataye gaye rate se thoda zyada nikalta hai.

Formula: A = P × (1 + r/4)4n
P = jama rakam, r = saalana byaj (decimal me), n = saal.

FD ke fayde aur kamiyan

Fayda: return pehle se tay, market ka koi asar nahi, paisa surakshit. Bank me ₹5 lakh tak DICGC insurance bhi hota hai. Isi wajah se emergency fund aur 1-3 saal ke lakshya ke liye FD sabse theek hai.

Kami: return mehngai se thoda hi upar hota hai. Agar FD 7% de rahi hai aur mehngai 6% hai, to aapki asli badhotri sirf 1% ke aaspaas hai. Lambe samay ke lakshya ke liye sirf FD par nirbhar rehna paise ko peeche chhod deta hai.

Tax ka hisaab

FD ka byaj aapki income me judta hai aur slab ke hisaab se tax lagta hai. Ek tay limit se zyada byaj hone par bank TDS kaat leta hai. Agar aapki total income tax ke daayre me nahi aati, to saal ki shuruaat me bank ko Form 15G (senior citizen ke liye 15H) dekar TDS rukwa sakte hain.

2 chhoti tips

  • Senior citizen ko aam taur par 0.5% zyada dar milti hai — ghar me buzurg hain to unke naam FD karwana faydemand.
  • FD todne par penalty lagti hai aur byaj kam ho jaata hai. Isliye ek badi FD ki jagah 3-4 chhoti FD banayein — zaroorat par sirf ek todni padegi.

Teen asli misaal — number ke saath

Misaal 1 — aam FD. ₹5,00,000 ki FD, 7% dar, 5 saal, quarterly compounding. Maturity lagbhag ₹7,07,000 — yaani kareeb ₹2.07 lakh byaj.

Misaal 2 — senior citizen. Wahi ₹5 lakh, par senior citizen ko aam taur par 0.5% zyada milta hai — 7.5%. Maturity lagbhag ₹7,25,000. Sirf aadhe percent se ₹18,000 ka farak.

Misaal 3 — tax ka asar. Upar wali FD me ₹2.07 lakh byaj bana. Agar aap 30% slab me hain, to ismein se lagbhag ₹62,000 tax me chala jaayega. Haath me kareeb ₹1.45 lakh bacha — yaani asli return 7% nahi, lagbhag 4.9% nikla.

Yahi sabse zaroori baat hai: FD ka jo rate dikhta hai wo tax se pehle ka hai. Apne slab ke hisaab se asli return nikaal kar hi doosre vikalpon se tulna karein.

Log yahan galti kya karte hain

  1. Tax ka hisaab na lagana. FD ka byaj poori tarah aapki income me judta hai aur slab ke hisaab se tax lagta hai. 30% slab wale ke liye 7% ki FD asal me ~4.9% deti hai.
  2. Form 15G/15H na bharna. Agar total income tax ke daayre me nahi aati, to saal ki shuruaat me ye form dene se TDS katna hi band ho jaata hai. Warna refund ke liye ITR bharna padta hai.
  3. Poora paisa ek hi FD me daalna. Zaroorat padne par poori todni padti hai aur penalty lagti hai. Laddering karein — ₹5 lakh ki ek ki jagah ₹1-1 lakh ki paanch FD.
  4. Ek hi bank me badi rakam rakhna. Har bank me jama rakam par ₹5 lakh tak DICGC insurance hota hai (mool + byaj milakar). Badi rakam 2-3 banko me baantna zyada surakshit hai.
  5. Chhote co-operative bank ke oonche rate ke peeche bhaagna. Wahan thodi zyada dar milti hai par risk bhi zyada hota hai.

Bharat me FD ki khaas baatein

  • Compounding: zyadatar bank quarterly compounding karte hain. Isi liye 7% ki FD ka asli yield thoda zyada nikalta hai.
  • Premature withdrawal: beech me todne par do nuksan — penalty (aam taur par 0.5-1%) aur byaj us samay tak ki dar par mil jaata hai, poori avadhi wali dar par nahi.
  • Tax-saving FD: 5 saal ka lock-in hota hai aur Old Regime me 80C ki chhoot milti hai — par byaj phir bhi taxable rehta hai.
  • Sweep-in FD: savings account se juda hota hai — zaroorat padne par paisa apne aap aa jaata hai. Emergency fund ke liye ye sabse suvidhajanak vikalp hai.
  • Senior citizen: aam taur par 0.5% extra milta hai, aur unke liye byaj par TDS ki limit bhi zyada hoti hai.

Savdhi jama (FD) ka byaj — cumulative ya masik

Log ise alag-alag naam se dhoondhte hain — FD, savdhi jama, ya fixed deposit. Hisaab ek hi hai, par do kism hoti hain aur unka farak bada hai.

Cumulative FD: Maturity = Rakam × (1 + r÷4)4×t
Yahan r saalana byaj hai aur t saal. Zyadatar bank me byaj har teen mahine judta hai.
  • Cumulative — byaj judta rehta hai aur poora paisa maturity par milta hai. Yahan compounding kaam karti hai, isliye kul rakam zyada banti hai.
  • Non-cumulative (masik byaj) — byaj har mahine ya teen mahine me khaate me aa jaata hai. Rakam nahi badhti, par ek nishchit aay milti rehti hai. Bujurgon aur retire ho chuke logon ke liye yahi zyada kaam ka hai.

₹5 lakh, 7% byaj, 5 saal — dono ka farak:

KismKya milega
Cumulativelagbhag ₹7.07 lakh (byaj karib ₹2.07 lakh)
Masik byajlagbhag ₹2,917 har mahine + mool dhan waapas
Ek baat jo zyadatar log dhyaan nahi dete — FD ka byaj poori tarah tax me aata hai, aapke slab ke hisaab se. 30% slab wale ke liye 7% ki FD asal me lagbhag 4.9% ki reh jaati hai, aur mehngai ghatane ke baad kuch khaas nahi bachta. Agar aapki kul saalana aay tax ke daayre se neeche hai, to bank me Form 15G (60 se kam umar) ya 15H (60+) zaroor bhariye — TDS katega hi nahi. Har saal April me, aur har bank me alag se dena hota hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

FD par byaj kitni baar judta hai?

Zyadatar bank quarterly (har 3 mahine) compounding karte hain, isliye asli return bataye rate se thoda zyada nikalta hai.

FD par TDS kab katta hai?

Ek saal me byaj ek tay limit paar kare to bank TDS kaat leta hai. Income tax ke daayre me na aane par Form 15G/15H dekar rokwa sakte hain.

FD beech me tod sakte hain?

Haan, par penalty lagti hai aur byaj kam mil sakta hai. Isliye badi FD ki jagah kai chhoti FD banana behtar rehta hai.

FD ya mutual fund — kya behtar?

1-3 saal aur suraksha ke liye FD. 5 saal se lambe lakshya ke liye mutual fund aam taur par zyada return deta hai, par risk ke saath.

Kya FD ka paisa surakshit hai?

Har bank me ₹5 lakh tak (mool + byaj) DICGC ka insurance hota hai. Isliye badi rakam alag-alag bank me baantna samajhdaari hai.

Savdhi jama byaj dar ki ganana kaise karein?

Cumulative FD me: rakam × (1 + byaj÷4) ki power (4 × saal). Upar wale calculator me rakam, dar aur saal daal dijiye — maturity aur kul byaj dono nikal aayenge.

Savdhi jama masik byaj kitna milega?

Non-cumulative FD me byaj har mahine ya teen mahine milta hai. ₹5 lakh par 7% ke hisaab se lagbhag ₹2,917 mahina, aur mool dhan maturity par waapas.

Byaj calculator me cumulative chunein ya masik?

Paisa badhana ho to cumulative, kyunki wahan byaj judta rehta hai. Har mahine aay chahiye to non-cumulative. Ek hi rakam par dono ka natija kaafi alag hota hai.
