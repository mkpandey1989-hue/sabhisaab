Chamoli: Puranik Bhugol: Kedarkhand Aur Badrikashram

Kedarkhand Kya Hai

Skanda Purana ke do mukhya khand Uttarakhand se jude mane jaate hain: Kedarkhand, jo Garhwal kshetra ke tirthon aur nadiyon ka varnan karta hai, aur Manaskhand, jo Kumaon kshetra ke liye mana jata hai. Is parampara me Himalaya ke is kshetra ko “Devbhoomi” aur “Tapobhoomi” kaha gaya hai. Chamoli ka Badrikashram, Alaknanda ghati, Panch Prayag aur Joshimath is Kedarkhand ke dharmik bhugol ka hissa mane jaate hain.

Man se likhi hui sloka-sankhya galat hone par badi galti ban jati hai. Is hisse ko poora karne ke liye mool granth ya kisi pramanik anuvad ka naam, khand aur adhyay-sankhya ke saath jodein.

Badrikashram Ki Manyata

Parampara ke anusar Badrikashram wo sthan hai jahan Bhagwan Vishnu ne Nar aur Narayan ke roop me tapasya ki. Isliye iske paas ke parvat “Nar” aur “Narayan” parvat kehlate hain. Badri (jangli ber) ke vriksh ke naam par is sthan ka naam Badrinath pada, aisi manyata hai. Ye sab dharmik manyatayein hain.

Mahabharat Se Jure Sthan

Mana gaon ke paas Vyas Gufa hai, jisse manyata ke anusar Maharshi Vyas ne Mahabharat ki rachna ya uska lekhan jodkar dekha jata hai. Paas me Bhim Pul hai, jo Saraswati nadi par prakritik chattan ka ek pul hai, jiska sambandh kai log Pandavon ki Swargarohan yatra se jodte hain. Swargarohini shikhar Uttarkashi zile me hai.

Saraswati Aur Keshav Prayag

Mana ke paas Alaknanda me milne wali Saraswati nadi aur unka sangam Keshav Prayag dharmik drishti se mahatvapurn mana jata hai (Chamoli: Pavitra Sangam). Saraswati nadi ka Mana ke aage ka srot aur uski pauranik pehchan alag-alag manyataon me alag hai.

Kalidas Aur Himalaya

Sanskrit ke mahakavi Kalidas ke granthon (Meghdoot, Kumarasambhava) me Himalaya ka sundar varnan milta hai. Kuch log Meghdoot ki “Alaka” nagri ko Himalaya ke is kshetra se jodte hain, par ye vidwanon ke beech ek bahas ka vishay hai.

Chamoli: Pramukh Mandir, Gurudwara Aur Math

Badrinath Dham

Badrinath Alaknanda ke kinare lagbhag 3,133 meter ki oonchai par hai. Ye Char Dham aur Chhote Char Dham dono me ginta hai. Mandir ke mukhya pujari Rawal hote hain, jo parampara se Kerala ke Namboodiri Brahmin hote hain. Mandir ke prabandhan ka kaam Shri Badrinath-Kedarnath Mandir Samiti (BKTC) karti hai (Mandir Samiti ka kanoon 1939 ka mana jata hai, iska sahi naam aur saal Samiti ki site se confirm karein). Parampara me Adi Shankaracharya ko Badrinath ki punahsthapna se joda jata hai.

2026 ki kapat tithiyan (BKTC ki ghoshna ke anusar):

  • Kapat khule: 23 April 2026, subah 6:15 baje. Tithi Basant Panchami par Narendra Nagar ke Tehri Raj Darbar me tay hui thi.
  • Kapat band hone ki tithi: 16 November 2026, raat 9:37 baje (kark lagn me). Ye tithi Vijayadashami (20 October 2026) ko Rawal Amarnath Namboodiri aur Samiti ke adhikariyon ki upasthiti me ghoshit hui.
  • Panch Puja: 12 November se shuru: 12 November ko Shri Ganesh ji ke kapat band, 13 November ko Adi Kedareshwar ji ke kapat band, 14 November ko Khadag-Pustak ka vachan aur Ved path vishram, 15 November ko Mata Lakshmi ko garbhgriha me virajman hone ke liye aamantran, aur 16 November ko mukhya kapat band.
  • Char Dham ke anya kapat (2026): Gangotri 10 November, Kedarnath aur Yamunotri 11 November.
  • Kai yatra websites ne pehle 6, 13 ya 20 November jaisi tithiyan likhi thi, par official tithi 16 November hai. Isse saaf hai ki ek hi baat ke liye alag-alag blog par bharosa na karein.

Sheetkaleen gaddi sthal: Kapat band hone ke baad Badri Vishal ki puja do jagah hoti hai: Pandukeshwar (jahan Uddhav ji aur Kuber ji ki murtiyan rehti hain) aur Joshimath ke Narsingh Mandir (jahan Adi Shankaracharya ki gaddi pahunchti hai). Pichhle saal (2025) ki prakriya ke anusar Shankaracharya ki gaddi 27 November ko Narsingh Mandir, Jyotirmath pahunchi thi.

Badrinath ke paas ke sthal: Tapt Kund (garam paani ka kund), Narad Kund, Brahma Kapal (pind-daan ka sthan), Mana gaon, Vyas Gufa, Bhim Pul, Vasudhara jharna.

Panch Kedar Me Se Do: Rudranath Aur Kalpeshwar

Panch Kedar me se Rudranath (Gopeshwar ke paas ke jungle aur bugyal se paidal trek ke baad) aur Kalpeshwar (Urgam ghati me) Chamoli zile me hain. Baaki teen (Kedarnath, Tungnath, Madhmaheshwar) Rudraprayag zile me hain. Rudranath ek kathin trek ke baad pahunchne wala mandir hai, jabki Kalpeshwar tak paidal marg chhota hai aur ise Panch Kedar me saal bhar khula rehne wala mana jata hai (kai yatra sources me aisa likha hai, par mandir samiti se confirm karein).

Joshimath (Jyotirmath)

Jyotirmath (Joshimath) Adi Shankaracharya dwara sthapit char mathon me se uttar ka math mana jata hai. Yahan ka Narsingh Mandir Badrinath ke sheetkaleen gaddi sthal ke roop me mahatvapurn hai. Joshimath ke Jyotirmath peeth ke Shankaracharya ke pad ko lekar kuch vivad bhi rahe hain, isliye page par kisi vyakti ko “asli” ya “nakli” Shankaracharya na kehein.

Gopeshwar Ka Gopinath Mandir

Gopeshwar me Gopinath Mandir Chamoli ka ek mahatvapurn Shiv mandir hai. Mandir ke aangan me ek lohe ka trishul hai jise parampara se aur kuch itihaskaron ke anusaar kai sadiyon purana mana jata hai. Trishul par ek lekh hone ki baat aam hai. Is trishul ke upar ASI ya kisi shodh-patra ka naam dekar likhna behtar hoga.

Nanda Devi Ke Mandir

  • Nanda Devi Siddhapeeth, Kurud (Nandanagar): Nanda Devi Badi Jat 2026 ka praarambhik sthan (Chamoli: Melein, Jatra Aur Doli Yatra).
  • Nauti gaon ka Nanda Devi mandir: Nanda Raj Jat samiti ka kendra.
  • Badhangarhi mandir (Tharali kshetra, Gwaldam ke paas): Kshetra ki Nanda Devi / Durga se jude mandir ki parampara. Ek itihaskaar sandarbh ke anusar 1670 me Chand raja Baz Bahadur ne Badhangarh par hamla karke Nanda Devi ki murti Almora le jaane ka zikra milta hai (Wikipedia ke anusar).
  • Latu Devta mandir, Wan gaon: Latu Devta Nanda Devi ke “bhai” mane jate hain, aur Raj Jat ke raste me Wan gaon ke Latu Devta mandir ka mahatva hai. Parampara ke anusar mandir ke pujari aankhon par patti bandhkar puja karte hain (Wikipedia aur anya yatra sources me ye varnan milta hai, par is parampara ke baare me sthaniya jankar se confirm karein).

Hemkund Sahib Aur Lokpal Lakshman Mandir

Hemkund Sahib ek Sikh gurudwara hai jo Hemkund (Lokpal) jheel ke kinare lagbhag 15,000 se 15,200 feet (lagbhag 4,570 se 4,630 meter) ki oonchai par hai, aur isse duniya ka sabse ooncha gurudwara kaha jata hai. Ye Sikhon ke dasve Guru, Guru Gobind Singh ji se jura hai, jinka zikr Dasam Granth me is sthan ke prasang me mana jata hai. Jheel ke kinare Lokpal Lakshman Mandir bhi hai, jise Lakshman ji ki tapasya se joda jata hai.

2026 ka season (news reports ke anusar):

  • Lokpal Lakshman mandir ke kapat 22 May 2026 ko aur Hemkund Sahib ke kapat 23 May 2026 ko khule.
  • Management Trust ke adhyaksh ne yatra ke samapan ki tithi 10 October 2026 ghoshit ki thi, aur isi din Hemkund Sahib tatha Lokpal Lakshman mandir ke kapat sheetkaal ke liye band hue.
  • ETV Bharat ke anusar 2026 me lagbhag 3,84,573 shraddhaluon ne darshan kiye.
  • 2027 ke season ki tithi ki ghoshna abhi nahi hui hai (Oct 2026 ke ek yatra sources ke anusar). Taaza jaankari ke liye Management Trust ki site dekhein.

Yatra ka raasta: Rishikesh se Govindghat tak sadak, phir Pulna hote hue Ghangaria tak lagbhag 9 se 10 km ka paidal marg, aur Ghangaria se Hemkund Sahib tak lagbhag 6 km ki kadi chadhai (sources ke anusar). Yatra me registration zaroori hai. Oonchai ki wajah se altitude sickness ka jokhim hota hai (Chamoli: Glacier Aur Snowfield).

Anya Pramukh Mandir

  • Anusuya Devi mandir (Mandal ke paas): Mandir ki jaankari ke liye sthaniya sources dekhein.
  • Karnaprayag ka Uma Devi mandir aur Nandprayag ka Gopal mandir: Prayag ke paas ke mandir.
  • Gauchar, Pipalkoti, Pokhri aur Gairsain ke sthaniya devi-devta ke mandir.
  • Har mandir ke liye ek chhota block rakhein: Naam, sthan, devta, tithi/mela, kaise pahunchein, aur kuch sthaniya parampara.

Mele Aur Utsav (Mandir Se Jude)

Badrinath me September me Mata Murti Ka Mela lagne ka zikra milta hai (yatra sources me, Mandir Samiti se tithi confirm karein). Is mele me Nar-Narayan ki mata Mata Murti ke darshan hote hain, aisi manyata hai.

Mandir Me Jaane Ke Niyam

Mandir me darshan, vastra aur tasveer ke niyam mandir samiti tay karti hai. Mandir ke andar tasveer ya video ka niyam kai jagah alag hota hai.

Bansi Narayan Mandir: Saal me Sirf Ek Din Khulne Wala Mandir

Seedha jawab: Bansi (Vanshi) Narayan Chamoli ki Urgam ghati ke upar sthit Bhagwan Vishnu ka prachin mandir hai. Lok-manyata ke anusar iske kapat saal me sirf Raksha Bandhan ke din khulte hain, jab aas-paas ke gaon ki mahilayein yahan rakhi bandhne aati hain (pushti karein: paidal doori aur tithi sthaniya mandir samiti se confirm karein).

Mandir Kalpeshwar (Panch Kedar) ke kshetra me hai, isliye Urgam ghati aane wale yatri Kalpeshwar ke saath iski yojana banate hain. Raasta ghane jungle aur bugyal se hokar jata hai, isliye sthaniya guide ke saath jayein.

Chamoli: Ishta Devta, Gram Devta Aur Jagar

Gram Devta Ki Parampara

Garhwal ke har gaon ka apna “gram devta” ya “kul devta” hota hai, jo gaon ki raksha karne wale mane jaate hain. Ye devta aksar sthaniya naam wale hote hain (jaise Bhumiyal, Narsingh, Nanda Devi, Latu, Bhairav), aur har devta ki apni kahani, mandir aur utsav hota hai. Is parampara me aastha bahut gehri hoti hai.

Kumaon Aur Anya Kshetra Ke Devta: Chamoli Ke Nahi

Inme se zyadatar Kumaon (Golu, Haru Heet, Airi, Kalbisht), Jaunsar kshetra (Mahasu) ya Uttarkashi (Pokhu) ke devta hain. Aap ek chhota note de sakte hain: “Kumaon me Golu Devta ko nyay ke devta ke roop me poojte hain.” Ye baat aapke page ko Chamoli ke liye adhik sahi banayegi.

Jagar Kya Hota Hai

Jagar ek paramparik anushthan hai jisme jagariya (gayak) devta ki gatha gaata hai, aur dhol-damau jaise vaadya bajte hain. Isme maana jata hai ki devta kisi vyakti ke sharir me “avatarit” hote hain, jise sthaniya bhasha me alag-alag naam se pukara jata hai (jaise kuch jagah “pashwa”, “dangariya” ya anya naam). Ye shabd aur unke arth kshetra aur parampara ke hisab se badalte hain, isliye sahi naam apne gaon se confirm karke jodein. Jagar ki vidhi, samay (kai raat tak chal sakta hai) aur niyam gaon-gaon me alag hote hain.

Jagar Se Jude Sensitive Pahlu

Jagar aastha ka vishay hai. Isliye:

  • Kisi bhi shaarirak ya mansik bimari ke ilaaj ke roop me jagar ko na batayein. Bimari ke liye doctor ki salah lein.
  • Jagar ki recording ya tasveer sirf anumati ke saath hi lagayein.

Chamoli: Pragaitihasik Kaal, Shailashray Aur Pashan Avshesh

Chamoli Me Insaan Kab Se Hain

Uttarakhand ke itihas ke sandarbhon me ye baat milti hai ki yahan rock paintings, rock shelters, purane patthar ke auzaar aur megaliths (bade patthar ke smarak) se pata chalta hai ki pahadon me insaan pragaitihasik kaal se rehta aaya hai. Is sandarbh me Chamoli ke kai sthan mahatvapurn hain.

Lakhudiyar Almora zile me hai aur Bankot Pithoragarh zile me, Chamoli me nahi. Chamoli ke sthalon ko pehle rakhein.

Malari Ki Khoj

Malari (Dhauliganga ghati) me hue utkhanan se alag-alag kaal ke avshesh mile. Kuch sources me ise lagbhag 1500 BCE ke Vaidik kaal se jodkar bataya jata hai, jabki ek ASI jaankari me isme pehle alag period ke saath mitti ke bartan aur loha-taamba pighlane ke saboot likhe gaye hain. Alag-alag kaal ke dave sources me alag milte hain.

Megalith Aur Cup-marks

Megalith ek tarah ke patthar ke smarak ya kabr hote hain. Chamoli ke Amarpur me aayatakar cist kabr wale megalith ka zikra hai. Cup-marks (patthar par chhote gol gaddhe) ke bare me ek shodh-patra me kaha gaya hai ki kai jagah ye rock paintings ke saath bhi paye gaye. Inka sahi arth abhi vidwanon ke beech bahas ka vishay hai.

Dhyan Rakhne Wali Baatein

Ye sthal naazuk hain aur kai jagah surakshit nahi hain. ETV Bharat ke ek report me sthaniya logon ne Gorkhya Udiyar ke sanrakshan ki maang ki thi.

  • Yahan ki tasveer sirf door se lein.
  • Kisi bhi mile hue avshesh ya patthar ko na uthayein, aur khoj hone par Archaeological Survey of India ya zila prashasan ko batayein.

Chamoli: Kuninda Aur Katyuri Kaal

Kuninda: Sikkon Wali Pracheen Satta

Kuninda ek pracheen Himalayi ganrajya tha, jise dusri sadi BCE se tisri sadi CE ke aaspaas ka mana jata hai. Kuninda ke sikke Garhwal aur Kumaon ke kshetra me bhi mile hain, jinme raja Amoghabhuti ke sikke prasiddh hain. Chamoli ka Kuninda kaal se sambandh kitna gehra tha, iske bare me alag-alag itihaskar alag raay dete hain.

Katyuri Kaal

Katyuri vansh Uttarakhand ke pehle badi rajya-satta wale vanshon me mana jata hai. Kai itihaskaaron ke anusar Katyuri raj Garhwal aur Kumaon dono kshetron par fela tha, aur iski rajdhani pehle Joshimath ke aaspaas (kuch sources me Kartikeyapur naam se) aur baad me Baijnath ke Katyur ghati (Bageshwar zile) me mani jati hai. Iska kaal kai sources me 7vi se 11vi sadi ya usse alag diya jata hai. Wikipedia ke ek sandarbh me 4th se 14th sadi ke beech Katyuri prabhav ka zikra hai, jabki anya me kam.

Pandukeshwar Ke Tamrapatra

Chamoli ke Pandukeshwar (Yogdhyan Badri mandir) se Katyuri kaal ke taamre ke patre (tamrapatra) mile hain, jinme rajaon ke daan aur anya vivaran darj hain. Inme Lalitashuradeva, Padmatadeva aur Subhiksharaja jaise raja ke naam ka zikra kai itihas sources me aata hai (naam aur kaal ko kisi pramanik itihas pustak se pushti karein karke hi likhein). Ye tamrapatra Uttarakhand ke itihas ke sabse mahatvapurn srot me ginte jaate hain.

Jyotirmath Aur Adi Shankaracharya Ka Samay

Adi Shankaracharya ko aam taur par 8vi sadi ka mana jata hai, aur unka Jyotirmath se sambandh parampara me hai. Mandiron me Katyuri shaili ki patthar ki shikhar-yukt sthapatya (Nagara shaili) ke udaharan milte hain, jise Kumaon ke Katarmal Sun Temple jaise mandiron se jodkar dekha jata hai (wo Almora me hai, Chamoli me nahi).

Chamoli: Panwar (Garhwal) Rajvansh Aur Garh

Garhwal Ka Naam Garh Se

“Garhwal” shabd “garh” (kila) se jura mana jata hai. Parampara ke anusar Garhwal kshetra pehle kai chhote rajyon (garhon) me bata hua tha, jin par Rana, Rai ya Thakur kehlane wale sthaniya sardar shasan karte the. Inki sankhya paramparik roop se 52 garh bataayi jati hai, par ek sandarbh me kaha gaya hai ki naye adhyayan me iski sankhya 160 se zyada nikali hai (Tribune ke ek lekh me Tehri ke Purana Darbar ke sanrakshak ka mat). Isliye “52 garh” ko ek paramparik sankhya ke roop me hi likhein.

Kanak Pal Aur Chandpur Garhi

Panwar vansh ke sansthapak Kanak Pal ke baare me kaha jata hai ki wo Malwa (Dhar) se Badrinath ke darshan ke liye aaye, aur Chandpur Garhi ke raja Bhanu Pratap se mile. Bhanu Pratap ke koi putra nahi tha, isliye unhone apni putri ka vivah Kanak Pal se karaya aur apna rajya unhe saunp diya. Chandpur Garhi Chamoli ke Karnaprayag ke paas hai. Is ghatna ka saal sources me alag milta hai: kuch 688 CE, kuch 823 CE aur kuch 888 CE batate hain. Isliye page par “9vi sadi ke aaspaas ya usse pehle” jaisi range likhna sahi hai.

Ajay Pal Aur Garhwal Ka Ekikaran

Raja Ajay Pal ko un garhon ko apne adhin karne wala mana jata hai, jisse is kshetra ko “Garhwal” naam mila. Unke samay ke bare me sources me bada fark hai: Wikipedia aur kuch sarkari dastavez 1358 CE (14vi sadi) ke aaspaas batate hain, jabki Tribune ke ek lekh me unka kaal 1500 se 1519 CE likha gaya hai. Rajdhani Chandpur se Devalgarh aur phir (kai sources me 1517 ke aaspaas) Srinagar (Garhwal) pahunchi. Ek sandarbh me ye bhi kaha gaya hai ki Ajay Pal ke naam ka koi shilalekh un garhon par nahi mila aur unse jude daave baad ke vrittanton aur maukhik parampara par aadharit hain.

Chamoli Ke Pramukh Garh

Chamoli kshetra ke jin garhon ke naam kai sources me milte hain:

  • Chandpur Garhi (Karnaprayag ke paas): Kanak Pal se judi kahani ka kendra.
  • Lobha (Lohba) Garh: Paramparik 52 garhon ki suchi me shamil mana jata hai, aur sthaniya itihas me ise Negi vansh ki lobhan shakha se joda jata hai.
  • Badhangarhi (Tharali kshetra): Mandir aur kile ke roop me prasiddh.
  • Nagpur Garhi: Nagpur pargana ki garhi.
  • Garhon ki poori suchi aur unki sthiti ke liye HNB Garhwal University ya sthaniya shodh-patra dekhein. Man se 52 naam likhne se bachein, kyunki naam ki spelling aur sthan alag-alag sources me alag milte hain.

Garhwal Aur Kumaon Ke Chand Raja Ke Beech Yuddh

Garhwal ke Panwar aur Kumaon ke Chand rajaon ke beech kai yuddh hue. Ek sandarbh me 1670 ke aaspaas Chand raja Baz Bahadur ka Badhangarh par aakraman aur Nanda Devi ki murti ko Almora le jaane ka zikra aata hai. Garhwal ke raja Fateh Shah (1684 se 1716 ke aaspaas, jo unke kaal ka shikhar mana jata hai) ka bhi itihas me naam aata hai.

Gunth: Mandir Ko Zameen Ka Daan

“Gunth” mandiron ko raja dwara diya gaya zameen ka daan hota tha, jisse mandir ka kharch chalta tha. Badrinath ke saath bhi aisi zameen ki parampara jodi jati hai. Udaharan ke liye Gauchar ki chaudi samtal zameen ko Panwar rajaon dwara Badrinath mandir ko daan di gayi zameen mana jata hai (Gauchar mela aur Wikipedia ke vivaran ke anusar). Ye zameen aaj bhi mandir ke adhikar me batayi jati hai.

Chamoli: Gorkha Shasan Aur Anglo-Nepal Yuddh

Gorkhyani: Gorkha Shasan Ka Samay

Nepal ke Gorkha shasakon ne 1790 ke aaspaas Kumaon par kabza kiya, aur 1803-04 me Garhwal par. Garhwal ke raja Pradyumna Shah 1804 me Dehradun ke Khurbura ke yuddh me maare gaye (kai itihas sources ke anusar). Is shasan ko log “Gorkhyani” kehte hain, jise kaafi kathor mana jata hai. Kai sources me iske daur ki bhari lagan aur logon ko dasa (gulam) ke roop me bechne ki prathayein darj hain.

Anglo-Nepal Yuddh (1814 se 1816)

British East India Company aur Nepal ke beech 1814 me yuddh chhidha. Almora me Gorkha sena ne 1815 me samarpan kiya (kai sources me 27 April 1815 ki tithi aati hai) aur yuddh ka ant Sugauli ki sandhi se hua (2 December 1815 ko hastakshar, March 1816 me pushti). Sandhi ke tahat Garhwal aur Kumaon ke bade hisse British ko mile.

Sandhi Ke Baad Garhwal Ka Batwara

Sandhi ke baad Garhwal do hisson me bata. Alaknanda ke purv ka hissa (“British Garhwal”) British ke adhin aaya, aur Chamoli ke zyadatar kshetra British Garhwal me aaye (seema ka sahi vivaran sources me alag-alag milta hai, isliye gazetteer se confirm karein). Pashchim ka hissa Raja Sudarshan Shah ko mila, jinhone Tehri ko rajdhani banaya (Tehri Riyasat). Chamoli ke kshetra me British shasan ka asar adhik raha.

Chamoli Me Gorkha Kaal Ki Pehchan

Chamoli kshetra mein Gorkha shasan se jude kisi khaas kile ya yuddh-sthal ka pakka naam kisi prakashit srot mein nahi milta, isliye yahan koi naam anumaan se nahi likha gaya.

Chamoli: British Kaal Aur Tehri Riyasat

British Garhwal Ka Prashasan

1815 ke baad Garhwal ka British hissa pehle Kumaon ke Commissioner ke adhin chalaya gaya, aur baad me Pauri Garhwal zila bana, jiska mukhyalaya Pauri hua. Chamoli ka kshetra British Garhwal ka hissa tha aur Pauri Garhwal zile ke tehsil ke roop me chalta tha. 1960 me Chamoli ko alag zila banaya gaya (Chamoli District: Executive Summary Aur Poori Pehchan). British kaal me gaon-gaon ki zameen ke “settlement” (zameen ke maap-tol aur lagan ka nirdharan) kai baar kiye gaye. Itihas ke sandarbhon me inme se kuch pramukh settlement ke prabhari adhikariyon ke naam ye milte hain: Traill, Batten, Beckett aur Pauw. Inme har ek ka settlement kis saal hua aur Chamoli par uska kya asar pada, ye jaankari kisi pramanik itihas pustak ya zila gazetteer se leni chahiye.

Gazetteer: Purane Kaal Ki Sachchi Dastavezi

British adhikari E. T. Atkinson ka “Himalayan Districts of the North-Western Provinces of India” aur H. G. Walton ka “British Garhwal: A Gazetteer” (1910) us samay ke kshetra, gaon, kheti aur samaj ka vistar se varnan dete hain (Chamoli: Purani Tasveerein, British Gazetteer Aur Archival Maps). In pustakon se aap Chamoli ke gaon, fasal, vyapar aur yatra ke raaste ke bare me aitihasik jaankari nikal sakte hain, par unki bhasha me us samay ke pakshpaat bhi ho sakte hain. Isliye unhe sandarbh ke roop me padhein, par aankhein band karke na maanein.

Forest Niyam Aur Gaon Ke Jungle

British kaal me jungle ke upyog ke niyam kade hue, aur Forest Act jaise kanoon aaye. Isse gaon walon ke jungle se chara, lakdi aur anya vanopaj ke paramparik adhikar prabhavit hue. Isi ke jawab me 1930 ke dashak (lagbhag 1931) me Van Panchayat ki vyavastha bani (Chamoli: Van Panchayat Aur Samudayik Van Adhikar). Ye ek vajah hai ki Garhwal me jungle aur gaon ke bhawnatmak rishte ka itihas itna gehra hai, aur isi parampara se aage chalkar 1970 ke dashak me Chipko Andolan ka janm hua (Chamoli: Swatantrata Sangharsh, Coolie Begar Aur Rajya Andolan).

Garhwal Rifles

Garhwal Rifles ki sthapana British kaal me 1887 ke aaspaas hui mani jati hai, jisme Garhwal ke jawan bharti kiye gaye. Regiment ka kendra Lansdowne (Pauri Garhwal) me hai, Chamoli me nahi. Pratham vishwa yuddh me Garhwal Rifles ke do jawanon ko Victoria Cross mila:

  • Naik Darwan Singh Negi VC: Chamoli ke Kafarteer gaon (Karnaprayag ke paas, lagbhag 25 km door) ke nivasi. Unka janm 4 March 1883 ko hua aur 24 June 1950 ko unka nidhan Kafarteer me hua. 1914 me Festubert (France) ke yuddh me unki bahaduri ke liye unhe Victoria Cross mila. Ek Tribune lekh ke anusar jab King George V ne unse unki ichha puchhi, to unhone Karnaprayag me ek school kholne ki maang ki, jise British sarkar ne poora kiya. Lansdowne me Garhwal Rifles ke museum ka naam unke naam par Darwan Singh Museum rakha gaya hai.
  • Rifleman Gabar Singh Negi VC: Tehri Garhwal ke Chamba ke nivasi, Chamoli ke nahi.
  • Darwan Singh Negi Chamoli ke gaurav ke roop me page par zaroor shamil karein.

Tehri Riyasat

Alaknanda ke pashchim ke hisse me Raja Sudarshan Shah ne 1815 me Tehri ko apni rajdhani banaya. Tehri Riyasat 1949 tak ek riyasat ke roop me chali aur phir Bharat me vileen ho gayi (kai sources me 1 August 1949 ki tithi aati hai). Chamoli ka kshetra British Garhwal me tha, isliye Tehri Riyasat ke raaj ka asar Chamoli par kam raha, par rajya ke hisse ke roop me Tehri ke Raj Darbar se Badrinath kapat ki tithi ki ghoshna ki parampara aaj bhi chali aati hai (Chamoli: Pramukh Mandir, Gurudwara Aur Math).

Badrinath Marg, Chatti Aur Dak Bungalow

British kaal me Badrinath yatra ka raasta paidal aur ghoda-gadha ka tha. Raste me “chatti” (padav) hote the, jahan yatri rukte the. Kai chatti naam aaj bhi jagah ke naam me bache hain, jaise Hanuman Chatti. Raste me British shasan ke samay Dak Bungalow aur anya sarkari vishram-grih bhi bane.

Gohna Tal Aur 1894 Ki Baadh

Birahi Ganga ghati me bhuskhalan se banne wali Gohna (Gaurna) jheel ka zikra British kaal ke records me milta hai. 1893 me bhuskhalan se jheel bani aur 1894 me toot gayi, jisse Alaknanda me baadh aayi (Chamoli Ke Aapda Itihas Aur Seekhein). Is ghatna ke vistar ke liye mool dastavez dekhein.

Chamoli: Swatantrata Sangharsh, Coolie Begar Aur Rajya Andolan

Coolie Begar: Chamoli Ka Sambandh Aur Sachchai

Coolie Begar (jabardasti mazdoori) ke khilaf January 1921 me Uttarayani ke mele par Bageshwar (Saryu-Gomti ke sangam) me bada andolan hua, jise Kumaon ke Badri Datt Pandey jaise netaon ne netritva diya (ye jaankari alag-alag itihas sources me milti hai). Ye andolan Bageshwar zile ka hai, Chamoli ka nahi. Garhwal me bhi is begar pratha ke khilaf awaz uthi thi.

Peshawar Kand (1930) Aur Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali

23 April 1930 ko Peshawar me ek andolan me Garhwal Rifles ke sainikon ko nihatthe pradarshankariyon par goli chalane ka aadesh mila, jise Chandra Singh Garhwali (Chander Singh Bhandari) ne nahi mana. Is ghatna ko “Peshawar Kand” kehte hain. Unka janm Pauri Garhwal ke Masaun (Meason) gaon me hua tha, Chamoli me nahi.

Chipko Andolan: Chamoli Ki Dharti Se Duniya Tak

Chipko Andolan Chamoli ki sabse mashhoor aitihasik dein hai. Iske mukhya tathya:

  • 1964: Chandi Prasad Bhatt ne Gopeshwar me Dasholi Gram Swarajya Sangh (DGSS) (jise baad me Mandal bhi kaha gaya) ki sthapna ki, jiska uddeshya jungle ke sansadhanon se chhote udyog chalana tha. Shuruaat khet ke auzaar banane ki ek chhoti workshop se hui.
  • 1970: Alaknanda ki baadh ke baad gaon walon me jungle ki suraksha ki chinta badhi.
  • 1973 (March): Allahabad ki Simon (Symonds) company ke thekedar tennis ke racket ke liye ash (angu) ke ped katne Gopeshwar pahunche.
  • 24 April 1973: Mandal gaon ke paas Chandi Prasad Bhatt aur DGSS ke karyakartaon ke netritva me gaon walon ne ped katne se roka. Ek sandarbh ke anusar 14 ash ke ped bachaye gaye. Isi ko Chipko ki shuruaat mana jata hai. “Chipko” shabd ka sambandh Garhwali shabd “angalwaltha” (gale lagna) se jodkar bataya jata hai.
  • 26 March 1974: Reni gaon (Hemalghati) me Gaura Devi ke netritva me gaon ki mahilaon ne jungle me ped katne aaye mazdooron aur thekedar ke logon ko roka. Us din purush gaon se door muavze ke naam par bulaye gaye the. Ek Tribune lekh ke anusar Gaura Devi ke saath lagbhag 27 mahilayein aur ladkiyan thi. Isi ghatna ne Chipko ko rashtriya aur antarrashtriya pehchan di.
  • Mukhya naam: Gaura Devi, Chandi Prasad Bhatt, Sunderlal Bahuguna, Govind Singh Rawat (Joshimath), Dhoom Singh Negi, Shamsher Singh Bisht aur Chipko ke kavi Ghanshyam Raturi jaise naam kai sources me aate hain.
  • Puraskar: Chandi Prasad Bhatt ko 1982 me Ramon Magsaysay Puraskar mila, aur Chipko andolan ko 1987 me Right Livelihood Award mila (Britannica aur anya sandarbh ke anusar). Sunderlal Bahuguna ko 2009 me Padma Vibhushan mila.
  • Asar: Himalaya ke jungle me vyavsayik katai par rok lagi (1980 ke aaspaas 15 saal ka pratibandh ek sandarbh me likha hai), aur Forest Conservation Act 1980 jaise kadam aage chale (sandarbh ke anusar).

Dhyan dein: Chipko ke itihas me kai vichar aur vyakti shamil hain, aur kuch bahas bhi rahi hai ki is andolan ka kya shreya kise milna chahiye. Isliye page par kisi ek ko “akele sansthapak” na kehein. Is vishay par Chamoli ke Reni aur Mandal gaon ke logon ki kahaniyan jodna aapke page ko bahut mazboot banayega.

Uttarakhand Rajya Andolan (1994)

1994 me Uttarakhand ko alag rajya banane ka andolan tez hua. Is andolan me kai jagah police ki goli chali. 1 September 1994 ko Khatima, 2 September 1994 ko Mussoorie aur 2 October 1994 ko Muzaffarnagar ke Rampur Tiraha par hui ghatnayein rajya andolan ke sabse dukhad ghatnayein manee jaati hain.

Chamoli: Lok-Kathayein, Veer Aur Rahasya

Chamoli Ki Apni Lok-Kathayein

Chamoli ki lok-kathayein aam taur par devi-devta, tirth aur himalayi pradesh se judi hain. Kuch pramukh:

  • Nanda Devi ki kahani: Nanda Devi ko Himalaya ki beti mana jata hai, jo apne maike (Nauti ya Kurud) se sasural (Kailash) jaati hain. Raj Jat isi “vida” ka utsav hai (Chamoli: Melein, Jatra Aur Doli Yatra).
  • Roopkund ka rahasya: Roopkund ke kinare milne wale kankalon ki ek lok-katha hai ki ek raja ki Raj Jat me yatra ke dauran ye log marre gaye. Vaigyanik adhyayan (jo ek antarrashtriya patrika me 2019 me chhape, aur jinhe media me khoob charcha mili) ne dikhaya ki ye kankal ek hi ghatna ke nahi hain aur alag-alag samay ke logon ke hain, aur isme alag-alag vansh ke log bhi the. Sahi jaankari ke liye us adhyayan ko padhein, aur kisi ek katha ko “akhiri sach” na batayein.
  • Dronagiri aur Hanuman: Ek prasiddh lok-manyata ke anusar Dronagiri gaon ke log Hanuman ji ki puja nahi karte, kyunki unki manyata ke anusar Ramayan me Hanuman ne Dronagiri parvat se sanjeevani booti lekar jaate hue yahan se parvat ka ek hissa utha liya tha.
  • Gaurdev aur Gaura-Parvati ki kathayein: Kai gaon ki apni lok-katha hoti hai. Apne gaon ke buzurgon se puchkar likhein.

Achhari (Aanchari): Pahadi Pariyan

Pahadi lok-manyata me Achhari (kuch jagah “Aanchari”) oonche bugyal aur jungle me rehne wali pariyan hain, jinke bare me kaha jata hai ki wo chamakdaar rang ke kapde pehne logon ko attract karti hain. Ye lok-manyata hai, aur oonchai par trekkers ko suraksha ki salah dene ka ek tareeka bhi (jaise bugyal me akele na jayein, mausam dekhein) isse jod diya jata hai.

Veer Aur Veeranganayein: Kaun Chamoli Ke Nahi

Inme se:

  • Madho Singh Bhandari ka Maletha gaon Pauri-Tehri kshetra se mana jata hai.
  • Rajula-Malushahi Kumaon (Johar-Bageshwar kshetra) ki lok-gatha hai.
  • Tilu Rauteli ko Pauri Garhwal ke Gurad kshetra se jodkar bataya jata hai.
  • Rami Baurani Kumaon se judi gatha hai.
  • Ye sab Garhwal-Kumaon ki lokpriya gathayein hain, par Chamoli ki nahi.

Raj Jat Aur Chausingha Khadu Ki Katha

Parampara ke anusar chaar sing wala mendha (Chausingha Khadu) Raj Jat ka netritva karta hai aur Homkund par chhod diya jata hai, jise log Kailash ki or jata mante hain. Raj Jat ki kai kahaniyan hain, jaise Nanda Devi ke sasural jaane ki, Latu Devta ki aur Kansuwa ke Kunwar parivar ki. In kahaniyon ka ek hissa “isi tarah suna gaya” ke roop me likhna sahi hai (Chamoli: Melein, Jatra Aur Doli Yatra).

Lok-Kathayein Ko Sambhalne Ka Tareeka

Lok-kathayein dheere-dheere bhoole ja rahi hain. Aap ye kar sakte hain:

  • Gaon ke buzurgon se kahaniyan sunkar likhein ya record karein (unki anumati ke saath).
  • Har kahani ke saath bataayein ki ye kisne sunayi aur kis gaon ki hai.
  • Kahaniyon ko “lok-katha” ya “manyata” ke roop me likhein. Unhe aitihasik tathya na banayein.
