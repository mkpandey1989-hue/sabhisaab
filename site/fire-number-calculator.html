25 guna wala niyam

FIRE number = Saal ka kharcha ÷ (Nikaasi dar ÷ 100)
4% nikaasi par ye seedha saal ke kharche ka 25 guna ban jaata hai.

Mahine ka kharcha ₹50,000? Saal ka ₹6 lakh. FIRE number = ₹1.5 crore.

Mahine ka kharcha25x (4% par)33x (3% par)
₹30,000₹90 lakh₹1.2 crore
₹50,000₹1.5 crore₹2 crore
₹75,000₹2.25 crore₹3 crore
₹1,00,000₹3 crore₹4 crore

Gaur kariye — ye number aapki salary par nahi, aapke kharche par tika hai. Do log ek hi salary par, par jiska kharcha kam hai uska lakshya lagbhag aadha hoga.

4% rule kahan se aaya aur Bharat me kitna chalta hai

4% ka niyam America ke ek purane adhyayan se aaya hai, jisme dekha gaya ki agar har saal corpus ka 4% nikaala jaye to paisa lagbhag 30 saal chal jaata hai.

Par Bharat me kuch cheezein alag hain:

  • Mehngai zyada hai — yahan aam taur par 6% ke aas-paas, jo us adhyayan ki mehngai se upar hai.
  • Ilaaj ka kharcha tezi se badhta hai — aur budhape me sabse bada kharcha wahi banta hai.
  • Sarkari pension ya social security nahi hoti zyadatar logon ke liye. Wahan wo ek safety net hai, yahan nahi.
  • Parivaar ki zimmedari — maa-baap, bhai-behan, bacchon ki shaadi. Ye kharche plan me aksar nahi jode jaate.
Isi liye Bharat me samajhdaar log 3-3.5% ki nikaasi dar lete hain, 4% nahi. Iska matlab corpus 25x ki jagah 28-33x hona chahiye. Thoda zyada lagta hai, par jaldi paisa khatam ho jaane se behtar hai.

Number me ye cheezein zaroor jodiye

Sirf aaj ka kharcha lene se number kam nikalta hai. Ye bhi jodiye:

  • Health insurance ka premium — umar ke saath ye tezi se badhta hai, aur retirement ke baad ye zaroori hai
  • Ghar ki marammat — har 5-7 saal me ek bada kharcha
  • Gaadi badalna — 8-10 saal me ek baar
  • Bacchon ki padhai aur shaadi — ye alag lakshya hai, isse FIRE corpus me mat milaiye
  • Maa-baap ka ilaaj — agar zimmedari aap par hai
  • Ghoomna aur shauk — FIRE ke baad samay zyada hoga, aur kharcha bhi
Ek zaroori baat jo log bhool jaate hain — corpus se nikaale gaye paise par tax lagta hai. Agar aapko haath me ₹50,000 mahina chahiye, to nikaalna usse zyada padega. Isliye kharche me tax ka anuman bhi jodiye.

FIRE ke alag roop — sabko poora chhodna zaroori nahi

FIRE ka matlab sirf "naukri chhod do" nahi hai. Kuch alag roop hain:

  • Lean FIRE — kam kharche me chalna. Chhote sheher ya gaon me ye kaafi jaldi mumkin hai, kyunki wahan mahine ka kharcha kam hota hai.
  • Coast FIRE — itna paisa jama kar lena ki ab aur nivesh na kariye, aur wahi paisa 60 saal tak apne aap badh kar kaafi ho jaye. Ab aap koi bhi kaam kar sakte hain, sirf aaj ka kharcha nikaalne ke liye. Ye lakshya sabse jaldi milta hai.
  • Barista FIRE — kuch corpus, aur saath me part-time kaam. Poora bojh corpus par nahi.
  • Fat FIRE — aaram wali zindagi ke liye bada corpus.
Bahut se logon ke liye Coast FIRE sabse practical lakshya hai. Ek baar wo number chhoo lene ke baad naukri ka dabaav bahut kam ho jaata hai — aap kam salary wala pasandeeda kaam chun sakte hain, kyunki retirement ka intezaam pehle se ho chuka hai.

Aur ek imaandaar baat: FIRE ka number chhoo lena aakhir nahi hai. Bina kaam ke din kaise katenge, samaj se judav kaise rahega, aur mann kaise lagega — ye sawaal paise se kam nahi hain. Bahut se log FIRE ke baad kuch na kuch kaam karte hi rehte hain, par ab apni marzi se.

Bharat me 4% ka niyam thoda kam rakhna behtar hai

4% ka niyam Amerika ke purane aankdon se nikla hai, jahan mehngai lambe samay tak 2-3% rahi. Bharat me mehngai iske dugne ke aas-paas rehti hai. Isliye yahan 4% nikaalna thoda tez pad sakta hai — 3% se 3.5% zyada surakshit maana jaata hai, aur uska matlab hai zyada corpus.

Do galtiyan jo FIRE ka hisaab bigaad deti hain: pehli — aaj ke kharche par corpus nikaalna. 15 saal baad 6% mehngai par Rs 50,000 ka kharcha lagbhag Rs 1.20 lakh ho jaayega. Doosri — health insurance ko bhool jaana. Naukri chhodte hi company ka cover khatam ho jaata hai, aur 45 ke baad apna premium tez badhta hai. Ise kharche me alag se joda karein, warna corpus theek lagega par sach me kam padega.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

FIRE number kaise nikalta hai?

Saal ka kharcha ÷ nikaasi dar. 4% par ye saal ke kharche ka 25 guna banta hai — 50,000 mahine ke kharche par lagbhag 1.5 crore.

Bharat me 4% rule chalta hai?

Poori tarah nahi. Yahan mehngai zyada hai, ilaaj mehnga hai aur sarkari pension nahi hoti. Isliye 3-3.5% ki dar aur 28-33x corpus zyada surakshit maana jaata hai.

Coast FIRE kya hai?

Itna paisa jama kar lena ki aur nivesh kiye bina wo 60 saal tak apne aap kaafi ho jaye. Uske baad sirf aaj ka kharcha kamana hota hai — ye sabse jaldi milne wala lakshya hai.

Corpus me bacchon ki padhai ka paisa jodna chahiye?

Nahi. Wo alag lakshya hai. FIRE corpus sirf aapke rozana ke kharche ke liye hona chahiye, warna paisa jaldi khatam ho jaata hai.

Bharat me 4% ka niyam chalta hai?

Ye niyam Amerika ke aankdon se bana hai jahan mehngai 2-3% thi. Bharat me mehngai zyada hai, isliye 3% se 3.5% nikaalna zyada surakshit maana jaata hai — yaani kharche ka 29 se 33 guna corpus.

FIRE number me apne ghar ki keemat ginni chahiye?

Nahi. Jis ghar me aap rehte hain usse kharcha nahi nikalta. Corpus me sirf wo paisa gina jaata hai jisse aamdani aa sakti hai. Apne ghar ka faayda alag hai — kiraya bachta hai, isliye aapka mahine ka kharcha kam ho jaata hai.

Naukri chhodne ke baad health insurance ka kya karein?

Naukri me rehte hue hi apni alag policy le lijiye. Company ka cover chhodte hi khatam ho jaata hai, aur nayi policy me umr ke hisaab se premium lagta hai plus waiting period dobara shuru hota hai. Ye kharcha FIRE ke hisaab me alag se jodiye.

Apna ghar hone se FIRE number kitna ghat jaata hai?

Kaafi. Mahine ka jitna kiraya bachta hai, corpus usse 25 se 33 guna kam chahiye. Rs 15,000 kiraya bachne ka matlab lagbhag Rs 45 se 60 lakh kam corpus.
