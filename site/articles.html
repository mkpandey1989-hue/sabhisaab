Yahan wo sawaal hain jo log sach me poochhte hain — "5000 ki SIP se 10 saal me kitna milega", "prepayment ke baad EMI kam karun ya tenure", "12 lakh CTC me haath me kitna aayega". Har lekh me asli numbers hain, table hain, aur wo baatein hain jo aam taur par koi nahi batata.

Sab Hinglish me hai, aur har lekh ke saath us topic ka calculator juda hua hai taaki aap apne number daal kar khud dekh sakein.

  • Credit Card Minimum Due — Sabse Mehnga Jaal
    3.5% mahina asal me 51% saalana hai. Interest-free period kaise khatam hota hai, aur nikalne ke 4 tarike.
  • Salary Slip Kaise Samjhein — CTC Se In-Hand Tak
    ₹12 lakh CTC par haath me sirf ₹79,000 kyun aate hain. Har line ka matlab, aur offer letter me poochhne wale 4 sawaal.
  • Zameen Kharidne Se Pehle Kaun Se Kaagaz Dekhein
    Khatauni, shajra, dakhil-kharij, raasta aur circle rate. Uttarakhand ke khaas niyam bhi.
  • Bijli Ka Bill Kaise Banta Hai Aur Kaise Kam Karein
    Slab rate ka ganit, kaunsa upkaran kitni unit khaata hai, aur meter khud test karne ka tarika.
  • Nali, Mutthi Aur Bisi — Uttarakhand Ka Zameeni Naap
    1 nali kitne square feet, 1 nali me kitni mutthi, aur khatauni ke hectare ko nali me kaise badlein.
  • Bigha Kitna Hota Hai — Har Rajya Ka Alag Hisaab
    UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, MP, Bengal — har rajya me bigha alag. Pakka-kachcha ka farak bhi.
  • Uttarakhand Farmer ID (UKFR) Kaise Banti Hai
    Portal ka poora step, OTP ki dikkat, aur CSC ko paise diye bina khud kaise karein.
  • Percentage Kaise Nikale — 7 Aasaan Tarike
    500 me 387 kitne percent, ulta percentage, aur mun me nikalne ke shortcut.
  • FD Ka Byaj Kaise Banta Hai — Poora Hisaab
    Quarterly compounding, TDS ki seema, aur pre-mature todne par kitna kam milta hai.

Guide aur lekh me farak

Guide batati hai ki cheez kya hai — SIP kya hai, PPF kya hai, GST kaise nikalta hai. Lekh us cheez ko aapki zindagi ke ek khaas sawaal se jodta hai.

"SIP kya hai" ek guide ka sawaal hai. "₹5,000 mahina lagayein to 10 saal me kitna milega, aur usme se tax kaatne ke baad haath me kya aayega" — ye lekh ka sawaal hai. Doosra sawaal log Google par zyada poochhte hain, kyunki wahi unke saamne khada hota hai.

Ye lekh kaise likhe gaye hain

Har lekh ka dhaancha ek jaisa hai, taaki aapka waqt na lage:

  • Sabse upar chhota jawab — agar aap sirf number dekhne aaye hain to wahin mil jaayega, neeche padhne ki zaroorat nahi
  • Asli numbers wali tables — taaki aap apni sthiti se milaa sakein
  • Wo baat jo aam taur par nahi batayi jaati — jaise minimum due bharne par interest-free period khatam ho jaata hai, ya pahad me rakba dhalaan ka nahi upar se dekhi jagah ka hota hai
  • Sawaal-jawab aur us topic ka calculator, taaki aap apna number khud nikal sakein

Har lekh me sarkari source ka link bhi diya hai — income tax portal, RBI, EPFO, AgriStack — kyunki paise aur zameen ke mamle me kisi ek website par bharosa karna theek nahi, aur hum bhi koi apwaad nahi hain.

Topic kaise chune jaate hain

Wo sawaal jo log sach me poochhte hain, aur jinke jawab Hindi ya Hinglish me theek se nahi milte. Angrezi me "credit card interest" par hazaaron lekh hain; "minimum due bharne se kya hota hai" par apni bhasha me kaam ke jawab bahut kam hain.

Isi tarah zameen, salary slip, bijli ka bill — ye rozmarra ke sawaal hain jinpar aam taur par ya to bahut takneeki jawab milte hain, ya bahut halke. Beech ki jagah khaali hai, aur yahi jagah bharne ki koshish hai.

Naye lekh jodte rehte hain. Koi khaas sawaal hai jispar lekh chahiye, to Contact page se bata dijiye.

Kyun "asli" numbers, gol number nahi

Bahut jagah aapko "maan lijiye ₹100 ka nivesh" jaise saaf-suthre gol number milte hain. Wo samajhne me aasan hote hain par kaam nahi aate, kyunki asli zindagi ₹100 me nahi chalti.

Isiliye in lekhon me wahi rakam li gayi hai jo log sach me lagate hain — ₹5,000 ki SIP, ₹40 lakh ka home loan, ₹12 lakh ka CTC, ₹50,000 ka credit card bill. Number gol nahi hai, par aapki apni sthiti se milta-julta hai, aur wahi is baat ko yaad rehne layak banata hai.

Aur har lekh ke saath uska calculator juda hai, taaki aap apna khud ka number daal kar dekh sakein — kyunki aakhir me sirf aapka number hi mayne rakhta hai, kisi misaal ka nahi.
