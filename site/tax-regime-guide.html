Old vs New Tax Regime — Kaunsa Chunein?

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Har saal ITR bharte samay ek sawaal sabse zyada pareshaan karta hai — Old Regime chunein ya New Regime? 2020 me New Regime aaya tha, aur uske baad se sarkar ne ise itna aakarshak bana diya hai ki ab ye default ban chuka hai. Lekin iska matlab ye nahi ki har kisi ke liye New Regime hi sahi hai. Kis ke liye kya behtar hai, ye aapke income aur deductions par depend karta hai. Is guide me hum dono ko aasaan bhasha me samjhenge, taaki aap khud sahi faisla kar sakein aur zyada tax na bharein.

Dono regime me farak kya hai?

Seedhi baat ye hai — New Regime me tax ki dar kam hai, lekin chhoot (deductions) lagbhag khatam hain. Old Regime me tax ki dar thodi zyada hai, par aap 80C, HRA, home loan byaj, medical insurance jaise kai kharchon par chhoot le sakte hain. Yaani New Regime "kam rate, koi bahana nahi" wala hai, aur Old Regime "zyada rate, par kharcho par bachat" wala. Dono ka maqsad ek hi hai — aapse uchit tax lena — bas raasta alag hai.

New Regime kis ke liye achha hai?

Agar aap zyada nivesh ya kharch par chhoot claim nahi karte — jaise naye job wale, ya wo log jinke paas home loan ya bade 80C nivesh nahi hain — to aam taur par New Regime kam tax deta hai. New Regime me standard deduction bhi milta hai, aur ek nishchit income tak rebate ke chalte tax zero ho jaata hai. Isi liye zyadatar salaried logon ke liye ise default banaya gaya hai.

  • Kam deductions wale log
  • Jinhe simple, jhanjhat-free filing chahiye
  • Naye kamane wale jinke abhi bade nivesh ya loan nahi hain
  • Wo log jo har rupee ko chhoot me lagana nahi chahte

Old Regime kise faayda deta hai?

Old Regime tab faydemand hota hai jab aapke deductions bade hon. Agar aap PPF, ELSS ya EPF me 80C ka poora ₹1.5 lakh laga rahe hain, HRA le rahe hain, home loan ka byaj de rahe hain, aur health insurance ka premium bhar rahe hain — to in sab ko jodkar aapki taxable income itni ghat jaati hai ki Old Regime ka zyada rate hone ke baawjood total tax kam pad jaata hai.

Aasaan tareeka: Apne saare deductions jodein. Agar ye rakam kaafi badi hai (aam taur par ₹3-4 lakh se upar, aapki income ke hisaab se), to Old Regime jeet sakta hai. Kam ho to New Regime. Pakka jaanne ke liye dono ka number nikaalein.

Ek chhota example

Maan lijiye do log hain, dono ki income ek jaisi hai. Pehla vyakti sirf naukri karta hai, koi khaas nivesh nahi — uske liye New Regime me tax kam banega kyunki wo waise bhi deductions use nahi kar raha. Doosra vyakti home loan, PPF aur insurance sab claim karta hai — uski taxable income Old Regime me itni ghat jaati hai ki uska tax New Regime se kam ho jaata hai. Yahi wajah hai ki ek hi salary par do logon ke liye alag regime sahi ho sakta hai. Isiliye kisi doston ki dekha-dekhi regime chunna theek nahi — apna hisaab khud lagayein.

Calculator kaise istemaal karein?

Sab Hisaab ka Old vs New Regime tool istemaal karna aasaan hai:

  1. Apni saalana income daalein.
  2. Old Regime me jitne deductions claim karte hain (80C, HRA, home loan byaj etc.) unki total rakam daalein.
  3. Batayein ki aap salaried hain ya nahi (standard deduction ke liye).
  4. Tool dono regime ka tax dikhayega aur batayega kaunsa kam pad raha, aur kitni bachat ho rahi.

Faisle se pehle ye zaroor sochein

Tax slab aur rebate ki limit sarkar har budget me badal sakti hai. Isliye result ko ek anuman ki tarah lein aur filing se pehle incometax.gov.in par latest slab zaroor confirm karein. Kai log New Regime chun lete hain sirf isliye ki wo default hai, bina ye jaane ki Old Regime unke liye zyada bachat kar sakta tha — isi liye ek baar dono ka hisaab lagana samajhdaari hai. Agar aapke deductions har saal badalte hain (jaise home loan ka byaj dheere-dheere ghatta hai), to har saal dobara tulna karna theek rehta hai.

Ant me ek zaroori baat — tax bachana achha hai, par sirf tax bachane ke liye galat jagah paisa lagana theek nahi. Pehle apna lakshya aur zaroorat dekhein, phir uske hisaab se nivesh chunein. Tax ki bachat uska bonus honi chahiye, wajah nahi. Sahi regime chunkar aap saal me hazaron rupaye bacha sakte hain — bas thodi der ka hisaab kaafi hai.

Aam galtiyan jo log karte hain

Pehli galti — bina hisaab lagaye default (New Regime) chun lena. Doosri galti — sirf tax bachane ke chakkar me aisi jagah paisa lagana jo aapke lakshya se mel nahi khaati, jaise zaroorat na hote hue bhi mehanga insurance lena. Teesri galti — HRA ya home loan byaj jaise deductions bhool jana jabki aap unke haqdaar the. Aur chauthi — regime ek baar chunkar bhool jana; aapki income aur kharche har saal badalte hain, isliye har saal ek baar dono ka number milaana chahiye. In chhoti galtiyon se bachkar aap har saal achhi-khasi rakam bacha sakte hain.

Kitni chhoot ke baad purani regime faayde me hai

Faisla ek hi baat par tikta hai — aapki kul chhoot kitni hai. Aam taur par ye seema kaam karti hai:

Aapki kul chhoot (80C + HRA + byaj + 80D)Aksar behtar
Bahut kam ya kuch nahiNayi regime
Sirf 80C bhar paate hainNayi regime
80C + health insuranceLagbhag barabar — dono gino
80C + HRA (kiraye par rehte hain)Purani regime
80C + home loan ka byajPurani regime
Andaaza lagane ki jagah dono taraf ka number nikaal lena hi sahi tareeka hai — 5 minute lagte hain aur hazaron ka farak nikal aata hai. Do baatein yaad rakhiye: naukri wale har saal regime badal sakte hain, isliye ek saal ka faisla hamesha ke liye nahi hai. Aur company ko saal ke shuru me jo bataya, wo bhi ITR bharte waqt badla ja sakta hai — bas TDS ka hisaab thoda upar-neeche ho jaata hai. Slab aur seemaayein har budget me badalti hain, isliye har saal dobara ginna zaroori hai. Hum CA nahi hain.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya main har saal regime badal sakta hoon?

Salaried log aam taur par har saal ITR bharte waqt regime chun sakte hain. Business income wale logon par kuch shartein lagti hain, isliye CA se confirm karein.

New Regime default hai — kya Old Regime khatam ho gaya?

Nahi. New Regime default zaroor hai, par Old Regime abhi bhi available hai. Aapko filing ke waqt use chunna padta hai.

Kaun sa regime kam tax deta hai?

Ye aapke deductions par depend karta hai. Bade deductions wale ke liye aam taur par Old, kam wale ke liye New. Calculator se pakka number nikaalein.

Kya calculator ka result final hota hai?

Nahi, ye ek anuman hai. Latest slab incometax.gov.in par confirm karein ya CA se salah lein.
