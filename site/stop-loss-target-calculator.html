Level nikaalna

Stop-loss = Entry − (Entry × SL% ÷ 100)
Target = Entry + (Entry × Target% ÷ 100)

Entry ₹500, stop-loss 2%, target 6%:

  • Stop-loss: ₹490
  • Target: ₹530
  • R:R = 30 ÷ 10 = 1:3

Ye teen number sauda karne se pehle tay hone chahiye. Sauda ke baad tay karne par bhaav aapka faisla karta hai, aap nahi.

Stop-loss kahan lagana chahiye

Sirf percentage se stop-loss lagana aadha tarika hai. Behtar tarika ye hai ki wo chart par kisi asli jagah par ho:

  • Pichhle support ke thoda neeche — jahan se bhaav pehle palta tha
  • Aaj ke ya pichhle din ke low se neeche — intraday me kaafi istemaal hota hai
  • Kisi bade average (jaise 20 ya 50 din ka) ke neeche
Ek aam galti — stop-loss bilkul round number par lagana (jaise ₹500 ya ₹1,000). Un jagahon par bahut saare orders jama hote hain aur bhaav aksar wahan chhukar wapas aa jaata hai. Thoda upar-neeche lagana behtar rehta hai.

Aur ek zaroori baat: stop-loss ko share ke utaar-chadhav ke hisaab se rakhiye. Jo share roz 4-5% hilta hai, us par 1% ka stop-loss lagbhag hamesha lag jayega — chahe aapka anuman sahi ho. Aise share me ya to stop-loss chaura rakhiye aur quantity kam kariye, ya us share ko chhod dijiye.

Target kaise tay kariye

Target sirf "achha lagne wala number" nahi hona chahiye. Do tarike jo kaam karte hain:

  • R:R se ulta hisaab — pehle stop-loss tay kariye (jo chart batata hai), phir usse dugna ya teen guna target rakhiye. Isse target apne aap tay ho jaata hai.
  • Chart par agla resistance — jahan bhaav pehle ruka tha. Us se aage ka target rakhna aksar poora nahi hota.
Ek practical tarika jo bahut traders istemaal karte hain — target par aadha bech dijiye aur bache hue par stop-loss entry par le aaiye. Ab bache hue hisse par nuksan ka khatra khatam ho gaya — bura hua to us hisse me nuksan zero, achha chala to aur munafa. Ye tarika mann ka bojh bhi kam karta hai.

Aur target lagate waqt charges zaroor jodiye. 1% ka target rakhne par brokerage aur STT ke baad haath me lagbhag kuch nahi bachta. Hisaab Brokerage Calculator me dekhiye.

Trailing stop-loss — munafa bachane ka tarika

Ek sthiti jo aksar aati hai: bhaav target ki taraf gaya, par target chhoone se pehle palat gaya, aur aap khaali haath rah gaye.

Iska hal hai trailing stop-loss — jaise-jaise bhaav upar jaye, stop-loss bhi upar khiskaate jaiye. Neeche kabhi mat laaiye.

Entry ₹500, SL ₹490. Bhaav ₹520 hua to SL ₹505 kar dijiye — ab nuksan ka sawaal hi nahi, munafa pakka hai. Bhaav ₹540 hua to SL ₹525.

Do niyam:

  • Trailing SL kabhi neeche nahi — sirf upar. Neeche laana wahi purani galti hai.
  • Bahut paas mat rakhiye — warna har chhote jhatke par sauda kat jayega aur badi chaal chhoot jaayegi.

Stop-loss ki doori se hi tay hota hai ki jeet sambhav hai ya nahi

Isliye 1:2 se neeche ka trade lena aksar ghaate ka sauda hai. 1:3 par aap 4 me se 3 baar galat hokar bhi barabar reh sakte hain — charges se pehle. Do aadat jo sabse zyada nuksan karti hain: (1) stop-loss ko "thoda peeche" khiskaana — isse ek trade poore mahine ka munafa kha jaata hai; (2) target aate hi dar kar nikal jaana par ghaate me rukna — isse anupaat ulta ho jaata hai. Stop-loss aur target dono order lagane se pehle tay kariye, baad me nahi. Ye tool sirf jaankari ke liye hai, koi trading salah nahi.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Stop-loss kitne percent par lagana chahiye?

Sirf percentage se nahi — chart par kisi asli jagah par, jaise pichhle support ke neeche. Aur share jitna hilta ho, stop-loss utna chaura chahiye.

Round number par stop-loss lagana theek hai?

Nahi. 500 ya 1,000 jaise round number par bahut orders jama hote hain aur bhaav aksar wahan chhukar wapas aa jaata hai. Thoda upar-neeche lagaiye.

Trailing stop-loss kya hota hai?

Bhaav upar jaane par stop-loss bhi upar khiskaana. Isse munafa bachta hai. Niyam ek hi hai — trailing SL kabhi neeche nahi laana.

Target par poora bech dena chahiye?

Ek kaam ka tarika hai — target par aadha bechiye aur bache hue par stop-loss entry par le aaiye. Tab nuksan ka khatra khatam ho jaata hai.
