Simplify karne ka tarika

Bhinn ko chhota karne ka matlab hai — upar aur neeche dono ko usi number se baant dena, jab tak aur na bant sake.

Sabse tez tarika: dono ka HCF nikaaliye aur dono ko usse baant dijiye.

24/36:

  • 24 aur 36 ka HCF = 12
  • 24 ÷ 12 = 2, aur 36 ÷ 12 = 3
  • Jawab: 2/3

Agar HCF nikaalna na aaye to dheere-dheere bhi kar sakte hain — pehle 2 se, phir 2 se, phir 3 se. Jawab wahi aayega, bas kadam zyada lagenge.

Kaise pata chale ki ab aur nahi bant sakta? Jab upar aur neeche wale number me 1 ke alawa koi common factor na bache. Tab bhinn apne sabse chhote roop me hai.

Ek hi cheez teen tareeke se likhi ja sakti hai:

Bhinn → Decimal: upar ko neeche se bhaag dijiye
Decimal → Percentage: 100 se guna kariye

2/3 = 0.667 = 66.7%

Bhinn asli zindagi me kahan aati hai

  • Recipe — 3/4 cup atta, 1/2 chammach namak. Aur jab logon ki ginti badalti hai to yahi bhinn guna karni padti hai. Uske liye Recipe Scaler hai.
  • Zameen ka batwara — "teen bhaiyon me se do ka hissa" matlab 2/3. Kaagaz par hissa aksar bhinn me hi likha hota hai.
  • Nirmaan me mix — concrete ka 1:2:4 asal me bhinn ka hi khel hai. Cement Sand Bajri Calculator me poora hisaab hai.
  • Naap-tol — "aadha kilo", "paav bhar", "sawa meter" — ye sab bhinn hain.
  • Discount aur bikri — "1/3 off" ka matlab lagbhag 33% chhoot.
  • Auzaar ke size — 1/2 inch, 3/8 inch. Plumbing aur hardware me sab kuch bhinn me hi milta hai.
Ek chhota gyan jo mistri aur plumber roz istemaal karte hain — 1/2 inch ka matlab 4/8 inch hai. Jab tak dono bhinn ka neeche wala number ek na ho, unki tulna nahi ho sakti. Isi liye 3/8 chhota hai 1/2 se — dekhne me bada lagta hai.

Bacchon ki aam galtiyan

Bhinn me bacche jahan sabse zyada atakte hain:

  • Jodte waqt neeche wala number bhi jod dena. 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5 galat hai. Sahi jawab 5/6 hai. Jodne ke liye pehle neeche wala number barabar karna padta hai.
  • Guna me bhi wahi galti ulta. Guna me neeche wala barabar karne ki zaroorat nahi hoti — upar se upar, neeche se neeche. 1/2 × 1/3 = 1/6. Ye jodne se aasaan hai, par bacche isme zyada ghabraate hain.
  • Bada neeche wala matlab badi bhinn samajhna. 1/8, 1/4 se chhota hai. Ek roti ko 8 me baantiye to hissa chhota milega, 4 me baantiye to bada. Ye misaal bachche ko turant samajh aati hai.
Sikhane ka sabse achha tarika — roti, chocolate ya seb se. Kaagaz par 3/4 samajhna mushkil hai; chaar tukdon me se teen uthana aasaan hai. Ek baar samajh aa jaye, phir kaagaz par bhi baith jaata hai.

Bhinn (fraction) rozmarra me kahan aati hai

Class ke baad lagta hai fraction kaam ka nahi hai. Asal me ye roz saamne aati hai, bas naam nahi liya jaata.

Zameen ke kagaz me bhinn ko chhota karna kabhi apne mann se mat kariye. Khatauni me hissa aksar bade harey me likha hota hai — jaise 128/1024 — kyunki us gaon ke poore khate ka hara ek hi rakha jaata hai. Ganit me 128/1024 = 1/8 hai, par kagaz par 1/8 likh dene se doosre hisse-daaron ke number se milaan tootta hai. Isliye samajhne ke liye chhota kariye, aur likhne ke liye wahi number rakhiye jo record me darj hai. Sauda ya batwara karte waqt tehsil ke record ka number hi maanya hota hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Bhinn ko simplify kaise karte hain?

Upar aur neeche wale number ka HCF nikaaliye aur dono ko usse baant dijiye. 24/36 me HCF 12 hai, isliye jawab 2/3 aata hai.

Kaise pata chale ki bhinn sabse chhote roop me hai?

Jab upar aur neeche wale number me 1 ke alawa koi common factor na bache.

1/2 + 1/3 kitna hota hai?

5/6. Neeche wale number ko jodna galat hai — pehle dono ka neeche wala number barabar karna padta hai.

1/8 bada hai ya 1/4?

1/4 bada hai. Ek roti ko 8 tukdon me baantne par hissa chhota milta hai, 4 me baantne par bada.
