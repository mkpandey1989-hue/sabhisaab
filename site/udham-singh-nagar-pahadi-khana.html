Udham Singh Nagar ke Mashhoor Mukhya Vyanjan

Ek Zila, Paanch Thaliyan: Punjabi, Tharu, Bengali, Pahadi aur Terai ka Desi Khana

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar ka khana ek thali me nahi, paanch thaliyon me banta hai: Punjabi (sarson ka saag, makki ki roti), Tharu (dhikri, ghonghi, sidhara), Bengali (machher jhol, luchi), Pahadi (bhatt ki churkani, gahat ki dal) aur Terai ka desi gramin khana. Yahi iski sabse badi khasiyat hai.

Kyunki yahan alag-alag samuday ek saath rehte hain, isliye ek hi shehar me aap Punjabi dhaba, Bengali mithai ki dukan aur Pahadi bhojnalay teeno dekh sakte hain. Neeche samuday-wise mukhya vyanjan diye gaye hain.

SamudayMukhya vyanjan
Punjabi / SikhSarson ka saag aur makki ki roti, chole-bhature, parathe, lassi, dal makhani, kadhi-chawal
TharuDhikri, bagiya, ghonghi (ghonghe ki sabzi), sidhara, jaad (chawal ka paiya)
BengaliMachher jhol, bhaat, luchi-aloo dum, khichuri, rasgulla, sandesh, mishti doi
Pahadi (Kumaoni-Garhwali)Bhatt ki churkani, gahat ki dal, kafuli, mandua ki roti, jhangore ki kheer, aloo ke gutke
Terai graminDal-bhaat, makki ki roti aur sarson ka saag, aloo-gobhi, kadhi, gur aur ghee ke saath roti

Dhyan dein: Tharu vyanjanon ke naam aur banane ke tareeke alag gaon aur parivar me alag ho sakte hain. Yahan diya gaya vivaran samanya jaankari hai, kisi ek parivar ka tareeka nahi.

Pahadi parivaron ka khana yahan kyun milta hai?
Almora, Pithoragarh, Champawat aur Garhwal ke bahut se parivar Rudrapur, Kashipur aur Haldwani me bas chuke hain. Isliye Pahadi aatte, dalen aur masale yahan ke bazar me aasani se mil jaate hain.

Udham Singh Nagar: Paramparik Roti aur Chawal ke Pakwan

Makki ki Roti, Basmati aur Dhikri

Seedha jawab: Terai ki roti aur chawal ki pehchan makki ki roti (sardiyon me), gehun ke tandoori parathe aur basmati chawal hai. Tharu parivaron me chawal ke aate se bani dhikri aur bagiya bahut pasand ki jati hai. Pahadi parivar mandua aur jhangora ke vyanjan bhi banate hain.

  • Makki ki roti: Sardiyon me sarson ke saag ke saath, makkhan aur gur ke saath parosi jati hai.
  • Tandoori roti aur paratha: Punjabi dhabon ki pehchan.
  • Basmati chawal: Uttarakhand basmati ke kshetra me hone ke kaaran yahan ka chawal mashhoor hai. Pulao aur biryani me iska istemal hota hai.
  • Dhikri aur Bagiya (Tharu): Chawal ke aate ko bhaap me pakaya jata hai aur chutney ya sabzi ke saath khaya jata hai. Bagiya me kuch jagah dal ya gur ki bharai hoti hai.
  • Luchi (Bengali): Maide ki tali hui phooli roti, aloo dum ke saath.
  • Mandua ki roti (Pahadi): Pahadi parivaron ke ghar me pakne wali taakat bhari roti.

Udham Singh Nagar: Chutney, Raita, Achaar aur Masala Namak

Achaar, Chutney aur Sarson ka Tadka

Seedha jawab: Terai me aam, nimbu, mirch aur gajar-muli ke achaar ghar-ghar me banate hain. Tharu aur Pahadi parivar bhi apni chutney banate hain, jaise lahsun-mirch ki chutney, dhaniya-pudina ki chutney aur bhangjeera ya til ki chutney. Sardiyon me gur aur sarson ka tel rasoi ke mukhya ang hain.

  • Achaar: Aam, nimbu, hari mirch, gobhi-gajar-shalgam ka mix achaar.
  • Chutney: Dhaniya-pudina, lahsun-mirch, imli-gur ki meethi chutney.
  • Pahadi chutney: Bhangjeera (perilla beej) aur til ki chutney, jo Pahadi parivar Terai me bhi banate hain.
  • Raita: Kheere, boondi aur pudina ka raita garmi me khaya jata hai.
  • Pisyun Loon (Pahadi): Silbatte par lahsun, hari mirch aur dhaniya ke saath pisa hua namak, Pahadi parivaron ki khaas rasoi.

Pisyun Loon Terai me Kaise Banta Hai?

Seedha jawab: Pisyun loon banane ke liye silbatte par moti namak, lahsun ke patte ya kali, hari mirch, jeera aur hara dhaniya ek saath pisa jata hai. Terai me Pahadi parivar ise apni purani yaad ke roop me banate hain, jahan lahsun aur dhaniya aasani se mil jaata hai.

Pisyun loon silbatte par hi banta hai, mixer me nahi, kyunki hath se pise hue namak ka swad alag hota hai. Ise roti, mandua ki roti, ya taze kheere ke saath khaya jata hai. Pahadi parivar ise apne mool-gaon ki yaad dilane wale swad ke roop me yaad karte hain.

Udham Singh Nagar: Jungli Khane Layak Cheezein aur Mausami Saag

Sarson ka Saag, Bathua, Palak aur Mausami Saag

Seedha jawab: Terai ke khet aur medh par sardiyon me sarson, bathua, palak, methi aur chaulai ka saag milta hai. Jungle ke kinare rehne wale Tharu parivar kuch jangli saag aur kandmool bhi khate hain. Lagbhag har ghar me sardiyon ka mausami saag zaroor banta hai.

  • Sarson ka saag: Sardiyon ki sabse mashhoor sabzi, makki ki roti ke saath.
  • Bathua aur Chaulai: Rabi ke mausam me kheton me apne aap ugte hain, raita ya parathe me upyogi.
  • Methi aur Palak: Parathe, dal aur saag me istemal.
  • Kandmool aur jangli saag (Tharu): Jungle ke kinare ke gaon me kuch parivar parampara se kuch jangli saag aur kandmool khate hain. Jungle se kuch bhi todne se pehle van vibhag ke niyam zaroor dekhein, kyunki sanrakshit kshetron me ise manaa kiya gaya hai.

Suraksha: Jangli saag ya phal ko bina jaankari ke na khayein. Kai paudhe zehrile ho sakte hain. Kisi anubhavi gaon ke vyakti se pehchan karwane ke baad hi upyog karein.

Udham Singh Nagar: Paramparik Mithai, Tyohar ke Pakwan aur Prasad

Gur, Jalebi, Rasgulla aur Bal Mithai

Seedha jawab: Terai ki mithai ki pehchan ganne se bani chizen hain: gur, shakkar, rab aur gur ki jalebi. Bengali parivar rasgulla, sandesh aur mishti doi laye, aur Pahadi parivar bal mithai aur singori ka swad. Is mel se Udham Singh Nagar ki mithai ki dukanon me kaafi vividhta milti hai.

MithaiKis samuday se judi hai
Gur, shakkar, rabTerai ke ganna kisan
Jalebi, imartiUttar Bharat ki aam mithai
Gajar ka halwa, gajak, til ke ladooSardiyon ki mithai
Rasgulla, sandesh, mishti doiBengali parivar
Bal mithai, singoriKumaon (Almora) ke Pahadi parivar
Jhangore ki kheer, aarsePahadi tyohaar ke mithai
Kada prasad (halwa)Gurudwara ka prasad

Sardiyon me ganne ke ras se tayyar taze gur ki khushboo yahan ke mausam ki pehchan hai. Gurudwara me milne wala kada prasad bhi Terai ki mithai parampara ka mahatvapurn hissa hai.

Udham Singh Nagar: Paramparik aur Mausami Peya

Lassi, Ganne ka Ras aur Chai

Seedha jawab: Terai ke sabse mashhoor peya hain: gaadhi lassi, taze ganne ka ras, doodh-chai aur sardiyon me gur ki chai. Tharu samaj me chawal se bana paramparik "jaad" ya "paiya" pada jata hai. Pahadi parivar buransh ka sharbat aur malta ka juice yaad karte hain.

  • Lassi: Punjabi parivaron ki dein, makkhan ke saath ya meethi.
  • Ganne ka ras: Kolhu par nikala hua taza ras, garmi aur sardi dono me.
  • Chai: Gud-adrak ki chai sardiyon ki pehchan.
  • Tharu jaad / paiya: Chawal se banne wali paramparik peya, jo Tharu samaj ke tyoharon ka hissa rahi hai. Yeh sanskritik jaankari hai. Sharab ka sevan sehat ke liye hanikarak ho sakta hai aur kanoon ke dayre me hi hona chahiye.
  • Buransh ka sharbat: Pahadi kshetron ka sharbat, Terai me bhi bazar me milta hai.
  • Mentha sharbat: Garmi me thandak ke liye pudina-mentha ka sharbat.

Udham Singh Nagar: Terai Rasoi: Bartan, Chulha aur Rasoi ki Banawat

Kadhai, Tandoor, Chulha aur Bhatti

Seedha jawab: Terai ki rasoi me loha ki kadhai, mitti ka chulha, tandoor, silbatta aur gur ki bhatti pramukh hain. Tharu ghar me mitti ka chulha aur dhenki hoti hai. Punjabi ghar me tandoor, aur Pahadi parivar me loha ki kadhai aur silbatta. Aaj gas aur induction ka chalan bhi bahut hai.

  • Loha ki kadhai: Sabzi aur saag pakane ke liye, loha ka swad aur taakat dono deti hai.
  • Mitti ka chulha: Gramin aur Tharu gharon me lakdi aur upla se chalta hai.
  • Tandoor: Punjabi dhabon aur gharon ki roti-naan ke liye.
  • Silbatta: Chutney aur masala pisne ka paramparik tarika.
  • Gur bhatti: Ganne ka ras ubalne ki badi bhatti, sardiyon me chalti hai.
  • Handi aur matka: Dahi jamane, paani thanda rakhne aur dal pakane me upyogi.

Swasth rasoi tip: Loha ki kadhai me khatta khana (jaise imli ya tamatar wali sabzi) ghanton tak na rakhein, kyunki isse swad badal sakta hai.

Udham Singh Nagar: Mini FAQ

Udham Singh Nagar ka sabse mashhoor khana kaun sa hai?
Yahan koi ek khana nahi, kyunki Punjabi, Tharu, Bengali, Pahadi aur gramin khana sab milte hain. Sardiyon me sarson ka saag aur makki ki roti sabse lokpriya hai.
Tharu samaj ka mukhya khana kya hai?
Dhikri, bagiya, ghonghi aur sidhara jaise vyanjan Tharu samaj se jude bataye jaate hain. Banane ka tareeka parivar-parivar me alag hota hai.
Udham Singh Nagar me gur kab bante hain?
Mukhya taur par sardiyon me, jab ganne ki kataai hoti hai.
Kya yahan Pahadi khana milta hai?
Haan, Rudrapur, Kashipur aur Haldwani me Pahadi parivaron ki badi abadi hone se Pahadi aate, dalen aur masale aasani se milte hain.
Terai ki mithai kaun si mashhoor hai?
Gur, shakkar, jalebi, rasgulla aur gurudwara ka kada prasad yahan ki mithai ki pehchan hain.
