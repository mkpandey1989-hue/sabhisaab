Ulta hisaab hi sahi hisaab hai

Log aksar aise sochte hain — "5,000 mahina de sakta hoon, dekhte hain kitna banta hai." Ye galat shuruaat hai.

Sahi tarika ulta hai — pehle lakshya, phir rakam. Kyunki lakshya asli hai: bacchi ki fees 2035 me lagegi, ghar ka down payment 5 saal me chahiye, gaadi 3 saal me. In tareekhon ko aap nahi badal sakte.

Kuch aam lakshya aur unka mahina (12% return maankar):
₹10 lakh, 5 saal me → lagbhag ₹12,200/mahina
₹25 lakh, 10 saal me → lagbhag ₹10,900/mahina
₹50 lakh, 15 saal me → lagbhag ₹10,000/mahina
₹1 crore, 20 saal me → lagbhag ₹10,100/mahina

Is table me ek baat gaur karne layak hai — chaaron me mahine ki rakam lagbhag barabar hai, par natija 10 guna alag. Farak sirf samay ka hai. Yahi compounding hai, aur yahi wajah hai ki jaldi shuru karna sabse badi cheez hai.

Return kitna maanein — jhooth mat boliye apne aap se

Calculator me return ka box aapke haath me hai, aur yahi wo jagah hai jahan log apne aap ko dhokha dete hain. 18% daal dene se mahine ki rakam chhoti dikhti hai — par lakshya poora nahi hota.

Vaastavik anuman:

KahanAnuman returnKis samay ke liye
Equity mutual fund11-13%7 saal se zyada
Hybrid / balanced9-11%3-7 saal
Debt fund6-8%1-3 saal
RD / FD6-7.5%Kisi bhi samay
Ek pakka niyam: 3 saal se kam ke lakshya ke liye equity SIP mat kijiye. Bacche ki fees agle saal deni hai aur bazaar us waqt 20% gir gaya — to lakshya adhoora reh jayega. Chhote samay ke liye RD ya debt fund hi sahi hai, chahe return kam ho.

Aur ek imaandaar baat — mutual fund ka return koi guarantee nahi hai. Ye pichhle saalon ka anuman hai, waada nahi. Isliye 12% par hisaab lagaiye par mann me 10% rakhiye. Thoda zyada daal dena kabhi nuksan nahi karta.

Mahine ka number bahut bada aa raha hai to

Ye aam baat hai. Jawab dekh kar lagta hai "itna to mumkin hi nahi". Char raste hain, aur teen sahi hain:

  • Samay badhaiye. Sabse taakatwar upay. 10 saal ki jagah 13 saal karne se mahine ki rakam kaafi gir jaati hai, kyunki compounding ko zyada samay milta hai.
  • Step-up SIP kariye. Aaj chhoti rakam se shuru kariye aur har saal 10% badhaiye. Salary badhne ke saath SIP badhti hai, aur takleef mehsoos nahi hoti. Iska asar Step-up SIP Calculator me dekhiye — ye chaunka dega.
  • Lakshya chhota kariye. Kabhi-kabhi imaandaar jawab yahi hota hai. ₹15 lakh ki gaadi ki jagah ₹9 lakh wali, ya thoda chhota ghar.
  • Return badha kar likh dena — ye galat rasta hai. Isse sirf kaagaz par number theek dikhta hai, zindagi me nahi.

Agar aapke paas ek musht rakam pehle se hai, to us par bhi hisaab lagaiye — Lumpsum Calculator me. Aksar thodi lumpsum + chhoti SIP milakar kaam ban jaata hai.

Ek lakshya, ek SIP — inhe mila mat dijiye

Sabse aam galti ye hai ki log ek hi SIP se sab kuch karna chahte hain. Isse do nuksan hote hain — na pata chalta hai ki kaunsa lakshya kahan tak pahuncha, aur ek zaroorat par poora paisa nikal jaata hai.

Alag-alag SIP kariye, alag naam ke saath:

  • Bacche ki padhai → ek SIP, lamba samay, equity
  • Ghar ka down payment → doosri SIP, 5 saal, hybrid
  • Gaadi → teesri, 3 saal, debt ya RD
Kai app aur fund house me SIP ko naam dene ki suvidha hoti hai. "Riya ki college fees" likha SIP tod dena bahut mushkil ho jaata hai — ye chhota sa manovaigyanik jugaad sach me kaam karta hai.

Do baatein aur:

Saal me ek baar jaanchiye — kya lakshya rasta par hai? Bazaar achha gaya to shayad aap aage hain; kharab gaya to thoda badhana pad sakta hai. Har mahine dekhna faltu hai, saal me ek baar kaafi hai.

Lakshya paas aane par surakshit jagah le aaiye. Jab 1-2 saal bache hon, to equity se nikaal kar FD ya debt me daal dijiye. Warna aakhri saal me bazaar girne se saari mehnat par paani phir sakta hai. Ye ek kadam bahut se logon ka lakshya bacha leta hai.

Bacche ki padhai ka poora plan is guide me hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Target ke liye SIP ka hisaab kaise lagta hai?

Ulta hisaab hota hai — jitni rakam chahiye, uska samay aur anuman return daalkar mahine ki kist nikalti hai. Upar wale calculator me apna target daal kar dekh lijiye.

Return kitna maanna chahiye?

Lambe samay ki equity SIP ke liye 11-12% ka anuman theek hai. 3 saal se kam ke lakshya me equity mat lijiye — wahan RD ya debt fund sahi hai.

Mahine ka number bahut zyada aa raha hai to?

Samay badhaiye, ya step-up SIP kariye (har saal 10% badhana), ya lakshya chhota kariye. Return ka number badha dena sabse galat rasta hai.

Ek hi SIP se saare lakshya poore ho sakte hain?

Ho to sakte hain par theek nahi hai. Alag-alag SIP rakhiye — tab pata chalta hai kaunsa lakshya kahan tak pahuncha, aur ek zaroorat par poora paisa nahi nikalta.
