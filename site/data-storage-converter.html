Chain saada hai — har baar 1024

8 Bit = 1 Byte
1024 Byte = 1 KB
1024 KB = 1 MB
1024 MB = 1 GB
1024 GB = 1 TB

1024 kyun, 1000 kyun nahi? Kyunki computer 2 ke hisaab se ginta hai, aur 1024 = 2 ka 10vaan ghaat hai. Hum 10 ke hisaab se ginte hain, computer 2 ke — yahi poora antar hai.

Bit aur Byte alag hain — chhota "b" bit hai, bada "B" Byte. Ek Byte me 8 bit hote hain. Ye antar internet ki speed samajhne me sabse zyada kaam aata hai (neeche samjhaya hai).

64 GB ka phone 64 GB kyun nahi dikhta

Ye sabse aam sawaal hai, aur iske do kaaran hain — dono asli.

Pehla — company ka hisaab alag hota hai. Storage banane wali company 1 GB matlab 1,000,000,000 byte maanti hai. Par computer aur phone 1 GB matlab 1,073,741,824 byte ginte hain.

Isi antar se lagbhag 7% kam dikhta hai. 64 GB ka phone lagbhag 59.6 GB dikhata hai — isme kisi ne dhokha nahi diya, sirf do alag hisaab hain. 1 TB ki hard disk lagbhag 931 GB dikhati hai, usi wajah se.

Doosra — operating system apni jagah leta hai. Android ya Windows khud kai GB ghera hota hai. Isi liye 64 GB ke phone me aksar 45-50 GB hi khaali milta hai.

Dono milakar 64 GB ka phone lagbhag 45-50 GB deta hai. Ye normal hai aur har company me hota hai.

Internet ki speed — yahan sabse zyada dhokha hota hai

Ye baat jaan lena bahut paisa aur gussa bachata hai.

Internet ki speed Mbps me hoti hai — yaani megaBIT per second. Download ki raftaar MBps me dikhti hai — megaBYTE per second. Aur 1 Byte = 8 bit.

Iska matlab: 100 Mbps ka connection lagbhag 12.5 MB per second download deta hai. 100 MB per second nahi.

Isi liye "100 Mbps" wale plan par 1 GB ki file download hone me lagbhag 80 second lagte hain, 10 second nahi.

Company jhooth nahi bolti — bas wo bit me likhti hai aur hum Byte me sochte hain. Plan lete waqt yahi ek baat dhyaan me rakhiye, phir speed kabhi kam nahi lagegi.

1 GB pen drive me 1 GB file kyun nahi aati

Wajah ye hai ki company aur computer, dono "GB" ka matlab alag lete hain. Company 1000 ke hisaab se ginti hai, computer 1024 ke.

Likha haiCompany (1000)Computer me dikhta hai
1 GB1,00,00,00,000 byte0.93 GB
64 GB64 arab byte~59.6 GB
128 GB128 arab byte~119 GB
1 TB1 lakh crore byte~931 GB
Isliye 1 TB ki hard disk me 931 GB hi dikhta hai — ye dhokha nahi hai. Sahi naam "GiB" (gibibyte) hai jo 1024 par chalta hai, par koi likhta nahi. Ek baat aur: naye phone ya laptop me 15-25 GB system aur pehle se padi apps le leti hain. Yaani 128 GB ke phone me asli khaali jagah aksar 100-105 GB ke aas-paas hoti hai. Kharidte waqt isi hisaab se sochiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

1 GB me kitne MB hote hain?

1024 MB. Aur 1 MB me 1024 KB. Computer 1024 ke hisaab se ginta hai kyunki wo 2 ke ghaat par chalta hai.

64 GB ka phone 64 GB kyun nahi dikhata?

Do wajah — company 1 GB matlab 1 arab byte maanti hai jabki phone 1.07 arab ginta hai (lagbhag 7% kam), aur operating system khud kai GB leta hai.

100 Mbps ka matlab 100 MB per second hai?

Nahi. Mbps me chhota b bit hai. 100 Mbps lagbhag 12.5 MB per second deta hai, kyunki 1 Byte me 8 bit hote hain.

1 ghanta YouTube me kitna data lagta hai?

480p par lagbhag 500 MB, aur 1080p par lagbhag 2 GB. Quality kam karna data bachane ka sabse aasaan tarika hai.
