AgriStack Farmer ID Kaise Banayein — Maine Khud Ghar Baithe Kiya

Manoj Kumar Pandey (Uttarakhand) · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Apna anubhav

Ye sirf jaankari hai — nivesh/tax salah nahi.
Mera gaon (Uttarakhand). Yahi ki zameen ke liye maine Farmer ID banai.

Namaste. Main Manoj hu, Uttarakhand se. Kuch samay pehle maine apne pariwar ki zameen ke liye AgriStack Farmer Registry (jise Kisan Registry ya Farmer ID bhi kehte hain) khud, ghar baithe, apne phone/laptop se banai. Hamare paas 350 se zyada khasra number the aur 4 alag khata — pehle laga tha bahut mushkil hoga, par thoda dhyan se karne par ho gaya. Yahan main bilkul wahi tarika likh raha hu jo maine kiya, taaki aap bhi bina kisi dalal ko paise diye, khud kar sakein.

Pehle ye zaroor jaan lein: Agar aap PM-Kisan ki kisht lete hain, to ab Farmer ID zaroori hoti jaa rahi hai — bina iske kisht ruk sakti hai. Isliye time rehte bana lein.
➤ Uttarakhand Portal kholein — ukfr.agristack.gov.in

Registration se pehle ye 4 cheezein paas rakhein

Maine sab pehle ye tayaar rakha, taaki beech me rukna na pade:

CheezKyun zaroori
Aadhaar card (mobile se linked)OTP isi linked number par aayega. Agar number linked nahi hai to pehle wo karwana padega.
Khatauni / Khasra (B1)Zameen kiske naam, kaunsa khasra — ye record. Uttarakhand me bhulekh.uk.gov.in se dekh sakte hain.
Bank passbookAccount number aur IFSC — taaki yojana ka paisa seedhe account me aaye.
Chalu mobileOTP aur aage ke update ke liye.
Mera anubhav: Sabse zyada dhyan Aadhaar se linked mobile par dein. Mere ek rishtedaar ka number linked nahi tha, to OTP hi nahi aa raha tha — pehle wo theek karwana pada. Isliye ye sabse pehle check karein.

Poora Step-by-Step (jaise maine kiya)

1

Official portal kholein

Apne phone/laptop me browser me jaayein ukfr.agristack.gov.in (Uttarakhand ke liye). Doosre rajya ka apna portal hota hai — jaise UP ke liye upfr.agristack.gov.in.

💡 Dhyan: sirf .gov.in waali official site kholein. Bahut fake sites hain jo paise maangti hain — unse door rahein. Registration free hai.
2

"Farmer" chunein aur login/register par jaayein

Home page par aapko "Farmer" ka option milega. Uspe click karein. Pehli baar ke liye "New User / Register" jaisa option chunein.

3

Aadhaar number daalein aur OTP se verify karein

Apna Aadhaar number daalein. Aapke Aadhaar se linked mobile par ek OTP aayega — use daal kar verify karein. Isse aapki pehchaan confirm hoti hai.

💡 OTP na aaye to: number Aadhaar se linked hai ya nahi — ye pehle check karein. Ya thodi der baad "Resend OTP" dabayein.
4

Apni jaankari bharein (jo apne aap nahi aayi)

Aadhaar se aapka naam, pata aam taur par apne aap aa jaata hai. Baaki jaankari — jaise mobile number, aur account — check karke sahi karein.

5

Zameen (khasra) jodein — ye sabse important step

Ab apni zameen ki detail jodni hoti hai — apna gaon (village), khata aur khasra number chunein. Portal aapke record se zameen dikha deta hai; aap apni zameen tick/select karein.

💡 Mere paas 350+ khasra aur 4 khata the — ek-ek karke sabhi khata ki zameen jodni padi. Thoda samay laga par sab select ho gaye. Jaldbazi me koi khasra chhoot na jaaye, dhyan se dekh lein.
6

Bank detail daalein

Apna account number aur IFSC daalein (passbook se). Isi account me yojana ka paisa aayega, isliye ekdum sahi bharein — ek galat digit se paisa atak sakta hai.

7

Sab check karein, consent dein aur Submit karein

Aakhir me poori jaankari ek baar padh lein — naam, zameen, bank sab sahi hai? Phir data use ki consent (sehmati) par tick karke Submit karein.

8

Enrollment ID sambhaal kar rakhein

Submit karne ke baad aapko ek Enrollment ID milegi. Iska screenshot ya photo le lein — isi se aage status check hota hai. Meri application submit ke baad status "Registered - Pending Approval" dikha, jo baad me approve hui.

➤ Status Check karein — Enrollment ID se

Submit ke baad kya hota hai? (Approval)

Application submit hone ke baad wo gaon ke patwari/revenue vibhag ke paas verify ke liye jaati hai. Verify hone ke baad status Approved ho jaata hai aur aapki Farmer ID ban jaati hai. Mere case me is process me lagbhag 10 din lage. Jaldi na ho to dhairya rakhein — ya patwari se poochh sakte hain.

Status kaise dekhein: Wahi portal (ukfr.agristack.gov.in) par "Check Enrollment Status" me apni Enrollment ID ya Aadhaar daal kar aap kabhi bhi status dekh sakte hain — Pending hai ya Approved.

Ghar se na ho paaye to?

Agar khud karne me dikkat ho (jaise OTP na aa raha ho, ya net slow ho), to najdeeki CSC (Jan Seva Kendra) par jaakar bhi Farmer ID banwa sakte hain. Wahan thodi fees lag sakti hai. Ya AgriStack helpline 1800-180-1551 par baat kar sakte hain.

Ye galtiyan mat karna (maine seekha)

  • Fake website: Google par "farmer registry" search karne par bahut fake sites aati hain jo paise maangti hain. Sirf ukfr.agristack.gov.in (.gov.in) hi kholein.
  • Galat bank detail: Ek digit galat = paisa atak. Passbook dekh kar do baar check karein.
  • Khasra chhod dena: Agar kai khata/khasra hain, to sab jodein — warna poori zameen register nahi hogi.
  • Mobile linked na hona: OTP na aaye to pehle Aadhaar-mobile link check karein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Farmer ID banane ke paise lagte hain?

Nahi. Official portal par registration bilkul free hai. CSC se karwane par unki thodi service fee lag sakti hai. Koi site paise maange to wo fake hai.

Kya PM-Kisan ke liye Farmer ID zaroori hai?

Haan, ab dheere-dheere zaroori kiya jaa raha hai. Bina Farmer ID ke aage ki kisht ruk sakti hai, isliye bana lena behtar hai.

Meri zameen kisi aur (pita/dada) ke naam hai, to?

Registration us vyakti ke Aadhaar aur record se hota hai jiske naam zameen hai. Agar record purana hai to pehle uttaradhikaar/naamantaran (mutation) karwana pad sakta — patwari se poochhein.

Status "Pending" bahut din se hai, kya karu?

Verification me kuch din lagte hain. Zyada der ho to apne gaon ke patwari/revenue office ya helpline 1800-180-1551 par sampark karein.

Ek se zyada khata/khasra jod sakte hain?

Haan. Maine khud 4 khata aur 350+ khasra jode the. Ek-ek karke sab select karne hote hain.

Ek baat dil se: Pehli baar dekhne me ye process bada lagta hai, par ghabrayein mat. Aaraam se, ek-ek step karein. Maine bina kisi ki madad ke, ghar baithe kar liya — aap bhi kar lenge. Kisi step me atkein to niche Contact page se mujhe likh sakte hain.
