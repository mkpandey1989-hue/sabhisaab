Pahadi Virasat: Buzurgon Ki Duniya, Tyohar Aur Lok-Kala

Buzurgon ki din-charya: bina ghadi, bina bijli ka jeevan

Purane pahadi gaon me din bhinsar (bhor se pehle) se shuru hota tha, jab mahilayein okhal me dhan kutti thi aur chakki pisti thi. Dopahar ghas-lakdi aur paani ke kaam me, aur shaam byaali (raat ke khane) aur aag ke paas kahaniyon me beeti thi.

  • Bhinsar: subah ka dhundhla ujala, din ki pehli aawaz murge ki baang aur okhal-chakki ki.
  • Dopahar: jungle se ghas ya lakdi ka bhari (gatthar) laana, naule ya dhare se paani laana.
  • Byaali / Biyakhun: shaam ka samay, jab pashu goth me bandhe jate the aur chulhe ke aas-paas parivar baithta tha.
  • Aana: buzurg paheliyan aur kahawatein sunate the, jise “aana” kehte hain.

Bina ghadi ke samay batane ke liye pahadi buzurg dhoop-chhaon (“gham”), pahadi chotii par suraj ki kiran aur pakshiyon ki boli dekhte the. Ye gyaan aaj lupt ho raha hai, isliye ise sahejna is page ka ek maksad hai.

Purane naap-tol ke paimane

Pahadon me anaj naapne ke purane paimane mutthi, pao, mana, patha, doon aur khandi the. In maanon me kshetra ke hisaab se antar milta hai, isliye neeche diye gaye vazan lagbhag aur parampara par adharit hain.

Purane ghar aur khet ke aujaar

  • Okhal aur musal: angan me gada patthar ka gadda aur lakdi ka moosal, dhan kutne ke liye.
  • Jandra: haath se chalne wali patthar ki chakki.
  • Gharat (panchakki): gadhere ke paani se chalne wali aata chakki, pahadon ki puraani green-energy engineering.
  • Supa: ringal se bani soop, anaaj saaf karne ke liye.
  • Theki / Paria: lakdi ka bartan jisme dahi jamaya jata tha.
  • Nisuda (hal) aur jua: halka lakdi ka hal jise terrace kheti me kandhe par utha kar le jaya ja sakta tha.
  • Dhumala: chulhe ke upar ka lakdi ka machan jahan dhuen ke asar se beej aur lakdi sukhayi jati thi.

Padyal / Huda: bina paise ka sahyog

Gaon ke log mil-jul kar ek-dusre ke khet me ropai, gudai ya chhat dalne ka kaam karte the, aur badle me malik sabko bhojan karata tha. Is samuhik shram-parampara ko alag-alag kshetron me “padyal” ya “huda” jaise naamon se jana jata hai. Ye sahyog ki bhavna aaj bhi kai gaon me zinda hai.

Pahadi ghar ki banaavat

Paramparik pahadi ghar patthar, lakdi aur mitti se bante hain. Nichli manzil (goth ya obra) me pashu bandhe jate hain, jiski garmi se upar ka kamra sardi me garam rehta hai.

  • Pathal: slate (patthar) ki chhat jo garmi me thandi aur sardi me garam rehti hai.
  • Tibari / Chaja: lakdi ki nakkashi wala khula baramda.
  • Kholi: nakkashidar mukhya chaukhat/dwar.
  • Khlian: ghar ke aage patthar se pata aangan.
  • Koti Banal shaili: patthar aur lakdi ki kai manzil wali bhukamp-sahan shaili, jo Uttarkashi kshetra me prasiddh hai.

Pahadi paridhan aur gehne

  • Mahilaon ke paridhan: pichora (rang-birangi odhni), ghaghri, angra, aur sar par dhaatu (dupatta). Rang-wali pichora vivah aur shubh avsaron par pahni jati hai.
  • Purushon ke paridhan: mirzai, sadri, churidar pajama aur pahadi topi.
  • Pramukh gehne: nathuli (badi naath), galobandh (gale ka gehna), hansuli, pahunchi, bulak, murkhi aur tergule.

Aipan aur lok-kala

Aipan Kumaon ki prasiddh ritual lok-kala hai jisme geru (laal mitti) par chawal ke geele ghol (biswar) se pooja ke aangan, chaunki aur dehri par alpana banayi jati hai. Ye kala GI-tag wali pahadi kalaon me gini jati hai. Aipan ke mukhya roop Lakshmi chauki, Saraswati peeth, Shiv peeth aur Ashta Dal Kamal jaise hote hain.

Lok-sangeet aur nritya

  • Jagar: devta ya purvajon ko dhol-damau ya hudka ki thap par avahan karne ki parampara.
  • Pawada/Bhadau: veeron ki gaatha geet.
  • Khuded geet: shadishuda mahilaon ke maika (mait) ki yaad ke geet.
  • Basanti, Chhopati, Chaiti, Jhora, Chanchari, Bair: ritu aur tyohar ke geet.
  • Nritya: Chholiya (talwar-dhaal ka yuddh-nritya), Pandav Nritya, Thadiya, Chaufula, Jhumelo, Chanchari.
  • Vadya: Dhol-Damau, Ransingha, Bhankora, Masakbeen, Hudka, Algoza (judwa bansuri).

Ramman: UNESCO virasat

Ramman, Chamoli ke Saloor-Dungra gaon ka dharmik utsav aur ritual-natya, UNESCO ki Intangible Cultural Heritage list me shamil hai (2009). Ye Garhwal ki gram-devta aur Ramayan se jude lok-natya ki jeevant parampara hai.

Vyapar aur paidal yatra ka itihas

Sadak aane se pehle gaon ke log 8 se 10 din paidal chalkar Haldwani, Ramnagar, Kotdwar ya Rishikesh ke mandi tak jate the. Raste me dharamshala aur chatti par rukte the. Bhotia vyapari bhed-bakriyon par saman lad kar Tibet se namak, suhaga aur oon laate the aur badle me gaon se anaj le jate the.

Pahadi Khana Aur Anaaj

Pahadi khane ki pehchan

Pahadi khana moti anaaj, daalon aur jungle ki hari sabziyon par adharit hota hai, aur lohe ki kadhai me dhimi aanch par pakaya jata hai. Ise sehat ke liye bhi achcha maana jata hai, par kisi bhi khaas aahar ya medical sthiti me doctor ki salah lein.

Pramukh pahadi anaaj

  • Mandua (Finger millet / Koda): mandua ki roti aur halwa.
  • Jhangora (Madira / Barnyard millet): jhangore ki kheer aur bhaat.
  • Kauni, Cheena, Ogal, Phaphar (Buckwheat): pahadi anaaj aur pseudo-cereal.
  • Lal chawal: pahadi laal chawal.
  • Barahnaja: kheti ki ek purani paddhati jisme ek hi khet me baarah tarah ki fasalein milkar ugayi jati hain, jo mitti aur jaiv-vividhta ke liye achchi maani jati hai.

Pramukh pahadi daalein aur masale

  • Gahat (Kulath): gahat ki daal aur gahat ke parathe.
  • Bhatt: kaala/safed soyabean; bhatt ki churkani aur jhol.
  • Rajma: Munsiyari, Chakrata aur Harsil ka rajma prasiddh hai.
  • Bhangjeera: perilla beej ki chutney.
  • Timur: pahadi mirch jaisa sugandhit masala, chutney aur namak me.
  • Jambu/Faran: pahadi tadke ki jadi-booti.

Pahadi mithai aur snacks

Arsa, Singori (Almora ki prasiddh khoya mithai jo maalu ke patte me lipti hoti hai), Bal Mithai (Almora ki chocolate-jaisi mithai), Jhangore ki kheer, Rote, Puwa, Singal aur Gulgule.

Pahadi peya

Buransh ka sharbat, malta ka juice, gud-adrak ki chai, aur bhotia ilaakon me jau/mandua se bani paramparik fermented peya (chhyang) sthaniya rivaaj me hai. Alcohol aur nasheele peya ke upyog ke liye apne kshetra ke kanoon aur ek jimmedar nirnay ka dhyan rakhein.

Pahadi rasoi ke bartan

Loh ki kadhai, silbatta, kansi ki thali, bhaddu (bhaari pital ka bartan), parat aur mitti ka chulha aur dhumala.

GI-tag aur sthaniya utpad

Uttarakhand ke kai sthaniya utpadon ko GI (Geographical Indication) tag mila hai, jaise Tamta (tambe ka kaam), Ringal craft, Aipan, Thulma, Bhotia Dann, Munsiyari Rajma, Jhangora, Mandua, Gahat, Buransh juice aur Chyura tel. Poori aur taza list ke liye GI Registry ka official record dekhein.

Pahadi Calendar, Tyohar Aur Kheti Ka Saal

Pahadon me mahina Sankranti se kaise gina jata hai?

Uttarakhand ke kai gaon me lok-parampara me hindu mahina Sankranti (suraj ke ek rashi se doosri me jaane ka din) se shuru maana jata hai. Isi liye Phool Dei, Harela, Ghughutiya jaise tyohar sankranti ke din manaye jate hain.

Har saal ki exact tithi hindu panchang aur sthaniya samiti ki ghoshna se tay hoti hai, aur Garhwal-Kumaon ke alag kshetron me thoda antar bhi ho sakta hai.

Pramukh tyohar: kya aur kyun

Phool Dei (Chaitra Sankranti): bachche gaon ke ghar-ghar jaakar dehri par phool daalte hain aur lok-geet gaate hain; badle me unhe chawal, gud aur paise milte hain. Ye basant aur nayi shuruaat ka tyohar hai.

Bikhauti / Bissu (Vaishakh Sankranti): nayi fasal ka tyohar, jise Kumaon aur Garhwal dono me alag naam aur parampara se manaya jata hai. Jaunsar-Bawar me Bissu mela prasiddh hai.

Harela (Shravan Sankranti ke aaspaas): isme saat ya paanch tarah ke anaaj ko bokar hariyali ugayi jati hai aur Shravan ki sankranti ko usse kaatkar sar par rakha jata hai. Ye hariyali, kheti aur paryavaran ka tyohar hai.

Nanda Devi mela (Bhadrapad): Nainital aur Almora kshetra me bada mela, jisme Nanda Devi aur Sunanda Devi ki pooja hoti hai. Nainital me ise Nandashtami ke aaspaas manaya jata hai.

Khatarua (Kumaon, Ashwin Sankranti ke aaspaas): sardi ke aagman par alav (bonfire) jalaya jata hai aur pashuon ke liye jadi-booti aur chara lekar tyohar manaya jata hai.

Egas / Budhi Diwali (Garhwal): Diwali ke lagbhag gyarah din baad manaye jane wala tyohar. Ise kuch kshetron me “Igas” bhi kehte hain.

Ghughutiya / Uttarayani (Makar Sankranti): bachche aata aur gud ke ghughute (khilone ke aakaar ke pakwan) ka haar pehenkar kauvon ko bulate hain. Bageshwar ka Uttarayani mela isi se jur kar prasiddh hai.

Baithaki Holi (Kumaon): Paush maah ke aaspaas se bethak me raag-raagini ke saath gaayi jaane wali Holi ki parampara.

Bhitauli (Chaitra): ye bhai-behan ke pyaar ka tyohar hai, jisme vivahit behan ke maayke se bhai uphar aur pakwan lekar aata hai.

Pahadi kheti ka saal

  • Baisakh-Jyeth: gehun aur jau ki katai; dhan aur mandua ki bwai ki taiyari.
  • Asadh-Shravan: ropai aur nirai-gudai ka mausam.
  • Bhadrapad-Ashwin: dhan, mandua, jhangora aur ramdana ki katai; Sharad ritu ka utsav.
  • Kartik-Margashirsha: gehun, sarson aur masoor ki bwai.
  • Poos-Magh: thand me ghar ke bheetar ke kaam, lakdi aur chare ka sangrah.

Is calendar me di gayi paramparayen kshetra ke hisaab se badalti hain. Apne gaon ki parampara aap hame bhej sakte hain, jise jaanch kar yahan jora ja sakta hai.

Uttarakhand ki Pahadi Virasat: Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Aipan kya hai?
Aipan Kumaon ki ritual lok-kala hai jisme geru (laal mitti) par chawal ke ghol se pooja ke aangan aur chaunki par alpana banayi jati hai.
Phool Dei tyohar kab manaya jata hai?

Pandav Nritya kahan hota hai?
Pandav Nritya Garhwal ka paramparik lok-nritya hai, jo kai gaon me tyohar aur utsav ke samay hota hai.
Harela tyohar kab hota hai?
Harela Shravan Sankranti ke aaspaas manaya jata hai. Isme beej bokar hariyali ugayi jati hai.
