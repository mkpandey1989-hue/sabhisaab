Uttarkashi ko "Dev Bhoomi" ka dil kaha jata hai, kyonki yahan Ganga aur Yamuna ke udgam hain, Shiv ki "Uttar ki Kashi" hai, Mahabharat ke Kaurav-Karna se jude mandir hain, aur gaon-gaon ke apne devta hain. Is hisse mein hum mandiron, devta-parampara, puraane itihas, Tilari Kand jaise sangharsh aur lok-kathaon ko ek saath likh rahe hain, aur har baat ko "manyata", "kathan" ya "itihas" ke roop me alag-alag bata rahe hain.

Kumaon aur Chamoli ke devta-sthal: Golu Devta, Nanda-Sunanda, Latu Devta (Wan, Chamoli), Airi, Haru Heet, Bhumiya jaise devta mukhya roop se Kumaon aur Chamoli ke hain. Uttarkashi ke devta Mahasu, Pokhu, Someshwar, Kandar, Hari Maharaj jaise hain. Isliye hum unhi ka vivaran de rahe hain.

Uttarkashi: Paurānik Bhugol Aur Kedarkhand Ka Sandarbh

Kedarkhand, Skanda Puran Aur Uttarkashi

Garhwal ka puraana dharmik naam Kedarkhand maana jata hai. Parampara ke anusaar Skanda Puran ke ek khand me Kedarkhand ka varnan hai, aur Uttarkashi ko wahan "Saumya Kashi" ya "Uttar Kashi" jaisa sthan bataya jata hai.

Is bare me ye baatein dhyan me rakhne wali hain:

  • Kedarkhand Himalaya ke us hisse ka puraanik naam hai jisme Garhwal aata hai. Kumaon ke liye Manaskhand naam aata hai.
  • Lok-manyata hai ki Kali yug me jab Kashi (Varanasi) par sankat aayega, tab Shiv Himalaya ki is Kashi me nivas karenge. Isi manyata se Uttarkashi ko "Uttar ki Kashi" kaha jata hai (Uttarkashi Naam Ki Utpatti).
  • Varuna aur Asi ke sangam ki samanta Banaras se jodi jaati hai (Uttarkashi Naam Ki Utpatti).

Ye granth bahut bade hain, aur unke alag-alag sanskaran me adhyay aur shlok ki sankhya alag ho sakti hai. Aap agar shodh kar rahe hain, to Sanskrit ke vidwan ya kisi pramanik granth-sanskaran se milaan zaroor karein.

Mahabharat se jude Uttarkashi ke sthal (lok-manyata ke anusaar):

Dharali ka Kalp KedarLok-manyata me Pandavon ke vanvas se juda shiv mandir (Uttarkashi: Mukhya Mandir, Dham Aur Teerth)

Uttarkashi: Mukhya Mandir, Dham Aur Teerth

Gangotri Dham

Gangotri maa Ganga ka dham hai, jo Bhagirathi ke bayein kinare par sthit hai. Mandir ka nirman Gorkha senapati Amar Singh Thapa se jodkar bataya jata hai, aur baad me ise Tehri aur anya shasakon dwara punarnirmit kiye jaane ka zikr milta hai.

Unchailagbhag 3,100 se 3,400 meter (sources me antar)
NirmanGorkha senapati Amar Singh Thapa se judi parampara
Tithi ka matbhedKai sources 18vi sadi likhte hain, par Gorkha shasan Garhwal me 1803-04 se shuru hua maana jata hai, isliye tithi par matbhed hai
Khulna aur band honaAkshaya Tritiya ke aas-paas khulta hai, Diwali ke aas-paas band hota hai
Sheetkaleen nivasMukhba (Mukhimath) gaon, Harsil ke paas
Mukhya kathaRaja Bhagirath ki tapasya se Ganga ka dharti par aana. Shiv ne unki dhara ko apni jataon me sambhala

Bhagirath Shila: Gangotri me ek shila ko Bhagirath ki tapasya sthali ke roop me mana jata hai.

Yamunotri Dham

Yamunotri maa Yamuna ka dham hai. Mandir ka nirman Tehri ke Maharaja Pratap Shah se jodkar bataya jata hai, aur Surya Kund aur Divya Shila yahan ke mukhya darshaniya sthal hain.

MurtiKaale sangmarmar (black marble) ki Yamuna ji ki murti

Kharsali ka Shani Mandir: Kharsali gaon me Shani Dev ka ek mandir hai, jise lok-manyata me Yamuna ka bhai maana jata hai. Mandir lagbhag 7,000 feet ki unchai par bataya jata hai.

Zaroori note: Dham ke kapat ki exact tithi, darshan ka samay, registration aur yatra ke niyam har saal badalte hain. Taaza jaankari ke liye mandir samiti aur Uttarakhand ke official yatra portal ko dekhein (Uttarkashi: Kaise pahunchein, kahan rukein, Uttarkashi: Permit, Registration Aur Suraksha Niyam).

Uttarkashi Shahar Ke Mandir

Uttarkashi shahar ke mukhya mandir Kashi Vishwanath, Shakti Mandir (Trishul wala), Kuteti Devi, Bhairav, Ekadash Rudra, Gyaneshwar aur Kandar Devta ke mandir hain.

MandirMukhya baat
Kashi Vishwanath MandirShiv ko samarpit, shahar ka sabse bada aur mukhya mandir. Parampara ke anusaar Parashuram se juda; 1857 me Tehri ki Maharani Khaneti (Sudarshan Shah ki patni) ne purane sthan par vartaman mandir banwaya
Shakti MandirVishwanath mandir ke saamne, Devi Shakti ko samarpit
Shakti ka TrishulPital ya lohe aur tambe ka bhari trishul, lagbhag 6 meter (kuch sources me 26 feet) ooncha. Isse devi Durga dwara asuron par phenka gaya trishul manne ki manyata hai. Iski aayu ke bare me kuch sources 1,500 saal puraani batate hain, par is par matbhed hai
Kuteti Devi MandirHari Parvat par Bhagirathi ke kinare, gaon ki raksha karne wali devi. Mandir ke pujari kuch peediyon se is seva me bataye jate hain

Trishul ki ek lok-manyata: Kai logon se suna jata hai ki trishul ko puri takat se hilane par wo nahi hilta, par ungli ka halka dabav dene par kaanpta hai. Aap isse ek kahani ke roop me hi dekhein, aur mandir me trishul ke saath zabardasti ya chhed-chhad na karein.

Kuteti Devi ki katha (lok-kathan): Kaha jata hai ki Kota (Rajasthan) ke ek raja Gangotri ki yatra par aaye aur unka dhan-thaila kho gaya. Aage chalkar raja ki putri ka vivah Vishwanath mandir ke pujari ke chune hue ladke se hua. Putri apne kul-devta Kuteti Devi se door hone se dukhi thi. Dono ne devi se prarthana ki, aur sapne ke anusaar apne khet me teen khushbudaar patthar paye. Usi jagah Kuteti Devi ka mandir bana. (Ye ek lok-katha hai.)

Lok-vishwas: Ek lok-vishwas ye bhi hai ki Kurukshetra ke baad Duryodhana ke saathi aur samarthak is ilaake me aakar rahe aur unhone Duryodhana aur Karna ke mandir banaye. Ye ek lok-vishwas hai, ise pramanit itihas nahi maana ja sakta.

Mandiron ki lakdi ki vastukala: Tons ghati ke mandir aur gaon lakdi ki naqqashi aur patthar ke kaam ke liye jaane jaate hain.

Kalp Kedar (Dharali): Ek Khoyi Hui Dharohar

Kalp Kedar Dharali me sthit ek prachin Shiv mandir tha, jise Kedarnath jaisi shaili ka aur Pandavon se juda manne ki lok-manyata thi. 5 August 2025 ki Kheer Ganga ki baadh me iska bacha hua gumbad bhi malbe me dab gaya.

Is mandir ke bare me kuch jaankari alag-alag sources me milti hai:

  • Ye mandir bahut samay se zameen ke andar dabaa hua maana jata tha, aur sirf iska upari hissa (shikhar) dikhta tha.
  • Parampara ke anusaar Kheer Ganga ka paani shivling par bahta tha. Kai sources ye bhi kehte hain ki mandir ki khudaai 1945 me hui thi, aur kuch kehte hain ki 1980 ke dashak ki koshish me shivling nahi mila. In baaton me matbhed hai.
  • Kai samachar sources me ise "Katyuri shaili" ka bataya gaya.
  • 2025 ki baadh me iske upar malbe ki moti parat jam gayi.

Taaza khabar ke liye zila prashasan aur bharosemand samachar sources dekhein.

Uttarkashi: Gram Devta, Ishta Devta Aur Jagar

Uttarkashi Ke Mukhya Devta

Uttarkashi me har ghati ke apne devta hain. Barahat kshetra ke Kandar Devta aur Hari Maharaj, Tons ghati ke Mahasu aur Pokhu Devta, aur Raithal ke Someshwar Devta sabse mashhoor hain.

DevtaKshetraParichay
Kandar DevtaBarahat (Uttarkashi shahar kshetra)Magh Mela ke mukhya devta
Hari MaharajBaragaddi (Barahat kshetra)Magh Mela me doli ke saath aate hain
Someshwar DevtaRaithal aur anya gaon (paanch gaon ke saanjhe devta)Anduri Utsav aur Pandav Nritya se jude
Pokhu DevtaTons ghati (Netwar)Nyay ke devta
Mahasu DevtaTons-Jaunsar kshetraMahasu ko Shiv ka roop aur nyay ka devta mana jata hai. Mahasu ke chaar bhaiyon ki katha lok-parampara me prasiddh hai

Duryodhana, KarnaTons ghati ke gaonDaan-veer aur sahasi yodha ke roop me pooje jaate hain

Mahasu Devta ka mukhya mandir Hanol (Dehradun zile, Chakrata ke paas) me hai. Uttarkashi ke Tons kshetra ke log bhi Mahasu ko apna devta maante hain.

Devta ke chaar bhai: Mahasu ke chaar bhai (Botha, Pavasi, Basik aur Chalda) ka naam lok-parampara me milta hai. Naamon ki spelling aur kahani alag-alag ilaake me alag ho sakti hai.

Jagar Aur Devta-Pujan

Jagar ek gayan-anushthan hai, jisme dhol-damau ke saath devta ki gatha gayi jaati hai, aur parampara ke anusaar kisi vyakti (jise "dangri" ya "pashwa" kaha jaata hai) par devta ka avatar aata hai.

Jagar ke bare me kuch saaf baatein:

1.Ye ek aastha ka vishay hai.

2.Jagar kab hota hai: Parivar ya gaon me kisi samasya, shaadi, ya devta ki pooja ke avsar par.

3.Jagar me kya hota hai: Jaagri (gayak) devta ki katha gaata hai, dhol-damau bajta hai, aur devta ka avatar aaya vyakti sawaal ka jawab deta hai.

4.Nyay ki parampara: Kai gaon me aaj bhi jhagde ya samasya ke liye devta ke saamne baat rakhne ki parampara hai.

Kisi bimari ke liye doctor se hi sampark karein. Aastha aur ilaaj dono ki apni jagah hai.

Uttarkashi: Pre-History, Prachin Puratatva Aur Purola Ki Khudai

Uttarkashi Ka Sabse Puraana Praman: Purola (ASI Dwara Sanrakshit Sthal)

Uttarkashi ka sabse puraana khudai wala sthal Purola hai, jo Kamal nadi ke bayein kinare par hai. Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) ke Dehradun Circle ke anusaar yahan ki khudai me sabse puraani parat se Painted Grey Ware (PGW) ke avshesh mile hain, aur ek eagle (Garuda) ke aakar ki eent ki vedi (Syenachiti) mili hai.

BaatASI Dehradun Circle ke anusaar
SthalPurola, Kamal nadi ke bayein kinare, zila Uttarkashi
SthitiKendra dwara sanrakshit sthal (notification 1996)
Khudai kisne kiHemwati Nandan Bahuguna Garhwal University, Srinagar (Garhwal)
Sabse puraani paratPainted Grey Ware (PGW) ke avshesh
Anya milanMitti ki murtiyan (terracotta figurines), manke, kumhar ki mohar, ghode ke dant aur haddi ke tukde
Sabse mahatvapurn khojEent ki vedi, jise khudai karne wale ne "Syenachiti" pehchana. Ye udte hue baaz (Garuda) ke aakar ki hai, jiska sir purab ki taraf aur par phaile hue hain
Vedi ke beech ka kakshPottery jo lagbhag pehli sadi isa-purv se doosri sadi isvi ki maani gayi
Doosri vastueinKuninda ka taambe ka sikka aur ek patli sone ki patti jis par manushya ki aakriti ukeri gayi hai (khudai karne wale ne use Agni pehchana)

Is khoj ka mahatva ye hai ki ye Uttarkashi ke kshetra me bahut prachin vaidik-kaleen anushthan-parampara ki taraf ishara karti hai. Dehradun zile ke Jagatgram (Vikasnagar) me bhi ASI ne yagya-vedi ka zikr kiya hai, aur ASI ke anusaar poore desh me aisi vedi bahut durlabh hain.

Dhyan dein: Hum yahan sirf wohi likh rahe hain jo ASI ne apne portal par likha hai. Isse zyada tithi ya detail ke liye ASI ki report aur khudai karne wale vidwanon ke paper dekhne chahiye. Cup-marks, megaliths ya Lakhudiyar jaise shail-chitra Almora ke hain, Uttarkashi ke nahi.

Uttarkashi: Kuninda, Ganeshvara-Guha Aur Katyuri-Malla Kaal

Kuninda Ka Sikka, Shakti Stambh Ka Lekh Aur Nepal Ke Malla

Purola me Kuninda ke taambe ka sikka milna (ASI ke anusaar) ye batata hai ki Uttarkashi ka kshetra us prachin Himalayi gan-rajya se judi vyapar ya rajneetik parampara ke daayre me aata tha.

Shakti Stambh (Trishul) ka lekh: Uttarkashi ke Vishwanath mandir ke saamne ke Shakti Stambh par likhe lekhon ke bare me ye baatein alag-alag vidwanon ke vivaran me milti hain:

LekhKya kaha jata hai
Ganeshvara aur Guha ka lekhSanskrit lekh me Raja Ganeshvara aur unke putra Guha ka naam aata hai. Lekh ke anusaar stambh ko "Shakti Stambh" kaha gaya aur ise Guha ki vijayon ki yaad me khada kiya gaya. Is par vidwan S. Srikanta Sastri ka 1940-41 ka lekh prakashit hua tha. Kuch sources ise 5vi sadi ka bataate hain, aur Nagvanshi shasan se jodte hain
Lipi aur bhashaEk vivaran ke anusaar lekh Brahmi lipi me hai (Shiv Prasad Dabral ki "Uttarakhand ka Itihas" ka sandarbh)
Nepal ke Malla shasakon ka lekhEk doosri parat me Nepal ke Malla vansh ke shasak Ashokachalla aur Kansdevachalla ka lekh bhi bataya jata hai. H. G. Walton ne Gazetteer of Garhwal Himalaya me stambh ko Malla vansh ke shasak se jodne ki baat likhi. ASI Dehradun Circle ke anusaar Malla log 1191 se 1223 ke beech Uttarakhand ke kuch hisson par kabza karte the
Nam ka itihasEk sandarbh ke anusaar "Uttarkashi" naam 1860-70 ke aas-paas prachalan me aaya

Dhyan dein: Lekh ki tithi aur kis shasak se judaav ke bare me vidwanon me matbhed hai. Is ka seedha matlab ye hai ki Shakti Stambh Uttarkashi ki sabse puraani likhit dharohar me se ek hai.

Katyuri aur Chand shasan: Katyuri rajya ki rajdhaniyan Chamoli (Joshimath) aur Bageshwar (Baijnath) ke aas-paas maani jaati hain, aur Paurava-Varman ke Talesvara tamra-patra Almora me mile hain.

Uttarkashi: Panwar (Garhwal) Rajya Aur Garh

Garhwal Ke Garh Aur Uttarkashi

Garhwal ka itihas "bavan (52) garhon" se jodkar bataya jata hai, jahan chhote-chhote raja alag-alag garh me shasan karte the. Baad me Panwar vansh ne inhe ek kar Garhwal rajya banaya (parampara ke anusaar Raja Ajay Pal).

Rawain ka itihas ka ek aham pehlu: Rawain pargana (Yamuna ghati) 1815 se 1824 tak Angrezon ke paas raha, aur phir use Tehri riyasat (Sudarshan Shah) ko saunp diya gaya. Ye jaankari Tilari Kand ke sandarbh me samajhni zaroori hai (Uttarkashi: Swatantrata Andolan, Tilari Kand, Chipko Aur Rajya Andolan).

Uttarkashi: Gorkha Shasan (Gorkhyani) Aur Anglo-Nepal Yuddh

Garhwal Me Gorkha Shasan (1803-1815)

1803 ke aas-paas Nepal ki Gorkha sena ne Garhwal par hamla kiya. Garhwal ke raja Pradyumna Shah ki Khurbura ke yuddh me maut ke baad Gorkhon ka shasan phail gaya, aur 1815 me Angrezon ne Gorkhon ko hata diya.

BaatJaankari
Gorkha shasan ki shuruat1803-1804 ke aas-paas Garhwal me
Gorkha senapatiAmar Singh Thapa Garhwal ke governor ke roop me jaane jaate hain
Gorkha shasan ka ant1815 me, Anglo-Nepal yuddh ke baad
Sugauli ki sandhiDecember 1815 me hastakshar aur March 1816 me pushti maani jaati hai. Isse Garhwal ka bata hua rajya (British Garhwal aur Tehri riyasat) bana
Gangotri mandir ka sandarbhGangotri mandir ko Amar Singh Thapa se jodkar bataya jata hai (Gangotri Dham)

Gorkhyani: "Gorkhyani" shabd Gorkha shasan ke us daur ke liye prayog hota hai, jise logon ki yaad me kathor kar-vyavastha aur dabdabe ke roop me yaad kiya jata hai.

Uttarkashi: British Kaal Aur Tehri Riyasat

Tehri Riyasat Aur Uttarkashi

1815 me Sudarshan Shah ne Garhwal ke pashchimi hisse me Tehri Garhwal riyasat ki sthapna ki. Uttarkashi ka bahut bada hissa Tehri riyasat me aata tha, aur 1949 me ye riyasat Bharat me vilay ho gayi.

Uttarkashi: Swatantrata Andolan, Tilari Kand, Chipko Aur Rajya Andolan

Tilari (Tiladi) Kand: 30 May 1930

Tilari Kand (Tiladi Kand, Rawain Dhandak) 30 May 1930 ko Tiladi Sera gaon me hua, jo vartaman Barkot ke paas Yamuna ke kinare hai. Tehri riyasat ki sena ne van adhikaron ke liye ikatthe hue gaon walon par goli chalayi.

Pichhli kahani (kaarandkaaran):

  • Rawain ke log jungle par paani, charagah, chaara, eendhan aur tyoharon ke liye nirbhar the.
  • 1927-28 ke van-bandobast me Tehri riyasat ne in adhikaron ko seemit kiya.
  • Pashu par per-sir kar (lagbhag Rs 1 prati pashu) aur kai pratibandh lage.
  • Raja Narendra Shah us samay Europe me the, aur shasan ki zimmedari Diwan Chakradhar Juyal ke paas thi.

Ghatnakram:

TithiGhatna

Maut ki sankhya par matbhed: Sarkari ya aadhikarik sankhya 18 batayi jati hai, jabki sthaniya vivaranon me 80 se 200 se zyada log maare jaane ki baat kahi jati hai. Maare gaye logon ke naam bhi alag-alag sources me alag likhe gaye hain.

Sena ke adhikari ke bare me: Kuch vivaranon me kaha gaya hai ki Tehri sena ke commander Colonel Sundar Singh ne goli chalane se mana kar diya, jis par unhe hata diya gaya. Ye baat bhi sabhi sources me ek jaisi nahi hai.

Parinam: Janata ke dabaav me Raja Narendra Shah ne kuch van-kar aur pratibandh hataye, par kai vivaranon ke anusaar sangrakshit van bina muavaze ke riyasat ke paas chale gaye.

Is ghatna ko log "Garhwal ka Jallianwala Bagh" kehte hain. Is ghatna ka mahatva isliye bhi hai ki ye "jal, jungle, zameen" par adhikar ke liye hui sabse pehli bade paimane ki ladai maani jaati hai.

Chipko Andolan Aur Uttarkashi

Chipko andolan ka sabse mashhoor chehra Gaura Devi aur Reni (Chamoli) hai, par Uttarkashi me bhi jungle bachane ke andolan hue. Ek sandarbh ke anusaar 1974 me Uttarkashi ke paas Vyali van kshetra me gaon walon ne ped katne ke khilaf sangharsh chheda tha.

Chipko ke bare me ye baat saaf rahe ki Reni (Chamoli) iski sabse mashhoor ghatna hai. Uttarkashi ka zikr usi vyapak andolan ke ek hisse ke roop me hi aata hai.

Uttarakhand Rajya Andolan (1994)

1994 me Uttarakhand rajya ki maang me Uttarkashi me bhi pradarshan, dharne aur bandh hue. 9 November 2000 ko Uttarakhand rajya bana.

Is vishay me galat naam ya aankda likhna andolankariyon ke saath anyay hoga.

Uttarkashi: Lok-Kathayein Aur Rahasya-Katha

Achhari/Aanchhri Aur Bugyal Ki Kahaniyan

Pahad ke kai oonche bugyal aur jheelon se "Achhari" ya "Aanchhri" ki kahaniyan jodi jaati hain, jinhe hawa me rehne wali pariyan ya bandhan-mukt aatmayein mana jata hai. Ye ek lok-vishwas hai, aur Uttarkashi ke kai bugyal-trek se jude gaon me bhi aisi kahaniyan sunai jaati hain.

Kya kaha jata hai (lok-vishwas): Kahani ke anusaar Achhari bugyal ya jheel ke aas-paas rehti hain, aur tez rang ke kapde ya khushboo wale logon ko lubhati hain. Isi vishwas se kai jagah chatak rang ke kapde pehanne se bachne ki salah di jaati hai.

Isliye aap sirf kahani ke roop me sunein. Asli khatra bugyal me mausam, dhundh, rasta bhatakna aur oonchai ki bimari hai. Isliye bugyal ya jheel ke trek par hamesha guide ke saath jayein, mausam dekhein aur raat ko akele na nikalein. Ye zaroori suraksha-niyam hain.

Veer Yodha Aur Lok-Gaathaen

Garhwal ki kai prasiddh lok-gaathaen, jaise Madho Singh Bhandari (Tehri), Tilu Rauteli (Pauri Garhwal) aur Rajula-Malushahi (Kumaon), Uttarkashi ki apni gaathaen nahi hain.

Madho Singh Bhandari ki Maletha ki kul (guhl) ki kahani Tehri Garhwal ki hai. Tilu Rauteli Pauri Garhwal ki veerangana hai, aur Rajula-Malushahi Kumaon ki prem-gaatha hai.

Uttarkashi: Chhote Sawal-Jawab

Uttarkashi ka sabse mashhoor mandir kaun sa hai?
Kashi Vishwanath Mandir, jo shahar ka sabse bada aur mukhya Shiv mandir hai.
Kashi Vishwanath Mandir Uttarkashi kisne banwaya?
Parampara ke anusaar ise Parashuram se jodkar bataya jata hai, aur 1857 me Tehri ki Maharani Khaneti ne purane sthan par vartaman mandir banwaya.
Shakti Mandir ka trishul kitna ooncha hai?
Kai sources ke anusaar lagbhag 6 meter (kuch jagah 26 feet likha hai).
Shakti Mandir kahan hai?
Shakti Mandir Uttarkashi shahar me Kashi Vishwanath Mandir ke saamne hai.
Kuteti Devi Mandir kahan hai?
Kuteti Devi Mandir Uttarkashi shahar me Hari Parvat par Bhagirathi ke kinare bataya jata hai.
Gangotri mandir kisne banwaya?
Parampara ke anusaar Gorkha senapati Amar Singh Thapa ne, par tithi par matbhed hai.
Gangotri ki sheetkaleen pooja kahan hoti hai?
Sardi me Gangotri ki pooja Mukhba (Mukhimath) gaon me hoti hai.

Yamunotri mandir kisne banwaya? Parampara ke anusaar Tehri ke Maharaja Pratap Shah ne.

Yamunotri ki sheetkaleen pooja kahan hoti hai? Kharsali gaon me.

Kharsali ka Shani Mandir kiska hai?
Kharsali me Shani Dev ka mandir hai, jinhe lok-manyata me Yamuna ka bhai maana jata hai.
Duryodhana Mandir Uttarkashi me kahan hai?
Tons ghati me Mori ke paas Jakhol aur Osla jaise gaon ke aas-paas bataya jata hai.

Karna Mandir Uttarkashi me kahan hai? Netwar ke paas ke gaon (Sarnaul ya Devra) me bataya jata hai.

Pokhu Devta Mandir kahan hai?
Pokhu Devta Mandir Netwar gaon me Tons nadi ke kinare bataya jata hai.
Pokhu Devta kaun hain?
Pokhu Devta Tons ghati ke nyay ke devta maane jaate hain.
Mahasu Devta ka mukhya mandir kahan hai?
Mahasu Devta ka mukhya mandir Hanol (Dehradun zile) me hai.
Kandar Devta kaun hain?
Kandar Devta Barahat (Uttarkashi) kshetra ke mukhya devta maane jaate hain.
Kalp Kedar kahan tha?
Kalp Kedar Dharali (Uttarkashi) me tha, jo 5 August 2025 ki baadh me malbe me dab gaya.
Dharali me baadh kab aayi thi?
5 August 2025 ko Kheer Ganga (Kheer Gad) me baadh aayi thi.
Tilari Kand kab hua tha?
Tilari Kand 30 May 1930 ko hua tha.

Tilari Kand kahan hua tha? Tiladi Sera gaon me, jo Barkot ke paas Yamuna ke kinare Rawain ghati me hai.

Tilari Kand kisne karwaya?
Tehri riyasat ki sena ne, Diwan Chakradhar Juyal ke aadesh ke roop me bataya jata hai, jab Raja Narendra Shah Europe me the.
Tilari Kand me kitne log maare gaye?
Sarkari sankhya 18 batayi jati hai, jabki sthaniya vivaranon me 80 se 200 se zyada ki baat kahi jati hai.
Tilari Kand ko aur kya kehte hain?
Ise Rawain Dhandak, Tiladi Kand aur "Garhwal ka Jallianwala Bagh" bhi kehte hain.
Tiladi Shahid Diwas kab manaya jata hai?
Har saal 30 May ko.
Tilari Kand ka karan kya tha?
Tehri riyasat ke van-niyam, jinse gaon walon ke jungle ke adhikar seemit hue the.
Rawain pargana kab Tehri riyasat ko saunpa gaya?
Rawain pargana 1815 se 1824 tak Angrezon ke paas raha aur phir Tehri riyasat ko saunpa gaya.
Tehri riyasat ka Bharat me vilay kab hua?
1 August 1949 ko.
Gorkha shasan Garhwal me kab tak raha?
1803-04 ke aas-paas se 1815 tak.
Sugauli ki sandhi kab hui?
December 1815 me hastakshar aur March 1816 me pushti maani jaati hai.
Chipko andolan ka sabse mashhoor gaon kaun sa hai?
Reni (Chamoli zila), jahan Gaura Devi ne andolan ka netritva kiya.
Kya Chipko Uttarkashi me bhi hua tha?
Ek sandarbh ke anusaar 1974 me Uttarkashi ke paas Vyali van kshetra me gaon walon ne ped katne ke khilaf sangharsh chheda tha.
Kya Hemkund Sahib Uttarkashi me hai?
Nahi, Hemkund Sahib Chamoli zile me hai.
Achhari kya hoti hain?
Achhari ya Aanchhri pahad ke bugyal aur jheelon se jodi jaane wali pariyon ki lok-kahani hain. Ye ek lok-vishwas hai.
Jagar kya hota hai?
Jagar dhol-damau ke saath devta ki gatha gaane ka paramparik anushthan hai.
Dodital me Ganesh ji ka janm kyon bataya jata hai?
Ye ek lok-vishwas hai ki Ganesh ji ka janm Dodital par hua tha.
Purola ki khudai me kya mila?
ASI Dehradun Circle ke anusaar Purola (Kamal nadi ke kinare) me Painted Grey Ware, mitti ki murtiyan, ghode ke dant-haddi, Kuninda ka taambe ka sikka aur baaz (Syenachiti) ke aakar ki eent ki vedi mili hai.
Syenachiti kya hoti hai?
Syenachiti udte hue baaz (eagle) ke aakar ki eent ki yagya-vedi hoti hai. Purola me aisi vedi milne ka zikr ASI ne kiya hai.
Kuninda ka sikka Uttarkashi me kahan mila?
ASI ke anusaar Purola ki khudai me Kuninda ka taambe ka sikka mila hai.
Shakti Stambh (Trishul) par kis raja ka lekh hai?
Ek Sanskrit lekh me Raja Ganeshvara aur unke putra Guha ka naam aata hai, aur ek doosre lekh me Nepal ke Malla shasakon ka. Tithi par vidwanon me matbhed hai.
Uttarkashi naam kab prachalan me aaya?
Ek sandarbh ke anusaar 1860-70 ke aas-paas.
