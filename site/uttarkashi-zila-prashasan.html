Uttarkashi District Ka Executive Summary Aur Pehchan

Uttarkashi Overview, Nickname Aur Core Identity

Uttarkashi Uttarakhand ke Garhwal mandal ka sabse uttari zila hai, jiska mukhyalaya Uttarkashi shahar hai. Yahin Gangotri se Bhagirathi (Ganga ki mukhya dhara) aur Yamunotri se Yamuna nikalti hain, isliye ise "Ganga-Yamuna ka udgam kshetra" kaha jata hai.

Uttarkashi sirf ek zila nahi, ek poora Himalayi sansaar hai. Uttar me Tibet (China) ki seema lagti hai, pashchim me Himachal Pradesh, aur zile ke andar Gangotri National Park aur Govind Pashu Vihar National Park jaise bade sanrakshit kshetra aate hain. Yahan Harsil ki seb ki baghichiyan hain, Har Ki Dun ki trekking hai, Dayara Bugyal ka barf hai, Dodital ka jungle hai, Nelong ki ooncha registani ghati hai, aur Tons-Rupin-Supin ghati ka Jaunsar-Rawain sanskriti kshetra bhi hai.

Zile ke kai naam aur pehchan hain:

  • "Uttar ki Kashi": Kashi (Varanasi) ki tarah yahan bhi Vishwanath Mandir, Manikarnika Ghat aur Kedar Ghat hain.
  • Ganga-Yamuna ka Udgam: Gangotri aur Yamunotri dono isi zile me hain.
  • Mountaineering ki Rajdhani: Nehru Institute of Mountaineering (NIM) Uttarkashi me hai, jahan se Bharat ke kai pahaadi abhiyaan ki training hoti hai.
  • Seb aur Rajma ka kshetra: Harsil, Mukhba, Dharali ke aas-paas ke baagh aur Mori-Naitwar-Purola kshetra ki fasal.

Is page ka maksad ye hai ki aap ek hi jagah par Uttarkashi ke bare me wo sab padh saken jo alag-alag website par bikhra hua milta hai.

Uttarkashi Naam Ki Utpatti (Etymology)

Uttarkashi ka matlab hai "Uttar disha ki Kashi". Manyata ke anusaar Kashi (Varanasi) ke baad Himalaya ki god me Shiv ka ye doosra Kashi hai, isliye yahan ka naam "Uttar + Kashi" pada.

Naam ki kahani ke kai pehlu hain, aur hum unhe "manyata" ya "ek mat" ke roop me hi likh rahe hain:

1.Dharmik manyata: Puranik kathaon me kaha jata hai ki Kali yug me jab Kashi par sankat aayega, tab Shiv Himalaya ki is Kashi me nivas karenge. Is manyata ko Skanda Puran ke Kedarkhand se jodkar bataya jata hai.

2.Varuna aur Asi ka sangam: Banaras ki tarah yahan bhi Varuna (Varunavat Parvat ki dhara) aur Asi Ganga (Asi nadi) Bhagirathi se milti hain. Varanasi ka naam bhi isi Varuna-Asi se bana hai, isliye log is samanta ko Uttarkashi ki "Kashi" pehchan se jodte hain.

3.Puraana naam "Barahat": Purane granthon aur lok-manyata me Uttarkashi ke purane naam "Barahat" ka zikr milta hai. Bahut se buzurg abhi bhi shahar ke puraane hisse ko "Barahat" ya "Barahat ka bazaar" kehte hain.

Uttarkashi shahar ka dharmik kendra Kashi Vishwanath Mandir (jise parampara me Parashuram se jodkar bataya jata hai), Shakti Mandir (Trishul wala mandir), Ekadash Rudra Mandir, Gyaneshwar Mandir aur Kuteti Devi Mandir hai. Mandir ke bahar ka ghat, Manikarnika ghat, shahar ki asli pehchan hai.

Etymology ke bare me itihaaskaron me ek mat nahi hai.

Uttarkashi: Collectorate Aur Revenue Administration

District Headquarters, Vikas Bhawan Aur Collectorate Ki Location

Uttarkashi ka zila mukhyalaya Uttarkashi shahar me hai, jo Bhagirathi nadi ke bayein kinare basa hai. Zilaadhikari (DM) ka Collectorate (Kalektret) aur Vikas Bhawan isi shahar me sthit hain.

Uttarkashi shahar samudra tal se lagbhag 1,150 se 1,350 meter ki unchai par maana jata hai (alag-alag sources me alag figure milte hain, kyunki shahar ka kshetra upar-neeche phaila hai). Shahar ke aas-paas Matli, Dunda, Maneri, Gyansu, Bhatwari aur Netala jaise ilaake aate hain, jo yatra ke raste par bahut kaam aate hain.

Collectorate me aam taur par ye kaam hote hain: zameen ke record, Tehsil ka sanchalan, DM ke aadesh, Janata Milan, Dharmik yatra ki vyavastha, aur aapda prabandhan ki baithak. Kisi bhi sarkari kaam ke liye DM Office, Tehsil, Vikas Bhawan (Mukhya Vikas Adhikari ka karyalaya) aur Zila Panchayat alag-alag jagah par bhi ho sakte hain, isliye jaane se pehle phone ya official website se confirm karna behtar hai.

Patwari Chowki: Rajasv Police aur Niyamit Police ka Ilaka

Uttarakhand ke bahut se pahadi ilaakon me aaj bhi Patwari "Revenue Police" ka kaam karte hain, yaani chhote aapradhik maamlon ki shuruaati jaanch aur kabza Patwari ke paas hota hai. Is vyavastha par Uttarakhand High Court ne 2018 me nirdesh diye the aur tab se kshetra dheere-dheere regular police ko diye ja rahe hain.

Uttarkashi jaise zile me, jahan gaon bahut bikhre hain aur sadkein door tak nahi pahunchti, Patwari ka dayra badi bhumika nibhata hai. Patwari ka kaam:

  • Zameen ke record (Khasra, Khatauni) ka rakhrakhav
  • Fasal kharab hone ya aapda me nuksan ka survey
  • Praman-patra ke liye sthalatri jaanch (sthai niwas, jati, aay)
  • Chhote jhagde me shuruaati report

Kaun sa gaon abhi Revenue Police me hai aur kaun sa Regular Police me, ye samay ke saath badal raha hai. Isliye is bare me taza jaankari ke liye apne nazdeeki thane ya tehsil se poochna sabse bharosemand tareeka hai.

Zila Panchayat, Kshetra Panchayat Aur Gram Panchayat Ki Ginti

Uttarkashi me Zila Panchayat (jo Uttarakhand ke 13 Zila Panchayat me se ek hai) zile ke vikas ka sabse upar ka nikay hai. Neeche 6 Kshetra Panchayat (block ke hisab se) aur har block ke andar kai Gram Panchayat hote hain.

Gram Panchayat aur Zila Panchayat ke seats ki exact sankhya parisiman ke saath badalti rehti hai. Update hote hi ye section bhar diya jayega.

Aabaad Gaon aur Khaali (Bhootiya) Gaon

Census-aadharit ek suchi (villageinfo.in) ke anusaar Uttarkashi me kul 729 gaon hain (kuch jagah 707 bhi milta hai). Kai gaon me palayan ke karan aabadi bahut kam reh gayi hai, aur kuch seema par ke gaon 1962 ke baad kaafi samay tak khali rahe.

Uttarkashi ki ek khaas kahani Nelong ghati ki hai. Jaadh bhotia samuday ke gaon (Jadung aur Nelong) 1962 ke Bharat-China yuddh ke baad khali karwa liye gaye the. Aaj ye ilaake seema suraksha aur "Vibrant Villages" jaisi yojanaon ke chalte phir se charcha me hain.

Palayan ki baat karein to Uttarkashi ke kai gaon me naujawan rozgaar ke liye Dehradun, Delhi ya doosre shehron me jaate hain. Isse khet banjar ho rahe hain, aur ghar khali ho rahe hain. Phir bhi Harsil-Dharali jaisi jagahon par homestay aur seb ke baagh se log wapas bhi aa rahe hain.

Uttarkashi: Urban Local Bodies Aur Civic Administration

Kya Uttarkashi mein Nagar Nigam Hai?

Uttarkashi zile me koi Nagar Nigam (Municipal Corporation) nahi hai. Zile ki sarkari District Industrial Plan (2025) ke anusaar yahan 6 nagar nikay (municipality) hain, aur zile ka sabse bada shahari nikay Uttarkashi shahar hai.

Uttarakhand mein ab 11 Nagar Nigam hain (Dehradun, Rishikesh, Haridwar, Roorkee, Haldwani, Kashipur, Rudrapur, Kotdwar, Srinagar, Almora aur Pithoragarh); Uttarkashi zile mein koi Nagar Nigam nahi, yahan chhote nagar nikay hi hain. Uttarkashi me Barkot, Chinyalisaur, Naugaon aur Mori jaise kasbe bhi nagar nikay ke roop me vikas pa rahe hain. Kaun sa nikay kis category (Nagar Palika Parishad ya Nagar Panchayat) me hai, iski exact list ke liye Urban Development Department ka taza notification dekhna chahiye.

Nagar Panchayats Aur Cantonment Board

Zile ki sarkari District Industrial Plan (2025) ke anusaar Uttarkashi me koi Cantonment Board nahi hai. Harsil me Army ka bada base hai, lekin wo Cantonment Board nahi balki seema kshetra ka sainik thikana hai.

Seema kshetron me Army, ITBP aur SDRF/NDRF ki tainati yahan ke gaon ki arthvyavastha aur sadak-suvidha dono par asar daalti hai. Harsil, Dharali aur Mukhba me sena ke karan hi kai sadkein, bijli aur network ki suvidha pahunchi.

Uttarkashi: Political Aur Electoral Profile

Lok Sabha Constituency Aur Current Parliamentary Representation

Uttarkashi zila Tehri Garhwal Lok Sabha seat ka hissa hai. 2024 ke Lok Sabha chunav me BJP ki Mala Rajya Laxmi Shah ne ye seat 2,72,493 voton ke antar se jeeti thi.

Tehri Garhwal Lok Sabha seat me 14 Vidhan Sabha khand aate hain, jinme se 3 (Purola, Yamunotri, Gangotri) Uttarkashi ke hain. Baaki Tehri Garhwal aur Dehradun zile ke hisse hain. Is seat ka itihas 1957 se shuru hota hai.

Uttarkashi me 3 Vidhan Sabha seat hain: Purola (SC ke liye aarakshit), Yamunotri aur Gangotri. Inme Purola ek hi seat SC ke liye aarakshit hai, ST ke liye koi seat nahi hai.

Zile Se Shuru Hue Pramukh Rajneetik Andolan

Uttarkashi aur uske Rawain kshetra ne Uttarakhand ke itihas ke kai bade andolan dekhe hain. Inme sabse yaad rakhne wala Rawain Dhandak (Tilari Kand, 30 May 1930) hai, jisme Tehri riyasat ke khilaf kisan khade hue the.

Ye kuch pramukh andolan hain jinka zikr is zile ki rajneeti aur samajik chetna me aata hai:

  • Rawain Dhandak / Tilari Kand (1930): Van niyam aur kar ke virodh me, Tilari ke maidan me kisan ikatthe hue the. Is ghatna ko Garhwal ka "Jallianwala Bagh jaisa kand" bhi kaha jata hai.
  • Tehri Riyasat ke khilaf Praja Mandal ki chetna: Tehri Garhwal riyasat ke andar Uttarkashi ka kshetra bhi shaamil tha.
  • Uttarakhand Rajya Andolan (1994): Pure Garhwal ki tarah Uttarkashi me bhi pradarshan, dharne aur bandh hue.

Uttarkashi: Law Enforcement, Security Aur Border Forces

District Police Setup, Kotwali, Thanas Aur Reporting Outposts

Uttarkashi zile ka police sanchalan Zila Police (SP/SSP Uttarkashi) ke paas hai. Shahar me Kotwali hai, aur tehsil mukhyalayon aur yatra ke raaston par thane aur chowkiyaan hain.

Gangotri aur Yamunotri yatra ke samay yahan police, SDRF aur Yatra Prabandhan ki alag vyavastha hoti hai. Yatra season me kai jagahon par temporary police post, medical post aur traffic control hota hai.

Army, ITBP Aur Sainik Thikane

Uttarkashi Bharat-China seema se laga hua zila hai, isliye yahan ITBP aur Bharatiya Sena ki upasthiti seema kshetron me rehti hai. Harsil me Sena ka bada base hai.

Seema ke kshetron me Nelong-Jadung ghati, Gartang Gali aur Harsil-Mukhba marg strategic hain.

Uttarkashi shahar me Nehru Institute of Mountaineering (NIM) bhi hai, jo 1965 me sthapit hui thi aur Bharat ka ek mukhya mountaineering training sansthan hai.

Uttarkashi: Judiciary Aur Legal Infrastructure

District Court, Family Court Aur Legal Aid

Uttarkashi me District aur Sessions Court sthit hai. Zila Vidhik Seva Pradhikaran (DLSA) ke zariye jaruratmand log muft kanooni salah aur sahayata le sakte hain.

Gaon wale logon ke liye Tehsil str par Lok Adalat aur Legal Aid Clinic ki suvidha hoti hai. Zameen ke vivad, parivarik jhagde aur chhote maamlon me gaon ke buzurg aaj bhi pehle Gram Panchayat ya Nyay Panchayat me baat karke hal nikalte hain, aur baad me Tehsil ya Court jaate hain.

Sub-Registrar Daftar (Zameen Registry)

Zameen ki registry (bikri-patra, daan-patra, vasiyat) Sub-Registrar Office me hoti hai. Uttarkashi me Sub-Registrar karyalaya tehsil mukhyalayon par hain.

Registry ke samay ye cheezein zaroori hoti hain: Khatauni ki nakal, Aadhaar, PAN, do gawaah, Stamp Duty ka bhugtan aur Circle Rate ki jaankari. Pahadi zile me nap-tol (Nali, Mutthi) aur Hectare ka antar ho sakta hai, isliye registry se pehle Tehsil me naapai karwana aur Khatauni jaanchna zaroori hai.

SDRF/NDRF Bases, Flood Shelters Aur Emergency Helipads

Yatra marg par SDRF ki tainati hoti hai, aur badi aapda ke samay NDRF ki team bhi bulai jati hai. Kai sthalon par heli-se rescue ke liye helipad banaye gaye hain.

Yatra se pehle ye cheezein zaroor karein: mausam ka taza alert dekhein, bhaari barish me yatra rok dein, aur raste ki taza sthiti local police ya yatra helpline se pata karein. Monsoon (June se September) me landslide aur baadh ka khatra sabse zyada rehta hai.

Uttarkashi: Vehicle Registration (RTO) Aur Transport Codes

RTO / ARTO Code, Office Location Aur Licensing Services

Uttarkashi ka vehicle registration code UK-10 hai. Gaadi ka registration, driving licence aur permit se jude kaam Uttarkashi ke ARTO karyalaya me hote hain.

Learning licence aur kai seva ab online (Parivahan portal) par shuru ho jati hain, par driving test aur gaadi ka nirikshan karyalaya me hota hai. Taxi aur tourist vehicle ke liye alag permit lagta hai, aur yatra marg ke liye Char Dham Yatra ka alag registration ek sarkari portal par karna padta hai.

Hill Driving Rules Aur Green Cess / Eco-Tax

Pahadi sadkon par safal safar ke liye din ke ujale me chalna, sadak par landslide ke maujooda alert dekhna aur jaam ke samay dhairya rakhna zaroori hai. Bahari rajya ki gaadiyon par Green Cess jaise sulk lagne ke niyam samay ke saath badalte hain.

Pahad ki sadak par ye adat daalein:

1.Charhai wali gaadi ko pehle raasta dein.

2.Mod par horn bajayein, par bina wajah nahi.

3.Raat ki yatra se bachein, khaaskar barish ke mausam me.

4.Engine brake ka istemal karein, neeche utarte waqt gear mein rakhein.

5.Fuel pehle bhar lein: zile ke upar ki ghatiyon me petrol pump bahut door-door hain.

Gaadi le jane se pehle Uttarakhand Transport Department ya Uttarkashi ARTO se taza dar pata karein.

Teen: Purola (SC), Yamunotri aur Gangotri.

Uttarkashi Tehri Garhwal Lok Sabha seat me aata hai.
Uttarkashi ki aabadi kitni hai?
Census 2011 ke anusaar Uttarkashi ki aabadi 3,30,086 hai.
Uttarkashi ka kshetrafal kitna hai?
Lagbhag 8,016 varg kilometre.
Uttarkashi me sabse kam aabadi ghanatva kyon hai?
Zile ka bada hissa ooncha pahad, glacier aur jungle hai, isliye yahan aabadi ghanatva lagbhag 41 prati varg km hai, jo Uttarakhand me sabse kam hai.
Uttarkashi ka vehicle code kya hai?
UK-10.

Uttarkashi shahar ka STD code 01374 hai.

Uttarkashi ko "Uttar ki Kashi" kyon kehte hain?
Manyata hai ki Kashi (Varanasi) ki tarah yahan bhi Vishwanath Mandir, Manikarnika Ghat aur Varuna-Asi ka sangam hai, isliye ise Uttar ki Kashi kehte hain.
Uttarkashi me Gangotri aur Yamunotri dono kyon hain?
Kyunki Bhagirathi (Ganga ki mukhya dhara) aur Yamuna dono ka udgam isi zile ke Himalaya me hai.
Nehru Institute of Mountaineering kahan hai?
Nehru Institute of Mountaineering (NIM) Uttarkashi shahar me hai.

Tilari Kand (Rawain Dhandak) 30 May 1930 ko Rawain kshetra me hua tha.
