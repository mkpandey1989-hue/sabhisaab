Paisa Kahan Rakhein? FD, RD, SIP Aur PPF Ki Samajh

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~8 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Paisa bach to gaya, par rakhein kahan? FD, RD, SIP, PPF, savings account — itne options me confusion hona aam hai. Sacchai ye hai ki koi ek "best" jagah nahi hoti; har option ek alag zaroorat ke liye banaa hai. Ye topic guide aapko sahi jagah chunne ka tarika samjhaati hai — lakshya, samay aur risk ke hisaab se.

Pehle lakshya aur samay tay karein

Paisa kahan rakhna hai, ye is baat par nirbhar karta hai ki kab chahiye. Teen tarah ke lakshya hote hain:

  • Short-term (0-2 saal): Emergency fund, agli chhutti. Yahan suraksha aur liquidity zaroori — savings account, FD.
  • Medium-term (2-5 saal): Gaadi, ghar ka down payment. RD, FD, ya kam-risk fund.
  • Long-term (5+ saal): Retirement, bachche ki padhai. Yahan SIP aur PPF chamakte hain.

Galat jagah chunne se ya to return kam milega, ya zaroorat ke waqt paisa phasa hoga. Isliye lakshya pehle tay karein, product baad me.

FD aur RD — surakshit aur seedha

FD (Fixed Deposit) me ek saath paisa daal kar fix byaj milta hai. RD (Recurring Deposit) me har mahine thoda daalte hain. Dono surakshit hain — bank me ₹5 lakh tak DICGC se insured.

Fayda — return pehle se tay rehta hai aur market ka utaar-chadhaav asar nahi karta. Par yaad rahe, ye surakshaa bank ki hai, koi sarkari waada nahi — DICGC ka bima ek bank me ₹5 lakh tak hi hai. Nuksan — byaj poora taxable hai (aapke slab par), aur return mehngai se thoda hi upar hota hai. Emergency fund aur short-term goal ke liye FD/RD behtareen hain, par lambe samay me daulat banane ke liye ye akele kam padte hain.

SIP — lambe samay ka hathiyar

SIP me aap har mahine mutual fund me thoda paisa daalte hain. Ye market-linked hai — risk hai, par lambe samay (7+ saal) me FD se kaafi zyada return (aam ~12%) ki sambhavna rehti hai.

SIP ka jaadu compounding aur "rupee cost averaging" me hai — market girne par sasti units milti hain. Short-term me SIP me utaar-chadhaav hota hai, isliye ye sirf lambe lakshya ke liye hai. Retirement aur bachche ki padhai jaise 10-15 saal door ke goal ke liye SIP sabse achha option maana jaata hai.

PPF — surakshit aur tax-free

PPF (Public Provident Fund) sarkari, poori tarah surakshit, aur tax-free byaj deta hai. 15 saal ka lock hota hai, par yahi iski taakat bhi hai — paisa jama rehta hai aur compounding bina rukavat chalti rehti hai.

PPF EEE category me aata hai — nivesh, byaj aur maturity teeno tax-free (old regime me 80C chhoot bhi). Return FD se thoda behtar aur guaranteed. Ye SIP jaisi tez growth to nahi deta, par jo poori suraksha chahte hain unke long-term (retirement) ke liye PPF shaandaar hai. SIP aur PPF dono rakhna ek santulit tarika hai.

Sahi mix kaise banayein?

Koi ek jagah sab kuch nahi. Ek samajhdaar tarika:

  • Emergency fund (3-6 mahine ka kharch): savings account + FD me.
  • Short-term goal: FD/RD me.
  • Long-term wealth: SIP (equity) me — sabse zyada growth.
  • Safe long-term + tax: PPF me.

Is tarah aapke paas suraksha, liquidity aur growth teeno rehte hain. Sab ande ek tokri me na rakhein — alag-alag jagah, alag lakshya ke liye. Aur jaldi shuru karein, kyunki har saal ki deri compounding ka fayda ghata deti hai.

Paisa kab chahiye — usi se jagah tay hoti hai

Sabse pehla sawaal "kitna return milega" nahi hai. Pehla sawaal ye hai ki ye paisa kab wapas chahiye. Samay tay ho jaaye to jagah apne aap chun jaati hai.

Aur ek baat jo bechne wala nahi bataata: 30% slab wale ke liye 7% ki FD par tax ke baad lagbhag 4.9% bachta hai. Mehngai 6% ho to paisa badhne ke bawajood khareedne ki taakat ghat rahi hoti hai. Ye anuman hai, koi sifarish nahi.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Paisa kahan rakhein — FD ya SIP?

Lakshya par nirbhar. Short-term aur suraksha ke liye FD; long-term (7+ saal) growth ke liye SIP.

FD ka byaj tax-free hai?

Nahi, FD ka byaj poora taxable hai aapke slab par. PPF ka byaj tax-free hota hai.

SIP surakshit hai?

SIP market-linked hai, short-term me risk hai. Lambe samay (7+ saal) me risk kam aur return achha hota hai.

PPF me paisa kab tak phasta hai?

15 saal ka lock, par byaj tax-free aur poori tarah surakshit. Long-term ke liye achha.

Emergency fund kahan rakhein?

Savings account aur FD me — jahan zaroorat par turant paisa mile (liquidity).
