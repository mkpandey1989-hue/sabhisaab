Is hisse mein: Tehri Garhwal ki jansankhya, samaj, bhasha, ghar ki banawat, pehnava, gehne, lok-sangeet, nritya, vaadya-yantra, sanskar aur tyohar. "Tehri Garhwal ki bhasha kya hai", "Tehri nath kya hai", "Garhwali shadi ki rasmein" jaise sawal yahan seedhe poochhe jate hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Aabadi, Samuday aur Anusuchit Janjati

Seedha jawab: Census 2011 ke anusar Tehri Garhwal ki jansankhya 6,18,931 hai. Yahan mahilao ki sankhya purusho se zyada hai (1,077 mahilayein prati 1,000 purush), jiska bada karan purusho ka rozgaar ke liye bahar jana hai. Anusuchit jati lagbhag 16.5% aur anusuchit janjati sirf 0.14% hai.

Mool Samuday

  • Mukhya samuh: Garhwali Brahmin, Rajput aur Shilpkar (Anusuchit Jati ke kai upsamuh).
  • Jaunpuri samaj: Jaunpur block me Jaunpuri sanskriti ka alag lok-jeevan hai, jo padosi Jaunsar-Bawar se milta-julta hai.
  • Anusuchit Janjati: Janjati ki sankhya bahut kam hai. Isliye Bhotia, Jaunsari, Buksa, Tharu aur Van Raji jaise janjatiyon ka prabhav Tehri ke andar kam hai. Ye samuh mukhya roop se Uttarkashi, Chamoli, Pithoragarh, Dehradun (Jaunsar-Bawar) aur Udham Singh Nagar me rehte hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Samaj ki Banawat, Upnaam aur Gotra

Seedha jawab: Garhwal ke samaj me teen mukhya varg dikhte hain: Brahmin, Rajput (Thakur) aur Shilpkar. Tehri me aam surnames me Uniyal, Bahuguna, Naithani, Nautiyal, Raturi, Dobhal, Saklani, Semwal (Brahmin) aur Rawat, Negi, Bisht, Panwar, Rana, Bhandari, Chauhan (Rajput) ate hain.

Zaroori: Surname kisi vyakti ki jati ya parampara ke bare me sthaiyi nishkarsh nahi dete.

Mool Gaon (Mool Niwas / Thaat) aur Vivah ke Niyam

  • Mool niwas / thaat: Parivar ka asli gaon, jahan kul-devta ka sthan hota hai. Palayan ke baad bhi log shaadi aur puja ke liye wapas ate hain.
  • Gotra aur vivah: Paramparik vivah me apne gotra me shaadi nahi hoti. Jati aur gotra ke niyam parivar ke hisab se alag ho sakte hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Bhasha, Up-boliyan aur Roz ke Shabd

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal ki mukhya bhasha Garhwali hai (Census ke anusar lagbhag 90% logo ki matri bhasha). Jaunpur kshetra me Jaunpuri boli jati hai. Hindi sampark bhasha hai.

Tehri Garhwal ki Boli

  • Garhwali ki boliyan: Garhwali bhasha ki kai boliyan hain. Tehri me Gangapariya (Tehri-Garhwali) aur Jaunpuri prasiddh hain.
  • Anya boliyan: Rathi, Badhani, Dasholya, Salani aur Srinagariya Garhwal ke anya hisso me prachalit hain.
  • Anya bhashayein: Nepali aur Jaunsari bhi kam sankhya me boli jati hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Vernacular Pahadi Architecture (Pahadi Ghar)

Seedha jawab: Pahadi ghar patthar aur lakdi se bante hain, jinki chhat pathal (slate) ki hoti hai. Ground floor me goth (pashu-ghar) hota hai jiski garmi upar ke kamre ko sardi me garm rakhti hai. Pehli manzil par tibari (lakdi ki nakkashi wala barandah) aur chhaja hote the.

Pathar-Lakdi ka Nirman, Bakhli, Pathal ki Chhat aur Devdar ki Nakkashi

  • Pathal ki chhat: Chaude slate ke patthar, jo garmi me thande aur sardi me garm rehte hain.
  • Lakdi ki nakkashi: Deodar ya chir ki lakdi par kholi (dwar), tibari aur jharokho par nakkashi.
  • Bakhli: Kai gharo ki ek hi lambi qatar jo ek aangan ko saajha karti hai.
  • Bhukamp-rodhak tareeka: Uttarkashi ke Yamuna ghati ka prasiddh "Koti Banal" shaili ka ghar lakdi aur patthar ki mishrit parat se bhukamp me tika rehne ke liye jana jata hai. Is shaili ka prabhav pasos ke Garhwal me bhi mana jata hai.

Tehri Garhwal: Paramparik Pehnawa aur Kapde

Seedha jawab: Garhwali mahilao ka paramparik pehnava ghaghri (lehnga jaisa), angadi (choli), pichora (odhani) aur sardiyo me oon ki shawl (lava/gati) hai. Purusho ka pehnava mirjai, sadri, kurta-pajama aur pahadi topi hai.

Mahilaon ka Pehnava

  • Ghaghri aur angadi: Roj ke aur tyohar ke vastra.
  • Pichora: Shaadi aur tyohar par odhne wala saffron/lal rang ka odhna.
  • Dhatu: Sir par bandhne ka kapda.
  • Lava / gati: Oon ka lapeta hua vastra, jise sardi me kamar par bandh kar rakha jata tha.

Purushon ka Pehnava

  • Mirjai aur sadri: Purushon ki baniyan-jaisi upar ki jacket.
  • Oonni coat aur churidar: Sardi ke kapde.
  • Pahadi topi: Aaj Uttarakhand ki pehchan ban chuki hai, jo har tyohar aur rajyik samaroh me pehni jati hai.

Tehri Garhwal: Paramparik Pahadi Gehne

Tehri Garhwal: Anushthan ki Lok-Kala: Aipan, Jyunti aur Lakdi ka Kaam

Seedha jawab: Aipan mukhya roop se Kumaon ki ritual chitrakala hai. Garhwal me bhi shubh avsaro par dwar aur aangan ko rang aur chawal ke ghol se sajane ki parampara hai, jiska roop aur naam ilake ke hisab se badal jata hai.

Aipan, Geru-Biswar ki Vidhi aur Matrika Chitra

  • Aipan: Geru (lal mitti) ki sataah par chawal ke geele aate (biswar) se ungliyo se banaye gaye alankaran.
  • Kumaon-Garhwal ka farak: Aipan naam aur uski prasiddhi Kumaon me zyada hai. Tehri-Garhwal me shubh avsaro par dwar-patra (door decoration) aur zameen par rangoli jaise alankaran dikhte hain.
  • Lakdi ki nakkashi: Tibari, kholi aur mandir ke dwar par deodar ki nakkashi Garhwal ki apni khaas pehchan hai.
  • Ringal ka kaam: Ringal (pahadi baans) se tokri, supa, chatai aur chhoti-moti vastue banai jati hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Lok-Sangeet, Lok-Gaathayein aur Gayan Parampara

Seedha jawab: Garhwal ke lok-sangeet me jagar (devta ka aahvan), pawada (veer gatha), mangal geet (shaadi ke geet), khuded geet (mayke ki yaad ke geet), jhumelo aur chaunfula shamil hain.

Jagar, Pawada, Khuded, Chaiti & Mangal Geet

  • Jagar: Dhol-damau ke saath devta ki gatha gayi jati hai aur devta kisi vyakti (dangariya) par avtarit hota hai.
  • Pawada: Veer yoddhao ki gatha, jaise Madho Singh Bhandari, Teelu Rauteli.
  • Mangal geet: Shaadi ki alag-alag rasmo par gaye jane wale shubh geet.
  • Khuded geet: Basant ritu me mayke ki yaad me gaye jane wale bhavuk geet.
  • Chaiti aur Bhitauli ke geet: Chaitra maah ke geet, jinme bhai-behen ke milan ka zikr hota hai.

Tehri Garhwal: Paramparik Lok Nritya

Seedha jawab: Garhwal ke mashhoor lok-nritya hain Thadiya, Chaunfula, Jhumelo, Chhopati, Tandi (Jaunpuri), Pandav Nritya aur Harul. Jaunpur kshetra ka Raso aur Tandi nritya alag pehchan rakhte hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Paramparik Vadya Yantra aur Bajgi/Auji Samuday

Seedha jawab: Garhwal ke mukhya vaadya hain dhol, damau, ransingha, bhankora, masakbeen (bagpipe), turri aur hurka. Dhol-damau bajane ka kaam paramparik roop se Auji / Bajgi samudaay ke kalakar karte aye hain.

Dhol-Damau (Dhol Sagar), Ransingha, Masakbeen, Hurka & Bhankora

  • Dhol aur damau: Shaadi, jagar aur har tyohar me bajne wale mukhya dhol. Dhol ke taalo ka shastra "Dhol Sagar" kehlata hai.
  • Ransingha: Tambe ki bhaari, S-aakar ki turhi.
  • Masakbeen: Garhwali bagpipe, jo shaadi aur sainik samaroh me bajta hai.
  • Hurka: Kumaon ka mukhya vaadya, par Garhwal me bhi kuch jagah.
  • Auji / Bajgi kalakar: In kalakaro ki parampara aur unke samman par aaj chintan ho raha hai, kyoki naye dour me inke liye aajivika ka sankat hai.

Tehri Garhwal: Pahadi Life-Cycle Rituals (Sanskars) & Weddings

Seedha jawab: Garhwali shaadi kai din chalne wali rasmo ka silsila hai, jisme ganesh puja, haldi, baraat ka swagat, kanyadan, saptapadi aur bidaai shamil hain. Dhol-damau aur mangal geet shaadi ka mukhya hissa hain.

Namkaran, Janeu & Pahadi Wedding Customs

  • Namkaran: Navjat shishu ka naam rakhne ki rasm.
  • Janeu (upanayan): Brahmin aur kuch Rajput parivaro me.
  • Shaadi: Pahadi shaadi me barat ka dhol-damau ke saath aana, dulhe ka swagat aur vidhi se kanyadan hota hai. Kuch ilako me dulhan ke ghar par shaam ko dulhe ke paon dhone ka ritual bhi hai.
  • Bidaai: Beti ki bidaai ke samay ke karun geet garhwali sanskriti ka sabse bhavuk hissa hain.

Note: Rasme ilake, jati aur parivar ke hisab se alag hoti hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Mausami Tyohar
