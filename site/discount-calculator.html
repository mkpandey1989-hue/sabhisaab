Ganit kaise chalta hai

Discount hamesha MRP par lagta hai, aur bachat = MRP × discount%. Final keemat = MRP − bachat. Do discount ek saath lage to wo jud'te nahi, ek ke baad ek lagte hain — "50% + 20%" ka matlab 70% nahi, balki 60% hota hai.

Formula: Bachat = Price × Discount% ÷ 100
Final = Price − Bachat

Number ke saath teen misaal

Misaal 1 — seedha discount. ₹57,500 ki cheez par 20% discount. Bachat ₹11,500 aur dena padega ₹46,000.

Misaal 2 — do discount. ₹2,000 par pehle 50%, phir 20% extra. Pehle ₹1,000 bacha, phir ₹1,000 par 20% = ₹200. Final ₹800 — yaani kul 60% discount, 70% nahi.

Misaal 3 — GST ke saath. ₹1,000 par 30% discount = ₹700. Uspar 18% GST lage to dena ₹826. Bahut jagah tag par GST alag hota hai.

Ye baatein aksar chhoot jaati hain

  1. Do discount ko jod dena. "40% + 10%" ka matlab 50% nahi, 46% hota hai.
  2. Badhi hui MRP par khush hona. Sale se pehle MRP badha kar phir bada discount dikhana aam chaal hai. Purani keemat check karein.
  3. Delivery aur GST na jodna. Online me final keemat me delivery aur tax jud kar bachat kam ho jaati hai.
  4. Zaroorat ke bina kharidna. "₹5,000 bacha" tab hi sach hai jab wo cheez aapko waise bhi chahiye thi.
  5. Return policy na dekhna. Sale ke saaman par aksar return nahi hota. Kharidne se pehle padh lein.

Bharat me dhyaan dene layak

  • MRP: Bharat me MRP me tax shaamil hota hai — MRP se zyada koi nahi le sakta.
  • Bank offer: Card ke discount ki aam taur par ek adhiktam seema hoti hai.
  • Exchange offer: Purane saaman ki keemat aur naye par discount alag-alag ginein.
  • No-cost EMI: Ismein aksar discount hata diya jaata hai — dono ki tulna karein.
  • Bill lena: Warranty aur return ke liye pakka bill zaroori hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Discount kaise nikaalein?

Price ko discount% se guna karke 100 se bhaag dein — wahi bachat hai.

50% + 20% ka matlab 70% hota hai?

Nahi, 60% hota hai. Dusra discount pehle wale ke baad ki keemat par lagta hai.

MRP me GST hota hai?

Haan, MRP me sab tax shaamil hote hain.

Ulta kaise nikaalein?

₹800 ki cheez ₹2,000 ki thi to discount = (2000−800)/2000 × 100 = 60%.

Kya dukaan MRP se zyada le sakti hai?

Nahi, ye kanoonan galat hai.
