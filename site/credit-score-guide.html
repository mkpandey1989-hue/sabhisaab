Credit Score (CIBIL) Kaise Sudhaarein?

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Jab aap loan ya credit card ke liye apply karte hain, to bank sabse pehle ek number dekhta hai — aapka credit score (jise aam taur par CIBIL score kaha jaata hai). Ye teen ank ka number (aam taur par 300 se 900 ke beech) batata hai ki aap paisa waapas karne me kitne bharosemand hain. Achha score sasta loan aur jaldi approval dilata hai, kharaab score aapki application rukwa deta hai. Is guide me hum samjhenge ki credit score kya hai, kaise banta hai, aur ise kaise sudhaarein.

Credit score kaise banta hai?

Aapka score aapke paise wapas karne ki aadat par based hota hai. Ye cheezein sabse zyada asar daalti hain:

  • Time par payment: EMI aur credit card ka bill time par bharna sabse bada factor hai. Ek bhi late payment score girata hai.
  • Credit utilization: Aapke card ki limit ka kitna hissa aap istemaal karte hain. Limit ka 30% se kam use karna achha maana jaata.
  • Credit ki umar: Purane card aur loan achhe se chalaye gaye hon to score ko fayda hota hai.
  • Naye loan/enquiries: Kam samay me bahut saare loan ke liye apply karna score ko neeche laata hai.
  • Credit ka mix: Secured (home loan) aur unsecured (card) dono ka sahi mel achha rehta.
Yaad rakhein: Credit score aapka bank balance nahi dekhta. Ameer hone se score achha nahi hota — score sirf ye dekhta hai ki aap udhaar time par chukaate hain ya nahi.

Score kharaab hone ki aam wajahein

Zyadatar log inhi galtiyon se score girate hain — credit card ka bill "minimum due" bharkar baaki chhod dena, EMI ki date bhool jana, card ki poori limit istemaal kar lena, ya ek saath kai jagah loan ke liye apply karna. Ek aur badi wajah hai apne credit report me galti — kabhi-kabhi koi aisa loan bhi aapke naam dikhta hai jo aapne liya hi nahi. Isi liye saal me ek baar apni report zaroor check karni chahiye.

Score sudhaarne ke asaan tarike

  1. Har bill time par bharein: Auto-pay laga dein taaki date na bhoolein. Ye sabse bada aur jaldi asar dikhane wala kadam hai.
  2. Card ka use limit ke 30% se kam rakhein: Agar limit ₹1 lakh hai to ₹30,000 se kam istemaal karein.
  3. Purana card band mat karein: Purane card ki umar aapke score ko madad karti hai.
  4. Ek saath kai loan ke liye apply na karein: Har application ek "enquiry" chhodti hai jo score girati hai.
  5. Report me galti sudhwaayein: Koi galat entry dikhe to credit bureau me dispute daalein.

DTI aur score ka rishta

Bank sirf score nahi, aapka Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio bhi dekhta hai — yaani aapki income ka kitna hissa pehle se EMI me jaa raha hai. Agar aapki aadhi salary EMI me chali jaati hai, to achhe score ke baawjood naya loan milna mushkil ho sakta hai. Isliye naya loan lene se pehle apna DTI check karein. Sab Hisaab ka DTI calculator ye turant bata deta hai ki aapki income ke hisaab se aapka karza kitna healthy hai.

Kitna time lagta hai sudhaarne me?

Credit score raatoraat nahi sudharta — ye ek dheemi, lagataar chalne wali cheez hai. Agar aap aaj se time par payment shuru karein aur card ka use kam rakhein, to aam taur par kuch mahinon me sudhaar dikhne lagta hai, aur 6-12 mahine me achha farak aa sakta hai. Sabse zaroori baat — ek baar score achha ho jaaye to use banaye rakhna. Ek missed payment mahinon ki mehnat ko peeche dhakel sakta hai.

Aakhir me, achha credit score koi jaadu nahi, sirf achhi aadat ka nateeja hai — time par chukaana, zaroorat bhar hi udhaar lena, aur apni report par nazar rakhna. Ye teen aadatein aapka score dheere-dheere upar le jaayengi, aur jab kabhi bade loan (ghar, gaadi) ki zaroorat padegi, to yahi score aapko sasta byaj aur jaldi approval dilaayega.

Naye logon ke liye score kaise shuru karein?

Bahut se students aur naye kamane wale poochte hain — "mera to koi loan hi nahi, score kaise banega?" Sahi baat hai, bina kisi credit history ke score ban-na mushkil hota hai. Shuruaat karne ka safe tareeka hai ek basic credit card lena (ya secured card, jo FD ke against milta hai), use halka-phalka istemaal karna, aur har mahine poora bill time par bharna. Kuch mahinon me aapki ek achhi history ban-ne lagti hai. Yaad rahe — card lena galat nahi, use galat tareeke se chalana galat hai. Card ko udhaar ki tarah mat dekhein, use ek payment tool ki tarah dekhein jise aap poora chukate hain.

Ek myth jo aksar sunne me aata hai

Log samajhte hain ki credit card ka istemaal na karna score ke liye achha hai. Sach ulta hai — agar aapke paas card hai par aap use bilkul istemaal nahi karte, to bureau ke paas dekhne ke liye koi record hi nahi hota. Thoda istemaal karke time par chukaana, na istemaal karne se behtar hai. Isi tarah "minimum due" bharna ek trap hai — isse late fee to nahi lagti par baaki rakam par bhaari byaj chalta rehta hai aur aapki utilization high dikhti hai. Hamesha poora bill bharne ki koshish karein, minimum nahi.

Score kis cheez se kitna banta hai

Credit score ek jaadu nahi, ganit hai. Lagbhag ye bantwara hota hai:

KyaWazanSabse aasaan sudhaar
Samay par bhugtan~35%Auto-debit laga dijiye
Limit ka kitna istemaal~30%30% se neeche rakhiye
Kitne saal purana record~15%Purana card band mat kijiye
Kitne tarah ke karze~10%Apne aap ban jaata hai
Nayi poochh-taachh (enquiry)~10%Ek saath kai loan apply mat kijiye
Sabse jaldi asar dikhane wali cheez doosri line hai. Rs 1 lakh limit par Rs 70,000 kharch dikhna score gira deta hai, chahe aap poora bill samay par bhar rahe hon. Bill date se pehle thoda paisa bhar dene se limit ka istemaal kam dikhta hai aur score 1-2 mahine me hi upar chadhta hai. Aur ek baat jo ulta lagti hai — purana credit card band karne se score girta hai, kyunki aapka record chhota ho jaata hai. Na chalaana ho tab bhi khula rehne dijiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Achha credit score kitna hota hai?

Aam taur par 750 ya usse upar ka score achha maana jaata hai aur loan/card jaldi approve hone me madad karta hai. Bank apne niyam ke hisaab se dekhte hain.

Credit score check karne se score girta hai?

Aap khud apna score check karein (soft enquiry) to nahi girta. Bank jab loan ke liye check karta hai (hard enquiry) to thoda asar padta hai.

Score sudhaarne me kitna time lagta hai?

Time par payment aur kam card use se aam taur par 6-12 mahine me achha sudhaar dikh sakta hai. Ye dheere-dheere hone wali cheez hai.

Bina credit card ke score banega?

Loan (jaise consumer/EMI) time par chukaane se bhi score banta hai, par credit ka koi record na ho to score ban-na mushkil hota hai.
