Uttarkashi ka khaana sidha-saada hai, par uska swad pahad ki hawa, loh ki kadhai aur gaon ke anaaj se aata hai. Yahan ki rasoi me mandua ki roti, phaanu, kafuli, chainsoo, jhangora ki kheer, bhang (bhangjeera) ki chutney aur pisyun loon sabse zyada mashhoor hain. Gangotri-Yamunotri ke yatra marg par zyadatar khaana shakahari (sattvik) milta hai, aur Harsil, Dharali, Sankri, Barkot jaise sthanon par aap local Garhwali thali bhi dhoondh sakte hain.

Is hisse mein hum khane ke har pehlu ko likh rahe hain: mukhya vyanjan, roti-chawal, chutney aur namak, jangli phal-sabzi, mithai, pey aur rasoi ke bartan.

Zaroori note: Neeche di gayi recipe gharelu andaaze par aadharit hain. Matra aur samay ghar, mausam aur anaaj ke hisaab se badalte hain. Jangli phal, patte ya mushroom ko bina sahi pehchan ke kabhi na khayein.

Uttarkashi: Zile Ke Mukhya Vyanjan (Iconic Main Course)

Kafuli, Phaanu, Chainsoo, Thechwani, Bhatt Ki Churkani, Gahat Ki Daal Aur Aaloo Ke Gutke

Kafuli palak aur methi se bani gadhi hari sabzi hai, phaanu bhigoyi hui daalon se bana gaadha tadka hai, aur chainsoo bhuni hui kaali urad ki daal se bani daal hai. Ye teeno Garhwal ki sabse mashhoor rasoi me ginti hain aur Uttarkashi me bhi bahut bante hain.

VyanjanMukhya samagriKaise banta hai (sankshep)Kab/kis ke saath
KafuliPalak, methi, chawal ya aata ka gholLoh ki kadhai me dhimi aanch par pakate hain, jisse sabzi gaadhi aur hari rehti haiChawal ya roti ke saath, sardi me
PhaanuKai tarah ki daalein (jaise gahat, urad, matar)Daalein raat bhar bhigoyi jaati hain, peesi jaati hain, phir kam masale me pakai jaati hainBhaat ke saath
ChainsooKaali urad ki daalDaal ko bhunkar peesa jaata hai, phir lohe me pakate hainBhaat ke saath, sardi me

Bhatt ki churkaniKaale bhatt (soyabean)Bhatt ko bhunkar peesa jaata hai, phir lohe ki kadhai me pakate hainBhaat ke saath
Gahat ki daalGahat (kulath)Daal dheere pakti hai, aksar bhaddu jaise bhaari bartan meGarm bhaat ke saath, sardi me

Ek khaas baat: Kafuli ko aksar Uttarakhand ka "rajya vyanjan" bhi kaha jata hai (ye ek lokpriya pehchan hai, sarkari ghoshna nahi).

Phaanu aur chainsoo me antar: Phaanu me kai daalein bhigoyi aur peesi jaati hain, jabki chainsoo me sirf bhuni hui kaali urad ki daal ka istemal hota hai. Dono ka swad aur ghanapan alag hota hai.

Ek aasaan Phaanu ka ghar ka tareeka (andaaze ke saath):

1.Gahat aur matar jaisi daalein raat bhar bhigo dein.

2.Subah unko sil-batte par ya mixie me coarse (mote) peesen.

3.Loh ki kadhai me tel garam karke jakhiya ya jeera ka tadka lagayein.

4.Peesa hua mishran daalein, haldi aur namak milayein, aur dhimi aanch par pakayein, jab tak wo gaadha na ho jaye.

5.Garam bhaat ke saath parosein.

Ek aasaan Kafuli ka ghar ka tareeka:

1.Palak aur methi ko dhokar baareek kaat lein.

2.Loh ki kadhai me tel garam karke jakhiya ya jeera ka tadka lagayein, phir adrak aur haldi daalein.

3.Palak aur methi daalein aur halka sa pakayein.

4.Thoda chawal ya aate ka ghol milayein taaki sabzi gaadhi ho jaye.

5.Dhimi aanch par pakne dein, aur garam roti ya bhaat ke saath parosein.

Pehchan ki cheez: Uttarkashi ke kai gaon me daal ya sabzi me jakhiya ka tadka lagta hai (Jakhiya ke bare me Uttarkashi: Pahadi Daalein, Tilhan Aur Masale me padha hoga).

Uttarkashi: Paramparik Roti, Bread Aur Chawal Ki Taiyari

Mandua Ki Roti, Gahat Ke Parathe, Jhangore Ka Bhaat Aur Baaki

Mandua ki roti Uttarakhand ki sabse paramparik roti hai, jo mandua (finger millet) ke aate se banti hai. Isko aksar ghee, gud, makhan ya hari chutney ke saath khaya jata hai.

Roti / BhaatSamagriKhaas baat
Mandua ki rotiMandua ka aata, paaniGaadhe, kaale-bhoore rang ki roti. Sardi me khaas pasand ki jaati hai
Gahat ke paratheGehun ka aata, gahat ki daal ki bharvanGarm tasir ki daal bhara hua paratha
Chhoi / Lesu rotiGehun ka aata, mandua ya daal ki bharvanBhari hui moti roti
Jhangore ka bhaatJhangora (barnyard millet)Chawal jaisa pakaya jaata hai, halka aur jaldi pachne wala maana jata hai
Gahat ras-bhaatGahat ka ras aur chawalSardi ka mausam wala khaana

Mandua ki roti banane ke liye kuch tips:

1.Aate ko garm paani se goondhein, thanda paani karne par aate me jod nahi aata.

2.Hatheli se thapkar roti banayein, belan ka istemal kam karein.

3.Loh ke tave par dhimi aanch par sekein.

4.Garam hone par ghee ya makhan lagakar parosein.

Mandua ka aata dhaan ya gehun ke aate ki tarah chikna nahi hota, isliye shuru me roti tootti hai. Thoda abhyas ke baad ye aasaan lagta hai.

Gangotri aur Harsil me aap mandua ki roti, jhangora ki kheer aur daal-bhaat jaise paramparik khaane khane ki ummeed kar sakte hain (Incredible India ke Gangotri ke vivaran me bhi isi tarah ke vyanjanon ka zikr hai), par hotel aur dhabe ke menu har saal alag ho sakte hain.

Uttarkashi: Chutney, Raita, Achar Aur Pisyun Loon

Bhangjeera, Bhang Ke Beej Ki Chutney, Pahadi Kheere Ka Raita, Timur, Darim Aur Linguda/Mooli Achar

Uttarkashi ke khane ka asli swad chutney me chhupa hai. Bhangjeera (perilla seeds) ya bhang ke beej ki chutney, timur ki chutney, aur pahadi kheere ka raita sabse mashhoor hain.

Chutney / RaitaSamagriSwad
Bhang ke beej ki chutneyBhang ke beej (hemp seeds), hari mirch, namak, neebuKhatta-teekha, thoda tel jaisa swad
Bhangjeera ki chutneyBhangjeera (perilla seeds), mirch, namakAlag nuttiness wala swad
Timur ki chutneyTimur (Sichuan pepper jaisa masala), hara dhaniya, mirchZubaan par halki jhanjhanahat
Pahadi kheere ka raitaPahadi (peela) kheera, dahi, sarson ki tez khushbooThanda, tez sarson ke swad wala
Darim ki chutneyJangli anaar ke dane, namakKhatti
Mooli ka acharPahadi mooli, sarson ka tel, masaleSardi ke dino ka achar

Ek zaroori spashtikaran (bhang ke beej ke bare me): Bhang ki chutney me sirf bhang ke beej (hemp seeds) ka istemal hota hai, jo paramparik gharelu khane ka hissa hain. Isme nasha nahi hota, aur ye bhang ki patti ya sookhi ghaas se alag hain. Bhang ke paudhe ke upyog par kanooni niyam bahut sakht hain.

Pisyun Loon (Sil-Batte Par Peesa Hua Swaadisht Namak)

Pisyun loon sil-batte par peesa hua masaledaar namak hai, jisme namak ke saath lehsun ke patte, hari mirch, hara dhaniya, jeera aur timur milaye jaate hain. Ye pahad me roti, kheere ya phal ke saath khaya jaata hai.

Aasaan tareeka (andaaze se):

1.Sil par namak, lehsun ke patte (ya lehsun), hari mirch aur hara dhaniya rakhein.

2.Thoda jeera aur timur milayein.

3.Batte se baareek peeste hue sab milayein.

4.Taaza khayein, ya kaanch ki shishi me kuch din rakhein.

Garmi me kheere ke upar ya sardi me santre ya galgal (bade neebu) ke saath pisyun loon pahadi gharon ki pehchan hai. Pisyun loon ko "swad ka jaadu" kehte hain, kyonki ye sirf namak se kai guna zyada swaad deta hai.

Uttarkashi: Jungle Ki Khaane Layak Cheezein Aur Mausami Saag

Bicchu Ghas (Kandali), Linguda, Kankoda, Ghingaru, Kaphal, Hisalu, Kilmora, Bedu Aur Buransh Ka Sharbat

Uttarkashi ke jungle aur gaon ke aas-paas barish aur garmi ke mausam me kai jangli saag aur phal paye jaate hain. Inme Kandali (bicchu ghas), Linguda, Kafal, Hisalu, Kilmora, Bedu aur Buransh ka sharbat sabse mashhoor hain.

NaamKya haiMausamUpyog

Buransh ka sharbatBuransh (rhododendron) ke phool ka rasBasant meThanda pey

Buransh ke bare me: Buransh Uttarakhand ka rajya vriksh hai. Iske phool vasant me khilte hain aur iska sharbat gaon me bahut pasand kiya jata hai. Phool aur jungle ke paudhon ko bahut zyada na todein, kyonki ye jungle ka ek zaroori hissa hain.

Suraksha ke niyam (kripya zaroor padhein):

1.Kisi bhi jangli paudhe ko bina sahi pehchan ke na khayein. Kuch paudhe zehreele hote hain.

2.Kandali (bicchu ghas) ko dastane pehnkar todein, aur pakane ke baad hi khayein. Kacchi pattiyan chubhti hain.

3.Linguda aur fern jaise paudhe ko achhi tarah pakakar hi khayein. Kacchi ya adhkachchi sabzi se bachein, aur pehli baar khane se pehle gaon ke kisi jaankar se poochein.

4.Jungle me mushroom (gucchi jaise) bina visheshagya ke na todein ya khayein. Zehreele mushroom khatarnak hote hain.

5.National Park ya sanrakshit jungle me phool, phal ya paudhe todna kanooni roop se mana ho sakta hai.

Ye saari cheezein mausam par nirbhar hain, isliye aap agar trek ya gaon ki yatra par ja rahe hain, to local logon se poochkar hi inka swaad lein.

Uttarkashi: Paramparik Mithai, Tyohar Ke Pakwan Aur Prasad

Arsa, Singal, Puwa, Rote, Jhangora Ki Kheer Aur Gulgule

Pahad ki mithai sidhi-saadi hoti hai: gud, chawal ya aata aur tel ya ghee me talna. Inme Arsa, Singal, Puwa, Rote aur Jhangora ki kheer sabse mashhoor hain.

Singori, Bal Mithai aur Chocolate ke bare me ek sachchai: Singori (khoya ko Maalu ke patte me lapetkar banayi gayi mithai) aur Bal Mithai (bhoone khoye aur cheeni ke goloon wali mithai) ki asli pehchan Kumaon (Almora) se judi hai. Ye mithaiyan Uttarkashi ke kai mithai ki dukaano par mil sakti hain, par ye Uttarkashi ki paramparik mithai nahi hain.

Arsa banane me ek sujhaav: Gud ke ghol ko achhi tarah garm karein (jab tak ek taar na ban jaye), phir chawal ke aate me milayein. Aate ko bahut zyada paani na dein, warna Arsa tel me fail jaata hai.

Uttarkashi: Paramparik Pey Aur Fermented Drinks

Chhyang / Jaan Jaise Paramparik Pey, Buransh Sharbat, Seb Ka Juice Aur Gud-Adrak Ki Chai

Pahad me kai samuday apne tyohar aur samajik mauka par ghar me banaye gaye paramparik fermented pey peete hain. Isme Chhyang aur Jaan jaise naam aate hain.

Is vishay par ek zaroori baat saaf hai:

  • Ye pey sanskriti ka hissa hain. Jaunsar-Bawar jaise kshetron ke adhyayanon me paramparik fermented khane aur peyon ka zikr milta hai.
  • Ghar me banai gayi sharab ya sharab jaisa pey bechna ya bina license banana kanooni apradh ho sakta hai.
  • Yatra ke dham kshetron (Gangotri, Yamunotri) me nasha mana hota hai.

Garmi aur sardi ke gair-nashili pey:

PeyKab aur kaise
Buransh ka sharbatBasant me taaza phool se ya bazaar ki bottle se, thanda pey
Seb ka juiceSeb ke mausam me, Harsil aur Mori kshetra me aksar milta hai
Gud-adrak ki chaiSardi me, har chai ke thele par
Chaas aur lassiGarmi me, dahi se bani

Uttarkashi: Pahadi Rasoi Ki Banawat Aur Bartan

Loh Ki Kadhai, Sil-Batta, Kansa Ki Thali, Bhaddu, Parat Aur Mitti Ka Chulha

Pahadi rasoi ke bartan zyadatar loha, pital, kansa ya mitti ke hote hain. Ye bartan dheemi aanch par pakane ke liye bane hain aur har bartan ka khane ke swad me ek yogdan hota hai.

Bartan / ChizKaam
Loh ki kadhaiKafuli, saag aur daal pakane ke liye. Lohe me pakaya hua khaana swad me alag maana jata hai
Sil-battaChutney, namak aur masale peesne ke liye. Peesne se masale ki khushboo khilti hai
Kansa ki thaliKhaane ke liye, paramparik aur bhaari
Bhaddu / bhaari bartanDaal ko dheemi aanch par pakane ke liye. Naam ilaake ke hisaab se alag ho sakta hai
ParatAata goondhne ke liye badi thali jaisi
Mitti ka chulhaLakdi ya upla jalakar kaam karne wala chulha
Dhuan sukhane ki pattiChulhe ke upar ki patti ya jaali, jahan mas, lakdi ya beej dhuan se sukhaye jaate the (naam ilaake ke hisaab se alag)

Loh ki kadhai ka vigyan (saral bhasha me): Lohe me pakne wala khaana thoda sa lohe ka ansh le leta hai, aur isliye logon me ye dharna hai ki isme pakaya khaana gunkari hota hai. Kadhai ko pakane ke baad sukha kar rakhein, warna usme jung lag jaati hai.

Kansa aur pital ke bartan ki suraksha: Kansa aur pital me khatta khana (jaise dahi, imli, ya neebu) pakana ya rakhna nahi chahiye. Sahi kalai (tin ki parat) wale bartan hi use karein.

Aaj ke samay me: Gaon me abhi bhi paramparik chulhe aur sil-batte ka upyog ho raha hai, par kai ghar ab LPG aur mixer ka istemal bhi karte hain. Paramparik bartan ki kala bhi dheere-dheere kam ho rahi hai, isliye in bartano ko bachane ki koshish zaroori hai.

Uttarkashi: Chhote Sawal-Jawab

Uttarkashi ka mashhoor khaana kya hai?
Mandua ki roti, phaanu, kafuli, chainsoo, jhangora ki kheer aur bhang ke beej ki chutney Garhwal ke saath Uttarkashi me bhi mashhoor hain.
Kafuli kya hota hai?
Kafuli palak aur methi se bani gaadhi hari sabzi hai, jo loh ki kadhai me dhimi aanch par pakayi jaati hai.
Phaanu kya hota hai?
Phaanu bhigoyi aur peesi hui kai daalon se bana gaadha tadka hai, jo bhaat ke saath khaya jaata hai.
Chainsoo kya hota hai?
Chainsoo bhuni hui kaali urad ki daal se bani daal hai, jo bhaat ke saath khayi jaati hai.
Thechwani kya hai?
Thechwani kooti hui mooli ya aaloo ki sabzi hai, jo masale ke saath pakayi jaati hai.
Bhatt ki churkani kya hai?
Bhatt ki churkani kaale bhatt (soyabean) ko bhunkar aur peeskar bani sabzi hai.
Aaloo ke gutke kya hote hain?
Aaloo ke gutke uble aaloo ko jakhiya ke tadke me bhunkar banaye jaane wale sookhe aaloo hain.
Mandua ki roti kya hoti hai?
Mandua ki roti finger millet (mandua) ke aate se bani paramparik pahadi roti hai.
Jhangora ki kheer kaise banti hai?
Jhangora ko doodh aur gud ya cheeni ke saath dhimi aanch par pakane se jhangora ki kheer banti hai.
Gahat ki daal kis mausam me khayi jaati hai?
Gahat ki daal aam taur par sardi me khayi jaati hai, kyonki ise garm tasir wali maana jaata hai.
Jakhiya kya hai?
Jakhiya ek jangli sarson jaisa beej hai, jiska tadka pahadi daal aur sabzi me lagta hai.
Bhang ki chutney me nasha hota hai kya?
Bhang ki chutney me sirf bhang ke beej (hemp seeds) ka istemal hota hai, jisme nasha nahi hota, par bhang ke paudhe ya patti ka upyog kanooni roop se sakht niyamon me aata hai.
Timur kya hai?
Timur ek pahadi masala hai, jo zubaan par halki jhanjhanahat deta hai aur chutney me daala jaata hai.
Pisyun loon kya hota hai?
Pisyun loon sil-batte par peesa hua masaledaar namak hai, jisme lehsun ke patte, hari mirch, dhaniya aur timur milaye jaate hain.
Kandali ya bicchu ghas kya hai?
Kandali stinging nettle hai, jise pakakar pahad me saag banaya jaata hai.
Linguda kya hai?
Linguda fern ki komal nayi pattiyon se bani pahadi sabzi hai, jise hamesha achhi tarah pakakar khana chahiye.
Kafal kya hai?
Kafal pahad ka khatta-meetha laal jangli phal hai, jo garmi me pakta hai.

Hisalu kya hota hai? Hisalu jangli peela-narangi phal hai, jo jangli raspberry jaisa hota hai.

Kilmora kya hai?
Kilmora ek jangli khatta phal hai, jo garmi me milta hai.
Bedu kya hai?
Bedu jangli anjeer ka pahadi naam hai.
Buransh ka sharbat kaise banta hai?
Buransh ke phool ke ras se sharbat banta hai, jo basant ke mausam me pasand kiya jaata hai.
Buransh Uttarakhand ka rajya phool hai ya vriksh?
Buransh Uttarakhand ka rajya vriksh hai, aur Brahma Kamal rajya phool hai.
Arsa kya hai?
Arsa chawal ke aate aur gud ke ghol se bani, sarson ke tel ya ghee me tali jaane wali paramparik mithai hai.
Singal kya hai?
Singal gehun ke aate aur gud ke ghol se bani, jalebi jaisi tali jaane wali pahadi mithai hai.
Puwa kya hota hai?
Puwa gehun ke aate aur gud ke ghol ko tel me talkar banayi gayi meethi poori jaisi cheez hai.
Rote kya hota hai?
Rote moti, meethi roti hai jise gehun ke aate, gud aur ghee se banakar devta ko arpit kiya jaata hai.
Singori aur Bal Mithai kahan ki hain?
Singori aur Bal Mithai ki pehchan Kumaon (Almora) se judi hai, ye Uttarkashi ki paramparik mithai nahi hain.
Kya Gangotri aur Yamunotri me non-veg khaana milta hai?
Dham kshetron me aam taur par shakahari (sattvik) khaana hi milta hai.
Chhyang kya hota hai?
Chhyang pahad ke kuch samudayon ka paramparik fermented pey hai, par ghar me banakar bechna ya bina license sharab banana kanooni apradh ho sakta hai.
Loh ki kadhai me khana pakane se kya hota hai?
Lohe me pakne wala khaana thoda sa lohe ka ansh le leta hai.
Sil-batta kya hota hai?
Sil-batta chutney aur masale peesne ka paramparik patthar ka upkaran hai.
Kansa ki thali me khatta khana-khana chahiye ya nahi?
Kansa ya pital ke bartan me khatta khana pakana ya rakhna nahi chahiye, kyonki wo bartan ke saath pratikriya kar sakta hai.
Jungle ke mushroom khane chahiye ya nahi?
Bina visheshagya ki pehchan ke jungle ke mushroom nahi khane chahiye, kyonki kuch mushroom zehreele hote hain.
