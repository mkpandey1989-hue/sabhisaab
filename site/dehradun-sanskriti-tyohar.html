Is hisse mein: Dehradun ki aabadi aur janjatiyan (Jaunsari, Buksa, Bhotia ke Census aankde), Hindi-Garhwali-Jaunsari ka bhasha-naksha, Kath-Kuni makaan (Hanol aur Lakhamandal), Jaunsari kapde aur gehne (Jhangel, Chora, Dhantu, Nath, Bulak), Harul-Raso-Nati nritya, Bissu-Maroj-Magh Mela-Budhi Diwali, Mahasu Devta ke Jagra aur Doon ke Jhanda Mela, Tibetan Mindrolling aur Sikh-Ramraiya samaj ka parichay.

Bahut se shabd Kumaon ya Garhwal ke hain, Dehradun ke nahi. Jaise Chholiya, Aipan, Nanda Raj Jat, Bhotia (Marchha/Tolchha/Johari/Shauka), Van Raji aur Tharu. Dehradun me ye nahi hain ya bahut kam hain. Jahan zaroori hai wahan “yeh Dehradun me nahi hai” saaf likha gaya hai, aur uski jagah Dehradun ki asli parampara di gayi hai. Jahan koi parampara ab kam ho gayi hai ya kanoon ke hisaab se alag hai, wahan likha gaya hai.

Jahan sources me pakka praman nahi mila wahan hai.

Dehradun: Aabadi, Samuday aur Anusuchit Janjati

Dehradun ki aabadi 16,96,694 (Census 2011) hai. Isme Anusuchit Janjati (ST) lagbhag 1,11,663 (6.58%) aur Anusuchit Jati (SC) lagbhag 2,28,901 (13.49%) hai. ST me sabse bada samuday Jaunsari hai, jiski aabadi Dehradun me 86,516 hai.

Census ke Aankde: Aabadi, Ling Anupat, Saksharta aur Gaon-Shahar

Dehradun me purush 8,92,199 aur mahila 8,04,495 hain, yaani sex ratio 902 hai. Hill zilon ki tarah yahan mahilaon ki sankhya zyada nahi hai, kyunki Dehradun shehar me bahar se kaam ke liye aaye purush bhi bade pairmaane par hain. Shehri aabadi 55.52% aur gramin 44.48% hai.

Jaunsari samuday: Jaunsari Uttarakhand ki sabse badi janjati hai (poore rajya me lagbhag 88,664 Census data ke hisaab se; kuch sources me 1,37,000 tak batate hain, jisme parampara se jude log bhi ginte jate hain). Yeh mukhya roop se Jaunsar-Bawar me rehte hain, jo Tons aur Yamuna ke beech ka pahadi kshetra hai. Samuday ke log Mahasu Devta ko apna mukhya devta mante hain. Dehradun: Samaj ki Banawat, Upnaam aur Gotra

Dehradun ka samaaj paanch dhaaron se bana hai: Garhwali (Brahmin, Rajput, Shilpkar), Jaunsari-Bawari (Khas Brahmin-Rajput aur Koltas ya Shilpkar), Doon ke purane vyapari aur kisan samuday, Punjabi-Sikh aur Gorkha-Tibetan pravasi.

Dehradun me Garhwali surnames jaise Rawat, Negi, Bisht, Bhandari, Nautiyal, Dobhal, Uniyal, Semwal, Joshi aam hain. Jaunsar-Bawar ki sthaniya samajik vyavastha: Jaunsar-Bawar me paramparik roop se Khas (Brahmin-Rajput) aur Koltas (shilpkar/Anusuchit Jati) samuday rehte aaye hain. Sthaniya parampara me sanyukt parivar, gaon ki panchayat aur devta (Mahasu) ke aadesh ka mahatva raha hai. Kanoon ke hisaab se sab nagrik barabar hain, aur jati ke aadhar par bhed-bhav gair-kanooni hai.

Dehradun me kaunse surname zyada hain?

Jaunsar-Bawar ke paramparik shilpkar/Anusuchit Jati samuday ka sthaniya naam.

Mool Gaon (Mool Niwas / Thaat) ki Pehchan aur Vivah ke Niyam

Garhwal me “mool niwas” ya “thaat” purkhon ke gaon ko kehte hain, jisse parivar ki pehchan judi hoti hai. Vivah me gotra ka dhyan rakha jata hai (sagotra vivah nishedh). Jaunsar-Bawar me kuch parampara alag hain, jaise kanya ke vivah par vadhu-paksh ki or se kuch len-den ki parampara ka zikr milta hai.

  • Mool niwas aur Dehradun: Dehradun ke bahut se Garhwali parivar ka mool gaon Pauri, Tehri, Chamoli ya Uttarkashi me hai. Wo Dehradun me rozgar ke karan bas gaye hain. Isse “mool niwas” ka mudda Dehradun me bhi mahatvapurn ban jata hai (Dehradun mein Zameen Khareedne ke Kanoon aur Section 143 me bhu-kanoon dekhein).
  • Gotra aur vivah: Hindu paramparik vivah me gotra ka dhyan rakha jata hai. Yeh dharmik/samajik parampara hai.
  • Jaunsar-Bawar ki sthaniya parampara: Wikipedia ke ek lekh me Jaunsar-Bawar me “bride price” ki parampara ka zikr hai. Nirnay lene se pehle sthaniya samajik sansthaon se baat karein.
Mool niwas kya hota hai?
Purkhon ka mool gaon, jisse pehchan jodi jati hai.
Kya Dehradun me sagotra vivah hota hai?
Paramparik roop se nishedh mana jata hai.

Dehradun: Bhasha, Up-boliyan aur Roz ke Shabd

Dehradun me teen bhasha-parivar ek saath hain: Hindi (shehri aur sarkari), Garhwali (pravasi aur Doon ke Garhwali gaon) aur Jaunsari (Jaunsar-Bawar ka Pashchimi Pahari bhasha-vargh). Jaunsari ko 7.43% log apni mother tongue batate hain.

Dehradun mein Boli Jaane Wali Boliyan

Jaunsari ek Pashchimi Pahari Indo-Aryan boli hai, jo Himachal ke Sirmauri aur Mahasui se judti hai. Jaunsar-Bawar kshetra me do upa-kshetra hain: Jaunsar (niche ka hissa) aur Bawar (upar ka baraf wala hissa, jisme Kharamba peak aata hai).

Dehradun: Vernacular Pahadi Architecture (Pahadi Ghar)

Jaunsar-Bawar ke makaan aur mandir Kath-Kuni (Kothi Banal) shaili me patthar aur deodar lakdi se bante hain, jisme pathal (slate) ki dhalwan chhat aur lakdi par nakkashi hoti hai. Hanol ka Mahasu Devta mandir aur Lakhamandal mandir iske mashhoor udaharan hain.

Kath-Kuni (Koti Banal) Shaili, Pathal ki Chhat aur Devdar ki Nakkashi

Kath-Kuni ya Kothi Banal shaili patthar aur lakdi ki baari-baari ki parton se bani hoti hai, jisse makaan bhookamp ke jhatke sahne layak bante hain, aisa sthaniya manyata aur kai vidwan manate hain. Jaunsar-Bawar me makaan aam taur par patthar-lakdi se bante hain aur slate ki tiles se dhake hote hain.

Pathal (slate) ki chhat: “Pathal” us grey patthar ka sthaniya naam hai jisse chhat banti hai. Kushal karigar ise naap kar, tarashkar chhat banate hain. Jaunsar-Bawar ke makaan ke gable (tikon) wali dhalwan chhat par lakdi ki kalatmak nakkashi ek jaani-pehchani visheshta hai.

Doon ghati ki vastukala (bonus): Dehradun shehar ki imaraton me Forest Research Institute (FRI) ki vishal Greco-Roman style ki imarat (1920 ke dashak ke ant me puri), Doon School ka Chandbagh parisar, IMA ka Chetwode Hall aur Ghantaghar (Clock Tower) prasiddh hain. Mussoorie me British kaal ke Gothic church aur purane bungalow hain (nirman ke saal).

Kath-Kuni kya hota hai?
Patthar aur lakdi ko baari-baari se jodkar banayi jane wali pahadi shaili. Patthar aur lakdi ki pahadi shaili.

9vi sadi ka mana jata hai.
Lakhamandal kahan hai?

Ghar ki Banawat: Goth, Chakh, Tibari aur Aangan (Khalian)

Pahadi ghar me neeche ki manzil me aam taur par pashu (goth/obra) rehte hain, jisse upar ki manzil garm rehti hai. Upar ki manzil me rehne aur rasoi ke kamre hote hain. Chaja, tibari aur chakh jaise shabd Garhwal-Kumaon ke makaan ke hisse hain.

  • Goth / Obra: Neeche ki manzil, pashu-shala. Pashuon ki garmi se upar ki manzil garm rehti hai (paramparik gyan).
  • Chaja / Tibari: Lakdi ka barandah ya baithak ka sthan. Nakkashidar tibari Garhwal-Kumaon me prasiddh hai.
  • Chakh / Khalian: Chakh upar ki baithak ya khuli jagah, khalian patthar ka aangan.

Sthaniya naamon ki list gaon se hi milegi.

Pahadi ghar me neeche pashu kyun rakhte hain?
Taaki unki garmi upar ki manzil ko garm rakhe.
Tibari kya hoti hai?
Lakdi ki nakkashidar baithak ya barandah.

Dehradun: Paramparik Pehnawa aur Kapde

Jaunsar-Bawar ka paramparik pehnawa oon se bana hota hai. Purush Jhangel (oon ka paajama) aur Chora (oon ka lamba chola) pehente hain, jise oon ki patti (Manjan) se kamar par kasa jata hai. Mahilayein Ghagra, Jhoga/Cholori aur sir par Dhantu pehnti hain.

Mahilaon ka Pehnava: Ghagra, Jhoga/Cholori, Dhantu aur Waistcoat

Jaunsari mahilaon ka Ghagra bahut sari pleat wala hota hai. Boorhi mahilayein aam taur par kaala ghagra aur nai umr ki ladkiyan chatak rang pehnti hain. Ghagra ke neeche ke kinare ki patti ko “Lavaan” kehte hain.

Men’s Wear: Jhangel, Chora, Manjan, Thalka & Pahadi Topi

Jaunsari purush oon ka paajama (Jhangel) aur oon ka lamba chola (Chora) pehente hain. Chora ko kai tarah se bandha ja sakta hai aur kamar par oon ki patti (Manjan) lagti hai. Tyohar par Thalka ya Lohiya coat pehna jata hai.

  • Jhangel: Bheda ki oon ka paajama.
  • Chora: Oon ka lamba chola.
  • Manjan: Oon ki kamar-patti.
  • Pahadi topi: Garhwal-Kumaon me pehni jane wali topi. Dehradun ke Jaunsar me sthaniya topi ka naam aur shaili ke liye.
  • Purushon ke gehne: Ek source ke anusar purushon ne British-kaal ke niyamon ke prabhav se gehne pehna band kar diya tha.
Jaunsari purush kya pehente hain?
Jhangel aur Chora.

Manjan kya hota hai? Oon ki patti jo Chora ko kamar par kasti hai.

Dehradun: Paramparik Pahadi Gehne

Jaunsari mahilayein Tungal, Bulak, Nath aur Utraiyan jaise gehne pehnti hain. Jaunsar, Bawar aur Jaunpur ka Nath Tehri ke Nath se zyada bada hota hai (Tribune ki ek report ke anusar).

Nath, Bulak, Tungal, Utraiyan, Phulli, Beera & Galobandh

Dehradun: Anushthan ki Lok-Kala: Aipan, Jyunti aur Lakdi ka Kaam

Aipan (geru aur biswar se zameen/deewar par banne wali rangoli) mukhya roop se Kumaon ki parampara hai. Dehradun ke Kumaoni parivar ise tyoharon par banate hain. Jaunsar-Bawar ki apni kala lakdi ki nakkashi, dwar-chaukhat aur mandiron ki lakdi ki kalakari me dikhti hai.

Aipan, Geru, Biswar aur Jaunsar ki Nakkashi Parampara

Aipan lal geru (mitti) par safed chawal ke ghol (biswar) se ungliyon se bante hain. Dehradun ke Kumaoni parivar Diwali, Dussehra aur shubh avsar par ise banate hain. Dehradun ke Jaunsar me lakdi par nakkashi (deodar ki lakdi par) mandir aur ghar ki khaas pehchan hai.

  • Aipan ke prakar: Lakshmi chauki, Saraswati peeth, Shiv peeth, Ashta Dal Kamal jaise (purane sandarbh ke anusar, Kumaoni parampara).
  • Technique: Geru (lal mitti) se aadhar, uske upar biswar (geele chawal ka ghol) ko aakhiri teen ungliyon se lagakar aakriti.
  • Jyunti / Matrika patt: Kumaon ka ritual wall-painting, jo shubh avsar par banta hai.
  • Dehradun me: Aipan Dehradun ke Kumaoni samaj ke ghar aur mele-pradarshaniyon me dekha jata hai. Garhwal-Jaunsar ki alag lok-kala-shailiyon ke naam ke liye pakka source nahi mila.

Jaunsar ka lakdi ka kaam: Hanol aur Lakhamandal jaise mandiron ki Kath-Kuni imarat, lakdi ke dwar aur chhat ke kinare ki nakkashi, sthaniya shilpkar (kai ghar-ghar me purani parampara) ki kalatmak kushalta dikhati hain. In karigaron ki aaj ki sthiti aur sanrakshan ki yojnayen Dehradun: Jal-Vidyut Pariyojanayein, Baandh aur Navikaraniya Urja aur Dehradun: Shiksha: University, College, Boarding School aur Sansthan me dekhein.

Aipan kya hota hai? Geru aur chawal ke ghol se banne wali Kumaoni rangoli.

Kya Aipan Dehradun ki parampara hai?
Kumaoni parivar ise Dehradun me banate hain; mool Kumaon ki parampara hai.
Biswar kya hota hai?
Geele chawal ka ghol jisse Aipan banta hai.

Mela ek nayi lambi dhwaja (jhanda) ke arohan se shuru hota hai, aur lakhon shraddhalu Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, Uttar Pradesh aur Himachal se aate hain.

Jaunsar-Bawar’s Melas: Bissu, Magh Mela (Maroj) & Hanol-Thaina Mahasu Jagra

Jaunsar-Bawar ke mele Bissu (fasal-kataai), Magh Mela (Maroj) aur Mahasu Jagra hain. Hanol ka Mahasu Devta mandir Tons ke dayen kinare, Tyuni-Mori road par hai aur Dehradun se lagbhag 190 km door hai.

Mahasu Devta ki lok-katha: Maanyata hai ki Mahasu Devta chaar bhai hain: Botha, Pavasi, Vasik aur Chalda. Lok-katha ke anusar ek rakshas (Kirmir) ke atyachar se logon ko bachane ke liye Huna Bhatt ki prarthna par chaaron bhai prakat hue. Botha Hanol me rehte hain, Pavasi Hanol, Lakhamandal, Authana aur Uttarkashi me ghoomte hain, aur Chalda poore kshetra me ghoomkar nyaay karte hain. Mahasu ko “nyay ke devta” bhi kaha jata hai (ASI/ETV Bharat ke sandarbh me Lakhamandal ke liye bhi likha gaya hai). Yeh lok-katha hai, aitihasik praman nahi.

Hanol mandir ke visheshta (sources se):

  • Tyuni-Mori road par, Hanol gaon me; Tons nadi ke kinare.
  • 9vi sadi me bana hua mana jata hai; ASI ki sanrakshit imarat.
  • Kath-Kuni (Kothi Banal) shaili.
  • Do gol patthar jinhe “pavitra mann wala vyakti hi utha sakta hai” aisi manyata hai.
  • Mahasu ke mukhya parv: Bissu, Jagda, Paush ki Dhaal, Budhi Diwali.

Doli / Devta yatra: Mahasu Devta ki doli ya yatra ke samay-sarni ke liye mandir samiti ya sthaniya prashasan se jaankari lein. Dehradun ke Jaunsar me Mahasu ka nirantar bhraman (jaise Pavasi Mahasu ka alag-alag gaon me samay bitana) ek sthaniya parampara hai.

Anya mele: Nanda Raj Jat (12-year Himalayan Kumbh with Chausingha Khadu), Gauchar, Jauljibi, Thal, Bageshwar aur Syalde-Bikhoti Dehradun ke mele nahi hain.

Dehradun ke anya mele aur utsav (jaankari ke liye, tithi badalti hai):

  • Tapkeshwar Mahadev Mela: Mahashivratri par Tapkeshwar mandir me.
  • Rishikesh International Yoga Festival: March me, Rishikesh me.
  • Mussoorie ke utsav: Mussoorie me sardi aur garmi me paryatan utsav aur literary events hote hain; taaza calendar Paryatan Vibhag se dekhein.

Jaunsar-Bawar ke mukhya devta, chaar bhai ke roop me poojey jate hain.

Tyuni-Mori road par, Tons kinare, Dehradun se lagbhag 190 km.
