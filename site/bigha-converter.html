Zameen kharidni ho ya bechni, sabse pehla confusion naap ka hota hai — bigha, biswa, gaj, acre, hectare. Har rajya me bigha ka naap alag hota hai. Ye converter kisi bhi ikai ko doosri me badal deta hai, turant.

Bigha har rajya me alag — yahan ~27000 sqft. Apne ilaake ka confirm karein.

Bigha har rajya me alag kyun hai

Bigha koi sarkari, tay ikai nahi hai — ye purane zamane ka sthaniya naap hai jo har ilaake me alag banta gaya:

  • Uttarakhand / UP: lagbhag 27,000 varg foot
  • Bihar: kareeb 27,220 varg foot
  • Rajasthan: pucca bigha lagbhag 27,225, kachcha bigha lagbhag 17,424
  • Bengal: lagbhag 14,400 varg foot
  • Madhya Pradesh: lagbhag 12,000 varg foot

Kahin-kahin to ek hi zile ke do tehsil me farak mil jaata hai.

Isi liye: zameen ke har kagaz me naap varg foot ya varg meter me likhwayein. Sirf "2 bigha" likhne se baad me vivad ho sakta hai — aur zameen ke vivad saalon chalte hain.

Pahad me zameen nali aur mutthi me boli jaati hai. Uske liye Nali-Mutthi Converter aur poora vivaran is lekh me hai.

Ye ikaiyan kabhi nahi badaltin

  • 1 Acre = 43,560 sq ft = 4,047 sq meter
  • 1 Hectare = 10,000 sq meter = 2.47 acre
  • 1 Gaj (sq yard) = 9 sq ft
  • 1 Marla = 272.25 sq ft (uttar Bharat)
  • 1 Katha = 720 sq ft (Bihar me aam)
  • 1 Cent = 435.6 sq ft (dakshin Bharat)

Sarkari kagzon aur naksha me isi liye acre/hectare ka istemaal hota hai — ye poore desh me ek jaise hote hain.

Uttarakhand — nali aur mutthi ka naap

Pahadon me zameen ka naap aksar nali aur mutthi me hota hai:

  • 1 nali ≈ 240 varg meter (kareeb 2,583 varg foot)
  • 1 nali = 16 mutthi

Purani khatauni aur registry ke kagzon me yahi ikaiyan milti hain, jabki nayi registry aam taur par varg meter me hoti hai. Isi wajah se ek hi zameen ke do kagzon me alag-alag naap dikh sakta hai — ek me "5 nali", doosre me "1200 sq m". Dono ek hi baat keh rahe hain, bas ikai alag hai.

Zameen kharidte waqt — 6 zaroori baatein

  1. Khatauni aur naksha dono nikalwayein aur unka naap aapas me milaayein
  2. Zameen par jaakar khud naap karwayein — kagaz aur zameen me farak aam baat hai
  3. Chauhaddi (chaaron taraf ki simaa) kagaz me saaf likhi honi chahiye
  4. Naap varg foot/meter me likhwayein, sirf bigha ya nali me nahi
  5. Purani aur nayi ikai me farak ho to patwari/lekhpal se likhit me confirm karwayein
  6. Rasta (aane-jaane ka haq) kagaz me darj hai ya nahi — ye bhool jaana bahut mehnga padta hai

Rate ki tulna kaise karein

Zameen ka daam aksar "per bigha" ya "per nali" me bataya jaata hai, jabki do jagah ki tulna karne ke liye per square foot nikalna zaroori hai. Pehle poora rakba varg foot me badlein, phir kul daam se bhaag dein — asli tasveer saaf ho jaayegi.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

1 bigha me kitne square feet hote hain?

Ye rajya par depend karta hai. UP-Uttarakhand me lagbhag 27,000 sq ft, Rajasthan me kai jagah 17,424 sq ft, Bengal me kareeb 14,400 sq ft.

1 acre me kitne square feet?

43,560 sq ft. Acre har jagah ek jaisa hota hai, isliye sarkari kagzon me iska istemaal hota hai.

Nali kya hoti hai?

Uttarakhand ke pahadi ilaakon ki naap ki ikai. Ek nali lagbhag 240 sq meter (kareeb 2,583 sq ft) hoti hai, aur 1 nali = 16 mutthi.

Zameen ke kagaz me kaunsi ikai likhwani chahiye?

Hamesha square feet ya square meter. Sirf bigha likhne se baad me vivad ho sakta hai.

1 gaj (square yard) me kitne square feet hote hain?

1 gaj (square yard) = 9 square feet. Ye har jagah same rehta hai.

Uttarakhand me bigha kitna hota hai?

Lagbhag 6,804 square feet, yaani karib 756 gaj. Pahad me zameen aksar nali aur mutthi me boli jaati hai.

5 bigha in square feet kitna hoga?

UP pucca ke hisaab se 1,36,125 sq ft, aur Uttarakhand ke hisaab se lagbhag 34,020 sq ft. Apne rajya ka naap confirm kar lijiye.

Bigha to sq ft kaise convert karein?

Apne rajya ka naap chunkar upar wale converter me bigha ki ginti daal dijiye. Ek hi number har jagah nahi chalta — isi liye rajya chunna zaroori hai.

1 bigha in gaj kitna hota hai?

UP pucca bigha me 3,025 gaj, Rajasthan kaccha me 1,936 gaj, aur West Bengal me 1,600 gaj.
