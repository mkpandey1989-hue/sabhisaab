GST Calculator

Bill banana ho ya check karna ho ki daam me GST juda hai ya nahi — ye calculator dono kaam karta hai. Rakam daalein, GST rate chunein, aur batayein ki rakam me GST juda hai ya jodna hai. Baaki hisaab tool khud kar dega.

GST jodne aur hatane ka farak

Ye do bilkul alag hisaab hain aur log yahi galti karte hain.

Kis cheez par kaunsa GST — roz kaam aane wali list

GST ki dar cheez ke hisaab se badalti hai. Neeche wo cheezein hain jo aam taur par saamne aati hain.

KyaAam dar
Bina brand ke anaj, doodh, sabzi0%
Cheeni, chai, masale, packet wala khana5%
Ghee, makhan, computer, business class ticket12%
Sabun, TV, fridge, restaurant (AC), kapde ki mehngi range18%
Gaadi, tambaku, thandi drink, luxury saaman28% (+cess)
Bill par ek cheez zaroor jaanchiye: agar aap ek hi state me kharid rahe hain to bill par CGST aur SGST aadhi-aadhi dikhni chahiye — 18% ka matlab 9% + 9%. Doosre state se saaman aa raha hai to poora 18% IGST me dikhega. Kai chhote bill par dukaandar ek hi line me "GST 18%" likh dete hain, jo galat hai. Aur dar samay-samay par GST Council badalti rehti hai, isliye badi kharid se pehle cbic-gst.gov.in par confirm kar lijiye.
GST jodna (exclusive): GST = Rakam × rate ÷ 100
GST hatana (inclusive): Base = Rakam × 100 ÷ (100 + rate)

Misaal: ₹1,000 par 18% jodna hai to GST ₹180 aur total ₹1,180. Par agar ₹1,000 me pehle se 18% GST juda hua hai, to base ₹847 aur GST ₹153 hoga — ₹180 nahi. Yahi wajah hai ki seedha 18% ghatana galat jawab deta hai.

CGST, SGST aur IGST

  • Ek hi rajya me bikri: GST aadha-aadha bat jaata hai — CGST (kendra) + SGST (rajya). 18% ka matlab 9% + 9%.
  • Doosre rajya me bikri: poora IGST lagta hai — 18% ka poora 18%.

Customer ke liye total wahi rehta hai, bas bill par batwara alag dikhta hai.

Aam GST slabs

  • 0%: khuli anaj, doodh, sabziyan jaisi zaroori cheezein
  • 5%: packaged khaane ki cheezein, chhoti sewayein
  • 12%: kuch processed food, business class hawai safar
  • 18%: sabse aam slab — zyadatar sewayein aur saamaan
  • 28%: luxury cheezein, gaadi, tambaku

Rate samay-samay par badalte rehte hain, isliye bade bill ke liye taaza rate gst.gov.in par ya apne CA se confirm karna behtar hai.

Reverse charge aur composition — do khaas mamle

Reverse charge (RCM): aam taur par GST bechne wala jama karta hai. Par kuch khaas mamlon me ye zimmedari kharidne wale par aa jaati hai — jaise kisi bina-registration wale se kuch sewayein lena, ya goods transport agency ki seva. Aise me aapko khud GST jama karna padta hai, aur uska credit bhi aap le sakte hain.

Composition Scheme: chhote vyapariyon ke liye ek saral rasta. Ismein kam flat dar par tax dena hota hai aur kagzi kaam bahut kam ho jaata hai. Par teen shartein hain — ITC nahi milta, bill par GST alag se charge nahi kar sakte, aur doosre rajya me bech nahi sakte.

Bill par kya-kya likha hona chahiye

  • Apna aur khareedar ka GSTIN
  • Invoice number aur tarikh (silsilewaar)
  • HSN code (saaman) ya SAC code (seva)
  • Rate aur GST ka batwara — CGST+SGST ya IGST
  • Kul rakam shabdon me

Adhoora bill khareedar ka ITC atka deta hai, aur uspar penalty bhi lag sakti hai.

Aam galtiyan

  1. Inclusive daam par seedha rate ghatana — ye galat jawab deta hai (upar formula dekhein)
  2. Bina registration ke GST charge karna — ye kanoonan galat hai
  3. Galat rajya ka code — CGST/SGST ki jagah IGST ya ulta lagana
  4. Return me deri — late fee aur byaj to lagta hi hai, khareedar ka credit bhi atak jaata hai

Teen asli misaal — number ke saath

Misaal 1 — GST jodna (exclusive). Maal ka daam ₹10,000, rate 18%. GST = ₹1,800. Customer ko bill milega ₹11,800 ka. Isme CGST ₹900 + SGST ₹900 (ek hi rajya me), ya IGST ₹1,800 (doosre rajya me).

Misaal 2 — GST hataana (inclusive). Aapko ₹11,800 mila jisme GST shaamil hai. Base = 11800 × 100 ÷ 118 = ₹10,000, GST = ₹1,800. Agar aap seedha 18% ghata dete (11800 - 2124 = 9676), to jawab galat aata. Yahi sabse aam galti hai.

Misaal 3 — Input Tax Credit. Aapne maal kharida ₹50,000 ka aur ₹9,000 GST diya. Phir becha ₹80,000 ka aur ₹14,400 GST vasoola. Sarkar ko aapko dena hoga sirf ₹5,400 (14,400 - 9,000). Yahi ITC ka asli fayda hai.

Log yahan galti kya karte hain

  1. Inclusive daam par seedha rate ghatana. Upar misaal 2 me dikh gaya — jawab galat aata hai. Hamesha 100/(100+rate) wala formula lagayein.
  2. Bina registration ke GST charge karna. Ye kanoonan galat hai aur ispar penalty lagti hai. Registration ho tabhi bill par GST alag se dikhayein.
  3. Galat rajya ka code lagana. Ek hi rajya me CGST+SGST, doosre rajya me IGST. Customer ke liye total wahi rehta hai, par return me galti pakdi jaati hai.
  4. Adhoora bill banana. Bill par GSTIN, invoice number, HSN/SAC code, rate aur GST ka batwara — sab hona chahiye. Adhoora bill khareedar ka ITC atka deta hai.
  5. Return me deri. Late fee aur byaj to lagta hi hai, aapke khareedar ka credit bhi ruk jaata hai — aur wo agli baar aapse maal nahi lega.

Bharat me GST ki khaas baatein

  • ITC ki shart: aapko credit tabhi milega jab supplier ne apna return bhara ho. Isliye supplier chunte waqt dekh lein ki wo time par return bharta hai ya nahi.
  • Composition Scheme: chhote vyapariyon ke liye kam flat dar aur kam kagzi kaam. Par ITC nahi milta, bill par GST alag charge nahi kar sakte, aur doosre rajya me bech nahi sakte.
  • Reverse Charge (RCM): kuch mamlon me GST bechne wala nahi, kharidne wala jama karta hai — jaise bina-registration wale se seva lena ya goods transport agency ki seva.
  • Registration kab zaroori: ek tay turnover paar karne par. Par kuch halaton me turnover chahe kitna bhi ho registration zaroori hai — jaise doosre rajya me bechna ya online platform par bechna.
  • Rate badalte rehte hain: GST Council samay-samay par rate badalti hai. Bade faisle se pehle gst.gov.in par ya CA se taaza rate confirm karein.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

GST inclusive aur exclusive me kya farak?

Inclusive matlab daam me GST pehle se juda hai. Exclusive matlab GST alag se jodna hai. Dono ka hisaab alag hota hai.

₹1000 par 18% GST kitna?

GST ₹180, total ₹1,180. Par agar ₹1,000 me GST pehle se juda hai to GST ₹153 aur base ₹847 hoga.

CGST aur SGST kya hai?

Ek hi rajya ke andar bikri par GST aadha kendra (CGST) aur aadha rajya (SGST) ko jaata hai. Doosre rajya me poora IGST lagta hai.

Kya har business ko GST lena zaroori hai?

Ek tay turnover limit ke baad zaroori hota hai, aur kuch dhandhon me pehle se. Apni sthiti CA se confirm karein.

GST number bina bill par GST le sakte hain?

Nahi. Bina GST registration ke bill par GST charge karna galat hai.

Personal loan par GST lagta hai?

Loan ki rakam par nahi, par uske charges par lagta hai — processing fee, prepayment charge aur bounce charge. Aam taur par 18%. Isi liye ₹2,000 ki processing fee asal me ₹2,360 padti hai.
