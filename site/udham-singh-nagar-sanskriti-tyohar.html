Udham Singh Nagar: Aabadi, Samuday aur Anusuchit Janjati

Census 2011: Abadi, Ling Anupat, Saksharta aur SC/ST Aankde

Seedha jawab: Census 2011 ke hisab se Udham Singh Nagar ki abadi 16,48,902 thi, kshetrafal 2,542 varg km aur janghanatva lagbhag 649 vyakti per varg km. Ling anupat 920 mahilayein per 1000 purush tha, prabhavi saksharta (7 saal se upar) lagbhag 73.1% thi, Anusuchit Jati 14.45% aur Anusuchit Janjati 7.46% thi.

Pahadi zilon me purush kamane bahar jaate hain, isliye wahan mahilaon ka anupat zyada dikhta hai. Udham Singh Nagar ka Terai me hona aur bahar se aakar bas jaane wale parivaron ka zyada hona, isliye yahan ling anupat alag kahani kehta hai.

Dhyan dein: Census 2011 ke baad Census 2021 jo hona tha, wo der se hua. Isliye sarkari taur par abhi 2011 ke aankde hi maane jaate hain. Aaj ki anumanit abadi 20 lakh se upar batayi jaati hai, par wo anuman hai, sarkari ginti nahi. Gramin aur shehri abadi ka sahi anupat dekhne ke liye Census ka "Primary Census Abstract" table dekhein.

Dharm ke hisab se abadi (Census 2011): Hindu lagbhag 66.98%, Muslim lagbhag 22.58% aur Sikh lagbhag 9.87%. Is mile-jule dhaanche ki wajah se yahan Gurudwara, Mandir, Masjid aur Church paas-paas milte hain.

Kaunsi mukhya bhashaen bolte hain log?
Hindi (62.4%), Punjabi (10.1%), Bengali (7.9%), Urdu (6.4%), Kumaoni (5.2%), Bhojpuri (3.6%) aur Tharu (2.9%). Yeh aankde Census 2011 ke matri-bhasha ke hain aur Udham Singh Nagar: Bhasha, Up-boliyan aur Roz ke Shabd me vistar se samjhaye gaye hain.

Zile ki Janjatiyan: Tharu aur Buksa (Bhuksa)

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar ki do mukhya Anusuchit Janjatiyan Tharu aur Buksa hain. Rana Tharu mukhya roop se Khatima aur Sitarganj kshetra me aur Buksa Bajpur aur Gadarpur vikas khand ke kshetra me bahut rehte hain. Bhotia, Jaunsari aur Van Raji jaisi janjatiyan yahan ki mool janjatiyan nahi hain.

Udham Singh Nagar ke Terai me asli janjati-pehchan Tharu aur Buksa ki hai.

Tharu samaj ki kuch khaas baatein (Uttarakhand Tribal Welfare ke anusar):

  • Kuch upsamuhon me mahilaon ki bhumika sampatti sambhalne aur parivar ke nirnay lene me mazboot maani jaati hai.
  • Samaj jadi-bootiyon aur jungle se jude gharelu ilaaj ka gyan rakhta hai.
  • Tokri banana, bans ka saman aur ghar ki deewaron par chitrakari inki pehchan me ginayi jaati hai.
  • Maghe Sankranti, Chaitra Sankranti, Maghi aur Hariyari jaise tyohar kheti aur pitron ki pooja se jude hain.
  • Lok-nritya me "Sakwa Hurdang" aur "Lathi nritya" ka naam aata hai.

Buksa samaj ki kuch khaas baatein (Uttarakhand Tribal Welfare ke anusar):

  • Naam "Bhuksa" ko "jungle ke nivasi" se jodkar dekha jata hai. Yeh ek mat hai, pakka etihasik tathya nahi.
  • Samaj kul-aadharit hai aur bade-buzurgon ya panchayat ki bhumika mahatvapurn hai.
  • Haryali Teej aur Baikunth Chaturdashi jaise tyohar maane jaate hain, saath me Holi, Diwali aur Dussehra bhi.
  • Kuch buzurg jadi-bootiyon se gharelu ilaaj karte hain.
  • Kam saksharta aur naye ilakon me padhai-naukri ke liye palayan chunautiyan hain.

Dhyan dein: Tharu aur Buksa samajon ki parampara, peshe aur riti-riwaj gaon-gaon me thode alag ho sakte hain. Yahan diya gaya vivaran samanya jaankari hai. Kisi ek parivar ya ek gaon ki parampara ko puri janjati ka niyam na maanein.

Mini India ka asli matlab: Janjatiyon ke saath yahan Punjabi, Bengali, Pahadi, Purvanchali aur Muslim samaj bhi bade star par rehte hain. Isi wajah se Udham Singh Nagar ko "Mini India" kaha jata hai.

Mool Niwas (Moolgaon), Gotra aur Vivah ke Niyam

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar me bahut se parivar kahin aur ke mool niwasi hain. Pahadi parivaron ka "Moolgaon" Almora, Pithoragarh ya Garhwal me, Punjabi parivaron ka paitrik ilaka Pakistan wale Punjab me, aur Bengali parivaron ka Purvi Bengal (aaj ka Bangladesh) me raha hai. Isiliye yahan "kahan se ho?" aam sawal hai.

Gotra aur vivah: Hindu samajon me vivah ke samay gotra ka dhyan rakha jata hai. Aam taur par apne gotra me vivah nahi kiya jata. Sikh samaj me "got" ki parampara hai aur Bengali samaj me "gotra" ke saath "ghar-wali" gaon ki parampara bhi hoti hai. Tharu aur Buksa samajon me kul-aadharit niyam hote hain, jo gaon ke buzurgon dwara tay hote hain.

Mool Niwas aur Sthayi Niwas Praman Patra: Naukri, school-college ya sarkari yojana ke liye aksar "Sthayi Niwas" ya "Mool Niwas" praman patra mangta hai. Yeh tehsil se Uttarakhand sarkar ke online Seva Portal ya tehsil karyalay ke madhyam se banta hai. Yojana ke anusar niyam aur dastavez badal sakte hain, isliye aavedan se pehle taaza notification dekh lein.

Samajik sauhardya: Zile ke alag-alag samajon me aapsi vivah, vyapar aur padosi-bhav aam baat hai. Yahi mile-jule samaj ki wajah se yahan ke tyohar sab milkar manaate hain.

Kumaoni ke kuch aam shabd (Pahadi parivaron me):

  • Pailagi: Bade-buzurgon ko charan sparsh ya pranam.
  • Dajyu: Bade bhai ke liye samman bhara sambodhan.
  • Bhula: Chhote bhai ya ladke ke liye pyar bhara sambodhan.
  • Thik chha: Theek hai.
  • Kasa chha: Kaise ho?

Dhyan dein: Kumaoni, Punjabi aur Bengali ke upar diye shabd samanya roop ke hain. Boli gaon-gaon me badalti hai, isliye isko kisi vyakaran ya shabdkosh ki jagah na maanein.

Sabko jodne wali kahavatein (Hindi me):

  • "Jaisa des, vaisa bhes": Jahan jao, wahan ke tareeke apnao.
  • "Jahan chah, wahan raah": Iccha ho to raasta mil hi jata hai.
  • "Ekta me bal hai": Mil-jul kar rehne se hi taakat aati hai.
  • "Mehnat ka phal meetha": Mehnat ka nateeja achha hota hai. Terai ke kisano ki yahi soch rahi hai.

Punjabi ka prasiddh jayghosh: "Jo bole so nihal, Sat Sri Akal" Sikh samaj me ullas aur shraddha ke saath bola jata hai.

Udham Singh Nagar: Vernacular Architecture: Terai ke Ghar aur Basti ka Roop

Tharu Ghar, Punjabi Kothi aur Bengali Basti: Teen Alag Sthapatya Shailiyan

Seedha jawab: Himalaya ke "Koti Banal" jaise patthar-lakdi ke ghar Udham Singh Nagar me nahi milte, kyunki yeh Terai ka maidani ilaka hai. Yahan ki asli vernacular sthapatya Tharu parivaron ke mitti-ghaas-baans wale ghar, Punjabi kheton ke beech bani kothiyan aur Bengali gaon ka alag basti-dhaancha hai.

Tharu ghar (paramparik):

  • Mitti ki moti deewaren, ghaas ya phoos ki chhat aur bans ka istemal samanya pratha ke hisab se bataya jata hai.
  • Ghar ke andar mitti ki kothi (anaaj rakhne ka bartan) aur chulha mukhya hote hain.
  • Angan aur gaushala ek hi parisar me hote hain.
  • Deewaron par rangeen chitrakari karne ki parampara hai (dekhein Udham Singh Nagar: Ritual Folk Art: Aipan, Tharu Chitrakari, Phulkari…).

Dhyan dein: Aaj kal gaon me bhi pakke makan aur seemant ki chhat banne lagi hain. Purane mitti ke ghar kam hote ja rahe hain. Upar diya vivaran paramparik shaili ka hai.

Punjabi kothi aur farm-house shaili:

  • Kheti ke beech bada aangan, uske aas-paas kamare aur pashuon ke liye alag jagah.
  • Bahut se parivaron me tractor aur thresher ke liye alag shed hota hai.
  • Chhat par paani ki tanki aur bada darwaza aam baat hai.

Bengali basti ka dhancha:

  • Chhote ghar, aangan ke saath tulsi manch aur kai jagah ghar ke saamne chhota pokhar (talab).
  • Sanskritik bhawan, Kali Mandir ya Durga Mandap ka basti ke beech me hona.

Pahadi parivaron ke ghar: Yahan ke shehron me Pahadi parivar bhi aam makanon me rehte hain, lekin ghar ke andar chhote mandir me Pahadi devta aur Aipan jaisi sajavat dikhti hai.

Ghar ka Naksha, Aangan aur Gaushala

Seedha jawab: Terai ke gramin ghar me aangan sab kuch hota hai. Wahin bachche khelte hain, anaaj sukhaya jata hai aur shaam ko parivar baithta hai. Gaushala (pashu-sthaan) ghar ke paas hota hai, kyunki yahan dairy aur pashupalan ka bada mahatva hai.

  • Aangan: Sabka milne ka sthaan, tyoharon aur shadi ke kaam yahin hote hain.
  • Baithak / Chaupal: Gaon ke buzurgon ke baithne ki jagah, khaskar Punjabi aur Hindi gaon me.
  • Gaushala / Haveli: Bhains aur gaay ka sthaan.
  • Khalihan: Fasal sukhane aur maadne ka khula maidan.
  • Kuan / Handpump: Paani ka paramparik sadhan, jo aaj bore-well aur tube-well me badal gaya hai.

Udham Singh Nagar: Paramparik Pehnawa aur Kapde

Mahilaon ke Paramparik Vastra

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar me mahilaon ka paramparik pehnava samuday ke hisab se badalta hai: Punjabi mahilayein salwar-kameez aur phulkari dupatta, Bengali mahilayein taant ya dhakai jaisi sari, Pahadi mahilayein tyohar par rangwali pichhora aur ghaghri, aur Tharu mahilayein rangeen lehnga ya ghaghra aur odhni pehanti hain.

Purushon ke Paramparik Vastra aur Pagdi

Seedha jawab: Purushon ka rozana ka pehnava kurta-pajama ya shirt-pant hai. Tyohar aur shadi me Punjabi purush kurta-pajama aur pagdi, Bengali purush dhoti-Punjabi, Pahadi purush kurta-pajama aur Pahadi topi, aur Muslim purush kurta-pajama aur topi pehente hain.

  • Pagdi (turban): Sikh samaj me yeh dharmik pehchan aur samman ka pratik hai, sirf fashion nahi.
  • Dhoti-Punjabi: Bengali tyoharon aur shadi me khaas.
  • Pahadi topi: Kumaoni aur Garhwali tyoharon ki pehchan, jo Pahadi parivar yahan bhi pehente hain.
  • Kheti ke kapde: Gaon me mazdoor aur kisan rozana dhoti, kurta ya lungi aur gamchha istemal karte hain.

Phulkari kya hai? Punjab ki paramparik kadhai, jisme dupatte ya shawl par rangeen resham ke dhaage se phool-patte bune jaate hain. Shadi-byah me yeh shagun ka pratik maani jati hai.

Udham Singh Nagar: Paramparik Gehne

Samuday ke Hisab se Gehne

Seedha jawab: Gehne yahan sirf sajaavat nahi, shagun aur pehchan bhi hain. Punjabi mahilayein kangan, jhumke aur kaleere pehanti hain, Bengali mahilayein shankha-pola aur sone-chandi ke kangan, Pahadi mahilayein nathuli, galobandh aur pahunchi, aur Tharu mahilayein bhaari chandi ke gehne.

Kiski kya khaasiyat?

  • Kaleere (Punjabi): Dulhan ke haath me bandhe jaane wale sunhari-chandi ke chhatri-jaise latkan, shadi ki ek pehchan.
  • Shankha-pola (Bengali): Shadishuda Bengali mahila ke haath me pehni jaane wali safed shankh aur laal lakh ki churiyan, jo suhag ki nishani maani jati hai.
  • Nathuli aur galobandh (Pahadi): Paramparik Pahadi shringar, jo aaj shadi aur tyoharon par hi dikhta hai.

Sona-chandi kharidne ki tip: Gehne kharidte samay BIS hallmark dekhein. Hallmark wale gehne ki shuddhta ki guarantee hoti hai. Rudrapur aur Kashipur me bade sarraf bazar hain.

Udham Singh Nagar: Ritual Folk Art: Aipan, Tharu Chitrakari, Phulkari aur Alpana

Zile ki Alag-Alag Kala Parampara

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar me ek nahi kai kala-parampara milti hain: Pahadi parivaron ki Aipan (geru aur chawal ke ghol se bani pooja ki rangoli), Bengali parivaron ki Alpana, Punjabi parivaron ki Phulkari kadhai aur Tharu samaj ki deewar-chitrakari aur bans ki bunai.

Aipan (Pahadi parivar): Kumaon ki paramparik pooja-kala. Geru (laal mitti) ki sataha par chawal ke geele aate (biswar) se ungliyon ki madad se bani akritiyan. Lakshmi chauki, Saraswati peeth aur Ashta Dal Kamal jaise nakshe Diwali, vivah aur pooja me banaye jaate hain. Udham Singh Nagar me Pahadi parivar jab bhi pooja karte hain to Aipan banate hain.

Alpana (Bengali parivar): Chawal ke aate ke ghol se angan, chaukhat aur pooja-sthaan par bani rangoli. Lakshmi Puja aur Durga Puja ke samay yeh khaas hoti hai.

Phulkari (Punjabi parivar): Dupatte aur shawl par resham ki kadhai (dekhein Udham Singh Nagar: Paramparik Pehnawa aur Kapde).

Tharu deewar-chitrakari aur bans-kala: Tharu ghar ki deewaron par mitti aur rang se bani chitrakari aur bans ki tokriyan, jo samaj ki pehchan maani jaati hain. Yeh kala dheere-dheere kam ho rahi hai, isliye isko bachane ki koshishein jaruri hain.

Dhyan dein: Is section me kuch kala-roop (jaise Aipan aur Alpana) pahadi aur Bengali parivaron ke saath yahan aaye. Ye Udham Singh Nagar ki mool kala nahi, balki yahan basne wale samajon ki jeevit parampara hain. Tharu aur Buksa ki kala asal mool parampara hai.

Gurbani kirtan: Gurudwaron me roz subah-shaam shabad-kirtan hota hai. Nanakmatta Sahib aur Kashipur ke Gurudwaron me gurupurab par kirtan darbar hota hai.

Dhyan dein: Tharu aur Buksa ke geeton ke bol aur raag samuday ke andar zubani chale aate hain. Unka likhit sangrah kam hai. Is parampara ko record karne wale sanghathan ya vidyalayon ki madad leni chahiye.

Udham Singh Nagar: Paramparik Lok Nritya

Tharu Nritya, Bhangra-Giddha aur Pahadi Chholiya

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar ke lok-nritya me Tharu samaj ka "Sakwa Hurdang" aur "Lathi nritya" (Uttarakhand Tribal Welfare ke anusar), Punjabi samaj ka Bhangra aur Giddha, Bengali samaj ka Dhunuchi nritya aur Pahadi samaj ka Chholiya (talwar-dhal nritya) shamil hain.

Bhangra kya hai? Punjab ka josheela nritya, jo kheti aur Baisakhi ki khushi se juda hai. Yahan ke kisan-pradhan Sikh parivaron me yeh khaas lokpriya hai.

Dhunuchi nritya kya hai?
Durga Puja ke samay aarti me mitti ke dhunuchi (dhoop-patra) ko haath me lekar dhak ki taal par kiya jaane wala nritya.

Dhyan dein: Sakhiya nritya Nepal ke Tharu samaj se juda prasiddh nritya hai. Udham Singh Nagar ke Tharu samaj ke liye Uttarakhand sarkar ke tribal welfare vibhag ne "Sakwa Hurdang" aur "Lathi nritya" ka naam diya hai.

Udham Singh Nagar: Jeevan ke Sanskar aur Vivah

Janm, Vivah aur Antim Sanskar: Kai Parampara, Ek Zila

Seedha jawab: Janm, nam-karan, vivah aur antim sanskar ki riti-riwaj Udham Singh Nagar me samuday ke hisab se alag hain: Sikh parivaron me Anand Karaj, Bengali parivaron me Subho Drishti aur Saat Paak, Pahadi parivaron me Suwal Pathai aur Dhuli Arghya, Tharu aur Buksa me kul ki riti, aur Muslim parivaron me Nikah.

Antim sanskar: Hindu aur Sikh parivaron me daah-sanskar, Muslim parivaron me dafn aur Ishai parivaron me kabristan me. Yeh sabhi sanskar nagar palika, nagar nigam ya gaon ke shamshan, kabristan aur dahan-ghat par hote hain.

Dhyan dein: Har parivar ki riti kuch na kuch alag hoti hai. Yahan diya gaya vivaran sirf samanya samajh ke liye hai, kisi parivar ya samuday ko ek niyam me bandhne ke liye nahi.

Shaadi ke liye zaroori sarkari kaam: Shaadi ke baad vivah ka panjikaran (registration) aavashyak hai. Isse naam badalna, passport banana aur sarkari yojanaon ka labh lena aasan hota hai. Uttarakhand me vivah panjikaran ke niyam aur samay-seema ke liye taaza notification dekhein.

Udham Singh Nagar: Mausami Tyohar

Saal bhar ka Tyohar Calendar

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar me saal bhar tyohar chalte rehte hain: Lohri aur Maghi, Holi, Baisakhi, Chaiti Navratri, Eid, Raksha Bandhan, Janmashtami, Durga Puja aur Dussehra, Diwali, Guru Nanak Jayanti, Christmas aur Pahadi Harela, Phool Dei aur Ghughutiya. Is tarah sabhi dharmon ke tyohar milkar zile ko "Mini India" banate hain.

Dhyan dein: Tyoharon ki tithi har saal badalti hai kyunki yeh panchang aur chandra-calendar par chalti hain. Sahi tithi ke liye taaza panchang ya sarkari calendar dekhein.

Kuch khaas Pahadi tyohar jo yahan bhi manaye jaate hain:

  • Harela: Shravan ke 10 din pehle 7 anaaj bokar hariyali ka tyohar.
  • Phool Dei: Chaitra Sankranti par bachche gharon ki dehleez par phool rakhte hain.
  • Ghughutiya / Uttarayani: Makar Sankranti par ata-gur ke ghughute banakar kauwon ko khilane ki parampara.
  • Bhitauli: Chaitra me bhai ka behen ke ghar jaakar upahar dena.

Tharu aur Buksa tyohar (Uttarakhand Tribal Welfare ke anusar):

  • Tharu: Maghe Sankranti, Chaitra Sankranti, Maghi aur Hariyari. Yeh khet aur fasal se jude hain.
  • Buksa: Haryali Teej aur Baikunth Chaturdashi. Saath me Holi, Diwali aur Dussehra.

Bengali tyohar: Durga Puja (Mahalaya se Vijaya Dashami), Kali Puja, Lakshmi Puja, Saraswati Puja aur Poila Baisakh (Bengali naya saal). Dineshpur aur Shaktigarh jaise ilakon me Durga Puja ke pandal dekhne layak hote hain.

Sikh tyohar: Lohri, Maghi, Baisakhi (Khalsa Panth sthapna din), Bandi Chhor Divas (Diwali ke saath) aur Guru Nanak Jayanti. Nanakmatta Sahib me khaas bheed hoti hai.

Udham Singh Nagar: Purane Mele, Jatra aur Shobha Yatra

Chaiti Mela Kashipur aur Anya Mele

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar ka sabse prasiddh mela Kashipur ka Chaiti Mela (Maa Bal Sundari Mandir, Chaiti Devi) hai, jo Chaitra Navratri ke samay lagta hai aur door-door se shraddhalu aakar darshan karte hain. Iske alawa Gurudwara Nanakmatta Sahib par gurupurab aur Maghi par bheed, aur zile ke alag-alag shehron me Dussehra aur Ramlila ka bada aayojan hota hai.

Chaiti Mela kyun mashhoor hai?
Yeh mela Kashipur ke Maa Bal Sundari (Chaiti Devi) mandir par lagta hai. Navratri ke samay mandir me mela lagta hai aur bade paimane par shraddhalu aate hain. Mandir ke aas-paas dukane, jhoole aur khane-pine ki stalls lagti hain.

Chaiti Mela jaane ke tips:

  • Bheed bahut hoti hai, isliye subah jaldi jaane ki koshish karein.
  • Bacchon aur buzurgon ko bheed me hamesha saath rakhein.
  • Vahan jaane ka poora rasta aur parking ki jaankari mela prashasan ya nagar nigam se confirm karein.
  • Mela ke dino me aas-paas ke raaston par trafic badal sakta hai, isliye yatra se pehle taaza trafic advisory dekhein.

Dhyan dein: Mela ki tithi, vyavastha aur trafic yojana har saal badalti hai. Taaza jaankari ke liye zila prashasan ya nagar nigam ki official suchna dekhein.

Mele me kya khaas milta hai: Pooja ka saman, chunariyan, khilone, jhoole, gol-gappe, jalebi, paapad aur kharidari ka pura bazar.

Udham Singh Nagar: Mini FAQ

Udham Singh Nagar ki abadi kitni hai?
Census 2011 ke hisab se 16,48,902. Yeh Uttarakhand me teesra sthaan hai.
Udham Singh Nagar me kaun si janjatiyan rehti hain?
Mukhya roop se Tharu (Rana Tharu) aur Buksa. Dono Anusuchit Janjati hain.
Kashipur ka Chaiti Mela kab lagta hai?
Chaitra Navratri ke samay, aam taur par March-April me. Sahi tithi ke liye us saal ka panchang dekhein.
Kya Sakhiya nritya Udham Singh Nagar ka nritya hai?
Sakhiya Nepal ke Tharu samaj ka nritya hai. Udham Singh Nagar ke liye sarkari tribal welfare vibhag ne Sakwa Hurdang aur Lathi nritya ka naam diya hai.
Udham Singh Nagar me Bengali kahan zyada rehte hain?
Dineshpur, Shaktigarh aur Bengali basti wale gaon me. Yeh vibhajan ke baad purnavas yojanaon me basaye gaye the.
