Charges ka poora dhaancha

Screen par jo P&L dikhta hai, wo aapka nahi hota. Beech me kai cheezein aati hain, aur intraday me ye itni badi ho jaati hain ki chhote trades me pura munafa inhi me chala jaata hai.

  • Brokerage — broker ki fee. Discount broker me per order flat hoti hai, full-service me percentage me.
  • STT — sarkari tax. Intraday me sell side par, delivery me dono taraf, aur options me alag tarah se. Ye sabse bhaari charge hota hai.
  • Exchange transaction charge — NSE/BSE ka apna charge.
  • GST — brokerage aur exchange charge ke upar.
  • SEBI turnover fee aur stamp duty — chhote, par har trade par.

Natija ye hota hai ki din me 10 trade karne wale trader ka break-even point kaafi upar khisak jaata hai. Isliye "kitna kamaya" se pehle "kitna kata" nikaalna zaroori hai. Intraday P&L Calculator saare charges ke baad ka net number deta hai, aur brokerage guide me har charge alag se samjhaya hai.

Position size — wo ek cheez jo account bachati hai

Naye trader ka sabse aam sawaal hota hai "kaunsa share lein". Purane trader ka sawaal hota hai "kitna lein". Doosra sawaal zyada mayne rakhta hai.

Aam usool ye hai ki ek trade me apni poonji ka 1-2% se zyada risk par nahi lagana chahiye. Yaani ₹1 lakh ke account par ek trade me zyada se zyada ₹1,000-₹2,000 ka nuksan. Isse position size apne aap nikal aata hai:

Kitne share = (Kitna nuksan jhel sakte hain) ÷ (Entry − Stop-loss)

Misaal: ₹1 lakh account, 1% risk = ₹1,000. Share ₹500 par khareeda, stop-loss ₹490. Prati share risk ₹10. Toh 100 share — yaani ₹50,000 ki position. Zyadatar log yahan ₹1 lakh ki poori position le lete hain, aur ek galat trade unka 10% kha jaata hai.

Trading ka tax — ye alag hota hai

Intraday ko speculative business income maana jaata hai, F&O ko non-speculative business income, aur delivery me rakhe share ko capital gain. Teeno par niyam alag hain, aur ITR form bhi alag ho jaata hai.

Sabse zaroori baat jo naye log nahi jaante: F&O ka nuksan aage le jaaya ja sakta hai aur aane wale saalon ke munafe se ghataya ja sakta hai — par tabhi jab aap us saal samay par ITR bharein. Bahut log nuksan hua isliye ITR bharte hi nahi, aur wo fayda hamesha ke liye kho dete hain. Poori jaankari ITR-3 guide me hai.

Isliye bahut chhote trade zyada baar karna sabse mehnga tarika hai.
