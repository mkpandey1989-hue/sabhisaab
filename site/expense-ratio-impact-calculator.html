Expense Ratio Impact Calculator — Chhota Sa Kharch, Bada Sa Farak

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Expense ratio wo saalana kharch hai jo fund house aapke paise me se kaatta hai. Ye aapke bank se nahi katta, isliye kabhi dikhta nahi — roz thoda-thoda NAV me se kam ho jaata hai.

Isiliye 1.8% aur 0.6% ka farak sunne me mamooli lagta hai. Neeche apna nivesh aur saal daalkar dekhiye ki 20-25 saal me yahi farak kitne lakh ka ban jaata hai.

Ye kharch dikhta kyun nahi

Expense ratio saal ke aakhir me ek bill ki tarah nahi aata. Fund har kaam ke din uska 365vaan hissa NAV me se kaat leta hai. Jo NAV aap app me dekhte hain, wo pehle hi kharch nikaalne ke baad ka hota hai.

Iska matlab do baatein hain. Pehli — jo return aapko dikh raha hai wo already saaf hai, usme se aur kuch nahi katega. Doosri, aur zyada zaroori — aapko kabhi mehsoos hi nahi hota ki kitna gaya. Isiliye log expense ratio ko halke me lete hain.

Sochne ka aasaan tarika: 1.8% expense ratio ka matlab hai ki aapka fund har saal 12% kamaaye tabhi aapko 10.2% milega. Fund ko har saal 1.8% ka extra pahaad chadhna padta hai, sirf barabar rehne ke liye.

Direct aur Regular plan — wahi fund, do keemat

Har mutual fund do roop me milta hai. Regular plan me distributor ka commission shaamil hota hai; direct plan me nahi. Fund manager, portfolio, sab kuch bilkul ek jaisa hota hai.

Farak lagbhag 0.7% se 1% ka hota hai. Chhota lagta hai. 25 saal ki 10,000 mahine ki SIP me yahi farak 20 lakh se upar chala jaata hai. Ye faisla galat nahi hai — agar aapka agent sach me aapko galat samay par bechne se rokta hai, to wo kharch waajib hai. Par ye jaankar chunna chahiye, anjaane me nahi.

Kam expense ratio hamesha behtar nahi hota

Ye baat imaandaari se kehni chahiye. Sasta fund apne aap achha fund nahi ban jaata.

  • Index fund aur ETF me kharch sabse kam hota hai, kyunki wahan koi chunaav karna hi nahi hota — wo bas index ki nakal karte hain. Yahan sasta hona seedha faayda hai.
  • Active fund me manager chunaav karta hai. Agar wo lambe samay tak index se upar rehta hai, to thoda zyada kharch waajib ho sakta hai. Par ye aage bhi hoga, iska koi vaada nahi hota.
  • Bahut chhote fund me kharch aksar zyada hota hai. Fund bada hone par SEBI ke niyam ke hisaab se seema apne aap kam ho jaati hai.

Seedha niyam: do fund lagbhag ek jaise lagein, tab kam kharch wala chuniye. Ek achha fund sirf isliye chhodna ki uska kharch 0.2% zyada hai — ye samajhdaari nahi hai. Kharch ek cheez hai, ekmatra cheez nahi.

Regular se direct me jaate waqt ye dekh lein

Badalna aasaan hai, par ek chhoti si laagat aati hai jo log bhool jaate hain. Purane units bechne par exit load aur capital gain tax dono lag sakte hain.

Isliye zyadatar sthiti me sabse saaf rasta ye hai: purana nivesh jahan hai wahin rehne dijiye, aur nayi SIP direct plan me shuru kijiye. Isse na load lagta hai, na tax, aur aage ka poora paisa kam kharch par chalta hai.

Agar purana paisa shift karna hi hai to pehle Exit Load Calculator me apna number daalkar dekh lijiye ki kitna katega. Kai baar 3-4 mahine ruk jaane se load poora bach jaata hai.

Ek baat jo aksar nahi batayi jaati: kuch apps direct plan dikhate hain par unme SIP shuru karne par regular plan me chala jaata hai. Nivesh ke baad statement me plan ka naam khud padh lijiye — usme saaf likha hota hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Expense ratio mere account se kab katta hai?

Alag se kabhi nahi katta. Fund har kaam ke din uska ek chhota hissa NAV me se kam kar deta hai. Jo NAV aapko dikhta hai, wo kharch nikaalne ke baad ka hota hai.

Direct aur regular plan me fund alag hota hai kya?

Nahi. Portfolio aur fund manager bilkul ek hi hote hain. Farak sirf kharch ka hai, kyunki regular plan me distributor ka commission shaamil rehta hai.

Kitna expense ratio zyada maana jaata hai?

Equity active fund me 1.5% se upar zyada maana jaata hai, index fund me 0.3% se upar. Ye har fund ki size aur prakaar par nirbhar karta hai.

Regular se direct me badalne par tax lagta hai?

Haan, kyunki purane units bechne padte hain. Isliye kai log purana nivesh waise hi chhodkar nayi SIP direct plan me shuru karte hain.

Kya kam kharch wala fund hamesha zyada deta hai?

Nahi. Kharch ek hissa hai, poori kahani nahi. Do fund ek jaise lagein tab kam kharch wala chunna theek hai, par sirf kharch dekhkar achha fund chhodna samajhdaari nahi.
