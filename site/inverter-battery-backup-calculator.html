Inverter Battery Backup Calculator — Light Jaane Par Kitne Ghante Chalega

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: October 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Battery par likha hota hai 150Ah, aur dukaandaar kehta hai "6-7 ghante aaraam se". Par ghar me pankhe, light aur TV ek saath chalein to backup kitna milega, ye ek seedhe hisaab se nikalta hai.

Seedha jawab: backup (ghante) = battery Ah × volt × battery ki ginti × istemaal hone wala hissa × inverter ki kshamta ÷ load (watt). 150Ah ki ek 12V battery aur 200 watt load par lagbhag 6 ghante.

Neeche apni battery aur ghar ka load daaliye. Backup ke ghante aur sahi inverter ka size dono dikh jaayenge.

Hisaab ka tarika — Ah ko watt-hour me badliye

Battery ka Ah akela kuch nahi batata. Use volt se guna karne par hi asli urja (watt-hour) milti hai.

150Ah × 12V = 1,800 watt-hour. Par ye poori kaam nahi aati. Lead-acid battery ko poora khaali nahi kiya jaata — inverter lagbhag 80% par kaat deta hai. Phir inverter khud 15% ke aas-paas bijli kha jaata hai. Yaani 1,800 × 0.8 × 0.85 = 1,224 watt-hour asal me kaam aate hain.

Ab ise load se bhaag dijiye. 200 watt ke load par 1,224 ÷ 200 = 6.1 ghante. Load 400 watt ho to 3 ghante. Backup seedha load ke ulte anupaat me ghatta hai.

Ghar ke saamaan kitna watt lete hain

Misaal: 3 purane pankhe (225 W) + 4 LED bulb (40 W) + router (12 W) = lagbhag 277 W. 150Ah battery par lagbhag 4.4 ghante.

Yahi teen pankhe BLDC ke hon to load 225 se ghatkar lagbhag 95 W. Kul load 147 W, aur backup 4.4 se badhkar lagbhag 8.3 ghante. Nayi battery khareedne se pehle pankhe badalna aksar sasta aur zyada faayde ka padta hai.

Kitne VA ka inverter chahiye

Inverter ka size VA me likha hota hai, load watt me. Ghar ke saamaan ka power factor lagbhag 0.8 maana jaata hai.

Zaroori VA = load (watt) ÷ 0.8. 600 watt load ke liye kam se kam 750 VA, aur thodi gunjaish ke saath 900-1000 VA ka inverter theek rehta hai.
Ghar ka loadInverter (lagbhag)Aam battery set-up

Inverter ka VA backup nahi badhata. Backup sirf battery ki urja aur load se tay hota hai. Bada inverter sirf zyada saamaan ek saath chalane deta hai.

Jahan bijli roz lambi jaati hai, wahan lithium ka zyada istemaal hone wala hissa aur lambi umar uski keemat ko kuch had tak sahi thehrate hain. Kabhi-kabhaar ki katauti ke liye tubular battery aam taur par kaafi hai.

Backup kam kyun milta hai — 5 aam wajah

  • Battery purani ho gayi — 3-4 saal baad urja dheere-dheere ghat jaati hai.
  • Paani ka star kam — lead-acid battery me plate sookh kar kharab hoti hain.
  • Garam jagah — battery ko hawadaar, chhaya wali jagah rakhiye.
  • Motor wala saamaan — fridge, pump ya mixer shuru hote waqt kai guna bijli khinchte hain.
  • Battery poori charge nahi hui — baar-baar katauti me battery aadhi charge par hi chalti rehti hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

150Ah battery kitne ghante chalti hai?

12V ki 150Ah battery me lagbhag 1.2 kWh kaam aati hai. 200 watt load par lagbhag 6 ghante, 100 watt par lagbhag 12 ghante aur 400 watt par lagbhag 3 ghante.

Inverter backup ka formula kya hai?

Backup (ghante) = Ah × volt × battery ki ginti × 0.8 × 0.85 ÷ load watt. Lithium battery me 0.8 ki jagah lagbhag 0.9 lete hain.

Kitne VA ka inverter lena chahiye?

Load (watt) ko 0.8 se bhaag dein, phir thodi gunjaish jod dein. 600 watt load ke liye 900-1000 VA ka inverter theek rehta hai.

Bada inverter lene se backup badhta hai?

Nahi. Backup battery ki urja aur load se tay hota hai. Bada inverter sirf zyada saamaan ek saath chalane deta hai.

BLDC pankhe se backup kitna badhta hai?

BLDC pankha 28-35 watt leta hai jabki purana 70-80 watt. Teen pankhe badalne se backup lagbhag dugna ho sakta hai.
