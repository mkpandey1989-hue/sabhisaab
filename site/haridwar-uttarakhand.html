201. Haridwar Ganga Aarti: Samay, Kahan Baithein aur Jankari

201.1 Ganga Aarti ka Samay (Subah aur Sham), Kitni Der Chalti Hai aur Sahi Sthaan

Seedha jawab: Har Ki Pauri par Ganga Aarti roz subah aur sham hoti hai. Sham ki Aarti ka samay suryast ke saath badalta hai: garmi me lagbhag 6:00 se 7:30 baje ke beech aur sardi me lagbhag 5:00 se 6:30 baje ke beech (sources me thoda antar hai). Subah ki Aarti lagbhag 5:00 se 6:50 baje ke beech hoti hai. Aarti aam taur par 45 minute se 1 ghante chalti hai.

Isliye page par sabse surakshit niyam ye likhein: "Aarti suryast ke aas-paas shuru hoti hai; 1 se 1.5 ghante pehle pahunchein." Aarti dekhne ke 7 vyavharik tips:

  • Aarti se 1 se 1.5 ghante pehle pahunchein; Kumbh/Kartik Purnima/Somvati Amavasya jaise dino me aur bhi pehle.
  • Kam bheed ke vikalp: Subhash Ghat (Har Ki Pauri ke bagal me) aur Malviya Dweep (nadi ke us paar chhota dweep) kuch blogs ke anusar kam bheed wale hain.
  • "VIP seating" ya "booking" ke daave: Kuch blogs ₹500-₹1,000 ke "donation seating" ka zikr karte hain. Ye pakka sarkari niyam nahi hai; kisi ke kehne par paise dene se pehle rasid aur naam zaroor lein, aur online "Aarti booking" ke liye keval jaane-pehchane aur adhikrit sroto par bharosa karein.
  • Dona-diya: Rate pehle tay karein.
  • Suraksha: Bheed me bachche ka haath pakdein; railing/zanjeer ke bahar na jayein.
  • Mobile aur paise: Jeb me nahi, aage ki bag me.
  • Ganga Bandh: Har saal kuch dinon ke liye Ganga ki dhara nahar ki safai/marammat ke karan band hoti hai (aam bol-chal me "Ganga Bandh"); us dauran ghat par paani kam hota hai. Tithi sinchai vibhag ki ghoshna se dekhein.
