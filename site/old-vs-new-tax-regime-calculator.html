Dono regime me farak

India me ab do tax system hain. Old regime me tax dar zyada hai par 80C, HRA, home loan jaise bahut saare deductions milte hain. New regime me tax dar kam hai par lagbhag koi deduction nahi (sirf standard deduction). Kaunsa sasta — ye aapke deductions par nirbhar karta hai.

Seedha niyam

Ek aasan soch: agar aap bahut saare deductions lete hain (80C poora, HRA, home loan byaj) to old regime aksar sasta padta hai. Agar aap kam ya koi deduction nahi lete to new regime sasta hota hai, kyunki uski dar kam hai. Beech me ho to calculator me dono dekh kar decide karein.

Ek misaal

₹12 lakh income, ₹2 lakh deductions (80C + HRA). Old regime me deductions ke baad tax kam ho jaata hai. New regime me deduction nahi, par dar kam. Dono ka hisaab calculator lagakar dikhata hai kaunsa sasta. Aksar zyada deductions par old jeeta, kam par new.

Kaunse deductions old me milte hain

  • 80C — PPF, ELSS, life insurance, EPF, home loan mool (₹1.5 lakh tak)
  • 80D — health insurance premium
  • HRA — kiraye par rehne walon ke liye
  • Home loan byaj — Section 24 (₹2 lakh tak)
  • 80CCD(1B) — NPS me extra ₹50,000

Agar aap ye sab le rahe hain to old regime dekh lein. NPS ka fayda samajhne ke liye NPS wala lekh aur poora tulnaatmak vivaran tax regime guide me hai.

Regime chunne se pehle apna hisaab kaise banayein

Log aksar poochte hain "kaunsa regime achha hai?" — is sawaal ka koi ek jawab nahi hai. Jawab is baat par tika hai ki aap sach me kitni deduction claim karte hain, kitni kar sakte hain uspar nahi.

Ek saada tarika ye hai. Kaagaz par teen line likhiye:

  • Line 1 — jo deduction aap pehle se le rahe hain: EPF ka apna hissa, bacchon ki school fees, jeevan bima ka premium, home loan ka mool dhan.
  • Line 2 — home loan ka byaj, agar ghar par loan chal raha hai.
  • Line 3 — HRA, agar kiraye ke ghar me rehte hain aur rent receipt de sakte hain.
In teeno ka jod hi asli faisla karta hai. Ye jod jitna bada hoga, old regime utna faydemand hoga. Jod chhota ho — yaani aap koi khaas deduction claim nahi karte — to new regime aam taur par aage nikal jaata hai, kyonki uski slab zyada udaar hoti hai.

Sabse badi galti ye hai ki log deduction "kar sakte hain" maankar old regime chun lete hain, phir saal bhar wo nivesh karte hi nahi. Aise me old regime me rehna ulta mehnga pad jaata hai. Isliye upar wali teen line me sirf wahi likhiye jo aap sach me karte hain.

Faisla kab lena hai aur kya badla ja sakta hai

Do alag-alag samay par ye faisla saamne aata hai, aur dono ka matlab alag hai:

  • Saal ke shuru me, employer ko batate waqt — isse aapki TDS katauti tay hoti hai. Ye ek anuman hai, aakhri faisla nahi.
  • ITR bharte waqt — asli faisla yahi hai. Salary wale log aam taur par ITR bharte waqt regime badal sakte hain, chahe saal ke shuru me kuch aur bataya ho.

Iska ek bahut kaam ka matlab nikalta hai: saal ke shuru me galat chun liya to ghabraiye mat. Zyada TDS kat gaya hoga to ITR me refund aa jayega, kam kata hoga to antar bharna padega. Faisla ITR ke waqt hi pakka hota hai.

Karobar ya professional aay wale logon ke liye niyam alag aur sakht hai — unke liye baar-baar regime badalna mumkin nahi hota. Aisi sthiti me CA se ek baar salah lekar hi faisla lena chahiye, kyunki galti saalon tak chalti hai.

Aur ye dhyaan rakhiye ki slab aur deduction ke niyam har budget ke baad badal sakte hain. Jo hisaab is saal fit baitha, agla saal wahi rahega ye zaroori nahi. Har saal ITR se pehle dono taraf ka hisaab ek baar dobara laga lena hi sahi aadat hai. Poore vivaran ke liye tax regime guide aur income tax guide dekh sakte hain. Sarkari niyam ke liye hamesha incometax.gov.in hi aakhri source hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Old ya new regime — kaunsa chunein?

Bahut deductions (80C, HRA, home loan) lete hain to old aksar sasta. Kam ya koi deduction nahi to new sasta. Dono ka hisaab lagakar dekhein.

New regime me kya deductions milte hain?

Lagbhag koi nahi — sirf standard deduction. 80C, HRA, home loan byaj jaise fayde new regime me nahi milte.

Har saal regime badal sakte hain?

Salaried log aam taur par har saal chun sakte hain. Business income wale ke liye niyam alag hote hain. CA se confirm karein.

Rebate kya hai?

Ek tay taxable income tak tax poora maaf ho jaata hai. Ye seema aur niyam budget me badalte hain, isliye current dar confirm karein.

Saal ke shuru me chuna hua regime baad me badal sakte hain?

Salary wale log aam taur par ITR bharte waqt badal sakte hain — shuru me diya gaya jawab sirf TDS katauti ke liye hota hai. Karobar wali aay par niyam sakht hai, wahan CA se salah lena chahiye.

Mujhe kaunsa regime chunna chahiye?

Ye is par nirbhar hai ki aap sach me kitni deduction claim karte hain. HRA, home loan byaj aur nivesh ka jod bada hai to old, warna aam taur par new regime aage nikalta hai. Dono taraf ka hisaab is calculator me laga kar dekhiye.
