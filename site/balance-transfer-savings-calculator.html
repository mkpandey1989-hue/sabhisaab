Balance Transfer Calculator — Loan Shift Karne Me Bachat Kitni

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute

Dopahar me phone aata hai. Sir, aapka home loan 9.2% par chal raha hai, hamare paas 8.4% me shift karwa dijiye, lakhon bach jayenge. Number sunkar lagta hai ki turant haan keh dena chahiye.

Seedha jawab: transfer tabhi faayde ka hai jab rate ka antar 0.5% se zyada ho aur loan ke 7 saal se zyada bache hon. Ek shart bhi poori na ho, to fee bachat kha jaati hai.

Neeche bakaya rakam, dono rate, bacha hua samay aur nayi bank ki kul fee daaliye. Asli bachat aur fee wasool hone ka samay dono saamne aa jayenge.

Bachat ka asli ganit — teen number

Balance transfer ka faisla teen cheezon par tikta hai, aur teeno ek saath dekhne padte hain.

NumberKya batata hai
Rate ka antar0.5% se kam ho to aam taur par mehnat bekaar
Bacha hua samay7 saal se kam bacha ho to faayda tez ghatta hai
Kul feeProcessing, legal, valuation, stamp — sab jodkar

Doosra number sabse kam samjha jaata hai. Home loan me shuruaati saalon me EMI ka bada hissa byaj hota hai, aur aakhri saalon me principal. Isliye loan ke aakhir me rate ghatane se bachat bahut kam hoti hai — wahan byaj bacha hi kitna hai.

Mota niyam: transfer tabhi soch me aata hai jab rate ka antar 0.5% se zyada ho aur loan ke 7 saal se zyada bache hon. Ek shart bhi poori na ho to zyadatar maamlon me mehnat ka faayda nahi nikalta.

Fee me kya-kya jodna bhool jaate hain

Bank zero processing fee ka offer deta hai aur log usi par khush ho jaate hain. Kharch sirf wahi nahi hota.

  • Processing fee — aam taur par rakam ka ek pratishat, ya tay rakam
  • Legal aur technical valuation ka kharch
  • Purani bank se documents nikaalne ka charge
  • Nayi bank me mortgage ke stamp duty jaise rajya ke kharch
  • Nayi insurance policy jo aksar saath me bech di jaati hai

Aakhri wali sabse mehngi hoti hai. 25 lakh ke loan par ek insurance 40,000 se upar ki ho sakti hai, aur wo aksar loan me hi jod di jaati hai — yaani uspar byaj bhi lagta hai. Ye lena zaroori nahi hota, par aksar zaroori bata diya jaata hai.

Upar wale calculator me apni kul fee daaliye, sirf processing fee nahi. Tabhi fee wasool hone me kitne mahine wala number sach bolega.

Transfer se pehle apni hi bank se baat kijiye

Ye sabse kam istemaal hone wala aur sabse aasan tarika hai. Zyadatar bank purane grahak ko chhodna nahi chahte.

Aapki bank aksar ek chhoti si conversion fee lekar aapko apne hi naye aur kam rate par shift kar deti hai. Ye rakam balance transfer ki kul fee se kaafi kam hoti hai, aur na kagaz banane padte hain, na property ke document ek bank se doosri me jaate hain.

Karne ka tarika seedha hai. Doosri bank ka likhit offer lijiye, apni bank ki shakha me dikhaiye, aur poochiye ki wo isse milaa sakte hain ya nahi. Bahut baar jawab haan hota hai, aur poora kaam ek hafte me ho jaata hai.

Nahi mila tabhi transfer ka soch. Kam se kam aapke paas ek asli offer to hoga jo mol-bhaav me kaam aayega.

Transfer ke baad EMI wahi rakhiye

Yahan ek chhoti si baat lakhon ka farak deti hai. Rate kam hone par bank aapse poochta hai — EMI kam karein ya samay?

25 lakh ke loan par 0.8% rate girne se EMI lagbhag 1,300 kam hoti hai. Wo 1,300 jeb me le lena aasan lagta hai. Par usi rakam ko EMI me bane rehne dijiye, to loan kai mahine pehle khatam hota hai aur bachat kai guna badi ho jaati hai.

Poora antar dekhna ho to Loan Prepayment Calculator me EMI ghatane aur samay ghatane, dono vikalp aazma lijiye.

Kaagaz aur samay — kya-kya lagega

Prakriya seedhi hai par ismein teen-chaar hafte lag jaate hain. Pehle se pata ho to jhanjhat kam hota hai.

  • Purani bank se foreclosure letter aur bakaya ka hisaab
  • Pichhle saal ka statement aur property ke asli document ki list
  • Aay ke kagaz, ITR aur bank statement — jaise naye loan me lagte hain
  • Nayi bank purani bank ko seedha bhugtaan karti hai, paisa aapke haath nahi aata
  • Documents purani bank se nayi bank tak pahunchne me 2-4 hafte
Ek baat jo aksar bhool jaate hain: transfer poora hone ke baad purani bank se No Objection Certificate aur property ke document milne ki rasid zaroor lijiye. Aur jab poora loan khatam ho, tab registry office me mortgage hatane ki entry bhi karwaiye — warna aage bechte waqt dikkat aati hai.

Floating rate wale loan me RBI ke niyam ke hisaab se aam taur par foreclosure charge nahi lagta. Fixed rate wale me lag sakta hai, isliye ye pehle poochna chahiye.

Bachat samay ke saath ghatti kyun hai

Ek hi loan, ek hi rate ka antar — bas transfer kab kiya, wo alag. 25 lakh ke loan par 0.8% ka antar.

Aakhri stambh dhyaan se dekhiye. 3 saal bache hon to bachat lagbhag 35 hazaar hai, aur transfer ki fee hi 15-20 hazaar baith jaati hai. Mehnat, kagaz aur mahine bhar ka intezaar — sab ek chhoti si rakam ke liye.

Jo galtiyan sabse zyada dikhti hain

Transfer me nuksan rate se nahi hota, in chhoti baaton se hota hai.

  • Sirf processing fee ginna. Legal, valuation, stamp aur nayi insurance milakar rakam aksar do-teen guna nikalti hai.
  • Apni bank se baat na karna. Zyadatar bank ek chhoti conversion fee lekar apne hi naye rate par shift kar deti hain — bina kagaz ke jhanjhat ke. Doosri bank ka likhit offer haath me ho to baat jaldi ban jaati hai.
  • Rate girte hi EMI ghata lena. Har mahine ka 1,300 jeb me achha lagta hai, par EMI wahi rakhne se loan kai mahine pehle khatam hota hai.
  • Teaser rate par jaana. Pehle saal ka kam rate dekhkar shift kiya aur doosre saal wo wapas upar — ye aam kahani hai. Reset ka niyam pehle padh lijiye.
  • NOC aur document na lena. Purani bank se No Objection Certificate aur property ke asli kagaz ki rasid lena mat bhooliye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Balance transfer kab faayde ka hota hai?

Jab rate ka antar aam taur par 0.5% se zyada ho aur loan ke 7 saal se zyada bache hon. Dono me se ek shart bhi poori na ho to faayda bahut kam bachta hai.

Transfer par kaunse kharch lagte hain?

Processing fee, legal aur valuation charge, stamp jaise rajya ke kharch, aur aksar ek nayi insurance policy. Sabko jodkar hi asli bachat nikalti hai.

Purani bank se hi rate kam karwa sakte hain?

Haan, aur ye sabse aasan rasta hai. Zyadatar bank ek chhoti conversion fee lekar apne hi naye rate par shift kar deti hain, bina kagaz ke jhanjhat ke.

Transfer par foreclosure charge lagta hai?

Floating rate wale vyaktigat loan par aam taur par nahi lagta. Fixed rate wale me lag sakta hai, isliye letter lene se pehle ye poochh lena chahiye.

Rate kam hone par EMI ghataayein ya samay?

Samay ghatana zyada bachat deta hai. EMI wahi rakhne se loan jaldi khatam hota hai aur kul byaj kaafi kam ho jaata hai.
