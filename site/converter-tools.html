Converter Tools — Bigha, Nali, Length, Weight, Temperature

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · 9 tools
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Bharat me zameen ka naap har rajya me alag hai — aur yahi sabse badi confusion hai. UP ka bigha, Uttarakhand ka nali, Rajasthan ka bigha: teeno alag-alag square feet ke barabar hote hain. Isliye "1 bigha kitna hota hai" ka ek jawab hai hi nahi, jawab is baat par nirbhar hai ki aap kis zile me hain.

Bigha Converter me rajya chunkar sahi number milta hai. Uttarakhand walon ke liye nali-mutthi wala page alag se banaya hai, kyunki pahad me naap ka tarika hi alag hai aur internet par iski sahi jaankari bahut kam milti hai.

Iske alawa yahan roz kaam aane wale converter bhi hain — length aur weight (cm, meter, kilometre, kilo, gram, tola), temperature, speed aur data storage. Zameen ke kaagaz ka koi bhi faisla lene se pehle apne tehsil ya patwari ke record se number ek baar milaa lein.

Bigha har jagah alag kyun hai

Angrezon se pehle Bharat me naap ka koi ek rashtriya paimana tha hi nahi. Har riyasat, kai baar to har pargana, apna naap chalata tha. Ye naap aksar zameen ki upaj par based the — "itni zameen jisme itna beej lage" ya "itni zameen jise ek jodi bail ek din me jot le".

Isi wajah se aaj bhi bigha UP me lagbhag 27,000 sq ft, Rajasthan ke kuch hisson me 17,000 ke aas-paas, aur Bengal me lagbhag 14,400 sq ft hota hai. Bihar aur Madhya Pradesh me apne alag number hain. "1 bigha kitna hota hai" ka ek jawab hai hi nahi — jawab is baat par nirbhar karta hai ki aap kis zile me khade hain.

Isliye Bigha Converter me pehle rajya chuniye, tabhi number sahi aayega. Poora byora is page par hai.

Uttarakhand: nali, mutthi aur bisi

Pahad me naap ka tarika hi alag hai. Yahan bigha nahi, nali chalti hai, aur uske chhote hisse ko mutthi kehte hain. Kumaon aur Garhwal dono me ye shabd chalte hain, par gaon-gaon me thoda farak mil jaata hai.

Ek baat khaas dhyaan dene layak hai: pahadi zameen samtal nahi hoti. Kaagaz par rakba hamesha upar se dekhi gayi jagah ka hota hai, dhalaan ka nahi. Isliye agar aap dhalaan wale khet par tape lekar chalenge to naap zyada aayega — par record wahi purana number rakhega. Ye galti nahi hai, tarika hi aisa hai, aur yahi baat sauda karte waqt sabse zyada galatfahmi paida karti hai.

Poora hisaab nali-mutthi wale page par hai, aur zameen kharidne se pehle kaagaz kaise dekhein wo yahan.

Roz ke converter

Zameen ke alawa yahan wo converter bhi hain jo har din kaam aate hain — length (cm, inch, foot, meter, kilometre), weight (kilo, gram, tola, quintal), temperature, speed aur data storage.

Tola khaas taur par sone-chaandi ke lene-den me chalta hai — 1 tola lagbhag 11.66 gram hota hai. Quintal kheti me: 1 quintal = 100 kilo, aur 1 metric ton = 10 quintal. Ye number gaon me fasal bechte waqt roz kaam aate hain.

Yaad rakhein: kisi bhi converter ka number registry, bank loan ya court ke kaam me kaanooni proof nahi hota. Ye sirf mota-moti andaza dene ke liye hai. Asli rakba hamesha tehsil ke record aur patwari ki paimaish se hi maana jaata hai.
