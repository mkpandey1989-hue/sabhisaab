EMI kaise banti hai

EMI teen cheez par tay hoti hai: loan ki rakam, byaj dar, aur avdhi (kitne saal). Rakam aur byaj zyada ho to EMI badhti hai; avdhi lambi ho to EMI ghatti hai — par lambi avdhi me kul byaj zyada dena padta hai.

EMI = [P × r × (1+r)ⁿ] ÷ [(1+r)ⁿ − 1]
Jahan P = loan rakam, r = mahine ka byaj (saalana÷12÷100), n = kul mahine.

Ek misaal

₹7 lakh ka car loan, 9.5% byaj, 5 saal (60 mahine) ke liye. EMI lagbhag ₹14,700 aati hai. Poore 5 saal me total ~₹8.8 lakh dena padta hai — yaani ~₹1.8 lakh sirf byaj. Isiliye down payment zyada dekar loan chhota rakhna sasta padta hai.

Car loan lete waqt 4 baatein

  • On-road price par loan nahi milta — bank ex-showroom ki ek hissedari (aksar 80-90%) deta hai. Baaki down payment aapko dena hota hai.
  • Processing fee aur insurance alag se judte hain — inhe bhi jod kar dekhein.
  • Chhoti avdhi behtar — EMI thodi zyada aati hai par kul byaj bahut kam. Ho sake to 3-5 saal.
  • Prepayment charge poochhein — jaldi chukane par penalty to nahi.

Naye vs purani gaadi ka loan

Nayi gaadi par byaj kam (aksar 9-11%), purani (used) gaadi par zyada (12-15%) hota hai, kyunki bank ka risk zyada. Avdhi bhi purani gaadi par chhoti milti hai. Loan lene se pehle do-teen bank ka rate milaayein — chhota sa farak bhi 5 saal me hazaron ka ho jaata hai.

Gaadi ka asli kharcha sirf EMI nahi hai

Showroom me baat hamesha EMI par hoti hai — "sirf ₹18,000 mahina". Par gaadi ka mahina isse kaafi upar baithta hai. Ek aam hatchback/sedan ka mota anuman:

  • EMI — jo is calculator se nikla
  • Petrol/diesel — 800 km mahina chalayein to lagbhag ₹5,000-6,000
  • Insurance — saal me ₹8,000-25,000, yaani mahine ka ₹700-2,000
  • Service aur marammat — saal me ₹8,000-15,000
  • Parking, toll, dhulai — sheher par nirbhar
Yaani ₹18,000 ki EMI wali gaadi asal me ₹26,000-28,000 mahina maangti hai. Budget banate waqt sirf EMI dekhna sabse aam galti hai — aur wahi galti pehle saal me hi tang kar deti hai.

Isi liye purani gaadi lene wale aksar zyada aaraam me rehte hain — EMI chhoti hoti hai aur upar wale kharche wahi ke wahi. Nayi gaadi ka sabse bada nuksan pehle teen saal me hi ho jaata hai, jab uski keemat sabse tezi se girti hai.

Down payment kitna dena chahiye

Bank aksar gaadi ki keemat ka 80-90% tak loan de deta hai. Dene ka matlab ye nahi ki lena chahiye.

Kam se kam 20% down payment ka niyam apne liye bana lijiye. Do wajah:

  • Kul byaj kam — loan chhota hoga to poore tenure me lakhon ka farak nahi, par 40-60 hazaar ka farak aaraam se padta hai.
  • Gaadi ki keemat se loan zyada na ho — nayi gaadi showroom se nikalte hi apni keemat ka bada hissa kho deti hai. Kam down payment par shuru ke saalon me aisi sthiti aa jaati hai jahan bacha hua loan gaadi ki bazaar keemat se zyada hota hai. Us waqt gaadi bechkar loan chukana bhi mumkin nahi rehta.

Tenure ka bhi wahi hisaab — 7 saal ka car loan EMI to chhoti kar deta hai, par saatve saal tak aap us gaadi ki kist bhar rahe honge jiski keemat aadhi se bhi kam ho chuki hogi. 5 saal se lamba car loan aam taur par samajhdaari nahi hai.

Aur ek cheez — showroom se milne wala insurance aksar bahar se mehnga hota hai. Pehle saal majboori ho sakti hai, par renewal ke waqt do-teen jagah bhaav zaroor dekhiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Car loan par kitna down payment dena padta hai?

Aam taur par ex-showroom price ka 10-20%. Bank baaki 80-90% deta hai. Zyada down payment se EMI aur kul byaj dono kam.

Kitne saal ka car loan lena chahiye?

3-5 saal behtar. Lambi avdhi se EMI kam dikhti hai par kul byaj bahut zyada ho jaata hai.

Used car par loan milta hai?

Haan, par byaj thoda zyada aur avdhi chhoti. Bank gaadi ki umar aur condition dekh kar tay karta hai.

EMI time par na di to?

Late fee lagti hai aur credit score girta hai. Baar-baar chhootne par gaadi bhi zabt ho sakti hai.

Down payment kitna dena chahiye?

Kam se kam 20%. Isse kul byaj kam padta hai aur aisi sthiti nahi aati jahan bacha hua loan gaadi ki bazaar keemat se zyada ho jaye.

7 saal ka car loan lena theek hai?

Aam taur par nahi. EMI chhoti dikhti hai par aakhri saalon me aap us gaadi ki kist bhar rahe hote hain jiski keemat kaafi gir chuki hoti hai. 5 saal tak seemit rakhna behtar hai.
