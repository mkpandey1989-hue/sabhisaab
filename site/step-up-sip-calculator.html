Hisaab ke peeche ki baat

Aam SIP me kist saalon tak wahi rehti hai. Step-up SIP me har 12 mahine baad kist ek tay percent se badh jaati hai. Har mahine ka paisa apne baaki bache samay tak return kamata hai, aur agle saal badi kist us par jud jaati hai. Nateeja ye ki nivesh thoda-thoda badhta hai par corpus kaafi tezi se.

Formula: Har saal: nayi kist = purani kist × (1 + step-up%)
Har mahine: value = (purani value + kist) × (1 + mahine ka return)

Teen sthitiyan, teen jawab

Misaal 1 — 20% step-up. ₹4,000 se shuruaat, har saal 20% badhotri, 13% return, 6 saal. Nivesh ₹4,76,636 aur value lagbhag ₹6,76,439.

Misaal 2 — aam SIP se tulna. ₹5,000 mahina, 12% return, 15 saal — corpus lagbhag ₹25.2 lakh. Wahi SIP har saal 10% badhaate rahein to lagbhag ₹42.5 lakh. Yaani kareeb ₹17 lakh ka farak.

Misaal 3 — chhoti shuruaat. ₹2,000 se shuru, 10% saalana step-up, 12% return, 20 saal. Corpus lagbhag ₹32 lakh — jabki bina step-up ke ye kareeb ₹20 lakh hi rehta.

Kahan log chook jaate hain

  1. Itna badhana ki nibh na sake. 25-30% saalana step-up kaagaz par sundar lagta hai, par do saal baad kist bhaari padne lagti hai. Salary ki badhotri se thoda kam rakhein.
  2. Step-up ko apne aap hone wala maan lena. Kai jagah ye khud nahi badhta — ya to fund me top-up feature chalu karna padta hai, ya har saal khud badhani padti hai. Ek reminder laga lein.
  3. Beech me band kar dena. Step-up SIP ka poora fayda aakhri saalon me aata hai, jab kist sabse badi hoti hai. 5 saal me band kar dene se asli faayda haath se nikal jaata hai.
  4. Emergency fund ke bina shuru karna. Pehle 4-6 mahine ka kharcha alag rakhein. Warna zaroorat padne par SIP hi todni padti hai.
  5. Return zyada maan lena. 15-18% maan kar plan banane se number bade dikhte hain par asli me poore nahi hote. 11-12% par plan banayein.

Yahan ki khaas baatein

  • Top-up feature: Zyadatar fund house SIP shuru karte waqt hi "annual top-up" ka vikalp dete hain — percent ya fixed rakam me.
  • Bank mandate: Auto-debit ki limit thodi oonchi rakhein, warna step-up ke baad mandate fail ho sakta hai.
  • Salary hike ke saath: Appraisal ke agle mahine step-up karna sabse aasaan rehta hai — badhi hui salary ki aadat lagne se pehle.
  • Tax: Equity fund par 12 mahine baad long-term gain lagta hai. ELSS me 3 saal ka lock-in har kist par alag se lagta hai.
  • Anushasan: Step-up ka asli faayda tab hai jab 10 saal se zyada chale.

Har saal 10% badhane se kitna farak padta hai

Step-up ka asli jaadu shuru me nahi, aakhri saalon me dikhta hai — kyunki tab tak kisht kaafi badi ho chuki hoti hai.

Step-up ka sabse bada faayda ganit nahi, aadat hai. Salary badhne par kharcha apne aap badh jaata hai — ise "lifestyle creep" kehte hain. Step-up SIP badhi hui salary ka ek hissa pehle hi rok leta hai, kharch hone se pehle. 10% step-up rakhna aasaan hai kyunki aam taur par salary isse zyada badhti hai, isliye haath me aane wala paisa bhi har saal badhta rehta hai. Zyadatar app me ye setting SIP shuru karte waqt ek baar lagti hai aur phir apne aap chalti rehti hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Step-up SIP kya hoti hai?

Aisi SIP jisme har saal kist ek tay percent se badh jaati hai — jaise ₹5,000 se shuru karke har saal 10% zyada.

Kitna step-up rakhna theek hai?

5% se 15% ke beech zyadatar logon ke liye theek rehta hai, kyunki salary bhi lagbhag isi rafter se badhti hai.

Kya step-up baad me band kar sakte hain?

Haan, kabhi bhi rok sakte hain. Par jitna lamba chalega, faayda utna hi zyada.

Ye aam SIP se kitna behtar hai?

15-20 saal me aam taur par 50-70% zyada corpus ban jaata hai, wahi return maan kar.

Kya ismein extra charge lagta hai?

Nahi, ye sirf kist ki rakam badhane ka tareeka hai — koi alag fees nahi.
