On Road Price Calculator — Ex-showroom Se On-road Tak Ka Poora Hisaab

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~7 minute

Website par gaadi ki keemat 8.99 lakh likhi thi. Showroom pahunche to quotation 10.6 lakh ka bana. Beech ke 1.6 lakh kahan se aaye — ye sawaal lagbhag har kharidaar ke mann me aata hai.

Seedha jawab: ex-showroom ke upar aam taur par 15% se 20% aur judta hai — RTO tax, road safety cess, insurance aur handling. Isme se sirf insurance aur accessories par mol-bhaav hota hai; RTO tax sarkari hai aur usme koi chhoot nahi milti.

Neeche ex-showroom keemat aur apne rajya ka RTO pratishat daaliye. On-road keemat ka poora breakup saamne aa jayega.

Quotation me kya-kya hota hai

Ek aam quotation me 6-8 line hoti hain. Har line ka matlab jaan lena zaroori hai, kyunki dabaav sirf do-teen line par hi daala ja sakta hai.

LineKya haiMol-bhaav ho sakta hai?
Ex-showroomGaadi ki keemat, GST aur cess ke saathDiscount ke roop me, kabhi-kabhi
RTO tax aur registrationRajya sarkar ka taxNahi, bilkul nahi
InsurancePehle saal ka policyHaan, sabse zyada yahin
Handling ya logisticsDealer ka chargeHaan, aur ye hataaya bhi ja sakta hai
Fastag aur baakiTay chhoti rakamNahi
Accessories, coating, warrantyDealer ka apna saamaanHaan, aur ye zaroori nahi hote

Teesri aur aakhri line par dhyaan dijiye. Yahi do jagah hain jahan sabse zyada paisa bachta hai, aur dono par aapka poora haq hai.

Insurance dealer se hi lena zaroori nahi hai

Ye baat sabse kam logon ko pata hoti hai aur sabse zyada paisa yahin bachta hai. Gaadi ka insurance aap kisi bhi company se le sakte hain — dealer se lena kanooni majboori nahi hai.

Dealer ke quotation me pehle saal ka insurance aksar 38-45 hazaar ka hota hai. Wahi cover seedha company ki site se aksar 22-28 hazaar me mil jaata hai. Ek hi gaadi, wahi cover, aur 12-15 hazaar ka antar — sirf isliye ki policy kahan se li.

Do baatein dhyaan me rakhiye. Pehli — nayi gaadi par teesre pakshe ka cover kai saal ka ek saath lena hota hai, isliye tulna karte waqt dono quotation ek jaisi avadhi ke honi chahiye. Doosri — zero depreciation cover pehle 3-5 saal me aam taur par faayde ka rehta hai, use hataakar sasta karna theek nahi.

Apni gaadi ka IDV aur cover samajhna ho to Car Insurance IDV Calculator dekh lijiye.

RTO tax rajya ke hisaab se badalta hai

Ek hi gaadi ka on-road bhaav do rajyon me lakh rupaye tak alag ho sakta hai, kyunki road tax rajya tay karta hai. Tax aam taur par gaadi ki keemat, eendhan aur kabhi-kabhi lambaai par nirbhar karta hai.

Aam dhaanchaLagbhag pratishat
Sasti gaadi (10 lakh tak)7% se 12%
Beech ki gaadi (10-20 lakh)10% se 15%
Mehngi gaadi (20 lakh se upar)13% se 20%
Electric gaadiKai rajyon me kaafi kam ya shunya

Aakhri line dhyaan dene layak hai. Kai rajya electric gaadi par road tax me badi chhoot dete hain, aur usse on-road keemat ka poora ganit badal jaata hai. Petrol gaadi se tulna karte waqt sirf ex-showroom dekhna galat tasveer deta hai.

Doosre rajya me sasta tax dekhkar gaadi wahan register karwana aam taur par kaam nahi karta. Gaadi ko us rajya me lagataar rakhne par kuch mahine ke baad wahan dobara register karwana aur tax bharna padta hai. Bachat ke chakkar me do baar tax bharne ki naubat aa jaati hai.

Ek nazar me — 9 lakh ki gaadi ka poora breakup

Ex-showroom 8.99 lakh, 10% RTO tax wale rajya me — paisa is tarah banta hai.

Sabse chhota tukda — handling charge — sabse zyada bahas ka vishay hota hai. Wo dealer ka apna charge hai aur uske liye koi sarkari niyam nahi hai. Maang karne par kai dealer use ghata dete hain ya hata dete hain.

Jo galtiyan sabse zyada dikhti hain

Showroom me jaldi me liye gaye faisle mahinon tak chubhte hain.

  • Sirf EMI par baat karna. Salesman EMI ghatane ke liye samay badha deta hai. Gaadi wahi, EMI chhoti, par kul kharch bahut zyada. Poochne wala number on-road keemat aur kul chukane wali rakam hai.
  • Accessories package le lena. Coating, seat cover, extra light — aksar bahar se aadhe daam me mil jaate hain. Ye package zaroori nahi hota, chahe kuch bhi kaha jaye.
  • Insurance dealer se hi lena. Sabse bada bachne wala paisa yahin hai.
  • Extended warranty jaldi me lena. Ye aksar gaadi ki warranty khatam hone se pehle kabhi bhi li ja sakti hai. Turant faisla karne ki koi zaroorat nahi.
  • Mahine ke shuru me kharidna. Mahine ke aakhri hafte me dealer ka target poora karne ka dabaav hota hai, aur wahi sabse achha mol-bhaav ka samay hota hai.
Ek aur baat — quotation par likhi discount wali line dhyaan se padhiye. Kai baar discount ex-showroom par dikhaya jaata hai par usi rakam ke accessories jodkar wapas le liya jaata hai. Faisla hamesha aakhri on-road number par kijiye, beech ki line par nahi.

Gaadi ka asli kharch on-road par khatam nahi hota

Kharid ki keemat sirf shuruaat hai. Gaadi ka asli kharch aage ke saalon me aata hai, aur usme teen cheezein sabse badi hoti hain.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

On-road keemat ex-showroom se kitni zyada hoti hai?

Aam taur par 15% se 20% zyada. Isme RTO tax, insurance, handling aur chhote charge shaamil hote hain. Mehngi gaadi aur ooncha road tax wale rajya me ye pratishat aur badh jaata hai.

Insurance dealer se lena zaroori hai?

Nahi. Aap kisi bhi company se le sakte hain aur ismein aksar 10-15 hazaar tak bachte hain. Bas cover aur avadhi dono quotation me ek jaisi honi chahiye.

RTO tax me mol-bhaav ho sakta hai?

Nahi. Wo rajya sarkar ka tay tax hai aur usme koi chhoot nahi milti. Mol-bhaav sirf insurance, handling aur accessories par hota hai.

Handling charge dena zaroori hai?

Ye dealer ka apna charge hai, koi sarkari niyam nahi. Maang karne par kai dealer ise ghata dete hain ya hata dete hain.

Doosre rajya me register karwana sasta padta hai?

Aam taur par nahi. Gaadi ko lagataar doosre rajya me rakhne par wahan dobara register karwana aur tax bharna padta hai, jisse do baar kharch ho jaata hai.
