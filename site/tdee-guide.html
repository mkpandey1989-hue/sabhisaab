TDEE Kya Hai? Roz Ki Calorie Zaroorat Ki Guide

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Vazan ghatana ho ya badhana, sabse pehla sawaal yahi hota hai — "mujhe roz kitni calorie chahiye?" Iska jawab hai TDEE. Is guide me hum samjhenge ki TDEE kya hai, BMR se kaise alag hai, kaise nikalta hai, aur apne lakshya ke hisaab se kitni calorie leni chahiye. Bina sahi number jaane diet plan andhere me teer chalane jaisa hai.

TDEE kya hai?

TDEE ka matlab hai Total Daily Energy Expenditure — yaani din bhar me aapka sharir kitni calorie jalata hai. Ismein sab kuch shaamil hai: saans lena, dil dhadakna (ye BMR hai), chalna-firna, kaam karna, khaana pachaana aur exercise.

Agar aap TDEE ke barabar calorie khaate hain, to vazan waisa hi rehta hai. Isse kam khaayenge to ghatega, zyada khaayenge to badhega. Poora weight management isi ek number ke aaspaas ghoomta hai — isliye ise samajhna sabse pehla kadam hai.

BMR aur TDEE me kya farak?

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) wo calorie hai jo aapka sharir bina kuch kiye, sirf zinda rehne ke liye jalata hai — jaise saans, dil ki dhadkan, dimaag aur sharir ka taapmaan. Ye TDEE ka sabse bada hissa (60-70%) hota hai.

Rishta: TDEE = BMR × activity factor
Jitni zyada activity, utna bada factor (1.2 se 1.9 tak)

Do log ka BMR lagbhag same ho sakta hai, par jo zyada active hai uska TDEE zyada hoga — kyunki wo din bhar zyada calorie jalata hai. Isi liye athlete ko aam desk-job wale se kaafi zyada khaana chahiye.

TDEE kaise nikalta hai?

Aaj sabse bharosemand formula hai Mifflin-St Jeor. Pehle BMR nikalte hain, phir activity factor se guna karte hain.

Din bhar ka kharcha kis-kis me jaata hai

TDEE sirf exercise se nahi banta. Uske chaar hisse hote hain:

HissaKya haiKul ka
BMRSote hue bhi jalne wali calorie~60-70%
NEATChalna, kaam, khada rehna~15-30%
Khana pachanaKhaane ko todne me lagti calorie~10%
ExerciseGym, daud, khel~5-10%
Table dekhkar ek baat saaf ho jaati hai — exercise sabse chhota hissa hai. Ek ghante ki gym lagbhag 300-400 calorie jalati hai, jo ek samose-jalebi me wapas aa jaati hai. Isliye "aaj gym gaya tha, kuch bhi kha lenge" wali soch sabse aam galti hai. Asli farak NEAT daalta hai — din bhar me kitna chalte hain, lift lete hain ya seedhi. Roz 8,000-10,000 kadam chalne wala baithe rehne wale se 400-600 calorie zyada jalata hai, bina kisi gym ke. Ye aam jaankari hai, medical salah nahi.
Mifflin-St Jeor:
Purush: BMR = 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×umar + 5
Mahila: BMR = 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×umar − 161

Activity factor: bahut kam (1.2), halka (1.375), madhyam (1.55), zyada (1.725), athlete (1.9). Ek badi galti — log apni activity zyada aank lete hain, jisse TDEE zyada aa jaata hai aur vazan nahi ghatta. Agar do vikalp ke beech confuse ho, kam wala chunna surakshit hai.

Weight loss aur gain ke liye calorie

TDEE mil gaya to plan simple ho jaata hai:

  • Vazan ghatana: TDEE se lagbhag 500 calorie kam khaayein. Hafte me karib aadha kilo ghatega.
  • Vazan banaye rakhna: TDEE ke barabar khaayein.
  • Muscle badhana: TDEE se ~300-500 calorie zyada, saath me protein aur weight training.

Bahut kam khaana (1000+ calorie deficit) ulta padta hai — muscle ghatta hai, metabolism dheema hota hai, aur chhoda to vazan tezi se wapas aa jaata hai. Dheere aur tikaoo tarika hamesha sabse accha hota hai.

TDEE ke saath dhyan rakhein

Yaad rahe, TDEE ek anuman hai, exact number nahi. Sabse accha tarika — 2-3 hafte apna vazan aur roz ki calorie track karein. Agar vazan waisa hi hai, to wahi aapka asli TDEE hai; ghat raha hai to aap deficit me ho.

Sirf calorie kaafi nahi hoti — protein (muscle bachaane ke liye), paani, 7-8 ghante neend aur fiber-wali sabzi bhi utni hi zaroori hain. Aur agar koi bimari ho jaise thyroid, diabetes ya PCOD, to diet plan doctor ya registered dietician se hi banwayein.

Indian diet me calorie sambhaalna

Number jaan lena ek baat hai, use asli khaane me badalna doosri. Indian diet me sabse bada dhyaan ghee, tel aur cheeni par dena chahiye — ek chammach ghee me ~45 calorie hoti hai, aur do-teen roti par ghee lagate-lagate calorie chupke se badh jaati hai.

Portion samajhna zaroori hai: ek katori dal, do roti, ek katori sabzi, thoda chawal — ye ek santulit thali hai. Meetha aur tala hua (samosa, pakauda, mithai) kabhi-kabhi theek, roz nahi. Protein Indian veg diet me aksar kam reh jaata hai — dal, paneer, dahi, soya aur ande (agar khaate hain) se ise poora karein. Aur meethe drinks (cold drink, packaged juice) me "chhupi" calorie sabse zyada hoti hai — inki jagah paani ya nimbu-paani behtar hai. Chhote badlaav lambe samay me bada farak laate hain.

Neend, paani aur consistency

Calorie aur macro se aage do cheezein hain jo log bhool jaate hain — neend aur paani. Kam neend (5-6 ghante) se bhookh badhane wale hormone gadbad ho jaate hain, jisse agle din zyada khaane ka mann karta hai. 7-8 ghante ki neend vazan sambhaalne me seedha madad karti hai.

Paani bhi zaroori hai — kai baar pyaas ko hum bhookh samajh lete hain. Khaane se pehle ek glass paani peene se overeating kam hoti hai. Sabse badi baat hai consistency — ek din ka perfect diet nahi, balki hafton-mahinon ka औसत mayne rakhta hai. Kabhi zyada kha liya to ghabrayein nahi, agle din normal par aa jaayein. Dheere-dheere banayi aadat hi tikaoo rehti hai; sakht diet jise aap 2 hafte se zyada na jhel sakein, wo kaam nahi karti.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

TDEE ka matlab kya hai?

Total Daily Energy Expenditure — din bhar me sharir kitni calorie jalata hai.

BMR aur TDEE me farak?

BMR aaram ki calorie hai; TDEE me activity bhi judti hai (BMR &times; activity factor).

Vazan ghatane ke liye kitni calorie?

TDEE se lagbhag 500 kam — hafte me karib aadha kilo ghatega.

Kaunsa formula sabse accha hai?

Mifflin-St Jeor aaj sabse bharosemand maana jaata hai.

Kya TDEE exact hota hai?

Nahi, anuman hai. 2-3 hafte vazan track karke asli zaroorat pata chalti hai.
