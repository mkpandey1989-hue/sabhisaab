Kitni units bechni hongi

Break-even units = Fixed cost ÷ (Bikri daam − Per unit lagat)

Beech wale hisse ko contribution kehte hain — har ek bikri par kitna paisa fixed kharche bharne ko milta hai.

Misaal: chai ki dukaan. Mahine ka kiraya + bijli + ek ladke ki salary = ₹50,000. Ek chai ₹15 me bikti hai, banane ka kharcha ₹6.

  • Contribution per chai: 15 − 6 = ₹9
  • Break-even: 50,000 ÷ 9 = 5,556 chai mahine me
  • Yaani roz lagbhag 185 chai — sirf kharcha nikaalne ke liye

Isse zyada bikri jo hogi, uska har ₹9 seedha munafa hai. Aur isse kam par ghaata.

Ye ek number karobar shuru karne se pehle nikaal lena chahiye. "Roz 185 chai bik jayegi kya?" — is ek sawaal ka imaandaar jawab bahut se galat faisle rok deta hai.

Fixed aur variable cost — antar samajhna zaroori hai

Fixed cost — jo har mahine lagta hai, chahe bikri ho ya na ho:

  • Dukaan ka kiraya · staff ki salary · bijli ka fixed charge · license aur bima · loan ki EMI · internet aur phone

Variable cost — jo har bikri par lagta hai:

  • Kachcha maal · packing · gas ya eendhan · commission · delivery ka kharcha
Sabse aam galti — apni salary ko fixed cost me na jodna. Agar aap khud dukaan par 10 ghante de rahe hain, to wo mehnat muft nahi hai. Apne liye ek waajib salary tay kariye aur use fixed cost me jodiye. Warna karobar munafe me dikhta hai aur asal me aap sirf apni mazdoori nikaal rahe hote hain.

Isi tarah ghar walon ki madad, apni gaadi ka istemaal, aur ghar ki jagah — in sab ki keemat hoti hai, chahe cash me na dikhe.

Break-even ghatane ke teen raste

1. Fixed cost kam kariye
Sabse tez asar. Chhoti jagah, kam staff, ya kaam ke hisaab se part-time. ₹50,000 se ₹40,000 hone par break-even 5,556 se girkar 4,444 ho jaata hai — 20% kam bikri me kaam ban jayega.

2. Daam thoda badhaiye
Chai ₹15 se ₹17 karne par contribution ₹9 se ₹11 ho jaata hai. Break-even 5,556 se 4,545. Sirf ₹2 badhane se 18% kam bikri chahiye. Daam badhana sabse taakatwar upay hai, par grahak ka dhyaan rakhna zaroori hai.

3. Per unit lagat kam kariye
Thok me kharidna, kam barbaadi, sasta supplier. ₹6 se ₹5 hone par bhi break-even kaafi girta hai.

Teeno me se sabse jaldi kaam karne wala aksar barbaadi kam karna hota hai — kharab hone wala saman, zyada bana hua maal, ya bina hisaab ka udhaar. Ye bina kisi nayi laagat ke seedha munafa badhata hai.

Break-even ke aage — asli munafa

Break-even par pahunchna jeetna nahi hai, sirf ghaata khatam hona hai. Asli lakshya usse aage hai.

Munafe ka lakshya jodkar hisaab:

Zaroori units = (Fixed cost + Chahiye munafa) ÷ Contribution

Upar wale misaal me ₹30,000 munafa chahiye:
(50,000 + 30,000) ÷ 9 = 8,889 chai mahine, yaani roz lagbhag 296.

Aur ye baatein bhi hisaab me rakhiye:

  • Mausam — kai karobar me kuch mahine bahut achhe aur kuch bilkul thande hote hain. Saal ka ausat dekhiye, ek mahine ka nahi.
  • Udhaar — bikri hui par paisa nahi aaya, to wo bikri kaagaz par hai. Udhaar ka hisaab alag rakhiye.
  • Shuruaati mahine — naye karobar me pehle 3-6 mahine aksar break-even se neeche rehte hain. Utne mahine ka kharcha alag se rakh kar hi shuruaat kariye.

Chhoti dukaan ka sabse aam dhokha: log daam ghatakar bikri badhane ki sochte hain, par table dekhiye — Rs 100 par laagat Rs 60 se Rs 75 hone par breakeven 1,500 se 2,400 unit ho jaata hai, yaani 60% zyada bechna padega sirf barabar aane ke liye. Fixed kharche me dukaan ka kiraya, bijli, staff ki tankhwah aur apni tankhwah bhi honi chahiye — apni tankhwah na jodna sabse aam galti hai, jisse dhandha "chalta hua" lagta hai par ghar kharch nahi nikalta.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Break-even kaise nikalta hai?

Fixed cost ÷ (bikri daam − per unit lagat). Jo number aayega, utni units bechne par kharcha nikal aata hai.

Apni salary bhi fixed cost me jodni chahiye?

Haan. Agar aap khud kaam kar rahe hain to wo mehnat muft nahi hai. Bina ise jode karobar munafe me dikhta hai jabki asal me aap sirf apni mazdoori nikaal rahe hote hain.

Break-even kam karne ka sabse tez tarika kya hai?

Daam thoda badhana. Chai 15 se 17 karne par break-even 18% gir jaata hai. Uske baad fixed cost ghatana aur barbaadi kam karna.

Naya karobar shuru karne se pehle kya dekhein?

Break-even nikaal kar poochiye — kya itni bikri sach me ho payegi? Aur shuruaati 3-6 mahine ka kharcha alag se rakh kar hi shuruaat kariye.
