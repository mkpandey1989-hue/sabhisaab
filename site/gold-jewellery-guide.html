Hallmark aur HUID — shuddhta ki pehli jaanch

Bharat me gahne par hallmark lagana zaroori hai. Hallmark wala gahna teen nishaan ke saath aata hai:

  • BIS ka logo — tikone jaisa nishaan
  • Carat aur shuddhta — jaise 22K916, yaani 22 carat aur 91.6% sona
  • HUID — 6 akshar ka alag pehchaan number, jo har gahne ka apna hota hai
HUID sabse kaam ka nishaan hai, aur sabse kam log ise jaanchte hain. BIS CARE app me "Verify HUID" me ye number daaliye. App batata hai ki gahna kitne carat ka darj hai, kis prakaar ka hai, aur kis jeweller ne hallmark karwaya. Agar app me carat ya gahne ka prakaar bill se alag dikhe, to wahin dukaan par baat kar lijiye.

Purane gahne par HUID nahi hota, kyunki ye niyam baad me aaya. Aise gahne bechne ya badalne par shuddhta jaanch ki machine (XRF ya karat meter) se dekhi jaati hai. Jaanch apne saamne karwaiye.

Carat ka matlab — 22 aur 18 me kya chunein

Carat batata hai ki gahne me kitna hissa shuddh sona hai. Baaki hissa taamba, chaandi jaisi dhaatu ka hota hai jo gahne ko mazboot banata hai.

CaratShuddh sona10 gram me sonaKiske liye theek
2499.9%9.99 gSikka, biscuit — gahna nahi, bahut naram
2291.6%9.16 gChain, kangan, mangalsutra
1875%7.5 gHeere aur pathar wale gahne
1458.5%5.85 gRoz pehnne wale halke gahne

18 carat ka gahna sasta lagta hai kyunki usme sona kam hai. Par making aksar utni hi lagti hai. Isliye 18 carat par making ka pratishat dhyaan se dekhiye — wo sone ki chhoti keemat par lag kar bhi rupaye me bada ho sakta hai.

Bhav ka hisaab: 22 carat ka bhav = 24 carat ka bhav × 916 ÷ 999. Jaise 24 carat ₹14,900 per gram ho to 22 carat lagbhag ₹13,662 per gram.

Making, wastage aur GST — bill padhne ka tarika

Bill me teen line dekhiye aur teeno ko rupaye me likhwaiye:

  1. Sone ki keemat = carat ka bhav × sone ka wazan (pathar ka wazan alag)
  2. Making aur wastage = jeweller ka kaam. Mol-bhaav isi par hota hai.
  3. GST = naye ready gahne par sona + making jodkar 3%
Bahut si jagah likha milta hai ki making par 5% GST lagta hai. Wo tab sahi hai jab aap apna sona dekar sirf banwai karwate hain. Dukaan se naya ready gahna lene par poora gahna ek hi saamaan hai, aur uske poore bill par 3% lagta hai. Bill me making par alag se 5% dikhe to poochhiye.

Pathar wale gahne par ek baat aur — pathar ka wazan sone ke wazan me na jude. Bill me "gross weight" aur "net weight" alag likhwaiye. Sone ka bhav sirf net weight par lagna chahiye.

Purana sona badalte waqt nuksan kahan hota hai

Purane gahne ke badle naya lena aam baat hai. Par yahin sabse zyada paisa chupke se nikalta hai.

Ek misaal: 10 gram purana 22 carat gahna, bhav ₹13,662 per gram. Sone ki keemat ₹1,36,620. Agar 5% katauti hui to haath me ₹1,29,789 ki value aayi — ₹6,831 ka farak sirf ek line me. Ek hi dukaan ka exchange offer lene se pehle doosri dukaan se sirf "kharid" ka bhav bhi poochh lijiye.

Exchange me GST ka hisaab dukaan alag-alag tareeke se lagaati hain. Bill me ye saaf likha hona chahiye ki GST kis rakam par laga.

Gahna, sikka ya kuch aur — kab kya theek

Gahna pehnne ki cheez hai. Uski making aur GST wapas nahi aate — 10 gram ki chain par ye lagbhag 13% hai. Isliye sone me nivesh karna ho to gahna sabse mehnga raasta hai.

  • Bank ya mint ka sikka — making kam, par bechte waqt bank wapas nahi leta; jeweller apni katauti se leta hai.
  • Gold ETF ya gold mutual fund — making nahi, bechna aasaan, SEBI ke niyam ke andar.
  • Digital gold — app par chhoti rakam se milta hai, par SEBI ne saaf kaha hai ki ye uske niyantran me nahi aata. Badi rakam lagaane se pehle ye baat yaad rakhiye.

Sone par loan lena ho to gahne ka hisaab alag hota hai — Gold Loan Calculator dekh lijiye. Sona aur SIP ki lambi tulna Gold vs SIP me hai.

Dukaan par jaane se pehle chhoti checklist

  1. Subah ka bhav ek bharosemand jagah se dekh kar likh lein.
  2. Carat pehle tay karein — 22 ya 18.
  3. Making rupaye me poochhein, pratishat me nahi.
  4. Gahne par BIS logo, carat aur HUID dekhein, aur HUID app me jaanchein.
  5. Gross aur net weight alag likhwaayein.
  6. Pakka GST bill lein, jisme HUID likha ho.
  7. Exchange me katauti ka pratishat pehle poochhein.
  8. Dukaan ki buyback niti likhit me lein.

Ye aath kaam 10 minute lete hain aur hazaaron bacha sakte hain. Bill ka poora hisaab pehle se nikaal kar jaana ho to calculator me apne number daal lijiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

HUID number kya hota hai?

HUID 6 akshar ka alag pehchaan number hai jo hallmark ke saath har gahne par lagta hai. Ise BIS CARE app me daalkar gahne ka carat aur jeweller ki jaankari jaanch sakte hain.

22K916 ka matlab kya hai?

Iska matlab hai gahna 22 carat ka hai aur usme 91.6% shuddh sona hai. Baaki hissa doosri dhaatu ka hota hai jo gahne ko mazboot banata hai.

Purane gahne par HUID na ho to kya karein?

Purane gahnon par HUID nahi hota kyunki ye niyam baad me aaya. Bechte ya badalte waqt shuddhta machine se apne saamne jaanchwaiye aur report maangiye.

Exchange me kitni katauti hoti hai?

Aam taur par 2% se 10% tak, dukaan aur gahne ke hisaab se. Katauti ka pratishat pehle poochh lein aur doosri dukaan se bhi kharid ka bhav le lein.

Nivesh ke liye gahna lena theek hai?

Aam taur par nahi. Making aur GST wapas nahi aate, jo lagbhag 10-15% hota hai. Nivesh ke liye sikka, gold ETF ya gold mutual fund sasta raasta hai.
