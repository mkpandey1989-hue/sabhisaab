CGPA se percentage kaise nikalta hai

Formula bahut saada hai:

Percentage = CGPA × Multiplier

CBSE me multiplier 9.5 hota hai. Yaani 8.5 CGPA ka matlab 8.5 × 9.5 = 80.75%.

Ye 9.5 kahan se aaya? CBSE ne kai saal ke topper marks ka ausat nikaalkar ye number tay kiya tha. Isi liye ye ek anuman hai, koi ganit ka niyam nahi — aur isi liye har jagah 9.5 nahi chalta.

Kuch aam multiplier:

KahanMultiplier8.5 CGPA =
CBSE (10th/12th)9.580.75%
Kai state board9.580.75%
Kai engineering university1085%
Kuch university (CGPA − 0.75) × 10alag formula77.5%

Farak dekhiye — ek hi 8.5 CGPA ka jawab 77.5% se 85% tak ja sakta hai. 7.5% ka antar. Job application aur higher studies me itna antar mayne rakhta hai.

Apna sahi multiplier kaise pata karein

Andaza lagane ki zaroorat nahi. Teen jagah se pakka jawab milta hai:

  • Marksheet ke peeche — zyadatar board aur university marksheet ke peeche ya neeche chhote akshar me formula chhaap dete hain. Sabse pehle wahin dekhiye.
  • University ki website — "CGPA to percentage conversion" ya "equivalence certificate" wale page par likha hota hai.
  • Exam section / registrar office — ye sabse pakka rasta hai, aur yahi jagah aapko likhit conversion certificate bhi de sakti hai.
Zaroori: agar aap job, scholarship ya kisi bade form ke liye percentage bhar rahe hain, to sirf calculator ka number likhna kaafi nahi hai. Apni university se conversion certificate le lijiye. Kai employer aur zyadatar sarkari bharti me yahi maanga jaata hai, aur baad me na ho to application ruk jaati hai.

Aur ek baat — kabhi apne CGPA ko upar wale multiplier me se jo sabse achha percentage de, wo chun mat lijiye. Form me galat percentage likhna baad me application radd karwa sakta hai, chahe galti anjaane me hui ho.

CGPA, SGPA aur percentage — teen alag cheezein

Log in teeno ko mila dete hain, par matlab alag hai:

  • SGPA — ek semester ka grade point ausat. Sirf usi semester ke subject ka.
  • CGPA — aaj tak ke saare semester ka jodkar nikala gaya ausat. Ye poori padhai ki tasveer hai.
  • Percentage — CGPA ko multiplier se guna karke banaya gaya number.

Isi liye "meri SGPA 9 hai" aur "meri CGPA 9 hai" do bilkul alag baatein hain. Form aksar CGPA maangta hai, aur agar galti se SGPA bhar diya (jo aam taur par zyada hota hai) to wo galat jaankari ban jaati hai.

Ek aur uljhan — grade aur grade point. A1 grade ka grade point 10 hota hai, A2 ka 9, aur aage isi tarah. CGPA in grade points ka ausat hai, grade ke akshar ka nahi.

Percentage ke saath aage kya karna hai

Percentage nikalne ke baad aksar agla sawaal aata hai — "isse kaam ban jayega?" Kuch aam sthitiyan:

  • Sarkari bharti — aksar percentage me minimum maanga jaata hai (jaise 50% ya 60%). Yahan conversion certificate lagbhag hamesha zaroori hota hai.
  • Private job — zyadatar jagah CGPA hi chal jaata hai, form me dono option hote hain.
  • Vidhesh me padhai — wahan aksar GPA (4-point scale) maanga jaata hai, jo Indian CGPA se bilkul alag cheez hai. Sirf 9.5 se guna karke wo number nahi banta — uske liye alag conversion hota hai.
Ek chhoti aadat jo bahut kaam aati hai — apna CGPA, percentage aur conversion formula ek jagah likh kar rakh lijiye. Form bharte waqt har baar marksheet dhoondhne ki zaroorat nahi padegi, aur galat number likhne ka khatra khatam.

Marks se seedha percentage nikaalna ho to Marks Percentage Calculator zyada seedha hai. Aur percentage ka poora ganit samajhna ho to percentage kaise nikale wala lekh dekh lijiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

CGPA ko percentage me badalne ka formula kya hai?

CGPA × multiplier. CBSE me multiplier 9.5 hota hai, yaani 8.5 CGPA = 80.75%. Kai university 10 ya apna alag formula istemaal karti hain — apni marksheet ke peeche dekhiye.

Sab jagah 9.5 se guna kar sakte hain?

Nahi. 9.5 CBSE ka formula hai. Kai engineering university 10 se guna karti hain aur kuch (CGPA − 0.75) × 10 istemaal karti hain. Ek hi CGPA ka jawab 77.5% se 85% tak badal sakta hai.

Job form ke liye calculator ka number likh sakte hain?

Aam samajhne ke liye theek hai, par sarkari bharti aur scholarship me apni university se conversion certificate lena zaroori hota hai. Bina uske application ruk sakti hai.

SGPA aur CGPA me kya farak hai?

SGPA ek semester ka ausat hai, CGPA aaj tak ke saare semester ka. Form aksar CGPA maangta hai — galti se SGPA bharna galat jaankari ban jaata hai.
