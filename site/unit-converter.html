Formula aasaan bhasha me

Har unit ko pehle ek base unit me badla jaata hai — lambai ke liye meter, wazan ke liye gram. Phir base se aapki chuni hui unit me. Isi wajah se koi bhi do unit aapas me badli ja sakti hain. Bharat me sona-chandi ke liye tola aaj bhi chalta hai — 1 tola lagbhag 11.66 gram hota hai.

Formula: Lambai: 1 m = 100 cm = 1000 mm, 1 km = 1000 m, 1 inch = 2.54 cm, 1 foot = 12 inch
Wazan: 1 kg = 1000 g, 1 quintal = 100 kg, 1 tola ≈ 11.66 g

Misaal se samajhiye

Misaal 1 — cm se km. 12,000 cm = 0.12 km. Kyunki 1 km = 1,00,000 cm.

Misaal 2 — foot se meter. 10 foot = 3.048 meter. Ghar ka naksha banate waqt kaam aata hai.

Misaal 3 — tola se gram. 2 tola sona = lagbhag 23.32 gram. Sarafa me aaj bhi tola me hi baat hoti hai.

Ye table yaad rakhne layak hai. Ghar banate waqt sabse zyada foot aur meter ka badalna padta hai, aur mandi me quintal aur kg ka. Videsh se saman mangwate waqt pound aur kg ka farak sabse zyada dhokha deta hai.

Ek chhoti si salah — naapte waqt hamesha ek hi unit me kaam karein aur aakhir me badlein. Beech-beech me badalte rehne se rounding ki galtiyan jud jaati hain aur bade kaam me ye farak dikhne lagta hai. Mistri ko naap dete waqt unit saaf likh kar dein.

Videshi recipe me sabse badi gadbad: cup wazan nahi, maatra naapta hai. 1 cup aata lagbhag 120 gram hai par 1 cup cheeni lagbhag 200 gram — kyunki dono ka ghanatv alag hai. Isliye "cup se gram" ka koi ek pakka number nahi hota, cheez ke hisaab se badalta hai. Baking me ye antar seedha natije par asar daalta hai. Sabse surakshit tarika: baking ke liye kitchen scale se gram me naapein, cup se nahi.

Aksar hone wali galtiyan

  1. Foot aur feet me uljhan. Dono ek hi hain — 1 foot, 2 feet. Ginti ka farak hai, unit ka nahi.
  2. Square unit ko seedha badalna. 1 meter = 3.28 foot, par 1 square meter = 10.76 square foot. Area me guna do baar lagta hai.
  3. Tola ko 10 gram maan lena. 1 tola lagbhag 11.66 gram hota hai — 10 nahi. Sone ke hisaab me ye farak bada ho jaata hai.
  4. Quintal aur ton mila dena. 1 quintal = 100 kg, 1 metric ton = 1000 kg.
  5. Purani unit ka anuman lagana. Gaz, bigha, nali jaisi unit jagah-jagah alag hoti hain — hamesha sthaneey maap confirm karein.

Bharat me ye baatein mayne rakhti hain

  • Sarafa: Sona-chandi tola aur gram dono me bikta hai. Bill me gram hi likha jaata hai.
  • Anaaj: Mandi me quintal chalta hai — 1 quintal = 100 kg.
  • Zameen: Bigha, biswa, nali aur mutthi rajya ke hisaab se alag hote hain. Uske liye area converter dekhein.
  • Construction: Naksha meter me banta hai par mistri foot me kaam karta hai — dono ka hisaab rakhein.
  • Doodh: Litre me bikta hai, par fat aur SNF par rate tay hota hai.
