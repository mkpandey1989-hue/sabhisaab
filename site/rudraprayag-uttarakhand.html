Apne Buzurgon ki Baatein Kaise Likhein (Oral History Guide)

Rudraprayag Uttarakhand ka Garhwal mandal ka woh zila hai jahan Alaknanda aur Mandakini nadiyon ka sangam (Prayag) hai. Yahin Kedarnath, Tungnath, Madhmaheshwar, Chopta, Deoria Tal, Ukhimath, Guptkashi aur Triyuginarayan jaise prasiddh sthal aate hain. Zila 16 September 1997 ko bana tha.

Namaskar! Yeh page Rudraprayag zile ke baare me sabse poori aur seedhi bhasha me likhi gayi guide hai. Chahe aap Kedarnath yatra ki planning kar rahe hon, Tungnath-Chandrashila trek ka plan bana rahe hon, Rudraprayag me zameen ya khatauni check karna chahte hon, apne purane gaon ke buzurgon ke shabd dhundh rahe hon, ya UKPSC / UKSSSC ki taiyari kar rahe hon, yahan aapko sab kuch ek jagah milega.

Is page ko Garhwal ke ek-ek gaon, ek-ek bugyal, ek-ek tyohar aur ek-ek purane shabd ko dhyan me rakh kar banaya gaya hai. Jahan aankde badalte rehte hain (jaise kapat kholne ki tithi, circle rate, ya fees), wahan hum aapko official portal ka rasta bhi batate hain taki aap hamesha taaza jankari le sakein.

Rudraprayag: aur aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

Log Google par aksar chhote aur seedhe sawal puchhte hain, jaise "Rudraprayag se Kedarnath kitna door hai" ya "Chopta me kya karein". Neeche aise 150 sawal aur unke jawab hain, jo is page ki jankari par aadharit hain. Jahan jankari samay ke saath badalti hai (taareekh, fees, kiraya), wahan jawab me hamne likha hai ki official strot dekhein.

A. Pehchan aur Bhugol

Rudraprayag kis rajya me hai?
Uttarakhand me, Garhwal mandal me.
Rudraprayag ka naam kaise pada?
Shiv ke Rudra roop ke naam par.
Rudraprayag me kaun si nadiyan milti hain?
Alaknanda aur Mandakini.
Rudraprayag Panch Prayag me kaun sa hai?
Panch Prayag me se ek, jo Vishnuprayag, Nandprayag, Karnaprayag, Rudraprayag aur Devprayag hain.
Rudraprayag zila kab bana?
16 September 1997 ko.
Rudraprayag zile ka mukhyalaya kahan hai?
Rudraprayag shehar me.
Rudraprayag ka kshetrafal kitna hai?
Census ke anusar lagbhag 1,984 varg km.
Rudraprayag ki aabadi kitni hai?
Census 2011 me 2,42,285.
Rudraprayag me kitni mahilayein hain prati 1,000 purush?
Census 2011 me 1,114.
Rudraprayag me kaun si boli-boli jati hai?
Mukhya roop se Garhwali.
Rudraprayag me kitne vikas khand hain?
Teen: Ukhimath, Augustmuni aur Jakholi.
Rudraprayag me vidhan sabha seat kitni hain?
Do: Kedarnath aur Rudraprayag.
Rudraprayag kaun si Lok Sabha seat me aata hai?
Garhwal Lok Sabha seat me.
Rudraprayag ka vahan code kya hai?
UK 13. 246171. 01364.
Rudraprayag shehar kitni unchai par hai?
Lagbhag 895 meter.
Rudraprayag me sabse unchi jagah kaun si hai?
Zile me Kedarnath kshetra ke aas-paas ke hiv-shikhar sabse unche hain.
Rudraprayag me koi airport hai kya?
Nahi. Sabse nazdeek bada airport Jolly Grant (Dehradun) hai.
Rudraprayag me railway station hai kya?
Abhi chalu station nahi hai. Nazdeek Rishikesh hai.

B. Yatra aur Pahunch

Rishikesh se Rudraprayag kitni door hai?
Lagbhag 140 km. Lagbhag 380 se 400 km.
Haridwar se Rudraprayag kitni door hai?
Lagbhag 165 km. Lagbhag 170 se 185 km.
Rudraprayag se Karnaprayag kitni door hai?
Lagbhag 30 km. Lagbhag 75 km.
Rudraprayag se Guptkashi kitni door hai?
Lagbhag 40 km. Lagbhag 85 se 90 km.
Delhi se Rudraprayag kaise jayein?
Delhi se Haridwar ya Rishikesh tak train ya bus, phir wahan se taxi ya bus. Hawai jahaz se Jolly Grant jaakar wahan se taxi le sakte hain.
Rishikesh se Rudraprayag kitne ghante lagte hain?
Aam taur par 5 se 6 ghante, mausam aur traffic par nirbhar.
Rudraprayag me bus aur taxi kahan se milti hain?
Rudraprayag shehar ke bus aur taxi stand se, jahan Kedarnath aur Badrinath marg alag hote hain.
Rudraprayag se Badrinath kaise jayein?
Alaknanda ghati ke raaste Karnaprayag aur Chamoli hote hue. Mandakini ghati ke raaste Guptkashi aur Sonprayag hote hue Gaurikund tak gaadi, aage paidal ya pony, palki, helicopter.
Raat me pahad ka safar karna chahiye kya?
Nahi. Pahadi raaste par andhere me safar jokhim bhara hota hai.
Barish me Rudraprayag ki yatra safe hai kya?
Barsat me bhu-skhalan ka khatra badh jata hai. Yatra se pehle mausam aur sadak ki advisory zaroor dekhein.
Rudraprayag ki yatra ke liye sabse achha samay kaun sa hai?
Aam taur par garmi ke shuruaati mahine aur barsat ke baad ka samay, par Kedarnath ke kapat khulne ke mausam ke hisab se tay karein.
Rudraprayag me petrol pump kahan milte hain?
Rudraprayag, Agastyamuni, Guptkashi jaise bade kasbon me. Door ilakon ke liye pehle se tanki bhar lein. Mukhya kasbon me. Door ke gaon me nakad saath rakhein.
Rudraprayag me network kaisa rehta hai?
Mukhya kasbon me achha, par ghati aur gaon me kamzor ho sakta hai. Backup SIM rakhna accha hai.
Rudraprayag ki yatra ke liye kya saath rakhein?
Garam kapde, barsati, zaroori dawa, torch, power bank aur pehchan patra.
Pahad me yatra ke liye sabse zaroori suraksha niyam kya hai?
Mausam ki jankari lena, andhere se pehle pahunchna aur sthaniya advisory maanna.

Ukhimath ke Omkareshwar mandir me.
Kedarnath ropeway kab banega?

Kedarnath ropeway ki lambai kitni hai?
12.9 km.
Kedarnath aapda kab hui thi?
15 se 17 June 2013 ke beech.
Kedarnath me altitude sickness ho sakta hai kya?
Haan, kuch logon me. Dheere chadhein, paani piyein aur lakshan dikhne par neeche utarein.
Kedarnath ke liye pony ya palki ke daam kaun tay karta hai?

Kedarnath helicopter booking kahan se karein?

Kedarnath yatra me buzurg kya savdhani rakhein?
Yatra se pehle doctor se jaanch karwayein aur dheere chalein.
