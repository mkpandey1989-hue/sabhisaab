Pithoragarh: UKPSC, PCS aur UKSSSC ke liye 100 Ek-Line GK Tathya

Pithoragarh ke 100+ Tathya

Ye one-liner Patwari, Lekhpal, Police Constable, SI, Forest Guard, VDO/VPDO aur Lower/Upper PCS jaise exam ki taiyari ke liye chhote, seedhe tathya hain.

A. Zila ki Pehchan aur Prashasan

  • Pithoragarh zila 24 February 1960 ko bana.
  • Ye zila Almora zile se alag karke banaya gaya.
  • Zile ka mukhyalay Pithoragarh shehar hai.
  • Pithoragarh Kumaon Mandal ka zila hai.
  • Pithoragarh Uttarakhand ka sabse purvi zila hai.
  • Zile ka kshetrafal lagbhag 7,090 vargkm hai (Census).
  • Kshetrafal me Pithoragarh Kumaon Mandal ka sabse bada zila mana jata hai.
  • Census 2011 me jansankhya 4,83,439 thi.
  • Census 2011 me purush 2,39,306 the.
  • Census 2011 me mahilayein 2,44,133 thi.
  • Ling anupat lagbhag 1,020 mahilayein per 1,000 purush tha.
  • Saksharta dar 82.25% thi (Census 2011).
  • Janghanatva lagbhag 68 vyakti prati vargkm tha.
  • Champawat zila 15 September 1997 ko Pithoragarh se alag hokar bana.
  • Uttarakhand rajya 9 November 2000 ko bana.
  • Pithoragarh ka vahan code UK-05 hai.
  • Pithoragarh shehar ka PIN code 262501 hai.
  • Pithoragarh ka STD code 05964 hai.
  • Census 2011 ke samay zile me 6 tehsil the: Pithoragarh, Munsiari, Dharchula, Didihat, Gangolihat, Berinag.
  • Kshetrafal me sabse bada tehsil Munsiari hai.
  • Abadi me sabse chhota tehsil Munsiari hai.
  • Zile me aam taur par 8 vikas khand bataye jate hain.
  • Zile me koi Cantonment Board nahi hai.

Pithoragarh: Is Zile se Pichhle Saalon ke Sawal (PYQ)

PYQ ki Imandaar Sthiti

Nakli ya yaaddasht se likha PYQ galat chhap jaye to student ko exam me nuksan hota hai.

Sahi PYQ kahan milenge:

  • UKPSC aur UKSSSC ki official website par purane question paper aur answer key.
  • Recognised coaching ya pustakon ke “Uttarakhand GK PYQ” sangrah (answer key official se mila kar).
  • Library me exam-wise purane paper ke bound volume.

Pithoragarh se kis tarah ke sawal aam taur par poochhe jate hain (pattern, PYQ nahi):

  • Zila ka gathan aur sthapna.
  • Nadiyan aur sangam.
  • Himalayi chotiyan aur darre.
  • Sanrakshit kshetra aur rajya ke pratik.
  • Janjatiyan (Shauka, Raji).
  • Mele, tyohar aur nritya.
  • Mahan vyaktitva.
  • Hastshilp aur khan-paan.

First in Pithoragarh (Pratham Vyaktitva aur Sthan)

“Pratham” ki Imandaar Sthiti

Exam ya website par galat “pratham” likhna sabse aam aur sabse nuksan dene wali galtiyon me se hai.

Kahan se pushti karein:

  • Zila Sankhyiki Patrika aur District Gazetteer.
  • Zila Abhilekhagar (records office) aur Kumaon University ke itihas vibhag ki shodh.
  • Chunav Aayog ke purane chunav record (pehle vidhayak aur sansad ke liye).
  • Sthaniya itihaskar jaise Shekhar Pathak ki kitaabein (Pithoragarh: Is Zile par Likhi Pramukh Kitabein aur Shodh).

Exam ke liye: UKPSC ki taiyari me rajya ka itihas, sanskriti aur bhugol alag se padhna padta hai; zile-vishesh kitaab sahayak ke roop me upyogi hain.

Pithoragarh mein Coaching, Pustakalay aur Reading Room

Taiyari ki Jagah

Pithoragarh shehar me exam ki taiyari ke liye kuch library aur reading room ho sakte hain. Kisi coaching ka naam likhna vigyapan jaisa lagta hai aur galat hone par students ko dhoka ho sakta hai.

Kaise dhoondhein (surakshit tareeka):

  • Zila pustakalaya ya sarkari library: Zila Soochna Adhikari ya Zila Shiksha karyalay se pooch lein.
  • Mahavidyalaya ki library: Pithoragarh ke sarkari degree college ki library me aksar students ko sadasyata mil jati hai (college se).
  • Pehle ek hafte ki demo class lekar tay karein, aur fees ek saath bahut zyada na dein.
  • Online taiyari: UKPSC aur UKSSSC ki official site, free YouTube lectures aur sarkari portal par purane papers se bhi achhi taiyari ho sakti hai.

Pithoragarh: Fauj Bharti (Agniveer ARO) ke Maidan aur Physical Training

Fauj ki Bharti aur Taiyari

Pithoragarh ke bahut se yuvaon ka sapna fauj ya ardhsainik bal me jaana hai, aur ab fauj me bharti Agnipath (Agniveer) yojna ke tahat hoti hai. (Bharti ki shartein, sharirik maapdand, umr, shiksha aur rally ke sthaan har saal badalte hain; official site joinindianarmy.nic.in aur Army Recruiting Office (ARO) ki notification se pushti karein.)

ARO kahan hai: Pithoragarh zile ke yuvaon ke liye aam taur par Almora ka ARO bataya jata hai, par exact zimmedari ARO ki notification se pakki karein.

Sharirik taiyari ke aam tips (sirf fitness ki salah):

  • Dheere-dheere daud badhayein: Roz thodi daud se shuru karke dheere distance aur speed badhayein.
  • Pahadi unchai ka fayda: Chadhai wali sadak par daud aam taur par stamina badhati hai, par ghutne aur pairon ka dhyan rakhein.
  • Sahi khana, neend aur paani.
  • Chot lage to doctor se milein, aur dard dab kar daud na lagayein.
  • Doctor ki jaanch: Taiyari shuru karne se pehle kisi doctor se apni fitness jaanch karwa lena achha hai.
  • Agent aur “seat pakki” ke daave se saavdhan: Fauj ki bharti me koi paise lekar naukri nahi dilata. Aisi koi baat ho to police ko batayein.

Pithoragarh: Polytechnic, ITI, Nursing College aur Kaushal Vikas Kendra

Takneeki Shiksha

Zile me sarkari aur nijee ITI, polytechnic aur nursing ke kuch sansthan ho sakte hain. (Sansthan ki manyata, seat aur dakhile ki prakriya official source se pushti karein; ITI ke liye NCVT, polytechnic ke liye AICTE aur nursing ke liye Indian Nursing Council ki manyata dekhein.)

Dakhile ke aam steps:

  • Official portal: Uttarakhand Prashikshan evam Sewayojan Vibhag ya Takniki Shiksha Parishad ki website par dakhile ki taarikh dekhein.
  • Zaroori dastavez: Marksheet, jati aur nivas praman patra, photo, Aadhaar.
  • Counselling ya merit list: Sansthan ya vibhag ki list ke anusar.
  • Seat aur fees: Sansthan se likhit roop me puchh lein.

Nijee sansthan se saavdhan: Dakhile se pehle uski manyata (AICTE, NCVT, INC) aur placement ke daave ko official site par check karein.

Pithoragarh: Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya (JNV), Kendriya Vidyalaya (KV) & Rajiv Gandhi Navodaya

Sarkari Awasiya aur Din ke School

Pithoragarh zile me Kendriya Vidyalaya aur Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya jaise sarkari school hone ki jaankari hai. (KV ki sankhya aur JNV ka sthaan ke liye KVS aur Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti ki official website se pushti karein.)

Navodaya Vidyalaya me dakhila (aam jaankari): JNV me dakhila kaksha 6 ke liye ek chayan pariksha (JNVST) se hota hai, jo gramin kshetra ke bachchon ke liye avsar deti hai. Aavedan ki tithi, umr seema aur prakriya Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti ki official website par dekhein.

Anya sarkari school: Rajya sarkar ke Atal Utkrisht Rajkiya Vidyalaya jaise adarsh school bhi kuch kasbon me hain. Kisi school me bachche ka naam likhwane se pehle uski manyata aur suvidha ki jaankari ek baar khud dekh lein.

Pithoragarh: Chhatron ke liye Chhatravritti, Hostel aur Coaching Yojanayein

Chhatravritti aur Sahayata

Pithoragarh ke vidyarthi kendra aur rajya sarkar ki kai chhatravritti aur sahayata yojnaon ke liye aavedan kar sakte hain, jaise National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in), Samaj Kalyan Vibhag ki SC/ST/OBC chhatravritti aur Mukhyamantri Abhyudaya jaisi coaching yojna. (Yojna ki sthiti, patrata, aay seema aur taarikh har saal badalti hain; official portal se pushti karein.)

Aavedan ke tips: Aadhaar, bank khata (Aadhaar se linked), jati aur aay praman patra, nivas praman patra aur marksheet taiyar rakhein, aur aakhri taarikh se pehle aavedan karein. Kisi agent ko chhatravritti dilwane ke naam par paise na dein.

Pithoragarh: Rojgar Karyalay aur Rojgar Mele

Rozgar Karyalay aur Sewayojan Portal

Pithoragarh me zila sewayojan (rozgar) karyalay hai, jahan berozgar yuva panjikaran kara sakte hain, aur Uttarakhand ka Sewayojan portal bhi aavedan ke liye upalabdh hai. (Karyalay ka pata, samay aur portal ka address Uttarakhand Prashikshan evam Sewayojan Vibhag se pushti karein.)

Aam taur par steps:

  • Online panjikaran: Sewayojan portal par apni shaikshanik yogyata, Aadhaar aur mobile se khaata banayein.
  • Dastavez: Marksheet, jati aur nivas praman patra, photo.
  • Naukri ki suchna: Rozgar mela, nijee company ke saath career mela aur sarkari bharti ki soochna portal aur karyalay se milti hai.
  • Dhyan dein: Rozgar mela me bhi kisi ko naukri ke naam par paise na dein; asli bharti me aam taur par shulk nahi liya jata.
