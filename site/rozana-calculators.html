Percentage — teen alag sawaal, teen alag tarike

"Percentage" ek shabd hai par uske peeche teen alag sawaal chhupe hote hain, aur log inhe aapas me mila dete hain:

  • 500 ka 20% kitna hai? → 500 × 20 ÷ 100 = 100
  • 387, 500 ka kitna percent hai? → 387 ÷ 500 × 100 = 77.4%
  • 400 se 500 hua, kitna percent badha? → (500 − 400) ÷ 400 × 100 = 25%

Teesre me sabse zyada galti hoti hai. Log 100 ko 500 se bhaag de dete hain aur 20% nikal aata hai — jo galat hai. Badhotri hamesha purani rakam par nikalti hai, nayi par nahi. Poora tarika yahan examples ke saath hai.

Discount ka jaal

"50% + extra 20% off" ka matlab 70% off nahi hota. Doosra discount pehle wale ke baad bachi keemat par lagta hai.

₹1,000 ki cheez: pehle 50% off → ₹500. Ab uspar 20% off → ₹400. Yaani kul chhoot 60%, 70% nahi. Sale ke season me ye ek chhota hisaab kaafi paisa bachaata hai. Discount Calculator ye apne aap kar deta hai.

Ek aur cheez dekhne layak: "MRP ₹2,000, ab ₹799" jaise offer me MRP aksar badha kar likha hota hai. Chhoot ka percentage nahi, asli chukane wali keemat dekhiye aur usi ko doosri jagah se milaayiye.

Marks, CGPA aur bill

Bachche ke marks ka percentage seedha hai — kul mile hue ank ÷ kul ank × 100. Par CGPA alag hai. Kai board aur university CGPA ko percentage me badalne ke liye apna alag formula rakhte hain (kai jagah CGPA × 9.5 chalta hai). Aakhri number hamesha apne board ya university ke niyam se hi milaayein — kisi general calculator ka number form bharte waqt nahi chalega.

Bill baantne me sabse aam jhagda tip aur GST ka hota hai. Saaf tarika ye hai: pehle poora bill (GST aur tip milakar) jodiye, phir logon ki sankhya se baantiye. Agar kisi ne kuch mehnga alag mangwaya hai to use pehle alag kar dijiye, phir baaki baantiye.
