Rudraprayag: Aabadi, Samuh aur Janjatiyan

Census ke Aankde

Census 2011 ke anusar Rudraprayag zile ki aabadi 2,42,285 thi. Census 2011 ke anusar saksharta 81.30% thi aur mahilaon ka anupat purushon se zyada maana jata hai, kyunki bahut se purush rozgar ke liye bahar jate hain.

Zile ki lagbhag 20% aabadi Anusuchit Jati (SC) aur 0.16% Anusuchit Janjati (ST) maani gayi hai (Census based sources). Gaon ki zyadatar aabadi gramin hai aur shehri aabadi bahut kam hai. Pahadi zilon me mahila-purush anupat aksar zyada isliye hota hai kyunki purush sena, hotel aur doosre shehron me naukri karte hain.

Rudraprayag ki aabadi kitni hai?
2,42,285 (Census 2011).
Rudraprayag me kitne log padhe-likhe hain?
Lagbhag 81% (Census 2011 based, aas-paas ka aankda).

Pramukh Samuh

Rudraprayag ki aabadi mukhya roop se Garhwali bhashi Brahmin, Rajput aur Shilpkar samudayon se bani hai. Zile me Bhotia ya Jaunsari jaisi badi janjatiyan nahi hain.

Bhotia, Jaunsari aur Buksa janjatiyon ka adhik prabhav Chamoli, Uttarkashi aur Dehradun (Jaunsar-Bawar) jaise doosre zilon me hai. Isliye Rudraprayag ke bare me inka zikr "padosi kshetra ke saath" hi dena chahiye.

Rudraprayag: Samaj, Upnaam aur Gotra

Pramukh Upnaam

Rudraprayag aur Garhwal me aam upnaam Rawat, Negi, Bisht, Bhandari, Kandari, Panwar, Rana, Chauhan, Nautiyal, Semwal, Bhatt, Dimri, Thapliyal, Uniyal, Gairola, Maithani aur Joshi hain.

Brahmin samudaay me Nautiyal, Semwal, Dimri, Thapliyal, Uniyal, Gairola, Bhatt, Maithani jaise upnaam aam hain. Rajput samudaay me Rawat, Negi, Bisht, Bhandari, Kandari aur Panwar milte hain. Shilpkar samuday me Arya, Tamta, Das jaise upnaam hote hain. Upnaam se gotra aur gaon ki pehchan jodi jati hai.

Mool Niwas (Thaat)

Pahadi parivar apne mool gaon ko "Thaat" ya "Mool Niwas" kehte hain, bhale hi wo aaj Delhi ya Dehradun me rehte hon. Shadi, kul-devta ki puja aur zameen ke record me mool gaon ka naam bahut maayne rakhta hai.

Rudraprayag: Bhasha, Boli aur Rozmarra ke Shabd

Rudraprayag me Boli

Rudraprayag zile me Garhwali bhasha boli jati hai. Garhwali ki kai upbolian hain, aur Rudraprayag-Chamoli ke kshetra me Srinagariya aur Nagpuriya boli ka prabhav milta hai.

Garhwali Devanagari me likhi jati hai aur iska apna sahitya hai. Rudraprayag zile me neeche ke gaon aur upar ke gaon ki boli me bhi halka antar hota hai.

Rudraprayag: Pahadi Ghar aur Vaastu

Pathal ki Chhat aur Lakdi ki Nakkashi

Purane Garhwali ghar patthar aur lakdi se bante the. Chhat par slate ke patthar (pathal) bichhaye jate the. Pahadi ghar bhukamp se bachne ke liye lachila dhancha rakhte the.

Pahadi ghar me neeche ki manzil "goth" ya "obra" kehlati hai jahan gaay-bhains bandhi jati thi. Pashu ki garmi se upar ki manzil sardi me garam rehti thi. Upar ki manzil me rehne ke kamre aur rasoi hoti thi.

Tibari, Chhaja, Kholi aur Khalian

Tibari lakdi ka bana teen mehrabon wala sundar baramda hota hai. Kholi mukhya dwar ka lakdi ka chaukhat aur Khalian ghar ke aage patthar se paata hua aangan hai.

Rudraprayag aur Chamoli ke kuch gaon me aaj bhi purani tibari wale ghar milte hain, jo ab "heritage homestay" ke roop me ban rahe hain.

Rudraprayag: Paramparik Pehnawa

Mahilaon ka Pehnawa

Garhwali mahilayein Ghagri, Angra, Pichhoda aur Gulaband jaise gahne pehenti hain. Shadi me rang-birange pichhoda ki bhi parampara hai.

Pichhoda (Kumaon me Pichora) lal-peele rang ka dupatta hota hai. Garhwali shaadi me bhi gulabandh, nathuli aur hansuli jaise gehne pehne jate the.

Purushon ka Pehnawa

Purush kurta-pajama, mirzai (jacket), kaali Pahadi topi aur sardi me oon ka kot pehente hain. Topi pahad ki pehchan hai.

Rudraprayag: Pahadi Gahne

Nathuli, Gulaband aur Hansuli

Nathuli (bahut badi nath), Gulaband (gale ka lal gahna) aur Hansuli (chaandi ka mota haar) Garhwali shaadi ki pehchan hain. Yeh gahne ab naye roop me fashion me bhi aa rahe hain.

Garhwali Nath Uttarakhand ki shaadi ka sabse prasiddh gahna hai. Purane gahne bhari hote the aur parivar me peedhi dar peedhi chalte the.

Rudraprayag: Kala aur Lok-Chitra

Aipan aur Aipan

Aipan Kumaon ki prasiddh lok-kala hai, jo geru (lal mitti) aur biswar (chawal ka ghol) se banti hai. Garhwal me bhi ghar ki chaukhat par aur tyohar par rangoli jaise likhai/chitra banaye jate hain.

Rudraprayag zile me Aipan Kumaon jitna prachlit nahi hai, par tyohar par chowk aur rangoli banane ki parampara hai.

Rudraprayag: Lok-Geet aur Sangeet

Jagar, Pawada, Khuded aur Mangal Geet

Jagar devta ko jagane ka geet hai. Pawada veeron ki gaathayein hain. Khuded virah ke geet hain. Mangal geet shadi me gaye jate hain. Chaiti aur Basanti basant ke geet hain.

Garhwal ka lok-sangeet dhol-damau, hurka (damaru jaisa vadya) aur ransingha jaise vadyon ke saath gaya jata hai.

Rudraprayag: Lok-Nritya

Pandav Nritya, Jhumelo, Thadya aur Chaufula

Pandav Nritya Mahabharat ki kahani par adharit ek sanskritik anushthan hai jo Garhwal (khaaskar Rudraprayag aur Chamoli) me bahut prachlit hai. Jhumelo, Thadya aur Chaufula bhi lokpriya lok-nritya hain.

Pandav Nritya Garhwal me kai gaon me har saal ek khaas samay par hota hai. Isme gaon ke log pandavon ke patra bankar naachte hain. Yeh Kedarnath se judi manyata se bhi jodkar dekha jata hai.

Rudraprayag: Lok-Vadya

Dhol, Damau aur Ransingha

Dhol aur Damau Garhwal ke sabse mukhya vadya hain. Inhe bajane wale ko Auji ya Bajgi kehte hain. Ransingha tambe ka bada S-aakar ka sinha jaisa vadya hai jo devta ke yatra me bajta hai.

Dhol Sagar ek purani mukhik parampara hai jisme dhol ke taalon ka gyan hai. Yeh taal shubh avsaron aur devta ko bulane me alag-alag bajte hain. Hurka, Bhankora, Masakbeen aur Thali bhi pramukh vadya hain.

Rudraprayag: Sanskar aur Vivah

Janm se Antim Sanskar

Pahadi parivar me namkaran, annaprashan, janeu aur vivah jaise sanskaron ko dhol-damau ke saath manaya jata hai. Shadi me mangal geet aur dhuliyargh (barat ke aagman par) jaisi rasmein hoti hain.

Garhwali vivah me haldi, mangal snan, barat aur kanyadan jaisi rasmein hoti hain. Is par kul-devta aur ishta devta ki puja pehle ki jati hai.

Rudraprayag: Mausami Tyohar

Phool Dei, Harela, Bagwal aur Egaas

Phool Dei (Chaitra Sankranti par bachche dehri par phool rakhte hain), Harela (barsat ke mausam me hariyali ka tyohar), Egaas (Budhi Diwali) aur Makar Sankranti Garhwal-Kumaon ke pramukh tyohar hain.

Phool Dei, Harela aur Ghughutiya mukhya roop se Kumaon me adhik prachlit hain, par Garhwal me bhi inhe manane ka ek naya rujhan hai. Egaas (Egas-Bagwal) Garhwal ka khaas tyohar hai jo Diwali ke 11 din baad manaya jata hai.

Rudraprayag: Mele, Jatra aur Doli Yatra

Pramukh Mele

Rudraprayag zile me Agastyamuni ka Baisakhi mela, Triyuginarayan ka Baman Dwadashi mela, Kedarnath ki Doli yatra aur Madhmaheshwar ke kapat band hone ki doli yatra bahut prasiddh hain.

Kedarnath ke kapat har saal sardi me band hote hain aur Baba ki utsav doli Ukhimath ke Omkareshwar mandir me le jayi jati hai. Doli yatra ke samay gaon-gaon me bhakt swagat karte hain. Kapat kholne aur band karne ki tithi har saal alag hoti hai (Mahashivratri ke din Ukhimath me tithi ghoshit ki jati hai), isliye taaza tithi ke liye Badrinath-Kedarnath Temple Committee (BKTC) ki website dekhein.

Kedarnath ki doli sardi me kahan rehti hai?
Ukhimath ke Omkareshwar mandir me.
Kedarnath ke kapat kab khulte hain?
Har saal alag tithi par, aam taur par April-May me. Tithi Mahashivratri par Ukhimath me ghoshit hoti hai.

Rudraprayag: Vistaar se: Sanskriti, Mele aur Doli Yatra ki Gehri Jankari

Pahad ki sanskriti sirf geet aur nritya nahi, balki ek poora samajik jeevan hai. Mele, doli yatra, shaadi aur tyohar gaon ko ek saath jodte hain. Is hisse me hamne zile ke sabse mahatvapurn mele aur doli yatraon ke bare me pakki jankari likhi hai, aur samaj ke bare me kuch zaroori baatein bhi, jinhe samman ke saath samajhna chahiye.

48 se 49: Samaj aur Upnaam (Vistaar)

Upnaam, Jati aur Samman

Upnaam kisi ka mool niwas ya parivar ki pehchan batate hain, par upnaam se kisi ki yogyata, kaam ya samman tay nahi hota. Bharat ke Samvidhan ke Anuchhed 17 ke tahat chhua-chhut khatm kar di gayi hai aur uska paalan karna ya badhawa dena kanooni apradh hai.

Rudraprayag aur Garhwal ke gaon me alag-alag samudaay ek saath rehte aaye hain. Pahad me kheti, mele aur tyohar ek saath milkar manane ki parampara rahi hai. Purani samajik rudhiyon me se kuch ab bhi kai jagah dikhti hain, jaise mandir me pravesh ya sahbhoj me bhed. Yeh na sirf anuchit hai, balki kanoon ke khilaf bhi hai.

Is page ka maqsad kisi samudaay ko unch ya neech dikhana nahi hai. Hum sirf yeh batate hain ki zile me kaun si boliyan, parampara aur upnaam milte hain. Agar aapko kisi bhi roop me bhed-bhav ka saamna karna pade, to najdiki police thana, Samaj Kalyan Vibhag ya zila prashasan se sampark karein. Anusuchit jaati aur janjaati ke saath atyachar ke maamle me alag se kanoon hai, jiske tahat shikayat darj ki ja sakti hai.

55 se 57: Lok-Geet, Nritya aur Vadya (Vistaar)

Pandav Leela aur Pandav Nritya

Zila prashasan ki website ke anusar Rudraprayag kshetra me Pandavon ki yaad me aaj bhi Pandav Leela manayi jati hai. Yeh Garhwal ki ek mahatvapurn lok-parampara hai, jisme Mahabharat ki kathaon ko nritya, geet aur vadya ke saath dikhaya jata hai.

Pandav Nritya mukhya roop se sardi ke mahine me, jab kheti ka kaam kam hota hai, gaon me aayojit kiya jata hai. Isme gaon ke log pandavon ke paatra nibhate hain aur Dhol-Damau ke saath nritya karte hain. Kai gaon me yeh aayojan kai din tak chalta hai. Is parampara ko Kedarnath se judi kathaon se bhi joda jata hai, kyunki manyata hai ki Pandav yahan shiv ki khoj me aaye the.

Iska seedha aur sahi roop gaon ke jaankar logon se hi seekha ja sakta hai.

Mele, Jatra aur Doli Yatra (Vistaar)

Zile ke Pramukh Mele

Zila prashasan ke anusar Rudraprayag ke pramukh melon me Hariyali Devi Mela, Madhmaheshwar Mela (Ukhimath) aur Triyuginarayan Mela shamil hain. Kartik Purnima par bhi Rudraprayag me mela lagta hai. Har mela kisi mandir, doli yatra ya mausam se juda hai.

Madhmaheshwar ki Doli Yatra

Madhmaheshwar Panch Kedar me dusra kedar maana jata hai. Sardiyon me barf ke karan yahan ka raasta chhe mahine band rehta hai, aur utsav murti Ukhimath ke Omkareshwar mandir me virajti hai. Garmiyon me, aam taur par April ya May me, doli Ukhimath se Madhmaheshwar ki taraf jati hai aur is avsar par mela lagta hai.

Is yatra me gaon ke log doli ke saath paidal chalte hain. Raaste me kai padav aate hain. Madhmaheshwar ka trek Ransi gaon ke raaste aam taur par 16 se 18 km ka bataya jata hai. Ransi me Rakeshwari Devi ka mandir hai, jinhe kai log Madhmaheshwar ki sangini maante hain.

Doli yatra ke samay mandir aur raaste me yeh dhyan rakhein:

  • Doli ke saath chalne wale logon ke raaste me rukawat na daalein.
  • Shor aur drone ke upyog se bachein. Yeh dharmik avsar hai.
  • Mandir aur doli ke saamne photo lene se pehle sthaniya logon se poochhein.
  • Raaste me kachra na failayein.

Kedarnath aur Tungnath ki Doli

Kedarnath ki utsav doli Ukhimath se Kedarnath tak jati hai aur kapat kholne ki taareekh Mahashivratri par Ukhimath me ghoshit hoti hai (Rudraprayag: Deshi Mahine aur Pahadi Panchang). Tungnath ki sheetkaleen gaddi Makkumath me hoti hai (Rudraprayag: Pavitra Kund, Bugyal aur Choti ka Jod). Kapat kholne aur band karne ki taareekhein har saal badalti hain, isliye yatra se pehle Badrinath-Kedarnath Mandir Samiti ki official jankari dekh lein.

Kartik Swami aur Hariyali Devi

Kartik Swami mandir tak jaane ke liye Rudraprayag se Pokhri marg par lagbhag 38 km door Kanakchauri gaon tak gaadi jaati hai, jahan se lagbhag 3 km ka paidal raasta mandir tak jata hai. Hariyali Devi ka mandir Rudraprayag aur Karnaprayag ke beech ke marg ke paas sthit hai, aur yahan Hariyali Devi Mela lagta hai. Dono jagah jaane se pehle mausam aur mele ki taareekh jaan lein.

Sanskar aur Vivah (Vistaar)

Garhwali shaadi me aam taur par kai din ki rasmein hoti hain: tilak ya sagai, haldi, barat ka aana, kanyadaan aur phere, aur phir bidaai. Dhol-Damau ki dhun har rasam ka hissa hoti hai. Ghati aur parivar ke hisab se vidhi aur naam alag ho sakte hain.

Mausami Tyohar (Vistaar)

Tyoharon ka Jud-ao Kheti se

Pahadi tyohar kheti ke mausam se jude hain. Phooldei (Chait Sankranti) basant ka swagat karta hai, Harela (Saun Sankranti) paudhe lagane ka tyohar hai, aur Makar Sankranti sardi ke ant ka sanket maani jati hai. Rudraprayag ke melon me bhi yahi mausam dikhta hai, jaise garmi me doli yatra aur Bhadrapad me Triyuginarayan mela. Tyoharon ka poora calendar Rudraprayag: Deshi Mahine aur Pahadi Panchang me hai.

Is sab ka saar yeh hai ki pahad ki sanskriti prakriti, kheti aur aastha ka ek jeevit sangam hai. Isse jeevit rakhne ka sabse accha tarika hai ki naye bachche in melon aur tyoharon me hissa lein aur buzurgon se unki kahaniyan sunein.
