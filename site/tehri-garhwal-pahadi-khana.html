Tehri Garhwal ke Mashhoor Mukhya Vyanjan

Seedha jawab: Garhwal ke (aur Tehri ke) mashhoor main-course me kafuli, phaanu, chainsoo, baadi, thechwani, gahat ki dal aur jholi (kadhi) aate hain. Ye zyadatar loha ki kadhai me, kam masale aur sthaniya anaj-dal se pakaye jate hain.

Kafuli par ek chhoti recipe:

  • Palak aur methi ko dho kar kaat lein.
  • Loha ki kadhai me tel garam karke jakhiya ya jeera ka tadka dein.
  • Pisa hua saag daalkar halki aanch par pakayein.
  • Chawal ke aate ya dal ke paani se gada karein.
  • Garam bhaat ke saath parosein.

Ye kyu khaas hai: Loha ki kadhai me pakne se khane me iron ki matra badhti hai aur swad alag aata hai.

Tehri Garhwal: Paramparik Roti aur Chawal ke Pakwan

Seedha jawab: Mandua ki roti aur jhangore ka bhaat Garhwal ke sabse prasiddh roti-chawal hain. Tyoharon par gahat ke parathe aur swale (bhari hui puri) bhi bante hain.

Mandua ki Roti, Gahat ke Parathe, Jhangora ka Bhaat & Gahat Ras-Bhaat

  • Mandua ki roti: Mandua ke aate ki kaali, moti roti. Sardiyo me garm rehne ke liye khayi jati hai. Sath me ghee ya ghar ka makhan aur gur ya chutney.
  • Gahat ke parathe / bhari roti: Gehun ke aate me gahat ki dal ki pithi bharkar banaye gaye parathe.
  • Jhangora ka bhaat: Chawal ki jagah jhangora ko pakakar khaya jata hai. Isme gluten nahi hota, isliye aaj kal shehri bazar me isse "healthy" bata kar bechte hain.
  • Swale (Swala): Dal ki pithi bharkar tali hui puri, tyoharon aur shaadi me.
  • Rot (Roat): Aate, gud aur ghee se bana meetha mota roth, devta ki puja me chadhaya jata hai.

Tehri Garhwal: Chutney, Raita, Achaar aur Pisyun Loon

Seedha jawab: Pahadi swad ka asli raaz uski chutney aur pisyun loon (sil-batte par pisa masaledar namak) me hai. Bhangjeera ki chutney, bhang ke beej ki chutney, pahadi kheere ka raita aur timur ki chutney sabse prasiddh hain.

Bhangjeera Chutney, Hemp Seed Chutney, Pahadi Kheere ka Raita, Darim Chutney & Timur Chutney

Savdhani (bhang ki chutney par): Bhang (hemp) ke beej khane ke liye hote hain, lekin kuch jagah iske patte nashe ke roop me bhi prayog hote hain, jo kanoon ke tahat niyantrit hai.

Pisyun Loon (Haath se Pisa Masala Namak)

  • Kya hai: Sil-batte par lahsun ke patte, hari mirch, dhaniya, jeera aur timur ke saath pisa hua namak.
  • Kaise khate hain: Roti, mandua ya taaze fal (jaise malta, kheera) ke saath.

Tehri Garhwal: Jungli Khane Layak Cheezein aur Mausami Saag

Seedha jawab: Pahad ke jungle ki khaane layak cheeze: kandali (bicchu ghas / bichhu booti, nettle) ka saag, linguda (fiddlehead fern), kafal, hisalu, kilmora, ghingaru, bedu (Himalayan fig) aur buransh ka sharbat. Inme se kai mausmi hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Paramparik Mithai, Tyohar ke Pakwan aur Prasad

Seedha jawab: Garhwali meethe me arsa (chawal-gud ka tala hua gola), rot (aate-gud ki meethi roti), jhangora ki kheer, puwa aur gulgule shamil hain. Ye zyadatar tyoharon, shaadi aur devta ki puja me bante hain.

Singodi / Singori jaisi mithai Kumaon ka mashhoor meetha hai, jo maalu ke patte me lipti hoti hai. Garhwal me iska prachalan sthaniya swaad ke hisab se alag-alag hai.

Tehri Garhwal: Paramparik Peya aur Khameer Wale Pey

Seedha jawab: Pahadi paya me buransh ka sharbat, malta ka juice aur gud-adrak ki chai sabse prasiddh hain. Chhyang jaisi anaj ki paramparik fermented drink mukhya roop se oonchai ke Bhotia ilako (Uttarkashi, Chamoli, Pithoragarh) me banti hai, na ki Tehri me.

Buransh Squash, Malta Juice & Gud-Adrak ki Chai

  • Buransh ka sharbat: Buransh ke phoolo se bana laal sharbat. Garmiyo me thanda aur swasth peya.
  • Malta ka juice: Sardiyo ke mausam me mil jata hai.
  • Gud-adrak ki chai: Sardi me gud, adrak aur chai ke patte ki garam chai.
  • Chhachh (matha) aur lassi: Dahi se bana thanda pey, garmi me.
  • Sharab ke bare me: Kuch ilako me ghar ki bani paramparik sharab (jaise Jaunsari kshetra me) apni sanskriti ka hissa hai, par ise promote karna ya recipe dena is page ka vishay nahi hai.

Tehri Garhwal: Pahadi Kitchen Architecture & Utensils

Seedha jawab: Pahadi rasoi ke mukhya bartan hain loha ki kadhai, silbatta (pisne ka pathar), kansi ki thali, bhaddu (bhari pital ka bartan) aur mitti ka chulha. Chulhe ke upar ka mancha (dhumala) dhuye se lakdi aur beej sukhane ke kaam aata tha.

Ek lok-parampara ki baat: Pahadi ghar me chulhe ki aag ko kabhi bujhne nahi diya jata tha, aur aag mangne padosi ke ghar jana ek aam baat thi (Tehri Garhwal: Bina Maachis aur Bijli ke Aag aur Roshni ka Jugaad me vistar).

Tehri Garhwal: Pahadi khana aur mithai: Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Garhwal ke mashhoor main-course vyanjan kaun se hain?
Kafuli, phaanu, chainsoo, baadi, thechwani, gahat ki dal aur jholi (kadhi), jo zyadatar loha ki kadhai mein kam masale se bante hain.
Garhwal ki mukhya roti-chawal kaun si hain?
Mandua ki roti aur jhangore ka bhaat; tyoharon par gahat ke parathe aur swale bhi bante hain.
Garhwali meethe pakwan kaun se hain?
Arsa, rot, jhangora ki kheer, puwa aur gulgule, jo tyohar, shaadi aur devta ki puja mein bante hain.
Kya Chhyang Tehri ka paramparik peya hai?
Nahi. Chhyang mukhya roop se oonchai ke Bhotia ilakon (Uttarkashi, Chamoli, Pithoragarh) mein banta hai; Tehri mein buransh ka sharbat, malta juice aur gud-adrak ki chai prasiddh hain.
