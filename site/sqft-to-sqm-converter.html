Square Feet ↔ Square Meter

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me 2-3 min me samajh
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Property ka area kabhi sq ft me, kabhi sq meter (carpet area), kabhi gaj me hota hai. Ek value daaliye aur baaki sab units me turant badla hua dekh lijiye — flat, plot ya makaan, kisi bhi naap ke liye.

Formula aasaan bhasha me

Sabhi area units ko ek base (square meter) me badal kar phir doosri units nikaalte hain. Sabse aam relation: 1 square meter = 10.7639 square feet, aur 1 square yard (gaj) = 9 square feet.

Aam relation:
1 sq meter = 10.7639 sq ft
1 gaj (sq yard) = 9 sq ft = 0.8361 sq m
1 sq ft = 144 sq inch

Builder aksar "super built-up" area sq ft me batate hain, jabki registry aur RERA carpet area sq meter me maangte hain — isliye ye conversion property khareedte waqt bahut kaam aata hai.

Misaal se samajhiye

Misaal 1 — flat ka carpet area. 1,000 sq ft ka flat = 92.9 sq meter. RERA form me carpet area sq meter me hi likhna hota hai.

Misaal 2 — plot gaj me. 200 gaj ka plot = 1,800 sq ft = 167.2 sq meter. North India me plot aksar gaj me bikte hain.

Misaal 3 — room tiling. 12 ft × 10 ft = 120 sq ft = 11.15 sq meter. Tile ya carpet kharidte waqt sq meter kaam aata hai.

Flat ke kagaz me teen area hote hain — teeno alag

Builder ka "1,200 sq ft" aur ghar me sach me milne wali jagah ek nahi hoti. RERA ke baad carpet area likhna zaroori hai, par vigyapan me aksar super built-up dikhta hai.

Isliye per sq ft ka rate tulna karte waqt pehle poochhein ki rate kis area par hai. ₹5,000 per sq ft super built-up aur ₹5,000 per sq ft carpet me lagbhag 35% ka antar hota hai — wahi flat kaafi mehnga pad jaata hai. Agreement me carpet area sq metre me likha hota hai, isliye badalna aata hona chahiye: 1 sq m = 10.764 sq ft. Aur loading (super built-up aur carpet ka antar) har builder ka alag hota hai, aam taur par 25% se 40% tak — ise likhit me maang lein.

Aksar hone wali galtiyan

  1. Carpet aur built-up mix karna. Built-up/super built-up area carpet se 20-30% zyada hota hai — conversion se pehle jaanein kaunsa area hai.
  2. Gaj aur sq ft ulat lena. 1 gaj = 9 sq ft. Log kabhi-kabhi 1:1 maan lete hain jo bilkul galat hai.
  3. Length ko area maanna. Feet aur square feet alag hain — area ke liye lambai × chaudai chahiye.
  4. Round-off se bada farak. Bade plot me thoda round-off bada farak la deta hai — poora number lein.
  5. Local unit chhodna. Bigha, biswa, nali jaise local naap sq ft se alag hote hain — unke liye area guide dekhein.

Bharat me ye baatein mayne rakhti hain

  • RERA: RERA carpet area sq meter me maangta hai — builder ke sq ft ko yahan badal lein.
  • North vs South: North India plot gaj/sq ft me, South aksar sq ft/cent me. Local unit samajhna zaroori.
  • Registry: Registry documents me area aksar sq meter me hote hain.
  • Home loan: Bank valuation carpet area par hota hai — super built-up par loan nahi milta.

Aur thoda samajhte hain

Property khareedte waqt sabse bada bhram "carpet area vs built-up vs super built-up" ka hota hai. Carpet area wo hai jispar aap sach me chal sakte hain (deewaron ke andar ki jagah). Built-up me deewaron ki motai judti hai, aur super built-up me lift, seedhi aur common area ka hissa bhi. Builder aksar super built-up sq ft me rate batate hain (jo bada dikhta hai), jabki RERA aur registry carpet area sq meter me maangte hain. Isliye conversion karte waqt pehle ye pakka karein ki aap kaunsa area badal rahe hain.

North aur South India me naapne ka tareeka bhi alag hai. North me plot aksar "gaj" (square yard) me bikte hain, jahan 1 gaj = 9 sq ft. South me "cent" aur "ground" jaise unit chalte hain. Agar aap dusre rajya me property dekh rahe hain, to local unit ko pehle sq ft ya sq meter me laana zaroori hai, warna keemat ka andaza bilkul galat baith sakta hai. Bade plot me thoda sa round-off bhi lakhon ka farak la deta hai, isliye poora number lena behtar hai.
