Ghar Kharidein Ya Kiraye Par Rahein?

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

"Kiraya dena to paisa barbaad karna hai" — ye baat humein bachpan se sunai gayi hai. Isi wajah se log jaldbaazi me ghar khareed lete hain, chahe uske liye poori zindagi ki bachat aur bada loan hi kyun na lagana pade. Lekin sach thoda alag hai. Ghar kharidna kai baar sahi faisla hota hai, par har baar aur har kisi ke liye nahi. Is guide me hum thande dimaag se dekhenge ki ghar kharidein ya kiraye par rahein, aur kaunse sawaal poochne chahiye faisle se pehle.

Kiraya vs EMI — asli tulna

Sabse pehla farak paise ka hai. Kiraye me aap har mahine ek rakam dete hain jo wapas nahi aati, par aap bade loan aur down payment ke bojh se azaad rehte hain. Ghar kharidne me aap down payment dete hain, phir har mahine EMI, jisme shuruaat ke saalon me zyada hissa sirf byaj hota hai. EMI kiraye se aksar zyada hoti hai — par uske badle aap ek asset (ghar) bana rahe hote hain jo aapka apna hai. Sab Hisaab ka Buy vs Rent tool aapko dono ka mahine ka farak turant dikha deta hai.

Yaad rakhein: Ghar ki keemat sirf EMI nahi hoti. Registry, stamp duty, maintenance, property tax, marammat aur society charges bhi jodne padte hain. Kiraye me ye zyadatar cheezein makan-maalik ki zimmedari hoti hain.

Kab kharidna samajhdaari hai?

  • Aap us shehar me lambe samay (7-10 saal+) tak rukne wale hain. Ghar kharidne ke shuruaati kharche (stamp duty, registry) tabhi vasool hote hain jab aap kaafi saal rukein.
  • Aapke paas achhi down payment hai aur EMI aapki income ka bahut bada hissa nahi khaa rahi.
  • Aapki naukri/kamai stable hai aur aapko bar-bar shehar badalne ki zaroorat nahi.
  • Aap apna ghar chahte hain — man ki shanti aur suraksha ki bhi keemat hoti hai.

Kab kiraya behtar hai?

  • Aap naye shehar me hain aur pakka nahi ki kitne saal rukenge.
  • Aapki naukri me transfer hote rehte hain.
  • Ghar ki keemat aapki income ke muqable bahut zyada hai (jahan kiraya kam par ghar bahut mehnga ho).
  • Aap down payment ka paisa achhe se invest karke ghar se zyada growth pa sakte hain.

Ek soch jo log bhool jaate hain

Bahut se log sirf "EMI vs kiraya" dekhte hain, par asli tulna isse gehri hai. Ghar kharidne me jo bada paisa down payment aur EMI me jaata hai, agar wahi paisa kiraye par rehkar SIP me lagaya jaaye to kitna banega? Kabhi-kabhi mehnge shehron me kiraya par rehkar bachat ko invest karna ghar kharidne se zyada faydemand nikal sakta hai. Doosri taraf, ghar ek emotional aur suraksha ki cheez hai — budhaape me apna ghar hone ka sukoon paise me nahi tola ja sakta. Isiliye faisla sirf number par nahi, apni zindagi ki zaroorat par bhi hona chahiye.

Faisle se pehle ye 4 sawaal poochein

  1. Main is shehar me kitne saal rukne wala hoon?
  2. Kya EMI meri income ka 40% se kam rahegi, ya ye mujhe daba degi?
  3. Down payment ke baad kya mere paas emergency fund bachega?
  4. Agar main kiraye par rahoon aur bachat invest karoon, to 10 saal me kya banega?

In sawaalon ke jawab imaandari se dein, phir calculator par number milaayein. Aksar tasveer khud saaf ho jaati hai.

Ant me — ghar kharidna galat nahi, aur kiraya dena bewakoofi nahi. Dono apni jagah sahi hain. Galti tab hoti hai jab log doston ke dabav me, bina hisaab lagaye, apni himmat se zyada bada loan le lete hain. Pehle apni sthiti dekhein, EMI ka bojh samjhein, aur tab hi zindagi ka ye bada faisla lein. Jaldbaazi me liya ghar kai baar khushi ki jagah tension le aata hai.

Chhupe kharche jo ghar ke saath aate hain

Ghar kharidte waqt log sirf keemat aur EMI dekhte hain, par asli kharcha isse zyada hota hai. Registry aur stamp duty me ghar ki keemat ka achha-khasa hissa lagta hai. Uske baad har saal property tax, society ka maintenance, paint aur marammat, aur kabhi-kabhi badi repair ka kharcha bhi aapka hi hota hai. Kiraye par rehne wale ye zyadatar cheezein makan-maalik ke sar chhod dete hain. Isliye jab aap tulna karein, to sirf EMI ko kiraye se mat milaayein — in saare chhupe kharchon ko bhi jodein, tabhi asli tasveer saaf hoti hai.

Emotion aur number — dono zaroori

Ghar sirf ek nivesh nahi, ek ghar hai. Apni deewaron par keel thokne ki azadi, budhaape me kiraye ki chinta se mukti, aur "ye apna hai" ka sukoon — inki keemat kisi calculator me nahi aati. Isliye faisla theek se number par bhi hona chahiye aur dil ki zaroorat par bhi. Kai baar number kehta hai kiraya sasta hai, par ek pariwar ke liye apna ghar hona zyada zaroori hota hai — aur ye bilkul theek hai. Bas ye faisla soch-samajh kar, apni himmat ke andar rehkar lein, dabav me aakar nahi.

Baat ko number me rakhne se faisla saaf ho jaata hai. Maan lijiye Rs 50 lakh ka ghar, 20% down payment, 8.5% par 20 saal ka loan, aur usi jaisa ghar Rs 15,000 kiraye par.

Wo kharcha jo tulna se gayab rehta hai: stamp duty, registry, brokerage aur interior milakar keemat ka lagbhag 8% se 12% ban jaata hai — aur ye paisa ghar bechte waqt wapas nahi milta. Isi ek wajah se aksar breakeven 4-5 saal me nahi, 7 se 8 saal me aata hai. Isliye asli sawaal "EMI kiraye se kitni zyada hai" nahi hai — sawaal ye hai ki aap us shehar me kitne saal tak pakka rukenge. 3-4 saal me tabaadla ya naukri badalne ki sambhavna ho, to ganit lagbhag hamesha kiraye ke paksh me jaata hai.

Kis shehar me ganit kis taraf jhukta hai

Ek hi sawaal ka jawab shehar ke hisaab se badal jaata hai. Faisla rental yield par tikta hai — yaani ghar ki keemat ke muqable saal bhar ka kiraya kitna hai.

Apna number nikaalna aasaan hai — jis ghar me aap rehte hain uska mahine ka kiraya 12 se guna kariye, aur usi jaise ghar ki bazaar keemat se bhaag dijiye. Bade shehron me ye aksar 2 se 3% ke beech aata hai; chhote shehron aur kasbon me 4-5% tak bhi.

Yahi wajah hai ki ek hi salah sabke liye theek nahi baithti. Jahan Rs 1 crore ke flat ka kiraya Rs 20,000 hai (2.4%), wahan kiraye par rehkar antar nivesh karna aksar aage nikal jaata hai. Aur jahan Rs 40 lakh ke ghar ka kiraya Rs 14,000 hai (4.2%), wahan EMI kiraye ke aas-paas hi baith jaati hai aur ghar bhi apna ho jaata hai. Isliye "ghar to lena hi chahiye" ya "kiraya hi sahi hai" — dono baatein bina apna number nikaale bekaar hain. Pehle apni gali ka hisaab nikaaliye, phir faisla.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya kiraya dena paisa barbaad karna hai?

Zaroori nahi. Kiraya aapko flexibility deta hai aur bade loan ke bojh se bachaata hai. Agar bachat ko invest kiya jaaye to ye samajhdaari bhi ho sakti hai.

EMI income ka kitna hona chahiye?

Aam salah hai ki total EMI aapki monthly income ke 40% se kam rahe, taaki baaki kharche aur bachat par asar na pade.

Kitne saal rukein to ghar kharidna faydemand?

Aam taur par 7-10 saal ya zyada, taaki stamp duty aur registry jaise shuruaati kharche vasool ho sakein.

Buy vs Rent calculator kya batata hai?

Ye aapke ghar ki keemat, down payment aur loan ke hisaab se EMI nikaal kar use aapke abhi ke kiraye se tulna karta hai.

Kitne saal rukne ka pakka ho to kharidna faayde ka hai?

Aam taur par 7 saal ya usse zyada. Kyunki stamp duty, registry, brokerage aur interior par keemat ka 8-12% shuru me hi chala jaata hai aur wo wapas nahi aata. Utna nikalne me hi kai saal lag jaate hain.

Kiraya EMI se kam hai — to kharidna galat faisla hai?

Sirf isse faisla nahi hota. Dekhna hoga ki EMI aur kiraye ka antar aap kahin nivesh kar rahe hain ya nahi, aur us shehar me kitne saal rukenge. Antar bacha kar kharch kar dena kiraye ka faayda khatam kar deta hai.

Apne shehar ke liye ye hisaab kaise nikaalein?

Mahine ka kiraya 12 se guna kariye, phir usi jaise ghar ki bazaar keemat se bhaag dijiye. Jawab 2% se kam aaye to kiraya sasta padta hai, 4% se upar aaye to kharidna faayde ka. Beech me ho to faisla is baat par ki aap kitne saal rukenge.

Chhote shehar me kharidna aasaan kyun lagta hai?

Kyunki wahan keemat ke muqable kiraya zyada hota hai. Isse EMI aur kiraye ka antar chhota ho jaata hai, aur breakeven jaldi aata hai. Bade shehron me keemat bahut upar hoti hai par kiraya usi anupaat me nahi badhta.
