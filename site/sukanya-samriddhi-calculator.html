Ganit kya kehta hai

SSY me 15 saal tak jama karna hota hai, par khaata 21 saal me maturity par pahunchta hai. Aakhri 6 saal me aap kuch nahi daalte, phir bhi poori rakam par byaj chalta rehta hai. Byaj saalana joodta hai aur sarkar har teen mahine me dar review karti hai. Yahi 6 saal ka "khaali" samay is yojana ka sabse bada faayda hai.

Formula: 15 saal tak: balance = (balance + saalana jama) × (1 + byaj)
agle 6 saal: balance = balance × (1 + byaj)

Teen udaharan jo saaf kar dein

Misaal 1 — chhoti jama. Har saal ₹25,000, 7.5% dar. 15 saal me kul ₹3,75,000 jama hoga aur 21 saal baad maturity lagbhag ₹10,83,291 banti hai.

Misaal 2 — poori limit. Har saal ₹1,50,000 (adhiktam), 8.2% dar. Jama ₹22.5 lakh, maturity lagbhag ₹69 lakh ke aaspaas.

Misaal 3 — chhoti shuruaat. Har saal ₹12,000 (₹1,000 mahina), 8.2% dar. Jama ₹1.8 lakh, maturity lagbhag ₹5.5 lakh.

Ye chook aksar hoti hai

  1. Saal me kam se kam rakam na daalna. Har saal ek nyoontam rakam daalni zaroori hai. Chhoot gayi to khaata band ho jaata hai aur usse chalu karne ke liye penalty ke saath purani rakam bharni padti hai.
  2. Maturity dar ko pakka maan lena. Sarkar har teen mahine me dar badalti hai. 21 saal me dar kai baar badlegi, isliye calculator ka number ek anuman hai — pakka wada nahi.
  3. Umar ki seema chook jaana. Khaata beti ke janm se 10 saal ki umar tak hi khul sakta hai. Ye samay nikal gaya to phir mauka nahi milta.
  4. Do se zyada khaate ki koshish. Ek parivaar me aam taur par do betiyon tak hi khaata khul sakta hai (judwa ki alag chhoot hai).
  5. Saal ke aakhir me jama karna. 5 April se pehle jama karne par us poore saal ka byaj milta hai. Baad me daalne par mahine chhoot jaate hain.

Bharat me kya alag hota hai

  • Kahan khulta hai: Post office aur zyadatar sarkari bank me. Beti ka janm praman patra, maa-baap ka pehchan patra aur pata chahiye.
  • Tax: Old Regime me 80C ki chhoot milti hai, aur byaj aur maturity dono par tax nahi lagta — yaani EEE.
  • Aanshik nikasi: Beti ke 18 saal ke hone par ya 10vi ke baad padhai ke liye balance ka 50% tak nikala ja sakta hai.
  • Jama ki seema: Saal me kam se kam ₹250 aur adhiktam ₹1.5 lakh.
  • Verify karein: Maujooda byaj dar aur niyam India Post ya bank ki official site par ek baar dekh lein — ye samay-samay par badalte hain.

SSY ke wo niyam jo hisaab badal dete hain

SSY me paisa 21 saal baad milta hai par jama sirf 15 saal karna hota hai. Beech ke 6 saal me kuch jama nahi hota, phir bhi byaj chalta rehta hai.

Ek chhoti si baat jo poore 21 saal me achha antar daal deti hai: SSY ka byaj mahine ke sabse kam balance par joda jaata hai. Isliye saal ki raqam 5 April tak jama karne se us poore saal ka byaj banta hai, jabki March me jama karne par lagbhag ek saal ka byaj chhoot jaata hai. Doosri baat — byaj ki dar sarkar har teen mahine tay karti hai, isliye calculator ka jawab ek anuman hai; poore 21 saal ek hi dar nahi rehti. Asli dar hamesha nsiindia.gov.in par confirm karein.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

SSY me kitne saal jama karna padta hai?

15 saal tak jama karna hota hai, par khaata 21 saal me maturity par pahunchta hai.

Kya SSY ka byaj tax-free hai?

Haan, byaj aur maturity dono par tax nahi lagta. Old Regime me jama par 80C ki chhoot bhi milti hai.

Beti ki umar kitni honi chahiye?

Khaata janm se 10 saal ki umar tak khul sakta hai.

Beech me paisa nikal sakte hain?

18 saal ke baad padhai ya shaadi ke liye aanshik nikasi ho sakti hai. Poora paisa 21 saal par milta hai.

Byaj dar badalti rehti hai kya?

Haan, sarkar har teen mahine me review karti hai. Isliye ye calculator sirf anuman deta hai.

Zaroori baat: Ye calculator sirf jaankari ke liye hai. Ye SEBI-registered nivesh salah nahi hai. Byaj dar, tax slab aur sarkari niyam samay-samay par badalte hain — bade faisle se pehle official website ya apne salahkaar se ek baar zaroor confirm kar lein.
