UPS Pension Calculator — Unified Pension Scheme Me Kitni Pension Milegi

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) 1 April 2025 se laagu hai. Isme pension bazaar ke utaar-chadhaav par nahi, aapki aakhri 12 mahine ki ausat basic pay par tay hoti hai. NPS me pension corpus se banti hai; UPS me pehle rakam tay hoti hai, corpus baad ki baat hai.

Neeche apni ausat basic pay, qualifying service aur retirement ke waqt ka DA daaliye. Assured payout, family payout aur ek-baar milne wala lump sum — teeno turant dikh jayenge.

UPS ka poora formula — teen alag number

Log aksar UPS ko ek hi rakam samajh lete hain. Asal me isme teen alag cheezein banti hain, aur teeno ka hisaab alag tarah se hota hai.

KyaFormulaMisaal (basic 80,000 · 30 saal)
Assured payout50% × ausat basic × (service ÷ 25), 25 saal par poora₹40,000/mahina
Family payout60% of assured payout₹24,000/mahina
Lump sum10% of (basic + DA) × har poore 6 mahine₹7.44 lakh (60 block)

Dhyaan dijiye — assured payout sirf basic pay par banta hai, basic + DA par nahi. Par lump sum me DA bhi jodkar gina jaata hai. Yahi wajah hai ki DA jitna upar hoga, lump sum utna bada nikalega, jabki mahine ki pension wahi rahegi.

Payout shuru hone ke baad usme Dearness Relief judta hai — bilkul usi tarah jaise chalte hue karmchari ko DA milta hai. Isliye retirement ke 10 saal baad haath me aane wali rakam aaj ke number se kaafi upar hoti hai.

25 saal ka niyam aur 10,000 wali farsh

Poora 50% tabhi milta hai jab qualifying service 25 saal ya usse zyada ho. Usse kam par rakam anupaat me kat jaati hai — 25 saal ko 300 mahine maankar.

ServiceAusat basic 80,000 par payoutKya laga
28 saal₹40,000Poora 50%
20 saal₹32,000240 ÷ 300 ka anupaat
14 saal₹22,400168 ÷ 300 ka anupaat
11 saal₹17,600Anupaat, par 10,000 se upar
8 saal—Assured payout nahi, corpus wapas

10 saal ya usse zyada service par kam se kam ₹10,000 mahina pakka hota hai. Ye farsh un logon ke liye hai jinki basic pay chhoti hai — jaise 12 saal service aur 45,000 basic wale ka anupaatik hisaab 10,800 banta hai, jo farsh ke bilkul upar hai. 40,000 basic par wahi hisaab 9,600 nikalta hai, aur tab farsh kaam aati hai.

Swechchhik sevanivritti (VRS) me ek baat alag hai. 25 saal poore hone par VRS le sakte hain, par payout us tarikh se shuru hota hai jab aap saamanya taur par retire hote. Beech ke saal khaali jaate hain — ye baat kaafi log der se samajhte hain.

Lump sum ka hisaab — gratuity se alag cheez hai

Retirement par milne wala lump sum aur gratuity do alag rakam hain. Log dono ko ek hi samajh baithte hain aur planning me ek rakam kam ya zyada gin lete hain.

  • Lump sum — mahine ki emoluments (basic + DA) ka 10%, har poore 6 mahine ki service par. 30 saal = 60 block = 6 mahine ki emoluments ke barabar.
  • Gratuity — ye apne service niyam ke hisaab se alag milti hai. UPS chunne se ye band nahi hoti.

Misaal se saaf hoga. Basic 80,000 aur DA 55% ho to emoluments 1,24,000 banti hai. 30 saal ki service par 60 block bante hain, yaani 1,24,000 ka 10% guna 60 — lagbhag ₹7.44 lakh. Aur zaroori baat: ye rakam lene se mahine ki assured payout me koi kami nahi hoti. Ye purani commutation wali vyavastha se bilkul alag hai, jahan ek hissa lene par pension ghat jaati thi.

Aadhe-adhoore 6 mahine nahi gine jaate. 29 saal 5 mahine ki service par 58 block hi bante hain, 59 nahi. Retirement ki tarikh agar aapke haath me hai (jaise VRS me), to ek-do mahine ka farak seedha lump sum me dikhta hai.

Benchmark corpus — wo shart jo brochure me nahi dikhti

Yahan wo hissa hai jo aksar chhoot jaata hai. UPS me do corpus hote hain — ek aapka apna (aapka 10% + sarkar ka 10%), aur ek pool corpus jisme sarkar 8.5% alag se daalti hai.

Retirement par aapka apna corpus pool me transfer hota hai, par sirf benchmark corpus ki seema tak. Benchmark wo rakam hai jo default niveshit vikalp par chalne se banti. Agar aapka corpus benchmark se kam nikla, to assured payout usi anupaat me ghata diya jaata hai — jab tak aap khud kami ki bharpai na kar dein.

SthitiPayout par asar
Corpus benchmark ke barabar ya zyadaPoora assured payout, extra rakam aapko wapas
Corpus benchmark se kamPayout usi anupaat me kam, ya kami khud bharni padegi

Iska seedha matlab ye hai: UPS me alag investment vikalp chunkar zyada return kamane ki koshish ka faayda seemit hai, par nuksan poora aapka hai. Zyada return milne par pension nahi badhti, sirf surplus rakam haath me aati hai. Kam return milne par pension par seedha khatra aata hai. Isliye zyadatar logon ke liye default vikalp par bane rehna hi samajhdaari hai.

UPS ya NPS — kis ke liye kya theek baithta hai

Ye ek baar chunne wala faisla hai, isliye jaldbaazi thik nahi. Neeche do-teen asli sawaal hain jinse tasveer saaf hoti hai.

Mota-moti niyam: jinki service 25 saal ke aas-paas ya zyada hai aur jinhein mahine ki tay rakam chahiye — unke liye UPS aaram deta hai. Jinki service kam hai, jo bazaar samajhte hain aur jinke paas doosre nivesh bhi hain — unke liye NPS ka daayra bada rehta hai. Hum kisi ek ki sifarish nahi kar rahe; dono number apne saamne rakhkar dekhna zaroori hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

UPS me pension kis rakam par banti hai?

Sirf aakhri 12 mahine ki ausat basic pay par. DA usme nahi jodte. Haan, payout shuru hone ke baad usme Dearness Relief alag se judta rehta hai.

25 saal se kam service par kitna milega?

Anupaat me. 25 saal ko 300 mahine maankar hisaab lagta hai — jaise 20 saal par 240 batey 300, yaani poore payout ka 80%. 10 saal ya usse upar par kam se kam 10,000 mahina milta hai.

Lump sum lene par mahine ki pension ghat jaati hai kya?

Nahi. UPS ka lump sum assured payout se alag hai aur use ghataata nahi. Ye purani commutation se alag vyavastha hai, jahan hissa lene par pension kam ho jaati thi.

Gratuity UPS me bhi milti hai?

Haan. Retirement gratuity aur death gratuity apne service niyam ke hisaab se milti rehti hai. Lump sum uske alawa hai.

UPS chunne ke baad NPS me wapas ja sakte hain?

Ye ek baar ka vikalp maana gaya hai. Badalne ki koshish se pehle apne DDO ya pension portal se likhit sthiti confirm kar lijiye, kyunki niyam samay-samay par spasht kiye jaate hain.
