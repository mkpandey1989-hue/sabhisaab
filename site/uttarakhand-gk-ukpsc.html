UKPSC / UKSSSC GK Corner

Uttarakhand GK: 50 Zaroori Ek-Line Tathya

Ye ek-line tathya UKPSC, UKSSSC, Patwari, Police, Forest Guard jaisi pariksha ki taiyari me kaam aate hain. Pariksha se pehle apne official syllabus aur latest sarkari data se milan zaroor karein.

  • Uttarakhand bharat ka 27th rajya hai, jo 9 November 2000 ko bana.
  • Rajya ka naam 1 January 2007 se Uttaranchal se badalkar Uttarakhand kiya gaya.
  • Dehradun rajya ki antarim (sheetkalin) rajdhani hai; Gairsain (Bhararisain) grishmakalin rajdhani ghoshit hai.
  • Uttarakhand ka Uchch Nyayalaya Nainital me hai.
  • Rajya me 13 zile hain: Garhwal me 7 aur Kumaon me 6.
  • Kshetrafal me sabse bada zila Uttarkashi aur sabse chhota Champawat hai.
  • Aabadi me sabse bada zila Haridwar aur sabse kam aabadi wala zila Rudraprayag hai (Census 2011).
  • Nanda Devi (7,816 m) rajya ki sabse unchi chotii hai.
  • Rajya pashu Kasturi mrig, rajya pakshi Himalayan Monal, rajya phool Brahma Kamal aur rajya vriksh Buransh hai.
  • Rajya titli Common Peacock hai.
  • Jim Corbett National Park bharat ka sabse purana national park hai (1936).
  • Rajaji National Park ka naam C. Rajagopalachari ke naam par hai.
  • Uttarakhand me 6 national park hain: Corbett, Rajaji, Nanda Devi, Valley of Flowers, Gangotri aur Govind.
  • Nanda Devi aur Valley of Flowers national park UNESCO World Heritage Site hain.
  • Valley of Flowers ko Frank Smythe ne 1931 me prasiddhi dilai.
  • Bhagirathi aur Alaknanda Devprayag me milkar Ganga banti hain.
  • Alaknanda nadi Satopanth aur Bhagirath Kharak glacier kshetra se nikalti hai.
  • Bhagirathi ka udgam Gaumukh (Gangotri glacier) maana jata hai.
  • Yamuna ka udgam Yamunotri ke paas Champasar glacier kshetra hai.
  • Panch Prayag: Vishnuprayag, Nandprayag, Karnaprayag, Rudraprayag aur Devprayag.
  • Panch Kedar: Kedarnath, Tungnath, Rudranath, Madhyamaheshwar aur Kalpeshwar.
  • Panch Badri: Badrinath, Yogdhyan Badri, Bhavishya Badri, Vriddha Badri aur Adi Badri.
  • Tungnath duniya ke sabse unche Shiv mandiron me ginta jata hai.
  • Badrinath mandir Alaknanda ke kinare, Nar aur Narayan parvat ke beech hai.
  • Kali nadi (Sharda) Uttarakhand aur Nepal ke beech sima banati hai.
  • Ramganga nadi Jim Corbett National Park se hokar behti hai.
  • Tehri Dam Bhagirathi par bana hai aur bharat ke sabse unche baandhon me ginta jata hai.
  • Chipko andolan 1970 ke dashak me Chamoli se shuru hua; Gaura Devi ka naam Reni gaon (1974) se juda hai.
  • Chipko andolan ke pramukh netaon me Sunderlal Bahuguna aur Chandi Prasad Bhatt ke naam aate hain.
  • Coolie Begar ka ant 1921 me Bageshwar ke Uttarayani mela se juda hai.
  • Badri Datt Pande ko Kumaon Kesari kaha jata hai.
  • Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali 1930 ke Peshawar Kand se prasiddh hue.
  • Tilari ki ghatna 30 May 1930 ki maani jati hai.
  • Tehri riyasat ka bharat me vilay 1949 me hua.
  • Gorkha shasan Kumaon me 1790 ke aaspaas aur Garhwal me 1804 ke aaspaas shuru hua.
  • Anglo-Nepalese yuddh ke baad Sugauli ki sandhi hui (1815-16).
  • Kumaon Regiment ka mukhyalay Ranikhet me aur Garhwal Rifles ka Lansdowne me hai.
  • Indian Military Academy Dehradun me hai (1932 me sthapit).
  • Forest Research Institute (FRI) Dehradun ki sthapna 1906 me Imperial Forest Research Institute ke roop me maani jati hai.
  • IIT Roorkee ka itihas Thomason College (1847) se shuru hota hai.
  • GB Pant University of Agriculture and Technology Pantnagar bharat ka pehla krishi vishwavidyalaya maana jata hai.
  • Nehru Institute of Mountaineering Uttarkashi me hai.
  • Ramman (Saloor-Dungra, Chamoli) UNESCO ki Intangible Cultural Heritage list me hai (2009).
  • Aipan Kumaon ki prasiddh ritual lok-kala hai.
  • Chholiya talwar-dhaal wala lok-nritya hai.
  • Nanda Devi Raj Jat lagbhag 12 saal me hone wali Himalayan paidal yatra hai.
  • Jageshwar Dham Almora me aur Baijnath Bageshwar me hai.
  • Kainchi Dham Nainital zile me Neem Karoli Baba ke ashram ke liye prasiddh hai.
  • Mussoorie ko “Queen of the Hills” kaha jata hai.
  • Haridwar me Kumbh mela lagta hai.

Mega FAQ: Uttarakhand Se Jude 200 Sawaal-Jawab

Uttarakhand ki basic jaankari

Uttarakhand kab bana?
Uttarakhand 9 November 2000 ko Uttar Pradesh se alag hokar bana aur bharat ka 27th rajya bana. Shuru me iska naam Uttaranchal tha, jo 1 January 2007 se Uttarakhand kar diya gaya.
Uttarakhand ki rajdhani kaun si hai?
Dehradun rajya ki antarim (sheetkalin) rajdhani hai. Gairsain (Bhararisain), zila Chamoli ko grishmakalin rajdhani ghoshit kiya gaya hai. Rajdhani se judi sthiti badalti rehti hai, isliye taza sarkari suchna dekhein.
Uttarakhand me kitne zile hain?
Uttarakhand me 13 zile hain. Garhwal mandal me 7 (Uttarkashi, Chamoli, Rudraprayag, Tehri Garhwal, Dehradun, Pauri Garhwal, Haridwar) aur Kumaon mandal me 6 (Nainital, Almora, Pithoragarh, Bageshwar, Champawat, Udham Singh Nagar) zile hain.
Uttarakhand ko Devbhoomi kyun kehte hain?
Yahan Char Dham, Panch Kedar, Panch Badri, Panch Prayag aur hazaron prachin mandir hain, aur Ganga-Yamuna ka udgam bhi yahin hai. Isi dharmik aur sanskritik mahatva ke kaaran ise Devbhoomi kehte hain.
Uttarakhand ki sabse unchi chotii kaun si hai?
Nanda Devi, jiski unchai lagbhag 7,816 meter hai. Ise poori tarah bharat ke andar sthit sabse unchi chotii maana jata hai.
Uttarakhand ka rajya pashu, pakshi, phool aur vriksh kya hai?
Rajya pashu Kasturi mrig (Alpine Musk Deer), rajya pakshi Himalayan Monal, rajya phool Brahma Kamal aur rajya vriksh Buransh hai. Rajya titli Common Peacock hai.
Uttarakhand ki aabadi kitni hai?
Census 2011 ke anusar Uttarakhand ki aabadi lagbhag 1.01 crore (10,086,292) thi. Agli Census ke aankde aane par ye badal jayega.
Garhwal aur Kumaon me kya farak hai?
Dono mandal ki boli, parampara, tyohar aur itihas me kaafi samanta aur kuch farak hai. Garhwal me Char Dham aur Panwar vansh ka itihas hai, jabki Kumaon me Chand vansh, Jageshwar-Baijnath aur Kumaoni sanskriti hai. Vistrit tulna Uttarakhand guide (sab zile) me dekhein.
Uttarakhand ki bhasha kya hai?
Rajya ki sarkari bhasha Hindi hai, aur Sanskrit dusri sarkari bhasha hai. Garhwali, Kumaoni aur Jaunsari yahan ki pramukh lok-boliyan hain.
Uttarakhand ke kitne National Park hain?
6 National Park hain: Jim Corbett, Rajaji, Nanda Devi, Valley of Flowers, Gangotri aur Govind. Nanda Devi aur Valley of Flowers UNESCO World Heritage Site hain.

Ghoomne aur yatra se jude sawaal

Kedarnath kitni unchai par hai?
Kedarnath lagbhag 3,583 meter ki unchai par Rudraprayag zile me hai. Yahan pahunchne ke liye Gaurikund se paidal ya sarkari anumati wali alag suvidhaon ka istemal hota hai.
Kya Rishikesh-Karnaprayag rail line chalu ho gayi hai?
Nahi. Ye rail line abhi nirmanadheen hai aur is marg par koi yatri rail nahi chal rahi. Completion date ke bare me alag-alag official bayan 2028-2029 ke beech mile hain. Taaza sthiti ke liye Railway Ministry/RVNL ki official suchna dekhein.

Yatra aur yatayat se jude sawaal

Haldwani se Nainital kitni door hai?
Haldwani se Nainital lagbhag 35 km door hai.
Kya Uttarakhand me online hotel book karna surakshit hai?
Registered aur reviewed hotel chunein, advance payment se pehle booking ki pushti karein aur sirf sarkari ya vishwasniya platform istemal karein.
Kedarnath ka trek kahan se shuru hota hai?
Kedarnath ka paidal marg Gaurikund se shuru hota hai.
Winter me kaun se pahadi sthal ghoomne layak hain?
Auli, Chopta, Mussoorie, Nainital, Kedarkantha (trek) aur Munsiyari ke kuch kshetra barf ke liye pasand kiye jate hain, par sadak ki sthiti aur mausam dekhna zaroori hai.

Zameen, ghar aur paise se jude sawaal

1 Gaj kitne square feet ka hota hai?
1 Gaj (square yard) = 9 square feet.
1 Square meter kitne square feet ka hota hai?
1 square meter lagbhag 10.76 square feet hota hai.
Gol Khata kharidne me kya jokhim hai?
Sajhe khate me kai hissedar hote hain, isliye bina sabki sahmati ke kharidne par vivad ka jokhim rehta hai.
Dakhil-kharij (mutation) kya hai?
Zameen bikne ya bantwara hone ke baad revenue record me naya malik ka naam darj karne ki prakriya.
Van Panchayat kya hai?
Gaon ke jungal ka samuhik prabandh karne wali vyavastha, jo 1931 se chali aa rahi hai.
Uttarakhand me kheti ki zameen ko ghar ke liye kaise badlein?
Dhara 143 ke antargat gair-kheti upyog ke liye prakriya hoti hai. Sthaniya tehsil/SDM karyalay aur vakil se prakriya aur 2025 ke naye niyam pata karein.
Uttarakhand Farmer ID kya hai?
Kisan ki pehchan aur sarkari yojanaon se jodne wali digital ID. Sab Hisaab par UKFR guide diya gaya hai.
KCC kya hai aur kaise banta hai?
Kisan Credit Card kisan ke liye kam byaj par loan ki suvidha hai. Banwane ki prakriya ke liye Sab Hisaab ki KCC guide dekhein.
Home loan EMI kaise nikalein?
Loan rashi, byaj dar aur avadhi ke hisaab se EMI nikalti hai. Sab Hisaab ka EMI Calculator isme madad karega.
Pahadon me solar lagana kaisa hai?
Dhoop wale kshetra me solar upyogi ho sakta hai. Subsidy aur gananaon ke liye Sab Hisaab ke PM Surya Ghar aur inverter calculator dekhein.

Sanskriti, bhasha aur khane se jude sawaal

Bhitauli kya hoti hai?
Chaitra maah me bhai apni vivahit behan ke ghar uphar aur pakwan lekar jata hai.
Nanda Devi Raj Jat kitni lambi yatra hai?

Kumbh mela Haridwar me kab lagta hai?

Nityanand Swami.
Uttarakhand ke pehle rajyapal kaun the?
Surjit Singh Barnala.
Uttarakhand ke vartaman mukhyamantri kaun hain?
Pushkar Singh Dhami, 4 July 2021 se. Pad par kaun hai, ye badal sakta hai; taaza jaankari ke liye official source dekhein.

5 seat hain.

Uttarakhand me kitni Rajya Sabha seat hain? 3 seat hain.

Uttarakhand Vidhan Sabha me kitni seat hain?
70 seat hain.
Uttarakhand ka rajya geet kya hai?
“Uttarakhand Dev Bhumi Matri Bhumi” rajya geet ke roop me maanya hai.
Uttarakhand ke kaun se zile Nepal se lagte hain?
Pithoragarh, Champawat aur Udham Singh Nagar ka Nepal se sima sampark hai.

Uttarakhand ke kaun se zile Tibet (China) se lagte hain? Uttarkashi, Chamoli aur Pithoragarh ki sima Tibet (China) se judti hai.

Alaknanda aur Bhagirathi ka sangam kahan hai?
Devprayag me, jisse Ganga banti hai.
Uttarakhand ka sabse purana national park kaun sa hai?
Jim Corbett National Park, jo bharat ka bhi sabse purana national park hai (1936).
Uttarakhand me tiger reserve kaun se hain?
Jim Corbett aur Rajaji Tiger Reserve.
Nanda Devi Biosphere Reserve kis zile me hai?
Mukhya roop se Chamoli zile me (Pithoragarh aur Bageshwar ki sima ke paas).
Uttarakhand me kaun se vishwavidyalaya prasiddh hain?
HNB Garhwal University, Kumaon University, GB Pant University (Pantnagar), Doon University aur IIT Roorkee ka naam prasiddh hai.
Pantnagar vishwavidyalaya kis liye prasiddh hai?
GB Pant University of Agriculture and Technology bharat ka pehla krishi vishwavidyalaya maana jata hai.
Uttarakhand me kaun si sena ki regiment prasiddh hai?
Garhwal Rifles aur Kumaon Regiment.
Garhwal Rifles ka kendra kahan hai?
Lansdowne (Pauri Garhwal).
Kumaon Regiment ka kendra kahan hai?
Ranikhet (Almora).
Coolie Begar kis saal khatam hui?
1921 me Bageshwar ke Uttarayani mela se is pratha ke virodh ka andolan safal hua.
Tehri riyasat kab bharat me mili?
1949 me.
Sugauli ki sandhi kab hui?
1815-16 me, Anglo-Nepalese yuddh ke baad.
Uttarakhand ke kaun se zile ko “Kumaon ka dwar” kehte hain?
Haldwani (Nainital zila) ko Kumaon ka pravesh dwaar kaha jata hai.
Haridwar ko “Gateway of Gods” kyu kehte hain?
Kyunki yahin se Ganga pahadon se maidanon me pravesh karti hain aur ye Char Dham yatra ka pravesh dwaar maana jata hai.
Rishikesh ko “Yoga Capital of the World” kyun kehte hain?
Yoga, dhyan aur ashramon ke kaaran Rishikesh ko ye pehchan mili hai.

Mussoorie ko Queen of the Hills kaha jata hai.
Uttarakhand ke prasiddh jharne kaun se hain?
Kempty Fall, Tiger Falls, Sahastradhara, Birthi Falls aur Bhatta Fall.

Auli (Chamoli) skiing ke liye prasiddh hai.
