Credit Score 750+ Kaise Karein — Poora Tarika

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~7 minute
Chhota jawabSeedha jawab: credit score 750+ karne ke liye har EMI aur card bill samay par bharein, credit card ki limit ka 30% se kam istemaal karein, purane card band mat karein, aur baar-baar naya loan mat maangein. Score dheere badhta hai — 3-6 mahine lagte hain, par ye seedha paisa bachata hai.

Credit score hota kya hai

Credit score 300 se 900 ke beech ka ek number hai jo batata hai ki aap paisa udhaar lekar samay par chukate hain ya nahi. Jitna zyada score, bank ko utna bharosa. 750 se upar ka score achha maana jaata hai — ispar loan sasta aur aasan milta hai.

300-549: kharab · 550-649: theek · 650-749: achha · 750-900: bahut achha

Score kam ho to kya nuksan

Kam score seedha aapki jeb par asar daalta hai. Kam score par: (1) loan ka byaj zyada lagta hai, (2) loan mana bhi ho sakta hai, (3) credit card ki limit kam milti hai. Ek achha score personal loan ka byaj 14% se 11% tak la sakta hai — yaani hazaron ki bachat. Iska asar Personal Loan EMI me dekh sakte hain.

Score badhane ke 5 asli tarike

  1. Har payment samay par — ye sabse bada factor hai (score ka lagbhag aadha hissa). Ek bhi EMI ya card bill late hua to score girta hai. Auto-pay laga dein taaki bhoolein na.
  2. Credit utilization 30% se kam — agar card ki limit ₹1 lakh hai, to ₹30,000 se zyada istemaal na karein. Zyada istemaal batata hai ki aap paise ke liye card par nirbhar hain.
  3. Purane card band mat karein — purana card aapki lambi credit history dikhata hai, jo achhi baat hai. Band karne se history chhoti ho jaati hai aur score gir sakta hai.
  4. Baar-baar loan/card mat maangein — har baar jab aap apply karte hain, bank aapka score check karta hai (hard inquiry). Bahut saari inquiry kam samay me score girati hai.
  5. Credit mix rakhein — thoda secured (home/car loan) aur thoda unsecured (card) — dono ka mishran achha maana jaata hai.

Kitna time lagta hai

Credit score raaton-raat nahi badhta. Achhi aadaton ka asar dikhne me 3 se 6 mahine lagte hain. Isliye agar aage koi bada loan (ghar/gaadi) lena hai, to score par kaam abhi se shuru kar dein, loan ke waqt nahi.

Kya BILKUL nahi karna

  • Minimum due mat bharein — poora bill bharein. Minimum due bharne se baaki par bhaari byaj lagta hai aur ye jaal ban jaata hai.
  • EMI mat chhodein — ek default bhi score ko mahinon peeche le jaata hai.
  • Card settle mat karwayein — "settlement" score par bahut bura daag chhodta hai. Poora chukana hi behtar.
  • Doosron ke loan ke guarantor soch samajh kar banein — wo default kare to aapka score bhi girta hai.

Apna score kaise dekhein

Aap saal me ek baar apni credit report muft dekh sakte hain (CIBIL, Experian jaise bureau se). Kai bank aur app bhi muft score dikhate hain. Report me galti bhi ho sakti hai — jaise koi loan jo aapne liya hi nahi. Aisi galti dikhe to bureau se sahi karwayein, ye bhi score sudharta hai.

Score dekhna khud se score nahi girata (ye "soft inquiry" hai). Sirf jab aap loan/card ke liye apply karte hain (hard inquiry) tab thoda asar padta hai.

Score achha hone ka asli fayda

750+ score sirf ek number nahi — ye aapko mol-bhaav ki taakat deta hai. Achhe score par aap bank se kam byaj maang sakte hain, kyunki bank aapko "surakshit" grahak maanta hai. Ye taakat poore jeevan me lakhon bacha sakti hai — home loan, car loan, personal loan har jagah. Isliye credit score par kaam karna sabse sasta "nivesh" hai jo aap kar sakte hain.

Zaroori: ye lekh aam jaankari ke liye hai. Har bureau ka score thoda alag ho sakta hai. Apni report khud dekhein aur bade faisle se pehle apne bank se baat karein.

Naya credit history kaise banayein

Kai logon ka score isliye kam ya "NA" (not available) hota hai kyunki unhone kabhi credit liya hi nahi. Bank ke paas aapka koi record hi nahi, to wo bharosa kaise kare? Aise me score banane ke aasan tareeke:

  • Ek chhota credit card lein — aur use samajhdaari se istemaal karein (30% se kam, poora bill samay par). 6 mahine me history banne lagti hai.
  • Secured card lein — agar normal card nahi mil raha, to FD ke against secured card milta hai. Ye score banane ka aasan raasta hai.
  • Chhota consumer loan — mobile ya appliance ka EMI par lena aur samay par chukana bhi history banata hai.

Shuruaat me score dheere banta hai, par ek baar 6-12 mahine ki achhi history ban gayi, to score tezi se upar aata hai. Sabra rakhein — pehla card zimmedaari se chalana hi aage bade loan (ghar/gaadi) ka raasta kholta hai.

Yaad rakhein: credit score banane ka matlab karza me doobna nahi hai. Matlab hai — thoda credit lekar use samay par, poora, anushasan se chukana. Yahi bank ko dikhata hai ki aap bharose ke laayak hain.

Kaunsa sudhaar kitne samay me dikhta hai

Score raton-raat nahi badalta. Har kaam ka apna samay hai:

Bakaya poora chukaya1-2 mahine

Sabse tez sudhaar report ki galti sudhaarne se aata hai, aur ye sabse kam log karte hain. Kai baar report me aisa loan dikhta hai jo aapne liya hi nahi, ya chukaya hua loan "bakaya" dikh raha hota hai. Saal me ek baar apni report muft dekhi ja sakti hai — galti mile to online vivaad (dispute) darj kariye, 30-45 din me sudhar jaati hai. Ek chetavni: "settlement" karwana kabhi achha nahi. Wo report me saalon tak dikhta hai aur poora chukane se kahin zyada nuksan karta hai. Paisa kam pad raha ho to bank se avadhi badhwaiye, settlement mat kijiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Credit score 750 karne me kitna time lagta hai?

Achhi aadaton (samay par payment, kam utilization) ka asar 3-6 mahine me dikhta hai. Kharab history se aa rahe hon to zyada time lag sakta hai.

Credit utilization kya hai?

Card ki limit ka kitna hissa aap istemaal karte hain. 30% se kam rakhna achha maana jaata hai. Zyada istemaal score girata hai.

Purana credit card band karna chahiye?

Nahi, agar koi charge nahi lagta. Purana card lambi credit history dikhata hai jo score ke liye achha hai.

Minimum due bharne se score girta hai?

Minimum due bharne se turant score nahi girta, par baaki par bhaari byaj lagta hai aur karza badhta hai. Poora bill bharna hamesha behtar.

Score muft me kaise dekhein?

Saal me ek baar bureau se muft report le sakte hain. Kai bank aur app bhi muft score dikhate hain. Dekhne se score nahi girta.
