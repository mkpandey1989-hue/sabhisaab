Executive Summary aur Champawat Zile Ki Pehchan

District Overview, Nickname aur Core Identity

Seedha jawab: Champawat Uttarakhand ke Kumaon Mandal ka purvi zila hai, jiska mukhyalaya Champawat shehar hai. Yeh 15 September 1997 ko bana, Nepal se Mahakali (Sharda) nadi ke paar lagta hai aur ise pyar se “Kali Kumaon” bhi kaha jata hai.

Champawat Uttarakhand ke un zilon me se hai jahan itihas, aastha aur seema, teeno ek saath milte hain. Yahan Chand rajvansh ki purani rajdhani ka itihas hai, yahan Purnagiri jaisa Shakti peeth hai jahan hazaron shraddhalu har saal pahunchte hain, yahan Devidhura ka Bagwal mela hai jahan Raksha Bandhan par pathron se hone wala yudh-numa anushthan hota hai, aur yahin se aage Terai ke Tanakpur aur Banbasa me pahad seedha maidan se mil jata hai.

Zile ki sabse badi khasiyat uski vividhta hai. Ek hi zile me aapko 1,600 meter ki thandi pahadi ghati bhi milti hai, jahan sardiyon me barf aur dhundh hoti hai, aur sirf kuch ghanton ki doori par 250 meter ke aas-paas ka garam, nadi-kinare ka Terai ilaka bhi. Isi wajah se yahan ki boli, khaan-paan, kheti aur tyohaar teeno me halka-halka antar milta hai.

Champawat ko log kin naamon se jaante hain:

  • Kali Kumaon: Purane samay me Kali nadi ke kinare ka Kumaon, yani Kumaon ka wo hissa jo Kali nadi se juda tha.
  • Chand rajaon ki prarambhik rajdhani: Lok-itihas aur Kumaon ke itihaaskaron ke anusar Chand shasan ki shuruaati rajdhani Champawat kshetra me thi, baad me rajdhani Almora gayi.
  • Kurmanchal ki dharti: Maanyata hai ki “Kumaon” naam ka sambandh Kurma avatar se hai, jiski katha Champawat ke Kandev (Kaandadev) parvat se jodi jaati hai.
  • Shakti peeth ka kshetra: Purnagiri Mata ka mandir Tanakpur ke upar Annapurna shikhar par sthit hai aur ise 51 Shakti peethon me maana jata hai.

Etymology: Champawat Naam Ki Utpatti

Seedha jawab: “Champawat” naam ka sambandh lok-kathao ke anusar Champawati naam ki rajkumari se jaana jata hai, jo Raja Arjundev ki putri maani jati hai. Is naam ke pichhe ek se adhik lok-vyakhyayein prachalit hain, isliye ise “lok-itihas ke anusar” hi samajhna theek hai.

Pahadi zilo ke naam aksar do tarah se bante hain: ya to kisi devi/devta ke naam se, ya kisi raja, rani ya kisi bhaugolik visheshta se. Champawat ka naam doosri shreni me aata hai.

Prachalit maanyatayein:

  • Champawati ki katha: Lok-kathao me kaha jata hai ki Chand-pehle ke rajaon me se ek Raja Arjundev ki putri Champawati ke naam par is sthan ko Champawat kaha gaya. Isi tarah ka naam-kaaran Himalayan kasbon me aam hai.
  • Champa ke ped ka sambandh: Kuch logon ki maanyata hai ki yahan pehle Champa (Plumeria / Magnolia ke prakaar ke ped) ki bahutayat thi, par yeh vaigyanik rup se pramanit nahi hai aur isko sirf lok-vyakhya samjhein.
  • Kurma avatar aur Kandev: Champawat ke paas Kandev (Kaandadev) parvat ke sath maanyata judi hai ki Vishnu ke Kurma (kachhua) avatar ne yahan tapasya ke samay sthaan liya. Isi “Kurma” se “Kurmanchal” aur phir “Kumaon” shabd ki utpatti maani jati hai.

Sabhisaab Research Note: Naam-utpatti par itihaaskaron me ekmat nahi hai. Hum yahan sirf wahi vyakhyayein likh rahe hain jo Kumaon ke lok-itihas aur parampara me aam taur par sunayi jati hain.

Historical Timeline: Zila Kab Aur Kaise Bana

Seedha jawab: Champawat zila 15 September 1997 ko Uttar Pradesh ke samay Pithoragarh aur Nainital zile ke kuch hisson ko milakar bana. Baad me 9 November 2000 ko Uttarakhand rajya bana aur Champawat naye rajya ka zila ban gaya.

NIC ke zila parichay ke anusar, Champawat ko Pithoragarh aur Nainital zilon se kaat kar banaya gaya tha. Is faisle ka kaaran bhaugolik kathinaiyan, bikhri aabadi aur pahadi bhoo-sanrachna batayi gayi thi. Yani door-door ke pahadi ilake ko ek naye mukhyalaya se jodne ka prayas.

Champawat: Collectorate aur Revenue Prashasan

Zila Mukhyalaya, Vikas Bhawan aur Collectorate Ki Location

Seedha jawab: Champawat zile ka mukhyalaya Champawat shehar hai. Zila Magistrate (DM) ka karyalaya yahin Collectorate parisar me hai, aur sabhi mukhya zila-star ke vibhagon ke karyalaya isi shehar me milte hain.

Zile ke sabhi mukhya prashasnik kaam Champawat se hi chalte hain. Kisi bhi sarkari kaam ke liye jaise zameen ka record, certificate, pension ya shikayat, aapko kisi na kisi star par Champawat ya apne tehsil/block tak jaana padta hai.

Zila star par kaun-kaun se mukhya karyalaya hote hain (aam sthiti):

  • Zila Magistrate (DM) aur Collectorate
  • Mukhya Vikas Adhikari (CDO) ka karyalaya
  • Zila Panchayat karyalaya
  • Zila Sevayojan (Employment) karyalaya
  • Mukhya Chikitsa Adhikari (CMO) ka karyalaya
  • Zila Shiksha Adhikari ke karyalaya
  • Van Pradesh (Forest Division) ke karyalaya
  • Zila Aapda Prabandhan Pradhikaran (DDMA) ka control room

Dhyan Dein: Zila Magistrate aur anya adhikariyon ke naam har kuch saal me transfer par badalte hain. Taaza naam aur sampark ke liye zile ki official website champawat.nic.in dekhein.

Champawat shehar ki sthiti: Champawat ek chhota pahadi shehar hai. 2011 ki janganana me Champawat nagar ki aabadi lagbhag 4,801 thi, yani yeh ek bahut chhota zila-mukhyalaya hai. Yahi chhota aakaar iska aakarshan bhi hai: shant, thanda aur kam bheed wala.

Registry, certificate ya court me jaane se pehle apne gaon ki tehsil ki sahi pushti kar lein. Kai baar gaon ek tehsil se doosri tehsil me chala jata hai.

Kanungo Circle aur Patwari Halqa: Tehsil ke neeche revenue prashasan Kanungo circle aur phir Patwari halqa (ya chauki) me banta hota hai. Har gaon kisi na kisi Patwari halqe ke antargat aata hai. Aapki zameen ka khasra-khatauni, naap-jokh, dakhil-kharij jaise kaam isi Patwari aur Kanungo ke raaste se tehsil tak jaate hain.

Patwari Chowki: Rajasv Police aur Niyamit Police

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand ke pahadi ilakon me lambe samay tak “Revenue Police” pranali chali, jisme Patwari hi kuch aapradhik kaam bhi sambhalte the. Ab dheere-dheere kai kshetra regular police ke antargat aa rahe hain, par sthiti kshetra-dar alag ho sakti hai.

Pahadi Uttarakhand me British samay se ek khaas vyavastha chali aa rahi thi. Ye isliye hui kyunki pahad me gaon door-door the aur thane bahut kam. Isliye Patwari ek saath do bhoomikayen nibhata tha: revenue adhikari ki aur chhote-moti aapradhik maamlo me police adhikari ki.

Revenue Police ki khaas baatein (aam jaankari):

  • Patwari halqe ko “Patwari Chowki” kaha jata tha.
  • Patwari ko chhoti-moti shikayat darj karne aur jaanch ka adhikar milta tha.
  • Gambhir aparadh me maamla regular police ko bhejna padta tha.
  • Gaon walo ke liye Patwari ek sulabh sarkari vyakti hota tha, kyunki wo unke zameen ke records bhi rakhta tha.

Aaj ke samay me kya badla hai: Rajya aur adalat dono ne samay-samay par is pranali ko badalne ki baat ki hai aur kai ilakon me regular police vyavastha lagayi ja rahi hai. Champawat zile ke Terai-Bhabar ilake (Tanakpur, Banbasa ka aas-paas) me sthiti pahad ke andruni gaon se alag hai, kyunki yahan regular thane pehle se hain.

Practical Salah: Agar aapko apne gaon me koi shikayat darj karani ho to pehle apne nikattam thana ya Patwari chowki ka pata karein. Na pata ho to zila police ya 112 (emergency) par poochh lein. Kaunsa gaon kis police ya revenue police ke antargat hai, iski taaza suchi zile ke police karyalaya se milti hai.

Champawat: Gramin aur Panchayati Raj Vyavastha

Vikas Khand (Development Blocks) Ki List

Seedha jawab: NIC ke anusar Champawat zile me 4 vikas khand (C.D. Block) hain. Aam taur par inke naam Champawat, Lohaghat, Pati aur Barakot bataye jate hain. Vikas khand gramin vikas yojanaon ka sabse neeche ka prashasnik star hai.

Vikas Khand ka sabse bada adhikari Khand Vikas Adhikari (BDO) hota hai, jo gram panchayaton ke vikas ke kaam dekhta hai, jaise MGNREGA, awas, sadak, shauchalaya, pey-jal aur anya yojanaye.

Block se jude kaam jo aapko kabhi na kabhi padte hain:

  • Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana ke form
  • MGNREGA job card
  • Gram panchayat ke vikas ke prastav
  • Vridhavastha aur widow pension ka verification
  • Mahila Mangal Dal aur Self Help Group registration

Census ke samay kuch sources me “Poornagiri” ko bhi CD Block ki tarah dikhaya gaya hai, kyunki wahan block aur tehsil ka naam ek hi table me diya gaya tha. Sarkari vikas khand ki antim suchi ke liye zile ki official site dekhein.

Gram Panchayat sankhya ka antar kyun milta hai?

Isse aap par galat aankde ka aarop nahi lagta.

Nagar Panchayat aur Chhavni Parishad (Cantonment Board)

Seedha jawab: Champawat zile me koi Cantonment Board (Chhavni Parishad) nahi hai, yani zile me sainya chhavni ka alag nagar nikay nahi hai. Yahan sirf nagar panchayat aur ek nagar palika parishad hain.

Uttarakhand me Cantonment Board Dehradun, Roorkee, Ranikhet, Lansdowne, Almora jaise sainya kendron me hain. Champawat zile me aisi koi board nahi hai, par yeh zila Kumaon Regiment ke sainikon ka bada bharti kshetra zaroor hai, jiska zikr Sena , ITBP aur SSB Ki Maujoodgi me hai.

Nagar panchayat ka kaam: Nagar panchayat me safai, nali, street light, sarkari shauchalaya, jal-nikasi, nagar ke chhote bazaar aur kuch registration (janam-mrityu) jaisi suvidhayen aati hain. Chhote pahadi kasbon ke liye yeh sabse kareebi prashasnik ikai hoti hai.

Champawat: Rajnitik aur Chunavi Parichay

Lok Sabha Kshetra aur Vartaman Sansadiya Pratinidhitva

Seedha jawab: Champawat zila Almora (SC, Anusuchit Jati ke liye aarakshit) Lok Sabha kshetra ka hissa hai. 2024 ke Lok Sabha chunav me is seat se Bharatiya Janata Party ke Ajay Tamta saansad chune gaye.

Almora Lok Sabha seat Uttarakhand ki 5 Lok Sabha seats me se ek hai aur Anusuchit Jati ke liye aarakshit hai. Isme Almora, Bageshwar, Pithoragarh aur Champawat zilon ke vidhan sabha kshetra aate hain.

Zile Se Nikle Pramukh Rajnitik aur Samajik Andolan

Seedha jawab: Champawat se judi sabse badi andolan-smritiyan Uttarakhand rajya andolan (1994), Kumaon ka Kuli-Begar virodh, Van-adhikar andolan aur Sharda aur Pancheshwar jaisi bandh-pariyojanaon se judi bahas hain.

  • Uttarakhand Rajya Andolan (1990 ke dashak): Poore Kumaon ki tarah Champawat kshetra ke yuvao, mahilao aur purva-sainikon ne alag rajya ki maang me bhaag liya. Sthaniya shaheedon ke naam ke liye zile ki Shaheed Smarak suchi dekhein.
  • Kuli-Begar andolan ki smriti: 1921 me Uttarayani mele ke samay Bageshwar me Kuli-Begar pratha ke virodh ka andolan Kumaon ka itihas badalne wala tha. Is andolan ki lehar Champawat jaise sabhi Kumaoni ilakon tak pahunchi.
  • Pancheshwar Bahuuddeshiya Pariyojana par bahas: Mahakali nadi par prastavit Pancheshwar baandh ke visthapan, paryavaran aur vikas par sthaniya sthar par lambe samay se bahas chalti rahi hai. Is pariyojana par pura jaankari Champawat: Bijli-Pariyojana, Dam Aur Nawikarniya Urja aur Champawat: Aane Wali Badi Pariyojanayein me milegi.
  • Van Panchayat aur ghaas-lakdi ke adhikar: Gaon-gaon me Van Panchayat vyavastha ke adhikaron ka sangharsh bhi sthaniya rajneeti ka hissa raha hai.

Sena (Kumaon Regiment), ITBP aur SSB Ki Maujoodgi

Seedha jawab: Champawat zila Kumaon Regiment me sainikon ki bharti ke liye jaana jata hai. Seema ke kaaran Nepal-seema par Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) ki maujoodgi rahti hai. Champawat Indo-Nepal seema ka pravesh sthal hai.

Kumaon Regiment ka mukhyalaya Ranikhet me hai, par Champawat, Pithoragarh aur Almora jaise zilon ke yuvao ki sena me bharti ki lambi parampara hai. Yahan har gaon me kisi na kisi parivar ka koi sadasya sena ya ardh-sainik bal me hota hai. Isi wajah se pahadi arthvyavastha ko “Money Order Economy” bhi kaha jata hai, jiska zikr Champawat: Zile Ki Arthvyavastha, Per Capita Income Aur Rozgar me hai.

Nepal seema: Champawat ka purvi hissa Mahakali (Sharda) nadi ke saath Nepal ko chhoota hai. Banbasa se Nepal ke Mahendranagar ka rasta bahut prasiddh hai. Seema par suraksha ki zimmedari SSB ke paas hoti hai, jo Indo-Nepal seema ki rakhwali ka kaam karta hai.

Yatri Salah: Nepal seema ki taraf jate samay apna Aadhaar, Voter ID ya passport zaroor rakhein, kyunki seema par checking hoti hai. Bina valid pehchan ke seema paar karne ki koshish na karein.

Champawat: Nyayik aur Kanooni Dhancha

Zila Adalat, Family Court aur Tehsil Legal Clinic

Seedha jawab: Champawat zile ki zila adalat ka kendra Champawat me hai. Anya nyayik kaam ke liye Lohaghat aur Tanakpur me bhi nyayik sthaan ho sakte hain. Zila Vidhik Seva Pradhikaran (DLSA) se gareeb logo ko muft kanooni sahayata mil sakti hai.

Muft kanooni madad kaise milti hai (DLSA): Zila Vidhik Seva Pradhikaran (District Legal Services Authority) aise logo ko muft vakil dilata hai jinki aay kam hai ya jo mahila, bachche, Anusuchit Jati/Janjati, durghatna peedit ya anya patra shreni me aate hain. Isse zameen-jaydad ya parivarik vivadon me aam aadmi ko bahut rahat milti hai.

Pahadi zameen vivad me aam sawal:

  • Bhai-bhai ke beech baant ka vivad (Hissedari)
  • Rasta aur nali ka vivad
  • Gol Khata (sanjha khata) ka bantwara
  • Mritak ke naam se khatauni ka namantaran

Sabhisaab Salah: Zameen ke vivad me pehle tehsil aur revenue adalat (SDM court) ka raasta dekhna chahiye. Civil court jaane se pehle apne gaon ke Patwari se khatauni ki nakal aur tehsil se hissedari ki jaankari le lein.

Sub-Registrar Karyalay (Zameen Registry)

Seedha jawab: Champawat zile me zameen ki registry sub-registrar karyalayon me hoti hai, jo zile ke mukhya tehsil kendron par sthit hote hain. Registry ke liye stamp duty aur circle rate ka pehle hi hisaab laga lena chahiye.

Registry karwane se pehle ye sab cheezein tay kar lein:

  • Khatauni aur khasra ki taaza nakal (Bhulekh se nikalen)
  • Seller aur buyer ki pehchan aur photo
  • Circle rate (Champawat: Property Registry, Stamp Duty Aur Circle Rate) aur stamp duty ka hisaab
  • NOC ya anya anumati agar kisi kshetra me zaroori ho
  • Gawah (witness) ki pehchan

Zaroori Chetavni: Uttarakhand me bahari logo ke liye zameen kharidne ke niyam alag hain. Is vishay par poori jankari Champawat: Zameen Kharidne Ke Niyam aur Dhara 143 me hai. Registry se pehle us section ko dhyan se padhein.

Champawat: Aapda Prabandhan aur Aapatkaleen Dhancha

DDMA Control Room aur Toll-Free Helpline

Seedha jawab: Zila Aapda Prabandhan Pradhikaran (DDMA) ka control room zile ki aapda ke samay main kendra hota hai. Zila stariya aapda helpline 1077 hai. Rajya-star par 1070 aur 112 bhi kaam karte hain.

Uttarakhand bhookamp, bhoo-skhalan (landslide), baadal fatne (cloudburst), baadh aur jangal ki aag, in sabke liye sanvedansheel rajya hai. Champawat me khaas taur par barsat me sadak band hone, Sharda nadi ki baadh aur jangal ki aag (garmi me) sabse zyada dekhi jati hain.

Aapda ke samay kya karein:

  • Pehle khud surakshit sthaan par jayein.
  • 112 ya 1077 par call karein.
  • Apni location (gaon, tehsil, najdeeki sthal) saaf bataein.
  • Nadi ya nale ke kinare na rukein.
  • Sarkari alert aur SMS par dhyan dein.

SDRF/NDRF Base, Baadh Shelter aur Helipad

Seedha jawab: Rajya aapda pratikriya bal (SDRF) aur zarurat padne par NDRF ki madad aapda me li jaati hai. Baadh ke samay Sharda nadi ke kinare Tanakpur aur Banbasa ke ilake sabse zyada sanvedansheel maane jate hain.

SDRF/NDRF ki exact tainaati aur shelter ki list suraksha karno se samay-samay par badalti rehti hai. Isliye hum yahan sirf aam jaankari de rahe hain. Taaza suchi ke liye zila aapda prabandhan adhikari ya champawat.nic.in dekhein.

Barsat me yatra ke liye aapda se bachne ke niyam: Julai se September ke beech lambi pahadi yatra se bachein. Sadak band hone ka report pehle se dekh lein aur raat ko pahadi sadak par gaadi na chalayein.

RTO Code UK-03, Karyalaya aur License Seva

Seedha jawab: Champawat zile ka vahan panjikaran code UK-03 hai. Champawat me RTO ka karyalaya hai aur Tanakpur me bhi Sub Regional/Assistant Regional Transport Office (ARTO) ka karyalaya hai, jo UK-03 code hi istemal karta hai.

RTO timing, fees aur contact number badalte rehte hain. Rajya parivahan vibhag ki official site (transport.uk.gov.in) se jaankari ki pushti karein.

Pahadi Driving Rules aur Green Cess

Seedha jawab: Pahadi sadakon par left-side rule, upar jane wali gaadi ko pehle raasta dene ka niyam aur mod par horn bajane ki parampara maani jati hai. Rajya me bahari vahano par Green Cess jaisa shulk lagu ho sakta hai, jiski sthiti samay-samay par badalti hai.

Pahadi gaadi chalane ke 10 suvarn niyam:

  • Pahad ki sadak par upar chadhti gaadi ko priority mili hoti hai, neeche utarti gaadi ko ruk kar raasta dena chahiye.
  • Mod par horn bajayein.
  • Barsat me bhu-skhalan wale hisson me na rukein.
  • Pichli raat ka mausam aur sadak ki sthiti jaruri dekhein.
  • Neeche utarte samay engine brake (low gear) ka istemal karein, sirf brake par nirbhar na rahein.
  • Raat ko lambi pahadi drive se bachein.
  • Jangli janwar (tendua, bhalu, haathi) sadak par aa sakte hain. Gaadi dheere chalayein.
  • Sardi me black ice aur kohre me saavdhani rakhein.
  • Petrol pump pehle se dekh kar chalein, pahad me aage ka petrol pump door ho sakta hai.
  • Ek spare tyre aur basic tool ka saman rakhein.

Green Cess ki sthiti: Uttarakhand me bahari rajyo ki kuch shreniyon ki gaadiyon par shulk lagane ki vyavastha samay-samay par badalti rahi hai. Yatra se pehle rajya parivahan vibhag ya “Uttarakhand Green Cess” ki taaza notification dekh lein.

Champawat District Ke Baare Me Sabse Zyada Puchhe Jane Wale Sawal

Champawat district kab bana?
Champawat zila 15 September 1997 ko bana.
Champawat kis zile se kaat kar bana?
NIC ke anusar yeh Pithoragarh aur Nainital zile ke hisson se milakar banaya gaya. Aam taur par ise Pithoragarh se alag hua zila bhi kaha jata hai.
Champawat zile ka mukhyalaya kya hai?
Champawat shehar.
Champawat me kitne tehsil hain?
NIC ke anusar 5 tehsil aur 2 up-tehsil. Census 2011 ke samay 4 tehsil the: Champawat, Lohaghat, Pati, Purnagiri.

Champawat me kitne block hain? 4 vikas khand.

Champawat ki jansankhya kitni hai?
Census 2011 ke anusar 2,59,648.
Champawat me kitne gaon hain?
Census me 717 (van gaon sahit) aur NIC ke anusar 705. Dono ginti ka tarika alag hai.
Champawat ka RTO code kya hai?
UK-03.

Champawat ka PIN code kya hai? 262523 (Champawat shehar). Tanakpur ka 262309.

Champawat ka STD code kya hai? 05965 (Champawat, Lohaghat); 05943 (Tanakpur, Banbasa).

Champawat kaun si Lok Sabha seat me aata hai?
Almora (SC).
Champawat me kitni vidhan sabha seats hain?
Do: Champawat (55) aur Lohaghat (54).
Kya Champawat me Nagar Nigam hai?
Nahi. Yahan Tanakpur Nagar Palika Parishad aur Champawat, Lohaghat Nagar Panchayat hain.
Champawat ka upnaam kya hai?
Kali Kumaon.
Champawat Nepal ke kitne paas hai?
Zile ki purvi seema Mahakali (Sharda) nadi se Nepal ko chhoti hai. Banbasa se Nepal ka Mahendranagar seema paar ka sabse aam raasta hai.
