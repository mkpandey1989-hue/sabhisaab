"Meri lambai ke hisaab se kitna wazan hona chahiye?" — sabse aam sawaal. Neeche lambai aur ling daaliye. Par neeche ka hissa zaroor padhiye, kyunki ye number jitna lagta hai utna pakka nahi hai.

Hisaab kaise lagta hai

Ye calculator Devine formula par chalta hai, jo sabse zyada istemaal hota hai:

Purush: 50 kg + 2.3 kg har inch ke liye jo 5 feet se upar hai
Mahila: 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg har inch ke liye jo 5 feet se upar hai

Lambai ke hisaab se anuman:

Ek baat gaur kariye — ye formula asal me dawa ki khuraak tay karne ke liye banaya gaya tha, sehat ka paimana banane ke liye nahi. Ye baad me aam istemaal me aa gaya.

Ek number kyun poora sach nahi hai

Do log ek hi lambai aur ek hi wazan ke ho sakte hain — ek khilaadi, doosra bilkul vyayam na karne wala. Wazan wahi, sehat bilkul alag.

Wajah ye hai ki wazan sirf ek number hai. Wo ye nahi batata ki us wazan me kitna maans-peshi hai aur kitni charbi. Aur sehat ke liye yahi antar sabse zyada mayne rakhta hai.

Isi liye "aadarsh wazan" ko ek daayra maaniye, ek number nahi. Upar wale number se 4-5 kg upar-neeche rehna bilkul theek hai — basha ye ki aap thakte na hon, kaam aaraam se hota ho aur report theek aati ho.

Ye cheezein number ko sahi tarah badalti hain: shareer ka dhaancha (chaudi haddi wale ka wazan zyada hoga aur wo theek hai) · maans-peshi kitni hai · umar · kheti ya mazdoori jaisa mehnat wala kaam.

Wazan se behtar paimane

Sirf taraazu par bharosa mat kijiye. Ye teen cheezein sehat ki zyada sachi tasveer deti hain:

  • Kamar ka naap — Bharatiyon ke liye sabse kaam ka number. Purush me 90 cm aur mahila me 80 cm se upar hone par khatra badhta hai, chahe wazan theek dikhe. Pet ki charbi sabse zyada nuksan karti hai.
  • Kamar aur lambai ka anupaat — kamar, lambai ke aadhe se kam honi chahiye. Ye BMI se bhi behtar paimana maana jaata hai. Waist-to-Height Calculator me dekhiye.
  • Khoon ki jaanch — sugar, cholesterol, BP. Ye taraazu se kahin zyada sach bolte hain.
Ek baat jo Bharat me khaas hai — hum log kam wazan par bhi diabetes aur dil ki bimari ke zyada khatre me hote hain, kyunki charbi pet ke aas-paas jama hoti hai. Isliye "wazan to theek hai" sunkar nishchint ho jaana galti hai. Kamar ka naap zaroor dekhiye.

Wazan theek karna hai to

Agar aapka wazan is daayre se kaafi upar ya neeche hai, to jaldbaazi sabse bada dushman hai.

Jo sach me kaam karta hai:

  • Dheeme badlaav — hafte me aadha kilo. Isse zyada tezi se ghataya wazan lagbhag hamesha wapas aata hai.
  • Ghar ka khaana — daal, sabzi, roti, dahi. Koi mehnga diet plan iss se behtar nahi hai.
  • Chalna — roz 30-40 minute. Gym ki zaroorat nahi.
  • Neend — 7-8 ghante. Kam neend bhookh badha deti hai, aur ye asar bahut bada hota hai.
  • Meetha peena band — cold drink, packet juice. Ye sabse aasaan aur sabse bada sudhaar hai.
Jo bilkul mat kijiye: khaana chhodna, ek hi cheez khaana, ya bina doctor ke wazan ghatane ki dawa/powder lena. Ye kuch hafte kaam karte dikhte hain aur baad me sehat ka nuksan kar jaate hain.

Agar wazan ko lekar mann pareshaan rehta ho, ya khaane ko lekar tanaav hota ho — to ye sirf wazan ki baat nahi rehti. Aise me doctor ya kisi bharosemand insaan se baat karna sabse zaroori kadam hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Ideal weight ka formula kya hai?

Devine formula: purush ke liye 50 kg + 2.3 kg har inch (5 feet se upar), mahila ke liye 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg. Ye ek anuman hai, pakka niyam nahi.

Is number se 5 kg zyada hoon, chinta ki baat hai?

Aam taur par nahi. Ise daayra maaniye, ek number nahi. Zyada zaroori hai kamar ka naap aur khoon ki jaanch — wo sehat ki zyada sachi tasveer dete hain.

Wazan theek hai par pet nikla hua hai — theek hai?

Ye dhyaan dene layak hai. Bharatiyon me pet ki charbi kam wazan par bhi khatra badhati hai. Purush me kamar 90 cm aur mahila me 80 cm se upar hone par doctor se baat kariye.

Wazan kitni tezi se ghatana chahiye?

Hafte me lagbhag aadha kilo. Isse tez ghataya wazan lagbhag hamesha wapas aa jaata hai, aur shareer ko nuksan bhi hota hai.
