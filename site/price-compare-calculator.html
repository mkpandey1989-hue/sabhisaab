Hisaab aur ek chaunkane wala sach

Per unit daam = Pack ka daam ÷ Quantity

Upar wale case me:

  • 500 ml @ ₹100 = ₹0.20 per ml
  • 900 ml @ ₹170 = ₹0.189 per ml — ye sasta hai

Ab ek doosra case: 1 kg @ ₹180, aur 5 kg @ ₹950.

  • 1 kg = ₹180 per kg
  • 5 kg = ₹190 per kg — bada pack mehnga hai
Yahi wo baat hai jo log nahi jaante — bada pack hamesha sasta nahi hota. Dukaan aur online store jaante hain ki log "bada matlab sasta" maan lete hain, aur kai baar bade pack par margin zyada rakh dete hain. Ye kanoon ke khilaaf nahi hai, bas hamari aadat ka faayda hai.

Kahan-kahan ye sabse zyada kaam aata hai

  • Ration ka saman — tel, atta, chawal, daal, cheeni. Yahan mahine me sabse zyada paisa jaata hai, isliye bachat bhi sabse zyada.
  • Sabun, shampoo, detergent, toothpaste — yahan pack size ki sabse zyada variety hoti hai aur per-unit daam sabse zyada upar-neeche.
  • Doodh aur dahi — chhote packet aksar kaafi mehnge padte hain.
  • Diaper aur baby saman — yahan antar bahut bada hota hai.
  • Mobile recharge — per din ka daam nikaal kar dekhiye, plan alag tasveer dikhate hain.
  • Online "combo offer" — 3 ka pack alag se khareedne se aksar mehnga nikalta hai.
Ek achhi baat — kanoon ke hisaab se packet par per unit daam (per kg ya per litre) chhapa hona chahiye. Wo chhote akshar me piche ya neeche hota hai. Ek baar dekhna seekh lijiye, phir calculator ki bhi zaroorat nahi padegi.

Sasta hamesha behtar nahi hota

Per unit daam ek number hai, poora faisla nahi. In baaton ko bhi jodiye:

  • Kharab hone wali cheez — 5 litre tel sasta hai par agar 2 mahine me kharab ho gaya, to sasta nahi raha. Utna hi lijiye jitna samay par khatam ho.
  • Expiry date — bada pack aksar isliye sasta hota hai kyunki expiry paas hai. Dabba palat kar dekh lijiye.
  • Rakhne ki jagah — ghar me jagah nahi hai to bada pack pareshani hai.
  • Zyada hone par zyada istemaal — ye sach hai. Bada shampoo aane par log zyada nikaalte hain. Bachat kagaz par reh jaati hai.
  • Ek saath bada paisa — agar mahine ka budget tight hai, to ₹950 ek saath dena mushkil ho sakta hai chahe wo per kg sasta ho.

Aur ek baat — quantity ki ikai ek hi rakhiye jab tulna kar rahe hon. Ek ko gram me aur doosre ko kilo me daalne par jawab galat aayega. Dono gram me, ya dono kilo me.

Dukaan ke wo tarike jo dhokha dete hain

Ye jaan lena kisi bhi calculator se zyada kaam ka hai:

  • Pack chhota, daam wahi. Sabse aam tareeka. Pehle 1 litre ₹120 me tha, ab 900 ml ₹120 me. Daam nahi badha, par per unit 11% mehnga ho gaya. Isse "shrinkflation" kehte hain aur ye lagbhag har cheez me hota hai.
  • "Buy 1 Get 1" par pehle se badha daam. Offer se pehle ka bhaav yaad rakhiye.
  • "20% extra free" — nikaal kar dekhiye, kai baar normal pack hi sasta hota hai.
  • Aankhon ke star par mehnga saman — sasta aksar neeche ya sabse upar wale rack par rakha jaata hai. Jhukiye aur dekhiye.
  • Combo aur hamper — alag-alag lene par aksar sasta padta hai.
Sabse aasaan aadat — ration ki 5-6 cheezein jo har mahine aati hain, unka per unit daam ek baar likh kar phone me rakh lijiye. Phir har baar hisaab nahi lagana padega, aur daam badhte hi turant pata chal jayega. Ek parivaar ke liye ye saal bhar me hazaaron ki bachat hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Bada pack hamesha sasta hota hai?

Nahi. Kai baar bade pack par margin zyada rakha jaata hai. Per unit daam nikaal kar hi pata chalta hai — aur kai baar chhota pack sasta nikalta hai.

Per unit daam packet par likha hota hai?

Haan, kanoon ke hisaab se per kg ya per litre daam chhapa hona chahiye. Wo chhote akshar me piche ya neeche hota hai.

Tulna karte waqt kya dhyaan rakhein?

Dono ki quantity ek hi ikai me daaliye — dono gram me ya dono kilo me. Aur expiry date aur samay par khatam hoga ya nahi, ye bhi dekhiye.

Shrinkflation kya hai?

Pack chhota kar dena par daam wahi rakhna. 1 litre ki jagah 900 ml, daam ₹120 hi — yaani per unit 11% mehnga. Ye lagbhag har cheez me hota hai.
