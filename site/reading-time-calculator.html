Reading Time Calculator — Padhne Me Kitna Samay

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~5 minute

Samay ka hisaab kaise lagta hai

Padhne ka samay = Shabd ÷ Raftaar (shabd per minute)

Aam raftaar:

KyaShabd/minute
Mann me padhna (aam)200-250
Dheeme ya mushkil vishay130-150
Tez padhne wale300+
Zor se bolna (bhaashan)120-140
Khabar padhne wale (news)150-160

Bolne ki raftaar padhne se kaafi kam hoti hai — kyunki bolte waqt rukna padta hai, saans leni padti hai, aur zor dena padta hai. Isi liye ek hi text padhne me 4 minute aur bolne me 7 minute le sakta hai.

Bhaashan aur presentation ke liye

Ye page ka sabse kaam ka istemaal yahi hai. Mota anuman:

5 minute~650

Ek zaroori salah — jitna samay mila hai, uska 80% ka bhaashan likhiye. 5 minute mile to 520 shabd, 650 nahi. Kyunki stage par log dheere bolte hain, beech me rukte hain, taali bajti hai, aur ghabraahat me kuch dohra bhi jaata hai. Poora samay bhar dene wale hamesha samay se aage nikal jaate hain.

Aur ek baat — likhkar zaroor bolkar dekhiye, ghadi ke saath. Kaagaz par 5 minute ka lagne wala bhaashan bolne par 7 minute ka nikal sakta hai. Ye ek abhyaas bahut sharmindagi bacha leta hai.

Blog aur website ke liye

Aaj kal zyadatar blog upar likhte hain "5 min read". Iska faayda ye hai ki padhne wale ko pehle se pata hota hai ki kitna samay lagega — aur wo aksar padhna shuru kar deta hai.

  • 800 shabd — lagbhag 4 minute
  • 1,200 shabd — lagbhag 6 minute
  • 2,000 shabd — lagbhag 10 minute
Hindi aur Hinglish ke liye ek antar hai — Hinglish padhne me thodi dheemi hoti hai, kyunki dimaag ko Roman akshar padh kar Hindi ka shabd banana padta hai. Isliye Hinglish content me 200 ki jagah 170-180 shabd per minute maanna zyada sahi rehta hai.

Aur ye number sirf shabd ginti se nahi banta. Tasveer, table aur list padhne wale ko rokte hain. Agar page me bahut table hain to asli samay thoda zyada lagega.

Padhne ki raftaar badhana

Speed reading ke bade-bade daave bahut hote hain. Sach ye hai ki raftaar thodi badhayi ja sakti hai, par samajh ki keemat par nahi.

Jo sach me kaam karta hai:

  • Mann me bolna kam kariye. Zyadatar log padhte waqt mann me shabd bolte hain — isi se raftaar bandh jaati hai. Sirf aankh se dekhne ka abhyaas kariye.
  • Ungli ya pen se line follow kariye. Aankh bhatakti nahi aur wahi line dobara nahi padhi jaati.
  • Ek-ek shabd ki jagah tukdon me dekhiye — 3-4 shabd ek saath.
  • Pehle poore page par nazar daaliye — heading aur list dekh lijiye, phir padhiye. Dimaag ko pehle se dhaancha mil jaata hai.
Par ek imaandaar baat — har cheez tez nahi padhi jaani chahiye. Kanooni kaagaz, kisi bhi agreement, bank ka form, ya bima ki policy — ye dheere aur do baar padhne wali cheezein hain. Yahan tezi seedha nuksan karti hai.

Shabd aur akshar ki poori ginti Word Counter me hai. Aur likhne ki raftaar naapni ho to Typing Speed Calculator dekhiye.

Padhne ki raftaar — kis cheez me kitni

Ek hi raftaar sab par nahi lagti. Kahani tez padhi jaati hai, aur karar (agreement) bahut dheere. Reading time ka anuman isi liye badal jaata hai.

Kis tarah ka lekhShabd/minute (lagbhag)
Kahani, blog, khabar220 – 260
Padhai ki kitaab150 – 200
Technical ya kanooni kagaz80 – 130
Zor se bolkar padhna130 – 150
Video ya podcast ki script likh rahe hain to yahi aakhri line kaam ki hai. Bolne ki raftaar padhne se kaafi dheemi hoti hai — lagbhag 130-150 shabd per minute. Iska matlab 10 minute ke video ke liye lagbhag 1,300 se 1,500 shabd ki script chahiye, 2,500 nahi. Blog par dikhne wala "5 min read" chup-chaap padhne ke hisaab se hota hai, isliye usi lekh ko bolne me lagbhag dugna samay lagta hai. Aur ek baat: table aur number wale hisse padhne me dher zyada samay lete hain, kyunki aankh ruk kar tulna karti hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Padhne ki aam raftaar kitni hoti hai?

Mann me padhne ki aam raftaar 200-250 shabd per minute hai. Zor se bolne ki 120-140 — isi liye bolne me zyada samay lagta hai.

5 minute ke bhaashan me kitne shabd?

Lagbhag 650. Par 520 ke aas-paas likhiye — stage par log dheere bolte hain aur beech me rukna padta hai.

Hinglish padhne ka samay alag hota hai?

Haan, thoda zyada. Hinglish me 200 ki jagah 170-180 shabd per minute maanna zyada sahi rehta hai.

Padhne ki raftaar kaise badhaye?

Mann me bolna kam kariye, ungli se line follow kariye aur shabdon ko tukdon me dekhiye. Par agreement, form aur policy hamesha dheere padhiye.
