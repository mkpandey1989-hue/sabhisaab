SIP Kya Hai? Poori Jaankari Aur Free Calculator

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026

SIP yaani Systematic Investment Plan, aaj ke samay me nivesh ka sabse aasaan aur lokpriya tareeka ban chuka hai. Isme aap ek saath bada paisa lagane ke bajaye, har mahine ek chhoti nishchit rakam (jaise ₹500, ₹1000 ya ₹5000) mutual fund me lagate hain. Is page par hum SIP ko bilkul aasaan bhasha me samjhenge, aur aap neeche diye gaye free SIP calculator se apne nivesh ka anumanit future value bhi nikaal sakte hain.

SIP kaise kaam karta hai?

Jab aap SIP shuru karte hain, to har mahine aapke bank account se ek tay rakam apne aap kat kar mutual fund me lag jaati hai. Us paise se aapko fund ke "units" milte hain. Jab market neeche hota hai to zyada units milte hain, jab upar hota hai to kam. Is tarah lambe samay me aapki average cost santulit ho jaati hai — ise rupee cost averaging kehte hain. Yahi SIP ki sabse badi taakat hai.

Compounding — SIP ka asli jaadu

SIP me sabse bada fayda chakravriddhi (compounding) ka hota hai. Iska matlab hai ki aapke paise par jo return milta hai, wo return bhi aage return kamata hai. Jitna lamba samay, utna zyada asar. Isi liye kaha jaata hai — SIP me jaldi shuru karna aur lambe samay tak jamā rehna sabse zaroori hai.

Ek misaal: Agar aap har mahine ₹5,000, 20 saal tak, 12% average return par lagate hain, to aap kul ₹12 lakh jamā karenge — par compounding ke chalte wo lagbhag ₹50 lakh tak ho sakta hai (anuman). Yahi chhoti-chhoti rakam ka bada natija hai.

SIP ke mukhya fayde

  • Chhoti shuruat: Sirf ₹500/mahina se bhi shuru kar sakte hain.
  • Anushasan: Har mahine apne aap katta hai, isliye bachat ki aadat banti hai.
  • Market timing ki tension nahi: Kab market upar-neeche, ye sochne ki zaroorat nahi.
  • Lachkeela: Kabhi bhi rakam badha, ghata ya rok sakte hain.
  • Compounding: Lambe samay me paisa tezi se badhta hai.

Dhyan rakhne wali baatein

SIP ka return guaranteed nahi hota — ye market par depend karta hai. Calculator sirf ek anuman deta hai, taaki aapko andaaza ho. Asli return zyada ya kam ho sakta hai. Isliye apne lakshya (goal), risk lene ki kshamta aur samay ke hisaab se hi fund chunein, aur zarurat ho to ek registered financial advisor se salah lein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Kya SIP me paisa doob sakta hai?

SIP mutual fund me lagta hai jo market se juda hota. Short term me value ghat-badh sakti, par lambe samay (5-10 saal+) me aam taur par acha rehta. Return guaranteed nahi hota.

Sabse kam kitne se SIP shuru kar sakte hain?

Kai fund ₹100-500/mahina se bhi SIP allow karte hain.

Kya SIP kabhi bhi band kar sakte hain?

Haan, SIP kabhi bhi rok ya pause kar sakte hain, isme koi penalty nahi hoti (kuch ELSS fund me lock-in hota).

SIP aur lumpsum me kya farak hai?

SIP me thoda-thoda har mahine lagta, lumpsum me ek saath poora. Dono ke apne fayde hain.

Rupee cost averaging — SIP ka asli jaadu

SIP ki sabse badi khoobi return nahi, balki ye hai ki wo aapko market ka samay pakadne ki zaroorat se azaad kar deti hai.

Jab market oopar hota hai, aapki tay rakam se kam units milte hain. Jab market neeche hota hai, usi rakam se zyada units mil jaate hain. Saalon me aapki average kharid keemat apne aap santulit ho jaati hai. Isi ko rupee cost averaging kehte hain.

Isi liye: girta market SIP ka dushman nahi, dost hai. Wahi waqt hota hai jab aap saste me zyada kharid rahe hote hain. Us waqt SIP band karna sabse badi galti hai.

Step-up SIP — sabse kam istemaal hone wala hathiyar

Zyadatar log SIP ek baar shuru karke saalon tak wahi rakam chalate rehte hain, jabki unki salary badhti rehti hai. Agar aap har saal apni SIP sirf 10% badha dein, to 15-20 saal me corpus lagbhag dugna ho sakta hai — bina kisi extra mehnat ke.

Kai fund house me "step-up" ya "top-up" ka vikalp hota hai jo apne aap har saal rakam badha deta hai. Ek baar laga dein, phir bhoolna bhi nahi padta.

Kaunsa fund chunein

Hum kisi khaas fund ki sifarish nahi karte, par kuch aam baatein samajh lena zaroori hai:

  • Samay 5 saal se kam — equity fund se bachein; debt ya hybrid behtar
  • Samay 7-10 saal se zyada — equity fund aam taur par is samay-seema me achha karte hain
  • Expense ratio — jitna kam, utna behtar. Chhota sa farak bhi 20 saal me bada ho jaata hai
  • Direct plan — regular se sasta padta hai kyunki usme commission nahi hoti
  • 3-4 fund kaafi hain — 10 fund rakhne se diversification nahi, sirf confusion badhta hai

Tax ka hisaab

Mutual fund par tax tab lagta hai jab aap units bechte hain — har saal nahi. Equity aur debt fund ke niyam alag hote hain, aur samay-samay par badalte rehte hain. ELSS fund me 3 saal ka lock-in hota hai par 80C me chhoot milti hai (Old Regime me).

SIP me har kist alag nivesh mani jaati hai, isliye bechte waqt har kist ka samay alag gina jaata hai. Badi rakam nikalte waqt CA se salah lena behtar rehta hai.

SIP me 5 aam galtiyan

  1. Market girte hi SIP band karna — sabse mehngi galti
  2. Har saal fund badalna — fund ko kaam karne ka samay dena zaroori hai
  3. Bina lakshya ke SIP — pehle tay karein paisa kis liye hai, phir samay aur fund chunein
  4. Salary badhne par SIP na badhana
  5. Emergency fund banaye bina SIP shuru karna — phir dikkat par SIP hi todni padti hai
