Weight Loss Ki Basic Samajh: Calorie, Protein Aur Neend

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~8 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Internet crash diet aur "7 din me 5 kilo" ke jhaanse se bhara hai. Sach ye hai ki vazan ghatana ek simple vigyaan par tika hai — calorie deficit, protein, neend aur tikaoo aadatein. Ye topic guide bina kisi jhaanse ke, sehatmand aur tikaoo tarika samjhaati hai jo sach me lambe samay tak kaam karta hai.

➤ TDEE Calculator se apni calorie jaanein

Vazan ghatane ka asli usool

Sabse pehli sacchai — vazan tabhi ghatta hai jab aap jitni calorie jalate hain, usse kam khaate hain. Ise calorie deficit kehte hain. Koi bhi "magic diet" isi usool par kaam karta hai, chahe wo maane ya na maane.

Pehle apna TDEE (roz ki calorie zaroorat) nikaalein, phir usse ~500 calorie kam khaayein — hafte me lagbhag aadha kilo ghatega. Ye dheera lagta hai, par yahi tikaoo hai. Tez ghatana (crash diet) aksar wapas aa jaata hai aur sehat bigaadta hai, isliye jaldbaazi se bachein.

Protein sabse zaroori

Deficit me sirf vazan nahi, muscle bhi ghat sakta hai — aur hum wo nahi chahte. Isse bachne ka tarika hai protein. Vazan ghatate waqt kam se kam 1.6-2 gram protein per kg body weight lein.

Protein do kaam karta hai — muscle bachata hai aur pet zyada der bhara rakhta hai (bhookh kam). Indian diet me protein aksar kam reh jaata hai. Dal, paneer, ande, chicken, soya, dahi, sprouts — inhe har meal me shaamil karein. Shakahari log bhi in cheezon se aaram se protein poora kar sakte hain.

Neend aur paani ka role

Ye do cheezein log sabse zyada ignore karte hain. Kam neend (7 ghante se kam) bhookh badhane wale hormone badha deti hai — agle din zyada khaane ka mann karta hai, khaaskar meetha aur junk.

7-8 ghante ki achhi neend vazan ghatane me seedha madad karti hai. Paani bhi zaroori — kai baar pyaas ko bhookh samajh lete hain. Khaane se pehle paani peene se portion control bhi hota hai. Sirf diet nahi, ye "chhoti" aadatein milkar bade result deti hain.

Exercise: kitni zaroori?

Vazan ghatane me 80% kaam diet ka hai, 20% exercise ka — par exercise phir bhi zaroori hai. Ye muscle bachati hai, sehat sudharti hai aur mood behtar karti hai.

Shuruaat aasaan rakhein — roz 30-40 minute tez walk se hi kaafi farak padta hai. Muscle bachaane ke liye hafte me 2-3 din weight ya resistance training jodein. Sirf cardio (running) par nahi, thodi strength training bhi karein. Jo exercise aap lambe samay tak kar sakein, wahi aapke liye sabse achhi hai.

Aam galtiyan aur tikaoo aadatein

Log yahan phisalte hain:

  • Crash diet — bahut kam khaana, phir chhod dena. Vazan wapas aa jaata hai.
  • Har hafte plan badalna — sharir ko samay dein, 3-4 hafte ek plan par rahein.
  • "Healthy" junk — kuch cheezein sehatmand dikhti hain par calorie zyada (jaise packaged juice, zyada matra me dry fruit).
  • Sirf weighing machine dekhna — vazan roz upar-neeche hota hai (paani ki wajah se); hafte ka ausat dekhein.

Sabse zaroori — tikaoo aadatein banayein, na ki temporary diet. Dheere, lagataar badlav hi asli aur permanent result deta hai. Sabr rakhein — sehatmand vazan ek din me nahi ghatta.

Shuruaat ka sabse pehla number hai aapka TDEE — kitni calorie par aapka wazan na badhta hai na ghatta. TDEE Calculator se wo nikaal lijiye, phir Macro Calculator se protein-carb-fat ka baantwara tay kijiye. Bina ye do number jaane diet plan sirf andaaza hi rehta hai.

Wazan ghatne ka ganit — number me

Wazan ghatana koi rahasya nahi hai, saada hisaab hai. Ek kilo charbi me lagbhag 7,700 calorie hoti hai. Isse aage ka poora plan khud nikal aata hai.

Do baatein jo aksar galat samjhi jaati hain. Pehli — "pet ki charbi ghatane wali exercise" jaisi koi cheez nahi hoti. Shareer charbi poore badan se ghataata hai, aap chun nahi sakte kahan se. Crunches se pet ki maansi mazboot hoti hai, uske upar ki charbi nahi jaati. Doosri — shuruaati hafte me wazan tezi se girta dikhta hai. Uska bada hissa paani hota hai, charbi nahi. Isliye pehle 2 hafte ke number dekh kar na khush hona chahiye na nirash. Asli rujhaan 4-6 hafte me dikhta hai. Roz ki jagah hafte me ek baar, subah, khaali pet naapein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Vazan ghatane ka sabse zaroori usool?

Calorie deficit — jitni calorie jalate hain usse kam khaana. TDEE se ~500 kam se shuru karein.

Kitna protein lena chahiye?

Vazan ghatate waqt 1.6-2 gram per kg body weight — muscle bachaane aur bhookh kam karne ke liye.

Neend ka vazan se kya lena-dena?

Kam neend bhookh ke hormone badha deti hai; 7-8 ghante neend vazan ghatane me madad karti hai.

Exercise kitni zaroori hai?

Vazan me 80% diet, 20% exercise — par exercise muscle bachati aur sehat sudhaarti hai. Roz 30-40 min walk se shuru karein.

Crash diet kaam karti hai?

Nahi, tez ghatana aksar wapas aa jaata hai aur sehat bigaadta hai. Dheera aur tikaoo tarika behtar hai.
