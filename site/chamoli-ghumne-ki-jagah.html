Chamoli: Hill Station Aur Shaant Gantavya

Chamoli Ke Hill Station

Chamoli me bhid-bhad se door shaant jagahein bhi hain:

  • Auli: Skiing aur Himalayi nazaron ke liye mashhoor, lagbhag 2,800 se 3,000 meter ki oonchai par bataya jata hai (ropeway ka upari terminal lagbhag 3,010 m bataya gaya hai).
  • Joshimath: Yatra ka mukhya padav, Narsingh mandir aur Jyotirmath ke liye jana jata hai.
  • Gopeshwar: Zila mukhyalaya, Gopinath mandir, Rudranath trek ka base.
  • Gwaldam (Tharali kshetra): Kumaon-Garhwal ki seema ke paas shaant kasba (Kausani Bageshwar zile me hai, Chamoli me nahi). Gwaldam ke prasiddh sthal aur homestay ki jaankari ke liye sthaniya sources dekhein.
  • Gairsain (Bhararisain): Grishmkalin rajdhani, ghati ke nazare.
  • Pokhri: Pokhri kshetra ki shaant ghatiyan.

Auli Ki Sthiti: Ropeway Ka Sach

Joshimath se Auli jaane ka mukhya sadhan sadak (lagbhag 16 km) hai. Joshimath-Auli ropeway ke bare me online alag-alag jaankari milti hai. Kai purane travel blog ise chalu likhte hain, jabki taaza (Aug aur Nov 2026 ke) sources ke anusar ye ropeway 5 January 2023 ko Joshimath ke bhu-dhansaav ke dauran ek tower ke paas dararein aane par band hui thi aur uske khulne ki koi ghoshit tithi nahi hai. Ropeway ka sahi status trip se pehle sthaniya prashasan ya ropeway ke sanchalak se confirm karein.

Chamoli: Offbeat Gaon Aur Seema Ke Gaon

Seema Ke Gaon: Mana, Niti, Malari

  • Mana: Badrinath ke paas, Saraswati nadi ke kinare. Is gaon ko kai sarkari aur yatra sources “Bharat ka pehla gaon” (seema ke pehle gaon ke roop me) kehte hain. Mana ke Bhotia samuday ke log garmiyon me yahan rehte hain aur sardiyon me neeche chale jate hain.
  • Niti aur Malari: Dhauliganga ghati ke gaon, Niti Pass ke raste me.
  • Dronagiri: Ek lok-manyata ke liye jana jata hai (Chamoli: Lok-Kathayein, Veer Aur Rahasya).
  • Seema ke gaon aur uske aage ke kshetra me jaane ke liye prashasan ya Army/ITBP ki anumati ke niyam ho sakte hain. Videshi nagrikon ke liye niyam alag hote hain.

Vibrant Villages Karyakram

Seema ke gaon ke vikas aur palayan rokne ke liye Kendra sarkar ki Vibrant Villages Programme chalayi gayi hai, jo Union Budget 2022-23 me ghoshit hui thi aur 2025-26 tak chalti thi. Ye Himachal, Uttarakhand, Arunachal, Sikkim aur Ladakh ke seema ke gaon par laagu hai. Is yojana ka sahi vistar aur aage ka rup official source se dekhein. Mana jaise gaon ka paryatan isse jura hua ho sakta hai.

Trek Ke Raste Ke Gaon

  • Lohajung, Didna, Wan: Roopkund trek ke base gaon, jahan homestay milte hain (Wikipedia ke anusar).
  • Sagar: Rudranath trek ka base (kai trek sources me).
  • Urgam: Kalpeshwar ghati ka gaon.
  • Reni: Chipko Andolan (Gaura Devi) ka gaon (Chamoli: Swatantrata Sangharsh, Coolie Begar Aur Rajya Andolan).
  • Kurud: Nanda Devi Siddhapeeth ke liye jana jata hai (Chamoli: Melein, Jatra Aur Doli Yatra).
  • Saloor-Dungra: Ramman utsav ka gaon (Chamoli: Mausmi Tyohar).
  • Mandal: Chipko ki shuruaat ka sthan (Chamoli: Swatantrata Sangharsh, Coolie Begar Aur Rajya Andolan).
  • In sabhi ke liye ek-ek chhota block likhein: Kahan hai, kya khaas hai, kab jayein.

Zimmedar Paryatan Ke Niyam

Gaon me jaane par logon ki privacy ka dhyan rakhein, bina anumati tasveer na lein (khaaskar mahilaon ki), mandir me jaane se pehle sthaniya niyam poochhein, aur plastic wapas le aayein. Isse gaon walon ka bharosa banta hai.

Seema Ke Khali Gaon

Aayog ne sarkar ko in gaon me dobara aabadi basane ke upay sujhaye. Ye jaankari news reports me aayi hai, aur kisi naye surveykshan ke baad sankhya badal sakti hai.

Palayan Rokne Ke Prayas

  • Vibrant Villages Programme (seema ke gaon ke liye).
  • Homestay, trek aur gramin paryatan se aay badhana (Chamoli: Homestay Registration Aur Subsidy).
  • Millet aur jaivik utpad ko bazaar tak pahunchana (Chamoli: Desi Anaj: Mandua, Jhangora Aur Barahnaja Pranali aur Chamoli: Bagwani, Phal-Sabzi Aur Nakdi Fasal).
  • Gaon ke school aur swasthya kendra ko majboot karna.
  • Page par is hisse me kisi bhi sarkari yojana ka naam likhne se pehle uski taaza sthiti dekhein.

Heritage Walk: Khali Gaon Ko Dekhne Ka Tareeka

Khali gaon ko “bhootiya gaon” jaisa dikhane ya ghost tourism ke naam par bechna sahi nahi hai, kyunki ye wahan ke logon ki yaadon aur ghar se jura hai. Isliye is hisse me:

  • Kisi bhi khali gaon ke naam, sthan aur kahani likhne se pehle gaon ke purane log ya unke parivar se baat karein.
  • Gaon ko “heritage” ke roop me dikhayein: purane ghar ki banawat, patthar ki deewarein, pathal ki chhat, mandir, dhara.
  • Aise ghar ya khet ko na chhuein jo kisi ki sampatti ho.

Roopkund Trek: Taaza Sthiti (2026)

Pehle ka hal: 2018 se Uttarakhand High Court ne bugyalon par raat ke camping par rok lagayi thi, jisse Roopkund trek kareeb aath saal chalana mushkil raha.

2026 ka badlav: Kai trekking sources ke anusar 20 July 2026 ko Supreme Court ne camping wale nirdesh set aside kiye, aur Badrinath Forest Division ne 27 July 2026 ke patra me Chamoli ke range officers ko iska paalan karne ko kaha. Iske baad Roopkund trek ke registration phir se shuru hue. Ye jaankari mukhya roop se trekking companies ki websites par hai.

Abhi bhi zaroori: Forest permit, registered operator se booking, group size aur kachre ke niyam. Permit ki fees aur online portal ki sthiti ke liye Forest Department se confirm karein.

Roopkund ki trek-bhasha: “Skeleton Lake” ke bare me ek sandarbh me ye bhi likha hai ki Chamoli ke Zila Magistrate ne report kiya tha ki trekkers aur anya log kankal ko khachchar par le ja rahe hain aur is kshetra ko surakshit karne ki salah di thi (Wikipedia ke anusar). Isliye kankal ya haddi uthana ya le jaana galat aur kanooni roop se jokhim bhara hai.

Kuari Pass Aur “Lord Curzon Trail”

Kuari Pass trek ko kai jagah “Lord Curzon Trail” bhi kehte hain. Kaha jata hai ki 1905 ke aaspaas Lord Curzon ne is raste se paidal yatra ki thi (is baat ke liye ek pramanik itihas sandarbh jodna behtar hoga). Kuari Pass ki oonchai sources me 3,640 se 3,876 m ke beech milti hai, isliye range likhein.

Trek Ki Taiyari Aur Suraksha

  • Acclimatisation: Oonchai me dheere chadhein. Ek din me bahut zyada oonchai na badhayein.
  • Altitude sickness ke lakshan: Sar dard, ulti, chakkar, saans phoolna. Aise me neeche utarna sabse surakshit hai.
  • Kapde: Layer wale garam kapde, waterproof jacket, achhe jute.
  • Pani aur khana: Pani zyada piyein, kuch energy wala khana saath rakhein.
  • Guide aur permit: Registered operator aur forest permit ke saath hi trek karein.
  • Emergency: Apni trek ki jaankari kisi ko batakar jayein. Mobile network ooncha kshetra me kaam nahi karta.
  • Mausam: Mansoon me bhuskhalan ka jokhim. Trek se pehle IMD ka purvanumaan dekhein.

Trek Ke Niyam (Leave No Trace)

Kachra wapas le aayein, jungle ki lakdi na jalaayein, phool na todein, aur jaanwaron ko pareshan na karein. Bugyal ki ghaas dheere ugti hai (Chamoli: Alpine Bugyal Aur Payar).

Chamoli: Parvatarohan Aur Rock Climbing

Himalayi Shikhar Aur Anumati

Chamoli ke shikhar (Nanda Devi, Kamet, Trisul, Changabang, Dunagiri aadi, Chamoli: Pramukh Himalayi Shikhar) Himalayan parvatarohan ke liye vishwa-prasiddh hain. Inme chadhne ke liye aam taur par Indian Mountaineering Foundation (IMF) ki anumati chahiye. Seema ya sanrakshit kshetra me aur bhi permit lagte hain. Kuch shikhar sainya ya paryavaran karan se band ya niyantrit ho sakte hain.

Parvatarohan Ke Prashikshan Sansthan

Uttarakhand me parvatarohan ke prashikshan ke liye kai sansthan hain. Kisi sansthan me daakhila lene se pehle uska naam IMF ya Uttarakhand Adventure Sports ki suchi se pakka karein.

Chattan Chadhai (Rock Climbing)

Rock climbing ke liye sahi sthan ka naam aur suraksha niyam ek pramanit operator se hi lein.

Pehli Chadhai Ka Itihas

Chamoli ke kai shikhar par pehli chadhai ke bare me itihas sources me alag-alag tithiyan milti hain (jaise Devistan I ki pehli chadhai 1961 aur Devistan II ki 1979 ek source me likhi hai, Chamoli: Pramukh Himalayi Shikhar).

Chamoli: Winter Sports: Auli Aur Skiing

Auli Me Skiing

Auli Bharat ke jaane-maane ski kshetron me se ek hai. Yahan sardiyon me barf ke maidano par skiing aur snowboarding hoti hai. Ek vyapak travel guide ke anusar 2025-26 ke season me Auli me asli pehli barf-bari 23 January 2026 ko hui thi (December kaafi sookha raha), aur 3 November 2026 tak 2026-27 ke season ki pehli barf-bari nahi hui thi. Isse dikhta hai ki barf ka samay saal-dar-saal badal sakta hai, isliye “December me hamesha barf hoti hai” jaisa vaada na karein.

Ski Kaise Seekhein

Auli me skiing ke course aur ski ke saman kiraye par milte hain. Kai jagah ye GMVN (Garhwal Mandal Vikas Nigam) aur nijee operators dwara chalaye jate hain, par kimat aur course ki jaankari har saal badalti hai.

Artificial Lake Aur Snow-Making

Auli me ek artificial lake hai jisse snow-making ke liye pani liya jata hai. Barf kam girne par snow-making ski ke maidan ko tayyar rakhne me madad karti hai. Is par paryavaran ke sawal bhi uthe hain (paani aur bijli ka istemal), isliye ek santulit tarah se likhein.

Auli Pahunchne Ke Raaste

Joshimath se Auli lagbhag 16 km sadak se hai. Sardiyon me barf ki wajah se sadak band ho sakti hai. Ropeway ke bare me sthiti Auli Ki Sthiti: Ropeway Ka Sach me di gayi hai. Hamesha taaza sthiti confirm karein.

Sardi mein Aur Kya Karein

Kuari Pass me winter trek (barf ke saath), Gorson Bugyal ka snow trek aur Tapovan ke garam kund ka anubhav sardiyon me kiya ja sakta hai. Inme se kisi ke liye bhi anubhavi guide aur mausam ki jaankari zaroori hai.

Sawal-Jawab: Winter Sports

Aam taur par December se February, par saal-dar-saal badalta hai (2025-26 me pehli asli barf-bari 23 January ko hui thi).

Haan, course aur kiraye ka saman milta hai.

Chamoli: Jal-Krida Aur Machhli Shikar

Chamoli Me Rafting?

Rafting ke liye Rishikesh aur Devprayag-Rudraprayag ke aaspaas ke hisse jaane jate hain. . Kisi bhi nadi me rafting ke liye registered operator, life jacket aur sahi mausam zaroori hai.

Nadi Me Nahane Ka Jokhim

Alaknanda aur anya nadiyan mansoon me achanak bhar sakti hain, aur Raini jaise hadse (Chamoli Ke Aapda Itihas Aur Seekhein) yaad dilate hain ki nadi ke kinare rukna jokhim bhara hai. Bachon aur buzurgon ko nadi ke bahav se door rakhein.

Machhli Shikar (Angling)

Himalayi nadiyon me trout aur anya thandi-nadi ki machhliyan hoti hain, par machhli pakadne ke liye Machhli Palan vibhag ya Forest vibhag ki anumati aur niyam lagu ho sakte hain. Iske liye vibhag se jaankari lein.

Sawal-Jawab: Jal-Krida

Kya Chamoli me rafting hoti hai?
Chamoli me niyamit commercial rafting ke bare me pakki jaankari nahi mili; Rishikesh aur Devprayag ke aaspaas zyada prachalit hai.
Kya nadi me nahana surakshit hai?
Mansoon me khaaskar nahi.

Chamoli: Aero Sports, Cycling Aur Camping

Paragliding Aur Aero Sports

Paragliding aur anya hawai khel ke liye sthan aur operator ka pramanik hona zaroori hai. Paragliding ya aise khel ke liye registered aur license-prapt operator se hi karein.

Cycling Aur Mountain Biking

Pahadi sadkon par cycling ke liye achhi fitness aur sahi saman chahiye. Sadak par trakon aur bheed ka dhyan rakhein.

Camping

Camping ka sabse bada sawal ye hai ki camping kahan ki ja sakti hai. Bugyal me camping par 2018 ke High Court ke aadesh ke kuch nirdesh July 2026 me Supreme Court ne set aside kiye (Bugyal Me Camping: 2018 Ka High Court Aadesh Aur July 2026 Ka Supreme…). Par:

  • Forest Department ke niyam aur permit abhi bhi lagu hain.
  • National park (Valley of Flowers, Nanda Devi) ke andar camping nahi ki ja sakti.
  • Nadi ke kinare aur naale ke paas camping jokhim bhara hai.
  • Isliye camping ke liye registered operator ya Forest Department se jaankari lein.

Drone Aur Photography Ke Niyam

Drone udane ke liye Bharat sarkar ke Drone Rules ke tahat zone ke niyam hote hain. Seema kshetra, sainya kshetra aur sanrakshit kshetra me drone ya photography par rok ho sakti hai.

Kya Valley of Flowers me camping kar sakte hain? Nahi, national park ke andar raat ko rukna mana hai.

Kya seema kshetra me drone se tasveer le sakte hain?
Aam taur par nahi, anumati aur niyam ke bina nahi.

Chamoli: Stargazing Aur Photography Spots

Aasman Dekhne Ke Liye Chamoli

Oonche parvatiya kshetra, kam aabadi aur saaf hawa Chamoli ko tara-darshan (stargazing) ke liye achha bana sakte hain. Jahan raat me roshni kam hoti hai (jaise bugyal, trek ke padav, oonche gaon), wahan aasman me taare zyada dikhte hain. Kisi vishesh jagah ko “Chamoli ka best stargazing spot” likhne se pehle us jagah ka khud ka anubhav ya kisi astronomy sanstha ki jaankari ho to hi likhein. Roshni ke pradushan (light pollution) ka stara Bortle scale ke roop me naapa jata hai.

Stargazing Ke Liye Salah

  • Amavasya ke aaspaas (chaand kam hone par) aasman sabse saaf dikhta hai.
  • Baadlon se rahit aur thande mausam (October se March ke aaspaas) me taare saaf dikhte hain.
  • Garam kapde, torch (lal roshni wali) aur sabr rakhein.
  • Raat me oonche kshetra me akele na jayein; jaanwaron aur mausam ka dhyan rakhein.

Photography Ke Liye Prasiddh Najare

  • Auli: Nanda Devi, Dunagiri aur Kamet jaise shikharon ke nazare, sunrise aur sunset.
  • Kuari Pass: 360 degree ka Himalayi nazara.
  • Valley of Flowers: Phoolon ki ghati ka nazara (niyam ke andar, sirf din me).
  • Badrinath: Alaknanda ke kinare subah ka mandir (kapat khulne ke dauran).
  • Bedni Bugyal: Ghaas ke maidan aur Trisul ke nazare.
  • Mana aur Vasudhara: Seema ke kshetra ki parvatiya sundarta (niyam dhyan me rakhein).
  • Kisi bhi jagah ke liye “golden hour” yani subah aur shaam ki roshni sabse achhi hoti hai.

Photography Ki Naitikta

Gaon walon ki, khaaskar mahilaon aur bachchon ki, tasveer bina anumati na lein. Mandir ke andar tasveer ya video ke niyam alag hote hain. Sena ya ITBP ki chowki aur seema ke ilake me tasveer lena mana ho sakta hai. Drone ka istemal bina anumati na karein (Drone Aur Photography Ke Niyam).

Sawal-Jawab: Stargazing Aur Photography

October se March ke aaspaas, amavasya ke dino me.

Nanda Devi aur Kamet jaise shikharon ke saaf nazare (mausam par nirbhar).
Kya Badrinath mandir ke andar photo le sakte hain?
Mandir ke niyam samay ke saath badal sakte hain, samiti ke niyam dekhein.

Chamoli: Taiyar Travel Itinerary: 3, 5, 7 Aur 10 Din

Aane Se Pehle: Kuch Zaroori Jaankari

  • Rishikesh se Badrinath lagbhag 295 km hai aur sadak se aam taur par 10 se 12 ghante lagte hain (mausam aur traffic par nirbhar).
  • Joshimath se Badrinath lagbhag 40 se 45 km hai.
  • Joshimath se Auli lagbhag 16 km hai.
  • Govindghat se Ghangaria lagbhag 13 se 14 km ka paidal marg hai (Govindghat se Pulna tak chhoti sadak ya pagdandi, phir Ghangaria tak).
  • Ye distance kai sources ke ausat hain. Apni yatra ke liye Google Maps se confirm karein.
  • Badrinath ke kapat 2026 me 23 April ko khule aur 16 November 2026 ko band hone tay the; har saal sardi me band rehkar April-May me phir khulte hain. Hemkund Sahib aur Valley of Flowers bhi sardiyon me band rehte hain.

3 Din Ka Itinerary (Weekend): Joshimath-Auli

  • Din 1: Rishikesh se Joshimath tak lamba sadak ka din (raste me Devprayag, Srinagar, Rudraprayag, Karnaprayag, Nandprayag aur Chamoli dekhte hue). Raat Joshimath me.
  • Din 2: Joshimath ke Narsingh mandir, phir Auli (sadak se). Auli me nazare ya snow (mausam ke hisab se). Raat Joshimath ya Auli me.
  • Din 3: Subah Tapovan ya Pandukeshwar, phir wapsi.
  • Sardiyon me Auli ke liye sadak ki sthiti (barf) aur ropeway ki sthiti (Auli Ki Sthiti: Ropeway Ka Sach) dekh lein.

5 Din Ka Itinerary: Badrinath Yatra (Mausam: Kapat Khulne Ke Baad)

  • Din 1: Rishikesh se Karnaprayag ya Chamoli, raat vishram.
  • Din 2: Joshimath aur Narsingh mandir.
  • Din 3: Joshimath se Badrinath (lagbhag 40 se 45 km), darshan, Tapt Kund.
  • Din 4: Mana gaon, Vyas Gufa, Bhim Pul, Vasudhara (ghoomne ki jaankari ke saath).
  • Din 5: Wapsi ya Pandukeshwar (Yogdhyan Badri).
  • Is itinerary ko April-May se October ke beech ke liye hi likhein.

7 Din Ka Itinerary: Valley of Flowers + Hemkund Sahib

  • Din 1: Rishikesh se Joshimath.
  • Din 2: Joshimath se Govindghat, phir Ghangaria ka paidal marg.
  • Din 3: Ghangaria se Valley of Flowers (din me jaana aur laut aana; raat ko Valley me rukna mana).
  • Din 4: Ghangaria se Hemkund Sahib ke darshan, wapsi Ghangaria.
  • Din 5: Ghangaria se Govindghat, phir Joshimath.
  • Din 6: Badrinath ya Auli.
  • Din 7: Wapsi.
  • Is itinerary ko June se October ke beech (Valley ke season me) likhein. Valley ke niyam (entry timing, permit, fees) Valley of Flowers: Yatri Ke Liye Zaroori Jaankari me hain.

10 Din Ka Itinerary: Chamoli Ka Poora Anubhav

  • Din 1 se 2: Rishikesh se Karnaprayag (Uma Devi mandir) aur Nandprayag.
  • Din 3: Gopeshwar (Gopinath mandir), Mandal (Chipko ki dharti ka zikra).
  • Din 4: Joshimath, Narsingh mandir.
  • Din 5: Badrinath, Mana, Vasudhara.
  • Din 6 se 8: Govindghat, Ghangaria, Valley of Flowers, Hemkund Sahib.
  • Din 9: Auli aur Tapovan.
  • Din 10: Wapsi.
  • Kayi baar ek yatra me bahut sare sthal rakhne se thakan hoti hai. Isliye kam sthal aur zyada samay ka sujhav dein.

Mausam Ke Hisab Se Yatra Ke Vikalp

  • Sardi (December se March): Auli (barf), Joshimath ke aaspaas ke sthal, Kuari Pass ka winter trek (anubhavi ke saath).
  • Garmi (April se June): Badrinath, Hemkund Sahib (khulne ke baad), Valley of Flowers (1 June se).
  • Mansoon (July se September): Valley of Flowers ka peak, par bhuskhalan ka jokhim; sadak band hone ki sambhavna.
  • Patjhad (October se November): Saaf mausam, Gauchar Mela (14 November se), par Badrinath aur Hemkund ke kapat band hone ka samay.
