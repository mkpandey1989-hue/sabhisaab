F&O Loss ITR-3 Me Kaise Dikhaye Aur Aage Le Jaayein

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~8 minute
Chhota jawabSabse zaroori baat: F&O ka nuksan tabhi aage le jaa sakte hain jab aap us saal samay par ITR-3 bharein. Bahut log 'nuksan hua to ITR kyun bharein' soch kar chhod dete hain — aur us nuksan ko future ke munafe se ghatane ka mauka hamesha ke liye kho dete hain.

Pehle: F&O ki income kis category me aati hai

Share bazaar ki teen alag income teen alag tarah se tax hoti hain, aur ITR form bhi alag ho jaata hai:

KaamIncome categoryITR form
Delivery me share (long/short term)Capital GainITR-2 / ITR-3
Intraday (equity)Speculative businessITR-3
F&O (futures/options)Non-speculative businessITR-3

F&O ko business income maana jaata hai — chahe aap full-time trader hon ya side me karte hon. Isiliye ITR-1 ya ITR-2 se kaam nahi chalta, ITR-3 hi bharna padta hai. Iska poora dhaancha ITR-3 guide me hai.

Nuksan ko aage le jaana — sabse bada fayda

Ye wo hissa hai jo zyadatar log nahi jaante, aur isi wajah se paisa chhod dete hain.

F&O ka non-speculative loss 8 saal tak aage le jaaya ja sakta hai. Aane wale kisi bhi saal jab aap F&O ya doosre business se munafa kamayein, to ye purana nuksan us munafe se ghata kar tax kam kar deta hai.

Ek misaal: is saal F&O me ₹2 lakh ka nuksan hua. Agle saal ₹3 lakh ka munafa hua. Agar aapne is saal ITR-3 me nuksan sahi dikhaya tha, to agle saal aap par tax sirf ₹1 lakh (3 − 2) par lagega, poore ₹3 lakh par nahi. Yaani seedha tax ki bachat.

Par ek shart hai: ye tabhi milega jab aapne nuksan wale saal ka ITR due date se pehle bhara ho. Late bhara to nuksan carry forward nahi hota — sirf usi saal ke munafe se set-off hota hai, aage nahi jaata. Isliye nuksan ke saal me ITR bharna zyada zaroori hai, kam nahi.

Set-off ke niyam — kya kisse ghatta hai

Non-speculative F&O loss ke set-off ke niyam thode udaar hain:

  • Usi saal: F&O loss ko salary chhod kar lagbhag kisi bhi income se set-off kar sakte hain — kirai ki income, doosre business ka munafa, capital gain, byaj — sabse. Sirf salary se set-off nahi hota.
  • Aage ke saal: carry-forward hone ke baad ye sirf business income (speculative + non-speculative) se ghatta hai.

Intraday (speculative) loss ke niyam sakht hain — wo sirf speculative munafe se hi ghatta hai, aur 4 saal aage jaata hai. Isliye dono ko ITR me alag-alag dikhana padta hai.

Turnover ka hisaab — yahan sabse zyada galti hoti hai

F&O me "turnover" ka matlab wo nahi jo aam bhaasha me lagta hai. Ye aapke saude ki kul value nahi hai — ye profit aur loss ka absolute jod hai.

Aam taur par maana jaata hai:

Turnover = har trade ke profit aur loss ka absolute (+) jod
(options me premium ko bhi jodne ka tarika alag ho sakta hai)

Misaal: teen trade — ₹10,000 profit, ₹8,000 loss, ₹5,000 profit. Turnover = 10,000 + 8,000 + 5,000 = ₹23,000 (loss ko bhi plus me gina). Ye samajhna zaroori hai kyunki audit ki zaroorat isi turnover par tay hoti hai.

Aaj lagbhag har broker saal ke ant me ek P&L statement aur turnover report deta hai. Ussi ko istemaal karein, khud har trade jodne ki galti se bachein.

Audit kab zaroori hai

Ye sabse uljhan wala hissa hai, aur niyam samay-samay par badalte hain. Mota-moti soch ye hai:

  • Agar turnover ek badi seema (crore me) se upar hai, to audit lag sakta hai.
  • Agar aapka munafa turnover ke ek tay percentage (aam taur par 6%) se kam hai — jo nuksan me hamesha hota hai — aur aapki kul income basic exemption se upar hai, to bhi audit ki zaroorat aa sakti hai.
  • Chhote traders jinki kul income tax-free seema se neeche hai, unke liye aksar audit nahi lagta.

Saaf baat: audit ke niyam har budget me badalte hain aur har vyakti ki sthiti alag hoti hai. Ye lekh aam jaankari ke liye hai. Audit lagta hai ya nahi, ye pakka karne ke liye ek CA se milna zaroori hai — hum CA nahi hain. Current niyam incometax.gov.in par bhi milte hain.

Kharche jo ghata sakte hain

Kyunki F&O business income hai, isse jude kharche munafe se ghatane ki ijazat hoti hai — jisse tax kam hota hai. Aam kharche:

  • Brokerage, exchange charges, STT, GST — jo trading par lage
  • Internet aur phone ka bill (trading ke hisse me)
  • Trading ki kitaabein, course, software subscription
  • Advisor ki fees, demat/account charges
  • Agar ghar se trade karte hain to bijli/rent ka waajib hissa

In sabke bill ya record rakhein. Ye chhoti rakam lagti hai par saal bhar me jud kar aachi khaasi ho jaati hai, aur seedha tax ghataati hai.

Kadam-dar-kadam kya karna hai

  1. Saal khatam hone par broker se P&L statement + turnover report lein
  2. Speculative (intraday) aur non-speculative (F&O) ko alag karein
  3. Jude hue kharche jod kar net loss/profit nikaalein
  4. Turnover dekhein — audit ki zaroorat hai ya nahi, CA se pakka karein
  5. ITR-3 me business income wale hisse me ye bharein
  6. Nuksan hai to carry-forward ka column zaroor bharein
  7. Due date se pehle file karein — warna carry-forward chala jaayega
  8. Form 26AS / AIS se apna TDS aur trading data milaayein

Ek galat soch jo mehngi padti hai

"Nuksan hi hua hai, sarkar ko dena kya hai, ITR kyun bharun?" — ye soch hazaron traders ko har saal nuksan me daalti hai.

Baat ulti hai. Nuksan ke saal me ITR bharna aapke faayde ki cheez hai, sarkar ke nahi. Wo nuksan ek "asset" ban jaata hai jo agle 8 saal tak aapka tax ghata sakta hai. Na bharne par ye asset hamesha ke liye khatam. Aur agar aage kabhi income tax notice aaya, to ITR na bharne par safai dena mushkil ho jaata hai — brokers ka data pehle se department ke paas AIS me pahunch jaata hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

F&O loss carry forward kitne saal hota hai?

Non-speculative F&O loss 8 saal tak aage jaata hai. Par sirf tabhi jab us saal ka ITR due date se pehle bhara gaya ho.

Kya F&O loss salary se ghata sakte hain?

Nahi. F&O loss ko usi saal salary chhod kar lagbhag har income se set-off kar sakte hain, par salary se nahi.

Turnover kaise nikalti hai?

Profit aur loss ka absolute (plus me) jod. Broker aam taur par saal ke ant me turnover report deta hai — wahi use karein.

Chhota trader hoon, audit lagega?

Ye turnover aur kul income par nirbhar karta hai, aur niyam badalte hain. Pakka karne ke liye CA se milna zaroori hai.

ITR-3 khud bhar sakte hain?

Simple case me haan, portal se. Par F&O, audit aur carry-forward me galti mehngi padti hai — pehli baar CA ki madad lena behtar hai.
