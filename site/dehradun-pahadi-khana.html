Dehradun ke Mashhoor Mukhya Vyanjan

Dehradun ke pahadi khane ki teen shaakhayein hain: Garhwali (kafuli, phaanu, chainsoo, bhatt ki churkani), Jaunsari (siddu, hindre, mashyada bhaat, aske) aur shehri (chhole bhature, chaat, momo, Punjabi khana). Jaunsar-Bawar ka khana Garhwali se kaafi alag hai.

Kafuli, Phaanu, Chainsoo, Thechwani, Bhatt ki Churkani, Dubuk, Ras-Bhaat, Gahat ki Dal & Jholi/Kaphli (Garhwali Pahadi Staples)

Yeh vyanjan Garhwal ke paramparik pahadi khane hain jo Dehradun ke Garhwali parivaron ke ghar aur kuch restaurant me banaye jate hain. Inme loha ki kadhai aur silbatta ka bahut kaam hota hai.

Jaunsar-Bawar ke Khaas Jaunsari Vyanjan (Chakrata, Kalsi, Tyuni)

Jaunsar-Bawar ka khana Dehradun zile ki sabse alag pahchan hai. Ek research review ke anusar yahan ke paramparik khane me siddu, chilra, aske (kapreudi), hindre, mashyada bhaat, taiya (babar), lembadi, khenda, baude, khobale aur kai doosre vyanjan hain. Ek anya sandarbh me Jaunsar-Bawar me lagbhag 250 prakar ke paramparik khane-pey ka zikr aaya hai.

Source: Plant Archives, Vol. 25, No. 1, 2025 me prakashit samiksha lekh, jisme Jaunsar-Bawar ke fermented aur non-fermented khane ka vivaran hai.

Jaunsari vyanjanKya hai
Siddu / SidkuBhaap me paka hua bhara hua pahadi roll. Do tarah ka: peethe ke siddu (chawal ya gehun ke aate ke khameer wale golon me kaali urad daal ya til bhari jati hai) aur aloo ke mishyare siddu (aloo-akhrot-masale ki bharai). Ghee ke saath khaya jata hai. Bishu, Jakholi aur Jagra mele ke samay banaya jata hai

Dhyan rakhein (bhang aur afeem ke baare me): Review me kuch dishes ke saath bhang (cannabis) aur afeem (opium) ka bhi zikr hai. Bhang ke patte-phool aur afeem ka khane me upyog NDPS kanoon ke dayre me aata hai aur gair-kanooni ho sakta hai. Bhangjeera (perilla) aur bhang ke beej (hemp seed) ki chutney alag cheez hai, aur uske baare me Dehradun: Chutney, Raita, Achaar aur Pisyun Loon me dekhein.

Kahan milta hai: Yeh khana mukhya roop se Chakrata, Kalsi aur Tyuni ke gaon ke ghar aur tyoharon par milta hai. Hanol, Lakhamandal aur Chakrata ke aas-paas ke homestay me kai bar Jaunsari khana parosne ki baat milti hai. Ek jagah ke liye “Siddu, aloo ke gutke, singori aur bal mithai” ka zikr bhi aaya hai (ki kaunsa homestay kab parosta hai).

Siddu kya hota hai?
Bhaap me paka hua bhara hua pahadi roll, Jaunsar-Bawar ka khaas vyanjan.
Chilra kya hota hai?
Gehun ke khameeri aate ka chilla, ghee aur shahad ke saath.
Aske kya hai?
Chawal-gehun ke aate ka mitti ke bartan me pakaya hua pahadi roti, rajma ke saath.
Mashyada bhaat kya hai?
Lal chawal aur urad daal ka pulao.
Koduwa kya hota hai?
Jaunsar-Bawar me saadi roti ko kehte hain.
Jaunsari khana Garhwali se alag kaise hai?
Siddu, chilra, aske, hindre jaise vyanjan sirf Jaunsar-Bawar me prachalit hain.

Dehradun: Paramparik Roti aur Chawal ke Pakwan

Mandua ki roti, gahat ke parathe, jhangore ka bhaat aur gahat ka ras-bhaat Garhwali pahadi thali ke staple hain. Dehradun ki Doon ghati ka sabse bada chawal-brand “Dehradooni Basmati” hai.

Mandua ki Roti, Gahat ke Parathe, Chhoi/Lesu Roti, Jhangore ki Bhaat & Gahat Ras-Bhaat

Mandua (finger millet) ki roti gehri bhoori hoti hai aur ghee, gud ya chutney ke saath khayi jati hai. Gahat ke parathe me kulath daal ki bharai hoti hai. Jhangora (barnyard millet) ka bhaat chawal ki jagah khaya jata hai.

Roti / bhaatKya haiKaun si ritu me
Mandua ki rotiRagi (mandua) ke aate ki gehri bhoori roti; ghee, gud ya chutney ke saathSardi me zyada
Gahat ke parathe (bhari roti)Gehun ki roti me kulath daal ki pitthi bhari jaati haiSardi
Chhoi / Lesu rotiGehun ke aate ki roti jisme mandua ki pitthi bhari jaati haiPahadi gharon me
Jhangore ka bhaatJhangora (barnyard millet) ko chawal ki tarah pakaya jata haiParamparik pahadi thali me
Gahat ka ras-bhaatGahat daal ka ras aur chawalSardi me

Jaunsari rotiyan aur breads:

  • Chilra (gehun ke khameeri aate ka chilla), aske (chawal-gehun ke ghol ka pakwan) aur taiya/babar (taley hue pahadi puri) Jaunsar-Bawar ke Khaas Jaunsari Vyanjan me bataye gaye hain.
  • Mashyada bhaat (lal chawal aur urad daal ka pulao) Jaunsari chawal ka sabse khaas vyanjan hai.
  • Budhi Diwali / Bissu par meethi puri: Jaunsar-Bawar me Diwali ek mahine baad (Budhi Diwali) manayi jati hai, aur is dauran meethi puri banane ki parampara ka zikr kai sources me milta hai (tithi aur parampara ke detail).

Dehradun ka Basmati pulao: Dehradooni Basmati ki pehchan uski khushboo hai (Red Rice , Satti & Pahadi Wheat me poori kahani). Basmati ka pulao aur biryani shehar ke restaurants me sabse prasiddh chawal-vyanjan hain. Dehradun ke bazar me asli “Dehradooni Basmati” ka naam lekar bikne wale chawal ki pehchan karna kathin ho sakta hai, kyunki is basmati ki kheti bahut seemit hai (2026 me lagbhag 75 hectare). Kharidte samay vikreta se source puchhein.

Mandua ki roti kaisi hoti hai?
Gehri bhoori, mote swad wali, ghee ya gud ke saath.
Jhangora kya hai?
Barnyard millet, chawal ki jagah.
Dehradooni Basmati kahan milti hai?
Seemit kheti hone se kum; vikreta se pata karein. Seemit; vikreta se puchhein.

Dehradun: Chutney, Raita, Achaar aur Pisyun Loon

Pahadi khane ki jaan uski chutney hai. Bhangjeera chutney, timur chutney, pahadi kheere ka raita aur pisyun loon (silbatta par pisa hua namak) Garhwal-Jaunsar dono ki rasoi me milte hain.

Bhangjeera Chutney, Hemp Seed Chutney, Pahadi Kheere ka Raita, Darim Chutney, Timur Chutney & Linguda/Mooli Achar

Bhangjeera (perilla seeds) aur bhang ke beej (hemp seeds) ki chutney dahi-roti ke saath khayi jati hai. Jaunsar-Bawar ke mashyada bhaat aur siddu jaise vyanjan akhrot, til aur bhangjeera ki chutney ke saath parose jate hain.

Chutney / raitaKya hai
Bhangjeera chutneyBhune hue bhangjeera (perilla) ke beej, namak-mirch aur nimbu ke saath
Bhang ke beej (hemp seed) ki chutneyHemp ke beej bhunkar silbatta par peese jate hain. Beej (seed) aur patte-phool me fark hai. Beej ka khane me upyog paramparik hai; patte, phool ya nasha wale hisse NDPS kanoon ke dayre me aate hain (rajya ke niyam)
Pahadi kheere ka raitaPahadi peele kheere ko kaddukas karke, dahi me rai ke teekhe swad ke saath
Timur chutneyTimur (Zanthoxylum) ke bhune beej, namak, mirch ke saath; tez swad aur jeebh par halki jhanjhanahat jaisa ehsaas
Darim (dadim) chutneyPahadi jungli anar ke beej ki khatti chutney
Linguda / Mooli ka acharLinguda (fern ki kopal) ya mooli ka achar; mausmi

Akhrot ki chutney: Jaunsar-Bawar ke khane me akhrot, til aur bhangjeera ki chutney ka zikr kai jagah aaya hai (mashyada bhaat ke saath).

Bhangjeera kya hai?
Perilla ke beej, pahadi chutney me.
Timur kya hai?
Pahadi masala (Zanthoxylum), chutney aur dal me.
Kya hemp seed chutney legal hai?
Beej ka khane me upyog paramparik hai; patte-phool ka nahi. Niyam badal sakte hain.

Pisyun Loon: Silbatte par Pisa Pahadi Namak

Pisyun loon ek pahadi masaledar namak hai jise silbatta par lahsun ke patte, hari mirch, dhaniya, jeera aur timur ke saath peesa jata hai. Isse mooli, kheere ya ruti ke saath khaya jata hai, aur yeh sardi-dhoop me ek khaas pahadi dawat ki tarah mashhoor hai.

  • Banane ka tareeka: Silbatta par namak, lahsun ke patte (ya lahsun), hari mirch, dhaniya, jeera aur timur ko peesa jata hai. Ratio ghar-ghar me alag hota hai.
  • Kahan milta hai: Pahadi parivaron me ghar me bantaa hai. Dehradun ke bazar me kuch dukano par “pahadi namak” ke naam se milne ki baat aati hai.
Pisyun loon kya hota hai?
Silbatta par pise hue masale wale pahadi namak.
Pisyun loon kis ke saath khate hain?
Mooli, kheere, roti ya fruit ke saath.

Dehradun: Jungli Khane Layak Cheezein aur Mausami Saag

Dehradun ke pahadi aur jungle ilake me bichhu ghas (kandali), linguda, kafal, hisalu, kilmora, bedu aur buransh ke phool jaise mausmi jungli khaane milte hain. Kafal Mussoorie aur Doon ki pahadiyon me April-June me bikta hai.

Bicchu Ghas (Kandali / Stinging Nettle), Linguda (Fiddlehead Fern), Kaphal, Hisalu, Kilmora, Bedu & Buransh Juice

Kandali (bichhu ghas) ki hari sabzi sardi aur basant me bahut paramparik hai. Kafal (Myrica esculenta) ka phal garmi me pahadi raaston par bikta hai. Buransh ke phool ka ras (squash) Mussoorie-Chakrata ke ilake me prasiddh hai.

Safety (bahut zaroori):

  • Jungle se kuch bhi tod kar na khayein agar aap use pehchante nahi. Kai jungli paudhe zehreele ho sakte hain.
  • Stinging nettle ko pakaye bina na khayein; pakane par chhune wale kaante nasht ho jate hain.
  • Sanrakshit jungle (Rajaji, Benog) me jungli phal ya paudhe todna kanoon se manaa hai.
  • Sirf paramparik jaankari ya bharose-mand sthaniya vikreta se hi kharidein.

Doon ke mausmi phal: Litchi (May-June), aam (June-July), jamun, aur kafal (April-June) Dehradun ke mausmi phal-calendar ke bade naam hain (mausam kuch badal sakta hai).

Kafal kab aata hai?
April se June.
Bichhu ghas kya hai?
Stinging nettle, pahadi saag.
Buransh ka ras kahan milta hai?
Mussoorie, Chakrata aur pahadi bazar me.
Linguda kya hai?
Fiddlehead fern, pahadi mausmi sabzi.

Dehradun: Paramparik Mithai, Tyohar ke Pakwan aur Prasad

Pahadi mithai me arsa, singori, bal mithai, jhangore ki kheer, rote, pua aur gulgule prasiddh hain. Dehradun me inke saath Kumar Sweets jaisi shehri mithai ki dukane aur Jaunsar ke apne tyohari vyanjan bhi hain.

Arsa, Singori, Bal Mithai, Jhangore ki Kheer, Rote, Puwa, Singal, Lagad & Gulgule

Bal mithai aur singori mukhya roop se Kumaon ki mithai hain, lekin Dehradun ke bazar aur Hanol jaise sthal ke saath jude sandarbh me bhi inka zikr milta hai. Garhwal ki arsa (gud aur chawal ki mithai) aur jhangore ki kheer paramparik vyanjan hain.

Jaunsari tyohari vyanjan:

  • Khenda/Baadi: Maa Kali ke prasad me aur prasav ke baad.
  • Khobale: January me.
  • Baude: Mahashivratri par.
  • Chewda aur Mudda: Diwali, Bishu aur Jakholi jaise tyoharon par.
  • Meethi puri: Bissu/Budhi Diwali ke samay, beti-bahin ko ghar se laaye jaane wale vyanjan me (Bissu Mela ke sandarbh me).

Dehradun ki shehri mithai: Clock Tower ke paas Kumar Sweets kai sources me prasiddh hai, jahan paan mithai, rasmalai aur falooda kulfi ka zikr milta hai. Paltan Bazaar ke shuruaati hisse me Gaylord Ice Cream Parlour bhi ek purana naam hai. Chaat ke liye “Chaat Gali” Ghantaghar ke paas ke ilake me batayi jati hai. Dukaan ke samay aur menu badalte rehte hain.

Dehradun: Paramparik Peya aur Khameer Wale Pey

Dehradun me chai ka bahut bada chalan hai: Landour ki ginger-lemon-honey chai, Clock Tower ki “Chai Galli” aur Tibetan butter tea ke saath pahadi gud-adrak ki chai. Jaunsar-Bawar ki paramparik pey (jaise soor, pakhoi, jaan) alcoholic hain aur sirf sanskritik jaankari ke liye bataye gaye hain.

Chhyang / Jaan / Soor (Traditional Fermented Drinks), Buransh Squash, Malta Juice & Gud-Adrak ki Chai

Jaunsar-Bawar ke samuday me tyoharon aur shaadiyon me paramparik fermented pey ka chalan raha hai. Ek research review me soor, pakhoi (pakhali), ghaingti aur jaan ka naam aaya hai. Yeh sab alcoholic hain, isliye yahan unki banane ki vidhi nahi di ja rahi. Alcohol banane aur bechne ke liye Uttarakhand Excise Act ke tahat license zaroori hai.

Dehradun: Pahadi Kitchen Architecture & Utensils

Pahadi rasoi ka khana swad me alag isliye hai kyunki uske bartan aur chulha alag hain: loha ki kadhai, silbatta, kansi (bronze) ki thali, bhaddu (moti pital ki bhaddu), parat aur mitti ka chulha jiske upar dhuan se sukhane ki jagah hoti hai.

Loh ki Kadhai, Silbatta, Kansi Thali, Bhaddu, Parat & Mud Chulha with Dhumala

Loha ki kadhai me dheemi aanch par daal-sabzi banane se swad gehra aur loha ka ansh khane me aata hai. Silbatta par peesi hui chutney aur masale ka swad mixer se alag hota hai. Jaunsar-Bawar me bhaap ke liye do bartan wala set aur mitti ke bartan ka bhi chalan hai.

Dehradun Shahar, Mussoorie-Landour aur Rishikesh ka Khana

B.1 Dehradun ka Street Food: Paltan Bazaar, Chaat Gali, Rajpur Road, Astley Hall

Dehradun ke prasiddh khaane ke ilake: Paltan Bazaar, Ghantaghar (Clock Tower) ka ilaka, Rajpur Road, Astley Hall, Dharampur, Chakrata Road aur Mussoorie Road. Chaat, chhole bhature, kachori, momo aur mithai yahan ke sabse zyada search hone wale khane hain.

B.2 Dehradun mein Tibbati, Gorkha, Punjabi-Sikh aur Doosre Samuday ka Khana

Dehradun me Tibetan basti (Clement Town, Mindrolling ke aas-paas), Gorkha parivar (sena ki parampara se), Punjabi-Sikh samuday (Guru Ram Rai Darbar ke saath) aur bahut se anya pravasi samuday rehte hain. Isi wajah se yahan momo, thukpa, Punjabi khana, aur Gorkha-style khane ek saath milte hain.

  • Tibetan: Momo, thukpa, tingmo, shaptak jaise vyanjan. Mussoorie ke Mall Road par Tibetan khane ke kai restaurant prasiddh hain (Kalsang aur Tavern ke naam kai sources me aate hain).
  • Gorkha: Nepali-Gorkha khana jaise dal-bhat, aloo ke achar, sel roti (Nepali tyohar ki roti) kuch ilake me milta hai.
  • Punjabi-Sikh: Langar ki parampara, dhaba-style khana aur sweet lassi, Rajpur Road ke aas-paas.
  • Majnu ka Tila ka saaf zikr: “Majnu ka Tila” Delhi ka Tibetan market hai. Dehradun ka koi Majnu ka Tila nahi hai, to is naam se search karne wale log galat jagah aate hain.

Dehradun me Tibetan khana kahan milta hai? Clement Town ke aas-paas aur Rajpur Road ke stall, aur Mussoorie ke Mall Road par.

Kya Dehradun me Majnu ka Tila hai?
Nahi, woh Delhi me hai.

B.3 Mussoorie aur Landour ka Khana (Char Dukan, Bakehouse, Kalsang)

Landour ka Char Dukan Mussoorie ka sabse purana khane ka kendra hai. Kai sources ke anusar, Landour me St. Paul’s Church ke 1840 me prathisthit hone ke baad is ilake me chaar dukanon ka bazar bana tha. Yahan ke bun omelette, pancake, waffle aur Maggi mashhoor hain, aur Ruskin Bond ne bhi iske bun omelette ke baare me likha hai.

B.4 Rishikesh ka Khana: Shuddh Shakahari, Chotiwala aur Cafe

Rishikesh ke mukhya dharmik aur ashram ilake me khana mukhya roop se shuddh shakahari hota hai aur alcohol aur non-veg paramparik roop se nahi milte. Chotiwala restaurant Ram Jhula ke aas-paas bahut purana aur prasiddh naam hai.

  • Veg-only ilake: Laxman Jhula, Ram Jhula, Swarg Ashram, Tapovan jaise ilake me aam taur par veg khana milta hai. Exact kanooni seema aur adhisuchna ke liye nagar nigam/zila prashasan se confirm karein. Kuch resort aur seema ke bahar ke ilake me niyam alag ho sakte hain.
  • Chotiwala: Ram Jhula ke paas ka purana restaurant, thali ke liye prasiddh.
  • Cafe culture: Tapovan aur Laxman Jhula me German Bakery, Little Buddha Cafe, Oasis jaise cafe, veg ya vegan khane ke saath. Brand/name ki list badalti rehti hai.
  • Juice aur ayurvedic cafe: Ram Jhula ke paas juice centre aur Tapovan ke “ayurvedic” menu wale cafe.
Kya Rishikesh me non-veg milta hai?
Mukhya ashram kshetra me aam taur par nahi; ilake ke hisaab se alag.
Rishikesh me thali kahan khayein?
Chotiwala (Ram Jhula ke paas) ka naam sabse zyada aata hai.
Rishikesh me alcohol milta hai?
Mukhya dharmik ilake me nahi; niyam ke liye prashasan se puchhein.
