WPM ka hisaab — gross aur net

Gross WPM = Kul shabd ÷ Minute
Net WPM = (Kul shabd − Galtiyan) ÷ Minute
Accuracy = (Sahi shabd ÷ Kul shabd) × 100

200 shabd, 5 minute, 10 galtiyan:

  • Gross WPM: 40 · Net WPM: 38 · Accuracy: 95%
Har jagah net WPM hi maana jaata hai. Galti ke saath tez type karne ka koi faayda nahi — sudhaarne me usse zyada samay lagta hai, aur sarkari test me galtiyan alag se kaati bhi jaati hain.

Aur ek baat — typing me "shabd" ka matlab aam taur par 5 akshar maana jaata hai, chahe asli shabd chhota ho ya bada. Isi liye alag-alag test me speed thodi alag aa sakti hai.

Sarkari bharti me post ke hisaab se alag maang hoti hai — clerk aur LDC jaisi post me aam taur par angrezi me lagbhag 35 WPM aur Hindi me lagbhag 30 WPM.

Zaroori — ye number bharti ke hisaab se badalte rehte hain. Apne exam ka official notification hi dekhiye, wahi aakhri jawab hai. Aur zyadatar sarkari test me speed ke saath accuracy ki bhi seema hoti hai.

Speed badhane ka sahi tarika

Sabse zaroori baat pehle — speed badhane ki koshish mat kijiye. Sahi tarika seekhiye, speed apne aap aayegi.

  • Touch typing seekhiye — bina keyboard dekhe. Ungliyan home row (ASDF - JKL;) par rehti hain aur har ungli ke apne akshar hote hain. Shuru me dheema lagega, par yahi ek cheez sabse bada farak laati hai.
  • Keyboard mat dekhiye — shuru ke 2-3 hafte mushkil hote hain, uske baad speed tezi se badhti hai.
  • Accuracy pehle. 98% par 30 WPM, 85% par 50 WPM se behtar hai.
  • Roz 15-20 minute — hafte me ek din 2 ghante se behtar. Ungliyon ki yaadasht rozana abhyaas se banti hai.
  • Baithne ka tarika — kalaai upar uthi hui, kohni 90 degree par, screen aankh ke star par. Galat tareeke se lambe samay tak type karne se kalaai me dard aata hai.
Hindi typing ke liye khaas baat — pehle tay kariye ki Mangal (Inscript) chahiye ya Krutidev (Remington). Sarkari bharti me aksar dono ka vikalp hota hai par layout bilkul alag hai. Ek seekh kar doosra shuru karna matlab dobara shuruaat — notification dekh kar hi chuniye.

Abhyaas ki yojana

  1. Aaj ki speed naapiye — 5 minute ka test, is calculator me daaliye. Ye aapki shuruaati line hai.
  2. Lakshya likhiye — jaise "30 se 40 WPM, 3 mahine me, 95% accuracy ke saath".
  3. Har hafte ek baar naapiye — roz nahi. Rozana naapne se nirasha hoti hai, kyunki sudhaar dheere aata hai.
  4. Number likh kar rakhiye — 8 hafte baad purana number dekhna sabse bada hausla deta hai.
Ek baat jo taiyaari karne walon ko pata honi chahiye — speed ek jagah ruk jaati hai. 35 WPM par pahunch kar do-teen hafte kuch nahi badalta. Ye normal hai, aur yahin zyadatar log haar maan lete hain. Jo lage rehte hain, unki speed uske baad achanak badhti hai.

Aur exam se pehle usi tarah ke keyboard par abhyaas kijiye jo exam center me hoga — laptop ke chhote aur desktop ke bade keyboard me kaafi farak padta hai.

Kitni speed kis kaam ke liye kaafi hai

Sarkari test me speed se zyada galti maayne rakhti hai. Zyadatar test me har galti par number kata jaata hai, aur kai jagah 5% se zyada galti hone par poora test fail maana jaata hai. Yaani 45 WPM par 8 galtiyan, 35 WPM par 1 galti se kharab natija deti hai. Abhyaas ka sahi kram ye hai: pehle bina dekhe typing ki aadat, phir accuracy 97% se upar, aur sabse aakhir me speed. Ulta karne par galti ki aadat pakki ho jaati hai aur baad me sudharna mushkil hota hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Gross aur net WPM me kya farak hai?

Gross me saare shabd gine jaate hain, net me galtiyan ghata di jaati hain. Har jagah net WPM hi maana jaata hai.

Sarkari bharti me kitni typing speed chahiye?

Post ke hisaab se alag — clerk jaisi post me aam taur par angrezi me lagbhag 35 aur Hindi me 30 WPM. Apne exam ka official notification hi dekhiye.

Speed kaise badhaye?

Touch typing seekhiye (bina keyboard dekhe), accuracy ko pehle rakhiye, aur roz 15-20 minute abhyaas kariye.

Speed ek jagah ruk gayi hai to?

Ye normal hai. Do-teen hafte kuch nahi badalta, phir achanak sudhaar aata hai. Yahin par zyadatar log haar maan lete hain.
