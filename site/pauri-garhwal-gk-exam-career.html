Patwari, Lekhpal, Police Constable, SI, Forest Guard, VDO/VPDO aur PCS ke liye 100 Tathya

A. Prashasan aur Jansankhya (Census 2011)

  • Pauri Garhwal ka zila mukhyalay Pauri hai.
  • Pauri Garhwal-Garhwal mandal ka zila hai.
  • Census 2011 me zile ki kul jansankhya 6,87,271 thi.
  • Census 2011 me lingaanupaat 1,103 (1,000 purushon par mahilayein) tha.
  • Census 2011 me zile ki saksharta dar 82.02% thi.
  • Zile me 15 vikas khand (block) hain.
  • Blocks: Bironkhal, Dugadda, Dwarikhal, Ekeshwar, Jaiharikhal, Kaljikhal, Khirsu, Kot, Nainidanda, Pabau, Pauri, Pokhra, Rikhnikhal, Thalisain, Yamkeshwar.
  • Kaljikhal ko sabse bada block bataya jata hai (Wikipedia ke anusar).
  • Tehsil ki sankhya: ek hi strot me 13 aur 15 dono ullekh hain; exam me zila prashasan (pauri.nic.in) ki vartaman suchi hi maanein.
  • Zile me mahilaon ki sankhya purushon se zyada hai (lingaanupaat 1,103 se saabit).
  • Pauri Garhwal ki jansankhya me Anusuchit Jati lagbhag 17.8% aur Anusuchit Janjati lagbhag 0.32% hai (Census 2011, Pauri Garhwal: Aabadi, Samuday aur Anusuchit Janjati).
  • Pauri Garhwal me kai gaon palayan ke karan khaali (ghost villages) hain (Pauri Garhwal: Palayan Vapasi ki Safal Kahaniyan).

B. Itihas

  • Garhwal me Panwar vansh ki rajdhani Srinagar (Garhwal) thi.
  • Parampara me Raja Ajay Pal ne 52 chhote garhon ko milakar ek rajya banaya (Pauri Garhwal: Panwar & Chand Kingdoms).
  • Garhwal me prasiddh 52 garh (chhote kile/thakurai) the.
  • Devalgarh ko Garhwal ki ek samay ki rajdhani bataya jata hai.
  • Gorkha shasan Garhwal me lagbhag 1803 se 1815 tak raha.
  • Sugauli ki sandhi (1815-16) ke baad Garhwal ka alaknanda ke purv ka hissa angrezon ke paas gaya (Pauri Garhwal: Gorkha Raaj aur Angrez-Nepal Yuddh).
  • Angrezi Garhwal ki seema ke liye Alaknanda aur Mandakini ka ullekh aata hai (Wikipedia ke anusar).
  • Lansdowne ki sthapna 1887 me Garhwal Rifles ki pehli battalion ke liye hui.
  • Lansdowne ka naam Viceroy Lord Lansdowne ke naam par rakha gaya.
  • Garhwal Rifles Regimental Centre Lansdowne me hai.
  • Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali ka janm 25 December 1891 ko hua (Pauri Garhwal se jude; gaon ka naam alag kitabon me alag milta hai).
  • Chandra Singh Garhwali ne Peshawar (23 April 1930) me nihatthe pathanon par goli chalane se inkaar kiya (Pauri Garhwal: Gorkha Raaj aur Angrez-Nepal Yuddh).
  • Garhwal Rifles ka naam Garhwali sainikon ki regiment ke roop me prasiddh hai.
  • Uttarakhand rajya ka gathan 9 November 2000 ko hua.
  • Pauri Garhwal ka naam Uttarakhand andolan (1994) ke shahidon se bhi juda hai: Jeet Bahadur Gurung (8 Aug 1994), Kotdwar (3 Oct 1994), Srinagar (10 Nov 1994) ki ghatnayein (Pauri Garhwal: Gorkha Raaj aur Angrez-Nepal Yuddh).

C. Bhugol aur Nadiyan

  • Alaknanda Pauri Garhwal ki mukhya nadi hai (Srinagar ke paas se behti hai).
  • Nayar nadi (Purvi aur Pashchimi) zile ki ek mahatvapurn nadi hai.
  • Ramganga Pauri Garhwal ke dakshin-purv me behti hai.
  • Khoh nadi Kotdwar ke aas-paas behti hai.
  • Devprayag par Alaknanda aur Bhagirathi milkar Ganga banti hain; Devprayag Tehri Garhwal zile me hai, Pauri ki seema ke paas.
  • Nawalika nadi ke daaien kinare par Jwalpa Devi mandir hai (Wikipedia ke anusar).
  • Pauri Garhwal ka pramukh jungle Chir aur Banj (oak) ka hai.
  • Uttarakhand ka rajya ped Burans (Rhododendron) hai.
  • Uttarakhand ka rajya pakshi Monal (Himalayan Monal) hai.
  • Uttarakhand ka rajya pashu Kasturi mrig (Musk deer) hai.
  • Uttarakhand ka rajya phool Brahma Kamal hai.
  • Bhabar kshetra Kotdwar ke aas-paas ka tarai-pahad ke beech ka bhaag hai.
  • Corbett Tiger Reserve ka kuch hissa Pauri Garhwal zile me aata hai (Kya Pauri Garhwal mein Bugyal Hai? , Pauri Garhwal: Parvatarohan, Chattan Chadhai aur Bouldering).

  • Sidhbali mandir (Kotdwar) Khoh nadi ke paas prasiddh hai.

  • Khirsu ek hill station hai (Pauri Garhwal ki 20 Sabse Zaroori Ghumne ki Jagah).
  • Lansdowne ek cantonment hill town hai.

  • Dhari Devi mandir Alaknanda ke kinare Srinagar ke paas prasiddh hai (Pauri Garhwal: Pramukh Mandir, Dham, Gurudwara aur Math).
  • Pauri ke Kandoliya ke aas-paas se Himalaya ke sheersh ka darshan hota hai (Pauri Garhwal ki 20 Sabse Zaroori Ghumne ki Jagah).
  • Gindi Mela (Dhangu/Dadamandi kshetra) Makar Sankranti par lagta hai (Pauri Garhwal: Pahadi Life-Cycle Rituals & Weddings).

  • Sadak par subah andhere me daudte samay reflective jacket/torch rakhein; pahadi sadak par gaadiyon ka khatra hai.
  • Gulbaag (leopard) ka khatra: Subah-shaam akele sunsan raaste par daudne se bachein, group me daudein (Pauri Garhwal: Jungle ke Prakar aur Dev Van).
  • Dhalaan par chadhne-utarne ke liye ghutno ka dhyan rakhein; achhe joote pehnein.
  • Dheere-dheere badhayein: Achanak zyada daudne se chot ho sakti hai.
  • Khaana aur neend: Poshtik khaana (mandua, dal, doodh) aur poori neend.
  • Medical fitness: Bharti me medical test hota hai; kisi bhi dard par doctor se milein.
  • Dalalon se savdhan: Bharti ke liye paisa maangne wale thag hote hain. Army bharti bina paise ke hoti hai.
  • Official site: Sirf joinindianarmy.nic.in par registration karein.

Pauri ke yuvaon ki parampara: Garhwal Rifles ki parampara ke karan Pauri Garhwal me sena me jaane ki parampara bahut purani hai (Pauri Garhwal: Mausami Tyohar , Pauri Garhwal: Arthvyavastha: GDP, Per Capita Aay aur Rozgar), aur bahut se gaon me “fauji parivar” hain.
