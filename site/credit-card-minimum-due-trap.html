Credit Card Ka “Minimum Due” — Sabse Mehnga Jaal

Minimum due ka asli matlab

Bill par do number hote hain: Total Due aur Minimum Due. Minimum due aam taur par kul rakam ka 5% hota hai.

Bill ki bhasha me likha hota hai ki minimum due bharne se "account regular rehta hai". Ye sach hai. Par jo nahi likha hota, wo ye hai:

  • Baaki poore balance par us din se byaj lagna shuru ho jaata hai
  • Aapka interest-free period khatam ho jaata hai — yaani ab jo bhi naya kharcha karenge, uspar bhi pehle din se byaj lagega
  • Byaj mahine ke hisaab se juda jaata hai, saal me nahi

Doosra point sabse mehnga hai aur zyadatar log ise samajhte nahi. Log sochte hain "purane balance par byaj lag raha hai, koi baat nahi, naya kharcha to safe hai." Nahi hai.

Byaj sach me kitna hai

Credit card ka byaj mahine me bataya jaata hai — jaise "3.5% per month". Sunne me chhota lagta hai. Saal ka hisaab lagaiye:

Saalana = (1 + 0.035)12 − 1 = lagbhag 51%

Aur ispar GST alag. Yaani asli lagat aur upar jaati hai.

Tulna kar ke dekhiye: home loan lagbhag 8-9%, personal loan 11-16%, gold loan 9-12%. Credit card ka revolving balance Bharat me sabse mehnga aam karza hai. Isse mehnga sirf saahukaar ka karza hota hai.

Ek asli misaal

₹50,000 ka bill hai, byaj 3.5% mahina, aur aap sirf minimum (5%) bharte hain, aage koi naya kharcha nahi:

Interest-free period kaise kaam karta hai

Card par 45-50 din ka interest-free period tabhi milta hai jab aap har mahine poora bill bharein. Jaise hi ek mahina minimum bhara, ye suvidha band ho jaati hai, aur tab tak band rehti hai jab tak aap poora balance clear na kar dein.

Iska matlab: minimum due wale mahine ke baad aapne agar ₹800 ka petrol bhi card se bhara, to us ₹800 par bhi usi din se byaj chalu. Log sochte hain unka sirf purana balance mehnga hai — asal me poora card mehnga ho chuka hota hai.

Nikalne ke 4 asli tarike

1. Personal loan se card chukana. Sunne me ajeeb lagta hai — ek karza se doosra karza. Par ganit saaf hai: 51% se 14% par shift karna faayde ka hai. Shart sirf ek — card ko phir se istemaal mat kijiye, warna do karze ho jaayenge.

2. Balance transfer. Kai bank kam byaj par doosre card ka balance le lete hain, kuch mahine ke liye. Processing fee jod kar dekhiye, aur promotional period ke baad ka rate zaroor poochh lijiye — wo aksar zyada hota hai.

3. EMI me convert karwana. Bank ko phone kijiye aur outstanding ko EMI me badalne ko kahiye. Rate lagbhag 13-18% aata hai, jo 51% se bahut behtar hai. Processing fee lagti hai, par phir bhi sasta padta hai.

4. Avalanche tarika (ek se zyada card hain to). Sabse zyada byaj wale card par saara extra paisa daaliye, baaki par sirf minimum. Wo khatam hone par agla. Ye ganit ke hisaab se sabse sasta raasta hai.

Credit score par asar

Minimum due samay par bharne se aapka payment history kharaab nahi hota — ye achhi baat hai. Par doosri cheez chupke se score girati hai: credit utilisation.

Agar ₹1 lakh ki limit me se ₹70,000 istemaal ho raha hai, to utilisation 70% hai. Aam samajh ye hai ki ise 30% se neeche rakhna behtar rehta hai. Zyada utilisation se score girta hai chahe aap har mahine samay par bhar rahe hon.

Card istemaal karne ka sahi tarika

  • Har mahine poora bill bhariye. Minimum ko option maaniye hi mat.
  • Auto-debit lagaiye — "Total Due" par, "Minimum Due" par nahi. Ye default aksar galat set hota hai.
  • Limit ka 30% se zyada istemaal na kijiye.
  • Cash withdrawal bilkul nahi. Ismein interest-free period hota hi nahi, byaj pehle din se lagta hai, aur upar se cash advance fee alag.
  • Jo cheez agle bill tak nahi chuka sakte, wo card se mat khareediye. Ye ek line poora jaal bacha leti hai.

Bank charge aur byaj samay-samay par badalte hain. Apne card ka current rate statement me ya RBI ke niyamon ke saath bank se confirm kar lijiye.

Reward points ka asli ganit

Card bechne wala sabse pehla tark yahi hota hai — "cashback milta hai, points milte hain". Ganit lagakar dekhiye.

Aam card par 1-2% cashback milta hai. Revolving balance par byaj lagbhag 40-50% saalana hai. Yaani ek mahine ka byaj hi saal bhar ke saare reward points kha jaata hai, aur bahut aage nikal jaata hai.

Reward points sirf un logon ke liye faayde ka sauda hain jo har mahine poora bill bharte hain. Baaki sabke liye wo ek chhota lalach hai jo mehnge karze ka darwaza kholta hai. Annual fee bhi jod kar dekhiye — kai cards ki fee, milne wale points se zyada hoti hai.

No-cost EMI sach me free hai?

"No-cost EMI" ka matlab ye nahi hota ki byaj lag hi nahi raha. Aam taur par teen me se koi ek hota hai:

  • Byaj ka paisa dukaandaar discount ke roop me pehle hi hata deta hai — yaani nakad me kharidne par jo chhoot milti, wo aapko nahi mili
  • Byaj lagta hai par saath me utna hi cashback aata hai — ye sach me lagbhag free hota hai
  • Processing fee alag se lag jaati hai, jo kabhi-kabhi ₹199 se ₹999 tak hoti hai, plus GST

Kharidne se pehle ek sawaal poochh lijiye: "Agar main poora paisa abhi doon to kya daam kam hoga?" Jawab haan hai, to no-cost EMI free nahi hai.

Agar card pehle se fasa hua hai to sabse pehle kya

Ghabraahat me log galat kaam kar dete hain — doosra card nikaal lete hain, ya app se instant loan le lete hain. Kram ye rakhiye:

  1. Card ko wallet se nikaal dijiye aur online saved card delete kar dijiye. Naya kharcha rukna sabse pehla kaam hai.
  2. Poora number likhiye — kitna balance, kaunsa rate, kis tareekh ko bill. Andaza mat lagaiye, statement kholiye.
  3. Bank ko call kijiye aur EMI conversion ka rate poochhiye. Ye ek phone call kai baar 51% ko 15% bana deta hai.
  4. Kharche ka hisaab banaiye — har mahine kitna extra daal sakte hain. 50/30/20 rule yahan kaam aata hai.
  5. Ek tareekh tay kijiye jab tak card zero hoga, aur usi hisaab se chaliye.

Aur sabse zaroori — ye sthiti sharm ki baat nahi hai. Bahut logon ke saath hoti hai. Jitni jaldi number saamne rakhenge, utni jaldi nikalenge.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Minimum due bharne se credit score kharaab hota hai?

Payment history kharaab nahi hoti kyunki payment samay par gayi. Par balance bada rehne se utilisation badhta hai, aur usse score gir sakta hai.

Credit card ka byaj kab se lagta hai?

Jis din kharcha hua, us din se — agar aapne pichla bill poora nahi bhara tha. Poora bharne wale logon ko hi interest-free period milta hai.

Cash withdrawal itna bura kyun hai?

Kyunki usme interest-free period hota hi nahi. Nikaalte hi byaj chalu ho jaata hai, aur alag se cash advance fee bhi lagti hai.

Card band karwa dein to?

Jaldi band karna score ko nuksan pahuncha sakta hai kyunki kul limit ghatne se utilisation badh jaata hai. Behtar hai ki balance zero karke card rakh lein aur istemaal na karein.

EMI me convert karwana theek hai?

Revolving balance rakhne se kaafi behtar hai. Processing fee aur GST poochh lein, phir bhi 51% ke muqable sasta padta hai.
