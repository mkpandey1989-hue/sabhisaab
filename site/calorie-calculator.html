Calorie Calculator — Roz Kitni Calorie Chahiye

Roz kitna khaana chahiye — ye sirf bhookh se tay nahi hota. Neeche apna wazan, lambai, umar aur dinacharya daaliye. Zaroorat ka anuman mil jayega.

Number kaise banta hai

Pehle shareer ki basic zaroorat (BMR) nikaali jaati hai, phir usme aapke din bhar ka kaam joda jaata hai.

Zaroorat = BMR × Kaam ka factor
BMR: (10 × wazan) + (6.25 × lambai) − (5 × umar) + 5 (purush) ya −161 (mahila)

70 kg, 170 cm, 30 saal ka purush, halka kaam:

  • BMR: lagbhag 1,617
  • Zaroorat (1.375 factor): lagbhag 2,220 calorie

Ye banaye rakhne ki zaroorat hai — isse kam khaayein to wazan ghatega, zyada khaayein to badhega.

BMR aur zaroorat me farak samajh lijiye — BMR wo hai jo bina kuch kiye jalti hai, aur zaroorat usme din bhar ki halchal jodkar banti hai. Isi liye do log ek hi wazan ke hon, par unki zaroorat alag ho sakti hai.

Number to mil gaya, par "2,200 calorie" ka matlab plate par kya hai? Aam anuman:

Do line jo poora khel badal deti hain — tel aur cheeni. Ek chammach tel lagbhag 120 calorie ka hai aur ek chammach cheeni lagbhag 20. Wahi sabzi kam tel me 100 ki hai aur zyada tel me 250 ki. Isliye khaane ki cheez badalne se pehle banane ka tarika badalna zyada aasaan aur zyada asardar hai.

Wazan ghatana ya badhana

  • Ghatana — zaroorat se 400-500 calorie kam. Hafte me lagbhag aadha kilo ghatta hai. Ye dheema lagta hai par isi tarah ghataya wazan wapas nahi aata.
  • Badhana — 300-500 calorie zyada, aur protein achha rakhiye. Warna sirf charbi badhegi.

Ghatate waqt ye zaroor rakhiye:

  • Protein — daal, doodh, dahi, paneer, ande, chana. Isse maans-peshi bachti hai aur pet bhara rehta hai.
  • Reshedar khaana — sabzi, salad, saabut anaj. Kam calorie me pet bharta hai.
  • Paani — kai baar pyaas ko bhookh samajh liya jaata hai.
Chetavni: BMR se neeche kabhi mat jaiye — lagbhag 1,200 calorie se kam khaana zyadatar logon ke liye theek nahi hai. Shareer apna kharcha ghata deta hai, thakan hoti hai, baal girte hain, mahilaon me periods bigadte hain — aur wazan phir bhi ruk jaata hai.

Number par kitna bharosa karein

Ye ek anuman hai, koi pakka niyam nahi. Do log ek jaise dikhne wale, phir bhi unki zaroorat 200-300 calorie alag ho sakti hai.

Ye cheezein number badal deti hain:

  • Maans-peshi kitni hai — zyada muscle matlab zyada calorie ki zaroorat
  • Thyroid ya koi bimari
  • Kuch dawaiyan
  • Umar — 30 ke baad zaroorat dheere-dheere ghatti hai
  • Garbhavastha ya doodh pilana — zaroorat kaafi badh jaati hai
Sabse achha tarika ye hai — 2-3 hafte isi hisaab se khaakar dekhiye. Wazan waisa nahi badla jaisa socha tha? To number 150-200 calorie upar-neeche kar lijiye. Aapka shareer kisi bhi formula se zyada sahi jawab deta hai.

Agar aapko diabetes, thyroid, gurde ya dil ki koi shikayat hai, ya aap garbhavastha me hain — to diet ka faisla apne doctor ya registered dietitian se hi lijiye. Aur agar khaane ko lekar mann me tanaav rehta ho, ya wazan ki chinta zindagi par bhaari pad rahi ho, to kisi doctor ya bharosemand insaan se baat kijiye. Ye page aam jaankari ke liye hai.

Activity level — yahi wo jagah hai jahan sabse badi galti hoti hai

Calorie ka poora hisaab is ek chunav par tika hai. Zyadatar log apne aap ko asli se ek darja upar rakh dete hain, aur natija 300-400 calorie zyada aa jaata hai.

LevelGuna (BMR par)Sach me kiske liye
Baithe rehna1.2din bhar kursi, koi exercise nahi
Halka1.375hafte me 1-3 din halki exercise
Madhyam1.55hafte me 3-5 din theek se exercise
Zyada1.725hafte me 6-7 din, ya mehnat ka kaam
Bahut zyada1.9khiladi, ya din me do baar training
Number ko sach maan kar mat baithein — 2 hafte jaanchein: ek kilo charbi me lagbhag 7,700 calorie hoti hai. Isliye roz 500 calorie kam khane par hafte me lagbhag 450 gram ghatna chahiye. Tarika ye hai — calculator ka number 2 hafte follow kariye aur hafte me ek baar, subah, khaali pet wazan naapiye. Wazan bilkul na hile to aapki asli zaroorat is number se kam hai; tezi se gire to zyada. Har calculator ka jawab lagbhag 10% aage-peeche ho sakta hai — asli jawab aapka apna wazan deta hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Roz kitni calorie chahiye?

Wazan, lambai, umar aur dinacharya par nirbhar. 70 kg, 170 cm, 30 saal ke halke kaam wale purush ko lagbhag 2,200 calorie — par ye ek anuman hai.

Ek roti me kitni calorie hoti hai?

Madhyam size ki bina ghee wali roti me lagbhag 100-120. Ghee ya tel lagne par ye kaafi badh jaati hai.

Wazan ghatane ke liye kitni calorie kam karein?

Zaroorat se 400-500 kam. Hafte me lagbhag aadha kilo ghatta hai. Isse tez ghataya wazan lagbhag hamesha wapas aa jaata hai.

Bahut kam khaane se jaldi wazan ghatega?

Nahi, ulta hota hai. Bahut kam khaane par shareer apna kharcha ghata deta hai — thakan, baal girna aur wazan ka ruk jaana. 1,200 calorie se neeche zyadatar logon ke liye theek nahi hai.

Hafte me kitna wazan ghatana theek hai?

Lagbhag 0.5% se 1% wazan hafte me — 70 kg ke liye 350 se 700 gram. Isse tez ghatane par maans bhi jaata hai aur wazan wapas aane ka khatra badh jaata hai. Apne doctor ya dietitian se apni sthiti ke hisaab se poochhein.

Calculator ka number galat lage to kya karein?

2 hafte usi par chalein aur hafte me ek baar subah wazan naapein. Wazan na hile to zaroorat is number se kam hai, tezi se gire to zyada. Apna wazan hi sabse sahi jawab deta hai.
