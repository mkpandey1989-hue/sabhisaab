TDS hai kya — ek line me

TDS ka matlab hai Tax Deducted at Source — paisa dene wala hi tax kaat kar sarkar ko jama kar deta hai, aur aapko bacha hua deta hai.

Zaroori baat ye hai — TDS koi alag tax nahi hai. Wo aapke saal ke tax ki hi ek advance kist hai. Agar aapka asli tax TDS se kam nikla, to poora antar refund me wapas aata hai.

Kis parAam dar
FD ya doosra byaj10%
Property ka kiraya10%

Professional ya technical fees10%
Contractor ko payment1-2%

Har cheez par ek chhoot ki seema hoti hai — us se kam rakam par TDS katta hi nahi. Aur ye darein aur seemayein budget ke saath badalti rehti hain, isliye taaza number income tax portal se hi confirm kariye.

PAN na dene par dugna katta hai

Ye wo baat hai jo sabse mehngi padti hai. Agar PAN nahi diya, ya PAN galat/nishkriya hai, to TDS lagbhag dugni dar par kat jaata hai — aksar 20%.

Aur usse bhi bada nuksan ye hai ki bina PAN wala TDS aapke khaate me nahi dikhta. Yaani wo paisa kat gaya par aap use refund me claim bhi nahi kar sakte. Wo poori tarah doob jaata hai.

Isliye do kaam zaroor kariye:

  • Bank, kirayedaar, ya client ko PAN de dijiye — aur sahi de dijiye.
  • PAN-Aadhaar link hona chahiye. Link na hone par PAN nishkriya ho jaata hai, aur tab bhi dugna TDS katta hai. Bahut se log tab jaante hain jab paisa kam aata hai.

Apna kata hua TDS dekhna ho to income tax portal par Form 26AS ya AIS kholiye. Wahan har kata hua rupaya dikhta hai. Saal me ek baar ise zaroor dekh lijiye — kabhi-kabhi kisi ne TDS kaata to hai par jama nahi kiya, aur wo wahin pakda jaata hai.

15G aur 15H — TDS rukwane ka tarika

Agar aapki kul saalana aay tax ke daayre se neeche hai, to TDS katna faltu hai — aap wo paisa saal bhar baad refund me hi wapas lenge. Isse bachne ke liye do form hain:

  • Form 15H — 60 saal ya usse upar ke logon ke liye
  • Form 15G — 60 se kam umar walon ke liye

Ye form bank me (ya jo paisa de raha hai, uske paas) jama karna hota hai. Har saal April me, kyunki ye ek saal ke liye hi chalta hai.

Do zaroori baatein: (1) Har bank me alag form dena hota hai — ek bank me diya hua doosre me nahi chalta. (2) Ye form tabhi bhariye jab aapki aay sach me tax ke daayre se neeche ho. Jhooti ghoshna par jurmana lagta hai.

Ye ek chhota kaam hai jo bujurgon aur grihiniyon ka bada jhanjhat khatam kar deta hai — warna saal bhar paisa kat kar padta rehta hai aur refund ke liye ITR bharni padti hai.

Kata hua TDS wapas kaise milta hai

Agar TDS aapke asli tax se zyada kat gaya, to wo refund me wapas milta hai. Rasta ek hi hai — ITR bharni padti hai. Bina ITR ke refund nahi aata, chahe kitna bhi zyada kata ho.

Kadam:

  1. Form 26AS aur AIS se dekhiye ki kitna TDS kata hai
  2. ITR me poori aay dikhaiye aur kata hua TDS bhariye
  3. Bank khaata pehle se pre-validate hona chahiye, warna refund atak jaata hai
  4. ITR bharne ke baad e-verify zaroor kariye — bina verify ke ITR maani hi nahi jaati
Sabse aam galti — ITR bhar dena par e-verify na karna. Aisi ITR "bhari hui" nahi maani jaati, aur refund kabhi nahi aata. Aadhaar OTP se ye 2 minute ka kaam hai.

Refund aam taur par kuch hafton me aa jaata hai. Der ho to portal par status dekh sakte hain.

Poori jaankari TDS Guide me hai. Apna tax dekhne ke liye Income Tax Calculator aur ITR-3 Guide dekhiye. Hum CA nahi hain — apne case ka pakka jawab CA ya incometax.gov.in se lijiye.

Official source: incometax.gov.in — TDS ki taaza darein aur seemayein yahin se confirm karein.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

TDS ka paisa wapas milta hai?

Haan, agar aapka asli tax TDS se kam hai. Par uske liye ITR bharna zaroori hai — bina ITR ke refund nahi aata.

PAN na dene par kya hota hai?

TDS lagbhag dugni dar (aksar 20%) par katta hai. Aur bina PAN wala TDS aapke khaate me nahi dikhta — yaani wo refund me bhi claim nahi ho sakta.

15G aur 15H me kya farak hai?

15H 60 saal ya usse upar ke logon ke liye, 15G usse kam umar walon ke liye. Dono har saal April me, aur har bank me alag dena hota hai.

Apna kata hua TDS kahan dekhein?

Income tax portal par Form 26AS ya AIS me. Saal me ek baar zaroor dekhiye — kabhi-kabhi TDS kata to hota hai par jama nahi kiya gaya hota.
