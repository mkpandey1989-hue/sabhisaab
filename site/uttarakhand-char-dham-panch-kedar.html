Dhaarmik Yatra: Char Dham, Panch Kedar, Panch Badri, Panch Prayag

Uttarakhand ke Char Dham kaun se hain?

Uttarakhand ke Char Dham hain Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath aur Badrinath. Yatra paramparik roop se Yamunotri se shuru hokar Gangotri, Kedarnath aur ant me Badrinath tak, yani pashchim se purv ki taraf ki jati hai.

Char Dham ke kapat kab khulte aur band hote hain?

Yamunotri aur Gangotri ke kapat aam taur par Akshaya Tritiya (April-May) ke din khulte hain. Kedarnath aur Badrinath ke kapat ki tithi parampara ke anusar tay hoti hai aur usi samay ke aaspaas khulte hain. Sardiyon me barf ke kaaran sabhi dham aam taur par Diwali ke aaspaas (Bhai Dooj tak) band ho jate hain.

Badrinath ke kapat kholne ki tithi Basant Panchami par Tehri ke rajparivar ki parampara ke saath ghoshit hoti hai. Kedarnath ki tithi Mahashivratri ke aaspaas Ukhimath me tay hoti hai. Har saal ki exact tithi ke liye Badrinath-Kedarnath Mandir Samiti aur Uttarakhand Tourism ke official ghoshnaye dekhein, kyunki ye tithi hindu panchang ke anusar saal-dar-saal badalti hai.

Char Dham 2026 Season: Kapat Kab Khule, Kab Band Hue (Ek Table me)

Seedha jawab: 2026 me Gangotri aur Yamunotri ke kapat 19 April (Akshaya Tritiya), Kedarnath ke 22 April aur Badrinath ke 23 April ko khule. Gangotri ke kapat 10 November, Kedarnath ke 11 November (Bhai Dooj, subah lagbhag 8:30 baje) ko band hue, aur Badrinath ke kapat band hone ki tithi 16 November 2026, raat lagbhag 9:37 baje tay thi. Agle saal ki tithi Basant Panchami (Badrinath) aur Mahashivratri (Kedarnath) par ghoshit hoti hai.

Char Dham Yatra registration aur taiyari

Char Dham yatra ke liye Uttarakhand sarkar ke official portal par registration aam taur par zaroori hota hai. Nakli ya anadhikrit websites se bachein aur sirf sarkari portal ka istemal karein.

  • Official portal: Uttarakhand Tourism ka registration aur tourist care portal (registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in). Niyam aur slot system har saal badal sakte hain, isliye yatra se pehle taaza adhisuchna dekhein.
  • Kya saath rakhein: pehchan patra, sehat se judi zaroori dawaiyan, garam kapde, barish ka saaman, aur Kedarnath/Badrinath ke trek par chadhne wale logon ke liye aaramdayak joote.
  • Unchai ki saavdhani: 3,000 m se upar Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) ka khatra hota hai. Dheere chalein, paani peete rahein, aur tabiyat bigadne par neeche utarna sabse surakshit upay hai.
  • Heli seva: Kedarnath aur kuch anya dhaamon ke liye helicopter seva sirf authorised operators se hi book karein.

Panch Kedar

Panch Kedar Bhagwan Shiv ke paanch pavitra sthal hain: Kedarnath, Tungnath, Rudranath, Madhyamaheshwar aur Kalpeshwar. Paramparik katha ke anusar Pandavon se bachne ke liye Shiv ne bail ka roop dhara aur unke alag-alag ang in paanch sthalon par prakat hue.

Panch Badri

Panch Badri Bhagwan Vishnu ke paanch mandir hain: Badrinath (Vishal Badri), Yogdhyan Badri, Bhavishya Badri, Vriddha Badri aur Adi Badri.

  • Badrinath (Vishal Badri), Chamoli: Alaknanda ke kinare mukhya dham.
  • Yogdhyan Badri, Pandukeshwar: Pandav raja Pandu ki tapasya sthali maana jata hai.
  • Bhavishya Badri, Subain (Joshimath ke paas): manyata hai ki bhavishya me Badrinath ka rasta band hone par yahan pooja hogi.
  • Vriddha Badri, Animath: Badrinath se purani pooja-sthali maani jati hai.
  • Adi Badri, Karnaprayag ke paas: Chandpur kshetra ke prachin mandiron ka samooh.

Kumbh Mela

Haridwar Uttarakhand ka vah sthal hai jahan Kumbh mela hota hai. Kumbh aam taur par har 12 saal me lagta hai, aur beech me Ardh Kumbh hota hai. Reports ke anusar agla Haridwar Kumbh 2027 me hone ki taiyari chal rahi hai. Exact tithiyon ke liye Haridwar Mela Pradhikaran aur Uttarakhand sarkar ki official ghoshna dekhein.

Nanda Devi Raj Jat

Nanda Devi Raj Jat Uttarakhand ki sabse lambi aur kathin dharmik paidal yatra hai jo lagbhag har 12 saal me hoti hai. Ise “Himalayan Mahakumbh” bhi kaha jata hai. Chausingya Khadu (chaar sing wala meda) is yatra ko neta maana jata hai.

Yatra Nauti (Chamoli) se shuru hokar Homkund tak jaati hai aur lagbhag 280 km ka kathin pahadi marg tay karti hai. 2026 me ise karne ki charcha thi, par Raj Jat Samiti ne ise 2027 me karne ki ghoshna ki hai. Exact tithi aur marg ke liye Nanda Devi Raj Jat Samiti, Nauti aur zila prashasan ki official ghoshna hi final maani jayein.

Sthaniya devta aur lok-aastha

Uttarakhand ki aastha sirf bade mandiron tak simit nahi hai. Har ghati ka apna gram-devta hota hai, jaise Golu Devta (nyay ke devta, Kumaon), Mahasu Devta (Jaunsar-Bawar, Dehradun/Uttarkashi/Tehri kshetra), Latu Devta (Wan gaon, Chamoli), Nanda Devi (poore kshetra ki ishta), Bhumiyal aur Haru Heet. Jagar aur Dangariya ki parampara me devta ko dhol-damau, hudka ke saath avahan kiya jata hai. Iske vistaar ke liye Pahadi virasat aur tyohar dekhein.

Siddh peeth aur Shakti peeth ki sthaniya manyatayein

Garhwal ke Tehri kshetra me Surkanda Devi, Kunjapuri Devi aur Chandrabadni ko tin siddh peeth maana jata hai. Haridwar me Mansa Devi, Chandi Devi aur Maya Devi ko mahatvapurn Shakti sthal maana jata hai. Kumaon me Purnagiri, Naina Devi aur Dunagiri ki bahut manyata hai.

Shakti peeth ki katha ke anusar Bhagwan Shiv Sati ke shareer ko lekar jab bhatke to unke ang alag-alag sthanon par gire. Kai sthaniya mandir apni manyata ke anusar in kathaon se judte hain. Ye aastha aur parampara ki baatein hain, aur alag-alag granth aur kshetra me alag manyatayein milti hain.

Sikh Teerth

  • Hemkund Sahib (Chamoli): lagbhag 4,300 meter ki unchai par.
  • Nanakmatta Sahib (Udham Singh Nagar): Nanak Sagar ke paas.
  • Reetha Sahib (Champawat): Sikh teerth sthal.

Buddh aur anya dharmik sthal

  • Dehradun me Buddh mandir aur Tibetan basti: Clement Town ka Buddh mandir aur Mindrolling Monastery.
  • Piran Kaliyar Sharif (Roorkee): Sufi dargah.
  • Rishikesh aur Haridwar ke ashram: yoga aur dhyan ke kendra.

Mandir jane ke niyam aur sheeshtachar

  • Kai mandiron me chamde ki vastu aur mobile ka istemal pratibandhit ho sakta hai.
  • Pahadi mandir me aksar vastra-niyam aur darshan ke samay tay hote hain.
  • Photography ke niyam mandir ke hisaab se alag hote hain; pehle poochh lein.
  • Prasad aur pooja saman sirf adhikrit dukan se hi lein.

Uttarakhand ke Teerth: Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Char Dham kaun se hain?
Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath aur Badrinath Uttarakhand ke Char Dham hain. Paramparik krama Yamunotri se shuru hokar Gangotri, Kedarnath aur Badrinath tak hai.
Jageshwar Dham kahan hai?
Jageshwar Dham Almora zile me hai, jo Devdar ke jungle ke beech prachin mandir samooh ke liye jana jata hai.
Baijnath mandir kahan hai?
Baijnath mandir Bageshwar zile me hai, jo Katyuri kaal ke mandir samooh ke liye jana jata hai.
Gangotri aur Yamunotri kis zile me hain?
Dono Uttarkashi zile me hain.
Badrinath tak sadak marg se kaun se shahar hokar jaate hain?
Aam taur par Rishikesh, Devprayag, Srinagar, Rudraprayag, Karnaprayag, Chamoli aur Joshimath hokar Badrinath pahunchte hain.
Gangotri aur Yamunotri ka sadak marg kaisa hai?
Dono dham ke liye Rishikesh, Chamba aur Uttarkashi hokar marg jaate hain. Yamunotri ke liye Barkot-Janki Chatti hokar paidal chadhai hoti hai.
Kya Hemkund Sahib ke liye sadak hai?
Govindghat tak sadak hai. Uske baad Ghangaria tak paidal ya khacchar marg aur phir Hemkund tak chadhai hoti hai.
