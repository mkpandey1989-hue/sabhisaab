Gratuity Kya Hai Aur Kitni Milegi?

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026

🔄 Aakhri update: July 2026 · Government yojana, tax slab aur charges samay-samay par badalte rehte hain. Ye sirf jaankari hai — nivesh/tax salah nahi.

Gratuity ek aisi rakam hai jo company apne employee ko lambe samay tak kaam karne ke badle deti hai — aam taur par jab wo naukri chhodta hai, retire hota hai, ya kisi aur wajah se company se alag hota hai. Ye ek tarah ka "shukriya" bonus hai jo kanoon (Payment of Gratuity Act) ke tehat milta hai. Is guide me hum samjhenge ki gratuity kis-kis ko milti hai, kaise banti hai, aur neeche calculator se aap apni anumanit gratuity nikaal sakte hain.

Gratuity kis-kis ko milti hai?

Gratuity ka haq tab banta hai jab aap ek hi company me kam se kam 5 saal lagataar kaam kar lein. 5 saal poore hone ke baad hi ye milti hai (kuch mamlon me, jaise employee ki mrityu ya viklaangta par, 5 saal ki shart laagu nahi hoti).

Gratuity ka formula

Formula: Gratuity = (15 × aakhri salary × naukri ke saal) ÷ 26

Yahan "salary" ka matlab hai Basic + DA (poori CTC nahi), aur 26 iska matlab mahine me 26 kaam ke din maane jaate hain.

Misaal: Agar aapki aakhri Basic+DA salary ₹40,000 hai aur aapne 10 saal kaam kiya, to gratuity = (15 × 40,000 × 10) ÷ 26 = lagbhag ₹2.3 lakh.

Saal ki ginti kaise hoti hai?

Agar aapne 10 saal 7 mahine kaam kiya, to 7 mahine 6 se zyada hone ke kaaran ise 11 saal maana jaata hai. Agar 6 mahine ya kam ho to us saal ko chhod diya jaata hai. Yani 6 mahine se upar = agla poora saal.

Gratuity par tax

Sarkari karmchariyon ki gratuity poori tax-free hoti hai. Private employees ke liye gratuity ₹20 lakh tak tax-free hai (formula ke hisaab se banne wali rakam tak). Isse zyada par tax lag sakta hai.

Calculator kaise use karein?

  1. Apni aakhri Basic + DA salary daalein.
  2. Company me kitne saal kaam kiya, wo daalein (5 se zyada).
  3. Result me anumanit gratuity dikhegi.

FAQ

Kya 5 saal se pehle gratuity milti hai?

Aam taur par nahi. Sirf employee ki mrityu ya sthayi viklaangta jaise mamlon me 5 saal ki shart laagu nahi hoti.

4 saal 7 mahine me gratuity milegi?

Kuch adaalati faislon ke hisaab se 4 saal 240+ din ko 5 saal maana gaya hai, par ye company aur mamle par depend karta. Apni company ki HR se confirm karein.

Gratuity aur PF ek hi cheez hai?

Nahi. PF (EPF) me har mahine salary se katta hai. Gratuity company deti hai, iske liye aapki salary se kuch nahi katta.

Gratuity ke liye kitne saal kaam karna zaroori hai?

Aam taur par 5 saal poore. Kuch sthitiyon me, jaise kaam ke dauraan mrityu ya viklangta, ye shart laagu nahi hoti.

Gratuity par tax lagta hai?

Ek tay seema tak chhoot milti hai. Us seema se upar wali rakam aay me judti hai. Seema samay ke saath badalti hai — apne CA ya official portal se confirm kar lijiye.

Saal ginne ka niyam — yahan log chook jaate hain

Gratuity ka hisaab poore saalon par lagta hai, par aakhri saal ke liye ek khaas niyam hai: agar aapne 6 mahine se zyada kaam kiya hai, to wo poora saal gina jaata hai.

  • 7 saal 8 mahine → 8 saal gine jaayenge
  • 7 saal 4 mahine → 7 saal hi rahenge

Gratuity kab milti hai, kab nahi

Sirf 5 saal poore karna hi shart nahi hai. Alag-alag sthitiyaan:

4 saal 7 mahine wali line lakhon ka farak daalti hai. Kai adalati faislon me 4 saal 240 din (lagbhag 7 mahine 20 din) ko poora 5va saal maana gaya hai. Isliye 4.5 saal ke aas-paas naukri chhodne ka soch rahe hain to do-teen mahine ruk jaana seedha paisa hai. Doosri baat — gratuity aakhri basic + DA par banti hai, poori CTC par nahi. Aur company chhodte waqt claim khud karna padta hai (Form I); apne aap nahi aata. 30 din ke andar dena kanoon me tay hai, der par byaj bhi maanga ja sakta hai.
Practical baat: agar aapki naukri 4 saal 9 mahine ki hai aur aap resign karne ja rahe hain, to kuch mahine ruk jaana faydemand ho sakta hai — 5 saal poore hote hi aap gratuity ke haqdaar ban jaayenge. Ye chhoti si planning kai baar lakh rupaye ki hoti hai.

Formula ka matlab

Formula hai: (aakhri basic + DA) × 15 ÷ 26 × naukri ke saal. Yahan 26 ka matlab mahine ke kaam ke din (Sunday chhod kar) aur 15 ka matlab har saal ke liye aadhe mahine ki salary. Yaani har poore saal ke badle lagbhag 15 din ki tankhwah.

Dhyaan dein — hisaab basic + DA par hota hai, poori CTC par nahi. Isi liye jinki salary me basic ka hissa kam hai, unki gratuity bhi kam banti hai.

Kis-kis sthiti me milti hai

  • Naukri chhodne par — 5 saal poore hone chahiye
  • Retirement par — 5 saal ki shart lagu
  • Company se nikale jaane par — 5 saal poore hon to milti hai
  • Mrityu ya viklangata par — 5 saal ki shart lagu nahi. Nominee ya karmchari ko milti hai

Isi liye nomination form bharna zaroori hai. Bahut se log join karte waqt ye form khaali chhod dete hain, aur baad me parivaar ko kagzi kaam me pareshaani hoti hai.

Tax ka niyam

Sarkari karmchariyon ki gratuity poori tarah tax-free hoti hai. Private company ke karmchariyon ke liye ek tay limit tak tax nahi lagta, us se upar slab ke hisaab se lagta hai. Ye limit sarkar samay-samay par badhati rehti hai, aur ye limit poore jeevan ki kul gratuity par gini jaati hai — har naukri par alag se nahi. Badi rakam ho to CA se confirm karna behtar hai.

Na mile to kya karein

  1. Pehle HR ko likhit me application dein aur uski copy apne paas rakhein
  2. Aam taur par 30 din ke andar bhugtan hona chahiye
  3. Na mile to apne ilaake ke Controlling Authority (Labour Commissioner) ke paas Form N me shikayat karein
  4. Deri par company ko byaj bhi dena pad sakta hai

Ek aur baat — gratuity aur EPF alag-alag cheezein hain. Company kabhi-kabhi CTC me gratuity dikhati hai; wo aapko tabhi milegi jab 5 saal poore honge, warna nahi.
