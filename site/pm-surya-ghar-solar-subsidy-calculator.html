PM Surya Ghar Solar Subsidy Calculator — Subsidy, Kharcha Aur Wasooli

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: October 2026 · Padhne me ~7 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Chhat par solar lagwane se pehle teen sawaal sabke mann me hote hain — sarkar kitna degi, jeb se kitna jayega, aur paisa kitne saal me wapas aayega. PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana me teeno ka jawab seedhe hisaab se nikalta hai.

Seedha jawab: ghar ki chhat ke solar par pehle 2 kW tak ₹30,000 per kW aur teesre kW par ₹18,000 subsidy milti hai — zyada se zyada ₹78,000. Uttarakhand, Himachal, J&K, Ladakh, North-East jaise vishesh rajyon me ye 10% zyada hai — ₹33,000 per kW aur zyada se zyada ₹85,800.

Neeche system ka size, apna rajya, kul keemat aur bijli ki dar daaliye. Subsidy, apna kharcha aur wasooli ka samay turant dikh jaayega.

Subsidy ka poora hisaab — kW ke hisaab se

Subsidy system ki keemat ka pratishat nahi hai. Ye har kW ki tay rakam hai. Isliye mehnga ya sasta vendor, subsidy wahi rehti hai.

1.5 kW₹45,000₹49,500
2 kW₹60,000₹66,000
2.5 kW₹69,000₹75,900
3 kW₹78,000₹85,800
4, 5 ya 6 kW₹78,000₹85,800
Aakhri line par dhyaan dijiye. 3 kW ke baad subsidy ek rupaya nahi badhti. 3 kW par subsidy ₹26,000 per kW padti hai, 6 kW par sirf ₹13,000 per kW. Isliye bada system tabhi lijiye jab bijli ki khapat sach me itni ho — sirf subsidy dekh kar nahi.

Vishesh rajyon me Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu-Kashmir, Ladakh, North-East ke rajya, Andaman-Nicobar aur Lakshadweep aate hain. Wahan pahad me saamaan le jaane ka kharcha zyada hai, isliye subsidy bhi 10% zyada rakhi gayi hai.

3 kW ka misaal — paisa kitne saal me wapas

Maan lijiye 3 kW system ki kul keemat ₹1,80,000 hai, bijli ₹6 per unit hai, aur ek kW roz lagbhag 4 unit banata hai.

Panel aam taur par 25 saal chalte hain. Yaani lagbhag 4 saal baad baaki 20 saal ki bijli lagbhag muft. Bas beech me inverter ek baar badalna pad sakta hai — uska kharcha bhi soch kar rakhiye.

Pahad me dhoop kam ghante milti hai aur monsoon me baadal rehte hain. Wahan "roz kitni unit" wale khaane me 4 ki jagah 3.2-3.5 daal kar bhi dekh lijiye. Asli unit chhat ki disha aur chhaya par bhi nirbhar karti hain.

"Muft bijli" ka asli matlab

Naam se lagta hai ki sarkar bijli muft degi. Aisa nahi hai.

"Muft bijli" ka matlab hai ki aapki chhat ka panel jo bijli banata hai, uske liye bill nahi dena. 3 kW system mahine me lagbhag 300-360 unit banata hai — isi se "300 unit muft" ki baat aati hai. Jo ghar 300 unit se zyada khapata hai, uska bill aayega, bas chhota.

Din me jo bijli aap istemaal nahi karte, wo net meter ke zariye grid me jaati hai. Raat ko grid se li bijli me wo ghat jaati hai. Bachi hui bijli ka kya bhav milega, ye aapke rajya ki bijli company ke niyam tay karte hain.

Apne ghar ki bijli khapat pehle samajh lijiye — Bijli Bill Calculator aur Appliance Bijli Kharch me dekh sakte hain.

Kaun le sakta hai aur shartein

  • Ghar aapka apna ho aur chhat par lagane ki jagah ho — lagbhag 10 square meter per kW, bina chhaya ke.
  • Ghar ka bijli connection aapke naam par ho. Ek connection par ek hi baar subsidy milti hai.
  • System grid se juda (on-grid) ho. Sirf battery wala off-grid system is subsidy me nahi aata.
  • Panel desh me bane cell wale (DCR) hon aur lagane wala vendor portal par registered ho.

Vendor kam daam ka laalach dekar unregistered saamaan laga de to subsidy atak sakti hai. Isliye vendor hamesha pmsuryaghar.gov.in ki list se chuniye.

Kai rajya central subsidy ke upar apni alag subsidy bhi dete hain. Wo is calculator me nahi judi — apne rajya ki bijli company se poochh lijiye.

Apply kaise karein — 6 kadam

  1. pmsuryaghar.gov.in par rajya, bijli company aur consumer number se registration.
  2. Login karke rooftop solar ke liye form bhariye.
  3. Bijli company se feasibility (manzoori) ka intezaar.
  4. Portal ke registered vendor se system lagwaiye.
  5. Net meter lagne aur jaanch (commissioning) ke baad portal par bank khata ki jaankari daaliye.
  6. Subsidy seedha bank khate me aati hai.

Pura paisa ek saath na ho to is yojana ke saath bina girvi ke loan ki suvidha bhi hai. Byaj dar bank se confirm kariye aur EMI EMI Calculator me nikaal lijiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

PM Surya Ghar me kitni subsidy milti hai?

Pehle 2 kW tak ₹30,000 per kW aur teesre kW par ₹18,000, yaani 3 kW par ₹78,000. Isse bade system par bhi subsidy ₹78,000 hi rehti hai.

Uttarakhand me solar subsidy kitni hai?

Uttarakhand vishesh rajyon me aata hai. Wahan pehle 2 kW tak ₹33,000 per kW aur teesre kW par ₹19,800 milte hain — zyada se zyada ₹85,800.

3 kW solar se mahine me kitni bijli banti hai?

Lagbhag 300 se 360 unit, dhoop aur chhat ki disha ke hisaab se. Pahad aur baadal wale mausam me thodi kam ban sakti hai.

Kya battery wale off-grid solar par subsidy milti hai?

Nahi. Subsidy sirf grid se jude (on-grid) rooftop system par hai, jo net meter ke saath lagta hai.

Solar ka paisa kitne saal me wasool hota hai?

Aam taur par 3 se 5 saal me, bijli ki dar aur system ki keemat ke hisaab se. Panel lagbhag 25 saal chalte hain.
