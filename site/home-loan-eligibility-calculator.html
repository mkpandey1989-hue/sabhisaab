Formula aur uska matlab

Bank aam taur par mahine ki income ka lagbhag 50% tak hi kul EMI jaane dete hain (income zyada ho to thoda oopar bhi). Isme aapki pehle se chal rahi saari EMI shaamil hoti hai. Bachi hui EMI kshamta ko byaj dar aur avadhi ke hisaab se ulta ginte hain, aur wahi loan ki anumanit rakam banti hai.

Formula: Max EMI = (income × 50%) − chalu EMI
Loan = EMI × [1 − (1+i)−n] / i

Teen misaal, asli number ke saath

Misaal 1 — chhoti income. Mahine ki income ₹27,000, chalu EMI ₹7,550, byaj 12%, avadhi 3 saal. Max nayi EMI ₹5,950 aur anumanit loan lagbhag ₹1,79,140.

Misaal 2 — bina purani EMI. Income ₹60,000, koi chalu EMI nahi, 8.5% byaj, 20 saal. Max EMI ₹30,000 aur loan lagbhag ₹34.7 lakh.

Misaal 3 — avadhi ka asar. Wahi ₹30,000 EMI, 8.5% byaj — 10 saal par loan lagbhag ₹24.2 lakh, aur 20 saal par ₹34.7 lakh. Avadhi badhane se loan badhta hai, par kul byaj bhi bahut badh jaata hai.

Sabse aam galtiyan

  1. Gross salary maan lena. Bank in-hand (net) salary dekhte hain, CTC nahi. CTC par hisaab lagane se number bahut oopar chala jaata hai.
  2. Credit card ka bakaya bhool jaana. Chalu credit card outstanding aur uska minimum due bhi aapki kshamta ghata deta hai.
  3. Registry aur down payment na ginna. Bank aam taur par property value ka 75-90% hi deta hai. Baaki paisa, stamp duty aur registry aapko dena hota hai.
  4. Credit score na dekhna. Kam score par ya to loan nahi milta ya dar oonchi ho jaati hai. Apply se pehle score dekh lein.
  5. Poori limit tak loan lena. Bank jitna de raha hai, utna lena zaroori nahi. EMI income ke 35-40% se neeche rakhna zyada aaram deta hai.

Bharat me kya alag hai

  • LTV: Bank aam taur par property value ka 75-90% tak deta hai. Baaki down payment aapka.
  • Co-applicant: Pati-patni ya maa-baap ko jodne se eligibility badh jaati hai, kyunki dono ki income judti hai.
  • Umar: Loan aam taur par retirement ki umar tak hi chalta hai, isliye zyada umar par avadhi chhoti milti hai.
  • Tax: Old Regime me home loan ke byaj aur mool par chhoot milti hai. New Regime me ye chhoot aam taur par nahi milti.
  • Ye anuman hai: Asli eligibility bank apni policy, credit score aur documents dekh kar tay karta hai.

Sanyukt (joint) loan — dono ki aay jodne par kya badalta hai

Akele ki aay se loan kam pad raha ho to joint home loan ek asli raasta hai. Dono ki aay jud jaati hai aur eligibility kaafi badh jaati hai.

Co-applicant kaun ban sakta hai: jeevansaathi, maata-pita, aur kuch maamlon me bhai. Zyadatar bank chahte hain ki co-applicant property me hissedaar bhi ho.

Misaal: pati ki aay ₹60,000 aur patni ki ₹40,000. Akele pati par lagbhag ₹35-40 lakh ka loan mil sakta hai; dono ki aay milakar wo lagbhag ₹60-65 lakh tak pahunch jaata hai. Exact number bank, umar aur pehle se chal rahi EMI par nirbhar karta hai.

Teen bade faayde

  • Zyada loan — dono ki aay judne se.
  • Tax ki chhoot dono ko alag-alag — purane regime me, agar dono co-owner hain aur dono sach me EMI de rahe hain. Ye faayda bada hota hai aur aksar chhoot jaata hai.
  • Mahila co-applicant par kai bank byaj me thodi chhoot dete hain, aur kai rajyon me stamp duty me bhi raahat milti hai.

Par ye baat bhi jaan lijiye: joint loan me dono ki zimmedari poori hoti hai. Ek na de paye to bank doosre se poora maangta hai, aur dono ka credit score saath me girta hai. Isliye rishta chahe koi bhi ho, property ke kaagaz aur hisse ki likha-padhi saaf rakhiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Bank kitna home loan deta hai?

Aam taur par mahine ki income ka lagbhag 50% tak kul EMI jaane dete hain, aur property value ka 75-90% tak loan.

Eligibility kaise badhaye?

Purani EMI khatam karein, co-applicant jodein, avadhi badhayein aur credit score sudhaarein.

Kya CTC par loan milta hai?

Nahi, bank in-hand salary aur bank statement dekhte hain.

Credit score kitna chahiye?

Zyadatar bank achha score maangte hain — jitna oopar, dar utni behtar.

Kya ye number pakka hai?

Nahi, ye sirf anuman hai. Asli manjoori bank ke documents check ke baad hi milti hai.

Sanyukt (joint) loan me eligibility kitni badh jaati hai?

Dono ki aay jud jaati hai, isliye loan lagbhag dedh se dugna tak ja sakta hai. Exact number bank, umar aur pehle se chal rahi EMI par nirbhar karta hai.

Joint home loan me tax ki chhoot dono ko milti hai?

Purane regime me haan — agar dono property ke co-owner hain aur dono sach me EMI de rahe hain. Ye faayda bada hai aur aksar chhoot jaata hai.

Aamdani ke hisaab se kitna loan mil sakta hai

Bank aam taur par aapki mahine ki aamdani ka 50-55% tak EMI maan leta hai. Neeche 8.5% par 20 saal ka anuman hai, ye maankar ki aur koi EMI nahi chal rahi.

Doosri — bank ghar ki keemat ka 75-80% hi deta hai, isliye 20-25% down payment aur upar se stamp duty aur registry ka paisa apne paas hona chahiye. Loan manzoor ho jaana aur ghar khareed paana, do alag baatein hain.
