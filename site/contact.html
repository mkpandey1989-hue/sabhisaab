Jawab kitne samay me milta hai

Sab Hisaab ek vyakti dwara chalayi jaane wali website hai — koi badi team nahi hai. Isliye har message main khud padhta hoon aur khud jawab deta hoon. Aam taur par 2-3 kaam ke din lag jaate hain. Kabhi zyada message aa jaayein to thodi der ho sakti hai, par jawab zaroor milta hai.

Agar aapne kisi tool me galti batayi hai, to wo sabse pehle dekhi jaati hai. Aisi shikayat aam taur par usi din ya agle din theek kar di jaati hai, kyunki galat hisaab se kisi ka nuksan ho sakta hai.

Galti batate waqt ye zaroor likhein

Taaki dikkat jaldi pakdi ja sake, message me itni jaankari de dein:

  • Kaunse tool ya guide ki baat hai (naam ya link)
  • Aapne kya value daali thi
  • Kya jawab aaya aur aapke hisaab se kya aana chahiye tha

Ho sake to ek screenshot bhej dein — usse baat turant samajh aa jaati hai.

Naya tool ya guide chahiye?

Bejhijhak likhein. Website par jude kai tools logon ke sujhaav se hi bane hain. Batayein ki aapko kis cheez ka hisaab lagana hai aur kis kaam ke liye — usi hisaab se tool banaya jaata hai. Sarkari yojana, form ya kisi local zaroorat se juda topic ho to aur behtar, kyunki aise vishay par saaf jaankari kam milti hai.

Kin baaton me hum madad nahi kar sakte

Ye saaf bata dena zaroori hai, taaki aapka samay bekaar na jaaye:

  • Vyaktigat nivesh salah nahi — kaunsa stock, fund ya policy lein, ye hum nahi bata sakte. Hum SEBI-registered advisor nahi hain.
  • Trading tips nahi — kya kharidein, kab bechein, target kya rakhein — aise sawaalon ka jawab nahi diya jaata.
  • ITR filing ya tax salah nahi — hum CA nahi hain. Guides me jaankari hai, par aapka apna case CA hi dekh sakta hai.
  • Kisi ka data nikalwana nahi — PAN, Aadhaar, khatauni ya kisi bhi vyakti ki nijee jaankari se juda kaam nahi kiya jaata.
  • Paid promotion ya guest post nahi — website par kisi product ya company ka prachaar paise lekar nahi kiya jaata.

Jawab kaun deta hai

Is website par koi team ya call centre nahi hai. Har sandesh Manoj Kumar Pandey khud padhte hain aur khud jawab dete hain. Isi wajah se jawab me thoda samay lagta hai, par jawab asli insaan ka hota hai, koi tayyar template nahi. Koi automatic chatbot ya "ticket number" wali cheez yahan nahi hai.

Nijee jaankari na bhejein

Savdhaani: email me apna PAN, Aadhaar number, bank account number, ATM PIN, OTP ya password kabhi na bhejein. Hum kabhi bhi ye jaankari nahi maangte. Agar koi Sab Hisaab ke naam par aisi jaankari maange, to wo dhokha hai — turant nazarandaz karein.

Aam sawaal jinka jawab page par pehle se hai

Kuch sawaal baar-baar aate hain. Inka jawab site par hi mil jaata hai, isliye pehle ek nazar yahan daal lein — aapka samay bachega:

  • "Mera data aapke paas jaata hai?" — Nahi. Har calculator aapke browser me chalta hai. Koi number hamare paas nahi aata. Poori baat Privacy Policy me likhi hai.
  • "Result save kaise karein?" — Har tool ke neeche "Result copy karein" ka button hai. Print ya PDF ke liye browser ka print vikalp chalega — header aur footer apne aap hat jaate hain.
  • "App kahan se download karein?" — Alag app nahi hai. Browser ke menu se "Add to Home screen" dabaiye, site app ki tarah khulne lagegi.
  • "Ye tool paise leta hai?" — Nahi. Saare tools free hain aur aage bhi rahenge.
