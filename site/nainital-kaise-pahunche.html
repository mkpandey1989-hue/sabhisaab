Nainital kaise pahunchein (hawai jahaz, train, sadak, bus), shehar me gaadi kahan khadi karein, kahan rukein, aur pahad me network, bijli aur paani ki asli sthiti kya hai. Yeh hissa safar ka poora plan banane me kaam aayega.

Zaroori baat: Train aur flight ka samay, kiraya, parking aur traffic ke niyam badalte rehte hain.

Nainital: Hawai aur Helicopter Seva (UDAN aur Charter)

Nazdeeki Airport, UDAN Udaan, Helicopter Seva aur Charter Booking

Nainital ka sabse nazdeeki hawai adda Pantnagar Airport (PGH) hai, jo Udham Singh Nagar zile me hai. Ye Nainital se lagbhag 70 km (2 se 3 ghante), Haldwani se lagbhag 26 km aur Ramnagar (Corbett) se lagbhag 80 km door hai.

Pantnagar se Nainital kaise jayein?

  • Hotel pickup: Kai Nainital hotel pehle se booking par airport transfer dete hain.
  • Taxi: Pantnagar se seedhe Nainital ke liye taxi.
  • Bus ya shared taxi: Pantnagar airport se seedhi bus nahi hoti. Pehle Haldwani pahunchein, phir Haldwani ya Kathgodam se Nainital ke liye bus ya shared taxi (Nainital: Bus Seva, Shared Taxi aur Sthaniya Yatayat).
Delhi se udna ya sadak?
Agar aapke paas Pantnagar ki udaan available hai, to samay bachta hai. Delhi se Nainital ki sadak yatra 7 se 8 ghante ki hoti hai aur weekend me jaam ki wajah se aur lambi ho sakti hai.

Helicopter seva: Oct 2025 ki ek sarvajanik report ke anusar Uttarakhand sarkar ki heli-seva me Nainital, Haldwani aur Pantnagar jaise sthan shamil bataye gaye hain. Niyamit schedule, kiraya aur booking ka tareeka (aam taur par Uttarakhand Civil Aviation Development Authority, UCADA, aur nijee operators ke zariye) badalta rehta hai. Nainital ke aas-paas helipad aur charter ki taaza sthiti ke liye UCADA ya zila prashasan se pooch lein. Aapatkaal me air ambulance ke baare me Nainital: Swasthya: Aspatal, Trauma Center aur Ayush dekhein.

Train ka samay, number aur chalne ke din badalte rehte hain. IRCTC ya National Train Enquiry System (NTES) par taaza jaankari dekhein.

Kathgodam se Nainital: Station se bahar taxi, shared taxi aur bus milti hain. Raasta lagbhag 35 km ka hai aur 1 se 1.5 ghante lagte hain; weekend aur peak season me zyada. Train se uttar kar subah jaldi niklein, taaki jaam se bach sakein.

Naye rail project:

  • Tanakpur-Bageshwar rail project: Ye Kumaon ke pahadi zilon (Champawat, Pithoragarh, Bageshwar) ke liye prastavit lambi pariyojana hai. Iski sthiti aur samay-seema badalti rahti hai; taaza jaankari Railway ya Uttarakhand sarkar se lein. Nainital zile se ye seedhe nahi guzarti.
  • Rishikesh-Karnaprayag rail project: Garhwal ki pariyojana hai; Nainital zile ke liye lagu nahi.

Nainital: Sadak, Rajmarg aur Jaam ki Jagah

Nainital ke Raaste, Jaam ki Jagah, Parking Niyam aur Green Cess

Nainital pahunchne ke mukhya raaste: Delhi ya Moradabad se Haldwani aur Kathgodam hote hue (Bhowali ke raaste), ya Ramnagar aur Kaladhungi hote hue. Kathgodam-Haldwani-Nainital marg ko sarvajanik sroton me aam taur par NH 109 / NH 87 ke naam se bhi bataya jata hai (NH ka numbering samay ke saath badla hai).

Nainital ke kareeb dikhne wale dooriyan (nainital.nic.in ke anusar lagbhag): Ramnagar 65 km, Delhi 310 km, Dehradun 300 km, Haridwar 245 km, Bareilly 140 km, Lucknow 400 km.

Jaam ke mukhya bindu:

  • Haldwani se Kathgodam, Ranibagh aur Bhowali ka raasta, khaaskar shanivar-ravivar aur lambe weekend par.
  • Nainital shehar ka pravesh (Tallital) aur Mall Road ke aas-paas.
  • Kainchi Dham ke aas-paas ka raasta, 15 June ke mele aur chhutti wale dino me.
  • Kaladhungi-Nainital marg par barish ke dauran bhuskhalan se rukawat.

Haal ke lambe weekend (October 2026) ki khabron me Haldwani se Nainital aur Bhimtal ke raaste par lambi qataren darj hui thi. Isliye weekend aur festival par subah jaldi nikalna ya do-teen din pehle ya baad me jaana behtar rehta hai.

Nainital shehar me gaadi aur parking ke niyam:

  • Parking: Sarkari site ke anusar garmi aur sharad ke mahino me Mall Road par parking pratibandhit hai. Gaadi Flats (Mallital), Tallital taxi stand aur Sukhatal ke KMVN parking jaise tay sthanon par khadi karein. Kuch hotel apne mehmano ko parking dete hain.
  • Parking ki kami: Nainital me parking ki jagah seemit hai, jabki peak season me rozana kai hazar gaadiyan aati hain. Pichhle saalon me prashasan ne bina parking wale tourist vehicle ko shehar ke bahar roka hai (jaise Bhowali, Kaladhungi aur Haldwani wale pravesh marg par). Ye niyam samay-samay par badalte hain, isliye safar se pehle zila prashasan ya nagar palika ki taaza advisory dekhein.
  • Mall Road par vehicle ka samay: Peak mahino me Mall Road par chalne wali gaadiyon ke liye alag samay tay hote hain. Taaza samay sthaniya traffic police se pooch lein.
  • Horn pratibandh: Sarvajanik khabron ke anusar 1 August 2026 se Nainital ki Mall Road ko horn-free zone ghoshit karne ki baat aayi hai, jisme horn bajane par jurmana lagne ka zikr hai. Iske sahi niyam aur jurmane ki rakam ke liye prashasan ki ghoshna dekhein.
  • Green cess: Uttarakhand sarkar ne bahar ke rajyon ki gaadiyon par “Green Cess” lagane ka prastav rakha tha (sarvajanik khabron me Rs 20 se Rs 80 ki charcha thi, jise FASTag se kaat liye jane ki baat thi, aur EV ya do-pahiya ko chhoot ki baat). Ye abhi lagu hai ya nahi, kitna hai, aur kin gaadiyon par, iski pushti sarkari ghoshna se hi karein.

Nainital me gaadi chalane ke tips: Pahadi modon par chadhne wali gaadi ko pehle raasta dein, barish me raat ko safar na karein, tez utaar par gear ka istemal karein, aur Mall Road me gaadi na le jaane ki koshish karein. Naye chalak apni capacity se bahar na jayein.

Zile ki mukhya sadkein PWD aur National Highway ke adheen aati hain.

Nainital shehar me: Shehar ke andar paidal chalna sabse aasan hai (Mall Road par aam gaadiyan seemit). Snow View, Naina Peak aur Hanuman Garhi jaise sthalon ke liye taxi ya paidal; Snow View ke liye ropeway bhi hai (Nainital ki 20 Sabse Zaroori Ghumne ki Jagah).

Hamesha sthaniya taxi stand ya sangh ki rate list maangein aur uski tasveer le lein. Kai logon ko “bina meter ke mol-bhav” me zyada paise dene padte hain; rate list pehle se hone par ye kam hota hai.

Chhote raaston ke liye: Mukteshwar, Ramgarh aur Pangot jaise ilakon ke liye Haldwani ya Nainital se shared taxi ya local jeep milti hai. Aakhri gaadi ka samay (shaam ko) pehle pooch lein, kyunki pahadi gaon me raat ko gaadi kam milti hai.

Nainital: Petrol Pump, EV Charging aur Mechanic

Petrol Pump, EV Charger aur Bharosemand Mechanic

Nainital, Bhowali, Bhimtal, Haldwani, Kaladhungi aur Ramnagar jaise bade kasbon me petrol pump milte hain. Pahadi gaon ya door ke ilakon me pump kam hote hain, isliye shehar chhodne se pehle tank bhar lein.

  • Petrol/diesel: Safar shuru karne se pehle (Haldwani, Kathgodam ya Nainital me) tank bhar lein, khaaskar Mukteshwar, Ramgarh, Pangot ya Betalghat jaisi jagahon ke liye. Google Maps par taaza list dekhein.
  • EV charging: Haldwani, Kathgodam aur Nainital ke aas-paas kuch EV charger lagne ki khabrein hain, par sankhya aur kaam karne ki sthiti badalti rahti hai. Safar se pehle EV charging app ya sarkari EV portal par check karein, aur pahad me chadhai ke liye battery ka hisaab (range) kam rakhkar chalein.
  • Mechanic aur tyre puncture: Sabse bharosemand sahulat Haldwani aur Kathgodam me milti hai; Nainital, Bhowali aur Bhimtal me bhi basic mechanic milte hain. Pahadi ghat sadak par safar se pehle brake, tyre, clutch aur coolant jaanch lein.
  • Pahadi sadak me khaas savdhani: Gaadi me spare tyre, jack, torch aur basic tool rakhein. Raat ko khaali sadak par akele rukne se bachein.
  • Tow aur emergency: Mushkil me 112 ya sthaniya road-side assistance ka number istemal karein (Nainital: Aapda Prabandhan aur Emergency Vyavastha).

Note: Pump aur charger ki list badalti rehti hai. Safar se ek din pehle Google Maps ya map app par taaza dekh lein.

Kaha rukna behtar hai?

  • Naini lake ke paas (Mallital ya Tallital): Paidal ghoomne ke liye aasan, par mehnga aur bheed wala.
  • Shehar ke thoda upar ya Bhowali ke raste: Shaant, par kabhi-kabhi chadhai.
  • Bhimtal, Sattal ya Naukuchiatal: Taal ke kinare shanti, Nainital ke 45 minute se 1 ghante ke andar.
  • Mukteshwar ya Pangot: Himalaya ke nazare aur shanti, par Nainital se door.
  • Ramnagar: Corbett ke liye.

Booking ke 7 niyam (bachne ke liye):

  • Official website ya sthaniya, pakke number par hi booking karein. Social media par aaye “offer” ya “bahut sasta kamra” par seedha paisa na bhejein.
  • Zyada advance token bhejne se pehle hotel ki reviews aur registration dekh lein.
  • Peak season me dam bahut badh jate hain; cancellation policy pehle padh lein.
  • Parking ki suvidha puchh lein, kyunki Nainital me parking ki kami hai (Nainital: Sadak, Rajmarg aur Jaam ki Jagah).
  • Lake-view ka matlab har hotel me alag hota hai; kamre ki asli tasveer maang lein.
  • Hotel ke andar alag se “shulk” ya “service charge” ke baare me pehle pooch lein.
  • Check-in aur check-out ka samay pakka kar lein; peak me kai hotel bahut sakht hote hain.

Wo badalte rehte hain aur kisi ek ko badhawa dena sahi nahi hoga. Apni jarurat (budget, location, parking) ke hisaab se khud compare karein.

Nainital: Registered Pahadi Homestays, Hostels & Ashrams

Homestay Scheme, Backpacker Hostels, Dharamshalas & Ashrams

Nainital ke pahadi gaon me kai parivaar sarkari homestay yojana ke tahat apne ghar me paryatakon ko rakhte hain. Ye ghar jaisa mahol, sthaniya khana aur gaon ka anubhav dete hain. Backpacker hostel, dharamshala aur ashram bhi kuch jagah milte hain.

  • Homestay: Uttarakhand sarkar gramin homestay ko badhawa deti hai (mukhya roop se Deen Dayal Upadhyay Griha Awas Homestay Yojana ke naam se). Sarkari site par registered homestay ki jaankari milti hai. Ghar me rukne se pehle uska registration aur reviews dekh lein.
  • Backpacker hostels: Nainital, Bhimtal aur Mukteshwar ke aas-paas kuch hostel dormitory-style kamre dete hain, jo akele ya budget yatriyon ke liye achhe hain. Sirf bharosemand review wale chunein.
  • Dharamshala aur ashram: Kuch mandir aur ashram parisar me yatriyon ke liye dharamshala hain. Kainchi Dham, Haidakhan ashram aur Gurudwara jaise sthalon me rukne ke niyam alag hote hain (kai jagah rukne ki anumati seemit hoti hai ya poorvanumati zaroori hoti hai). Seedhe jaane se pehle sambandhit samiti se pooch lein.
  • Gurudwara: Nainital ke Gurudwara me langar ka prabandh hota hai; rukne ke niyam alag-alag hote hain.

Homestay chunne ke 6 sawaal:

  • Kya ye sarkari registered hai?
  • Ghar tak sadak jaati hai ya paidal chalna padta hai?
  • Khana (shakahari/ maansahari) kaisa aur kab milta hai?
  • Network aur bijli-paani ki sthiti kaisi hai (Nainital: Mobile Network aur Internet: Asli Sthiti aur Nainital: Bijli, Solar aur Sardi mein Paani ki Sthiti)?
  • Raat ko jungle ke janwar ka khatra kitna hai?
  • Maalik se pehle phone par baat ho gayi hai ya nahi?

Akeli mahilaon aur parivaron ke liye: Booking se pehle ghar ke maalik se baat karein, location share karein, aur aise ghar chunein jinki achhi reviews hon aur jo sadak ke paas hon. Raat ko akele andhere raste par na chalein.

Nainital: Mobile Network aur Internet: Asli Sthiti

Mobile Network (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL) aur Bina Network ke Ilake

Nainital shehar, Haldwani, Bhimtal aur bade kasbon me mobile network aam taur par theek chalta hai. Ghati, ghane jungle (jaise Corbett ke core kshetra) aur kuch door ke pahadi gaon me signal kamzor ya nahi ho sakta hai.

  • Shehar aur kasbe: Jio, Airtel aur BSNL ke signal aam taur par milte hain; Vi ka network har jagah ek jaisa nahi hota. Peak season me bheed ke karan network dheema pad sakta hai.
  • Kamzor ya band signal ke ilake: Gehri ghatiyan, Corbett ke andar ke zone aur kuch door ke pahadi gaon me signal aa-jaa sakta hai.
  • Kaun sa network behtar? Ye jagah-jagah par alag hota hai. Safar se pehle sthaniya logon ya homestay maalik se pooch lein ki wahan kaun sa operator kaam karta hai. Aap Opensignal ya TRAI ke MyCall jaise coverage maps par bhi dekh sakte hain.
  • Do SIM ka faida: Pahad me do alag operator ke SIM rakhna madadgar hota hai.

Broadband aur Workation ki Taiyari

Haldwani aur Nainital shehar me fiber broadband (BSNL FTTH, JioFiber, AirFiber jaise vikalp) aam taur par uplabdh hain. Door ke gaon me broadband kam hai; wahan mobile hotspot par nirbhar rehna padta hai.

  • Workation ke liye: Booking se pehle homestay ya hotel se internet ki sahi speed aur backup (power cut me) ke baare me pooch lein. Speed ka screenshot ya test maangna theek rehta hai.
  • Backup: Do SIM, power bank aur mobile hotspot ka plan rakhein. Video call aur kaam ke samay ke liye behtar network wali jagah dhoondh lein.
  • Signal booster: Booster ka asar signal ke maujooda strength par nirbhar karta hai; bilkul signal na ho to booster kaam nahi karta.
  • Satellite internet: Bharat me satellite broadband ki sthiti aur uplabdhta badal rahi hai. Nainital zile me iske sahi rate aur seva ke liye taaza sarkari/ company ki jaankari dekhein.
  • Mausam ka asar: Barish aur barf me network aur bijli dono kamzor ho sakte hain.

Nainital: Bijli, Solar aur Sardi mein Paani ki Sthiti

Bijli, Backup aur Sardi mein Jame Pipe

Nainital zile me bijli ki aapoorti Uttarakhand Power Corporation Ltd (UPCL) karta hai. Shehar me aapoorti aam taur par theek hai, par monsoon ke bhuskhalan aur sardi me barf girne par door ke pahadi ilakon me bijli kat sakti hai.

  • Bijli kab jaati hai: Tez barish, aandhi, ped girna aur barf se line ko nuksan pahunchta hai. Door ke gaon me bahaal hone me zyada samay lag sakta hai.
  • Backup: Agar aapko pahad me rehkar kaam karna hai ya ghar banana hai, to inverter ke saath tubular battery lena behtar hai. Laptop aur sensitive saman ke liye pure sine-wave inverter surakshit rehta hai.
  • Solar: Chhat par solar ek achha vikalp ban sakta hai. Kendra sarkar ki PM Surya Ghar yojana ke tahat chhat ke solar par subsidy milti hai; iske niyam aur subsidy ki rakam sarkari portal par dekhein.
  • Sardi me paani ki samasya: Zyada thand me paani ke pipe jam sakte hain ya phat sakte hain.
  • Upay: Pipe ko moti kapde ya insulation se dhakein; raat ko nal se paani ki patli dhaar chalu rakhne ki salah di jati hai; bahar ki tanki par insulation lagayein; gharelu plumber se sardi ke pehle jaanch karwa lein.
  • Garmi me paani ki kami: Peak season me Nainital jaise shehar me paani ki aapoorti par bhaari dabaav padta hai. Hotel ya homestay me paani ke bachat ka dhyan rakhein.
  • Paani peene layak hai ya nahi: Naula ya dhara ka paani peene se pehle uski saaf-safai aur jaanch ki jaankari lein. Bimar hone se bachne ke liye bottle band paani ya filter ka paani behtar hai.

Nainital: Inner Line Permit (ILP), Van Anumati aur Suraksha Niyam

ILP ke Ilake, Drone ke Niyam aur Oonchai ki Bimari (AMS) se Bachav

Nainital zile me Inner Line Permit (ILP) ki zaroorat nahi hai, kyunki yahan koi antarrashtriya seema nahi lagti. ILP wale kshetra (jaise Niti, Mana, Nelang, Darma, Byans aur Chaudans ghati) Chamoli, Uttarkashi aur Pithoragarh zilon me hain.

BaatNainital zile ke liye
ILPLagu nahi
Seema chauki / border permitLagu nahi
Forest permitCorbett aur Reserve Forest me pravesh ya safari ke liye Van Vibhag ki anumati/ ticket zaroori

Drone ke niyam: Nainital me drone udaane se pehle Bharat ke drone niyam aur zila prashasan/ police ki anumati ke baare me jaanch lein. Corbett Tiger Reserve aur Raj Bhawan jaise sensitive sthalon ke paas drone ka istemal aam taur par vajrit ya anumati wala hota hai. Bina anumati drone udaane par kanooni karyawahi ho sakti hai.

Unchai ki bimari (AMS): Nainital aur uske aas-paas ki jagahein 2,600 meter tak hain, jahan AMS bahut kam logon ko hota hai. Kuch sanvedansheel logon ko tez chadhai ke baad sar dard, ji machlana ya thakan ho sakta hai. Aise me aaram karein, paani peeyein, aur thodi neeche ki unchai par chale jayein. Sar dard ya saans ki takleef gambhir ho to turant doctor ya najdeeki aspatal jayein. (Zyada unchai wale zilon, jaise Chamoli, Pithoragarh aur Uttarkashi, ke liye in zilon ke pages dekhein.)

Safar me surakshit rehne ke 10 niyam:

  • Mausam alert dekhein; barish me pahadi safar taal dein (Nainital: Aapda Prabandhan aur Emergency Vyavastha).
  • Raat ko pahadi sadak par gaadi na chalayein.
  • Jungle me akele ya raat ko na jayein; guldar aur bhaaloo ka khatra (Nainital: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh).
  • Nadi-nale ke kinare na rukein.
  • Taal me boating karte samay life jacket pehnein.
  • Zaroori dawaiyan, torch aur power bank saath rakhein.
  • Kisi ko apna plan bata kar jayein.
  • Aapatkaal me 112 (har tarah ki madad), 108 (ambulance), 1077 (zila aapda control room) dial karein (Nainital: Aapda Prabandhan aur Emergency Vyavastha).
  • Jaanwaron ko na chhedein aur na khilayein.
  • Sthaniya logon aur unki parampara ka samman karein.

Nainital: Kaise pahunchein, kahan rukein: Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Nainital kaise pahunchein?
Sabse nazdeeki railway station Kathgodam (~35 km) aur airport Pantnagar (~70 km) hai. Delhi se sadak ki doori lagbhag 300 km (7 se 8 ghante) hai. Kathgodam aur Haldwani se bus aur shared taxi milti hain (Nainital: Hawai aur Helicopter Seva).
Nainital ka nazdeeki railway station kaun sa hai?
Kathgodam (KGM), jo Nainital se lagbhag 35 km door hai. Nainital shehar me railway station nahi hai (Nainital: Railway Station aur Aane Wali Rail Pariyojanayein).
Nainital ka nazdeeki airport kaun sa hai?
Pantnagar Airport (PGH), Udham Singh Nagar me, jo Nainital se lagbhag 70 km door hai. Udaan ki sankhya kam hai, isliye pehle se jaanch lein (Nainital: Hawai aur Helicopter Seva). Pantnagar Airport, lagbhag 70 km door.
Kya Mall Road par gaadi chal sakti hai?
Mall Road par parking pratibandhit hai, aur peak mahino me gaadiyon ke chalne ke samay tay hote hain. Sarvajanik khabron ke anusar 1 August 2026 se Mall Road horn-free zone ghoshit karne ki baat aayi hai. Taaza niyam ke liye prashasan se pooch lein (Nainital: Sadak, Rajmarg aur Jaam ki Jagah).
Homestay chalane ke liye kya karna padta hai?
Paryatan Vibhag me registration, jagah ke kaagaz, aur shartein poori karni hoti hain. Sarkari homestay yojana (Deen Dayal Upadhyay Griha Awas Homestay) ka labh bhi liya ja sakta hai; shartein Paryatan Vibhag se pata karein (Nainital: Registered Pahadi Homestays, Hostels & Ashrams aur Nainital: Sarkari Yojanayein, ODOP aur Startup).
Delhi se Nainital kitni door hai?
Lagbhag 310 km, sadak se 7 se 8 ghante.
Nainital me parking kahan karein?
Flats, Tallital ya KMVN Sukhatal jaise tay sthanon par; Mall Road par parking pratibandhit.
Homestay ke liye subsidy kaise milti hai?
Deen Dayal Upadhyay Homestay Yojana ke tahat msy.uk.gov.in par aavedan; shartein badalti hain.
