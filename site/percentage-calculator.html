Percent ka hisaab har jagah kaam aata hai — discount, marks, GST, salary hike, interest. Ye calculator sabse aam sawaal ka jawab deta hai: X ka Y% kitna hota hai? Number aur percent daalein, jawab turant.

Neeche wo sawaal hain jo log sabse zyada khojte hain, seedhe jawab ke saath. Upar wale calculator me dusra mode chun kar aap apne khud ke number bhi daal sakte hain.

Sabhi me ganit ek hi hai — (hissa ÷ poora) × 100. Marksheet ho, target ho ya kisi kaam ka hissa — formula nahi badalta, bas number badalte hain.

Ek chhoti si baat jo bahut kaam aati hai: agar poora number 500 hai to har 5 marks lagbhag 1% ke barabar hote hain. Isse aap bina calculator ke bhi mota-moti andaaza laga sakte hain — 387 me se 375 (yaani 75%) ke oopar 12 marks bache, jo kareeb 2.4% hue. Kul milakar lagbhag 77.4%.

Aur agar poora number 100 nahi hai to seedha ginna mushkil hota hai. Aise me pehle 10% nikaal lein (500 ka 10% = 50), phir usi se aage badhein. Ye tareeka dukan, mandi aur exam teeno jagah kaam karta hai.

Percentage ka matlab

"Percent" ka matlab hai har sau me. Yaani 15% ka matlab "har 100 me 15". Isliye kisi bhi rakam ka percent nikalne ka formula bahut aasaan hai.

X ka Y% = (X × Y) ÷ 100
Misaal: 500 ka 15% = (500 × 15) ÷ 100 = 75

Teen aam sawaal aur unka formula

  • X ka Y% kitna? → (X × Y) ÷ 100
    500 ka 15% = 75
  • X, Y ka kitna percent hai? → (X ÷ Y) × 100
    75, 500 ka 15% hai
  • X se Y tak kitne % badha/ghata? → ((Y − X) ÷ X) × 100
    500 se 600 = 20% badha

Roz ke istemaal

Discount: ₹2,000 ki cheez par 30% chhoot = ₹600 kam, dena ₹1,400.

Marks: 450 me se 380 mile = (380 ÷ 450) × 100 = 84.4%.

Salary hike: ₹40,000 par 12% hike = ₹4,800 badha, nayi salary ₹44,800.

Tip: ₹850 ke bill par 10% = ₹85.

Ek galti jo sab karte hain

Agar koi cheez pehle 20% badhe aur phir 20% ghate, to wo wapas wahi nahi aati. Misaal: ₹100 par 20% badha to ₹120. Ab ₹120 par 20% ghata to ₹96 — ₹100 nahi. Wajah ye hai ki badhne ka percent ₹100 par laga tha aur ghatne ka ₹120 par. Share market aur discount ke mamlon me ye galti bahut mehngi padti hai.

Mun me percent nikalne ka trick

  • 10% — decimal ek jagah baayein khiskao (₹850 → ₹85)
  • 5% — 10% ka aadha
  • 20% — 10% ka dugna
  • 15% — 10% + uska aadha

Chaar asli misaal — rozana ke kaam se

1. Discount. ₹2,499 ki cheez par 40% off. Bachat = ₹999.60, dena hoga ₹1,499.40. Dukan me "40% + 10% extra" likha ho to wo 50% nahi hota — pehle 40% ghatega, phir bache hue par 10%. Asli discount lagbhag 46% banta hai.

2. Marks. 5 vishay, har ek 100 ka, kul 387 marks. Percentage = 387 ÷ 500 × 100 = 77.4%.

3. Salary hike. ₹35,000 se ₹42,000 hui. Badhotri = (42000 − 35000) ÷ 35000 × 100 = 20%.

4. GST nikalna. ₹5,900 me 18% GST shaamil hai. Base = 5900 × 100 ÷ 118 = ₹5,000, GST = ₹900. Seedha 18% ghatana galat jawab deta hai.

Log yahan galti kya karte hain

  1. Percentage jodna aur ghatana barabar maan lena. ₹100 par 10% badha to ₹110. Phir 10% ghata to ₹99 — ₹100 nahi. Ye sabse aam bhool hai.
  2. Do discount jod dena. "50% + 20% extra" 70% nahi hota. Pehle 50% (₹1,000 → ₹500), phir 20% (₹500 → ₹400). Asli discount 60% hua.
  3. Inclusive par seedha rate ghatana. Jab rakam me tax pehle se shaamil ho, to 100/(100+rate) wala formula lagta hai.
  4. Percentage point aur percent me farak na karna. Byaj 5% se 7% hua — ye "2 percentage point" ki badhotri hai, par asal me 40% ki badhotri hai.
  5. Ulta percentage. "20% badhne ke baad ₹6,000 hai, pehle kitna tha?" Jawab 6000 ÷ 1.2 = ₹5,000 hai, 6000 − 20% = ₹4,800 nahi.

Kaam ke shortcut

  • 10% nikalna: decimal ek jagah baayein khiskao. ₹850 ka 10% = ₹85
  • 5% nikalna: 10% ka aadha
  • 15% (tip): 10% + uska aadha
  • Ulta niyam: 8% of 50 = 50% of 8 = 4. Jo aasaan lage wo kar lein
  • Rule of 72: 72 ÷ byaj dar = paisa dugna hone ke saal

Bharat me kahan zyada kaam aata hai

  • GST: 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% — inclusive aur exclusive dono tarah ke hisaab
  • Loan: byaj dar, processing fee (loan rakam ka %), prepayment charge
  • Salary: hike %, PF katauti (basic ka 12%), HRA (basic ka 40/50%)
  • Nivesh: return %, expense ratio, exit load
  • Padhai: percentage, percentile aur CGPA — teeno alag cheezein hain

Percentage, percentile aur CGPA — teen alag cheezein

Ye teeno aksar mila diye jaate hain, jabki inka matlab bilkul alag hai:

  • Percentage: aapne kul me se kitna paaya. 500 me se 400 = 80%.
  • Percentile: aapse kitne pratishat log peeche hain. 90 percentile ka matlab 90% log aapse kam laaye — iska aapke marks se seedha koi rishta nahi.
  • CGPA: grade point ka aausat. Ise percentage me badalne ka formula har board aur university ka apna hota hai — ek hi formula sab jagah nahi chalta.

Competitive exam me aksar percentile hi maayne rakhta hai, percentage nahi. Isliye "mere 85% aaye" aur "main 85 percentile par hoon" — ye do bilkul alag baatein hain.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Percentage ka formula kya hai?

X ka Y% = (X × Y) ÷ 100. Misaal: 500 ka 15% = 75.

X, Y ka kitna percent hai — kaise nikaalein?

(X ÷ Y) × 100. Jaise 75, 500 ka 15% hai.

20% badhkar 20% ghatne se wahi rakam aati hai?

Nahi. ₹100 → 20% badha = ₹120 → 20% ghata = ₹96. Kyunki dono percent alag rakam par lage.

Discount ka hisaab kaise lagayein?

Daam ka discount percent nikaalein aur ghata dein. ₹2,000 par 30% = ₹600 kam, dena ₹1,400.

Marks ka percentage kaise nikaalein?

(Mile marks ÷ Kul marks) × 100. Jaise 450 me 380 = 84.4%.

2499 ka 40 percent kitna hoga?

₹999.60. Hisaab: 2499 × 40 ÷ 100. Mann me karna ho to pehle 10% nikaaliye (249.9), phir 4 se guna kar dijiye.

35500 ka 10 percent kitna hoga?

₹3,550. Kisi bhi number ka 10% nikaalne ke liye dashamlav ek jagah baayein khiska dijiye.

600 me se 380 ka percentage kitna hoga?

63.33%. Hisaab: 380 ÷ 600 × 100.
