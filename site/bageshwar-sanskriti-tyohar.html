Bageshwar: Aabadi, Samuday aur Anusuchit Janjati

Census 2011 ke anusar Bageshwar zile ki aabadi 2,59,898 thi, jisme 1,24,326 purush aur 1,35,572 mahilayein hain. Sex ratio lagbhag 1,090 mahilayein per 1,000 purush hai, jo Uttarakhand ke average (963) se kaafi zyada hai.

Mool Gaon (Mool Niwas / Thaat) ki Pehchan aur Vivah ki Paramparayein

Kumaon me "mool niwas" ya thaat wo purkhon ka gaon hai jisse parivaar ki pehchan judi hoti hai. Kul-devta, gotra aur gaon ka naam shaadi, puja aur khatauni me kaam aata hai.

  • Gotra: Brahmin aur Rajput parivaron me gotra ka mahatva rehta hai; shaadi me aam taur par ek hi gotra me rishta nahi hota.
  • Thaat / Mool niwas: Gaon ka purana ghar jahan kul-devta ka thaan hota hai.
  • Mait aur Sauras: Mait (mayka) aur Sauras (sasural) ka rishta lok-geeton ka sabse bada vishay hai.
  • Ghati se ghati shaadi: Pehle paidal raston ki wajah se shaadiyan aas-paas ki ghatiyon me hi hoti thi. Ab sadak aur mobile ki wajah se door ke rishte bhi aam hain.

Kumaoni aur Hindi me kya antar hai?
Kumaoni Indo-Aryan bhasha-parivar ke "Central Pahari" samuha me aati hai, aur Garhwali tatha Nepali ke kaafi kareeb maani jaati hai. Hindi ke bahut se shabd milte hain, par vyakaran, uchcharan aur kai shabd alag hain.

Census me bhasha: 2011 ke bhasha data me Bageshwar me Kumaoni mukhya bhasha hai; Garhwali bolne wale bahut kam (lagbhag 0.7%).

Bageshwar: Anushthan ki Lok-Kala: Aipan, Jyunti aur Lakdi ka Kaam

Aipan Kumaon ki sabse prasiddh paramparik kala hai. Isme geru (laal mitti) ke aadhar par chawal ke geele paste (biswar) se ungliyon se aakritiyan banti hain. Yeh shubh avsaron aur pooja ke samay ghar ke aangan aur chauki par banayi jaati hai.

Aipan ke Namoone, Geru-Biswar aur Jyunti/Matrika Chitra

Aipan kaise banta hai?
1. Geru (laal mitti) ko paani me ghol kar zameen ya chauki par lep lagate hain. Yeh aadhar rang banta hai. 2. Chawal ko bhigo kar pees lete hain aur uska patla geela paste (biswar) banate hain. 3. Teen ungliyon se is safed paste ki lakeeren aur bindu bana kar aakritiyan banate hain. 4. Pooja ke baad ise sukhne dete hain; yeh sthayi nahi hota.

Jyunti / Jyuti Matrika: Shaadi, Janeu, Janmashtami jaise avsaron par dewal par bani Matrika deviyon ki chitrakari, jisme laal aur peele rang ka upyog hota hai. Jyunti ke gahre pooja-vidhi ki jaankari Kumaoni buzurgon se hi milti hai (sthaniya pushti baaki).

Aipan kala ko kaise bachayein?
Aaj kai mahila samuh aur kalakar Aipan ko kapde, chaukiyon aur gift items par utaar kar rozgaar bana rahe hain.

Lakdi ki naksheedar kala: Kholi (mukhya dwar) aur chaukhat par ki gayi lakdi ki nakkashi Kumaoni ghar ki pehchan hai. Lakdi ke karigar aur unki dukanon ke baare me Bageshwar: Hathkargha, GI-Tag Utpad aur Sthaniya Hastshilp me hai.

Bageshwar: Lok-Sangeet, Lok-Gaathayein aur Gayan Parampara

Kumaon ke lok-sangeet me Jagar (devta ka aahvan), Jhora, Chanchari, Nyoli, Khuded geet, Malushahi, Bair-Bhagnaul aur Kumaoni Holi shamil hain. Bageshwar ke Uttarayani Mela me Jhora aur Chanchari khaas taur par gaye jaate hain.

Bageshwar: Paramparik Lok Nritya

Chholiya (talwar-dhaal ka naach), Jhora aur Chanchari Kumaon ke mukhya lok-nritya hain. Chholiya ke pradarshan Baijnath (Bageshwar) me bhi hote hain aur isme dhol, damau aur masakbeen (bagpipe) ke saath purush talwar ghumate hain.

Bageshwar: Paramparik Vadya Yantra aur Bajgi/Auji Samuday

Dhol aur damau Kumaon ke sabse zaroori vadya hain, jinhe bajane wale Bajgi/Auji (Das) samuday ke log hote hain. Hurka (hudka), ransingha, turi, nagara aur masakbeen jaise vadya bhi paramparik utsavon me bajaye jaate hain.

Dhol Sagar: Dhol-damau ki taalon ka ek paramparik granth/gyan, jise Dhol Sagar kaha jata hai. Isme alag-alag avsaron ke liye alag taal hoti hain. Iski taalon ki ginti 84 batayi jaati hai, aur yeh gyan zyadatar guru-shishya parampara se mukh-zubani chalta aaya hai.

Bajgi/Auji samuday ka mahatva: Mandir, mele aur shaadi me dhol-damau bajane wale samuday ke bina lok-parampara adhoori maani jaati hai.

Kumaoni shaadi ke kuch aam charan

Neeche likhe charan gaon, samuday aur parivaar ke hisaab se badal jaate hain.

  • Rishta aur Ganesh pooja: Rishta tay hone ke baad Ganesh pooja aur shubh muhurat nikala jata hai.
  • Suwal Pathai: Vivah se pehle ki ek rasm, jisme dulhan ya dulhe ke ghar se pooja-saman ya suwal bheja jaata hai.
  • Aipan aur Pichora: Aangan aur chauki par Aipan banta hai; dulhan Rangwali Pichora aur nath pehenti hai.
  • Baraat: Dhol-damau aur kai jagah masakbeen ke saath baraat nikalti hai; kuch jagah Chholiya nritya bhi hota hai.
  • Dhuli Arghya: Baraat ke pahunchne par shaam ke samay dulhe ke charan dhone ki rasm hoti hai.
  • Kanyadan aur Phere: Vivah ke mukhya vaidik charan.
  • Vidaai: Mait se vidaai ke samay "Khuded" aur vidaai-geet ka bhaav bahut gahra hota hai (Bageshwar: Bidaai aur Karun Geet).

Aaj ka badlav: Aaj bahut se parivaar shaadi me sadak-kinare resort ya hall ka upyog karte hain, par Aipan, pichora aur dhol-damau ki parampara kayam hai.

Shaadi kharch aur niyam: Dahej par kanoon ke tahat pratibandh hai. Yeh page kisi aise prathaon ko badhava nahi deta.

Uttarayani Mela (Bageshwar)

Uttarayani Mela har saal Makar Sankranti (lagbhag 14 January) par Saryu aur Gomti ke sangam par lagta hai aur aam taur par ek hafte tak chalta hai. Isme snan, Bagnath mandir ke darshan, lok-sangeet, dance aur bazaar hote hain.

Aitihasik mahatva1921 ka Coolie-Begar andolan

1921 ka Coolie-Begar andolan: Is mele ko 1921 me Coolie-Begar pratha ke virodh ki ek bade andolan ke roop me yaad kiya jata hai, jisme Kumaon ke logon ne begar (bina majduri kaam) ke khilaaf sangharsh kiya aur Saryu ke sangam par pratigya li. Isme Badri Datt Pandey ka naam prasiddh hai. Poori kahani Bageshwar: Azaadi ki Ladai, Kuli Begar aur Rajya Andolan me hai.

Gandhi ji ka aagman: Kuch sources ke anusar Mahatma Gandhi ne 1929 ki Kumaon yatra me is mele ka daura kiya tha.

Kab aana chahiye? Makar Sankranti ke aas-paas ke din sabse zyada bheed hoti hai. Rukne ke hotel pehle se book karein. Jaankari ke liye Bageshwar: Rukne ki Jagah: GMVN/KMVN, Hotel aur Resort dekhein.

Nanda Devi Raj Jat (12 saal me ek baar)

Nanda Devi Raj Jat Uttarakhand ki sabse lambi paidal yatra hai (lagbhag 280 km), jo 12 saal me ek baar hoti hai. Isme Garhwal aur Kumaon dono ke bhakt shamil hote hain.

Taza sthiti (7 November 2026): January 2026 me Raj Jat samiti ne yatra ko 2027 tak taalne ki baat kahi thi. Par September 2026 ki ek khabar me Kurud se yatra shuru hone ka zikr hai, aur donon ke baare me jaankari alag-alag hai. Kaun si tithi sahi hai, yeh Raj Jat samiti ya Chamoli zila prashasan ke sarkari ghoshna se hi confirm karein.

Kumaon ke kuch mandir jaise Almora, Nainital aur Bageshwar ke Nanda Devi parampara se jude hone ki baat kai sources me aati hai, par Bageshwar ki exact bhoomika ke liye sarkari suchna dekhein.

Chausingha khadu (chaar-sing wala bhed): Raj Jat ki paramparik pehchan yeh chaar-sing wala mendha hai jo yatra ka netritva karta hai, aisa mana jata hai.
