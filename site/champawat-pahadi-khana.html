Champawat Ka Khana Samajhne Ke Liye Ek Baat: Champawat ka khana Kumaoni rasoi ka hissa hai. Pahadi hisse me Bhatt, Gahat, Mandua aur Jhangora jaise anaj-dalhan chalte hain, jabki Lohaghat aur seema ke kshetron me Nepali asar (momo, thukpa jaise vyanjan) bhi dikhta hai. Yatra sources ke anusar Champawat me Bhatt ki Churkani, Bhang ki Chutney, Aloo ke Gutke, Gahat ki Dal, Mandua ki Roti, Gulgule, Jhangora ki Kheer, Singori aur Bal Mithai sabse zyada aajmaye jate hain.

Champawat: Zile Ke Pramukh Pahadi Vyanjan (Main Course)

Kafuli, Phaanu, Chainsoo, Thechwani, Bhatt Ki Churkani, Dubuk, Ras-Bhaat, Gahat Ki Dal aur Jholi

Seedha jawab: Champawat ke gharon me sabse zyada banne wale pahadi vyanjan Bhatt ki Churkani, Gahat ki Dal, Aloo ke Gutke, Kafuli (Kapa), Phaanu aur Dubuk hain. Inhe aam taur par loha ki kadhai me sarson ke tel ya ghee ke tadke ke saath dheemi aanch par pakaya jata hai.

Pahadi khane ki pehchan teen cheezo se hoti hai: loha ki kadhai, dheemi aanch aur jakhiya, jambu ya timur jaisa pahadi tadka. Is wajah se swad bahut alag aata hai.

Kyun pahadi dal alag lagti hai: Bhatt aur Gahat jaisi dal jyada der me galti hain aur dheemi aanch par pakne se unka asli swad aata hai. Loha ki kadhai ka paani aur sarson ka tel unhe ek alag gehrai dete hain. Isliye jab koi pahadi dal-bhat khata hai to kehta hai ki “bilkul ghar jaisa swad”.

Recipe 1: Bhatt Ki Churkani (Kumaoni Style)

Kitne log ke liye: 4 | Samay: lagbhag 1 ghanta (bhigone ka samay alag)

Samagri:

  • Bhatt (kala soybean): 1 cup
  • Sarson ka tel: 3 badi chammach
  • Jakhiya ya jeera: 1 chhoti chammach
  • Haldi: 1/2 chhoti chammach
  • Laal mirch powder: 1 chhoti chammach
  • Dhaniya powder: 1 chhoti chammach
  • Namak: swadanusar
  • Chawal ka aata (2 chammach, 1/2 cup paani me ghol kar): gaadha karne ke liye (optional)
  • Hara dhaniya

Vidhi:

  • Bhatt ko 6–8 ghante ya raat bhar bhigo dein.
  • Loha ki kadhai me bhatt ko halki aanch par bhoon lein jab tak halki khushbu na aaye.
  • Bhatt ko mote-mote peesh lein (bilkul paste na karein).
  • Kadhai me sarson ka tel garam karein, jakhiya ya jeera daalein.
  • Haldi, dhaniya aur laal mirch daalein aur 20 second chalayein.
  • Peesa hua bhatt aur 3–4 cup paani daalein.
  • Dhakkar 40–45 minute dheemi aanch par pakne dein. Beech-beech me chalate rahein.
  • Gaadhapan kam lage to chawal ke aate ka ghol daal kar 5 minute aur pakayein.
  • Hara dhaniya daal kar garam chawal ya mandua roti ke saath parosein.

Tip: Churkani ko iski gaadhi, thodi dani-daar banawat hi alag banati hai. Pressure cooker me bhi ban jati hai, par loha ki kadhai me swad behtar maana jata hai.

Recipe 2: Gahat Ki Dal

Samagri: Gahat 1 cup, namak, haldi 1/2 chhoti chammach, ghee ya sarson tel 2 chammach, jakhiya/jeera 1 chhoti chammach, lehsun 4 kali, sookhi laal mirch 2, hing 1 chutki.

Vidhi:

  • Gahat ko raat bhar bhigo dein.
  • Bhige gahat ko haldi aur namak ke saath pressure cooker me 6–7 seeti tak pakayein.
  • Dal ko halka ghot lein. Paani gaadha ya patla apni pasand se rakhein.
  • Alag kadhai me ghee garam karein, jakhiya, hing, kata lehsun aur sookhi laal mirch ka tadka lagayein.
  • Tadka dal par daal kar dhak dein.
  • Chawal ya Mandua roti ke saath parosein.

Sehat Ki Baat: Gahat ki dal sardi me khoob khayi jati hai aur isme protein achha hota hai. Isko kisi bimari (jaise pathri) ke ilaaj ki tarah na maane. Medical sthiti me doctor ki salah lein.

Recipe 3: Aloo Ke Gutke (Pahadi Tadka)

Samagri: Aaloo 4–5 (ubaal kar ya kachche tukde), sarson tel 2 chammach, jakhiya ya jambu 1 chhoti chammach, sookhi laal mirch 2, haldi 1/2 chhoti chammach, dhaniya beej kuchle hue 1 chhoti chammach, namak, hara dhaniya.

Vidhi:

  • Aaloo ko ubaal kar chhote tukdo me kaat lein (ya kachche tukde kar ke dheemi aanch par bhoone).
  • Kadhai me tel garam karein.
  • Jakhiya ya jambu, sookhi mirch aur dhaniya beej daalein.
  • Haldi aur namak daalein aur aaloo milakar 5–7 minute bhoone, jab tak halke kurkure na ho jaye.
  • Hara dhaniya daal kar parosein. Poori ya roti ke saath bahut achha lagta hai.

Recipe 4: Kafuli / Kapa (Palak-Methi Ki Gaadhi Sabzi)

Samagri: Palak 500 gram, methi 1 mutthi, chawal 2 chammach (1 ghanta bhigoye), loha ki kadhai, ghee ya tel 2 chammach, jakhiya 1 chhoti chammach, hara lehsun, hing, namak, sookhi mirch.

Vidhi:

  • Palak aur methi ko dho kar halka ubaal lein.
  • Bhige chawal ko peesh kar paste bana lein.
  • Ubale hue palak-methi ko mote peesh lein.
  • Kadhai me ghee garam karein, jakhiya, hing aur sookhi mirch daalein.
  • Palak-methi ka paste daalein aur 5 minute pakayein.
  • Chawal ka paste daal kar achhe se mila lein aur 15–20 minute dheemi aanch par gaadha hone tak pakayein.
  • Namak daal kar garam bhaat ke saath parosein.

Recipe 5: Bhang Ki Chutney (Sirf Beej Wali, Pahadi Swad)

Samagri: Bhang ke beej (hemp seeds) 1/2 cup, hari mirch 2, lehsun 3 kali, namak, nimbu ka ras ya galgal ka ras 1 chammach, hara dhaniya.

Vidhi:

  • Beej ko dheemi aanch par halke bhoon lein jab tak khushbu na aaye.
  • Silbatte ya mixer me beej, hari mirch, lehsun, namak aur dhaniya ko peesh lein.
  • Nimbu ya galgal ka ras milakar chutney ki gaadhapan swad anusaar rakhein.
  • Roti, bhaat ya pahadi thali ke saath parosein.

Zaroori Kanooni Chetavni: Bhang ki chutney me sirf beej (seeds) ka upyog hota hai, jo khane ke roop me chalan me hain. Bhang ke patte, phool ya kisi bhi tarah ka nashile padarth ka khane ya pine me upyog kanoon ke dayre me aata hai aur ye bilkul nahi hona chahiye.

Champawat: Paramparik Roti, Parathe aur Chawal Ke Vyanjan

Mandua Ki Roti, Gahat Ke Parathe, Lesu Roti, Jhangore Ka Bhaat

Seedha jawab: Pahad ki pramukh rotiyan Mandua (Ragi) ki roti aur Gahat ke bhare parathe hain. Mandua ki roti ko garam ghee, gud ya sabzi ke saath khaya jata hai. Jhangora ka bhaat chawal ki jagah khaya jata hai.

Recipe 6: Mandua Ki Roti

Samagri: Mandua ka aata 2 cup, garam paani (lagbhag 1 cup), namak 1/4 chhoti chammach, ghee parosne ke liye.

Vidhi:

  • Aate me namak milayein aur garam paani thoda-thoda daal kar naram aata gundhein. Mandua ka aata chipchipa hota hai, isliye paani dhyan se daalein.
  • Aate ki lui lekar hathon ya belan se mote-mote gol roti ki tarah belein. Belne ke liye halka sookha aata istemal karein.
  • Garam tawe par dono taraf se sekein.
  • Chulhe ki aanch par seedhe bhi sekh sakte hain.
  • Garam roti par ghee laga kar parosein.

Recipe 7: Jhangora Ki Kheer

Samagri: Jhangora 1/2 cup, doodh 1 litre, cheeni ya gud 1/2 cup, elaichi 2, kaju-badam-kishmish.

Vidhi:

  • Jhangora ko dho kar 30 minute bhigo dein.
  • Doodh ko ubaal kar dheemi aanch par rakhein.
  • Bheega jhangora daalein aur lagatar chalate hue 30–35 minute pakayein.
  • Jab gaadha ho jaye to cheeni ya gud aur elaichi daalein.
  • Mewa daal kar garam ya thanda parosein.

Gud Daalne Ka Tip: Gud ko doodh me tab daalein jab kheer thodi thandi ho jaye, warna doodh phat sakta hai.

Bhangjeera, Bhang, Darim, Timur Chutney aur Pahadi Raita

Seedha jawab: Pahadi thali ki pehchan uski chutney aur raite se hoti hai. Bhangjeera (Perilla beej) aur Bhang ke beej ki chutney, Darim (jangli anar) ki chutney, Timur ki chutney aur sarson-kheere ka raita sabse prasiddh hain.

Recipe 8: Pahadi Kheere Ka Raita (Rai Wala)

Yeh Kumaon ka sabse prasiddh raita hai. Isme peesi hui rai ki teekhi taseer hoti hai jo naak tak chadhti hai.

Samagri: Pahadi (peela mota) kheera 1, dahi 2 cup, rai ke daane 1 chhoti chammach, haldi 1/4 chhoti chammach, namak, hari mirch 1, bhuna jeera.

Vidhi:

  • Kheere ko chheel kar kaddu-kas kar lein aur nichod kar paani nikal lein.
  • Rai ke daane ko halka peesh kar ya kuchal kar paste bana lein.
  • Dahi ko phent lein aur usme kasa hua kheera daalein.
  • Rai ka paste, haldi, namak, hari mirch aur jeera milayein.
  • Thanda parosein. Rai tez lag sakti hai, isliye pehle thodi dalein.

Pisyun Loon: Silbatte Par Peesa Gaya Swadisht Namak

Seedha jawab: Pisyun Loon Kumaon ka khaas masaledar namak hai jise silbatte par lehsun ke patte, hari mirch, dhaniya, jeera aur timur ke saath peesa jata hai. Ise nimbu (galgal) ke saath roti, kheere ya phal par laga kar khaya jata hai.

Pisyun Loon ki vidhi (aam):

  • Silbatte par sendha ya mota namak daalein.
  • Hare lehsun ke patte, hari mirch, dhaniya, jeera aur timur dalein.
  • Sabko lagatar peesate rahein jab tak ek gaadha, hara-nama paste na ban jaye.
  • Galgal ka ras ya nimbu milakar kheere, mooli, phal ya roti ke saath khayein.

Champawat: Jungli Khane Wali Chizein aur Mausmi Saag

Bicchu Ghas (Sisunak), Linguda, Kafal, Hisalu, Kilmora aur Buransh

Seedha jawab: Pahad me mausam ke saath jungli saag aur phal milte hain: barsat me Bicchu Ghas (Nettle) aur Linguda, garmi me Kafal, Hisalu aur Kilmora, aur March-April me Buransh ke phool. Inhe saavdhani se aur sahi jaankari ke saath hi khana chahiye.

Linguda (Fiddlehead fern)BarsatAchhe se pakane ke baad sabzi/achar

Zaroori Surakshit Salah (Foraging Safety): Jungle se koi bhi saag, phal ya phool tabhi khayein jab aap use pakki tarah pehchante ho. Kai jungli paudhe zehrile hote hain aur kuch dikhne me ek jaise lagte hain. Buransh ke phoolon me bhi sirf ek khaas prajati (Rhododendron arboreum) ka sharbat khaya jata hai, sabhi buransh nahi. Agar sthaniya bujurg se pushti na ho to na khayein.

Bicchu Ghas (Nettle) ka saag kaise banta hai: Bicchu ghas me chhote kaante hote hain jo chhune par jalan paida karte hain. Isko dastane pehen kar ya chimte se todte hain. Ubaalne ya bhoonne ke baad kaante ka asar khatam ho jata hai aur isse sahi tarah pakaya gaya saag banta hai. Sardi me yeh pahadi gharon ka poshak saag hai.

Champawat: Mithai, Tyohaar Ke Pakwan aur Prasad

Arsa, Singori, Bal Mithai, Jhangore Ki Kheer, Rote, Puwa, Singal aur Gulgule

Seedha jawab: Kumaon ki prasiddh mithaiyon me Singori (khoye ko maalu ke patte me lapet kar), Bal Mithai (bhoone khoye par cheeni ki parat), Singal, Puwa aur Gulgule shamil hain. Rote (mote gud wale aate ki rotiyan) devta ko chadhane ka khaas prasad hai.

Dhyan Dein: Bal Mithai aur Singori ki mool pehchan Almora zile se judi maani jati hai, par Champawat ke bazaaron me bhi milti hain.

Recipe 9: Gulgule (Gud Wale)

Samagri: Gehun ka aata 1 cup, gud 1/2 cup (paani me ghula hua), saunf 1 chhoti chammach, soda ya baking powder ek chutki, tel talne ke liye.

Vidhi:

  • Gud ko thode garam paani me ghol kar chhaan lein aur thanda kar lein.
  • Aate me saunf aur soda milayein.
  • Gud ka paani daal kar gaadha ghol banayein (na bahut patla, na bahut gaadha).
  • Garam tel me chammach se chhote-chhote gole daal kar sunhera hone tak talein.
  • Garam ya thanda parosein.

Champawat: Paramparik Peya aur Khameer Wale Pey

Buransh Sharbat, Malta Juice, Gud-Adrak Ki Chai aur Sthaniya Parampara

Seedha jawab: Pahad ke prasiddh pey me Buransh ka sharbat, Malta aur Narangi ka juice aur sardi ki gud-adrak wali chai shamil hain. Seema ke ooncha bhotiya ilakon me paramparik anaj se bane madira-jaise sthaniya pey bhi dharmik aur samajik awsaron par chalan me rahe hain, par yeh Champawat ki mukhya pehchan nahi hai.

Gud-Adrak ki chaiSardi me pahadi gharon ki sabse pasandida chai

Madira (Alcohol) Wale Paramparik Pey Par Spasht Note: Kuch pahadi samudayo me anaj se bane paramparik pey (jaise Chhyang, Jaan) pehle se rahe hain. Sharab ka utpadan aur bikri Uttarakhand ke Aabkari (excise) niyamo ke antargat hai. Yeh sirf sanskritik jaankari ke liye hai.

Champawat: Pahadi Rasoi Ki Sanrachna aur Bartan

Loha Ki Kadhai, Silbatta, Kansi Thali, Bhaddu aur Mitti Ka Chulha

Seedha jawab: Pahadi rasoi me loha ki kadhai, silbatta, kansi (kaanse) ki thali, tambe ka bhaddu aur dhuyen se chalne wala mitti ka chulha mukhya hain. Chulhe ke upar “Dhumala” naam ka lakdi ka tand hota hai jisme beej, lehsun aur lakdi sukhaye jate hain.

Dhuye ka swad: Chulhe ke dhuye me sukhaye gaye masale, lehsun aur dal ka swad alag hota hai. Aaj kal gas aur induction ke chalan se yeh parampara kam ho rahi hai, par kai gharon me aaj bhi jinda hai.

Champawat Yatra Me Kya Khayein: Practical Guide

Seedha jawab: Champawat ke ek din ke khaane ka aasan plan: subah chai ke saath gulgule ya poori-aaloo, dopahar me Mandua roti-Gahat dal ya Bhatt ki Churkani, sham ko chai ke saath Singori ya Bal Mithai, raat me dal-bhaat aur raita.

Khane ke liye jagah chunte samay:

  • Chhoti dukano aur gharelu thaali dene wale dhabon ko aazmayein, wahan pahadi swad zyada milta hai.
  • Pahadi vyanjan ke liye pehle poochh lein ki aaj kya bana hai. Kai jagah Churkani ya Gahat dal sirf order par banti hai.
  • Reetha Sahib gurudwara me langar ki parampara hai. Wahan ka prasad sab ke liye hota hai.
  • Taaza review Google Maps par dekhein.

Pahadi khane ke baare me aam sawal:

Kya sab pahadi khana teekha hota hai?
Nahi. Zyadatar halke masale wale hote hain.
Kya vegetarian vikalp milte hain?
Haan, pahadi khana zyadatar shakahari hota hai.
Kya bachon ke liye surakshit hai?
Bachon ko mirch kam dein. Foraged (jangli) cheezein bachon ko na dein.

Champawat: Pahadi Khane Se Jude Sabse Zyada Puchhe Jane Wale Sawal

Champawat me kaun se khaane zaroor try karne chahiye?
Bhatt ki Churkani, Aloo ke Gutke, Gahat ki Dal, Mandua ki Roti, Bhang ki Chutney (beej wali), Jhangora ki Kheer, Singori aur Bal Mithai.
Bhatt ki Churkani kis cheez se banti hai?
Kale soybean (Bhatt) se, jise bhoon kar peesa jata hai aur loha ki kadhai me sarson ke tel me pakaya jata hai.
Mandua kya hota hai?
Ragi, yaani finger millet. Pahad ka paramparik mota anaj.
Jhangora kya hai?
Barnyard millet. Iska bhaat aur kheer banti hai.
Gahat ki dal kya hoti hai?
Kulath (Horse gram) ki dal, jo sardi me khoob khayi jati hai.
Aloo ke Gutke kya hai?
Pahadi masalon (jakhiya/jambu) me bhoone hue aaloo ke tukde.
Kafuli kya hai?
Palak aur methi ki gaadhi pahadi sabzi, jisme chawal ka lep daala jata hai.
Pisyun Loon kya hai?
Silbatte par lehsun, mirch, dhaniya aur timur ke saath peesa gaya masaledar namak.
Singori kya hai?
Khoye ki mithai jo maalu ke patte me lapeti jati hai.
Bal Mithai kahan ki prasiddh hai?
Mool pehchan Almora ki hai, par Kumaon ke kai bazaaron me milti hai, Champawat me bhi.
Rote kya hota hai?
Gud aur aate ki mote rotiyan, jo devta ko chadhane ka khaas prasad hain.
Buransh ka sharbat kab banta hai?
March-April me, jab Buransh ke phool khilte hain.
Kya jungli saag aur phal kisi bhi paudhe se tod kar khaye ja sakte hain?
Nahi. Sirf wahi khayein jinhe aap pakki tarah pehchante hain. Kai paudhe zehrile hote hain.
Pahadi khana teekha hota hai kya?
Zyadatar halke masale wala hota hai. Kuch chutney aur raita (rai wala) tez ho sakte hain.
