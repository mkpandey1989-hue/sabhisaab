Nainital ki 20 Sabse Zaroori Ghumne ki Jagah

Har Jagah ki Unchai, Mukhyalay se Doori aur Kitna Samay

Nainital zile ke sabse mashhoor 20 sthal: Naini Tal jheel, Naina Devi Mandir, Mall Road, Snow View, Naina Peak, Tiffin Top, Zoo, Hanuman Garhi-ARIES, Eco Cave Gardens, Land’s End, Bhimtal, Sattal, Naukuchiatal, Khurpatal, Kainchi Dham, Ghorakhal, Mukteshwar, Ramgarh, Pangot-Kilbury aur Jim Corbett.

Doori Nainital shahar (Mallital) se lagbhag sadak ki doori hai; sthaniya sadak ke hisaab se thodi alag ho sakti hai.

Kya khaas hai in me se kuch ke baare me:

  • Snow View Point aur Ropeway: Sarkari zile ki site ke anusar ropeway Snow View Point (2,270 m) ko Nainital ke Flats ke paas se jodta hai. Ropeway ka timing, band hone ka din aur ticket mausam aur sanchalan ke hisaab se badalte hain; kuch sources me alag-alag samay aur weekly off dikhta hai, isliye jaane se pehle sthaniya counter se pakka karein.
  • Nainital Zoo (G.B. Pant High Altitude Zoo): Sher ka Danda pahadi par, 1995 me janta ke liye khula. Ye Bharat ke gine-chune high-altitude zoo me se ek hai. Weekly band hone ka din alag sroton me alag likha hai (aam taur par Monday), isliye jaane se pehle pakka karein.
  • Naina Peak: Isse Himalaya aur Nainital ke aas-paas ka nazara milta hai. Paidal ya ghode se jaya ja sakta hai.
  • ARIES Manora Peak: Observatory ka Science Centre aam janta ke liye khula hai. Iske niyam Nainital: Taare Dekhna, Astro-Tourism aur Photography ki Jagah me diye gaye hain.
  • Corbett: Zones, mausam aur booking ke niyam Nainital: Hawai Khel, Paragliding, Mountain Biking aur Camping aur Nainital: National Park, Wildlife Sanctuary aur Sanrakshit Kshetra me hain.

Ek samajhdar tip: Nainital shehar me ek din me sab kuch dekhne ki koshish na karein. 2 se 3 din shehar ke liye aur baaki 2 se 3 din aas-paas (Bhimtal-Sattal-Naukuchiatal, Mukteshwar, Corbett) ke liye rakhein (dekhein Nainital: Taiyar Yatra Yojana).

Nainital: Hill Station aur Weekend par Ghumne ki Jagah

Bheed Wale Kasbe aur Himalaya Darshan Wale Shaant Gaon

Nainital zile me Nainital, Bhimtal, Mukteshwar aur Ramgarh popular hill station hain, jabki Pangot, Kilbury aur Dhari-Mukteshwar ke aas-paas ke chhote gaon kam bheed wale shaant ridge-top thikane hain, jahan se Himalaya ke saaf nazare milte hain.

Kab aur kitne din?

  • Weekend (2 raat): Nainital + Bhimtal ya Nainital + Mukteshwar (jaldi niklein).
  • Shaanti chahiye to: Mukteshwar ya Pangot me rukein aur Nainital ek din ke liye jayein.
  • Peak season (April se June, December): Nainital me bheed aur parking ki dikkat ho sakti hai, isliye chhote ya shaant thikano me rukne ka vikalp behtar hai.

Himalaya ke saaf nazare ke liye: Subah jaldi (suryoday ke aas-paas) aur baarish ke ek-do din baad aasman sabse saaf hota hai. Nanda Devi, Trishul, Nanda Kot aur Panchachuli jaisi chotiyan saaf din me dikhti hain (Nainital: Pramukh Himalayi Chotiyan aur Parvat Shrinkhla).

Ramgarh ka Tagore Top: Kavi Rabindranath Tagore se Juda Pahadi Sthal

Seedha jawab: Nainital zile ke Ramgarh me ek pahadi ko "Tagore Top" kaha jata hai, kyunki lok-sandarbhon ke anusar Nobel puraskar vijeta kavi Rabindranath Tagore 20vi sadi ke shuru me yahan rukte the aur yahan ek ashram/vidyalaya banane ki yojna se jude the (pushti karein: Tagore ke aane ka saal aur unki likhi rachnaon ka yahan se sambandh vishwasniya source se confirm karein). Ramgarh me Mahadevi Verma ki Meera Kutir bhi hai, isliye ise "sahitya ka gaon" bhi kehte hain.

Nainital: Kam Jaane-Pehchane aur Chhupe Gaon

Sadak Wale aur Paidal Wale Gaon: Slow Travel, Kalakar aur Birdwatcher ke Liye

Nainital zile ke Betalghat, Okhalkanda, Dhari, Ramgarh aur Bhimtal ke pahadi gaon me slow travel, birdwatching aur likhne-padhne ke liye shaant jagahein milti hain. Kuch gaon sadak ke kinare hain, kuch ke liye thodi paidal chadhai karni padti hai.

  • Road-head gaon: Jahan gaadi seedhe pahunch jati hai. Parivar ke saath safar aur bujurgon ke liye aasan.
  • Walk-in gaon: Jahan sadak ke baad kuch km paidal chalna padta hai. Zyada shanti aur prakriti ka anubhav, par samaan halka rakhein.
  • Kaun logon ke liye: Artists, writers, birdwatchers, photographers aur wo log jo bheed se door kuch din rehna chahte hain.

Offbeat jagah chunne ke liye 5 sawaal:

  • Sadak kaisi hai aur barish me band hone ka khatra kitna hai?
  • Network (mobile aur internet) milta hai ya nahi?
  • Najdeeki chikitsa suvidha kitni door hai?
  • Ghar ya homestay ka maalik sthaniya hai aur uski review kaisi hai?
  • Kya wahan jungle ke jaanwar (guldar, bhaaloo) ka khatra hai?

Safe aur zimmedar travel ke niyam:

  • Gaon me jaakar sthaniya logon ki ijazat ke bina tasveer na lein.
  • Plastic aur kachra waapas le jayein.
  • Sthaniya khana aur sthaniya log ki banayi cheezein kharidkar unki roji me madad karein.
  • Jungle ke raaste raat ko akele na chalein (Nainital: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh).

Aap sthaniya homestay ya gram pradhan se pooch kar apne liye behtar offbeat jagah dhoondh sakte hain.

Nainital: Khaali Gaon (Palayan) aur Virasat ki Sair

Khaali Pathar ke Gaon jo Eco-Tourism / Heritage Project Bane

Uttarakhand me palayan ki wajah se kai pahadi gaon khaali ya aadhe-khaali ho gaye hain. Sarkar aur kuch sansthayein inhe homestay aur virasat paryatan ke zariye dobara basane ki koshish kar rahi hain. Nainital ke pahadi gaon bhi isme shamil hain.

Palayan ki sthiti: Uttarakhand Gramin Vikas aur Palayan Aayog (2017-18) ne rajya me kai gaon ke khaali hone ki baat kahi thi. Nainital zile ke liye sahi aankde aayog ki report ya sarkari sroton se hi liye ja sakte hain (Aabaad aur Gair-Aabaad Gaon).

Dobara basane ke prayas:

  • Homestay: Uttarakhand sarkar gramin homestay ko badhawa deti hai (mukhya roop se Deen Dayal Upadhyay Griha Awas Homestay Yojana ke naam se). Iska labh aur shartein Paryatan Vibhag se pata karein.
  • Virasat paryatan: Purane pathal-chhat wale ghar, naule aur gharat ko dekhne ka anubhav.
  • Palayan ulatna (reverse migration): Work-from-hills aur jaivik kheti ke karan kuch parivar wapas gaon laut rahe hain (Nainital: Palayan Vapasi).

Heritage walk ke vikalp (Nainital shahar me):

  • Mall Road se Naina Devi Mandir tak: Shehar ka purana kendra, Flats maidan aur 1880 ke bhuskhalan ki yaad (Nainital Zila Kab aur Kaise Bana).
  • Angrezi kaal ki imaratein: St. John in the Wilderness church, Raj Bhawan (baahar se), Boat House Club ke aas-paas ka kshetra (Angrezi Kumaon Commissionery aur Hill Station Nainital).
  • Pahadi gaon me: Gaon ka naula, gharat, purane ghar aur mandir; gaon ke bujurg ya homestay maalik ko saath lekar dekhna behtar hota hai.

Savdhani: Khaali purane gaon me khandar jaisi imaratein ya girne wali deewarein khatarnak ho sakti hain, aur wahan jungli janwar bhi aa sakte hain. Akele ya raat ko na jayein.

Zile me ye nahi milta:

  • Base camp aur HAP rates: Zile ke liye lagu nahi.

  • Guide: Sthaniya, registered guide lena behtar hai; wo raste, janwar aur mausam ko jaante hain. Guide ke rate sthaniya sangh aur season ke hisaab se badalte hain, isliye pehle se tay kar lein.

Trek ke 8 niyam:

  • Subah jaldi shuru karein aur shaam se pehle laut aayein.
  • Paani, halka khana, torch aur first-aid saath rakhein.
  • Barish ya kohre me trek na karein.
  • Mausam ka alert dekhein (Nainital: Aapda Prabandhan aur Emergency Vyavastha).
  • Jungle me akele ya raat ko na jayein (guldar aur bhaaloo ka khatra, Nainital: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh).
  • Kachra waapas le jayein.
  • Jaanwar ko na chhedein aur na khilayein.
  • Apna plan kisi bharosemand ko bata kar jayein.

Nainital: Parvatarohan, Chattan Chadhai aur Bouldering

Kya Nainital mein Barf Wali Badi Chotiyan Hain?

Nainital zile me mountaineering ke liye koi IMF-suchibaddh barf choti nahi hai. Mountaineering seekhne ya chadhne ke liye log Uttarkashi ke Nehru Institute of Mountaineering (NIM) aur Chamoli, Pithoragarh ke abhiyaan kshetron me jaate hain.

  • Nainital zile me: Ooncha barf-parvat nahi, isliye IMF peak permit, porter aur HAP rates yahan lagu nahi.
  • Rock climbing aur rappelling: Naukuchiatal, Bhimtal aur Nainital ke aas-paas kuch adventure operators chhoti chattanon par rock climbing aur rappelling ke short sessions karwate hain. Safety, gear aur insurance ki jaankari pehle le lein, aur sirf registered operator ke saath jayein.
  • Bouldering: Pahadi chattano par bouldering ke liye sthaniya operators se sampark karein.
  • Mountaineering seekhna chahein to: NIM Uttarkashi jaise manyata prapt sansthan ke basic ya advance course ke baare me jaanein.

Safety: Rock climbing me helmet, harness aur rope ki jaanch zaroori hai. Bina training ya bina trained guide ke kisi bhi chattan par na chadhein.

Nainital: Sardi ke Khel: Skiing, Snowboarding aur Barf mein Trek

Kya Nainital mein Ski Slope Hai?

Nainital zile me skiing ya snowboarding ke liye koi mukhya slope nahi hai. Sardiyon me Nainital, Mukteshwar aur Ramgarh me kabhi-kabhi barf girti hai, par isliye wo snow sports ke liye bharosemand nahi hain. Skiing ke liye log Auli (Chamoli) jaate hain.

  • Snow ka anubhav: Sardiyon me (aam taur par December se February ke beech) Nainital aur Mukteshwar me halki se bhaari barf ho sakti hai, par har saal alag hota hai (Nainital: Mausam ke Zone aur 12 Mahine ka Mausam).
  • Snow View ropeway: Snow View Point tak ropeway ka nazara barf girne par bahut sundar hota hai, par ropeway aur sadak mausam ke karan band bhi ho sakte hain.
  • Skiing: Nainital zile me chairlift ya ski-course nahi hai. Auli (Chamoli) jaise ilakon me ski aur snow course hote hain.
  • Snow trek: Nainital zile me snow trek ke liye bhi bharosemand ya vyavasthit route nahi hai.

Barf me safar ki savdhani: Barf girne par pahadi sadak par phisalan hoti hai; gaadi me chain ya sahi tyre ke bina jaana khatarnak hai. Mausam alert (Nainital: Aapda Prabandhan aur Emergency Vyavastha) dekhein aur zaroorat padne par safar taal dein.

Nainital zile ke taalon me boating (paddle, row aur sail), aur kuch taalon me kayaking ki suvidha milti hai. Rafting zile ki pehchan nahi hai; uske liye Rishikesh jaisi jagah behtar hain. Corbett ke andar machhli pakadna kanooni roop se vajrit hai.

Taal me safety: Life jacket pehle se pehnein, bachchon ko akela na chhodein, aur tez hawa ya baarish me boating na karein.

Nainital: Hawai Khel, Paragliding, Mountain Biking aur Camping

Paragliding ki Jagah, Tandem Udaan, MTB Raaste aur Manzoor Camping

Naukuchiatal (aur Bhimtal) Nainital zile ka sabse mashhoor paragliding kshetra hai. Uttarakhand Paryatan ki sarkari site bhi Bhimtal, Kotabagh aur Nainital ko paragliding ke jaane-pehchane sthal batati hai. Monsoon ke dauran ye activity aam taur par band rehti hai.

  • Tandem paragliding: Naukuchiatal aur Bhimtal ke aas-paas licensed operators tandem flight karwate hain, jisme ek anubhavi pilot aapke saath udta hai.
  • Mausam: Aam taur par September-October se June tak, monsoon me nahi. Saaf aasman (October-November) aur thermals (March se May) ko acchha maana jata hai.
  • Licence aur anumati: Uttarakhand me paragliding operator aur tandem pilot ko Uttarakhand Paryatan Vikas Parishad ki technical committee ki manzuri se permission milti hai. Bina license wale operator se na udein.
  • Age aur weight: Alag-alag operator ki alag sharten hoti hain (kuch sources me minimum umra 14 saal aur vajan 35 se 80 kg ke beech batayi gayi hai). Booking se pehle operator se pooch lein.
  • Kharcha: Flight ki lambai aur oonchai ke hisaab se rate alag hote hain aur samay ke saath badlte rehte hain. Pehle se rate card maang lein aur insurance ke baare me pooch lein.
  • Savdhani: Safe equipment, helmet, reserve parachute aur pilot ki certification zaroor dekhein. Mausam kharab ho to flight na karein.
  • Itihas ka ek note: Pichle varshon me Uttarakhand High Court ne rajya me paragliding aur rafting ke liye saaf niyam banane ko kaha tha. Isliye aaj sirf niyam ke tahat chalne wale licensed operators hi bharosemand hain.

Mountain biking (MTB): Nainital aur Bhimtal ke aas-paas pahadi sadkon par cycling ki jaa sakti hai. Cycling me helmet pehnein aur pahadi sadak ke trafic ka dhyan rakhein.

Camping:

  • Forest-approved camping: Sattal, Naukuchiatal aur Corbett ke buffer zone ke aas-paas kai jagah paryatak camp chalte hain. Corbett ke core zone me camping nahi hoti.
  • Niyam: Jungle me khule me camp lagana, aag jalana ya raat ko rukna aam taur par Forest Department ki anumati ke bina ghair-kanooni hota hai. Sirf anumati prapt camp ya registered operator ke saath hi camp karein.

Nainital: Taare Dekhna, Astro-Tourism aur Photography ki Jagah

ARIES Vigyan Kendra, Raat ka Aasman, Milky Way aur Suryoday-Suryast

Nainital me astro-tourism ka sabse bharosemand sthal ARIES (Aryabhatta Research Institute of Observational Sciences) ka Science Centre hai, jo Manora Peak par (~1,951 m), Nainital bus stand se lagbhag 9 km door hai. Ye sabke liye muft hai, par niyam aur samay ka dhyan rakhna zaroori hai.

Nainital me aasman kitna saaf hai?
Nainital shehar ki roshni badhne se Manora Peak par observation mushkil hui, isliye ARIES ne naya bada observation kendra Devasthal (lagbhag 50 km door) me banaya. Isliye Nainital shehar ke aas-paas ko “bilkul andhera aasman” nahi kaha ja sakta. Ye achhi tarah dekhne ke liye shehar se door, unche aur kam roshni wale ilakon (jaise kuch shaant ridge-top gaon) me jaana behtar rehta hai.

Milky Way dekhne ke tips:

  • Chaand-rahit raat: Amavasya ke aas-paas ki raatein chuniye.
  • Mausam: Barish aur badal se door ka samay (aam taur par October-November aur March-April me aasman zyada saaf rehta hai).
  • Jagah: Shehar ki roshni se door, khuli jagah.
  • Aankhein adjust: Aankhon ko andhere me 20 se 30 minute rehne dein.
  • Garam kapde: Pahadi raaton me thand tez hoti hai.
  • Mobile ya camera: Tripod aur lambi exposure ka istemal karein.

Nainital: Ghumne ki jagah aur trek: Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Nainital ghoomne ka sabse achha samay kaun sa hai?
Aam taur par March se June (garmi se rahat) aur October-November (saaf aasman) sabse achhe maane jate hain. December se February me thand tez hoti hai aur kabhi-kabhi barf girti hai. July se September me barish aur bhuskhalan ka jokhim hota hai (Nainital: Mahine-dar-Mahine Yatra Guide).
Nainital me kitne din kaafi hote hain?
Nainital shehar ke liye 2 se 3 din kaafi hain. Bhimtal, Sattal, Naukuchiatal, Mukteshwar aur Corbett ke saath 5 se 10 din ka plan behtar rehta hai (Nainital: Taiyar Yatra Yojana). Shehar ke liye 2 se 3 din; aas-paas ke saath 5 se 10 din.
Naini jheel me boating kaise karein?
Jheel ke kinare boat counter se paddle, row aur sail boat ki booking hoti hai. Rate aur samay sthaniya boat sangh aur prashasan tay karte hain. Life jacket zaroor pehnein (Nainital: Paani ke Khel: Rafting, Kayaking aur Mahseer Machhli Pakadna).
Nainital ke aas-paas kaun si jheelein hain?
Bhimtal, Sattal (saat jheelon ka samooh), Naukuchiatal (nau kone), Khurpatal, Sariyatal, Harishtal aur Lokhamtal. Isi wajah se Nainital ko “Lake District of India” kehte hain (Nainital: Lakes, Kunds, Tals & Tarns).
Naini jheel ko bahari duniya ke saamne kisne laya (1841)?
British vyapari P. Barron ne, 1841 me.
Nainital me ghoomne ki sabse achhi jagah kaun si hain?
Naini jheel, Naina Devi Mandir, Snow View, Naina Peak, Bhimtal, Sattal aur Naukuchiatal.
Nainital me paragliding kahan hoti hai?
Naukuchiatal aur Bhimtal ke aas-paas, licensed operator ke saath.
नैनीताल घूमने का सबसे अच्छा समय कब है?
आम तौर पर मार्च से जून (गर्मी से राहत) और अक्टूबर-नवंबर (साफ आसमान)। जुलाई से सितंबर में बारिश और भूस्खलन का जोखिम रहता है।
नैनीताल घूमने के लिए कितने दिन काफी हैं?
शहर के लिए 2 से 3 दिन; भीमताल, सातताल, नौकुचियाताल, मुक्तेश्वर और कॉर्बेट के साथ 5 से 10 दिन।
नैनीताल के आसपास कौन सी झीलें हैं?
भीमताल, सातताल, नौकुचियाताल, खुरपाताल, सरियाताल, हरीशताल और लोखमताल। इसी वजह से नैनीताल को 'लेक डिस्ट्रिक्ट ऑफ इंडिया' कहते हैं।
नैनी झील में बोटिंग कैसे करें?
झील के किनारे बोट काउंटर से पैडल, रो और सेल बोट की बुकिंग होती है। लाइफ जैकेट जरूर पहनें।
नैनीताल में पैराग्लाइडिंग कहां होती है?
नौकुचियाताल और भीमताल के आसपास, लाइसेंस वाले ऑपरेटर के साथ।
