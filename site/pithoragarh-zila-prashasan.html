Pithoragarh: Executive Summary & District Identity (Zile ki Poori Pehchan)

Zile ki Pehchan: Ek Nazar Mein

Pithoragarh Uttarakhand ka sabse purvi zila hai aur Kumaon Mandal ka kshetrafal me sabse bada zila bhi. Iska mukhyalay Pithoragarh shehar Saur Ghati me hai, jise pyar se “Chhota Kashmir” (Mini Kashmir) kaha jata hai, kyunki ghati chaaro taraf se pahadiyon se ghiri, hari-bhari aur door tak failee hui hai.

Pithoragarh ki pehchan teen cheezon se hai. Pehli, yahan se Kailash Mansarovar Yatra ka raasta (Dharchula, Gunji, Lipulekh Darra) jata hai. Doosri, yahan Panchachuli ki barf se dhaki chotiyan, Milam glacier aur Munsiari ka high-altitude Himalayi nazara hai. Teesri, yahan ke log, jinme Shauka (Bhotia), Raji (Van Rawat) aur Kumaoni samaj shamil hain, sarhad par rehkar bhi apni sanskriti sambhale hue hain. Fauj, ITBP, SSB aur BRO ki mojoodgi ki wajah se ise “Seemant Zila” bhi kehte hain.

Sabhisaab ki rai: Agar aap Uttarakhand ko sirf Char Dham se jante hain, to Pithoragarh aapko ek bilkul alag Uttarakhand dikhayega: kam bheed, zyada pahad, zyada kahaniyan.

Pithoragarh Naam Kaise Pada: Naam ka Itihas

“Pithoragarh” naam ki utpatti ke baare me ek se zyada mat hain, aur koi ek mat poori tarah sarkari taur par tay nahi hai. Isliye hum yahan teen prachalit manyatayein bata rahe hain aur saaf likh rahe hain ki ye manyatayein hain, pakke itihas ke saboot nahi.

  • Rai Pithora mat: Ek lok-manyata ke anusar iska sambandh Rai Pithora (Prithviraj Chauhan) ke naam se joda jata hai. Is bare me itihaskaron me matbhed hai.
  • “Pith” aur “Garh” mat: Kuch log kehte hain ki yahan ek unchi pahadi par bana qila (garh) tha, aur uske aas-paas ki jagah Pithoragarh kehlayi. Yahan qila Gorkha shasan ke dauran 18th century ke ant me bana mana jata hai.
  • Saur ghati ki kahani: Saur Ghati ko lekar ye manyata bhi prachalit hai ki kabhi yahan ek bada sarovar (jheel) tha, jo samay ke saath sukh gaya aur upjau ghati ban gayi. Is wajah se “Saur” shabd ko “Sarovar” se joda jata hai. (lok-manyata)

Yaad rakhne wali baat: Pariksha ke liye “kisi ek mat ko hi sach” mat likhein. Sabse surakshit tareeka hai: “Naam ki utpatti ke baare me kai mat hain.”

Pithoragarh: Collectorate & Revenue Administration (Kalectret aur Rajasv Prashasan)

Zila Mukhyalay, Vikas Bhawan aur Collectorate Kahan Hai

Zila mukhyalay Pithoragarh shehar me hai, jahan Collectorate (DM Karyalay), Zila Panchayat aur zile ke zyadatar sarkari vibhagon ke karyalay hain. Zila Magistrate (DM) zile ka sabse bada prashasanik adhikari hota hai, jo rajasv, kanoon-vyavastha aur vikas karyon ka samanvay karta hai.

June 2026 ki ek news report me Pithoragarh ke Zila Magistrate ke roop me Ashish Bhatgai ka naam aaya hai (purane sources me Reena Joshi ka naam milta hai). (IAS adhikariyon ke transfer hote rehte hain, isliye current DM ka naam pithoragarh.nic.in se pushti karein.)

Aam janta ke liye kaam ki baat: Apne kaam ke liye pehle apni tehsil (Tehsildar/SDM karyalay) jayein. Collectorate tab jayein jab tehsil str par samadhan na ho ya DM ke janata darbar me baat rakhni ho.

Tehsil, Up-Tehsil aur Kanungo Circle ki Poori Suchi

Census 2011 ke samay Pithoragarh zile me 6 tehsil the: Pithoragarh, Munsiari, Dharchula, Didihat, Gangolihat aur Berinag. Uske baad naye tehsil aur upa-tehsil banne ki wajah se kuch websites par 11 se 13 tehsil ki ginti dikhti hai. Isliye tehsilon ki sankhya sarkari taur par abhi kya hai, ye official site se jaanchna zaroori hai.

Dilchasp tathya: Munsiari kshetrafal me zile ka sabse bada tehsil hai, par abadi me sabse chhota. Dharchula bhi kshetrafal me bahut bada hai. Dono ka zyadatar bhaag unchi himalayi ghatiyon me aata hai, isliye yahan abadi kam aur dooriyan zyada hain.

Naye tehsil/upa-tehsil: Thal, Bangapani, Tejam, Kanalichhina, Dewalthal, Jauljibi jaise naam alag-alag sources me milte hain. Inme se kaun sa abhi poora tehsil hai aur kaun sa upa-tehsil, ye notification se hi tay hoga.

Patwari Chowki, Rajasv Gaon aur Bhoo-Abhilekh Daftar

Pahad me zameen ka hisab Patwari rakhta hai, aur ek Patwari ke zimme kai gaon hote hain. Patwari ke upar Kanungo aur Tehsildar hote hain. Khatauni, nakal, naap-tol aur parivar register jaise zyadatar kaam patwari halqe se hi shuru hote hain.

Zile ke gaon ki ginti: Census 2011 par adharit alag-alag sites 1,572, 1,675 aur 1,749 jaise alag aankde dikhati hain. Iski wajah ye hai ki kuch sites aabad gaon ginti hain, kuch kul rajasv gaon, aur kuch naye banaye gaye hisse. Sarkari ginti Census Handbook ya Zila Sankhyiki Patrika se pushti karein.

Land record kahan dekhein: Online khatauni ke liye Uttarakhand ka Bhulekh portal hai.

Aabaad aur Khaali (Bhootiya) Gaon

Pithoragarh zile me bahut se gaon aise hain jahan abadi bahut kam ho gayi hai, aur kuch gaon bilkul khali (palayan wale) ho chuke hain. Iski mukhya wajah rozgar aur padhai ke liye shehron ki taraf palayan hai.

Vahan khali hote gaon, unki wajah aur wapas lautne wale logon ki kahaniyan hain.

Pithoragarh: Nagar Nikay aur Shahri Prashasan

Nagar Nigam aur Nagar Palika Parishad ki Suchi

Pithoragarh shehar zile ka sabse bada nagar nikay hai. Pehle yahan Nagar Palika Parishad thi; ab Pithoragarh Nagar Nigam (Municipal Corporation) hai, jiska pehla chunav 23 January 2025 ko hua (40 ward). Pehli mayor Kalpana Deolal (BJP) chuni gayin.

Census 2011 ke anusar zile ki shehri abadi lagbhag 69,605 thi, yani kul abadi ka chhota sa hissa. Baaki zyadatar log gaon me rehte hain.

Nagar Panchayat aur Chhavni Parishad (jahan hai)

Pithoragarh zile me koi Cantonment Board (Chhavni Parishad) nahi hai. Yahan fauj ki upasthiti hai, par vo alag se Cantonment Board ke roop me nahi chalti.

Nagar Panchayat wale kasbe: Dharchula, Didihat, Gangolihat aur Berinag jaise kasbe nagar panchayat ya chhote nagar nikay ke roop me jane jate hain. Kaun sa kasba abhi kis shreni me hai, ye bhi taza notification se pushti karein.

Pithoragarh: Political & Electoral Profile (Rajniti aur Chunav)

Lok Sabha Seat aur Maujooda Saansad

Pithoragarh zila Almora Lok Sabha seat ka hissa hai, jo Anusuchit Jati (SC) ke liye aarakshit hai. Is seat me chaar zile aate hain: Almora, Bageshwar, Champawat aur Pithoragarh.

2024 ke Lok Sabha chunav me is seat se BJP ke Ajay Tamta ne jeet darj ki, unhone Congress ke Pradeep Tamta ko lagbhag 2,34,097 voton ke antar se haraya. Ajay Tamta 2019 me bhi yahan se jeete the.

Vidhan Sabha Seatein aur SC/ST Aarakshit Kshetra

Pithoragarh zile me vidhan sabha ki 4 seat hain: Dharchula (42), Didihat (43), Pithoragarh (44) aur Gangolihat (45, SC aarakshit).

Panchayat Chunav ka Itihas aur Rajnaitik Padav

Pithoragarh ki rajniti me seemant zila hone ke karan sadak, bijli, mobile network, swasthya aur palayan jaise mudde hamesha mukhya rahe hain. Chunav me kai baar Dharchula-Munsiari jaise unche pahadi ilakon me sadak aur network sabse bada sawal bana hai.

Rajniti se jude kuch tathya: Zila Almora Lok Sabha seat ka hissa hai, aur is seat par Congress aur BJP, dono ko alag-alag samay par jeet mili hai. 2009 se ye seat SC aarakshit hai. Sirf tathya de rahe hain.

Pithoragarh: Law Enforcement, Security & Border Forces (Police aur Seema Suraksha)

Zile ki Police Vyavastha: Kotwali, Thane aur Chowkiyan

Zile ki police vyavastha Senior Superintendent/Superintendent of Police (SSP/SP) ke adhin hai, jinka karyalay Pithoragarh me hai. Zile me Kotwali Pithoragarh ke alawa kai thane aur rajasv police chowkiyan hain.

Pramukh thane: Pithoragarh, Thal, Jauljibi, Dharchula, Munsiari, Didihat, Berinag, Gangolihat jaise kasbon me thane ya police chowkiyan mili jati hain. Exact list Uttarakhand Police ki website se milaiye. Rajasv police (Patwari system) pahad ke unche gaon me abhi bhi pehli kadi hai.

Emergency number: Poore desh me 112 (emergency response), police ke liye 100, aur mahila helpline ke liye 1090/181 use ho sakte hain. (pushti karein, numbers badal sakte hain)

Fauj ki Regiment (Garhwal Rifles / Kumaon Regiment / Scouts), ITBP aur SSB Camp

Pithoragarh me bhartiya fauj ki upasthiti hai, aur yahan ke bahut se pariwar ke sadasya Kumaon Regiment, Kumaon Scouts ya anya fauji ikaiyon me sewa karte hain. Seemant zile me ITBP (Indo-Tibetan Border Police) China seema par aur SSB (Sashastra Seema Bal) Nepal seema par nigrani karti hai.

Seema par kya khaas hai: Nepal ke saath seema Kali (Mahakali) nadi ke saath chalti hai, aur kai jagah pul se aana-jaana hota hai. China (Tibet) ki taraf Lipulekh jaise darron se purane vyapar aur Kailash Mansarovar yatra ka raasta hai. Seema kshetra me jaane ke liye Inner Line Permit aur anya anumati ke niyam Pithoragarh: Kaise pahunchein, kahan rukein (Pithoragarh: Inner Line Permit , Van Anumati aur Suraksha Niyam) me diye gaye hain.

Agnishaman, SDRF aur Seema Chowkiyan

Zile me Fire Service ke station mukhyalay aur kuch bade kasbon me hain, par unche ilakon me aag ya aapda ke samay pahunchne me samay lag sakta hai. Isliye gaon ke log aapas me aur Yuvak/Mahila Mangal Dal ke saath milkar pehli madad karte hain.

Aapda ke samay SDRF aur zarurat par NDRF ki sahayata li jati hai.

Pithoragarh: Nyay Vyavastha aur Adalatein

Zila Nyayalay, Family Court aur Muft Kanooni Sahayata

Zile ka mukhya nyayalay “Zila evam Satra Nyayalay (District and Sessions Court)” Pithoragarh shehar me hai. Yahan diwani, faujdari aur parivarik mamlo ki sunwai hoti hai. Uttarakhand High Court Nainital me sthit hai.

Muft kanooni madad: Zila Vidhik Sewa Pradhikaran (DLSA) ke zariye gareeb aur zarurtmand logon ko free kanooni salah mil sakti hai. Tehsil str par bhi Vidhik Sewa Samiti hoti hai. (pushti karein, office address aur phone number)

Pithoragarh mein Sub-Registrar (Zameen Registry) Daftar

Zameen, makan ya dukan ki registry Sub-Registrar office me hoti hai, jo aam taur par tehsil mukhyalay par hota hai. Pithoragarh zile me registry ke liye aapko usi tehsil ke Sub-Registrar office jana padta hai jahan zameen sthit hai. (Kaun se tehsil me Sub-Registrar office abhi chal raha hai, ye Stamp evam Panjiyan Vibhag se pushti karein.)

Dhyan dein: Zameen kharidne se pehle Khatauni, nakal, naap aur kabze ki jaanch zaroor karein. Poora tareeka Pithoragarh: Zameen, nali-mutthi aur kheti (Pithoragarh mein Zameen Khareedne ke Kanoon aur Section 143) aur Pithoragarh: Sarkari seva aur portal (Pithoragarh: Bhulekh Uttarakhand: Khatauni Online Kaise Dekhein , Pithoragarh: Sampatti Registry, Stamp Duty aur Tehsil ke Circle Rate) me diya gaya hai.

Pithoragarh: Aapda Prabandhan aur Emergency Vyavastha

Aapda Control Room (DDMA) aur Toll-Free Helpline

Pithoragarh zile me aapda prabandhan ka mukhya karyalay Zila Aapda Prabandhan Adhikari (DDMA) ka hai, jiska Control Room (EOC) 24 ghante chalta hai. Zila Control Room ke liye aam taur par 1077 aur rajya Aapda control room ke liye 1070 number jaane jate hain. (pushti karein, number aur sampark vivaran official source se mila lein)

Pithoragarh me kaun si aapdayein aam hain: Barsat me bhooskhalan (landslide), badal fatna, nadi ka uphan, sadak band hona; sardi me barf; bhukamp ka khatra (Himalayi kshetra hone ki wajah se); aur kuch gaon me jungle ki aag.

SDRF / NDRF ke Thikane, Baadh Aashray aur Nikaasi ke Raaste

Zile me SDRF ki team zarurat par tainat ki jati hai, aur bade aapda ke samay NDRF ya fauj ki madad li jati hai. Helicopter se rahat aur bachav ka kaam bhi kiya jata hai jab sadak band ho jati hain.

Ghati ke logo ke liye salah: Barsat ke mausam me (June se September) safar karne se pehle sadak ki taza sthiti zaroor jaanch lein. Rasta band hone ki sthiti me kam se kam ek extra din ka samay aur zaruri saman saath rakhein.

Pichli badi ghatnayein (jo aam taur par yaad ki jati hain): 1998 me Kali nadi ke ilake me Malpa ke paas bhayanak bhooskhalan ek bahut badi traasdi thi. 2013 ki Uttarakhand aapda me bhi Kali aur Dhauliganga ghati ke ilakon me nuksaan hua tha. (Tarikh, mritkon ki sankhya aur ghatna ka vistar Pithoragarh: Pariyojana, aapda aur quiz (Pithoragarh: Purani Aapdayein, Bhukamp aur Sambhalne ki Kahani) me verified sources ke saath diya jayega.)

Pahadi Driving ke Niyam aur Green Cess

Pahad me gaadi chalane ke liye alag se koi “Hill Driving Licence” ka sarvajanik niyam general taur par nahi hai, par kai rajyon ki tarah bahari gaadiyon par entry/green tax ya cess lagne ke niyam samay-samay par aate rahte hain. Pithoragarh ke liye aaj kya lagu hai, ye Uttarakhand Parivahan Vibhag ki website se hi pakka karein.

Pahadi sadak par chalane ke 8 asaan niyam: (1) Chadhai par chadhne wali gaadi ko pehle raasta dein. (2) Mod par horn zaroor bajayein. (3) Neeche utarte samay engine gear me rakhein, sirf brake par bharosa na karein. (4) Rat me lambi drive na karein. (5) Barsat me bhooskhalan wale hisson me na rukein. (6) Petrol bhara rakhein, kyunki kai jagah pump door hote hain. (7) Network na ho to offline map download rakhein. (8) Sadak band ho to local logon ki salah maanein.
