Isliye BMI ke saath waist-to-height ratio bhi dekhna zyada kaam ka rehta hai, khaas kar Bharatiya logon me, jahan pet ke aas-paas ki charbi zyada mayne rakhti hai.

BMI ki seema — ye kya nahi batata

BMI sirf do cheezein jaanta hai: aapka wazan aur lambai. Isliye wo ye nahi bata sakta ki wazan muscle ka hai ya charbi ka. Ek pehalwaan aur ek nishkriya vyakti ka BMI ek jaisa aa sakta hai, jabki dono ki sehat me zameen-aasmaan ka farak hoga.

Bharatiya logon me ek aur baat mayne rakhti hai. Shodh batate hain ki dakshin Asia ke logon me usi BMI par charbi ka anupaat aur pet ke aas-paas ki charbi zyada milti hai. Isiliye kai vishesagya BMI ke saath kamar ka naap aur waist-to-height ratio dekhne ki salah dete hain.

Waist-to-height ka aam paimana yaad rakhna aasan hai: kamar ka naap lambai ke aadhe se kam hona chahiye. 5 foot 8 inch (68 inch) wale ke liye kamar 34 inch se kam. Ye ek naap kai baar BMI se zyada kaam ki jaankari deta hai.

Calorie ki zaroorat kaise nikalti hai

Do hisse hote hain. Pehla BMR — bilkul aaram karte hue bhi shareer ko jitni energy chahiye (saans, dil, dimaag, taapman). Ye aam taur par kul zaroorat ka 60-70% hota hai.

Doosra hai aapki din bhar ki halchal. BMR ko activity ke hisaab se guna karke kul zaroorat nikalti hai — baithe rehne wale kaam me kam, mehnat wale kaam me kaafi zyada.

Ek cheez dhyaan rakhne wali hai: ye saare formula anuman hain, naap nahi. Do logon ki asli zaroorat 10-15% tak alag ho sakti hai. Isliye number ko shuruaati bindu maaniye, aur do-teen hafte apne shareer ki pratikriya dekh kar adjust kariye.

Paani, neend aur wo cheezein jo calculator nahi batate

Paani ki zaroorat mausam, mehnat aur ilaake par nirbhar karti hai. Pahad me thandi hawa me pyaas kam lagti hai par shareer ka paani utna hi khatam hota hai — isliye trek ya khet ke kaam me pyaas ka intezaar kiye bina paani peete rehna behtar hai.

Aur ek baat jo koi calculator nahi batata: neend. Kam neend seedha bhookh badhati hai, mithai ki talab badhati hai aur wazan ghatane ki koshish ko mushkil bana deti hai. 7-8 ghante ki neend kisi bhi diet plan se zyada kaam karti hai.

Zaroori: Ye saare tools sirf aam jaankari ke liye hain. Ye kisi doctor ki jaanch, diagnosis ya ilaaj ki jagah nahi le sakte. Agar aap garbhvati hain, koi bimari hai, dawa chal rahi hai, ya khane-peene se juda koi sangharsh hai — to in numbers par akele bharosa mat kijiye, apne doctor se baat kijiye.

BMI se zyada kaam ki ek naap: kamar ka ghera lambai se bhaag dijiye. Ye 0.5 se kam rehna chahiye — yaani kamar aapki lambai ki aadhi se kam. Ye naap peth ki charbi pakadti hai, jo BMI se chhoot jaati hai. Isi wajah se dubla dikhne wala banda bhi andar se charbi wala ho sakta hai. Do naap saath dekhiye, kisi ek par faisla mat kariye.
