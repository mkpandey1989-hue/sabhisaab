Home Loan — Kitna Milega Aur Byaj Kaise Bachaein?

Ghar khareedna zindagi ka sabse bada faisla hota hai, aur zyadatar log iske liye home loan lete hain. Par loan lene se pehle teen cheezein samajhna bahut zaroori hai — kitna loan milega, EMI kya hogi, aur byaj kaise kam karein. Is guide me hum teeno ko aasaan bhasha me samjhenge, aur neeche free calculators se aap apni eligibility aur bachat khud nikaal sakte hain.

➤ Home Loan Calculators kholein

Kitna loan mil sakta hai? (Eligibility)

Bank aam taur par ye dekhta hai ki aapki EMI aapki monthly income ke lagbhag 50% se zyada na ho (aapke chalu loans ko ghata kar). Jaise agar aapki income ₹60,000 hai aur koi doosra loan nahi, to aapki EMI ki gunjaish lagbhag ₹30,000 tak ho sakti hai — aur usi hisaab se loan ki rakam tay hoti hai. Iske alawa aapka credit score (CIBIL), umar aur naukri ki sthirta bhi maayne rakhti hai.

EMI kis par depend karti hai?

  • Loan amount: Jitna zyada loan, utni zyada EMI.
  • Byaj dar: Thodi kam dar bhi lambe samay me laakhon bachati hai.
  • Tenure (saal): Zyada saal = kam EMI, par kul byaj zyada.

Byaj kaise kam karein? (Asli tarike)

  1. Zyada down payment: Jitna apni jeb se denge, utna kam loan, utna kam byaj.
  2. Behtar credit score: Achha CIBIL score kam byaj dar dilwata hai.
  3. Dar compare karein: Alag-alag bank ki dar dekh kar sabse kam chunein.
  4. Prepayment karein: Ye sabse taakatwar tarika hai (neeche detail me).

Prepayment — laakhon bachane ka tarika

Jab bhi aapke paas extra paisa aaye (bonus, savings), use loan me prepay kar dein. Isse aapka principal (asli rakam) ghatta hai, aur aage ka saara byaj bachta hai. Sabse bada fayda tab hota jab aap shuruaat ke saalon me prepay karte hain — kyunki us samay EMI ka zyada hissa byaj me jaa raha hota hai.

Misaal: ₹20 lakh loan, 9% byaj, 20 saal. Agar aap shuruat me ₹2 lakh ek baar prepay kar dein, to aap kai saal aur laakhon ka byaj bacha sakte hain. Exact bachat neeche Prepayment Calculator me dekhein.

Calculators kaise use karein?

  1. Eligibility: income aur chalu EMI daal kar dekhein kitna loan mil sakta.
  2. EMI: loan, byaj, saal daal kar monthly kisht nikaalein.
  3. Prepayment: ek baar ki extra rakam daal kar dekhein kitna byaj aur samay bacha.

FAQ

Fixed rate lu ya floating?

Floating rate market ke saath badalta (aam taur par sasta padta lambe samay me), fixed rate ek jaisa rehta. Zyadatar log floating chunte hain.

Prepayment par charge lagta hai?

Home loan (floating rate) par aam taur par koi charge nahi lagta. Fixed rate ya doosre loans me kabhi-kabhi lagta — bank se confirm karein.

Tenure lamba lu ya chhota?

Chhota tenure = zyada EMI par kam kul byaj. Lamba = kam EMI par zyada byaj. Apne budget ke hisaab se balance banayein.

Home loan ki EMI aay ka kitna percent honi chahiye?

40% se neeche rakhna behtar hai, aur ghar ke liye 30-35% aur bhi surakshit. Maintenance, property tax aur marammat milakar aap waise bhi 40-45% par pahunch jaate hain.

Home loan sirf tax bachane ke liye lena chahiye?

Nahi. Byaj hamesha tax ki chhoot se zyada hota hai. Ghar zaroorat ya rehne ke liye lijiye, tax bachat ko sirf ek bonus maaniye.

Loan lene se pehle — chhupe kharche

Home loan me sirf EMI nahi lagti. Kai kharche shuruaat me hi dene padte hain aur log inhe budget me nahi jodte:

  • Processing fee — loan rakam ka ek chhota percentage, aam taur par 0.25% se 1%
  • Legal aur technical valuation — bank apna vakeel aur engineer bhejta hai
  • Stamp duty aur registry — ye sabse bada kharch hota hai, aur ye loan me shaamil nahi hota
  • Insurance — kai bank loan ke saath property/life cover dete hain
  • MODT charges — kuch rajyon me property gehan rakhne ka shulk

Ye sab milkar ghar ki keemat ka 6-10% tak ho sakta hai — aur ye poora paisa aapki jeb se jaata hai. Down payment ke saath ye bhi jod kar hi plan banayein.

Fixed ya floating rate

Floating rate market ke saath ghatti-badhti hai. Shuruaat me sasti hoti hai aur prepayment par aam taur par koi charge nahi lagta. Fixed rate poore samay ek jaisi rehti hai — nishchitta milti hai par dar aam taur par zyada hoti hai.

Zyadatar logon ke liye floating hi behtar rehti hai, khaaskar isliye ki usme prepayment ka rasta khula rehta hai. Par agar aapki income tight hai aur EMI badhne se dikkat ho jaayegi, to fixed ki shanti bhi apni jagah sahi hai.

Prepayment — sabse bada hathiyar

Home loan me shuru ke saalon me EMI ka bada hissa byaj me jaata hai. Isi liye jitna jaldi prepayment karenge, utna zyada byaj bachega. 20 saal ke loan me pehle 5 saal me kiya gaya prepayment sabse zyada asar karta hai; aakhri saalon me karne se utna fayda nahi hota.

Do vikalp: prepayment ke baad aap EMI kam karwa sakte hain ya loan ka samay (tenure) kam karwa sakte hain. Tenure kam karwana aam taur par zyada paisa bachata hai, kyunki byaj kam saal ke liye lagta hai.

Saal me ek extra EMI dena bhi ek aasaan tarika hai — isse 20 saal ka loan aksar 2-3 saal pehle khatam ho jaata hai.

Eligibility kaise badhayein

  • Credit score sudhaarein — achha score sasti dar dilata hai; 0.5% ka farak bhi lakho ka hota hai
  • Co-applicant jodein — pati-patni dono ki income judne se loan zyada milta hai, aur dono ko tax chhoot bhi
  • Purane chhote loan chukayein — chalte loan aapki eligibility kam karte hain
  • Loan ka samay badhayein — EMI kam hoti hai aur eligibility badhti hai, par kul byaj zyada lagta hai

Kagaz ki jaanch khud karein

Bank apni taraf se legal jaanch karta hai, par wo apna paisa surakshit karne ke liye hoti hai — aapki suraksha ke liye nahi. Isliye khud bhi dekhein ki zameen/flat ka title saaf hai, builder ke paas zaroori manzoori hai, aur property par koi purana karza ya vivad to nahi. Ek achha property vakeel ki fees is kharche ke saamne bahut chhoti hai.
