Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) Calculator

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me 2-3 min me samajh
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) sarkar ki sone me nivesh ki yojana hai — ismein sone ki keemat ka fayda aur upar se 2.5% saalana byaj milta hai. Apna nivesh, sone ki anumaanit growth aur saal daaliye — SGB ka total return dekh lijiye. Ye un logon ke liye hai jo sone me nivesh to chahte hain par physical gold ka jhanjhat nahi.

Number kaise nikalta hai

SGB me do tarah ka fayda hota hai. Ek — sone ki keemat badhne par aapki nivesh value badhti hai. Doosra — nivesh rakam par har saal 2.5% fixed byaj, jo 6 mahine me milta hai. Total return = gold ki growth + byaj.

Do fayde:
Gold value = nivesh × (1 + growth%)saal
Byaj = nivesh × 2.5% × saal
Maturity 8 saal (5 saal baad exit option)

Sabse bada fayda: agar SGB maturity (8 saal) tak rakhein, to sone ki keemat par milne wala capital gain poori tarah tax-free hota hai — physical gold ya gold ETF me ye chhoot nahi milti.

SGB un logon ke liye achha vikalp maana jaata hai jo sone me lambe samay ke liye nivesh karna chahte hain, par physical gold ke jhanjhat (locker, making charge, milawat, chori ka dar) se bachna chahte hain. Ek RBI-backed kaagzi roop me sona rakhne ka fayda ye hai ki aapko keemat ka poora fayda milta hai, upar se 2.5% byaj, aur maturity par gain par tax bhi nahi. Par yaad rahe — sone ki keemat market me ghatti-badhti rehti hai, isliye SGB bhi short-term ke liye nahi, balki 5-8 saal ke lambe nazariye se hi lena chahiye. Physical sona pehne ke liye chahiye ho to SGB uski jagah nahi le sakta.

Asli zindagi ki teen misaal

Misaal 1 — 8 saal. ₹1 lakh, 8% gold growth, 8 saal. Gold value ~₹1.85 lakh + byaj (2.5% × 8 saal = ₹20,000) = ~₹2.05 lakh.

Misaal 2 — byaj ka fayda. Physical gold me sirf keemat badhti hai; SGB me upar se 2.5% byaj — 8 saal me ₹20,000 extra (₹1 lakh par).

Misaal 3 — tax-free gain. Maturity par sone ki keemat ka poora munafa tax-free — yahi SGB ka sabse bada plus point.

Ye galtiyan mat kijiye

  1. Gold growth guarantee maanna. Sone ki keemat girti-badhti hai — 8% sirf anuman hai, guarantee nahi.
  2. Byaj ko tax-free samajhna. 2.5% byaj taxable hai (aapke slab par); sirf maturity par capital gain tax-free.
  3. Beech me bechne par. 5 saal se pehle exchange par bechne par capital gain tax lag sakta hai.
  4. Physical gold jaisa samajhna. SGB kaagzi hai — na making charge, na locker, na milawat ka dar.
  5. Liquidity ki soch. SGB 8 saal ka hai (5 saal baad exit); turant paisa chahiye to ye theek nahi.

Bharatiya sthiti me khaas baatein

  • Sarkari: SGB RBI dwara sarkar ki taraf se jaari hota hai — poori tarah surakshit.
  • 2.5% byaj: Sone ki keemat ke alawa nivesh par 2.5% saalana byaj — physical gold me ye nahi.
  • Tax-free gain: Maturity (8 saal) tak rakhne par capital gain poori tarah tax-free.
  • No making charge: Physical gold jaisa making charge, locker aur milawat ka jhanjhat nahi.

Aur thoda samajhte hain

Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) sarkar (RBI) dwara jaari kiya jaata hai aur ye physical sona rakhne se behtar maana jaata hai — na chori ka dar, na making charge, na purity ki tension. Log "SGB return kaise nikale", "sovereign gold bond vs physical gold", "gold bond interest" search karte hain. SGB ki do kamai hoti hain: ek to sone ke bhaav ka badhna, aur doosra saalana 2.5% ka fixed byaj jo aapke nivesh par milta hai.

Sabse bada fayda — agar aap SGB ko maturity (8 saal) tak rakhte hain, to sone ke bhaav se hua munafa (capital gain) poori tarah tax-free hota hai. Par 2.5% wala byaj aapke slab ke hisaab se taxable hai. Ye sona ko "nivesh" ki tarah dekhne walon ke liye achha option hai. SGB ke aam sawaal — byaj, tax aur maturity

Sovereign Gold Bond ko log alag-alag naam se dhoondhte hain — SGB calculator, gold bond calculator, SGB return calculator, SGB fair value. Jawab neeche ek jagah hai.

SGB me kamai do jagah se hoti hai

  • Sone ka bhaav badhne se — maturity par us waqt ka bhaav milta hai
  • 2.5% saalana byaj — ye shuruaati nivesh par milta hai, aaj ke bhaav par nahi. Har 6 mahine me bank khaate me aata hai
Yahi wo baat hai jo sabse zyada galat samjhi jaati hai. 2.5% byaj us rakam par lagta hai jo aapne pehle din lagayi thi, na ki badhe hue bhaav par. ₹5,000 per gram par kharida hai to har saal ₹125 per gram byaj milega — chahe bhaav baad me ₹8,000 ho jaye.

SGB ki "fair value" kya hoti hai

Bazaar me SGB apni asli sone ki value se aksar thoda kam ya zyada par bikta hai. Wajah — bache hue saal, bacha hua byaj, aur kitni aasaani se bik raha hai. Isi antar ko fair value ka sawaal kehte hain. Agar aapko bazaar se SGB kharidna ho, to discount par milne wala purana SGB aksar naye se behtar sauda hota hai.

Sabse bada faayda — tax

SGB ko poore samay tak rakhne par maturity par milne wale munafe par capital gain tax ka niyam alag hai. Physical sone ya gold ETF me ye chhoot nahi milti. Byaj par tax lagta hai. Niyam badalte rehte hain — apne CA se ya rbi.org.in se confirm kar lijiye.

SGB par loan bhi mil jaata hai — bank ise gold loan jaisi security maan lete hain. Aur physical sone ke mukable isme na making charge lagta hai, na locker ka kiraya, na shuddhta ka shak. Iski asli kami sirf ek hai — paisa lambe samay ke liye fansta hai. Beech me bechna mumkin hai par tab wo tax chhoot nahi milti.

Chhote sawaal, chhote jawab

Sovereign Gold Bond kya hai?

Sarkar ki sone me nivesh yojana — sone ki keemat ka fayda + 2.5% saalana byaj.

SGB me kitna byaj milta hai?

Nivesh rakam par 2.5% saalana, 6 mahine me milta hai. Ye byaj taxable hai.

SGB ka gain tax-free hai?

Maturity (8 saal) tak rakhne par sone ki keemat ka capital gain tax-free hota hai.

SGB kitne saal ka hota hai?

8 saal, par 5 saal baad exit option milta hai.

Physical gold se behtar kyun?

2.5% extra byaj, tax-free gain, aur na making charge/locker/milawat ka dar.

SGB calculator se kya pata chalta hai?

Kitna nivesh kiya, kitna 2.5% saalana byaj mila, aur maturity par sone ke bhaav se kitni value bani — teeno milakar kul return.

SGB ka 2.5% byaj kis rakam par milta hai?

Shuruaati nivesh par, badhe hue bhaav par nahi. ₹5,000 per gram par kharida to har saal ₹125 per gram — chahe bhaav baad me kuch bhi ho.

Physical sona ya SGB — kaunsa behtar?

SGB me na making charge lagta hai, na locker ka kiraya, na shuddhta ka shak, aur upar se 2.5% byaj. Kami sirf ek — paisa lambe samay ke liye fansta hai.

SGB ka fair value kaise nikalta hai?

Aaj ke sone ke bhaav par bond ki value, aur uspar milne wala saalana byaj. Dono milakar hi asli return banta hai — sirf sone ka bhaav dekhna aadha hisaab hai.

SGB par loan mil sakta hai?

Haan, kai bank SGB ko girvi rakhkar loan dete hain. Shartein aur LTV bank ke hisaab se alag hoti hain — apne bank se confirm kar lijiye.

Zaroori baat: Sone ki keemat market me ghatti-badhti hai; ye anuman hai, guarantee nahi. Byaj taxable hai. SGB ke niyam RBI tay karta hai — nivesh se pehle official jaankari aur apne salahkaar se confirm karein.
