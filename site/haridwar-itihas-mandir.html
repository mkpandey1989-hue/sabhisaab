Haridwar: Puranon ka Bhugol: Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand

Puranon aur Mahakavyon mein Haridwar: Skanda Puran, Mahabharat, Kalidas aur Kumbh

Seedha jawab: Haridwar ko prachin granthon me Gangadwara, Mayapuri aur Kapilsthan jaise naamon se jana jata hai. Skanda Purana ke Kedarkhand me Haridwar (Gangadwara/Mayakshetra) ka mahatmya milta hai, Mahabharata ke Vana Parva ki tirth-yatra katha me Gangadwara ka ullekh hai, aur Kalidasa ke Meghaduta me Kankhal (Kanakhala) ka varnan mana jata hai.

Kumbh ki pauranik katha (lok-manyata): Samudra-manthan se nikle amrit-kalash ke liye devta-asur sangharsh me amrit ki bunden chaar sthalon par girin: Haridwar, Prayagraj, Ujjain aur Nashik. Manyata hai ki Haridwar me ye boond Brahmakund (Har Ki Pauri) par giri, isliye yahan snan ka vishesh mahatva hai.

Ganga ke avtaran ki katha: Raja Bhagirath ne apne purvajon (Sagar ke putron) ki mukti ke liye kathin tapasya karke Ganga ko prithvi par laane ki prarthana ki. Manyata hai ki Ganga ke vega ko Bhagwan Shiv ne apni jataon me dhaaran kiya aur fir dheere-dheere pravahit kiya. Haridwar wo sthan hai jahan Ganga pahadon se nikalkar maidan me aati hain, isiliye ise Gangadwara kaha gaya.

Sapta Puri (saat moksha-dayini nagariyan): Ayodhya, Mathura, Maya (Haridwar), Kashi, Kanchi, Avantika (Ujjain) aur Dwarka.

Darshan salah:

  • Samay: Mandir aam taur par subah se shaam tak khule rehte hain; navratri aur mela me samay badal jata hai. Taaza timing mandir ya official site se dekhein.
  • Vastra aur saman: Chandi Devi jaise mandiron me leather saman, sharab aur non-veg ke saath pravesh nishedh bataya gaya hai.
  • Bandar: Mansa Devi marg par bandar bahut hain; prasad aur mobile sambhal kar rakhein.
  • Bheed: Navratri, Somvati Amavasya aur Kumbh me bheed bahut hoti hai.
  • Ropeway: Samay aur sthiti (tez hawa/barish me band) check karein.

Winter seat (Gaddi sthal) ka note: Char Dham ke kapat sardiyon me band hone par devta-vigrah neeche ke gaddi-sthal me puje jate hain (jaise Badrinath ke Joshimath/Pandukeshwar, Kedarnath ke Ukhimath, Gangotri ke Mukhba aur Yamunotri ke Kharsali). Ye sthal Haridwar se aage hain.

Har Ki Pauri aur Ganga Aarti: Itihas, Samay aur Yatri Salah

Seedha jawab: Har Ki Pauri Haridwar ka sabse pavitra ghat hai, jahan roz subah aur sham Ganga Aarti hoti hai. Sham ki Aarti suryast ke samay hoti hai aur sabse bheed-bhari hoti hai. Manyata hai ki isi jagah amrit ki boond giri thi.

Har Ki Pauri ka itihas: Lok-katha ke anusar ise Raja Vikramaditya ne apne bhai Bhartrihari ki yaad me banwaya tha. Kuch samay ke baad ke shasakon (jaise Amber ke Raja Man Singh) dwara ghat ke vikas ka bhi ullekh milta hai. Aitihasik praman ke liye granth/shodh dekhein.

Ganga Aarti ke baare me:

  • Kab: Subah aur shaam, shaam ki Aarti suryast ke aas-paas; mausam ke saath samay badalta hai.
  • Kahan baithein: Ghat ke saamne ki seedhiyon par jaldi pahunch kar jagah lein.
  • Dona-diya: Patton ke dona me diya aur phool rakhkar Ganga me pravahit karte hain; keemat pehle tay karein.
  • Safety: Snan ke samay zanjeer/railing ke bahar na jayein; bheed me bachchon ka hath pakde rahein.
  • Photography: Bheed me mobile surakshit rakhein.

Chhote Sawal (Quick FAQ)

Ganga Aarti kitne baje hoti hai?
Sham ki Aarti suryast ke samay; taaza samay mausam se badalta hai.
Har Ki Pauri me entry fee hai?
Nahi, ghat par pravesh nishulk hai.
Ganga Aarti ke liye kab pahunchein?
Aarti se 1-2 ghante pehle.

Jagar, Jagariya aur Dangariya: Pahadi Devta Avahan ki Prakriya (Samajhne ke liye)

Seedha jawab: Jagar Pahadi parampara ki wo pooja hai jisme Jagariya (gayak) devta ya pitar ki gaatha gata hai aur Dangariya (madhyam) par devta ka avatran maana jata hai. Ye aastha aur lok-parampara ka vishay hai.

Teen bhumikayein:

  • Jagariya: Hudka/damaru ke saath devta ki gaatha gane wala gayak.
  • Dangariya / Dangri: Wo vyakti jis par devta ka "avatran" hona maana jata hai.
  • Sevak/yajman: Wo parivar jo jagar karwata hai.

Pradarshan-roop: Jagar rat bhar chalta hai, aur devta ke "naachne" aur nyay/upay batane ki manyata hoti hai.

Sanvedansheel vishay: Is parampara ko sammanpoorvak aur tathyatmak roop me likhein; "andhvishwas" jaise shabdon se bachein, aur kisi bhi chikitsakiya ya manovaigyanik dave ko na jodein.

Chhote Sawal (Quick FAQ)

Jagar kya hota hai?
Devta/pitar ke avahan ki gayan-puja.

Jagariya kaun hota hai? Gaatha gane wala gayak.

Haridwar: Pragaitihasik Kaal, Shila-Chitra aur Shailashray

Haridwar ka Pracheen Itihas: Bahadrabad (OCP Culture), Copper Hoards, Gurukul Kangri Archaeological Museum aur Pahadi Rock Shelters (Gateway Reference)

Seedha jawab: Haridwar jile ka Bahadrabad Ochre Coloured Pottery (OCP) sanskriti ka ek jana-mana sthal hai, aur Gurukul Kangri ke Archaeological Museum me OCP ke tukde aur Copper-Hoard sanskriti ki vastuyen rakhi hain. Rock shelters (Lakhudiyar aadi) Almora kshetra me hain, Haridwar me nahi.

OCP aur Copper Hoard (Haridwar ka gaurav):

  • OCP (Ochre Coloured Pottery) sanskriti aam taur par lagbhag 2000-1500 BCE ki maani jati hai aur Gangetic maidan ke upari hisse me failti thi.
  • Bahadrabad (Haridwar jila) ko iske pramukh sthalon me ginta jata hai.
  • Copper Hoard (taambe ke aujaar-hathiyar ke bhandar) kai sthalon par mile; vidvan inhe OCP se jodte hain, par sambandh par vidvaanon me matbhed hai.
  • Gurukul Kangri Archaeological Museum: Gurukul ke shuruaati varshon (1907-08) me sthapit; OCP aur Copper-Hoard sanskriti ke section ke liye jana jata hai.

Pahadi sthal (jile wale pages ke liye): Lakhudiyar (Almora) ke rangeen rock-shelter, Dungri aur Kimni (Chamoli), Bankot (copper hoard, Pithoragarh) aur okhali (cup-marks) wale megalith Kumaon ke hain.

Chhote Sawal (Quick FAQ)

Haridwar me kaunsa pracheen sthal hai?
Bahadrabad (OCP sanskriti).
OCP kya hai?
Ochre Coloured Pottery, ek Kansya-yugin purani sanskriti.
Gurukul Kangri museum kahan hai?
Kangri gaon (Haridwar) me.

Haridwar: Kuninda aur Katyuri Raajvansh ka Daur

Kuninda Rajya, Kalsi ka Ashok Shilalekh aur Haridwar ka Prachin Kaal (Xuanzang ka Mo-yu-lo)

Seedha jawab: Kuninda (Kulinda) prachin janapad Shivalik se Himalaya ke bhoo-bhaag me phaila mana jata hai, jisme aaj ke Uttarakhand-Himachal ke kshetra aate hain. Haridwar ke nazdik Dehradun ke Kalsi me Samrat Ashok ka shilalekh milta hai. Chini yatri Xuanzang (7vi sadi) ke "Mo-yu-lo" ko kai itihaskar Mayapur (Haridwar) se jodte hain.

Samanya jankari:

  • Kuninda: Ve silver aur copper ke sikke jaari karte the, jinme se kuch Uttarakhand ke kshetron me mile hain (jaise Amoghabhuti ke sikke Garhwal-Himachal kshetra me).
  • Kalsi shilalekh (Dehradun): Ashok ke 14 shilalekhon ka ek sanskaran Dehradun jile ke Kalsi me mila, jo Haridwar ke nikat ke Doon kshetra me aata hai.
  • Katyuri: Baijnath-Joshimath kshetra ke rajvansh; Haridwar se unka seedha sambandh nahi hai.

Sandarbh sambandhi savdhani: Haridwar-Xuanzang sambandh itihas-matabhed ka vishay hai.

Chhote Sawal (Quick FAQ)

Kuninda kaun the?
Himalaya ki talhatti ke prachin janapad.
Kalsi shilalekh kahan hai?
Dehradun ke Kalsi me.
Xuanzang kaun the?
7vi sadi ke Chini bauddh yatri.

Haridwar: Panwar (Garhwal) & Chand (Kumaon) Kingdoms

Haridwar ka Madhya-kaleen Shasan: Mughal Saharanpur Sarkar, Rohilla-Maratha-Gurjar Prabhav aur Garhwal-Chand ke Rajya (Reference)

Seedha jawab: Madhya-kaal me Haridwar-Roorkee kshetra Mughal-kaleen Saharanpur ke antargat tha. 18vi sadi me Rohilla, Maratha aur sthaniya Gurjar talluqdaron ka prabhav raha, aur 1803 ke baad British shasan aaya. Panwar (Garhwal) aur Chand (Kumaon) rajyon ke 52 garh pahadi jilon me hain, Haridwar me nahi.

Haridwar: British Garhwal / Kumaon Commissionery & Tehri Riyasat

British Kaal me Haridwar-Roorkee: Upper Ganga Canal, Thomason College, Bengal Sappers aur Railway

Seedha jawab: British kaal me Roorkee Bharat ka ek bada engineering kendra bana: Upper Ganga Canal (1842-1854), Thomason College of Civil Engineering (1847) aur Bengal Sappers ka centre. Haridwar tak railway line aur Kumbh ke prabandhan ne shehar ka naksha badal diya. Pahadi jilon ke Traill-Batten jaise bhoomi-bandobast Garhwal-Kumaon ke hain.

Haridwar: Azaadi ki Ladai, Kuli Begar aur Rajya Andolan

Haridwar-Roorkee ka Swatantrata Sangharsh: Kunja Bahadurpur (1822-24), Gurukul Kangri (1902), Gandhi ki 1915 ki Haridwar Yatra aur Uttarakhand Andolan

Seedha jawab: Haridwar jile ke Kunja Bahadurpur (Roorkee ke paas) me Raja Vijay Singh aur Senapati Kalyan Singh ke netritva me 1822-24 ka British-virodhi vidroh hua, jise 1857 se pehle ka ek prarambhik sangharsh mana jata hai. Gurukul Kangri (1902) ne rashtravadi shiksha ka kaam kiya, aur Mahatma Gandhi ne 1915 ke Kumbh ke samay Haridwar ki yatra ki.

Haridwar: Lok-Kathayein: Kimvadantiyan, Veer Yoddha aur Rahasyamay Kisse

Haridwar ki Lok-kathayein: Bhartrihari-Vikramaditya, Bhimgoda, Daksh Yagna, Gangajal ka Rahasya aur Pahadi Gatha (Madho Singh Bhandari, Rajula-Malushahi, Tilu Rauteli)

Seedha jawab: Haridwar ki prasiddh lok-kathayein hain: Bhartrihari aur Raja Vikramaditya ki katha (Har Ki Pauri), Bhim ki Bhimgoda katha, Daksh Prajapati ka yagya (Kankhal) aur Ganga ke avtaran ki katha. Pahadi gaathaon me Madho Singh Bhandari, Rajula-Malushahi aur Tilu Rauteli prasiddh hain.

Haridwar ki lok-kathayein (manyata ke anusar):

  • Bhartrihari aur Vikramaditya: Manyata hai ki raja Bhartrihari ne tyag kar Haridwar ke aas-paas tapasya ki, aur unke bhai Vikramaditya ne unki smriti me ghat banwaya, jo aage chalkar Har Ki Pauri kehlaya.
  • Bhimgoda: Lok-katha me Pandav Bhim dwara jameen par ghutna (goda) marne se jal ki dhara phooti; isi se Bhimgoda naam juda bataya jata hai. Aaj yahan Ganga nahar ka udgam hai.
  • Daksh yagna (Kankhal): Pauranik katha ke anusar Daksh Prajapati ke yagya me Sati ke aatm-daah ke baad Bhagwan Shiv ke gan Veerbhadra ne yagya ko dhwast kiya aur Daksh ko dand mila. Ye katha Kankhal ke Daksh Mahadev mandir se judi hai.
  • Ganga ka avtaran: Bhagirath ki tapasya aur Shiv ki jataon ki katha (Haridwar: Puranon ka Bhugol: Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand).

Gangajal ka rahasya: Kai log maante hain ki Gangajal lambe samay tak kharab nahi hota. Is par vaigyanik adhyayan alag-alag niskarsh dete hain (kuch me jeevaanu-rodhi gun ki charcha hai, kuch me pradushan ka asar dikhta hai).
