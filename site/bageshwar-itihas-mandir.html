Is hisse mein: Bagnath ki kahani (Markandeya, Shiva ka baagh roop, Saryu ka pravah), Baijnath ke 18 Katyuri mandir, Kot Bhramari, Kanda ke Nag aur Kali mandir, Kumaon ke devta aur Jagar, Katyuri se Chand, Gorkha aur British kaal tak ka itihas, 1921 ka Coolie-Begar andolan aur Bageshwar ki lok-kathayein.

Zaroori suchna: Dharmik kathayein aastha ki baatein hain aur itihas pramanon par chalta hai. Is hisse mein dono alag-alag likhe gaye hain: "Manyata" aur "Itihas". Jahan sources me tithi ya naam ka antar mila (jaise Bagnath mandir ke nirmaan ka saal), wahan dono saal diye gaye hain.

Bageshwar: Puranon ka Bhugol: Manaskhand

Bageshwar ka sambandh Skanda Purana ke Manaskhand (Kumaon ka puranic khand) se jodkar dekha jata hai. Bagnath mandir ka mahatva Skanda Purana me bataya jata hai, aur Markandeya rishi, Vasishtha rishi aur Saryu nadi ki katha isi parampara se judi hai.

Manaskhand kya hai? Skanda Purana ke ek hisse ko Kedarkhand (Garhwal) aur Manaskhand (Kumaon) me baanta jata hai. Manaskhand ka naam Mansarovar (Manas) se jodkar liya jata hai.

Saryu aur Markandeya ki katha (manyata)

Lok-manyata ke anusar Brahma ne Vasishtha rishi ko Saryu nadi ko neeche lane ko kaha. Raste me Markandeya rishi tapasya kar rahe the, jinke kaaran Saryu aage nahi badh paayi. Vasishtha ne Shiva se prarthna ki, aur Shiva ne baagh (tiger) ka roop dhar kar yeh samasya suljhayi.

Katha ka sakshipt rup: 1. Saryu Brahma ke aadesh se dharti par aa rahi thi. 2. Markandeya rishi us marg par tapasya me leen the, isliye pravah ruk gaya aur jal ikattha hone laga. 3. Vasishtha ne Shiva se madad maangi. 4. Shiva (aur kuch roopon me Parvati bhi) ne baagh ka roop liya aur ek gaay ke paas pahunche. 5. Gaay ko bachane ke liye Markandeya ki tapasya tooti aur wo uth gaye. 6. Isse Saryu ka pravah chal pada, aur Shiva ne Markandeya ko darshan diye. 7. Is sthan ko "Vyaghreshwar" kaha gaya, jo samay ke saath "Bageshwar" ho gaya.

Dhyan dein: Is katha ke alag-alag roop alag-alag sources me milte hain. Upar ka sar kai sthaniya aur online sources se milaakar likha gaya hai.

Baijnath aur Shiva-Parvati vivah ki manyata

Kai sources ke anusar Baijnath ke paas Gomti aur Garur Ganga ke sangam par Shiva-Parvati ka vivah hone ki manyata hai. Isi se Baijnath ko "Vaidyanath" (vaidyon ke swami) kaha jata hai. Yeh manyata hai, aitihasik tathya nahi.

Bageshwar: Pramukh Mandir, Dham, Gurudwara aur Math

Bageshwar zile ke sabse prasiddh mandir Bagnath (Bageshwar nagar), Baijnath ke 18 Katyuri mandir, Kot Bhramari Devi, Chandika, Kanda ke Kalika-Bhadrakali, Dholinag aur Pheninag hain. Mandiron me Maha Shivratri aur Uttarayani par sabse zyada bheed hoti hai.

Bagnath, Baijnath, Kot Bhramari, Chandika, Kanda & Nag Temples

Bagnath Mandir (Bageshwar nagar)

Bagnath mandir Saryu aur Gomti ke sangam par bana Shiva ka prachin mandir hai. Shiva yahan "Bagnath" ya "Vyaghreshwar" (baagh ke swami) ke roop me poojey jaate hain, aur isi mandir ke naam par nagar ka naam Bageshwar pada.

Bageshwar: Isht Devta, Gram Devta aur Jagar mein Devta Avtaran

Kumaon me Golu Devta (nyay ke devta), Nanda Devi, Bholanath, Haru-Sain, Bhumiyal aur Nag devta bahut maane jaate hain. Bageshwar kshetra me Nag devta (Dholinag, Pheninag) aur Kali/Bhadrakali ki pooja khaas taur par prasiddh hai.

Gram devta ka mahatva: Kumaoni gaon me har gaon ka apna ishta/gram devta hota hai, jinka chhota thaan (patthar ka mandir) gaon ke upar ya khet ke kinare hota hai. Kul-devta aur Pitra thaan ki jaankari Bageshwar: Pitra Puja, Shraddha aur Kutumb Kul-Devta Parampara me hai.

Jagar, Jagariya aur Dangariya: Kaise Hota Hai

Jagar me jagariya (gaayak) dhol-damau ya hurke ki thaap par devta ki gaatha gaata hai aur dangariya (madhyam) par devta ke "aane" ki manyata hoti hai. Dangariya poochhne wale ke sawalon ke jawab deta hai, aisa maana jata hai.

Jagar ke aam charan: 1. Taiyari: Ghar ya mandir ke aangan me sthaan banta hai, aur aam taur par Aipan banta hai. 2. Aahvan: Jagariya devta ka naam leke gaatha shuru karta hai. 3. Avatar: Manyata hai ki dangariya par devta aate hain, aur wo kaampta ya jhumta hai. 4. Prashn-uttar: Parivaar apni samasyayein pooch sakta hai, aur jawab devta ke roop me diye jaate hain. 5. Samapan: Aarti, bhog aur prasad ke saath.

Sammaan ke saath: Jagar gahri aastha ki parampara hai. Sirf parampara ka vivaran hai. Bimari ya sankat me hamesha doctor se bhi salah lein.

Bageshwar: Kuninda aur Katyuri Raajvansh ka Daur

Bageshwar ka sabse puraana likhit itihas Katyuri kaal se milta hai. Baijnath (Kartikeyapura) Katyuri rajaon ki rajdhani rahi, aur Bageshwar ke Bagnath mandir me ek purana Sanskrit shilalekh hai jo Katyuri vansh ke naam batata hai.

Kuninda Sikke, Katyuri Rajdhaniyan, Shilalekh aur Pathar ke Mandir

Kuninda (pehle ka daur)

Kuninda ek prachin Himalayi gana-rajya tha, jiske sikke Himalaya ke kai kshetron me mile hain. Bageshwar zile se Kuninda sikke milne ka koi verified, sthal-wise record abhi nahi mila.

Bageshwar shilalekh: Bageshwar ke Bagnath mandir parisar me ek purana Sanskrit shilalekh hai, jise itihaskar Katyuri raja Bhudev se jodte hain. Pandukeshwar (Chamoli) ke Lalitasuradeva ke tamrapatra aur Bageshwar ke shilalekh milkar Katyuri vansh (Nimbara, Ishtaganadeva, Lalitasuradeva, Bhudev) ki jaankari dete hain. Ullekh sources me naam ki spelling alag-alag milti hai.

Pandukeshwar Chamoli zile me hai, Bageshwar me nahi. Bageshwar ka mukhya praman patthar ka shilalekh hai.

Katyuri raja Bhudev ke baare me kya kaha jata hai?
Kuch sources ke anusar Bhudev ne Bageshwar shilalekh me apne ko Brahman-bhakt aur Bauddh-virodhi roop me likha, aisa ullekh milta hai. Isse us samay ke dharmik badlav ki jhalak milti hai.

Katyuri mandir aur Baijnath: Mandir ki jaankari Bageshwar: Pramukh Mandir, Dham, Gurudwara aur Math me hai.

Katyuri raja kahan ke the?
Katyur ghati (Baijnath-Garur kshetra) unki rajdhani thi.
Kartikeyapura kya hai?
Baijnath ka prachin naam.
Katyuri vansh kab tak raha?
Mote taur par 13vin sadi tak, uske baad kai chhote rajya bane.

Bageshwar: Panwar (Garhwal) & Chand (Kumaon) Kingdoms

Bageshwar Kumaon ke Chand rajya ka hissa raha, jiski rajdhani aakhir me Almora thi. Bagnath mandir ke vartaman roop ka shrey Chand raja Laxmi Chand ko diya jata hai.

Chand Raja, Katyur-Danpur Pargana, Garhwal-Kumaon Yuddh aur Goonth Zameen

Sahi sandarbh: Garhwal ke 52 garh (Panwar rajya) ki baat hai. Bageshwar Kumaon ka zila hai, isliye yahan "52 garh" ki jagah Kumaon ke pargana aur Chand shasan ki jaankari di gayi hai.

Goonth bhumi (mandir ko diya gaya zameen ka daan): Chand aur Panwar rajaon ne mandiron ko gaon, zameen ya aay ka daan diya, jise goonth kehte hain. Isse mandiron ka kharcha chalta tha.

Katyur ki jagah Chand: 13vin sadi ke baad Katyuri rajya toot gaya, aur Chand rajya ne dheere-dheere Kumaon ko ek kiya. Baijnath ke Katyuri vanshaj kuch samay tak alag rahe, jo kai sources ke anusar 1565 ke aas-paas Chand rajya me mil gaye.

Bageshwar: Gorkha Raaj (Gorkhyani) aur Angrez-Nepal Yuddh

Gorkha shasan Kumaon me 1790 se 1815 tak raha. Is daur ko "Gorkhyani" kaha jata hai aur sthaniya logon ke liye yeh kathin samay maana jata hai. 1815 ki Sugauli sandhi ke baad Kumaon British shasan me aa gaya.

Gorkhyani ka arth: Kumaon me Gorkha shasan ko kathor kar-vyavastha, begar aur dar ke daur ke roop me yaad kiya jata hai. Isi se "Gorkhyani" shabd pahadi lok-smriti me aaya.

Sugauli sandhi ka asar: Is sandhi ke baad Kumaon aur Garhwal ka bada hissa British ko mila aur Nepal ki seemayein badal gayin. Tehri Garhwal alag riyasat ke roop me bacha. Kumaon ka British prashasan Bageshwar: Angrezi Kumaon Commissionery aur Zameen Bandobast me hai.

Bageshwar: Angrezi Kumaon Commissionery aur Zameen Bandobast

1815 ke baad Bageshwar British Kumaon ka hissa bana. British kaal me zameen ka bandobast (settlement), van niyam aur paidal marg bane, aur Kausani jaise sthal Gandhi ji ke aagman se prasiddh hue.

Bageshwar aur British: British kaal me Bageshwar Almora zile ka hissa tha, aur zila alag 1997 me bana (Bageshwar: Saar aur Zile ki Pehchan). Kausani me chai ke bagan aur sanatorium jaise vikas ka itihas Bageshwar: Chhavni Kasbe, Angrezi Kabristan aur Girjaghar me hai.

Kausani aur Gandhi ji: Mahatma Gandhi ne 1929 ki Kumaon yatra me Kausani me kuch din bitaye, aur aisa maana jata hai ki yahan unhone Gita par "Anasakti Yoga" ki bhumika likhi. Kausani ke Anasakti Ashram ka zikr Bageshwar ki 20 Sabse Zaroori Ghumne ki Jagah me aayega.

Paidal marg aur dak bungalow: British kaal me bane paidal raste aur rest houses ki Bageshwar-wise suchi Bageshwar: Dak Bungalow, Van Vishram Grih aur Angrezi Daur ki Imaratein me hai.

Bageshwar: Azaadi ki Ladai, Kuli Begar aur Rajya Andolan

Bageshwar bharat ke swatantrata sangram me 1921 ke Coolie-Begar andolan ke liye jaana jata hai. 13 se 14 January 1921 ko Uttarayani mele ke din Saryu ke sangam par hazaron logon ne begar ke register nadi me baha diye.

Kuli Begar Ant (1921), Kumaon Parishad aur Doosre Andolan

Coolie-Begar kya tha?

Coolie-Begar British kaal ki wo pratha thi jisme gaon ke log bina majduri ke angrezi adhikariyon ka saman dhote the. Utar, Begar aur Burdayash isi ke roop the. Gaon ke pradhan ke paas "coolie register" me naam likhe hote the.

Pratigya ka bhaav: Kai sources ke anusar Badri Datt Pandey ne Saryu ke jal aur Bagnath mandir ko sakshi maan kar pratigya li ki log ab begar nahi karenge.

Kitne log the? Alag-alag sources me 10,000 se 40,000 tak ki bheed ka ullekh milta hai.

Gandhi ji ne ise kya kaha?
Kai sources ke anusar Gandhi ji ne ise "raktheen kranti" (bloodless revolution) kaha tha (Young India reference).
Badri Datt Pandey kaun the?
Almora ke patrakar aur netaa, jinhone "Almora Akhbar" aur "Shakti" jaise patron me begar ke khilaaf likha. Unki kitab "Kumaon ka Itihas" Kumaon ke itihas ka mahatvapurn granth hai. Wo 1955 me Almora se sansad bhi bane.

Aaj uttarayani ka mahatva: Isi ghatna ke karan Bageshwar ke Uttarayani mele ko sirf dharmik nahi balki rajneetik jagrukta ka bhi pratik maana jata hai. 2021 me is andolan ki 100vin saalgirah par smaaran aayojan hue.

Coolie Begar andolan kahan hua?
Bageshwar me, Saryu-Gomti ke sangam par.

Kab hua? January 1921, Uttarayani mele ke aas-paas.

Kumaon Kesari kise kaha jata hai?
Badri Datt Pandey ko.

Anya andolan (sahi sandarbh)

Ye ghatnayein Bageshwar ki nahi hain.

Bageshwar: Lok-Kathayein: Achhari, Veer Yoddha aur Rahasyamay Kisse
