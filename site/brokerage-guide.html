Share Trading Ke Charges — Poori Samajh (Intraday vs Delivery)

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026

🔄 Aakhri update: July 2026 · Government yojana, tax slab aur charges samay-samay par badalte rehte hain. Bahut naye traders sochte hain ki share ₹100 me khareeda, ₹105 me becha, to ₹5 ka poora profit mila. Par asli me kai chhote-chhote charges kat jaate hain, aur kabhi-kabhi to profit se zyada charges ban jaate hain (khaas kar chhote trade me). Is guide me hum har charge ko aasaan bhasha me samjhenge, aur neeche free calculator se aap apna asli net profit dekh sakte hain.

Trading par kaunse-kaunse charges lagte hain?

ChargeKya hai
BrokerageBroker ki fees. Discount broker (Zerodha, Upstox) me delivery aksar free, intraday par ~₹20 ya 0.03% (jo kam ho).
STTSecurities Transaction Tax — sarkar ka tax. Delivery me buy+sell dono par, intraday me sirf sell par.
Exchange chargeNSE/BSE ki fees (bahut chhoti, turnover par).
GSTBrokerage + exchange charge par 18%.
SEBI chargeRegulator ki namaatra fees (₹10 per crore).
Stamp dutySarkar ka, sirf buy side par.

Intraday aur Delivery me farak

Delivery (share ko kuch din/mahine rakhna) me STT dono taraf lagta (0.1%), par discount broker me brokerage aksar zero. Intraday (same din khareed-bech) me brokerage lagta (₹20 type), par STT sirf sell par (0.025%). Isliye chhote intraday trade me charges profit ka bada hissa kha sakte hain.

Asli baat: Chhote amount me baar-baar intraday karna — charges ki wajah se — aksar ghaate ka sauda hota hai. Calculator me apna trade daal kar dekhein ki net me kitna bacha.

Charges kaise kam karein?

  • Discount broker use karein (flat fees).
  • Kam par zyada trade (overtrading) se bachein — har trade par charges.
  • Delivery me discount broker par brokerage zero hota — long-term ke liye faydemand.

FAQ

STT kya wapas milta hai?

Nahi, STT ek tax hai jo wapas nahi milta. Ye har eligible trade par sarkar leti hai.

Charges profit se minus hote hain kya ITR me?

Haan, trading business me ye charges kharch (expense) maane jaate hain aur profit se minus hote hain. Detail ke liye ITR-3 guide dekhein.

Calculator bilkul sahi number deta hai?

Ye discount-broker rates ka achha anuman deta hai. Aapke broker ki exact fees thodi alag ho sakti — final contract note se milaayein.

Chhote saude mehnge kyun padte hain?

Kyunki kuch charge tay rakam ke hote hain, jaise DP charge. ₹2,000 ke sauda me wahi ₹15-20 ek percent se zyada ban jaate hain, jabki ₹50,000 ke sauda me wo lagbhag kuch nahi.

Delivery aur intraday ke charge me kya farak hai?

Delivery me STT kharidne aur bechne dono par lagta hai aur bechne par DP charge bhi. Intraday me brokerage lagti hai par STT sirf bechne par — saude zyada hone se kul charge phir bhi jama ho jaate hain.

Charges kahan-kahan lagte hain

Trade karte waqt sirf brokerage nahi katti — kai chhote-chhote charge lagte hain jo milkar bade ho jaate hain. Aam taur par ye lagte hain:

  • Brokerage — broker ki fees. Discount broker aam taur par flat rakam leta hai, purane full-service broker percentage.
  • STT (Securities Transaction Tax) — sarkar ka tax. Intraday aur delivery ke liye alag dar.
  • Exchange charges — NSE/BSE ka apna shulk.
  • GST — brokerage aur exchange charges par lagta hai.
  • SEBI charges aur stamp duty — chhoti rakam, par har trade par.
  • DP charges — sirf tab jab aap delivery wale share bechte hain. Ye per-trade lagta hai, per-share nahi.

Chhoti trade me charges kyun bhaari padte hain

Kuch charge fixed hote hain — chahe aap ₹2,000 ka trade karein ya ₹2 lakh ka. Isliye chhoti rakam par ye percentage me bahut bade lagte hain. Misaal ke liye, ₹2,000 ke sauda par ₹40-50 charges ka matlab hai ki aapka trade 2-2.5% ghaate se shuru ho raha hai. Munafa banane ke liye pehle itna to kamana hi padega.

Isi liye: bahut chhoti rakam ke bar-bar trade karna nuksan ka sauda hai. Broker ka business chalta hai, aapka nahi.

Delivery aur intraday me farak

Delivery me share aapke demat me aate hain aur aap jab chahein bech sakte hain. Intraday me usi din kharid-bech poora karna hota hai. Intraday par brokerage aam taur par kam hoti hai aur STT bhi kam, par DP charge bechne par lagta hai (delivery me), intraday me nahi. Doosri taraf intraday me leverage milta hai — aur wahi sabse bada khatra hai, kyunki nuksan bhi utna hi bada hota hai.

Breakeven — asli number

Trade karne se pehle ye sochein ki share ko kitna badhna chahiye ki aap sirf barabar par aayein. Charges dono taraf lagte hain — kharidte waqt bhi aur bechte waqt bhi. Aam taur par chhoti rakam par ye 1-2% ho jaata hai, badi rakam par 0.1-0.3%. Ye number jaane bina target lagana andhere me teer chalane jaisa hai.

Charges kam karne ke tarike

  • Discount broker chunein — flat fees percentage se aam taur par sasti padti hai
  • Bar-bar trade kam karein — har trade par charges lagte hain
  • Chhoti rakam ke trade se bachein — fixed charges bhaari pad jaate hain
  • Contract note roz padhein — usme har charge alag likha hota hai; log ise kabhi nahi kholte
