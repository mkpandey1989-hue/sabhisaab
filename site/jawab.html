Kaun se sawal aate hain

Dimag hilane wali paheliyan, ulte-pulte shabd aur muhavre, purane zamaane ke naap aur sikke, shuddh Hindi naam, sudoku, sankhya series, coding-decoding, paise aur discount ke ganit, aur Uttarakhand ki GK. Sawal family ke saath dekhne layak hote hain aur kisi ko gumraah karne wale aankde nahi hote.

Paheli jaldi hal karne ke 5 aasaan tareeke

1. Sawal do baar padhein. Zyaadatar galti jaldbaazi mein hoti hai. Sawal mein chhupa shabd jaise "pehle", "baad mein", "kul" ya "bacha hua" poora matlab badal deta hai.

2. Vikalp se ulta chalein. Chaaron vikalp ko sawal mein rakh kar dekhein. Jo vikalp sharton ko poora na kare, use hata dein. Aksar do vikalp turant hat jaate hain aur faisla aasaan ho jaata hai.

3. Chhote number se aazmaayein. Discount, byaj ya percentage ke sawal mein pehle 100 rupaye maan kar hisaab lagaayein. Pattern samajh aate hi asli number par wahi niyam lagaayein.

4. Series mein farak dekhein. Lagataar do sankhyaon ka farak, phir farak ka farak dekhein. Gunaa, varg (square) aur pichhli do sankhyaon ka jod sabse aam pattern hain.

5. Shabd wale sawal mein jad (root) dhoondhein. Shabd ko tod kar dekhein: "anu" matlab baad mein, "agra" matlab aage, "a" ya "an" matlab nahi. Isse anjaan shabd ka arth bhi andaaze se sahi nikal aata hai.
