Fasal Munafa Calculator

Kheti se asal me kitna munafa hua — ye pata karna zaroori hai, par log aksar sirf bikri dekh kar khush ho jaate hain, kharcha bhool jaate hain. Ye calculator saara kharcha (beej, khaad, majdoori, sinchai) aur bikri lekar aapka net munafa aur per bigha fayda batata hai.

Asli munafa kaise nikaalein

Kheti ka munafa sirf "kitne me becha" nahi hai. Asli munafa = bikri − saara kharcha. Aur kharcha me sab kuch jodna zaroori hai — beej, khaad, dawa, majdoori, sinchai, diesel, aur khud ki mehnat bhi.

Net munafa = Kul bikri − (beej + khaad + majdoori + sinchai + baaki)
Per bigha munafa = Net munafa ÷ kitne bigha

Ek misaal

2 bigha me fasal bechi ₹80,000 me. Kharcha: beej ₹5,000, khaad ₹12,000, majdoori ₹15,000, sinchai ₹8,000 = ₹40,000. Net munafa = 80,000 − 40,000 = ₹40,000. Per bigha = ₹20,000. Munafa 50%. Ab pata chalta hai ki asal me kitna baccha.

Jo kharche log bhool jaate hain

  • Apni mehnat — agar aap khud kaam karte hain, to uski keemat bhi jodein (nahi to munafa zyada dikhta hai).
  • Diesel/bijli — pump, tractor, thresher ka kharcha.
  • Transport — mandi tak fasal le jaane ka.
  • Bhandaran — agar fasal rakhi to uska kharcha aur nuksan.
  • Bija/khaad udhaar par liya to uska byaj bhi.

Munafa badhane ke tareeke

Ye hisaab dekh kar pata chalta hai ki kharche me kahan bachat ho sakti hai. Jaise — khaad-dawa sahi maatra me (zyada nahi), majdoori ki jagah kuch kaam khud, ya fasal ka sahi samay par sahi mandi me bechna. Fasal ke liye loan chahiye to Kisan Credit Card sasta padta hai. Zameen ka maap nikaalna ho to Nali-Mutthi ya Hectare-Bigha-Acre converter dekhein.

Apni mazdoori ko kharche me jodna kyun zaroori hai

Ye wo ek baat hai jo kheti ke hisaab ko sabse zyada dhokha deti hai. Kisan apna aur ghar walon ka shram kharche me nahi jodta, isliye har fasal munafe wali dikhti hai.

Par sochiye — agar wahi din aap kisi aur ke khet me ya kisi aur kaam me lagate, to kuch to milta. Us chhoote hue kamai ko hi arthashastra me laagat maana jaata hai.

Aasaan tarika: fasal me lage kul din giniye (bijai, nirai, sinchai, katai, mandi tak le jaana) aur apne ilaake ki mazdoori dar se guna kar dijiye. Yahi number is calculator ke "kul laagat" wale box me jodiye. Ab jo munafa dikhega, wo asli hai.

Bahut baar is hisaab ke baad pata chalta hai ki fasal ne sirf mazdoori nikaali, munafa kuch nahi. Ye nirash karne wali baat nahi hai — ye faisla lene ka aadhaar hai. Isi hisaab se pata chalta hai ki kaunsi fasal chhodni hai aur kis par mehnat badhani hai.

Pahadi kheti ke woh kharche jo maidan me nahi hote

Pahad me kheti ka hisaab maidan se alag baithta hai, aur ye antar aksar poore munafe ko kha jaata hai:

  • Dhulai — khet se sadak tak fasal aksar sar par ya khachar par jaati hai. Ye alag kharcha hai jo maidan me lagbhag hota hi nahi.
  • Bikhre hue tukde — ek parivaar ke khet kai jagah hote hain. Har tukde tak jaane-aane ka samay khud ek badi laagat hai.
  • Bandar aur jangli janwar — rakhwali me raat-raat bhar jagna padta hai. Ye samay bhi mehnat hai, aur nuksan alag se.
  • Mandi ki doori — sthaaniya bhaav aur mandi bhaav me bada antar hota hai. Mandi tak le jaane ka bhada aur samay jodne ke baad kai baar sthaaniya bech dena hi behtar nikalta hai.
  • Sinchai barish par nirbhar — ek saal achha, doosra saal kuch nahi. Isliye ek saal ke munafe par lambi yojana mat banaiye — do-teen saal ka ausat dekhiye.

Isi liye pahad me kai kisan ab nakdi fasal aur baagwani ki taraf ja rahe hain — kam rakbe me zyada mulya. Chhote rakbe ka hisaab nikaalne ke liye Nali-Mutthi Converter se rakba tay kar lijiye, phir yahan bhariye.

Sarkari yojana aur kisan pehchaan ke liye Farmer ID (UKFR) aur Kisan Credit Card wale lekh isi site par hain — sasta karz hi wo ek cheez hai jo kheti ka munafa sabse zyada badhati hai.

Kisan aksar sirf beej aur khaad ginta hai. Poori list ye hai:

Apni mehnat ko kharche me na ginna sabse badi galti hai. Agar aap 20 din khud kaam karte hain aur wahi din mazdoori par jaakar kama sakte the, to wo asli kharcha hai. Ise jode bina "munafa" hamesha zyada dikhta hai, aur agli fasal ka faisla galat ho jaata hai. Doosri baat — mandi ka bhaav bechne ke din ka lijiye, sunne wale bhaav ka nahi. Aur adat, tulai aur bhaadey ke baad haath me jo aata hai, wahi asli daam hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kheti ka munafa kaise nikaalein?

Kul bikri me se saara kharcha (beej, khaad, majdoori, sinchai, transport) ghata dein. Jo bacha wo net munafa. Use bigha se baant kar per bigha fayda milta hai.

Kharche me kya-kya jodna chahiye?

Beej, khaad, dawa, majdoori, sinchai, diesel/bijli, transport, bhandaran, aur udhaar liya to byaj. Apni mehnat bhi jodein to asli tasveer milti hai.

Per bigha munafa kyun dekhein?

Isse pata chalta hai ki har bigha kitna de raha hai, aur agli baar kaunsi fasal ya kitni zameen me kya lagana behtar hoga.

Munafa kam ho to kya karein?

Kharche me bachat dekhein (sahi maatra me khaad-dawa), sahi mandi aur samay par bechein, aur zyada munafe wali fasal par vichaar karein.

Apni mehnat ko bhi laagat me jodna chahiye?

Haan. Fasal me lage kul din ginkar apne ilaake ki mazdoori dar se guna kariye aur kul laagat me jodiye. Bina iske har fasal munafe wali dikhti hai.

Ek saal ke munafe par bharosa kar sakte hain?

Nahi. Barish, bhaav aur nuksan har saal badalte hain. Do-teen saal ka ausat dekhkar hi kisi fasal ke baare me faisla lena chahiye.
