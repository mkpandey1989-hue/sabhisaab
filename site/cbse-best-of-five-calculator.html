Hisaab ke peeche ka tarika

CBSE ka niyam: paanch main subject ke marks percentage me ginte hain. Agar chhata subject (aksar 6th additional) me marks zyada hain, to wo kam wale ki jagah le leta hai. Isliye "best of 5" — 6 me se top 5 marks ka औसत.

Formula: Percentage = (top 5 subject ke marks ka jod ÷ (5 × max marks)) × 100
Sabse kam number wala 1 subject apne aap chhoot jaata hai.

Ye tool aapke 6 number me se sabse chhota apne aap hata deta hai aur baaki 5 ka percentage nikaalta hai — isse aksar percentage thoda badh jaata hai.

Number ke saath 3 udaharan

Misaal 1 — 6th subject bacha gaya. Marks: 88, 76, 91, 82, 69, 94. Sabse kam 69 chhoot gaya. Top 5 = 88+76+91+82+94 = 431. Percentage = 431/500 × 100 = 86.2%.

Misaal 2 — additional subject kaam aaya. Agar aapka additional (jaise IT/painting) me 95 hai aur main subject me 65, to 65 chhoot jaata hai aur 95 gin liya jaata hai — percentage upar.

Misaal 3 — sab barabar. Agar sab subject me 80 hai, to koi bhi ek chhode, percentage 80% hi rahega.

Ye galtiyan aksar hoti hain

  1. Saare 6 subject ka औसत nikaalna. CBSE 5 subject ka percentage deta hai, 6 ka nahi. 6th sirf backup ke liye hai.
  2. Additional subject ko ginna bhoolna. Agar 6th (additional) me marks zyada hain to wahi ginte hain — isse percentage badhta hai.
  3. Language dono ko main maanna. Niyam thoda vistrit hai; aam taur par 1 language + best 4 chalte hain. School se confirm karein.
  4. Practical marks alag ginna. CBSE me theory+practical ka combined marks hi ginte hain, alag nahi.
  5. Grace marks jodna. Board ke declared marks hi lein — grace pehle se juda hota hai.

India me ye dhyaan rakhein

  • Class 10/12 dono: Best of 5 aam taur par dono me lagoo hota hai, par college admission apne cutoff subject bhi dekh sakte hain.
  • College cutoff: DU jaise college "best of 4" ya specific subject maangte hain — sirf best-of-5 par bharosa na karein.
  • Additional subject: 6th subject lena faydemand hai — kamzor subject ki jagah le kar percentage bacha leta hai.
  • Official marksheet: Board khud percentage nahi likhta; aap best-of-5 se nikaalte hain. Form me yahi likha jaata hai.

Ek kadam aage

"Best of 5" ka asli faayda un chhaatron ko hota hai jinke ek-do subject kamzor hain par baaki achhe. Maan lijiye kisi ka Maths me 62 hai par Painting (additional) me 95 — to Maths chhoot jaata hai aur percentage kaafi upar chala jaata hai. Isi wajah se samajhdaar chhaatra ek additional subject zaroor lete hain jisme scoring aasan ho, taaki wo "safety net" ban jaaye. Ye ek chhoti si planning hai jo board ke percentage me bada farak la deti hai.

Par ek zaroori chetavni — college admission hamesha "best of 5" nahi maante. Delhi University aur kai professional course "best of 4" ya specific subject (jaise course-related subject) maangte hain. Iska matlab ye ki aapka board percentage kuch aur ho sakta hai aur admission ke liye ginaa jaane wala percentage kuch aur. Isliye jis college me apply karna hai, uska cutoff niyam pehle se padh lena samajhdari hai — sirf best-of-5 ke number par plan mat banaiye.

Best of Five me kaunsa subject chhoot jaata hai

Niyam saaf hai — 6 vishay me se sabse kam number wala ek vishay percentage se bahar ho jaata hai. Par aksar galti chunne me hoti hai.

VishayMarksBest of 5 me
English78haan
Maths65haan
Science72haan
Social Science81haan
Hindi88haan
Computer (6th, additional)59nahi — ye chhoot gaya

Upar ke misaal me best of five ka jod 384 hua, yaani 76.8%. Saare 6 jodne par 73.8% aata. Yaani ek kamzor vishay hatane se lagbhag 3% ka farak pad gaya.

Do baatein jo aksar galat samjhi jaati hain: pehli — ye niyam CBSE ki marksheet par nahi chhapta. Marksheet par saare vishay ke number aate hain; best of five ka hisaab college aur school apne admission ke niyam ke hisaab se lagate hain. Doosri — kai jagah shart hoti hai ki English aur ek core subject ginna zaroori hai, chahe number kam ho. Isliye admission form bharne se pehle us college ka niyam padh lein — sirf calculator ke number par form mat bhariye.

Aapke sawaal, seedhe jawab

Best of 5 ka matlab kya hai?

6 subject me se top 5 marks ka percentage — sabse kam wala ek subject chhoot jaata hai.

Kaunsa subject chhodte hain?

Jismein marks sabse kam hon. Tool khud sabse chhota number hata deta hai.

Kya additional subject ginte hain?

Haan, agar usme marks zyada hain to wo kam wale main subject ki jagah le leta hai.

College admission me chalega?

Aksar haan, par kuch college best-of-4 ya khaas subject maangte hain — cutoff dekhein.

Language subject ka niyam?

Aam taur par 1 language + best 4 subject. Apne school se pushti karein.

Zaroori baat: Best of 5 ka niyam school aur college ke hisaab se thoda alag ho sakta hai. Admission ke liye us college ka apna niyam (best of 4, khaas subject) zaroor dekhein.
