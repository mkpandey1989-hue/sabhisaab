Almora: Saar aur Zile ki Pehchan

Zile ki Pehchan: Ek Nazar Mein

Seedha jawab: Almora Uttarakhand ke Kumaon mandal ka ek pramukh pahadi zila hai, jise “Kumaon ki Sanskritik Nagri” kaha jata hai. Zila mukhyalay Almora shahar hai, jo lagbhag 1,640 meter ki unchai par ek ghode ki naal jaisi ridge par basa hai. Census 2011 me jansankhya 6,22,506 thi.

Almora sirf ek zila nahi, Kumaon ki aatma ka ek bada hissa hai. Yahan ki Bal Mithai aur Singodi se lekar Jageshwar Dham ke mandir samuh, Katarmal ke Surya Mandir, Binsar ke jungle, Kasar Devi ki shanti aur Ranikhet ki cantonment hawa tak, ek hi zile me itihas, adhyatm, prakriti aur fauji parampara saath chalti hain.

Almora kyun khas hai? 12 Pehchan

  • Kumaon ki Chand rajvansh ki pramukh rajdhani rahi (16vi sadi ke ant se).
  • Bal Mithai aur Singodi jaisi geographical pehchan wali mithaiyon ki nagri.
  • Jageshwar Dham: 100 se zyada chote-bade mandiron ka samuh, deodar ke ghane jungle me.
  • Katarmal Surya Mandir: Kumaon ka pramukh Surya Mandir, Katyuri kaal ki shilpa kala.
  • Kasar Devi: shanti, dhyan aur videshi yatriyon ka pasandida thikana.
  • Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary: Himalaya ke 300 degree se zyada nazaare.
  • Ranikhet: cantonment, golf course aur Kumaon Regiment ki sainya parampara.
  • Dwarahat: “mandiron ki nagri”, Dunagiri aur Pandukholi ke saath.
  • Swami Vivekananda, Pt. Govind Ballabh Pant, Pt. Nehru jaise naamon se judi itihasik smritiyan.
  • Kumaoni sanskriti ka kendra: Uday Shankar ka India Culture Centre yahin tha.
  • Hawalbagh, Syalde, Someshwar aur Bhikiyasain jaisi ghatiyon me gaon ki jeevan-shaili.
  • Palayan (migration) ka sabse bada udaharan bhi: 2001 se 2011 me jansankhya me giravat.

Etymology: Almora Naam ki Utpatti

Seedha jawab: Almora naam ke piche sabse prachlit lok-katha “Kilmora” (Almora) naam ki ek jangli ghas/paudhe se judi hai, jo Katarmal Surya Mandir ke bartan chamkane ke kaam aati thi. Isliye shahar ko pehle “Kilmora” aur phir “Almora” kaha gaya. Yeh lok-manyata hai, itihasik praman nahi.

Kisi bhi shahar ke naam ke piche ek se zyada kahaniyan hoti hain, aur Almora bhi isse alag nahi. Ise do hisson me samjhein:

Lok-manyata (Folk Etymology): Kehte hain ki Katarmal ke Surya Mandir ke pujari apne tambe ke bartan saaf karne ke liye ek khatti ghas ka upyog karte the, jise sthaniya bhasha me Kilmora/Almora kaha jata tha. Yeh paudha is ridge par bahutayat me ugta tha. Dheere dheere is jagah ka naam us paudhe ke naam par pad gaya. Isi tarah ke naam Kumaon me kai jagah milte hain, jaise Kilmora ya Kafal jaise phal-paudhon par bane gaon ke naam.

Kya yeh sach hai?
Itihaskaar is lok-katha ko sahi ya galat siddh nahi karte. Itna zaroor hai ki Almora naam ki purani sthaniya pehchan, Chand rajaon ke samay se pehle, “Khagmara Kot” jaise kilon aur Rajapur jaise naamon ke saath bhi jodi jati hai, jiski charcha Almora: Panwar & Chand Kingdoms aur Almora: Purane Garh / Kot, Baoli aur Khandhar Mahal me ki jayegi.

Dhyan dein: Almora ko log aksar “Almond” ya “Alma” se jod dete hain, jo galat hai. Almora ka Almond ya Alma shabd se koi sambandh nahi hai.

Historical Timeline: District Kaise Bana (Parent District & Notification Date)

Seedha jawab: Almora shahar ki neev Chand raja Balo Kalyan Chand ne 1560 ke dashak me rakhi (aam taur par 1568 likha jata hai). 1815 me British ne Kumaon par adhikaar kiya. Baad me Almora se Pithoragarh (1960) aur Bageshwar (1997) alag zile bane, aur 2000 me Uttarakhand rajya bana.

Almora: Collectorate aur Rajasv Prashasan

Zila Mukhyalay, Vikas Bhawan aur Collectorate Kahan Hai

Seedha jawab: Almora zile ka mukhyalay Almora shahar hai, jahan Zilaadhikari (DM) ka Collectorate, Mukhya Vikas Adhikari ka karyalay aur anya zila-stariya vibhag sthit hain. Sarkari kaam ke liye sabse bharosemand soochna Almora zile ki official NIC website par milti hai.

Zila prashasan ka sabse bada adhikari Zilaadhikari (District Magistrate) hota hai, jo Collector ke roop me rajasv ka bhi pramukh hai. DM ke saath Apar Zilaadhikari (ADM), Mukhya Vikas Adhikari (CDO), Zila Vikas Adhikari aur Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) milkar zile ka kaam sambhalte hain.

Sarkari kaam ke liye tips:

  • Tehsil ya Collectorate jaane se pehle karyalay ka samay aur chhutti ka din dekh lein; pahadi zilon me karyalay aksar 10 baje se 5 baje tak khulte hain.
  • Zaroori certificate (nivas, jati, aay) ke liye pehle Apuni Sarkar portal par online avedan karna samay bachata hai (Almora mein E-District / Apuni Sarkar Sevayein).
  • Janata Darshan ya Tehsil Diwas ki tithi (Almora: CM Helpline 1905 aur Zile mein Shikayat Nivaran) par shikayat lekar jane se jaldi sunwai ki sambhavna badhti hai.

Tehsil, Up-Tehsil aur Kanungo Circle ki Poori Suchi

Seedha jawab: Census 2011 me Almora zile ko 9 tehsilon me ginaa gaya tha. Sabhi sources me samaan roop se milne wale naam hain: Almora, Bhikiyasain, Chaukhutia, Dwarahat, Jainti, Ranikhet, Someshwar aur Sult (Salt). Naye tehsil/up-tehsil ki ghoshna hone par sankhya badal sakti hai.

Tehsil rajasv prashasan ki sabse chhoti prabhavi ikai hai. Har tehsil ka mukhiya Tehsildar hota hai, jiske neeche Naib Tehsildar, Kanungo aur Patwari kaam karte hain. Zameen ka naap, khatauni ka rakhrakhav, rajasv vasooli, praman patra aur zameen ke vivad sabse pehle isi star par aate hain.

Zameen ki khatauni me naam badalwana (dakhil-kharij), fasal ki girdawari aur praman patra ki jaanch Patwari ke zariye hi aage badhti hai.

Patwari Chowki (Rajasv Police aur Niyamit Police)

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand ke kai pahadi kshetron me British kaal se “Rajasv Police” pranali chali aa rahi hai, jisme Patwari rajasv ke saath police ka kaam bhi karta hai. Almora ke kuch gramin ilakon me yeh vyavastha rahi hai. 2018 ke baad High Court ke nirdesh par ise dheere-dheere samapt karne ki prakriya chali.

Rajasv police ka vichar 19vi sadi me tab aaya jab pahadon me aparadh kam the aur bade thane banana mehnga tha. Patwari ko zameen ka hisaab rakhne ke saath chhoti-moti kanooni jimmedari bhi di gayi. Samay ke saath aparad ka swaroop badla, aur sadak, vahan aur paryatan badhne se ek hi adhikari par itne kaam ka bojh nyayochit nahi raha.

Aaj ki sthiti kaise samjhein:

  • Kisi gaon me aapko FIR darj karwani ho, to pehle pata karein ki wo ilaka regular thane ke antargat hai ya Patwari chowki ke.
  • Gambhir aparadh ya durghatna me 112 (ERSS) par sampark sabse tez raasta hai.
  • Rajasv police se regular police me badlav ki sthiti zile ke hisaab se badalti rehti hai, isiliye nirnay se pehle Zila Police ya DM karyalay se pushti karein.

Almora: Gaon aur Panchayati Raj Vyavastha

Vikas Khand (Development Blocks) ki Poori Suchi

Seedha jawab: Almora zile me 11 Vikas Khand (blocks) hain: Hawalbagh, Takula, Dhauladevi, Lamgara, Bhaisiyachhana, Dwarahat, Tarikhet, Bhikiyasain, Chaukhutia, Syalde aur Salt (Sult). Gramin vikas ki zyadatar yojnayein isi block star par chalti hain.

Block (Vikas Khand) ka sabse bada adhikari Khand Vikas Adhikari (BDO) hota hai. MGNREGA, Pradhan Mantri Awas, swachh bharat, gramin sadak aur paanch saalana vikas yojnayein block ke zariye gaon tak pahunchti hain.

Zila Panchayat Seats, Kshetra Panchayat & Total Gram Panchayats

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand me teen-stariya panchayati raj lagu hai: Zila Panchayat, Kshetra Panchayat (Block star) aur Gram Panchayat. Almora zile me Gram Panchayaton ki sankhya hazaron ke paas hai, aur chunav har paanch saal me hote hain. Taaza sankhya Panchayati Raj Vibhag se hi pushti karein.

Teen-stariya vyavastha ko aasan bhasha me yun samjhein:

  • Gram Panchayat: Gaon ya kuch gaon milkar. Mukhiya Pradhan hota hai.
  • Kshetra Panchayat: Block star par. Pramukh (Block Pramukh) chuna jata hai.
  • Zila Panchayat: Poore zile ke liye. Adhyaksh chuna jata hai.

Pahadi zilon me ek gaon ki jansankhya kam hone se Gram Panchayat chhote hote hain, aur kai baar ek Gram Panchayat me 3 se 6 gaon bhi aa jate hain. Isse Pradhan ko bahut bade bhaugolik kshetra me kaam karna padta hai, jo pahadi panchayati raj ki sabse badi chunauti hai.

Aabaad Gaon aur Khaali (Bhootiya) Gaon

Seedha jawab: Census 2011 ke anusar Almora zile me lagbhag 2,289 gaon darj the. Palayan ke karan kai gaon aaj aabadi-vihin ya lagbhag khaali ho chuke hain, jinhe “Bhutiya” ya “Ghost Village” kaha jata hai. Almora aur Pauri Garhwal palayan se sabse zyada prabhavit zilon me ginaye jaate hain.

2001 me zile ki jansankhya 6,32,866 thi, jo 2011 me ghat kar 6,22,506 reh gayi. Yani lagbhag 1.6 pratishat ki giravat, jabki rajya ki kul jansankhya badhi thi. Yeh aankda sidha-sidha palayan ki kahani kehta hai.

Palayan ke mukhya karan:

  • Rozgar ki kami aur kheti me jangli janwaron ka nuksan (bandar, suar, tendua).
  • Sadak, aspatal aur school se door gaon.
  • Paani ke strot sookhna.
  • Naukri aur behtar shiksha ke liye mahanagron ki taraf jana.

Ulta palayan (Reverse Migration) aur gaon bachane ki kahaniyan Almora mein Palayan Vapasi ki Safal Kahaniyan me vistar se aayengi.

Almora: Nagar Nikay aur Shahri Prashasan

Nagar Nigam aur Nagar Palika Parishad ki Suchi

Seedha jawab: Almora shahar ab Nagar Nigam hai. 2024 me ise nagar nigam ka darja mila aur 23 January 2025 ko chunav hue. Nagar Nigam me 40 ward hain aur pehle mayor Ajay Verma (BJP) bane. Nagar Nigam Bhawan Almora me sthit hai.

Almora pehle Nagar Palika Parishad tha, jiske 11 ward the (Census 2011). Nagar Nigam banne par shahar ki seema badhi aur ward 40 ho gaye. Nagar Nigam ka matlab hai bade bajat, zyada adhikar aur zyada sarkari anudan, lekin saath me badhi hui jawabdehi bhi.

Nagar Panchayats & Cantonment Boards (Chhavni Parishad)

Seedha jawab: Almora zile me Nagar Nigam ke alawa Dwarahat jaise chote kasbe Nagar Panchayat ke roop me sanchalit hote rahe hain, aur Ranikhet aur Almora shahar ke chhavni kshetra apne-apne Cantonment Board se prashasit hote hain. Cantonment kshetra Raksha Mantralaya ke antargat aata hai, na ki seedhe Nagar Nigam ke.

Cantonment (Chhavni) kshetra me nagarpalika wale niyam nahi chalte. Yahan bhawan nirman, bazaar aur kai sevaon par Cantonment Board aur Sena ke niyam lagu hote hain. Ranikhet is wajah se baki pahadi shahron se alag hai: yahan saaf-safai, hariyali aur anushasan ka alag hi star milta hai.

Ranikhet Cantonment: Ranikhet Kumaon Regiment Centre (KRC) ka mukhyalay hai. Yahan Naga Regimental Centre ki bhi upasthiti hai. Yeh shahar aaj bhi sainya parampara ka garv se pratinidhitva karta hai.

Note: Almora Cantonment aur anya Census-kasbon ka darja samay ke saath badal sakta hai, isiliye taaza sthiti Raksha Sampada vibhag aur Zila Nagar Vikas karyalay se pushti karein.

Almora: Rajneetik aur Chunavi Parichay

Lok Sabha Seat aur Maujooda Saansad

Seedha jawab: Almora zila Almora (SC) Lok Sabha kshetra ka hissa hai, jo Anusuchit Jati ke liye aarakshit hai. Is seat me Almora ke alawa Pithoragarh, Bageshwar aur Champawat zile ke kuch vidhansabha kshetra bhi aate hain. 2024 ke chunav me Ajay Tamta (BJP) is seat se sansad bane.

Almora Lok Sabha seat Kumaon ki kai bhaugolik aur sanskritik ikaiyon ko ek saath jodti hai. Yahan ke matdata sena, rojgar, palayan, sadak aur paryatan jaise mudde sabse zyada uthate hain. Is seat ke antargat 14 vidhansabha kshetra aate hain: Dharchula, Didihat, Pithoragarh, Gangolihat, Kapkot, Bageshwar, Dwarahat, Salt, Ranikhet, Someshwar, Almora, Jageshwar, Lohaghat aur Champawat.

Note: Sansad ya vidhayak ke naam chunav ya upchunav ke baad badal sakte hain.

Almora se Shuru Hue Pramukh Rajnaitik Andolan

Seedha jawab: Almora ka rajneeti aur patrakarita me bada yogdan raha hai. “Almora Akhbar” (1871) Kumaon ke pramukh shuruaati akhbaron me se ek tha. Swatantrata andolan, coolie-begar virodh aur Uttarakhand rajya andolan, teeno me Almora kshetra ki sakriya bhoomika rahi.

  • Almora Akhbar (1871): Buddhi Ballabh Pant dwara shuru, Kumaon me jan-jagran ka shuruaati manch.
  • Pt. Govind Ballabh Pant: Almora kshetra ke Khoont gaon se, baad me United Provinces ke pehle mukhyamantri aur desh ke grih mantri.
  • Swatantrata Sangram: Pt. Jawaharlal Nehru ko 1930 ke dashak me Almora jail me rakha gaya tha.
  • Coolie-Begar Andolan (1921): Uttarayani mele par Saryu-Gomti ke sangam par begar pratha ke virodh me pran liya gaya; yeh kshetra us samay Almora zile me aata tha (Almora: Azaadi ki Ladai, Kuli Begar aur Rajya Andolan).
  • Uttarakhand Rajya Andolan (1990 ka dashak): Almora, Ranikhet aur aas-paas ke kasbon me bade pradarshan hue.

Fauj ki Regiment (Kumaon Regiment), ITBP aur SSB Camp

Seedha jawab: Almora ki sabse badi sainya pehchan Ranikhet me sthit Kumaon Regiment Centre hai. Almora zila kisi antarrashtriya seema par nahi hai, isiliye yahan ITBP ya SSB ki seema-chowkiyan nahi milti. Lekin Kumaon ke hazaron javan is zile se hain.

Yeh baat aksar galat likhi jaati hai, isiliye saaf kar dein: Almora zile ki seema Nepal ya Tibet se nahi lagti. Antarrashtriya seema Pithoragarh, Champawat aur Chamoli jaise zilon me aati hai. Almora ka sainya mahatva uske sainya kendron aur pariwaron ke zariye hai.

Kumaon Regiment ke bare me aasan jankari: Kumaon Regiment Bharatiya Sena ki sabse purani aur sabse zyada sajaye gaye regimentson me se ek hai. Iska Regimental Centre Ranikhet me hai, jahan Kumaon Regiment Museum bhi dekhne layak hai (Almora: Pramukh Mandir, Dham, Gurudwara aur Math aur Almora ki 20 Sabse Zaroori Ghumne ki Jagah me).

Almora: Nyayalay aur Kanooni Vyavastha

Zila Nyayalay, Family Court aur Muft Kanooni Sahayata

Seedha jawab: Almora me Zila evam Satra Nyayalaya sthit hai, jo zile ki sabse badi nyayik ikai hai. Iske alawa Ranikhet, Dwarahat aur Someshwar jaise kasbon me bhi nyayik suvidhayein hain. Muft kanooni madad ke liye Zila Vidhik Seva Pradhikaran (DLSA) se sampark kiya ja sakta hai.

Zameen ke mukadmon ke liye pahadi tehsil me kya hota hai: Zameen ke jyadatar vivad pehle Tehsildar ya SDM court me suljhaye jate hain (rajasv adalat), aur apeel aage Zilaadhikari ya Commissioner Kumaon tak jati hai. Civil kaise bhi mukadmon ke liye zila nyayalay aata hai.

Muft Kanooni Sahayata: Mahila, Anusuchit Jati/Janjati, bachche aur kam aay wale vyaktiyon ko DLSA ke zariye muft vakil milta hai. Iske liye National Legal Services Authority helpline 15100 bhi hai.

Sub-Registrar Daftar (Zameen Registry)

Seedha jawab: Zameen ki registry Sub-Registrar karyalay me hoti hai, jo Almora ke saath-saath Ranikhet, Dwarahat aur Someshwar jaise pramukh tehsil kendron par sthit hote hain. Registry se pehle stamp duty, circle rate aur khatauni ki jaanch zaroor karein.

Registry se pehle 7 zaroori jaanch:

  • Bhulekh portal par khatauni me bechne wale ka naam aur khata sankhya check karein (Bhulekh Uttarakhand: Almora ki Khatauni Online Kaise Dekhein).
  • Zameen “Nap” hai ya “Benap”, yeh saaf karein (Almora: Pahadi Land Classification & Revenue Vocabulary).
  • Gol Khata/Sanjha Khata me sabhi hissedaron ki sahmati hai ya nahi (Almora: Pahadi Land Classification & Revenue Vocabulary).
  • Bahari vyakti ke liye 250 sq. meter ki seema (Almora mein Zameen Khareedne ke Kanoon aur Section 143).
  • Circle rate aur stamp duty ka sahi hisab (Almora: Sampatti Registry, Stamp Duty aur Tehsil ke Circle Rate).
  • Zameen par koi loan ya mukadma to nahi.
  • Sadak, paani aur pahunch ka raasta (rasta-e-aam) ka adhikar.

Almora: Aapda Prabandhan aur Emergency Vyavastha

Aapda Control Room (DDMA) aur Toll-Free Helpline

Seedha jawab: Har zile ki tarah Almora me bhi Zila Aapda Prabandhan Pradhikaran (DDMA) hai, jiska pramukh Zilaadhikari hota hai. Aapda ki sthiti me 1077 (zila control room), 112 aur 108 par sampark kiya ja sakta hai. Barish me sadak band hone ki jankari bhi yahin se milti hai.

Almora Himalaya ke bhukamp kshetra me aata hai aur barsaat me bhuskhalan, sadak band hona aur badal phatne jaisi ghatnayein hoti rehti hain. Jungle ki aag (Vanal) garmi me sabse badi aapda hai (Almora: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh).

Aapda ke samay kya karein (Almora yatra ke liye):

  • Monsoon (Jun-Sep) me yatra karne se pehle sadak ki sthiti check karein.
  • Raat me pahadi sadak par yatra na karein, khaaskar barish me.
  • Mobile network na milne par BSNL ya landline ka vikalp dhoondhein (Almora: Mobile Network aur Internet: Asli Sthiti).
  • Apatkaal ke liye ek paper par sabhi zaroori number rakhein.

SDRF / NDRF ke Thikane, Baadh Aashray aur Emergency Helipad

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand SDRF rajya ki apni aapda pratikriya force hai, jo pahadi zilon me tainat rehti hai. NDRF ki tukdiyan zarurat padne par Dehradun aur Haridwar jaise kendron se pahunchti hain. Heli-ambulance aur emergency landing ke liye chuninda helipad Zila Prashasan ke paas darj hote hain.

Note: SDRF/NDRF ke exact thikane aur helipad ki suchi suraksha karno se samay-samay par badalti hai. Isiliye hum yahan sirf sidhanth batate hain; saamayik sthiti ke liye DDMA Almora se pushti karein.

Almora: Gaadi Registration (RTO) aur Parivahan Code

RTO / ARTO Code, Daftar aur Licence Sevayein

Seedha jawab: Almora zile ke vahanon ka registration code UK-01 hai. Driving licence, vahan registration aur tax jaise kaam Parivahan vibhag ke karyalay (RTO/ARTO) me hote hain. Zyadatar sevayein ab Parivahan portal par online ho gayi hain, isiliye office jaane se pehle online avedan karna behtar hai.

Parivahan seva me kya milta hai:

  • Learner licence aur permanent driving licence
  • Vahan registration aur transfer
  • Fitness aur permit
  • Challan bhugtan

Tip: Uttarakhand me vahan chalane wale bahari log ko pahadi sadak par Almora se Ranikhet, Jageshwar ya Binsar jane se pehle gaadi ki brake, clutch aur tyre ki jaanch zaroor karni chahiye. Pahadi dhalaan par engine brake ka istemal karein.

Pahadi Driving ke Niyam aur Green Cess / Eco-Tax

Seedha jawab: Pahadi sadak par aage wali gaadi ko pehle nikalne dena (upar chadhne wale ko pehle raasta), horn dekar mod par sankaet dena aur raat me high-beam ka sanyam jaise niyam chalte hain. Rajya ke bahari vahanon par green cess ya eco-tax ke niyam samay-samay par badalte hain, isliye pehle official sources se check karein.

Pahadi gaadi chalane ke 10 golden rules:

  • Upar chadhne wali gaadi ko pehle raasta dein.
  • Utarte waqt neutral me gaadi na chalayein; niche ke gear me engine brake use karein.
  • Har mod par horn dein.
  • Barish me dhire chalayein; sadak par malba aa sakta hai.
  • Gaadi ko kabhi bhi dhalaan par bina handbrake khadi na karein.
  • Raat ke 9 baje ke baad lambi pahadi yatra se bachein.
  • Petrol/diesel ka tank aage ke “last petrol pump” se pehle bharwa lein (Almora: Petrol Pump, EV Charging aur Mechanic).
  • Drone, loud horn aur sharab pikar gaadi chalana mana hai.
  • Zaroori document (RC, licence, insurance) saath rakhein.
  • Kisi bhi dhalaan par selfie lene ke liye sadak ke beech me gaadi na rokein.

STD Code aur Jan Seva Kendra (CSC / Apuni Sarkar)

Seedha jawab: Almora aur Someshwar ke landline ke liye STD code 05962 hai. Ranikhet aur Dwarahat jaise kshetron ke liye 05966 prachalit hai. Sarkari certificate ke avedan ke liye Common Service Centre (CSC) aur Apuni Sarkar portal sabse aasan raaste hain.

CSC/Apuni Sarkar se kaun si seva milti hai?

  • Sthayi Niwas (Domicile), Jati aur Aay praman patra
  • Parivar register ki nakal
  • Bijli, paani aur anya bill bhugtan
  • Aadhaar update, PAN aur bank sevayein

Almora ke gaon me CSC aksar gaon ke hi kisi yuva udyami dwara chalaye jate hain, jo ghar baithe seva ke liye pahli kadi hote hain. Details ke liye Almora mein E-District / Apuni Sarkar Sevayein aur Almora: Aadhaar Seva Kendra, Passport Seva aur CSC dekhein.
