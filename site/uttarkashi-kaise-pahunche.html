Uttarkashi tak pahunchna aasan hai, par uske aage Gangotri, Yamunotri, Harsil, Sankri aur seema ke ilaake tak ka safar mausam, sadak aur permit par nirbhar karta hai. Uttarkashi me apna airport aur rail station nahi hai. Zyadatar log Dehradun ya Rishikesh se sadak ke raaste aate hain. Is hisse mein hum hawai, rail, sadak, bus, petrol, hotel-homestay, mobile network, bijli-paani aur permit ki jaankari ek saath de rahe hain.

Dhyan dein: Kai jaankari (kiraya, timing, network, permit) har saal ya mahine badalti hai.

Uttarkashi: Hawai Aur Helicopter Connectivity

Nearest Airport, Airstrip Aur Helipad

Uttarkashi ka sabse nazdeeki chalu airport Jolly Grant Airport (Dehradun) hai, jo Uttarkashi se lagbhag 160 se 165 km door hai. Uttarkashi me koi nagrik (commercial) airport nahi hai.

Helicopter se Char Dham: Char Dham ke liye helicopter packages kai companies bechti hain, aur seva aam taur par Dehradun (Sahastradhara helipad) se shuru hoti hai. Ek source ke anusaar Kharsali helipad se Yamunotri mandir tak aage lagbhag 6 km paidal ya khachchar ka safar hota hai, aur Harsil helipad se Gangotri tak lagbhag 25 km sadak ka safar hota hai. Helicopter se bhi ye "door-to-door" seva nahi hai.

Helicopter booking me savdhani: Sirf sarkari-manjoor aur registered operator se hi booking karein, aur fake website se bachein. Official yatra portal par maanya service ki jaankari milti hai.

Charter ki process (aam jaankari): Charter ke liye pehle Uttarakhand Civil Aviation ya registered operator se baat karein, aur mausam ke karan udaan radd hone ki sambhavna ke liye vikalp rakhein.

Uttarkashi: Sadak, Highway Aur Bridge

Mukhya Rashtriya Rajmarg (NH) Aur Raaste

Uttarkashi ke mukhya raaste NH-34 (Gangotri raasta) aur NH-134 (purana NH-94, Yamunotri raasta) hain. Rishikesh se Chamba aur Dharasu hote hue Uttarkashi aur phir Harsil aur Gangotri jaate hain. Dharasu se ek raasta Barkot aur Hanuman Chatti ki taraf Yamunotri jaata hai.

Raaste ke aam bottleneck:

  • Barish ke samay Dharasu-Barkot, Syanachatti aur Harsil ke aas-paas bhuskhalan se raasta band ho jata hai. July 2026 me Syanachatti me Bailey bridge ki khabar aayi thi, jo Yamunotri raaste par bridge ya sadak ke nuksan se jude vyavdhan ka sanket deti hai.
  • 2025 ki Dharali baadh me Harsil ke paas Gangotri highway ka bada hissa kshatigrast hua tha. Raasta aur yatra kai hafte band rahe, aur baad me yatra phir se shuru hui (September 2025 ki reports ke anusaar).
  • Chaudi karne ke kaam ke karan jagah-jagah rukavat aur ek-tarfa chalne wale samay (gate system) hote hain.

Safar ki salah: Raat me pahad par yatra se bachein, aur shaam hone se pehle apne padav (Uttarkashi, Barkot, Rudraprayag jaise shahar) tak pahunch jayein. Taaza road status ke liye Uttarakhand PWD, Zila Aapda Prabandhan Pradhikaran ya apne hotel se poochein.

BRO aur strategic roads: Seema ke ilaake (Bhaironghati se Nelong) me sadak mukhya roop se seema-suraksha aur sena ke upyog ki hai.

Uttarkashi: Bus, Shared Taxi Aur Local Transit

Bus Aur Taxi

Dehradun, Rishikesh aur Haridwar se Uttarkashi, Barkot, Gangotri aur Yamunotri ke raaste ke liye sarkari aur nijee bus chalti hain. Sankri aur Mori ki taraf ke liye Dehradun se bus aur jeep milti hain.

Pehli subah ki bus / jeep ka samay aur per-seat kiraya: Ye jaankari har mausam aur saal badalti rehti hai. Jaane se pehle bus adde (ISBT / taxi stand) par ya Uttarakhand Parivahan Nigam ki website par poochein.

Char Dham ke liye vahan ka niyam: Char Dham route par chalne wale vahano ko Green Card aur Trip Card jaise kaagaz ki zaroorat hoti hai. Vyavsayik vahano ke liye Transport Department ka green card / trip chart permit zaroori bataya gaya hai, aur nijee vahan ke liye greencard.uk.gov.in par panjikaran ka zikr milta hai. Taaza niyam Transport Department ki website par dekhein.

Local autos aur taxi: Uttarkashi shahar me chhoti doori ke liye taxi aur auto milte hain. Raaste par bhaav-taal se pehle kiraya tay kar lein.

Uttarkashi: Petrol Pump, EV Charging Aur Mechanic

Fuel Aur Gaadi Ki Madad

Uttarkashi shahar me petrol pump aasani se milte hain. Shahar se aage Gangotri ya Har Ki Dun jaise ooche ilaake ki taraf jaane se pehle tank bhar lena sabse surakshit rehta hai.

Salah:

1.Uttarkashi se Gangotri ya Harsil ke liye nikalne se pehle ya Dehradun se Sankri ke liye nikalne se pehle (Mori / Purola tak) tank bhar lein.

2.Extra tyre, jack, puncture-repair kit aur torch saath rakhein.

3.EV gaadi se jaane wale log charging ke liye pehle hi vyavastha confirm karein, kyonki pahad me charging ka network seemit hai.

4.Pahad me gaadi ki servicing (brake, clutch, tyre) yatra se pehle shehar me hi karwa lein.

Sarkari Aur Nijee Rukne Ke Vikalp

Uttarkashi: Homestay, Hostel Aur Ashram

Homestay Yojana, Hostel Aur Dharamshala

Uttarakhand sarkar ki Pandit Deendayal Upadhyay Griha Awas (Homestay) Yojana ke tahat gaon ke gharon ko homestay ke roop me panjikrit karne ki suvidha hai. Isse gaon walon ko aay milti hai aur paryatak ko gaon ka asli anubhav.

  • Homestay ke fayde: Gaon ka khaana (mandua ki roti, kafuli, jhangora ki kheer jaise), local guide, aur gaon walon ko seedhi aay.
  • Registered homestay chunne ka tareeka: Uttarakhand Paryatan Vibhag ki website par panjikrit homestay ki jaankari dekhein, aur booking se pehle phone par kamre aur suvidha ki jaankari lein.
  • Hostel (backpacker): Kai private hostel Uttarkashi aur Sankri jaise sthanon par chalte hain.
  • Ashram aur dharamshala: Mandir samiti aur ashram sasti aur saral vyavastha dete hain, par suvidha seemit hoti hai. Rehne ke niyam (sirf shakahari khaana, samay ki paabandi) ka dhyan rakhein.

Homestay me rehte samay: Gaon ke niyam aur parampara ka samman karein, bijli aur paani ka kam istemal karein, aur kachra wapas le jayein.

Uttarkashi: Mobile Network Aur Broadband

Mobile Network Ki Sachchai

Uttarkashi shahar aur badi sadak ke kasbon me mobile network aam taur par mil jata hai. Oonchi ghatiyon, trek ke raaston (Har Ki Dun, Dayara, Gaumukh, Dodital) aur seema ke ilaakon me network bahut kam ya bilkul nahi hota.

Kaun si company (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL) kis gaon me chalti hai, ye ghati-dar-ghati badalta hai, aur network ki sthiti 4G / 5G ke vistaar ke saath badalti rehti hai.

Trek par jaane wale logon ke liye zaroori salah:

1.Apni yojana kisi ko batakar jayein, aur kab tak wapas aayenge ye bhi.

2.Trek par network hone par bharosa na karein. Offline map aur downloaded dastavez rakhein.

3.Oonche trek ke liye guide ke paas satellite phone ya SOS ki vyavastha poochein.

Fiber Aur Broadband (Workation Ke Liye)

Uttarkashi me workation ya remote kaam karne ke liye pehle ye check karein: fiber ya broadband ki uplabdhta, bijli ka backup, aur network ka asli speed. Kai jagah "wi-fi" ka daava hota hai, par asal me mobile hotspot par chalta hai.

Booking se pehle homestay waale se asli speed test ka screenshot maangein, aur ek backup network (do alag SIM) saath rakhein.

Uttarkashi: Bijli, Solar Aur Sardi Ka Paani

Bijli Ki Sthiti

Uttarakhand me bijli vitaran UPCL (Uttarakhand Power Corporation Limited) karta hai. Pahad me barish, bhuskhalan aur barf ki wajah se bijli kai baar kai ghante ya kai din ke liye chali jaati hai.

Yahan ki aam salah, jo khaaskar rehne ya workation ke liye aane walon ke liye hai:

1.Inverter aur battery: Lambe cut ke liye achhi quality ka inverter aur tubular battery rakhein. Bijli ke upkaran (laptop, router) ke liye pure sine-wave inverter behtar hota hai.

2.Solar backup: Gaon me kuch ghar solar backup rakhte hain. Booking se pehle poochein.

3.Power bank: Phone aur router ke liye chhota power bank saath rakhein.

Sardi Me Paani Ki Sthiti

Sardi me oonche ilaakon (Harsil, Sankri, Gangotri) me paani ke pipe jam jate hain aur barf pighalne tak paani ki dikkat ho sakti hai. Gaon ke log aksar paani ko dhaara, naula ya ubalte tanki se lete hain.

Aam samadhan: Pipe ko dhakkar rakhna, raat ko tap thoda khula rakhna taaki paani bahta rahe, aur garam paani ki alag vyavastha. Sardi me jaane se pehle homestay waale se poochein ki garam paani aur bijli ki kya vyavastha hai.

2026 ke naye niyam (reports ke anusaar): Real-time tracking, medical suvidha (kai ambulance aur helicopter ambulance), vahan ki jaanch aur bade vahano par pratibandh jaise prabandh ki khabrein aayi thi. Yatra band hone par: 2026 me Gangotri 10 November ko aur Yamunotri 11 November ko winter ke liye band hue. Isliye abhi (November ke baad) dham ki yatra sambhav nahi hai, par Mukhba aur Kharsali me darshan hote hain.

Nelong, Gartang Gali Aur Seema Ke Permit

Nelong, Gartang Gali, Gaumukh aur Kedartal jaise sthalon ke liye Gangotri National Park ka permit lagta hai. Seema ke kshetra (Nelong) ke liye Inner Line se jude niyam alag se hote hain, aur kai reports me raat ke thaharne par pratibandh aur videshi paryatakon par rok ka zikr hai.

  • Permit kahan se: Uttarkashi me Gangotri National Park ka karyalay, Bhaironghati ka barrier aur Gangotri ka permit office (kai reports ke anusaar). Kaun sa permit kahan milta hai, ye saal-dar-saal badalta hai. Isliye pehle Gangotri National Park ke karyalay ya zila prashasan se confirm karein.
  • Zaroori kaagaz: Pehchan patra (Aadhaar ya passport), photocopy aur passport size photo (aam taur par).
  • Harsil: Harsil kshetra me videshi paryatakon ke liye inner line ki chhoot ke bare me purani reports milti hain. Niyam badalte rehte hain, isliye taaza sthiti zila prashasan se poochein.

Drone, Photography Aur Seema Ke Niyam

  • Drone: Bina anumati ke drone udaana seema ke ilaakon aur sanrakshit kshetron me kanooni roop se pratibandhit ho sakta hai. Drone na le jayein, ya pehle anumati lein.
  • Sainik thikane: Sena, ITBP ya sainik upkaran ki tasveer na lein.
  • Sanrakshit kshetra: National Park me paudhe, jaanwar ya patthar na uthayein.

Oonchai Ki Bimari (AMS) Se Bachav

AMS (Acute Mountain Sickness) oonchai par kam oxygen ki wajah se hoti hai aur kisi ko bhi ho sakti hai. Sir dard, ulti, chakkar aur thakan iske aam lakshan hain.

1.Dheere chadhein: Ek din me bahut zyada oonchai na badhayein.

2.Paani peete rahein aur sharab ya nashe se bachein.

3.Lakshan hone par aage na badhein. Aaraam karein, aur agar lakshan na ghatein to neeche utrein.

4.Gambhir lakshan (saans lene me dikkat, behoshi, chalne me asantulan) me turant neeche utrein aur doctor / helpline se sampark karein.

5.Dawai: Koi bhi dawai (jaise altitude ki dawai) doctor ki salah ke bina na lein.

6.Beemari wale log (dil, phephde, blood pressure) doctor se jaanch karwaakar hi oonche trek par jayein.

Emergency numbers (aam jaankari): 112 (pure desh ka emergency number) aur 108 (ambulance) aam taur par chalte hain. Zile ke control room aur Uttarakhand ke aapda helpline ke number (jaise 1077 aur 1070) ke baare me DDMA Control Room Aur Helpline Numbers dekhein, aur jaane se pehle unhe official source se confirm karein.
