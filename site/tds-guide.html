TDS Kya Hai? Poori Jaankari

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Kabhi aisa hua hai ki FD ka byaj aapke account me ummeed se kam aaya? Ya salary se koi rakam "TDS" ke naam par kat gayi? Ye TDS hi hai — Tax Deducted at Source. Iska matlab hai ki jo aapko paisa de raha hai, wahi pehle se thoda tax kaat kar sarkar ke paas jama kar deta hai. Is guide me hum samjhenge ki TDS kya hai, kis-kis cheez par katta hai, aur agar zyada kat jaaye to wapas kaise milta hai — sab bilkul aasaan bhasha me.

TDS aakhir hai kya?

Sarkar chahti hai ki tax poore saal thoda-thoda karke aata rahe, ek saath saal ke aakhir me nahi. Isi liye kai payments par jo paisa de raha hai (jaise bank, company, kirayedaar) wo pehle se ek chhota hissa tax ke roop me kaat leta hai aur aapke PAN ke against sarkar ko de deta hai. Ye kati hui rakam aapke Form 26AS aur AIS me dikhti hai, aur ITR bharte waqt aapke total tax me adjust ho jaati hai. Is tarah tax chori kam hoti hai aur sarkar ke paas paisa time par aata rehta hai.

Kis-kis par TDS katta hai?

Aam roz ki zindagi me TDS in cheezon par milta hai:

  • FD ka byaj: Agar ek saal me bank se byaj ek tay limit se zyada ho, to bank TDS kaat leta hai.
  • Salary: Company aapki income ke hisaab se har mahine thoda TDS kaat kar jama karti hai.
  • Kiraya: Agar kiraya badi rakam ka ho, to kirayedaar ko TDS kaatna padta hai.
  • Professional/contractor fees: Freelancer, consultant ya contractor ko payment par TDS katta hai.
  • Lottery/game jeet: Ispar sabse zyada dar se TDS katta hai.
PAN nahi diya to? Agar aapne payment karne wale ko apna PAN nahi diya, to TDS bahut zyada dar (aam taur par 20% ya usse zyada) se kat sakta hai. Isliye jahan zaroori ho, PAN zaroor dein — warna bina wajah zyada rakam kat jaayegi.

TDS aur asli tax me farak

Ye samajhna zaroori hai — TDS koi alag ya extra tax nahi hai. Ye sirf aapke tax ki advance vasooli hai. Saal ke aakhir me jab aap apna asli tax nikalte hain, to jitna TDS pehle kat chuka hai wo ghata diya jaata hai. Agar TDS aapke asli tax se zyada kat gaya, to aapko refund milta hai. Agar kam kata, to baaki tax aapko bharna padta hai. Isliye TDS katne ka matlab ye bilkul nahi ki aapse zyada tax le liya gaya.

Zyada TDS kat gaya to wapas kaise milega?

Bahut se log — khaaskar wo jinki income tax limit se kam hai — pareshaan hote hain ki FD par TDS kyun kata. Aise me do raaste hain:

  1. Form 15G/15H: Agar aapki total income tax ke daayre me nahi aati, to bank ko saal ki shuruaat me Form 15G (senior citizen ke liye 15H) dekar TDS katne se rok sakte hain.
  2. ITR se refund: Agar TDS kat chuka hai, to ITR bharte waqt use claim karein — jitna zyada kata hoga, wo refund ke roop me wapas aa jaayega.

Calculator kaise kaam aata hai?

Sab Hisaab ka TDS calculator aapko ek mota andaaza deta hai:

  1. Rakam daalein jispar TDS katna hai.
  2. Category chunein — FD byaj, kiraya, professional fees waghairah.

Yaad rahe, ye sirf aam dar par based anuman hai. Har section ki apni limit aur shartein hoti hain jo samay-samay par badalti rehti hain, isliye badi rakam par CA se confirm karna behtar hai.

Kuch zaroori tips

Saal me ek baar apna Form 26AS aur AIS zaroor check karein — isme dikhta hai ki aapke PAN par kitna TDS jama hua. Kabhi-kabhi galti se doosre ka TDS aapke naam ya galat PAN par chala jaata hai, isliye milaan karna zaroori hai. FD kai banks me baant kar rakhne se byaj alag-alag dikhta hai par aapki total income to ek hi hoti hai — isliye tax bharte waqt saara byaj jodein. Agar aap freelancer hain to aapke clients TDS kaat kar dete hain, jise aap ITR me claim kar sakte hain aur refund pa sakte hain.

Kul milakar, TDS se ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi. Ye sirf tax vasoolne ka ek tareeka hai, koi nuksan nahi. Bas apna PAN sahi jagah dein, Form 26AS check karte rahein, aur time par ITR bharein — jo zyada kata hoga wo aakhir me wapas mil hi jaayega. Thodi si samajh aur time par filing, bas itna hi kaafi hai.

TDS aur advance tax me farak

Kai log TDS aur advance tax me confuse ho jaate hain. TDS wo hai jo doosra aapki payment se kaat kar jama karta hai — isme aapko kuch karna nahi padta. Advance tax wo hai jo aapko khud saal ke beech me kist-kist me bharna padta hai, khaaskar jab aapki income par TDS poora cover nahi karta (jaise business ya capital gains). Agar aapka saara tax TDS se cover ho raha hai, to alag se advance tax ki zaroorat nahi. Par agar TDS ke baad bhi tax bachta hai aur wo ek tay rakam se zyada hai, to advance tax bharna zaroori ho jaata hai, warna byaj lag sakta hai. Iski jaankari apne CA se zaroor lein.

Kya TDS ek extra tax hai?

Nahi. TDS aapke asli tax ki advance vasooli hai. ITR bharte waqt ye adjust ho jaata hai, aur zyada kata ho to refund milta hai.

FD par TDS kaise rokein?

Agar aapki income tax ke daayre me nahi aati, to bank ko Form 15G (ya senior citizen ke liye 15H) dekar TDS rok sakte hain.

PAN na dene par kya hota hai?

Bina PAN ke TDS bahut zyada dar (aam taur par 20% ya usse zyada) se kat sakta hai. Isliye PAN zaroor dein.

Kata hua TDS kahan dikhta hai?

Aapke Form 26AS aur AIS me, jo incometax.gov.in par login karke dekh sakte hain.
