Almora: Hawai aur Helicopter Seva (UDAN aur Charter)

Nazdeeki Airport, Helipad, UDAN Heli-Seva aur Charter Booking

Seedha jawab: Almora ke sabse nazdeek ka hawai adda Pantnagar Airport (PGH) hai, jo Wikipedia ke anusar Almora se lagbhag 120 se 127 km door hai. 1 October 2025 ko Haldwani-Almora helicopter seva shuru hui, jiska kiraya Rs 2,500 bataya gaya. Almora zile me khud ka koi niyamit vyavsayik hawai adda nahi hai.

Almora ek pahadi shahar hai, isliye yahan seedhi flight nahi aati. Zyadatar log Delhi se Pantnagar tak udkar ya Kathgodam tak train se aate hain, aur baaki safar sadak se hota hai. Helicopter ek naya vikalp hai, jo UDAN jaisi yojanaon ke tahat chalaya ja raha hai.

Pithoragarh-Almora helicopter (Heritage Aviation, Dec 2024 ki report): - 7-seater helicopter, kiraya Rs 2,500 prati yatri - Rojana do udaan, UDAN yojana ke antargat - Report me samay ka ullekh hai (Pithoragarh se 10:00 AM aur 1:40 PM), par report ke samay-suchi me kuch jagah anuchit antar hai. Isliye samay aur din ke liye sirf official booking site dekhein.

Zaroori sachchai: Helicopter seva mausam par bahut nirbhar karti hai. Badal, dhund aur barish me udaan radd ho sakti hai. Isliye is par poora bharosa karke roz ke safar ya pakki connection wali yatra na banayein. Pakka plan sadak ya train ka rakhein.

Charter (nijee udaan): Charter helicopter ke liye Uttarakhand ki nagar vimanan authority (UCADA) ya approved operators se sampark kiya jata hai.

Almora: Railway Station aur Aane Wali Rail Pariyojanayein

Nazdeeki Railway Station, Trainein aur Tanakpur-Bageshwar Rail Line

Seedha jawab: Almora zile me abhi koi railway station nahi hai. Sabse nazdeek ka bada railhead Kathgodam (KGM) hai, jo sadak se Almora se lagbhag 90 km door hai. Tanakpur-Bageshwar nayi rail line ka final survey ho chuka hai, par kaam shuru nahi hua, aur report me Almora ka naam nahi hai.

Kathgodam station: ek nazar me (Wikipedia ke anusar): - Code: KGM, North Eastern Railway ka Izzatnagar mandal - Broad gauge line, platform ki sankhya alag jagah 3 aur 4 likhi hai - Nainital se lagbhag 35 km, Pantnagar Airport se lagbhag 33 km - Kathgodam se Almora ki doori: KMVN ke Dinapani TRH ke page ke anusar Kathgodam se Dinapani 121 km hai aur Dinapani Almora se lagbhag 30 km hai, yaani Kathgodam-Almora lagbhag 90 km

Kathgodam se chalne wali prasiddh gaadiyan (Wikipedia me ullekhit):

Note: Gaadiyon ke naam, samay aur chalne ke din badalte rehte hain. Booking se pehle IRCTC ya Indian Railways ki official site par dekh lein. Wikipedia ke anusar Kathgodam se pehle metre-gauge par chalne wali Kumaon Express aur Nainital Express ko gauge conversion ke baad dobara shuru karne ki baat likhi gayi hai.

Aur railheads: Haldwani (Kathgodam ke bagal ka shahar), Lalkuan aur Ramnagar bhi Kumaon ke railheads hain. Almora ke liye sabse aasan raasta Kathgodam hi hai.

Tanakpur-Bageshwar rail line: PTI ki 31 July 2025 ki report ke anusar lagbhag 170 km lambi is line ka final survey poora ho gaya hai. Centre ne rajya sarkar se formal manzuri maangi hai, aur Mukhyamantri ne mukhya sachiv ko ise tez karne ko kaha hai. Report me station ke naam, kul kharch ya Almora ka zikr nahi hai.

Rishikesh-Karnaprayag rail project: Yeh Garhwal ke liye hai aur Almora se juda nahi hai. Almora ke liye kisi bhi nayi rail line ke bare me, jab tak sarkari ghoshna na ho, “ummeed” hi likha ja sakta hai, “pakka” nahi.

Almora: Sadak, Highway, Pul aur Seema Sadak (BRO)

Rashtriya Rajmarg, Jaam ki Jagah aur Samrik Sadkein

Zaroori note: Highway ke number aur raste kabhi-kabhi badalte rehte hain (kuch jagah NH 309A aur NH 309B ka bhi zikr aata hai). Is liye gaadi chalane se pehle Google Maps aur NHAI ki taaza adhisuchna dekh lein.

Aam raste (Delhi se Almora): 1. Delhi se Moradabad, Rampur, Haldwani aur Kathgodam, phir Bhowali, Kainchi Dham aur Almora ki taraf pahadi sadak. 2. Kathgodam ke baad Jeolikote ka raasta bhi Almora, Ranikhet aur Bageshwar ki taraf jata hai (Wikipedia ke anusar).

Samasya wale hisse: Peak season aur weekend me Kathgodam se Bhowali ke beech aur Kainchi Dham ke aas-paas bheed aur jam lag sakta hai. Barsaat me bhuskhalan se raste band ho sakte hain. Kis jagah par bar-bar dikkat aati hai, iski pakki suchi sarkar ya PWD se hi milegi.

BRO aur seema ke raste: Almora zila kisi antarrashtriya seema par nahi hai. Isliye BRO ki Shivalik ya Hirak jaisi strategic seema sadkein Almora ke liye lagu nahi hoti. Yeh Pithoragarh aur Chamoli jaise seema wale zilon ke liye zyada mahatvapurn hain.

Gaadi se yatra ke 6 niyam: 1. Yatra se pehle gaadi ki service aur brake check karein. 2. Pahadi sadak par rukne ke liye kisi chaudi jagah par hi gaadi lagayein. 3. Barsaat me raat ki yatra se bachein. 4. Tank bhara rakhein (Almora: Petrol Pump, EV Charging aur Mechanic). 5. Mausam aur sadak ki sthiti ke liye 1077 par sampark karein ya zila prashasan ki jaankari dekhein. 6. Offline map download karke rakhein, kyunki network kamzor ho sakta hai (Almora: Mobile Network aur Internet: Asli Sthiti).

Almora ke taxi stand aur bus stand: Almora shahar me bus aur taxi ke alag-alag stand hain, jinke naam aur jagah sthaniya logon se pooch kar tay karein. Nanda Devi mandir bus stand se lagbhag 500 m par hone ki jaankari Almora ki 20 Sabse Zaroori Ghumne ki Jagah me di gayi hai.

Taxi book karne ke tips: 1. Safar se pehle kiraya pehle se tay kar lein, khaaskar tourist season me. 2. Gaadi ka registration number aur driver ka phone number kisi parivarik vyakti ko bhej dein. 3. Raat ki pahadi yatra ke liye sirf bharosemand driver chunein. 4. Mahila akeli yatri Almora: Akeli Mahila Yatri: Suraksha aur Madad ki safety guide dekhein.

Almora: Petrol Pump, EV Charging aur Mechanic

Petrol Pump, EV Fast Charger aur Bharosemand Mechanic

Seedha jawab: Almora shahar, Ranikhet, Dwarahat aur bade kasbon me petrol pump milte hain. Jageshwar, Binsar aur ooche gaon ki taraf jane se pehle Almora me tank bhara lena sabse surakshit hai.

“Last petrol pump” ka niyam: Pahad me jahan petrol pump kam hote hain, wahan ek safe tareeka yeh hai ki jab tank aadha ho jaye, tab bhar lein. Jageshwar Almora se lagbhag 35 km aur Binsar lagbhag 26 se 30 km door hai. Aane-jane ka mila kar 70 km se zyada ka safar hota hai, isliye Almora se hi tank bhara lein.

EV (electric gaadi) ke liye saavdhani: - Pahadi chadhai me gaadi ki battery tezi se khatam hoti hai, kyunki chadhai me zyada bijli lagti hai. - Charging station par poori tarah bharosa na karein. Jane se pehle EV charging map app ya UPCL / UREDA ki taaza suchi dekh lein. - Agar charging ka vikalp kam ho to EV se pahad ki lambi yatra na karein.

Mechanic aur puncture: - Haldwani, Almora aur Ranikhet bade mechanic hub mane ja sakte hain. - Chhote gaon me tyre puncture aur chhoti marammat ke liye sthaniya mistri mil jate hain, par gaadi ke bade kharabi ke liye shahar tak jana padta hai. - Pahad par safar karne se pehle gaadi me stepney, jack, torch aur first-aid kit zaroor rakhein.

PWD Inspection House aur Forest Rest House (FRH): Yeh aam taur par sarkari adhikariyon ke liye hote hain, aur aam yatri ke liye booking ke niyam alag hote hain. Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary ke andar rehne ke niyam van vibhag tay karta hai.

Hotel aur heritage property: Wikipedia ke anusar Binsar me Grand Oak Manor (Sir Henry Ramsay ka purana grishmakaleen ghar, ab heritage hotel) aur Mary Budden Estate (lagbhag 8,000 feet par, restore kiya gaya aupniveshik ghar) jaisi property ullekhit hain. Booking se pehle unki taaza sthiti aur reviews dekh lein.

Budget aur lodge: Almora shahar, Kasar Devi aur Binsar road par budget hotel, guest house aur homestay milte hain. Mall Road ke aas-paas ke hotel me shor aur bheed zyada ho sakti hai. Shaant mahaul ke liye Kasar Devi ya Binsar ke taraf rukna behtar rehta hai.

Almora: Registered Pahadi Homestays, Hostels & Ashrams

Deendayal Homestay, Hostel, Dharamshala aur Ashram

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand ki Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grah Aawas (Home-Stay) Vikas Yojana me pahadi kshetra ke liye 33% ya Rs 10 lakh (jo kam ho) tak ka capital subsidy aur 5 saal ke liye byaj subsidy ka praavdhan bataya gaya hai.

Yojana ke mukhya praavdhan (Drishti IAS ki report ke anusar):

Uske liye Uttarakhand Paryatan Vibhag ki official website dekhein.

Homestay chunne ke 7 niyam (yatri ke liye): 1. Dekhein ki property Uttarakhand Paryatan Vibhag me panjikrit hai ya nahi. 2. Paani aur geyser ki suvidha ke bare me pehle puchhein. Almora me paani ki kami aam hai (Almora: Bijli, Solar aur Sardi mein Paani ki Sthiti). 3. Raasta road-head tak hai ya paidal, ye pehle se pooch lein. 4. Bijli ki kami me backup hai ya nahi. 5. Mahila yatri akeli ho to kisi parivarik homestay ko chunein (Almora: Akeli Mahila Yatri: Suraksha aur Madad). 6. Khane ki sthaniya vyavastha (Kumaoni thali, Almora: Samaj ki Banawat, Upnaam aur Gotra) pehle se bata dein. 7. Bahut kam kiraye wale ya aise homestay se bachein jinka koi review nahi hai.

Hostel aur backpacker thikane: Almora aur Kasar Devi me budget travellers ke liye hostel aur guest house milte hain.

Dharamshala aur ashram: Jageshwar, Chitai Golu Devta, Dunagiri aur Kasar Devi jaise dhaarmik sthalon par dharamshala aur ashram ki suvidha aam taur par milti hai. Rehne ke niyam, daan aur samay har sansthan ke alag hote hain. Mandir samiti ya ashram se pehle sampark kar ke hi jayein.

Yatri ke liye 6 salah: 1. Jane se pehle apne operator ki coverage map dekhein. 2. Offline Google Maps download karke rakhein. 3. Do operator ki SIM saath rakhein, agar zaroorat ho. 4. Emergency number 112 aur 108 pehle se save karein. 5. Trek ya jungle me akele na jayein, aur kisi ko apna raasta bata kar jayein. 6. Power bank hamesha saath rakhein.

Fiber Broadband aur Signal Booster: Workation ke liye Sachchai

Seedha jawab: Workation ke liye sabse pehle sthayi (fixed) broadband ki jaankari lein. Almora shahar me BSNL, Jio ya Airtel ki fiber seva mil sakti hai, par gaon me nahi bhi mil sakti. Kisi bhi remote homestay me “WiFi hai” kehne par bhi speed test pehle se maang lein.

Remote kaam karne wale ke liye checklist: - Property se pehle video call ya speed test screenshot maangein. - Do alag operator ka backup (mobile hotspot) rakhein. - Zaroori meeting ke liye shahar ke WiFi wale cafe ya co-working space ki jaankari rakhein. - Bijli ka backup (inverter) hai ya nahi, ye bhi puchhein (Almora: Bijli, Solar aur Sardi mein Paani ki Sthiti).

Signal booster par saavdhani: Bina anumati ke mobile signal booster ya repeater ka upyog bharat me niyam ke anusar galat ho sakta hai. Kharidne se pehle Doorsanchar Vibhag (DoT) ke niyam dekhein. Sahi samadhan aam taur par service provider ke dwara diya gaya Wi-Fi calling ya fixed broadband hota hai.

Almora: Bijli, Solar aur Sardi mein Paani ki Sthiti

Bijli (UPCL), Inverter-Solar, Jame Pipe aur Paani ki Kami

Seedha jawab: Almora me bijli ki aapoorti Uttarakhand Power Corporation Limited (UPCL) karti hai. Toofan, barish aur barf me line kharab hone se bijli kat sakti hai. Paani Almora ki sabse badi samasya hai: Down To Earth ke ek lekh ke anusar Almora me prakritik jalsrot (naula aur dhara) pichhle lagbhag 150 saalon me 360 se ghat kar 60 reh gaye.

Bijli ke liye tayyari: - Homestay ya ghar me pure sine-wave inverter aur tubular battery lagane ki salah di jati hai, taaki computer, router aur chhote upkaran surakshit rahein. - Solar panel aur battery backup lambe samay ke liye sahara de sakta hai. Subsidy aur yojana ki jaankari ke liye UREDA ya UPCL se pooch lein. - Barf girne ya toofan me line kharab hone par mahino ki nahi, aksar kuch ghante se kuch din ki dikkat hoti hai. Exact aankde ke liye UPCL ki report dekhein.

Almora ka paani: asli kahani (Down To Earth ke lekh ke anusar): - Almora shahar paramparik roop se naula aur dhara par nirbhar tha. Lekh ke anusar inki sankhya 360 se ghat kar 60 reh gayi, aur bachi hui bhi mausami hoti ja rahi hain. - Shahar ka paani mukhya roop se Shyahi Devi gravitational system (British dwara 1932 me banaya gaya, jungle ke saat srot se lagbhag 35 km door se paani lata hai) aur Kosi nadi se aata hai. - 2005 me sadak ke kinare lagaye gaye hand pump jaldi sookh gaye, kyunki unhe bina vaigyanik yojna ke lagaya gaya tha. - Lekh ke anusar kuch hotel zameen ke srot khareed kar paani bechte hain, jisse sthaniya logon ke liye kami badhti hai.

Yatri ke liye paani bachane ke tareeke: 1. Chhota snaan karein, geyser ke paani ka dhyan rakhein. 2. Plastic ki bottle ki jagah refill-able bottle rakhein. 3. Homestay ko puchhein ki paani ka kya srot hai. 4. Garmi me (April se June) paani ki kami zyada hoti hai. Is samay jaane se pehle homestay se baat kar lein.

Sardi me paani ke pipe jamna: Almora shahar me bahut kam jagah paani ke pipe jamte hain, par ooche ilake (Binsar, Dinapani jaise 2,000 m se upar) me raat ka taapmaan kabhi-kabhi shunya ke aas-paas jata hai. Pipe ko ghaas, jute ya foam se dhak dene aur raat ko tap thoda khula chhodne se pipe phatne ka khatra kam hota hai.

Almora: Inner Line Permit (ILP), Van Anumati aur Suraksha Niyam

Seema Permit, Drone par Rok aur Oonchai ki Bimari (AMS) se Bachav

Seedha jawab: Almora zile ko Inner Line Permit (ILP) ki zarurat nahi hai, kyunki yeh zila kisi antarrashtriya seema par nahi hai. Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary me pravesh ke liye van vibhag ki anumati aur shulk lagta hai. Almora ki unchai 2,500 m se kam hai, isliye yahan Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) ka khatra bahut kam hai.

ILP kahan lagta hai (sandarbh ke liye):

IlakaZila (aam taur par)Almora se sambandh
Niti aur Mana ghatiChamoliAlag zila - Joshimath (Chamoli) ke raste jaate hain, Almora se nahi; ILP Chamoli prashasan deta hai
Darma, Byans aur Chaudans ghatiPithoragarhAlag zila, ILP wahan ke SDM office se
Nelang ghatiUttarkashiAlag zila

Note: Yeh sirf yatri ko bhramit hone se bachane ke liye hai. ILP ki prakriya aur shulk ke liye sambandhit zila ke SDM office ya official portal ko dekhein.

Forest permit (Binsar): Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary me pravesh ke liye van vibhag ka shulk hota hai. Fees, camping ke niyam aur gaadi ke pravesh ki shartein badalti rehti hain.

Drone ke niyam: Bharat me drone udane ke liye DGCA ke niyam aur Digital Sky platform lagu hote hain. Wildlife sanctuary, sainik chhavni (jaise Ranikhet) aur mandir ke aas-paas drone udane ke liye aam taur par anumati chahiye hoti hai. Bina anumati ke drone udane par karyavahi ho sakti hai. Almora zile me seema nahi hai, par Ranikhet chhavni ek sainik kshetra hai, isliye wahan khaas saavdhani rakhein.

AMS (Acute Mountain Sickness) ki samajh: AMS aam taur par 2,500 m se upar ki unchai par jaldi chadhne se hota hai. Almora, Binsar aur Kasar Devi isse kam unchai par hain, isliye yahan ka khatra bahut kam hai. Lekin agar aap yahan se Pithoragarh ya Chamoli ke oonchai wale ilake me jaane wale hain, to ye jaankari kaam aayegi.

AMS ke lakshan: Sar dard, chakkar, ulti, bhookh na lagna, neend na aana. Kisi me yeh lakshan dikhein to aage chadhna band karein, aaram karein aur zarurat par neeche utar jayein. Gambhir sthiti me turant 108 par sampark karein.

Yatra ki suraksha ke 10 niyam: 1. Yatra se pehle mausam aur sadak ki sthiti dekhein. 2. Apna plan kisi parivarik vyakti ko bata dein. 3. Raat me jungle ke raaste na chalein. 4. Janwar ko khana na dein aur unke paas na jayein (Almora: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh). 5. Aag sirf tay jagah par jalayein. 6. Plastic aur kachra wapas le jayein. 7. Nadi ya nale me nahane ya photo ke liye na utarein. 8. Pahadi gaadi chalaane me tez gati se bachein. 9. Zaroori dawaiyan aur first-aid kit saath rakhein. 10. Emergency me 112 (police aur aapatkal), 108 (ambulance) aur 1077 (aapda control room) par sampark karein.
