Bageshwar: UKPSC, PCS aur UKSSSC ke liye 100 Ek-Line GK Tathya

Ye 100 tathya Patwari/Lekhpal, Police Constable/SI, Forest Guard, VDO/VPDO aur UKPSC ki prarambhik pariksha ke liye zaroori Bageshwar ke mool tathyon ko ek jagah dete hain. Yaad karne ke liye inhe 8 samuhon me baanta gaya hai.

Sabhi Pramukh Tathya (1 se 100)

A. Prashasanik (1 se 25)

  • Bageshwar zila 15 September 1997 ko Almora se alag hokar bana.
  • Us samay Uttar Pradesh ki Mukhyamantri Mayawati thin.
  • Uttarakhand rajya 9 November 2000 ko bana; Bageshwar uske 13 zilon me se ek hai.
  • Zile ka mukhyalaya Bageshwar nagar hai, jo Saryu-Gomti sangam par hai.
  • Bageshwar Kumaon mandal ka zila hai.
  • Zile ka kshetrafal lagbhag 2,241 sq km hai (Census 2011; zila website 2,246).
  • Census 2011 me aabadi 2,59,898 thi.
  • Purush 1,24,326 aur mahilayein 1,35,572 thin.
  • Sex ratio lagbhag 1,090 mahilayein per 1,000 purush hai.
  • Saksharta dar 80.01% (7 saal se upar ki aabadi par) hai.
  • Lagbhag 96.5% aabadi gaon me aur 3.5% shahar me rehti hai.
  • Anusuchit Jati ki aabadi lagbhag 27.7% aur Anusuchit Janjati ki lagbhag 0.76% hai.
  • Aabadi ghanatv lagbhag 116 vyakti per sq km hai.
  • Parivaron ki sankhya 58,046 (Census 2011) thi.
  • Zile me 3 development block hain: Bageshwar, Garur aur Kapkot.
  • Census 2011 me 4 tehsil the: Bageshwar, Garur, Kapkot aur Kanda.
  • Kshetrafal me sabse bada tehsil Kapkot (lagbhag 1,080 sq km) hai.
  • Aabadi me sabse bada tehsil Bageshwar (1,00,103) hai.
  • Census me lagbhag 947 gaon darj the.
  • Zile me regular thane Bageshwar, Garur, Jhiroli, Kanda aur Kapkot hain.
  • Bageshwar Almora Lok Sabha (SC aarakshit) kshetra me aata hai.
  • Vidhan Sabha seat Bageshwar (SC), kshetra sankhya 47 hai.
  • Bageshwar vidhan sabha seat ka nirman 1967 me hua (Wikipedia ke anusar).
  • Zile ka STD code 05963 hai.
  • Mukhya pin code 263642 aur RTO code UK-02 hai.

B. Bhoogol, nadi aur glacier (26 se 45)

  • Saryu nadi ka udgam Sarmul (Kapkot block) se hai.
  • Bageshwar nagar ki unchai lagbhag 1,004 m hai.
  • Bageshwar me Saryu Gomti se milti hai.
  • Saryu aage Pancheshwar me Kali (Sharda) se milti hai, jo Bageshwar zile ke bahar hai.
  • Gomti nadi Katyur ghati (Garur-Baijnath) se hokar behti hai.
  • Pindar nadi Pindari glacier se nikalkar Alaknanda ki taraf jaati hai.
  • Pindari glacier lagbhag 3.2 km lamba (Uttarakhand Tourism) hai.
  • Kafni glacier lagbhag 3,810 m ki unchai par hai.
  • Zile ki sabse unchi choti Nanda Kot (6,861 m) hai.
  • Mrigthuni 6,855 m, Maiktoli 6,803 m, Panwalidwar 6,663 m, Nanda Khat 6,611 m ki hain.
  • Sunderdhunga ghati Pindar ghati ki ek upghati hai, jiska trek adhiktam lagbhag 4,320 m tak jata hai.
  • Dhakuri pass lagbhag 2,940 m par hai.
  • Kausani lagbhag 1,890 m ki unchai par hai.
  • Baijnath lagbhag 1,125 m par hai.
  • Dwali Pindar aur Kafni nadiyon ka sangam sthal hai.
  • Khati Pindari trek ka aakhri bada gaon hai.
  • Loharkhet (lagbhag 1,760 m) par Forest check post hai.
  • Phurkia Pindari trek ka aakhri padaav (lagbhag 3,200 m) hai.
  • Pindari ka kshetra Nanda Devi Biosphere Reserve ke niyamon me aata hai.
  • Pindari trek ke liye Forest entry permit chahiye; kai sources ke anusar ILP nahi.

C. Itihas aur swatantrata sangram (46 se 62)

  • Katyuri rajaon ki rajdhani Kartikeyapura (aaj ka Baijnath) thi.
  • Bageshwar ke Bagnath mandir ka patthar ka shilalekh Sanskrit me hai aur Katyuri raja Bhudeva se juda hai.
  • Pandukeshwar ke tamrapatra (Lalitashura Deva, 853 ee.) Chamoli zile me hain, Bageshwar me nahi.
  • Bagnath mandir ke vartaman roop ka shrey Chand raja Laxmi Chand ko diya jata hai (1450 ya 1602).
  • Chand rajya ki aakhri rajdhani Almora thi.
  • Kumaon me Gorkha shasan 1790 se 1815 tak raha.
  • Sugauli ki sandhi December 1815 (anumodan March 1816) me hui.
  • Coolie-Begar andolan January 1921 me Bageshwar ke Uttarayani mele me hua.
  • Is andolan ke mukhya neta Badri Datt Pandey the, jinhe "Kumaon Kesari" kaha gaya.
  • Kumaon Parishad ki sthapna 1916 me hui.
  • Mahatma Gandhi 1929 me Kausani me lagbhag 14 din ruke.
  • Gandhi ji ne Kausani me Gita par "Anasakti Yoga" ki bhumika likhi (kai sources ke anusar).
  • Hindi ke Chhayavadi kavi Sumitranandan Pant ka janm 20 May 1900 ko Kausani me hua.
  • Pant ko Jnanpith Award 1968 me "Chidambara" par mila.
  • Pant ko Padma Bhushan 1961 me mila.
  • Pant ko Sahitya Akademi (1960) "Kala aur Boodha Chand" par mila.
  • Kausani me Lakshmi Ashram ki sthapna Sarla Behn (Catherine Heilman) ne ki (saal 1946/1948).

D. Sanskriti, mela aur boli (63 se 82)

  • Uttarayani Mela Makar Sankranti (lagbhag 14 January) par Saryu-Gomti sangam par lagta hai.
  • Ghughutiya tyohar Makar Sankranti par manaya jata hai.
  • Phool Dei Meen Sankranti (Chait ke pehle din) par manaya jata hai.
  • Harela Karka Sankranti (lagbhag 16 July) par manaya jata hai.
  • Egaas (Budhi Diwali) Diwali ke lagbhag 11 din baad hoti hai.
  • Bhitauli Chait mahine ka tyohar hai, jisme bhai behen ko upahar bhejta hai.
  • Aipan geru (laal mitti) aur biswar (chawal ka paste) se bani paramparik kala hai.
  • Rangwali Pichora Kumaon ki dulhan ka laal-peela odhna hai.
  • Jagar devta ke aahvan ki parampara hai; jagariya gata hai aur dangariya devta ka madhyam hota hai.
  • Chholiya talwar-dhaal ka nritya hai, jo Baijnath (Bageshwar) me bhi dekha gaya hai.
  • Jhora, Chanchari aur Chhapeli Kumaon ke lok-nritya/geet hain.
  • Kumaoni Holi ke teen roop: Baithki, Khari aur Mahila Holi.
  • Kumaoni Holi me dhamar taal 16 matra ka hota hai (kai sources ke anusar).
  • Danpur kshetra ki boli Danpuriya hai.
  • Gangoli boli Gangoli-Ganai kshetra (Kanda ke aas-paas) se jodi jaati hai.
  • Kumaoni ko UNESCO ne "unsafe" bhasha shreni me rakha hai.
  • Census 2011 me Kumaoni bolne wale lagbhag 19.8 lakh the.
  • Golu Devta Kumaon ke nyay ke devta hain; Chitai (Almora) sabse prasiddh mandir hai.
  • Nanda Devi Raj Jat har 12 saal me hone wali lagbhag 280 km ki yatra hai (2026 ki tithi).
  • Khuded geet virah aur mait ki yaad ke geet hain.

E. Mandir aur paryatan (83 se 92)

  • Bagnath me Shiva Vyaghreshwar ke roop me poojey jaate hain.
  • Markandeya rishi aur Saryu ki katha Bageshwar ke naam se judi hai.
  • Baijnath me Gomti ke tat par lagbhag 18 Katyuri patthar ke mandir hain.
  • Bageshwar se Baijnath lagbhag 26 km aur Kausani lagbhag 38 se 40 km hai.
  • Kot Bhramari mandir Baijnath se lagbhag 3 km door hai.
  • Kausani Tea Estate ki sthapna Wikipedia ke anusar 2000-2001 me hui (itihas ke dave).
  • Pindari trek ka raasta Song/Loharkhet, Khati, Dwali, Phurkia hokar jata hai.
  • Bageshwar ka nearest airport Pantnagar aur nearest railhead Kathgodam hai.
  • Tanakpur-Bageshwar rail line (lagbhag 170 km) ka DPR bana hai (laagat lagbhag Rs 48,692 crore); sanction baaki hai.
  • NH 309A Bageshwar se hokar Berinag-Kanda-Bageshwar-Takula-Almora ko jodta hai.

F. Arthvyavastha, shiksha aur anya (93 se 100)

  • Bageshwar ka ODOP zila yojana ke anusar kiwi hai.
  • 2021-22 me GDDP lagbhag Rs 3,226 crore (current prices) tha.
  • 2021-22 me per capita income Rs 98,755 tha.
  • Uttarakhand High Court ne 6 January 2025 ko Bageshwar me soapstone khanan par roak lagayi.
  • Sarju II (12.6 MW) aur Sarju III (10.5 MW) Saryu par Kapkot ke paas ke chhote hydro projects hain.
  • Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Simar ki sthapna 2004 me hui.
  • Rajeev Gandhi Navodaya Vidyalaya, Bahuli ki sthapna 2009 me hui.
  • Bageshwar ke youth ke liye Agniveer bharti ARO Almora ke antargat hoti hai.

Yaad karne ki trick: Har samuh ko ek kahani me baandhein. Jaise "1997 me Mayawati ne Almora se Bageshwar nikala; Saryu-Gomti ke sangam par Bagnath hai; wahi 1921 me Kumaon Kesari ne begar roka." Aisi chhoti kahaniyan raw tathyon se jyada yaad rehti hain.

Kahan padhein (digital)? Atkinson ke Himalayan Gazetteer ke khand FIBIS ke anusar Digital Library of India, Archive.org, Deutsche Digital Bibliothek aur Panjab Digital Library par uplabdh bataye gaye hain; kuch jagah registration chahiye. Link badal sakte hain, isliye in sansthanon ke naam se khojein.

Pariksha ke liye kaise padhein?
1. Atkinson ko "sandarbh ke roop me" padhein (purani, angrezi, gazetteer shaili). 2. Pandey ke "Kumaon ka Itihas" se chronology (Katyuri, Chand, Gorkha, British) pakki karein (Bageshwar: Itihas, mandir aur lok-katha). 3. UKPSC ki pustak ya NCERT-jaisi sarkari kitab se rajya ka GK padhein.

Shodh-patra: Kumaun University ke itihas vibhag, Uttarakhand Abhilekhagar aur kuch Indian history journals me Kumaon par shodh milta hai.
