Capital Gain Tax Calculator (Shares)

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~4 minute

Share ya equity mutual fund bechne par jo munafa hota hai uspar capital gain tax lagta hai. Ye kitna hoga, ye is baat par nirbhar karta hai ki aapne kitne samay tak rakha (short ya long term). Ye calculator khareed-bikri value aur avdhi lekar tax ka anumaan deta hai.

Capital gain kya hai

Jab aap share ya mutual fund khareed kar zyada daam par bechte hain, to jo munafa hota hai use capital gain kehte hain. Ispar tax lagta hai. Kitna tax lagega, ye do cheez par nirbhar: (1) aapne kitne samay tak rakha, (2) equity hai ya kuch aur.

1 saal se kam rakha = Short Term (zyada tax)
1 saal ya zyada rakha = Long Term (kam tax, kuch chhoot ke saath)

Short vs Long term

Short term (STCG): agar equity 1 saal se kam rakha, to munafe par ek tay dar (aam taur par ~20%) tax lagta hai. Long term (LTCG): 1 saal ya zyada rakha, to ek seema tak (aam taur par ~₹1.25 lakh) ka munafa tax-free, aur usse upar ~12.5% tax. Isliye lambe samay tak rakhna tax me faayda deta hai.

Ek misaal

₹1 lakh ke share, ₹1.5 lakh me beche, 8 mahine baad. Munafa ₹50,000, aur avdhi 1 saal se kam — yaani short term. Tax ~20% = ~₹10,000. Agar wahi 13 mahine baad bechte to long term hota, aur ₹50,000 munafa exemption seema ke andar aane se tax lagbhag zero ho sakta tha. Yahi farak samay ka hai.

Zaroori baatein

  • Nuksan (loss) ko aage le jaa sakte hain aur future munafe se ghata sakte hain — par tabhi jab ITR samay par bharein.
  • Dar aur chhoot har budget me badalti hai — current niyam zaroor confirm karein.
  • F&O alag hai — wo business income hai, capital gain nahi. Uske liye F&O wala lekh dekhein.
  • ITR sahi form — capital gain ke liye ITR-2 ya ITR-3. ITR-3 guide dekhein.

Kharch jo aapke munafe se ghata sakte hain

Tax munafe par lagta hai, bikri ki keemat par nahi. Aur munafa nikaalte waqt kai kharche ghataye ja sakte hain — jinhe log aksar bhool jaate hain aur zyada tax bhar dete hain.

Property bechne par aam taur par ye ghataye ja sakte hain:

  • Khareedne ki keemat — aur uske saath us waqt ki stamp duty aur registration fees.
  • Sudhaar par kharcha — jaise nayi manzil banwana ya bada nirmaan. Saada rang-rogan ya marammat nahi.
  • Bechne ka kharcha — dalali (brokerage), vigyapan, vakeel ki fees.
Iska seedha matlab ye hai ki purane kaagaz sambhal kar rakhna paisa bachata hai. 15 saal purani registry, sudhaar ke bill, dalali ki rasid — ye sab bechte waqt tax ghatate hain. Jinke paas ye kaagaz nahi hote, unhe zyada munafa dikhana padta hai aur zyada tax dena padta hai.

Share aur mutual fund me hisaab isse saada hai — wahan broker ka statement khud hi kharide-beche ki keemat aur charges bata deta hai.

Tax bachane ke jaayaz raste

Capital gain par tax ghatane ke kuch jaayaz raste niyam me hi diye gaye hain. Ye chhupane ke tareeke nahi hain — ye kanoon ka hissa hain:

  • Doosri property me nivesh — makaan bechkar munafa doosre residential makaan me lagane par chhoot ka pravdhaan hai. Samay seema aur shartein sakht hain, isliye pehle se planning zaroori hai.
  • Kuch khaas bonds — property ke long term munafe ko niyam me batayi gayi khaas bonds me lagane par chhoot milti hai. Yahan bhi samay seema hoti hai — bikri ke baad kuch hi mahine.
  • Nuksan ko munafe se milana — usi saal ka koi doosra nuksan munafe se ghataya ja sakta hai, aur bacha hua nuksan aage ke saalon ke liye le jaaya ja sakta hai. Iske liye ITR samay par bharna zaroori hai — der se bharne par ye haq chhoot jaata hai.
  • Sahi samay par bechna — short term aur long term ki seema paar hone se pehle bechne par tax kaafi zyada padta hai. Kuch hafton ka intezaar kai baar bade antar ka kaaran banta hai.
Chetavni: capital gain ke niyam, dar aur seema har budget me badal sakti hai, aur property ka maamla peechda hota hai. Bade sauda se pehle CA se ek baar salah zaroor lijiye — uski fees us tax ke saamne kuch bhi nahi hoti jo galti se zyada bhara ja sakta hai.

Property ke maamle ka poora vivaran property bechne par capital gain tax wale lekh me hai. Sarkari niyam ke liye hamesha incometax.gov.in hi aakhri source hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Share par kitna tax lagta hai?

1 saal se kam rakha (short term) to ~20%. 1 saal ya zyada (long term) to ek seema tak tax-free, usse upar ~12.5%. Dar budget me badalti hai.

Long term me kitna tax-free hai?

Aam taur par ~₹1.25 lakh tak ka long-term munafa tax-free, baaki par ~12.5%. Ye seema samay-samay par badalti hai.

Nuksan par tax lagta hai?

Nahi. Nuksan ko aage 8 saal tak le jaa sakte hain aur future capital gain se ghata sakte hain, agar ITR samay par bhara ho.

F&O ka tax isse alag hai?

Haan, F&O business income hai (capital gain nahi). Uska hisaab aur ITR alag hota hai.

Kya dalali aur registry ka kharcha munafe se ghata sakte hain?

Haan. Khareedte waqt ki stamp duty aur registration, bade sudhaar par kharcha, aur bechte waqt ki dalali — ye sab munafe se ghataye ja sakte hain. Isi liye purane bill aur rasid sambhal kar rakhna chahiye.

Nuksan ko munafe se milana mumkin hai?

Haan, niyam ke andar. Bacha hua nuksan aage ke saalon ke liye bhi le jaaya ja sakta hai, par iske liye ITR samay par bharna zaroori hai.
