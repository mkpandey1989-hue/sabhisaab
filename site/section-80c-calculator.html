Section 80C Tax Saving

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me 3-4 min ka padhaav
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Old tax regime me 80C sabse bada tax-saving hathiyaar hai — ₹1.5 lakh tak ki chhoot. Apne PPF, ELSS, LIC, EPF, tuition fee sab yahan jodiye, dekhiye kitni chhoot mili aur aapke slab par kitna tax bacha.

Formula ka matlab

Section 80C ke andar aane wale kai nivesh aur kharch milkar ₹1.5 lakh tak ki income par chhoot dete hain. Is chhoot par aapka tax nahi lagta — yaani bachat = chhoot × aapka slab.

Formula:
Kul 80C = EPF + PPF + ELSS/LIC + tuition + home loan principal (adhiktam ₹1,50,000)
Tax bachat = chhoot × slab%

Dhyan rahe: ₹1.5 lakh se upar jo bhi 80C me daalein, us par extra chhoot nahi milti. Aur ye poora fayda sirf old regime me hai — new regime me 80C nahi chalta.

Teen sthiti, teen number

Misaal 1 — poora fayda. EPF 30k + PPF 50k + ELSS 40k + tuition 20k = ₹1,40,000. 20% slab par bachat = ₹28,000. ₹10,000 aur daal kar ₹1.5 lakh poora kar sakte hain.

Misaal 2 — seema paar. Agar 80C me ₹2 lakh daal diya, to chhoot sirf ₹1.5 lakh milegi. Baaki ₹50,000 par koi tax fayda nahi.

Misaal 3 — 30% slab. ₹1.5 lakh poora bhara, slab 30%. Tax bachat = ₹45,000 (+ cess). Zyada income wale ko 80C se sabse zyada fayda.

Log kahan galti karte hain

  1. New regime me 80C maanna. New regime (default) me 80C ki chhoot nahi milti. Fayda sirf old regime me hai.
  2. ₹1.5 lakh se upar daalna. Seema ₹1.5 lakh hai. Usse zyada nivesh acha hai par extra tax chhoot nahi deta.
  3. EPF ko bhoolna. Aapki salary se kat raha EPF pehle se 80C me aata hai — use jodna na bhoolein, warna double nivesh ho jaayega.
  4. ELSS ko FD samajhna. ELSS me 3 saal lock aur market risk hota hai; tax-saver FD alag (5 saal lock) hoti hai.
  5. Home loan principal chhodna. Home loan ki EMI ka principal hissa bhi 80C me aata hai — statement se lein.

Yahan ki zaroori baatein

  • 80C ke andar: EPF/VPF, PPF, ELSS, life insurance premium, tuition fee (2 bachche), home loan principal, NSC, tax-saver FD, SSY.
  • 80CCD(1B): NPS me ₹50,000 extra chhoot — ye 80C ki ₹1.5 lakh seema se alag hai.
  • Old vs New: Agar 80C + home loan + HRA milakar chhoot zyada hai to old regime faydemand ho sakta hai — dono compare karein.
  • Proof rakhein: Sabhi receipt/statement sambhal kar rakhein — employer ya ITR me proof maang sakte hain.

Ek kadam aage

80C ka sabse bada sawaal hota hai — "kis cheez me daalein?" Iska jawab aapki umar aur zaroorat par nirbhar hai. Jawaan aur risk le sakne wale ke liye ELSS (equity-linked saving scheme) accha vikalp hai kyunki uska lock-in sirf 3 saal hai aur lambe samay me return zyada mil sakta hai. Jinhe surakshit aur pehle se tay rakam chahiye, unke liye PPF ya tax-saver FD theek hai. Aur agar aapki salary se EPF already kat raha hai, to wo pehle se 80C bhar raha hota hai — bahut baar log ise ginna bhool kar zaroorat se zyada nivesh kar dete hain.

Ek chalaak strategy jo kam log jaante hain — NPS ka 80CCD(1B) wala ₹50,000 80C ki ₹1.5 lakh seema se poori tarah alag hai. Iska matlab ye ki agar aapka 80C already full hai aur aap 20% ya 30% slab me hain, to NPS me ₹50,000 daal kar ₹10,000-₹15,000 aur tax bacha sakte hain. Bas dhyan rahe NPS ka paisa retirement tak kaafi had tak locked rehta hai, isliye utna hi daalein jitna lambe samay tak na chahiye.

Aapke sawaal, seedhe jawab

80C me adhiktam kitni chhoot?

₹1,50,000 tak. Usse upar nivesh par extra tax chhoot nahi milti.

Kya new regime me 80C chalta hai?

Nahi. 80C ki chhoot sirf old tax regime me milti hai.

Kitna tax bachta hai?

Chhoot &times; aapka slab. Jaise ₹1.5 lakh par 20% slab me ₹30,000 bachat.

EPF 80C me aata hai?

Haan, aapki salary se kat raha EPF/VPF 80C me ginte hain.

NPS ki extra chhoot?

80CCD(1B) me NPS par ₹50,000 alag chhoot, jo 80C se bahar hai.

80C me kya-kya gina jaata hai — poori list

Log aksar sirf LIC aur PPF ginte hain, aur limit poori hone ke baad bhi aur nivesh kar dete hain. Poori list:

KyaKhaas baat
PPF, EPF (apna hissa)Pehle se kat raha hai — pehle ye giniye
Home loan ka mool hissaByaj nahi, wo alag section me
Bacchon ki school feeSirf tuition fee, 2 bacchon tak
Jeevan bima ka premiumApna, patni/pati aur bacchon ka
ELSS mutual fund3 saal lock, 80C me sabse chhota lock
Sukanya Samriddhi, NSC, 5-saal FDSab isi ek limit me
Ghar ki stamp duty aur registrySirf jis saal ghar khareeda
Pehle jodiye, phir nivesh kijiye: zyadatar naukri walon ka EPF, bacchon ki fee aur home loan ka mool milakar hi limit ka bada hissa bhar jaata hai. Uske baad "tax bachane" ke liye khareedi gayi policy ka koi tax faayda nahi hota. Isliye March me jaldbaazi karne se pehle ye teen cheezein jod lijiye — kai baar pata chalta hai ki kuch aur karne ki zaroorat hi nahi thi. 80C ki chhoot sirf purani regime me milti hai.
Zaroori baat: Ye anuman hai aur sirf old tax regime par lagoo hai. Tax niyam, seema aur eligibility badalti rehti hai. Filing se pehle incometax.gov.in ya apne CA se confirm karein.
