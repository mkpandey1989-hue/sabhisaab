Word Counter — Shabd, Akshar Aur Padhne Ka Samay

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~5 minute

Neeche apna text paste kar dijiye. Shabd, akshar, vaakya, paragraph aur padhne me lagne wala samay — sab apne aap gin jayega, likhte-likhte.

Shabd kaise gine jaate hain

Tarika saada hai — jahan space aata hai, wahan shabd toota maana jaata hai. Do space ho ya das, ek hi break ginta hai.

  • Akshar (space ke saath) — poore text ki lambai, space aur enter bhi shamil.
  • Akshar (bina space) — sirf akshar aur chinh. Ye wo number hai jo aksar form aur SMS ki limit me maanga jaata hai.
  • Vaakya — poorn viraam, sawaal ya vismay chinh se gine jaate hain.
  • Paragraph — khaali line se alag hue hisse.

Ek baat jaan lena zaroori hai: "1,000 words" ka matlab har jagah bilkul ek jaisa nahi hota. Word file, Google Docs aur ye tool — teeno me 5-10 shabd ka antar aa sakta hai, kyunki hyphen wale shabd aur number ko har system thoda alag ginta hai. Isi liye limit ke ekdam kinare par mat rukiye.

Padhne aur bolne ke samay ka hisaab

Padhne ka samay = Shabd ÷ 200  |  Bolne ka samay = Shabd ÷ 130

Ek aam padhne wala mann me lagbhag 200 shabd per minute padhta hai. Bolte waqt raftaar kam ho jaati hai — lagbhag 130 shabd per minute, kyunki rukna, saans lena aur zor dena padta hai.

Isi hisaab se:

ShabdPadhne meBolne me
300~2 min~2 min
600~3 min~5 min
1,000~5 min~8 min
2,000~10 min~15 min

Speech ya presentation taiyaar kar rahe hain to bolne wala number dekhiye. 5 minute ka bhaashan lagbhag 650 shabd ka hota hai — isse zyada likhne par aap jaldbaazi me bolenge aur sunne wale ko samajh nahi aayega.

Kahan word limit sach me mayne rakhti hai

Kuch jagah limit sakht hoti hai aur paar karne par kaam ruk jaata hai:

  • Exam ka answer — "150 shabdon me likhiye" ka matlab lagbhag 150 hota hai, 300 nahi. Zyada likhne par extra number nahi milte, aur samay barbaad hota hai.
  • Sarkari form aur application — yahan box me akshar ki limit hoti hai, shabd ki nahi. "Bina space wale akshar" wala number dekhiye.
  • SMS — ek SMS 160 akshar ka hota hai. Usse zyada par do SMS ka paisa katta hai.
  • Website ka title aur description — title lagbhag 60 akshar, description 160 tak. Isse lamba Google search me kat jaata hai.
  • Blog aur article — yahan limit nahi hoti, par 800-1,000 shabd se kam wale page ko Google aksar patla maanta hai.

Aapka text kahin nahi jaata

Ye baat saaf keh dena zaroori hai. Aap jo text yahan paste karte hain, wo sirf aapke browser me ginta hai. Wo kisi server par nahi bheja jaata, kahin save nahi hota, aur page band karte hi khatam ho jaata hai.

Isi liye aap yahan apna niji likha hua bhi paste kar sakte hain — application, thesis ka hissa, letter, kuch bhi. Hum tak wo pahunchta hi nahi.

Phir bhi ek aam salah — kisi bhi website par apna bahut niji ya gopniya document paste karne se pehle soch lena hamesha achhi aadat hai. Har website ye baat saaf nahi likhti.

Sirf padhne ka samay chahiye to Scientific Calculator zyada seedha hai. Number ko shabdon me badalna ho to Number to Words dekhiye.

Achha likhne ke liye ginti ka istemaal

Word counter ka asli faayda limit check karna nahi hai — apna likha sudharna hai. Kuch paimane jo kaam karte hain:

  • Vaakya ki lambai. Shabd ÷ vaakya kariye. Agar jawab 25 se upar aa raha hai, to aapke vaakya lambe hain aur padhne wala beech me atak raha hoga. 15-20 ke aas-paas rakhna sabse aasaan padhne wala hota hai.
  • Paragraph ka size. Mobile par 3-4 line se lamba paragraph deewar jaisa dikhta hai. Paragraph ki ginti dekhkar bade blocks tod dijiye.
  • Bolne ka samay. Kuch bhi likhne ke baad "bolne me kitna" dekhiye. Agar 8 minute aa raha hai aur aapke paas 5 hain, to abhi kaatna hai — stage par nahi.
Ek purani lekhak ki aadat: pehla draft likh lene ke baad shabd giniye, phir 10% kaatne ki koshish kariye bina matlab badle. Lagbhag hamesha ye ho jaata hai, aur likha hua behtar ban jaata hai.

Hindi aur Hinglish ke liye ek baat khaas hai — Hinglish me shabd angrezi se lambe padte hain, isliye utne hi shabdon me akshar zyada bante hain. Agar kahin akshar ki limit hai (jaise SMS ya form ka box), to "bina space wale akshar" wala number dekhiye, shabd wala nahi.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Shabd kaise gine jaate hain?

Space ke aadhaar par. Do space ho ya das, ek hi break maana jaata hai. Word aur Google Docs se 5-10 shabd ka antar aa sakta hai, isliye limit ke ekdam kinare par mat rukiye.

5 minute ka bhaashan kitne shabd ka hota hai?

Lagbhag 650 shabd. Bolne ki aam raftaar 130 shabd per minute hoti hai — isse zyada likhne par jaldbaazi me bolna padta hai.

Mera text kahin save hota hai?

Nahi. Ginti aapke browser me hi hoti hai. Text kisi server par nahi jaata aur page band karte hi khatam ho jaata hai.

SMS me kitne akshar aate hain?

Ek SMS 160 akshar ka hota hai. Usse zyada par do SMS ka paisa katta hai — isliye 'bina space wale akshar' wala number dekhiye.

Vaakya kitne lambe hone chahiye?

Shabd ko vaakya se bhaag dijiye. 15-20 shabd per vaakya sabse aasaani se padha jaata hai. 25 se upar jaane par padhne wala beech me atakne lagta hai.
