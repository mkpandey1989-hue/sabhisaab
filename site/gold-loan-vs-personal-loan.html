Gold Loan Vs Personal Loan — Kaunsa Lena Chahiye?

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~7 minute
Chhota jawabSeedha jawab: Gold loan ka byaj aam taur par personal loan se kaafi kam hota hai (kyunki aapka sona bank ke paas girvi rehta hai), aur ye jaldi mil jaata hai. Par tenure chhota hota hai aur EMI na bharne par sona neelaam ho sakta hai. Personal loan mehnga hai par koi cheez girvi nahi rakhni padti.

Dono me buniyaadi farak

Gold loan — aap apna sona (gehne/sikke) bank ya NBFC ke paas girvi rakhte hain, aur badle me loan milta hai. Isse secured loan kehte hain, kyunki bank ke paas aapka sona surakshaa ke roop me hota hai.

Personal loan — bina kuch girvi rakhe, sirf aapki income aur credit score ke aadhar par milta hai. Isse unsecured loan kehte hain.

Yahi buniyaadi farak baaki sab cheezein tay karta hai — byaj, process, risk, sab.

Gold loan sasta kyun hai

Kyunki bank ke paas aapka sona hai. Agar aap paisa nahi chukate, to bank sona bech kar apna paisa nikaal leta hai. Bank ka risk kam, isliye byaj kam.

Personal loan me bank ke paas kuch nahi hota — agar aap nahi chukate to bank ko vasooli me mehnat karni padti hai. Bank ka risk zyada, isliye byaj zyada. Yahi poori kahani hai.

LTV (Loan to Value): gold loan me aapko sone ki poori keemat nahi milti, ek hissa milta hai. Ye anupaat RBI tay karta hai aur samay-samay par badalta hai. Baaki hissa isliye chhoda jaata hai taaki sone ka daam thoda gire to bhi bank surakshit rahe.

Gold loan ka asli risk

Sabse bada risk EMI ka dhyaan na rakhna hai. Kyunki loan chhoti avdhi ka hota hai, EMI thodi zyada aati hai. Do-teen kist chhoot gayi to bank aapke sone ko neelaam kar sakta hai — aur wo sona aksar parivaar ka, peedhiyon purana, ya bhavnaon se juda hota hai.

Isliye gold loan sirf tab lein jab aapko pakka bharosa ho ki aap samay par chuka denge. "Sona to apna hi hai" soch kar laapravaahi karna sabse mehngi galti hai.

Ek aur baat: kuch gold loan me EMI ki jagah "bullet repayment" hota hai — yaani poora byaj+mool ant me ek saath. Ye tab theek hai jab aapko pakka pata ho ki paisa kab aayega (jaise fasal bikne par). Warna ant me bada bojh aa jaata hai.

Personal loan kab behtar hai

Personal loan mehnga hai, par kuch sthitiyon me yahi theek hai:

  • Aapke paas sona nahi hai ya girvi nahi rakhna chahte
  • Lambi avdhi chahiye — 3-5 saal me aaram se chukana hai
  • Bada amount chahiye jo sone ki value se zyada ho
  • Aapka credit score achha hai — tab byaj kaafi kam mil jaata hai

Agar aapka credit score achha hai (750+), to personal loan ka byaj kaafi kam ho sakta hai — kabhi-kabhi gold loan ke aas-paas. Isliye credit score sudharna seedha paisa bachata hai.

Ek teesra raasta jo log bhool jaate hain

Kai baar sabse sasta raasta koi loan hai hi nahi. Poochhne layak sawaal:

  • Kya FD tod sakte hain? FD par 6-7% mil raha hai aur loan 14% le rahe hain — to FD todkar kaam chalana sasta padta hai (emergency fund wali FD chhod kar).
  • Kya credit card ka EMI option hai? Kabhi-kabhi (par card ka revolving balance sabse mehnga — yahan dekhein).
  • Kya thoda ruk sakte hain? Agar kharch talne layak hai, to loan se bachna hi sabse sasta hai.

Loan hamesha aakhri raasta hona chahiye, pehla nahi.

Faisla kaise karein

  1. Kitna paisa aur kab tak chahiye? Chhoti avdhi + sona hai → gold loan. Lambi avdhi → personal loan.
  2. Dono ka byaj poochhein aur EMI calculator me kul byaj nikaalein.
  3. Sona neelaam ka risk jhel sakte hain? Nahi → personal loan surakshit.
  4. Credit score achha hai? Haan → personal loan ka rate bhi kam aa sakta hai.
  5. Prepayment/foreclosure charge poochhein — jaldi chukane par penalty to nahi.

Zaroori: byaj dar, LTV aur charges har bank/NBFC me alag hote hain aur badalte rehte hain. Hum koi lender ya scheme suggest nahi karte. RBI ke niyam rbi.org.in par milte hain. Apne do-teen banko ka rate khud milaayein.

Do asli sthitiyan

Sthiti 1 — kisan ko fasal tak paisa chahiye. Do mahine baad fasal bikegi, tab tak beej-khaad ke liye ₹50,000 chahiye. Yahan gold loan (ya KCC) behtar hai — kam byaj, chhoti avdhi, aur fasal bikte hi chuka denge. Personal loan ki lambi avdhi aur zyada byaj yahan faltu hai. KCC ka poora tarika yahan hai.

Sthiti 2 — shaadi ya ilaaj ka bada kharcha. ₹3 lakh chahiye jo 3-4 saal me chukana hai, aur sona itna nahi hai. Yahan personal loan theek hai — bada amount, lambi avdhi, aur EMI aaram se banti hai. Gold loan ki chhoti avdhi me itni badi EMI mushkil hoti.

Yaani koi ek "behtar" nahi hai — zaroorat tay karti hai. Rakam kitni, avdhi kitni, sona hai ya nahi, aur EMI jhel sakte hain ya nahi. In chaar sawaalon ka jawab hi faisla de deta hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Gold loan me sona wapas kab milta hai?

Poora loan (mool + byaj) chukane par turant. Jab tak loan chalta hai, sona bank ke locker me surakshit rehta hai.

EMI nahi di to kya hota hai?

Bank pehle yaad dilata hai, phir mauka deta hai. Baar-baar chhootne par notice ke baad sona neelaam ho sakta hai. Isliye samay par chukana zaroori hai.

Kya gold loan par credit score chahiye?

Zyada mayne nahi rakhta, kyunki sona girvi hai. Isliye kam score wale ya bina income proof wale logon ko bhi mil jaata hai.

Personal loan sasta kaise ho sakta hai?

Achha credit score (750+), sthir income, aur ek se zyada bank ka rate milaakar. Kabhi-kabhi ye gold loan ke aas-paas aa jaata hai.

Prepayment par charge lagta hai?

Personal loan me kabhi-kabhi foreclosure charge hota hai. Gold loan me aksar nahi. Lene se pehle likhit me confirm karein.
