IDV hai kya, aur wo itni zaroori kyun hai

IDV ka poora naam hai Insured Declared Value. Seedhi bhasha me — ye wo adhiktam rakam hai jo insurance company aapko de sakti hai.

Ye do sthitiyon me poori milti hai: gaadi chori ho jaye, ya itni kharab ho jaye ki sudharna keemat se mehnga pade. Isi ko total loss kehte hain. Baaki chhote claim me IDV ki koi bhoomika nahi hoti.

Kam IDV rakhne ka asli nuksan

Agent kehta hai — IDV thodi kam kar dein, premium 900 rupaye kam ho jayega. Sunne me theek lagta hai. Ganit dekhiye.

900 rupaye bachane ke liye 96,000 ka jokhim. Yaani 106 saal tak premium bachana pade tab ja kar ye nuksan poora hoga. Isliye IDV kabhi ghatani nahi chahiye — premium kam karna ho to voluntary deductible badhaiye, IDV nahi.

Ek baat aur — agar gaadi par loan chal raha hai to kam IDV aur bhi khatarnak hai. Total loss par claim seedha bank ko jaata hai, aur agar wo bakaya loan se kam nikla, to bacha hua paisa aapko apni jeb se bharna padta hai — bina gaadi ke.

Zero depreciation cover — kab lena chahiye

Saadharan policy me claim ke waqt purzon par depreciation lagta hai. Yaani bumper 5,000 ka hai to aapko 3,000 hi milte hain, baaki apni jeb se.

Zero depreciation cover me ye katauti nahi lagti, poore purze ka paisa milta hai. Premium lagbhag 15-20% zyada hota hai.

Ek chhoti si baat jo bahut kaam aati hai: zero dep me bhi saal me kitni baar claim liya ja sakta hai, iski seema hoti hai. Ye policy ke kagaz me likhi hoti hai aur lene se pehle padh leni chahiye.

Ek nazar me — No Claim Bonus ka faayda

Claim na lene par har saal premium par chhoot badhti jaati hai. Ye chhoot poore premium par nahi, sirf apni gaadi wale hisse par milti hai.

Isi wajah se chhota claim lena aksar ghaate ka sauda hota hai. 6,000 ka scratch claim kiya, aur 45% ki chhoot girkar 20% par aa gayi — agle saalon me premium ka nuksan claim se zyada baith jaata hai.

Mota niyam: claim ki rakam agle do saal me hone wale premium ke nuksan se kam ho, to claim mat lijiye. Chhoti denting-painting apni jeb se karwana sasta padta hai.

Jo galtiyan sabse zyada dikhti hain

Car insurance me nuksan aksar policy se nahi, in aadaton se hota hai.

  • Sirf sasta premium dekhna. Do quotation ki tulna se pehle dekhiye ki IDV, zero dep aur cover ki sharten ek jaisi hain ya nahi. Sasta aksar kam cover ka hi doosra naam hota hai.
  • NCB naye gaadi par transfer na karwana. Ye bonus gaadi ka nahi, aapka hota hai. Nayi gaadi lene par purani policy se NCB certificate lekar aage le jaya ja sakta hai. Bahut log ye bhool jaate hain aur 45% ki chhoot chhod dete hain.
  • Policy lapse hone dena. 90 din se zyada gap ho gaya to NCB poori tarah khatam ho jaati hai.
  • Accessories declare na karna. Baad me lagaya music system ya CNG kit policy me likha na ho to uska claim nahi milta.
  • Chhote claim lena. Do-teen hazaar ke claim se NCB ka nuksan kai guna bada hota hai.
Ek baat jo aksar nahi batayi jaati: total loss par aapko IDV milti hai, gaadi ki bazaar keemat nahi. Do alag cheezein hain. Isliye renewal ke waqt IDV ko us daayre me rakhiye jisme wo gaadi sach me bik sakti hai — usse kam bilkul nahi.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

IDV ka matlab kya hota hai?

Insured Declared Value — wo adhiktam rakam jo gaadi chori hone ya poori tarah kharab hone par insurance company degi. Chhote claim me iski bhoomika nahi hoti.

Premium kam karne ke liye IDV ghatani chahiye?

Nahi. Isse premium kuch sau rupaye bachta hai par claim me lakhon ka nuksan ho sakta hai. Premium kam karna ho to voluntary deductible badhaiye.

Zero depreciation cover kab tak lena chahiye?

Aam taur par gaadi ke pehle 5 saal tak, khaas kar mehngi gaadi aur roz chalne wali gaadi par. Bahut purani gaadi par premium faayde se zyada pad jaata hai.

No Claim Bonus nayi gaadi par mil sakta hai?

Haan. NCB gaadi ka nahi, aapka hota hai. Purani policy se certificate lekar naye vahan ki policy par le jaya ja sakta hai.

Chhota claim lena theek hai?

Aam taur par nahi. Agar claim ki rakam agle do saal ke premium ke nuksan se kam hai, to apni jeb se karwana sasta padta hai.
