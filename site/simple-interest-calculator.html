Number kaise nikalta hai

Simple interest me har saal barabar byaj banta hai. ₹1 lakh par 8% saalana matlab har saal ₹8,000 — chahe 2 saal ho ya 20. Compound interest me byaj bhi mool me jud kar aage byaj kamata hai, isliye samay badhne par dono ka farak tezi se badhta jaata hai.

Formula: SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100
Kul rakam = P + SI
P = mool, R = saalana dar, T = saal

Asli zindagi ki teen misaal

Misaal 1 — lamba samay. ₹4,00,000 par 8% dar, 35 saal. Byaj = 4,00,000 × 8 × 35 ÷ 100 = ₹11,20,000, aur kul rakam ₹15,20,000.

Misaal 2 — chhota lenden. ₹50,000, 12% dar, 2 saal. Byaj ₹12,000, kul ₹62,000.

Misaal 3 — simple vs compound. ₹1 lakh, 10% dar, 10 saal. Simple interest se byaj ₹1,00,000 (kul ₹2 lakh). Wahi compound (saalana) se kul lagbhag ₹2,59,000. Yaani ₹59,000 ka farak sirf byaj jodne ke tareeke se.

Ye galtiyan mat kijiye

  1. Mahine ki dar ko saalana samajh lena. "2% byaj" bolne wale aksar mahine ka matlab rakhte hain — jo saal ka 24% hota hai. Hamesha poochhein ki dar mahine ki hai ya saal ki.
  2. Adhoore saal ka hisaab galat lagana. 8 mahine ka matlab 0.67 saal hota hai. Poora saal ginne se byaj zyada nikal aata hai.
  3. Loan ko simple maan lena. Zyadatar bank loan reducing balance par chalte hain, simple interest par nahi. EMI wale loan ke liye EMI calculator istemaal karein.
  4. Likhit kaagaz na banana. Nijee lenden me rakam, dar, tareekh aur wapasi ka samay likhit me rakhein — baad ka jhagda bach jaata hai.
  5. Bahut oonchi dar maan lena. Mahine 5-10% maangne wale saal ka 60-120% le rahe hote hain. Aise lenden se door rahein.

Bharatiya sthiti me khaas baatein

  • Kahan lagta hai: Kai gold loan, kuch vehicle loan, chhoti avadhi ke nijee lenden aur padhai ke sawaal.
  • Bank loan: Home aur personal loan aam taur par reducing balance par hote hain — wahan EMI calculator theek rehta hai.
  • FD/RD: Bank inpar compounding karte hain, isliye simple interest ka number kam nikalta hai.
  • Flat rate ka jaal: "Flat 6%" sunne me sasta lagta hai, par reducing balance me ye lagbhag 11-12% ke barabar padta hai.
  • Likhawat: Kisi ko udhaar dete waqt tareekh, dar aur rakam likhit me lein.

Byaj ke aam sawaal — sainkda, mahina aur saal

Log ise kai naam se khojte hain — byaj calculator, sadharan byaj calculator, simple byaj, monthly byaj. Sab ek hi hisaab hain, bas dekhne ka tarika alag hai.

Sadharan byaj = Mool × Dar × Samay ÷ 100
Mahine ka byaj = Saal ka byaj ÷ 12

Gaon ka "rupaye sainkda" hisaab

Bharat ke bade hisse me byaj aaj bhi rupaye sainkda per mahina me bola jaata hai. "Do rupaye sainkda" ka matlab hai ₹100 par ₹2 per mahina.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Simple interest ka formula kya hai?

SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100, jahan P mool, R saalana dar aur T saal hai.

Simple aur compound interest me kya farak hai?

Simple me byaj sirf mool par lagta hai. Compound me bana hua byaj bhi aage byaj kamata hai.

Mahine ka byaj kaise nikaalein?

Saalana dar ko 12 se bhaag dein. 12% saalana = 1% mahina.

Kya bank FD simple interest deti hai?

Nahi, zyadatar bank quarterly compounding karte hain.

Aadhe saal ka byaj kaise ginein?

T me 0.5 daalein. 6 mahine = 0.5 saal.

Byaj kaise nikalte hain?

Sadharan byaj = mool × dar × samay ÷ 100. ₹1 lakh par 12% saalana ka matlab saal ka ₹12,000 aur mahine ka ₹1,000.

Do rupaye sainkda ka matlab kya hai?

₹100 par ₹2 per mahina — yaani 24% saalana. Sunne me chhota lagta hai par ye bank ke personal loan se lagbhag dugna hai.
