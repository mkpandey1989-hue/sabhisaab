Uttarkashi me ghumne ke liye aapko ek hi cheez chunni nahi hai, kyonki yahan ek saath yatra (Gangotri-Yamunotri), trek (Har Ki Dun, Kedarkantha, Dayara, Dodital, Gaumukh), seema-darshan (Nelong, Gartang Gali), aur gaon ka ghar-jaisa tourism (Harsil, Raithal, Sankri) sab milta hai. Is hisse mein hum zile ki ghumne ki jagahon, trek ki jaankari, adventure ke vikalp, 3-5-7-10 din ke plan aur mahine-ke-hisaab se guide ko likh rahe hain.

Ek zaroori baat: Yahan likhi permit, fee, mausam aur raste ki sthiti saal-dar-saal badalti hai. Hum jahan koi figure ya niyam likh rahe hain, wahan uska saal ya source saath me likh rahe hain. Jaane se pehle Gangotri National Park, Uttarkashi Forest Division, zila prashasan aur Char Dham yatra ke official portal se taaza jaankari zaroor lein. Trek aur oonche ilaake me jaane se pehle apni sehat, mausam aur guide ka dhyan rakhein.

Uttarkashi: Khali Gaon Aur Heritage Walks

Nelong Aur Jadung: Seema Ke Khali Gaon

Nelong aur Jadung seema ke wo gaon hain jinhe 1962 ke yuddh ke baad khali karwa liya gaya tha. Gaon walon ke purvaj lagbhag 1720 ke aas-paas yahan base maane jaate hain, aur baad me unhe Harsil aur Bagori jaise sthan par basaya gaya.

Aaj ye ghati charcha me hai, kyonki seema-paryatan ko badhava dene ki yojnayein bani hain. Ek report ke anusaar Nelong-Jadung Bharat-China seema (LAC) se lagbhag 45 km door hain, aur ghati me aane-jaane ke niyam ko aasaan karne ki charcha chal rahi thi.

Gartang Gali: Lakdi ka ek lamba raasta jo chattan ki khadi deewar par bana hai. Ise lagbhag 150 saal puraana bataya jata hai, aur lok-kathan ke anusaar ise Peshawar ke Pathanon ne banwaya tha. Isse Bhotia samuday yak ke saath Tibet se vyapar karte the. 1962 ke baad ye raasta band raha. Kai saal baad ise sudhar kar paryatakon ke liye khola gaya (kai reports me 2021 ka zikr hai, aur 2024 me ise phir se khole jaane ka bhi). PWD dwara iski marammat par lagbhag Rs 65 lakh kharch hone ki report mili thi.

Palayan par vistrit chhan-been Uttarkashi: Basna, workation, retirement (Palayan Aur Wapsi: Uttarkashi Ki Asli Tasveer) me hogi.

Permit Aur Niyam: Jo Pehle Se Jaanna Zaroori Hai

1.Gangotri National Park: Gaumukh-Tapovan, Kedartal, Nelong, Gartang Gali jaise raaston ke liye permit lagta hai. 2026 me park April 1 ko khula, aur ek report ke anusaar Gaumukh trek ke liye roz ki seema 150 logon tak hai.

2.Govind Pashu Vihar: Har Ki Dun, Kedarkantha jaise trek ke liye Sankri aur Taluka ke forest check post par entry aur permit.

3.Dayara Bugyal: Uttarkashi Forest Division ke permit se. 2026 ki kuch reports ke anusaar bugyal par raat ke camping par rok hai aur camp jungle me neeche lagaye jaate hain. Ye sthiti badal sakti hai, isliye taaza jaankari lein (Uttarkashi: Bugyal / Payar).

4.Inner Line / seema ke ilaake: Nelong jaise kshetron ke liye alag permit lagta hai. Pehle ki reports me raat ka thaharna mana tha, aur videshi paryatak ke liye ye ghati band thi. Taaza sthiti zila prashasan aur park karyalaya se poochein.

Trek Ki Suraksha: Is Par Samjhauta Na Karein

  • Oonchai ki bimari (AMS) kisi ko bhi ho sakti hai. Sir dard, ulti ya chakkar aaye to aage na badhein, neeche utrein.
  • Mausam ghante me badal sakta hai. Rain-cover, garam kapde aur raste ka khana saath rakhein.
  • Akele na jayein. Registered guide aur operator ke saath jayein, khaaskar Kedartal, Kalindi Khal, Bali Pass jaise kathin trek par.
  • Mobile network oonchai par nahi hota. Apni yojana kisi ko batakar jayein.
  • Beema aur emergency: Oonche trek ke liye travel insurance aur SOS ki vyavastha dekh lein.
  • Pichhle saal ki ghatnayein: Kedartal aur Kalindi Khal jaise kshetron me trekkers ke laapata hone ki khabrein aayi hain (Uttarkashi: Bhugol, nadiyan aur pahad, Unclimbed Peaks, Sacred Peaks Aur Mountaineering Rules). Isliye kathin raaston ke liye ye zaroori hai ki aap puri taiyari, permit aur anubhavi guide ke saath jaayein.

Tapovan se Aage Nandanvan: Gangotri Glacier ke Paar ka Trek

Seedha jawab: Gaumukh-Tapovan trek ke baad kuch anubhavi trekker Gangotri glacier paar karke Nandanvan tak jaate hain, jo Bhagirathi chotiyon ke saamne ek ooncha ghaas ka maidan hai. Uttarkashi: Mountaineering, Rock Climbing Aur Bouldering

NIM Aur Pahadi Abhiyan

Uttarkashi shahar me Nehru Institute of Mountaineering (NIM) hai, jo 1965 me sthapit hui thi aur Bharat ka ek pramukh mountaineering training sansthan hai. Zile ki kai chotiyan mountaineering ke liye jaani jaati hain.

Zaroori niyam:

1.Chotti ke liye IMF (Indian Mountaineering Foundation) ki anumati leni padti hai.

2.National Park ke andar Forest Department ka permit lagta hai.

3.Guide, porter aur camp ke liye registered operator le jana behtar hai.

4.NIM basic aur advanced jaise kai course chalata hai. Courses ki taaza list aur fees NIM ki website se dekhein.

Porter aur HAP (High Altitude Porter) ki dar bhi badalti rehti hai.

Uttarkashi: Sardi ke Khel

Snow Trek Aur Skiing

Sardi me Uttarkashi ke Dayara Bugyal aur Kedarkantha jaise kshetra barf se dhak jaate hain, aur ye snow trek ke liye prasiddh hain. Kai sources Dayara Bugyal ko skiing ke liye bhi batate hain.

  • Kedarkantha sardi ka sabse mashhoor trek hai, jisme barf se dhake pine-oak ke jungle aur Himalaya ke nazaare dikhte hain.
  • Dayara Bugyal sardi me barf se dhak jata hai. Kai sources isme skiing ka zikr karte hain.

Dayara ya Kedarkantha me skiing aam taur par ek anaupcharik (informal) gatividhi hai, aur iske liye pehle se guide aur safety ki vyavastha zaroori hai.

Sardi ke trek ki safety: Barf me raasta bhatakna, thand lagna (hypothermia) aur himdaah (frostbite) ke khatre hote hain. Garam kapde ki parat, gaiter, crampon ya micro-spikes aur experienced guide ke bina na jayein.

Uttarkashi: Water Sports Aur Angling

Rafting Aur Machhli Pakadna

Uttarkashi me rafting ke bare me alag-alag websites alag baat likhti hain: kuch Bhagirathi par rafting ka zikr karti hain, kuch Mori ya Yamuna ka.

Rafting ke liye Rishikesh (jo Uttarkashi zile ke bahar hai) alag se prasiddh hai. Agar aap Uttarkashi ke kisi nadi-kshetra me rafting karna chahte hain, to sirf Uttarakhand Paryatan Vibhag me panjikrit operator chunein, life jacket aur helmet zaroor pehnein, aur barsaat me kabhi na jayein.

Angling (machhli pakadna): Dodital me trout machhli ka zikr milta hai. Uttarakhand me machhli pakadne ke liye Machhli Palan / Forest Vibhag ke niyam aur anumati lagti hai, aur kai sanrakshit kshetron me ye mana hota hai. Bina anumati kisi bhi jheel ya nadi me machhli na pakdein.

Uttarkashi: Paragliding, MTB Aur Camping

Aero Sports, Cycling Aur Camping

Agar aap kisi operator se paragliding ya anya aero sport ke liye sampark kar rahe hain, to ye zaroor check karein:

1.Operator Uttarakhand Paryatan Vibhag ya sambandhit adhikaran me panjikrit hai.

2.Pilot ke paas valid license aur anubhav hai.

3.Beema (insurance) ki vyavastha hai.

4.Mausam aur hawa ki sthiti sahi hai.

Camping: Trek ke raaston par Forest Department ke maanya camp-sthal hote hain. Bugyal par raat ke camping par rok ka zikr Uttarkashi: Bugyal / Payar aur Permit Aur Niyam: Jo Pehle Se Jaanna Zaroori Hai me hai. Nadi ke kinare ya gadhere ke paas camp lagana khatarnak hai (Local Gad, Gadhere Aur Gorge Formations). Camp me aag sirf anumati wali jagah par jalayein, aur kachra wapas le jayein.

Uttarkashi: Chhote Sawal-Jawab

Uttarkashi me ghumne ki sabse achhi jagah kaun si hai?
Gangotri, Yamunotri, Harsil, Dayara Bugyal, Dodital, Har Ki Dun aur Kedarkantha sabse zyada dekhi jaane wali jagahein hain.
Uttarkashi se Gangotri kitni door hai?
Lagbhag 99 se 100 km.

Uttarkashi se Gartang Gali kitni door hai? Lagbhag 85 se 90 km.

Gartang Gali kya hai?
Gartang Gali Nelong ghati me chattan par bana lagbhag 150 saal puraana lakdi ka raasta hai, jo kabhi Tibet se vyapar ka marg tha.
Gartang Gali ka entry fee kitna hai?
2024 ki ek report me Bharatiya paryatak ke liye Rs 150 aur videshi ke liye Rs 600 bataya gaya tha, par ye rate badal sakte hain.
Nelong ghati me raat ruk sakte hain kya?
Pehle ki kai reports ke anusaar Nelong ghati me raat ka thaharna mana hai, par niyam badal sakte hain, isliye park karyalaya se poochein.
Kya videshi paryatak Nelong ghati ja sakte hain?
Kai reports ke anusaar ye ghati videshi paryatakon ke liye band hai, par niyam badal sakte hain, isliye zila prashasan se poochein.
Gangotri National Park 2026 me kab khula?
Ek report ke anusaar Gangotri National Park 1 April 2026 ko khula.
Gaumukh trek ke liye roz kitne log ja sakte hain?
Ek report ke anusaar roz ki seema 150 logon tak hai.
Gaumukh-Tapovan trek kitne din ka hai?
Lagbhag 5 din (lagbhag 38 km), aur kai operators 7 se 8 din ka package bhi dete hain.
Har Ki Dun trek kitne din ka hai?
Aam taur par 5 se 7 din.
Har Ki Dun trek ka base camp kahan hai?
Sankri (lagbhag 1,950 meter), jo Mori block me hai.
Har Ki Dun trek kitna kathin hai?
Aam taur par aasan se madhyam, par Ruinsara Tal ke saath gol raasta madhyam se kathin maana jata hai.
Kedarkantha trek kitne din ka hai?
Aam taur par 4 din (Sankri se gol chakkar, lagbhag 17 km).
Kedarkantha ki oonchai kitni hai?
Kedarkantha shikhar lagbhag 3,800 meter ka hai.
Dayara Bugyal trek kitne din ka hai?
Aam taur par 3 se 4 din.
Dayara Bugyal ke liye base gaon kaun sa hai?
Raithal (aur doosre raaste se Barsu).
Kya Dayara Bugyal par raat ko camping kar sakte hain?
2026 ki kuch reports ke anusaar bugyal par raat ke camping par rok hai aur camp neeche jungle me lagaye jaate hain. Taaza jaankari Uttarkashi Forest Division se lein.
Dodital trek kahan se shuru hota hai?
Agoda gaon se (Sangamchatti ke raaste).
Dodital-Darwa Top trek kitne din ka hai?
Aam taur par 3 din (gol raasta lagbhag 39 km).
Yamunotri ka paidal raasta kitna lamba hai?
Janki Chatti se lagbhag 5 se 6 km (kai sources anusaar).
Kedartal trek kaisa hai?
Kedartal Gangotri kshetra ka kathin aur oonchai wala trek hai, aur isme anubhavi guide aur permit zaroori hain.
Kya Kalindi Khal sabke liye hai?
Nahi, Kalindi Khal bahut kathin aur kewal anubhavi trekkers ke liye hai.
Nehru Institute of Mountaineering kahan hai?
Uttarkashi shahar me, aur ye 1965 me sthapit hui thi.
Mountaineering ke liye kis permit ki zaroorat hoti hai?
Indian Mountaineering Foundation (IMF) ki anumati aur Forest Department ka permit.
Uttarkashi me skiing hoti hai kya?
Kuch sources Dayara Bugyal ko skiing ke liye batate hain, par yahan koi sarkari ski resort ya chairlift verified nahi hai.

Uttarkashi me rafting hoti hai kya? Alag-alag websites alag baat likhti hain.

Dodital me machhli pakad sakte hain kya?
Bina Machhli Vibhag ya Forest Department ki anumati ke kisi bhi jheel me machhli na pakdein.
Gangotri 2026 me kab band hua?
Gangotri Dham 10 November 2026 ko Annakoot ke din winter ke liye band hua.

Yamunotri 2026 me kab band hua? Yamunotri Dham 11 November 2026 ko Bhai Dooj ke din winter ke liye band hua.

Gangotri aur Yamunotri 2026 me kab khule?
Dono dham 19 April 2026 ko Akshaya Tritiya par khule.
Gangotri band hone ke baad darshan kahan hote hain?
Sardi me Maa Ganga ki pooja Mukhba (Harsil ke paas) me hoti hai.

Yamunotri band hone ke baad darshan kahan hote hain? Sardi me Maa Yamuna ki pooja Kharsali gaon me hoti hai.

Uttarkashi jaane ka sabse achha mahina kaun sa hai?
Trek aur yatra ke liye aam taur par May-June aur September-October ko achha samay maana jata hai.
Barish me Uttarkashi jaana chahiye ya nahi?
July-August me bhuskhalan aur raste band hone ka khatra zyada hota hai, isliye is samay yatra aur trek se bachna behtar hai.
Dharali aur Harsil abhi ghoomne layak hain kya?
2025 ki baadh ne kshetra ko nuksan pahunchaya, isliye hotel aur raste ki taaza sthiti poochkar hi jayein.
Oonchai ki bimari (AMS) ke lakshan kya hain?
Sir dard, ulti, chakkar aur thakan. Aise lakshan me aage na badhein, neeche utrein aur doctor se milein.
Uttarkashi me winter me kya karein?
Snow trek (Dayara, Kedarkantha), Magh Mela, aur Mukhba aur Kharsali me winter darshan.
Uttarkashi 3 din me kaise ghoomein?
Pehle din Uttarkashi ke mandir, doosre din Harsil-Mukhba, aur teesre din Gangnani hokar wapsi ek achha plan hai.

Uttarkashi: Ghumne ki jagah aur trek: Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

उत्तरकाशी में घूमने की सबसे अच्छी जगह कौन सी है?
गंगोत्री, यमुनोत्री, हर्षिल, दयारा बुग्याल, डोडीताल, हर की दून और केदारकांठा।
उत्तरकाशी से गंगोत्री कितनी दूर है?
लगभग 99 से 100 किमी।
गरतांग गली क्या है?
नेलांग घाटी में चट्टान पर बना लगभग 150 साल पुराना लकड़ी का रास्ता, जो कभी तिब्बत से व्यापार का मार्ग था।
हर की दून ट्रेक कितने दिन का है?
आम तौर पर 5 से 7 दिन; बेस कैंप सांकरी (लगभग 1,950 मीटर, मोरी ब्लॉक) है।
केदारकांठा ट्रेक कितने दिन का है?
आम तौर पर 4 दिन (सांकरी से गोल चक्कर, लगभग 17 किमी)। शिखर लगभग 3,800 मीटर का है।
