Aakhri update: August 2026

Sab Hisaab par diye gaye saare tools aur jaankari sirf shaikshanik aur aam jaankari ke maqsad se hain. Inhe professional salah nahi maana jaaye.

Tax aur Sarkari Yojana

Tax, GST aur sarkari yojana ke niyam aur dar samay-samay par badalte rehte hain. Yahan diya gaya result ek anuman hai. Asli aur taaza jaankari ke liye official government website ya ek CA se confirm karein.

Zimmedari

In tools ke istemaal se hue kisi bhi nuksan, galti ya faisle ke liye Sab Hisaab ya iska maalik zimmedar nahi hoga. Har result ko istemaal se pehle khud verify karein.

Tools ki seemayein

Is website ke saare calculator aam formula par bane hain aur ek anuman dete hain. Asli rakam kai cheezon par depend karti hai jo calculator me nahi aatin — jaise bank ke apne charges, company ke niyam, aapki vyaktigat sthiti, aur samay-samay par badalne wale sarkari niyam. Isliye kisi bhi tool ka result final figure maan kar faisla na lein.

Sarkari niyam badalte rehte hain

Tax slab, byaj dar, chhoot ki seema aur yojanaon ke niyam sarkar samay-samay par badalti hai. Hum jaankari update karte rehte hain, par ho sakta hai kisi page par purani jaankari reh jaaye. Isliye taaza jaankari ke liye hamesha official website (jaise incometax.gov.in, gst.gov.in, epfindia.gov.in) dekhein ya apne CA/salahkaar se confirm karein.

Nivesh aur trading ka jokhim

Share bazaar, mutual fund aur F&O me nivesh me jokhim hota hai aur poora paisa doob sakta hai. Pichhle return bhavishya ki garanti nahi hote. Hum SEBI-registered advisor nahi hain, koi tips nahi dete aur kisi bhi stock, fund ya scheme ki sifarish nahi karte.

Sehat se judi jaankari

BMI, calorie aur doosre sehat ke tools sirf aam jaankari ke liye hain. Ye kisi doctor ki jaanch ya salah ki jagah nahi le sakte. Sehat se juda koi bhi faisla lene se pehle apne doctor se salah lein.

Baahri link

Kuch pages par doosri websites ke link ho sakte hain. Un websites ki content, policy ya suraksha ke liye hum zimmedar nahi hain.

Galti mile to

Agar aapko kisi tool ya guide me galti lage, to Contact page se zaroor batayein. Aisi shikayat sabse pehle dekhi jaati hai aur jald se jald theek ki jaati hai.
