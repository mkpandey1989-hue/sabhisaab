Videsh paisa bhejte waqt bank ek hissa TCS ke roop me kaat leta hai. Ye extra tax nahi hai — ye aapke apne income tax me adjust ho jaata hai. Par jeb se to jaata hi hai, isliye pehle se pata hona chahiye.

Neeche rakam aur maksad chuniye. Kitna TCS katega aur bank se kitna kul dena padega — dono dikh jayega.

Abhi ke rate — ek nazar me

Rate maksad par nirbhar karta hai, rakam par nahi. Yahi baat sabse zyada galatfehmi paida karti hai.

Do baatein dhyaan me rakhiye. Pehli — 10 lakh ki seema poore saal ki hai, har transaction ki nahi. Doosri — wo aapke PAN par jodi jaati hai, isliye alag bank se bhejne par bhi seema wahi rehti hai.

Education loan wali chhoot sabse kam istemaal hoti hai

Ye TCS ka sabse bada aur sabse kam pehchana gaya faayda hai. Agar videsh ki fees kisi manyata praapt sansthan ke education loan se ja rahi hai, to TCS bilkul nahi lagta — rakam chahe jitni ho.

18 lakh ki fees apne paise se bheji to 8 lakh par 2% yaani 16,000 TCS. Wahi fees education loan se gayi to shunya. Aur agar ek hissa loan se bhi jaye, to utna hissa chhoot me aa jaata hai. Loan sirf paise ke liye nahi — is chhoot aur 80E ki byaj wali chhoot ke liye bhi soch sakte hain.

Shart ye hai ki loan nirdhaarit sansthan se ho aur bank ko uske kagaz dikhane hon. Bank bina kagaz dekhe chhoot nahi deta. Isliye remittance se pehle sanction letter aur disbursement ka proof taiyaar rakhiye.

Tour package ka niyam sabse alag hai

Tour package par 10 lakh wali seema laagu hi nahi hoti. Wahan pehle rupaye se hi TCS lagta hai. Par rate ab kaafi kam hai, isliye pehle ke mukable ye ab bahut kam chubhta hai.

Ek baat jo log dhyaan nahi dete: package tab maana jaata hai jab tour operator se ek saath booking ho. Agar aap flight alag, hotel alag aur visa alag lete hain aur apne card se kharch karte hain, to wo LRS ke saamanya niyam me aata hai — jahan 10 lakh ki seema lagti hai.

Airport par liya gaya forex aur travel card bhi LRS me hi gine jaate hain. Yaani wo bhi aapki saalana seema me judte hain.

TCS wapas kaise milta hai

Sabse zaroori baat — TCS aapka paisa hai, sarkar ka nahi. Wo Form 26AS aur AIS me aapke PAN ke saamne dikhta hai aur ITR me poora adjust hota hai.

  • Aapka tax TCS se zyada hai — utna kam bharna padega.
  • Aapka tax TCS se kam hai — antar refund me aa jayega.
  • Aap par tax banta hi nahi — poora TCS refund me milta hai.
Naukri karne walon ke liye ek chhota tarika hai: bade remittance ki jaankari apne HR ko de dijiye. Us sthiti me salary ka TDS us hisaab se kam ho jaata hai, aur aapka paisa saal bhar phasta nahi. Ye suvidha bahut kam log istemaal karte hain.

ITR bharne me der ho jaye to alag se fees lagti hai — wo ITR Late Fee Calculator me dekh lijiye.

Paisa bhejne se pehle chaar cheezein

Bank counter par khade hokar sochne se behtar hai ki ye pehle taiyaar ho.

  • Maksad theek se likhiye. Form A2 me likha maksad hi rate tay karta hai. Padhai ka paisa galti se saamanya me chala gaya to 2% ki jagah 20% kat jaata hai.
  • Saal ka hisaab rakhiye. 10 lakh ki seema PAN par jodi jaati hai, isliye alag bank se bhejne ka koi faayda nahi.
  • Loan ke kagaz saath rakhiye agar padhai ka paisa loan se ja raha hai.
  • LRS ki apni seema alag hai — ek saal me 2.5 lakh US dollar tak. Ye TCS se alag cheez hai, dono ko mila mat dijiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

TCS extra tax hai kya?

Nahi. Wo aapke apne income tax me adjust ho jaata hai aur zyada kata ho to refund me wapas aata hai. Wo Form 26AS aur AIS me aapke PAN ke saamne dikhta hai.

10 lakh ki seema har transaction par hai ya saal bhar ki?

Poore vitteey varsh ki, aur wo aapke PAN par jodi jaati hai. Alag-alag bank se bhejne par bhi seema wahi rehti hai.

Padhai ke liye paisa bhejne par kitna TCS lagta hai?

Apne paise se bhejne par seema ke upar wale hisse par 2%. Agar paisa nirdhaarit education loan se ja raha hai to bilkul nahi lagta.

Tour package par bhi 10 lakh ki chhoot milti hai?

Nahi. Tour package par seema laagu nahi hoti, wahan pehle rupaye se hi TCS lagta hai. Flight aur hotel alag-alag lene par niyam alag ho jaata hai.

Airport se liya forex bhi ismein aata hai?

Haan. Forex card aur nakad videshi mudra bhi LRS me hi gine jaate hain aur aapki saalana seema me judte hain.
