Goal Ke Liye Kitni SIP Chahiye? Poori Guide

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~7 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Ghar ka down payment, bachche ki padhai, ya retirement — har bade lakshya ke liye ek sawaal hota hai: "har mahine kitna daaloon?" SIP ise aasaan banati hai. Is guide me hum goal-based SIP planning samjhenge — target se ulta chal kar monthly SIP kaise nikaalein aur kin baaton ka dhyaan rakhein.

Goal-based SIP kya hai?

Aam SIP me log "5,000 mahina daal deta hoon" sochte hain, bina ye jaane ki aakhir me kitna banega. Goal-based SIP ulti soch hai — pehle target tay karo (jaise 10 saal me ₹20 lakh), phir hisaab lagao ki uske liye har mahine kitni SIP chahiye.

Ye tarika zyada asardaar hai kyunki aapko pata hota hai ki aap kis lakshya ke liye nivesh kar rahe hain. Har goal (ghar, gaadi, padhai, retirement) ke liye alag SIP rakhna planning ko saaf aur trackable banata hai — aur motivation bhi banaye rakhta hai.

Monthly SIP kaise nikalti hai?

Ye SIP formula ko ulta chala kar kaam karta hai. Normal me monthly amount se maturity nikalti hai; goal-based me maturity (target) se monthly amount.

Zaroori cheezein:
Target rakam (kitna chahiye)
Samay (kitne saal)
Anumaanit return (equity me aam ~12%)

Misaal: 10 saal me ₹20 lakh chahiye, 12% return maan kar — monthly SIP lagbhag ₹8,700 aati hai. Calculator ye hisaab turant kar deta hai, aap sirf teen number daalein aur apna target monthly nivesh jaan lein.

Jaldi shuru karne ka jaadu

Compounding ka sabse bada fayda samay se aata hai. Same target ke liye jitne zyada saal, utni kam monthly SIP chahiye.

₹20 lakh ka goal, 12% return:
10 saal me: ~₹8,700/mah
15 saal me: sirf ~₹4,000/mah

Sirf 5 saal jaldi shuru karne se monthly SIP lagbhag aadhi ho jaati hai! Isi liye kaha jaata hai — sabse accha samay kal tha, aur uske baad aaj hai. Chhoti rakam se hi sahi, par shuruaat aaj karein — der compounding ka fayda kha jaati hai.

Step-up SIP se aur aasaan

Har saal aapki salary badhti hai, to SIP kyun na badhe? Step-up SIP me aap har saal SIP thodi (jaise 10%) badha dete hain. Isse same goal kam shuruaati SIP me mil jaata hai.

Misaal: ₹8,700 fix ki jagah aap ₹6,000 se shuru karke har saal 10% badhaayein — to bhi wahi ₹20 lakh ka goal pura ho sakta hai, aur shuruaat aasaan rehti hai. Jaise-jaise income badhti hai, SIP badhti hai — ye natural aur tikaoo tarika hai jo budget par ek saath bojh nahi daalta.

Mehngai aur return ka dhyaan

Do zaroori baatein. Pehli — mehngai (inflation). 10 saal baad ₹20 lakh ki value aaj se kam hogi. Isliye target ko thoda bada rakhein, ya real (inflation-adjusted) return se plan karein taaki aapka lakshya us waqt sach me poora ho.

Doosri — return ka anuman. Equity me lambe samay (7+ saal) me ~12% aam raha hai, par ye guarantee nahi. 18-20% maan kar plan banana risky hai. Aur sabse badi galti — market girne par SIP band karna. Tabhi to sasti units milti hain; SIP ka poora fayda lambe samay tak lagataar chalane me hai, na ki ghabra kar rukne me.

Saath me ye bhi kaam aayega — SIP Goal Calculator aur SIP Calculator zaroor dekh lein — hisaab aur samajh dono ek jagah mil jaate hain.

Lakshya ki raqam aaj ki nahi, us saal ki lein

Goal SIP me sabse aam galti yahi hai — log aaj ka kharcha likh dete hain. 15 saal baad wahi cheez kaafi mehngi ho jaati hai, aur SIP kam pad jaati hai.

Aaj ka kharcha6% mehngai par 10 saal baad15 saal baad
₹5 lakh (shaadi)~₹8.95 lakh~₹11.98 lakh
₹10 lakh (padhai)~₹17.9 lakh~₹23.9 lakh
₹25 lakh (ghar ka down payment)~₹44.8 lakh~₹59.9 lakh
Padhai ke liye 6% bhi kam hai: school aur college ki fees aam mehngai se tez badhti hai — aksar 8% se 10% saalana. Isliye bachche ki padhai ka lakshya banate waqt 10% ka anuman lena zyada surakshit hai. Doosri baat: lakshya jitna paas ho, utna kam risk. 3 saal me chahiye to equity SIP theek nahi — us waqt bazaar gira to poora plan bigadta hai. 3 saal se kam ke lakshya ke liye RD ya debt fund, 7 saal se zyada ke liye equity — yahi seedha niyam hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Goal ke liye kitni SIP chahiye kaise pata karein?

Target, saal aur return daalein — calculator zaroori monthly SIP nikaal deta hai.

Kitna return maanein?

Equity me lambe samay ke liye ~12% aam hai, par ye guarantee nahi.

Jaldi shuru karne se kya farak?

Bahut. 5 saal zyada milne par monthly SIP lagbhag aadhi ho sakti hai.

Step-up SIP kya hai?

Har saal SIP thodi badhaana — same goal kam shuruaati SIP me mil jaata hai.

Mehngai ka dhyaan kaise rakhein?

Target ko thoda bada rakhein, kyunki bhavishya me paise ki value aaj se kam hogi.
