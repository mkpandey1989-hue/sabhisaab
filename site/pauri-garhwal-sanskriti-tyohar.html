Is hisse ka maksad: Pauri Garhwal ki asli pehchan yahan ke log, unki boli, ghar, pehnava, geet, naach, tyohar aur mele hain. Is hisse mein hum Garhwali samaj ko bahut kareeb se samjhenge. Pauri Garhwal ki sanskriti poore Garhwal ki sanskriti ka hissa hai, par yahan Srinagar-Pauri-Kotdwar-Lansdowne ke apne rang bhi hain.

Imaandari ka niyam: Pahadi riti-riwaj gaon, patti aur parivaar ke hisab se badal jaate hain. Jo baat poore Garhwal me samaan hai use “Garhwali” kaha gaya hai, aur jo Kumaon ki khaas parampara hai (jaise Aipan, Chholiya, Ghughutiya) use saaf bataya gaya hai.

Pauri Garhwal: Aabadi, Samuday aur Anusuchit Janjati

Seedha jawab: Census 2011 ke anusaar Pauri Garhwal ki abadi 6,87,271 hai, jisme lagbhag 87% log gaon me aur lagbhag 12.89% shehri ilako me rehte hain. Sex ratio 1,103 mahilayein prati 1,000 purush hai (male palayan ki wajah se). Saksharta 82.02% hai. Scheduled Caste lagbhag 17.8% aur Scheduled Tribe sirf 0.32% hain.

Ye aankde kya kehte hain?

  • Mahila adhik kyon? Pauri Garhwal ke bahut se purush Army, Paramilitary, hotel aur shehri naukriyon me bahar rehte hain. Isliye gaon me mahilayein, bujurg aur bachche zyada dikhte hain.
  • Aabadi ghat rahi hai: 2001 se 2011 ke beech Pauri Garhwal un zilon me tha jahan aabadi ghati. Ye “Palayan” (migration) ka sabse bada sabut hai.
  • Mahilayein hi kheti sambhalti hain: Pahadi gaon ki arthvyavastha ka bada bojh mahilaon par rehta hai, aur isiliye “Mahila Mangal Dal” jaisi sansthayein bahut important hain (Pauri Garhwal: NGOs, SHGs aur Mahila Mangal Dal).
  • Kotdwar ka alag rang: Bhabar ke Kotdwar kshetra me plains jaise mishrit samaj milta hai (Hindi/Urdu bolne wale, Muslim aabadi ka bada hissa yahin hai), jabki ooncha pahadi kshetra pratyaksh Garhwali bhasha-bhashi hai.

Pauri Garhwal: Samaj ki Banawat, Upnaam aur Gotra

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal me mukhya roop se Brahmin (jaise Nautiyal, Dhasmana, Dobhal, Kukreti, Bahuguna, Dabral), Rajput (Rawat, Negi, Bisht, Bhandari, Rautela, Kandari) aur Shilpkar (Arya, Das jaise) samuday hain. Mool Niwas (Thaat) ki pehchan aur gotra-anusar vivah niyam pahadi samaj ki jad hain.

Mool Gaon (Mool Niwas / Thaat) ki Pehchan aur Vivah ki Paramparayein

Seedha jawab: Pahadi samaj me har parivaar ka ek “Mool Niwas” (Thaat) gaon hota hai, aur parichay ki shuruat aksar “Aap kis gaon ke ho?” se hoti hai. Vivah me saamanya taur par apne gotra aur nikat sambandh me vivah nahi hota; ye niyam alag-alag parivar aur samuday me thoda alag ho sakte hain.

Mool Niwas ka mahatva:

  • Pehchan ka sawal: “Aap kaunse pargana/patti/gaon ke ho?” Pauri Garhwal me bahut common hai.
  • Kul-Devta aur Gram-Devta: Har parivaar ka apna Kul-Devta aur gaon ka Gram-Devta hota hai, jiske Thaan (mandir) par saal me ek baar pooja hoti hai.
  • Zameen ka rishta: Mool Niwas ki zameen aaj bhi Gol-Khata (joint khata) me hoti hai, jiska zikr hum Pauri Garhwal: Pahadi Land Classification & Revenue Vocabulary me kar chuke hain.
  • Palayan ke baad bhi jud’av: Delhi, Mumbai ya Dehradun me base log aaj bhi tyohar aur Devta-pooja ke liye gaon aate hain. Isi liye “Reverse Migration” ki kahaniyan (Pauri Garhwal: Palayan Vapasi ki Safal Kahaniyan) bhi bahut hain.

Vivah ki saamanya soch (jo har jagah ek jaisi nahi hoti): Saga ya ek hi gotra me vivah aamtaur par varjit mana jata hai. Pehle pehal “Patti/Valley” ke aadhar par rishta dhoondha jata tha, ab shehron me rehne wale parivaar zyada vyapak rishte dhoondhte hain.

Pauri Garhwal: Bhasha, Up-boliyan aur Roz ke Shabd

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ki mukhya boli Garhwali hai (lagbhag 83.3% logon ki matribhasha). Srinagar ke aas-paas bolli jaane wali Srinagariya ko Garhwali ka “mankit/standard” roop mana jata hai. Salani (Malla, Talla aur Ganga Salan kshetra), Rathwali/Rathi (Chandpur, Devalgarh) aur Chandpuri bhi Pauri ke ilake ki boliyan hain.

Pauri Garhwal ki Sthaniya Boliyan

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ki mukhya boliyan Srinagariya, Salani aur Rathi hain. Salani ko Garhwali ki sabse zyada boli jaane wali boliyon me ginte hain aur ye Pauri Garhwal ke Salan kshetra me hai. Chandpur block ki boli Chandpuri hai. Badhani, Dasholiya, Majhkumaiya, Soriyali, Gangoli jaise naam doosre kshetron (Chamoli, Tehri, Kumaon) ke hain.

Pauri Garhwal: Vernacular Pahadi Architecture (Pahadi Ghar)

Seedha jawab: Paramparik Garhwali ghar patthar aur lakdi se banta hai, jisme neeche Goth/Obra (pashu), upar rehne ke kamre, Chaja/Tibari (nakkashidaar baithak) aur Kholi (nakkashidaar chaukhat) hoti hai. Chhat me Pathal (slate) lagti thi. Koti-Banal jaisi bhukamp-rodhak lakdi-patthar shaili mukhya roop se Bhagirathi aur upper Garhwal kshetra ki hai.

Koti Banal Shaili (900 Saal Purani Bhukamp-Rodhi Imarat), Bakhli, Pathal ki Chhat aur Devdar ki Nakkashi

Seedha jawab: Koti-Banal shaili me patthar ki parat aur deodar ki lakdi ki patti ek saath chunkar bhukamp se bachne wali imarat banai jaati hai (Uttarkashi/Tehri/Rudraprayag kshetra me prasiddh). Pauri Garhwal ke purane gharon me bhi patthar-lakdi ka milaan, Pathal ki chhat aur nakkashidaar Kholi milti hai. Bakhli me kai ghar ek lambe aangan ke saath juday hote hain.

Pahadi ghar ki “science”:

  • Bhukamp ke liye: Patthar aur lakdi ki parton ka ye milaan ek lachila dhancha banata hai.
  • Sardi ke liye: Neeche Goth me bandhe pashuon ki garmi se upar ka kamra thoda garm rehta tha.
  • Barish ke liye: Dhalawan chhat se paani aur barf phisalkar neeche aa jaati hai.

Pauri Garhwal: Paramparik Pehnawa aur Kapde

Seedha jawab: Garhwali mahilayein paramparik roop se Ghaghri-Angra, Pichora/Dhoti, Dhatu (sir ka kapda) aur Gulubandh jaise gehne pehenti thi. Purush Mirjai, Sadri, Churidar-pajama aur pahadi topi pehente the. Shadiyon me Pichora (laal-peela dupatta) khaas hai, par aaj sari, lehnga, suit aam hain.

Pauri Garhwal: Paramparik Pahadi Gehne

Seedha jawab: Garhwali gehne me Nathuli (bari nath), Gulubandh (laal makhmal par sone ke chaukor tukde wala choker), Hansuli (chaandi ka mota haar), Pauncha/Pahunchi (kalai ka gehna), Bulak (naak ke beech ka gehna), Murkhi (kaan ke chhote gehne) aur Bichhuwe (chaandi ke paon ke gehne) mukhya hain. Ye mool roop se pahadi parampara ke hain, aur shaadi me khaas hain.

Pahadi Gehne: Nathuli, Galobandh, Hansuli, Timaniya, Pahunchi aur Bulak

Seedha jawab: Nathuli Garhwal ki sabse prasiddh nath hai jo shaadi me dulhan pehenti hai. Gulubandh (Galobandh) laal/kaale makhmal par sone ke tukdon se bana choker hai. Hansuli chaandi ka thos haar hai. Pauncha kalai ki gehna hai. Bulak naak ke beech me pehni jaati hai.

Devta Doli Yatra kya hai?
Gaon ke Gram-Devta ki doli (palki) ko saal me ek ya do baar gaon ke dharmik sthalon, nadi ke kinare aur doosre gaon ke mandir tak le jaya jata hai. Dhol-Damau, Ransingha aur Jagar ke saath ye shobha-yatra nikalti hai. Is dauran gaon ke log apne ghar se bhet (daan) dete hain, aur Devta ka “Aashirwaad” lete hain.
