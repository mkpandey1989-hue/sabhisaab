English Word Meaning — Matlab, Uchcharan Aur Misaal

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~5 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

English shabd likhiye — uska matlab, uchcharan, vaakya me misaal aur milte-julte shabd mil jayenge. Ye English-to-English dictionary hai, aur neeche bataya hai ki yahi aapke liye behtar kyun hai.

English-to-English dictionary behtar kyun hai

Zyadatar log English shabd ka Hindi matlab dhoondhte hain. Wo shuruaat me theek hai, par ek jagah jaakar wahi aadat rok deti hai.

Wajah ye hai ki har English shabd ka theek Hindi jod nahi hota. Misaal ke liye diligent ka Hindi "parishrami" batata hai — par asli matlab hai "dhyaan se, lagaataar aur savdhani ke saath kaam karne wala". Sirf ek shabd se wo poora matlab nahi aata.

Aur ek badi baat — English-to-English padhne se aap shabd ko English me sochna shuru karte hain. Tab bolte waqt dimaag ko har baar Hindi se anuvaad nahi karna padta, aur bolna apne aap tez aur natural ho jaata hai. Ye interview aur presentation me sabse zyada farak laata hai.

Shuru me mushkil lagega. Ek tarika ye hai — pehle English matlab padhiye, samajh na aaye to hi Hindi dekhiye. Do-teen hafte me hi aadat ban jaati hai.

Part of speech — jo aksar galti karata hai

Har shabd ke saath uska part of speech likha hota hai. Ye sirf vyakaran ka niyam nahi — isse pata chalta hai ki shabd ko vaakya me kahan rakhna hai.

Kya likha haiMatlabMisaal
nounnaam wala shabdHe made a calculation
verbkaam wala shabdPlease calculate the total
adjectivegun batane walaa calculated risk
adverbkaam ka tarikaHe spoke calmly
Ek hi shabd kai roop me aata hai aur matlab badal jaata hai. Isi liye upar teen tak matlab dikhaye jaate hain — sirf pehla matlab padhkar mat rukiye. Kai baar aapke vaakya ke liye doosra ya teesra matlab sahi hota hai, aur yahi galti likhne me sabse zyada dikhti hai.

Shabd yaad rakhne ka tarika jo sach me chalta hai

List banakar ratna lagbhag kabhi kaam nahi karta. Jo chalta hai:

  • Vaakya me seekhiye, akele nahi. "Tenure = kaarykaal" ratna bekaar hai. "My loan tenure is 20 years" yaad rehta hai, kyunki usme matlab aur istemaal dono hain.
  • Apna vaakya banaiye — apni zindagi se juda hua. Jo shabd aapke apne kaam se judta hai, wo bhoolta nahi.
  • Dohraiye badhte antaraal par — aaj, kal, teen din baad, ek hafte baad. Isse "spaced repetition" kehte hain aur ye sabse aajmaya hua tarika hai.
  • Din me 5 shabd, roz. Hafte me ek din 50 shabd se behtar. Saal me ye lagbhag 1,800 shabd ban jaate hain.
  • Uchcharan zaroor dekhiye — galat uchcharan ek baar baith gaya to sudharna mushkil hota hai.
  • Padhte waqt likhiye — jo shabd akhbaar ya kitaab me mile, use turant dekhiye. Sandarbh ke saath aaya shabd sabse jaldi baithta hai.

Kis kaam me ye sabse zyada kaam aata hai

  • Sarkari exam — SSC, banking aur doosre exam me vocabulary, synonym aur antonym poochhe jaate hain. Yahan synonyms bhi dikhte hain.
  • Naukri ka interview — apne kaam se jude shabd ka sahi matlab aur uchcharan aatmvishwas deta hai.
  • Email aur report likhna — shabd ka theek matlab pata ho to likha hua saaf aur professional lagta hai.
  • Bacchon ka homework — matlab ke saath vaakya bhi milta hai, jo school me hi maanga jaata hai.
  • Kaagaz padhna — loan agreement, bima policy, ya kisi form me aane wale mushkil shabd. Yahan matlab jaanna sirf padhai nahi, paise ka maamla hai.
Ye ek khuli English dictionary sewa se jaankari laata hai. Kabhi-kabhi koi naya ya bahut khaas shabd nahi milta — aisa hone par spelling dobara dekh lijiye. Aur ye English shabdon ke liye hai, isliye Hindi ya Hinglish shabd yahan nahi milenge.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

English-to-English dictionary behtar kyun hai?

Kyunki har English shabd ka theek Hindi jod nahi hota, aur English me matlab padhne se aap English me sochna shuru karte hain — jisse bolna tez aur natural ho jaata hai.

Part of speech kyun zaroori hai?

Usse pata chalta hai ki shabd vaakya me kahan aayega. Ek hi shabd noun, verb aur adjective ke roop me alag matlab deta hai — isliye sirf pehla matlab padhkar mat rukiye.

Naye shabd kaise yaad rakhein?

Vaakya me seekhiye, akele nahi. Apna vaakya banaiye, aur badhte antaraal par dohraiye — aaj, kal, teen din baad, ek hafte baad. Din me 5 shabd kaafi hain.

Hindi shabd ka matlab mil jayega?

Nahi. Ye English shabdon ki dictionary hai. Hindi ya Hinglish shabd yahan nahi milenge.
