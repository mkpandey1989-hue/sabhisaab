Tehri Garhwal: Zila Parichay: Ek Nazar Mein

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal Uttarakhand ke Garhwal mandal ka ek pahadi jila hai. Iska mukhyalay New Tehri hai, vahan ka vehicle registration code UK-09 hai, aur ye Tehri Dam (bharat ka sabse oonchha baandh, 260.5 meter) ki wajah se poori duniya me jaana jata hai. Bhagirathi aur Bhilangana nadiyan yahan ki jeevan-rekha hain.

Zile ki Pehchan: Ek Nazar Mein

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal ko "Bhagirathi-Bhilangana ghati ka jila", "Tehri Dam wala jila" aur "Purane Tehri Riyasat ki virasat" ke naam se jaana jata hai. Ye Uttarakhand ke un chune hue jilon me hai jahan ek hi jagah par Himalaya ki barf, jheel, ghana jungle, Ganga ka Sangam aur rajshahi itihas milte hain.

Tehri Garhwal Uttarakhand ke Garhwal Mandal me aata hai. Iski uttar disha me Uttarkashi, dakshin me Pauri Garhwal, purv me Rudraprayag aur pashchim me Dehradun jila hai. Yani ye jila Garhwal ke lagbhag beech me baitha hai, aur isi wajah se Rishikesh, Dehradun, Mussoorie, Uttarkashi aur Kedarnath-Badrinath ke raaste me aata hai.

Tehri Garhwal ko khaas kyu banata hai?

  • Engineering ka chamatkar: Tehri Dam ne ek poora purana shahar (Old Tehri) apne andar sama liya aur uske upar ek nayi jheel bana di. Aaj usi jheel par water sports hote hain.
  • Adhyatmik ghati: Devprayag (jahan Bhagirathi aur Alaknanda milkar "Ganga" kehlati hain), Surkanda Devi, Kunjapuri, Chandrabadni aur Sem Mukhem Nagraja jaise mandir.
  • Hill station: Dhanaulti, Kanatal, Chamba aur Mussoorie ke paas ke ilake me pine, deodar aur buransh ke jungle.
  • Trekking: Nag Tibba, Sahastra Tal, Khatling Glacier, Panwali Kantha, Masar Tal aur Kedartal jaise raaste.
  • Rajshahi itihas: Panwar (Shah) vansh ka Tehri Riyasat 1815 se 1949 tak yahan raj karta raha.
  • Lok-sanskriti: Garhwali aur Jaunpuri sanskriti, Pandav Nritya, Jagar, Ramman jaisi parampara (Garhwal ke anya hisson me) aur Jaunpur ka alag lok-jeevan.

Etymology: Jile ke Naam ka Itihasik Mool

Seedha jawab: "Tehri" shabd ko aam taur par "Trihari" se joda jata hai, jiska arth hai wo sthan jo man, vachan aur karm, tino ke paapo ko dho de. "Garhwal" ka matlab hai garhon (kilon) ki bhoomi, kyunki 1803 se pehle yahan 52 chhote-chhote garh hue karte the.

"Tehri" shabd ki vyakhya:

  • Trihari: Sabse prachalit mat. Tri (teen) + Hari (harna ya door karna), yaani teeno prakar ke paapo ka haran karne wali bhoomi.
  • Brahma ki tapobhoomi: Lok-manyata hai ki shrishti se pehle Brahma ji ne yahan tapasya ki thi. District ki official site bhi isi pehlu ko pahchan ke roop me batati hai. Muni-ki-Reti aur Tapovan jaise sthan rishi-munio ki tapasya se jude maane jate hain.
  • Prayag ki pehchan: Purane Tehri shahar ka sthan Bhagirathi aur Bhilangana ke sangam par tha. Is sangam ko sthaniya parampara me Ganesh Prayag ke naam se bhi yaad kiya jata hai (sthaniya manyata).

"Garhwal" shabd ki vyakhya:

  • Garh ka arth hai kila. Wal ka arth hai wala. Yani "garhon wala pradesh."
  • Itihaskaron ke anusar rajya me 52 garh the, jinka ekikaran Raja Ajaypal ne kiya. Is baat ka vistar Tehri Garhwal: Itihas, mandir aur lok-katha (Tehri Garhwal: Panwar & Chand Kingdoms) me aayega.

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal jila August 1949 me bana, jab Tehri Riyasat ka vilay Uttar Pradesh me hua. Iske baad 1960 me Uttarkashi alag jila bana aur 1997 me Rudraprayag ke liye is jile ka kuch hissa liya gaya. 2000 me Uttarakhand rajya bana to ye usi ka jila ban gaya.

Tehri Garhwal: Collectorate & Revenue Administration (Jila Prashasan aur Rajasv)

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal ka Collectorate, Vikas Bhawan aur District Magistrate (DM) ka karyalay New Tehri me hai. Jile me kai tehsil aur up-tehsil hain, jinke neeche kanungo circle aur patwari chowkiyan aati hain.

Zila Mukhyalay, Vikas Bhawan aur Collectorate Kahan Hai

Seedha jawab: Jila mukhyalay New Tehri me hai, jo Tehri Dam ki jheel ke paas ek planned shahar hai. Yahin Collectorate, Vikas Bhawan (jila vikas se jude vibhag), District Court aur jile ke adhikansh sarkari karyalay hain.

  • Sthan: New Tehri, Tehri Dam jheel ke kinare ek oonchi pahadi par basa hai.
  • Jila Magistrate (DM): Jile ke sabse bade prashasnik adhikari.
  • Vikas Bhawan: Mukhya Vikas Adhikari (CDO) ka karyalay, jahan se block-star ke vikas karya chalte hain.
  • Kaise pahunche: Rishikesh se NH-94 ke raste Chamba hote hue New Tehri pahunchte hain. Dehradun se Mussoorie-Dhanaulti-Chamba ka raasta bhi hai.

Tehsil, Up-Tehsil aur Kanungo Circle ki Poori Suchi

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal me kai tehsil hain, jinme Tehri, Ghansali, Devprayag, Narendranagar, Kirtinagar, Pratapnagar, Jakhnidhar, Dhanaulti, Kandisaur-Nainbagh aur Gaja shamil hain. Madan Negi, Balganga (Chamiyala) aur Paw Ki Devi jaise up-tehsil bhi hain.

⚠ Dhyan dein: Uttarakhand me nayi tehsil aur up-tehsil samay-samay par ghoshit hoti rehti hain. Sources me tehsil ki sankhya 7 se 11 tak alag-alag milti hai.

Patwari Chowki (Rajasv Police aur Niyamit Police ka Ilaka)

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand ke pahadi ilakon me lambe samay tak Revenue Police ki vyavastha rahi, jisme patwari hi police ka kaam bhi sambhalte the. Ye vyavastha angrezo ke samay se chali aa rahi thi. Ab sarkar ne ise charno me regular police ko saunpne ki prakriya shuru ki hai.

Revenue Police kya hai?

  • Pahadi gaon me patwari rajasv adhikari ke saath hi chhoti-moti police shaktiyan bhi rakhta tha, jaise jhagde, chhoti chori aur ilake ki suchna.
  • 2022 ke Ankita Bhandari hatya kand (Pauri Garhwal) ke baad is vyavastha par sawal uthe, aur rajya me adhikansh ilake regular police ko dene ka nirnay liya gaya.
  • Tehri Garhwal me kaun-kaun se ilake regular police me aa chuke hain aur kaun-kaun abhi baaki hain, iski taza list ke liye SSP Tehri office ya jila prashasan se confirm karein.

Aam aadmi ke liye matlab: Agar aapka gaon revenue police ke daayre me ho, to aapko FIR ke liye patwari ya tehsil se sampark karna padta tha. Regular police ke ilake me seedha thane me jaate hain.

  • Gram Panchayat: Lagbhag 928 (Wikipedia ke aankdo ke anusar).
  • Kshetra Panchayat: Har block me ek Kshetra Panchayat hoti hai, yaani jile me 9.

Aabaad Gaon aur Khaali (Van / Bhootiya) Gaon

Seedha jawab: Census 2011 ke anusar Tehri Garhwal me lagbhag 1,862 gaon the (kuch sources 1,847 batate hain). Uttarakhand ke Palayan Aayog ke anusar rajya me 1,700 se zyada gaon "bhutiya gaon" (khali gaon) ban chuke hain. Tehri Garhwal ke apne bhutiya gaon ki sankhya ke liye Aayog ki taza report dekhein.

Palayan ke mukhya karan:

  • Rozgaar aur naukri ke liye shehron ki taraf jana.
  • Shiksha aur swasthya sewao ki kami.
  • Jangli janwaron (suar, bandar, tendua) se kheti ka nuksan.
  • Sadak aur paani ki samasya.

Reverse migration (Palayan Vapasi): Corona ke baad kai yuva wapas gaon aaye aur homestay, orchard aur dairy ka kaam shuru kiya. Iski kahaniyan Tehri Garhwal: Basna, workation, retirement (Tehri Garhwal: Palayan Vapasi : Prerna ki Kahaniyan) me hongi.

Tehri Garhwal: Political & Electoral Profile (Rajneeti aur Chunav)

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal jile ki rajneeti Tehri Garhwal Lok Sabha seat se judi hai, jiski saansad (2024 me) Mala Rajya Laxmi Shah (BJP) hain, jo Tehri ke purane shahi parivar se aati hain. Jile me 6 Vidhan Sabha seat hain.

Lok Sabha Seat aur Maujooda Saansad

  • Seat ka naam: Tehri Garhwal Lok Sabha kshetra.
  • Vistar: Ye seat sirf Tehri jile tak seemit nahi hai. Isme Tehri Garhwal ke saath Uttarkashi aur Dehradun jile ke kuch vidhan sabha kshetra bhi aate hain.
  • Saansad: 2024 ke chunav me Mala Rajya Laxmi Shah (BJP) vijayi rahi.
  • Shahi parivar ki bhumika: Tehri ke Panwar (Shah) parivar ki sadasya hone ke karan seat ka ek alag raajnaitik aur bhavnatmak mahatva hai.

Tehri Garhwal se Shuru Hue Pramukh Rajnaitik Andolan

  • Sridev Suman ka balidan (1944): Tehri Riyasat ke virudh nagrik adhikaron ki ladai. 84 din ke anshan ke baad Suman ji ka nidhan hua.
  • Rawain Dhandak (1930): Van adhikaron ko lekar Tilari kshetra ka aandolan.
  • Kirtinagar aandolan (1948): Riyasat ke ant ki ladai.
  • Tehri Baandh Virodhi Aandolan: Sunderlal Bahuguna ki aguwai me lagbhag 1980 ke dashak se 2004 tak chala aandolan.
  • Chipko aandolan: Garhwal ke jungle bachane ka aandolan, jisme Bahuguna ji ki badi bhumika thi.
  • Uttarakhand rajya aandolan (1990 ke dashak): Is jile se bhi kai aandolankari shamil hue.

Tehri Garhwal: Police, Suraksha aur Seema Bal

Seedha jawab: Jila police ka netritva SSP Tehri Garhwal karte hain. District portal ke anusar jile me 11 police station hain. Garhwal Rifles (jinka regimental centre Lansdowne me hai) me Tehri ke yuvao ka bada yogdaan raha hai.

Zile ki Police Vyavastha: Kotwali, Thane aur Chowkiyan

  • SSP karyalay: New Tehri.
  • Police station (Thana): 11 (portal ke anusar). Mukhya thane New Tehri, Narendranagar, Kirtinagar, Devprayag, Chamba, Ghansali, Pratapnagar, Muni Ki Reti, Dhanaulti jaise sthano par hain.
  • Chowki: Door-daraj ke ilako me police chowki bhi hain.
  • Emergency number: 112 (police, fire aur ambulance ke liye ekikrit).

Fauj ki Regiment (Garhwal Rifles), ITBP aur SSB Camp

  • Garhwal Rifles: Regiment ke jawan Garhwal ke jilo se aate hain, jinme Tehri bhi shamil hai. Regimental Centre Lansdowne me hai.
  • Fauj ki parampara: Tehri ke gaon-gaon me "fauji parivar" ek aam baat hai. Isi karan jile ki arthvyavastha ka bada hissa money-order economy se chalta hai (Tehri Garhwal: Arthvyavastha: GDP, Per Capita Aay aur Rozgar).
  • ITBP/SSB: Is jile me bade border camps nahi hain, kyunki ye antarrashtriya seema se door hai. Nearest border-force sthan Uttarkashi aur Dehradun ki taraf hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Nyayalay aur Kanooni Vyavastha

Zila Nyayalay, Family Court aur Muft Kanooni Sahayata

  • Jila evam Satra Nyayalaya: New Tehri.
  • Family Court aur anya adalat: Jila mukhyalay me. Court ke complex aur judges ki list ke liye jila nyayalaya ki official site dekhein.
  • District Legal Services Authority (DLSA): Muft kanooni sahayata (free legal aid) ke liye jile me DLSA hai. Zarooratmand, mahilayen, SC/ST aur kam aay wale log iska labh le sakte hain. Helpline: 15100 (National Legal Services Authority).

Sub-Registrar Daftar (Zameen Registry)

Seedha jawab: Zameen ki registry tehsil ke Sub-Registrar karyalay me hoti hai. Tehri Garhwal me tehsil mukhyalayo par Sub-Registrar karyalay hain. Registry ke liye stamp duty, circle rate aur Aadhaar-based verification zaroori hai. Is vishay ka vistar Tehri Garhwal: Registry, Stamp Duty aur Circle Rate me hai.

SDRF / NDRF ke Thikane, Baadh Aashray aur Emergency Helipad

  • SDRF: Rajya Aapda Pratikriya Bal. Tehri me tainaati aur nearest base ki jaankari DDMA se lein.
  • NDRF: Nearest bada base Dehradun ki taraf hai.
  • Helipad: New Tehri aur jile ke kuch sthano par emergency helipad hain. Char Dham yatra ke samay yahan se bhi heli sewa chalti hai.
  • Jheel aur baadh: Tehri Dam ki jheel ke star me badlav par neeche ke kshetra (Devprayag, Rishikesh) ko alert diya jata hai.

Itihas ki badi aapdayein: 1991 ka Uttarkashi bhukamp (6.8 tivrata), 2012 ki Bhagirathi ghati ki baadh aur 2013 ki Kedarnath aapda ka asar Tehri par bhi pada. Vistar ke liye Tehri Garhwal: Itihaas ki Aapdayein, Bhukamp aur Seekh dekhein.

Tehri Garhwal: Gaadi Registration (RTO) aur Parivahan Code

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal ka vehicle registration code UK-09 hai. RTO / ARTO karyalay New Tehri me hai.

RTO / ARTO Code, Daftar aur Licence Sevayein

  • Code: UK-09 (Tehri Garhwal).
  • Sewayein: Learner's license, permanent driving license, vehicle registration aur transfer, fitness, permit.
  • Online: Zyadatar sewayein Parivahan Sewa portal (parivahan.gov.in) par online uplabdh hain.
  • Pahadi Driving: Pahadi sadko par drive karne ke tips (gear, brake, overtaking) Tehri Garhwal: Kaise pahunchein, kahan rukein me aayenge.

Pahadi Driving ke Niyam aur Green Cess / Eco-Tax

  • Green Cess: Uttarakhand me bahar ke rajyo ki gaadiyo par rajya seema par "Green Cess" lagne ki vyavastha hai. Dar aur niyam samay ke sath badalte hain, isliye yatra se pehle taza jankari lein.
