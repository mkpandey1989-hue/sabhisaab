Uttarakhand ki har pariksha me zile se jude sawal aate hain. Patwari, Lekhpal, Police Constable, Forest Guard, VDO aur PCS jaisi parikshaon me Rudraprayag ke bare me poochhe gaye tathya yaad rakhna aasan hai agar woh ek jagah saaf likhe hon. Is hisse mein hamne yahi kiya hai.

Ek zaroori baat. Kuch aankdon me alag-alag sources me thoda antar milta hai, jaise literacy rate. Parikshaon ke liye hamesha latest official source aur apni coaching ke notes se bhi milaan kar lein.

Rudraprayag: UKPSC, PCS aur UKSSSC ke liye 100 One-Liner District GK

Rudraprayag ke 100 Mukhya Tathya

Rudraprayag zila 16 September 1997 ko bana, iska mukhyalaya Rudraprayag hai, aur Census 2011 me iski aabadi 2,42,285 thi. Yeh Uttarakhand ka sabse kam aabadi wala zila hai aur yahan Kedarnath, Tungnath aur Madhmaheshwar teen Panch Kedar sthit hain.

A. Sthapana, Prashasan aur Seema (1 se 15)

  • Rudraprayag zila 16 September 1997 ko bana.
  • Zile ka mukhyalaya Rudraprayag shehar hai.
  • Yeh Garhwal mandal ka hissa hai.
  • Zila Chamoli, Tehri Garhwal aur Pauri Garhwal ke hisson se milkar bana.
  • Zile me teen vikas khand hain: Ukhimath, Augustmuni aur Jakholi.
  • Census 2011 me zile me 3 tehsil the: Rudraprayag, Ukhimath aur Jakholi. Baad me Basukedar tehsil bhi bana.
  • Zile me do vidhan sabha seat hain: Kedarnath aur Rudraprayag.
  • Kedarnath vidhan sabha ki kram sankhya 07 aur Rudraprayag ki 08 hai.
  • Zila Garhwal Lok Sabha kshetra ka hissa hai.
  • Zile ka vahan panjikaran code UK 13 hai.
  • Zile ki official website rudraprayag.gov.in hai.
  • Zile ka kshetrafal Census ke anusar 1,984 varg km hai.
  • Census 2011 me zile ki aabadi 2,42,285 thi.
  • Rudraprayag 2011 me Uttarakhand ka sabse kam aabadi wala zila tha.
  • Zile ki aabadi rajya ki kul aabadi ka lagbhag 2.4% thi.

B. Janganana ke Aankde (16 se 28)

  • Zile me purush 1,14,589 aur mahilayein 1,27,696 thi.
  • Zile ka ling anupat 1,114 mahilayein prati 1,000 purush tha.
  • Yeh ling anupat rajya ke 963 se bahut zyada hai.
  • Aabadi ghanatva 122 vyakti prati varg km tha.
  • Census 2011 ke anusar saksharta lagbhag 81.3% thi, jisme purush 93.9% aur mahilayein 70.35% thi. Kuch sources me 82.09% bhi milta hai.
  • Anusuchit jaati ki aabadi lagbhag 19.68% aur anusuchit janjaati ki 0.16% thi.
  • Zile me 94.5% log Garhwali ko matri bhasha batate hain.
  • Zile me lagbhag 99% log Hindu hain.
  • 2001 se 2011 ke beech aabadi vriddhi lagbhag 6.53% rahi.
  • Census 2011 me zile me 688 gaon aur 2 shehar dikhaye gaye.
  • Zile ki lagbhag poori aabadi gramin ilakon me rehti hai.
  • NFHS-5 (2019-21) me zile ka anumanit ling anupat aur bhi zyada dikhaya gaya.
  • Zile ki jyadatar aabadi Garhwali boli bolti hai.

C. Nadiyan aur Prayag (29 se 40)

  • Rudraprayag Panch Prayag me se ek hai.
  • Panch Prayag hain: Vishnuprayag, Nandprayag, Karnaprayag, Rudraprayag aur Devprayag.
  • Rudraprayag me Alaknanda aur Mandakini ka sangam hai.
  • Zile ka naam Shiv ke Rudra roop par pada hai.
  • Kinvadanti hai ki Narad muni ne yahin Shiv ki aradhana ki thi.
  • Mandakini nadi Kedarnath ke upar ke hiv-kshetra (Chorabari glacier ke aas-paas) se nikalti hai.
  • Sonprayag me Son Ganga Mandakini se milti hai.
  • Mandakini Rudraprayag me Alaknanda se milti hai.
  • Alaknanda aage Devprayag me Bhagirathi se milkar Ganga banti hai.
  • Madhyamaheshwar Ganga Madhmaheshwar kshetra ki mukhya dhara hai.
  • Vasuki Ganga Sonprayag ke paas Mandakini me milti hai.
  • Kali Ganga Kalimath kshetra ki dhara hai.

D. Tirth aur Mandir (41 se 62)

  • Kedarnath 12 Jyotirlingon me se ek hai.
  • Kedarnath mandir lagbhag 3,583 meter ki unchai par hai.
  • Panch Kedar me Kedarnath, Tungnath, Rudranath, Madhmaheshwar aur Kalpeshwar aate hain.
  • Panch Kedar me se Kedarnath, Tungnath aur Madhmaheshwar Rudraprayag zile me hain.
  • Rudranath aur Kalpeshwar Chamoli zile me hain.
  • Tungnath lagbhag 3,680 meter par hai aur ise duniya ka sabse unchai par sthit Shiv mandir maana jata hai.
  • Chandrashila shikhar Tungnath ke upar lagbhag 4,000 meter par hai.
  • Madhmaheshwar mandir lagbhag 3,497 meter par hai.
  • Kedarnath aur Madhmaheshwar ki sheetkaleen gaddi Ukhimath ke Omkareshwar mandir me hai.
  • Tungnath ki sheetkaleen gaddi Makkumath me hai.
  • Mahashivratri par Ukhimath me Kedarnath ke kapat kholne ki taareekh ghoshit hoti hai.
  • Triyuginarayan ko Shiv-Parvati ke vivah sthal ke roop me manyata hai.
  • Triyuginarayan me akhand dhuni jalne ki manyata hai.
  • Gaurikund Kedarnath ke paidal marg ka prarambhik bindu hai.
  • Gaurikund se Kedarnath ka paidal marg lagbhag 16 km hai.
  • Guptkashi me Vishwanath mandir hai.
  • Ukhimath me Omkareshwar mandir hai.
  • Agastyamuni ka naam Agastya rishi se juda hai.
  • Kalimath ko ek pramukh Shakti sthal maana jata hai.
  • Kartik Swami mandir (Kanakchauri) ek pramukh tirth hai.
  • Koteshwar mandir Alaknanda ke tat par Rudraprayag ke paas ek gufa mandir hai.
  • Sonprayag Kedarnath marg ka ek mukhya padav hai.

E. Paryatan aur Prakritik Sthal (63 se 78)

  • Chopta ko Mini Switzerland kaha jata hai.
  • Chopta Tungnath-Chandrashila trek ka aadhar shivir hai.
  • Chopta se Tungnath ka paidal marg lagbhag 3.5 km hai.
  • Deoria Tal lagbhag 2,400 meter par ek Himalayan jheel hai.
  • Deoria Tal Sari gaon se paidal pahuncha jata hai.
  • Deoria Tal me Chaukhamba ka pratibimb dikhta hai.
  • Vasuki Tal Kedarnath ke upar ek unchi jheel hai.
  • Chorabari Tal (Gandhi Sarovar) Kedarnath ke upar sthit hai.
  • Kedarnath Wildlife Sanctuary lagbhag 975 varg km me phaila hai.
  • Kedarnath Wildlife Sanctuary ki sthapana 1972 me hui.
  • Yeh Uttarakhand ka sabse bada protected area maana jata hai.
  • Is sanctuary me Kasturi mrig (Musk Deer) milta hai.
  • Himalayan Monal, Bharal aur Himalayan Tahr bhi yahan milte hain.
  • Brahmakamal unchai par khilne wala pavitra phool hai.
  • Rudraprayag shehar lagbhag 895 meter ki unchai par hai.
  • Zile me unchai lagbhag 600 meter se 6,000 meter se zyada tak badalti hai.

F. Aapda, Itihas aur Vyakti (79 se 90)

  • Kedarnath aapda June 2013 me hui thi.
  • 2013 ki aapda ne Mandakini ghati me bahut tabahi machayi.
  • Jim Corbett ne Rudraprayag ke adamkhor tendue ko 2 May 1926 ko maara tha.
  • Is tendue ne lagbhag 1918 se 1926 tak 125 se zyada logon ko maara tha.
  • Corbett ki kitab The Man-Eating Leopard of Rudraprayag 1948 me prakashit hui.
  • Rudraprayag Kedarnath aur Badrinath ke raaste ka milan bindu hai.
  • Rishikesh se Rudraprayag lagbhag 140 km door hai.
  • Rudraprayag se Karnaprayag lagbhag 30 km door hai.
  • Rudraprayag se Guptkashi lagbhag 40 km door hai.
  • Rudraprayag se Sonprayag lagbhag 75 km door hai.
  • Mandakini par Singoli-Bhatwari (99 MW) jal vidyut pariyojna hai.
  • Mandakini par Phata-Byung (76 MW) pariyojna bhi hai.

G. Shiksha, Sanskriti aur Samanya (91 se 100)

  • JNV Rudraprayag Bansu Jakhdhar me hai.
  • Kendriya Vidyalaya Agastyamuni me hai.
  • Rajiv Gandhi Navodaya Vidyalaya Rudraprayag me hai (sthapana 2009).
  • Garhwali zile ki mukhya boli hai.
  • Jhumelo aur Jhoda Garhwal ke lok-geet aur nritya hain.
  • Pandav Nritya Garhwal ka prasiddh lok-nritya hai.
  • Jagar devta ko jagane ki gayan parampara hai.
  • Phaanu, Chainsu aur Arsa Garhwal ke prasiddh pahadi vyanjan hain.
  • Ringal pahadi bans hai, jisse tokri aur chatai banti hain.
  • Maiti Andolan ke pranetaa Kalyan Singh Rawat Chamoli zile ke hain.

Yeh 100 tathya zile ki shuruaati taiyari ke liye hain. Aap in par apne kuch nishchit sawal bana kar bhi dekh sakte hain.

Zile ke Pratham Vyaktitva aur Sthan (Firsts of Rudraprayag)

Pratham Tathya, Jo Pakke Hain

Hum yahan sirf unhi "pratham" ko likh rahe hain jinki jankari pakki hai. Jaise zila 1997 me bana, aur Rudraprayag Uttarakhand ke un zilon me hai jahan ling anupat bahut zyada hai.

Pariksha ke hisaab se "pratham" wale sawal bahut mahatvapurn hote hain.

Jo tathya pakke hain:

  • Zila 16 September 1997 ko bana, jo Uttarakhand rajya banne se pehle ka hai. Isliye is zile ki shuruat Uttar Pradesh ke samay me hui thi.
  • Rudraprayag adamkhor tendue ki kitab ki wajah se duniya bhar me pehchana gaya.
  • Kedarnath rajya ki sabse badi dharmik yatraon me se ek hai.

Rudraprayag: Coaching, Library aur Reading Room

District HQ me Taiyari Kaise Karein

Pahad me taiyari ke liye sabse badi zaroorat shaanti aur niyamit padhai hai. Rudraprayag shehar aur Guptkashi, Agastyamuni jaise kasbon me sarkari aur nijee pustakalay hain. Kai students ab online coaching aur free YouTube classes se bhi taiyari karte hain.

Taiyari ke liye aap yeh tarike apna sakte hain:

  • Zila pustakalay ya college library me jaakar niyamit padhai karein.
  • Apne dost ke saath chhota study group banayein.
  • UKPSC aur UKSSSC ke official syllabus aur pichhle saal ke papers se shuru karein.
  • Uttarakhand ka itihas, bhugol aur sanskriti ke liye state-specific kitabein padhein.
  • Net kamzor ho to papers aur notes download karke offline rakh lein.
  • Roz ke time-table me current affairs ka samay alag rakhein.

Pahad ke students ke liye ek samasya hai ki padhai ka mahaul kam hota hai. Isliye jo log shehar me rehkar taiyari karte hain, unhe wahan ki library aur rooms ki suvidha zaroor dekhni chahiye.

Rudraprayag: Army Agniveer aur Physical Training

Sena Bharti ki Taiyari

Garhwal ke yuva sena me bahut bade sankhya me jate hain. Agniveer bharti ke liye aapko pehle joinindianarmy.nic.in par online aavedan karna hota hai, phir likhit pariksha aur physical test hote hain. Aapke zile ka ARO (Army Recruitment Office) wahin se pata chal jata hai.

Garhwal Rifles ka Regimental Centre Lansdowne (Pauri Garhwal) me hai, jo Rudraprayag ke paas ka sabse bada sainik kendra hai. Aapka bharti kshetra kaun sa ARO sambhalta hai, yeh jaankari joinindianarmy.nic.in par zile ke naam se milti hai.

Agniveer ki shartein, umar seema aur selection process samay-samay par badalti hain. Isliye aavedan se pehle taaza notification zaroor padhein.

Physical taiyari ke liye kuch zaroori sujhav:

  • Daud ki practice ke liye mukhya sadak par akele subah ke andhere me na daudein, kyunki pahad ki sadak par gaadi aur janwar dono ka khatra hota hai. Stadium ya surakshit raste ka upyog karein.
  • Pahad me daudne se sahansheelta apne aap badhti hai, isliye pahadi yuva ko iska faayda hota hai.
  • Dheere dheere daud ka samay badhayein, ek hi din me bahut zyada na karein, taaki chot na lage.
  • Pushups, pull-ups aur stretching ka niyamit abhyas rakhein.
  • Santulit aahar aur neend ka dhyan rakhein.
  • Medical test me aankh, kaan aur sharir ki jaanch hoti hai. Apni kisi bhi sharirik samasya ke liye pehle doctor se salah lein.

Agar aap fauji parivar se hain to apne buzurgon se bharti ka tarika aur unke anubhav zaroor sunein.

Rudraprayag: Polytechnic, ITI, Nursing aur Skill Centre

Technical Shiksha ke Raaste

ITI, polytechnic aur nursing jaise courses ke liye admission sarkari portal ya counselling ke zariye hota hai. Seat ki sankhya aur tareekhein har saal badalti hain. Isliye taaza jankari ke liye Uttarakhand ke technical education aur skill development vibhag ki website dekhein.

Aap yeh kadam apna sakte hain:

  • ITI ke liye 8th ya 10th ke baad ke course dekhein, jisme electrician, fitter, COPA jaise trade hote hain.
  • Polytechnic ke liye 10th ke baad diploma engineering ka option dekhein.
  • Nursing ke liye Uttarakhand Nursing Council aur sambandhit university ki prakriya dekhein.
  • Skill development ke short course Kaushal Vikas aur PMKVY jaise kendra ki yojanaon me dekhein.
  • Admission se pehle sansthan ki manyata aur placement ka record pooch lein.

Pahad ke yuvaon ke liye hotel management, electrician aur driving jaise kaushal ki bahut maang hai, kyunki yahan paryatan aur nirman dono badh rahe hain.

Rudraprayag: JNV, KV aur Rajiv Gandhi Navodaya Vidyalaya

Zile ke Pramukh Sarkari Awasiya aur Kendriya Vidyalaya

Rudraprayag me Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Bansu Jakhdhar me, Kendriya Vidyalaya Agastyamuni me aur Rajiv Gandhi Navodaya Vidyalaya Rudraprayag me hai. Teeno CBSE se jude hain aur inme se JNV aur RGNV awasiya (boarding) hain.

VidyalayaSthanJaankari
Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya (JNV)Bansu Jakhdhar, RudraprayagCo-ed, CBSE, awasiya, 1987 me sthapit
Kendriya Vidyalaya (KV)AgastyamuniCBSE, Class I se XII
Rajiv Gandhi Navodaya Vidyalaya (RGNV)RudraprayagCo-ed awasiya, CBSE, 2009 me sthapit

JNV me Class 6 me pravesh ke liye Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti ki chayan pariksha deni hoti hai. Aavedan aur taareekhein navodaya.gov.in par dekhein. KV me pravesh ke liye kvsangathan.nic.in par aavedan hota hai. RGNV ke liye Uttarakhand sarkar ki prakriya dekhni hoti hai.

Zile me sarkari inter college bhi hain, jaise Rudraprayag ka Atal Utkrisht Government Inter College. Sarkari school ki padhai nishulk ya kam kharch me hoti hai. Yadi aapka bachcha 5th class me hai to JNV ki pariksha ke liye pehle se taiyari shuru kar dein.

Rudraprayag: Scholarship, Hostel aur Coaching Yojana

Vidyarthiyon ke liye Madad

Anusuchit jaati, janjaati, pichhda varg aur kamzor aay varg ke vidyarthiyon ke liye post-matric aur pre-matric scholarship milti hain. Rashtriya star par National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) aur rajya star par Samaj Kalyan Vibhag isme madad karta hai.

Zile ke vidyarthiyon ke liye scholarship ke kuch aam raaste:

  • National Scholarship Portal par aavedan karein.
  • Uttarakhand ke Samaj Kalyan Vibhag ki website par rajya ki yojanayein dekhein.
  • School ya college ke principal se scholarship ke form ke bare me poochhein.
  • Aay ka praman patra (Rudraprayag: E-District / Apuni Sarkar Sevayein) pehle se banwa lein.
  • Bank khate ko Aadhaar se jod kar rakhein.

Awasiya school ya hostel ki suvidha ke liye Samaj Kalyan Vibhag ke hostel ki jankari zila karyalaya se milti hai. Mukhyamantri star ki free coaching yojanayein alag-alag rajyon me alag hain. Uttarakhand me jo yojana lagu hai uski jankari ke liye Samaj Kalyan Vibhag ya Zila Karyalaya se sampark karein.

Scholarship ke form ki antim taareekh nikalne ke baad wapas nahi khulti. Isliye form samay par bharein.

Rudraprayag: Rojgar Karyalaya aur Career Mela

Sewayojan Portal par Registration

Uttarakhand me berozgaron ke registration ke liye Sewayojan portal (sewayojan.uk.gov.in) aur zila rojgar karyalaya hota hai. Wahan apna naam, shiksha aur kaushal ki jankari darj karne se naukri aur career mela ki soochna milti hai.

Registration ke liye aam taur par Aadhaar, shaikshanik praman patra aur sthai niwas chahiye. Portal par aap apni yogyata ke hisaab se rojgar mele aur khali padon ki jankari dekh sakte hain. Zila rojgar karyalaya samay-samay par career mela bhi karata hai.

Naukri ki khoj me yeh sujhav kaam aayenge:

  • Sarkari naukri ki soochna ke liye sirf official website par bharosa karein. UKPSC, UKSSSC aur Sewayojan portal sabse sahi strot hain.
  • Naukri dilane ke naam par paise maangne walon se savdhan rahein. Sarkari bharti me koi bhi dalal ya paise nahi lagte.
  • Apna resume saaf aur chhota rakhein.
  • Online interview ke liye achhi internet vali jagah dhoondh lein.
  • Rudraprayag me paryatan, homestay aur guide ka kaam bhi ek achha rojgar vikalp hai (Rudraprayag: Homestay Registration aur Tourism Subsidy).
