Rudraprayag: Pahadi Zameen Naap: Nali, Mutthi aur Bigha Calculator

1 Nali Kitna Hota Hai? (Exact Math)

1 Nali = 2,160 square feet = 240 square yard (Gaj) = lagbhag 200.67 square meter. Yeh lagbhag 1 acre ka 20wan hissa hai. 1 Hectare me lagbhag 49.83 Nali aate hain.

Uttarakhand ke pahadi zilon (Rudraprayag, Chamoli, Tehri, Pauri, Almora aur Pithoragarh) me zameen naapne ki sabse mukhya ikai Nali hai. Sarkari record (Bhulekh khatauni) me zameen Hectare ya varg meter me likhi hoti hai, par gaon me log ab bhi Nali aur Mutthi me hi baat karte hain.

Mutthi Kya Hai? (1 Nali = 16 Mutthi)

1 Mutthi ek Nali ka 16wan hissa hai, yani 135 square feet ya 15 gaj. Purane samay me jitni zameen par ek mutthi (haath bhar) beej boya ja sake, use ek mutthi zameen kaha jata tha.

Mutthi aur Nali ka yeh sambandh gaon ke hisab-kitab me badi aasani deta hai. Jaise agar 6 Nali 8 Mutthi zameen hai, to total = (6 x 2,160) + (8 x 135) = 12,960 + 1,080 = 14,040 square feet.

Dhyan dein: Nali shabd ka istemal anaj naapne ke liye bhi hota tha (1 Nali anaj lagbhag 2 kilo). Isliye "zameen ki Nali" aur "anaj ki Nali" ko mat milayein. Anaj ke paimane Rudraprayag: Purane Naap-Tol ke Paimane me diye gaye hain.

Bigha, Biswa aur Biswansi

Bigha ki maap alag-alag jagah par alag hoti hai. Isliye zameen khareedne se pehle apne Patwari ya Bhulekh record se pakka kar lein ki us gaon me Bigha ka kya matlab hai.

Pahad me kuch kshetron me 1 pucca Bigha lagbhag 2.5 Nali maana jata hai, par yeh sabhi gaon ke liye sahi nahi hai. Biswa aur Biswansi bigha ke chhote hisse hain. Yeh aankde plains aur hills me alag hote hain. Isliye is conversion ko legal ya registry ke kaam me bina Patwari ki puchhtachh ke mat istemal karein.

Rudraprayag: Pahadi Zameen ke Prakar aur Revenue Shabdavali

Talaon, Upraon aur Katil

Pahad me zameen unchai aur pani ki upalabdhta ke hisab se bati jati hai. Talaon pani wali neechi zameen hai, Upraon sukhi siddhidaar zameen hai, aur Katil/Ijran dhalan wali ya kabhi-kabhi bone wali zameen hai.

PrakarMatlabKheti
TalaonNadi ya gadhere ke paas sinchit zameenDhan, gehun
Upraon (Avval/Doyam)Siddhidaar sukhi zameen jo barish par nirbhar haiMandua, jhangora, daalen
Katil / IjranDhalan wali ya kabhi-kabhi kheti wali zameenKabhi kabhi fasal

Garhwal me sinchit khet ko "Sera" bhi kaha jata hai. Yeh shabd Rudraprayag aur uske aas-paas ke gaon me aam hai.

Nap, Benap aur Gol Khata

Gol Khata woh sanyukt khatauni hai jisme ek hi khate me kayi bhai-chachere ke naam bina baante hue zameen darj hoti hai. Pahad me yeh sabse badi kanooni uljhan hai.

Gol Khata ki wajah se zameen ke batwaare, registry aur loan me dikkat aati hai. Zameen ko alag-alag khate me baantne ke liye Tehsil me Hissedari (batwara) ka prakriya karni padti hai.

Khasra, Khatauni aur Hissedari

Khasra zameen ka plot number hota hai aur Khatauni uska malikana record. Dono Bhulekh Uttarakhand portal par online dekhe ja sakte hain. Prakriya Rudraprayag: Bhulekh Uttarakhand: Khatauni Online Kaise Dekhein me di gayi hai.

Zameen Kharidne ke Kanoon (Rudraprayag Zila)

Bahar ke Logon ke Liye Naya Niyam (Naya Land Law)

Uttarakhand vidhan sabha ne February 2025 me land law sanshodhan paas kiya, aur reports ke anusar Rajyapal ki manzoori ke baad yeh kanoon May 2026 me lagu hua. Ab bahar ke log (jo Uttarakhand ke mool niwasi nahi hain) Rudraprayag samet 11 zilon me kheti ya bagwani ki zameen nahi kharid sakte. Wo sirf ghar ke liye 250 square meter tak ki aawasiya zameen kharid sakte hain, affidavit ke saath.

Ab niyam kuch is tarah hain (media reports ke anusar):

  • Bahari vyakti agricultural/horticultural zameen 11 zilon me nahi kharid sakta. Sirf Haridwar aur Udham Singh Nagar is niyam me alag hain.
  • Ghar ke liye 250 square meter (lagbhag 1.25 Nali) tak ki zameen, parivar ke liye ek baar, affidavit ke saath.
  • Affidavit me bataana hota hai ki khareedar ya uske parivar ne Uttarakhand me pehle 250 sq m se zyada aawasiya zameen nahi kharidi hai.
  • Vyavsayik/audyogik/chikitsa jaise upyog ke liye kharidi gayi zameen par 3 saal me kaam shuru karna zaroori bataya gaya hai, nahi to zameen sarkar ke paas ja sakti hai.
  • 30 saal tak ki lease ka praavdhan bhi bataya gaya hai.

Zaroori baat: Kanoon me samay-samay par sanshodhan hota rehta hai aur rules ke detail (jaise nagar nikay ke andar aur bahar alag niyam) ko lekar alag-alag reports me alag baat milti hai. Isliye jab bhi zameen kharidni ho, sub-registrar karyalaya, tehsil aur kisi Uttarakhand ke vakil se confirm zaroor karein.

Kya bahari log Rudraprayag me zameen kharid sakte hain?
Kheti ya bagwani ki zameen nahi. Ghar ke liye 250 sq m tak aawasiya zameen, shartein aur affidavit ke saath.
Kya Uttarakhand ke mool niwasi par yeh rok lagti hai?
Mool niwasi par yeh seema is roop me lagu nahi hoti. Par detail ke liye tehsil se pooche.

Section 143 aur Circle Rate

Section 143 ke tahat kheti ki zameen ko gair-kheti (jaise ghar, hotel, dukan) ke upyog me badla jata hai. Yeh prakriya tehsil/SDM karyalaya se hoti hai aur iske baad hi sahi taur par kisi aur kaam me zameen ka istemal kiya ja sakta hai.

Section 143 ki fees aur steps samay ke saath badalte hain. Taaza prakriya aur dar ke liye zila prashasan ya tehsil se sampark karein.

Rudraprayag: Van Panchayat aur Community Forest Rights

Van Panchayat Kaise Kaam Karti Hai

Van Panchayat gaon ke logon dwara chalaya jaane wala sanyukt jungle prabandhan hai. Uttarakhand me yeh vyavastha 1931 ke aas-paas shuru hui thi aur aaj bhi gaon ke ghaas, lakdi aur pani ke srot ki raksha karti hai.

Sarpanch aur panchayat sadasya jungle me ghaas katne, lakdi lene aur pashu charane ke niyam banate hain. Garhwal me kai gaon me ghaas katne ke liye ek tay din hota tha jise gaon ke log mil kar manate the.

Rudraprayag: Paramparik Engineering: Gharat, Guhl aur Terrace Farming

Gharat (Panchakki)

Gharat pahad ki purani panchakki hai jo gadhere ke pani ke bahav se chalti hai. Isme lakdi ka turbine (bhirnd) pathar ke paat ko ghumata hai aur thande peese hue aate ka swad kuch alag hota hai.

Gharat bina bijli aur bina diesel ke chalne wali green energy ka behtareen udaharan hai. Kai gaon me gharat ke saath ek "Gharatwala" parivar bhi hota tha, jise aate ke badle anaj milta tha.

Guhl (Kul) aur Pathar ki Deewar wale Khet

Guhl pahadi sinchai nahar ko kehte hain jo gadhere ya nadi se pani khet tak le jati hai. Siddhidaar khet (terrace) ki deewar bina cement ke sirf patthar jama kar banai jati hai.

Is vyavastha ko banane aur chalane me poore gaon ka sahyog hota tha. Kisi guhl ke mool-nirmaan ki kahaniyan lok-kathaon me bhi milti hain.

Rudraprayag: Desi Anaj aur Mote Anaj (Barahnaja)

Barahnaja Pranali

Barahnaja ek paramparik mishrit kheti hai jisme ek hi khet me 12 ya usse zyada fasal ek saath ugayi jati hain. Isse mitti ki upjau banti rehti hai aur ek fasal fail hone par bhi bhukhmari ka khatra nahi rehta.

Barahnaja ki shuruaat Tehri ke Beej Bachao Andolan se jodkar dekhi jati hai. Rudraprayag me bhi gaon ke log kai fasal ek saath ugate the, jaise mandua, jhangora, cheena, gahat, bhatt, rajma, ramdana, kheera, kaddu aur til.

Mandua, Jhangora aur Cheena

Mandua (finger millet), Jhangora (barnyard millet) aur Cheena (proso millet) pahad ke sabse purane mote anaj hain. Yeh protein, calcium aur fibre se bharpoor hote hain aur kam pani me ugte hain.

Lal Chawal aur Pahadi Gehun

Garhwal me chaaval ki kuch desi kisme lal rang ki hoti hain, jise log "Lal Chawal" kehte hain. Pahadi gehun bhi alag swad ka hota hai.

Rudraprayag: Pahadi Daalen, Tilhan aur Masale

Daalen

Pahad ki pramukh daalen Gahat (kulthi / horse gram), Bhatt (kaala soyabean), Rayans, Naurangi, Lobia aur Rajma hain. Gahat ki daal garmi aur pathri ke gharelu nuskhon me bhi istemal hoti hai.

Tilhan aur Masale

Bhangjeera (perilla), Til, Sarson, Jakhiya, Jambu (faran) aur Timur pahadi tadke aur chutney ke khaas masale hain. Jambu aur Faran jaise pahadi tadke ki khushboo alag hoti hai.

Rudraprayag: Bagwani aur Cash Crops

Phal

Rudraprayag ki neechi aur madhya ghatiyon me malta, santra, nimbu, aam, akhrot aur kai jagah seb aur aadoo jaise phal ugte hain. Phal ki kheti ka mukhya aadhaar chhote terrace khet hain.

Sabzi aur Masale ki Fasal

Pahadi aloo, adrak, haldi, pyaaz, matar aur gobhi gaon ki nakdi fasal hain. Yatra marg ke paas ke gaon ko tourists aur yatri ke baazar ka bhi faayda milta hai.

Rudraprayag: Floriculture, Mushroom, Madhumakkhi aur Chai

Madhumakkhi Paalan aur Phool

Pahad me madhumakkhi paalan ek bahut lokpriya upay hai. Purane gharon ki deewar me bhi madhumakkhi ka chhatta (jaljala/maun) banaya jata tha. Aaj kal log lakdi ke box me bhi ise karte hain.

Rudraprayag: Pashupalan, Dudh aur Chaarwahe

Desi Gaay, Bakri aur Chhani

Pahadi gaay (jise Badri gaay bhi kaha jata hai) chhoti hoti hai, par uska dudh aur ghee gunkari maana jata hai. Garmi me pashu bugyal me chhani (mausami jhopdi) me rakhe jate hain.

Gaon me pashu ko goth (ground floor ki gaushala) me rakha jata hai (Rudraprayag: Pahadi Ghar aur Vaastu). Gaay ke byaane ke baad pehle kuch din ke dudh (Bhuj/Khees) ko pehle devta ko chadhaya jata tha.

Rudraprayag: Vistaar se: Zameen, Kheti aur Pashupalan ki Gehri Jankari

Pahad ki zameen aur kheti ek doosre se jude hain. Yahan zameen chhoti, terrace wali aur bikhri hui hoti hai, aur kheti aksar paani aur mausam par nirbhar rehti hai. Is hisse me hamne zameen ke naye kanoon ki taaza sthiti, van panchayat aur zile ki kheti ki jankari ko gehrai se likha hai. Zameen ke kisi bhi lain-dein se pehle vakeel aur tehsil se zaroor poochhein.

Zameen Kharidne ke Kanoon (Vistaar)

Naya Land Law: Ab Tak ki Sthiti

Haridwar aur Udham Singh Nagar is rok se alag hain. Reports ke anusar Rajyapal ki manzoori ke baad yeh kanoon May 2026 me lagu hua.

Zameen Kharidne se Pehle Ki Jaanch Suchi

Zameen kharidne se pehle yeh jaanch zaroor karein:

  • Khatauni: bhulekh.uk.gov.in par seller ka naam aur zameen ka rakba dekhein (Rudraprayag: Bhulekh Uttarakhand: Khatauni Online Kaise Dekhein).
  • Zameen ka prakar: Kheti, bagwani, aawasiya ya jungle? Jungle ki zameen mool niwasi ke liye bhi nahi kharidi ja sakti.
  • Daakhil-kharij: Pichhli registry ke baad naam chadha ya nahi?
  • Bojh (loan ya mukadma): Zameen par koi loan, mukadma ya dusre ka dawa to nahi?
  • Sadak aur raasta: Zameen tak jaane ka raasta kiske naam par hai?
  • Paani aur bijli: Paani ka strot aur bijli ka connection kitna door hai?
  • Aapda ka khatra: Zameen nadi ke paas, landslide wali dhaal par ya baadh ke raaste me to nahi (Rudraprayag: Building Bylaws, Map Approval aur Bhukamp ke Niyam , Rudraprayag: Jalvayu Badlav, Glacier aur Zameen ki Sthirta)?
  • Naap: Nali aur varg meter me ek hi rakba likha hai ya nahi (Rudraprayag: Pahadi Zameen Naap: Nali, Mutthi aur Bigha Calculator)?
  • Registry: Sub-Registrar karyalaya se hi registry karwayein (Rudraprayag: Property Registration, Stamp Duty aur Circle Rate).

Bina kaagaz ki jaanch ke, sirf bhai-bandhu ya dalal ke kehne par zameen na kharidein.

Van Panchayat aur Community Forest (Vistaar)

Van Panchayat ki Parampara

Uttarakhand ki Van Panchayat vyavastha 1931 ke aas-paas Kumaon Forest Panchayat Rules ke roop me shuru hui, jisme gaon ke log apne jungle ka prabandhan khud karte hain. Yeh duniya ki sabse purani samudayik jungle vyavasthaon me gina jata hai.

Van Panchayat ke bare me yeh jaanna zaroori hai:

  • Gaon ke log milkar ek van panchayat chunte hain, jo gaon ke jungle ke upyog ke niyam banata hai.
  • Jungle se ghaas, chara aur lakdi lene ke niyam gaon ke log tay karte hain.
  • Jungle ki raksha, aag se bachav aur paudhe lagane ka zimma bhi panchayat ka hota hai.
  • Aay aur kharch ka hisab rakha jata hai, aur kuch hissa jungle ke vikas par lagaya jata hai.

Zila Van Vibhag ya Revenue Vibhag se sampark karke aap apne gaon ki van panchayat ka record dekh sakte hain.

Van panchayat ke saamne aaj ki chunautiyan hain: gaon khali hone se jungle ki dekhbhal karne wale kam hain, aag ka khatra badh raha hai aur jangli janwar ki wajah se log jungle jaane se dar rahe hain. Isi liye kai jagah naye yuvaon ko is vyavastha se jodne ki zaroorat batayi jati hai.

37 se 39: Daal, Bagwani aur Naye Rojgar (Vistaar)

Kheti ko Rozgar Banane ke Tarike

Chhoti zameen ko faydemand banane ka ek tarika hai ki kam jagah me zyada keemat wali fasal ugayi jayein, jaise sabziyan, adrak, rajma ya bagwani, aur unhe seedhe bazaar ya samooh ke zariye bechna.

Zile ke KVK ke anusar zile me upaj badhane ke liye kuch uddeshya hain:

  • Mausam ke bahar ki sabziyan (off-season vegetables) aur protected cultivation (polyhouse).
  • Tikau aur gehri kheti se chhote aur seemant kisanon ko laabh pahunchana.
  • Paani bachane ke upay.
  • Prashikshan aur taknik jankari.

Praktikal salah:

  • Pehle ek chhote khet par nayi fasal ugakar dekhein.
  • Mitti ki jaanch KVK ya Krishi Vibhag se karwayein.
  • Samooh (SHG) banakar upaj bechne se achha daam mil sakta hai (Rudraprayag: NGO, Mahila Mangal Dal aur Self-Help Group).
  • Adrak, rajma aur mandua jaise utpaad ki packing aur brand par dhyan dein.
  • Aajkal online bazaar ke zariye bhi kuch kisan apni upaj bech rahe hain.

Fasal ke Minimum Support Price (MSP) aur bazaar bhav ke liye Krishi Vibhag ya mandi ki official jankari dekhein.

Sabziyon aur phalon ke saath madhumakkhi paalan aur mushroom jaise sahayak kaam bhi kuch gaon me kiye jate hain. Inke liye prashikshan KVK ya Udyan Vibhag se liya ja sakta hai.

Pashupalan (Vistaar)

Pashu aur Doodh ka Mahatva

Pahad me gay, bhains, bakri aur bhed gaon ki arthvyavastha ka mahatvapurn hissa hain. Gobar kheti ki khaad banta hai, aur doodh aur ghee gharelu upyog aur bikri dono ke kaam aate hain.

KVK ke anusar zile me pashupalan ke saath murgi aur bhed-bakri palan bhi kiya jata hai. Pahadi gay ki ek desi nasl "Badri" ke naam se bhi jani jati hai, jise rajya me badhawa diya jata hai. Pashu prabandhan ki kami ko KVK ne ek samasya bataya hai, isliye prashikshan zaroori hai.

Pashupalan ke liye kuch sujhav:

  • Pashu ko balanced chara aur saaf paani dein.
  • Samay par tika karwayein. Pashu chikitsa ke liye najdiki Pashu Chikitsalaya ya Zila Pashu Chikitsa Adhikari se sampark karein.
  • Goth saaf aur surakshit rakhein, aur shaam se pehle pashu andar bandh dein, kyunki tendua ka khatra rehta hai (Rudraprayag: Pet ke Saath Yatra).
  • Doodh ke liye gaon me sahkari samiti banane se achha daam milta hai.
  • Chara ugane ke liye ghaas aur bhimal jaise ped lagayein.

Gaon ke buzurg pashu ka bahut khayal rakhte the, kyunki pashu ghar ka hissa hota tha. Is parampara me aadhunik jankari jod kar pashupalan ko aur fayda wala banaya ja sakta hai.

Kya bahari log Rudraprayag me kheti ki zameen kharid sakte hain? Nahi. Naye land law ke tahat 11 pahadi zilon me kheti aur bagwani ki zameen bahari logon ke liye pratibandhit hai.
