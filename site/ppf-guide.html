PPF yaani Public Provident Fund, Bharat sarkar ki ek bahut lokpriya aur surakshit (safe) bachat yojana hai. Isme aapka paisa poori tarah sarkar ki guarantee ke saath badhta hai, aur ismein milne wala byaj aur maturity dono tax-free hote hain. Jo log bina risk ke lambe samay ke liye paisa jamā karna chahte hain, unke liye PPF ek behtareen vikalp hai. Neeche free PPF calculator bhi hai.

PPF ki khaas baatein

  • Lock-in: 15 saal (baad me 5-5 saal aur badha sakte hain).
  • Byaj: Sarkar har 3 mahine (quarter) me tay karti hai — aam taur par FD se thoda zyada.
  • Jamā: Ek saal me kam se kam ₹500, zyada se zyada ₹1.5 lakh.
  • Tax: Jamā, byaj aur maturity — teeno tax-free (EEE category).
  • Surakshit: Sarkari guarantee, isliye risk lagbhag zero.

PPF me compounding ka fayda

PPF me byaj har saal judta hai aur agle saal us bade amount par phir byaj milta hai. 15 saal ka lamba samay compounding ko poora mauka deta hai. Isliye jitni jaldi shuru karenge, maturity par utna bada fund banega.

Misaal: Agar aap har saal ₹1.5 lakh, 15 saal tak, 7.1% byaj par jamā karte hain, to aap kul ₹22.5 lakh jamā karenge aur maturity lagbhag ₹40 lakh+ ho sakti hai (byaj dar par nirbhar).

PPF Calculator kaise use karein?

  1. Har saal jamā karne wali rakam daalein.
  2. Abhi ki byaj dar daalein (sarkar ki current dar — badalti rehti hai).
  3. Saal daalein (15 ya zyada).
  4. Result me maturity, total jamā aur byaj dikhega.

Dhyan dein

PPF ki byaj dar har quarter badal sakti hai, isliye calculator me current dar daalein. Ye lambe samay ka product hai — beech me poora paisa nikalna mushkil hota (kuch shart par partial withdrawal 7ve saal se milta hai).

FAQ

PPF me sabse kam kitna jamā karna zaroori?

Har saal kam se kam ₹500 jamā karna zaroori, warna account inactive ho jaata.

Kya PPF ka paisa 15 saal se pehle nikal sakte?

Poora nahi, par 7ve saal se kuch shart par partial withdrawal milta hai.

PPF ya FD — behtar kya?

PPF tax-free + lambe samay ke liye behtar; FD short term + jab kabhi paisa chahiye tab behtar. Dono ka apna kaam.

15 saal baad PPF ka kya karein?

Aap ise 5-5 saal ke liye badha sakte hain — nayi jama ke saath ya bina. Byaj tax-free rehta hai, isliye bahut log ise band nahi karte.

5 tarikh wala niyam — sabse zaroori trick

PPF ka byaj har mahine ki 5 tarikh se mahine ke aakhir tak ke sabse kam balance par lagta hai. Iska seedha matlab hai — agar aapne 6 tarikh ko paisa daala, to us mahine ka byaj us rakam par milega hi nahi.

Do sunhare niyam:
1. Har mahine daalte hain to 5 tarikh se pehle daalein.
2. Saal bhar ka ek saath daalte hain to 1 se 5 April ke beech daalein — poore saal ka byaj mil jaayega.
Yahi paisa 31 March ko daala to lagbhag ek saal ka byaj haath se nikal jaata hai.

PPF ka EEE darja

PPF un girey-chune vikalpon me hai jinhe EEE darja mila hai — teeno stage par tax nahi:

  • Jo daala — 80C me chhoot (Old Regime me)
  • Jo byaj mila — tax-free
  • Jo maturity par mila — poora tax-free

FD ka byaj tax lagta hai, mutual fund par capital gains lagta hai — PPF par kuch nahi. Isi liye lambe samay ke liye ye itna taakatwar hai, chahe dar dikhne me kam lage.

Loan aur nikasi ka niyam

  • Saal 3 se 6: jama rakam ke ek hisse par loan mil sakta hai, kam byaj par
  • Saal 7 ke baad: ek tay hisse ki aanshik nikasi allowed
  • Beech me band karna: sirf khaas halaton me (gambhir bimari, uchch shiksha, NRI banna) — 5 saal ke baad aur byaj me katauti ke saath

15 saal ke baad — sabse bada faisla

Maturity par teen raaste hote hain: poora paisa nikaal lein, 5 saal ke liye badha dein aur naya paisa daalte rahein, ya badha dein par naya paisa na daalein (khaate par byaj milta rahega).

Bahut se log 15 saal poore hote hi band kar dete hain — jabki tab tak compounding sabse tez chal rahi hoti hai. Shuru ke saalon me byaj chhota hota hai, aakhir ke saalon me hi asli badhotri aati hai. Agar paise ki turant zaroorat nahi hai, to account badha dena aam taur par zyada faydemand rehta hai.

Kuch aur kaam ki baatein

  • Ek vyakti ka ek hi PPF khaata ho sakta hai. Do khul gaye to doosra band kar diya jaata hai aur us par byaj nahi milta.
  • Naabalig bachche ke naam se khaata khul sakta hai, par maa-baap aur bachche ki kul jama seema milakar hi gini jaati hai.
  • Saal me ₹500 na daalne par khaata inactive ho jaata hai — chhoti penalty dekar chalu karwana padta hai.
  • PPF ki rakam par court ki kurki nahi lagti — ye ek badi suraksha hai jo kam log jaante hain.
