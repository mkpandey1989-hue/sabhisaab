Tehri Garhwal: UKPSC, PCS aur UKSSSC ke liye 100 One-Liner District GK

Seedha jawab: Neeche Tehri Garhwal ke 100 one-liner tathya diye hain, jo Patwari/Lekhpal, Police Constable/SI, Forest Guard, VDO/VPDO aur UKPSC (Lower/Upper PCS) jaisi pariksha ke liye kaam ke hain. ka matlab: is tathya ko exam se pehle ek baar official source (jila website, Census, Gazetteer) se milayein.

Prashasan aur Bhugol (1 se 25)

  • Tehri Garhwal ka jila mukhyalay New Tehri hai.
  • Tehri Garhwal ka RTO code UK-09 hai.
  • Census 2011 ke anusar Tehri Garhwal ki abadi 6,18,931 hai.
  • Tehri Garhwal me 9 vikas khand (block) hain: Bhilangana, Chamba, Devprayag, Jakhnidhar, Jaunpur, Kirtinagar, Narendranagar, Pratapnagar aur Thauldhar.
  • Jile ki uttar dishaa me Uttarkashi, purv me Rudraprayag, dakshin me Pauri Garhwal aur paschim me Dehradun jila hai.
  • Tehri Garhwal ka kshetrafal 3,642 sq km hai.
  • Ghansali Tehri Garhwal ka sabse bada tehsil hai.
  • Bhilangana block (mukhyalay Ghansali) jile ka sabse bada block maana jaata hai.
  • Jile me lagbhag 928 gram panchayat aur 76 nyay panchayat hain.
  • Jile me 6 Vidhan Sabha kshetra hain.
  • Tehri Garhwal Lok Sabha kshetra me Tehri ke alawa aas-paas ke kshetra shamil hain.
  • Tehri Garhwal me 4 nagar palika (Tehri, Narendranagar, Chamba, Devprayag) aur Kirtinagar tatha Muni ki Reti jaise chhote nikay hain.
  • New Tehri ka PIN code 249001 hai.
  • Ghansali ka PIN code 249155 hai.
  • Tehri ka STD code 01376 hai.
  • Tehri Garhwal ki sabse mukhya nadi Bhagirathi hai.
  • Bhilangana nadi Khatling Glacier se nikalti hai.
  • Bhagirathi aur Alaknanda ka sangam Devprayag me hai, jahan se Ganga naam milta hai.
  • Aglar nadi Jaunpur kshetra se behti hai aur Yamuna ki sahayak hai.
  • Henwal nadi Ganga ki sahayak nadi hai.
  • Tehri Lake Uttarakhand ki sabse badi jheel maani jaati hai.
  • Tehri Garhwal Seismic Zone IV me aata hai.
  • Nag Tibba (lagbhag 3,000 m) Tehri Garhwal ki lokpriya choti aur trek hai.
  • Surkanda Devi mandir lagbhag 2,750 m ki oonchai par hai.
  • Dhanaulti lagbhag 2,286 m par hai aur Mussoorie se lagbhag 24 km door hai.

Itihas aur Riyasat (26 se 50)

  • Tehri Riyasat ki neev 1815 me Raja Sudarshan Shah ne rakhi.
  • Sudarshan Shah ke pita Raja Pradyuman Shah the, jo 1804 me Khurbura ki ladai me Gorkhaon se maare gaye.
  • Garhwal me Gorkha shasan lagbhag 1803 se 1815 tak raha.
  • 1815 ki Sugauli sandhi ke baad British ne Gorkhaon ko hataya; Alaknanda ke purv ka hissa British Garhwal bana aur paschimi hissa Raja Sudarshan Shah ko mila.
  • Tehri Riyasat ki pehli rajdhani Tehri (purana Tehri) thi.
  • Pratapnagar, Kirtinagar aur Narendranagar ko kramshah Raja Pratap Shah, Kirti Shah aur Narendra Shah ne basaya.
  • Narendranagar ko Raja Narendra Shah ne lagbhag 1919 me basaya.
  • Tehri Riyasat ke antim shasak Manavendra Shah the.
  • Tehri Riyasat ka Bharat me vilay 1949 me hua (Uttar Pradesh me, 1 August 1949).
  • Uttarkashi jila 1960 me Tehri Garhwal se alag karke banaya gaya.
  • Rudraprayag jila 1997 me Tehri, Chamoli aur Pauri ke kuch hisson se banaya gaya.
  • Uttarakhand rajya ka gathan 9 November 2000 ko hua.
  • Sridev Suman (janm 25 May 1915, Jaulgaon) Tehri Riyasat ke virudh aandolan ke pramukh netaa the.
  • Sridev Suman ki 25 July 1944 ko 84 din ke upvaas ke baad shahadat hui.
  • Gabbar Singh Negi, VC ka janm 21 April 1895, Manjud (Chamba), Tehri Garhwal me hua.
  • Gabbar Singh Negi ko Pratham Vishwa Yuddh ke Neuve Chapelle (1915) ke yuddh ke liye Victoria Cross (maranoprant) mila.
  • Uttarakhand ke pehle VC vijeta Darwan Singh Negi (Chamoli) the, Gabbar Singh Negi doosre.
  • Chamba me har saal April ke aas-paas Gabbar Singh Mela lagta hai.
  • Sundarlal Bahuguna ka janm 9 January 1927 ko Maroda (Tehri) me hua.
  • Sundarlal Bahuguna ko Padma Vibhushan (2009) mila.
  • Chipko aandolan 1973 me Chamoli se shuru hua; Sundarlal Bahuguna ne ise pahad me failaya.
  • Henwalghati kshetra Chipko aandolan ke ek charan se juda hai.
  • Madho Singh Bhandari (Malethha, Tehri) 17vi sadi ke Garhwal ke senapati the.
  • Garhwal ka naam "Garh" (kile) se aaya hai; yahan kai chhote garh the.
  • "Garhwal ka Itihas" ke lekhak Pt. Harikrishna Raturi hain.

Tehri Dam aur Bijli (51 se 65)

  • Tehri Dam Bhagirathi nadi par bana hai.
  • Tehri Dam ki oonchai 260.5 meter hai.
  • Tehri Dam earth and rockfill prakar ka baandh hai.
  • Tehri Hydro Power Plant ki kshamta 1,000 MW (4 x 250 MW) hai.
  • Tehri HPP ki machine 2006-07 me grid se judi.
  • Koteshwar HEP ki kshamta 400 MW (4 x 100 MW) hai.
  • Koteshwar baandh Tehri se lagbhag 22 km neeche hai.
  • Tehri Pumped Storage Plant ki kshamta 1,000 MW hai.
  • Tehri PSP ki chaaron units April 2026 tak commercial ho chuki hain.
  • Tehri Hydro Power Complex ki kul kshamta 2,400 MW hai.
  • Tehri Dam banane wali company THDC India Limited hai.
  • THDC ka mukhyalay Rishikesh me hai.
  • Purana Tehri shahar jheel me doob gaya.
  • Dobra-Chanti pul 8 November 2020 ko khula.
  • Dobra-Chanti pul ki kul lambai lagbhag 725 m, main span lagbhag 440 m hai; ye India ka sabse lamba motorable single-lane suspension pul maana jaata hai.

Paryatan aur Tirth (66 se 80)

  • Devprayag Panch Prayag me se ek hai.
  • Raghunath mandir Devprayag me hai.
  • Kunjapuri, Chandrabadni aur Surkanda Devi Tehri ke teen pramukh Shaktipeeth (lok-manyata) hain.
  • Sem Mukhem Nagraja ka prasiddh sthan hai (Pratapnagar kshetra).
  • Khatling Glacier Bhilangana ka strot hai.
  • Ghuttu Khatling aur Panwali Kantha trek ka mukhya dwar hai.
  • Sahastra Tal aur Masar Tal Khatling route se jude oonche tal hain.
  • Panwali Kantha ek mashhoor oonchai wala bugyal hai.
  • Kanatal Chamba ke paas ek lokpriya paryatak sthal hai.
  • Chamba Rishikesh-Gangotri marg ka chauraha hai.
  • Muni ki Reti aur Tapovan Tehri Garhwal me hain.
  • Laxman Jhula ka pashchimi kinara (Tapovan) Tehri me aur purvi kinara (Jonk) Pauri me hai.
  • Shivpuri aur Kaudiyala rafting ke liye prasiddh hain.
  • Narendranagar ke purane Rajmahal parisar me Ananda in the Himalayas resort hai.
  • Ghansali se Gaurikund (Kedarnath ki taraf) lagbhag 127 km hai.

Sanskriti, Bhasha aur Kheti (81 se 95)

  • Tehri Garhwal ki mukhya boli Garhwali hai; Jaunpur kshetra me Jaunpuri aur Rawain kshetra me Rawaini boli jaati hai.
  • Garhwali bhasha Madhya Pahadi (Central Pahari) samuh ki hai.
  • Baajuband Garhwali ka sawal-jawab wala lok-geet hai.
  • Jhumelo mahilaon ka gol ghera banakar kiya jaane wala geet-nritya hai.
  • Jagar devta ko jagane ki gaayan-gatha hai.
  • Pandav Nritya me Mahabharat ke prasang hote hain.
  • Phool Sankranti (Phooldei) Chait mahine ki pehli tithi par manayi jaati hai.
  • Ghee Sankranti Bhado mahine ki Sankranti hai.
  • Makar Sankranti (Uttarayani / Khichdi Sankranti) Magh mahine me manayi jaati hai.
  • Igas (Egas) Diwali ke baad manayi jaati hai.
  • Budhi Diwali Jaunpur-Rawain kshetra me manayi jaati hai.
  • Barahnaja baarah prakar ke anaaj ki mishrit kheti hai.
  • Jhangora aur Mandua Tehri ke pramukh pahadi anaaj hain.
  • Kafal garmi me pakne wala pahadi phal hai (Myrica esculenta).
  • Kilmora Berberis jaati ki pahadi jhaadi hai.

Tehri Garhwal: Pichhle Saalon ke PYQ: Sahi Tarika aur Practice Sawal

Asli PYQ ke liye UKPSC (psc.uk.gov.in) aur UKSSSC (sssc.uk.gov.in) ki official website ke "Previous Papers / Answer Key" section dekhein. Neeche 25 practice sawal (hamare dwara likhe, verified tathyon par aadharit) aur PYQ khojne ka tarika diya hai.

Asli PYQ kaise khojein

  • UKPSC ki website → "Examination" → "Previous Papers / Question Papers".
  • UKSSSC ki website → "Previous Papers / Answer Keys".
  • Har paper me "Tehri", "Devprayag", "Tehri Dam", "Garhwal", "Gabbar Singh", "Sridev Suman" jaise shabd khojein.
  • Ek Excel me record karein: Saal | Pariksha | Sawal | Sahi jawab | Source.
  • Answer key ya adhikrit paper se hi jawab milayein, coaching ya blog ki list par nahi.

Bina source ke "PYQ" likhna galat aur bharamak hota hai.

Exam ke liye 5 salah

  • Pehle syllabus dekhein: UKPSC / UKSSSC ke official syllabus me "Uttarakhand ka itihas, bhugol, sanskriti, arthvyavastha" ke topic.

  • Mock test dein aur galatiyon ko dobara padhein.

Tehri Garhwal: Army Bharti (Agniveer ARO), Training aur Physical Preparation

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal ke naujawan Army Agniveer bharti ARO Lansdowne (Army Recruiting Office, Lansdowne) ke antargat dete hain. Is ARO me 7 jile aate hain: Chamoli, Dehradun, Haridwar, Pauri Garhwal, Rudraprayag, Tehri Garhwal aur Uttarkashi. Bharti rally aam taur par Kotdwar me "VC Gabbar Singh Camp" me hoti hai. Tehri Garhwal: Rozgar Karyalay aur Career Mela

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand me employment exchange (rozgar karyalay) me registration eservices.uk.gov.in par online hota hai, ya CSC aur nazdeeki employment exchange se bhi. Registration ke liye Uttarakhand sarkar dwara jari niwas praman patra (domicile) aur anya dastavez chahiye. Rojgar ki jaankari rojgar.uk.gov.in par bhi milti hai. "Sewayojan portal" Uttar Pradesh ka hai, Uttarakhand ka nahi.

Registration ka process

  • eservices.uk.gov.in (Apuni Sarkar) par jaayein.
  • "Don't have a user account" par click karke login banayein.
  • "Registration in Employment Exchange" sewa chunein.
  • Form bharein aur dastavez upload karein.
  • Registration card (online) download karein.
  • Naukri ki soochna portal par dekhte rahein.

Zaroori dastavez (aam taur par):

  • Uttarakhand sarkar ka niwas praman patra (Tehri Garhwal: E-District / Apuni Sarkar: Praman Patra aur Sewayein).
  • Aadhaar card.
  • Shaikshik yogyata ke praman patra.
  • Jati praman patra (agar aarakshit varg me hain).
  • Photo aur mobile number.

Rojgar mela aur career mela:

  • Skill Development & Employment Department samay-samay par rozgar mela / job fair lagata hai. Taaza tithi aur sthan rojgar.uk.gov.in aur jila prashasan ki website par dekhein.
  • National Career Service (ncs.gov.in) par bhi sarkari aur niji naukri ki jaankari.

Rozgar ke naye raaste (Tehri ke naujawan ke liye):

Naukri ki thagi se bachav (bahut zaroori):

  • Naukri dilane ke naam par paisa maangne wale agent dhokhebaaz hote hain. Sarkari bharti muft hai (sirf aavedan fees, jo notification me likhi hoti hai).
  • Fake job website / WhatsApp message se savdhan.gov.in website hi bharosemand hai.
  • Cyber fraud ki shikayat: 1930.

Uttarakhand sarkar dwara jari niwas praman patra aur anya dastavez.
Rojgar mela kab lagta hai?
Samay-samay par; taaza jaankari rojgar.uk.gov.in aur jila prashasan par dekhein.
