Dehradun: Saar aur Zile ki Pehchan

Dehradun Uttarakhand ka sabse zyada pehchana jaane wala zila hai, jiska mukhyalaya Dehradun shehar hai. Yeh Garhwal mandal me aata hai, 3,088 sq km me faila hai aur 2011 ki janganana me iski aabadi 16,96,694 thi, jo rajya me Haridwar ke baad doosri sabse badi hai.

Zile ki Pehchan: Ek Nazar Mein

Dehradun ko log “Doon Valley”, “Dera Doon” aur “Doon” ke naam se jaante hain. Yeh Uttarakhand ki interim (shitkaleen) rajdhani hai, aur Himalaya tatha Shivalik pahadiyon ke beech ki ek lambi ghati (Dun) me basa hai.

Uttarakhand ke 13 zilon me Dehradun sabse alag pehchan rakhta hai, kyunki yahan ek hi zile me shehar, pahad, jungle, nadi aur sarhad ki sanskriti ek saath milti hai:

  • Rajdhani pehchan: 9 November 2000 ko rajya banne ke baad se Dehradun rajya ki interim rajdhani hai. Vidhan Sabha, Secretariat, DGP aur zyadatar sarkari karyalay yahin hain. Gairsain (Chamoli) ko grishmkaleen rajdhani ghoshit kiya gaya hai.
  • Shiksha aur sansthan pehchan: Forest Research Institute (FRI), Indian Military Academy (IMA), Doon School, Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC), Survey of India, Wadia Institute of Himalayan Geology, Indian Institute of Petroleum jaise bade sansthan isi zile me hain.
  • Paryatan pehchan: Mussoorie (“Pahadon ki Rani”), Landour, Rishikesh (“Yoga Capital”), Sahastradhara, Robber’s Cave (Guchhu Pani), Tapkeshwar, Chakrata, Lakhamandal, Kalsi aur Dakpathar.
  • Sanskritik pehchan: Garhwali, Jaunsari, Punjabi-Sikh (Ramraiya), Gorkha, Tibetan aur pravasi samaj ka milan. Chakrata-Kalsi ka Jaunsar-Bawar kshetra apni alag boli, tyohar aur Mahasu Devta ki parampara ke liye jaana jata hai.

Dehradun Naam Kaise Pada: Naam ka Itihas

“Dehradun” do shabdon se bana hai: “Dehra” (dera yaani dera/shivir) aur “Dun” (do pahadiyon ke beech lambi ghati). Maanyata hai ki 1675-76 ke aaspaas Guru Ram Rai ne yahan apna dera lagaya, jisse yeh shehar “Dera-Dun” aur phir “Dehradun” kehlaya.

Is naam ke baare me log teen alag baatein karte hain:

  • Guru Ram Rai ka Dera (sabse prachalit mat): Saatve Sikh Guru Har Rai ke bade putra Baba Ram Rai ne Doon ghati me apna dera dala. Garhwal ke Raja Fateh Shah ne unhe gaon daan diye. Dera ke aas-paas basti basi, jahan aaj Shri Guru Ram Rai Darbar Sahib hai. Is darbar ka vartaman bhavan 1707 tak poora hua mana jata hai.
  • “Dun” ka matlab: Garhwali me “Dun” ya “Doon” us ghati ko kehte hain jo Lesser Himalaya aur Shivalik ke beech lambi hokar bichi ho. Kotli Doon, Patli Doon, Pinjore Doon bhi isi tarah ki ghatiyan hain.
  • Dronnagari wali lok-manyata: Kuch log kehte hain ki Dronacharya ne yahan tap kiya, isliye ise “Dronnagari” ya “Dron Ghati” bhi kaha gaya. Tapkeshwar Mahadev mandir ko Dronacharya se joda jata hai.

British kaal me naam: Angrezi raj me sarkari naam “Dehra Dun” tha. Purane gazetteer aur maanchitron me aapko “Dehra”, “Dehra Doon” aur “Dera Dun” bhi milenge. Aaj bhi purane log “Dehra” kehte hain.

Dehradun naam kaise pada?
Dehra (dera/camp) aur Dun (ghati) se. Guru Ram Rai ke dera ki wajah se. Dera + Dun se.
Dehradun ka purana naam kya tha?
British kaal me “Dehra Dun” tha; shuruaati naam “Dera Ram Rai” bhi mana jata hai.
Doon ka matlab kya hota hai?
Do pahadiyon ke beech lambi chaudi ghati.
Dehradun ko Doon Valley kyun kehte hain?
Kyunki yeh Shivalik aur Himalaya ke beech ki Dun ghati me hai.

Dehradun Zila Kab aur Kaise Bana (Mool Zila aur Adhisoochna)

Dehradun kisi naye zile ki tarah kisi parent zile se kata nahi hai, balki yeh British kaal se hi ek alag prashasnik ikai raha hai. Sarkari record ke anusar 1871 se Dehradun ek alag zila ke roop me chal raha hai, jabki isse pehle yeh kabhi Saharanpur aur kabhi Kumaon mandal ke saath juda raha.

Yeh sawal sabse zyada search hota hai, isliye ise saaf shabdon me samjhiye. Bageshwar, Rudraprayag jaise zile 1990 ke baad bane. Dehradun ki kahani alag hai: yeh purani prashasnik ikai hai, jiski seema aur sanrachna samay-samay par badli.

Dehradun: Collectorate aur Rajasv Prashasan

Dehradun ka zila prashasan Dehradun nagar se chalta hai, jahan Zila Adhikari (DM) ka karyalay, Collectorate aur Vikas Bhawan sthit hain. Revenue prashasan ke liye zila tehsilon, kanungo circles aur patwari halkon me banta hua hai.

Zila Mukhyalay, Vikas Bhawan aur Collectorate Kahan Hai

Dehradun nagar hi zila mukhyalaya hai. Yahan Collectorate (DM Karyalay), Vikas Bhawan, Zila Panchayat, Treasury aur zile ke zyadatar vibhagon ke karyalay hain. Rajya ka Secretariat bhi isi shehar me hai, isliye yahan zila aur rajya, dono star ka prashasan ek saath chalta hai.

Dehradun me kisi bhi sarkari kaam ke liye teen star ke karyalay aapko milenge: zila star (Collectorate/DM), rajya star (Secretariat, directorates) aur kendriya sansthan (Survey of India, FRI, ONGC jaise). Isliye log aksar ek hi jagah ka pata dhundhte hue galat daftar pahunch jate hain. Hamesha pehle official portal par vibhag ka pata dekh lein.

Patwari Chowki: Revenue Police aur Regular Police ka Kshetra

Uttarakhand ke pahadi hisson me kai jagah Patwari ko hi police ke adhikar dete the, yeh Revenue Police system hai. Dehradun ke shehri aur maidani ilake me regular police chalti hai. Chakrata-Kalsi jaise durgam pahadi ilakon me revenue police ki sthiti samay ke saath badli hai.

Revenue police ek British kaal ki vyavastha hai, jisme gaon ka Patwari zameen ka record rakhne ke saath-saath chhote aparadhon ki jaanch aur gaon ki suraksha ka kaam bhi karta tha. Uttarakhand High Court ne is vyavastha par sawal uthaye hain, aur rajya sarkar ne dheere-dheere revenue police ke ilake ko regular police thanon me badalne ki prakriya chalayi hai.

Dehradun ke liye saaf baat:

Aam admi ke liye iska matlab: Agar aap Chakrata-Kalsi ke kisi door-daraaz gaon me jaa rahe hain aur koi shikayat ya FIR karni ho, to pehle pata karein ki wo gaon kis thana ya patwari chowki ke adhikar me aata hai.
