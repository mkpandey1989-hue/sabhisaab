Notice Period Buyout Calculator — Jaldi Chhodne Par Kitna Dena Hoga

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Naya offer letter haath me hai. Joining 15 din baad maang rahe hain. Purani company ka notice period 3 mahine ka hai. HR kehti hai — buyout kar lijiye. Aur tab sawaal aata hai: buyout ka matlab kitna paisa?

Seedha jawab: buyout aam taur par basic pay ya gross salary ka utne mahine ka hota hai jitne din aap kam kaam kar rahe hain. Kaunsa — ye aapke appointment letter me likha hota hai, aur dono me lagbhag do guna ka antar hota hai.

Neeche apni salary, notice period aur kitne din serve kar rahe hain — ye daaliye. Buyout ki rakam saamne aa jayegi.

Basic par ya gross par — ek line ka farak

Appointment letter me ek chhoti si line hoti hai, aur usi par poora hisaab tikta hai. 90,000 gross aur 40,000 basic wale ke liye 60 din ka buyout kitna hoga, dekhiye.

Antar₹1,00,000

Ek hi naukri, ek hi notice period, aur ek lakh ka antar — sirf isliye ki letter me kaunsa shabd likha tha. Isliye resign karne se pehle wo line dhoondhna sabse pehla kaam hona chahiye.

Kuch company letter me salary likh deti hain aur baad me use gross maan leti hain. Agar aapke letter me shabd saaf nahi hai, to HR se likhit me poochiye ki hisaab kis par lagega. Mail par mila jawab baad me kaam aata hai.

Tax ka wo hissa jo sabse zyada chubhta hai

Yahan ek baat hai jo bahut kam logon ko pehle se pata hoti hai, aur wo saal bhar tak chubhti hai.

Agar company aapki aakhri salary me se buyout ki rakam kaat leti hai, tab bhi aam taur par aapki poori salary par TDS kaata jaata hai. Yaani salary mili nahi, par tax uspar bhi lag gaya. Kaati gayi rakam alag se ghatai nahi jaati.

Aakhri mahine ki salary₹90,000

Haath me aaya₹10,000

Is vishay par alag-alag raay rahi hai aur kuch maamlon me rahat bhi mili hai, par company aam taur par poori salary hi dikhati hai. Isliye ise apne saal ke tax ke hisaab me pehle se jod lena behtar hai. Rakam badi ho to CA se ek baar baat kar lena chahiye.

Ek achhi khabar bhi hai — notice pay ki recovery par GST nahi lagta. Ye baat sarkari spashtikaran se saaf ho chuki hai.

Naya employer buyout de dega kya

Bahut company deti hain, par sabhi nahi — aur jo deti hain unki apni sharten hoti hain.

  • Poora ya aanshik — kuch poora buyout deti hain, kuch ek tay seema tak.
  • Joining bonus ke roop me — aur uspar aapke slab me tax lagta hai. Yaani 80,000 mila to haath me 55-56 hazaar hi aaya.
  • Bond ke saath — ek saal ke andar chhodne par ye rakam wapas karni padti hai. Ye shart offer letter me chhoti si line me hoti hai.
  • Rasid par — purani company ki full and final statement dikhani hoti hai.

Maangne ka sahi samay offer par baat karte waqt hai, joining ke baad nahi. Us waqt ye ek saamanya baat maani jaati hai. Baad me maangna mushkil ho jaata hai.

Ek nazar me — teen raste

Jaldi jaana hai to teen hi raste hote hain, aur teeno ka kharch alag hai.

Ek chautha rasta bhi hota hai jo aksar sabse sasta padta hai — bachi hui chhuttiyan notice period me adjust karwana. Kai company iski ijaazat deti hain. Isse din ginti me aa jaate hain aur buyout ki rakam apne aap ghat jaati hai.

Jo galtiyan sabse zyada dikhti hain

Naukri badalne ki khushi me ye chhoti baatein reh jaati hain, aur baad me mahengi padti hain.

  • Resign pehle, letter baad me padhna. Notice period aur buyout ka niyam resign se pehle padh lijiye. Resign ke baad mol-bhaav ki jagah bahut kam bachti hai.
  • Naye employer se buyout ki baat na karna. Ye offer ke waqt maangi jaane wali saamanya cheez hai.
  • Zubaani wada. Manager ne keh diya ki 30 din me chhod denge — par HR ke record me kuch nahi. Har baat mail par lijiye.
  • Relieving letter ka dhyaan na rakhna. Buyout dena aur relieving letter milna do alag cheezein hain. Naye employer ko ye letter chahiye hota hai, aur bina iske joining ruk sakti hai.
  • PF aur gratuity ki tarikh bhool jaana. Gratuity ke liye poore 5 saal chahiye. 4 saal 8 mahine par nikalna bade nuksan ka sauda ho sakta hai — kuch hafte ruk jaane se poori gratuity mil jaati hai.
Aakhri point sabse mehnga hai. 40,000 basic wale ke liye 5 saal ki gratuity lagbhag ₹1.15 lakh banti hai. Us rakam ke liye do mahine ruk jaana lagbhag hamesha faayde ka sauda hota hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Buyout basic par lagta hai ya gross par?

Ye aapke appointment letter par nirbhar hai. Dono me lagbhag do guna ka antar hota hai, isliye resign se pehle wo line padh lena zaroori hai.

Buyout ki rakam par tax me chhoot milti hai?

Aam taur par company poori salary dikhati hai aur uspar TDS kaat leti hai, chahe rakam salary se hi kaati gayi ho. Rakam badi ho to CA se salah lena behtar hai.

Notice pay recovery par GST lagta hai?

Nahi. Sarkari spashtikaran ke baad ye baat saaf ho chuki hai ki notice pay ki recovery par GST nahi lagta.

Bachi hui chhutti notice period me adjust ho sakti hai?

Kai company ismein ijaazat deti hain. Isse notice ke din ginti me aa jaate hain aur buyout ki rakam ghat jaati hai. Ye HR se likhit me confirm karwa lijiye.

Buyout dene ke baad relieving letter pakka mil jaata hai?

Dono alag cheezein hain. Buyout ke saath full and final settlement aur relieving letter dono maangna chahiye, kyunki naye employer ko ye kagaz chahiye hota hai.
