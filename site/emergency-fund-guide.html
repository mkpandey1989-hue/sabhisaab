Emergency Fund Kya Hai? Kitna Aur Kahan Rakhein

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Zindagi me kabhi bhi achanak paisa ki zaroorat pad sakti hai — naukri chhoot jaana, koi medical kharcha, ghar ya gaadi ki badi repair, ya koi anhoni. Emergency fund wahi paisa hai jise aap sirf aisi hi sachhi zaroorat ke liye alag rakhte hain, taaki mushkil waqt me aapko mehnga loan lena ya apni SIP/FD todni na pade. Financial planning me sabse pehla kadam nivesh nahi, balki emergency fund hota hai.

Emergency fund kitna hona chahiye?

Simple rule: apne 3 se 6 mahine ke zaroori kharche ke barabar. "Zaroori kharche" matlab wo cheezein jo emergency me bhi rukengi nahi — ghar ka rent ya home loan EMI, ration, bijli-paani-gas, baccho ki fees, aur doosre loan ki kisht. In sabka jod nikaalein aur 3 se 6 se guna karein.

Ek misaal: Agar aapke mahine ke zaroori kharche ₹30,000 hain, to emergency fund ₹90,000 (3 mahine) se ₹1,80,000 (6 mahine) ke beech hona chahiye.

Kitne mahine ka rakhein — aapki situation par depend

  • Government / stable job: 3-4 mahine kaafi hai.
  • Private job, ek hi income: 6 mahine rakhein.
  • Freelancer / business / trader (income upar-neeche): 9-12 mahine, kyunki income guaranteed nahi.

Emergency fund kahan rakhein?

Sabse badi galti — emergency ka paisa share, mutual fund ya crypto me lagana. Ye paisa turant aur bina nuksan ke milna chahiye. Isliye:

  • Bank savings account: ek hissa yahan, taaki minute me nikal sake.
  • Sweep-in ya short-term FD: thoda zyada byaj, zaroorat par tod bhi sakte hain.
  • Liquid mutual fund: 1 din me paisa aa jaata hai, savings se thoda behtar return, risk bahut kam.

Achha tareeka: aadha bank me (turant), aadha FD ya liquid fund me (thoda badhe bhi).

Emergency fund kaise banayein?

  1. Chhota target lein: pehle 1 mahine ka kharcha jama karein, phir 3, phir 6.
  2. Automatic transfer: salary aate hi ek fixed rakam alag account me daal dein — "pehle khud ko pay karo".
  3. Extra paisa idhar daalein: bonus, tax refund, gift ka paisa seedha emergency fund me.
  4. Alag account rakhein: jiska ATM card roz jeb me na ho, taaki galti se kharch na ho.

Kab use karein, kab nahi?

Emergency hai: naukri jaana, hospital, ghar/gaadi ki urgent repair, family emergency. Emergency NAHI hai: sale me phone, chhutti ka trip, shaadi ki shopping, ya "achha deal mil raha hai" wali investment. Aur sabse zaroori — jab bhi emergency fund use karein, use dobara bhar dein.

Chhoti si baat, badi taakat

Emergency fund aapko amir nahi banata, par garib hone se bachata hai — kyunki mushkil waqt me aapko 30-40% byaj wale personal loan ya credit card ke jaal me nahi phasna padta. Ye "peace of mind" har investment se keemti hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Emergency fund kitna hona chahiye?

Aam taur par aapke 3 se 6 mahine ke zaroori kharcho ke barabar. Stable job me 3-4 mahine, private job me 6 mahine, aur freelancer/trader ke liye 9-12 mahine behtar hai.

Emergency fund kis jagah rakhein taaki turant nikal sake?

Savings account, sweep-in/short-term FD ya liquid mutual fund me — jahan se paisa turant bina nuksan mile. Share ya crypto me emergency ka paisa nahi rakhna chahiye.

Kya SIP se pehle emergency fund banana chahiye?

Haan. Emergency fund financial planning ka sabse pehla kadam hai. Ise banane ke baad hi SIP/nivesh par focus karein, warna emergency me SIP todni pad sakti hai.

Kisan ya karobari ko kitna fund rakhna chahiye?

9-12 mahine ka, kyunki aay saal bhar ek jaisi nahi aati. Naukri wale ke liye 6 mahine aur pakki sarkari naukri me 3-4 mahine kaafi hai.

Emergency fund se pehle SIP shuru kar sakte hain?

Behtar nahi. Bina fund ke ek chhoti musibat SIP tudwa deti hai ya credit card par le jaati hai. Ek hi apwaad — company ka EPF ya matching contribution mat chhodiye.

Kitna rakhna chahiye — apne hisaab se

"3 se 6 mahine" ek aam salah hai, par ye sabke liye ek jaisi nahi hoti. Aapki sthiti ke hisaab se number badalta hai:

  • Sarkari naukri, ek kamane wala: 3-4 mahine kaafi
  • Private naukri, do log kamane wale: 4-6 mahine
  • Private naukri, ek kamane wala + bachche: 6-9 mahine
  • Business, freelancing ya thekedari: 9-12 mahine — kyunki aamdani har mahine ek jaisi nahi hoti
  • Buzurg maa-baap ki zimmedari: upar likhe se 2-3 mahine aur jod lein

Hisaab lagate waqt kharcha lein, salary nahi. Kiraya/EMI, ration, bijli-pani, school fees, dawaiyan, aane-jaane ka kharch — bas itna. Bahar khaana aur ghoomna is hisaab me nahi aata, kyunki mushkil waqt me wo band ho jaata hai.

Paisa kahan rakhein

Emergency fund ka maqsad return kamana nahi, balki zaroorat par turant milna hai. Isliye:

  • Savings account — 1 mahine ka kharcha, turant nikal jaata hai
  • Sweep-in FD — baaki hissa. FD ka byaj milta hai par zaroorat par apne aap savings me aa jaata hai
  • Liquid fund — 1-2 din me paisa aa jaata hai, FD se thoda behtar return

Kitne mahine ka fund chahiye — sabke liye alag

"6 mahine" ek aam salah hai, par sabke liye sahi nahi.

Fund ka size utna zaroori nahi jitna ye ki wo kahan rakha hai. Emergency fund ka kaam kamai karna nahi, turant milna hai. Isliye use savings khate, sweep-in FD ya liquid fund me rakhiye — share, ELSS ya lambi FD me nahi. Ek aam galti ye bhi hai ki log emergency fund me poora saal ka kharcha jodte hain; jodna sirf zaroori kharcha chahiye — kiraya, khana, EMI, bijli, school fee, dawa. Ghoomna aur shauk ismein nahi aate, warna number itna bada dikhta hai ki log shuru hi nahi karte.
Yahan mat rakhein: share market, equity mutual fund, ya lock-in wali koi bhi scheme. Jab zaroorat padegi, ho sakta hai market neeche ho — aur aapko nuksan me bechna pade. Emergency fund ka poora maqsad hi yahi hai ki aisi nobat na aaye.

Banaya kaise jaaye

Ek saath 6 mahine ka kharcha jodna mushkil lagta hai — isliye tukdon me karein:

  1. Pehla lakshya: ₹10,000-25,000. Ye chhoti dikkaton (gaadi kharab, phone toota, chhoti bimari) ke liye kaafi hai
  2. Doosra lakshya: 1 mahine ka kharcha
  3. Teesra: 3 mahine
  4. Chautha: apna poora target

Har mahine salary aate hi ek tay rakam alag khaate me auto-transfer kar dein. Bache hue paise se bachat karne ki koshish aam taur par nahi chalti.

Istemaal kab karein

Emergency fund emergency ke liye hai — sale, shaadi ya chhutti ke liye nahi. Sahi istemaal: naukri chali jaana, achanak medical kharch, ghar ya gaadi ki zaroori marammat, pariwar me koi sankat.

Aur sabse zaroori — istemaal karne ke baad use dobara bharein. Bahut se log ek baar kharch karke wapas nahi bharte, aur agli dikkat par phir wahi problem aa jaati hai.
