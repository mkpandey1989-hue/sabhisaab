Delivery Date Calculator — Anumanit Tareekh

Aakhri period (LMP) ki tareekh se anumanit delivery date nikaali jaati hai. Neeche wo tareekh chuniye — due date, chal rahe hafte aur trimester dikh jayenge.

Hisaab kaise lagta hai

Ye Naegele’s rule par chalta hai, jo duniya bhar me istemaal hota hai:

Delivery date = Aakhri period ki pehli tareekh + 280 din (40 hafte)

Ek baat jo aksar uljhati hai — ginti garbh thehrne se nahi, aakhri period se shuru hoti hai. Isi liye jab aapko "8 hafte" bataya jaata hai, tab garbh ko lagbhag 6 hafte hue hote hain. Ye tareeka isliye chuna gaya kyunki period ki tareekh yaad rehti hai, garbh thehrne ki nahi.

Trimester:

  • Pehli — hafta 1 se 12
  • Doosri — hafta 13 se 26
  • Teesri — hafta 27 se delivery

Ye tareekh kitni pakki hoti hai

Saaf baat — ye ek anuman hai, tareekh nahi.

Bahut kam bacche theek due date par hote hain. Zyadatar delivery due date ke do hafte pehle se do hafte baad ke beech kahin bhi hoti hai, aur wo bilkul normal hai.

Aur ye hisaab tabhi theek baithta hai jab aapka period chakra lagbhag 28 din ka niyamit ho. Agar chakra chhota, lamba ya anishchit hai, to ye tareekh kaafi aage-peeche ho sakti hai. Aisi sthiti me sirf is calculator par mat jaiye.

Sabse pakki tareekh doctor ke ultrasound se milti hai — khaaskar pehli trimester wale scan se. Doctor us hisaab se due date badal bhi sakte hain, aur wahi maani jaati hai.

Har trimester me kya dhyaan rakhein

Pehli trimester (1-12 hafte)

  • Jaldi se jaldi doctor se milna aur pehla check-up
  • Folic acid — doctor jo bataye, wahi
  • Ulti aur thakan aam hai; bahut zyada ho to doctor ko bataiye
  • Koi bhi dawa lene se pehle doctor se poochiye — bina poochhe koi dawa nahi

Doosri trimester (13-26 hafte)

  • Aam taur par sabse aaraam wala samay
  • Zaroori scan aur jaanchein isi dauraan hoti hain
  • Bacche ki harkat mehsoos hone lagti hai

Teesri trimester (27+ hafte)

  • Check-up aur baar-baar hote hain
  • Aspatal ka bag pehle se taiyaar rakhiye
  • Bacche ki harkat rozana dhyaan dijiye — kam lage to turant doctor
Turant doctor se milna zaroori hai agar: khoon aa raha ho · tez ya lagataar pet dard ho · paani ki thaili phat jaye · bacche ki harkat achanak kam ya band ho jaye · tez sar dard, dhundhla dikhna ya achanak sujan ho · bukhaar ho. In me se kuch bhi ho to intezaar mat kijiye.

Taiyaari — sehat ke saath paisa bhi

Bacche ke aane se pehle kuch cheezein tay kar lena bahut tanaav bachata hai:

  • Health insurance dekhiye — zyadatar policy me maternity ke liye waiting period hota hai, aksar 2-4 saal. Isliye ye pehle se hona chahiye. Ab lene par shayad is baar kaam na aaye.
  • Aspatal ka kharcha pehle se poochiye — normal aur operation dono ka. Ye jaankar rakhna behtar hai.
  • Maternity leave ke niyam — apni company ke HR se likhit me poochiye. Aur pita ke liye bhi leave ka niyam dekh lijiye.
  • Emergency fund — is samay iska hona sabse zyada kaam aata hai. Emergency Fund Calculator
  • Sarkari yojanaein — kai rajyon aur kendra ki yojanaein garbhvati mahilaon ke liye hain. Apne aanganwadi ya sarkari aspatal se poochiye — jaankari muft milti hai.
  • Bacche ke bhavishya ki bachat — jaldi shuru karne ka sabse bada faayda hota hai. Sukanya Samriddhi aur padhai ka fund dekh lijiye.
Ye page sirf ek anuman aur aam jaankari deta hai. Ye kisi bhi tarah se doctor ki jagah nahi le sakta. Garbhavastha me har sawaal ka jawab apne doctor ya ANM se hi lijiye — aur regular check-up kabhi mat chhodiye.

Cycle 28 din ka na ho to due date khisak jaati hai

Har due date calculator ek maani hui baat par chalta hai — ki aapka cycle 28 din ka hai aur ovulation 14ve din hua. Asal me bahut si mahilaon ka cycle 26 se 34 din ka hota hai. Cycle lamba hai to ovulation der se hota hai, aur utne hi din due date aage badh jaati hai.

Wo baat jo aksar nahi batayi jaati: due date par theek us din bahut kam bacche paida hote hain — lagbhag 20 me se 1. Zyadatar janm us tareekh se 2 hafte pehle se 2 hafte baad ke beech hota hai. Isliye due date ko ek tareekh maanne ki jagah ek mahine ki khidki maaniye. Pehli sonography (11-13 hafte) ki naap LMP se zyada bharosemand hoti hai — doctor aksar usi se due date theek karte hain.

Kaunsi jaanch kis hafte me — taaki koi tareekh na chhoote

Due date pata chalne ke baad asli kaam ye hai ki aage ki tareekhein pehle se likh li jaayein. Bharat me zyadatar jaanch hafte ke hisaab se hoti hai, mahine ke nahi — aur ek-do hafte aage-peeche hone par kuch jaanch ka matlab hi badal jaata hai.

Do baatein jo aksar der se pata chalti hain: pehli — sarkari yojnaon (jaise PMMVY aur JSY) me paisa registration aur jaanch ki tareekh se juda hota hai, isliye pehli tikaai (ANC registration) aanganwadi ya PHC me jitni jaldi ho jaaye utna behtar. Doosri — NT scan ki khidki sirf kuch hafte ki hoti hai; nikal gayi to wo jaanch dobara nahi ho sakti. Isliye due date pata chalte hi calendar me ye tareekhein likh lijiye. Ye tool sirf anuman deta hai — poora bharosa apni doctor ki di hui tareekh par hi kijiye, aur upar wale hafte har mahila ke liye thode alag ho sakte hain.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Delivery date kaise nikaali jaati hai?

Aakhri period ki pehli tareekh me 280 din (40 hafte) jode jaate hain. Ginti garbh thehrne se nahi, period se shuru hoti hai.

Kya bacha isi tareekh par hoga?

Due date ek anuman hai, tareekh ka waada nahi. Doctor iska istemaal janch ka samay tay karne me karte hain — ise "us din baccha hoga" maan lena hi sabse aam galatfehmi hai.

Mera period niyamit nahi hai, to?

Tab ye hisaab kaafi aage-peeche ho sakta hai. Aise me doctor ke ultrasound se nikaali gayi tareekh hi sahi maani jaati hai.

Kab turant doctor se milna chahiye?

Khoon aana, tez pet dard, paani ki thaili phatna, bacche ki harkat kam ho jaana, tez sar dard ya achanak sujan — in me se kuch bhi ho to intezaar mat kijiye.

Mera cycle 32 din ka hai, due date badal jaayegi?

Haan, lagbhag 4 din aage khisak jaayegi. Calculator 28 din ka cycle maanta hai. Cycle jitne din lamba, utne din ovulation der se hota hai aur utni hi due date aage badhti hai.

Kitne bacche theek due date par paida hote hain?

Bahut kam — lagbhag 20 me se 1. Zyadatar janm due date se 2 hafte pehle se 2 hafte baad ke beech hota hai. Isliye ise ek tareekh ki jagah ek mahine ki khidki samjhein.

Sonography wali due date aur is calculator wali alag kyun hai?

Calculator aakhri period ki tareekh se ginta hai, jabki sonography bacche ki naap se. Naap zyada seedha saboot hai, isliye doctor aksar pehli sonography ke baad due date thodi badal dete hain. Aisa hona bilkul aam baat hai.

Sarkari yojna ka paisa lene ke liye kya dhyan rakhein?

Jaldi se jaldi ANC registration karwaiye aur har jaanch ka record MCP card me chadhwaiye. Yojnaon me kist aksar registration aur jaanch ki tareekh se judi hoti hai. Poori shart apne aanganwadi kendra ya PHC se hi confirm kar lijiye.
