Uttarakhand Farmer ID (UKFR) — Poori Jaankari

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~8 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Sarkar ki kheti se judi yojanaon ka faayda ab dheere-dheere ek pehchan number se juda ja raha hai — Farmer ID. Uttarakhand me ise UKFR (Uttarakhand Farmer Registry) ke zariye banaya jaata hai. Ye page batata hai ki registration me hota kya hai, kaunse kagaz taiyaar rakhne chahiye, kis kadam par log sabse zyada atakte hain, aur status kaise dekha jaata hai.

Farmer ID hai kya

Farmer ID ek pehchan number hai jo kisan ko uski zameen ke record se jodta hai. Soch ke dekhein — jaise Aadhaar aapki vyaktigat pehchan hai, waise hi Farmer ID aapki "kisan wali" pehchan hai, jisme aapki zameen ka byora bhi juda hota hai.

Iska maqsad ye hai ki jab koi yojana aaye, subsidy mile, ya fasal ka koi laabh baante, to baar-baar naye kagaz na dene padein. Ek baar record ban gaya to system khud dekh lega ki aapke paas kitni zameen hai aur aap patra hain ya nahi.

Ye kaam desh bhar me AgriStack ke tahat ho raha hai, aur har rajya apna portal chalata hai. Uttarakhand ka portal UKFR hai. Aage chal kar kheti se judi zyadatar sarkari suvidhaayein isi ID se judne ki ummeed hai — isi liye ise banwa lena samajhdaari hai.

Registration ke 5 kadam

Poori process ko ek nazar me dekhein to aisi dikhti hai:

Kadam 1 — Portal par jaana

Uttarakhand Farmer Registry ka portal kholein. Wahan kisan ke liye alag aur CSC/operator ke liye alag login hota hai. Khud kar rahe hain to kisan wala vikalp chunein. Upar wali tasveer me yahi page dikh raha hai.

Kadam 2 — Aadhaar se e-KYC

Apna Aadhaar number daalein. Yahi wo kadam hai jahan system aapki pehchan pakki karta hai. Dhyan rakhein ki Aadhaar me mobile number juda hona chahiye — warna OTP aayega hi nahi.

Kadam 3 — OTP verify karna

Aadhaar se jude mobile par ek OTP aata hai. Use daal kar aage badhna hota hai. Kuch jagah biometric (ungli) se bhi verify hota hai, khaas kar CSC par.

Kadam 4 — Zameen ka byora chunna

Ab system aapke naam se judi zameen dikhata hai — khasra number ke saath. Aapko wo khasra chunne hote hain jo aapke hain. Yahi sabse zyada dhyan dene wala kadam hai, kyunki ek hi parivaar ki zameen kai numbers me bati hoti hai.

Kadam 5 — Submit aur ID milna

Sab kuch jaanch kar submit karna hota hai. Iske baad aavedan jaanch ke liye jaata hai. Manjoori milne par Farmer ID ban jaati hai, jise portal se download kiya ja sakta hai.

Kaunse kagaz taiyaar rakhein

KagazKyun chahiye
Aadhaar cardPehchan aur e-KYC ke liye
Aadhaar se juda mobileOTP aane ke liye — sabse zaroori
Khatauni / khasra numberZameen ka byora milane ke liye
Bank passbookKhaate ka byora, jahan maanga jaaye
Mobile ya computerPortal kholne ke liye

In paanch me se sabse zyada dikkat Aadhaar se juda mobile number me aati hai. Bahut se logon ka purana number band ho chuka hota hai, ya Aadhaar banwate waqt kisi aur ka number de diya gaya tha. Aise me pehle nazdeeki Aadhaar seva kendra jaakar mobile number update karana padta hai — uske bina aage koi kadam nahi chalta.

Dusri aam dikkat khatauni ki hai. Agar zameen abhi tak pita ya dada ke naam par hai aur batwara kagaz par nahi chadha hai, to system aapko us zameen ka maalik nahi maanega. Ye kaam tehsil se pehle karana padta hai.

Status kaise dekhein

Aavedan karne ke baad wo turant manjoor nahi hota. Beech me jaanch hoti hai. Status dekhne ka tareeka aam taur par ye hota hai — portal par jaayein, apne registered mobile ya Aadhaar se login karein, aur "status" ya "apna aavedan dekhein" wale hisse me jaayein. Wahan aapka aavedan kis star par hai, ye dikh jaata hai.

Aam taur par teen tarah ke status milte hain. Pending matlab aavedan jama ho gaya hai aur jaanch baaki hai. Approved matlab Farmer ID ban gayi hai aur download ho sakti hai. Rejected ya "objection" matlab kuch kami hai, jise theek karke dobara bhejna padta hai.

Kitne din lagte hain — ye tay nahi hai. Kagaz saaf hon to kuch din me kaam ho jaata hai. Zameen ke record me koi dikkat ho to zyada samay lag sakta hai, kyunki tab tehsil ka kaam beech me aa jaata hai. Agar kaafi samay tak status nahi badla, to apne kshetra ke krishi vibhag ya CSC se sampark karna behtar rehta hai.

Aam galtiyan aur unka hal

1. OTP nahi aa raha. Iski sabse badi wajah hai Aadhaar se mobile number juda na hona. Aadhaar kendra jaakar number update karayein. Doosri wajah network hoti hai — pahad me signal kam ho to thodi der baad ya kisi doosri jagah se koshish karein.

2. Zameen dikh hi nahi rahi. Iska matlab record me aapka naam us khasra par nahi hai. Batwara, virasat ya naam ki galti — teeno wajah ho sakti hain. Ye tehsil ka kaam hai, portal se theek nahi hoga.

3. Naam ki spelling alag hai. Aadhaar me kuch aur, khatauni me kuch aur. Aisa bahut hota hai. Dono me se ek ko sudhwana padta hai — aam taur par jo galat hai wahi.

4. Kuch khasra chhoot jaana. Jaldi me log sirf ek-do khasra chun lete hain. Baad me poori zameen ka faayda nahi milta. Submit karne se pehle saari list dhyan se dekhein.

5. Adhoora chhod dena. Beech me page band ho gaya to bahut log dobara shuru nahi karte. Aavedan aam taur par draft me pada rehta hai — login karke aage badhaya ja sakta hai.

6. Kisi ko paisa de dena. Sarkari portal par registration ke liye alag se koi bada shulk nahi hota. CSC par tay sarkari fees hoti hai. Koi bada paisa maange to sochein.

Khud karein ya CSC se

Agar aapko mobile chalana aata hai, Aadhaar se mobile juda hai aur zameen ka record saaf hai — to khud kar lena sabse aasaan hai. Kul milakar 10-15 minute ka kaam hai.

Par agar zameen kai khasra me bati hai, naam me galti hai, ya aapko form bharne me jhijhak hoti hai — to CSC (jan seva kendra) ya krishi vibhag ke karmchari se karana behtar hai. Wahan operator ko roz ka anubhav hota hai aur wo galtiyan pehle hi pakad leta hai.

Ek salah — jo bhi karayein, apne saamne karayein. Aadhaar OTP kisi ko phone par mat batayein. Aur kaam hone ke baad apni Farmer ID ki copy zaroor le lein, chahe print ho ya mobile me photo. Aage kisi bhi yojana me wahi number kaam aayega.

UKFR login aur Farmer ID — aam dikkatein aur hal

Log ise kai naam se dhoondhte hain — UKFR, UKFR login, ukfr.agri, farmer id uttarakhand, farmer registry. Sab ek hi cheez hain.

UKFR ka matlab hai Uttarakhand Farmer Registry — rajya ke kisaanon ka digital record, jisme aapki pehchaan aur aapki zameen jodi jaati hai. Isi se aapko Farmer ID milti hai.

Login me sabse zyada dikkat kahan aati hai

  • Aadhaar se juda mobile number band ho gaya — OTP wahin jaata hai. Ye sabse aam wajah hai. Pehle nazdeeki CSC ya Aadhaar kendra par mobile update karwaiye, phir hi portal kholiye.
  • Naam ka farak — Aadhaar me kuch aur, khatauni me kuch aur. Ek akshar ka antar bhi kaam rok deta hai. Tehsil me record durust karwana padta hai.
  • Zameen ka record online nahi chadha — kai gaon me abhi bhi purana record hai. Patwari se milna padta hai.
  • Portal par bheed — aakhri tareekh ke paas server dheema ho jaata hai. Subah jaldi ya raat me koshish kariye.
Ek zaroori chetavni — ye kaam bilkul muft hai. CSC par tay sarkari shulk ke alawa koi paisa nahi lagta. Agar koi "jaldi karwa dunga" kehkar hazaar-do hazaar maange, to wo galat hai. Aur apna Aadhaar OTP kisi ko mat bataiye — na CSC wale ko, na kisi phone call par.

Farmer ID kis kaam aati hai

Kisan Samman Nidhi, fasal bima, subsidy, KCC, MSP par bikri, aur aane wali kai yojanaon me ab yahi pehchaan maangi jaati hai. Ek baar ban jaye to baar-baar kaagaz dene ki zaroorat kam ho jaati hai.

Sawaal-jawab

Farmer ID kya hoti hai?

Ye ek pehchan number hai jo kisan ko uski zameen ke record se jodta hai, taaki sarkari yojanaon me baar-baar kagaz na dene padein.

Uttarakhand me Farmer ID kaise banayein?

Uttarakhand Farmer Registry portal par Aadhaar se e-KYC karke, OTP verify karke aur apni zameen ke khasra chun kar aavedan jama karna hota hai.

Kaunse kagaz chahiye?

Aadhaar card, Aadhaar se juda chalu mobile number, khatauni ya khasra number aur bank passbook.

OTP nahi aa raha to kya karein?

Sabse aam wajah ye hai ki Aadhaar se mobile number juda nahi hai. Aadhaar seva kendra jaakar mobile update karayein.

Status kaise check karein?

Portal par apne registered mobile ya Aadhaar se login karke status wale hisse me dekh sakte hain.

Kitne din me ID ban jaati hai?

Koi tay samay nahi hai. Kagaz saaf hon to kuch din, aur record me dikkat ho to zyada samay lag sakta hai.

Kya iske liye paisa lagta hai?

Sarkari portal par alag se bada shulk nahi hota. CSC par tay sarkari fees lagti hai.

UKFR kya hota hai?

Registry se pehle har yojana me alag-alag kagaz maange jaate the. Ab ek hi ID se PM Kisan, fasal bima aur subsidy — teeno ka record ek jagah judta hai.

UKFR login me OTP nahi aa raha, kya karein?

OTP Aadhaar se jude mobile number par jaata hai. Wo number band ho gaya ho to pehle CSC ya Aadhaar kendra par mobile update karwaiye — yahi sabse aam wajah hai.

Farmer ID banwane me kitna paisa lagta hai?

Kuch nahi. CSC par tay sarkari shulk ke alawa koi paisa nahi lagta. Koi hazaar-do hazaar maange to wo galat hai.

Farmer ID kis kaam aati hai?

Kisan Samman Nidhi, fasal bima, subsidy, KCC aur MSP par bikri — ab in sab me yahi pehchaan maangi jaati hai.
