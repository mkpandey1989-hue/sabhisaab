Is hisse ko Samajhne Ke Liye: Champawat Kumaon ka woh hissa hai jahan se “Kumaon” naam ki shuruaat maani jati hai (NIC ke anusar Kali Kumaon naam pehle isi chhote ilaake ke liye chalta tha). Yahan ki sanskriti me pahadi Kumaoni parampara, Nepal ki seema ka asar, aur Terai-Bhabar ke mile-jule samaj, teeno dikhte hain. Jahan koi parampara poore Kumaon me common hai aur Champawat ki khaas nahi, wahan humne seedha likha hai. Isse aapko sahi jaankari milti hai aur galat daave se bachte hain.

Champawat: Jansankhya, Samajik Vibhajan aur Anusuchit Janjatiyan

Census Aankde: Jansankhya, Sex Ratio, Saksharta aur Gramin-Shehri Vibhajan

Seedha jawab: Census 2011 ke anusar Champawat zile ki jansankhya 2,59,648 hai, jisme 1,31,125 purush aur 1,28,523 mahilayein hain. Sex ratio lagbhag 980 mahilayein prati 1000 purush hai, jo Uttarakhand ke 963 ke average se zyada hai.

Champawat Uttarakhand ke chhote zilon me aata hai, par yahan ka jansankhya dhaancha kaafi dilchasp hai. Neeche ki table Census 2011 aur sarkari data portals par aadharit hai.

Mahila purush anupaat zyada kyun?
Pahadi zilon me purush rozgar ke liye bahar jate hain (sena, hotel, plains ki naukri), isliye gaon me mahilayein zyada rehti hain. Champawat ka 980 ka sex ratio isi purani “money-order” aur palayan ki kahani ka ek hissa hai. Isi vishay par aage Champawat: Zile Ki Arthvyavastha, Per Capita Income Aur Rozgar (arthvyavastha) me detail hai.
Saksharta ka do-do aankda kyun milta hai?
Yeh sabse zyada puchhe jane wala sawal hai. Census me kul 1,77,726 log saksharat (literate) hain. Agar yeh sankhya kul jansankhya (2,59,648) se bhaag di jaye to lagbhag 68.45% aata hai. Par Census ki asli “effective literacy” 7 varsh se upar ki aabadi par nikali jati hai (2,59,648 minus 37,028 bachche = 2,22,620), aur tab dar lagbhag 79.83% aati hai. Isliye kuch websites 68.45% aur kuch 79.83% likhti hain, dono apni jagah sahi hain, bas alag tareeke se nikali gayi hain. Sarkari aur Wikipedia jaise sources aam taur par 79.83% hi likhte hain.

Yeh sabhi aankde Census 2011 ke hain, kyunki uske baad ke zila-wise naye Census aankde abhi uplabdh nahi hain. Amar Ujala ki 11 November 2026 ki ek khabar ke anusar Uttarakhand me Census 2027 ki self-enumeration 16 November se aur ghar-ghar ganana 1 December se shuru hone wali hai.

Champawat Me Rehne Wale Samudaay aur Anusuchit Janjatiyan

Seedha jawab: Champawat me Anusuchit Janjati (ST) ki aabadi sirf lagbhag 0.52% hai. Uttarakhand sarkar ke Janjatiya Kalyan vibhag ki suchi ke anusar Tharu Terai ke hisson me (Champawat ke kuch hisson sahit) aur Raji (Van Rawat) Pithoragarh-Champawat kshetra me paye jate hain.

Champawat ke sandarbh me sahi tasveer yeh hai:

  • Bhotia (Marchha, Tolchha, Johari, Shauka, Rung): Yeh mukhya roop se Pithoragarh, Chamoli aur Uttarkashi ke ooncha Himalayi ilaakon me hain. Champawat me inki bada aabadi nahi hai.
  • Jaunsari: Yeh Dehradun ke Jaunsar-Bawar kshetra ka samudaay hai. Champawat se iska koi seedha sambandh nahi.
  • Tharu: Mukhya roop se Udham Singh Nagar ke Terai me. Janjatiya Kalyan vibhag ki suchi Champawat ke kuch hisson ko bhi shamil karti hai, jo Terai-Bhabar ki taraf Tanakpur-Banbasa ke aas-paas ka ilaka hai.
  • Buksa: Mukhya roop se Terai-Bhabar ke Udham Singh Nagar, Nainital aur aas-paas ke zilon me. Champawat me inki upasthiti sources me alag-alag milti hai.
  • Van Raji / Van Rawat (Banrawat): Yeh pahadi jangalon me rehne wala bahut chhota samudaay hai, jise sarkari suchi me Pithoragarh aur Champawat kshetra se joda gaya hai. Poore Uttarakhand me inki aabadi 1,000 se kam batayi jati hai.

Anya samudaay: 2011 ke Census me Champawat me mother-tongue ke roop me Bengali (lagbhag 519 log), Bhojpuri (lagbhag 462) aur Garhwali (lagbhag 561) bhi darj hain. Isse pata chalta hai ki Terai-Bhabar aur shehri ilakon me bahar se aakar bas chuke parivar bhi hain. Tanakpur aur Banbasa Nepal seema aur plains se judne ke karan mile-jule samaj ke liye jane jate hain.

Janjatiya suchi sarkar badalti rehti hai. Kisi bhi janjatiya praman-patra ya laabh ke liye Samaj Kalyan / Janjatiya Kalyan vibhag ya tehsil se pushti zaroor karein.

Champawat: Samajik Dhancha, Upnaam aur Gotra

Champawat Me Aam Taur Par Milne Wale Upnaam (Surnames)

Seedha jawab: Champawat me Kumaon ke aam upnaam milte hain, jaise Pant, Joshi, Pandey, Bhatt, Upadhyay, Bisht, Mehra/Mahara, Karki, Bora, Dhek, Rawat aur Shilpkar samudaay ke Arya, Tamta, Ram jaise naam. Kisi bhi upnaam ka matlab poori tarah ek hi gaon ya jaati se tay nahi hota.

Yeh suchi kisi ke gaon ya parivar ko “tay” karne ke liye nahi hai. Yeh sirf yeh batati hai ki log Champawat me kin naamon ko sabse zyada sunte hain.

Devidhura ke chaar “Kham” ek khaas samajik pehchan hain. Bagwal mele me bhaag lene wale chaar kul-samuh hain: Walig, Lamgadiya, Gahadwal (Gaharwal) aur Chamyal. Yeh sirf mela ke liye nahi, in kulon ki Barahi Devi ki seva ki purani parampara bhi hai (iska poora vivaran Champawat: Prasiddh Mele aur Doli Yatrayein me hai).

Shilpkar samudaay ki parampara: Dholi, Auji aur Das samudaay ke log sadiyon se pahad ke dhol-damau, shehnai aur shaadi-byaah ke baaje ko jinda rakhe huye hain. Inke bina Kumaoni jagar aur shaadi adhoori mani jati hai. Aaj ke samay me samaj me bhedbhaav kanooni roop se aparaadh hai aur Sabhisaab is par kisi bhi bhedbhaav ko badhava nahi deta.

Mool Niwas (Thaat), Gotra aur Vivah Ke Niyam

Seedha jawab: Kumaoni samaj me “Mool Niwas” ya “Thaat” ka matlab parivar ka purana pushtaini gaon hota hai, jahan unka kul-devta aur pitra-sthan hota hai. Vivah me aam taur par ek hi gotra me shaadi nahi hoti aur parivar ke gaon, kul aur “mait-sauras” ka bahut mahatva hota hai.

Kai pahadi parivar ab Haldwani, Dehradun, Delhi ya videsh me bas gaye hain, par ve har saal ya shadi ke maukon par apne “thaat” lautte hain. Isi liye Champawat jaise zilon me khaali padi purani haveliyan aur band gharon ke sath-sath “gharwapsi” ke udaharan bhi dikhte hain (iski charcha Palayan Aur Wapsi: Champawat Ki Tasveer me).

  • Mait: Mahila ka maayka. Pahadi lokgeeton ka sabse bhavuk shabd.
  • Sauras: Sasural.
  • Kul-devta: Parivar ke sabse mukhya devta, jinki puja har nayi fasal aur shubh avsar par hoti hai. Zile me Golu Devta ke alawa Bhumiyal, Airi, Chaumu aur Haru Heet jaise gram-devtaon ki manyata Kumaon me aam hai.

Champawat: Boli, Upbolian aur Aam Bolchal Ke Shabd

Champawat Ki Boli: Kumaoni, Kali Kumaon aur Kumaiya

Seedha jawab: Champawat zile ki mukhya boli Kumaoni hai. 2011 Census me lagbhag 78.19% log Kumaoni, 19.35% Hindi aur 2.46% anya bhashayein bolte hain. “Kali Kumaon” shabd Champawat ke aas-paas ke kshetra ki hi pehchan hai, aur “Kumaiya” ko is ilaake ki pramukh upboli mana jata hai.

Kumaoni bhasha me 20 se zyada upbolian ginayi jati hain, jaise Kumaiya, Majh Kumaiya, Soryali, Gangoli, Johari, Danpuriya, Askoti, Pachhai, Phaldakoti, Chaugarkhyali aur Khasparjiya. Vidwanon ke beech inke vibhajan par ek raay nahi hai.

Champawat me boli ka asli swaroop: - Pahadi hissa (Champawat, Lohaghat, Pati, Devidhura): Kumaoni ki Kali Kumaon / Kumaiya shaili. Nepal ki seema se jude hone ke karan kuch shabdon aur lahje me Nepali ka halka asar milta hai. - Terai-Bhabar hissa (Tanakpur, Banbasa): Yahan Hindi ka prabhav zyada hai aur Kumaoni ke sath Hindi, Nepali aur kuch parivaron me Bhojpuri ya Bengali bhi sunayi deti hai. - Likhit roop: Kumaoni ki apni koi aadhikarik lipi nahi hai, ise Devnagari me likha jata hai. Uttarakhand sarkar Kumaoni aur Garhwali ko badhava dene ke liye alag-alag kadam uthati rehti hai.

Upbolian kahan se kahan tak chalti hain, iska koi ek sarkari naksha nahi hai. Agar aap bhasha-vigyan ke jaankar hain to hume sudhaar bhejein.

Champawat: Pahadi Vaastukala (Ghar Banane Ka Tarika)

Kumaoni Ghar Ki Shaili: Pathal Ki Chhat, Kholi aur Bakhai

Seedha jawab: Kumaon ke paramparik gharon me patthar ki deewarein, “pathal” (slate) ki dhalvan chhat, lakdi ki nakkashidar “kholi” (darwaza) aur ghar ke aage patthar ka aangan hota hai. Kai gaon me ek hi kul ke ghar ek pankti me bane hote hain, jise “bakhai” kaha jata hai.

Yeh dhyan rakhein ki Koti Banal mukhya roop se Uttarkashi aur Rudraprayag-Garhwal ki Bhagirathi-Yamuna ghati ki shaili hai, na ki Champawat ki. Champawat jaise Kumaon ke zilon me ghar ki banawat thodi alag hoti hai:

Kumaoni ghar ki 4 khaas baatein: 1. Goth (neeche ka kamra): Neeche pashu bandhe jate hain, unki garmi se upar ka kamra sardi me garm rehta hai. 2. Dhumala / Taad: Chulhe ke upar lakdi ka machan jahan lakdi sukhayi jati hai aur dhuye se beej bhi surakshit rehta hai. 3. Aangan (Khlian): Patthar se bana, jahan dhan-mandua sukhaya jata hai aur shadi-byah, jagar jaise karya hote hain. 4. Pathal ki chhat: Garmi me thandi aur sardi me garm rehti hai.

Terai-Bhabar me ghar: Tanakpur aur Banbasa me paramparik pathar-lakdi ke ghar kam hain. Yahan ke zyadatar ghar eent aur cement se bane hain, kyunki yahan ki garmi aur barsat ka dhang pahadi hisse se alag hai.

Aapke liye Tip: Agar aap purana pahadi ghar kharidne, marammat karne ya homestay me badalne ka soch rahe hain to Champawat: Building Bylaws, Map Approval Aur Bhookamp Ke Niyam (building bylaws) aur Champawat: Pahad Me Ghar Banane Ki Lagat (hills me ghar ka nirman kharch) zaroor padhein. Purane pathal ke ghar ki marammat ka kharch aur permission alag se jaanna zaroori hai.

Champawat: Paramparik Pehnawa aur Vastra

Mahilaon Ka Pehnawa: Pichhora, Ghaghra-Angdi aur Gehnon Ka Mel

Seedha jawab: Kumaon ki paramparik shaadi aur tyoharon par mahilayein “Rangwali Pichhora” (peele-laal rang ka dupatta jis par swastik aur bindiyan hoti hain) pehenti hain. Roz ke pehnawe me ghaghra-angdi aur sardi me oon ke kapde chalte the.

  • Rangwali Pichhora: Peele (haldi jaisa) rang ka dupatta, jis par laal rang ki bindiyan aur swastik ya shubh chinh hote hain. Shaadi, naamkaran aur puja me pehna jata hai.
  • Ghaghra aur Angdi: Lamba ghera wala ghaghra aur chhoti angdi (choli), jo purane samay ka aam pehnawa tha.
  • Oon ke kapde: Zile ke ooncha thande hisson me bhed ki oon se bane shawl, topi aur kambal ka upyog hota tha.
  • Aaj ka pehnawa: Aaj zyadatar mahilayein saree, suit ya salwar-kameez pehenti hain. Pichhora sirf shaadi, puja aur tyohar tak simit ho gaya hai.

Purushon Ka Pehnawa: Kurta-Pajama, Pahadi Topi aur Janeu

Seedha jawab: Kumaon ke purushon ka paramparik pehnawa kurta, churidar pajama aur sir par “pahadi topi” (Kumaoni topi) hai. Brahmin aur Rajput samuh me janeu (pavitra dhaaga) dhaaran karne ki parampara hai.

  • Kurta-churidar pajama: Khari Holi gane wale kalakar safed kurta-churidar aur safed topi pehnte hain.
  • Pahadi topi: Aaj yeh Uttarakhand ki pehchan ban chuki hai, khaas karke samaroh aur neta-sammaan me.
  • Oon ka coat aur waistcoat: Thande kshetron me sardi me pehna jata tha.

Mirjai mukhya roop se Garhwal ke Jaunsar kshetra se jura hai, isliye humne ise Champawat ke Kumaoni pehnawe me nahi gina.

Champawat: Lok-Kala: Aipan, Jyunti Matrika aur Kasth-Kala

Aipan: Zameen aur Deewar Par Likhi Jane Wali Pavitra Kala

Seedha jawab: Aipan Kumaon ki pavitra rangoli-jaisi lok-kala hai, jo “geru” (laal mitti) ke aadhar par “biswar” (geele chawal ke ghol) se banayi jati hai. Dehri, puja-sthal aur aangan me tyohar aur sanskaron par Lakshmi Chauki, Saraswati Chauki aur Shiv Peeth jaise aakar banaye jate hain.

Aipan kaise banta hai?
1. Geru: Laal mitti ko gharkar ya paani me ghol kar aadhar banaya jata hai. 2. Biswar: Geele pisey chawal ka safed ghol, jise ungliyon (aam taur par aakhiri teen ungliyon) se rekhaon me banaya jata hai. 3. Aakar: Swastik, Lakshmi ke pair, Surya, ashtadal kamal, Saraswati Chauki aur Shiv Peeth jaise shubh chinh.

Kab banata jata hai: Diwali, naamkaran, janeu, shaadi, Dussehra aur anya shubh karyon par. Har tyohar ka apna alag Aipan hota hai.

Jyunti Matrika: Yeh devi-patt (devi ke chitra) hain, jinhe Janmashtami ya Nanda Ashtami jaise avsaron par ghar ki deewar par banaya jata hai.

Kasth-kala (lakdi ki nakkashi): Kholi (dwar) ke upar aur khidkiyon me deodar, chir ya anya lakdi par nakkashi ki parampara rahi hai, par Champawat me iska vistrit sarkari data abhi simit hai.

Aaj ke liye Tip: Aipan par aadharit hastkala aaj Uttarakhand me lokpriya ho rahi hai (greeting cards, wall hangings, sarees). Isse Self-Help Groups ko rozgar ke naye mauke mil rahe hain. Iski charcha Champawat: Hastkala, GI Tag Aur Sthaniya Shilp aur Champawat: NGO, SHG Aur Mahila Mangal Dal me aur aage hai.

Champawat: Lok-Sangeet, Gathayein aur Gayan Parampara

Jagar, Hudkiya, Holi, Nyoli aur Malushahi

Seedha jawab: Kumaoni lok-sangeet me Jagar (devta ko jagane ka geet), Hudkiya ki gathayein, Baithki-Khari-Mahila Holi, Nyoli, Chhapeli aur Malushahi jaisi prem-gathayein mukhya hain. Champawat me Holi aur Jagar ki parampara sabse jeevit dikhti hai.

Champawat, Lohaghat aur Tanakpur me yeh parampara aaj bhi jeevit hai.

Champawat: Paramparik Lok-Nritya

Chholiya, Chhapeli, Jhora aur Hurka Baul

Seedha jawab: Chholiya Kumaon ka prasiddh talwar-dhaal nritya hai, jo aam taur par shaadi ki baraat me hota hai. Iske alawa Chhapeli, Jhora, Chanchari aur Hurka Baul (khet ki ropai ka nritya-geet) Kumaoni nrityon me prasiddh hain.

  • Chholiya (Chhaliya): Talwar aur dhaal ke saath kiya jane wala veer nritya. Dhol, damau, turri aur ransingha ki thaap par nartak yuddh ka abhinay karte hain. Kumaon ke Pithoragarh, Champawat aur Almora kshetra me shaadi ki baraat me isse shubh mana jata hai. Iski utpatti ko kuch lok-kathayein purane yoddha-parivaron se jodti hain, par iska koi ek pakka aitihasik praman nahi diya ja sakta.
  • Chhapeli: Yugal nritya jisme premi-premika sawal-jawab karte hue naachte hain.
  • Jhora: Gol ghere me haath pakadkar kiya jane wala saamuhik nritya.
  • Chanchari: Geet aur taal ke saath chalne wala saamuhik nritya.
  • Hurka Baul: Dhan ki ropai ke samay Hudka ki thaap par gaya jane wala shram-geet.

Yeh mukhya roop se Garhwal ki parampara hai, na ki Champawat ki. Isi tarah “Thadiya” aur “Chaufula” Garhwal me adhik prachalit hain.

Champawat: Paramparik Vadya-Yantra aur Bajgi/Auji Samudaay

Dhol-Damau, Hudka, Ransingha, Masakbeen aur Dhol Sagar

Seedha jawab: Kumaon ke mukhya paramparik vadya Dhol-Damau, Hudka, Ransingha, Turri, Nagara aur Masakbeen (pahadi bagpipe) hain. Inhe bajane ki parampara sadiyon se Auji, Dholi aur Das jaise samudayon ne nibhayi hai.

Dhol Sagar kya hai?
Yeh dhol ke taal aur shabdon ki ek paramparik vidya hai, jisme alag-alag avsaron ke liye alag taal hote hain, jaise shaadi, devta ki aarti, yuddh ya mrityu. Yeh gyan guru se shishya ko mukh-sooch se jata raha hai. Aaj iske jaankar kam bache hain, isliye ise bachane ke liye kai sansthayein kaam kar rahi hain.

Champawat: Jeevan Ke Sanskar aur Vivah Parampara

Naamkaran se Vivah aur Antim Sanskar Tak

Seedha jawab: Kumaoni samaj me janm se mrityu tak ke sanskar Hindu parampara ke anusar hote hain, jinme Aipan, Pichhora, Shakunakhar geet aur kul-devta ki puja ka vishesh sthan hai. Vivah me Dhuliarghya (dulhe ka swagat), Kanyadan aur Bidaai mukhya charan hain.

Kumaoni vivah ke mukhya charan (sankshep me): 1. Ganesh aur Matrika puja: Shubh karya ki shuruaat. 2. Aipan aur Shakunakhar: Ghar sajaya jata hai aur mahilayein mangal geet gati hain. 3. Baraat ka aagman aur Dhuliarghya: Shaam ke samay kanya paksh dulhe ka swagat karta hai aur uske pair dhulate hain. Chholiya nritya aur dhol-damau isi samay bajte hain. 4. Kanyadan aur phere: Mukhya vaidik vidhi. 5. Pichhora aur Nath: Dulhan ki pehchan ban chuke paramparik vastra aur gehne. 6. Bidaai: Dulhan ko Mait chhodne ka bhavuk kshan, jahan Nyoli jaise geet yaad aate hain.

Antim sanskar: Hindu parampara ke anusar nadi ya shamshan ghat par hota hai aur uske baad shok aur shraddha ke niyam parivar aur kul ki parampara ke hisaab se badalte hain.

Vivah ke charan aur naam gaon, kul aur pandit ki parampara ke hisaab se badalte hain. Yeh sankshipt marg-darshak hai, kisi ek parivar ka niyam nahi. Apne pandit ya buzurgon se zaroor salah lein.

Phool Dei kaise manayi jati hai?
Chaitra ki Sankranti par bachche saphed-peele-gulabi phool, chawal aur gud lekar har ghar ki dehri (kholi) par phool dalte hain aur lok-geet gaate hain. Badle me ghar ke log unhe gud, chawal aur paise dete hain. Yeh Basant ke swagat ka tyohar hai.

Bhitauli ka asli bhaav: Pahad ki shaadi-shuda behen apne maayke se saal bhar door rehti thi. Chaitra me bhai uske liye pakwan lekar usse milne jata tha. Isi “milne ki khushi” ko Bhitauli kaha gaya. Isse Nyoli geet bhi jude hain.

Harela kab? Shravan ki Sankranti ke din (aam taur par Julai ke madhya me). Ghar ke andar das din pehle 5 ya 7 prakar ka anaj boya jata hai. Harela ke din uski hari fasal ko katkar devta ko chadhaya jata hai aur parivar ke sir par rakha jata hai. Mausam aur kheti ke liye shubhkamna ka bhaav ismein hai.

Tyohar ki sahi tithiyan har saal Hindu panchang ke anusar badalti hain. Tithi ke liye kisi bhi saal ka Kumaoni panchang dekhein.

Champawat: Prasiddh Mele aur Doli Yatrayein

Purnagiri Mela: Kumaon Ka Sabse Lamba Mela

Seedha jawab: Purnagiri Mela Tanakpur ke paas Purnagiri Devi mandir me Chaitra Navratri ke dauran lagta hai. Sources ke anusar yeh Vishuwat Sankranti se lagbhag 40 din chalta hai aur Holi ke baad Kumaon ke sabse lambe melon me gina jata hai.

  • Kahan: Purnagiri mandir Tanakpur se lagbhag 20 km door, Kali (Sharda) nadi ke daayein kinare Annapurna parvat par hai.
  • Oonchai: Sarkari aur yatra sources me ise lagbhag 1,676 meter (5,500 feet) likha gaya hai. Kuch jagah galat taur par 3,000 meter bhi likha milta hai, isliye hum 1,676 meter hi maante hain.
  • Kab: Chaitra Navratri (Maarch-April). Kuch sources ke anusar mela Vishuwat Sankranti se lagbhag 40 din chalta hai. Purnagiri mandir ko 108 Siddhpeethon me ginte hain.
  • Rajya sarkar ka saath: 16 March 2025 ko Mukhyamantri Pushkar Singh Dhami ne Tanakpur me Maa Purnagiri Mela ka udghatan kiya tha, jo is mele ke sarkari mahatva ko dikhata hai.
  • Raasta: Sources ke anusar Tunyas tak lagbhag 17 km ka gaadi ka raasta hai, aur wahan se lagbhag 3 km ki padyatra hai.

Purnagiri yatra ka poora vivaran (raasta, samay, dharamshala, safety) Champawat: Pramukh Mandir, Dham aur Dharmik Sthal aur Champawat: Hawai Jahaz Aur Helicopter me detail me aayega.

Devidhura Ka Bagwal Mela: Raksha Bandhan Par Pathar Nahi, Fal-Phool

Seedha jawab: Bagwal Devidhura ke Barahi Dham me Raksha Bandhan ke din hone wala paramparik mela hai, jisme chaar “Kham” (kul-samuh) ek-doosre par pehle pathar, ab zyadatar fal aur phool barsate hain. Raksha Bandhan 2026 (28 August) par bhi yeh fal-phool se hua tha.

  • Kaun-se chaar Kham: Walig, Lamgadiya, Gahadwal (Gaharwal) aur Chamyal.
  • Kahan: Devidhura, Champawat zile ka Barahi Dham, jise Shaktipeeth maana jata hai.
  • Kya hota hai: Chaaro Kham do taraf ban kar mandir ke aangan me ek-doosre par barsaat karte hain. Parampara ke anusar yeh tab tak chalta tha jab tak ek aadmi ke sharir ke barabar khoon na beh jaye, jise devi ko prasann karne ka tarika maana jata tha.
  • Badlav: Uttarakhand High Court ke nirdesh ke baad pathar ki jagah fal (seb, nashpati) aur phool ka istemal badhaya gaya. Kuch saalon me chot lagne ki khabrein aati rahi hain, isliye prashasan suraksha aur pratham-upchar ki taiyari karta hai.
  • 2026 ka mela: PTI ki report ke anusar 28 August 2026 ko Mukhyamantri Dhami ki upasthiti me Bagwal fal aur phool se hua, jo lagbhag aath minute chala.
  • Mela ki khaasiyat: Is mele me Kumaon aur Nepal se log aate hain. Devidhura Lohaghat se lagbhag 45 km door hai (Champawat se doori raaste ke hisaab se alag-alag batayi jati hai), isliye wahan jane ke liye thoda pehle se yojana banayein.

Dhyan rakhne wali baatein (agar aap Bagwal dekhne jaa rahe hain): 1. Bheed bahut hoti hai, isliye subah jaldi pahunchein. 2. Ghere ke andar na jayein, sirf prashasan ke batae gaye sthal par rahein. 3. Baccho aur buzurgon ko pehle se surakshit jagah par rakhein. 4. Rukne ke liye pehle se booking karein, kyunki Devidhura me hotel kam hain.

Champawat: Sabse Zyada Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQ)

Champawat ki jansankhya kitni hai?
Census 2011 ke anusar 2,59,648 hai (purush 1,31,125, mahila 1,28,523).
Champawat ka sex ratio kitna hai?
Lagbhag 980 mahilayein prati 1000 purush, jo Uttarakhand ke average 963 se zyada hai.
Champawat ki saksharta dar kitni hai?
7 varsh se upar ki aabadi par lagbhag 79.83% (effective literacy). Kul jansankhya par nikala jaye to lagbhag 68.45% aata hai. Dono dar alag tareeke se nikali jati hain.
Champawat me kaun-si boli-boli jati hai?
Mukhya boli Kumaoni hai. 2011 me lagbhag 78.19% log Kumaoni, 19.35% Hindi bolte the.
Kali Kumaon kya hai?
Kali nadi ke kinare ka woh kshetra jise NIC ke anusar kabhi “Kumaon” ka mool kshetra mana gaya. Aaj ise Champawat zile ka upnaam bhi mana jata hai.
Champawat me kitne log Anusuchit Janjati ke hain?
Lagbhag 1,339 log, yani lagbhag 0.52% (Census 2011).
Champawat me Bhotia ya Jaunsari samudaay hain kya?
Nahi, yeh mukhya roop se Pithoragarh, Chamoli, Uttarkashi aur Dehradun ke Jaunsar-Bawar ke samudaay hain. Champawat me inki badi aabadi nahi hai.
Aipan kya hota hai?
Geru aur biswar (chawal ke ghol) se banayi jane wali pavitra Kumaoni lok-kala, jo tyoharon aur sanskaron par banti hai.
Pichhora kya hota hai?
Peele rang ka dupatta jis par laal bindiyan aur swastik hote hain, jo shaadi aur puja me pehna jata hai.
Chholiya nritya kya hai?
Kumaon ka talwar-dhaal wala veer nritya, jo aam taur par shaadi ki baraat me hota hai.

Phool Dei kab manayi jati hai? Chaitra ki Sankranti (aam taur par March ke madhya) ke din.

Bagwal mela kab hota hai aur kahan?
Raksha Bandhan ke din Devidhura ke Barahi Dham me, Champawat zile me.
Kya Bagwal me ab bhi pathar chalte hain?
Ab zyadatar fal aur phool ka upyog hota hai, kyunki High Court ke nirdesh ke baad pathar hataye gaye. Phir bhi kuch jagah chot lagne ki khabrein aa sakti hain, isliye sambhal kar jayein.
Purnagiri mela kab lagta hai?
Chaitra Navratri (March-April) me, Vishuwat Sankranti se lagbhag 40 din tak chalne wale mele ke roop me jana jata hai.
