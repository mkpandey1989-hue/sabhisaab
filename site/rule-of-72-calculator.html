Rule of 72 kya hai

Rule of 72 ek jaldi wala tareeka hai ye jaanne ka ki compounding se paisa kitne saal me dugna hoga. Bas 72 ko byaj dar se baant do — jo aaya, utne saal me paisa double.

Double hone ke saal = 72 ÷ byaj dar
Ya: chahiye dar = 72 ÷ kitne saal me double chahiye

Misaal se samjhein

Agar aapka paisa 8% par badh raha hai, to 72 ÷ 8 = 9 saal me double ho jayega. Agar 12% par hai, to 72 ÷ 12 = 6 saal. Yaani jitni zyada dar, utni jaldi paisa dugna. Ye seedha dikhata hai ki thodi zyada dar lambe samay me kitna bada farak laati hai.

Ye kaam kyun ka hai

  • Jaldi andaaza — bina calculator ke, dimaag me hi hisaab lag jaata hai.
  • Vikalpon ki tulna — FD 7% vs mutual fund 12% — dono kitne saal me double, turant pata.
  • Mehngai ka asar — 6% mehngai par aapke paise ki keemat 12 saal me aadhi ho jaati hai (72÷6).

Dhyaan rakhein

Rule of 72 ek anumaan hai, ekdum theek nahi — par bahut kareeb hota hai, khaaskar 6-10% dar ke beech. Asli, sahi hisaab ke liye compound interest calculator istemaal karein. Mehngai ko dhyaan me rakhkar asli return samajhne ke liye ye rule bahut kaam aata hai — kyunki jo paisa 7% FD me badh raha hai, wo agar mehngai 6% hai to asal me bahut dheere badh raha hai.

Mehngai par Rule of 72 — doosri taraf ka sach

Rule of 72 sirf paisa badhne ka hisaab nahi hai. Isse ulta karke ye bhi pata chalta hai ki aapke paise ki keemat aadhi kab ho jayegi.

72 ko mehngai dar se bhaag dijiye. 6% mehngai par 72 ÷ 6 = 12. Yaani 12 saal me aaj ke ₹100 sirf ₹50 jitna saman khareed payenge.

Isi liye "FD me paisa surakshit hai" wali baat aadhi sach hai. 6.5% FD par 72 ÷ 6.5 ≈ 11 saal me paisa dugna. Par 6% mehngai par 12 saal me keemat aadhi. Yaani 11-12 saal ki mehnat ke baad haath me lagbhag utna hi rehta hai jitna shuru me tha — aur us par tax alag se lagta hai.

Ye ek jhatka dene wala hisaab hai, par isse ek saaf baat nikalti hai: kisi bhi nivesh ko dekhte waqt sirf return mat dekhiye, return minus mehngai dekhiye. Usi ko asli (real) return kehte hain, aur asli daulat wahi se banti hai.

Kahan ye rule kaam nahi karta

Rule of 72 ek anuman hai, formula nahi. Teen jagah ye galat jawab deta hai:

  • Bahut oonchi dar par — 25-30% se upar ye rule asli number se kaafi hat jaata hai. Wahan poora compound formula hi theek hai.
  • Jahan return har saal badalta ho — mutual fund ya share me kisi saal 20%, kisi saal minus 10% hota hai. Rule of 72 sirf ek stable dar par chalta hai. Isliye equity me ise "mota anuman" maaniye, waada nahi.
  • Jahan beech me paisa daalte ya nikalte rahein — SIP ya har mahine jama karne wale plan me ye rule laagu nahi hota. Uske liye SIP calculator hi sahi hai.

Iska sabse achha istemaal kya hai? Dimaag me turant tulna karna. Kisi ne 12% ka plan dikhaya — 72 ÷ 12 = 6, yaani 6 saal me dugna. Ab poochiye: kya ye 6 saal me sach me dugna karega, aur kis risk par? Ye ek sawaal kai galat nivesh se bacha leta hai.

Aur agar koi "5 saal me paisa dugna" ka waada kare, to ulta lagaiye — 72 ÷ 5 ≈ 14.4% saalana. Jo isse bahut zyada waada kare, us par bharosa karne se pehle bahut soch lena chahiye.

Kis dar par paisa kitne saal me dugna

72 ko byaj dar se bhaag dijiye — jawab lagbhag saalon me aa jaata hai. Ye seedha table hai:

Saalana returnDugna hone meChauguna hone me
6% (FD jaisa)12 saal24 saal
8% (PPF jaisa)9 saal18 saal
10%7.2 saal14.4 saal
12% (equity ka anuman)6 saal12 saal
15%4.8 saal9.6 saal
Isi niyam ka ulta istemaal zyada kaam ka hai: mehngai 6% hai, to aapke paise ki keemat 12 saal me aadhi reh jaayegi. Isliye 6% ki FD me paisa "badh" nahi raha, sirf mehngai ke barabar chal raha hai — aur tax kaatne ke baad to peeche. Nivesh chunte waqt sawaal ye nahi ki return kitna hai, sawaal ye hai ki mehngai se kitna upar hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Rule of 72 kaise kaam karta hai?

72 ko byaj dar se baant do — jo aaye, utne saal me paisa double. Jaise 8% par 9 saal (72÷8).

Ye kitna sahi hota hai?

Ek accha anumaan hai, khaaskar 6-10% dar ke beech. Ekdum theek hisaab ke liye compound interest calculator use karein.

Mehngai ke liye bhi kaam aata hai?

Haan. 6% mehngai par paise ki keemat 72÷6 = 12 saal me aadhi ho jaati hai. Isse mehngai ka asar samajhna aasan.

Zyada dar se kya farak?

Jitni zyada dar, utni jaldi paisa double. 12% par 6 saal, 8% par 9 saal — chhota farak bhi lambe samay me bada asar.

SIP par Rule of 72 laga sakte hain?

Nahi. Ye rule ek baar jama kiye gaye paise par chalta hai. Har mahine jama karne wale nivesh ke liye SIP calculator hi sahi jawab dega.
