Pahadi Shabdkosh: 120+ Lupt Hote Theth Pahadi Shabd

Pahadi bhasha ka ye shabdkosh kyun zaroori hai?

Ye shabdkosh Garhwali-Kumaoni boliyon ke ghar, kheti, mausam, rishtedari aur bhavnaon se jude 120 se zyada shabd ek jagah deta hai. Ye shabd lupt hote ja rahe hain, isliye inhe likhna sanskriti bachane ka kaam bhi hai.

Ye shabd aur unke arth Garhwal-Kumaon ki lok-boliyon se liye gaye hain. Shabd ka uchcharan aur arth gaon-gaon me thoda badal sakta hai.

Buzurgon ka mausam vigyan: prakriti ke sanket

Buzurg pahadi prakriti ke sanketon se mausam ka andaza lagate the. Ye manyatayein anubhav par adharit hain aur vaigyanik roop se har baar sahi hone ki guarantee nahi hain.

  • Kali cheentiyon ka ande lekar oopar chadhna: buzurg isse jaldi barish aane ka sanket maante the.
  • Ghughuti aur “Kafal Pakyo” jaisi chidiya ki boli: mausam badalne aur fal pakne ka sanket.
  • Buransh ka zyada khilna: garmi adhik padne ka sanket maana jata tha.
  • Chandrama ke charo taraf mandal (halo): barish ya barf ki sambhavna ka sanket.
  • Titar ka bolna: mausam badalne ka sanket.

Lori aur bachchon ke khel-geet

Pahadi lori aur khel-geet me Ghughuti, Kauwa, Chand aur pahadi jaanwar bahut aate hain. Ye geet bachon ko sulane, ghutno par jhulane aur unhe apni boli sikhane ka tareeka the.

Pahadi Shabdkosh: Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Garhwali aur Kumaoni me kya farak hai?
Dono Central Pahadi bhasha samooh ki boliyan hain. Uchcharan aur kuch shabd alag hain, par kai shabd aur geet milte-julte hain.
Jagar kya hai?
Jagar devta ya purvajon ko dhol-damau ya hudka ki thap par avahan karne ki parampara hai.
Dhol-Damau kya hai?
Dhol-Damau Uttarakhand ka prasiddh lok-vadya jod hai, jo sanskar, jagar aur tyohar me bajta hai.
Egas (Budhi Diwali) kab hoti hai?
Egas Diwali ke lagbhag gyarah din baad Garhwal me manayi jati hai.
Kafuli kya hai?
Kafuli palak ya methi se bani gaadhi pahadi sabzi hai.
Chainsoo kya hai?
Chainsoo bhuni hui urad ki daal se bana paushtik pahadi vyanjan hai.
Mandua kya hota hai?
Mandua (finger millet) pahadi moti anaaj hai, jisse roti aur halwa banta hai.
Jhangora kya hai?
Jhangora (Madira/barnyard millet) pahadi anaaj hai, jisse kheer aur bhaat banta hai.
Singori kya hai?
Singori Almora ki khoye se bani mithai hai jo maalu ke patte me lipti hoti hai.
Ramman kya hai?
Ramman Chamoli ke Saloor-Dungra gaon ka dharmik utsav aur ritual-natya hai, jo UNESCO ki virasat suchi me hai.
Uttarakhand me Holi kaise manayi jati hai?
Kumaon me Baithaki Holi aur Khadi Holi ki parampara hai, jisme raag-raagini ke saath Holi gayi jati hai.
Pahadi shaadi ki parampara kaisi hoti hai?
Pahadi vivah me dhol-damau, mangal geet, aipan aur sthaniya riti-riwaj hote hain. Parampara kshetra ke hisaab se alag hoti hai.
Uttarakhand me kaun si bhasha rajya ki sarkari bhasha hai?
Hindi sarkari bhasha hai aur Sanskrit dusri sarkari bhasha hai.
