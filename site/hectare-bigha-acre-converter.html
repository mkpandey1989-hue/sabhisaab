Zameen ki ikaiyan

India me zameen kai ikaiyon me naapi jaati hai. Kuch sarkari hain (hectare, square meter), kuch paramparik (bigha, acre). Sabse badi dikkat ye hai ki bigha ka maap har rajya me alag hota hai — UP me alag, Rajasthan me alag, Uttarakhand me alag.

1 Hectare = 10,000 m² = ~2.47 acre
1 Acre = 4,047 m² = ~43,560 sq ft
1 Bigha (pucca) ≈ 2,529 m² (rajya se badalta hai)

Bigha kyun alag hota hai

Bigha ek paramparik ikai hai jo angrezon se pehle se chali aa rahi hai, aur har ilaake ne apne hisaab se ise tay kiya. Isiliye "pucca bigha" aur "kaccha bigha" jaise farak hain, aur ek rajya ka bigha doosre se mel nahi khata. Isliye ye converter ek anumaan deta hai — registry ya patwari ka aankda hi antim hai.

Ye converter kab kaam aata hai

  • Zameen bechte/khareedte waqt — jab document ek ikai me ho aur aapko doosri chahiye.
  • Loan ke liye — bank aksar hectare ya square meter maangta hai.
  • Sarkari yojana ke form me — jaise PM-Kisan, jahan zameen hectare me bharni hoti hai.
  • Do zameenon ki tulna — dono ko ek ikai me laakar.

Uttarakhand ke liye khaas

Uttarakhand ke pahadi ilaakon me zameen aksar nali aur mutthi me naapi jaati hai, jo bigha se bhi alag hai. Uske liye alag Nali-Mutthi Converter hai. Zameen ke kaagaz kharidne se pehle zaroor dekh lein — poori list yahan hai.

Kaagaz par kaunsi ikai likhi hoti hai

Zameen ka sauda karte waqt sabse badi uljhan yahi hai ki har taraf alag ikai bolti hai. Ek hi zameen ke liye:

  • Sarkari kaagaz (khatauni, khasra) — aam taur par hectare me, kabhi-kabhi are aur square metre me.
  • Gaon ki baat-cheet — bigha, nali, mutthi, kaccha-pakka bigha — jo bhi us ilaake ka chalan ho.
  • Property dealer ya builder — square feet me, kyunki usme number bada dikhta hai.
  • Bank ka loan kaagaz — aam taur par square metre ya hectare.
Isi liye sauda tay karne se pehle ek kaam zaroor kijiye — jo ikai kaagaz par likhi hai, usi ikai me baat kariye. "Do bigha" sunkar sar mat hilaiye; khatauni me hectare me kitna likha hai, wo dekhiye aur yahan convert karke milaiye.

Hectare aur acre poore desh me ek jaise hain — inme koi confusion nahi. Sirf bigha badalta hai, aur wahi sabse zyada istemaal hota hai. Isi wajah se do gaon ke beech bhi "bigha" ka matlab alag ho sakta hai.

Sauda karte waqt jo galtiyan mehngi padti hain

Zameen ke maap me chhoti si galti bade paise ki galti ban jaati hai. Teen cheezein dhyaan me rakhiye:

  • Bigha ki dar maankar mat chaliye. Ek gaon me bigha 968 sq ft ho sakta hai, doosre me 27,000 sq ft se upar. Ye 25 guna se zyada ka antar hai. Sthaaniya patwari ya tehsil se hi confirm kijiye.
  • Kaagaz ka rakba aur zameen ka rakba alag ho sakta hai. Saalon me medh sarak jaati hai, raasta nikal jaata hai, koi hissa kat jaata hai. Registry se pehle amin ya sarkari maapak se paimaish karwana sabse surakshit hai — kharcha kam hai, bachaav bada.
  • Rate per unit poochte waqt ikai poochiye. "Das lakh per bigha" sunne me saaf lagta hai, par jab tak bigha ka naap tay na ho, ye vaakya adhoora hai.

Aur ek baat jo pahadi ilaakon me khaas hai — dhalan wali zameen. Kaagaz par rakba samtal (horizontal) maap se likha jaata hai, jabki chalte waqt zameen zyada dikhti hai. Isliye "dikhne me to zyada lag rahi hai" wali baat par sauda mat kijiye, kaagaz par jo hai wahi asli hai.

Zameen ke kaagaz ke poore hisaab ke liye zameen kharidne se pehle kaunse kaagaz dekhein wala lekh bhi padh lijiye — registry se pehle wo checklist bahut kaam ki hai.

Ek hectare me kitna kya

Sarkari kagaz aur yojnaon me hectare likha hota hai, par kisan bigha me sochta hai.

Sarkari yojna me ye antar seedha paise ka hota hai. Kai yojnaon me madad "per hectare" tay hoti hai, par zameen kagaz me bigha ya nali me likhi hoti hai. Galat badalne par aavedan me rakba galat chala jaata hai, aur baad me sudhaar karana mushkil hota hai. Isliye form bharne se pehle apne khatauni ka rakba dekhiye — usme aksar hectare pehle se likha hota hai, badalne ki zaroorat hi nahi padti. Bigha ka number rajya ke hisaab se badalta hai, isliye upar wala sirf UP ke pucca bigha ka hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

1 hectare me kitne bigha hote hain?

Ye rajya par nirbhar karta hai kyunki bigha har jagah alag hai. Pucca bigha (~2529 m²) ke hisaab se lagbhag 4 bigha. Registry ka aankda dekhein.

1 acre kitne square feet ka hota hai?

1 acre = 43,560 square feet = ~4,047 square meter. Ye ikai poore India me same hai.

Bigha ka maap fixed kyun nahi?

Bigha paramparik ikai hai jo har ilaake ne apne hisaab se tay ki. Isliye UP, Rajasthan, Uttarakhand sab me alag hai.

Registry me kaunsa maap chalega?

Registry me jo maap (aksar square meter ya hectare) likha hai wahi kanooni roop se maany hai. Converter sirf samajhne ke liye.

Hectare aur acre bhi ilaake se badalte hain kya?

Nahi. Hectare aur acre poore desh me — balki duniya bhar me — ek jaise hain. Sirf bigha, nali, mutthi jaisi sthaaniya ikaiyan badalti hain.

Kaagaz me hectare likha hai, gaon me bigha bolte hain — kya karun?

Sauda hamesha us ikai me tay kariye jo khatauni ya khasra par likhi hai. Baat-cheet bigha me hoti rahe, par likha-padhi kaagaz wali ikai me hi honi chahiye.
