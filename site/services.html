Custom Tools aur Website — Kaam Karwayein

Sab Hisaab · Aakhri update: August 2026

Sab Hisaab par aapne 154 calculator dekhe honge. Ye saare tools, unka code aur poori website maine khud banaye hain. Agar aapko bhi apne kaam ka koi calculator, tool ya website chahiye, to main use bana sakta hoon.

Neeche saaf-saaf likha hai ki main kya kaam kar sakta hoon, kya nahi kar sakta, aur kaam kaise chalta hai. Ye padh kar aapko andaaza ho jaayega ki aapka kaam ban paayega ya nahi.

1. Custom calculator aur tools

Ye mera sabse mazboot kaam hai. Is website ke 154 calculators isi ka namoona hain — aap unhe khud chala kar dekh sakte hain.

  • Business ka apna calculator — dukaan ka margin, transport ka kiraya, contractor ka estimate, printing ka rate, kisi bhi dhandhe ka apna hisaab
  • Quotation ya estimate tool — customer detail bhare, quotation ban jaaye, PDF download ho jaaye
  • Excel ko web tool banana — aapke paas jo Excel sheet chal rahi hai, use website par chalne wale tool me badalna, taaki phone se bhi chale
  • Website me lagne wala widget — aapki maujooda website me embed ho jaaye
  • Chart wala dashboard — kharcha, bikri ya data ko graph me dikhane wala tool

2. Website banana

  • Chhoti business website — dukaan, hotel, school, coaching, workshop, kisi bhi dhandhe ke liye 4-6 page ki saaf website
  • Landing page — ek product ya service ke liye ek hi page, jismein poori jaankari aur sampark form ho
  • Portfolio ya resume website
  • Purani website ka sudhaar — dheemi, purani ya mobile par kharab dikhne wali site ko theek karna
Saari websites static hoti hain — yaani bahut tez chalti hain, mobile par saaf khulti hain, aur hosting ka kharcha lagbhag zero hota hai (Cloudflare Pages par free). Isi tarah ye website bhi bani hai.

3. SEO aur setup ka kaam

  • Google Search Console setup aur sitemap submit
  • Schema (structured data) lagana
  • Website ki speed aur mobile view theek karna
  • Meta title, description aur internal linking ka dhaancha
  • Google Analytics setup

Dhyan dein — SEO setup ka matlab ye nahi ki ranking pakki ho jaayegi. Ranking Google tay karta hai, aur usme mahine lagte hain. Main sirf technical taraf theek kar sakta hoon, jo zaroori pehla kadam hai.

Ye kaam main nahi karta

Ye saaf bata dena zaroori hai, taaki aapka samay bekaar na jaaye:

  • ITR filing ya tax salah — main CA nahi hoon. Guides me jaankari hai, par aapka apna case CA hi dekh sakta hai. Haan, F&O turnover jaisa calculator bana sakta hoon.
  • Kisi ka sarkari form khud bharna — AgriStack, PM-Kisan ya koi bhi form. Kisi doosre ka Aadhaar, OTP ya nijee jaankari main nahi sambhalta. Guide bana sakta hoon, form khud nahi bhar sakta.
  • Nivesh ya trading salah — main SEBI-registered advisor nahi hoon. Kaunsa stock, fund ya policy lein — iska jawab nahi de sakta.
  • Trading bot ya auto-execution system — ye SEBI ke daayre me aata hai, isliye nahi banata. Backtesting ya calculator theek hai.
  • Payment gateway aur e-commerce — paise ka lenden shaamil hone se risk badh jaata hai, isliye abhi nahi leta.
  • Login/user account wala system, ya mobile app — ye mere kaam ke daayre se bahar hai.

Kaam kaise chalta hai

  1. Aap zaroorat batayein — neeche form bharein ya email karein
  2. Baat-cheet — main sawaal poochunga taaki kaam theek se samajh aaye
  3. Scope aur kharch — kya-kya banega, kitne din lagenge, kitna kharch — sab likhit me
  4. Aap haan kahein — kuch advance, baaki kaam poora hone par
  5. Kaam banta hai — beech me aapko dikhaya jaata hai
  6. Delivery — poora code aapka, aur 2 round ke badlaav shaamil
Kharch ke baare me: yahan koi fixed rate list nahi hai, kyunki har kaam alag hota hai. Ek chhota calculator aur ek poori website me bahut farak hai. Aap zaroorat batayein — main dekh kar saaf-saaf batata hoon ki kitna lagega. Kaam shuru karne se pehle likhit me kharch tay ho jaata hai, baad me kuch nahi badhta.

Do baatein pehle se

Jhoothi ummeed nahi dunga. Agar mujhe lage ki koi kaam mere bas ka nahi hai, to main saaf mana kar dunga. Aadha-adhoora kaam karke aapka paisa aur samay barbaad karne se behtar hai ki pehle hi bata dun.

Koi garanti nahi de sakta ki website se bikri badhegi ya Google par pehle number par aa jaayegi. Main technical kaam theek se kar sakta hoon; nateeja bazaar, aapke kaam aur samay par depend karta hai. Jo aisi garanti de, usse savdhaan rahein.

Kaam ka namoona

Sabse achha namoona yahi website hai. 154 calculators khol kar chalayein, SIP calculator ya bigha converter dekh lein, ya koi bhi guide padh lein. Jo yahan dikh raha hai, waisa hi kaam main aapke liye kar sakta hoon.

Neeche form bharein. Hum 2-3 kaam ke din me aapko email par jawab denge — kya ban sakta hai, kitna samay lagega aur kitna kharch aayega.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya aap CA ya financial advisor hain?

Nahi. Main na CA hoon na SEBI-registered advisor. Main sirf technical kaam karta hoon — calculator, tool aur website banana. Tax ya nivesh ki salah nahi deta.

Kharch kitna aayega?

Ye kaam par depend karta hai — ek chhota calculator aur poori website me bahut farak hai. Aap zaroorat batayein, main dekh kar likhit me bata dunga. Kaam shuru hone se pehle rakam tay ho jaati hai.

Kitna samay lagta hai?

Ek chhota calculator aam taur par 1-2 din, aur ek chhoti website 3-5 din. Kaam dekhne ke baad hi pakka samay bataya jaata hai.

Kya code mera hoga?

Haan. Kaam poora hone par saara code aapka hota hai. Aap use jahan chahein host kar sakte hain.

Badlaav kitne baar ho sakte hain?

2 round ke badlaav kharche me shaamil hote hain. Uske baad ke bade badlaav alag se tay hote hain.

Kya aap ITR bhar dete hain ya AgriStack form bhar dete hain?

Nahi. Main kisi ka sarkari form ya ITR khud nahi bharta, aur kisi ka Aadhaar/OTP nahi sambhalta. Iske liye CA ya CSC center se sampark karein.

Hosting ka kharcha kitna hoga?

Static website Cloudflare Pages jaisi jagah par lagbhag free host ho jaati hai. Sirf domain ka saalana kharcha aata hai.
