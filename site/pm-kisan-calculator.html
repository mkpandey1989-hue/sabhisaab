PM Kisan Calculator — Kitni Kist Mili, Kitna Paisa Aaya

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: October 2026 · Padhne me ~7 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi me har kisan parivaar ko saal me ₹6,000 milte hain — ₹2,000 ki teen kist me. Par kai kisan bhaiyon ko ye pata nahi hota ki unhe ab tak kitni kist mili, kaun si chhooti, aur kyun.

Taaza sthiti: aakhri jaari kist 23vi hai, jo 20 June 2026 ko aayi. 24vi kist August–November 2026 ki avadhi ki hai. Uski tareekh sarkar ne abhi ghoshit nahi ki hai — pakki tareekh sirf pmkisan.gov.in par aayegi.

Neeche likhiye ki aapko pehli kist kaun si mili thi aur beech me kitni kist nahi aayi. Ab tak ka poora hisaab aur agli kist ki avadhi turant dikh jaayegi.

Kist ki teen avadhi aur unka number

Saal ko teen hisson me baanta gaya hai. Har hisse ke liye ek kist hoti hai. Kist kis mahine aati hai, ye alag baat hai — kist kis avadhi ki hai, ye tay hai.

AvadhiKis kist kiMisaal
April se JulySaal ki pehli kist23vi kist = Apr–Jul 2026
August se NovemberSaal ki doosri kist24vi kist = Aug–Nov 2026
December se MarchSaal ki teesri kist25vi kist = Dec 2026–Mar 2027
Kist number se avadhi nikalne ka aasaan tarika: pehli kist Dec 2018–Mar 2019 ki thi. Uske baad har kist agli chaar mahine ki avadhi ki hai. Isi niyam se calculator aapki pehli kist ki avadhi batata hai — aur wahi aapka asli registration samay batata hai.

Ye isliye kaam ki baat hai kyunki chhooti kist ki shikayat karte waqt avadhi poochhi jaati hai, sirf number nahi.

Pattern dekhiye — kist aksar avadhi ke beech ya aakhir me aati hai, shuruaat me nahi. Isliye avadhi shuru hote hi paisa na aaye to ghabraane ki zaroorat nahi.

Social media par har mahine "kal aayegi kist" wale message chalte hain. Bharosa sirf pmkisan.gov.in aur sarkar ki ghoshna par kijiye.

Kist kyun rukti hai — teen sabse aam wajah

Zyadatar maamlon me kist kisi ek kagaz ki kami se rukti hai, yojana se naam hatne se nahi.

  • eKYC nahi hui — ye zaroori hai. Mobile par OTP se, Common Service Centre (CSC) par fingerprint se, ya PM Kisan app me chehre se ho jaati hai.
  • Bank khata Aadhaar se juda nahi — paisa DBT se aata hai. Khata Aadhaar se linked aur DBT ke liye chalu hona chahiye. Ye bank jaakar ho jaata hai.
  • Zameen ka record (land seeding) nahi juda — rajya ka krishi ya rajasva vibhag aapke bhulekh ko PM Kisan se jodta hai. Ye patwari ya krishi karyalay se hota hai.
Kami door hone ke baad ruki hui kist kai baar baad me ek saath aa jaati hai — par ye har baar pakka nahi hai. Isliye kami pata chalte hi theek karwaiye. Jitni der, utni kist ka khatra.

Uttarakhand me Farmer ID (UKFR) ka kaam bhi chal raha hai. Uska tarika Uttarakhand Farmer ID Guide me likha hai.

Kise milta hai, kise nahi

Yojana zameen-maalik kisan parivaar ke liye hai. Parivaar matlab pati, patni aur nabalig bachche. Ek parivaar ko ek hi laabh milta hai.

In logon ko nahi milta, chahe zameen ho:

  • Jo pichhle saal income tax bhar chuke hon
  • Sarkari naukri wale ya ₹10,000 se zyada pension paane wale (Group D aur multi-tasking staff ko chhodkar)
  • Maujooda ya purane mantri, saansad, vidhayak aur ooncha sanvaidhanik pad rakhne wale
  • Registered doctor, engineer, vakeel, CA aur architect jo apna kaam karte hon
  • Sansthagat zameen-maalik, jaise trust ya company

Galat tareeke se liya paisa sarkar wapas maangti hai. Kai rajyon me aise kisanon ko notice aaye hain. Agar aap in shartoon me aate hain to khud naam hatwa lena hi samajhdaari hai.

Status kaise jaanchein aur shikayat kahan karein

  1. pmkisan.gov.in kholiye aur "Know Your Status" chuniye.
  2. Registration number daaliye. Yaad na ho to wahi "Know your registration number" se mobile ya Aadhaar se nikal jaata hai.
  3. Status me eKYC, land seeding aur Aadhaar-bank linking teeno ke saamne "Yes" dikhna chahiye.

Teeno "Yes" hon aur phir bhi kist na aaye to PM Kisan helpline 155261 ya 1800-115-526 par baat kijiye. Portal par shikayat (grievance) bhi darj ki ja sakti hai.

Kisan Credit Card ka laabh bhi isi registration se aasaan hota hai — tarika Kisan Credit Card guide me dekhiye. Kheti ka munafa nikaalna ho to Fasal Munafa Calculator kaam aayega.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

PM Kisan ki 24vi kist kab aayegi?

24vi kist August se November 2026 ki avadhi ki hai. Iski tareekh sarkar ne abhi ghoshit nahi ki hai. Pakki jaankari pmkisan.gov.in aur sarkari ghoshna se hi lein.

PM Kisan me saal me kitna paisa milta hai?

Har yogya kisan parivaar ko saal me ₹6,000 milte hain, ₹2,000 ki teen kist me. Paisa seedha Aadhaar se jude bank khate me DBT se aata hai.

PM Kisan ki kist kyun ruk jaati hai?

Sabse aam wajah eKYC na hona, bank khata Aadhaar se na juda hona, ya zameen ka record (land seeding) na juda hona hai. Status page par teeno dekh sakte hain.

eKYC kaise karein?

Teen tarike hain — pmkisan.gov.in par Aadhaar OTP se, CSC centre par fingerprint se, ya PM Kisan mobile app me chehre (face authentication) se.

Ruki hui kist baad me milti hai?

Kami door hone ke baad kai maamlon me ruki kist baad me aa jaati hai, par ye har baar pakka nahi hai. Isliye kami jaldi theek karwana zaroori hai.
