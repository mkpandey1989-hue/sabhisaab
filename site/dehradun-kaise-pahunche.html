Jahan point Dehradun ke liye lagu nahi hota, wahan saaf likha gaya hai.

Dehradun: Hawai aur Helicopter Seva (UDAN aur Charter)

Dehradun ka hawai adda Jolly Grant Airport (IATA: DED) hai, jo shehar se lagbhag 25-27 km, Rishikesh se lagbhag 20 km aur Haridwar se lagbhag 35 km door hai. 2025-26 me yahan se lagbhag 18.7 lakh yatriyon ne safar kiya (AAI aankde, Wikipedia ke anusar). Heli-services ke liye Sahastradhara Road ka heliport aur Char Dham ke ilake ke helipad (Phata, Sersi, Guptkashi) use hote hain.

Jolly Grant Airport: Kahan Hai, Udaanein aur Kaise Pahunchein

Jolly Grant Airport Doiwala ke paas, Jauligrant gaon me hai (PIN 248140). Isse 1974 me banaya gaya tha. Maujooda passenger terminal October 2021 me inaugurate hua (AAI/Wikipedia ke anusar), aur terminal ka 42,776 sq meter ka design peak ghanton me lagbhag 1,800 yatriyon ko sambhalne layak bataya gaya hai. Runway lagbhag 2,140 meter (7,000 ft) lambi, asphalt ki hai.

Airlines aur routes: Pichle kuch saalon me Air India, Air India Express, IndiGo aur Alliance Air ke naam alag-alag samay par aaye hain, aur Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Ahmedabad, Lucknow, Amritsar jaise sheher jude hain. Kis airline ki kaunsi flight kab chalti hai, ye roz badalta hai. Taaza route ke liye airline ki site ya AAI ki site dekhein.

International: Airport ko kuch jagah “international” kaha jata hai, par scheduled antarrashtriya yatri-seva seemit hai. Zyadatar videshi yatri Delhi ke raste aate hain.

Airport se shehar kaise jayein: Taxi, app-cab, bus aur private gaadi. Jolly Grant se Rishikesh (lagbhag 20 km) aur Haridwar (lagbhag 35 km) bhi aasaan hai, isliye yatri aksar Rishikesh seedha jate hain.

Char Dham season: May se November ke beech airport par bheed badh jati hai. Is samay flight pehle se book karein.

Dehradun me Sahastradhara Road ke paas ek heliport ka zikr kai sources me hai, jahan se Char Dham ke liye heli-services chalti hain (Phata, Sersi, Guptkashi jaise helipad ke saath). Heli-yatra ki booking aam taur par sarkari portal (IRCTC Heli Yatra ya Uttarakhand Civil Aviation Development Authority, UCADA) ke zariye hoti hai.

Dehradun: Railway Station aur Aane Wali Rail Pariyojanayein

Dehradun ka mukhya railway station Dehradun Junction (DDN) hai. Delhi se Vande Bharat Express lagbhag 4 ghante 45 minute me, Shatabdi lagbhag 6 ghante me aur Jan Shatabdi lagbhag 5 ghante 45 minute me Dehradun pahunchati hai. Rishikesh me Yog Nagari Rishikesh station 2021 me khula, aur Rishikesh-Karnaprayag rail line abhi nirman me hai (pehli train ke liye Dec 2028-2029 ka target).

Dehradun aur Rishikesh Station, Trainein aur Rishikesh-Karnaprayag Rail

Dehradun ka station Delhi-Dehradun main line par hai. Delhi (Anand Vihar) se Vande Bharat Express sabse tez train hai (lagbhag 4 ghante 45 minute), uske baad Shatabdi aur Jan Shatabdi. Ek taaza khabar ke anusar 18 July 2026 ko Ramnagar-Dehradun Express ko hari jhandi dikhayi gayi.

Dehradun: Sadak, Highway, Pul aur Seema Sadak (BRO)

14 April 2026 ko Delhi-Dehradun Expressway ka udghatan hua. Yeh lagbhag 210-213 km lamba, lagbhag Rs. 12,000 crore ka corridor hai, jisse Delhi-Dehradun ka safar 6 ghante se ghatkar lagbhag 2.5 ghante ka ho gaya hai. Isme Rajaji National Park ke andar Asia ka sabse lamba elevated wildlife corridor bhi hai.

Delhi-Dehradun Expressway, NH aur Pahadi Sadkein

Delhi-Dehradun Expressway Akshardham (Delhi) se shuru hokar Dehradun tak jaata hai, Uttar Pradesh me Baghpat aur Saharanpur hokar. Speed limit 100 km/h batayi gayi hai, aur 7 interchange, 2 rail overbridge aur 10 bade pul hain (reports ke anusar).

Bottlenecks (aksar jam): Dehradun-Rishikesh raasta (Char Dham season me), Mussoorie ke entry point (peak season me), Chakrata-Kalsi raasta (monsoon me landslide), Jolly Grant-Rishikesh raasta. Taaza road-status ke liye Google Maps aur Uttarakhand Police ki road-advisory dekhein.

Dehradun ki koi antarrashtriya seema nahi hai (Antarrashtriya Seema aur Antarrajyiya Seema), isliye Dehradun zile me BRO ke strategic border roads ka koi bada project nahi hai. BRO ki pahunch mukhya roop se Uttarkashi, Chamoli, Pithoragarh jaise seema wale zilon me hai.

Dehradun: Bus Seva, Shared Taxi aur Sthaniya Yatayat

Dehradun ka mukhya bus adda Maharana Pratap ISBT (Inter State Bus Terminal) hai. Yahan se Delhi, Haridwar, Rishikesh, Mussoorie, Chakrata aur anya sheharon ke liye bus milti hain. Uttarakhand Parivahan Nigam (UTC) ki sarkari bus ke saath private bus aur shared taxi bhi chalte hain.

ISBT, Roadways Bus, Shared Taxi aur Shahar ka Aavagaman

Dehradun me bus, shared taxi (Maxx/Bolero/jeep), auto-rickshaw aur app-cab sab milte hain. Mussoorie, Chakrata aur Rishikesh ke liye bus-taxi ki sewa din bhar chalti hai, par peak season me jaldi bhar jati hai.

KMOU/GMOU/TGMO buses: KMOU (Kumaon Motor Owners Union), GMOU (Garhwal Motor Owners Union) aur TGMO (Tehri Garhwal Motor Owners) Uttarakhand ke alag-alag ilakon ki sahkari sansthayein hain. Safety: Pahadi raaston par raat me safar na karein, aur monsoon me khaaskar Chakrata, Kalsi ka raasta band ho sakta hai. Driver aur gaadi ka registration dekhein.

Dehradun: Petrol Pump, EV Charging aur Mechanic

Mussoorie registration (poori jaankari):

  • Kab shuru: 1 August 2025 (Outlook Traveller, PTI ke anusar).
  • Kahan: Dehradun Smart City portal (dsclservices.org.in).
  • Kya daalna hai: Indian mobile number (ya videshi ke liye email), aane-jane ki tithi, yatriyon ki sankhya, vehicle number (agar apni gaadi hai), OTP verification.
  • Kyun: Mussoorie me 2022-2024 ke beech paryatak lagbhag do-guna ho gaye, jisse jam aur bheed badhi. NGT ne May 2025 me tourist data jama karne ka nirdesh diya tha.
  • QR code aur checkpoint: Registration ke baad QR code milta hai, jise Kimadi, Kempty Fall aur Kuthal Gate jaise entry points par dikhana pad sakta hai. Kai reports ke anusar yeh system mukhya roop se peak season me lagu hota hai, saal bhar nahi.
  • Hotel ki bhumika: Hotel, guest house aur homestay ko bhi portal par register hona hota hai aur guest ka data upload karna hota hai.
  • Dhyan rakhein: Niyam ka swaroop (mandatory vs. peak-season) sthiti ke hisaab se badal sakta hai. Safar se pehle taaza jaankari dekhein.

Suraksha, Emergency aur Oonchai ki Bimari (AMS)

Dehradun zile ki sabse unchi jagahein lagbhag 2,700-3,000 m hain (Mundali, Moila Top, Kharamba). Yahan AMS (Acute Mountain Sickness) ka khatra bahut kam hai, lekin bahut tezi se unchai par jaane par halka sirdard ho sakta hai. Dehradun se shuru hone wale bade treks (Kedarkantha, Har Ki Dun) me AMS asli chinta hai.

Samanya safety (medical salah nahi, sirf jaankari):

  • Emergency: 112 (police/fire/ambulance), 108 (ambulance), 1077 (zila aapda control room).
  • AMS ke lakshan: sirdard, chakkar, ulti, thakan. Lakshan badhne par aur unchai par na jayein, aur neeche utarein. Gambhir hone par doctor ya hospital jayein.
  • Paani aur dheere chadhna: Zyada paani piyein aur dheere chadhein. Trek se pehle apne doctor se salah lein, khaaskar agar dil, fefde ya blood-pressure ki samasya ho.
  • Monsoon: Nadi aur naale ke paas na jayein (Dehradun: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh).
  • Jangli janwar: Sham ke baad jungle me akele na jayein; Rajaji aur gaon ke raaston par hathi aur tendua ka khatra (Dehradun: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh).
  • Adventure sports: Sirf licensed operator, life jacket/helmet zaroori.
  • Cantonment aur photography: Sainik kshetra ke board aur adhikari ka palan.
