Number kaise banta hai

Celsius aur Fahrenheit ka sirf paimana hi nahi, shuruaati bindu bhi alag hai. Paani 0°C par jamta hai par 32°F par. Isi liye pehle 32 ghatana ya jodna padta hai, phir 9/5 ka guna. Kelvin me koi minus nahi hota — wo sabse thandi mumkin haalat se shuru hota hai.

Formula: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
K = °C + 273.15

Teen asli sthitiyan

Misaal 1 — thandi jagah. 35°F = 1.67°C. Yaani lagbhag jamne wali thand.

Misaal 2 — bukhar. 100°F = 37.8°C. Saamanya shareer ka taapmaan 98.6°F yaani 37°C hota hai.

Misaal 3 — oven. 180°C = 356°F. Videshi recipe me F likha ho to yahi badalna padta hai.

Ye table rozana kaam aati hai. Bukhaar naapte waqt sabse zyada uljhan hoti hai — purane thermometer Fahrenheit me hote hain aur naye digital Celsius me. 100°F ka matlab lagbhag 37.8°C hota hai, yaani halka bukhaar.

Khana banate waqt oven ki setting ka dhyan rakhein. Videshi recipe me 350°F likha hota hai, jo 177°C banta hai. Seedha 350 rakh dene se saman jal jaata hai. Isi tarah 180°C wali recipe Fahrenheit me 356°F hoti hai — yaani lagbhag wahi.

Ek cheez aur samajh lena zaroori hai — Celsius aur Fahrenheit ka farak aur value alag cheez hai. Agar mausam 5°C se badh kar 10°C hua, to Fahrenheit me ye 41°F se 50°F hua — yaani 5°C ka farak 9°F ke barabar hai, 41°F ke barabar nahi. Farak nikalte waqt 32 nahi jodte, sirf 9/5 se guna karte hain. Naye log yahi galti sabse zyada karte hain.

Ghar me bukhaar naapte waqt do baat dhyan rakhein. Pehla, digital thermometer me aksar setting badal kar C ya F chuna ja sakta hai — ek baar dekh lein ki kaunsa mode chalu hai. Dusra, bagal (armpit) se naapne par reading muh ke muqable thodi kam aati hai, isliye doctor ko batate waqt ye bhi bata dein ki kahan se naapa tha. 100°F se oopar jaane par doctor se salah lena hi sahi rehta hai.

Fridge aur AC me bhi ye hisaab kaam aata hai. Fridge ka thanda hissa aam taur par 3-5°C par rakha jaata hai, jo 37-41°F banta hai. AC 24-26°C par sabse kam bijli kharch karta hai. Videshi manual me agar Fahrenheit likha ho to yahi formula lagayein.

Yahan aksar galti hoti hai

  1. 32 jodna bhool jaana. Sirf 9/5 ka guna karne se jawab poori tarah galat aata hai.
  2. Ulta formula lagana. C se F me pehle guna phir jod; F se C me pehle ghatao phir guna. Kram badla to nateeja badal jaata hai.
  3. Kelvin me degree lagana. Kelvin ke saath "degree" nahi bolte — sirf 300 K.
  4. Farak ko value samajhna. 5°C ka farak 9°F ke barabar hota hai, 41°F ke nahi.
  5. Body temperature yaad na rakhna. 98.6°F = 37°C — ye yaad rakhna rozmarra me kaam aata hai.

Bharatiya sandarbh me

  • Mausam: Bharat me hamesha Celsius chalta hai.
  • Bukhar: Ghar ke purane thermometer aksar Fahrenheit me hote hain, digital me dono option milte hain.
  • Khaana: Videshi recipe F me hoti hain, Indian oven C me.
  • Fridge: Fridge 2-5°C aur freezer −18°C ke aaspaas rakha jaata hai.
  • Padhai: School ke ganit me yahi formula poochha jaata hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

98.6 F kitna Celsius hota hai?

37°C — ye saamanya shareer ka taapmaan hai.

C se F ka formula kya hai?

(°C × 9/5) + 32.

0 Celsius kitna Fahrenheit hai?

32°F.

Kelvin kya hota hai?

Vigyaan me istemaal hone wala paimana. K = °C + 273.15.

Kaunsa taapmaan dono me barabar hota hai?

−40 par — −40°C = −40°F.

Fahrenheit kya hota hai?

Taapman naapne ki ek ikai, jo mukhya roop se America me chalti hai. Usme paani 32°F par jamta hai aur 212°F par ubalta hai. Bharat me Celsius chalta hai.

Celsius kitna hai — Fahrenheit se kaise badlein?

C = (F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9. Jaldi ka anuman: F me se 30 ghataiye aur aadha kar dijiye. 98°F ka matlab lagbhag 37°C — shareer ka normal taapman.
