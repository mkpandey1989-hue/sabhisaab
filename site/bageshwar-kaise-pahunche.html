Bageshwar: Hawai aur Helicopter Seva (UDAN aur Charter)

Bageshwar ka sabse nazdeeki airport Pantnagar (Udham Singh Nagar) hai, jo zila portal ke anusar lagbhag 210 km aur ek aur sarkari-jaisi suchi ke anusar 217 km door hai. Sadak se lagbhag 4.5 ghante lagte hain. Bageshwar me khud ka koi airport ya verified helipad nahi mila.

  • Emergency evacuation: Himalayan trek par emergency me helicopter evacuation ki vyavastha beema aur rescue agency par nirbhar karti hai. Trek se pehle apna beema aur rescue coverage check karein.

Bageshwar: Railway Station aur Aane Wali Rail Pariyojanayein

Bageshwar me abhi koi rail line ya station nahi hai. Sabse nazdeeki railhead Kathgodam (Haldwani ke paas) hai, jo Bageshwar se sadak se lagbhag 150 se 180 km door hai (route aur source ke hisaab se). Tanakpur-Bageshwar nayi rail line ka survey aur DPR ban chuka hai, par abhi sanction aur nirman shuru nahi hua.

Delhi, Lucknow, Howrah se trains (Kathgodam tak)

Sadak ki asli sthiti (yatri ke liye)

  • Monsoon (July se September): Pahadi raaste par bhooskhalan se sadak band hone ka khatra badhta hai (Bageshwar: Tehsil, block aur prashasan me bhi). Yatra se pehle taaza sthiti dekhein.
  • Pul aur pagdandi: Pindari raaste par 2013 ki badh se bahut se raaste aur pul toote the, aur baad me asthayi pul ki baat aayi; is par aur jaankari ke liye Bageshwar: Trekking ki Poori Suchi dekhein.
  • Raat ki yatra: Pahadi raaste par raat me gaadi chalane se bachein, khaas kar barsat me.
  • Sadak ka road-head: Loharkhet se Kharkiya ke beech sadak hone ka zikr taaza guides me hai, par ye badal sakta hai.

Sthaniya driver sabse achchi jaankari dete hain.

Kausani, Baijnath aur Bageshwar nagar ke private hotel/resort

Kausani aur Baijnath ke paas private resort aur hotel milte hain. Kai travel sites me The Heritage Resort (Baijnath Road, Garur ke paas) jaise naam dikhte hain, par yahan kisi vyavsayik hotel ko recommend nahi kiya ja raha. Hotel ka rate, suvidha aur review har roz badalte hain.

Hotel ke "starting price" alag-alag travel blogs me alag aur purane milte hain. Season (Uttarayani Mela, Shivratri, May-June) me daam kaafi badalte hain. Booking se pehle hotel se seedhe rate aur cancellation niyam puchein.

Luxury aur budget ka antar: Kausani me kai resort himalayan view ke saath infinity pool jaisi suvidhayein dete hain (kuch listing me aisa zikr hai). Bageshwar nagar me budget lodge aur chhote hotel zyada hain.

Rukne ka sahi samay: Uttarayani Mela (lagbhag 14 January) aur Maha Shivratri ke dino me Bageshwar me hotel jaldi bhar jate hain. 3 se 4 hafte pehle booking karein.

Bageshwar: Mobile Network aur Internet: Asli Sthiti

Bageshwar nagar, Garur aur Kausani jaise bade qasbon me mobile network aam taur par milta hai, par Pindari-Kafni-Sunderdhunga ke ooche raaston (Khati ke aage) par network bahut kam ya bilkul nahi milta. Kai trek reports ke anusar Khati me sirf BSNL ka signal din me kuch ghante milta tha, par ye jaankari purani ho sakti hai.

Sardi me paani ki dikkat: Ooche pahadi gaon me bahut thand me pipe ka paani jam sakta hai. Bageshwar nagar (lagbhag 1,000 m) me ye kam hota hai, par ooche gaon me hota hai. Bageshwar: Inner Line Permit (ILP), Van Anumati aur Suraksha Niyam

Bageshwar zila Inner Line Permit wale seema kshetra me nahi aata. Pindari glacier trek ke liye ILP ya RAP nahi chahiye, par Nanda Devi Biosphere Reserve ke liye Forest Department ka entry permit chahiye. Ye permit aam taur par Loharkhet ya Song ke forest check post par ya Bageshwar ke forest office me milta hai.

Inner Line Permit (SDM/Online), Seema par Drone Rok aur AMS se Bachav

Sahi sandarbh: Niti aur Mana (Chamoli), Nelang (Uttarkashi), Darma, Byans aur Chaudans (Pithoragarh) ke ILP ki baat hai. Ye ilake Bageshwar zile me nahi hain, isliye Bageshwar ke liye in permit ki jarurat nahi hai. Pithoragarh ke Milam/Ralam jaise treks ke liye alag niyam hote hain (kai sources me likha hai).

Drone ke niyam

Drone ka istemal Bharat me niyam-niyantran ke adheen hai. Sanrakshit kshetra (jaise Biosphere Reserve), mandir parisar aur sensitive jagahon par drone ke liye pehle anumati ya pratibandh ho sakta hai. Bageshwar zila antarrashtriya seema par nahi hai, par Pindari ka kshetra Nanda Devi Biosphere Reserve me aata hai, isliye drone uda ne se pehle Forest Department se puchein.

AMS (Acute Mountain Sickness / unchai ki bimari) se bachav

Pindari ka Zero Point lagbhag 3,660 se 3,816 m aur Sunderdhunga lagbhag 4,320 m ki unchai tak jata hai. 3,000 m ke upar AMS ka khatra badhta hai.

Bachav ke upay: 1. Dheere chadhein; ek din me bahut zyada unchai na badhayein. 2. Paani zyada peeyein; sharab aur dhoomrapan se bachein. 3. Zarurat par rest-day rakhein (Dwali ya Phurkia me). 4. AMS ki dawa (jaise Diamox) ka upyog sirf doctor ki salah se karein. 5. Lakshan ho to guide ko turant batayein; "thoda aur chal lete hain" na sochein.

Emergency numbers (aam): Aapatkaleen sahayata ke liye 112 (rashtriya aapatkaleen number), aur Uttarakhand me 108 (ambulance) ka upyog hota hai. Aapda prabandhan ke helpline (jaise 1070/1077) ke liye taaza number apne zila prashasan se confirm karein.

Trek ke niyam (suraksha): 1. Check post par registration karwayein. 2. Guide ke saath chalein, khaas kar Sunderdhunga me. 3. Mausam kharab ho to rukein; barsat me nadi paar na karein. 4. Travel aur rescue beema karwayein. 5. Jungli janwar se doori rakhein; khaana khule me na chhodein. 6. Plastic wapas laayein.

Pindari ke liye ILP chahiye?
Nahi; Forest entry permit chahiye.
Permit kahan milta hai?
Loharkhet/Song forest check post ya Bageshwar forest office.
AMS kab hota hai?
Aam taur par 3,000 m ke upar, tezi se chadhne par.
Bageshwar me drone uda sakte hain?
Sanrakshit kshetra aur mandir me anumati zaroori ho sakti hai.
