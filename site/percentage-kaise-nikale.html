Percentage Kaise Nikale — 7 Tarike, Aasaan Bhasha Me

Percent ka matlab kya hai

Percent ka seedha matlab hai — sau me se kitna. "Cent" shabd ka matlab hi sau hota hai. Jab hum kehte hain 77.4%, to matlab hai har sau me se 77.4 hissa.

Isi wajah se percentage nikalna asal me sirf do kaam hai — pehle hissa nikalna (bhaag dena), phir use sau ke paimane par le aana (100 se guna karna). Jitne bhi tarike hain, sab isi ek soch se nikalte hain.

Buniyaadi formula:
Percent = (hissa ÷ poora) × 100
Hissa = (percent × poora) ÷ 100

Ye do line yaad ho jaayein to niche ke saare sawaal apne aap hal ho jaate hain.

500 me 387 kitna percent hai

Ye Google par sabse zyada poochhe jaane wale sawaalon me se hai — aur ye ulta sawaal hai. Yahan aapke paas hissa (387) aur poora (500) dono hain, aur percent nikalna hai.

Hisaab: (387 ÷ 500) × 100 = 0.774 × 100 = 77.4%

Yaani 387, 500 ka 77.4 percent hai. Marksheet me yahi hisaab lagta hai — 500 me se 387 number aaye to percentage 77.4% hui.

Ab isi sawaal ka seedha roop dekhein: "500 ka 77.4% kitna hai?" Yahan hisaab ulta chalega — (77.4 × 500) ÷ 100 = 387. Dono jawab ek doosre se jude hain, bas sawaal ka roop badla hai.

7 tarah ke percentage sawaal

1. X ka Y% kitna hai. 2,400 ka 15% = (15 × 2400) ÷ 100 = 360. Discount aur GST me yahi lagta hai.

2. X, Y ka kitna percent hai. 387 out of 500 = 77.4%. Marks aur target me yahi.

3. Kitne ka Y% = X hota hai. Kis number ka 20% = 350? Jawab = (350 × 100) ÷ 20 = 1,750. Ye tab kaam aata hai jab discount ke baad wali keemat pata ho.

4. X se Y tak kitna % badha. 31,000 se 35,500 = ((35500 − 31000) ÷ 31000) × 100 = 14.52%. Salary hike me yahi.

5. X se Y tak kitna % ghata. 800 se 640 = ((800 − 640) ÷ 800) × 100 = 20% kami.

6. X me Y% jodna. 5,000 me 18% GST = 5,000 × 1.18 = 5,900.

7. X me se Y% hatana (reverse). Bill 5,900 hai jisme 18% GST shaamil hai — mool keemat = 5,900 ÷ 1.18 = 5,000. Ye sabse zyada galat kiya jaane wala hisaab hai; log 5,900 me se 18% ghata dete hain, jo galat jawab deta hai.

Dimag se jaldi nikalne ke tarike

Calculator hamesha haath me nahi hota. Kuch chhote tarike bahut kaam aate hain.

10% nikalna: dashamlav ek jagah baayein khiskao. 850 ka 10% = 85.

5% nikalna: 10% ka aadha. 850 ka 5% = 42.5.

20% nikalna: 10% ka dugna. 850 ka 20% = 170.

15% nikalna: 10% + 5%. 850 ka 15% = 85 + 42.5 = 127.5.

Ulta karke: 4% of 75 nikalna mushkil lagta hai, par 75% of 4 aasaan hai — dono ka jawab 3 hai. Percentage me aap dono numbers palat sakte hain.

1% nikalna: dashamlav do jagah baayein. 3,600 ka 1% = 36. Isse koi bhi percent bana lein — 7% = 36 × 7 = 252.

5 galtiyan jo sabse zyada hoti hain

1. Do percent jod dena. "50% + 20% off" ka matlab 70% nahi, 60% hota hai. Dusra discount pehle ke baad bachi keemat par lagta hai.

2. Ulta percent seedha ghata dena. 100 me 20% badha to 120 hua. Ab 120 me se 20% ghatao to 96 aata hai, 100 nahi. Badhna aur ghatna barabar nahi hote.

3. Percent aur percentage point mila dena. Byaj dar 5% se 6% hui — ye 1 percentage point ki badhotri hai, par 20% ki badhotri hai. Khabar padhte waqt ye farak bada hota hai.

4. GST ulta nikalte waqt galti. GST wale bill se mool keemat nikalne ke liye bhaag dena hota hai (1.18 se), ghatana nahi.

5. Base badal jaana. "30% zyada" kis cheez se zyada — ye saaf hona chahiye. Base badalne se poora jawab badal jaata hai.

Rozana zindagi ki misaalein

Marksheet. 500 me se 387 = 77.4%. Board ke result me yahi hisaab lagta hai. Agar sirf 4 vishay ginne hain to poora bhi usi hisaab se badalna padta hai.

Dukan ki chhoot. ₹57,500 par 20% chhoot = ₹11,500 bachat, dena hoga ₹46,000.

Salary hike. ₹31,000 se ₹35,500 = 14.52% hike. Par mehngai 6% hai to asli badhotri lagbhag 8.5% hi hui.

Bank ka byaj. ₹1 lakh par 7% saalana = ₹7,000 saal ka. Par compounding ho to thoda zyada.

Kheti. 10 nali me 1,200 kg upaj hui aur 8% mandi me kharab nikla — matlab 96 kg ka nuksan, bacha 1,104 kg.

Har jagah ganit wahi hai — bas naam badal jaata hai. Ek baar do buniyaadi formula samajh me aa gaye to koi bhi percentage ka sawaal atka nahi sakta.

Sawaal-jawab

500 me 387 kitna percent hota hai?

77.4 percent. Hisaab: (387 ÷ 500) × 100 = 77.4%.

500 ka 77.4% kitna hota hai?

387. Hisaab: (77.4 × 500) ÷ 100 = 387.

Percentage nikalne ka formula kya hai?

Percent = (hissa ÷ poora) × 100. Aur hissa = (percent × poora) ÷ 100.

Marks ka percentage kaise nikalein?

Mile hue marks ko poore marks se bhaag dein aur 100 se guna karein. 387 out of 500 = 77.4%.

50% + 20% discount ka matlab kitna hai?

60%, 70% nahi. Dusra discount pehle discount ke baad bachi keemat par lagta hai.

GST wale bill se mool keemat kaise nikalein?

Bill ko 1.18 se bhaag dein (18% GST ke liye). Ghatana galat jawab deta hai.

Percent aur percentage point me kya farak hai?

5% se 6% hona 1 percentage point ki badhotri hai, par 20% ki badhotri hai.
