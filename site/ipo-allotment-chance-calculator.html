IPO Allotment Chance Calculator — Lottery Ka Asli Ganit

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Retail hisse me IPO jab zyada bhar jaata hai, to allotment kaabiliyat se nahi lottery se hota hai. Har poore lot wale application ko ek parchi milti hai, aur computer usme se chunta hai.

Neeche retail subscription kitne guna hua, aur aapne kitne alag PAN se apply kiya — ye daaliye. Ek application par mauka aur kam se kam ek allotment milne ka mauka, dono dikh jayenge.

Retail me lottery kaise chalti hai

Niyam saaf hai. Agar retail hissa poora bhar jaaye, to koshish ki jaati hai ki har applicant ko kam se kam ek lot mile. Jab itne lot bhi nahi bachte, tab computer se parchi nikalti hai.

Retail subscriptionKya hota hai
1 guna se kamSabko poora milta hai
1 se 2 gunaSabko kam se kam ek lot, baaki anupaat me
2 guna se zyadaLottery — ek lot ya kuch nahi
20 gunaLagbhag 20 me se 1 application ko
Sabse bada bhram: log sochte hain ki 5 lot ka apply karne se chance 5 guna ho jaata hai. Nahi hota. Lottery me 5 lot wale application ki bhi ek hi parchi banti hai, aur jeetne par bhi milta ek hi lot hai. Baaki paisa block rehkar wapas aa jaata hai.

Chance badhane ka ek hi imaandaar tarika

Ek hi PAN se do application nahi chalte — dono radd ho jaate hain. Par parivaar ke alag-alag sadasya apne-apne PAN aur demat se alag application laga sakte hain. Har application ki apni parchi banti hai.

Iska ganit seedha hai. 8 guna subscription par ek application ka mauka lagbhag 12.5% hai. Teen alag application par kam se kam ek milne ka mauka lagbhag 33% ho jaata hai. Chaar par lagbhag 41%.

Par yaad rahe — har application ka paisa alag se block hota hai. Teen application ka matlab teen guna paisa kuch dinon ke liye rukega.

Chhote aur bade HNI hisse ka niyam alag hai

Retail seema tak hi lottery chalti hai. Usse upar ka hissa alag tarah se banta hai, aur wahan zyada paisa lagane se sach me zyada milta hai.

HissaRakamAllotment ka tarika
Retail2 lakh takLottery, ek lot
Chhota HNI2 se 10 lakhAnupaat me, ek tihaai hissa
Bada HNI10 lakh se uparAnupaat me, do tihaai hissa

Anupaat ka matlab hai ki 10 guna subscription par aapke apply kiye hue ka lagbhag dasvaan hissa mil jaata hai — kuch na kuch pakka milta hai. Iska doosra pehlu ye hai ki HNI hisse me kai log udhaar lekar apply karte hain, aur us udhaar ka byaj listing ke faayde se bada bhi ho sakta hai.

Aam nivesh karne wale ke liye retail hissa hi theek hai. 2 lakh ki seema paar karke HNI me jaana ek alag khel hai, jisme paisa aur jokhim dono kai guna hote hain.

Application bhartey waqt jo galtiyan sabse mehngi padti hain

Har IPO me kuch application sirf takneeki wajah se radd ho jaate hain. Ye poori tarah bachi ja sakti hain.

  • Cut-off price nahi chuna — kam daam par bid lagane se agar price upar tay hua to application bahar ho jaata hai.
  • UPI mandate samay par accept nahi kiya — mandate ki apni aakhri tarikh hoti hai, aur wo IPO band hone ke saath khatam ho jaati hai.
  • Khaate me paisa kam — block tabhi hota hai jab poora paisa maujood ho.
  • Ek hi PAN se do application — dono radd.
  • Adhoore lot — lot ke guna me hi apply hota hai, beech ki sankhya nahi chalti.

Ek chhoti si aadat kaam ki hai: mandate accept karne ke baad bank app me "blocked amount" ek baar khud dekh lena. Jab tak rakam block nahi dikhti, application poora nahi hua.

Ek imaandaar baat — IPO kamai ka pakka rasta nahi hai

Listing par faayda milta bhi hai aur nahi bhi. Kai IPO issue price se neeche khulte hain, aur kuch mahinon me aur neeche chale jaate hain. Ye kisi bhi tarah ka pakka munafa nahi hai — is baat par koi daava nahi kiya ja sakta.

Do baatein dhyaan me rakhne layak hain. Pehli, allotment milne par company ka kaam-kaaj dekhkar tay kijiye ki rakhna hai ya bechna — sirf listing ka bhaav dekhkar nahi. Doosri, jo paisa 5-7 din ke liye block hota hai, wo utne din kahin aur kaam nahi aata.

Ek chhota hisaab jo log nahi karte: 15,000 ka lot, 3 application, yaani 45,000 lagbhag 6 din block. Saal me 20 IPO me aisa karne par kaafi paisa saal bhar ghoomta rehta hai. Isse pehle dekh lijiye ki wahi paisa liquid fund me rakhne par kya milta.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Zyada lot apply karne se allotment ka chance badhta hai?

Retail hisse me nahi. Lottery me har application ki ek hi parchi banti hai, chahe aap 1 lot lagayein ya 5. Jeetne par bhi ek hi lot milta hai.

Ek hi PAN se do application laga sakte hain?

Nahi. Dono radd ho jaate hain. Parivaar ke doosre sadasya apne PAN aur demat khaate se alag application laga sakte hain.

IPO ka paisa kab tak block rehta hai?

Aam taur par 5 se 7 din. Allotment na milne par block apne aap hat jaata hai aur paisa usi khaate me chalu ho jaata hai.

Cut-off price chunna zaroori hai kya?

Retail me yahi sabse surakshit hai. Isse aap us price par apply karte hain jo aakhir me tay hoga, aur sirf kam bid ki wajah se bahar hone ka khatra nahi rehta.

Allotment ka pata kahan chalta hai?

Registrar ki website par PAN ya application number se, aur exchange ki site par bhi. Bank me block hata dikhna aksar sabse pehla ishaara hota hai.
