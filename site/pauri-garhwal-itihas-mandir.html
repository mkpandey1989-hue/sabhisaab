Is hisse ka maksad: Pauri Garhwal sirf ek zila nahi, ek “jeeti-jaagti kahani” hai. Yahan Mahabharat ke Kanvashram se lekar Panwar rajaon ke 52 garh tak, Gorkha-kaal ki peeda se lekar Peshawar Kaand ke Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali tak, aur Tilu Rauteli ki veer-gatha se lekar 1994 ke Uttarakhand Andolan tak sab kuch judta hai. Is hisse mein hum mandir, Devta, itihas aur lok-katha ko ek saath samjhenge.

Jahan koi baat sirf lok-manyata hai, use “manyata” kaha jayega, itihas nahi.

Pauri Garhwal: Puranon ka Bhugol: Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand

Seedha jawab: Skanda Purana ke Kedarkhand me Garhwal kshetra ka varnan milta hai, jabki Kumaon kshetra ko Manaskhand kaha jata hai. Pauri Garhwal ka sabse prasiddh Puranik sambandh Kanvashram (Mahabharat ki Shakuntala-Dushyant katha aur Kalidas ke Abhigyan Shakuntalam) se hai. Neelkanth Mahadev ka sambandh Samudra Manthan ki katha se joda jata hai.

Skanda Puran, Mahabharat aur Kalidas ke Granthon mein Zikr

Seedha jawab: Skanda Purana ke Kedarkhand me Garhwal ke tirth, nadiyan aur Shiv-sthal ka varnan hai. Mahabharat ke Adi Parva me Kanva Rishi ke ashram (Malini nadi ke kinare) ki Shakuntala katha aati hai. Van Parva me Pandavon ki Himalaya-tirthyatra aur Mahaprasthanika Parva me unki Himalaya-yatra ka varnan hai. Kalidas ki Abhigyan Shakuntalam ka sthan Malini ke kinare maana jata hai.

Ek zaroori spashtikaran (Verse number kyon nahi diye?): Puranon aur Mahabharat ke shlok-sankhya (verse number) alag-alag sanskaran (edition) me alag hoti hai. Agar aap shodh ya UKPSC taiyari ke liye exact reference chahte hain, to Gita Press ya Critical Edition (BORI) ka sanskaran dekhein.

Pauri Garhwal ki “Puranik pehchan” ke 4 stambh:

  • Kanvashram (Kotdwar): Katha ke anusaar Indra ne Menaka ko Vishwamitra ki tapasya bhang karne bheja. Unki putri ko Malini nadi ke kinare Kanva Rishi ne paala, jo aage chal kar Shakuntala bani. Shakuntala aur Raja Dushyant ke putra Bharat ke naam par “Bharatvarsh” ka naam padne ki manyata hai.
  • Neelkanth Mahadev (Yamkeshwar block): Samudra Manthan me nikle halahal vish ko Shiv ne pee liya, jisse unka kanth neela ho gaya. Manyata hai ki Shiv ne yahi baith kar tapasya ki.
  • Kamleshwar Mahadev (Srinagar): Lok-katha ke anusaar Bhagwan Vishnu (ya Shri Ram) ne yahan Shiv ki kamal ke phoolon se puja ki. Isi se mandir ka naam Kamleshwar pada.
  • Dhari Devi (Kalyasaur): Devi Bhagwat ke anusaar 108 Shakti-peethon me ginti. Devi ki upar ki murti yahan aur neeche ka hissa Kalimath (Rudraprayag) me maani jaati hai.

Shodh ki baat: “Pauri Garhwal ka Puranik itihas” ya “Kanvashram kahan hai” jaise sawal par log kam jaankari wale pages par pahunchte hain. Hamara maksad in sabhi sandarbhon ko ek jagah dena hai.

Pauri Garhwal: Pramukh Mandir, Dham, Gurudwara aur Math

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ke mukhya mandir hain Kandoliya Devta (Pauri), Kyunkaleshwar Mahadev (Pauri ke paas), Kamleshwar Mahadev (Srinagar), Dhari Devi (Kalyasaur), Neelkanth Mahadev, Jwalpa Devi, Danda Nagraja, Tarkeshwar Mahadev, Ekeshwar, Binsar Mahadev, Sidhbali Hanuman (Kotdwar) aur Kanvashram. Gurudwara Pipli me Guru Nanak ki yatra ki yaad me hai.

Architecture Style (Katyuri/Nagara Stone Shikhara with wooden canopy ‘Chhatri’), Opening/Closing Dates (Kapat Khulne aur Band Hone ki Tithi), Winter Seat (Gaddi Sthal) & Pooja Timings

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ke mandir Char Dham jaise nahi hain, isliye inke kapat sardiyon me band nahi hote aur “Gaddi Sthal” ka niyam lagu nahi hota. Ye saal bhar khule rehte hain, par mele aur Navratri ke samay bheed zyada hoti hai. Zyadatar mandir patthar ke hain aur kai Nagara-shaili (shikhar wali) ya sthaniya Garhwali shaili me hain.

Yatra se pehle zaroor jaan lein (Practical Guide):

  • Samay: Aam taur par mandir subah 5-6 baje se shaam 7-8 baje tak khule rehte hain. Mele ya Navratri ke din bheed aur samay badal sakte hain. Safar se pehle sthaniya pujari ya homestay se phone par poochh lein.
  • Dress code: Mandir me saadgi wale kapde pehnein, aur chamde ki vastu (belt, purse) mandir ke andar na le jayein.
  • Mausam: Sardi me Binsar, Khirsu, Danda Nagraja jaise oonche sthalon par raat me bahut thand ho sakti hai. Barish me landslide se raaste band ho sakte hain.
  • Doori ka andaza: Pauri se Kyunkaleshwar kareeb hai; Neelkanth Rishikesh-Swarg Ashram ki taraf se zyada aasan hai (Pauri Garhwal: Hawai aur Helicopter Seva me raaste dekhein).

Pauri Garhwal ke mandiron ki 3 shailiyan:

  • Nagara/Shikhara shaili: Patthar ke oonche shikhar wale mandir (jaise Kyunkaleshwar ke bare me kaha jata hai).
  • Sthaniya Garhwali shaili: Chhoti patthar ki imarat, slate (pathal) ki dhalawan chhat, jinke samne chhota aangan.
  • Aadhunik mandir: Nayi imarat jo purane sthan par banayi gayi (jaise Dhari Devi ka naya mandir).

Dhyan dein: Kuch mandiron ki oonchai, doori aur tithiyan alag sources me thodi alag milti hain. Page me di gayi sankhyaen “lagbhag” hain; yatra se pehle Zila Paryatan Vibhag (pauri.nic.in) se confirm karein.

Rajrajeshwari Mandir, Devalgarh: Panwar Rajvansh ki Kuldevi

Seedha jawab: Devalgarh (Srinagar ke paas) me sthit Rajrajeshwari Mandir ko Garhwal ke Panwar rajvansh ki kuldevi ka sthan mana jata hai. Devalgarh ko Raja Ajay Pal ke samay Garhwal rajya ki ek rajdhani ke roop me jana jata hai, isliye yeh mandir itihas aur aastha dono ke liye mahatvapurn hai (pushti karein: mandir ka nirman-kaal aur Srinagar se sahi doori sthaniya stara/ASI-rajya puratatva vibhag se confirm karein).

Pauri Garhwal: Isht Devta, Gram Devta aur Jagar mein Devta Avtaran

Seedha jawab: Garhwal me har gaon ka apna Gram-Devta aur har parivaar ka Kul-Devta hota hai. Pauri Garhwal me mukhya Devta hain Nagraja, Bhairav, Bhumiyal (Kshetrapal), Narsingh, Nanda Devi, Jwalpa Devi, Dhari Devi aur sthaniya Devta jaise Kandoliya aur Ghandiyal. Jagar vo anushthan hai jisme Jagariya Devta ki gatha gaata hai aur Dangariya (medium) me Devta avatarit mana jata hai.

Golu Devta (Nyay ke Devta), Nanda-Sunanda, Latu Devta (Blindfold priest worship at Wan), Mahasu Devta (Four brothers: Botha, Pavasi, Vasik, Chalda), Pokhu Devta, Someshwar, Bhumiyal, Airi, Kalbisht, Haru Heet, Chaumu (Protector of cattle) & Gangnath

Seedha jawab: Golu Devta mukhya roop se Kumaon ke nyay-Devta hain (Garhwal ke kuch hisson me Gwill naam se poojit hain). Latu Devta ka mandir Wan (Chamoli) me hai. Mahasu Devta Jaunsar-Bawar aur Uttarkashi kshetra ke hain. Pauri Garhwal me Nagraja, Bhairav, Bhumiyal, Narsingh aur Nanda Devi zyada prachlit hain.

Zaroori saaf baat: soochi poore Uttarakhand ke Devtaon ki hai. Pauri Garhwal me inme se kai (Latu, Mahasu, Pokhu, Someshwar) poojit nahi hain. Isiliye table me saaf likha hai kaunsa Devta kahan ka hai, taaki Pauri ke Devtaon par bharosa bana rahe.

Pauri Garhwal ke Kul-Devta aur Gram-Devta ki 4 pehchan:

  • Thaan (Than): Gaon ya khet ke kone me patthar ki chhoti sthapana ya mandir.
  • Saal me ek puja: Ashadh ya Kartik jaise mahine me gaon-wasi Devta ki puja karte hain.
  • Dhol-Damau aur Jagar: Bina Dhol ke Devta ka aahvan puri tarah nahi hota.
  • Bhet aur Bhog: Gud, ghee, nariyal, aur rote (meetha) Devta ko chadhaye jaate hain.

Jagar, Jagariya, Dangariya aur Devta Khel: Kaise Hota Hai

Seedha jawab: Jagar me Jagariya (gaane wala) Hudka aur Thali (ya Dhol-Damau) par Devta ki gatha gaata hai. Gatha ke dauran Dangariya (jisme Devta avatarit mana jata hai) kaampta hai aur Devta ki bhasha me jawab deta hai. Ant me Devta se “Prashna” (kasht ka karan) aur “Nivaran” poochha jata hai.

Pauri Garhwal: Pragaitihasik Kaal, Shila-Chitra aur Shailashray

Seedha jawab: Garhwal Himalaya me prachin shail-chitra (rock shelters) aur megalithic avshesh ke sabse charchit sthal Chamoli, Almora aur Uttarkashi jaise zilon me darj hain. Pauri Garhwal ke baare me praman wali, saarvajanik taur par prakashit prehistoric soochi seemit hai.

Prachin Chitrit Gufayein (Lakhudiyar), Pashan Yug ke Auzaar aur Mahapashan Avshesh

Seedha jawab: Lakhudiyar (Almora) aur Kimni (Chamoli) Uttarakhand ke prasiddh shail-chitra sthal hain. Copper hoards Bankot (Pithoragarh) aur Garhwal-Kumaon ke kai sthalon se mile hain. Pauri Garhwal me Kanvashram aur Kotdwar-Bhabar kshetra ka sambandh pracheen kaal se joda jata hai, par iske liye ASI (Archaeological Survey of India) ki report dekhna behtar hai.

Aap khud kaise jaanch sakte hain?

  • Archaeological Survey of India (ASI), Dehradun Circle ki report dekhein.

  • Sthaniya gaon ke buzurgon se “kisi purani sthapana” ya “patthar ke upar gadhe huye gol nishan” ke bare me poochhein.

Pauri Garhwal: Kuninda aur Katyuri Raajvansh ka Daur

Seedha jawab: Himalaya ke talhati kshetra me Kuninda raajvansh (lagbhag 2vi sadi ISA purv se 3ri sadi tak) ke sikke mile hain. Baad me Katyuri raajvansh ne Garhwal-Kumaon par shasan kiya, jiski rajdhani Joshimath/Kartikeyapura aur Baijnath rahi. Pauri Garhwal ke bhabar kshetra ka sambandh bhi inhi pracheen satta-kendron se joda jata hai.

Kuninda Sikke (Amoghbhuti), Katyuri Rajdhaniyan, Tamrapatra (Pandukeshwar/Bageshwar) aur Pathar ke Mandir

Seedha jawab: Kuninda sikke (jaise Amoghabhuti ke chaandi aur taambe ke sikke) Himalayan talhati kshetra me mile hain. Katyuri rajao ki tamrapatra (taambe ki patti par lekh) Pandukeshwar (Chamoli) aur Bageshwar se mili hain. Pauri Garhwal ke Kyunkaleshwar jaise patthar ke mandiron ko prachin mandir-shaili se jod kar dekha jata hai.

Pauri Garhwal: Panwar (Garhwal) & Chand (Kumaon) Kingdoms

Seedha jawab: Garhwal me Panwar vansh ki shuruat Kanak Pal se maani jaati hai, jisne Chandpur Garhi ke raja ki putri se vivah kar saasan sambhala (traditional tithi 823 ISA). Raja Ajay Pal (lagbhag 1358) ne 52 chhote garhon ko mila kar Garhwal rajya banaya. Rajdhani Chandpur Garhi se Devalgarh aur phir Srinagar (Pauri Garhwal) gayi. Panwar vansh 1804 tak raha.

Is Zile ke Garh aur Pargana, Raajaon ke Yuddh aur Goonth Zameen

Seedha jawab: “52 Garh” Garhwal ke un chhote kilon ko kehte hain jo alag-alag sardaron ke paas the. Ajay Pal ne inhe ek kiya. Inme Chandpurgarh, Devalgarh, Kandara Garh aur Langur Garh jaise naam aate hain. Pauri Garhwal me Devalgarh, Langur Garh, Chaundkot (Tilu Rauteli ka kshetra) aur Srinagar mukhya sthal hain. Goonth (mandir ko di gayi zameen) raja ki dharmik daan-parampara thi.

Dhyan dein (tithiyon me fark): Rajdhani badalne ki tithi alag sources me alag milti hai. Ek source (infobox) Devalgarh 1512-1517 aur Srinagar 1517-1804 dikhata hai, jabki ek doosra text Mahipati Shah ke 1622 ke baad Srinagar ko rajdhani banane ki baat kehta hai. Isliye hum nishchit varsh ke bajay “17vi sadi tak Srinagar prasiddh rajdhani rahi” jaise safe wording prayog kar rahe hain.

52 garhon ki soochi alag-alag itihaaskaron (jaise Pandit Harikrishna Raturi) me thodi alag milti hai. Isliye hum kisi ek soochi ko “final” kehkar nahi chhapte. Agar aap 52 Garh ki soochi sahi source se dena chahte hain, to Garhwal ka Itihas (H.K. Raturi) aur Shiv Prasad Dabral ke granth dekhein.

Goonth (Gunth) kya hai?
Raja dwara mandir ya Brahmin ko di gayi kar-mukt zameen, jise Goonth kehte the. Is zameen ki aay mandir ke kharch me lagti thi. Aaj bhi Pauri ke kai mandiron ke naam par purani zameen ka record Bhulekh me milta hai (Pauri Garhwal: Bhulekh Uttarakhand: Khatauni Online Kaise Dekhein me dekhein).

Pauri Garhwal: Gorkha Raaj (Gorkhyani) aur Angrez-Nepal Yuddh

Seedha jawab: Gorkha sena ne 1791 me Kumaon aur 1803-04 me Garhwal par adhikar kiya. Raja Pradyumna Shah 1804 me Khurbura (Dehradun) ki ladai me maare gaye. Garhwal par Gorkha shasan lagbhag 1815 tak raha, jise log “Gorkhyani” kehte hain. Anglo-Nepalese yuddh ke baad 1815-16 ki Sugauli sandhi se Garhwal ka poorvi hissa (Pauri Garhwal) angrezon ke paas aa gaya.

Gorkha Raaj (1790/1804-1815), Kile, Bhaari Kar aur Sugauli Sandhi

Seedha jawab: Gorkha shasan me bhaari kar, begar aur gulami ke kaaran Garhwal ki aabadi ghati. Ek source ke anusaar 1811 ki revenue vyavastha ne kai parivaron ko karz aur gulami me dhakel diya. 1815 me Angrez aur Raja Sudarshan Shah ki madad se Gorkha haare, aur Sugauli sandhi ke baad poorvi Garhwal (Pauri) angrezon ko mila, pashchimi hissa Raja Sudarshan Shah ko (Tehri Riyasat).

Gorkhyani ki yaad: Garhwali lok-smriti me Gorkha shasan ko ek bhayanak daur ki tarah yaad kiya jata hai. Buzurg bachchon ko “Gorkhyani” ke kisse sunate the. Kotdwar kshetra ke bare me ek source batata hai ki yahan Gorkha shasan lagbhag 12 saal raha. Gorkhon ke qile aur chowkiyon ke avshesh Garhwal ke kai kasbon me aaj bhi sthaniya itihas ka hissa hain.

Dhyan dein: Langur Garh jaise kilon ki Gorkha-kaal me bhoomika ke bare me alag-alag sources me alag jaankari hai. Hum yahan sirf wahi dete hain jo bharosemand source me confirm hai.

Pauri Garhwal: British Garhwal / Kumaon Commissionery & Tehri Riyasat

Seedha jawab: 1815 ke baad Pauri Garhwal angrezon ke adheen “British Garhwal” bana. Garhwal ka zila 1839-40 ke aas-paas alag hua aur mukhyalaya Pauri me aaya (jaisa Pauri Garhwal: Tehsil, block aur prashasan me bataya). Zameen ka bandobast (Settlement) Traill, Batten, Beckett aur Pauw ke daur me hua. Lansdowne chhavni ki sthapna 1887 me hui aur Kotdwar tak rail 1890 me pahunchi.

Traill, Batten, Beckett aur Pauw ke Bandobast (1815-1896), Van Kanoon, Chhavni, Dak Bungalow aur Ghode ke Raaste

Seedha jawab: G.W. Traill ne 1815 ke baad ke shuruati bandobast kiye. Baad me J.H. Batten (1840 ke dashak), Beckett (1860 ke dashak) aur Pauw (1890 ke dashak) ke bandobast hue. In sabhi me zameen ka lekha-jokha, lagaan aur patwari-vyavastha ka aadhar bana. Ye wahi aadhar hai jis par aaj bhi Bhulekh ka kuch record tika hai.

Pauri Garhwal: Azaadi ki Ladai, Kuli Begar aur Rajya Andolan

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ke Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali (janm 25 Dec 1891, Meason, Patti Chauthan) ne 23 April 1930 ke Peshawar Kaand me nihatthe Pathan satyagrahiyon par goli chalane se inkaar kiya. 1994 ke Uttarakhand Andolan me Pauri, Kotdwar aur Srinagar ne bhi balidan diye. Coolie Begar ki praatha ka virodh Kumaon (1921, Bageshwar) aur Garhwal me bhi hua.

Kuli Begar (1921), Van Satyagrah, Peshawar Kand, Chipko aur 1994 ke Shaheed

Seedha jawab: Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali ko Peshawar Kaand (23 April 1930) ke liye yaad kiya jata hai. 1994 ke andolan me 8 August ko Pauri me Jeet Bahadur Gurung pehle shahid bane, 3 October ko Kotdwar me do andolankari maare gaye aur 10 November ko Srinagar ke Sriyantra Tapu me do yuvaon ki hatya hui. Coolie Begar (1921 Saryu pledge) mukhya roop se Kumaon ki ghatna hai; Tilari Kand (1930) Tehri Riyasat ki; Chipko (Gaura Devi, 1974) Chamoli ka.

Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali (1891-1979):

  • Peshawar Kaand: 23 April 1930 ko Peshawar me satyagraha ke dauran unhone Garhwal Rifles ki platoon ke saath nihatthe pradarshankariyon par goli chalane ke angrezi aadesh ko manne se inkaar kiya.
  • Saza: Court-martial hua, sampatti zabt hui aur unhe Abbottabad jail bheja gaya. Barrister Mukundi Lal ke prayas se kai sainikon ki mrityudand ki saza umra-qaid me badli.

  • Nidhan: 1 October 1979.
  • Samman: 23 April 1994 ko dak tikat jaari hua. Pauri Garhwal: Lok-Kathayein: Achhari, Veer Yoddha aur Rahasyamay Kisse

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ki sabse prasiddh lok-gatha Tilu Rauteli ki hai, jo Gurad (Gurrad Talla), Chaundkot, Pauri Garhwal ki veer yoddha kanya maani jaati hain. Unhe “Garhwal ki Rani Laxmibai” kaha jata hai. Madho Singh Bhandari (Tehri) ki Maletha Guhl katha aur Rajula-Malushahi (Kumaon) doosri prasiddh lok-gathayein hain.

Achhari-Matri ki Kahaniyan, Veer Yoddha aur Rahasyamay Lok-Kathayein

Seedha jawab: Tilu Rauteli (17vi sadi ke uttarardh ki manyata) ne 15 se 20 saal ki umra me 7 yuddh ladne ki katha prasiddh hai. Wo apne ghode “Binduli” par savaar rehti thi. Unki mrityu Nayar nadi ke kinare hui maani jaati hai. Jagar me unhe “Ranbhoot” ke roop me yaad kiya jata hai aur Thadya geeton me unki gatha gayi jati hai.

Achhari (Aanchri) aur Matri ki manyata: Garhwal-Kumaon ke oonche bugyal aur jungle (jaise Pauri ka Doodhatoli kshetra) ke baare me ye lok-manyata hai ki wahan Achhari (van-apsarayen) rehti hain jo shor, chatak laal-peele kapde ya tez gandh se naraz ho sakti hain. Isiliye bujurg oonche bugyal me shaant rehne, chatak rang ke kapde na pehnne aur phool na todne ki salah dete hain. Ye lok-manyata prakriti ke prati sammaan sikhati hai.

Pauri ke “rahasyamayi kisse” (sthaniya manyatayein):

  • Dhari Devi ki murti ka sthan-parivartan (2013): 16 June 2013 ko Dhari Devi ki murti ko hydropower project ke liye anya sthan par le jaya gaya aur usi samay Uttarakhand me Kedarnath-aapda aayi. Sthaniya shraddhalu ise Devi ka krodh maante hain. Ye aastha ki baat hai; vaigyanik drishti se aapda ka karan bhaari barish, glacier aur badal ka fatna tha (Pauri Garhwal: Historical Disasters, Earthquakes aur Resilience History me vistar).
  • Kamleshwar me raat bhar deepak: Vaikunth Chaturdashi ki raat santan-prapti ki manokamna se dampati haath me deepak thaam kar khade rehte hain, aisi prasiddh manyata hai.
  • Binsar Mahadev ka ekant: Ghane jungle me bane is mandir ke bare me kai sthaniya kisse sunaye jaate hain.
  • Badi Utsav (Gwarh aur Khothgee gaon): Khirsu ke paas ke gaon me naksheedar lakdi ki “Badi” murti ko rassi se lagbhag 500 meter neeche utarne ki sthaniya parampara hai.

Zaroori: In rahasyamayi kisson ko hum “aastha” ke roop me prastut karte hain.
