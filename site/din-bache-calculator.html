Hisaab kaise hota hai

Ye do tareekhon ke beech ka antar nikaalta hai — aaj se lekar aapki chuni hui tareekh tak.

Dhyaan dene layak do baatein:

  • Aaj ka din nahi gina jaata. Agar aaj 8 tareekh hai aur lakshya 10 hai, to jawab 2 din hai (na ki 3). Yahi aam samajh bhi hai.
  • Leap year apne aap sambhal jaata hai. February me 29 din hone par bhi hisaab sahi rehta hai.
Ek kaam ki baat — agar tareekh peeche ki chun li, to jawab batata hai ki us din ko kitne din ho gaye. Yaani ye dono taraf kaam karta hai: aage ka intezaar aur peeche ki yaad, dono.

Kahan ye sabse zyada kaam aata hai

  • Exam ki tayaari — bache hue din pata hone se syllabus baant lena aasaan ho jaata hai. "60 din hain, 6 subject hain" — matlab har subject ko 10 din.
  • Shaadi ya koi bada aayojan — kaam ki list aur budget dono ka samay tay ho jaata hai.
  • ITR aur tax ki tareekh — advance tax ki kisten aur ITR ki aakhri tareekh, dono par nazar.
  • Loan ki aakhri EMI — kitne din baad aap karze se aazad honge. Ye dekhna sach me hausla deta hai.
  • Bima ya document ka renewal — gaadi ka insurance, passport, license, RC.
  • Delivery ya koi medical tareekh
  • FD ya nivesh ki maturity — taaki paisa aane par pehle se yojana ho
Ek aadat jo bahut pareshani bachati hai — saal ki shuruaat me apni saari zaroori tareekhein ek jagah likh lijiye: bima ka renewal, ITR, school ki fees, EMI ka aakhri din. Phir har mahine ek baar dekh lijiye ki kaun paas aa raha hai. Bhoolna sabse mehnga padta hai — kyunki jurmana aur late fee usi se aati hai.

Bache hue din ko kaam me kaise laayein

Number dekh lena aasaan hai; uska istemaal karna asli baat hai.

  • Ulta baantiye. 90 din hain to aakhri 10 din dohraane ke liye chhod dijiye, aur bache 80 me kaam baantiye. Jo log poore samay ko naya padhne me laga dete hain, unka dohraav reh jaata hai.
  • Buffer rakhiye. Har yojana me 10-15% samay khali chhodiye — beemari, mehmaan, ya koi achanak kaam aa hi jaata hai.
  • Bade kaam ko tukdon me todiye. "Shaadi ki tayaari" ek kaam nahi hai; wo 30 chhote kaam hain. Har ek ki apni tareekh honi chahiye.
  • Aakhri tareekh se pehle ka lakshya rakhiye. ITR ki aakhri tareekh 31 hai to apne liye 20 rakh lijiye. Aakhri dinon me portal dheema chalta hai aur galti ki gunjaish sabse zyada hoti hai.

Ek chhoti si baat jo bahut logon ke kaam aati hai — sarkari kaagaz me aksar "90 din ke andar" ya "30 din ke bheetar" likha hota hai. Wo din kaagaz ki tareekh se gine jaate hain, aapke haath me aane ki tareekh se nahi. Isliye tareekh dekhte hi hisaab laga lijiye, warna samay nikal jaata hai.

Din ginne ka faayda tab hai jab kaam bant jaaye

"Exam me 90 din bache" sunkar ghabrahat hoti hai. Usi 90 din ko hafton me baant dein to kaam saaf dikhta hai.

Bache dinKya karna chahiye
90 se 45Poora syllabus ek baar — naya padhna sirf isi daur me
45 se 20Dohraav + har vishay ka ek poora paper
20 se 7Sirf apne likhe note aur galtiyon ki list
7 se 0Kuch naya nahi — neend, formula sheet, aur aaram
Ek chhoti ginti jo soch badal deti hai: 90 din me se Sunday aur pehle se tay chhuttiyan hataiye. Aksar 90 din sikud kar 70-75 asli din reh jaate hain. Isi tarah shaadi, form ki aakhri tareekh ya visa ke kaam me bhi — bache din me se sarkari chhutti aur bank band hone wale din hata kar dekhiye. "Bahut samay hai" wala dhokha yahi ginti todti hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Aaj ka din ginti me aata hai?

Nahi. Aaj 8 tareekh hai aur lakshya 10 hai to jawab 2 din aata hai — yahi aam samajh bhi hai.

Peeche ki tareekh daal sakte hain?

Haan. Tab jawab batata hai ki us din ko kitne din ho gaye — ye dono taraf kaam karta hai.

Leap year ka hisaab sahi rehta hai?

Haan, wo apne aap sambhal jaata hai. February me 29 din hone par bhi jawab sahi aata hai.

Sarkari kaam me '30 din ke andar' kis din se ginte hain?

Aam taur par kaagaz ya notice par likhi tareekh se, aapke haath me aane ki tareekh se nahi. Isliye tareekh dekhte hi hisaab laga lena behtar hai.
