Pauri Garhwal: Saar aur Zile ki Pehchan

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal Uttarakhand ke Garhwal Mandal ka zila hai jiska mukhyalaya Pauri hai. Yeh zila apni Garhwal Rifles ki sainik parampara, Kotdwar jaise pravesh-dwar, Srinagar jaise puraane rajdhani-shahar, Lansdowne chhavni aur Khirsu-Neelkanth jaise tirth-paryatan sthalon ke liye jaana jata hai.

Zile ki Pehchan: Ek Nazar Mein

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ka kshetrafal 5,329 varg kilometre (Census 2011) hai aur Census 2011 me abadi 6,87,271 thi. Yeh Garhwal Mandal ka mukhyalaya zila hai jahan Commissioner Garhwal ka karyalaya Pauri me hai.

Pauri Garhwal ko samajhne ke liye sabse pehle ek baat yaad rakhiye: yeh Uttarakhand ke un zilon me se hai jahan “mitti se zyada fauj ki vardi ki khushbu” aati hai. Yahan se Garhwal Rifles ke hazaron jawan aur afsar, aur desh ke sabse unche sainik padon tak pahunchne wale naam (jaise Chief of Defence Staff rahe General Bipin Rawat, jinka mool pradesh Pauri Garhwal hai) nikle hain. Zile ke gaon-gaon me “pensioner” parivaar, “bharti ki daud” ki ladkon ki practice aur “chhutti par aaya fauji” ka drishya aam hai.

Ek zaroori observation: Census 2001–2011 ke beech Pauri Garhwal ki abadi me lagbhag -1.41% ki giravat darj hui, yani zila apni abadi badhane ke bajay ghata. Iska seedha karan palayan (out-migration) hai.

Zile ke 5 sabse bade shahar/kasbe jinse zila pehchana jata hai:

  • Pauri – Zila mukhyalaya, Kandoliya devta ki pahadi par basa, Himalaya ke 180° view ke liye mashhoor.
  • Kotdwar – Garhwal ka “Pravesh Dwar” (Gateway of Garhwal), zile ka sabse bada vyaparik aur aabadi wala shahar.
  • Srinagar (Garhwal) – Alaknanda kinare ka puraana rajdhani shahar, Medical college aur Garhwal Central University ka campus (HNB Garhwal University) yahin hai.
  • Lansdowne – Garhwal Rifles Regimental Centre wali sainik chhavni, 1,700 meter ke aaspaas ka shaant hill station.
  • Satpuli / Dugadda / Thalisain – Nayar ghati aur sthaniya mandi/bazaar ke kendra.

Pauri Garhwal Naam Kaise Pada: Naam ka Itihas

Seedha jawab: “Garhwal” shabd “Garh” (kila) aur “wal” (swami/wale) se bana mana jata hai, yani “kilon wala pradesh”. “Pauri” naam ke arth ke baare me koi ek sarvamanya pramaan nahi hai, lekin sthaniya manyata ise pahadi dhalaan par seedhi-numa (step jaisi) basavat se jodti hai.

Garhwal naam kaise pada?
Madhyakaleen Garhwal kai chhote-chhote rajyon (Gadhpatiyon) me bata tha. Parampara ke anusaar yahan 52 Garh (kile) the, aur har Garh ka ek Garhpati (chhota raja) hota tha. Baad me Panwar vansh ke raja Ajay Pal ne in sab Garhon ko apne adheen karke ek Garhwal rajya banaya. Isiliye pradesh ka naam “Garhwal” pada. Yeh vyakhya itihaaskaron aur sthaniya itihas dono me sabse zyada maani jati hai.

Pauri naam ke baare me prachalit mat:

  • Pahadi dhalaan ka “seedhi” jaisa roop: Pauri shahar Kandoliya pahadi par taras (terraces) ki tarah upar-neeche basa hai, isliye kuch log ise “Pauri (seedhi)” se jodte hain.
  • Sthaniya shabd-parampara: Kuch buzurg ise kisi puraane gaon, pargana ya devsthan ke naam se juda hua batate hain.
  • Naam ke mool ko lekar itihaaskaron me ekmat nahi hai, isliye is par “ek hi sahi jawab” likhna theek nahi hoga.

Zile ke baaki mashhoor naamon ka arth (sthaniya maanyata ke anusaar):

Kotdwar“Kot” (kila/gadh) + “dwar” (darwaza), yani Garhwal ka pravesh-dwar. Purane naam “Khohdwar/Kauddwar” bhi sunne ko milte hain.
Srinagar“Shri” (sampann/pavitra) + “nagar”. Manyata hai ki yahan Shri Yantra ki sthapana se jodkar isse Srinagar kaha gaya.

Pauri Garhwal Zila Kab aur Kaise Bana (Mool Zila aur Adhisoochna)

Seedha jawab: Angrezon ne 1815 ke baad Garhwal ko Kumaon ke saath prashasan me rakha, 1839 me Garhwal alag zila bana aur 1840 me mukhyalaya Pauri laya gaya. 1960 me Chamoli alag zila bana aur 1997 me Rudraprayag zila Pauri samet kuch zilon se kaat kar banaya gaya, jisse Pauri Garhwal apne vartaman roop me aaya.

Zaroori Note (Parent District ka sawal): Rudraprayag zila Chamoli, Tehri Garhwal aur Pauri Garhwal ke kuch kshetron ko milakar banaya gaya. Sainik itihas ka ek khaas mod: 1887 me Garhwal Rifles ki pehli paltan Kaludanda (aaj ka Lansdowne) pahunchi aur baad me ye jagah Garhwal Rifles Regimental Centre ke roop me vikasit hui. Is ek ghatna ne Pauri Garhwal ki aarthik aur samajik pehchan ko hamesha ke liye “sainik zila” bana diya.

Pauri Garhwal: Collectorate aur Rajasv Prashasan

Seedha jawab: Zila prashasan ka sabse bada adhikari Zila Magistrate (DM) hota hai jiska karyalaya (Collectorate) Pauri me hai. Revenue ki drishti se zila 6 up-khandon aur 13 tehsilon me banta hai, jahan SDM aur Tehsildar zameen, pramaan-patra aur kanoon-vyavastha sambhalte hain.

Zila Mukhyalay, Vikas Bhawan aur Collectorate Kahan Hai

Seedha jawab: Zila mukhyalaya Pauri hai. Zila Magistrate ka karyalaya (Collectorate), Vikas Bhawan (jahan zila ke vikas vibhag baithte hain) aur Mandal Aayukt (Commissioner, Garhwal) ka karyalaya Pauri shahar me sthit hain.

Pauri me kya-kya milta hai?

  • Collectorate (Zila Magistrate Karyalaya): Zila ka sabse bada prashasanik kendra.
  • Vikas Bhawan: PWD, Krishi, Shiksha, Swasthya jaise zila-stariya vibhagon ke karyalay.
  • Mandal Aayukt Karyalaya (Garhwal Commissionery): Garhwal Mandal ke 7 zilon ka prashasanik mukhyalaya.
  • Zila Nyayalay (District Court) aur anya sarkari karyalay.

Aam logon ke liye salah: Pauri shahar pahadi par basa hai, isliye karyalay ke samay se pehle pahunchne ke liye shared taxi ya apni gaadi ka samay dhyan me rakhiye. Subah ki pehli gaadiyon ka samay Pauri Garhwal: Patti, gaon aur bazaar (Pauri Garhwal: Pramukh Bus Adde, Taxi Stand aur Booking) me diya gaya hai.

Kanungo circle kya hota hai?
Tehsil ke andar kai Kanungo Circle (Rajaswa Nirikshak Mandal) hote hain. Har circle me kai Patwari Halqa aate hain. Kanungo Patwariyon ka supervision karta hai aur Tehsildar ko report karta hai.

Patwari Chowki: Revenue Police aur Regular Police ka Kshetra

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand ke pahadi gaon ke bade hisse me aaj bhi Patwari/Lekhpal “Revenue Police” ki bhumika nibhata aaya hai, jabki shehri kasbon me regular police thana hota hai. Is vyavastha me zameen ka record aur chhote apradhon ki prathmik jaanch ek hi adhikari ke paas hoti thi.

Revenue Police kya hai?
Angrezon ke samay (1800s ke aarambh me) pahadon me tainaat kam police bal ki jagah Patwari ko hi zameen ka record rakhne ke saath-saath kanoon-vyavastha ka bhi adhikar diya gaya. Isse “Patwari Chowki” ek aisi jagah ban gayi jahan gaon wale zameen ki problem bhi lekar aate the aur chhote jhagde bhi.

Ab kya sthiti hai?
Revenue police vyavastha par adalatein aur sarkar samay-samay par vichar karti rahi hain, aur hal ke saalon me is vyavastha ko dhire-dhire regular police me badalne ki charcha tez hui hai. Aapke gaon par kaunsa niyam lagu hai, iska sabse sahi jawab zila police karyalay (SSP Office, Pauri) ya tehsil se hi milega.

Aam gaonwale ke liye practical tips:

  • Koi bhi FIR ya shikayat ho to pehle poochhiye ki aapka gaon kis thane ya Patwari chowki ke adheen hai.
  • Zameen ka naksha/khasra/khatauni ke liye Patwari ya online Bhulekh portal sahi raasta hai (Pauri Garhwal: Bhulekh Uttarakhand: Khatauni Online Kaise Dekhein).
  • Emergency me seedha 112 dial kijiye.

Zila Panchayat Seats, Kshetra Panchayat & Total Gram Panchayats Count

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal me teen-star ki panchayati vyavastha hai: Gram Panchayat (gaon), Kshetra Panchayat (block) aur Zila Panchayat (zila). Seatein aur Gram Panchayat ki sankhya delimitation ke saath badalti rehti hain, isliye sahi aankda ke liye State Election Commission ki taza notification dekhein.

Teen-star Panchayati Raj:

  • Gram Panchayat: Gaon ya kuch gaon milakar. Pradhan aur Ward Sadasya chune jate hain.
  • Kshetra Panchayat (Block Panchayat): Pramukh aur Kshetra Panchayat Sadasya (BDC).
  • Zila Panchayat: Zila Panchayat Adhyaksh aur Zila Panchayat Sadasya.

Chunav ki avadhi: Uttarakhand me Panchayat chunav 5 saal me hote hain.

Mere gaon ka Pradhan kaun hai?
Iska sabse pakka jawab block ke BDO karyalaya ya zila panchayat ki list se milega, kyunki Pradhan ke naam har chunav ke baad badal sakte hain.

Aabaad aur Gair-Aabaad (Khaali) Gaon

Seedha jawab: Census 2011 ke anusaar Pauri Garhwal me lagbhag 3,100 se zyada gaon darj hain (alag-alag sarkari suchiyon me 3,142 se 3,500+ tak ka fark dikhta hai). Palayan ke karan zile ke kai gaon aaj aadhe-khali ya poori tarah khali ho chuke hain, jinhe “Bhootiya Gaon / Ghost Villages” kaha jata hai.

Gaon ki sankhya me farak kyun?
Alag-alag website aur report me kabhi tehsil ke hisab se, kabhi “revenue gaon” aur “abad gaon” ko alag-alag ginne se aankdon me farak aa jata hai. Isliye jab bhi aap kisi report ka hawala dein, “Census 2011 ke anusaar” zaroor likhein.

Palayan ki sthiti: Uttarakhand Gramin Vikas evam Palayan Aayog (2018) ki rapat me Pauri Garhwal aur Almora ko sabse zyada palayan se prabhavit zilon me ginaa gaya. Isi karan zile me kai gaon “Bhootiya Gaon” kehlane lage hain, jahan ghar khandhar ban chuke hain aur khet jungle me badal gaye hain.

Isse kya hota hai?

  • Khet banjar hote hain aur jangli suar, bandar, tendue kheti ko nuksan pahunchate hain.
  • Gaon ke Kul-Devta Mandir, Naula aur purane rastay dekhrekh ke abhav me bigadte hain.
  • Doosri taraf, kuch gaon Homestay, Organic Kheti aur Heritage Walk ke zariye wapas basne lage hain (Pauri Garhwal: Khaali Gaon aur Virasat ki Sair , Pauri Garhwal: Palayan Vapasi ki Safal Kahaniyan).

Nagar Panchayat aur Chhavni Parishad (jahan hai)

Seedha jawab: Satpuli, Swargashram (Jaunk) aur Thalisain Nagar Panchayat hain. Lansdowne ek Cantonment Board (Chhavni Parishad) hai jahan nagrik prashasan sena ke adheen hota hai.

Cantonment me kya alag hota hai?
Chhavni ilake me bijli, safai, nirman aur building rules par sena ke niyam bhi lagu hote hain. Isliye Lansdowne me ghar banane ya hotel kholne se pehle Cantonment Board ki anumati zaroori hoti hai (Pauri Garhwal: Building Bylaws, Map Approval aur Seismic Rules).

Pauri Garhwal: Rajneetik aur Chunavi Parichay

Lok Sabha Seat aur Maujooda Saansad

Pauri Garhwal se Shuru Hue Pramukh Rajnaitik Andolan

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ka itihas “jan-andolanon” se bhara hai: Uttarakhand rajya andolan (1990 ke dashak), Dola-Palki jaise samajik sudhar andolan aur paani, jungle aur kheti bachane ke sthaniya andolan yahan ki mitti se jude hain.

  • Uttarakhand Rajya Andolan (1994): Srinagar, Pauri aur Kotdwar jaise kshetron ne is andolan me badi bhoomika nibhayi.
  • Dola-Palki Andolan: Shilpkar (Dalit) samaj ke vivah me dola-palki ka adhikar paane ki ladai, jo Garhwal ke samajik sudhar itihas ka mahatvapurn adhyay hai.
  • Paani aur Pahad bachao andolan: Dudhatoli kshetra me sukhi nadiyon ko punarjivit karne ke jan-prayas.

Source note: Andolanon ki tithi aur pramukh vyaktiyon ke baare me sahi jaankari ke liye Uttarakhand ke itihas ki pramanik pustakon (jaise Shiv Prasad Dabral ya Badri Datt Pandey ki rachnayein) ka sandarbh lein.

Pauri Garhwal: Police, Suraksha aur Seema Bal

Seedha jawab: Zila police ka netritva Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) Pauri karte hain. Lansdowne me Garhwal Rifles Regimental Centre sthit hai, jo zile ko Indian Army ka ek mahatvapurn bhartiya kendra banata hai.

Fauj ki Regiment (Garhwal Rifles / Kumaon Regiment / Scouts), ITBP aur SSB Camp

Seedha jawab: Lansdowne me Garhwal Rifles Regimental Centre hai. Is regiment ka itihas 1887 se juda hai aur yahan ka Darwan Singh Regimental Museum seena-taan sainik virasat ka pratik hai.

Lansdowne aur Garhwal Rifles:

  • Garhwal Rifles Regimental Centre (GRRC): Garhwal Rifles ki bharti aur prashikshan ka mukhya kendra.
  • Darwan Singh Regimental Museum: Garhwal Rifles ki veerta aur itihas ki jhalak. Isi regiment ke Darwan Singh Negi aur Gabar Singh Negi ne Pratham Vishwa Yuddh me Victoria Cross jeeta tha.
  • Parade ground aur War Memorial: Lansdowne ke bahut se paryatak sirf yahan ka mahaul dekhne aate hain.

Note: Sainik kshetra me photography aur pravesh par kuch pratibandh ho sakte hain. Jaane se pehle sthaniya niyam dekh lein.

Pauri Garhwal: Nyayalay aur Kanooni Vyavastha

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ka Zila evam Satra Nyayalay (District & Sessions Court) Pauri me hai. Kotdwar me Additional District Judge ka nyayalay bhi hai, aur zile ke kai tehsilon me Munsif/Nyayik Magistrate nyayalay kaam karte hain.

Zila Nyayalay, Family Court aur Muft Kanooni Sahayata

Seedha jawab: Mukhya nyayalay Pauri me hai. Kotdwar me bhi Additional District Judge ke star ka nyayalay hai, jisse zile ke dakshini hisse ke logon ko suvidha milti hai.

Zile ke nyayik kendra: Pauri, Srinagar, Satpuli, Rikhnikhal, Lansdowne, Dhumakot, Kotdwar, Yamkeshwar aur Kalagarh.

Muft kanooni madad: Zila Vidhik Seva Pradhikaran (DLSA) Pauri aur Taluk-star par Legal Services Clinics zarooratmand logon ko muft salah dete hain. Mahilaon, SC/ST, bachchon aur gareeb vyaktiyon ke liye yeh seva khas taur par upyogi hai. Helpline: NALSA 15100.

Pauri Garhwal mein Sub-Registrar (Zameen Registry) Daftar

Seedha jawab: Zameen ki registry (sale deed, gift deed, will) Sub-Registrar karyalay me hoti hai, jo Pauri, Srinagar, Kotdwar, Lansdowne jaise tehsil mukhyalayon par uplabdh hai.

Registry se pehle ye 5 kaam zaroor karein:

  • Bhulekh par khasra/khatauni ki sahi entry check karein (Pauri Garhwal: Bhulekh Uttarakhand: Khatauni Online Kaise Dekhein).
  • Dakhil-Kharij (Mutation) aur Hissedari ki sthiti saaf karein.
  • Stamp duty aur circle rate ka hisaab lagwayein (Pauri Garhwal: Property Registration, Stamp Duty aur Tehsil Circle Rates).
  • Agar baahar ke rajya ke vyakti hain to 250 varg meter ke niyam jaanein (Pauri Garhwal mein Zameen Khareedne ke Kanoon aur Section 143).
  • Registry ke baad Dakhil-Kharij zaroor karwayein.

Pauri Garhwal: Aapda Prabandhan aur Emergency Vyavastha

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal bhukamp, bhuskhalan aur badal fatne jaise khatron ke liye sanvedansheel pahadi zila hai. Zila Aapda Prabandhan Pradhikaran (DDMA) ka Control Room 24x7 chalta hai, aur 112, 1070 tatha 1077 jaise numbers emergency me upyogi hain.

SDRF / NDRF ke Thikane, Baadh Aashray aur Emergency Helipad

Seedha jawab: Aapda ke samay SDRF (State Disaster Response Force), police, Army aur Fire Service milkar bachav karte hain. Pauri Garhwal ka bhugol aisa hai ki Alaknanda, Nayar aur Ramganga ke kinare ke ilake barsat me sabse zyada khatre me rehte hain.

Pauri Garhwal ke sabse aam aapda khatre:

  • Bhuskhalan (Landslide): Barsat me sadak band hona sabse aam samasya hai.
  • Badal Fatna (Cloudburst) aur Flash Flood: Nadi aur gadhere ke kinare ke gaon sabse zyada prabhavit.
  • Bhukamp: Poora Uttarakhand sanvedansheel seismic zone me aata hai (Pauri Garhwal: Bhoo-vigyan, Mitti ke Prakar aur Bhookamp Zone).
  • Jungle ki aag: Chir pine ke jungle me garmi me aag lagna aam hai (Pauri Garhwal: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh).

Ghar ka Emergency Kit: Torch, extra battery, pani, dawai, power bank, important documents ki photocopy aur 112/1077 ke numbers.

Pauri Garhwal: Gaadi Registration (RTO) aur Parivahan Code

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ka RTO code UK-12 hai (RTO Pauri), jabki Kotdwar ARTO ka code UK-15 hai. Pauri RTO ka STD code 01368 aur Kotdwar ARTO ka STD code 01382 hai.

Zile ka RTO/ARTO Code, Daftar aur Licence Sevayein

Pahadi Driving ke Niyam aur Green Cess / Eco-Tax

Seedha jawab: Pahadi sadakon par “hill driving” ke alag niyam nahi hote, lekin sadak ke sanket, one-way timing aur mausam ki chetavni mananaa zaroori hai. Uttarakhand me bahari rajyon ke vahanon par Green Cess/Eco-tax ke niyam lagu hain, jinki dar badalti rehti hai.

Pahadon me gaadi chalane ke 8 sunehre niyam:

  • Chadhai chadhte hue neeche aane wali gaadi ko pehle raasta dein (niyam anusaar jo sadak sanket kahe).
  • Mod par horn bajayein.
  • Barsat me bhuskhalan zone me gaadi na rokein.
  • Utarte samay lower gear me chalein, brake par bharosa na karein.
  • Raat me lambi pahadi yatra se bachein.
  • Pehle se sadak ki sthiti (Pauri Garhwal: Sadak, Highway, Pul aur Seema Sadak) check karein.
  • Fuel station ka dhyan rakhein, ki aage kitni door milega (Pauri Garhwal: Petrol Pump, EV Charging aur Mechanic).
  • Sabse zaroori: Seat belt aur helmet pahnein.

Green Cess ke liye: Taza dar aur lagu hone wale kshetra Uttarakhand Parivahan Vibhag ki official site par dekhein, kyunki ye niyam badalte rehte hain.
