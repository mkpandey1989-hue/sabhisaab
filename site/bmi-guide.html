BMI Kya Hai Aur Kaise Nikaalein?

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

BMI yaani Body Mass Index, ek aasaan number hai jo aapke weight aur height ke aadhaar par batata hai ki aapka wazan aapki lambai ke hisaab se sahi hai, kam hai ya zyada. Ye sehat ka ek mota-mota andaaza dene ka aasaan tarika hai. Neeche free BMI calculator hai jahan sirf weight aur height daal kar aap turant apna BMI jaan sakte hain.

BMI kaise nikalta hai?

BMI ka formula hai: weight (kg) ÷ (height × height in meters). Jaise agar kisi ka wazan 70 kg aur lambai 1.7 meter hai, to BMI = 70 ÷ (1.7 × 1.7) = lagbhag 24.2. Ghabrayein mat — calculator ye khud kar deta hai.

BMI ka matlab (categories)

  • 18.5 se kam: Wazan kam hai (underweight).
  • 18.5 – 24.9: Normal / sehatmand.
  • 25 – 29.9: Wazan zyada (overweight).
  • 30 se zyada: Motapa (obese).
Note: BMI ek aam andaaza hai, poora sch nahi. Ye muscle aur fat ka farak nahi samajhta — isliye ek fit pehelwan ka BMI bhi "zyada" dikh sakta hai. Isliye ise sirf ek shuruati sanket ki tarah lein.

Sehatmand rehne ke aasaan tarike

  • Santulit khana — sabzi, dal, protein, kam tala-bhuna.
  • Roz thodi shaaririk gatividhi (walk, exercise).
  • Paani zyada piyein, neend poori lein.
  • Zyada cheeni aur processed food kam karein.

FAQ

Kya BMI sabke liye sahi hai?

BMI aam logon ke liye mota andaaza deta, par athletes, bachchon, garbhwati mahilaon aur budhon ke liye alag maanak hote. Doctor se salah behtar.

Normal BMI kitna hona chahiye?

Aam taur par 18.5 se 24.9 ke beech normal maana jaata.

BMI kam karne ka jaldi tarika?

Koi "jaldi" surakshit tarika nahi — santulit khana + niyamit exercise + dhairya. Kisi bhi diet se pehle expert se milein.

Bharatiyon ke liye BMI ka paimana alag hai?

Haan. Dakshin Asia ke logon me kam BMI par bhi khatra badh jaata hai, isi liye yahan ki seemayein paschimi deshon se kam rakhi gayi hain. Kamar ka naap BMI se bhi zyada kaam ka paimana hai.

BMI theek hai par pet nikla hua hai — chinta ki baat?

Haan, dhyaan dene layak hai. BMI ye nahi batata ki charbi kahan jama hai. Purush me kamar 90 cm aur mahila me 80 cm se upar hone par doctor se baat kar lijiye.

Bharatiya logon ke liye alag paimana

BMI ka maanak paimana (18.5-24.9 normal) paschimi logon par aadharit hai. Kai bharatiya visheshagya kehte hain ki hamare shareer me ek hi BMI par charbi zyada hoti hai — khaaskar pet ke aas-paas. Isi liye bharat me kai jagah 23 se upar ko hi savdhaani ki shreni maana jaata hai, 25 se nahi.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki ghabra jaayein. Iska matlab sirf itna hai ki agar aapka BMI 23-25 ke beech hai, to use "bilkul theek" maan kar bhool mat jaaiye — kamar ka naap bhi dekh lijiye.

Kamar ka naap — BMI se behtar signal

Pet ki charbi (visceral fat) sabse khatarnak hoti hai kyunki wo andar ke ango ke aas-paas jamti hai. Ise naapne ka sabse aasaan tarika BMI nahi, kamar ka naap hai.

Waist-to-Height ratio: kamar (cm) ÷ height (cm). Ye 0.5 se kam hona aam taur par behtar maana jaata hai. Yaani aapki kamar aapki height ke aadhe se kam honi chahiye.

Naapne ka sahi tarika: subah khaali pet, saans chhod kar, naabhi ke level par fita lapetein — pet andar kheenchein nahi.

BMI zyada hai to kya karein

Sabse pehle — ghabrayein nahi aur koi crash diet shuru mat kar dijiye. Bhookhe rehkar ghataya gaya wazan aam taur par wapas aa jaata hai, aur beech me sehat kharab ho jaati hai.

  • Roz 30-40 minute chalein. Gym zaroori nahi. Tez chalna sabse aasaan aur sabse kaargar shuruaat hai.
  • Cheeni aur maida kam karein. Meethi chai, biscuit, namkeen, cold drink — inhi se sabse zyada calorie chupke se andar jaati hai.
  • Protein badhayein. Daal, ande, doodh, paneer, chana. Isse bhookh kam lagti hai aur muscle bachi rehti hai.
  • Neend poori karein. Kam neend se bhookh badhane wale hormone badh jaate hain — ye baat kam log jaante hain.
  • Dheere ghataayein. Hafte me aadha kilo kaafi hai. Isse ghataya wazan wapas nahi aata.

BMI kam ho to

18.5 se kam BMI bhi dhyan dene layak hai. Ismein kamzori, bar-bar bimar padna aur haddiyon ki kamzori ka khatra rehta hai. Yahan bhi ilaaj junk food nahi — dood, ghee, dry fruits, daal-chawal jaisi poshak cheezein aur thodi weight training se muscle banana behtar rehta hai. Agar khaate hue bhi wazan na badhe, to doctor se jaanch karwa lein — thyroid ya paachan ki koi dikkat ho sakti hai.

BMI ek shuruaati ishara hai, faisla nahi. Wo poori aabadi ke liye banaya gaya paimana hai, ek insaan ke liye nahi. Iske saath do saral naap zyada kaam ke hain — kamar ka ghera (purushon me 90 cm aur mahilaon me 80 cm se kam behtar maana jaata hai) aur kamar bataa lambai (0.5 se kam). Ye dono peth ki charbi pakadte hain, jo BMI se chhoot jaati hai. Teeno saath dekhiye. Aur kisi bhi nateeje par pahunchne se pehle doctor se hi salah lijiye — ye tool medical salah nahi deta.
Yaad rakhein: BMI ek number hai, poori kahani nahi. Aapki neend, energy, blood pressure, sugar aur roz ki phurti — ye sab milkar sehat banate hain. Kisi bhi bade badlaav se pehle doctor se salah zaroor lein.
