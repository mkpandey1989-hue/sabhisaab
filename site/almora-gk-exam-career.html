Almora: UKPSC, PCS aur UKSSSC ke liye 100 Ek-Line GK Tathya

Almora ke 100 Tathya (Patwari, Lekhpal, Police, SI, Forest Guard, VDO aur PCS ke liye)

Seedha jawab: Neeche Almora zile ke 100 chhote tathya hain. Jahan “” likha hai, wahan sources me antar ho sakta hai; kitaab ke hisaab se mila lein.

A. Bhugol aur Prashasan (1 se 30)

  • Almora Uttarakhand ke Kumaon mandal ka ek zila hai.
  • Zila mukhyalay Almora shahar hai.
  • Almora shahar lagbhag 1,640 m ki unchai par ek ghode ki naal jaisi ridge par basa hai.
  • Census 2011 ke anusar zile ka kshetrafal 3,144 varg km hai.
  • 2011 ki Census me jansankhya 6,22,506 thi.
  • 2011 me mahila-purush anupat 1,139 mahilayein per 1,000 purush tha (Census 2011).
  • 2001 se 2011 ke beech jansankhya vriddhi dar lagbhag −1.73% rahi.
  • Census 2011 ke anusar zile ki saksharta dar 80.47% thi.
  • Zile ki lagbhag 90.2% aabadi ki matrbhasha Kumaoni darj hui.
  • Almora ka vahan registration code UK-01 hai.
  • Almora shahar ka PIN code 263601 hai.
  • Almora ka STD code 05962 hai.
  • Pithoragarh zila 24 February 1960 ko Almora se alag hua.
  • Bageshwar zila 15 September 1997 ko bana.
  • Kausani ab Bageshwar zile me aata hai, Almora me nahi.
  • Almora zile me 11 community development blocks hain (Wikipedia).
  • Blocks: Bhainsiyachhana, Bhikiyasain, Chaukhutia, Dhaula Devi, Dwarahat, Hawalbagh, Lamgara, Salt, Syalde, Takula, Tarikhet.
  • Zile me 6 vidhan sabha seat hain: Dwarahat, Salt, Ranikhet, Someshwar (SC), Almora aur Jageshwar.
  • Someshwar vidhan sabha seat SC ke liye aarakshit hai.
  • Almora Lok Sabha seat ka hissa Almora zila maana jata hai (sansadiya kshetra ki sahi seemayein pushti karein).
  • Sabse nazdiki railway station Kathgodam hai (lagbhag 90 km).
  • Sabse nazdiki hawai adda Pantnagar hai (lagbhag 127 km).
  • Almora se Haldwani ki doori lagbhag 89 km hai.
  • Almora se Delhi ki sadak doori lagbhag 378 km maani jati hai (Wikipedia).
  • Almora ke liye mukhya rashtriya rajmarg sandarbh NH 109 aur NH 309A hain (Almora: Railway Station aur Aane Wali Rail Pariyojanayein).
  • Zile ki mukhya nadiyon me Kosi, Suyal aur Ramganga ke upari kshetra aate hain.
  • Kosi nadi Almora shahar ke paas se behti hai aur shahar ke paani ka ek srot hai.
  • Suyal nadi ke kinare Lakhudiyar ke shaila-chitra hain.
  • Almora zila 1,000 se 2,500 m ke beech ki unchai me phaila hai (aam jaankari).
  • Almora me Inner Line Permit ki zaroorat nahi hoti, kyunki zila antarrashtriya seema par nahi hai.

B. Itihas aur Swatantrata Sangram (31 se 55)

  • Almora ki sthapna Chand raja Balo Kalyan Chand ne ki (1563 ya 1568, source ke hisaab se).
  • Chand rajaon ne rajdhani Champawat se Almora layi.
  • Chand vansh se pehle Kumaon par Katyuri rajaon ka prabhav tha.
  • Gorkhaon ne Almora par 1790-91 ke aas-paas adhikar kiya (tithi par sources me antar).
  • Almora ka angrezi aur Gorkha yudh 1815 ke ant (april) me hua.
  • Gorkha sardar Bam Shah ne April 1815 me aatmasamarpan kiya (26/27 April).
  • Sugauli ki sandhi 4 March 1816 ko hui.
  • Sugauli sandhi ke baad Kumaon angrezon ke adhikar me aaya.
  • Kumaon Parishad ki sthapna 1915-16 ke aas-paas hui.
  • Coolie Begar andolan 14 January 1921 ko Bageshwar (Uttarayani mele) me hua.
  • Coolie Begar andolan ke netaon me Badri Datt Pandey ka naam aata hai.
  • Badri Datt Pandey ko “Kumaon Kesari” kaha jata hai.
  • Hargovind Pant Kumaon ke ek pramukh swatantrata senani aur rajneta rahe.
  • Govind Ballabh Pant ka janm Almora zile ke Khoont gaon me hua (Wikipedia ke anusar Almora se jude).
  • Govind Ballabh Pant Uttar Pradesh ke pehle Mukhyamantri (1950 se) maane jaate hain (kitaab se).
  • Almora Akhbar ki shuruaat 1871 me hui.
  • Almora Akhbar ko Kumaon ka ek prabhavshali Hindi saptahik mana jata hai.
  • Lakhudiyar ke shaila-chitra Almora ke Barechhina ke paas hain.
  • Lakhudiyar ke chitra 1968 me Dr M. P. Joshi dwara khoje gaye bataye jaate hain.
  • Ranikhet ki cantonment lagbhag 1869 se maani jati hai.
  • Ranikhet Kumaon Regiment Centre ka mukhyalay hai.
  • Swami Vivekananda teen baar Almora aaye (1890, 1897, 1898 bataye jaate hain; pushti karein).
  • Uday Shankar ne 1930 ke dashak ke ant me Almora me India Culture Centre sthapit kiya.
  • Kumaon me Chand kaal ke mahal Malla Mahal aur Talla Mahal Almora me hain.
  • Lal Mandi Qila (Fort Moira) Almora shahar ka ek purana qila hai.

C. Dharm, Sanskriti aur Paryatan (56 se 80)

  • Jageshwar Dham me lagbhag 124 mandir ek samuh me aur 15 mandir Dandeshwar samuh me bataye jaate hain (Wikipedia).
  • Jageshwar Dham bhagwan Shiv se juda hai.
  • Katarmal Surya Mandir Almora zile me hai aur 9vi sadi ka maana jata hai.
  • Chitai Golu Devta mandir Almora ke paas hai.
  • Golu Devta ko nyay ke devta mana jata hai.
  • Chitai me stamp paper par arzi aur ghanti chadhane ki parampara hai.
  • Kasar Devi ka kshetra Swami Vivekananda aur kai videshi sadhakon se jura bataya jata hai.
  • Dunagiri (Dwarahat ke paas) Hanuman aur Sanjivani ki lok-katha se judi hai.
  • Dwarahat apne mandir samuh ke liye prasiddh hai.
  • Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary 1988 me sanctuary ghoshit hui.
  • Binsar se Himalaya ki choti shreniyon ke nazaare dikhte hain (saaf mausam me).
  • Almora ki prasiddh mithai Bal Mithai hai.
  • Bal Mithai ki Joga Lal Sah ki dukaan Lal Bazar me 1865 se bataayi jati hai.
  • Singori mithai maalu ke patte me lipat kar di jati hai.
  • Almora Lakhori Mirchi ko GI tag 8 November 2023 ko mila.
  • Almora Lakhori Mirchi Uttarakhand ki pehli mirch hai jise GI tag mila.
  • Tamta (tamba ke bartan) ko 2021 me GI tag mila (PARI ke anusar).
  • Tamta shilp Almora ke Tamta Mohalla se jura hai.
  • Aipan Kumaon ki paramparik lok-chitrakala hai.
  • Ringal (pahadi baans) se tokriyan aur saman bante hain.
  • Syalde Bikhauti mela Almora zile ke Syalde me hota hai.
  • Phooldei tyohar Chaitra Sankranti par manaya jata hai.
  • Harela hariyali ka tyohar hai, lagbhag Karka Sankranti ke aas-paas.
  • Ghughutiya (Uttarayani) tyohar Makar Sankranti par manaya jata hai.
  • Almora ki Baithaki aur Khadi Holi prasiddh hai.

Almora: Fauj Bharti (Agniveer ARO) ke Maidan aur Physical Training

Sena Bharti ka Maidan aur Training

Seedha jawab: Kumaon ke yuva fauj me jaane ke liye prasiddh hain (Almora: Panwar & Chand Kingdoms). Ranikhet me Kumaon Regiment Centre hai. Indian Army ki official bharti site (joinindianarmy.nic.in) par zila-wise ARO dekhein.

Agniveer taiyari ke liye aam salah (fitness ke saath, chikitsiya salah nahi): 1. Pehle doctor se fitness jaanch karwayein, khaaskar agar dil, saans ya jodon ki koi dikkat ho. 2. Dheere badhayein: Roz thodi doori se shuru karke dheere-dheere badhayein. Achanak zyada daudne se chot ka khatra hota hai. 3. Warm-up aur cool-down zaroor karein. 4. Pahadi dhalan: Pahad me chadhai-utarai ka abhyas fitness me madad karta hai, par utarai me ghutne par dabav padta hai, isliye dhyan rakhein. 5. Sadak par daudte samay suraksha: Subah ke andhere me gaadi aur jangli janwar (Almora: Jadi-Booti aur Paramparik Paudha-Gyan) ka khatra hota hai; reflective kapde, torch aur samooh me daudein. 6. Khan-paan: Santulit aahar, paani aur neend ka dhyan rakhein. 7. Likhit pariksha ki taiyari bhi utni hi zaroori hai.

Dhyan dein: Bharti ke niyam (umar, uchai, physical test ke standard) badalte hain. Sirf official notification par bharosa karein. Kisi dalal ya agent ko “pakki bharti” ke naam par paisa na dein.

Sainik parampara ka sandarbh: Kumaon Regiment aur Kumaon ka fauji itihas Almora: Panwar & Chand Kingdoms aur Almora: Hill Station aur Weekend par Ghumne ki Jagah me hai.

Almora: Rojgar Karyalay aur Rojgar Mele

Zila Rojgar Karyalaya aur Sewayojan Portal

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand ka Sewayojan Vibhag rojgar panjikaran aur career mela aayojit karta hai. Panjikaran ke liye rajya ke Sewayojan portal par jaayein (E-District par rozgar panjikaran ki seva ka zikr bhi hai, Almora mein E-District / Apuni Sarkar Sevayein).

Panjikaran ka aam tareeka: 1. Official portal par sign up karein. 2. Shaikshik yogyata, pehchaan aur nivas ke dastavez upload karein. 3. Rozgar karyalaya se rozgar mela ya vacancy ki jaankari prapt karein. 4. Panjikaran ko samay-samay par naveekaran karein (agar niyam ho).

Career mela: Zila prashasan aur Sewayojan Vibhag samay-samay par rozgar mela lagate hain. Taareekh Almora ke DM karyalay ya zila website se dekhein.

Bahut zaroori: Rozgar ke naam par advance paisa maangne wali fake job offer se bachein. Sarkari naukri ke liye koi dalal nahi hota.
