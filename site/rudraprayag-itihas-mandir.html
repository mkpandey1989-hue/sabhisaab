Rudraprayag: Pauranik Bhugol: Kedarkhand se Rishta

Kedarkhand aur Puran

Skanda Puran ke Kedarkhand hisse me Garhwal Himalaya ka dharmik varnan milta hai. Rudraprayag zila isi Kedarkhand ka dil hai, kyunki yahin Kedarnath, Tungnath aur Madhmaheshwar jaise tirth hain.

Mahabharat ki kathao me Pandavon ke svargarohan (Mahaprasthan) ka marg Himalaya ki oor bataya jata hai. Kedarnath se juda sabse prasiddh prasang yeh hai ki Pandav apne pap ke prayashchit ke liye Shiv ji ko dhoondhne Himalaya aaye, aur Shiv ji ne bail ka roop dharan kar liya.

Mahakavi Kalidas ke naam ko bhi is kshetra ke saath Kalimath jaise sthan ke karan jodkar dekha jata hai. Is par vidwanon me alag-alag mat hain, isliye ise paramparik manyata ke roop me hi likhna theek hai.

Rudraprayag: Pramukh Mandir aur Teerth

Kedarnath Dham

Kedarnath Dham Rudraprayag zile ka sabse bada tirth hai. Yeh 12 Jyotirlingon aur Panch Kedar me se ek hai aur lagbhag 3,583 meter ki unchai par Mandakini ke kinare sthit hai. Kapat aam taur par April-May me khulte hain aur Oct-Nov me bandh hote hain.

Mandir patthar ke bade-bade silabon se bana hai. Parampara ke anusar Adi Shankaracharya ne 8vi shatabdi me is kshetra ko punah sthapit kiya aur mandir ke peeche unki samadhi hai. Mukhya pujari Karnataka ke Veerashaiva parampara ke Rawal hote hain.

2013 ki aapda me mandir ke peeche ek badi shila (jise ab Bhim Shila kehte hain) ruk gayi thi aur mandir ke mukhya dhancha ko bachane ka kaaran maani gayi. Iske baad punarnirman kaam kiya gaya.

Panch Kedar me se Tungnath aur Madhmaheshwar

Panch Kedar me se teen Rudraprayag zile me hain: Kedarnath (kuba), Tungnath (bhuja) aur Madhmaheshwar (nabhi). Baaki do, Rudranath (mukh) aur Kalpeshwar (jata), Chamoli zile me hain.

Rudraprayag: Gram Devta, Ishta Devta aur Jagar

Gram Devta aur Jakh

Garhwal ke har gaon ka ek gram devta hota hai. Rudraprayag zile me Jakh devta, Nagraja, Bhairav, Ghantakarna aur Narsingh jaise devtaon ki puja hoti hai.

Jagar aur Dangariya

Jagar me Jagariya dhol-damau ya hurke ke saath devta ki gatha gaata hai, aur Dangariya (madhyam) par devta avtarit hone ki manyata hai. Yeh Uttarakhand ki sabse purani adhyatmik parampara hai.

Jagar kisi sankat, bimari ya shubh avsar par karwaya jata hai. Isme raat bhar gaan chalta hai.

Rudraprayag: Pre-History aur Purani Basti

Jankari ki Sthiti

Garhwal Himalaya me pre-historic basti ke chinh milte hain, jaise rock shelter aur chattanon par chitra. Rudraprayag zile me is par abhi shodh kam hua hai. Ise honest tarike se likhna zaroori hai ki exact sthal aur pramaan ke liye Archaeological Survey of India aur HNB Garhwal University ke shodh ko dekhein.

Rudraprayag: Kuninda aur Katyuri Kaal

Kuninda aur Katyuri

Kuninda rajya ke chinh Himalaya ke is kshetra me Mauryan ke baad ke kaal me milte hain. Katyuri rajao ne lagbhag 7vi se 11vi shatabdi ke beech Kumaon aur Garhwal par shasan kiya aur kai mandir banwaye.

Katyuri shaili ke patthar ke mandir Garhwal-Kumaon me aaj bhi dikhte hain. Joshimath ko Katyuri rajao ki purani rajdhani maana jata hai.

Rudraprayag: Panwar (Garhwal) Rajvansh aur Garh

Garh aur Panwar Shasan

Garhwal me purane samay me 52 chhote-chhote garh (kile) the. Baad me Panwar vansh ke raja Ajay Pal ne in sabko ek kar ke Garhwal rajya bana diya aur uski rajdhani baad me Srinagar bani.

Rudraprayag kshetra Panwar rajaon ke Garhwal rajya ka hissa raha. Mandiron ko "goonth" ke roop me zameen di jati thi, jisse mandir ka kharcha chalta tha.

Rudraprayag: Gorkha Shasan

Gorkhyani

Garhwal par Gorkha shasan lagbhag 1804 se 1815 tak raha. Gorkha shasan ko pahad me "Gorkhyani" kehte hain aur ise kadi kar-vyavastha aur atyachar ke liye yaad kiya jata hai. 1815 ki Sugauli ki sandhi ke baad Angrezon ne Gorkhon ko haraya.

Gorkha shasan se judi kai kahaniyan Garhwal ke gaon me aaj bhi sunai jati hain.

Rudraprayag: Angrez Kaal aur Tehri Riyasat

Angrezi Garhwal

1815 ke baad Garhwal do hisson me bat gaya: British Garhwal (Alaknanda ke purv ka hissa) aur Tehri Riyasat (pashchim ka hissa). Rudraprayag zile ke kuch hisse British Garhwal me aur kuch hisse Tehri riyasat me aate the.

Angrez prashasan me Dak Bungalow, paidal pul, bridle path aur forest rules bane. Is samay ki kai imaratein aaj bhi Garhwal me dekhi ja sakti hain.

Rudraprayag: Swatantrata Andolan, Chipko aur Rajya Andolan

Chipko aur Uttarakhand Andolan

Chipko Andolan 1970 ke dashak me Garhwal me shuru hua tha, jisme gaon ke log ped kaatne se rokne ke liye ped se lipat jaate the. Chipko ke shuruaati charan me Rudraprayag zile ke Phata-Rampur kshetra ka bhi zikr milta hai.

Uttarakhand rajya andolan 1994 me tez hua aur 9 November 2000 ko Uttarakhand alag rajya bana. Rudraprayag zile ke logon ne bhi is andolan me hissa liya.

Rudraprayag: Lok-Katha aur Rahasyamayi Kisse

Pramukh Kisse

Rudraprayag ki sabse prasiddh kahaniyan hain: Narad Muni ki sangeet sadhna, Shiv ka bail roop me chhipna aur Pandavon ka Kedar tak aana, Triyuginarayan ka vivah aur Jim Corbett ke dwara aadmkhor tendue ka shikar.

Pahadon me Achhari (van-pariyan) ki kahaniyan bhi prasiddh hain, jiske anusar unchai wale bugyal me lal ya chamakdar kapde pehnne par pariyan aakarshit ho sakti hain. Yeh kahaniyan bachon ko shor machane aur akele jungle me jane se rokne ke kaam aati thi.

Kedarnath ki kahani kya hai?
Mahabharat ke baad Pandav Shiv ji ko dhoondhte Himalaya aaye. Shiv ji bail ke roop me chhip gaye. Bhim ne use pakadna chaha to bail zameen me sama gaya. Uska pishtha-bhag Kedarnath me, bhuja Tungnath me, mukh Rudranath me, nabhi Madhmaheshwar me aur jata Kalpeshwar me prakat hui.

Rudraprayag: Vistaar se: Itihas ki Gehri Jankari

Rudraprayag ka itihas Garhwal ke itihas se alag nahi hai. Yahan ke tirth, gaon aur raaste sadiyon ke rajvanshon, hamlon aur badlavon ke gawah rahe hain. Is hisse me hamne Garhwal ke itihas ki pakki taareekhein aur ghatnayein likhi hain, aur batayi hain ki unka Rudraprayag se kya rishta hai. Jahan alag-alag sources me taareekh me antar mila hai, wahan hamne use khula likha hai.

Gram Devta aur Jagar (Vistaar)

Jagar Kya Hai

Jagar Garhwal ki ek paramparik gayan aur anushthan paddhati hai, jisme Jagri (gaayak) Hurka ya Dhol-Damau ke saath devta ya purvajon ki kathayein gaata hai, aur Dangariya (jisme devta avatarit mana jata hai) nritya karta hai. Yeh aastha ka maamla hai aur ghati-ghati me alag roop me hota hai.

Jagar ke mukhya patra:

  • Jagri: woh gayak jo kathayein gaata hai.
  • Dangariya: woh vyakti jis par devta ka avatarit hona maana jata hai.
  • Hurka aur Dhol-Damau: jagar ke vadya.
  • Parivar ya gaon: jo jagar karwata hai, aksar sankat, manauti ya kul-devta ki puja ke liye.

Jagar ek jeevit lok-parampara hai. Is bare me aastha rakhne wale gaon ke jaankar hi sahi marg dikhate hain. Bimari ya sankat me aastha ke saath-saath doctor ki salah bhi zaroor lein (Rudraprayag: Puchhyari, Gantua aur Deva-Prashna Parampara).

64 se 65: Puraani Basti, Kuninda aur Katyuri (Vistaar)

Garhwal ke Pracheen Rajvansh

Garhwal ke prachin itihas me Kuninda aur Katyuri rajvanshon ka zikr aata hai. Kuninda kaal ke sikke Garhwal-Kumaon ke ilake me mile maane jate hain, aur Katyuri kaal ke tamrapatra Chamoli ke Pandukeshwar se mile hain (Rudraprayag: Purane Tamrapatra, Shila-Lekh aur Bhasha ka Vikas).

Jo hum jaante hain:

  • Garhwal aur Kumaon ke prachin itihas me Kuninda, phir Katyuri aur uske baad Panwar rajvansh aate hain.
  • Katyuri kaal ke tamrapatra Pandukeshwar (Chamoli) me mile hain, jo Sanskrit me hain.
  • Prachin kaal me Kedarnath aur Badrinath ke tirth ka mahatva dharmik granthon me milta hai.

Adi Shankaracharya ko parampara me Kedarnath ke punah sthapna se joda jata hai, aur Kedarnath me unki samadhi maani jati hai. November 2021 me Kedarnath me Adi Shankaracharya ki murti ka anavaran hua tha.

Prachin itihas ke liye aap Archaeological Survey of India (Dehradun Circle) aur HNB Garhwal University ke itihas vibhag ke record dekh sakte hain. Agar aapko zile me kisi purane sthal ki pakki jankari ho, to bhejiye.

Panwar Rajvansh aur Garh (Vistaar)

Garhwal Rajya ka Banna

Parampara ke anusar Garhwal rajya ki sthapana Kanak Pal ne Chandpur Garhi se ki. Baad me Raja Ajay Pal ne Garhwal ke 52 chhote garhon ko ek saath jodkar ek rajya banaya. Rajdhani Chandpur Garhi se Devalgarh aur phir Srinagar (Garhwal) gayi.

Garhwal rajya ke itihas ki mukhya baatein:

Alag alag sources me taareekhon me antar milta hai. Kuch sources me Kanak Pal ka kaal 823 aur kuch me 888 baataya gaya hai. Ajay Pal ke samay ke liye bhi alag-alag shatabdi di jati hain. Srinagar ko rajdhani banane ki taareekh 1506, 1517 ya 1519 baatayi jati hai. Itihas ki kitabon me in antar ka vistrit zikr hai (Rudraprayag: Zile par Likhi Gayi Mukhya Kitabein aur Shodh).

Garhwal ka naam "garh" yaani kile se aaya hai. Garhon me se kai ab khandhar hain, aur kai gaon ke naam aaj bhi unse jude hain. Rudraprayag kshetra ke Nagpur ilake ko bhi in garhon ki parampara se joda jata hai (Rudraprayag: Prachin Garh, Kot, Baoli aur Khandhar).

Gorkha Shasan (Vistaar)

Gorkhyani aur Uska Prabhav

Gorkhon ne 1803 me Kumaon aur Garhwal par hamla kiya aur lagbhag 12 saal Garhwal par shasan kiya. Is daur ko Garhwal me "Gorkhyani" kaha jata hai, aur yeh shabd katl-e-aam aur lootmaar ke liye prayog hota hai.

Garhwal ke raja Pradyumna Shah 1804 ke aas-paas Gorkhon se ladte hue maare gaye, aur unka beta Sudarshan Shah angrez sanrakshan me raha. Gorkha shasan 1815 me tab khatam hua jab Anglo-Nepal yuddh me angrezon ne Gorkhon ko haraya.

Gorkha shasan ke daur ko log aaj bhi dukh se yaad karte hain. Kai kahawaton aur kahaniyon me iska zikr milta hai. Is daur me log bade paimane par gaon chhodkar bhaage the, aur kuch ko dass bana liya gaya tha.

Angrez Kaal aur Tehri Riyasat (Vistaar)

Rudraprayag Kahan Aata Tha

Treaty of Sugauli ke baad Garhwal do hisson me bata. Alaknanda aur Mandakini ke purv ka hissa angrezon ke paas gaya aur British Garhwal kehlaya. Pashchim ka hissa Raja Sudarshan Shah ko mila aur wahan Tehri riyasat bani.

Yeh baat Rudraprayag ke liye khaas mahatva rakhti hai. Rudraprayag Alaknanda aur Mandakini ka sangam hai. Zile ke jo hisse baad me Chamoli aur Pauri Garhwal se aaye (jaise Ukhimath, Augustmuni aur Khirsu block ka hissa), woh dono purane zile British Garhwal ka hissa the. Jakholi aur Kirtinagar block ka jo hissa Tehri se aaya, woh Tehri riyasat ke antargat tha (Rudraprayag: Zile ki Purani Pattiyan aur Parganas).

Angrez kaal me Rudraprayag ka sangam yatri marg ka ek padav tha, aur isi daur me adamkhor tendue ki ghatna hui (Rudraprayag: Purane Vyapar Marg aur Jhula Pul). Isliye zile ke itihas me angrez kaal ek mahatvapurn adhyay hai, jisme prashasan, sadak aur yatra vyavastha me kai badlav aaye.

Tehri aur British Garhwal ke is purane bantware ka asar aaj bhi zile ki boli aur sanskriti me thoda dikhta hai.

Swatantrata Andolan, Chipko aur Rajya Andolan (Vistaar)

Jan Andolan ki Parampara

Uttarakhand ka rajya andolan 1994 me tez hua aur 9 November 2000 ko Uttarakhand (tab Uttaranchal) alag rajya bana. Chipko andolan 1970 ke dashak me Chamoli zile se shuru hua aur Garhwal ke jangalon ko bachane ki ek prasiddh mahila-pradhan muhim bani.

Chipko andolan ki shuruat Chamoli zile me hui thi, Rudraprayag zile me nahi. Par Garhwal ke jangal aur gaon ki mahilayein is andolan ka bada hissa thi, aur uski prerna poore Garhwal me phaili. Rajya andolan me bhi poore pahad ki mahilayein aur yuva aage rahe.

Lok-Katha aur Rahasyamayi Kisse (Vistaar)

Paramparik Kathayein

Rudraprayag ki lok-kathayein mandiron aur prakriti se jude hain. Triyuginarayan ki katha me Shiv-Parvati ke vivah ki manyata hai, Kedarnath ki katha me Pandavon aur Shiv ke bail roop ka prasang hai, aur Kalimath ko Mahakavi Kalidas se joda jata hai.

Ye kathayein dharmik manyata aur lok-parampara ka hissa hain. Inme se kisi ko itihas ka pakka tathya samajhna zaroori nahi hai, par yeh jaankari ke liye sanskriti ka hissa hain.

  • Triyuginarayan: manyata hai ki yahan Shiv aur Parvati ka vivah hua tha, aur us vivah ki agni abhi tak jalti hai (Rudraprayag: Pramukh Mandir aur Teerth).
  • Kedarnath: manyata hai ki Pandav Shiv ko dhoondhte hue aaye, aur Shiv ne bail ka roop dharan kar liya (Rudraprayag: Pauranik Bhugol: Kedarkhand se Rishta).
  • Madhmaheshwar: manyata hai ki bail ka madhya bhag (nabhi) yahan prakat hua.
  • Kalimath: parampara me kavi Kalidas ko yahan se joda jata hai, par vidwanon me is par mat bhed hai.
  • Rudraprayag: manyata hai ki Narad muni ne yahan Shiv ki aradhana ki thi.

Aisi kathayein ek peedhi se doosri peedhi tak gaaye aur sunaye jate hain. Aap apne ghar ke buzurgon se sunkar inhe likh sakte hain, taaki yeh bachi rahein.
