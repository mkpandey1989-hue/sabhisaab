Carpet, built-up, super built-up — yahi asli khel hai

Ek hi flat ke teen alag area hote hain. Aur builder aksar sabse bada waala bolta hai:

  • Carpet area — jitne farsh par aap chal sakte hain. Kamre, kitchen, bathroom. Yahi asli jagah hai.
  • Built-up area — carpet + deewaron ki motai + balcony. Carpet se lagbhag 10-15% zyada.
  • Super built-up area — built-up + seedhi, lift, lobby, club, garden ka aapka hissa. Carpet se 25-40% tak zyada.
Misaal: "1,200 sq ft ka flat, ₹5,000 per sq ft" = ₹60 lakh. Par agar wo 1,200 super built-up hai, to carpet shayad 800-850 hi hoga. Matlab jis jagah me aap sach me rahenge uska asli rate ₹7,000+ per sq ft baith raha hai.

Isi liye hamesha carpet area par rate poochiye. Do builders ki tulna karni ho to dono ka carpet rate nikaal kar tulna kariye — super built-up par tulna karna bekaar hai, kyunki har builder apna hisaab alag lagata hai.

RERA ke aane ke baad builder ko carpet area batana hota hai. Agreement me dhoondhiye — wo likha hoga.

Kul keemat me aur kya-kya judta hai

Jo number upar nikla wo sirf shuruaat hai. Registry tak pahunchte-pahunchte aur bhi lagta hai:

  • Stamp duty aur registration — rajya ke hisaab se, aam taur par keemat ka 5-8%
  • GST — under-construction property par lagta hai; ready-to-move (completion certificate wale) par nahi
  • Parking — aksar alag se, lakh rupaye tak
  • Maintenance advance — 1-2 saal ka ek saath maanga jaata hai
  • Club aur society membership — one-time
  • Bijli-paani ka connection
  • Home loan ki processing fee aur legal charges
Mota anuman: flat ki keemat ke upar 10-15% aur jodiye. ₹60 lakh ka flat asal me ₹66-69 lakh me padta hai. Budget banate waqt yahi number rakhiye, warna aakhir me paisa kam pad jaata hai — aur wo sabse buri jagah hai.

Stamp duty ka hisaab Stamp Duty Calculator me lagaiye — aur dhyaan rakhiye ki wo circle rate par lagti hai, aapke sauda par nahi (jo bhi zyada ho).

Rate sahi hai ya zyada — kaise pata karein

Dealer ka bataya rate bazaar ka rate ho ye zaroori nahi. Teen jagah se cross-check kariye:

  • Sub-registrar office ka circle rate — ye sarkari kam se kam dar hai. Bazaar bhaav aam taur par isse upar hota hai, par bahut upar ho to sawaal poochiye.
  • Aas-paas hui registry — pichle 6 mahine me usi ilaake me kis rate par sauda hua. Ye sabse pakka number hai.
  • Kiraya se jaanch — us jaisi property ka saalana kiraya nikaaliye aur keemat se bhaag dijiye. Agar 2% se kaafi neeche aa raha hai, to property mehngi hai. Iska poora hisaab Rental Yield Calculator me hai.

Aur ye baatein rate ko sach me badalti hain: floor (upar mehnga ya sasta, sheher par nirbhar), corner flat, dhoop ka rukh, lift se doori, sadak ka saamna, aur sabse zyada — kab tak ban kar milega. Under-construction sasta milta hai par risk zyada hota hai.

Kharidne se pehle kaagaz — ye kabhi mat chhodiye

Sabse mehnga sauda aur sabse kam jaanch — property me yahi hota hai. Kam se kam ye dekhiye:

  • Title clear hai ya nahi — zameen sach me bechne wale ki hai?
  • Khatauni/khasra ya society ka record — naam kis par chadha hai
  • Loan ya kisi bank ka bojh to nahi (encumbrance certificate)
  • Naksha paas hai ya nahi — bina paas naksha wali building me loan bhi mushkil
  • RERA number — under-construction ke liye zaroori
  • Completion aur occupancy certificate — ready flat ke liye
Ek vakeel se kaagaz jaanch karwana ₹5,000-15,000 ka kaam hai. Ek galat sauda 50 lakh ka. Ye kharcha kabhi mat bachaiye — property ke maamle me sabse bada nuksan yahin hota hai, aur wo wapas nahi aata.

Poori checklist zameen kharidne se pehle kaunse kaagaz me hai. Zameen ka rakba badalna ho to Hectare-Bigha-Acre Converter aur pahad me Nali-Mutthi Converter dekhiye.

Rs 50 lakh ke flat par asli kharcha kitna

Vigyapan me sirf base price hota hai. Poora hisaab:

KyaLagbhag
Base priceRs 50,00,000
Stamp duty aur registryRs 3,00,000 – 4,00,000
GST (sirf under-construction par)0 ya 1-5%
Parking, club, bijli-paani connectionRs 1,50,000 – 3,00,000
Brokerage (1-2%)Rs 50,000 – 1,00,000
Interior aur zaroori saamanRs 2,00,000+
Asli kharchaRs 57-60 lakh
Yahi wajah hai ki loan manzoor hone ke baad bhi paisa kam pad jaata hai. Bank base price ka 75-80% deta hai — stamp duty, registry aur interior par loan nahi milta, wo poora jeb se jaata hai. Isliye Rs 50 lakh ke flat ke liye lagbhag Rs 17-20 lakh apne paas hone chahiye. Ready flat me GST nahi lagta, under-construction me lagta hai — ye ek antar hi lakhon ka hota hai. Sauda pakka karne se pehle builder se ek "all-inclusive" likhit hisaab maang lijiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Carpet aur super built-up area me kitna farak hota hai?

Super built-up aam taur par carpet se 25-40% zyada hota hai. Isliye hamesha carpet area par rate poochiye — wahi asli jagah hai jahan aap rahenge.

Flat ki keemat ke upar aur kitna lagta hai?

Mota anuman 10-15% — stamp duty, registration, GST (under-construction par), parking, maintenance advance aur loan ke charges milakar.

Rate sahi hai ya nahi, kaise pata karein?

Sub-registrar ka circle rate dekhiye, aas-paas pichle 6 mahine me hui registry ka rate pata kariye, aur kiraya se yield nikaal kar jaanchiye.

Under-construction sasta hai, lena chahiye?

Sasta hota hai par risk zyada — der, adhoora project, ya badla hua naksha. RERA number, builder ka purana record aur poore kaagaz dekhe bina mat lijiye.
