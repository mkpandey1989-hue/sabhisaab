Is hisse mein: Manaskhand aur Puran se judi pehchan, prasiddh mandir, ishta devta aur gram devta, prachin itihas, Katyuri-Chand-Gorkha-Angrez kaal, azadi ka sangharsh aur lok-kathayein. Itihas aur dharm se jude aankde aur tithiyan alag-alag kitabon me alag milte hain. Jahan matbhed hai, wahan humne “maana jata hai” ya likha hai, taaki aap kisi ek mat ko hi sach na samjhein.

Pithoragarh: Puranon ka Bhugol: Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand

Manaskhand aur Kumaon ka Sthan

Puranon ke anusar Himalaya ke is hisse me Kumaon ko “Manaskhand” aur Garhwal ko “Kedarkhand” kaha jata hai, aur Pithoragarh Manaskhand kshetra ka hissa mana jata hai. Manaskhand ka naam Kailash-Mansarovar ke kshetra se jod kar bataya jata hai.

Pithoragarh ka Kailash se rishta: Kailash Mansarovar Yatra ka Bharat ki taraf se ek mukhya raasta Pithoragarh zile se Lipulekh darra hokar jata hai, aur Dharchula, Tawaghat, Gunji, Kalapani jaise padav isi marg me aate hain.

Pithoragarh: Pramukh Mandir, Dham, Gurudwara aur Math

Pithoragarh ke Pramukh Mandir aur Dharmik Sthal

Pithoragarh zile ke sabse prasiddh dharmik sthal Hat Kalika (Gangolihat), Patal Bhuvaneshwar, Thal Kedar, Mostamanu, Chhipla Kedar, Narayan Ashram aur Adi Kailash-Om Parvat kshetra hain. (Har sthal ki unchai, dooriyan, samay aur sthiti, yatra se pehle zaroor pushti karein.)

Dhyan dein: Adi Kailash aur Om Parvat jaisi jagah seema ke bahut paas hain, aur wahan jane ke liye Inner Line Permit, health check aur acclimatization zaroori hai (Pithoragarh: Kaise pahunchein, kahan rukein, Pithoragarh: Inner Line Permit , Van Anumati aur Suraksha Niyam).

Kailash Mansarovar Yatra 2026: Taza Jaankari (News ke Anusar)

Kailash Mansarovar Yatra ka Lipulekh marg Pithoragarh se hokar jata hai, aur 2026 ki yatra ke liye 10 batch (har batch me lagbhag 50 yatri) ka plan bataya gaya hai. Kumaon Mandal Vikas Nigam (KMVN) is marg ki nodal agency hai, aur Dharchula me uska base camp hai.

2026 ki news reports me kya aaya:

  • Yatra Videsh Mantralay (MEA) ke tahat hoti hai, aur yatri computerised draw se chune jate hain.
  • Lipulekh marg se 10 batch aur Sikkim ke Nathu La marg se bhi 10 batch ka plan bataya gaya tha.
  • Lipulekh se pehla batch July ke shuru me Tanakpur se rawana hua aur kuch din baad Lipulekh darra paar kar Tibet pahuncha.
  • Yatra ka kul shulk (fee) news me lagbhag Rs 2.09 lakh bataya gaya, jo 20% badha hua kaha gaya. (Fee har saal badal sakti hai; official MEA ya KMVN site se pushti karein.)
  • MEA ka kahna hai ki Lipulekh marg 1954 se Kailash yatra ka lamba marg raha hai.

Seema vishay ka dhyan: Lipulekh kshetra ko lekar Nepal ke alag daave rahe hain.

Aam aadmi ke liye: Kailash Mansarovar Yatra ke liye aavedan aur chayan sarkari prakriya se hota hai. Koi bhi agent ya website se “pakka seat” ke naam par paisa dene se pehle MEA ki official website (kmy.gov.in) se jaankari mila lein.

Pithoragarh: Isht Devta, Gram Devta aur Jagar mein Devta Avtaran

Golu Devta, Hari Jyu, Bhumiya, Nag Devta aur Anya

Kumaon me kai lok-devta puje jate hain, jinme Golu Devta (nyay ke devta), Bhumiya (kheti aur bhoomi ke devta), Nag Devta aur Hat Kalika prasiddh hain. Pithoragarh me bhi har gaon ka apna gram devta aur kul devta hota hai, jiski puja parivar ya gaon milkar karta hai.

DevtaKis roop me puje jate hain
Golu DevtaNyay ke devta; kai jagah manokamna ke liye arzi bhi lagayi jati hai
Bhumiya (Bhumyal)Gaon aur kheti ki raksha ke devta
Nag DevtaNag roop me puje jate hain, Berinag aur aas-paas prasiddh
Hat Kalika (Kali)Shakti ka roop, Gangolihat me mukhya mandir

Jagar: Jagar me dhol-damau aur geet ke zariye devta ya purvajon ko “jagaya” jata hai aur wo kisi ke andar aate hain, aisa mana jata hai. Sirf paramparik jaankari de rahe hain. (Jagar ki riti gaon aur jagariye ke hisab se alag hoti hai.)

Pithoragarh: Pragaitihasik Kaal, Shila-Chitra aur Shailashray

Prachin Avshesh

Kumaon aur Uttarakhand ke kai hisson me pre-historic chitra, chattaani ashray aur cup-marks (pathar par pyaale jaise gadde) mile hain, aur Pithoragarh bhi Kumaon ke prachin itihas ka hissa raha hai.

Kyun mahatvapurn hai: Prachin avshesh batate hain ki Himalaya me insaan hazaron saal se rehta aaya hai, aur purane vyapar marg Tibet se judte the.

Pithoragarh: Kuninda aur Katyuri Raajvansh ka Daur

Kuninda aur Katyuri

Kumaon ke prachin itihas me Kuninda (lagbhag 2nd century BCE se kuch shatabdi tak) aur uske baad Katyuri raja ka naam aata hai, jinki rajdhani Baijnath (Kartikeyapura) ke aas-paas mani jati hai. Katyuri shasan ka vistar Kumaon aur Garhwal dono me bataya jata hai. (Samay aur seema ke baare me itihaskaron me matbhed hai.)

Askot ke Pal raja: Pithoragarh zile ke Askot kshetra ke Pal shasak khud ko Katyuri vansh se jodte aaye hain, aur Askot rajya ka apna ek lamba itihas raha hai. (Is vansh aur uski sthiti ke baare me sthaniya itihas ki kitabon se pushti karein.)

Pithoragarh: Panwar (Garhwal) & Chand (Kumaon) Kingdoms

Chand Shasan aur Pithoragarh

Katyuri ke baad Kumaon par Chand vansh ka shasan ubhra, jinki rajdhani pehle Champawat aur baad me Almora rahi, aur Pithoragarh ka ilaka bhi Chand rajya ke prabhav me raha. Pithoragarh ke aas-paas Chand kaal ke kai mandir aur qile ke avshesh mane jate hain. (Kaun sa qila ya mandir kis raja ne banwaya, iske liye sthaniya itihas ki kitabon aur ASI report se pushti karein.)

Doti aur Nepal ka sambandh: Kali nadi ke us paar ka kshetra (Doti) aur is paar ka Pithoragarh-Kumaon ka ilaka itihas me kai baar ek doosre se jude aur bhid chuke hain. Is par Pithoragarh: Patti, gaon aur bazaar me “Parganas aur Pattiyon” me vistar hoga.

Pithoragarh: Gorkha Raaj (Gorkhyani) aur Angrez-Nepal Yuddh

Gorkha Shasan aur Sugauli Sandhi

Lagbhag 1790 ke aas-paas Gorkhaon ne Kumaon par adhikar kiya, aur unka shasan 1815 tak chala, jab Angrez-Nepal yuddh ke baad Sugauli sandhi ho gayi. Is dauran kar bhaari the aur logon ke liye “Gorkhyani” ka samay kathin mana jata hai. (Exact saal aur ghatnayein itihas ki kitabon se pushti karein.)

Pithoragarh ka qila: Pithoragarh shehar ke upar bana purana qila (Pithoragarh Fort) Gorkha shasan ke dauran banaya gaya mana jata hai. (Ye maanyata hai; iska nirman saal aur adhikari sthiti ke liye ASI ya sthaniya itihas se pushti karein.)

Pithoragarh: British Garhwal / Kumaon Commissionery & Tehri Riyasat

Angrezi Shasan

1815 ke baad Kumaon angrezon ke adhin aaya, aur Pithoragarh bhi Kumaon Commissionery ka hissa bana. Angrezon ne Kumaon me zameen ka bandobast (settlement) kiya, jungle ke niyam banaye aur sadkein-pul banwaye. (Bandobast ke saal aur adhikari naam, jaise Traill aur Batten, kitabon se pushti karein.)

Angrezi kaal ki nishaniyan: Purane dak bungalow, forest rest house, church aur sarkari imaratein kai kasbon me aaj bhi mili jati hain. Is par Pithoragarh: Patti, gaon aur bazaar (Pithoragarh: Dak Bungalow, Van Vishram Grih aur Angrezi Daur ki…) me alag se hai.

Pithoragarh: Azaadi ki Ladai, Kuli Begar aur Rajya Andolan

Azadi ka Sangharsh aur Uttarakhand Andolan

Kumaon me azadi ke andolan me Coolie Begar Andolan (1921, Bageshwar kshetra me Uttarayani ke mele par) ek bada mod mana jata hai, aur iska asar Pithoragarh jaise ilakon me bhi dikha.

Rajya andolan: Alag Uttarakhand rajya ke liye 1990 ke dashak me hua andolan Pithoragarh me bhi chala. 9 November 2000 ko Uttarakhand alag rajya bana. (Pithoragarh ke andolankariyon ke naam aur ghatnayein pushti karein karke hi likhein.)

Pithoragarh: Lok-Kathayein: Achhari, Veer Yoddha aur Rahasyamay Kisse

Lok-Kathayein

Pahad ki lok-kathayein pahar, jungle aur bugyal se judi hain: Achhari (pariyan jo ooncha bugyal me rehti hain), jungle ke devta aur veer yoddhaon ki kahaniyan har gaon me suni jati hain.

Aam kathayein (lok-manyata ke roop me):

  • Achhari: Mana jata hai ki ooncha bugyal me pariyan rehti hain jo chamkile kapde pehne logon ko apni taraf kheench leti hain.
  • Jangal ke devta: Kai jungle me jaane se pehle devta ko pranam karne ki parampara hai.
  • Panchachuli ki kahani: Pandavon ke paanch chulhe wali kahani (Pithoragarh: Bhugol, nadiyan aur pahad, Pithoragarh: Pramukh Himalayi Chotiyan aur Parvat Shrinkhla me).
  • Kali nadi ki kahani: Kali ke udgam aur Kalapani ke Kali Mata mandir se judi kathayein.

Ye sab lok-manyatayein hain, itihas nahi.
