Sariya Weight Calculator — Ek Sariya Kitne Kilo Ki, Kitne Ka

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: October 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Sariya dukaan par kilo ke bhav bikti hai, par site par ginti piece me hoti hai. Isi do hisaab ke beech sabse zyada galti aur nuksan hota hai.

Seedha jawab: sariya ka wazan (kg per meter) = motaai (mm) × motaai ÷ 162. Jaise 12 mm sariya = 12 × 12 ÷ 162 = 0.889 kg per meter. 12 meter ki ek poori sariya lagbhag 10.67 kg ki hoti hai.

Neeche sariya ki motaai, lambai, ginti aur kilo ka bhav daaliye. Kul wazan, daam aur ek ton me kitni sariya aati hai — sab dikh jaayega.

Ye table site par sabse zyada kaam aati hai. Supervisor ko ginti chahiye, dukaandaar ko kilo. Dono ko ek hi kaagaz par mila lijiye.

D²/162 formula kahan se aaya

Steel ka ghanatva lagbhag 7,850 kg per ghan meter hai. Sariya ek gol chhad hai, to uska wazan = ghanatva × gol hisse ka kshetrafal × lambai.

7,850 × (3.1416 ÷ 4) × D² ÷ 10,00,000 = D² ÷ 162.2. Site par aasaani ke liye isse D² ÷ 162 likhte hain. Isliye ye formula koi andaza nahi, seedha steel ke ghanatva se nikla hai — aur yaad rakhna aasaan hai.

Misaal: 16 mm sariya ka wazan = 16 × 16 ÷ 162 = 1.58 kg per meter. 12 meter ki sariya = 18.96 kg.

Wazan ki chhoot — jahan chupka nuksan hota hai

TMT sariya ka standard IS 1786 hai. Ismein maana gaya hai ki sariya ka asli wazan kaagzi wazan se thoda kam ya zyada ho sakta hai.

Yahan asli khel hai. Agar aap kilo ke bhav kharid rahe hain par site par piece se gin rahe hain, to halki sariya ka nuksan aapka hai. 100 sariya 12 mm ki, har ek 5% halki — to lagbhag 53 kg kam steel, jiska paisa aapne diya. Isliye tol ki parchi par piece ki ginti bhi likhwaiye, aur kuch sariya alag se tol kar dekhiye.

Ghar me kitni sariya lagegi — mota andaza

Asli matra structural engineer ke design aur drawing se hi nikalti hai. Phir bhi budget ke liye ek mota andaza kaam aata hai.

  • Aam RCC ghar me lagbhag 3.5 se 4.5 kg sariya per square foot (built-up area) maani jaati hai.
  • 1,000 sq ft ke ek manzil ke ghar par lagbhag 3.5 se 4.5 ton.
  • Pahad ya bhookamp zone me column aur beam bhaari hote hain, isliye ye andaza upar ki taraf rakhiye.

Ye sirf kharche ka andaza hai, design nahi. Sariya kam karke paisa bachaana ghar ki mazbooti se samjhauta hai. Cement, ret aur bajri ka hisaab Cement Sand Bajri Calculator me, aur eent ka Eent Calculator me dekh lijiye.

Kharidte waqt 5 baatein

  1. Sariya par company ka naam aur grade (jaise Fe 500 ya Fe 500D) ubhra hona chahiye.
  2. Har lot ka test certificate maangiye.
  3. Kilo ke bhav le rahe hain to tol ki parchi aur piece ki ginti dono likhwaiye.
  4. Jang (rust) ki halki parat aam hai, par parat utarne lage to wo sariya mat lijiye.
  5. Bhav me loading, katai aur bhaada juda hai ya nahi, pehle poochhiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

12 mm sariya ka wazan kitna hota hai?

12 mm sariya ka wazan 0.889 kg per meter hai. 12 meter ki ek poori sariya lagbhag 10.67 kg ki hoti hai, aur ek ton me lagbhag 94 sariya aati hain.

Sariya ka wazan nikalne ka formula kya hai?

Wazan (kg per meter) = motaai (mm) × motaai ÷ 162. Ise lambai (meter) se guna karne par ek sariya ka wazan aa jaata hai.

1 ton me 8 mm ki kitni sariya aati hai?

12 meter wali 8 mm sariya lagbhag 4.74 kg ki hoti hai, isliye ek ton me lagbhag 211 sariya aati hain.

Sariya ka wazan kam kyun nikalta hai?

IS 1786 me wazan me chhoot hai — 10 mm tak ±7%, 16 mm tak ±5% aur usse upar ±3%. Isliye asli wazan kaagzi wazan se thoda alag hota hai.

Ghar me kitni sariya lagti hai?

Mota andaza 3.5 se 4.5 kg per square foot built-up area hai. Asli matra structural engineer ke design se hi tay hoti hai.
