Bachche Ki Padhai Ka Fund Kaise Banayein?

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
Chhota jawabSeedha jawab: bachche ki padhai ka kharcha 15-18 saal me mehngai se kai guna badh jaata hai. Isliye jaldi shuru karein aur SIP jaise badhne wale vikalp me lambe samay ke liye lagayein. Beti ke liye Sukanya (SSY) bhi achha. Jitna jaldi shuru, utna kam mahina lagta hai.

Padhai ka kharcha kitna badhega

Higher education (engineering, medical, ya videsh) ka kharcha har saal tezi se badh raha hai — aksar aam mehngai se bhi zyada. Aaj jo course ₹10 lakh ka hai, 15 saal baad wo ₹25-30 lakh ka ho sakta hai. Isliye aaj ki keemat dekh kar nahi, future ki keemat dekh kar planning karni chahiye. Mehngai ka asar Inflation Calculator me dekh sakte hain.

Aaj ka kharcha × mehngai (15-18 saal) = future me asli zaroorat
Jitni jaldi shuru, utna kam mahina lagega

Jaldi shuru karne ka jaadu

Ye sabse zaroori baat hai. Bachche ke janm ke saath shuru karne wala aur 8 saal ki umar me shuru karne wala — dono ko ek hi lakshya tak pahunchne ke liye bahut alag mahina dena padta hai. Jaldi shuru karne wale ko compounding ke zyada saal milte hain, isliye har mahine kam paisa lagta hai. Der karne par wahi lakshya mushkil aur mehnga ho jaata hai.

Kahan lagayein — vikalp

  • SIP (equity mutual fund) — 10-15 saal jaise lambe goal ke liye sabse achha, kyunki lambe samay me mehngai se tez badhta hai. SIP Calculator me hisaab dekhein.
  • Sukanya Samriddhi (SSY) — sirf beti ke liye, sarkari, tax-free, achha byaj. Beti ki padhai/shaadi ke liye khaas. PPF vs Sukanya dekhein.
  • PPF — surakshit, tax-free, kisi bhi bachche ke liye. SIP ke saath santulan ke liye.

Ek achha tareeka — mix

Sirf ek jagah nahi, mix rakhna samajhdaari hai. Ek hissa SIP me (badhotri ke liye), ek hissa PPF ya SSY me (surakshaa ke liye). Isse paisa badhta bhi hai aur ek hissa pakka surakshit bhi rehta hai. Jaise-jaise bachcha bada ho aur padhai paas aaye, dheere-dheere equity (SIP) se paisa surakshit jagah (FD/PPF) me le aayein, taaki ain waqt par market giravat se nuksan na ho.

Kitna mahina lagega

Maan lijiye 15 saal baad ₹25 lakh chahiye, aur SIP ausatan 11% badhti hai. To lagbhag ₹5,500-6,000 mahina se ye ban sakta hai. Agar aap 5 saal der se shuru karein (10 saal bache), to yahi lakshya ~₹11,000 mahina maangega — lagbhag dugna. Yahi jaldi shuru karne ka faayda hai. Apna number SIP Calculator me daal kar dekh lein.

Zaroori baatein

  • Alag rakhein — padhai ka paisa doosre kharchon me na milaayein.
  • Bima le lein — kamane wale ka term plan, taaki kuch ho bhi jaye to bachche ki padhai na ruke.
  • Har saal badhaayein — income badhe to SIP bhi thodi badhaayein (step-up).
  • Goal paas aane par surakshit karein — aakhri 2-3 saal me equity se nikaal kar FD/PPF me.

Zaroori: ye lekh aam jaankari ke liye hai, nivesh salah nahi. Mutual fund me jokhim hota hai. Apni sthiti ke hisaab se SEBI-registered advisor se salah lein.

Ek chhoti misaal — jaldi vs der

Do parivaar, dono ko 15 saal baad bachche ki padhai ke liye ₹25 lakh chahiye. Pehla parivaar bachche ke janm se hi ₹6,000 mahina SIP shuru karta hai. Doosra 5 saal wait karta hai, phir sochta hai — ab to sirf 10 saal bache, aur use ~₹11,000 mahina daalna padta hai usi lakshya ke liye.

Dono ne mehnat ki, par pehle parivaar ne lagbhag aadha mahina dekar wahi lakshya paa liya — sirf jaldi shuru karke. Ye compounding ka kamaal hai: paisa jitne zyada saal lagta hai, utna zyada khud badhta hai. Der karne se aapko wahi lakshya paane ke liye apni jeb se zyada dena padta hai.

Sabse zaroori — abhi shuru karein

Log aksar "thoda settle ho jaayein, phir shuru karenge" soch kar taalte rehte hain. Par bachche ki padhai ka samay rukega nahi — wo aayega hi. Isliye chhoti rakam se bhi abhi shuru karna, badi rakam se der se shuru karne se behtar hai. ₹2,000 mahina se bhi shuru karein, aur income badhne par badhaate rahein.

Ek baar aadat ban gayi aur paisa apne aap katne laga, to 15-18 saal me ye chhoti-chhoti rakam ek bada fund ban jaata hai — jo ain waqt par bachche ke sapno ke saath khada hoga, aur aapko loan ya tanaav se bachaayega. Padhai ke fund ke liye sabse achha din aaj hai.

Jitni jaldi shuru, utni chhoti kist

Maan lijiye 15 saal baad Rs 25 lakh chahiye (aaj ke Rs 12-13 lakh ki padhai, mehngai ke baad). 12% anuman par mahine ki kist:

Bacche ke janm ke saal shuru karna aur 10 saal ki umr me shuru karna — dono me kist tigni ho jaati hai. Ek aur baat jo aksar chhoot jaati hai: padhai ki mehngai aam mehngai se tez badhti hai, isliye 6% ki jagah 8-10% maankar lakshya banana zyada surakshit hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Bachche ki padhai ka fund kab shuru karein?

Jitni jaldi ho sake — aadarsh roop se bachche ke janm ke saath. Jaldi shuru karne se compounding ka fayda milta hai aur mahina kam lagta hai.

Kahan paisa lagayein?

Lambe goal (10-15 saal) ke liye SIP sabse achha. Beti ke liye Sukanya bhi. Surakshaa ke liye PPF. Mix rakhna sabse behtar.

Kitna mahina lagega?

Lakshya aur samay par nirbhar. Jaise 15 saal me ₹25 lakh ke liye ~₹5,500-6,000/mahina (11% par). Der se shuru karne par lagbhag dugna.

Padhai ka paisa SIP me surakshit hai?

Lambe samay me SIP mehngai se tez badhti hai, par beech me upar-neeche hoti hai. Goal paas aane par ise FD/PPF me le aayein taaki ain waqt par nuksan na ho.

Sukanya ya SIP — beti ke liye?

Dono. Sukanya surakshit aur tax-free, SIP zyada badhotri. Beti ke liye dono milaakar rakhna sabse achha.
