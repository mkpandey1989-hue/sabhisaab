Peeche ka ganit

Dono time ko minute me badal kar farak nikaalte hain. Agar end time start se chhota hai (jaise raat 10 baje se subah 6 baje), to hum maante hain ki din badla hai aur 24 ghante jod dete hain.

Formula:
Kul minute = (end − start) − break
Agar end < start to end me 24 ghante jod dete hain (agli subah).

Result ghante:minute aur sirf minute — dono me milta hai, taaki payroll ya billing dono me kaam aaye.

Kuch misaal jo saaf kar dein

Misaal 1 — office duty. 9:30 se 6:15, 30 min lunch. Kul = 8 ghante 45 min − 30 min = 8 ghante 15 minute working.

Misaal 2 — night shift. Raat 10:00 se subah 6:00. End chhota hai, to +24 ghante — kul 8 ghante. Break jodne par ghata dijiye.

Misaal 3 — overtime. Duty 8 ghante ki hai, aaj 10 ghante 30 min kaam hua. 2 ghante 30 min overtime — is se overtime pay bhi nikaal sakte hain.

Jahan log atakte hain

  1. Aadhi raat paar bhoolna. Night shift me end time chhota dikhta hai — tool khud 24 ghante jod deta hai, par manual me log galti karte hain.
  2. Break na ghatana. Lunch/break ko working hours se ghatana zaroori hai, warna ghante zyada dikhte hain.
  3. AM/PM ki galti. 12-hour format me AM/PM ulat jaane se poora hisaab bigadta hai — ye tool 24-hour input leta hai.
  4. Seconds ki soch. Ye tool minute tak ka hisaab deta hai; second ki zaroorat kam padti hai.
  5. Din ki ginti mila dena. Ye ek din ke andar ka samay ginta hai — kai din ke liye date difference tool use karein.

Bharat ke hisaab se dhyaan dein

  • Payroll: Kai jagah salary ghante ke hisaab se banti hai — ye tool exact working hours de deta hai.
  • Overtime: Nirdhaarit ghante se zyada kaam = overtime. Yahan se nikaal kar per-hour se pay bana sakte hain.
  • Freelance: Client ko ghante ke hisaab se bill karte hain to ye seedha kaam aata hai.
  • Break niyam: Kai jagah 5-6 ghante par break dena zaroori hota hai — HR niyam dekhein.

Thodi aur gehraai se

Working hours ka hisaab tab pecheeda ho jaata hai jab shift aadhi raat ko paar karti hai. Maan lijiye koi raat 10 baje se subah 6 baje tak kaam karta hai — agar aap seedha "6 minus 10" karenge to negative aa jaayega. Isi liye ye tool khud samajh leta hai ki end time chhota hone ka matlab hai agli subah, aur 24 ghante jod kar sahi 8 ghante nikaal deta hai. Night shift, hospital duty, security guard jaise kaam me ye chhoti si baat bade payroll error se bachaati hai.

Freelancer aur consultant ke liye ye tool billing me kaam aata hai. Agar aap client se ghante ke hisaab se paisa lete hain, to har din ka accurate working time nikaal kar mahine bhar jod lena aasaan ho jaata hai. "Decimal ghante" wala number khaaskar isliye diya gaya hai — kyunki per-hour rate se guna karne ke liye 8 ghante 15 minute ko 8.25 ghante me badalna padta hai, aur wo yahan seedha mil jaata hai. Break ghatana na bhoolein, warna billed hours zyada dikhenge.

Isi tarah ke aur hisaab ke liye Date Difference aur Salary Per Hour bhi kaam ke hain — ek hi jagah se saara kaam nipat jaata hai.

Raat ki shift aur 12-ghante ka format — yahin galti hoti hai

Do samay ke beech ka antar nikalna seedha lagta hai, par do jagah log phaste hain — aadhi raat paar karna, aur 12 baje ka AM/PM.

SamayLog likhte hainAsli antar
10:00 PM se 6:00 AM16 ghante (galat)8 ghante
11:30 PM se 7:15 AM—7 ghante 45 minute
12:00 AMdopahar samajh lete hainaadhi raat
12:00 PMaadhi raat samajh lete haindopahar
Salary aur overtime ke kagaz me isi se sabse zyada jhagda hota hai. Raat ki shift ka samay aadhi raat paar karta hai, isliye ghante ginte waqt din badal jaata hai — aur haath se ginne par aksar 16 ghante nikal aate hain. Sabse surakshit tarika: samay ko 24-ghante ke format me likhein (22:00 se 06:00), aur agar dooja number chhota ho to usme 24 jod dein. Sarkari, hospital aur airline ke kagaz isi wajah se 24-ghante ka format istemaal karte hain — usme AM/PM ki galti ka mauka hi nahi rehta.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Do time ke beech samay kaise nikalein?

Start aur end time daalein; tool ghante aur minute me farak de dega.

Night shift ka hisaab hoga?

Haan. Agar end time start se chhota hai to tool agli subah maan kar sahi ginta hai.

Break ghata sakta hoon?

Haan, lunch/break minute daalein, wo working hours se ghata diya jaayega.

Overtime kaise nikalein?

Kul working hours me se nirdhaarit ghante ghata dein — bacha samay overtime hai.

Kya second bhi ginega?

Ye tool minute tak ginta hai, jo working-hours ke liye kaafi hota hai.

Zaroori baat: Ye ek saamany samay-ganak hai. Payroll, overtime aur break ke apne niyam company/kanoon ke hisaab se hote hain — final hisaab ke liye apne HR niyam dekhein.
