Formula aasaan bhasha me

Dono tareekhon ko ginti me badal kar farak nikala jaata hai, isliye mahine ke chhote-bade hone aur leap year ka hisaab apne aap sahi rehta hai. Nateeja din me aata hai, aur usi ko 7 se baant kar hafte bhi dikh jaate hain.

Formula: Farak (din) = Doosri tareekh − Pehli tareekh
Hafte = din ÷ 7

Misaal se samajhiye

Misaal 1 — chhota farak. 29-06-2026 se 14-07-2026 tak 15 din yaani 2 hafte 1 din.

Misaal 2 — notice period. 1 August ko resign kiya aur 60 din ka notice hai to aakhri din 30 September banta hai.

Misaal 3 — leap year. 1 Feb 2024 se 1 Mar 2024 tak 29 din, kyunki 2024 leap year tha. 2025 me yahi 28 din hote.

Ye table dikhati hai ki "mahina" aur "30 din" ek cheez nahi hain. Contract me agar "90 din" likha hai to ginti din ki hogi, aur agar "3 mahine" likha hai to tareekh se tareekh tak. Dono ka farak 1-2 din ka ho sakta hai, jo notice period ya cheque ki validity me maayne rakhta hai.

Sarkari kaam me ek aur cheez dhyan rakhein — kai jagah "working days" likha hota hai, jisme Sunday aur sarkari chhutti nahi ginte. 15 working days ka matlab asli me 21-22 din ho jaata hai. Form bharte waqt ye line dhyan se padhein.

Tareekh ka antar kahan-kahan kaam aata hai

Ye tool sirf "kitne din" ke liye nahi hai. Kai jagah din ki ginti par seedha paisa ya kanooni haq tika hota hai.

KaamKyun din ginna zaroori
Notice periodResignation se aakhri din tak — kam pade to recovery katti hai
FD ka samayKuch din pehle todne par premature penalty lagti hai
Capital gain24 mahine se ek din kam ya zyada — tax ka niyam badal jaata hai
Visa aur passportAksar yatra ke din se 6 mahine ki validity chahiye
Warranty / insuranceClaim ki aakhri tareekh nikalne ke liye
Ek baat jo galat ginti kara deti hai: "kitne din" aur "kitne din baad" ek nahi hote. 1 tareekh se 10 tareekh ke beech 9 din ka antar hai, par ye 10 din ki avadhi hai (dono din ginkar). Bank byaj aur hotel ka kiraya avadhi se ginte hain, par notice period aksar antar se. Kagaz me hamesha likhein ki dono taraf ke din shaamil hain ya nahi — isi ek line se baad ka jhagda bach jaata hai.

Aksar hone wali galtiyan

  1. Pehla din ginna ya na ginna tay na karna. Notice period aur interest ke hisaab me ye ek din ka farak mayne rakhta hai. Pehle tay kar lein.
  2. Mahine ko 30 din maan lena. Har mahine 30 din ka nahi hota. Sirf din ginna zyada sahi rehta hai.
  3. Leap year bhool jaana. Har 4 saal me February 29 din ki hoti hai.
  4. Chhutti na ginna. Kaam ke din ginne hain to weekend aur chhutti alag se ghatani padti hain.
  5. Format galat daalna. dd-mm-yyyy aur mm-dd-yyyy me farak hai — 03-04 ka matlab 3 April bhi ho sakta hai aur 4 March bhi.

Bharat me ye baatein mayne rakhti hain

  • Notice period: Zyadatar company 30, 60 ya 90 din ka notice maangti hain.
  • Gratuity: 5 saal poore hone ka hisaab isi se lagta hai — job join aur last working day ka farak.
  • Bank: FD ki maturity aur loan ke din isi tarah gine jaate hain.
  • Visa: Visa ki validity ke din ginte waqt aakhri din ko lekar dhyan rakhein.
  • Court/kaagaz: Kaanooni samay-seema me aam taur par din hi gine jaate hain, mahine nahi.
