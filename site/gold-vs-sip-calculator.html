Gehne me nivesh shuru hone se pehle hi peeche ho jaata hai

Ye baat dukaan par kabhi nahi batayi jaati. Gehna kharidte waqt aap sirf sone ka daam nahi dete. Uske upar making charge aur GST dono lagte hain.

RoopUpari kharchBechte waqt

Sikka ya barLagbhag 3-6% + GSTThoda kam nuksan
Gold ETF ya gold fundSirf chhota saalana kharchBazaar bhaav par

Matlab ye: 1 lakh ka gehna kharidne par aapka asli sona lagbhag 85-88 hazaar ka hota hai. Sone ka bhaav pehle 15% badhega, tab jaakar aap barabar par aayenge. Yahi wajah hai ki gehna pehenne ke liye achha hai, par nivesh ke naam par wo shuruaat se hi peeche khada hota hai.

Purana gehna badalte waqt kai jagah 5-8% ka ghisaav bhi kaat liya jaata hai. Yaani ek hi sona do baar making charge bhar chuka hota hai. Bill par making, GST aur ghisaav teeno alag-alag likha hai ya nahi — ye zaroor dekh lijiye.

Dono ka swabhaav alag hai, isliye ladaai bekaar hai

Sona aur equity ek doosre ke pratidwandi nahi hain. Dono alag samay par kaam karte hain, aur yahi unki asli upyogita hai.

Sone ka asli kaam portfolio ko sthir rakhna hai. Jab bazaar girta hai, sona aksar mazboot rehta hai aur ghabraahat kam karta hai. Isi wajah se salahkaar aam taur par kul paise ka 5% se 10% hissa sone me rakhne ki baat karte hain — poora paisa nahi.

Ek asli misaal — 15 saal, 5,000 mahina

Neeche 9% (sona) aur 12% (equity) ke anuman par 15 saal ka hisaab. Kul jama 9 lakh.

Gehne aur gold ETF ka farak dekhiye — sona wahi hai, return wahi hai, sirf roop alag hai, aur teen lakh ka antar aa gaya. Agar sona lena hi hai to roop chunna bhaav chunne jitna hi zaroori hai.

Tax dono par lagta hai, par tarika alag hai

Dono me faayde par tax lagta hai. Niyam samay-samay par badalte hain, isliye bechne se pehle apne saal ka niyam confirm kar lena zaroori hai.

  • Equity fund — ek saal se zyada rakhne par lambi avadhi ka niyam lagta hai, jisme saal bhar me ek seema tak ka faayda chhoot me aata hai. Usse kam samay par ooncha rate.
  • Physical sona — bechne par faayde par tax lagta hai, aur avadhi ke hisaab se dar alag hoti hai. Bill sambhaal kar rakhna zaroori hai, warna kharid ki keemat saabit karna mushkil ho jaata hai.
  • Gold ETF aur gold fund — inka apna alag niyam hai jo pichhle kuch saalon me badla hai.
Ek baat jo bahut kaam ki hai: gehne ka purana bill na hone par bechte waqt kharid ki keemat saabit karna kathin ho jaata hai aur poora faayda tax ke daayre me aa sakta hai. Ghar ke sone ke bill ek jagah sambhaal kar rakhiye.

Apna tax slab ke hisaab se hisaab dekhna ho to Capital Gain Tax Calculator me number daal kar dekh lijiye.

Ghar ka sona kitna, ye kaise tay karein

Bharat me sona sirf nivesh nahi hai — shaadi, riwaaj aur suraksha sab isse jude hain. Isliye faisla poora ganit se nahi hota. Do saaf sawaal madad karte hain.

Pehla: ye sona pehnna hai ya rakhna hai? Pehnne ke liye gehna theek hai, usme making charge ek kharch hai, nuksan nahi. Sirf rakhne ke liye lena hai to gehne me lena mehnga sauda hai.

Doosra: kul paise ka kitna hissa sone me ja raha hai? Agar bachat ka aadha se zyada sone me pada hai, to portfolio ek taraf jhuk gaya hai. Sona kamai nahi deta — na byaj, na dividend, na kiraya. Wo sirf bhaav badhne ka intezaar karta hai.

Sabse santulit rasta zyadatar parivaaron ke liye yahi rehta hai: pehnne ka sona zaroorat ke hisaab se, aur nivesh wala hissa ETF ya fund me — kul paise ka lagbhag daswaan hissa. Baaki lambi badhotari ke liye SIP Calculator me apna lakshya dekh lijiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Gehne me nivesh karna theek hai ya nahi?

Pehnne ke liye theek hai. Sirf nivesh ke liye mehnga padta hai, kyunki making charge aur GST milakar shuru me hi 10-17% kam ho jaata hai aur wo bechte waqt wapas nahi milta.

Gold ETF aur physical sone me kya farak hai?

Sona dono me wahi hai. ETF me making charge, locker aur churi ka darr nahi rehta, aur bechna aasaan hota hai. Physical sone ko aap haath me rakh sakte hain, jo kai logon ke liye zaroori hai.

Kul paise ka kitna hissa sone me rakhna chahiye?

Aam taur par 5% se 10% ki baat ki jaati hai, taaki bura waqt sambhal jaaye. Ye ek aam raay hai, koi niyam nahi — aapki zaroorat alag ho sakti hai.

Kya sona equity se surakshit hai?

Sone ka bhaav bhi girta hai, kabhi kai saal tak ek jagah ruka rehta hai. Wo sthir hai, par uska bhi jokhim hai. Koi bhi nivesh bina jokhim ke nahi hota.

Sone ki SIP hoti hai kya?

Gold fund me mahine ki SIP ki ja sakti hai, bilkul doosre funds ki tarah. Physical sone me kuch dukaanein yojana chalati hain, par unke niyam alag hote hain aur unhe dhyaan se padhna chahiye.
