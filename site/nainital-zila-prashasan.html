Nainital: Saar aur Zile ki Pehchan

Zile ki Pehchan: Ek Nazar Mein

Nainital district Uttarakhand ke Kumaon division ka ek pramukh zila hai, jiska mukhyalay (headquarters) Nainital shahar me hai. Ise “Lake District of India” kaha jata hai, aur iska sabse bada shahar Haldwani hai jo “Gateway of Kumaon” ke naam se jaana jata hai.

Nainital district Uttarakhand ke dakshin-purvi hisse me, Kumaon Himalaya ki talhati se lekar Terai-Bhabar ke maidani ilake tak faila hua hai. Ek hi zile me aapko 2,600 meter se zyada unchi pahadi choti, taal (lakes), ghane jungle, Corbett Tiger Reserve ka ek hissa, aur Haldwani-Lalkuan ke upjau maidan ek saath mil jate hain. Isi vividhta ki wajah se yeh zila tourism, education, administration, nyay vyavastha aur vyapar, sabhi me Uttarakhand ke sabse mahatvapurn zilon me ginta hai.

Is zile ki pehchan ke kuch mukhya stambh:

  • Taal Nagari: Naini Tal, Bhimtal, Sattal, Naukuchiatal, Khurpatal, Malwa Tal, Harishtal aur Lokhamtal jaise taalon ki wajah se ise Lake District kaha jata hai. Uttarakhand ke sarkari audit documents me bhi isi upadhi ka zikr hai.
  • Nyay ki Rajdhani: Uttarakhand High Court rajya ke gathan (2000) ke baad se Nainital me sthit hai.
  • Hill Station Capital: British kaal me Nainital United Provinces ki grishmakalin rajdhani rahi aur aaj bhi Uttarakhand ka sabse jaana-mana hill station hai.
  • Kumaon ka Dwar: Haldwani aur Kathgodam, Kumaon ke pahadon ke liye railway aur sadak ka pravesh dwar hain.
  • Shiksha Kendra: Sainik School Ghorakhal, Kumaon University (Nainital campus), Govt. Medical College Haldwani, aur anek prasiddh boarding schools.

Nainital Naam Kaise Pada: Naam ka Itihas

Nainital naam “Naini” (Maa Naina Devi) aur “Tal” (jheel) se banta hai. Manyata hai ki yahan Sati ki aankh (nain) giri thi, isliye 51 Shakti Peethon me se ek ke roop me Naina Devi Mandir yahan sthit hai.

Naam ke peeche do mukhya paramparayein hain:

  • Shakti Peeth katha: Puranik katha ke anusar Sati ke jalte sharir ko lekar jab Bhagwan Shiv vichran kar rahe the, tab Sati ke angon ke tukde alag-alag sthanon par gire. Maanyata hai ki Nainital me unki aankhein (nain) giri aur wahan banne wali jheel “Naini Tal” kehlai. Taal ke kinare Naina Devi Mandir isi aastha ka kendra hai.
  • Trishi Sarovar: Skanda Puran ke Manas Khand me is jheel ka ullekh “Trishi Sarovar” ke roop me milta hai, jo teen rishiyon Atri, Pulastya aur Pulah se judi manyata hai. Kaha jata hai ki jab inhe pyaas lagi aur paani nahi mila, to unhone tapasya se is sarovar ka nirman kiya.

Aadhunik itihas me British vyapari P. Barron ne 1841 me is jheel ko “khoja” aur uske baad yahan angrezi bastiyan basane ka silsila shuru hua. Isi se pehle sthaniya Kumaoni log is jagah ko dharmik roop se jaante the.

Nainital: Collectorate aur Rajasv Prashasan

Zila Mukhyalay, Vikas Bhawan aur Collectorate Kahan Hai

Nainital zile ka mukhyalay Nainital shahar hai, jahan Zila Adhikari (District Magistrate / Collector) ka karyalay sthit hai. Haldwani me bhi bade prashasnik karyalay aur tehsil karyalay kaam karte hain, kyunki wahan zile ki sabse badi aabadi rehti hai.

Zile ka prashasnik dhancha is tarah hai:

  • District Magistrate (DM): Zile ke sarvochch revenue aur prashasnik adhikari.
  • Additional District Magistrate (ADM), City Magistrate, Sub-Divisional Magistrates (SDM): Alag-alag tehsil circles ke pramukh.
  • Chief Development Officer (CDO): Vikas yojnaon ke samanvay ke liye.
  • District Panchayat Raj Officer, District Supply Officer, Chief Medical Officer, District Education Officers aadi vibhagiya pramukh.

Nagrik apne kaam, jaise income certificate, jati praman patra, ya zameen se jude mamle, ke liye sabse pehle apni tehsil ya SDM karyalay se sampark karte hain. Zile ki official website nainital.nic.in par karyalayon ki suchi aur sampark ki jankari milti hai (dekhein Nainital: Jaanche Hue Sarkari Link aur Sandarbh).

Isliye kisi bhi sarkari kaam ke liye tehsil ki taaza suchi nainital.nic.in ya bhulekh.uk.gov.in par ek baar zaroor jaanch lein.

Kanungo Circle aur Patwari Halqa: Har tehsil ko kai kanungo circles me aur har circle ko kai patwari halqon me banta jata hai. Patwari zameen ke record (khasra, khatauni) ka sabse pehla sthaniya adhikari hota hai. Gaon ka patwari kaun hai, yeh jaanna zameen ki nakal, mutation (dakhil-kharij) aur aapda raahat ke liye bahut kaam aata hai.

Patwari Chowki: Revenue Police aur Regular Police ka Kshetra

Uttarakhand ke pahadi ilakon me lambe samay se “Revenue Police” (Patwari) vyavastha chali aa rahi hai, jahan patwari ek saath revenue aur samanya apradhon ki prathmik jaanch karta hai. Nainital ke maidani aur shahri ilakon me niyamit Police thana kaam karte hain.

Is vyavastha ko samajhne ke liye:

  • Regular Police ilake: Nainital shahar, Haldwani, Kathgodam, Lalkuan, Ramnagar, Kaladhungi jaise shahri aur maidani kshetra, jahan police thana aur chowkiyan hain.
  • Revenue Police ilake: Dur-daraz ke pahadi gaon, jahan patwari aur kanungo ki chowki prathmik kanooni karyawahi sambhalti hai.

Uttarakhand High Court aur rajya sarkar ke star par revenue police vyavastha ko dheere-dheere niyamit police me badalne ki charcha aur karyawahi chal rahi hai. Iska matlab hai ki kisi gaon ki jurisdiction samay ke saath badal sakti hai. Agar aapko apne gaon ka sahi police ya patwari kshetra jaanna hai, to tehsil karyalay ya zila police karyalay se likhit jaankari lena sabse surakshit hai.

Vikas Khand teen-stariya Panchayati Raj vyavastha ki beech ki kadi hai. Yeh Gram Panchayat aur Zila Panchayat ke beech me vikas yojnaon ki yojna, nigrani aur kriyanvayan karta hai. MGNREGA, PM Awas, Swachh Bharat, Jal Jeevan Mission jaisi yojnayein block star par hi gaon tak pahunchti hain.

Zila Panchayat Seats, Kshetra Panchayat & Total Gram Panchayats Count

Nainital zile me teen-stariya Panchayati Raj ke tahat Zila Panchayat, Kshetra Panchayat (Block Panchayat) aur Gram Panchayat kaam karte hain. Block-wise jod ke anusar zile me lagbhag 475 Gram Panchayat hain.

Teen star ka dhancha:

  • Zila Panchayat: Zile ke star par vikas yojnaon ki sabse upari sanstha. Iske sadasya zile ke alag-alag kshetron se chune jate hain aur Zila Panchayat Adhyaksh ka chunav hota hai.
  • Kshetra Panchayat (Block Panchayat): Har Vikas Khand me ek Kshetra Panchayat hoti hai jiska netritva Pramukh karta hai.
  • Gram Panchayat: Gaon ki sabse chhoti ikai, jiska netritva Gram Pradhan karta hai.

Zila Panchayat seats ki sankhya aur wards ka punargathan har Panchayat chunav se pehle State Election Commission aur Panchayati Raj Vibhag dwara ghoshit kiya jata hai. Isliye taaza seat sankhya ke liye Uttarakhand State Election Commission ki official suchna dekhna sabse bharosemand tareeka hai.

Aabaad aur Gair-Aabaad (Khaali) Gaon

Nainital zile me lagbhag 1,100 se 1,200 abaad gaon darj hain, aur kai pahadi gaon palayan ki wajah se aadhe-khaali ya poori tarah khaali ho chuke hain. Aise “bhutiya gaon” ya “Ghost Villages” ki charcha aur punarvaas ki koshishein Uttarakhand me tez hui hain.

Aankde alag-alag sarkari aur portal sandarbhon me thode alag milte hain: kuch sandarbh zile me lagbhag 1,097 gaon, kuch 1,182 aur kuch 1,198 abaad gaon batate hain, kyunki alag-alag varshon aur tehsil punargathan ke aadhar par ginti hui hai.

Palayan ka asar: Betalghat, Okhalkanda, Dhari aur Ramgarh jaise uchch pahadi ilakon me rozgar, shiksha aur swasthya sevaon ki kami ke chalte kai pariwar Haldwani, Delhi aur anya maidani shehron me ja base hain. Iske baad purane pathal (slate) ki chhat wale ghar, sookhe khet aur band mandir un gaon ki kahani batate hain.

Wapsi ki nayi lehar: Homestay yojana, jaivik kheti, aur work-from-hills ke chalte kuch gaon ab dobara aabad ho rahe hain. Iski vistrit charcha Nainital: Khaali Gaon aur Virasat ki Sair (Ghost Villages) aur Nainital: Palayan Vapasi (Reverse Migration Stories) me ki gayi hai.

Nagar Panchayat aur Chhavni Parishad (jahan hai)

Nainital zile ke chhote kasbon jaise Bhowali, Bhimtal aur Kaladhungi Nagar Panchayat star ke nikay rahe hain. Nainital shahar mein Nainital Cantonment Board (Chhavni Parishad) hai, jo Raksha Mantralaya ke adheen hai; Kumaon Regiment ka Regimental Centre Ranikhet (Almora zila) mein hai.

  • Bhowali: Nainital-Almora aur Nainital-Haldwani marg ka sangam; fal mandi (fruit market) aur TB Sanatorium ke liye prasiddh.
  • Bhimtal: Bhimtal taal ke kinare ka kasba; Bhimeshwar Mahadev Mandir aur Aquarium ke liye jaana jata hai.
  • Kaladhungi: Jim Corbett ke bachpan ke gaon “Chhoti Haldwani” ke paas ka kasba; Corbett Museum aur Corbett Falls yahin hain.

Cantonment ke bare me: Kumaon Regiment ka Regimental Centre Ranikhet me hai jo Nainital se lagbhag 60 km door hai. Nainital zile ke nivasi jo Army, ITBP ya anya bal me jaana chahte hain, unke liye Ranikhet aur Almora ke ARO se jura jaankari Nainital: Fauj Bharti aur Physical Training me di gayi hai.

Nainital: Rajneetik aur Chunavi Parichay

Lok Sabha Seat aur Maujooda Saansad

Nainital zile ke 5 Vidhan Sabha kshetra Nainital-Udham Singh Nagar Lok Sabha seat me aate hain, jiske sansad Ajay Bhatt (BJP) hain. Ramnagar Vidhan Sabha kshetra Garhwal (Pauri) Lok Sabha seat ka hissa hai.

  • Nainital-Udham Singh Nagar (Lok Sabha seat no. 4): Isme Nainital zile ke Lalkuan, Bhimtal, Nainital, Haldwani aur Kaladhungi kshetra shamil hain, saath hi Udham Singh Nagar zile ke Jaspur, Kashipur, Bajpur, Gadarpur, Rudrapur, Kichha, Sitarganj, Nanakmatta aur Khatima kshetra bhi. Is seat ki sthapana 2009 ke parisiman ke baad hui.
  • Garhwal (Pauri) Lok Sabha seat: Parisiman 2008 ke baad Ramnagar Vidhan Sabha kshetra Garhwal Lok Sabha seat me shamil kiya gaya, isliye Ramnagar ke matdata alag Lok Sabha kshetra me vote dete hain.

Itihas ki jhalak (Nainital-Udham Singh Nagar seat): 2009 me Congress ke K.C. Singh Baba, 2014 me BJP ke Bhagat Singh Koshyari, aur 2019 tatha 2024 me BJP ke Ajay Bhatt is seat se jeete.

Note: Rajnaitik pad aur sansad/vidhayak badalte rehte hain. Is section ki jankari November 2026 tak ke sarvajanik sroton par aadharit hai; chunav ke baad hamesha Election Commission ki official site se confirm karein.

Nainital se Shuru Hue Pramukh Rajnaitik Andolan

Nainital ne Kumaon ke rajnaitik aur samajik andolanon me hamesha mahatvapurn bhumika nibhai hai, khaaskar Uttarakhand rajya andolan, Coolie Begar virodh aur Chipko jaise vanrakshak andolanon ke samay me.

  • Uttarakhand Rajya Andolan (1990s): Alag rajya ki maang ke daur me Nainital aur Haldwani Kumaon ke pramukh sabha aur pradarshan kendron me rahe. Rajya ka gathan 9 November 2000 ko hua.
  • Rajya ka Nyayik Kendra: Rajya gathan ke saath hi Uttarakhand High Court Nainital me sthapit hua, jisse shehar ki rajnitik aur kanooni pehchan aur gehri hui.
  • Paryavaran aur Jungle Adhikar: Chir ke jungle, van panchayat aur Corbett kshetra se jude van adhikar ke mudde Nainital zile ki rajneeti me baar-baar uthte rahe hain.

Note: Is section ko Nainital: Azaadi ki Ladai, Kuli Begar aur Rajya Andolan (Freedom Struggle, Coolie Begar & Statehood Movement) ke saath padhein, jahan in andolanon ki poori kahani vistar se di gayi hai.

Nainital: Police, Suraksha aur Seema Bal

Zile ki Police Vyavastha: Kotwali, Thane aur Chowkiyan

Nainital zile ki police vyavastha Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) Nainital ke adheen chalti hai. Emergency me 112 (sabhi aapatkal ke liye), 100 (Police), 101 (Fire) aur 108 (Ambulance) dial karein.

Zile me police ka dhancha teen star par samajhna aasan hai:

  • Zila star: SSP aur unke adheen Senior/Additional SP, CO (Circle Officer) aur anya adhikari.
  • Thana / Kotwali star: Shehri aur maidani ilakon me Thana ya Kotwali, jahan FIR darj hoti hai. Nainital shahar ke Mallital aur Tallital kshetra, Haldwani, Kathgodam, Lalkuan, Ramnagar, Kaladhungi, Bhowali, Bhimtal aur Ramgarh jaise pramukh kasbon me police thana ya chowki sthit hain.
  • Chowki / Outpost star: Door-daraz ilakon, tourist spots aur highway par chhoti chowkiyan nigrani karti hain.

Tourist ke liye zaruri baatein: Peak season me Nainital ki Mall Road aur Tallital kshetra me traffic police ki vishesh tainati hoti hai. Taal ke aas-paas bheed, parking aur nasha-sevan sambandhi niyam sakhti se lagu kiye jate hain. Kisi bhi shikayat ke liye aap 112 par call kar sakte hain ya najdeeki thane me sampark kar sakte hain.

Online sevayein: Uttarakhand Police ki official website aur mobile app par FIR ki sthiti, character certificate (police verification), kirayedar verification jaisi sevayein uplabdh hain.

Fauj ki Regiment (Garhwal Rifles / Kumaon Regiment / Scouts), ITBP aur SSB Camp

Nainital zile me koi Regimental Centre nahi hai; Kumaon Regiment ka Regimental Centre Ranikhet (Almora zila) me hai, jo Nainital se lagbhag 60 km door hai. Zile ke kai yuva Kumaon Regiment aur anya senaon me sevarat hain.

  • Kumaon Regiment: Kumaon ke yuvaon ki sabse pasandida ikai; Ranikhet me iska mukhyalay hai.
  • Sainik School Ghorakhal (Nainital): Bharat ke prasiddh Sainik Schoolon me se ek, jahan se kai chhatra NDA aur senaon me gaye hain (dekhein Nainital: Shiksha: University, College, Boarding School aur Sansthan).
  • Ex-Servicemen: Zile me badi sankhya me purva sainik rehte hain. Unki sahayata ke liye Zila Sainik Kalyan evam Punarvas Karyalay (District Sainik Welfare Office) kaam karta hai.

ITBP aur SSB ki zile-star par tainati ki jaankari suraksha karanon se sarvajanik roop se sankshipt hi uplabdh hoti hai. Bharti se judi jaankari ke liye Nainital: Fauj Bharti aur Physical Training (Army Recruitment aur Physical Training) dekhein.

Nainital: Nyayalay aur Kanooni Vyavastha

Zila Nyayalay, Family Court aur Muft Kanooni Sahayata

Nainital Uttarakhand ki nyayik rajdhani hai, kyunki yahan Uttarakhand High Court sthit hai. Zile ka District & Sessions Court bhi Nainital me hai, jabki Haldwani me bhi bade nyayalay kaam karte hain.

  • Uttarakhand High Court (Nainital): Rajya ke gathan (2000) ke baad se Nainital me sthit; pura rajya ke mukadme yahin sune jate hain.
  • District & Sessions Court: Zile ke Civil aur Criminal mamlon ke liye.
  • Haldwani ke nyayalay: Haldwani ki badi aabadi ke karan wahan bhi kai court kaam karte hain. Kaun sa court kis mamle ko sunega, yeh kshetradhikar (jurisdiction) par nirbhar karta hai.
  • Family Courts: Vivah, talaq aur bharan-poshan ke mamlon ke liye alag nyayalay vyavastha rehti hai.

Muft kanooni sahayata: Zila Vidhik Seva Pradhikaran (DLSA) Nainital, gareeb, mahilaon, SC/ST aur anya patra logon ko muft vakil aur salah deta hai. NALSA ki toll-free helpline 15100 par bhi sampark kiya ja sakta hai.

Nainital mein Sub-Registrar (Zameen Registry) Daftar

Zameen ki registry Sub-Registrar karyalay me hoti hai. Nainital zile me Nainital, Haldwani, Ramnagar, Lalkuan jaise pramukh kendron par Sub-Registrar karyalay hain; aapki zameen jis tehsil me aati hai, wahin ke karyalay me registry hoti hai.

Zameen kharidte samay is kram ko samjhein:

  • Khatauni aur khasra ki jaanch: Zameen ka malik asli hai ya nahi, yeh bhulekh portal par dekhein (Nainital: Bhulekh Uttarakhand: Khatauni Online Kaise Dekhein).
  • Stamp duty aur circle rate: Tehsil ke anusar alag hota hai (Nainital: Sampatti Registry, Stamp Duty aur Circle Rate).
  • Registry: Dono pakshon, gawahon aur zaruri dastavezon ke saath Sub-Registrar ke saamne.
  • Dakhil-Kharij (Mutation): Registry ke baad nayi khatauni me naam chadhwana.

Bahari logon ke liye: Uttarakhand me bahari rajyon ke logon dwara zameen kharidne par vishesh niyam lagu hain (Nainital mein Zameen Khareedne ke Kanoon aur Section 143). Registry se pehle kisi sthaniya vakil se kagaz zaroor jaanch karwayein.

Nainital ki aapda ki pehchan: Pahadi dhalaan, kachchi chattanein aur mansoon ki tez baarish ke karan bhuskhalan yahan ka sabse bada khatra hai. 18 September 1880 ka Alma Hill bhuskhalan Nainital ke itihas ki sabse badi aapda thi. Aaj bhi Balia Nala jaise ilake nigrani me rehte hain. October 2021 ki asaadharan baarish me Nainital zile ke kai hisson me bhuskhalan aur baadh se bhari nuksan hua tha.

Safety tips: Baarish ke alert me pahadi sadkon par yatra se bachein, nadi-nalon ke kinare na rukein, aur safar se pehle sadak ki sthiti ki jaankari lein.

SDRF / NDRF ke Thikane, Baadh Aashray aur Emergency Helipad

Uttarakhand me bachav karya ke liye SDRF (State Disaster Response Force) rajya ki mukhya aapda pratikriya shakti hai, aur gambhir sthiti me NDRF ki tukdiyan bulai jati hain. Zile ke andar rahat shivir aur sthaniya aashray sthal DDMA dwara taiyar kiye jate hain.

  • SDRF: Bhuskhalan, baadh aur doobne jaisi ghatnaon me sabse pehle pahunchne wali tukdi.
  • NDRF: Bade star ki aapda me kendra se bulai jati hai.
  • Rahat shivir aur aashray: Baadh aur bhuskhalan ke samay school, community hall aur sarkari bhawan aashray ke roop me istemal hote hain.
  • Helipad: Aapatkal me patients ko nikalne ke liye zile ke kuch sthanon par helipad aur helicopter landing zone chinhit hain. Sthiti ke anusar air ambulance ki jaankari Nainital: Swasthya: Aspatal, Trauma Center aur Ayush me hai.

Pahadi Driving ke Niyam aur Green Cess / Eco-Tax

Bharat me pahadi sadkon ke liye alag se koi “Hill Driving Endorsement” nahi hai; standard driving licence hi manya hai. Uttarakhand me bahari rajya ki gaadiyon par entry ke samay Green Cess jaise sarkari shulk lag sakte hain, jinke dar aur chhoot badalte rehte hain.

  • Driving licence: Pahadi sadkon par gaadi chalane ke liye vaidh driving licence zaroori hai. Pahadon me gaadi chalane ka anubhav na ho to naye chalak ko vishesh savdhani aur dhimi gati se chalna chahiye.
  • Green Cess / Eco charges: Paryavaran sanrakshan ke naam par kuch pravesh binduon aur tourist kshetron me vahan shulk liya jata hai. Dar aur niyam samay-samay par badalte hain, isliye safar se pehle Transport Department ki official website dekh lein.
  • Nainital me traffic niyam: Mall Road aur taal ke aas-paas gaadiyon ki awajahi par pratibandh aur parking ke vishesh niyam hote hain. Peak season me yeh zyada sakhti se lagu hote hain.

Pahadi driving ke sunehre niyam: Chadhai par chadhne wali gaadi ko pehle raasta dena; modon par horn bajana; baarish me raat ko pahadi yatra se bachna; aur brake ki jaanch se pehle utaar par lambi dooriyan tay na karna.
