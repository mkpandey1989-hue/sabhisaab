EPF Withdrawal Tax Calculator — PF Nikaalne Par Kitna Tax

EPF ka poora paisa tax-free tabhi hota hai jab aapki lagataar sewa 5 saal poori ho chuki ho. Usse pehle nikaalne par poora corpus tax me aa jaata hai — aur wo bhi chaar alag hisson me bant kar.

Neeche apne PF ke hisse aur sewa ke saal daaliye. Tax aur TDS ka anuman turant dikh jayega.

Chaar hisse, chaar alag niyam

PF ka balance ek rakam dikhta hai, par tax me use chaar hisson me dekha jaata hai. Yahi baat sabse kam samjhi jaati hai.

Hissa5 saal se pehle nikaalne par

Aapke yogdaan ka byajDoosri aay maani jaati hai, slab par tax
Company ka yogdaanSalary maani jaati hai, slab par tax

Sabse zyada chaunkane wali baat pehli line me hai. Aapne jo paisa apni salary se kataya aur uspar 80C ki chhoot li — wo chhoot wapas gin li jaati hai. Yaani purane saalon ka bacha hua tax ab bharna padta hai.

5 saal ka niyam — ek company ka nahi, kul sewa ka

Ye galatfehmi bahut mehngi padti hai. 5 saal ek hi company me poore karna zaroori nahi hai. Zaroori ye hai ki aapki kul lagataar sewa 5 saal ki ho.

Aapne teen company badli — 2 saal, 1.5 saal, 2 saal. Har baar PF transfer kiya. Aapki lagataar sewa 5.5 saal hai, aur poora paisa tax-free hai. Par agar beech me ek baar nikaal liya, to ginti wahin toot gayi aur phir se shunya se shuru hoti hai.

Isi ek wajah se naukri badalte waqt PF transfer karna aur nikaalna nahi, sabse zaroori faisla hai. UAN ek hone se transfer aaj kaafi aasan hai aur online ho jaata hai.

Kuch sthitiyan apwaad hain — jaise sehat kharab hone par, company band ho jaane par, ya aise kaaran par jo aapke haath me na ho. Aise maamlon me 5 saal se pehle nikaalne par bhi rahat milti hai.

TDS aur asli tax do alag cheezein hain

EPFO nikaasi ke waqt TDS kaat leta hai, par wo aakhri tax nahi hai.

SthitiTDS
Sewa 5 saal ya zyadaNahi katta
Rakam 50,000 se kamNahi katta
5 saal se kam, rakam 50,000 se upar, PAN diya10%
PAN nahi diyaBahut ooncha rate

Asli tax aapke poore saal ki aay par banta hai. Aap 30% slab me hain to 10% TDS kaafi nahi hoga aur ITR me antar bharna padega. Aur agar us saal aapki aay kam thi — jaise naukri chhoot gayi thi — to kata hua TDS refund me wapas aa sakta hai.

Ek chhoti si baat jo kaam aati hai: jis saal aay kam ho, usi saal nikaalna sasta padta hai. Naukri chhootne ke baad wale saal me nikaali gayi rakam aksar kam slab me aati hai, kyunki us saal salary poore 12 mahine nahi mili hoti.

Nikaalne se pehle doosre raste dekh lijiye

PF nikaalna aakhri rasta hona chahiye, pehla nahi. Kuch sthitiyon me advance milta hai, jo poori nikaasi se behtar hai kyunki khaata chalu rehta hai aur 5 saal ki ginti nahi tootti.

  • Ghar kharidne, banane ya home loan chukane ke liye
  • Ilaaj ke liye — apna ya parivaar ka
  • Bachche ki padhai ya shaadi ke liye
  • Naukri chhootne par ek hissa

Har vikalp ki apni shart hoti hai — kitni sewa poori honi chahiye aur kitna hissa mil sakta hai. UAN portal par apni patrata dikh jaati hai aur arzi online hi lag jaati hai.

Aur ek baat — naukri ke beech me PF nikaal lena sirf tax ka nuksan nahi hai. Wo paisa retirement se nikal jaata hai. 30 saal ki umar me nikaale gaye 3 lakh 58 saal tak 8% par lagbhag 25 lakh ban sakte the. Ye nuksan tax se kahin bada hai.

Nikaalna hi pade to kram se ye kijiye

Faisla pakka ho to ye chaar kadam paisa aur sirdardi dono bachate hain.

  • Sewa ki ginti jaanch lijiye. Purane employer ka PF transfer hua tha ya nahi — UAN me passbook se dekh lijiye. Kai baar 5 saal pehle se poore hote hain aur log bina zaroorat tax bhar dete hain.
  • PAN zaroor dijiye. Bina PAN ke rate bahut ooncha ho jaata hai.
  • Aay kam ho to declaration dijiye. Saal ki kul aay chhoot ki seema se kam hai to nirdhaarit form dekar TDS rukwaya ja sakta hai.
  • ITR me poori rakam dikhaiye. EPFO ka data AIS me pehle se hota hai. Chhod dene par notice aata hai.

Apna PF kitna banega, ye EPF Calculator me aur poore niyam EPF Guide me dekh lijiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

5 saal ka niyam ek company ka hai ya kul sewa ka?

Kul lagataar sewa ka. Company badalne par PF transfer kar dein to purani sewa ginti me judti rehti hai. Beech me nikaal liya to ginti toot jaati hai.

PF transfer karne par tax lagta hai?

Nahi. Transfer nikaasi nahi maana jaata. Isse na tax lagta hai, na TDS, aur sewa ki ginti bhi bani rehti hai.

TDS kat gaya to kya poora tax ho gaya?

Nahi. TDS sirf ek advance hai. Asli tax aapke poore saal ki aay par banta hai — kam kata ho to antar bharna hoga, zyada kata ho to refund milega.

Apne yogdaan par bhi tax lagta hai?

Us rakam par seedha tax nahi lagta, par uspar li gayi 80C ki purani chhoot wapas gin li jaati hai. Yaani un saalon ka bacha hua tax ab dena padta hai.

5 saal se pehle nikaalne par koi chhoot milti hai?

Kuch sthitiyon me — jaise sehat kharab hone par ya company band ho jaane par. Aam istifa ya naukri badalna ismein nahi aata.
