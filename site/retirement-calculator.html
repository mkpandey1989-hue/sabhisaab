Ganit saaf bhasha me

Do kadam hote hain. Pehla — aaj ka mahine ka kharcha mehngai ke hisaab se retire ki umar tak badhaya jaata hai. Dusra — us badhe hue saalana kharche ko lagbhag 25 se guna kiya jaata hai. 25 ka number "4% rule" se aata hai: agar aap har saal corpus ka 4% hi nikaalein to paisa bahut lambe samay tak chalta hai.

Formula: Retire par mahine ka kharcha = aaj ka kharcha × (1 + mehngai)saal
Zaroori corpus ≈ saalana kharcha × 25

Misaal ke saath samajhiye

Misaal 1 — 27 se 60 saal. Aaj ka kharcha ₹25,000 mahina, mehngai 5%, retire umar 60. 33 saal baad wahi kharcha lagbhag ₹1,25,080 mahina ho jaata hai, aur zaroori corpus lagbhag ₹3.75 crore.

Misaal 2 — 40 se 60 saal. Aaj ka kharcha ₹40,000, mehngai 6%, 20 saal. Us waqt kharcha lagbhag ₹1,28,000 mahina aur corpus kareeb ₹3.85 crore.

Misaal 3 — mehngai ka asar. Wahi ₹25,000 kharcha, 33 saal — 5% mehngai par ₹1.25 lakh, aur 7% par lagbhag ₹2.3 lakh mahina. Sirf 2% ka farak, par zaroorat lagbhag dugni.

Sabse aam chook

  1. Aaj ke kharche ko hi maan lena. Sabse badi galti yahi hai. 30 saal me mehngai kharche ko 4-5 guna kar deti hai.
  2. Ilaaj ka kharcha chhod dena. Umar ke saath sehat ka kharcha sabse tezi se badhta hai. Alag se health insurance aur thoda buffer rakhein.
  3. Umar bahut kam maan lena. Aaj log 80-85 saal tak jee rahe hain. 60 par retire hone ka matlab 25 saal ka kharcha.
  4. EPF ko hi kaafi maan lena. EPF achha aadhaar hai par akela poora nahi padta. NPS, PPF, mutual fund — kuch aur bhi chahiye.
  5. Der se shuru karna. 30 se shuru karne aur 40 se shuru karne me har mahine ki zaroorat lagbhag teen guna ho jaati hai.

Bharat me khaas dhyaan

  • 4% rule: Ye Amerika ke data se aaya hai. Bharat me mehngai zyada hai, isliye 3-3.5% nikasi zyada surakshit maani jaati hai.
  • EPF/NPS: Naukri wale ke liye ye do sabse bade aadhaar hain — statement saal me ek baar zaroor dekhein.
  • Ghar: Apna ghar hone se retire ke baad kiraya bach jaata hai, jo corpus ki zaroorat kaafi ghata deta hai.
  • Health insurance: Retire se pehle hi le lein. Baad me lena mehnga aur mushkil dono hota hai.
  • Ye anuman hai: Ye rough hisaab hai. Bade faisle se pehle kisi salahkaar se baat karein.

Retirement ke liye kitna paisa chahiye — seedha jawab

Ye sawaal log kai tareekon se poochte hain — "retire hone ke liye kitne paise ki zaroorat hai", "sewanivritti ke liye kitna paisa hona chahiye". Jawab ek hi hai, aur wo rakam ka nahi, aapke mahine ke kharche ka hisaab hai.

Mota niyam: retirement ke waqt jo saalana kharcha hoga, uska 25 guna. Ye "4% rule" se nikalta hai — har saal corpus ka lagbhag 4% nikaalein to paisa lambe samay tak chalta hai.

7% mehngai maankar, 60 saal par retirement:

Aaj mahine ka kharchaUmar 30 par chahiyeUmar 45 par chahiye
₹25,000~₹5.8 crore~₹2.1 crore
₹40,000~₹9.3 crore~₹3.4 crore
₹60,000~₹14 crore~₹5.1 crore

Number dekhkar ghabraahat hoti hai. Do baatein raahat deti hain:

  • Ye poora paisa aapko jama nahi karna. Bada hissa compounding banati hai. 30 saal ki umar wale ko ₹5.8 crore ke liye lagbhag ₹15,000-20,000 mahina lagta hai — poore 5.8 crore nahi.
  • Retirement par kharche badalte hain. Bacchon ki fees khatam, home loan khatam, aana-jaana kam. Par ilaaj ka kharcha badh jaata hai — use zaroor jodiye.
Ek baat jo bahut log bhool jaate hain — EPF aur NPS pehle se aapke paas hain. Poora lakshya SIP se nahi banana. Apna EPF balance dekhiye, uspar aage ka anuman lagaiye, aur bacha hua hissa hi naye nivesh se poora kariye. Isse number aksar aadha rah jaata hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Mehngai kitni maan kar chalein?

Bharat me 5-6% ka andaaza aam hai. Ilaaj aur padhai ke liye isse zyada rakhna behtar hai.

Kab shuru karna chahiye?

Jitna jaldi utna achha. Pehle 10 saal ka nivesh sabse zyada kaam karta hai.

Kya EPF kaafi hai?

Zyadatar logon ke liye nahi. Ye ek hissa hai, poora jawab nahi.

Retirement ke liye kitna paisa chahiye?

Mota niyam — retirement ke waqt jo saalana kharcha hoga uska 25 guna. Aaj ₹40,000 mahine me chal raha hai aur aap 30 ke hain, to 7% mehngai par lagbhag ₹9 crore ka anuman aata hai.

Kya ye poora paisa khud jama karna padega?

Nahi. Bada hissa compounding banati hai. 30 saal ki umar me shuru karne par lagbhag ₹15,000-20,000 mahine ki SIP se ye lakshya ban sakta hai.

4% rule kya hai?

Retirement ke baad har saal apne corpus ka lagbhag 4% nikaalna. Isi se 25 guna wala niyam banta hai. Ye anuman hai, waada nahi.

EPF aur NPS bhi isme jodna chahiye?

Haan, zaroor. Wo paisa pehle se aapka hai. Use ghata kar dekhne par zaroori SIP ka number aksar aadha rah jaata hai.
