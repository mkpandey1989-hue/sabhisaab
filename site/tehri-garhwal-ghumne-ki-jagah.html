Tehri Garhwal ki 20 Sabse Zaroori Ghumne ki Jagah

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal ki sabse zyada dekhi jane wali jagah hain Tehri Lake aur Tehri Dam, Dhanaulti, Surkanda Devi, Kanatal, Dobra-Chanti Pul, Devprayag, Kunjapuri, Chandrabadni, Nag Tibba aur Ghuttu-Khatling.

Entry fee aur timing: Dhanaulti Eco Park, Tehri Dam aur anya jagah ke entry fee aur samay badalte rehte hain. Lagbhag har jagah ke liye "Kya fee lagti hai" ek aam sawal hai, isliye jaane se pehle official site ya sthaniya daftar se taza jaankari le lein.

Tehri Garhwal me sabse zyada log Dhanaulti, Tehri Lake, Surkanda Devi aur Kanatal dekhne aate hain. Trekking ke liye Nag Tibba sabse aasaan aur Khatling Glacier sabse prasiddh trek hai.

Tehri Garhwal: Hill Station aur Weekend par Ghumne ki Jagah

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal ke mukhya hill station Dhanaulti (2,286 m), Kanatal (lagbhag 2,590 m), Chamba aur Surkanda ki chotiyan hain. Ye Mussoorie aur Dehradun se weekend ke liye aasan hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Kam Jaane-Pehchane aur Chhupe Gaon

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal ke offbeat ilako me Ghuttu, Belak, Gangi (Khatling trek ka antim gaon), Pantwari, Sem Mukhem aur Reeh aate hain. Ye gaon pahad ki sacchi, shant zindagi dikhate hain.

Zimmedar paryatan ke niyam: Gaon me ghoomte samay gharon ki photo lene se pehle puchhein, kachra na phailayein, aur sthaniya homestay ko tarjeeh dein, taaki paisa gaon me hi rahe.

Tehri Garhwal: Khaali Gaon (Palayan) aur Virasat ki Sair

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand me palayan ki wajah se 1,700 se zyada gaon khali ho chuke hain (Palayan Aayog ke anusar). Kuch khali gaon aur purane pathar ke ghar ab heritage walk aur homestay me badle ja rahe hain.

Khaali Pathar ke Gaon jo Eco-Tourism Bane

  • Kya karein: Khali gaon ke purane makan ko homestay banakar paryatan se jodna.
  • Tehri ka vishesh udaharan: Old Tehri ka doobna aur New Tehri me visthapan is jile ka sabse bada "ghost town" ka adhyay hai (Tehri Garhwal: Lok-Kathayein: Achhari, Veer Yoddha aur Rahasyamay Kisse).

Tehri Garhwal: Trekking ki Poori Suchi (Aasan, Madhyam aur Kathin)

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal ka sabse prasiddh aasaan trek Nag Tibba (lagbhag 3,000 m, 16 km, 2 din) hai. Sabse prasiddh oonchai ka trek Ghuttu se Khatling Glacier (lagbhag 45 km route ke sath, kai din) aur Panwali Kantha / Kedarnath hain.

Nag Tibba trek ka tarika:

  • Dehradun se Pantwari tak gaadi (lagbhag 85 se 90 km, 4 se 5 ghante).
  • Pehle din Pantwari se lagbhag 4 km chadhkar camp (lagbhag 2,300 m).
  • Dusre din lagbhag 12 km (chadhai aur utarai milakar), summit 3,000 m aur Nagdevta mandir.
  • Best samay: Jan se Apr aur Sep se Dec; barsat (Jul se Sep ke shuru) me bachein.

Khatling ke raaste ki baat: Ghuttu Tehri se lagbhag 62 km hai, aur Gangi antim gaon hai jahan kuch sewayein milti hain. Uske aage khane-rukne ka intezam apna karna padta hai.

Savdhani (har trek par): Oonchai wale trek ke liye registered guide lein, AMS (altitude sickness) ke lakshan pehchanein, aur mausam dekh kar chalein. Tehri Garhwal: Inner Line Permit , Van Anumati aur Suraksha Niyam me AMS aur permit ki jaankari hai. Guide ke rate: Sthaniya guide union aur Forest Department se taza rate confirm karein.

Tehri Garhwal: Parvatarohan, Chattan Chadhai aur Bouldering

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal ki oonchi chotiyan (jaise Jogin group lagbhag 6,466 m) Khatling kshetra ke paas ki chotiyon me ginti hain. Is oonchai ke abhiyan ke liye Indian Mountaineering Foundation (IMF) ki permission aur Forest Department ke permit lagte hain.

IMF Permit, Porter ka Kiraya aur Base Camp

  • Permit: IMF aur Forest Department (Gangotri / Govind Pashu Vihar ke kshetra me alag niyam).
  • Base camp: Ghuttu, Gangi aur Khatling ke raaste.
  • Rock climbing aur bouldering: Rishikesh kshetra me kuch jagah prachalit hai. Tehri ke apne kshetro me iske sanrakshit sthal ke liye adventure operator se confirm karein.
  • Porter / Guide rate: Sthaniya union aur sarkari dar-suchi dekhein.

Tehri Garhwal: Sardi ke Khel: Skiing, Snowboarding aur Barf mein Trek

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal me organized ski slope ya chairlift nahi hai. Barf ka anand snow trekking (Nag Tibba, Surkanda, Dhanaulti) aur snow camping ke roop me liya jata hai. Skiing ke liye Chamoli ka Auli aur Uttarkashi ke kuch sthan jaane jate hain.

Barf mein Trek aur Sardi ki Gatividhiyan

  • Sabse achha samay: Dec se Feb (barf ke mauke).
  • Snow ke jagah: Dhanaulti, Surkanda, Nag Tibba, Kanatal.
  • Savdhani: Barf girne par sadak band ya phislan ho sakti hai. Gaadi me snow chains aur garm kapde rakhein. Raasta pehle check karein.

Tehri Garhwal: Paani ke Khel: Rafting, Kayaking aur Mahseer Machhli Pakadna

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal me water sports ke do mukhya kendra hain. Tehri Lake par boating, jet ski, banana boat, speed boat, kayaking aur parasailing jaisi gatividhiyan hoti hain. Kaudiyala se Rishikesh tak Ganga me rafting hoti hai, jo Grade III se IV ki rapids ke liye jani jati hai.

Rate aur samay: Har gatividhi ka kimat aur samay badalta rehta hai. Hamesha taza rate list dekhein.

Ganga mein Rafting (Kaudiyala se Rishikesh)

  • Kaudiyala: Tehri Garhwal ke Devprayag block me hai. Yahan se rafting ke sabse lambe routes shuru hote hain.
  • Kaudiyala se Rishikesh: Lagbhag 36 km, Grade III-IV.
  • Shivpuri se Rishikesh: Lagbhag 16 km, Grade II-III, chhote group ke liye.
  • Mausam: Aam taur par Sept ke antim se Jun tak. Barsat me nadi tez hoti hai, isliye band rehti hai.
  • Surakshaa: Hamesha registered operator se, life jacket aur helmet ke saath rafting karein.

Mahseer Angling

  • Mahseer: Ganga ki prasiddh machhli, jise "catch and release" ke niyam me pakda jata hai.
  • Niyam: Machhli pakadne ke liye Uttarakhand Forest/Fisheries department ka permit chahiye. Bina permit ke machhli pakadna kanoon ke khilaf hai.

Koti Colony: Tehri Lake par Water Sports ka Mukhya Kendra

Seedha jawab: New Tehri ke paas Tehri Lake ke kinare Koti Colony wo jagah hai jahan se zyadatar boating aur water sports (jaise jet ski, speed boat, kayaking, banana ride, parasailing) shuru hote hain. Yahan paryatan vibhag aur niji sanchalakon ki gatividhiyan hoti hain, aur jheel ke kinare floating huts/cottage jaise rehne ke vikalp bhi bataye jate hain (pushti karein: sanchalit gatividhiyan, rate aur suraksha niyam har season badalte hain).

Tehri Garhwal: Hawai Khel, Paragliding, Mountain Biking aur Camping

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal me paragliding ya MTB ke adhikrit sthal ke liye Uttarakhand Tourism / Adventure Sports ki taza list se confirm karein. Camping ke liye Dhanaulti, Kanatal, Nag Tibba aur Tehri Lake ke kinare prasiddh hain.

Paragliding, MTB aur Manzoor Camping

  • Paragliding: Uttarakhand me sirf adhikrit takeoff sthal par hi paragliding ki ijazat hai. Tehri kshetra me taza adhikrit sthal ke liye jila paryatan karyalay se poochein.
  • MTB aur cycling: Dhanaulti-Chamba aur Mussoorie ki taraf ki sadak cycling ke liye prasiddh hai.
  • Camping: Van vibhag ke kshetro me camping ke niyam hain, aur bugyal par raat me camping par roak hai (Tehri Garhwal: Bugyal / Payar). Hamesha adhikrit camp operator chunein.

Tehri Garhwal: Taare Dekhna, Astro-Tourism aur Photography ki Jagah

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal me sunrise ke liye Kunjapuri aur Surkanda, Himalaya view ke liye Dhanaulti aur Nag Tibba, aur tara-darshan ke liye Devprayag ki Nakshatra Vedhshala prasiddh hain.

Tehri Garhwal: Taiyar Yatra Yojana (3, 5, 7 aur 10 Din)

Seedha jawab: Tehri Garhwal ghumne ke liye 3 din ka weekend plan (Dhanaulti, Surkanda, Kanatal), 5 din ka parivar aur mandir plan, 7 din ka trekker plan (Nag Tibba aur Ghuttu) aur 10 din ka poora jila plan sabse zyada pasand kiye jate hain.

Weekend Escape (3 Din)

  • Din 1: Dehradun se Mussoorie hote hue Dhanaulti. Eco Park aur shaam ka nazara.
  • Din 2: Subah Kaddukhal se Surkanda Devi, phir Kanatal.
  • Din 3: Chamba hote hue wapsi ya New Tehri.

Family & Temple Circuit (5 Din)

  • Din 1: Rishikesh se Narendranagar aur Kunjapuri Devi (sunrise ke liye).
  • Din 2: Chandrabadni aur Devprayag.
  • Din 3: New Tehri, Tehri Dam aur Dobra-Chanti Pul.
  • Din 4: Tehri Lake par water sports aur Koteshwar Mahadev.
  • Din 5: Dhanaulti, Surkanda aur wapsi.

Backpacker / Trekker Circuit (7 Din)

  • Din 1 se 2: Pantwari aur Nag Tibba trek.
  • Din 3: Dhanaulti aur Surkanda.
  • Din 4: New Tehri se Ghuttu.
  • Din 5 se 7: Ghuttu se Gangi tak (Khatling ke raaste ka hissa) aur wapsi.

Poora Zila (10 Din)

  • Din 1 se 2: Narendranagar, Kunjapuri, Chandrabadni.
  • Din 3: Devprayag aur Kaudiyala (rafting).
  • Din 4 se 5: New Tehri, Tehri Lake, Dobra-Chanti Pul.
  • Din 6: Ghuttu aur Bhilangana ghati.
  • Din 7 se 8: Pantwari aur Nag Tibba.
  • Din 9 se 10: Dhanaulti, Surkanda, Kanatal aur wapsi.

Tehri Garhwal: Ghumne ki jagah aur trek: Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Tehri Garhwal ghoomne ka sabse achha samay kab hai?
Mausam ke hisab se March se June aur September se November sabse achhe hain. December se February me Dhanaulti, Surkanda aur Nag Tibba par barf dekhne ko mil sakti hai. July se September me barish ke kaaran landslide aur raaste band hone ka jokhim rehta hai.
Tehri me kya-kya dekhne layak hai?
Tehri Lake aur Dam, Dobra-Chanti Pul, Devprayag Sangam, Surkanda Devi, Kunjapuri aur Chandrabadni mandir, Dhanaulti, Kanatal, Nag Tibba aur Khatling Glacier.
Nag Tibba trek kitna mushkil hai?
Ye ek aasan se madhyam trek hai. Oonchai lagbhag 3,000 meter, rasta lagbhag 16 km (aana-jaana), aam taur par 2 din me poora hota hai. Pantwari gaon se shuru hota hai.
Rafting Tehri me kahan hoti hai?
Shivpuri aur Kaudiyala se Rishikesh ki taraf Ganga me. Monsoon me ye aam taur par band rehti hai.
Kya Tehri ghumne ke liye 3 din kaafi hain?
3 din me aap New Tehri, Tehri Lake, Dobra-Chanti Pul, Dhanaulti aur Surkanda Devi dekh sakte hain. Trek ya Devprayag ke liye 1 se 2 din aur rakhein.
Tehri me ghoomne ki jagah kaun si hain?
Tehri Lake, Dobra-Chanti pul, Dhanaulti, Surkanda Devi, Kanatal, Devprayag aur Nag Tibba.
Nag Tibba trek kitna lamba hai?
Lagbhag 16 km aana-jaana, 2 din me.
Tehri me rafting kahan hoti hai?
Shivpuri aur Kaudiyala me.
Tehri me akeli ladki ghoom sakti hai?
Aam taur par haan; raat ke safar aur sunsaan raaste par savdhani; emergency me 112 aur 181.
टिहरी में क्या-क्या देखने लायक है?
टिहरी झील और बांध, डोबरा-चांठी पुल, देवप्रयाग संगम, सुरकंडा देवी, कुंजापुरी और चंद्रबदनी मंदिर, धनौल्टी, कानाताल, नाग टिब्बा और खतलिंग ग्लेशियर।
धनौल्टी में कितने दिन रुकना चाहिए?
आम तौर पर 2 दिन (1 रात) काफी है; इसके साथ सुरकंडा और कानाताल भी देख सकते हैं।
नाग टिब्बा ट्रेक कितना मुश्किल है?
आसान से मध्यम। ऊंचाई लगभग 3,000 मीटर, रास्ता लगभग 16 किमी (आना-जाना), आम तौर पर 2 दिन में।
टिहरी में राफ्टिंग कहां होती है?
शिवपुरी और कौड़ियाला से ऋषिकेश की तरफ गंगा में। मानसून में आम तौर पर बंद रहती है।
क्या टिहरी घूमने के लिए 3 दिन काफी हैं?
3 दिन में न्यू टिहरी, टिहरी झील, डोबरा-चांठी पुल, धनौल्टी और सुरकंडा देवी देख सकते हैं। ट्रेक या देवप्रयाग के लिए 1-2 दिन और रखें।
