Rudraprayag ka khaana Garhwali rasoi ka dil hai. Yahan khaana swad se zyada taakat, thand aur mausam ke hisab se banta hai. Neeche likhe kuch vyanjan Garhwal aur Kumaon dono me milte hain, kuch sirf Garhwal me. Jahan naam ya vidhi thodi alag hoti hai, wahan humne use saaf likh diya hai.

Rudraprayag: Zile ke Prasiddh Mukhya Vyanjan

Kafuli, Phaanu, Chainsoo, Thechwani aur Baaki

Kafuli (palak aur methi ka gaadha saag), Phaanu (bhigi daal ka gadha vyanjan), Chainsoo (bhuni hui kaali urad daal ka saag), aur Thechwani (kuti hui mooli ya aloo ki sabzi) Garhwali rasoi ke sabse prasiddh vyanjan hain.

Chainsoo kya hota hai?
Bhuni hui kaali urad daal ko pees kar lohe ki kadhai me banaya jane wala Garhwali vyanjan.
Kafuli me kya dalte hain?
Palak aur methi (kabhi-kabhi pyaaz ya aate ka ghol), lohe ki kadhai me dheemi aanch par.
Phaanu kya hota hai?
Bhigoyi aur peesi hui daal (jaise gahat ya masoor) se bana gadha Garhwali vyanjan.

Chainsoo Banane ka Aasan Tareeka

Aasan bhasha me:

  • Kaali urad daal ko sookhi kadhai (lohe ki) me halki aanch par bhunein jab tak khushboo na aaye
  • Thanda hone par mota-mota pees lein
  • Lohe ki kadhai me sarson tel garam karein, jakhiya ya jeera aur hari mirch daalein
  • Peesi daal-daal kar thoda pani milayein aur 25 se 30 minute dheemi aanch par pakayein
  • Namak, haldi, hing daalein aur chawal ke saath parosein

Rudraprayag: Paramparik Roti aur Chawal

Mandua ki Roti, Gahat ke Parathe aur Jhangore ka Bhaat

Mandua ki roti kaale-bhoore rang ki hoti hai aur iska swad thoda meetha-kadva hota hai. Gahat ke parathe, Jhangore ka bhaat aur Gahat ras-bhaat sardi me khoob khaye jate hain.

Mandua ka aata sardi me sharir ko garmi deta hai. Ghee ya makkhan ke saath garam mandua ki roti pahad ki sabse purani aur mazedaar roti maani jati hai. Mandua me calcium aur fibre zyada hota hai.

Jhangora (Madira) ek chhota mota anaj hai jiska bhaat halka, naram aur pachne me aasan hota hai. Isse kheer bhi banti hai.

Rudraprayag: Chutney, Raita, Achaar aur Pisyun Loon

Chutney aur Raita

Bhangjeera chutney (perilla beej se), Bhang ke beej ki chutney, Timur chutney aur Pahadi Kheere ka Raita Garhwali khaane ki shaan hain. Yeh sabhi sil-batta par peesi jati hain.

Pahadi Raita ki khaas baat yeh hai ki isme kuti hui rai (sarson ke dane) milayi jati hai jo naak me chadhne wali teekhi tarang deti hai. Pahadi kheera aam kheere se bada aur peela ya hara hota hai.

Pisyun Loon

Pisyun Loon pahadi namak hai jise sil-batta par hari mirch, lehsun ke patte, dhaniya, jeera aur timur ke saath pees kar banaya jata hai. Sardi me yeh roti, kheere aur malta ke saath bahut mazedaar lagta hai.

Malta, santra ya kheera pisyun loon ke saath khana pahad ki dhoop me baith kar sardi ka sabse mazedaar nasta maana jata hai.

Rudraprayag: Jungle ki Sabziyan aur Mausami Saag

Kandali, Linguda aur Jungli Phal

Kandali (Bicchu Ghas / Stinging Nettle) ka saag Garhwal me bahut prasiddh hai. Linguda (pahadi fern ki kopal), Kankoda aur Jungli phal jaise Kaphal, Hisalu, Kilmora aur Bedu mausam me jungle se milte hain.

Bicchu Ghas ko chhune par jalan hoti hai, isliye isse nikalne ke liye dastane ya sookhi lakdi ka istemal karte hain. Pakane ke baad jalan khatam ho jati hai aur sabzi me lohe ki bharpoor matra hoti hai.

Rudraprayag: Paramparik Mithai aur Tyohar ke Pakwan

Arsa, Singal, Pua aur Baaki

Arsa (chawal aur gud ka bana meetha pakwan, sarson tel me tala hua), Singal, Pua, Rot (mota meetha roth) aur Gulgule pahad ke shubh avsaron aur tyohar ke prasiddh pakwan hain.

Arsa shadi, tyohar aur devta ko bhog lagane ke liye banaya jata hai. Rot ek mota mitha roth hota hai jo kisi manokamna poori hone par devta ko chadhaya jata hai. Singori aur Bal Mithai mukhya taur par Kumaon (Almora ki) mithai hain, jo ab Garhwal ke bazaar me bhi milti hain.

Arsa kya hota hai?
Bheege chawal ke aate aur gud ko milakar sarson ke tel me tala jane wala pahadi pakwan.

Rot kya hota hai? Gehun ke aate aur gud se bana mota meetha roth jo devta ko chadhaya jata hai.

Rudraprayag: Paramparik Peya

Buransh Sharbat, Malta Juice aur Gud-Adrak Chai

Buransh ke phool ka sharbat (squash) garmi me dil ki sehat aur thandak ke liye jana jata hai. Gud-adrak ki chai sardi me pahadi dhabon ki jaan hai.

Chhyang jaise khamir se bane paramparik pay mukhya roop se seemant ke Bhotia ilakon me bante hain. Rudraprayag me iski parampara aam nahi hai.

Rudraprayag: Pahadi Rasoi ka Dhancha aur Bartan

Lohe ki Kadhai, Sil-Batta aur Kansa ki Thali

Pahadi rasoi me lohe ki kadhai, sil-batta, kansa ki thali, bhaddu (bada pital ya tambe ka bartan) aur mitti ka chulha pramukh hote hain. Chulhe ke upar ek lakdi ka machan hota hai jise dhumala kehte hain.

Lohe ki kadhai me banne wale saag aur daal me iron ki matra badh jati hai. Sil-batta par peesi hui chutney ka swad mixer wali chutney se alag hota hai. Dhumala me beej, lehsun, makka aur geeli lakdi sukhayi jati hai (Rudraprayag: Anaj Store Karne ki Purani Engineering).

Rudraprayag: Kedarnath Yatra Marg par Kya Khayein?

Yatra marg (Rishikesh se Rudraprayag, Agastyamuni, Guptkashi, Sonprayag) par chai, paratha, daal-chawal, Maggi, pakode aur seasonal local khaana mil jata hai. Kedarnath trek ke raaste me garam chai, soup aur khichdi ke thele milte hain. Dar (rate) saal ke hisab se badalti hai, isliye kisi ek dar ko aakhri na maanen.

Rudraprayag me kya khana chahiye?
Mandua ki roti, Kafuli, Chainsoo, Gahat ki daal, Jhangore ki kheer aur Arsa.
Pahadi khana swasthya ke liye kaisa hota hai?
Mandua, jhangora aur gahat jaise anaj fibre, calcium aur protein me achhe maane jate hain. Kisi khaas bimari me khaan-paan badalne se pehle doctor se salah lein.

Rudraprayag: Vistaar se: Pahadi Khaane ki Gehri Jankari

Pahadi khaana sirf swad nahi, ek poori jeevan-paddhati hai. Isme wahi anaj, daalein aur sabziyan aati hain jo pahad ki mitti aur mausam me ugti hain. Is hisse me hamne un cheezon ko joda hai jo Uttarakhand ke sarkari pehchan (GI tag) wale khaane se judi hain, saath me yatriyon ke liye khane ki savdhaniyan aur gharon me khana banane ke tarike.

41 se 42: Mote Anaj aur Daalein (Vistaar)

Jin Pahadi Anaj aur Daalon ko Sarkari Pehchan Mili

December 2023 me Uttarakhand ko ek hi din me 18 naye GI tag mile, jisme Mandua, Jhangora, Gahat, Kala Bhat, Chaulai (Ramdana), Lal Chawal, Pahari Toor Dal aur Buransh Sharbat shamil hain. Inse pehle 9 aur Uttarakhand ke utpaad ko GI tag mil chuka tha, yaani kul 27.

GI tag ka matlab hai ki yeh utpaad ek khaas kshetra se juda maana jata hai aur uski nakal par kanooni rok lag sakti hai. Neeche ki table me wo utpaad hain jo Rudraprayag ki rasoi aur kheti se seedhe jude hain. Dhyan rahe ki yeh GI tag poore Uttarakhand ke utpaad ke liye hain, kisi ek zile ke liye nahi.

Kya Mandua aur Gahat Sachmuch Faydemand Hain

Mandua aur jhangora jaise mote anaj me fibre aur kai poshak tatva hote hain, aur mandua me calcium ka accha strot maana jata hai. Gahat ek protein wali daal hai, jo sardi ke khane me bahut istemal hoti hai. Ye sab pahadi khaane ke achhe hisse hain.

Par ek zaroori baat. Kisi bhi khane ko bimari ka ilaaj batana sahi nahi hai. Jaise gahat ki daal ke bare me pathri (kidney stone) ke ilaaj ka koi pakka vaigyanik praman nahi hai (Rudraprayag: Pahadi Vaidya aur Gharelu Nuskhe). Agar kisi ko kidney ki bimari, pathri ya madhumeh hai, to apne khane me badlaav se pehle doctor se zaroor poochhein.

Mandua ki Roti Banane ka Parampragat Tarika

Aasan bhasha me:

  • Mandua ka aata ek bartan me lein aur thoda namak milayein.
  • Garam paani dheere-dheere daalkar aata goondhein. Mandua ka aata gluten-free hota hai, isliye goondhne par woh aam aate jaisa nahi bandhta.
  • Haath ya belan se gol aur motti roti banayein.
  • Tawe par dono taraf halka sekein, phir seedhi aag par phulayein.
  • Garam roti par ghee ya makkhan lagakar sabzi ya chutney ke saath parosein.

Pahad me kai gharon me mandua ke aate me thoda gehun ka aata milakar bhi roti banate hain, jisse woh jaldi tootti nahi. Yeh sirf ek gharelu tarika hai, aur ghati ke hisab se alag ho sakta hai.

43 se 44: Chutney aur Jungle ki Sabziyan (Vistaar)

Bhang ki Chutney: Savdhani

Pahadi bhang ki chutney sirf bhang ke beej (hemp seeds) bhunkar pees kar banayi jati hai. Isme nashe wali patti ya phool nahi hote. Phir bhi is chutney ke bare me shabdon aur kanoon ka dhyan rakhein.

Bhang ke beej poshak hote hain aur pahad me sadiyon se chutney ke roop me khaye jate hain. Par Bharat me nashe wale bhang (cannabis) ke kai roop kanoon se pratibandhit hain. Isliye:

  • Nashe wale bhang ya ganja ke bare me kanooni jankari ke bina koi bhi cheez na banayein na bechein.
  • Yatriyon ko khilate samay yeh saaf batayein ki chutney sirf beej se bani hai.
  • Bachchon aur garbhvati mahilayon ko naye khane aadatan dene se pehle doctor se poochhein.

Jungle ki Sabziyan Todne ke Niyam

Jungle ki sabziyan jaise lingda (fern), kandali (nettle) aur timla jaan-pehchaan wale logon ke saath hi todni chahiye. Galat paudha ya jahrili mushroom khane se jaan ka khatra ho sakta hai.

Jungle ki sabziyan mausam ke saath milti hain aur gaon ki purani parampara ka hissa hain. Par:

  • Sirf wahi paudhe todein jinhe aap pakki tarah pehchante hain.
  • Naye ya anjaan mushroom ko kabhi na khayein. Kai jahrili mushroom khane jaise dikhte hain.
  • Sanrakshit kshetra aur Kedarnath Wildlife Sanctuary me paudhe todna kanoon ke khilaf ho sakta hai (Rudraprayag: National Park, Wildlife Sanctuary aur Conservation Reserve).
  • Lingda ko khane se pehle achhi tarah pakana chahiye, kyunki kuch fern kaccha khane par nuksan karte hain. Pakane ke tarike gaon ke buzurg se seekhein.
  • Kandali todte samay dastane pehnein, aur use pakane ke baad hi khayein (Rudraprayag: Bichhu Ghas aur Daam Therapy).

Tyohar ke Pakwan (Vistaar)

Tyohar ke Hisab se Khana

Pahadi tyohar kheti ke mausam se jude hain, isliye har tyohar ka apna khaas pakwan hota hai. Har ghati aur gaon me yeh alag ho sakta hai, isliye neeche diye gaye khane aam parampara ke roop me hain.

46 se 47: Peya aur Rasoi (Vistaar)

Garmi ka Sharbat aur Sardi ki Chai

Buransh sharbat garmi me thanda aur taazgi bhara peya hai, jise Uttarakhand ko GI tag mila hai. Isme buransh ke laal phoolon ka ras hota hai. Buransh ke phool ko khud todkar ras banane se pehle yaad rakhein ki buransh sanrakshit kshetron me bhi milta hai, aur sab phoolon ko khaya nahi jata. Doosri taraf, sardi me pahad me adrak, gud aur ghar ki bani chai ki parampara hai.

Lohe ki Kadhai aur Pahadi Bartan

Pahadi rasoi me lohe ki kadhai, silbatta (patthar ka peesne ka saadhan) aur pital ya tambe ke bartan aam hain. Lohe ki kadhai me khaana banane se khaane me thoda loh tatva aa sakta hai, jo kai logon ke liye acchi baat maani jati hai. Tambe aur pital ke bartan me khatti cheezein rakhne se bachna chahiye, aur unhe achhi tarah ghisna aur kalai karwana zaroori hota hai. Silbatta me peesi hui chutney ka swad alag hota hai, jo mixer me nahi aata.

Yatriyon ke liye Khane ki Savdhani (Vistaar)

Kedarnath jaise unchai wale ilake me bhaari, tel wala khana pachne me mushkil ho sakta hai. Halka, garm aur taaza khana khayein, aur paani zyada piyein. Sirf saaf-suthri dukanon se hi khayein.

Yatra me khane ke liye kuch zaroori sujhav:

  • Subah chadhai se pehle halka naashta karein, jaise poha, daliya ya roti-sabzi.
  • Raaste me thoda-thoda khate rahein aur paani peete rahein.
  • Bahut zyada chai ya tel wali cheezein na khayein, kyunki unchai par pet kharab hone ki sambhavna hoti hai.
  • Agar aapko kisi cheez se allergy hai (jaise soyabean, kyunki bhatt soyabean hi hai, ya moongfali), to khaana chunte samay dukandaar ko batayein.
  • Mandua ki roti ya jhangora ka bhaat jaisi sthaniya cheezein khaayein, par pehle aajmayein ki aapko raas aata hai ya nahi.
  • Baasi aur khule me rakha hua khana na khayein.
  • Doodh, dahi aur khaane ke saamaan ki taazgi dekhein.
  • Hand sanitizer ya saaf paani se haath dhoyein.
