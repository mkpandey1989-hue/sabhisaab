Is hisse mein: Jansankhya aur janjatiyan, samajik dhancha aur surname, boliyan, pahadi ghar, pehnawa aur gehne, aipan aur lok-kala, geet, nritya, vadya yantra, sanskar aur shaadi, tyohar aur mele. Sanskriti ek jeevit cheez hai: ek gaon ki riti doosre gaon me alag ho sakti hai. Yahan likhi baatein “aam taur par” ki hain, aur kisi samaj ya parivar ki riti ko sahi ya galat nahi bataya ja raha.

Pithoragarh: Aabadi, Samuday aur Anusuchit Janjati

Census ke Aankde: Aabadi, Ling Anupat, Saksharta aur Gaon-Shahar

Census 2011 ke anusar Pithoragarh ki jansankhya 4,83,439 thi, jisme 2,39,306 purush aur 2,44,133 mahilayein shamil thi, yani mahilayein zyada thi. Is ka bada karan pahad ke purushon ka naukri aur fauj ke liye bahar jana mana jata hai.

Mool Samuday: Shauka (Bhotia), Raji (Van Rawat) aur Anya

Pithoragarh me kai khaas samuday rehte hain: Shauka (Bhotia), Raji (Van Rawat), tathaa Kumaoni Brahmin, Rajput aur Shilpkar samaj.

Shauka (Bhotia): Johar, Darma, Vyans aur Chaudans ghati me rehne wala vyaparik samuday, jo purane samay me Tibet se vyapar karta tha. Inke upsamuday ghati ke naam se jane jate hain, jaise Johari, Darmiya, Byansi aur Chaudansi. (Ye Anusuchit Janjati (ST) me aate hain; kis upsamuday ki kya shreni hai, ye sarkari suchi se pushti karein.)

Raji (Van Rawat): Ek chhoti janjati, jo jungle aur nadi ke paas ghumantu ya ardh-ghumantu jeevan jeeti thi, aur aaj kuch gaon me basi hai. Ise Vishesh Roop se Kamzor Janjati (PVTG) me ginte hain.

Har samuday ki apni boli, riti aur itihas hai, aur use samman ke saath likhna zaroori hai.

Pithoragarh: Samaj ki Banawat, Upnaam aur Gotra

Pithoragarh ke Aam Upnaam (Surname)

Pithoragarh me aam taur par milne wale surname alag-alag samajo se jude hain, aur kai surname gaon ya ilake ke naam se bhi bane hain.

SamudayKuch aam surname (aam taur par)
BrahminJoshi, Pant, Pandey, Bhatt, Upadhyay, Tiwari, Papnoi
RajputBisht, Rawat, Dhami, Mahar, Karki, Bohra, Chand
Shilpkar / Anusuchit JatiTamta, Arya, Ram, Das, Prasad
Shauka (Bhotia)Pangtey, Rawat, Nabiyal, Gunjyal, Garbyal, Nitwal, Martolia, Dharmashaktu

Ye ek aam suchi hai, poori nahi. Surname kisi bhi vyakti ya samuday ke baare me koi ek rai banane ke liye nahi hai.

Gotra aur kul-devta: Kumaoni samaj me gotra aur kul-devta ki parampara mahatvapurn hai, aur har parivar ka apna “Thaan” (devta ka sthan) hota hai. Is par Pithoragarh: Vaidya parampara aur aastha me vistar se hai.

Pithoragarh: Bhasha, Up-boliyan aur Roz ke Shabd

Pithoragarh mein Boli Jaane Wali Boliyan

Pithoragarh me Hindi aur Kumaoni mukhya bhashayein hain, aur Kumaoni ki kai upboliyan jaise Soriyali, Askoti, Seeraali aur Johari bolte hain. Iske alawa Johar, Darma, Vyans aur Chaudans me Rung (Rang) samuday ki Tibeto-Burman bhasha-parivar ki boliyan (jaise Darmiya, Byansi, Chaudansi) bhi prachalit hain. (Boliyon ki exact suchi aur vargikaran bhashavigyan ki kitabon se pushti karein.)

BoliAam taur par kahan
SoriyaliSaur ghati (Pithoragarh shehar ke aas-paas)
AskotiAskot ka kshetra
SeeraaliSeera kshetra (Didihat-Dharamghar ke aas-paas)
JohariJohar ghati (Munsiari, Milam)
Darmiya, Byansi, ChaudansiDarma, Vyans, Chaudans ghati

Kuch roz ke Kumaoni shabd (aam taur par):

  • Kaisa chhai? (Kaise ho?)
  • Bhal chhu. (Main theek hu.)
  • Ghar jaye. (Ghar jao.)
  • Bhal lagyo. (Accha laga.)
  • Dhanyavad / Raam-Raam. (Namaste ke roop me.)

(Ye vakya Kumaoni ke aam roop ke hain; upboli me thode alag ho sakte hain.)

Pithoragarh: Vernacular Pahadi Architecture (Pahadi Ghar)

Pathal ki Chhat, Patthar-Lakdi ka Ghar & Bakhli

Pahadi ghar patthar, lakdi aur mitti se bante hain, aur chhat par kai jagah patthar ke “pathal” (slate) lagaye jate hain. Ye ghar thande ilake me garam aur garam ilake me thande rehte hain, aur bhukamp me kam nuksan ke liye jaane jate hain. (Ye anubhav aur sthaniya jaankari par adharit hai; vaigyanik jaanch alag se dekhein.)

Mukhya hissa:

  • Kholi / Aangan: Ghar ka aangan, jahan tyohar aur samuhik kaam hote hain.
  • Chhajja / Dehri: Lakdi ki naqqashi wali chaukhat aur khidki.
  • Bakhli: Kai ghar ek hi pangti me jude hue (kuch jagah), jo sath-sath rehne wale pariwaron ke liye hota hai.
  • Mandir kona: Ghar ke andar chhota sa mandir ya devta ka sthan.

Aaj ki sthiti: Naye ghar ab cement aur concrete se ban rahe hain, aur purane patthar-lakdi ke ghar kam hote ja rahe hain. Seemant ke ilake me purane ghar heritage ya homestay ke roop me bacha kar chalane ki koshish bhi hui hai.

Pithoragarh: Paramparik Pehnawa aur Kapde

Mahilaon aur Purushon ka Pehnava

Kumaoni mahilaon ka paramparik pehnawa pichhaura (laal-peeli chunni), ghaghra aur angra hai, aur tyohar ya shaadi me ye khaas roop se pehna jata hai. Pichhaura (Pichora) par aam taur par swastik aur anya shubh chinh bane hote hain. (Chinh aur rang ka mahatva gaon ke hisab se badal sakta hai; pushti karein.)

PehnawaKaun pehnta haiKab
PichhauraKumaoni mahilayeinShaadi, puja, tyohar
Ghaghra-AngraMahilayeinParamparik avsar
Kurta-Pajama, TopiPurushTyohar aur shaadi
Oon ke kapdeShauka aur unchai wale ilake ke logSardi me roz ke liye

Shauka kapde aur oon: Johar-Darma-Vyans ki ghatiyon me oon ki bunai bahut mashhoor rahi hai: oon ke kambal, dan (galeeche), thulma aur anya shaal. In par rang-birange “pattern” aur “bunai” khaas pehchan hain. (Naam aur design ke baare me Pithoragarh: Arthvyavastha aur shilp, Pithoragarh: Hathkargha, GI-Tag Utpad aur Sthaniya Hastshilp me alag se likha jayega.)

Pithoragarh: Paramparik Pahadi Gehne

Nath, Gulbandh, Hansuli & Aur Gehne

Kumaoni mahilayein paramparik gehno me Nath (badi nose ring), Gulbandh (gale ka gehna), Hansuli (chandi ka kada-jaisa gale ka gehna), Timaniya aur Bichhua pehnti hain. (Gehno ke asli naam, design aur istemal ilake aur samaj ke hisab se alag ho sakte hain; yahan aam taur par prachalit naam diye gaye hain.)

Shauka gehne: Seemant ki mahilayein kai baar chandi aur moonga-moti ke bhaari gehne pehnti thin, jo parivar ki samriddhi ka pratik mane jate the.

Nath ka mahatva: Shaadi me dulhan ki Nath ek khaas pehchan hai, aur kai ghar me ye parampara se parivar me aage badhi hai.

Pithoragarh: Anushthan ki Lok-Kala: Aipan, Jyunti aur Lakdi ka Kaam

Aipan / Eepan Motifs

Aipan Kumaon ki paramparik rangoli kala hai, jo geru (laal mitti) ke aadhar par chawal ke lep (biswar) se banayi jati hai, aur puja, tyohar aur sanskar me banayi jati hai.

Mukhya aakriti (aam taur par):

  • Lakshmi Chauki: Diwali par Lakshmi ke aasan ke roop me.
  • Saraswati Chauki: Vidya aur puja ke liye.
  • Shiv Peeth: Shiv puja ke liye.
  • Ashtadal Kamal: Aath patti wala kamal.

Kaise banta hai: Pehle geru se zameen ya deewar lipi jati hai, phir chawal ko peesa hua ghol ungliyon se laga kar design banaya jata hai. (Aipan kala ko kai jagah GI ya heritage pehchan ki charcha hai; sthiti ko pushti karein.)

Jyunti aur Lakdi ki Kaarigari

Jyunti ek paramparik chitra hai jo janam ya shubh avsar par ghar ki deewar par banayi jati hai. Pahad me lakdi par naqqashi (chaukhat, darwaze, khidki) ka bhi purana kaam raha hai, jo ab dheere-dheere kam ho raha hai.

Pithoragarh: Lok-Sangeet, Lok-Gaathayein aur Gayan Parampara

Jagar, Pawada, Khuded Geet, Chhapeli & Jhora

Pithoragarh aur Kumaon ke lok-sangeet me Jagar (devta ko bulane ke geet), Pawada (veer gatha), Khuded (door gaye apno ki yaad ke geet), Chhapeli aur Jhora jaise geet mashhoor hain.

Pawada / BhadauVeeron aur ladai ki kahaniyon ke geet
Khuded geetDoor gaye pati ya mait ki yaad me mahilaon ke geet

JhoraGol ghera bana kar gaye jaane wale samuhik geet-nritya

(Har geet ki ek hi parinibhasha nahi hai; ilake aur gayakon ke hisab se swaroop badalta hai.)

Pithoragarh: Paramparik Lok Nritya

Chholiya, Jhora, Chanchari & Hurkiya

Pithoragarh ka sabse mashhoor lok-nritya Chholiya (Chhaliya) hai, jisme talwar-dhaal ke saath yuddh-sa nritya hota hai, aur ye mukhya roop se shaadi ki baraat me kiya jata hai. (Chholiya ka itihas Chand shasan ya Katyuri kaal se jodkar bataya jata hai; ye ek aam manyata hai, pushti karein.)

ChholiyaShaadi ki baraat me, talwar-dhaal ke saath

Hurkiya Baul / Hurka nrityaHurka (damru jaisa vadya) ke saath kheti ke geet-nritya

Pithoragarh: Paramparik Vadya Yantra aur Bajgi/Auji Samuday

Dhol-Damau, Hurka, Ransingha, Masakbin & Others

Pahad ke mukhya vadya yantra Dhol, Damau, Hurka, Ransingha (S-akaar ka turhi), Bhankora aur Masakbin (bagpipe jaise) hain. Dhol-Damau shubh avsar aur Jagar me bajaya jata hai.

Auji / Bajgi samuday: Ye samuday paramparik roop se Dhol-Damau bajane ki kala me nipun mana jata hai, aur Dhol Sagar jaise paramparik gyan ko aage badhata hai. (Dhol Sagar me taalon ki sankhya jaise daave ke liye sanskritik shodh pushti karein.) Hum is samuday ko samman ke saath yaad karte hain, kyunki unke bina pahad ke tyohar adhure hain.

Pithoragarh: Pahadi Life-Cycle Rituals (Sanskars) & Weddings

Namkaran, Janeu, Pahadi Shaadi ki Rasmein

Pahadi shaadi me kai paramparik rasmein hoti hain, jaise Ganesh puja, Dhuli Arghya, Kanyadan aur Saptapadi, aur har ilake me inka thoda alag roop hota hai. Baraat me Dhol-Damau, aur kai jagah Chholiya nritya bhi hota hai.

Jeevan ke mukhya sanskar:

  • Namkaran: Bachche ka naam rakhne ka sanskar.
  • Annaprashan: Pehle anaj ka bhog.
  • Janeu (Upanayan): Brahmin aur kuch anya samajon me.
  • Vivah (Shaadi): Ganesh puja se lekar vidai tak.
  • Antim sanskar: Nadi ke kinare ya shamshan ghat par, riti ke anusar.

(Ye aam sanskar hain; samaj, parivar aur ilake ke hisab se riti badalti hai.)

Pithoragarh: Mausami Tyohar

Phool Dei, Harela, Bhitauli, Ghughutiya & Uttarayani

Pithoragarh aur Kumaon ke mukhya mausami tyohar Phool Dei, Harela, Bhitauli, Ghughutiya aur Uttarayani hain.

Hilljatra: Pithoragarh ke Kumaon aur aas-paas ke ilake me “Hilljatra” naam ka ek mukhauta-natya wala paramparik tyohar mana jata hai, jo kheti aur varsha ke saath jodkar dekha jata hai. (Iski tithi, itihas aur rasm ke bare me sthaniya jaankari aur sarkari paryatan source se pushti karein.)

Kandali: Chaudans ghati ke Rung (Rang) samuday me “Kandali” naam ke paudhe ke khilne par kuch saal me ek bada mahotsav hone ki baat suni jati hai.

Pithoragarh: Historic Melas, Jatras & Doli Processions

Jauljibi, Thal, Pancheshwar & Anya Mele

Pithoragarh ke sabse prasiddh mele Jauljibi Mela, Thal Mela (Baisakhi), Pancheshwar Mela aur kai dharmik jatrayein hain, jinme Jauljibi Mela vyapar aur sanskriti ke liye jaana jata hai.

Jauljibi Mela ka itihas: Is mele me purane samay me Nepal aur Tibet ke vyapari bhi aate the, aur ye seemapar ke vyapar ka ek bada kendra tha. (Mele ki tithi aur sthiti har saal zila prashasan se pushti karein.)

Hilljatra: Saur Ghati ka Mukhauta Utsav (Kumaur Gaon)

Seedha jawab: Hilljatra Pithoragarh ki Saur ghati ka prasiddh mukhauta (mask) lok-natya utsav hai, jo mukhya roop se Kumaur gaon aur aas-paas ke gaon (jaise Kanalichhina aur Askot kshetra) me Bhado mahine me, dhaan ki ropai ke baad manaya jata hai. Isme kalakar lakdi ke mukhaute pehen kar bail, halwaha, hiran aur kheti ke patra bante hain, aur "Lakhiya Bhoot" sabse mukhya patra hota hai (pushti karein: is saal ki tithi aur gaon-wise vidhi sthaniya samiti se confirm karein).

BaatJaankari
KahanKumaur gaon (Saur ghati) aur Pithoragarh ke kai gaon
KabBhado (Bhadrapad) mahina, Aathon/Gaura-Maheshwar parv ke aas-paas
Kya hota haiMukhaute pehne kalakar kheti, pashupalan aur gaon ke jeevan ka swang karte hain
Lakhiya BhootHilljatra ka mukhya patra, jise Shiv ka gan aur khushhali ka pratik mana jata hai
UtpattiLok-katha ke anusar yeh parampara Nepal ke Seemant (Sor-Seti) kshetra se aayi
Himachal se tulnaMukhauta parampara Kullu-Lahaul ke mukhauta nritya aur Chamoli ke Ramman (UNESCO) se milti-julti

Hilljatra sirf tamasha nahi, balki kheti ka utsav hai: dhaan ropne ke baad gaon ek saath khushiyan manata hai aur achhi fasal ki kaamna karta hai. Isliye isme bail, hal aur halwahe ke mukhaute sabse zyada dikhte hain. Lakdi ke ye mukhaute purane samay se parivaron me sambhal kar rakhe jate hain.

Pancheshwar Mela kab lagta hai?

Chholiya kya hai?
Kumaon ka paramparik talwar-dhaal nritya, jo mukhya roop se shaadi ki baraat me hota hai.
Harela tyohar kab aata hai?
Shravan mahine ke shuru me (July ke aas-paas); saat anaj bo kar hariyali manate hain.
