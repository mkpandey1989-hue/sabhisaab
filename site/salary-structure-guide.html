Salary Structure Samjhein: Basic, HRA, PF Aur CTC

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~8 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Salary slip me kai naam hote hain — basic, HRA, special allowance, PF, professional tax — aur zyadatar log inhe theek se nahi samajhte. Par har component aapke tax, in-hand aur future savings par asar daalta hai. Ye topic guide poore salary structure ko ek-ek karke, aasaan bhasha me kholti hai taaki aap apni slip ko sahi mayne me samajh sakein.

Basic salary — sabki jad

Basic salary aapke poore salary structure ki neenv hai. Zyadatar doosre component isi ke percentage me tay hote hain. Aam taur par basic, gross ka 40-50% hota hai.

Basic zyada hone ke fayde aur nuksan dono hain. Fayda — zyada PF aur gratuity jama hota hai (future bachat). Nuksan — PF zyada katne se in-hand thoda kam. Basic poori tarah taxable hota hai. Isliye basic ka level aapke long-term saving aur monthly cash dono ko tay karta hai — ye samajhna zaroori hai.

HRA — ghar ka kiraya

House Rent Allowance (HRA) un logon ke liye tax bachata hai jo kiraye par rehte hain. Ye aam taur par basic ka 40-50% hota hai. Agar aap rent dete hain, to HRA ka ek hissa tax-free ho sakta hai (old regime me).

HRA chhoot teen me se sabse kam wali hoti hai: (1) actual HRA mila, (2) metro me basic ka 50% / non-metro 40%, (3) rent − basic ka 10%. Rent receipt aur landlord ka PAN (agar rent ₹1 lakh/saal se zyada) zaroori hote hain. Dhyaan rahe — new regime me HRA chhoot nahi milti.

Special allowance aur baaki

Basic aur HRA ke baad jo bacha, wo aksar special allowance me daal diya jaata hai — ye poori tarah taxable hota hai aur "balancing figure" ki tarah kaam karta hai.

Kuch aur component: LTA (Leave Travel Allowance — travel par tax chhoot, kuch shartein), conveyance/telephone reimbursement, aur food coupons. Ye chhote tax fayde de sakte hain, par har company me nahi hote. In sabka jod hi aapki gross salary banata hai, jahan se katauti shuru hoti hai.

PF aur gratuity — future bachat

EPF me aapka 12% (basic ka) katta hai aur company bhi 12% daalti hai — dono aapke retirement fund me jama hote hain, 8%+ byaj ke saath. Ye salary slip me katauti dikhti hai par asal me aapki bachat hai.

Gratuity tab milti hai jab aap ek company me 5 saal poore karte hain — ye CTC me dikhti hai par monthly in-hand me nahi. Ye ek tarah ka "loyalty reward" hai jo naukri chhodne par ek saath milta hai. Dono milkar aapki majboot future savings banate hain, jise log aksar nazarandaz karte hain.

Sab jod kar: gross se in-hand

Ab poori tasveer: CTC me sab kuch (aapki salary + employer PF + gratuity + bonus). Gross = CTC me se employer PF, gratuity aur variable hata kar. In-hand = gross me se EPF, professional tax aur income tax ghata kar.

Kram: CTC → (employer PF, gratuity, bonus hatao) → Gross → (EPF, PT, tax hatao) → In-hand

Isi liye ₹10 lakh CTC ka in-hand ₹65-70k/mah ke aaspaas ho sakta hai, ₹83k nahi. Salary structure samajh kar aap job offer behtar compare kar sakte hain aur sahi component (jaise HRA, LTA) se tax bhi bacha sakte hain.

Structure samajh me aa gaya to agla kadam hai apne asli number nikalna. CTC to In-Hand par apna offer daaliye — poora breakup saamne aa jaayega. Aur agar kiraye par rehte hain to HRA Exemption zaroor dekhiye; yahi wo hissa hai jise log sabse zyada chhod dete hain.

Basic salary chhoti rakhne se kya-kya badal jaata hai

Salary slip me basic sirf ek line nahi hai — usi ek number par kai cheezein tiki hoti hain. Company basic kam rakhti hai to aaj ka in-hand thoda badh jaata hai, par aage ka faayda ghat jaata hai.

Isliye "special allowance" ka bada hissa dhyaan se dekhna chahiye. Kai company CTC ka bada bhaag special allowance me rakhti hain — wo poora taxable hota hai aur usse na PF banta hai, na gratuity, na HRA ki chhoot badhti hai. Aam taur par basic CTC ka 40% se 50% hona santulit maana jaata hai. Naya offer aane par apne purane aur naye break-up me sirf CTC nahi, basic ki tulna zaroor kariye.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Basic salary kitni honi chahiye?

Aam taur par gross ka 40-50%. Zyada basic = zyada PF/gratuity (future bachat) par thoda kam in-hand.

HRA chhoot kaise milti hai?

Old regime me, agar rent dete hain — teen me se sabse kam value tax-free. Rent receipt zaroori.

Special allowance par tax lagta hai?

Haan, ye poori tarah taxable hota hai.

Gratuity kab milti hai?

Ek company me 5 saal poore hone par, naukri chhodne ke samay ek saath.

CTC se in-hand kam kyun?

CTC me employer PF, gratuity aur bonus hote hain jo monthly haath me nahi aate.
