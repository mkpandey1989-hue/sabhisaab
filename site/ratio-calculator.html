Recipe ho, business ka profit-sharing ho, ya map ka scale — anupaat (ratio) har jagah kaam aata hai. Yahan aap ratio ko simplify kar sakte hain, ya kisi rakam ko diye anupaat me baant sakte hain. Do number daaliye ya rakam baantiye — jawab turant. Chahe partnership ka profit ho ya kitchen ki recipe, sahi anupaat samajhna paisa aur mehnat dono bachata hai.

Ganit kaise chalta hai

Ratio simplify karne ke liye dono number ka HCF (sabse bada common factor) nikaal kar dono ko usse bhaag dete hain. Rakam baantne ke liye pehle dono hisson ka jod nikaalte hain, phir har hisse ka anupaat lagate hain.

Simplify: 8:12 = (8÷4):(12÷4) = 2:3
Baantna: ₹5000 ko 8:12 me = pehle 8/20 × 5000, doosre 12/20 × 5000

Ratio ka matlab "kitne guna" hota hai, na ki asli matra. Isi liye 8:12 aur 2:3 barabar hain — dono ek hi anupaat dikhate hain.

Ratio ki sabse badi taakat ye hai ki wo matra badalne par bhi wahi rehta hai — 2:3 chahe rupaye 500 ka ho ya 5 lakh ka, anupaat same. Isi liye recipe ko double karna, business me nivesh ke hisaab se profit baantna, ya map ka scale samajhna — sab ratio se hota hai. Do cheezon ka ratio nikaalte waqt dhyan rakhein ki dono ek hi unit me hon (dono gram, ya dono rupaye) — warna anupaat galat aayega. Ratio ko percentage me badalna ho to pehle use fraction banayein, phir 100 se guna karein.

Ek aam istemaal partnership me hota hai — do log alag-alag paisa lagate hain aur profit unke nivesh ke anupaat me bantta hai. Jaise ek ne 8 lakh aur doosre ne 12 lakh lagaye, to profit 8:12 yaani 2:3 me bantega. Isi tarah shaadi, function ya trip ka kharch bhi logon me ratio se baanta ja sakta hai. Ratio samajhna ek aisi skill hai jo paise ke har hisaab me kaam aati hai.

Number ke saath teen misaal

Misaal 1 — simplify. 8 : 12 ka HCF 4 hai. Dono ko 4 se bhaag = 2 : 3. Sabse chhota roop.

Misaal 2 — profit baantna. ₹5,000 ko 8:12 (=2:3) me. Jod 5 hissa. Pehle ko 2/5 = ₹2,000, doosre ko 3/5 = ₹3,000.

Misaal 3 — recipe. Aata aur paani 2:1 me. 400g aata ke liye 200ml paani — anupaat banaye rakho.

Ye baatein aksar chhoot jaati hain

  1. Ratio ko amount samajhna. 2:3 ka matlab 2 aur 3 unit nahi, balki "2 guna aur 3 guna" — anupaat hai.
  2. Simplify na karna. 8:12 ko 2:3 karke samajhna aasaan hota hai — HCF se bhaag dein.
  3. Baantte waqt jod bhoolna. Rakam baantne ke liye pehle sabhi hisson ka jod (total parts) chahiye.
  4. Order badalna. A:B aur B:A alag hote hain — kaun pehle, dhyan rakhein.
  5. Teen-part ratio. A:B:C jaisa ho to teeno ka jod le kar baantein — ye tool do-part ka hai.

Bharat me dhyaan dene layak

  • Business: Partner ke beech profit/loss aksar ratio me baanta jaata hai — nivesh ke anupaat me.
  • Recipe/mixing: Cement-sand, aata-paani, sharbat — sab me anupaat zaroori.
  • Map/scale: Map ka scale (jaise 1:1000) bhi ek ratio hai.
  • Aspect ratio: Screen aur photo ka aspect ratio (16:9, 4:3) bhi isi tarah ka anupaat hai.

Ek kadam aage

Ratio ka istemaal itni jagah hota hai ki log dhyaan hi nahi dete — recipe me daal-chawal ka anupaat, business me profit-sharing, map ka scale, ya do dosto ke beech kharch ka bantwara. Isi liye "ratio kaise nikale", "ratio simplify karna", "ratio ko percentage me badlein" jaise search bahut hote hain. Ratio ko uski simplest form me laana (jaise 10:15 ko 2:3 karna) sabse zaroori step hai, jise ye tool khud kar deta hai — dono numbers ka HCF nikaal kar.

Ek aur kaam ka pehlu — ratio ko aage badhana. Agar recipe 2:3 ke anupaat me hai aur aap pehla hissa 6 rakhna chahte hain, to doosra 9 hoga (same ratio). Ye "part-part" aur "part-whole" ki samajh business aur padhai dono me kaam aati hai. Percentage nikalna ho to hamara Percentage Calculator, aur do cheezon ka daam compare karna ho to Price Compare (per unit) tool istemaal karein.

Aapke sawaal, seedhe jawab

Ratio kaise simplify karte hain?

Dono number ko unke HCF se bhaag dein — jaise 8:12 = 2:3.

Rakam ko ratio me kaise baantein?

Sabhi hisson ka jod nikaalein, phir har hisse ka anupaat rakam par lagayein.

2:3 ka matlab kya hai?

Pehla hissa doosre ka 2/3 hai — ye anupaat hai, asli matra nahi.

A:B aur B:A same hain?

Nahi, order mayne rakhta hai — kaun pehle aata hai wo zaroori hai.

Teen part wala ratio?

A:B:C ke liye teeno ka jod le kar baantein. Ye tool do-part ke liye hai.

Zaroori baat: Ye ek saamany ganit tool hai. Business profit-sharing jaise mamlon me likhit samjhauta aur salah zaroori hai — sirf ratio par nirbhar na rahein.
