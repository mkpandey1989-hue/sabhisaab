Haridwar: Saar aur Zile ki Pehchan

Zile ki Pehchan: Ek Nazar Mein

Seedha jawab: Haridwar Uttarakhand ka sabse zyada aabadi wala jila hai (Census 2011: 18,90,422) aur Garhwal Mandal ka sabse bada dharmik aur audyogik kendra. Ganga yahin pahadon se utarkar maidan me aati hai, isliye ise "Gateway of Gods" yaani Dev Dwar kaha jata hai.

Haridwar (हरिद्वार, purani spelling Hardwar) Uttarakhand ke dakshin-pashchim kone me, Delhi se lagbhag 210 km aur rajya ki rajdhani Dehradun se lagbhag 50 km door hai. Yahan teen cheezein ek saath milti hain jo kisi aur jile me is tarah nahi milti: sanatan dharm ki sabse badi teerth-nagri, Uttar Bharat ke bade industrial clusters me se ek (BHEL + SIDCUL), aur Roorkee jaisa engineering-education hub (IIT Roorkee).

Isi liye Haridwar ko log kai naamon se jaante hain: Gateway of Gods, Dev Dwar, Gangadwar, Mayapuri, Mokshadwar, Kumbh Nagri aur Dharm-Udyog ki Nagri. Sapta Puri (Bharat ki saat moksha-dayini nagariyon) me Haridwar ko "Maya" ya Mayapuri ke roop me ginaa jata hai.

Haridwar Naam Kaise Pada: Naam ka Itihas

Seedha jawab: "Haridwar" ka matlab hai "Hari ka dwar" (Bhagwan Vishnu ka dwar). Shaiv bhakt ise "Hardwar" yaani "Har (Shiv) ka dwar" kehte hain, kyunki yahin se Kedarnath ki yatra shuru hoti hai. Dono naam ek hi shehar ke liye prachalit hain.

Haridwar naam Sanskrit ke do shabdon se bana hai: Hari (Vishnu) aur Dwar (darwaza). Vaishnav parampara ke anusar ye Badrinath (Bhagwan Vishnu ka dham) ka dwar hai. Shaiv parampara ke anusar Har (Shiv) aur dwar milkar "Hardwar" bante hain, kyunki Kedarnath ki yatra isi dwar se aarambh hoti hai. Isi karan aaj bhi Uttar Bharat me Haridwar aur Hardwar dono spelling chalti hain; sarkari records me aksar "Hardwar" aur aam bol-chal me "Haridwar" milta hai.

Haridwar: Collectorate aur Rajasv Prashasan

Zila Mukhyalay, Vikas Bhawan aur Collectorate Kahan Hai

Seedha jawab: Haridwar jile ka Collectorate aur Vikas Bhawan Roshnabad (Haridwar) me hai, jo Haridwar railway station se lagbhag 12 km door sthit hai. Yahin District Magistrate, CDO, SSP aur jila nyayalay jaise mukhya karyalay hain.

Sarkari jila portal ke anusar jile ka mukhyalaya Roshnabad me hai aur yahan Collectorate, Vikas Bhawan, District Judiciary (Nyayalay), SP Office, Police Line, District Jail, District Sports Stadium aur Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya jaise pramukh sansthan sthit hain. Jila ka mukhya prashasnik adhikari District Magistrate (DM) / Collector hota hai, jo IAS cadre ka adhikari hota hai. Vikas karyon ke liye Mukhya Vikas Adhikari (CDO) niyukt hota hai, jinka karyalay Vikas Bhawan (Roshnabad) me hai.

Collectorate ke pramukh anubhag (sections): Chief Administrative Officer, Senior Administrative Officer, RA, GC, LAC (Land Acquisition), Stamp, BC-I/BC-II (Board of Revenue/Bhumi mamle), JA, ALC-I/II aur LBC-I/II. Aam nagrik ko inme se sabse zyada kaam Stamp, Tehsil/Revenue aur Arms License anubhag se padta hai.

Roshnabad kaise pahunche?

  • Har Ki Pauri / Railway station se auto, e-rickshaw, tempo ya city bus ke through Roshnabad jaya ja sakta hai (lagbhag 12 km).
  • Apni gaadi se Haridwar bypass / Roorkee road side se jaate waqt Google Maps me "Collectorate Haridwar Roshnabad" search karein.
  • Karyalay samay aam sarkari samay (lagbhag subah 10 se shaam 5) hota hai; chhuttiyon me band.

Dhyan dein: Current DM aur SSP ka naam time ke saath badalta hai.

Patwari Chowki: Revenue Police aur Regular Police ka Kshetra

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand ke kai pahadi ilakon me "Revenue Police" (Patwari/Lekhpal ko police adhikar) ki vyavastha rahi hai, lekin Haridwar jile ka adhikansh hissa maidani hone ke karan yahan niyamit civil police (Thana/Kotwali) ka kshetradhikar chalta hai.

Pahad ke gaon me jahan thane door hote hain, wahan prashasan ne itihas me Patwari ko hi police ke kuch adhikar diye the (isi ko Revenue Police kehte hain). Haridwar jila Shivalik ke tarai-bhabhar aur maidani kshetra me aata hai, jahan sadak aur aabadi ghani hai, isliye yahan SSP ke antargat Kotwali aur Thanon ka niyamit network kaam karta hai. Patwari/Lekhpal ka kaam yahan mukhyatah bhumi-abhilekh, khatauni aur rajasv vasooli tak simit hai.

Nagrik ke liye vyavharik matlab:

  • FIR ya complaint ke liye apne ilaake ke Thana/Kotwali jaayein ya e-FIR / Uttarakhand Police ke official channel ka istemal karein.
  • Zameen ke jhagde, naap-jokh, khatauni correction ke liye Tehsil / Lekhpal se milein.
  • Emergency me 112 (single emergency number) dial karein.

Zila Panchayat Seats, Kshetra Panchayat & Total Gram Panchayats Count

Seedha jawab: Haridwar jile me tri-stariya panchayati vyavastha hai: Zila Panchayat (jila star), Kshetra Panchayat (block star) aur Gram Panchayat (gaon star). Ek khaas baat: Haridwar ke panchayat chunav ka calendar Uttarakhand ke baaki jilon se alag chalta aaya hai.

Dhyan dein: Zila Panchayat ke territorial constituencies, Kshetra Panchayat sadasya aur Gram Panchayat ki exact sankhya har chunav se pehle State Election Commission Uttarakhand ki notification ke saath badalti hai.

Teen star ki vyavastha:

  • Zila Panchayat: Jila star par; adhyaksh (chairperson) ka chunav sadasyon dwara hota hai.

  • Gram Panchayat: Gaon star par; Pradhan seedhe janta dwara chuna jata hai.

Khaas baat: Haridwar jile ke panchayat chunav aam taur par baaki jilon se alag tithi par hote rahe hain.

Haridwar: Rajneetik aur Chunavi Parichay

Lok Sabha Seat aur Maujooda Saansad

Seedha jawab: Haridwar jila Haridwar Lok Sabha seat me aata hai. 2024 ke chunav me yahan se BJP ke Trivendra Singh Rawat (purv mukhyamantri) vijayi rahe. Is seat me kul 14 Vidhan Sabha kshetra hain: 11 Haridwar jile ke aur 3 Dehradun jile ke.

Haridwar Lok Sabha (Seat sankhya 5 in Uttarakhand): Ye seat 1977 me parisiman ke baad bani. 2009 tak ye Anusuchit Jati ke liye aarakshit thi; ab ye samanya seat hai. 2009 ke parisiman ke baad Dehradun jile ke teen kshetra (Dharampur, Doiwala, Rishikesh) bhi isme jode gaye, isliye ye seat do jilon me failti hai. 2024 me is seat par kul lagbhag 20.36 lakh matdata the aur matdan Phase 1 (19 April 2024) me hua.

Date-sensitive: Sansad ke sadasya ka naam agle chunav tak wahi rahega.

Haridwar mein Sub-Registrar (Zameen Registry) Daftar

Seedha jawab: Zameen ki registry Sub-Registrar karyalay me hoti hai. Haridwar jile me Haridwar, Roorkee, Laksar aur Bhagwanpur jaise tehsil-stariya Sub-Registrar karyalay prasiddh hain. Registry ke liye stamp duty aur registration fee jama karni hoti hai.

Registry ki basic prakriya (step-by-step):

  • Zameen ka khatauni / khasra / chain of title (purani registry) check karein.
  • E-stamp / stamp duty (circle rate ke hisaab se) ka bhugtan karein.
  • Sub-Registrar karyalay me khareedar, bechne wale aur do gawahon ke saath upasthit hon (aadhar, PAN, photo).
  • Biometric/photo, hastakshar aur registry ka dakhil-kharij.
  • Registry ke baad Dakhil-Kharij (Mutation) tehsil se karwayein, jisse khatauni me naam chade.

Vyavsayik Sujhav: Haridwar me tez shehri vikas ke karan zameen ke vivaad bahut hote hain. Registry se pehle Bhulekh (khatauni), Tehsil search aur Dispute search zaroor karwayein. Kisi bhi kaanooni salah ke liye adhivakta se milein; ye page kanooni salah nahi hai.
