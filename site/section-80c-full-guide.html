Section 80C Kya Hai? ₹1.5 Lakh Tax Bachat Ki Guide

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~7 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Old tax regime me Section 80C sabse popular tax-saving tarika hai. Ye aapko saal me ₹1.5 lakh tak ki income par tax chhoot deta hai — agar aap sahi jagah nivesh karein. Is guide me hum samjhenge ki 80C me kya-kya aata hai, kitna tax bachta hai, kaunsa option kiske liye theek hai, aur iske saath aur kaunsi chhoot milti hai.

Section 80C kya hai?

Section 80C Income Tax Act ka wo hissa hai jo kuch khaas nivesh aur kharch par tax chhoot deta hai. Aap saal me adhiktam ₹1,50,000 tak ki rakam apni taxable income se ghata sakte hain. Iska matlab — is rakam par aapka tax nahi lagta, jisse aapki kul tax dena wali rakam kam ho jaati hai.

Dhyan rahe: ye fayda sirf old tax regime me milta hai. New regime (jo ab default hai) me 80C ki chhoot nahi hoti, par slab rate kam rakhe gaye hain. Isliye dono ko compare karna zaroori hai — kabhi 80C ke saath old regime accha, kabhi bina jhanjhat new regime.

80C me kya-kya aata hai?

80C ke andar kaafi options aate hain — aapko sirf inhe milakar ₹1.5 lakh tak ka jod poora karna hota hai:

  • EPF/VPF: Salary se kat raha PF — ye pehle se 80C me aata hai.
  • PPF: Public Provident Fund, tax-free byaj, 15 saal lock.
  • ELSS: Tax-saving mutual fund, sabse kam lock (3 saal), market-linked.
  • Life insurance premium: Apni ya family ki term ya other policy ka premium.
  • Tuition fee: Do bachchon ki school/college fee.
  • Home loan principal: EMI ka principal hissa (byaj alag 24b me).
  • NSC, tax-saver FD (5 saal), Sukanya Samriddhi: Ye bhi 80C me aate hain.

Zaroori nahi ki sab me daalein — inme se koi bhi milakar ₹1.5 lakh poora karna hai.

Kitna tax bachta hai?

Bachat aapke tax slab par nirbhar karti hai. Formula simple hai: bachat = chhoot × aapka slab%.

Yaani jitni zyada aapki income aur slab, utni zyada 80C se bachat. Isi liye zyada kamane wale ko 80C se sabse zyada fayda hota hai. Kam income (jinka tax pehle se kam hai) wale ke liye new regime aksar behtar aur seedha padta hai.

Kaunsa 80C option chunein?

Har option ka apna mizaaj hai. PPF — poori tarah surakshit, tax-free byaj, par 15 saal lock. ELSS — sabse kam lock (3 saal) aur lambe samay me zyada return ki sambhavna, par market risk. Term insurance — sasta aur zaroori (family ki suraksha), premium 80C me. EPF — apne aap ho jaata hai, alag mehnat nahi.

Ek santulit tareeka: pehle EPF aur term insurance premium gino, phir jo bacha wo ELSS (agar risk le sakte hain) ya PPF (safe chahiye to) me daalein. Sabse badi galti — sirf tax bachane ke chakkar me mehngi endowment ya ULIP policy le lena jo na accha return deti hai, na theek suraksha. Product pehle apne liye theek hona chahiye, tax fayda baad me.

80C ke saath aur kya chhoot milti hai?

80C ki ₹1.5 lakh ke alawa kuch aur chhoot bhi hain jo alag se milti hain — inhe milakar old regime kaafi tax bacha sakta hai:

  • 80CCD(1B) — NPS: ₹50,000 extra chhoot, 80C se bilkul alag.
  • 80D — health insurance: Apni family ka premium (₹25,000, senior parents ke liye ₹50,000 tak).
  • 24(b) — home loan byaj: ₹2 lakh tak alag chhoot.
  • 80TTA — savings account byaj: ₹10,000 tak.

Filing se pehle in sab ko jod kar old vs new regime dono ka tax nikaalein — jahan kam tax lage, wahi chunein. Sirf 80C dekhkar faisla na karein.

80C ka nivesh kab tak karein?

80C ka fayda us financial year (1 April se 31 March) me hi milta hai jisme aap nivesh karte hain. Bahut log aakhri mahine (March) me jaldi-jaldi paisa daalte hain, jo do wajah se galat hai. Ek — aakhri waqt me galat product chun lene ka risk rehta hai. Do — agar aap saal bhar thoda-thoda (jaise SIP ya monthly PPF) daalein, to bojh nahi padta aur nivesh ki aadat banti hai.

Sabse accha tarika: saal ki shuruaat me hi apna 80C plan bana lein. Pehle EPF aur zaroori insurance gino, phir bacha hissa ELSS ya PPF me monthly baant dein. Isse na March me hadbadi hoti hai, na paisa ek saath nikalna padta hai. Aur proof (receipt, statement) saal bhar sambhal kar rakhein — employer ya ITR me maang sakte hain.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

80C me adhiktam kitni chhoot milti hai?

₹1,50,000 tak. Isse zyada nivesh par extra tax chhoot nahi milti.

Kya new regime me 80C chalta hai?

Nahi, 80C ki chhoot sirf old tax regime me milti hai.

80C me sabse accha option kaunsa?

Zaroorat ke hisaab se — ELSS (kam lock, zyada return, risk), PPF (safe, tax-free), term insurance (suraksha).

NPS ki extra chhoot kya hai?

80CCD(1B) me NPS par ₹50,000 alag chhoot, jo 80C ki ₹1.5 lakh se bahar hai.

EPF 80C me aata hai?

Haan, salary se kat raha EPF/VPF 80C me ginte hain.
