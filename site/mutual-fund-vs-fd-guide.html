Mutual Fund vs FD — Kahan Paisa Lagayein?

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

"Paisa FD me rakhoon ya mutual fund me lagaoon?" — ye shayad har middle-class ghar ka sabse aam sawaal hai. Papa-dada FD par bharosa karte aaye hain kyunki wo safe hai, par aajkal har taraf SIP aur mutual fund ki baat hoti hai. Sach ye hai ki dono ki apni jagah hai. Is guide me hum bina jargon ke samjhenge ki FD aur mutual fund me kya farak hai, kaun sa kab behtar hai, aur aapke paise ka kitna hissa kahan hona chahiye.

➤ FD Calculator kholein

FD kya deti hai?

Fixed Deposit ka sabse bada fayda hai — nishchitta. Aap paisa lagate hain, byaj dar pehle se tay hoti hai, aur maturity par aapko exactly utna hi milta hai jitna calculator batata hai. Market upar-neeche ho, aapki FD par koi farak nahi padta. Isi wajah se ye emergency fund, short-term goals (1-3 saal) aur un logon ke liye badhiya hai jinhe risk bilkul pasand nahi. Kami sirf itni hai ki FD ka return aam taur par mehngai (inflation) se thoda hi upar hota hai, isliye ispar paisa "safe" to rehta hai par tezi se badhta nahi.

Mutual Fund kya deta hai?

Mutual fund me aapka paisa kai logon ke paise ke saath milakar shares, bonds ya doosri jagah lagaya jaata hai, jise ek expert (fund manager) sambhaalta hai. Equity mutual fund lambe samay me FD se kaafi zyada return de sakte hain, par inme garaanti nahi hoti — value ghat-badh sakti hai. Short term me nuksan bhi dikh sakta hai, lekin 5-10 saal jaise lambe samay me aam taur par ye achha return dete aaye hain. SIP ke zariye aap har mahine thoda-thoda laga sakte hain, jisse market ki upar-neeche ka jhatka kam mehsoos hota hai.

Ek line me farak: FD = kam return, par pakka. Mutual fund = zyada return ki ummeed, par ghat-badh ke saath. Ek suraksha deta hai, doosra vriddhi (growth).

Kaun sa kab chunein?

  • 1-3 saal ka goal ya emergency fund: FD ya RD behtar. Yahan suraksha zaroori hai, growth nahi.
  • 5 saal ya usse zyada ka goal: Equity mutual fund (SIP ke zariye) aam taur par behtar, kyunki lambe samay me compounding kaam karta hai.
  • Bilkul risk nahi le sakte: FD me rahein, chahe return kam ho.
  • Thoda risk le sakte hain aur samay hai: Dono ko mix karein.

Inflation ka khel

Ye baat kam log samajhte hain — agar FD 7% de rahi hai aur mehngai 6% hai, to aapki asli badhotri sirf 1% ke aaspaas hai. Yaani paisa dikhne me to badha, par uski kharidne ki taakat utni nahi badhi. Isi wajah se lambe samay ke liye sirf FD par nirbhar rehna paise ko dheere-dheere "peeche" chhod deta hai. Mutual fund is mehngai ko maat dene ka mauka deta hai, par risk ke saath. Real return samajhne ke liye aap inflation-adjusted return tool bhi try kar sakte hain.

Tax ka farak

FD ka byaj aapki income me jud kar aapke slab ke hisaab se tax lagta hai, aur bank byaj par TDS bhi kaat sakta hai. Mutual fund par tax tab lagta hai jab aap paisa nikalte hain, aur wo capital gains ke niyam ke hisaab se lagta hai. Kis fund par kitna tax lagega ye samay-samay par badalta hai, isliye bade nivesh se pehle CA se ya official jaankari se confirm karna theek rehta hai.

Practical tarika

Zyadatar logon ke liye sahi jawab "ya to FD ya mutual fund" nahi, balki dono ka sahi mel hai. Pehle ek emergency fund (3-6 mahine ke kharch jitna) FD ya savings me rakhein — ye aapki suraksha ki deewar hai. Uske baad jo lambe samay ke lakshya hain (bachho ki padhai, retirement) unke liye SIP ke zariye mutual fund me lagayein. Is tarah aapko FD ki suraksha bhi milti hai aur mutual fund ki growth bhi. Sab Hisaab ke SIP aur FD calculators se aap dono ka anuman side-by-side dekh sakte hain.

Aakhri baat — kisi bhi cheez me sirf isliye paisa mat lagayein ki sab laga rahe hain. Apna lakshya, samay aur risk lene ki himmat dekhein, phir faisla karein. FD aur mutual fund dushman nahi, ek hi team ke do khiladi hain — sahi jagah dono ka istemaal hi asli samajhdaari hai.

SIP: mutual fund me ghusne ka aasaan darwaza

Agar mutual fund ka naam sunkar ghabrahat hoti hai, to SIP se shuruaat karna sabse aasaan hai. SIP me aap har mahine ek chhoti si rakam — jaise ₹500 ya ₹1,000 — apne aap fund me lagate hain. Isse do fayde hote hain. Pehla, aapki jeb par ek saath bojh nahi padta. Doosra, jab market neeche hota hai to aapko zyada units milte hain aur jab upar hota hai to kam — is tarah aapki average keemat sab par balance ho jaati hai, jise "rupee cost averaging" kehte hain. Isi wajah se naye niveshak ke liye lumpsum (ek saath bada paisa) ke muqable SIP kam tanav wala tareeka maana jaata hai. Aap chahein to pehle chhoti SIP se shuru karein, bharosa badhe to rakam badhaayein.

Ek aam galti se bachein

Sabse badi galti jo log karte hain — market girte hi ghabraakar SIP band kar dena ya paisa nikaal lena. Yahi wo waqt hota hai jab aapko saste me zyada units mil rahe hote hain. Mutual fund lambe khel ka saathi hai; use 6 mahine me judge mat karein. Doosri galti hai bina goal ke nivesh karna. Pehle tay karein ki paisa kis liye hai — 3 saal baad gaadi, ya 15 saal baad bachho ki padhai — phir uske hisaab se FD ya mutual fund chunein. Bina lakshya ke nivesh ek bina pate ki chitthi jaisa hai.

Sawaal "kaunsa behtar" nahi, "kab tak chahiye" hai. 1-3 saal me paisa chahiye to FD hi theek hai — us thode samay me market gir gaya to nuksan pakka ho jaata hai. 7 saal se zyada ka lakshya ho to FD me rakha paisa mehngai aur tax ke baad lagbhag badhta hi nahi. Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya mutual fund FD se hamesha behtar hai?

Nahi. Lambe samay ke liye equity mutual fund zyada return de sakta hai, par risk ke saath. Short term aur suraksha ke liye FD behtar hai.

Kya mutual fund me paisa doob sakta hai?

Value ghat-badh sakti hai aur short term me nuksan dikh sakta hai. Lambe samay (5-10 saal+) me aam taur par acha rehta, par return garaanti nahi.

Emergency fund FD me rakhoon ya mutual fund me?

Emergency fund suraksha ke liye hota hai, isliye FD ya savings behtar hai jahan value stable rehti hai.

Dono me se ek hi chunna zaroori hai?

Nahi. Zyadatar logon ke liye dono ka mel sabse achha hota — FD suraksha ke liye, mutual fund growth ke liye.
