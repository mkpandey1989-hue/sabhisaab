"8 glass paani piyo" har koi kehta hai. Par 50 kilo aur 90 kilo wale ki zaroorat ek jaisi nahi hoti. Neeche apna wazan daaliye — ek theek anuman mil jayega.

Anuman kaise lagta hai

Roz ka paani (litre) = Wazan (kg) × 0.033

70 kg wazan? 70 × 0.033 = lagbhag 2.3 litre, yaani 9-10 glass.

WazanPaani (anuman)Glass (250ml)
50 kg1.7 litre~7
60 kg2.0 litre~8
70 kg2.3 litre~9
80 kg2.6 litre~11
90 kg3.0 litre~12

Ye ek shuruaati anuman hai, koi pakka niyam nahi. Har insaan alag hai, aur kai cheezein is number ko badal deti hain.

Kab zyada chahiye aur kab kam

Zyada chahiye jab:

  • Garmi ho ya pasina aa raha ho — Bharat ki garmi me zaroorat 1 litre tak badh sakti hai
  • Mehnat ka kaam ya vyayam — har ghante ki kadi mehnat par 500-750 ml aur
  • Khuli dhoop me kaam — kheti, nirmaan, delivery ka kaam
  • Bukhaar, ulti ya dast — us waqt paani sabse zaroori ho jaata hai
  • Chai-coffee zyada peete hain — wo thoda paani baahar nikaalti hain
  • Garbhavastha ya doodh pilane wali maa — zaroorat kaafi badh jaati hai

Savdhaani jab:

  • Gurde ki bimari — kai maamlon me doctor paani seemit karne ko kehte hain
  • Dil ki kuch bimariyan — wahan bhi seema hoti hai
  • Kuch dawaiyan — jo shareer me paani rokti hain
In sthitiyon me ye calculator ka number mat maaniye — apne doctor ne jo kaha wahi sahi hai. Aur zaroorat se bahut zyada paani ek saath peena bhi theek nahi hota; shareer me namak ka santulan bigad sakta hai.

Paani sirf glass se nahi aata

Poora paani sirf peene se nahi milta. Lagbhag 20% khaane se aata hai:

  • Kheera, tarbooz, kharbooja — lagbhag 90% paani
  • Tamatar, santra, mausambi — kaafi zyada
  • Dahi, chaach, lassi — garmi me sabse achhi cheez
  • Daal, sabzi ka rasa, soup
  • Nariyal paani, nimbu paani

Isi liye jo log salad, phal aur daal-sabzi zyada khaate hain, unhe utna paani alag se nahi peena padta.

Ek cheez jo dhyaan dene layak hai — meetha thanda drink paani ka vikalp nahi hai. Usme cheeni itni hoti hai ki wo faayde se zyada nuksan karta hai. Garmi me nimbu-paani, chaach ya saada paani hi sabse behtar hai, aur sabse sasta bhi.

Kaafi paani ja raha hai ya nahi — kaise jaanein

Ginti rakhne se aasaan tarika ye hai — shareer khud bata deta hai.

Sabse bharosemand nishani: peshaab ka rang. Halka peela ya lagbhag saaf matlab theek hai. Gehra peela matlab paani kam ja raha hai. (Kuch dawaiyan aur vitamin rang badal dete hain — us par mat jaiye.)

Aur nishaaniyan ki paani kam hai: baar-baar pyaas, sookha munh, thakan, sar dard, chakkar, kam peshaab aana, sookhi twacha.

Ek aam galti — pyaas lagne ka intezaar karna. Pyaas lagne ka matlab hai ki shareer me paani pehle hi kam ho chuka hai. Bujurgon me to pyaas ka ehsaas hi kam ho jaata hai, isliye unhe yaad se paani dena chahiye — khaaskar garmi me.

Aadat banane ke saade tarike: bottle saamne rakhiye (dikhega to piyenge) · har khaane se pehle ek glass · subah uthte hi ek glass · kaam ke beech har ghante do-teen ghoont · bahar nikalte waqt bottle saath.

Apne shareer ke doosre number dekhne ho to BMI Calculator aur TDEE Calculator dekhiye.

Bharat ke mausam aur kaam ke hisaab se

Yahan ki garmi aur yahan ke kaam, dono ki apni zaroorat hai. Kitaabi 8 glass wala niyam Bharat me aksar kam pad jaata hai.

  • Maidani garmi (40°C+) — upar ke number me 700 ml se 1 litre tak aur jodiye. Khet, nirmaan ya delivery ka kaam hai to aur zyada.
  • Pahad ki thand — yahan sabse badi galti hoti hai. Thand me pyaas kam lagti hai, isliye log paani peena bhool jaate hain — par sookhi hawa aur oonchai dono paani nikaalti hain. Thand me bhi utna hi paani chahiye, chahe pyaas na lage. Garam paani ya chai ki jagah halka garam paani achha rehta hai.
  • Barsaat aur umas — pasina to aata hai par sookhta nahi, isliye pata nahi chalta ki paani nikal raha hai. Yahan bhi dhyaan chahiye.
  • AC me baithne wale — AC hawa se nami kheenchta hai. Pyaas nahi lagti par paani kam hota rehta hai.
Roza, vrat ya upvaas rakhne walon ke liye — jab peene ki ijaazat ho tab ek saath bahut zyada peene ke bajay thoda-thoda karke peena behtar rehta hai. Aur khaane me dahi, phal aur rasedar cheez rakhna madad karta hai.

Aur ek baat jo garmi me jaan bacha sakti hai — sirf paani kaafi nahi hota jab bahut pasina aaya ho. Us waqt namak-cheeni ka ghol (ORS), nimbu-paani ya chaach zyada kaam karte hain, kyunki pasine ke saath namak bhi nikalta hai. Agar chakkar, ulti ya bahut kamzori lage to ye lu lagne ki nishani ho sakti hai — turant chhaanv me jaakar doctor se milna chahiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Roz kitna paani peena chahiye?

Mota anuman: wazan (kg) × 0.033 litre. 70 kg wale ke liye lagbhag 2.3 litre. Garmi, mehnat aur bimari me zyada chahiye.

Chai aur coffee paani me ginte hain?

Thoda ginte hain, par wo thoda paani baahar bhi nikaalti hain. Inhe paani ka vikalp maankar mat chaliye — saada paani hi behtar hai.

Kaise pata chale ki paani kam ja raha hai?

Sabse aasaan nishani peshaab ka rang hai. Halka peela ya saaf matlab theek; gehra peela matlab paani badhaiye.

Zyada paani peena nuksan kar sakta hai?

Bahut zyada paani ek saath peena shareer ka namak santulan bigaad sakta hai. Aur gurde ya dil ki bimari me doctor paani seemit karne ko kehte hain — aisi sthiti me unki salah hi maaniye.

Thand me kam paani peena theek hai?

Nahi. Thand me pyaas kam lagti hai par shareer ki zaroorat wahi rehti hai — khaaskar pahadi ilaake me, jahan sookhi hawa aur oonchai dono paani nikaalti hain. Pyaas ka intezaar mat kijiye.
