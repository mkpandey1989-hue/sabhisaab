About Us

Aakhri update: August 2026

Sab Hisaab ek free online toolkit hai jise aam logon ki roz ki zaroorat ke liye banaya gaya hai. Yahan investment (SIP, PPF, NPS), loan (EMI), trading (P&L, position size, risk-reward), tax, GST, sehat aur zindagi ke roz ke 154 calculators ek hi jagah milte hain.

Humara maqsad

Logon ko saaf, tez aur bharosemand hisaab dena — bina kisi app, login ya charge ke. Har tool aapke apne browser me chalta hai, isliye aapka koi bhi data (salary, amount waghairah) humare server par nahi jaata.

Ye website kaun chalata hai

Sab Hisaab ko Manoj Kumar Pandey (Uttarakhand, India) dwara banaya aur maintain kiya jaata hai. Inhe share bazaar, finance aur technology me kaafi dilchaspi hai, aur khud ke anubhav se ye samajhte hain ki aam log ko saaf aur bharosemand hisaab kitna zaroori hai. Isi soch se ye website banai gayi — roz kaam aane wale tools ek aasaan jagah par.

Naye tools aur jaankari samay-samay par jodi jaati hai, taaki website hamesha kaam ki aur updated rahe. Kisi bhi tool ke baare me sawaal ho to Contact page par sampark karein.

Website par kya-kya hai

Sab Hisaab do hisson me banti hai — tools aur guides.

  • 154 calculators — nivesh (SIP, PPF, NPS, FD, RD, EPF), loan aur tax (EMI, GST, income tax, HRA, TDS), trading (P&L, position size, brokerage), rozana ke kaam (percentage, age, bijli bill, fuel), sehat (BMI, calorie, water intake), aur converter (bigha/gaj/acre, length, weight, temperature).
  • 38 guides — har topic ko aasaan Hinglish me samjhane wale lekh, jinme formula, example, FAQ aur savdhaaniyan sab shaamil hain.
  • Live tools — mausam, currency rate aur shabdon ke matlab, jo asli samay ka data laate hain.

Ye website kaise kaam karti hai

Poori website aapke apne browser me chalti hai. Jab aap koi rakam ya salary daalte hain, wo aapke phone/computer se bahar nahi jaati — na kisi server par, na kisi database me. Isi liye yahan login nahi maanga jaata aur na hi aapka koi khaata banta hai.

Website static HTML par bani hai, isliye ye mobile par bhi bahut tez khulti hai aur kam internet me bhi kaam karti hai — jo pahadi aur gramin ilaakon me zaroori hai.

Jaankari kaise taiyaar hoti hai

Har calculator ka formula pehle official srot se milaaya jaata hai — jaise tax slab incometax.gov.in se, GST rate gst.gov.in se, aur PPF/EPF ki dar sarkari notification se. Uske baad formula ko kai numbers par khud jaanch kar dekha jaata hai ki jawab sahi aa raha hai ya nahi.

Guides poori tarah apne shabdon me likhi jaati hain — kisi doosri website se copy nahi ki jaatin. Jahan hamara apna anubhav hai (jaise AgriStack, zameen ke kagaz, F&O ka tax), wahan wo saaf bataya jaata hai.

Hinglish hi kyun

Bharat me karodon log Hindi bolte hain par Devanagari me tez nahi padh paate, aur angrezi ke financial shabd unke liye ajnabi hain. Beech ka rasta wahi hai jo log WhatsApp par khud istemaal karte hain — roman akshar me Hindi. "Compounding" ki jagah "byaj par byaj", "tenure" ki jagah "kitne saal". Isi wajah se har page par chhote vaakya hain aur har number ke saath ye bhi likha hai ki wo number kehta kya hai.
