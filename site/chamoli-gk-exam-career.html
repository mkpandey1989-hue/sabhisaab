Chamoli Ke 100 One-Liner GK Tathya

Pehchan Aur Prashasan

  • Chamoli zile ka mukhyalaya Gopeshwar hai.
  • Chamoli zila 24 February 1960 ko bana.
  • Chamoli Garhwal mandal me hai.
  • 1997 me Chamoli se alag hokar Rudraprayag zila bana.
  • Census 2011 me Chamoli ki aabadi 3,91,605 thi.
  • Census 2011 ke anusar Chamoli ka kshetrafal 8,030 vargkilometer hai.
  • Chamoli ka population density 49 vyakti prati vargkilometer (Census 2011).
  • Chamoli ka linganupat lagbhag 1,019 mahilayein prati 1,000 purush hai.
  • Chamoli ki sakshrata dar (7 saal se upar) 82.65% hai (Census 2011).
  • Chamoli me 9 vikas khand hain.
  • Zila website ke anusar Chamoli me 12 tehsil hain.
  • Chamoli me 3 Vidhan Sabha kshetra hain: Badrinath, Karnaprayag aur Tharali (SC).
  • Chamoli Garhwal Lok Sabha kshetra me aata hai.
  • Chamoli ka vahan code UK-11 hai.
  • Gopeshwar ka PIN code 246401 aur STD code 01372 hai.
  • Joshimath ka PIN code 246443 aur STD code 01389 hai.
  • Badrinath ka PIN code 246422 hai.
  • Chamoli ki official website chamoli.gov.in hai.
  • Chamoli me 10 shahri nikay hain: 4 Nagar Palika Parishad aur 6 Nagar Panchayat.
  • Census 2011 ke anusar zile ka sabse bada shahar Gopeshwar (21,447) tha.
  • Chamoli me SC aabadi lagbhag 20.25% aur ST aabadi lagbhag 3.13% hai (Census 2011).
  • Chamoli me Hindu aabadi lagbhag 98.5% hai (Census 2011).
  • Chamoli ki ST aabadi mukhya roop se Bhotia samuday ki hai.
  • Uttarakhand me paanch Anusuchit Janjatiyan hain: Bhotia, Buksa, Jaunsari, Raji aur Tharu.
  • Zila Panchayat Chamoli ki 26 seats (2025 chunav ke anusar) hain.

Chamoli: Pariksha Taiyari: PYQ Ke Bajay Tareeka Aur Abhyas Prashn

PYQ Kahan Se Milenge

Uttarakhand ki pariksha ke asli purane sawal Uttarakhand Public Service Commission (UKPSC) aur Uttarakhand Adhinasth Sewa Chayan Aayog (UKSSSC) ki official sites ya unke dwara jari question paper se hi lene chahiye (Chamoli: Pramanik Official Links Aur References). Kai coaching sites aur apps “PYQ” ke naam par sawal dete hain, jinme galtiyan ho sakti hain.

Chamoli Se Jude Sawal Kis Prakar Ke Aate Hain

Pariksha me Uttarakhand ke GK me aam taur par ye vishay aate hain (kisi vishesh saal ke sawal nahi, balki vishay ka dhancha):

  • Zila, mukhyalaya, banne ki tithi, tehsil aur block ki sankhya.
  • Nadiyan aur prayag (Alaknanda, Dhauliganga, Nandakini, Pindar).
  • National park, UNESCO sthal aur biosphere reserve.
  • Mandir, Panch Badri, Panch Kedar, tyohar aur mele (Ramman, Gauchar Mela, Nanda Raj Jat).
  • Chipko Andolan aur Gaura Devi.
  • Rajya andolan aur swatantrata sangharsh.
  • GI tag aur hastshilp.
  • Hydro projects aur rail line.
  • Rajya ke naye kanoon (UCC, Bhu Kanoon sanshodhan).
  • Isliye taiyari ke liye ek hi tathya ko alag-alag roop me samajhna (jaise “Chamoli kaun sa prayag hai, kaun si nadi milti hai”) zyada kaam aata hai.

Chamoli: Coaching, Library Aur Reading Room

Gopeshwar Jaise Mukhyalaya Me Taiyari Ki Chunautiyan

Pahadi zile ke yuvaon ko pariksha ki taiyari ke liye kai baar bade shahar (Dehradun, Haldwani) jaana padta hai.

Muft Aur Online Sansaadhan (Sarkari)

Taiyari ke liye kuch sarkari muft sansaadhan hain:

  • SWAYAM: Bharat sarkar ka muft online course platform.
  • NPTEL: Technical aur anya vishayon ke online lectures.
  • DIKSHA: Shikshakon aur vidyarthiyon ke liye sarkari platform.
  • National Digital Library of India (NDLI): Muft digital pustakalaya.
  • Inki official site aur uplabdhta alag se check karein. Gaon me internet kamzor ho to download karke offline padhne ka vikalp dekhein.

Gaon Me Reading Room Banana

Gaon ke yuvaon ke liye ek chhota reading room ya library bhi ek bada kadam ho sakta hai. Iske liye:

  • Gram Panchayat, Van Panchayat ya sthaniya samiti se jagah ki baat karein.
  • Purani kitabein, akhbaar aur pariksha ki taiyari ki kitabein dan me jama karein.
  • Ek naamit sanchalak (jaise sevanivrit shikshak) ko zimmedari dein.
  • Sarkari yojana (jaise gram panchayat ya zila prashasan ki library ki yojana) ke bare me zila Panchayati Raj ya Shiksha vibhag se poochhein.

Chamoli: Agniveer Bharti Aur Physical Training

Agnipath Scheme Ke Mukhya Tathya

Chamoli ke bahut se yuva sena aur ardhsainik balon me jaana chahte hain. Isliye Agnipath scheme ki jaankari zaroori hai (2026 ke sources ke anusar):

  • Shuruaat: Jun 2022.
  • Umar: 17.5 se 21 saal (2022 ke pehle chakra ke liye upari seema ek baar 23 saal ki gayi thi).
  • Avadhi: 4 saal (jisme lagbhag 6 mahine ka prashikshan shamil).
  • Punah-niyukti: Har batch ka adhiktam 25% niyamit cadre me liya ja sakta hai, jo aur 15 saal ke liye seva karte hain. Baaki ko Seva Nidhi package aur skill certificate milta hai. Punah-niyukti guarantee nahi hai, ye performance aur sanghathan ki zaroorat par nirbhar hai.
  • Retention par charcha: 2026 ki kuch reports ke anusar sena ki alag-alag shakhayein 25% retention ko badhane par vichar kar rahi hain, par ye abhi tak lagu nahi hua hai (ek report ke anusar). Taaza sthiti sarkari adhisuchna se dekhein.
  • Pehli batch ka nikalna: Reports ke anusar pehle chakra ke lagbhag 18,300 Agniveer ki seva 31 December 2026 tak poori hone ka anumaan tha.

Agniveer Ke Baad Ke Mauke (Reports Ke Anusar)

  • Kendriya balon me aarakshan: Grih Mantralay ne 21 July 2026 ko Sansad me kaha ki CAPF me Constable (GD) ke 50% aur Assam Rifles me Rifleman ke 50% pad ex-Agniveer ke liye aarakshit hain (Outlook ki report ke anusar).
  • Raksha Mantralay: Coast Guard, defence civilian posts aur 16 Defence PSU me 10% aarakshan (report ke anusar).
  • Uttarakhand: Agniveer Horizontal Reservation Rules, 2025 ke tahat rajya ki seva me kuch uniformed Group C pad (police, fire, forest, jail, excise aur secretariat suraksha) me ex-Agniveer ke liye 10% kshaitij aarakshan hai, saath me umar me chhoot aur physical efficiency test se chhoot ke praavdhaan hain (newsonair aur Drishti IAS ke anusar). Ye aarakshan Uttarakhand ke sthayi niwasi ke liye hai.
  • Ex-servicemen ki suvidha: Ek report ke anusar 4 saal ke baad nikalne wale Agniveer ko ECHS (swasthya) aur canteen jaisi suvidhayein apne aap nahi milti.
  • Ye sab jaankari sarkari adhisuchna ke anusar badal sakti hai, isliye pariksha ya bharti se pehle Army ki official site aur Uttarakhand ke Karmik vibhag ki adhisuchna dekhein.

Bharti Ki Prakriya

  • Army ki official bharti website par registration aur online pariksha (Common Entrance Test) aur phir bharti rally me physical aur medical test hote hain.
  • Rally ka sthan aur tithi Army ki ghoshna par nirbhar karti hai. Newsonair ki report ke anusar 2026 me Uttarakhand ke yuvaon ne Champawat ke Banbasa me hue Agniveer bharti rally me bhaag liya tha (Feb-Mar 2026).
  • Chamoli ke yuvaon ke liye rally ka sthan har saal alag ho sakta hai. Army ki official ghoshna dekhein.

Physical Training Ka Tareeka (Aam Salah)

  • Daud (running), pushup, situp aur pull-up jaise abhyas dheere-dheere badhayein.
  • Aahaar santulit rakhein (dal, anaj, sabzi, doodh), aur paani zyada piyein.
  • Chot se bachne ke liye achhe jute pehnein aur warm-up karein.
  • Kisi bhi kathin abhyas se pehle doctor ki salah lein.
  • Oonchai wale kshetra me rehne wale yuvaon ko bharti me kabhi-kabhi lamba daud sunna padta hai; isliye apni taiyari ka reality check karein.
  • Physical standard (lambai, seena, daud ka samay) Army ki official adhisuchna me hote hain.

Dhokhe Se Bachein

Bharti ke naam par paise maangne wale dalalon se bachein. Sarkari bharti me koi dalal nahi hota. Shikayat ke liye 112 ya Army ki official helpline dekhein.

Chamoli: Polytechnic, ITI, Nursing Aur Skill Development

Chamoli Ke Sansthan

Chamoli me sarkari polytechnic, nursing college aur kai technical sansthan hain, jinki suchi Chamoli Ke Sarkari College aur Anya Sansthan Jo Sources Me Mile me di gayi hai (jaise Govt Polytechnic Gopeshwar, Institute of Technology Gopeshwar aur Govt College of Nursing Gopeshwar). Isliye ITI ke naam aur seat ki sankhya Uttarakhand ke Prashikshan vibhag ya sthaniya karyalay se confirm karein.

Dakhile Ki Prakriya

Polytechnic, ITI aur nursing ke dakhile ke liye Uttarakhand ki apni entrance ya merit ki prakriya hoti hai. Kaun si pariksha, kab aavedan aur kaun si shart hai, ye har saal badalta hai.

Kaushal Vikas

Skill India aur rajya ke kaushal vikas karyakram ke tahat alag-alag kshetra me muft ya sasta prashikshan milta hai. Pahadi zile ke liye paryatan, homestay prabandhan, hastshilp, oon ka kaam aur millet processing jaise kaushal rozgar de sakte hain (Chamoli: Zile Ki Arthvyavastha Aur Aajeevika). Prashikshan ke liye sarkari portal par “Skill India Digital” ya zila Kaushal Vikas karyalay dekhein. Kisi prashikshan sansthan ke naam par dhokhe se bachein.

Chamoli: JNV, KV Aur Anya Sarkari Awasiya School

Kendriya Vidyalaya

Kendriya Vidyalaya Gopeshwar ka phone zila website par 01372-251075 diya gaya hai (Anya Sansthan Jo Sources Me Mile). KV me dakhile ki prakriya Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan ki official site par hoti hai.

Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya (JNV)

JNV ek sarkari awasiya school hain jinme dakhila Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) ke zariye aam taur par kaksha 6 me hota hai (Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti ke niyam ke anusar). Isliye JNV ki list aur dakhile ki tithi Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti ki official site (navodaya.gov.in) se dekhein.

Anya Sarkari Awasiya School

Kuch rajya sarkar ke awasiya school aur ashram paddhati ke school (jaise Anusuchit Jati aur Janjati ke liye) hote hain. Is hisse ko apne zila ke Samaj Kalyan vibhag ya Shiksha vibhag se jaankari lekar bharein.

Kya Chamoli mein Sainik School Hai?

Chamoli me Sainik School nahi hai. Uttarakhand ka Sainik School Ghorakhal (Nainital) me hai. Dakhile ke liye All India Sainik School Entrance Examination (AISSEE) hoti hai (National Testing Agency ki jaankari ke anusar, taaza adhisuchna dekhein).

JNVST ke zariye, aam taur par kaksha 6 me.

Sainik School Ghorakhal (Nainital).

Chamoli: Scholarship, Hostel Aur Coaching Yojana

Pramukh Scholarship Portal

  • Rajya ka Samaj Kalyan vibhag: SC, ST aur OBC ke chhatron ke liye chhatravritti aur hostel ki jaankari deta hai (vibhag ki official site dekhein).
  • Scholarship ki patrata (aay seema, jaati, percentage), rashi aur aavedan ki antim tithi har saal badalti hai.

Aavedan Ke Aam Dastavez

  • Aadhaar aur bank khata (Aadhaar se juda hona zaroori ho sakta hai).
  • Jati praman patra aur aay praman patra (Kuch Pramukh Praman Patra).
  • Pichhli kaksha ki marksheet.
  • Fees ki rasid aur college ka praman.
  • Niwas praman patra.

Hostel Aur Coaching Madad

Kuch kaksha ke baad chhatravas aur SC/ST ke liye coaching ki madad ki yojanayein sarkar chalati hai. Kaun si yojana kis varg ke liye chalu hai, iski taaza jaankari Samaj Kalyan vibhag ke zila karyalay se lein.

Dhokhe Se Bachein (Scholarship, Hostel Aur Coaching Yojana)

Chamoli: Rozgar Karyalay Aur Career Mela

Zila Rozgar Karyalay

Rajya ke Sewayojan vibhag ke tahat zila star par rozgar karyalay (employment exchange) hote hain, jahan berozgar yuva apna panjikaran karwa sakte hain. Sewayojan vibhag ke online portal ki jaankari rajya ke official portal (uk.gov.in) se dekhein. Wo apne zila ke official sampark se confirm karein.

Career Mela Aur Rozgar Mela

Samay-samay par sarkar aur nijee sansthayein rozgar mela ya career mela lagati hain. Chamoli me aise mele ki tarikh aur sthan har baar alag hote hain. “Pradhan Mantri Rozgar Mela” jaise Kendra sarkar ke karyakram ki jaankari PIB ya sambandhit vibhag ki site par milti hai.

Rozgar Ke Sambhavit Kshetra

  • Sena aur ardhsainik bal (Agniveer, CAPF, Assam Rifles) (Chamoli: Agniveer Bharti Aur Physical Training).
  • Paryatan (homestay, guide, taxi, trek) (Chamoli Ke Top 20 Darshaniya Sthal).
  • Kheti aur millet processing, jadi-booti aur chai (Chamoli: Desi Anaj: Mandua, Jhangora Aur Barahnaja Pranali).
  • Hastshilp aur oon (Chamoli: Handloom, GI Aur Hastshilp).
  • Sarkari seva (UKPSC, UKSSSC) (Chamoli: Pariksha Taiyari: PYQ Ke Bajay Tareeka Aur Abhyas Prashn).
  • Naye udyam (MSY, PMFME) (Chamoli: Sarkari Yojana, ODOP Aur Startup).
  • Page par ye “rozgar ke raste” ek jagah rakhne se yuvaon ko disha milegi.

Rozgar Ke Naam Par Dhokha

Naukri dilaane ke naam par paise maangne wale thagon se savdhaan rahein. Sarkari naukri ke liye koi paisa nahi dena padta. Dhokhe ki shikayat ke liye 1930 aur cybercrime.gov.in.
