Pace aur speed — do alag tareeke

Pace = Samay ÷ Doori (minute per km)
Speed = Doori ÷ Samay (km per ghanta)

5 km, 30 minute me:

  • Pace: 30 ÷ 5 = 6:00 min/km
  • Speed: 10 km/h

Daudne wale log aam taur par pace istemaal karte hain, speed nahi. Wajah practical hai — "6 minute per km" se turant pata chal jaata hai ki 10 km me 60 minute lagenge. Speed se ye hisaab dimaag me lagana padta hai.

Kaunsa pace aam hai — aur tulna kis se karein

Pehle ek zaroori baat — doosron se tulna mat kijiye. Pace umar, wazan, anubhav, mausam aur raste par nirbhar karta hai. Pahad me 8:00 ka pace, maidan ke 6:00 se zyada mehnat maang sakta hai.

Phir bhi ek mota anuman:

  • Naya daudne wala — 7:30 se 9:00 min/km. Aur beech-beech me chalna bilkul theek hai.
  • Niyamit daudne wala — 5:30 se 7:00
  • Achha anubhavi — 4:30 se 5:30
Bharat me ek aur baat mayne rakhti hai — garmi aur umas. Wahi insaan sardi me jo pace aaraam se karta hai, garmi me usse 30-60 second dheema ho jaata hai. Ye kamzori nahi, shareer ka normal jawab hai. Isliye garmi me pace ka lakshya thoda dheema rakhiye aur paani zyada peejiye.

Sabse achhi tulna apne pichhle mahine se hai. Agar aapka pace dheere-dheere sudhar raha hai aur daudne ke baad thakan kam ho rahi hai, to sab theek chal raha hai.

Pace sudharne ke tarike jo sach me chalte hain

  • Zyadatar daud dheemi rakhiye. Ye ulta lagta hai par sach hai — anubhavi daudne wale apna 80% samay aasaan pace par daudte hain. Jis pace par aap baat kar sakein, wahi sahi hai.
  • Hafte me ek baar tez. Chhote-chhote tez tukde (jaise 1 minute tez, 2 minute aaram), 5-6 baar. Ye pace sabse tez sudharta hai.
  • Doori dheere badhaiye. Hafte me 10% se zyada mat badhaiye. Ye niyam chot se bachata hai, aur chot hi sabse badi rukavat hai.
  • Aaram ka din zaroori hai. Shareer daudte waqt nahi, aaram karte waqt mazboot hota hai.
  • Taang ki takat. Hafte me 2 din squat aur calf ka vyayam — ye pace me seedha farak laata hai.
  • Sahi joota. Ghisa hua joota chot ki sabse aam wajah hai. 600-800 km ke baad badal dena chahiye.

Race ki tayaari aur savdhaani

Agar 5K ya 10K ki taiyaari kar rahe hain:

  • Race se pehle ka pace — apne aam pace se thoda tez, par bahut nahi. Sabse aam galti shuruaat me bahut tez bhaagna hai; aakhri 2 km me sab kuch bikhar jaata hai.
  • Race se ek hafta pehle doori kam kar dijiye. Nayi mehnat ab kuch nahi badlegi, sirf thakan degi.
  • Naya kuch mat aazmaiye race wale din — naya joota, nayi drink, naya khaana. Jo abhyaas me chala, wahi chalaiye.
Sehat ki zaroori baat: agar aapki umar 40 se upar hai, ya BP, sugar, dil ki koi shikayat, ya lambe samay se koi vyayam nahi kiya — to daudna shuru karne se pehle doctor se ek baar jaanch karwa lijiye. Ye salah dara dene ke liye nahi hai; achanak zyada mehnat kai baar dikkat khadi kar deti hai, aur ek jaanch se wo bach jaati hai.

Daudte waqt seene me dard, saans bahut phoolna, chakkar ya bahut zyada thakan mehsoos ho to turant rukiye aur doctor se milye. Ye page aam jaankari ke liye hai, kisi medical salah ki jagah nahi.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Pace aur speed me kya farak hai?

Pace batata hai ek km me kitne minute lagte hain; speed batati hai ek ghante me kitne km. Daudne wale pace istemaal karte hain kyunki usse samay ka hisaab turant lag jaata hai.

Naye daudne wale ka pace kitna hona chahiye?

Aam taur par 7:30 se 9:00 min/km, aur beech-beech me chalna bilkul theek hai. Doosron se tulna mat kijiye — apne pichhle mahine se kijiye.

Pace sudharne ka sabse achha tarika kya hai?

Zyadatar daud dheemi rakhiye — jis pace par baat kar sakein. Hafte me sirf ek baar tez tukde kariye. Aur doori hafte me 10% se zyada mat badhaiye.

Garmi me pace dheema ho jaata hai, kya ye normal hai?

Bilkul. Garmi aur umas me 30-60 second dheema hona shareer ka normal jawab hai. Lakshya thoda dheema rakhiye aur paani zyada peejiye.
