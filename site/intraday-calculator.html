Peeche ka ganit

Gross P&L seedha hai — (sell − buy) × quantity. Uske baad dono taraf ke charges ghatte hain. Intraday me brokerage aam taur par per order fix ya turnover ka chhota percent hota hai, aur baaki charges turnover par lagte hain. Chhote trade me charges ka asar percent me bahut bada hota hai.

Formula: Gross P&L = (Sell − Buy) × Qty
Net P&L = Gross − (brokerage + STT + exchange + GST + stamp + SEBI)

Kuch misaal jo saaf kar dein

Misaal 1 — achha trade. 200 share ₹90 par khareede, ₹144 par beche. Gross ₹10,800, charges lagbhag ₹22.85, aur net +₹10,777.15.

Misaal 2 — chhota trade. 50 share par ₹1 ka faayda = ₹50 gross. Charges hi ₹25-40 tak ho sakte hain — yaani aadha munafa charges me.

Misaal 3 — roz ka trade. Roz 5 trade aur har trade par ₹40 charges = mahine ke lagbhag ₹4,000. Saal ka ₹48,000 sirf charges me.

Table dekh kar samajh aata hai ki charges kyun bade lagte hain — ye ek jagah se nahi, chhe alag jagah se katte hain. Isi liye chhote target par trade karne wale aksar profit me hote hue bhi account me nuksan dekhte hain.

Apne broker ka contract note har hafte ek baar zaroor kholein. Usme har charge alag line me likha hota hai. Agar aapko lagta hai ki charges zyada hain to plan badalna ya kam trade karna dono vikalp hain. ITR bharte waqt bhi yahi hisaab kaam aata hai.

Jahan log atakte hain

  1. Charges ko chhota samajhna. Chhote trade me charges hi munafa kha jaate hain. Har trade se pehle break-even nikaal lein.
  2. Stop-loss na lagana. Intraday me position din ke ant me apne aap band ho jaati hai — bina stop-loss ke nuksan bada ho sakta hai.
  3. Leverage ka galat istemaal. Zyada leverage se chhoti si girawat bhi poora capital le jaati hai.
  4. Nuksan ka hisaab na rakhna. Sirf jeete hue trade yaad rakhna aur haare hue bhool jaana sabse aam galti hai.
  5. Tips ke bharose chalna. Kisi ke kehne par trade lena bina apni samajh ke — ye seedha nuksan ka raasta hai.

Bharat ke hisaab se dhyaan dein

  • Charges: STT, exchange transaction fee, SEBI fee, GST aur stamp duty — sab alag-alag lagte hain.
  • Tax: Intraday ka munafa business income maana jaata hai aur slab ke hisaab se tax lagta hai. Iske liye ITR-3 bharna hota hai.
  • Contract note: Har din ka contract note sambhal kar rakhein — ITR bharte waqt kaam aata hai.
  • SEBI: Sirf registered broker aur registered advisor ke saath hi kaam karein.
  • Ye anuman hai: Asli charges har broker ke alag hote hain — apne broker ka charge list dekh lein.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Intraday me kitne charges lagte hain?

Broker ke hisaab se alag, par aam taur par brokerage + STT + exchange fee + GST + stamp duty.

Intraday ka munafa kaise tax hota hai?

Ye speculative business income hai aur aapke slab ke hisaab se tax lagta hai.

Kya intraday ka nuksan carry forward hota hai?

Speculative loss aage le ja sakte hain par sirf speculative munafe ke saamne — niyam ke liye salahkaar se poochhein.

Kaunsa ITR bharna hota hai?

Aam taur par ITR-3.

Kya ye tool asli charges deta hai?

Nahi, ye anuman hai. Asli number contract note me hota hai.
