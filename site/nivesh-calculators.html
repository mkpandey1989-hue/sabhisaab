Nivesh ka sabse mushkil hissa number nahi hota — faisla hota hai. Par faisla tab hi theek hota hai jab number saamne ho. Is page par Sab Hisaab ke saare nivesh se jude calculator ek jagah hain: har mahine ki SIP se lekar retirement ke corpus tak.

Agar aap shuruaat kar rahe hain to seedha SIP Calculator se shuru karein — ye batata hai ki chhoti si monthly rakam bhi lambe samay me kitni ban jaati hai. Jinka paisa ek saath aaya hai (bonus, gratuity, zameen bechne ka paisa) unke liye Lumpsum Calculator theek rahega. Ek baat yaad rakhein: har calculator ek anuman hai. Byaj dar, tax slab aur sarkari niyam badalte rehte hain, aur market ka return kabhi tay nahi hota. Number ko sochne ki shuruaat maaniye, aakhri jawab nahi.

Nivesh ke teen sawaal jo har calculator se pehle aate hain

1. Paisa kab chahiye? Yahi sabse zaroori sawaal hai, aur zyadatar log ise poochhte hi nahi. Jo paisa do saal me ghar ki registry me lagna hai, wo equity me nahi jaana chahiye — chahe return kitna bhi accha lag raha ho. Do saal me market neeche ho gaya to aap use majboori me nuksan par bechenge. Ulta, jo paisa 15 saal tak chhoona hi nahi, use FD me rakhna bhi nuksan hai, kyunki mehngai use dheere-dheere kha jaati hai.

2. Kitna nuksan jhel sakte hain? Ye kaagaz par nahi, pet me mehsoos hota hai. Agar ₹5 lakh ka nivesh ghatkar ₹3.5 lakh ho jaaye aur aapki neend ud jaaye, to aapke liye 100% equity theek nahi hai — chahe calculator kuch bhi kahe. Aisa portfolio jise aap bure waqt me chhod dete hain, wo kagazi return se bekaar hai.

3. Mehngai ke baad kya bachega? 7% ki FD sunne me theek lagti hai, par agar mehngai 6% hai to asli badhotri sirf 1% hui. Isi ko real return kehte hain. Lambe samay ke lakshya — retirement, bachche ki padhai — me hamesha mehngai ke baad wala number dekhna chahiye, warna 20 saal baad rakam badi dikhegi par khareedne ki taakat wahi ki wahi hogi.

Kis kram me shuru karein

Ye kram zyadatar Bharatiya parivaron ke liye theek baithta hai:

  1. Emergency fund — 6 mahine ka kharcha, FD ya savings me. Ye nivesh nahi hai, ye nivesh ki neev hai. Bina iske pehli hi museebat me aapki SIP tootegi.
  2. Term insurance aur health insurance — jinke upar parivaar nirbhar hai unke liye. Insurance ko nivesh mat samajhiye, ye alag cheez hai.
  3. Mehnga karza khatam — credit card ya personal loan chal raha hai to pehle wo. 40% byaj dete hue 12% kamane ka koi matlab nahi.
  4. PPF ya EPF — surakshit hissa, tax ka fayda, sarkari garanti.
  5. SIP — jo paisa 7 saal se zyada ke liye hai.

Zyadatar log ulte kram se chalte hain — pehle SIP, phir insurance, aur emergency fund kabhi nahi. Isi wajah se bure waqt me sab kuch ek saath toot jaata hai.

Char aam galtiyan

Har saal fund badalna. Pichle saal ka top performer agle saal top nahi rehta. Baar-baar badalne me exit load, tax aur compounding ka silsila — teeno ka nuksan hota hai.

Return ka anuman bahut ooncha rakhna. Calculator me 18% daal kar khush ho jaana aasan hai, par us number par zindagi ki planning karna khatarnak hai. 10-12% par plan banaiye; zyada mile to bonus.

Ek hi jagah sab kuch. Poora paisa FD me bhi galat hai aur poora equity me bhi. Umar aur zaroorat ke hisaab se baantiye.

Nominee na bharna. Har account, har fund, har policy me nominee bhariye. Ye 5 minute ka kaam parivaar ko baad me mahinon ki dauda-daudi se bachaata hai.

Sabse aam galti samay aur jagah ka mel na baithana hai. 2 saal baad chahiye paisa equity me lagana, aur 20 saal ka retirement ka paisa FD me rakhna — dono nuksan karte hain. Pehla isliye ki market gir gaya to samay hi nahi bachta sambhalne ka; doosra isliye ki mehngai aur tax ke baad FD me paisa lagbhag wahin ka wahin rehta hai.
