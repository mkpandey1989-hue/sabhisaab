Formula aur uska matlab

Average (mean) nikaalne ke liye sabhi number ka jod karte hain aur ginti se bhaag dete hain. Ye sabse aam ausat hai. Tool saath me median (beech ka number) bhi de deta hai, jo tab kaam aata hai jab koi number bahut bada/chhota ho.

Formula:
Average = sabhi number ka jod ÷ kitne number hain
Misaal: (85+90+78+92+88) ÷ 5 = 433 ÷ 5 = 86.6

Mean sabhi number ka asar leta hai, isliye ek bahut bada ya chhota number ise kheench sakta hai. Aise me median (beech wala) zyada sahi tasveer deta hai.

Average roz ki zindagi me har jagah kaam aata hai — student apne test marks ka ausat dekhta hai, dukaandaar rozana bikri ka ausat, aur ghar chalane wala mahine ke kharch ka. Par ek baat yaad rakhein: average poore data ki ek chhoti kahani hai, aur wo har detail nahi batata. Do class ka ausat same ho sakta hai par ek me sab barabar hon aur doosre me kuch bahut aage kuch bahut peeche. Isi liye median aur sabse bada/chhota number bhi dekhna zaroori hai — poori tasveer tabhi banti hai.

Padhai me average ka sabse aam istemaal hai — saare subject ke marks ka ausat nikaal kar apni overall performance samajhna. Business me rozana ya mahine ki bikri ka ausat trend batata hai. Par savdhaan rahein: agar kisi mahine ek bahut bada order aa gaya, to wo ausat ko upar kheench dega aur tasveer asli se behtar dikhegi. Aise me median dekhna zyada imaandaar rehta hai — isliye ye tool dono deta hai.

Teen misaal, asli number ke saath

Misaal 1 — marks ausat. 85, 90, 78, 92, 88. Jod 433, ginti 5. Average = 86.6.

Misaal 2 — kharch. Mahine ke 6 din ka kharch jod kar ausat nikaalein — roz ka ausat kharch pata chalega.

Misaal 3 — outlier ka asar. 10, 12, 11, 13, 200. Mean = 49.2 (bada number ne kheench liya), par median 12 — asli tasveer.

Sabse aam galtiyan

  1. Khaali value ginna. Comma ke beech khaali jagah ko 0 na ginein — tool sirf asli number leta hai.
  2. Mean aur median mix. Ek bahut bada number ho to mean bhatka deta hai — tab median dekhein.
  3. Weighted average bhoolna. Agar kuch number zyada zaroori (weight) hain to simple average galat — weighted chahiye.
  4. Galat separator. Number ko comma ya space se alag karein, bina kisi aur chinh ke.
  5. Ginti galat karna. Average ginti par nirbhar — ek number chhootne se jawab badal jaata hai.

Bharat me kya alag hai

  • Padhai: Subject ke marks ka ausat, CGPA se pehle ka basic ausat — sab yahan.
  • Ghar ka budget: Roz/mahine ke kharch ka ausat nikaal kar planning aasaan.
  • Median kab: Jab data me kuch number bahut alag hon (jaise salary), median zyada sahi hota hai.
  • Business: Rozana sales ka ausat, ausat order value ya har staff ka ausat target — sab ek jagah aasaani se nikaal sakte hain, bina calculator ke jhanjhat.

Thodi aur gehraai se

Average (औसत) sunne me aasaan lagta hai — sab jodo, ginti se bhaag do — par asli samajh tab aati hai jab mean, median aur mode ka farak pata ho. Isi liye log "average kaise nikale", "mean median mode ka farak", "marks ka average" jaise search karte hain. Mean tab galat tasveer deta hai jab ek-do bahut bade ya chhote number (outlier) ho — jaise ek class me 9 bachchon ke 50 marks aur 1 ke 100; mean 55 aa jaata hai jo asli tasveer nahi.

Aise me median (beech ka number) zyada sacchi tasveer deta hai, aur mode (sabse zyada baar aane wala) batata hai ki "aam" value kya hai. Salary, marks aur cricket ke average me ye teeno alag kahani kehte hain. Marks ka percentage nikalna ho to Marks Percentage, aur padhai ke aur hisaab ke liye CBSE Best of 5 dekh lein.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Average kaise nikalte hain?

Sabhi number ka jod karke ginti se bhaag dein.

Mean aur median me farak?

Mean sabka ausat; median beech ka number. Outlier hone par median behtar.

Kitne number daal sakta hoon?

Jitne chahein — comma ya space se alag karke daalein.

Weighted average deta hai?

Ye simple average deta hai. Weighted ke liye har number ka weight chahiye.

Negative number chalega?

Haan, negative number bhi ausat me sahi ginte hain.

Average, median aur mode — teeno ka farak

Teeno "beech ka number" batate hain, par jawab bahut alag aata hai. Misaal: 6 logon ki tankhwah (hazaar me) — 20, 22, 24, 25, 26, 200.

KyaKaise nikalta haiJawab
Average (mean)Sab jodkar ginti se bhaagRs 52,800
MedianKram me lagakar beech walaRs 24,500
ModeJo sabse zyada baar aayakoi nahi
Dekhiye kaise ek bada number poori tasveer bigaad deta hai. Average Rs 52,800 kehta hai, jabki 6 me se 5 logon ki tankhwah Rs 26,000 se kam hai. Isi wajah se "ausat aay" wale aankde aksar dhokha dete hain — kuch bahut bade number poore ausat ko upar kheench lete hain. Jahan ek-do number baaki se bahut alag hon, wahan median zyada sacchi tasveer deta hai. Yahi baat property ke bhaav, salary survey aur exam ke number me bhi lagu hoti hai.
Zaroori baat: Ye simple average (mean) aur median deta hai. Weighted ya vishesh ausat ke liye alag tarika chahiye — zaroorat ke hisaab se sahi method chunein.
