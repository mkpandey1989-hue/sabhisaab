Doodh ka rate kaise tay hota hai

Dairy doodh ki quality par rate deti hai, sirf litre par nahi. Do cheezein sabse zaroori hain: Fat (doodh me chiknai) aur SNF (Solid Not Fat — doodh ke baaki thos tatva jaise protein, lactose). Dono jitne zyada, rate utna zyada.

Aam tareeka: Per litre rate = (Fat% + SNF%) × rate per point
(Har dairy ka apna chart hota hai — ye ek saral formula hai)

Fat aur SNF kya hote hain

Fat doodh ki chiknai hai — bhains ke doodh me zyada (6-8%), gaay ke doodh me kam (3.5-5%). SNF me protein, lactose aur minerals aate hain, aam taur par 8-9%. Achhi quality ke doodh me dono theek hote hain. Paani milane se dono ghat jaate hain, isliye rate bhi gir jaata hai.

Ek misaal

10 litre doodh, fat 4.0%, SNF 8.5%, rate ₹7 per point. Per litre = (4.0 + 8.5) × 7 = ₹87.50. 10 litre ka kul = ₹875. Agar fat 5% ho jaaye to per litre ₹94.50 ho jaata hai — yaani quality seedha paise se judi hai.

Zyada rate paane ke tareeke

  • Pashu ka aahaar achha rakhein — sahi chaara aur khal se fat-SNF badhta hai.
  • Paani bilkul na milaayein — isse fat-SNF girta hai aur rate kam ho jaata hai (aur bharosa bhi).
  • Saaf-safai — doodh saaf ho to quality achhi rehti hai.
  • Apni dairy ka rate chart samjhein — har dairy ka formula alag, use jaan lein.

Har dairy ka rate chart alag kyun hota hai

Ek hi doodh ko do dairy alag daam par le sakti hain, aur dono theek ho sakti hain. Wajah ye hai ki rate teen cheezon par tay hota hai:

  • Fat ka rate — har dairy apna tay karti hai aur samay ke saath badalta rehta hai.
  • SNF ko kitna wazan mile — kai jagah "Fat + SNF/3" wala formula chalta hai, kai jagah fat aur SNF ke alag-alag rate hote hain (do-axis chart).
  • Pashu ki kism — bhains ke doodh me fat zyada hota hai (aam taur par 6-8%), gaay ke doodh me kam (3.5-5%). Isi liye ek hi litre par daam alag baithta hai.
Isi liye society badalne se pehle sirf "kitna rate de rahe ho?" mat poochiye. Poochiye — "aapka rate chart dikhaiye" aur apne doodh ke fat-SNF par uska hisaab lagaiye. Jo dairy fat ka rate zyada de par SNF ka kam, wo bhains wale ko faayda de sakti hai aur gaay wale ko nuksan.

Ye calculator "Fat + SNF/3" wale aam formula par chalta hai, jo desh ke bade hisse me istemaal hota hai. Aapki society ka chart alag ho to us hisaab se rate ka box badal kar dekh lijiye.

Fat aur SNF badhane ke asli tareeke

Doodh ka daam badhane ka seedha rasta hai — fat aur SNF badhana. Ye pashu ke khaan-paan aur rakh-rakhaav se judte hain:

  • Hara chaara aur sookha chaara ka santulan — sirf hara chaara dene se fat girta hai. Sookha chaara (bhusa, ghas) fat badhane me madad karta hai.
  • Doodh nikalne ka tareeka — aakhiri dhaar me fat sabse zyada hota hai. Thano ko poora khaali na karne par fat seedha girta hai. Ye sabse aam aur sabse aasaan sudhaar hai.
  • Do waqt ka doodh alag — subah aur shaam ke doodh me fat alag hota hai. Milakar dene par ausat baithta hai.
  • Mineral mixture — santulit khuraak me mineral mixture dene se SNF me sudhaar dikhta hai.
  • Paani ki uplabdhta — pashu ko saaf paani jitna chahiye utna milna chahiye. Paani ki kami SNF girati hai.

Aur wo baat jo har dairy wala jaanta hai — doodh me paani milane se SNF girta hai, chhupta nahi. Aaj kal machine se fat aur SNF dono naape jaate hain, aur SNF ka girna paani ki seedhi nishaani hai. Isse rate to girta hi hai, society se bharosa bhi jaata hai.

Kheti-pashupalan ka poora hisaab lagana ho to Fasal Munafa Calculator me chaare aur mazdoori ka kharcha jodkar dekh sakte hain — doodh ka asli munafa tabhi saaf dikhta hai.

SNF ko log halke me lete hain, par wahi asli farak daalta hai. Fat to chaara badalne se ghat-badh jaata hai, jabki SNF pashu ki sehat aur paani ki maatra batata hai. SNF girna aksar isharaa hota hai ki pashu ko poora paani nahi mil raha ya khanij (mineral) ki kami hai. Har dairy ka formula aur per kg rate alag hota hai — apni samiti ka rate chart ek baar dekh lijiye, warna hisaab milega nahi. Aur doodh naapne se pehle achhi tarah milaiye; upar ki malai wale namoone se fat zyada aa jaata hai jo baad me shikayat banti hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Doodh ka rate kaise nikalta hai?

Aam taur par fat% aur SNF% jodkar dairy ke rate per point se guna karte hain. Har dairy ka apna chart hota hai, wahi antim maanein.

Fat aur SNF me kya farak hai?

Fat doodh ki chiknai hai, SNF (Solid Not Fat) me protein-lactose-minerals aate hain. Dono jitne zyada, rate utna zyada.

Paani milane se kya hota hai?

Fat aur SNF dono girte hain, isliye rate kam ho jaata hai. Aur dairy ka bharosa bhi tootta hai. Paani bilkul nahi milana chahiye.

Bhains aur gaay ke doodh ka rate alag kyun hota hai?

Fat ki wajah se. Bhains ke doodh me fat aam taur par 6-8% hota hai aur gaay ke doodh me 3.5-5%. Rate fat par tay hota hai, isliye per litre daam alag baithta hai.

Fat kaise badhaya ja sakta hai?

Sookhe aur hare chaare ka santulan rakhiye, aur doodh nikalte waqt thano ko poora khaali kijiye — aakhiri dhaar me fat sabse zyada hota hai. Yahi sabse aasaan sudhaar hai.

Dudh ki fat rate kaise tay hoti hai?

Har dairy apna rate chart banati hai. Aam taur par 'Fat + SNF÷3' wala formula chalta hai, jisme fat ka apna rate hota hai. Isi liye ek hi doodh ka daam do dairy me alag ho sakta hai.
