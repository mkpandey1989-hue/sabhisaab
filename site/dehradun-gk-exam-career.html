Kuch sandarbh Dehradun ke nahi hain. “Mukhyamantri Abhyudaya Coaching Yojana” Uttar Pradesh ki yojna hai. Uttarakhand ki apni yojna “Mukhyamantri Yuva Bhavishya Nirman Yojana” hai (Sept 2026). Dehradun GK: 100 Tathya (Patwari, Lekhpal, Constable, SI, Van Rakshak, VDO, PCS)

A. Pehchan aur Prashasan (1-20)

  • Dehradun Garhwal mandal ka zila hai.
  • Dehradun Uttarakhand ki interim (shitkaleen) rajdhani hai; Gairsain grishmkaleen rajdhani hai.
  • Uttarakhand rajya 9 November 2000 ko bana.
  • Dehradun ka kshetrafal 3,088 sq km hai.
  • Dehradun ki aabadi (Census 2011) 16,96,694 hai.
  • Dehradun ka sex ratio 902 hai.
  • Dehradun ki saksharta 84.25% hai (Census 2011).
  • Dehradun me 6 vikas khand hain: Chakrata, Kalsi, Vikasnagar, Sahaspur, Raipur, Doiwala.
  • Purane record me 6 tehsil: Dehradun, Chakrata, Vikasnagar, Kalsi, Tyuni, Rishikesh.
  • Dehradun me 10 vidhan sabha seat hain.
  • Chakrata vidhan sabha seat ST ke liye aarakshit hai.
  • Rajpur Road vidhan sabha seat SC ke liye aarakshit hai.
  • Dehradun zila Tehri Garhwal aur Haridwar, dono Lok Sabha me hai.
  • Dehradun me 11 shehri nikay hain (2 Nagar Nigam, 4 Nagar Palika Parishad, 1 Nagar Panchayat, 4 Cantonment Board).
  • Dehradun aur Rishikesh Nagar Nigam hain.
  • Dehradun ke 4 cantonment board: Dehradun, Clement Town, Landour, Chakrata.
  • Dehradun ka RTO code UK-07 hai.
  • Dehradun GPO ka PIN code 248001 hai.
  • Dehradun ka STD code 0135 hai.
  • Uttarakhand High Court Nainital me hai, Dehradun me nahi.

B. Itihas (21-45)

  • Kalsi me Ashoka ke 14 Major Rock Edicts ek hi patthar par hain.
  • Kalsi ka shila-lekh Brahmi lipi aur Prakrit bhasha me hai.
  • Kalsi ka shila-lekh Yamuna aur Tons ke sangam ke paas hai.
  • Maanyata hai ki Kalsi ka shila-lekh 1860 me Forrest ne khoja.
  • ASI ne Kalsi shila-lekh ka notification 23-11-1909 ko kiya.
  • Jagatgram (Badhwala) me Ashwamedh yagya ki teen agni-vedi mili.
  • Jagatgram ki eenton par Raja Silavarman (Pona) ka zikr hai.
  • Lakhamandal ke shila-lekh me Rajkumari Ishwara ka zikr hai (6vi sadi).
  • Guru Ram Rai ka Doon me dera 1675-76 ke aas-paas mana jata hai.
  • Jhanda Mela Holi ke paanchve din Guru Ram Rai Darbar me hota hai.
  • Khurbura ka yuddh 1804 me hua; Raja Pradyumna Shah maare gaye.
  • Nalapani (Khalanga) ka yuddh 31 October se 30 November 1814 tak chala.
  • Nalapani me British General Rollo Gillespie maare gaye.
  • Nalapani me Gorkha commander Balbhadra Kunwar (Thapa) the.
  • Khalanga War Memorial Sahastradhara road par hai (ASI).
  • Dehradun alag zila ke roop me 1871 se (kai sources).
  • Dehradun municipality 1867 me bani.
  • Dehradun tak rail 1900 me aayi (Haridwar se, jo 1886 me juda tha).
  • FRI ki sthapna 1906 (IFRI) me hui.
  • IMA ki sthapna 1932 me hui.
  • Doon School ki sthapna 1935 me hui.
  • Mussoorie ka Christ Church 1836 ka hai.
  • Landour ka St Paul’s Church 1840 ka hai.
  • Mussoorie ka Camel’s Back Cemetery 1828 ka hai.
  • Nehru ko Dehradun jail me 1932-33, 1934, 1940-41 aur 1941 me rakha gaya.

C. Bhugol aur Paryavaran (46-70)

  • Doon ghati lagbhag 75-80 km lambi structural valley hai.
  • Dehradun ka sabse nichla bindu Raiwala (lagbhag 315 m) hai.
  • Kharamba Peak (lagbhag 3,084 m) Chakrata kshetra ka sabse unchaa prasiddh shikhar hai.
  • Mussoorie ki ausat unchai lagbhag 2,005 m hai.
  • Lal Tibba (Landour) lagbhag 2,275 m par hai.
  • Chakrata lagbhag 2,118 m par hai.
  • Mundali (Chakrata) lagbhag 2,776 m par hai.
  • Dehradun me glacier nahi hai.
  • Yamuna aur Tons Kalsi ke paas milti hain.
  • Song aur Suswa Doiwala ke paas milti hain.
  • Rispana aur Bindal Dehradun shehar ki do barsati nadiyan hain.
  • Asan Conservation Reserve Uttarakhand ka pehla Ramsar site hai (2020).
  • Asan Barrage 1967 me bana; wetland lagbhag 444 hectare.
  • Tiger Falls lagbhag 312 feet unchaa hai (Chakrata).
  • Kempty Falls Mussoorie se lagbhag 13 km hai.
  • Sahastradhara ka matlab hazaar dhara hai.
  • Rajaji ko national park 1983 me aur tiger reserve 2015 me banaya gaya.
  • Rajaji Dehradun, Haridwar aur Pauri, teen zilon me hai.
  • Benog (Mussoorie) Wildlife Sanctuary 2 September 1993 ko ghoshit hui.
  • Dehradun ka jungle lagbhag 1,477 sq km (43.70%) hai.
  • Dehradun aam taur par seismic zone IV me hai.
  • Dehradun ki saalana barish lagbhag 2,050-2,075 mm hai.
  • July aur August Dehradun ke sabse barish wale mahine hain.
  • Dehradun me Himalayan monal (rajya pakshi) oonche jungle me milta hai.
  • 15-16 September 2025 ko Dehradun me badal fata (Sahastradhara, Maldevta).

D. Sanskriti aur Samaj (71-85)

  • Dehradun me ST aabadi 1,11,663 (lagbhag 6.58%) hai.

  • Buksa Dehradun me 17,684 hain.
  • Jaunsar-Bawar Chakrata, Kalsi, Tyuni ka pahadi kshetra hai.
  • Jaunsari Pashchimi Pahari boli hai; lagbhag 7.43% matribhasha.
  • Mahasu Devta ke chaar bhai: Botha, Pavasi, Vasik, Chalda.
  • Hanol ka Mahasu mandir Tons ke kinare hai.
  • Lakhamandal Yamuna ke kinare Shiva mandir hai.
  • Budhi Diwali-Diwali ke ek mahine baad Jaunsar me manayi jati hai.
  • Bissu fasal-kataai ka mela hai.
  • Harul aur Raso Jaunsari nritya hain.
  • Kothar Jaunsar-Bawar ka lakdi ka anaj-bhandar hai.

  • Mindrolling ke Great Stupa ki unchai 220 feet hai.
  • Mindrolling Monastery Clement Town me hai; 1965 me punarsthapit.

Dehradun: Coaching, Sarkari Pustakalay aur Reading Room

Dehradun UKPSC, UKSSSC, NDA, CDS, SSB aur RIMC jaisi pariksha ki taiyari ka ek bada kendra hai. Yahan kai coaching centre aur library hain. Chunne ke liye neeche ke tareeke dekhein.

Coaching Choose Karne ka Tareeka & Libraries

Coaching chunne ke 7 sawal:

  • Faculty ka naam aur anubhav kya hai?
  • Result ke daave ka pakka praman (roll number, official result) hai kya?
  • Free demo class milti hai kya?
  • Fees ki poori list aur refund policy likhit me hai kya?
  • Batch me kitne bachche hain?
  • Study material aur test-series ka sample dekha?
  • Registration (GST, shop registration) aur reviews kaise hain?

Defence coaching: Dehradun me NDA, CDS, SSB aur RIMC ke liye kai institute hain (kuch sources me Doon Defence Dreamers jaise naam aate hain).

Libraries aur reading rooms:

  • Doon Library & Research Centre jaisa naam Dehradun ke prasiddh library ke roop me aata hai (pata, samay aur membership).
  • College aur university libraries (DAV PG, DBS PG, Doon University, FRI ki library).
  • Zila/rajya ki sarkari public libraries ke naam aur samay ke liye zila prashasan/Sanskriti Vibhag se puchhein.
  • Self-study reading rooms Dehradun ke kai ilake me hain; fees aur samay alag.

Free options: Uttarakhand sarkar ki “Mukhyamantri Yuva Bhavishya Nirman Yojana” (Sept 2026) free online coaching aur study material deti hai (Yuva Bhavishya Nirman Yojana, Scholarships & Hostels). Zyada jaankari wahan dekhein.

Dehradun me NDA coaching kahan milti hai?
Kai institute hain; apni jaanch ke baad chunein.
Library me kaise padhein?
Library ka member bankar ya college/university library se.
Kya free coaching hai?
Haan, Mukhyamantri Yuva Bhavishya Nirman Yojana (online).

Agniveer yojna par charcha: Agniveer yojna par samaj me alag-alag raay hain. Kuch log ise yuvaon ko sena se jodne ka mauka maante hain, jabki kuch log ise lekar chinta jatate hain. 2026 me Dehradun me ek rajnitik dal ne is yojna ke virodh me rally nikali (Amar Ujala report).

Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC), Dehradun:

  • Class 8 me admission; likhit pariksha (RIMCEE, NTA dwara), phir viva-voce aur medical.
  • 2026 ke liye registration NTA portal (exams.nta.ac.in) par; pariksha 6 December 2026 batayi gayi (ek source me 7 Dec likha hai, isliye confirm karein).
  • Application fee (ek report ke anusar): General/OBC/Defence wards ₹1,000, SC/ST ₹500.
  • Prospectus aur eligibility ke liye RIMC ki official site (rimc.edu.in) dekhein.

IMA (Indian Military Academy): Officer banne ka rasta NDA, CDS, TES ya Army ke anya entry se hota hai (UPSC/Army ki official site dekhein). IMA me pravesh sirf training ke liye hota hai.

Physical training: Dehradun ke Parade Ground, Raipur ka sports complex jaise maidan khel ke liye prasiddh hain (Stadium, Khel College aur Aayojan). Daudne ki practice ke liye raat me ya akele sunsan raaste par na jayein; Rajaji ke aas-paas ke jungle wale raaston par hathi aur tendua ka khatra hai (Chir ke Jungle ki Aag , Bhoo-dhansav, Badal Phatna aur Jaanwaron se…). Sirf khule maidan aur din ke samay ka upyog karein.

Dhyan rakhein: Bharti me paise lene-dene wale agent se bachein. Army ki bharti puri tarah online aur merit par hoti hai.
