Is hisse ka maksad: Pauri Garhwal ko log aksar “raaste ka zila” samajhte hain, jabki yahan Himalaya ke 180 degree view wale hill station, Corbett ka jungle, Ganga ke rafting-stretch, Neelkanth ki yatra aur Lansdowne ki fauji shaanti ek hi zile me hain. Is hisse mein hum yatri ke nazariye se sab kuch ek jagah dete hain.

Imaandari ka niyam: Pauri Garhwal me skiing, 6,000 meter ke shikhar ya Bhotia-seema ke pass nahi hain. Doori, oonchai aur shulk “lagbhag” hain; yatra se pehle Zila Paryatan Vibhag ya sthaniya homestay se confirm karein.

Pauri Garhwal ki 20 Sabse Zaroori Ghumne ki Jagah

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ke Top 20 sthal hain: Pauri (Kandoliya aur Chaukhamba Viewpoint), Kyunkaleshwar Mahadev, Khirsu, Danda Nagraja, Jwalpa Devi, Srinagar ka Kamleshwar Mahadev, Dhari Devi, Lansdowne, Bhulla Tal, Tarkeshwar Mahadev, Ekeshwar, Binsar Mahadev, Doodhatoli, Kanvashram, Sidhbali (Kotdwar), Neelkanth Mahadev, Lakshman Jhula/Swarg Ashram, Corbett (Dhikala-Kalagarh) aur Uffrainkhal.

Har Jagah ki Unchai, Mukhyalaya se Doori, Entry Fee, Parking aur Samay

Seedha jawab: Zyadatar sthal mandir ya viewpoint hain, jahan pravesh shulk nahi lagta. Corbett jaise sanrakshit kshetra me van vibhag ka shulk aur permit lagta hai. Ideal samay aam taur par 1-3 ghante (mandir/viewpoint) se lekar 1-2 din (Corbett, Doodhatoli trek).

Parking ka haal: Pauri aur Lansdowne jaise shaharon me parking sadak ke kinare hi milti hai. Khirsu, Danda Nagraja aur Tarkeshwar jaise sthalon par gaadi ek nishchit point tak jaati hai, uske aage kuch hissa paidal hota hai. Weekend aur Navratri me bheed ho sakti hai.

Is soochi ko kaise padhein (Quick Guide):

  • Parivaar aur bujurg: Pauri, Chaukhamba Viewpoint, Khirsu, Jwalpa Devi, Kamleshwar, Dhari Devi, Sidhbali.
  • Shaanti aur photography: Khirsu, Lansdowne, Tarkeshwar, Ekeshwar.
  • Adventure: Doodhatoli trek, Corbett safari, Ganga rafting (Pauri Garhwal: Paani ke Khel: Rafting, Kayaking aur Mahseer Machhli…).
  • Yatri (Pilgrim): Neelkanth, Kamleshwar, Kyunkaleshwar, Danda Nagraja, Kanvashram.

Beatles Ashram (Chaurasi Kutia) aur Lansdowne ka Tip-in-Top

Seedha jawab: Beatles Ashram, jise Chaurasi Kutia bhi kehte hain, Swarg Ashram ke paas Ganga ke purvi kinare Maharishi Mahesh Yogi ka purana dhyan-ashram hai, jahan February 1968 me Beatles band ke sadasya aaye the. Yeh kshetra ab Rajaji Tiger Reserve ke prabandhan me hai aur shulk dekar dekha ja sakta hai. Lansdowne ka Tip-in-Top (Tiffin Top) shehar ka sabse lokpriya Himalaya viewpoint hai.

Zile ka bhram: Beatles Ashram aur Laxman Jhula ka purvi kinara Rishikesh shehar (Dehradun zila) ke paas hain, lekin prashasnik roop se Pauri Garhwal me maane jaate hain. Isliye "Beatles Ashram kis zile me hai" ka jawab Pauri Garhwal hai (seema record se confirm karein).

Pauri Garhwal: Hill Station aur Weekend par Ghumne ki Jagah

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ke mukhya hill station hain Pauri, Khirsu, Lansdowne aur Uffrainkhal, sabhi lagbhag 1,650 se 1,700 meter ke aas-paas. Weekend ke liye Delhi se sabse seedha raasta Kotdwar-Lansdowne hai, aur Dehradun/Rishikesh se Srinagar-Pauri-Khirsu.

Lokpriya Kasbe aur Shaant Pahadi Gaon (Himalaya ke Nazaron Wale)

Seedha jawab: Pauri aur Lansdowne “town-type” hill station hain, jahan hotel, bazaar aur suvidha hai. Khirsu aur Uffrainkhal jaise sthal shaant, jungle-ghire hain, jahan homestay aur chhote resort milte hain. Pauri se Chaukhamba, Neelkanth aur Garhwal Himalaya ke shikhar dikhai dete hain (saaf mausam me).

Chaukhamba Viewpoint ka tip: Himalaya ke shikhar subah ke pehle 2-3 ghante (sunrise se pehle se 9 baje tak) me sabse saaf dikhte hain. Dhundh aur baadal ke karan dopahar me aksar nahi dikhte. Monsoon me visibility kam hoti hai.

Weekend plan ka “Seedha Formula”:

  • Delhi se: Kotdwar (raat) → Lansdowne (1 raat) → Pauri/Khirsu (1 raat) → wapas.
  • Dehradun/Rishikesh se: Srinagar → Pauri → Khirsu (1 raat) → Dhari Devi → wapas.
  • Shaanti-focused: Seedha Khirsu me 2 raat, bina kisi doosri jagah gaye.

Honest note: Pauri Garhwal ke hill station Nainital ya Mussoorie jaise bhaari-bheed wale nahi hain. Ye unke liye behtar hain jo kam bheed, kam shor aur zyada hariyali chahte hain.

Pauri Garhwal: Kam Jaane-Pehchane aur Chhupe Gaon

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal me offbeat anubhav ke liye Khirsu ke aas-paas ke gaon (jaise Gwarh aur Khothgee), Adwani (seb ke baghiche), Thalisain ka Doodhatoli kshetra aur kam-pravesh wale ghati-gaon sabse bharosemand vikalp hain. Yahan ke zyadatar gaon sadak se jude hain, par kai ke aage paidal rasta hai.

Sadak Wale aur Paidal Wale Gaon: Slow Travel, Kalakar aur Birdwatcher ke Liye

Seedha jawab: Road-head gaon (jahan gaadi pahunchti hai) parivaar aur bujurg ke liye aasan hain. Walk-in gaon (jahan 1-5 km paidal chalna padta hai) slow travel, birdwatching aur likhne-padhne wale logon ke liye shaant hote hain. Booking se pehle gaon ke pradhan ya homestay maalik se rasta aur network zaroor poochhein.

Offbeat yatra ke 7 niyam:

  • Pehle poochhein: Gaon ke pradhan ya homestay se rasta, baarish me sadak ki sthiti aur network poochhein.
  • Cash saath rakhein: Kai gaon me ATM aur UPI network kam chalta hai (Pauri Garhwal: Mobile Network aur Internet: Asli Sthiti aur Pauri Garhwal: Bank, ATM, Dakghar aur Mandi Samiti).
  • Aadar se aayein: Gaon me Devta-sthan (Thaan) ke aas-paas shor na karein, jootein bahar utarein.
  • Photo se pehle poochhein: Logon ki tasveer lene se pehle anumati lein.
  • Kachra wapas le jayein: Plastic gaon me jalta nahi, wapas laayein.
  • Jungli phal/phool na todein: Van-vibhag aur gaon ke niyam ka dhyan rakhein.
  • Sthaniya ko vyapaar dein: Gaon ki dukaan, homestay aur guide se kharidein, taaki paisa gaon me rahe.

Isliye hum sirf wahi naam dete hain jo pehle se paryatan me jaane jaate hain.

Pauri Garhwal: Khaali Gaon (Palayan) aur Virasat ki Sair

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal Uttarakhand ke un zilon me hai jo palayan se sabse zyada prabhavit hain. Rural Development and Migration Commission (2018) ki report ke anusaar yahan aur Almora me khali ho chuke “bhutiya gaon” ki sankhya sabse zyada darj hui. Kai khali gaon ab heritage-walk, homestay aur reverse-migration ke kendra ban rahe hain.

Khaali Pathar ke Gaon jo Eco-Tourism / Heritage Project Bane

Seedha jawab: Khali gaon ke patthar ke ghar, pathal ki chhat aur tibari-kholi wali nakkashi ek jeeti-jaagti dharohar hain. Kuch jagah parivaar ya yuva in ghar ko homestay ya cafe me badal rahe hain. Aap aise gaon me jaakar heritage walk kar sakte hain, par ghar ke maalik ki anumati aur safety (saanp, tootti chhat, jungli janvar) ka dhyan zaroori hai.

Khali gaon ka “heritage walk” kaise karein?

Palayan ke 4 mukhya karan (Samajhne ke liye):

  • Rozgar: Gaon me naukri ke avsar kam hain.
  • Shiksha aur swasthya: Achhe school aur aspatal door hain.
  • Jungli janvar se fasal ka nuksaan: Bandar, suar aur gulbaag kheti ko nuksaan pahunchate hain (Pauri Garhwal: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh).
  • Sadak aur network: Kuch gaon sadak ya mobile network se door hain.

Ulta palayan (Reverse Migration): Corona ke baad kai parivaar gaon laute. Kuch ne homestay, seb ke bagh ya mushroom/medicinal herbs ka kaam shuru kiya. Aisi kahaniyan Pauri Garhwal: Palayan Vapasi ki Safal Kahaniyan me di gayi hain.

Kya aap apne gaon ki “khali ghar ki kahani” jodna chahte hain?

Pauri Garhwal: Trekking ki Poori Suchi (Aasan, Madhyam aur Kathin)

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ke trek mukhya roop se easy se moderate hain. Doodhatoli (lagbhag 3,100 m, Thalisain se) sabse oonchi trek-manzil hai. Neelkanth Mahadev (Swarg Ashram se), Kyunkaleshwar (Pauri ke paas), Tarkeshwar, Ekeshwar aur Khirsu ke jungle trail din ke treks hain. Yahan Himalayan high-altitude pass trek nahi hain.

Ek Din ki Hike, Weekend Trek, Bugyal Trek aur Oonche Darre

Seedha jawab: Day hike: Kyunkaleshwar, Kandoliya-Pauri jungle walk, Neelkanth (Swarg Ashram se), Tarkeshwar. Weekend trek: Doodhatoli. Pauri Garhwal me 4,000+ meter ke pass-crossing treks nahi hain; wo Chamoli, Uttarkashi aur Pithoragarh me hain.

Guide rate ke baare me: Guide aur porter ke rate season, doori aur samuh ke hisab se badalte hain. Zila paryatan vibhag ya sthaniya union se rate poochh kar tay karein. Aam taur par day-guide ka rate kuch sau rupaye se shuru hota hai, par ye alag-alag ho sakta hai.

Sanrakshit kshetra ka niyam: Corbett aur Rajaji jaise kshetra me bina anumati ke trek ya camping nahi ki ja sakti. Pauri Garhwal: Inner Line Permit , Van Anumati aur Suraksha Niyam me permit ka vistar hai.

Pauri Garhwal: Parvatarohan, Chattan Chadhai aur Bouldering

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal me IMF-listed bade Himalayan shikhar nahi hain, kyunki zile ki sabse oonchi jagah (Doodhatoli kshetra) lagbhag 3,000 m ke aas-paas hai. Parvatarohan ke liye Chamoli, Uttarkashi ya Pithoragarh behtar hain. Pauri me trekking, nature walk aur chhoti rock-climbing jaisi gatividhiyan hi sambhav hain.

IMF ki Chotiyan, Van Anumati, Porter/HAP aur Base Camp

Seedha jawab: Himalayan Mountaineering ke liye Indian Mountaineering Foundation (IMF) ki permit aur peak-wise fee lagti hai. Pauri Garhwal me aisa koi mukhya peak nahi hai, isliye ye vishay Pauri ke liye lagbhag lagu nahi hoti. Zile se Chaukhamba, Trishul jaise shikhar sirf door se dikhte hain.

Ek zaroori vyavahaarik baat: Agar koi tour company “Pauri me peak climbing” ka package bechti hai, to us se IMF/van vibhag ki permit ki copy maangein. Bina permit ke yatra me jokhim aur jurmana ho sakta hai.

Pauri Garhwal: Sardi ke Khel: Skiing, Snowboarding aur Barf mein Trek

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal me skiing ya snowboarding ka koi sthaapit centre nahi hai. Sardi me Khirsu, Pauri ke oonche hisson, Binsar aur Doodhatoli jaise oonche sthalon par kabhi-kabhi barf girti hai. Snow-trekking aur “barf dekhne” ka anubhav milta hai, par ye har saal ek jaisa nahi hota.

Ski Dhalaan, Chairlift/Ropeway, Saman ka Kiraya aur GMVN/NIM ke Course

Seedha jawab: Skiing ke liye Auli (Chamoli) Uttarakhand ka mukhya kendra hai. Pauri me ski slope, chairlift ya ropeway ka koi sthaapit ski-kendra nahi hai. Barf ka anubhav January-February me oonche sthalon par mil sakta hai.

Sardi me Pauri Garhwal jaane ke tips:

  • Ice-forming sadak (khaaskar subah) par dheere chalayein.
  • Garm kapde, mazboot jooton aur thermos zaroor rakhein.
  • Barf girne ki khabar sthaniya homestay se poochh kar hi safar tay karein.
  • Heater ya bijli ke plan ke liye Pauri Garhwal: Bijli, Solar aur Sardi mein Paani ki Sthiti padhein.

Pauri Garhwal: Paani ke Khel: Rafting, Kayaking aur Mahseer Machhli Pakadna

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ke Ganga kinare ka hissa (Rishikesh ke aas-paas, Lakshman Jhula/Swarg Ashram kshetra) river rafting ke liye prasiddh hai. Rafting mukhya roop se Shivpuri/Kaudiyala se Rishikesh ke beech hoti hai. Fishing (angling) ke liye matsya aur van vibhag ki anumati zaroori hai, aur kuch jagah catch-and-release niyam hai.

Rafting ke Hisse aur Grade, Mahseer-Trout Machhli Pakadne ka Permit

Seedha jawab: Ganga me rafting ke popular stretch Brahmpuri/Shivpuri/Kaudiyala se Rishikesh tak hain. Rapids lagbhag Grade I se IV tak hote hain. Famous rapids me Roller Coaster, Golf Course, Three Blind Mice aur Black Money ke naam aate hain. Monsoon me rafting aam taur par band rehti hai.

Dhyan dein: Stretch ki lambai aur rapid ka grade operators ke hisab se thoda alag ho sakta hai. Hum yahan sirf aam jaankari de rahe hain. Booking se pehle operator ka registration, life jacket, helmet aur guide ka experience zaroor check karein.

Fishing (Angling) ke baare me: Garhwal ki nadiyon me Golden Mahseer aur Snow Trout jaisi machhliyan milti hain. Machhli pakadne ke liye Uttarakhand Matsya Vibhag aur Van Vibhag ki anumati/license zaroori hoti hai. Kai jagah catch-and-release ka niyam hai. Bina permit ke fishing par jurmana lag sakta hai. Corbett aur Rajaji jaise sanrakshit kshetron me fishing aam taur par pratibandhit hai.

Kanooni dhyan: Rafting aur fishing ke niyam, shulk aur mausam badalte rehte hain. Taaza jaankari ke liye Uttarakhand Paryatan Vibhag aur Zila Prashasan se sampark karein.

Pauri Garhwal: Hawai Khel, Paragliding, Mountain Biking aur Camping

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal me paragliding abhi mukhya roop se sthaapit paryatan gatividhi nahi hai. Mountain biking aur camping ke liye Khirsu ke jungle trail, Lansdowne ke aas-paas ke raste aur Ganga kinare ke camp (Rishikesh kshetra) bharosemand hain. Camping sirf anumati-praapt jagahon par karein.

Paragliding ki Jagah, Tandem Udaan ka Kiraya, MTB Raaste aur Manzoor Camping

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand me paragliding ke mukhya kendra Bhimtal, Naukuchiatal, Mussoorie aur Narendranagar kshetra hain. Aisa koi package mile to uska permit aur safety record check karein.

  • Akele jungle me na jayein, guide ya group ke saath jayein.

Pauri Garhwal: Taiyar Yatra Yojana (3, 5, 7 aur 10 Din)

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ke liye 4 itinerary: 3 din (Weekend), 5 din (Parivaar aur Mandir circuit), 7 din (Trekker/Backpacker) aur 10 din (Poora zila). Safar Delhi/Dehradun se maana gaya hai. Doori aur samay aam andaza hain, sadak ki sthiti aur mausam ke hisab se badal sakte hain.

Yatra Yojana: 3, 5, 7 aur 10 Din

Seedha jawab: 3 din: Kotdwar-Lansdowne-Pauri/Khirsu. 5 din: Kotdwar-Kanvashram-Lansdowne-Pauri-Khirsu-Srinagar-Dhari Devi. 7 din: Wahi + Doodhatoli trek. 10 din: Wahi + Corbett (Dhikala) + Neelkanth/Swarg Ashram.

Pauri Garhwal: Mahine-dar-Mahine Yatra Guide (January se December)

Seedha jawab: Pauri Garhwal ghoomne ka sabse achha samay October-November (saaf mausam, Himalaya view) aur March-April (buransh aur vasant) hai. Garmi me nichle ilake (Kotdwar, Srinagar) garm hote hain par Pauri-Khirsu sukhad. Monsoon (July-September) me landslide ka khatra aur rafting band rehti hai.
