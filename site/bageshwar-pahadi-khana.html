Bhatt ki Churkani kya hai aur kaise banti hai?

Bhatt ki Churkani kaale bhatt (black soybean) ko bhoon kar, pees kar aur chawal ke aate ke saath pakai jaane wali pahadi dish hai. Isme protein bahut hota hai, isliye pahadon ki thandi me yeh sabse pasandida khana hai.

Seedhi vidhi (ghar ke liye andaza, matra aapke swaad par): 1. Kaale bhatt ko dheemi aanch par lohe ki kadhai me bhoon lein, jab tak khushboo na aa jaye. 2. Thanda karke mote pees lein. 3. Kadhai me tel ya ghee garam karein, jakhiya ya jeera, hing, adrak-lahsun daalein. 4. Pisa hua bhatt aur thoda chawal ka aata paani me ghol kar daalein; haldi, mirch, namak milayein. 5. Dheemi aanch par lagatar chalate hue gaadha hone tak pakayein. 6. Garam chawal ya roti ke saath parosein.

Kaisa swaad aata hai?
Dhuaan-dar, mitti jaisa, thoda sa nutty. Lohe ki kadhai ka swaad isme sabse zyada aata hai.
Bhatt kya hota hai?
Pahadi kaala soybean, jo Uttarakhand ki zyadatar pahadi kheti me hota hai.
Churkani aur Churdkani me antar?
Dono ek hi dish ke alag spelling/uchcharan hain.
Bageshwar me Bhatt ki Churkani kahan milegi?
Ghar-ghar me aur kai sthaniya khane ki jagahon par.

Dubuk: pisi dal ki dheemi aanch wali dish

Dubuk (Dubke) me bhigoyi gahat ya bhatt ko pees kar paste banaya jata hai aur lohe ki kadhai me dheemi aanch par pakaya jata hai. Yeh Kumaon ki sardiyon ki sabse pasandida dishes me gini jaati hai.

  • Banti kaise hai: Dal raat bhar bhigoyi jaati hai, subah pees kar paste banti hai, phir tel/ghee me jakhiya-hing ka tadka laga kar paste ko pakaya jata hai.
  • Garhwal ke Phaanu se antar: Dono ki buniyadi vidhi milti-julti hai (bhigoyi dal ka paste), par naam aur masale kshetra ke hisaab se badalte hain.
  • Saath me kya khayein: Garam chawal, bhang ki chutney ya pahadi raita.

Ras-Bhaat: ek hi dish me kai dalon ka swaad

Ras-Bhaat Kumaon ka wo khana hai jisme chana, bhatt, rajma aur gahat jaisi kai dalon ko pakakar unka ras banaya jata hai aur chawal ke saath parosa jata hai. Dalen alag nikaal kar chawal me milakar khane ka bhi chalan hai. Yeh khana khaas tor par sardi aur tyoharon par pasand kiya jata hai.

Aloo ke Gutke: Uttarakhand ka sabse mashhoor snack-sabzi

Aloo ke Gutke pahadi aloo ko ubaal kar jakhiya ya jeera, lal mirch aur hare dhaniya ke saath bhoon kar banayi jaati hai. Yeh poori, roti, raita aur chai ke saath khayi jaati hai, aur safar me bhi pasand ki jaati hai.

  • Pahadi aloo ki khaasiyat: Chhota, thoda mitti-sa swaad wala aloo, jo plains ke aloo se alag mehsoos hota hai.
  • Tadka: Jakhiya (jungli sarson-jaisa beej) pahadi swaad ki pehchan hai.
  • Kyon safar me achcha: Sukhi sabzi hai, jaldi kharab nahi hoti, poori ya paratha ke saath yatra me le jaayi jaati hai.

Kafuli, Chainsoo aur Phaanu: Garhwal ke naam, Kumaon me bhi

Kafuli (palak-methi ka gaadha saag), Chainsoo (bhuni kali dal ki sabzi) aur Phaanu (pisi dal) Garhwali dishes mani jaati hain. Bageshwar me inka swaad ghar-ghar me banta hai, par Kumaoni naam alag ho sakte hain.

  • Kafuli me palak aur methi ko lohe ki kadhai me dheemi aanch par pakate hain aur chawal ya gehun ke aate se gaadha karte hain. Kai jagah ise "Kapa" kehte hain.
  • Chainsoo me dal pehle bhooni jaati hai, phir pees kar pakayi jaati hai; isliye iska swaad bhuna hua aur dhuaan-dar hota hai.
  • Phaanu me bhigoyi dal ko pees kar paste ki tarah pakaya jata hai.

Thechwani aur Jholi: sabse saral pahadi khane

Thechwani kuti hui mooli ya aloo ki teekhi sabzi hai, aur Jholi patla khatta jhol hai jo dahi aur besan se banta hai. Dono saral hain, par pahadi thali ki pehchan hain.

  • Thechwani: "Thechna" ka arth hai kootna ya kuchalna. Isliye iska naam padha.
  • Jholi: Dahi, besan aur hing ke saath banne wala patla jhol, kadhi jaisa, par zyada halka. Ise chawal ke saath khate hain.

Bageshwar ki thali: ek poora pahadi khana kaisa dikhta hai?

Ek saral Kumaoni thali me aksar chawal, mandua ki roti, ek dal (gahat/bhatt), ek saag ya sabzi (Aloo ke Gutke), raita aur chutney hoti hai. Meethe me jhangora ki kheer ya singori milti hai.

Bageshwar: Paramparik Roti aur Chawal ke Pakwan

Mandua ki roti, Gahat ke parathe aur Jhangora ka bhaat pahadi anaj ki pehchan hain. Mandua (ragi) gluten-free hota hai, aur pahadon me sardi me garm roti ke saath ghee ya gud khane ka chalan hai.

Mandua ki Roti, Gahat ke Parathe (Bedoo/Bhari Roti), Chhoi/Lesu Roti, Jhangore ki Bhaat & Gahat Ras-Bhaat

Mandua ki roti pahadi rasoi ki sabse prasiddh roti hai. Mandua (finger millet / ragi) ka aata garam paani se goondh kar haath se thap kar tawa aur aag par sekte hain. Yeh gluten-free aur pahadon ki sardi ke liye uttam maana jata hai.

Mandua ki roti kaise banti hai?

  • Mandua ka aata thoda sa chhaan lein.
  • Ubalte paani me aata daal kar jaldi se chalayein, ya garam paani se goondhein.
  • Thoda thanda hone par haath se masal kar naram goondh lein.
  • Peda bana kar haath se thap kar roti ka aakar dein (belna mushkil hota hai).
  • Garam tawa par sekein, phir seedhi aag par phulayein.
  • Ghee, gud, gahat ki dal ya bhang ki chutney ke saath khayein.

Tip: Mandua ka aata zyada sookha na rahe, warna roti tootegi. Garam paani sahi matra me ho.

Mandua ke baare me kya kaha jata hai: Kai sources ke anusar mandua me calcium chawal aur gehun se zyada hota hai. Exact matra variety aur jagah par nirbhar karti hai.

Gahat ke Parathe aur Bedoo/Bedu roti

Gahat ke parathe me gahat ki pisi hui dal ko aate me bharkar roti banayi jaati hai. Bedu roti, jo Garhwal-Kumaon dono me jaani jaati hai, me urad ki dal ki pithi bharkar roti ya poori banayi jaati hai. Sardi me ghee ya makkhan ke saath yeh bahut pasand ki jaati hai.

  • Gahat ka paratha: Pisi hui, masaledar gahat dal ka bharwan; garam khaya jaata hai.
  • Bedu roti: Urad ki pithi ka bharwan, poori jaise tali jaati hai ya tawe par sakti hai; saath me aloo ke gutke.

Gahat kya hota hai? Kulath ya horse gram, jo pahadon me garmaahat wali dal maani jaati hai.

Chhoi / Lesu roti

Lesu roti Kumaon ki ek roti hai jise gehun ya mandua ke aate se banate hain. Naam aur vidhi gaon ke hisaab se alag ho sakti hai. Gaon-wise vidhi ke liye local survey zaroori hai.

Jhangora ka bhaat aur Jhangora ki kheer

Jhangora (Madira, barnyard millet) ka bhaat chawal jaisa pakta hai aur halka, jaldi hajam hone wala maana jata hai. Isi se Jhangora ki kheer bhi banti hai, jo tyoharon aur dawat ki meethi dish hai.

  • Bhaat: Jhangora ko dho kar paani me ubalte hain, chawal jaisa.
  • Kheer: Doodh, gud ya cheeni, elaichi aur ghee ke saath dheemi aanch par.
  • Search: "Jhangora kya hota hai", "Jhangora ki kheer kaise banti hai", "Jhangora ke fayde".

Gahat Ras-Bhaat aur Lal chawal

Gahat ka ras aur lal chawal ka jodi pahadi sardiyon ka paramparik khana hai. Lal chawal (Ukhad Dhan) kai pahadi ilakon me ugta hai aur iska swaad mote, mitti-sa hota hai.

Mandua aur ragi ek hi hain?
Haan, mandua finger millet ka pahadi naam hai, aur ragi uska South India me prachlit naam.
Mandua ki roti ghee ke saath kyon khate hain?
Swaad aur sardi me garmi ke liye; yeh paramparik chalan hai.
Jhangora aur chawal me antar?
Jhangora ek chhota millet hai, chawal alag anaj hai.

Bageshwar: Chutney, Raita, Achaar aur Pisyun Loon

Bhang ki chutney, pahadi kheere ka raita aur pisyun loon (silbatte par pisa hua namak) pahadi khane ka asli swaad hain. Bhang ki chutney me sirf bhang ke beej (hemp seeds) use hote hain, jo non-psychoactive aur khaane ke liye hote hain.

Bhangjeera & Bhang ki Chutney, Pahadi Kheere ka Raita, Darim Chutney, Timur Chutney & Linguda/Mooli Achar

Bhang ki chutney (hemp seed chutney)

Bhang ki chutney bhune hue bhang ke beej, hari mirch, lahsun, namak aur nimbu ko silbatte par pees kar banti hai. Isme sirf beej use hote hain, patte ya paudhe ka koi nasha-wala hissa nahi.

Vidhi (andaza): 1. Bhang ke beej halke bhoon lein (jalne na dein). 2. Silbatte par hari mirch, lahsun, namak aur dhaniya patta ke saath pees lein. 3. Nimbu ka ras ya thoda paani daal kar chutney jaisi banayein. 4. Mandua ki roti, Dubuk ya chawal ke saath parosein.

Zaroori spashtikaran: Khaane me sirf beej ka istemal hota hai. Bhang ke paudhe ke patte, phool ya nasha-wale hisse khaane-peene me use karna kanooni roop se pratibandhit ho sakta hai. Uttarakhand ke niyam badalte rahte hain; is page ka maksad sirf pahadi khane ki jaankari dena hai.

Bhangjeera: Perilla ke beej, jo pahadi khane me chutney aur tadke me aate hain. Inka swaad nutty hota hai.

Pahadi kheere ka raita (kakdi ka raita)

Pahadi raita me bada, peela, ras bhara kheera kas kar dahi me milaya jata hai aur upar se pisi hui rai (sarson) ki teekhi mehek daali jaati hai. Yeh Kumaoni khane ka sabse mashhoor raita hai.

  • Kheera: Plains ke patle kheere se alag, bada aur ras-bhara.
  • Rai: Pisi hui rai ya sarson ki jhaal nak tak jaati hai, jo iska pehchan hai.
  • Saath me kya: Mandua ya gehun ki roti, aloo ke gutke, ya sardi ki dal.

Search: "Kumaoni raita kaise banta hai", "Pahadi kheere ka raita recipe".

Darim (Anardana) ki chutney

Darim ya jungli anaar (wild pomegranate) ke sukhe daane pahad me chutney aur khatta swaad dene ke liye use hote hain. Inhe anardana bhi kehte hain. Yeh khatti-meethi chutney sardi me roti ke saath khayi jaati hai.

Timur ki chutney

Timur (Zanthoxylum armatum) ek pahadi masala hai jiska swaad jeebh ko halka sunn kar dene wala aur khushbudar hota hai. Timur ki chutney aur timur wala namak pahadi swaad ki alag pehchan hain.

  • Timur kahan milta hai: Pahadi masale ki dukano aur melon me.
  • Istemal: Chutney, pisyun loon, aur kai dalon ke tadke me.

Linguda / Mooli ka achar

Linguda (fiddlehead fern) ki sabzi aur achar barsat ke mausam me pahadon me banate hain. Mooli ka achar sardi me sab jagah banta hai. Linguda ko jungle se tod kar, saaf karke hi khana chahiye, isliye ise local jaankar se hi len.

Pisyun Loon: Silbatte par Pisa Pahadi Namak

Pisyun Loon silbatte par pisa hua masaledar namak hai, jisme lahsun ke patte, hari mirch, dhaniya, jeera aur timur milaye jaate hain. Ise phal, mooli aur roti ke saath khaate hain.

Nimbu Saana: Galgal (bada pahadi nimbu) ko chheel kar dahi, bhang ke beej, gud aur namak ke saath masal kar khaya jata hai. Sardi ki dhoop me baith kar khana ek pahadi parampara hai (vidhi gaon-gaon me alag).

Bageshwar: Jungli Khane Layak Cheezein aur Mausami Saag

Bicchu ghas (sisunak), linguda, kafal, hisalu, kilmora, ghingaru aur bedu pahad ke jungli khane hain jo mausam ke hisaab se milte hain. Inme se zyadatar garmi aur barsat me paakte hain, aur inhe tabhi khaana chahiye jab aap unhe pehchante hon.

Safety note (zaroori): Jungle ke kisi bhi paudhe ya phal ko bina pehchane na khayein. Kai jungli paudhe zehreele ho sakte hain. Is section ka maksad sirf sanskriti aur jaankari dena hai, jungle se chunne ka nirdesh dena nahi. Van vibhag ke niyam aur sanrakshit kshetra ke chunne par pratibandh bhi lagoo ho sakte hain.

Jungli Saag-Phal: Bichhu Ghaas, Linguda, Kankoda, Ghingaru, Kafal, Hisalu, Kilmora, Bedu aur Buransh Ras

Bicchu ghas (Sisunak): jo chhune par jalti hai, par pakne par saag ban jaati hai

Bicchu ghas (Urtica dioica, Kumaon me aksar "Sisunak/Sisoon", Garhwal me "Kandali") ek jhanjhanahat paida karne wala paudha hai. Pakane par iska jalan khatam ho jata hai aur yeh pahadi saag ban jata hai.

  • Kaise pakta hai: Dastane pehen kar patte tode jaate hain, ubaale jaate hain, phir dal ya saag ki tarah pakaye jaate hain.
  • Kyon khaas: Kai sources ise iron aur anya poshak tatvon se bharpoor batate hain; exact matra ke liye kisi nutrition source ki zaroorat hogi.
  • Dusra upyog: Iske resheon se kapda bhi banta hai (Bageshwar: Hathkargha, GI-Tag Utpad aur Sthaniya Hastshilp me dekhein).
  • Search: "Bicchu ghas ka saag kaise banta hai", "Kandali ka saag", "Sisunak kya hai".

Kafal kyon khaas hai?
Kafal Uttarakhand ka sabse bhavnatmak jungli phal hai. Garmiyon me sadak kinare tokriyon me bikta hai, aur kai logon ke liye yeh bachpan ki yaad hai. Is phal par ek prasiddh Kumaoni lok-geet aur lok-katha bhi hai. (Lok-geeton par alag charcha Bageshwar: Lori aur Bachon ke Khel Geet me hai.)

Buransh (Rhododendron) ka sharbat

Buransh (Rhododendron arboreum) Uttarakhand ka rajya phool hai. Iske laal phoolon se bana sharbat (squash) pahadon me garmi me pasand kiya jata hai.

Dhyan dein: Rhododendron ki saari prajatiyan khane layak nahi hotin, aur kuch prajatiyon ke phoolon se bana shahad bhi nashili ya ashudh ho sakta hai. Sharbat hamesha pehchanshuda, bazaar se bike, label wale utpaad se hi lein.

  • Mausam: Buransh kai jagah march-april ke aas-paas khilta hai, unchai ke hisaab se badalta hai.
  • Swaad: Halka khatta-meetha.
  • Bageshwar me kahan: Kausani aur ooche kshetron ke jungle me buransh ke ped milte hain.

Ghughutiya aur ghughute: Bageshwar ki sabse khaas meethi parampara

Ghughutiya Makar Sankranti (14 January) par Kumaon me manaya jane wala tyohaar hai. Isme gehun ke aate aur gud se bane tale hue pakwan (ghughute) bachche mala ki tarah pehente hain aur kauwon ko khilate hain.

  • Kab: Aam taur par 14 January, Makar Sankranti ke din.
  • Kya banta hai: Gehun ka aata aur gud ki goondh se alag-alag aakar (jaise talwar, dhaal, damru, anar, star jaise) ke pakwan tale jaate hain.
  • Bachche kya karte hain: Subah ghughute ki mala pehen kar kauwon ko bulate hain aur khilate hain.
  • Bageshwar se rishta: Uttarayani Mela, jo Saryu-Gomti ke tat par Bageshwar me Makar Sankranti par lagta hai, isi tyohaar ke aas-paas hota hai.

Dhyan dein: Kaun se mahine/tithi par kab mela shuru hota hai aur kitne din chalta hai, yeh har saal badal sakta hai. Mela aam taur par Makar Sankranti ke aas-paas ek hafta chalta hai. Tithi ke liye sarkari suchna dekhein.

Singori

Singori khoya se bani, shankhu aakar ki mithai hai jise maalu ke patte me lapet kar parosa jata hai. Patte ki khushbu iski pehchan hai. Yeh Almora aur Kumaon kshetra ki mashhoor mithai maani jaati hai.

  • Kis cheez se banti hai: Khoya (mawa), cheeni, aur maalu ke patte.
  • Kab khayein: Taaza, kyunki khoya jaldi kharab ho sakta hai.
  • Bageshwar me milti hai? Kumaon ke kai bazaaron me milti hai; Bageshwar ki kisi khaas dukaan ka naam (sthaniya pushti baaki).

Bal Mithai aur "Chocolate" (Almora ki khoya mithai)

Bal Mithai bhune hue khoya aur cheeni ki gol-chhoti daano se dhaki bhoore rang ki mithai hai, jo Almora ki pehchan hai. "Chocolate" ka matlab yahan Almora ki hi ek alag khoya mithai se hai, na ki bazaar wali chocolate. Dono Kumaon me tyoharon ya yatra ki saugat ke roop me pasand ki jaati hain.

Jhangora ki kheer

Jhangora ki kheer jhangora (barnyard millet), doodh aur gud ya cheeni se bani meethi dish hai, jo tyoharon aur khaas mauko par banti hai.

Rote (Roth / Roat)

Rote gehun ke aate, gud aur ghee se bani moti, meethi roti jaisa prasad hai jo Devtaon ko chadhaya jata hai. Isi se Golu Devta jaise mandiron me manokamna poori hone par rote chadhane ka chalan hai.
