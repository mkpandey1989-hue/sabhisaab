Age Calculator

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Free · Bina login · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Apni ya kisi ki thik-thik umar jaanni ho to ye tool sabse aasaan hai. Janm tarikh daalein — umar saal, mahine aur din tak turant nikal aayegi. Form bharne, school admission, ya sarkari yojana ki patrata check karne me kaam aata hai.

Umar kaise ginte hain

Umar ginne ka sahi tarika ye hai ki pehle poore saal ginein, phir bache hue mahine, phir bache hue din. Sirf saal ghata dena (jaise 2026 − 1990 = 36) galat jawab de sakta hai, kyunki us saal ka janmdin aaya ya nahi ye bhi maayne rakhta hai.

Leap year: har 4 saal me February 29 din ki hoti hai. Ye tool leap year ka dhyan rakhta hai, isliye jawab bilkul theek aata hai.

Umar ka hisaab kahan-kahan kaam aata hai

Sirf janmdin jaanne ke liye nahi — bharat me umar ka theek hisaab kai jagah zaroori hota hai:

  • Sarkari naukri ka form: aayu seema (age limit) ki aakhri tarikh par aapki umar kitni hai, ye tay karta hai ki form bhar sakte hain ya nahi. Ek din ka farak bhi maayne rakhta hai.
  • Pension aur senior citizen ke fayde: 60 saal poore hone par FD par extra byaj, alag tax limit, aur SCSS jaisi yojanaein khulti hain.
  • Insurance premium: umar badhne par premium badhta hai. Kai company "nearest birthday" ke hisaab se umar leti hain — isliye kuch din ka farak premium badal sakta hai.
  • School admission: aam taur par ek tay tarikh (jaise 31 March) ko umar kitni hai, uspar class tay hoti hai.
  • Retirement planning: nivesh ke liye kitne saal bache hain, ye seedha corpus tay karta hai.

Umar teen tarah se gini jaati hai — aur teeno alag jawab dete hain

"Meri umar kitni hai" ka jawab ek nahi hota. Form ke hisaab se ginti badal jaati hai, aur yahi confusion ki jad hai:

  • Completed age (poori hui umar): aakhri janmdin par jo umar thi. Sarkari form, exam aur naukri me aam taur par yahi maani jaati hai.
  • Running age (chalti umar): completed age + 1. Kuch jagah ise "36ve saal me chal rahe hain" kaha jaata hai.
  • Nearest birthday: jo janmdin sabse paas hai. Kai insurance company premium isi hisaab se lagati hain — isliye janmdin ke aas-paas policy lene par premium badal jaata hai.

Passport, visa aur medical me saal-mahine-din teeno maange jaate hain; bacchon ke maamle me kabhi-kabhi kul din bhi. Form bharte waqt hamesha dekh lijiye ki wahan kaunsi umar maangi ja rahi hai — aam taur par completed age hi chalti hai.

Sarkari bharti me ek baat sabse zaroori hai — umar "cut-off date" par gini jaati hai, aaj ki tareekh par nahi. Notification me likha hota hai jaise "1 January ko umar 21 se 30 ke beech honi chahiye". Us tareekh par apni umar nikaaliye, aaj ki nahi. Har saal hazaaron form isi galti se reject hote hain.

Log yahan galti kya karte hain

  1. Saal ghata kar umar nikaal lena. 2026 − 1990 = 36 — ye tabhi sahi hai jab janmdin aa chuka ho. Nahi aaya to umar 35 hai. Form me ye galti seedha rejection kara deti hai.
  2. Leap year bhool jaana. 29 February ki janm tarikh wale logon ka hisaab alag hota hai — kuch jagah 28 Feb, kuch jagah 1 March maana jaata hai.
  3. "Aakhri tarikh" ki jagah "aaj" ki umar dekhna. Sarkari form me umar hamesha notification me di gayi cut-off date par gini jaati hai, form bharne ke din par nahi.
  4. Documents me alag-alag janm tarikh. Aadhaar, 10th ki marksheet aur PAN me tarikh alag hone se form atak jaata hai. Sarkari naukri me aam taur par 10th ki marksheet wali tarikh maani jaati hai.

Bharat me umar se judi khaas baatein

  • Aayu me chhoot (age relaxation): kai sarkari bhartiyon me alag-alag shreniyon ko upper age limit me chhoot milti hai. Ye chhoot notification me likhi hoti hai — form bharne se pehle wahi padhein.
  • Senior citizen: aam taur par 60 saal, aur "super senior" ke liye 80 saal — dono par tax aur bank ke niyam alag hote hain.
  • Naabalig: 18 saal se kam umar walon ke naam bank khaata, PPF ya nivesh maa-baap ke through hi hota hai.
  • NPS aur PPF: inme umar ki apni shartein hoti hain — jaise NPS me ek tay umar tak hi account khul sakta hai.
