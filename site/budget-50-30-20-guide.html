50/30/20 Budget Rule — Salary Ka Sahi Hisaab

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Har mahine salary aati hai aur pata hi nahi chalta kahan chali gayi? Ye har kisi ke saath hota hai. 50/30/20 rule ek bahut aasaan tareeka hai apni income ko teen hisso me baantne ka — taaki kharcha bhi ho, thodi mauj bhi ho, aur savings bhi bane. Ye percentage aapki in-hand (take-home) salary par lagti hai.

50% — Needs (zaroori kharche)

Ye wo kharche hain jo emergency me bhi rukenge nahi: ghar ka rent ya home loan EMI, ration, bijli-paani-gas-internet, aana-jaana, zaroori insurance, aur baccho ki school fees. Agar aapke needs 50% se zyada ja rahe hain (bade shehar me rent zyada), to Wants me se adjust karein — par Savings ko haath mat lagayein.

30% — Wants (shauk aur mauj)

Ye zindagi ka maza hai, par control me: bahar khaana, movies, OTT, naye kapde, gadgets, ghoomna-firamna. Wants galat nahi hain — bas 30% ke andar rahein. Yahi wo hissa hai jahan zyadatar log budget se bahar jaate hain.

20% — Savings aur karz

Ye aapke future ka hissa — sabse pehle nikaalein, baad me nahi:

  • Emergency fund (agar abhi tak nahi bana).
  • SIP / mutual fund long-term wealth ke liye.
  • PPF / NPS retirement ke liye.
  • Credit card / personal loan jaldi khatam karna — inka byaj bahut zyada hota hai, isliye ye "saving" jaisa hi hai.
Niyam: "salary aate hi 20% alag" — kharch ke baad jo bache wo save karne ki soch se aksar kuch nahi bachta.

Ek misaal — ₹40,000 salary

  • Needs (50%): ₹20,000
  • Wants (30%): ₹12,000
  • Savings/Loan (20%): ₹8,000

Apni salary ke hisaab se ye teen figure turant nikaalne ke liye upar diya 50/30/20 Budget Calculator use karein.

Ye rule sab par fit nahi baithta — aur theek hai

50/30/20 ek shuruaati dhaancha hai, patthar ki lakeer nahi. Kam salary ya bade shehar me needs 60% tak ja sakti hain — koi baat nahi, jitna ho sake savings karein. Achhi income par savings 30-40% tak le jaayein — jitna jaldi wealth banegi, utni jaldi financial freedom. Loan zyada ho to pehle high-interest loan par focus karein.

Track karna aasaan banayein

Mahine ke shuru me teen amount tay karein, kharche ek app ya notebook me likhein, mahine ke end me dekhein kahan zyada gaya, aur agle mahine sudhaarein. Dheere-dheere aadat ban jaati hai. Sabse zaroori — koi bhi budget follow karein, karein zaroor.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

50/30/20 rule kya hai?

Ye budgeting ka aasaan tareeka hai: in-hand salary ka 50% needs (zaroori kharche), 30% wants (shauk), aur 20% savings/loan payoff me lagayein.

50/30/20 kis salary par lagta hai — gross ya in-hand?

In-hand (take-home) salary par, yaani PF aur tax katne ke baad jo haath me aata hai us par.

Agar needs 50% se zyada ho to kya karein?

Bade shehar me aksar aisa hota hai. Aise me Wants (30%) me se thoda adjust karein, par Savings (20%) ko kam na karein. Dheere-dheere needs kam karne ki koshish karein.

Loan ki EMI kis hisse me aayegi?

Normal EMI zaroorat (50%) me. Extra prepayment bachat wale 20% me gina jaata hai, kyunki usse aapka net worth badhta hai.

Bharat me 50/30/20 poora nahi baithta, kya karein?

Ise dishaa maaniye, kanoon nahi. Bade sheher me kiraya hi 40-50% le leta hai. Zaroori sirf itna hai ki bachat ka hissa pehle nikle — chahe wo 20% ki jagah 10% ho.

Bharatiya ghar me ye rule kaise dhaalein

50/30/20 rule amreeka me bana tha, jahan kharche aur kamai ka dhaancha alag hota hai. Bharat me, khaaskar bade shehron me, kiraya hi salary ka 30-40% le jaata hai. Aise me 50% me sab zarooratein pura karna mushkil ho jaata hai.

Iska matlab rule bekaar hai? Nahi. Iska matlab hai ki anupat apni sthiti ke hisaab se badalna chahiye. Shuruaat me 60/20/20 se karein, aur jaise-jaise income badhe, zarooraton ka percentage apne aap girta jaayega — bashart aap shauk bhi utne hi na badha lein.

Kaunsa kharcha kis khaane me — uljhan wali cheezein

Niyam saada hai, par asli mushkil ye tay karna hai ki kharcha kahan rakha jaaye.

KharchaSahi khaana
Ghar ka kiraya ya home loan EMIZaroorat
Car loan ki EMIZaroorat (chukana pakka hai)
Health aur term insuranceZaroorat
Mobile ka rechargeZaroorat — par mehnga plan shauk
OTT, bahar khana, ghoomnaShauk
SIP, PPF, emergency fundBachat
Karza jaldi chukana (extra)Bachat
Aakhri line par dhyan dijiye — EMI se upar jo extra paisa aap loan chukane me daalte hain, wo kharcha nahi, bachat hai. Wo aapki net worth badha raha hai. Log ise kharche me ginkar nirash ho jaate hain ki "bachat to ho hi nahi rahi". Doosri uljhan bima ki hai: term aur health insurance zaroorat hain, par nivesh wali policy ka premium bachat me ginna chahiye. Aur ek asli salah — mahine ke shuru me bachat alag kar lijiye, aakhir me nahi. Jo bachta hai wo aksar kuch nahi bachta.
Asli maqsad: teeno khaano ka theek batwara nahi, balki ye pakka karna ki bachat ka hissa har mahine sabse pehle nikal jaaye — bache hue paise se nahi.

Kaunsa kharcha kis khaane me

50% — Zarooratein: kiraya ya home loan EMI, ration, bijli-pani, bachho ki fees, aane-jaane ka kharch, dawaiyan, insurance premium, phone ka basic bill.

30% — Shauk: bahar khaana, OTT subscription, ghoomna, naye kapde, gadgets, tyohaar ki shopping, restaurant.

20% — Bachat aur karza: SIP, PPF, emergency fund, aur karze ka wo hissa jo minimum se upar hai.

Confusion aksar yahan hota hai — mobile ka basic plan zaroorat hai, par mehnga phone har saal badalna shauk hai. Gaadi ki EMI zaroorat ho sakti hai, par teesri bike shauk hai. Imaandari se baantiye, tabhi ye kaam karega.

Rule chalane ka aasaan tarika

  1. Salary aate hi 20% alag khaate me daal dein — auto-transfer laga dein, taaki sochna hi na pade
  2. Ek mahine ka har kharcha likhein — sirf ek mahina. Isse asli tasveer dikh jaayegi
  3. Sabse bade 3 kharche dhoondein — inhi me sudhaar ka sabse zyada mauka hota hai
  4. Har 6 mahine dobara dekhein — salary badhi to bachat bhi badhni chahiye, sirf kharche nahi

Salary hike ka jaal

Sabse aam galti — salary badhte hi kharche utne hi badha lena. Ise "lifestyle inflation" kehte hain. ₹30,000 me guzara ho raha tha, ₹50,000 hote hi wo bhi kam padne lagta hai.

Iska aasaan ilaaj: jab bhi hike mile, uska kam se kam aadha seedha bachat me daal dein. Baaki aadhe se jeevan behtar karein. Isse aapko badhi salary ka mazaa bhi milega aur bachat bhi apne aap badhti jaayegi.

Karza zyada ho to

Agar aapki EMI hi income ka 40% se zyada le rahi hai, to pehle 50/30/20 ki chinta chhodiye. Pehla kaam hai mehnge karze (credit card, personal loan) khatam karna. Ek chhota emergency fund banayein, aur baaki sab kuch karza chukane me lagayein. Karza kam hone ke baad ye rule apne aap lagne lagega.
