Anusuchit Jati Aur Anusuchit Janjati

  • Anusuchit Jati (SC): 79,317, yaani zile ki aabadi ka lagbhag 20.25%.
  • Anusuchit Janjati (ST): 12,260, yaani lagbhag 3.13%.
  • Uttarakhand me kul paanch Anusuchit Janjatiyan hain: Bhotia, Buksa, Jaunsari, Raji (Van Raji) aur Tharu. Chamoli me Anusuchit Janjati ki aabadi mukhya roop se Bhotia samuday ki hai, jo uttari seemavarti ghatiyon me rehta hai. Buksa, Jaunsari aur Tharu mukhya roop se rajya ke anya hisson me hain, isliye Chamoli ke page par unka zikr “rajya ke anya kshetron me” ke roop me karein, taaki ghalat jaankari na jaye.

Bhotia Samuday

Chamoli ke Bhotia samuday ko aam taur par Marchha aur Tolchha kehte hain, jo Niti, Mana, Malari, Gamsali aur aaspaas ke kshetron me rehte the (gaon ke naam alag sources me alag-alag aate hain, isliye apne gaon se confirm karein). Ye samuday garmiyon me oonche gaon me rehta tha aur sardiyon me neeche ke gaon me aa jata tha. Pehle ye samuday Tibet ke saath vyapar karta tha: oon, namak, jadi-booti, heeng jaise saman ka adaan-pradan hota tha. 1962 ke Bharat-China yuddh ke baad ye vyapar band ho gaya, jiska asar samuday ki arthvyavastha par pada. Isi vyapar ko badhawa dene ke liye 1943 me Gauchar Mela shuru hua tha (Chamoli: Melein, Jatra Aur Doli Yatra dekhein). Aaj Bhotia samuday ke log paryatan, oon ke utpad, kheti aur sarkari naukri se judkar jeevan chalate hain. Bhotia kalakriti (jaise Bhotia Dann aur Thulma) ko GI tag mil chuka hai.

Chamoli: Samajik Dhancha, Surname Aur Gotra

Pramukh Samuday Aur Surname (Sankshipt Parichay)

Garhwal ka samaj paramparik roop se kuch pramukh vargon me baanta jata raha hai: Brahmin, Rajput (Kshatriya), Shilpkar (SC) aur Bhotia (ST). Neeche kuch prachalit surname diye gaye hain. Ye poori suchi nahi hai, aur koi bhi surname kisi ek kshetra ya samuday tak seemit nahi hota.

Rajput / KshatriyaNegi, Rawat, Bisht, Rana, Panwar, Bhandari, Kunwar, Rautela

Nauti gaon ke Nautiyal Brahmin Nanda Devi Raj Jat ki paramparik poojari parampara se jude mane jate hain (Wikipedia ke anusar). Kansuwa ke Kunwar parivar ko Raj Jat ka rajvanshi mana jata hai. Kisi bhi surname ke itihas ya “shreshthta” ke dave na karein, kyunki ye bahut samvedansheel vishay hai.

Mool Niwas (Thaat) Aur Pehchan

Pahadi log apne “mool niwas” yaani poorvajon ke gaon se bahut juda mehsoos karte hain. Kai parivar sheharon me basne ke baad bhi apne gaon ke kul-devta ke liye lautte hain. Iska ek sanskritik pahlu ye hai ki kai gaon ke saath log apne “thaat” (mool gaon) ko pehchan ke roop me batate hain.

Gotra Aur Vivah Ke Niyam

Hindu parampara me vivah ke liye gotra ka dhyan rakha jata hai. Garhwal me bhi iska chalan hai, par kuch jagah aur samuday ke hisab se niyam alag ho sakte hain.

Samajik Samanta Aur Kanoon

Chhua-chhut aur jati ke aadhar par bhedbhav ko Bharat ke Samvidhan ke Anuchhed 17 ne khatm kiya hai, aur Anusuchit Jati aur Anusuchit Janjati (Atyachar Nivaran) Adhiniyam, 1989 aise bhedbhav aur atyachar ke khilaf kanooni suraksha deta hai.

Chamoli: Bhasha, Boliyan Aur Aam Muhavare

Garhwali Bhasha Ek Nazar Me

Chamoli me bolchal ki mukhya bhasha Garhwali hai, jo Devanagari lipi me likhi jati hai. Census 2011 ke anusar Garhwali bolne wale lagbhag 22 se 23 lakh log batae jate hain (is aankde ko Census ki language table se confirm karein). Garhwali Bharat ke Samvidhan ki Aathvi Anusuchi me shamil nahi hai. Uttarakhand sarkar ne Sanskrit ko rajya ki dusri rajbhasha ghoshit kiya hai (2010 me). Hindi mukhya rajbhasha hai.

Chamoli Ki Pramukh Boliyan

Garhwali ki kai upabolian hain, jo kshetra ke hisab se badalti hain. Chamoli kshetra ke liye aam taur par ye naam liye jaate hain:

  • Nagpuriya (Nagpuri): Nagpur kshetra ki boli (Chamoli ke dakshini-pashchimi kshetra).
  • Dasholya (Dasholi): Dasholi kshetra ki boli.
  • Badhani: Badhan kshetra ki boli.
  • Painkhandi: Painkhanda ghati ki boli (Joshimath kshetra), jis ghati me Ramman tyohar hota hai.
  • Bhotia boliyan: Marchha aur Tolchha samuday ki boliyan.
  • Boliyon ke naam aur seema ke bare me vidwan alag-alag raay dete hain. Is par sthaniya shiksha ya sahitya ke jankar se baat karne ka sujhav bhi dein.

Chamoli: Pahadi Vastukala (Pahadi Ghar)

Pahadi Ghar Ki Banawat

Garhwal ke paramparik ghar patthar, mitti aur lakdi se bante the. Moti deewarein sardi aur garmi dono se bachaati hain, aur lakdi ke majboot dhanche ko bhukamp ke jhatkon me kuch had tak lachakdaar mana jata hai. Aam taur par ghar do ya teen manzil ke hote the. Neeche ki manzil me pashu aur saman, upar ki manzil me rehne ke kamre. Is banawat se pashuon ke sharir ki garmi upar tak aati thi, jo sardiyon me ghar ko garam rakhne me madad karti thi.

Ghar Ke Hisse Aur Unke Naam

Neeche diye gaye shabd Garhwal me prachalit hain. Gaon-gaon me naam aur matlab thode alag ho sakte hain.

Koti Banal Style Ke Bare Me Savdhani

Chamoli ke ghar ki pehchan slate ki chhat (pathal), nakkashi wale darwaze aur oonchai ke hisab se alag banawat se hoti hai.

Bhukamp Aur Paramparik Ghar

Kuch adhyayan paramparik pahadi ghar ko bhukamp ke liye kafi had tak upyukt batate hain kyunki isme lakdi ki patti aur patthar ki deewar ka milan hota hai. Par ye har ghar ke liye sahi nahi hota, khaskar jab ghar nadi ya dhalaan ke kinare, kamzor neev par bana ho. Naye ghar banane ke liye bhukamp-rodhi nirman ke niyam aur building bylaws ka paalan karein (Chamoli: Building Bylaws, Map Approval Aur Bhukamp Niyam). Aise dave na karein ki “puraane ghar kabhi nahi girte.”

Homestay Ke Roop Me Paramparik Ghar

Kai paryatak gaon ke paramparik ghar me rehna pasand karte hain. Ghar ko homestay banane ke liye registration aur suraksha ke niyam hote hain (Chamoli: Homestay Registration Aur Subsidy). Purane ghar me kuch badlav karne se pehle engineer ki salah lein.

Chamoli: Paramparik Pehnawa Aur Vastra

Mahilaon Ka Pehnawa

Paramparik roop se Garhwal ki mahilayein ghaghri (lambi ghagra-jaisi), angadi ya choli, aur dhoti ya orhni pehenti thi. Sardi me oon ke vastra aur shawl ka upyog hota tha. Shaadi aur tyohar par laal ya peele rang ke vastra aur gehne pehne jaate the. Rangwali Pichhoda (laal ya kesariya rang ka orhna jisme swastik jaise chinh hote hain) ko GI tag mila hai, par ise mukhya roop se Kumaon ki parampara maana jata hai.

Purushon Ka Pehnawa

Aam taur par kurta-pajama, waistcoat (sadri), oon ka kot aur sir par topi pehni jaati thi. Pahadi topi (Uttarakhandi topi) aaj ek pehchan ke roop me pehni jaati hai. Mirzai jaisi lambi jackets ka bhi zikr milta hai.

Bhotia Samuday Ke Vastra Aur Oon Ke Utpad

Oonche kshetra me rehne wale Bhotia samuday ne oon ke utpad banane ki lambi parampara banayi hai. Bhotia Dann (oon ka gaddha ya kaaleen) aur Thulma (oon ka kambal) ko GI tag mil chuka hai. Mana aur Niti jaise gaon ki mahilayein hastkargha par oon ke sweater, shawl aur mufflar banati hain, aur ye saman Badrinath aur Joshimath ke bazaar me bikte hain.

Bichhu Buti: Ghas Ke Rishon Ka Kapda

Himalayi nettle (kandali, jise Bichhu Buti kehte hain) ke rishon se bana kapda ek anokhi pehchan hai. Is “Uttarakhand Bichhu Buti Fabrics” ko December 2023 me GI tag mila, aur ek report ke anusar nettle ke ye rishe khaaskar Almora aur Chamoli zilon me paye jaate hain.

Sawal-Jawab: Pehnawa

Rangwali Pichhoda kahan ki parampara hai?
Mukhya roop se Kumaon ki, GI tag mila hai.
Thulma kya hota hai?
Oon ka kambal, jise GI tag mila hai.
Bichhu Buti kya hai?
Nettle ke rishon se bana kapda, GI tag mila hai.

Chamoli: Lok-Kala: Aipan, Jyunti, Mask Aur Lakdi Ki Kalakari

Chamoli Ka Ramman Mask: GI Tag Aur Kala

Chamoli ke Saloor-Dungra gaon ke tyohar Ramman me pehne jane wale lakdi ke mask (mukhote) apne aap me ek kala hain. “Chamoli Wooden Ramman Mask” ko December 2023 me GI tag mila. Is kala me lakdi ko tarashkar chehre ke mask banaye jate hain, jinhe Ramman ke pradarshan me pehna jata hai. Ek research paper ke anusar aaj aadhunikta ka asar is kala par pad raha hai, aur ise bachane ki zaroorat batayi gayi hai. Ramman ki poori jaankari Chamoli: Mausmi Tyohar me di gayi hai.

Aipan (Kumaon Ki Parampara)

Aipan geru (laal mitti) par chawal ke geele aate (biswar) se banayi gayi dehleez ya aangan ki kala hai, jo mukhya roop se Kumaon ki parampara hai. Aipan ko GI tag mila hai. Garhwal me bhi dehleez par shubh chinh banane ki parampara hai, par naam aur shaili alag hoti hai.

Jyunti (Matrika) Chitra

Jyunti ya Jyunti-matrika ek paramparik chitra-kala hai jo kai shubh avsaron par ghar ki deewar par banayi jati hai. Iski shaili aur naam kshetra ke hisab se alag ho sakte hain.

Likhai (Wood Carving) Aur Ringal Craft

Uttarakhand Likhai (lakdi ki nakkashi) ko December 2023 me GI tag mila, aur Ringal craft (Ringal bans se bani tokriyan aadi) ko pehle hi GI tag mil chuka tha. Chamoli ke gaon me lakdi ki nakkashi ghar ke darwazon aur mandir ke dwar par dekhi jati hai. Ringal ki kala bhi pahadi gaon ki aajeevika ka hissa hai.

Kalakar Aur Karigar Ka Samman

Kala ke is kshetra me kaam karne wale kalakar aur shilpkar aksar kam aamdani me rehte hain. Kisi vyakti ka phone number ya dukan ka pata sirf unki sahmati se likhein.

Sawal-Jawab: Lok-Kala

Chamoli Ramman mask ko GI tag mila hai?
Haan, December 2023 me.
Aipan kahan ki kala hai?
Mukhya roop se Kumaon ki.
Ringal craft kya hai?
Ringal bans se bani paramparik vastuein, jise GI tag mila hai.

Chamoli: Lok-Sangeet Aur Gayan Parampara

Jagar: Devta Ko Bulane Ki Gayan Parampara

Jagar Garhwal aur Kumaon me ek paramparik anushthan hai jisme gaan aur vaadya ke madhyam se devta ya ishta ko “jagaya” jata hai. Isme jagaria (jaagar gaane wala) devta ki gatha gata hai, aur dhol-damau jaise vaadya saath bajte hain. Jagar ka mukhya sthan dharmik aur samajik hai. Sachchai ye hai ki gaon me ise ek gehri aastha ke roop me liya jata hai.

Pawara (Veer Gatha)

Pawara ya Pawada veer yodhaon ki gathaon ko gaakar sunane ki parampara hai, jisme itihas aur lok-kathayein milti hain. Isme Garhwal ke purane garhon aur veeron ki kahaniyan aati hain. Gaon ke buzurgon se sunkar ek ya do gathaon ko apne shabdon me likhna aur unke source ka zikr karna ek achha tareeka hai.

Khuded Geet

Khuded geet vo geet hain jinme shaadi ke baad apne maike se door rehne wali mahilayein apni yaadon aur dukh ko vyakt karti hain. “Khud” shabd ka matlab hai yaad ya virah ki peeda. Ye geet pahadi mahilaon ke jeevan ka ek bhavnaatmak pahlu dikhate hain.

Mausmi Aur Sanskar Ke Geet

  • Mangal geet: Shaadi aur shubh avsaron par gaye jaate hain.
  • Chaiti / Basanti geet: Chaitra aur basant ritu ke geet.
  • Thadya (Thadiya) geet: Garhwal ke ek prasiddh geet-nritya ke saath gaye jaate hain.
  • Jhumelo geet: Mahilaon ke saamuhik gaayan ke geet.
  • Kai aur prakar bhi hain. Sahi naam aur gane ke bol ke liye gaon ki mahilayein sabse pramanik source hain. Apne shabdon me unka saar likhein.

Aadhunik Garhwali Sangeet

Garhwali gaayakon aur lok-kalakaron ne ek lambe samay se is sangeet ko aage badhaya hai.

Sawal-Jawab: Lok-Sangeet

Jagar kya hota hai?

Virah aur yaad ke geet, jo mahilayein gaati hain.

Chamoli: Paramparik Lok-Nritya

Pandav Nritya (Pandav Lila)

Pandav Nritya (jise Pandav Lila bhi kehte hain) Garhwal, khaaskar Chamoli aur Rudraprayag kshetra ki ek prasiddh parampara hai. Isme Mahabharat ke Pandavon ki kathayein nritya, gaayan aur abhinay ke roop me prastut ki jati hain. Ye aam taur par sardiyon ke mahinon (November se January ke aaspaas) me gaon ke saamuhik aayojan ke roop me hota hai. Is parampara me gaon ke log patron ka abhinay karte hain, aur devta ki aastha ke saath ise nibhate hain. Gaon-gaon me iski avadhi aur vidhi alag hoti hai, isliye apne gaon ki jaankari jodna sabse pramanik hoga.

Thadiya Aur Chaunphula

  • Thadiya (Thadya): Garhwal ka ek lokpriya geet-nritya, jisme log golaakar ghoomkar gaate aur nachte hain. Ye aam taur par sardi ya tyohar ke samay hota hai.
  • Chaunphula: Basant ke aaspaas mahilaon aur purushon dwara kiya jane wala saamuhik nritya (naam kuch jagah thoda alag ho sakta hai).
  • Jhumelo: Mahilaon ka saamuhik geet-nritya, jo kai Garhwali tyoharon me hota hai.

Anya Nritya

Chholiya (talwar-dhaal ka nritya) mukhya roop se Kumaon ki parampara mani jati hai. Barada Nati Jaunsar ki aur Langvir Nritya Tehri kshetra ki parampara hai.

Bhotia Samuday Ke Nritya

Oonche kshetra ke Bhotia samuday ki apni geet-nritya parampara hai. Ye aam taur par vyapar ke raste ki yaadon, mausmi pravas aur tyoharon se judi hai. Is par jaankari ke liye Bhotia samuday ke buzurgon ya sthaniya sanskritik sansthaon se baat karke apne shabdon me likhein.

Chamoli: Paramparik Vaadya Yantra

Dhol-Damau

Dhol aur Damau Garhwal ke sabse mukhya paramparik vaadya hain, jo har bade avsar (shaadi, mela, Jagar, Pandav Lila) me bajte hain. Dhol ka vaadan ek vishesh tal-prakriya se hota hai, jise Dhol Sagar ke naam se jana jata hai. Dhol Sagar ek maukhik (zubani) parampara hai jisme alag-alag avsar ke liye alag tal hote hain. Tal ki sahi sankhya (jaise 84 ya anya) alag sources me alag milti hai.

Chamoli: Jeevan-Sanskar Aur Vivah

Janm Se Vivah Tak Ke Sanskar

Hindu parampara ke solah sanskaron me se kai Garhwal me bhi nibhaye jaate hain, jaise Namkaran (naam rakhna), Annaprashan (pehla ann khilana), Chudakarma (mundan) aur Upanayan (Janeu). Har parivar apne kul-devta, pandit aur kshetra ki parampara ke hisab se inhe nibhata hai.

Garhwali Vivah Ki Kuch Pramukh Rasmein

Vivah me prachalit rasmein kshetra aur samuday ke hisab se badalti hain. Kuch aam taur par prachalit hain:

  • Ganesh puja: Shubh karya ki shuruaat.
  • Mangal geet: Mahilayein vivah ke geet gaati hain.
  • Dhuli arghya: Baraat ke aane par dulhe ka sammanpoorvak swagat aur arghya.
  • Kanyadan: Vivah ka mukhya anushthan.
  • Phere aur sindoor: Saat phere aur sindoor.
  • Bidaai: Dulhan ki vida.
  • Chamoli ke kuch kshetron me baraat aur dhol-damau ki bhoomika bahut mahatvapurn hai. Ye rasmein sabhi parivaron me ek jaisi nahi hoti.

Vivah Kharch Aur Samajik Dabav

Aajkal vivah ka kharch ek samajik chinta ka vishay hai. Dahej ek kanooni apradh hai (Dahej Nishedh Adhiniyam, 1961).

Antim Sanskar

Hindu parampara ke anusar antim sanskar ka kram aur vidhi parivar ki parampara aur pandit ke anusar hoti hai. Kai parivar Alaknanda ke kinare ya prayagon par antim sanskar karte hain. Is vishay par sahi aur samvedansheel bhasha me likhein, aur kisi ek vidhi ko sabke liye anivarya na batayein.

Chamoli: Mausmi Tyohar

Ramman: Saloor-Dungra Ka Vishwa-Prasiddh Utsav

Ramman Chamoli ki sanskriti ka sabse bada gaurav hai. Ye Chamoli ke Painkhanda ghati ke jode gaon Saloor-Dungra me har saal aam taur par April ke aakhir me (Baisakhi ke aaspaas) manaya jata hai, aur gaon ke devta Bhumiyal Devta (Bhumichetrapal) ko samarpit hai. UNESCO ne 2009 me Ramman ko “Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity” me shamil kiya.

Mukhya baatein (UNESCO aur sarkari Indian Culture portal ke anusar):

  • Ye ek dharmik utsav aur anushthanik natya (ritual theatre) hai.
  • Utsav Baisakhi ke din se shuru hota hai, jab Bhumiyal Devta kalash ke roop me mandir tak shobha yatra me aate hain.
  • Utsav me mask pehnkar nritya hota hai, aur ek varnan ke anusar pradarshan me 18 patra, 18 mask aur 18 taal hote hain (jo 18 Puranon ka pratik mane jate hain).
  • Pradarshan Bhumiyal Devta ke mandir ke aangan me hota hai.
  • Utsav ke ant me prasad ka saamuhik bhoj hota hai, aur Bhumiyal Devta saal bhar kisi ek parivar ke ghar me nivas karte hain, jiska chayan gram panchayat karti hai.
  • Saloor-Dungra ke gaon ke log is utsav ke aayojak aur arthik sahayak hain.
  • Bhandari parivar (Saloor gaon ke) ko Narsingh Devta ka pavitra mask pehnne ka paramparik adhikar bataya jata hai.
  • Ramman ke saath dhol, damau aur bheri jaise vaadya bajte hain.
  • Ek sandarbh ke anusar Ramman Mahotsav 2016 ke Gantantra Diwas parade ka bhi hissa raha. Ramman ke mask ko GI tag December 2023 me mila (Chamoli: Lok-Kala: Aipan, Jyunti, Mask Aur Lakdi Ki Kalakari).

Savdhani: Ramman ke kisi bhi pradarshan ya mask ki tasveer lene se pehle gaon ki samiti se anumati lein. Tarikh har saal Baisakhi ke baad pujari dwara ghoshit ki jati hai.

Phool Dei (Phooldei)

Chaitra mahine ki Sankranti par (March ke aakhir ya April ke shuru me) bachche gaon ke ghar-ghar ki dehleez par taaze phool daalte hain aur geet gaate hain. Gharon se unhe chawal, gud aur paise diye jate hain. Ye basant ke swagat ka tyohar hai aur Garhwal-Kumaon dono me manaya jata hai.

Baisakhi (Bikhauti)

Baisakh ki Sankranti ko Garhwal aur Kumaon me alag-alag naam se manaya jata hai (Bikhauti, Vishuwat Sankranti). Chamoli ke Saloor-Dungra me isi din Ramman ki shuruaat hoti hai (upar dekhein).

Ghee Sankranti (Ghyun Sankranti)

Bhadrapad mahine ki Sankranti par gaon me ghee ka sevan shubh mana jata hai. Mausam me aane wale phal aur anaj ko devta ko arpan kiya jata hai. Kai log is din ghee zaroor khate hain, aisa bataya jata hai ki jo ghee nahi khata use agle janm me ghongha (ghonghi) banna padta hai.

Nanda Ashtami

Bhadrapad ke shukla paksh ki Ashtami ko Nanda Devi ki puja hoti hai. Chamoli me Nanda Devi ke kai mandir hain, aur ye tyohar kai gaon me mele ke roop me manaya jata hai. Nanda Devi ki Lok Jat aur Raj Jat Nanda Ashtami ke aaspaas se jude utsav hain (Chamoli: Melein, Jatra Aur Doli Yatra dekhein).

Makar Sankranti (Uttarayani / Khichdi Sankranti)

Makar Sankranti (January) par Garhwal me khichdi ka mahatva hai aur nadiyon ke kinare snaan ke mele lagte hain. Kumaon me isi tyohar par “Ghughutiya” manaya jata hai, jisme aate aur gud ke pakwaan banaye jate hain aur kauwe ko khilaye jate hain. Ghughutiya Kumaon ki parampara hai.

Basant Panchami

Basant Panchami par Badrinath kapat kholne ki tithi tay hoti hai aur Nanda Raj Jat ki tithi bhi isi din ghoshit hoti hai. Isliye Chamoli me ye din bahut mahatvapurn hai.

Igaas (Budhi Diwali)

Diwali ke lagbhag 11 din baad Garhwal me “Igaas” (Budhi Diwali) manai jati hai. Is tyohar ki tithi aur manane ka tareeka kshetra ke hisab se alag hota hai, isliye apne gaon ki jaankari jodein.

Harela

Harela mukhya roop se Kumaon ka tyohar hai, jisme Shravan se pehle sat prakar ke anaj boye jate hain. Garhwal ke kuch hisson me bhi Harela manaya jata hai.

Pandav Lila Aur Sardi Ka Utsav

Sardi ke mahinon me Pandav Lila ka aayojan kai gaon me hota hai (Chamoli: Paramparik Lok-Nritya dekhein).

Chamoli: Melein, Jatra Aur Doli Yatra

Gauchar Mela: Chamoli Ka Sabse Prasiddh Mela

Gauchar Mela Chamoli ke Gauchar kasbe me har saal 14 November se shuru hota hai aur lagbhag ek hafte tak chalta hai (kuch sources 14 se 20 November batate hain). Gauchar Alaknanda ke baayen kinare, lagbhag 800 meter ki oonchai par, Badrinath marg par hai. Ye Uttarakhand ke sabse bade vyapar mele me se ek mana jata hai.

Itihas: Gauchar Mela pehli baar November 1943 me shuru hua, jise Garhwal ke tatkaleen Deputy Commissioner ke sujhav par shuru kiya gaya mana jata hai. Us samay Bharat-Tibet vyapar khula tha, aur Bhotia samuday ke log oon ke utpad, kasturi, heeng, shilajit aur anya saman bechte the aur badle me kapda, namak aur anya zaroori saman kharidte the. 1962 ke yuddh ke baad ye seema vyapar band ho gaya. Aazadi ke baad ye mela sarkar dwara vikas, udyog aur sanskritik mele ke roop me aayojit hone laga. Mele ki ek khaas baat ye hai ki 14 November Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru ke janmdin ke roop me bhi jaana jata hai (Uttarakhand Darshan ke anusar iska sambandh bataya gaya hai).

Aaj ka Gauchar Mela: Zila prashasan dwara aayojit is mele me vyapar, hastshilp pradarshani, khel aur sanskritik karyakram hote hain. Is saal ki exact tarikh aur karyakram Chamoli zila website par dekhein, kyunki ye prashasan ki ghoshna par nirbhar karte hain.

Nanda Devi Raj Jat Aur Badi Jat: 2026-27 Ki Sthiti

Nanda Devi Raj Jat Chamoli ki sabse badi dharmik yatra hai, jise “Himalayi Mahakumbh” kehte hain. Ye yatra lagbhag 12 saal me ek baar hoti hai aur lagbhag 280 kilometer paidal chalkar Homkund (Roopkund ke paas) par poori hoti hai. Yatra ka netritva Chausingha Khadu (chaar sing wala mendha) karta hai, jo parampara ke anusar Homkund par chhod diya jata hai. Pichhli Raj Jat 2014 me hui thi (2013 ki aapda ke karan 1 saal taali gayi thi).

2026-27 ki sthiti (news reports ke anusar):

  • January 2026 me Shri Nanda Devi Raj Jat Samiti, Nauti ne ghoshna ki ki ek saal ki tayyari ki zaroorat ke karan Raj Jat 2026 ke bajay 2027 me hogi. Is samiti ke anusar tithi ki ghoshna Basant Panchami 2027 par hogi.
  • Iske baad Kurud (Nandanagar) ke Siddhapeeth mandir ki samiti ne “Nanda Devi Badi Jat” ke naam se 2026 me yatra karne ka nirnay liya. Reports ke anusar ye yatra 5 September 2026 ko Kurud se shuru hui, jisme Nanda Devi ki palki ko pehle padav Charbang gaon pahunchna tha. Aage ka marg Wan aur Bedni Bugyal se hokar Shila Samudra aur Homkund tak bataya gaya, jahan sampann hone ke liye alag-alag sources me 20 se 30 September ke beech ki tithiyan likhi hain.
  • Dono samitiyon ki alag-alag raay rahi hai, aur is vishay me samaj ke kuch hisson me bahas bhi rahi. Ye likhein: “Is ghatna par sthaniya samitiyon ke alag-alag nirnay hain, taaza jaankari ke liye sambandhit samiti aur zila prashasan dekhein.”

Nanda Devi Lok Jat (Har Saal Ki Yatra)

Har saal Nanda Devi ki ek chhoti yatra, jise Lok Jat kehte hain, nikalti hai, jisme Nanda Devi ki palki gaon-gaon se hote hue Bedni Kund (Bedni Bugyal) ya Balpata tak jaati hai (reports ke anusar, har saal ki tithi Basant Panchami par tay hoti hai). Badi Jat aur Lok Jat me fark ye hai ki Badi Jat Homkund tak jaati hai.

Badhangarhi Aur Anya Mele

Chamoli ke Tharali (Gwaldam ke paas) ke Badhangarhi mandir me Chaitra ashtami ke avsar par mela lagne ka zikr milta hai. Is kshetra ka itihas Nanda Devi aur Chand rajvansh se bhi jura bataya jata hai. Anya melon (jaise Gopeshwar ki Shivratri aur Badrinath se jude utsav) ke naam aur tithi ke liye zila prashasan aur sthaniya sources dekhein.

Doli ke raaste aur tithi har devta aur gaon ke anusar alag hoti hain. Apne gaon ke devta ki doli ki jaankari, dates aur raaste gaon se pooch kar page par jodna aapko ek anokha aur sthaniya content dega.

Yatri Ke Liye Salah

Bhaari bheed wale mele aur yatra me suraksha ka dhyan rakhein. Mele me bhagdad se bachein, bachche saath rakhein, aur mausam aur road ki sthiti dekhein. Badi Jat ya Raj Jat jaisi kathin paidal yatra me medical fitness aur permit ki zaroorat hoti hai. Zila prashasan ke niyam dekhein.
