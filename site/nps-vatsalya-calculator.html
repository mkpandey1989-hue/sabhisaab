NPS Vatsalya Calculator — Bachche Ke Naam Par Kitna Banega

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~6 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

NPS Vatsalya September 2024 me shuru hua. Isme maata-pita bachche ke naam par pension khaata khol sakte hain, saal me kam se kam ₹250 se. 18 saal ki umar par ye khaata khud bachche ke saamanya NPS me badal jaata hai.

Neeche bachche ki abhi ki umar, mahine ka nivesh aur anumaanit return daaliye. 18 saal par corpus kitna hoga, aur agar wahi paisa 60 ki umar tak pada rahe to kitna banega — dono dikh jayega.

Khaata kaise khulta hai aur kitne paise se

Khaata eNPS portal se, ya bank aur post office ki chuni hui shakhaon se khulta hai. Bachcha 18 saal se chhota hona chahiye. Maata-pita ya kanooni abhibhaavak khaata chalate hain, par khaate ka maalik bachcha hi rehta hai.

₹250 saal ki seema sunne me bahut chhoti lagti hai, aur wahi iska sabse bada faayda bhi hai — khaata zinda rakhne ka kharch na ke barabar hai. Par isse corpus nahi banta. Asli farak mahine ke niyamit nivesh se aata hai, jo aap upar calculator me khud dekh sakte hain.

Ek saaf baat — ye padhai ka fund nahi hai

Yahi wo jagah hai jahan sabse zyada galatfehmi hoti hai. Naam me bachcha hai, isliye log ise college ya shaadi ka fund samajh lete hain. Ye pension ka khaata hai. Paisa 18 par bahar nahi aata — wo saamanya NPS me chala jaata hai aur wahan 60 tak ke apne niyam laagu ho jaate hain.

Sochne ka saaf tarika: NPS Vatsalya bachche ke 60 saal ke liye hai, uske 18 saal ke liye nahi. College ki fees 2040 me chahiye to uske liye alag jagah chahiye — ye khaata us kaam nahi aayega.

Beech me nikaalne ki thodi chhoot zaroor hai. Teen saal poore hone ke baad padhai, ilaaj ya vikalaangata jaisi sthiti me jama rakam ka ek chhota hissa nikaala ja sakta hai, wo bhi ginti ke baar. Ise regular nikaasi maankar chalna galti hogi.

Bachche ki padhai ka lakshya alag se banana ho to SIP Goal Calculator me saal aur rakam daalkar dekh lijiye — wahan paisa zaroorat ke waqt haath me aata hai.

Tax me kya milta hai, kis regime me

Budget 2025 ke baad Vatsalya me daala gaya paisa Section 80CCD(1B) ke andar aa gaya. Par teen sharton ke saath, jo aksar nahi batayi jaatin.

  • Chhoot ki seema ₹50,000 hai, aur ye aapke khud ke NPS ke saath milakar gini jaati hai — alag se upar nahi.
  • Ye faayda purane tax regime me hi milta hai. Naye regime me is tarah ki chhoot nahi milti.
  • Adhiktam do naabaalig bachchon tak ka hi hisaab liya jaata hai.

Matlab ye: agar aap pehle se apne NPS me poore 50,000 daal rahe hain, to Vatsalya me daala gaya paisa tax me kuch naya nahi bachaayega. Aur agar aap naye regime me hain, to tax bachat ke naam par ye khaata kholne ka koi tuk nahi — kholiye tabhi jab bachche ke lambe samay ke pension corpus ka vichaar pasand ho.

Apna 80CCD ka hisaab dekhna ho to Section 80C Calculator se seema pehle jaanch lijiye.

Samay ka jaadu — 5 saal ki umar aur 15 ki umar ka farak

Pension ke khaate me sabse badi taakat rakam nahi, saal hote hain. Neeche ek hi mahine ka nivesh, sirf shuruaat ki umar alag — aur 60 saal par natija kitna alag ho jaata hai, ye 10% saalana anuman par.

Upar ke saare number 10% saalana ka anuman maankar bane hain. NPS bazaar se juda hai — asli natija isse upar ya neeche dono ho sakta hai.

Kis parivaar ke liye ye theek hai, kiske liye nahi

Har cheez har kisi ke liye nahi hoti. Do saaf list se faisla aasan ho jaata hai.

Theek hai agar: aapki apni retirement ki planning pehle se chal rahi hai, aapke paas emergency fund hai, bachcha chhota hai, aur ye paisa aapko agle 15 saal kisi kaam nahi aana. Aisi sthiti me ek chhoti rakam yahan lagana bachche ke liye ek lambi shuruaat ban jaati hai.

Theek nahi agar: aapka apna pension khaata abhi khaali hai, upar se karza chal raha hai, ya aap ise college fund samajh kar khol rahe hain. Bachche ke liye sabse badi suraksha ye hai ki maata-pita ka apna paisa mazboot ho aur term insurance ho — uske baad hi bachche ke naam ka lamba khaata baat banti hai.

Ek baat jo brochure me nahi milegi: 18 saal par khaata bachche ka ho jaata hai. Uske baad wo use chalu rakhega ya nahi, ye uska faisla hoga. Isliye sirf paisa jodna kaafi nahi — bade hote bachche ko ye khaata kya hai, ye bhi batate rehna zaroori hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

NPS Vatsalya me kam se kam kitna daalna padta hai?

Saal me ₹250. Isse khaata chalu rehta hai. Corpus banane ke liye niyamit mahine ka nivesh zaroori hai.

18 saal par paisa mil jaata hai kya?

Nahi. Khaata bachche ke saamanya NPS Tier-1 me badal jaata hai aur wahan ke niyam laagu ho jaate hain. Ye padhai ya shaadi ke kharch ka fund nahi hai.

Tax chhoot kis section me milti hai?

Section 80CCD(1B) me, ₹50,000 tak. Par ye seema aapke khud ke NPS ke saath milakar gini jaati hai, aur faayda purane tax regime me hi milta hai.

Kya dada-dadi bhi paisa daal sakte hain?

Haan. Khaata abhibhaavak chalate hain, par yogdaan parivaar ka koi bhi sadasya kar sakta hai. Tax ki chhoot us vyakti ko milti hai jiske niyamon par shart poori hoti hai.

Beech me paisa nikaalne ka koi rasta hai?

Teen saal ke baad padhai, gambhir bimari ya vikalaangata jaisi sthiti me jama rakam ka ek seemit hissa, ginti ke baar nikaala ja sakta hai. Iska istemaal aapatkaal ke liye hai.
