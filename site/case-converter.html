Sarkari form me case ka niyam

Ye Bharat me bahut kaam ki baat hai. Zyadatar sarkari form me naam CAPITAL me maanga jaata hai, aur uska ek kaaran hai — capital akshar padhne aur data me daalne me kam galti karate hain.

  • PAN, Aadhaar, bank ka form — naam aksar capital me
  • Bharti aur exam ka form — form ke upar likha hota hai; dhyaan se padhiye
  • Cheque par naam — jaisa bank record me hai, waisa hi
Sabse zaroori baat — naam ki spelling har kaagaz me ek jaisi honi chahiye. PAN, Aadhaar, bank aur marksheet me naam alag hone par baad me bada jhanjhat hota hai — KYC atakti hai, refund ruk jaata hai, aur sudhaarne me mahine lag jaate hain. Case se zyada spelling ka milna zaroori hai.

Aur email address hamesha lowercase me likhiye. Waise to email me case se farak nahi padta, par lowercase likhna standard hai aur galti kam hoti hai.

Likhne me kaunsa case kahan

  • Heading — Title Case ya Sentence case. Dono chalte hain, par poori website me ek hi tarika rakhiye. Aadha Title Case aur aadha Sentence case bura dikhta hai.
  • Paragraph — Sentence case hi.
  • Button aur menu — chhote shabd me Title Case achha lagta hai.
  • Poora vaakya CAPITAL me — internet par ise chillana maana jaata hai. Message, email aur comment me isse bachiye. Ek-do shabd par zor dena ho to theek hai, poora vaakya nahi.
Ek baat jo padhne walon ke liye mayne rakhti hai — poora CAPITAL me likha text padhne me dheema hota hai. Aankh shabdon ko unke aakaar se pehchanti hai, aur capital me sab akshar ek jaise oonche ho jaate hain. Isi liye kitaab aur akhbaar me poore vaakya kabhi capital me nahi hote.

Aapka text kahin nahi jaata

Ye baat saaf keh dena zaroori hai — jo text aap yahan daalte hain wo sirf aapke browser me badalta hai. Wo kisi server par nahi jaata, kahin save nahi hota, aur page band karte hi khatam ho jaata hai.

Isi liye aap yahan apna niji text bhi daal sakte hain — application, letter, ya koi draft. Hum tak wo pahunchta hi nahi.

Phir bhi ek aam aadat rakhiye — kisi bhi website par password, OTP, bank ki detail ya poora Aadhaar number kabhi paste mat kijiye, chahe website kitni bhi bharosemand ho. Ye niyam har jagah laagu hota hai.

Ek chhoti si tikneeki baat: Hindi (Devanagari) me chhota-bada akshar hota hi nahi. Isliye case badalna sirf angrezi aur Hinglish (Roman akshar) par kaam karta hai. Devanagari text daalne par wo jaisa hai waisa hi rahega.

Doosre text ke tools: Word Counter, Reading Time Calculator aur Number to Words.

Do jagah case badalna nuksan kar deta hai — dhyaan rakhein. Pehla, password: usme chhota-bada akshar maayne rakhta hai, isliye case badalne se password hi badal jaata hai. Doosra, email ka domain aur URL: domain me case se farak nahi padta par URL ke aage wale hisse me kai server case ko alag maante hain, isliye link toot sakta hai. Aur Excel ki list saaf karte waqt sirf case theek karna kaafi nahi hota — naam ke aage-peeche ki khaali jagah (space) bhi hatani padti hai, warna do ek jaisi entry alag gini jaati hain.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Sarkari form me naam capital me kyun likhwate hain?

Capital akshar padhne aur data entry me kam galti karate hain. Par case se zyada zaroori hai ki naam ki spelling har kaagaz me ek jaisi ho.

Poora vaakya CAPITAL me likhna theek hai?

Nahi. Internet par ise chillana maana jaata hai, aur padhne me bhi dheema hota hai. Ek-do shabd par zor dena ho to theek hai.

Mera text kahin save hota hai?

Nahi. Badlaav aapke browser me hi hota hai, text kisi server par nahi jaata aur page band karte hi khatam ho jaata hai.

Hindi text par case badalta hai?

Nahi. Devanagari me chhota-bada akshar hota hi nahi, isliye ye sirf angrezi aur Roman akshar par kaam karta hai.
