Crypto Tax Calculator — VDA Par Tax Ka Poora Hisaab

Bharat me crypto ko Virtual Digital Asset (VDA) maana jaata hai. Ispar tax ka tarika baaki har nivesh se alag hai — ek hi flat rate, koi kharcha nahi ghata sakte, aur ghaate ka koi faayda nahi milta.

Neeche kharid aur bikri ki rakam daaliye. Tax, TDS aur haath me bachne wali rakam turant dikh jayegi.

Teen niyam jo crypto ko alag banate hain

Share aur mutual fund me holding ke samay se rate badalta hai. Crypto me aisa kuch nahi. Teen niyam hain, aur teeno kaafi sakht hain.

NiyamKya matlab
Flat 30% + cessEk din rakho ya paanch saal, rate wahi
Sirf kharid ki keemat ghategiExchange fees, gas fee, laptop, bijli — kuch nahi
Ghaata set-off nahiEk coin ka ghaata doosre ke faayde se nahi katega
Doosra niyam sabse mehnga hai. Aapne 2 lakh ka coin liya, 4,000 fees di, 3.2 lakh me becha. Aapka asli faayda 1.16 lakh hai, par tax 1.20 lakh par lagega. Yaani us 4,000 par bhi tax bharna pada jo aapne kabhi kamaya hi nahi.

Ghaata set-off na hone ka asli asar

Ye baat ek misaal se hi samajh aati hai. Maan lijiye ek saal me do sauday hue — ek me 1 lakh ka faayda, doosre me 80,000 ka ghaata.

NiveshFaayda/GhaataTax
Share bazaar+1,00,000 aur -80,000Sirf 20,000 par
Crypto+1,00,000 aur -80,000Poore 1,00,000 par

Aapki jeb me sirf 20,000 aaye, par tax 30,000 se upar ka banega. Yaani us saal aapka asli natija minus me chala gaya. Aur wo 80,000 ka ghaata agle saal bhi aage nahi le ja sakte — wo bas khatam ho jaata hai.

Isi ek niyam ki wajah se baar-baar trading karne wale kaafi log saal ke aakhir me hairaan hote hain. Jitne zyada sauday, utna zyada ye niyam chubhta hai.

1% TDS — extra tax nahi, par phasa hua paisa hai

Har bikri par exchange 1% TDS kaat leta hai. Ye alag tax nahi hai — ye aapke apne tax me hi adjust ho jaata hai, aur zyada kata ho to ITR me refund aata hai.

Par ek dikkat hai. Ye 1% faayde par nahi, poori bikri rakam par katta hai. Ghaate me becho tab bhi katta hai. Aur baar-baar trading karne walon ke liye ye paisa saal bhar ke liye phas jaata hai.

Ek chhota hisaab: 1 lakh ki poonji se aapne saal me 30 baar khareed-farokht ki. Har baar 1% TDS — kul 30,000 rupaye saal bhar ke liye sarkar ke paas jama ho gaye. Poonji utni hi thi, par ek tihaai hissa kaam se bahar chala gaya. Refund ITR ke baad hi milta hai.

Peer-to-peer ya videshi platform par sauda karte waqt TDS jama karne ki zimmedari kharidne wale par aa jaati hai. Ye baat kaafi log nahi jaante aur baad me notice ka saamna karte hain.

Gift, airdrop, staking aur mining ka kya

Sirf bechna hi tax ka mauka nahi hai. Kuch aur sthitiyan bhi tax lati hain.

SthitiAam taur par kya hota hai
Ek coin se doosra coinYe bhi bikri maani jaati hai, tax lagta hai
Airdrop ya reward milaMilte waqt uski keemat aay maani jaati hai
Staking ka byajAay me judta hai
Mining se mila coinAay, aur mining ka kharcha nahi ghatta
Rishtedaar se giftNirdhaarit rishton me chhoot ka niyam laagu hota hai

Coin se coin badalne wali baat sabse zyada chhoot jaati hai. Aapne rupaye nikaale hi nahi, phir bhi tax ka mauka ban gaya. Isliye har swap ka record rakhna zaroori hai — us din ke bhaav ke saath.

ITR me kaise dikhana hai

VDA ke liye return me alag hissa hota hai, jisme har sauda ki tarikh, kharid, bikri aur TDS bharna hota hai. Ye hissa chhod dena sabse aam galti hai, kyunki exchange ka data pehle se vibhag ke paas pahunch chuka hota hai.

  • Saal bhar ka statement har exchange se download kar lijiye.
  • AIS aur Form 26AS me kata hua TDS milaan kar lijiye.
  • Har coin ka hisaab alag rakhiye — kyunki ghaata milane ki chhoot nahi hai.
  • Videshi exchange par khaata ho to uski alag jaankari dena zaroori ho sakta hai.

Ye page sirf samajhne ke liye hai. Hum CA nahi hain aur ye tax salah nahi hai. Sauday zyada hon ya rakam badi ho to ek baar CA se milaan zaroor kara lijiye. ITR der se bharne par lagne wali fees dekhni ho to ITR Late Fee Calculator dekhiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Crypto par tax kitna lagta hai?

Faayde par flat 30% aur uspar cess. Holding kitni bhi lambi ho, rate nahi badalta. Ye baaki nivesh ke long term aur short term wale niyam se bilkul alag hai.

Crypto ka ghaata doosre faayde se adjust ho sakta hai?

Nahi. Ek coin ka ghaata doosre coin ke faayde se bhi nahi katta, aur agle saal aage bhi nahi le ja sakte.

1% TDS extra tax hai kya?

Nahi. Wo aapke apne tax me adjust ho jaata hai aur zyada kata ho to refund milta hai. Dikkat sirf itni hai ki paisa saal bhar ke liye phas jaata hai.

Exchange ki fees tax me ghat jaati hai?

Nahi. Sirf coin ki kharid keemat ghatti hai. Fees, gas fee aur baaki kharche tax ke hisaab me nahi aate.

Ek coin se doosra coin badla to tax lagega?

Haan, ise bikri maana jaata hai. Rupaye nikale bina bhi tax ka mauka ban jaata hai, isliye har swap ka record us din ke bhaav ke saath rakhna chahiye.
