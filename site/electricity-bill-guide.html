Bijli Bill Kaise Kam Karein? Poori Guide

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~7 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Har mahine bijli bill dekh kar chinta hoti hai — "itna kaise aa gaya?" Sach ye hai ki bill samajhna mushkil nahi, bas units, slab rate aur fixed charge ka khel hai. Is guide me hum bill ka poora hisaab, sabse zyada bijli khaane wale appliance, aur bill ghatane ke asli tarike samjhenge — taaki agla bill dekh kar aap chauk na jaayein.

Bijli bill me kya-kya hota hai?

Aapke bill ke teen bade hisse hote hain. Energy charge — jitni units (kWh) aapne use ki, unka rate. Fixed charge — connection aur meter ka mahine ka charge, jo bijli use na karne par bhi lagta hai. Aur bijli duty ya tax — state government ka tax, aksar 5% ke aaspaas.

In sab ke alawa kabhi-kabhi fuel surcharge, meter rent aur pichhle bill ka adjustment bhi jud jaata hai. Isliye do ghar same units use karke bhi thoda alag bill paa sakte hain. Bill par har line ek baar dhyaan se padhiye — tabhi samajh aata hai paisa kahan jaa raha hai.

Slab rate kya hota hai?

Zyada rajya me bijli ka rate "slab" me hota hai — matlab jitne zyada units, utna zyada per-unit rate. Jaise pehle 100 units sasta, 101-300 thoda mehnga, aur 300 ke upar aur mehnga.

Bill me kaunsa charge kis liye hota hai

Bill me sirf "unit × rate" nahi hota. Aam taur par ye hisse hote hain:

ChargeKis parBacha sakte hain?
Energy chargeKitne unit jaleHaan — kam istemaal se
Fixed chargeAapke connection ke kW parLoad kam karwakar
Fuel surchargeBijli ki laagat parNahi
Electricity dutyRajya ka taxNahi
Meter kirayaFixNahi
Fixed charge wo hissa hai jise log kabhi nahi dekhte. Wo aapke sweekrit load (kW) par lagta hai, chahe aap bijli use karein ya na karein. Kai ghar me load zaroorat se zyada likha hota hai — jaise AC hataane ke baad bhi purana load chalta rehta hai. Bijli daftar me arzi dekar load kam karwane se har mahine paisa bachta hai. Doosri baat — slab wala hisaab hota hai, yaani unit badhne par agli unit mehngi padti hai. Isliye 5 unit kam karna aapki soch se zyada paisa bachaata hai.
Misaal (aam slab):
0-100 units: ₹4/unit
101-300 units: ₹6/unit
301+ units: ₹7.5/unit

Isi wajah se agar aap 300 se 350 units par pahunch jaate hain, to wo aakhri 50 units sabse mehnge padte hain. Bill ka bada hissa upar wale slab me jaata hai. Isliye thodi si bachat bhi aapko neeche wale (saste) slab me rakh sakti hai.

Kaunsa appliance sabse zyada bijli khaata hai?

Sabse bada "bijli chor" hai AC — garmi me ye bill ka 40-50% tak le leta hai. Uske baad geyser aur water heater, fridge (jo 24 ghante chalta hai), aur purane tube-light ya bulb.

  • AC: 1.5 ton AC ghante me lagbhag 1.5 unit. Roz 8 ghante = 12 units = mahine ~360 units sirf AC se.
  • Geyser: 15-litre geyser ghante me ~2 units. Roz aadha ghanta = mahine ~30 units.
  • Fridge: Purana fridge mahine 50-70 units, naya 5-star ~30 units.
  • Bulb: 10 purane bulb (60W) badalkar LED (9W) karne se mahine me kaafi farak.

Yaani agar bill kam karna hai, to sabse pehla nishaana AC aur purane appliance hone chahiye.

Bill kam karne ke asli tarike

Sabse bada farak AC se aata hai. AC ko 24-26°C par chalayein — har ek degree neeche karne par lagbhag 6% zyada bijli lagti hai. Star-rated (5-star) aur inverter AC purane se 30-40% kam bijli lete hain.

Baaki tarike: purane bulb ko LED se badlein (80% tak kam bijli), fridge ko deewar se thoda door rakhein, geyser ka temperature kam rakhein, aur use na hone par appliance poori tarah switch off karein — standby mode me bhi thodi bijli jaati hai.

Lambe samay ke liye rooftop solar sabse bada bachat deta hai. Net metering se aap extra bijli grid ko bech bhi sakte hain, aur aam taur par 4-6 saal me lagat wapas nikal aati hai. Uske baad ki bijli lagbhag muft jaisi ho jaati hai.

Bill zyada aaye to kya karein?

Pehle apne pichhle bill se units compare karein — agar achanak badhe hain to koi appliance zyada chal raha hai ya meter me galti ho sakti hai. Meter reading khud check karein aur bill par diye reading se milaayein.

Agar bill galat lage to apne bijli board (DISCOM) ke customer care ya online portal par complaint kar sakte hain. Kai baar "average billing" (bina actual reading ke anuman) se bill zyada aata hai — actual reading dene par sudhaar ho jaata hai. Har mahine reading note karne ki aadat daal lein, taaki galti pakadna aasaan ho.

Season aur mausam ka asar

Bijli ka bill saal bhar ek jaisa nahi rehta. Garmi (April-June) me AC, cooler aur fridge zyada chalne se bill sabse zyada aata hai — kai ghar me ye normal se do-teen guna ho jaata hai. Sardi me geyser aur room heater bill badhaate hain. Barsaat me sabse kam.

Isliye budget banate waqt saal bhar ka औसत dekhein, sirf ek mahine ka nahi. Ek achhi aadat: har mahine ka bill aur units ek diary ya phone me note karein. Do-teen saal ka data ho jaaye to aapko pata rehta hai kaunse mahine bill badhega, aur aap us hisaab se kharch sambhaal sakte hain. Achanak bill dekh kar pareshaan hone se behtar hai pehle se taiyaar rehna.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Bijli bill kaise nikalta hai?

Units &times; per-unit rate + fixed charge, upar se bijli duty. Slab rate me zyada units par rate badhta hai.

AC ka bill kitna hota hai?

Garmi me AC bill ka 40-50% tak le sakta hai. 24-26&deg;C aur 5-star AC se kaafi bachat hoti hai.

Slab rate ka matlab kya hai?

Jitne zyada units, utna zyada per-unit rate. Aakhri units sabse mehnge padte hain.

Fixed charge kyun lagta hai?

Ye connection aur meter ka mahine ka charge hai, jo bijli use na karne par bhi dena padta hai.

Solar se kitni bachat hoti hai?

Rooftop solar bill kaafi ghata deta hai aur 4-6 saal me lagat wapas. Net metering se extra bijli bech bhi sakte hain.
