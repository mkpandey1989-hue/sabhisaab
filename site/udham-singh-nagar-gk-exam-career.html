Udham Singh Nagar: Exam ke Liye District GK: Ek-Line Tathya

Patwari, Lekhpal, Police, Forest Guard, VDO aur PCS ke Liye

Yaad karne se pehle numbers ko apni exam-book ya official site se milaa lein.

Prashasan aur sthapana

1. Udham Singh Nagar zile ka gathan 29 September 1995 ko hua (Udham Singh Nagar: Tehsil, block aur prashasan).

2. Zila Nainital zile se alag kar banaya gaya.

3. Zila mukhyalay Rudrapur hai.

4. Zile me 9 tehsil hain: Kashipur, Jaspur, Bajpur, Gadarpur, Rudrapur, Kichha, Sitarganj, Nanakmatta aur Khatima.

5. Zile me 9 Vidhan Sabha kshetra hain.

6. Lok Sabha seat ka naam Nainital-Udhamsingh Nagar hai.

7. Zila Kumaon mandal ka hissa hai.

8. Zile ka naam swatantrata senani Shaheed Udham Singh ke naam par hai (Udham Singh Nagar: Itihas, mandir aur lok-katha).

Janganana (Census 2011)

9. Zile ki aabaadi 16,48,902 thi.

10. Kshetrafal lagbhag 2,542 varg km.

11. Janghanatva lagbhag 649 vyakti per varg km.

12. Ling anupat 920 mahilayein per 1000 purush.

13. Prabhavi saksharta lagbhag 73.1% (7 saal se upar).

14. Dharm ke hisab se Hindu 66.98%, Muslim 22.58% aur Sikh 9.87%.

Bhugol

15. Zila Terai-Bhabar kshetra ka hissa hai, yaani maidani zila.

16. Zila Uttarakhand ke dakshin-purv me Uttar Pradesh aur Nepal ki seemaon ke paas hai (Udham Singh Nagar: Bhugol, nadiyan aur pahad).

17. Zile ki Nepal se seema Khatima kshetra ke paas lagti hai.

18. Mukhya nadiyan aur jalashay: Sharda kshetra, Kosi, Nanak Sagar, Tumaria, Baur, Dhora aur Haripura (Udham Singh Nagar: Mausam, jungle aur jeev-jantu aur 8).

Janjati aur sanskriti

19. Zile me Tharu aur Buksa janjatiyon ka nivas hai (Udham Singh Nagar: Sanskriti, tyohar aur mele).

20. Tharu aur Buksa Uttarakhand ki Scheduled Tribes me aate hain.

Shiksha aur sansthan

21. G.B. Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar zile me hai (sthapna varsh Udham Singh Nagar: Arthvyavastha aur shilp me; 1960 ke aaspaas jana jata hai).

22. IIM Kashipur zile me sthit hai.

23. Rajkiya Medical College Rudrapur zile me hai.

24. Pantnagar Airport (PGH) zile ka hawai adda hai.

Parivahan

25. Zile ke pramukh railway station: Kashipur Junction, Rudrapur City, Khatima.

26. Zile se NH 9 aur NH 109 guzarte hain (Udham Singh Nagar: Kaise pahunchein, kahan rukein).

Urja aur udyog

27. Khatima me hydropower station hai (lagbhag 41 MW, Udham Singh Nagar: Arthvyavastha aur shilp).

28. Rudrapur aur Pantnagar SIDCUL audyogik kshetra hain.

29. Zile me chini mill hain (Kichha, Sitarganj, Bazpur aadi, Udham Singh Nagar: Zameen, nali-mutthi aur kheti).

Itihas

30. 1 September 1994 ko Khatima me goli kand hua tha, jisme 7 log mare gaye the (Udham Singh Nagar: Itihas, mandir aur lok-katha).

31. Nanakmatta Sahib Sikh dharm ka pramukh teerth hai.

32. Chaiti Mela (Bal Sundari Devi) Kashipur ka prasiddh mela hai.

Yahan sirf wahi tathya diye gaye hain jinka source page me maujood hai. Is liye suchi 100 tak banaye jane ke bajay verified rakhi gayi hai. Exam ki taiyari ke liye ise aur books se milaakar badhayein.

Yaad rakhne ki Trick (Mnemonic): "9 tehsil, 9 vidhan sabha, 1995 gathan, Rudrapur HQ" ek saath yaad karein.
