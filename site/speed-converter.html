Kaunsi ikai kahan chalti hai

  • km/h — Bharat aur duniya ke zyadatar deshon me gaadi ki raftaar. Aapke speedometer par yahi hai.
  • m/s — vigyan aur padhai ki ikai. Physics ke saare formula isi me chalte hain.
  • mph — America aur Britain me sadak par. Videshi videos aur khabar me yahi milti hai.
  • knot — jahaz aur hawai jahaz me. 1 knot matlab 1 nautical mile per ghanta.
  • ft/s — kuch tikneeki kaam me.
Do sabse kaam ke number, jo yaad rakhne layak hain:
1 m/s = 3.6 km/h — physics ke sawaal me sabse zyada lagta hai
1 mile = 1.609 km — videshi speed limit samajhne ke liye

Dimaag me jaldi badalne ke tarike

m/s se km/h — 3.6 se guna. Aur agar guna mushkil lage, to mota tarika: number ko 3.5-4 guna kar dijiye. 20 m/s lagbhag 72 km/h hai.

km/h se m/s — 3.6 se bhaag. Mota tarika: aadha karke thoda aur kam. 100 km/h lagbhag 28 m/s hai.

mph se km/h — 1.6 se guna. Sabse aasaan trick: aadha jodiye aur thoda aur. 60 mph → 60 + 30 = 90, asli 96.5 — kaafi paas.

Bharat me aam speed limit: sheher me 40-60 km/h, highway par 80-100, aur express highway par 100-120 — par ye jagah aur gaadi ke hisaab se badalti hai, isliye board dekhna hi sahi hai.

Raftaar aur surakshaa ka rishta

Ek baat jo har gaadi chalane wale ko pata honi chahiye — raftaar dugni karne par rukne ki doori dugni nahi, chaar guna ho jaati hai.

Wajah ganit me hai — gaadi ki gati oorja raftaar ke varg ke hisaab se badhti hai. 40 se 80 karne par oorja chaar guna, aur brake ko wahi chaar guna oorja rokni padti hai.

Mota anuman (sookhi sadak, achhe brake):

RaftaarRukne me lagbhag
40 km/h~20 meter
60 km/h~36 meter
80 km/h~56 meter
100 km/h~80 meter

Aur ye sookhi sadak ka hisaab hai. Geeli sadak par ye doori lagbhag dugni ho jaati hai. Barsaat me raftaar kam rakhna sirf salah nahi, ganit hai.

Isme "reaction time" bhi jodiye — khatra dikhne se brake dabane tak lagbhag 1 second lagta hai, aur us ek second me 100 km/h par gaadi 28 meter chali jaati hai.

Padhne walon ke liye

Physics me raftaar ke sawaal me zyadatar galtiyan ikai badalne me hi hoti hain, formula me nahi. Kuch baatein:

  • Formula lagane se pehle sab kuch ek ikai me kar lijiye. Agar samay second me hai to raftaar m/s me honi chahiye — km/h me nahi.
  • Jawab ki ikai zaroor likhiye. Bina ikai ke jawab adhoora maana jaata hai aur number kat jaate hain.
  • Jawab ko tark se jaanchiye — agar cycle ki raftaar 200 m/s aa rahi hai, to kahin ikai badalne me galti hui hai.
Ek yaad rakhne layak baat — 18/5 aur 5/18. km/h se m/s ke liye 5/18 se guna, aur m/s se km/h ke liye 18/5 se. Ye do bhinn yaad ho jaayein to poora topic aasaan ho jaata hai.

Isi wajah se 60 se 80 karne par khatra thoda nahi, kaafi zyada badhta hai. Videsh me gaadi lein to yaad rakhein — wahan board aksar mph me hote hain.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

1 m/s me kitne km/h hote hain?

3.6 km/h. Ulta karne ke liye km/h ko 3.6 se bhaag dijiye — ya 5/18 se guna kariye.

60 mph kitne km/h hota hai?

Lagbhag 97 km/h. Jaldi ka tarika — number ka aadha jod dijiye: 60 + 30 = 90, asli 96.5 ke kaafi paas.

Knot kya hoti hai?

Jahaz aur hawai jahaz me istemaal hone wali raftaar ki ikai. 1 knot matlab 1 nautical mile per ghanta, yaani lagbhag 1.85 km/h.

Raftaar dugni karne par rukne ki doori kitni badhti hai?

Chaar guna, dugni nahi — kyunki gati oorja raftaar ke varg par nirbhar karti hai. Aur geeli sadak par ye doori aur bhi zyada ho jaati hai.
