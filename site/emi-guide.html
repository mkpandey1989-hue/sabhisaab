EMI Kya Hai Aur Kaise Kam Karein?

EMI ka poora matlab hai Equated Monthly Installment — yaani wo nishchit rakam jo aap har mahine loan chukane ke liye bank ko dete hain. Chahe home loan ho, car loan ho ya personal loan, EMI usi ka hissa hai. Is page par hum samjhenge ki EMI kaise banti hai, kis cheez par depend karti hai, aur ise kam kaise kiya jaa sakta hai. EMI kaise banti hai?

Har EMI ke do hisse hote hain — principal (asli loan ki rakam) aur interest (byaj). Shuruat me EMI ka zyada hissa byaj me jaata hai aur kam principal me. Jaise-jaise samay beetta hai, principal ka hissa badhta jaata hai. EMI teen cheezon par depend karti hai:

  • Loan amount (P): Jitna zyada loan, utni zyada EMI.
  • Byaj dar (R): Jitni zyada dar, utni zyada EMI.
  • Samay (N): Jitne zyada saal, EMI utni kam — par kul byaj zyada.
Formula: EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N − 1], jahan R mahine ka byaj hai aur N mahino ki sankhya. Ghabrayein mat — Sab Hisaab ka calculator ye sab apne aap kar deta hai.

EMI kaise kam karein? (5 asli tarike)

  1. Zyada down payment karein: Jitna zyada apni jeb se denge, utna kam loan, utni kam EMI.
  2. Kam byaj dar dhoondhein: Alag-alag bank ki dar compare karein. Thodi si kam dar bhi lambe samay me bada farak laati hai.
  3. Loan ki avadhi (tenure) badhaayein: Isse har mahine ki EMI ghat jaati hai — par yaad rahe, kul byaj badh jaata hai.
  4. Prepayment karein: Jab bhi extra paisa aaye (bonus, savings), loan me thoda extra chuka dein. Isse principal ghatta hai aur aage ka byaj bachta hai.
  5. Balance transfer: Agar doosra bank kam byaj de raha ho, to loan wahan shift kar sakte hain (charges dekh lein).

Prepayment ka kamaal

Loan jaldi khatam karne ka sabse achha tarika hai prepayment. Shuruat ke saalon me kiya gaya prepayment sabse zyada byaj bachata hai, kyunki us samay principal sabse zyada hota hai. Agar aap saal me ek-do extra EMI bhi de dete hain, to loan kai saal pehle khatam ho sakta hai aur laakhon ka byaj bach sakta hai.

  2. Byaj dar daalein (jaise 9%).

Alag-alag amount aur saal daal kar dekhein — aapko turant samajh aa jayega ki kaunsa combination aapke budget me sahi baithta hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Kam tenure lein ya zyada?

Kam tenure = zyada EMI par kam kul byaj. Zyada tenure = kam EMI par zyada kul byaj. Apne budget ke hisaab se balance banayein.

Kya EMI ke saath byaj dar badal sakti hai?

Floating rate loan me haan, dar badalne par EMI ya tenure badal sakta hai. Fixed rate me nahi.

Prepayment par charge lagta hai?

Home loan (floating) par aam taur par nahi, par personal/car loan me kabhi-kabhi charge hota. Bank se confirm karein.

Flat rate aur reducing rate me kitna farak hai?

Bahut. '10% flat' asal me lagbhag 18% reducing ke barabar hota hai. Loan lete waqt hamesha poochiye ki rate flat hai ya reducing.

Prepayment kab sabse zyada faayda deta hai?

Shuru ke saalon me. Us waqt EMI ka bada hissa byaj me jaa raha hota hai, isliye tab daala gaya paisa sabse zyada byaj bachata hai.

Amortisation — EMI ke andar ka sach

EMI har mahine ek jaisi rehti hai, par uske andar ka batwara badalta rehta hai. Shuru ke saalon me EMI ka bada hissa byaj me jaata hai aur mool rakam bahut dheere ghatti hai. Aakhri saalon me ulta hota hai.

20 saal ke home loan me pehle 5 saal me mool rakam aam taur par sirf 15-20% hi kam hoti hai — jabki aap paanch saal ki poori EMI de chuke hote hain. Ye baat jaan lena zaroori hai, kyunki isi se prepayment ka sahi samay samajh aata hai.

Loan lete waqt ye teen sawaal zaroor poochhiye: (1) dar fixed hai ya floating — floating me EMI aage badh bhi sakti hai; (2) prepayment par charge kitna hai — floating home loan par aam taur par charge nahi lagta, par personal aur car loan par lagta hai; (3) processing fee kitni hai aur uspar GST alag hai kya. Teeno baatein likhit me lijiye. Zyadatar log sirf EMI ka number poochhte hain, aur baaki teen cheezein baad me mehngi padti hain.
Isliye: prepayment shuru ke saalon me sabse zyada faydemand hoti hai. Aakhri saalon me karne se utna byaj nahi bachta, kyunki tab tak zyadatar byaj de hi chuke hote hain.

Loan ka samay — chhota ya lamba

Lamba samay lene se EMI kam ho jaati hai aur mahine ka bojh halka lagta hai. Par kul byaj bahut zyada ho jaata hai.

Misaal ke taur par, ek hi rakam par 15 saal aur 20 saal ke loan me EMI ka farak thoda hi hota hai, par kul byaj ka farak lakho me hota hai. Isliye agar EMI aapke budget me aa rahi hai, to kam saal chunna aam taur par behtar faisla hai.

Kitni EMI safe hai

Aam salah — aapki saari EMI milkar monthly income ke 40% se kam honi chahiye. Bank bhi yahi ratio (DTI) dekhte hain. Isse zyada ho jaaye to:

  • Bachat lagbhag khatam ho jaati hai
  • Ek mahina salary late hui to kist bounce ho sakti hai
  • Naya loan milna mushkil ho jaata hai
  • Sabse bada nuksan — emergency fund ban hi nahi paata

EMI bounce hone se kya hota hai

Kist samay par na jaaye to teen nuksan ek saath hote hain — bank ki penalty, bounce charge, aur sabse bada credit score par nishaan. Ek bhi missed EMI score kaafi gira deti hai aur uska asar mahino tak rehta hai.

Agar kisi mahine dikkat ho rahi ho, to kist rukne ka intezaar karne se behtar hai ki pehle bank se baat karein. Kai bank halaat samajh kar EMI ki tarikh badalne ya kuch samay ki chhoot dene ko taiyaar ho jaate hain.

Byaj bachane ke 5 practical tarike

  1. Saal me ek extra EMI — isse 20 saal ka loan aksar 2-3 saal pehle khatam ho jaata hai
  2. Bonus ka kuch hissa prepayment me — poora nahi, par kuch zaroor
  3. Prepayment ke baad tenure kam karwayein, EMI nahi — isse zyada byaj bachta hai
  4. Balance transfer — doosra bank kaafi sasti dar de raha ho to; par transfer ke kharche bhi jod kar dekh lein
  5. Credit score sudhaarein — agli baar sasti dar milegi
