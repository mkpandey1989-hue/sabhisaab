Company kuch bhi mileage bataye, asli mileage aapki gaadi hi batati hai. Tank full karke km note karein, agli baar full par litre aur km daaliye — aapki gaadi ka asli mileage (km/l) aur per-km fuel kharch turant mil jaayega. Isse aap showroom ke daave ke bajaye apni gaadi ka sach jaan sakte hain — aur fuel budget behtar bana sakte hain.

Formula aasaan bhasha me

Mileage nikaalne ka sabse sahi tarika hai "tank full method". Tank full karo, trip meter zero karo. Agli baar tank full karne par jitna fuel gaya aur jitni km chali — dono note karo. Mileage = km ÷ litre.

Formula:
Mileage = chali km ÷ bhara litre (km/l)
Per-km kharch = fuel rate ÷ mileage

Ye "tank-to-tank" tarika gaadi ke apne meter se zyada bharosemand hai. Kuch baar naap kar ausat lein — ek baar ka number traffic aur driving se prabhaavit ho sakta hai.

Mileage jaanna sirf jigyaasa nahi, paise ki bachat hai. Jab aapko apni asli mileage pata ho, to aap mahine ka fuel budget theek se laga sakte hain aur ye bhi samajh sakte hain ki servicing ke baad ya tyre pressure sahi karne se kitna farak pada. Do alag gaadi (jaise petrol vs CNG) ke beech chunte waqt bhi asli mileage sabse zaroori number hota hai. Yaad rakhein — showroom ka bataya mileage aksar aadarsh (ideal) haalat me hota hai, jabki asli sadak, traffic aur aapki driving style uska asli roop tay karti hai.

Bahut log purani gaadi ki girti mileage se pareshaan rehte hain par kabhi naapte nahi. Agar aap har mahine mileage note karein, to time rahte pata chal jaata hai ki engine, tyre ya air filter me kuch dikkat aa rahi hai — jab mileage achanak girne lage. Ye chhoti aadat lambe samay me bade repair aur fuel ke paise bachati hai. Isi liye tank-full method se kabhi-kabhi mileage zaroor check karte rahein.

Asli zindagi ke 3 example

Misaal 1 — bike. 450 km, 30 litre. Mileage = 15 km/l... ruko, 450/30 = 15 km/l? Nahi — 450/30=15. Per-km = 102/15 = ₹6.8 per km.

Misaal 2 — achhi mileage. 300 km, 6 litre. Mileage = 50 km/l (bike). Per-km = 102/50 = ₹2.04.

Misaal 3 — car city. 200 km, 20 litre = 10 km/l (city traffic). Highway par yahi car 15+ de sakti hai.

Yahan log phas jaate hain

  1. Tank full na karna. Sahi mileage ke liye dono baar tank exactly full karna zaroori — warna litre galat.
  2. Ek baar ka number maanna. Ek trip ka mileage traffic/AC se badalta hai — 2-3 baar naap kar ausat lein.
  3. Reserve me chalana. Reserve tak khaali kar ke naapne se number theek nahi aata.
  4. Company claim par bharosa. ARAI/company mileage aksar zyada hota hai — asli hamesha kam.
  5. Alag fuel station. Alag pump ki calibration thodi alag ho sakti hai — ek hi pump behtar.

Desi context — zaroori baatein

  • Tank full method: Sabse aasaan aur sahi — do full-tank ke beech ka hisaab.
  • City vs highway: City me stop-start se mileage kam, highway par zyada.
  • AC ka asar: AC on rakhne se mileage 10-15% girta hai.
  • Servicing: Sahi hawa, clean air filter aur time par service mileage sudhaarti hai.

Aur thoda samajhte hain

Gaadi kharidte waqt company jo mileage (ARAI figure) batati hai, wo lab conditions me nikala hota hai — asli sadak par, traffic, AC aur driving style ki wajah se wo aksar kam nikalta hai. Isi liye log "asli mileage kaise nikale", "petrol average kaise check karein", "km per litre calculator" search karte hain. Sabse bharosemand tarika "tank full method" hai: tank full karwao, trip meter zero karo, agli baar full karwate waqt daale gaye litre aur chali gayi km se mileage nikaalo.

Mileage sudharne ke chhote tarike — sahi tyre pressure, achanak brake/accelerator se bachna, aur zaroorat na ho to AC band. City me mileage highway se kaafi kam aata hai, isliye dono alag note karein. Poore safar ka petrol kharch jaanna ho to hamara Fuel Cost tool, aur petrol vs CNG/EV compare karna ho to Petrol vs CNG/EV tool istemaal karein — long drive se pehle dono kaam aate hain.

Chhote sawaal, chhote jawab

Gaadi ka asli mileage kaise nikalein?

Tank full karke km note karein; agli full par litre aur km daalein. Mileage = km &divide; litre.

Company mileage sahi hota hai?

Aksar nahi — ARAI/company claim zyada hota hai, asli mileage kam.

City aur highway me farak?

City me stop-start se mileage kam, highway par zyada hoti hai.

AC se mileage girta hai?

Haan, AC on rakhne se 10-15% tak kam ho sakti hai.

Kitni baar naapein?

2-3 baar naap kar ausat lein — ek baar ka number bhrmit kar sakta hai.

Petrol, CNG aur EV — 1,000 km ka asli kharcha

Sirf "mileage" dekhna adhoora hai. Poora hisaab (lagbhag bhaav par):

GaadiChalne ka kharchaSaal bhar ka service
Petrol (18 km/l)~Rs 5,600Zyada
Diesel (22 km/l)~Rs 4,300Sabse zyada
CNG (26 km/kg)~Rs 3,100Madhyam
EV (ghar par charge)~Rs 1,000Sabse kam
Chalne ka kharcha poori kahani nahi hai: CNG kit ki keemat, EV ki mehngi shuruaati keemat aur battery ki umr — ye teeno hisaab me aane chahiye. Aam taur par CNG ka faayda tabhi milta hai jab mahine me 1,000 km se zyada chalte hon. EV ghar par charge karne par hi sasti padti hai; bahar ke fast charger ka bhaav 3-4 guna hota hai. Aur ek baat jo bikri wale nahi batate — sabhi gaadiyon ka asli mileage kagaz wale number se lagbhag 15-20% kam hota hai.
Zaroori baat: Asli mileage driving, traffic, AC, hawa aur gaadi ki condition par nirbhar karta hai. Ek se zyada baar naap kar ausat lena zyada sahi rehta hai.
