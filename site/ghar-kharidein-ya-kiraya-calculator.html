Ghar Kharidein Ya Kiraye Par Rahein — Hisaab

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~5 minute
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Ye Bharat ka sabse bada aur sabse bhavnaatmak faisla hai. Neeche ghar ki keemat, kiraya aur baaki number daaliye — dono taraf ka hisaab saamne aa jayega. Par asli jawab neeche wale hisse me hai.

Dono taraf ka poora kharcha

Khareedne par:

  • Down payment (aur wo paisa ab kahin aur nahi lag raha)
  • Har mahine EMI
  • Stamp duty aur registration — ek baar, par bada
  • Maintenance aur society charge
  • Property tax
  • Marammat — jo pehle makan malik karta tha

Kiraye par:

  • Har mahine kiraya (jo har saal 5-10% badhta hai)
  • Security deposit (jo wapas mil jaata hai)
  • Baar-baar shifting ka kharcha aur pareshani
Asli tulna tabhi sahi hoti hai jab bachaya hua paisa nivesh me jaye. Agar EMI ₹40,000 hai aur kiraya ₹18,000, to kiraye par rehne wale ko wo ₹22,000 ka antar (aur down payment bhi) nivesh karna hoga. Agar wo kharch ho gaya, to kiraya sasta nahi raha.

Bharat ka rental yield ye faisla kaafi saaf kar deta hai

Ek number hai jo is bahas ko lagbhag khatam kar deta hai — rental yield.

Bharat me ghar ka kiraya, uski keemat ka aam taur par sirf 2-3.5% saalana hota hai. Aur home loan ka byaj lagbhag 8.5-9%.

₹60 lakh ka flat, kiraya ₹16,000 mahina = ₹1.92 lakh saal = 3.2% yield.
Usi ₹60 lakh par loan ka byaj lagbhag 8.5%.
Yaani rehne ke liye kiraya sasta padta hai — sirf paise ke hisaab se.

Par ye poora sach nahi hai, kyunki:

  • Ghar ki keemat badhti hai — aur wo badhotari poori keemat par milti hai, sirf down payment par nahi
  • Home loan par tax me chhoot milti hai (purane regime me), jo asli byaj kam kar deti hai
  • EMI khatam ho jaati hai, kiraya kabhi nahi — 20 saal baad ek taraf kharcha zero, doosri taraf kiraya dugna

Apna hisaab Rental Yield Calculator me lagakar dekh lijiye. Agar yield 3% se neeche hai, to us ilaake me ghar mehnga hai.

Ye baatein number me nahi aati — par sabse zyada mayne rakhti hain

Khareedne ke haq me:

  • Nishchintata. Makan malik kabhi khaali karne nahi kahega. Bacchon ka school nahi badlega.
  • Apni marzi. Deewar par keel, rang, marammat — koi poochne wala nahi.
  • Budhape ki surakshaa. 60 ki umr me kiraya bharna aur naya ghar dhoondhna sabse mushkil hota hai.
  • Samaj aur parivaar — Bharat me apna ghar sirf sampatti nahi, ek pehchaan hai. Ise hisaab me se nikaal dena imaandaari nahi hogi.

Kiraye ke haq me:

  • Naukri badalne ki azaadi. Ye sabse bada faayda hai, khaaskar shuruaati saalon me.
  • Bade karze ka bojh nahi — 20 saal ki EMI ek bada bandhan hai.
  • Paisa fansa nahi — zaroorat par nivesh se nikaala ja sakta hai, ghar se nahi.
  • Marammat aur maintenance ka jhanjhat nahi

Seedha jawab — kab kya kariye

Kharidiye jab:

  • Us sheher me kam se kam 7-10 saal rehne ka iraada ho. Isse kam me stamp duty aur registration ka kharcha hi nahi nikalta.
  • Down payment 20% + registration ka paisa taiyaar ho, bina emergency fund tode
  • EMI aay ke 40% ke andar rahe
  • Naukri ya aay sthir ho

Kiraye par rahiye jab:

  • Naukri ya sheher badalne ki sambhavna ho
  • Down payment ke liye emergency fund todna pad raha ho
  • EMI 40% se upar ja rahi ho
  • Us ilaake ka rental yield 2% se bhi neeche ho — wahan ghar bahut mehnga hai
Ek beech ka rasta jo bahut kaam karta hai — kiraye par rahiye aur down payment ka paisa nivesh me rakhiye, jab tak (1) sheher tay na ho jaye aur (2) rakam itni na ho jaye ki EMI aaraam se ja sake. Jaldbaazi me liya gaya ghar 20 saal tak takleef deta hai.

Poori tulna Buy vs Rent Guide me hai. EMI ka hisaab EMI Calculator me aur kitna loan milega ye Home Loan Eligibility me dekhiye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Ghar kharidna sasta hai ya kiraya?

Sirf paise ke hisaab se, Bharat me kiraya aksar sasta padta hai — kyunki rental yield 2-3.5% hai aur loan ka byaj 8.5%. Par ghar ki keemat badhna, tax chhoot aur nishchintata bhi jodni chahiye.

Kitne saal rehne wale hon to ghar kharidna chahiye?

Kam se kam 7-10 saal. Isse kam me stamp duty, registration aur shuruaati kharche hi nahi nikalte.

EMI aay ka kitna percent honi chahiye?

40% se zyada nahi — aur home loan ke liye 30-35% behtar hai, kyunki maintenance, property tax aur marammat alag se aate hain.

Down payment ke liye emergency fund tod dein?

Nahi. Ghar mil jayega par pehli musibat par aap credit card par honge. Down payment ke liye alag bachat kariye.
