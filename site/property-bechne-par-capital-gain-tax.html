Ghar Ya Zameen Bechne Par Capital Gain Tax

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~7 minute
Chhota jawabSeedha jawab: property bechne par jo munafa hota hai uspar capital gain tax lagta hai. 2 saal se zyada rakhi to long term (kam tax, aur naye ghar me paisa lagakar tax bacha sakte hain). 2 saal se kam to short term (aapke slab ke hisaab se tax). Ye YMYL vishay hai — CA se zaroor milein.

Property par capital gain kya hai

Jab aap ghar ya zameen khareed kar zyada daam par bechte hain, to jo munafa hota hai use capital gain kehte hain, aur ispar tax lagta hai. Kitna tax — ye is baat par nirbhar karta hai ki aapne property kitne samay tak rakhi.

2 saal se kam rakhi = Short Term (slab ke hisaab se tax)
2 saal ya zyada rakhi = Long Term (alag dar, par tax bachane ke raaste)

Short vs Long term

Short term (2 saal se kam): munafa aapki income me judta hai aur aapke tax slab ke hisaab se tax lagta hai — jo zyada ho sakta hai. Long term (2 saal ya zyada): ek alag (aksar kam) dar se tax, aur sabse badi baat — naye ghar me paisa lagakar ye tax bacha bhi sakte hain. Isliye property thodi lambi rakhna aksar tax me faayda deta hai.

Indexation — mehngai ka fayda

Long term me ek cheez milti hai jise indexation kehte hain. Iska matlab — aapki purani khareed keemat ko mehngai ke hisaab se badha kar maana jaata hai, jisse "munafa" kam dikhta hai aur tax kam lagta hai. Ye niyam samay-samay par badalte hain, isliye current niyam CA se confirm karein. Aam roop se ye long-term bechne walon ko raahat deta hai.

Tax bachane ke tareeke (long term)

Long term capital gain par tax bachane ke kuch kanooni raaste hain:

  • Section 54 — ghar bech kar doosra ghar khareedein (tay samay ke andar), to tax bach sakta hai.
  • Section 54EC — kuch khaas sarkari bonds me paisa lagakar (tay seema tak) tax bacha sakte hain.
  • Capital Gains Account Scheme — agar turant ghar nahi khareed pa rahe, to is khate me paisa rakhkar samay le sakte hain.

In sabki apni shartein aur samay-seema hoti hain. Ek chhoti galti se poora fayda ja sakta hai, isliye ye CA ki madad se hi karein.

Circle rate ka dhyaan

Property ka tax uski asli bikri keemat par ya circle rate (sarkari nyoontam dar) — jo zyada ho — par banta hai. Agar aap circle rate se kam par bechte hain, to bhi tax circle rate par lag sakta hai, aur kharidaar par bhi asar padta hai. Isliye bechne se pehle apne ilaake ka circle rate zaroor dekhein. Property ki khareed me stamp duty ka hisaab yahan hai.

Kaagaz aur record rakhein

Capital gain sahi nikalne ke liye ye record zaroori: khareed ka agreement aur date, bikri ka agreement aur date, ghar par kiye gaye bade sudhaar (renovation) ke bill, aur registry ke kaagaz. Ye sab tax kam karne aur sahi ITR bharne me kaam aate hain. Bina record ke munafa zyada dikh sakta hai aur tax bhi.

ITR aur zaroori salah

Property capital gain ko ITR-2 (ya business ho to ITR-3) me dikhana hota hai. Ye ek badi rakam aur uljha hua vishay hai — indexation, Section 54, circle rate sab me galti mehngi padti hai.

Zaroori: ye lekh sirf aam jaankari ke liye hai. Property capital gain YMYL (paise se juda gambhir) vishay hai aur niyam har budget me badalte hain. Hum CA nahi hain. Bechne se pehle ek CA se zaroor milein aur current niyam incometax.gov.in se confirm karein.

Ek aam galti jo mehngi padti hai

Bahut log property bech kar poora paisa doosri jagah (jaise business ya kharch) me laga dete hain, aur capital gain tax ka intezaam nahi karte. Phir ITR ke waqt bada tax saamne aata hai jiske liye paisa hi nahi bacha. Isliye bechne se pehle hi CA se hisaab lagwa lein ki kitna tax banega aur use kaise bachaya ya bhara jaayega.

Doosri aam galti — Section 54 me naya ghar khareedne ki samay-seema chook jaana. Iski tay avdhi hoti hai (khareedne ya banane ke liye). Agar aap us samay me ghar nahi khareed pate aur paisa "Capital Gains Account Scheme" me bhi nahi rakhte, to tax bachane ka mauka chala jaata hai. Ye chhoti si timing ki baat lakhon ka farak la deti hai.

Isliye property bechna sirf "khareedar dhoondhna" nahi hai — iski tax planning bechne se pehle honi chahiye. Thodi si taiyaari aur ek achhe CA ki salah aapka bada paisa bacha sakti hai. Ye kaam khud andaaze se karne ka nahi hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Property bechne par kitna tax lagta hai?

2 saal se kam rakhi to slab ke hisaab se (short term). 2 saal ya zyada to long term dar se, par naye ghar me paisa lagakar tax bacha sakte hain.

Indexation kya hai?

Long term me purani khareed keemat ko mehngai ke hisaab se badha kar maana jaata hai, jisse munafa aur tax kam ho jaata hai. Niyam badalte hain.

Property ka tax kaise bachayein?

Long term me Section 54 (doosra ghar khareed kar), 54EC (bonds me), ya Capital Gains Account Scheme se. Shartein hoti hain, CA se karein.

Circle rate kyun mayne rakhta hai?

Tax asli bikri keemat ya circle rate — jo zyada ho — par lag sakta hai. Circle rate se kam par bechne par bhi tax circle rate par ban sakta hai.

Kaunsi ITR bharni hoti hai?

Property capital gain ITR-2 me (business income ho to ITR-3). Ye badi rakam hai, CA ki madad lena behtar.
