Almora: Aabadi, Samuday aur Anusuchit Janjati

Census ke Aankde: Aabadi, Ling Anupat, Saksharta aur Gaon-Shahar

Seedha jawab: Census 2011 ke anusar Almora ki jansankhya 6,22,506 hai, jisme 2,91,081 purush aur 3,31,425 mahilayein hain. Sex ratio 1,139 hai, jo Uttarakhand me sabse zyada mana jata hai. Zile ki sakshrata dar 80.47% hai aur lagbhag 90% jansankhya gramin kshetra me rehti hai.

Almora ke aankde ek kahani kehte hain: purush kaam ki talash me pahad chhod kar mahanagron ki taraf gaye, aur gaon ko mahilayein sambhal rahi hain. Isi liye Almora me mahilayein purushon se zyada hain.

Aankdon se 5 mahatvapurn baatein:

  • Zile ki jansankhya 2001 se 2011 me ghati, jabki rajya ki badhi.
  • Lagbhag 10 me se 9 log gaon me rehte hain.
  • Zile me lagbhag 70% kamgaar kheti par nirbhar hain.
  • Mahila sakshrata purush se kam hai, jo ek vikas chunauti hai.

Seedha jawab: Almora me Anusuchit Janjati ki jansankhya sirf lagbhag 0.21% hai, isiliye yahan Bhotia, Jaunsari, Buksa, Tharu ya Van Raji jaise janjaati samudaay nahi basate. Ye samudaay mukhya roop se Pithoragarh, Chamoli, Dehradun, Udham Singh Nagar aur Champawat zilon me hain.

Almora: Samaj ki Banawat, Upnaam aur Gotra

Almora ke Pramukh Surnames aur Samajik Sanrachna

Seedha jawab: Almora ke pramukh surnames me Joshi, Pant, Pandey, Tiwari, Upreti, Bhatt (Brahmin samudaay), Bisht, Negi, Rawat, Bhandari, Mahara, Karki, Adhikari, Rautela (Kshatriya/Rajput), aur Arya, Tamta, Das jaise Shilpkar samudaay ke naam aam hain. Yeh sirf pehchan ki jankari hai.

Samvedansheelta ka note: Jaati ke aadhar par bhedbhav kanoonan apradh hai. Yeh suchi sirf sanskritik aur samajik adhyayan ke uddeshya se hai, kisi ko oonch-neech batane ke liye nahi. Surnames ke kai varg ek hi jaati ya alag-alag, dono ho sakte hain, isiliye surname se kisi ki jaati ya gotra ka andaza lagana galat ho sakta hai.

Mool Gaon (Mool Niwas / Thaat) aur Vivah ki Reetiyan

Seedha jawab: Pahadi parivar ka “Mool Niwas” ya “Thaat” wo purkhon ka gaon hota hai jahan kul-devta ka sthan aur purane ghar hote hain. Palayan ke baad bhi log shaadi, jagar aur kul-devta ki puja ke liye apne mool gaon laut-te hain.

Pahadi samaj me gaon ki pehchan ko bahut mahatva diya jata hai. Log apna parichay naam ke saath gaon ka naam jod kar dete hain (jaise “Joshi, Khola gaon ka”). Shaadi me gotra aur sagotra ka dhyan rakha jata hai, jisse ek hi gotra me shaadi nahi hoti.

Almora: Vernacular Pahadi Architecture (Pahadi Ghar)

Pathal Roofs, Bakhali, Kholi aur Pahadi Ghar ki Sanrachna

Seedha jawab: Kumaon ka paramparik pahadi ghar patthar aur lakdi se banta hai, jisme chhat par Pathal (slate patthar), mukhya darwaze par naqqashidar lakdi ki Kholi aur nichli manzil par Goth hota hai. Bakhali ek lambi qatar me jude gharon wali bastiyon ka naam hai.

Pahadi ghar bhukamp aur thand ka sahi mukabla karne ke liye bane the. Moti patthar ki deewarein garmi me thandak aur sardi me garmi deti hain. Lakdi ki beam bhukamp ke jhatke ko sokhti hai. Is vigyan ko aaj ke architect bhi seekh rahe hain.

Yeh shaili mukhya roop se Garhwal (Uttarkashi, Rudraprayag) ki hai. Almora/Kumaon me samaan sidhanton par bane ghar Bakhali aur Pathal-chhat wale ghar kehlate hain. Hum yahan Kumaon ke ghar ki baat kar rahe hain.

Ghar ki Banawat: Goth, Chaja, Tibari, Chakh aur Khalian

Seedha jawab: Pahadi ghar ki nichli manzil me gay-bhains ka Goth hota hai, jiski garmi upar ke kamron ko garm rakhti hai. Upar ki manzil me rehne ke kamre aur lakdi ka naqqashidar bramda (Chaja/Tibari) hota hai. Ghar ke aage patthar se paka kiya aangan Khlian kehlata hai.

Almora: Paramparik Pehnawa aur Kapde

Mahilaon ka Pehnava: Rangwali Pichhora, Ghaghri-Angra aur Dhatu

Seedha jawab: Kumaoni dulhan ka sabse khaas vastra Rangwali Pichora hai, jo pila-lal rang ka dupatta hota hai jis par swastik aur anya shubh chinh bane hote hain. Mahilayein pehle ghaghri-angra, choli aur dhatu (sar ka kapda) pehanti thi. Aaj saree aur salwar-kameez adhik chalte hain.

Rangwali Pichora ko Kumaon me suhag aur shubhta ka pratik mana jata hai. Shaadi, naamkaran aur tyoharon me ise pehna jata hai. Is par swastik, shankh aur chakra jaise chinh haath se chhape ya rangaye jate hain.

Men’s Wear: Mirjai, Sadri, Churidar Pajama & Pahadi Topi

Seedha jawab: Purushon ka paramparik pehnawa kurta, churidar pajama, sadri (jacket jaisi waistcoat), ooni coat aur Pahadi topi hai. Sardi me oon ke bhari kapde pehne jate the. Aaj ki pehchan me topi aur sadri tyoharon aur sanskritik karyakramon me dikhayi deti hain.

Note: Kapde ke sthaniya naam (Mirjai jaise) gaon aur kshetra ke anusar badal sakte hain.

Almora: Anushthan ki Lok-Kala: Aipan, Jyunti aur Lakdi ka Kaam

Aipan ke Namoone, Geru-Biswar Vidhi aur Jyunti Matrika Chitra

Seedha jawab: Aipan (Eepan) Kumaon ki ritual lok kala hai, jisme geru (laal mitti) ki sataha par chawal ke aate ke ghol (biswar) se tino ungliyon ki madad se sundar alpana banayi jati hai. Lakshmi chauki, Saraswati peeth aur Shiv peeth jaise roop shubh mauqon par banaye jate hain.

Aipan kaise banta hai?

  • Aangan, chaukhat ya puja ke sthaan ko geru (laal mitti) ke lep se rangte hain.
  • Chawal ko bhigo kar pees kar ghol (biswar) banate hain.
  • Biswar me ungliyon ko doba kar, antim teen ungliyon se rekhayein aur chinh banate hain.
  • Lakshmi ke pair, swastik, Ashtadal kamal aur anya shubh chinh banaye jate hain.

Jyunti/Matrika patta: Jyunti ek chitra-patta hai jo bachche ke janm ya shaadi par ghar ki deewar par banaya jata hai. Isme devi matrika ke roop chitrit hote hain.

Dhyan dein: Aipan sirf sajawat nahi, ek puja ka hissa hai. Aipan ko bina jaankari ke commercial design ki tarah chhapna ya bechna samudaay ke liye samvedansheel ho sakta hai.

Almora: Lok-Sangeet, Lok-Gaathayein aur Gayan Parampara

Jagar, Pawada, Nyoli, Chhopati, Jhora, Bair & Khadi Holi

Seedha jawab: Kumaon ki lok sangeet parampara me Jagar (devta ka aahwan), Pawada (veer gatha), Nyoli (virah ke geet), Jhora, Chhopati, Chanchari aur Bair (kavi-dwandwa) prasiddh hain. Almora ko khaas taur par Baithaki Holi aur Khadi Holi ke liye jana jata hai.

Almora aur Holi: Almora Kumaoni Baithaki Holi ka mukhya kendra mana jata hai. Basant Panchami ke baad se Holi tak raag-raaginiyon par aadharit geet gaye jate hain. Mahila Holi ki apni alag parampara hai.

Almora ke lok-kalakaar: Mohan Upreti (“Bedu Pako Baro Masa” geet ke saath) Almora se jude lok-sangeet ke mahatvapurn naam hain. Girda (Girish Tiwari) Almora ke Hawalbagh se aaye jan-kavi aur andolankari the. In vyaktitvon par Almora: Mashhoor Hastiyan, Shaheed aur Puraskar Vijeta me alag se vistar se aayega.

Almora: Paramparik Vadya Yantra aur Bajgi/Auji Samuday

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand ke lok-vaadya me Dhol-Damau, Hudka, Ransingha, Masakbeen aur Algoza mukhya hain. Dhol ke taalon ka gyan “Dhol Sagar” naam ki maukhik parampara me sanjoya gaya hai. Bajgi/Auji samudaay is vaadya parampara ke pramukh vahak hain.

Dhol Sagar: Dhol Sagar ek maukhik parampara hai, jisme alag-alag avsaron (shaadi, jagar, yatra) ke liye alag taal mane jate hain.

Bajgi/Auji samudaay: Yeh samudaay peedhi-dar-peedhi dhol-damau bajane ka kaam karta aaya hai. Samaj me inka bada sanskritik yogdan hai, par aarthik sthiti kamzor rahi hai. Aaj sarkar aur NGO inki kala ko bachane ke prayas kar rahe hain.

Almora: Pahadi Life-Cycle Rituals (Sanskars) & Weddings

Namkaran, Janeu, Vivah ki Reetiyan aur Antim Sanskar

Seedha jawab: Kumaoni sanskaron me namkaran, mundan, janeu (yagyopaveet), vivah aur antim sanskar mukhya hain. Vivah me Ganesh puja, Dhuli Arghya (dulhe ke pair dhone ki rasm), kanyadan aur bidaai khaas hain. Har gaon me rasmon me thoda bhed hota hai.

Pahadi vivah ke pramukh charan:

  • Ganesh puja aur haldi: Vivah ki shuruaat.
  • Baraat aur Chholiya: Baraat ke saath dhol-damau aur Chholiya nritya.
  • Dhuli Arghya: Shaam ko dulhe ke pair dhone ki rasm.
  • Kanyadan aur phere: Vedic vidhi se.
  • Bidaai: Mait se sauras ki yatra, jis par Khuded geet gaye jate hain (Almora: Bidaai aur Karun Geet).

Note: Suwal Pathai aur Anchali jaisi rasmon ke naam aur vidhi gaon ke anusar badalti hai.

Antim sanskar: Hindu parampara ke anusar daah sanskar nadi ke kinare kiya jata hai, aur terahvi tak shok ke niyam palan hote hain. Hum is vishay par sambandhit parivar ki bhavnaon ka sammaan karte hue sankshipt jankari dete hain.

Almora ka Dussehra: Almora ka Dussehra poore Kumaon me prasiddh hai. Yahan Ravana, Meghnad, Kumbhkaran aur anya paatron ke bade-bade putle banaye jate hain aur unki shobha-yatra nikalti hai. Putle banane wale kalakaar mahino pehle taiyari karte hain.

Nanda Devi Mela: Nanda Devi Kumaon aur Garhwal ki ishta devi hain. Almora ke Nanda Devi mandir ka mela Chand kaal se laga rehne ki manyata hai. Kumaon aur Garhwal ke bahut se log yahan darshan karne aate hain.

Nanda Raj Jat: Nanda Raj Jat Yatra Uttarakhand ki sabse lambi paidal yatra hai, jo lagbhag 12 saal me ek baar hoti hai aur isme chausingha khadu (char sing wala bhed) saath chalta hai. Yeh yatra mukhya roop se Chamoli zile se shuru hoti hai. Agli yatra ki tithi ke liye Nanda Devi Raj Jat Samiti ya Chamoli prashasan ki official ghoshna dekhein.

Note: Ghatnayein tithi par nirbhar hain aur har saal badalti hain. Mele ke samay aur sthan ke liye Almora Zila Prashasan ya Uttarakhand Paryatan Vibhag ki official soochna dekhein.

Almora Dussehra: Putla Parampara (Ravan Parivar ke Putle)

Seedha jawab: Almora ka Dussehra apne vishal aur kalatmak putlon (effigies) ke liye poore Uttarakhand me prasiddh hai. Shahar ke alag-alag mohalle Ravan, Kumbhkaran, Meghnad aur Ravan parivar ke anya patron ke putle banate hain, jinki shobhayatra Dashami ki shaam bazaar se nikalti hai aur uske baad dahan hota hai.

Almora ke Dussehra ki khaas baat yeh hai ki yahan sirf ek Ravan nahi, balki Ravan parivar ke kai putle bante hain aur har mohalla apne putle ko sabse sundar banane ki hod me rehta hai. Putle banane ka kaam Navratri se kai hafte pehle shuru ho jata hai. Shobhayatra me dhol-damau, nagade aur Kumaoni lok-vadya bajte hain. Bahut se log ise Kullu Dussehra (Himachal) ke baad pahad ka sabse dekhne layak Dussehra maante hain, haalaanki dono ki parampara bilkul alag hai: Kullu me devta-rath aate hain, Almora me putle aur Ramlila (pushti karein: putlon ki sankhya har saal badalti hai.

Kumaoni Ramlila: Almora ki Sangeetmay Ramlila

Seedha jawab: Kumaoni Ramlila ek geya (gaayi jane wali) natya parampara hai, jisme samvad raag-raaginiyon par gaye jate hain. Lok-sandarbhon ke anusar Kumaon ki pehli Ramlila Almora me 1860 ke aas-paas Badreshwar Mandir me hui thi. Isliye Almora ko Kumaoni Ramlila ka janm-sthan kaha jata hai.

Kumaoni Ramlila ko "opera shaili" ki Ramlila bhi kehte hain, kyunki isme bole jane wale samvad kam aur gaaye jane wale chhand (chaupai, doha, daadra, kaharwa taal) zyada hote hain. Purane samay me purush hi mahila patra nibhaate the; ab kai jagah mahilayein aur bachche bhi bhaag lete hain. Kalakaron ki "taalim" (rehearsal) Navratri se kai hafte pehle shuru ho jati hai. Navratri ke dino me shahar ke alag-alag mohallon ke manchon par raat ko Ramlila hoti hai aur Dashami ko putla dahan ke saath samapan hota hai.

Somnath Mela, Masi (Ramganga Ghati)

Seedha jawab: Somnath Mela Almora zile ki Ramganga ghati ke Masi kasbe me Vaishakh mahine me lagne wala purana mela hai. Yeh mela Somnath Mahadev ki pooja, lok-nritya aur purane samay me pashu aur saman ke vyapar ke liye jana jata hai (pushti karein: is saal ki tithi zila prashasan/mandir samiti se confirm karein).

Purane samay me yeh mela Ramganga ghati ke gaon ke liye saal ka sabse bada milan-sthal hota tha, jahan door-door se log saman kharidne-bechne aur rishtedaron se milne aate the. Aaj bhi mele me jhoda-chanchari aur lok-geeton ki parampara dikhti hai.
