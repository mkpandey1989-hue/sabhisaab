Old vs New Tax Regime — Kaunsa Kam Tax Dega

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Update: August 2026 · Padhne me ~5 minute

Do regime hain aur dono ka ganit alag hai. Sirf slab dekhne se jawab nahi milta — deduction se sab badal jaata hai. Neeche apni aay aur deduction daaliye, dono ka tax saath me dikh jayega.

Dono me asli farak kya hai

Ek line me — naye regime me slab udaar hai par chhoot nahi milti; purane me slab sakht hai par chhoot milti hai.

Purana regime me ye sab claim ho sakta hai: 80C (EPF, PPF, bima, bacchon ki fees, home loan ka mool dhan), HRA, home loan ka byaj, 80D (health insurance), 80E (education loan ka byaj), NPS, aur kai aur.

Naye regime me inme se lagbhag kuch nahi milta. Badle me slab kam hain aur standard deduction milta hai.

Isliye faisla ek hi baat par tika hai — aap sach me kitni deduction claim karte hain? Jitna zyada, utna purana regime achha. Jitna kam, utna naya.

Aur yahi sabse badi galti bhi hai — log deduction "kar sakte hain" maankar purana regime chun lete hain, phir saal bhar wo nivesh karte hi nahi. Aise me purana regime ulta mehnga pad jaata hai.

Mota anuman — kis par kya

Har case alag hota hai, par ek aam tasveer ye hai:

Ek aam paimana: agar aapki kul deduction (80C + HRA + home loan byaj + 80D milakar) kaafi badi rakam banti hai, to purana regime jeetne lagta hai. Par exact number aapki aay par nirbhar karta hai — isi liye calculator me dono daal kar dekhna hi sahi tarika hai, tukka nahi.

Yahan ek imaandaar baat — slab aur chhoot har budget me badal sakti hain. Isliye ye tulna har saal dobara kar lena chahiye. Jo pichhle saal fit baitha tha, is saal wahi rahega ye zaroori nahi.

Faisla kab lena hai aur kya badal sakta hai

Ye sawaal do baar saamne aata hai, aur dono ka matlab alag hai:

  • Saal ke shuru me, employer ko batate waqt — isse sirf aapki TDS katauti tay hoti hai. Ye ek anuman hai, aakhri faisla nahi.
  • ITR bharte waqt — asli faisla yahi hai.
Salary wale log aam taur par ITR bharte waqt regime badal sakte hain, chahe saal ke shuru me kuch aur bataya ho. Zyada TDS kata hoga to refund aayega, kam kata hoga to antar bharna padega. Isliye shuru me galat chunne par ghabraane ki zaroorat nahi.

Karobar ya professional aay wale logon ke liye niyam sakht hai — unhe baar-baar badalne ki ijaazat nahi hoti. Aisi sthiti me pehli baar hi soch-samajh kar chunna zaroori hai, aur CA se ek baar salah le lena behtar hai.

Sirf tax dekhkar faisla mat kijiye

Ye baat kam log kehte hain, par zaroori hai — purana regime sirf tax nahi bachata, wo nivesh ki aadat bhi banata hai.

80C ki wajah se log PPF, EPF, bima aur SIP me paisa daalte hain. Naye regime me wo majboori nahi rehti — aur bahut se log tab kuch bachate hi nahi. Tax to bach jaata hai, par bhavishya ka paisa nahi banta.

Agar aap naya regime chun rahe hain, to ek kaam zaroor kariye — jo paisa 80C me jaata tha, wahi rakam apne aap nivesh me daaliye, chahe tax ka faayda na mile. Wo aadat tax se zyada keemti hai.

Aur ye bhi dekhiye:

  • Health insurance tax ke liye nahi, apni surakshaa ke liye lijiye. Naye regime me 80D nahi milta, par bima ki zaroorat khatam nahi hoti.
  • Term insurance bhi wahi baat — parivaar ki surakshaa pehle, tax baad me.
  • Home loan sirf tax bachane ke liye mat lijiye. Byaj hamesha tax ki chhoot se zyada hota hai.

Poori jaankari Tax Regime Guide aur Income Tax Guide me hai. Hum CA nahi hain — bade faisle se pehle CA ya incometax.gov.in se confirm kariye.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kaunsa regime behtar hai?

Ye is par nirbhar hai ki aap sach me kitni deduction claim karte hain. HRA, home loan byaj aur 80C ka jod bada ho to purana, warna aam taur par naya.

Regime baad me badal sakte hain?

Salary wale aam taur par ITR bharte waqt badal sakte hain — saal ke shuru ka jawab sirf TDS ke liye hota hai. Karobari aay par niyam sakht hai.

Naye regime me kya-kya nahi milta?

80C, HRA, home loan ka byaj, 80D, 80E jaisi zyadatar chhootein nahi milti. Badle me slab kam hain aur standard deduction milta hai.

Har saal ye tulna karni chahiye?

Haan. Slab aur chhoot har budget me badal sakti hain, aur aapki apni deduction bhi. ITR se pehle ek baar dobara dekh lena sahi aadat hai.
