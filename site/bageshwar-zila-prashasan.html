Bageshwar: Saar aur Zile ki Pehchan

Bageshwar Uttarakhand ke Kumaon mandal ka ek pahadi zila hai jiska mukhyalaya Bageshwar nagar hai, jo Saryu aur Gomti nadi ke sangam par basa hai. Yeh zila 15 September 1997 ko Almora se alag karke banaya gaya aur 2011 ki janganana me iski aabadi 2,59,898 thi.

Zile ki Pehchan: Ek Nazar Mein

Bageshwar ko Kumaon ka ek pramukh teerth mana jata hai, kyunki yahan Bagnath Mahadev mandir aur Saryu-Gomti ka sangam hai. Kumaon me isko "Vyaghreshwar" naam se bhi jaana jata hai.

Bageshwar Uttarakhand ke 13 zilon me se ek hai aur Kumaon mandal ke 6 zilon (Almora, Bageshwar, Champawat, Nainital, Pithoragarh, Udham Singh Nagar) me aata hai. Is zile ki sabse badi pehchan teen cheezon se hai:

  • Dharmik pehchan: Bagnath (Vyaghreshwar) Shiv mandir, Baijnath ka Katyuri-kaleen mandir samooh, aur Saryu-Gomti sangam.
  • Prakritik pehchan: Pindari, Kafni aur Sundardhunga glacier ka base, jinki trek Bageshwar zile se hi shuru hoti hai. Kausani se dikhne wala Panchachuli, Nanda Devi aur Trishul ka Himalayan panorama bhi isi zile ke Garur tehsil me hai.
  • Aitihasik pehchan: 1921 ka Kuli-Begar Andolan, jisme Uttarayani mele ke din hazaaron logon ne Saryu-Gomti sangam par begar ke register bahaye. Isi se Uttarakhand me sabse pehle bina khoon-kharabe ek samajik kranti hui.

Bageshwar Naam Kaise Pada: Naam ka Itihas

"Bageshwar" shabd "Vyaghreshwar" se bana hai. Maanyata hai ki Maharshi Markandeya ke tap ko dekhne ke liye Bhagwan Shiv ne baagh (Vyaghra) ka roop liya aur Parvati ji gaay ban gayin. Isi se yeh sthan "Vyaghreshwar" aur phir "Bagnath" aur "Bageshwar" kehlaya.

Is naam ke peeche ki lok-katha Kumaon me bahut prasiddh hai:

  • Saryu nadi ke tat par Markandeya Rishi tap kar rahe the. Lok-katha ke anusar Saryu ka pravah ek badi shila se ruk gaya tha.
  • Rishi ka tap bhang na ho, isliye Bhagwan Shiv ne baagh ka roop liya aur Parvati ji ne gaay ka. Gaay ko dekhkar baagh ne uspar jhapta marne ka nataak kiya.
  • Gaay ki chikh sunkar Rishi ka dhyan toota, aur ve ruk gaye. Saryu ka pravah phir se behne laga.
  • Shiv-Parvati ne apne asli roop me darshan diye. Us sthan par Bagnath mahadev ki sthapna hui, aur nagar ka naam Vyaghreshwar (Bageshwar) pada.
Bageshwar naam kaise pada?
Shiv ke baagh roop se, jise Vyaghreshwar kaha gaya.
Bageshwar ka ancient naam kya tha?
Vyaghreshwar, aur lokbhasha me Bagnath.
Bageshwar ka mandir kisne banwaya?
Lok-parampara me Chand raja Lakshmi Chand ko Bagnath mandir ke vartaman swaroop se joda jata hai (16th century ke aaspaas).

Bageshwar Zila Kab aur Kaise Bana (Mool Zila aur Adhisoochna)

Bageshwar zila Almora zile se alag hokar 15 September 1997 ko Uttar Pradesh ke tatkaleen Mukhyamantri Mayawati ke kaarykaal me bana. 9 November 2000 ko Uttarakhand rajya banne par yeh nayi rajya ke 13 zilon me shaamil hua.

Zile ke banne ki kahani ek lambe andolan se judi hai. 1985 ke aaspaas se local logon aur rajnaitik dalon ne Bageshwar ko alag zila banane ki maang uthai. Karan tha ki Almora mukhyalaya door tha aur Garur, Kapkot jaise ilakon ke log tehsil ke kaam ke liye bahut dino ki yatra karte the.

Bageshwar: Collectorate aur Rajasv Prashasan

Zila Mukhyalay, Vikas Bhawan aur Collectorate Kahan Hai

Bageshwar nagar hi zila mukhyalaya hai. Yahan Collectorate (DM Karyalay), Vikas Bhawan, Zila Panchayat, Treasury aur zile ke zyadatar vibhagon ke karyalay milte hain. Sarkari kaam ke liye zile ke logon ko isi nagar me aana padta hai.

Patwari Chowki: Revenue Police aur Regular Police ka Kshetra

Uttarakhand ke pahadi ilakon me British kaal se "Revenue Police" vyavastha chali aa rahi hai, jisme Patwari aur Kanungo ko police ka bhi adhikar hota hai. Bageshwar ke bahut se gramin ilake isi vyavastha me aate hain, jabki Bageshwar, Garur, Kapkot jaise bade qasbe regular police thane ke adhin hain.

Is vyavastha ko samajhne ke liye:

  • Revenue police ilaka: Gaon me chori, jhagde ya durghatna ki sabse pehli report Patwari ko ki jaati thi. Patwari FIR darj karne ya jaanch karne ka adhikar rakhta tha.
  • Regular police ilaka: Nagar aur kasbe jahan thana ya police chowki hai, wahan Uttarakhand Police seedhe karyawahi karti hai.
  • Badlav: Uttarakhand High Court ne revenue police vyavastha ko khatm karne ki disha me nirdesh diye hain aur rajya sarkar dheere-dheere kuch ilakon ko regular police me la rahi hai. Aapka gaon abhi kis vyavastha me hai, yeh jaanne ke liye apne tehsil/thane se confirm karein (kyunki yeh list badalti rehti hai).
Revenue police kya hoti hai?
Patwari/Kanungo ko jo police jaise adhikar diye gaye hain, Uttarakhand ke pahadi ilakon ki khaas vyavastha.
Gaon me chori ho to kahan report karein?
Apne ilake ke thane ya Patwari se, aur emergency me 112 par.

Bageshwar se Shuru Hue Pramukh Rajnaitik Andolan

Bageshwar ka sabse bada rajnaitik andolan 1921 ka Kuli-Begar Andolan hai. Uttarayani mele ke din Badri Datt Pandey ke netritva me hazaaron logon ne Saryu-Gomti sangam par Kuli-Begar ke register bahaye aur is amanviya pratha ko khatm karwaya.

Kuli-Begar Andolan ki mukhya baatein:

  • Begar kya tha? British aur sthaniya zamindaron ke liye gaon walon se bina mazdoori ke saaman dhona, tent lagana aur seva karwana.
  • Kab hua? 14 January 1921, Makar Sankranti (Uttarayani) ke mele par.
  • Kahan hua? Bageshwar me Saryu aur Gomti ke sangam par.
  • Netritva: Badri Datt Pandey, jinhe is andolan ke baad "Kumaon Kesari" ki upadhi mili.
  • Mahatva: Isse Uttarakhand me bina hinsa ke ek bada samajik parivartan hua. Gandhi ji ne ise "raktheen kranti" kaha tha (is kathan ka sandarbh).

Isi parampara ke chalte Bageshwar ka Uttarayani mela aaj bhi poore Kumaon me sabse prasiddh melon me ginta jata hai.

Fauj ki Regiment (Garhwal Rifles / Kumaon Regiment / Scouts), ITBP aur SSB Camp

Bageshwar ki sainya parampara bahut mazboot hai. Zile ke bahut se parivaron me se kam se kam ek sadasya Indian Army, ITBP ya anya ardhsainik bal me hai. Kumaon se jude zile hone ke karan yahan se Kumaon Regiment ki bharti ki parampara jaani jati hai.

  • Kumaon Regiment: Kumaon ke yuvaon ki sabse bhari bharti isi regiment me hoti rahi hai. Regiment ka centre Ranikhet (Almora zila) me hai.
  • ITBP / SSB: Zile ke sheersh upari ilake Pithoragarh aur Chamoli seemaon ke kareeb hain. Bageshwar zile ki apni international seema (Tibet ya Nepal) nahi hai, isliye yahan border chowkiyan nahi hain.
  • Agniveer bharti: Bageshwar ke yuva Almora ARO ke tahat bharti rally me bhaag lete hain (Bageshwar: Army Recruitment aur Physical Training me visteer).

Bageshwar: Nyayalay aur Kanooni Vyavastha

Bageshwar me Zila evam Satra Nyayalay (District & Sessions Court) Bageshwar nagar me hai. Zile ke nagrikon ko registry, mukadme aur kanooni sahayata ke liye isi kendra se jude nyayik karyalayon par jaana padta hai.

Zila Nyayalay, Family Court aur Muft Kanooni Sahayata

Zila Nyayalay Bageshwar nagar me sthit hai aur Uttarakhand High Court ki suchi me Bageshwar ko MACT/District Judge ke kendra ke roop me darj kiya gaya hai. Kanooni sahayata ke liye Zila Vidhik Seva Pradhikaran (DLSA) bhi sthit hai.

  • Zila Nyayalay: Bageshwar (Zila Nyayadhish ka kendra).
  • DLSA (District Legal Services Authority): Gareeb aur zarooratmand logon ko muft kanooni salah aur vakil dilane wala sanstha. Muft salah ke liye Zila Nyayalay parisar ke DLSA karyalay me sampark karein.
  • Lok Adalat: Chhote vivaad aur sulah ke maamle yahan nipte jaate hain.

Bageshwar mein Sub-Registrar (Zameen Registry) Daftar

Zameen ki registry Sub-Registrar karyalay me hoti hai. Bageshwar zile me registry kaam tehsil star par hota hai, jisme Bageshwar mukhya kendra hai. Registry ke liye circle rate, stamp duty aur dastavez ki jaankari Bageshwar: Property Registry, Stamp Duty aur Circle Rate me di gayi hai.

Registry ke liye aam taur par ye dastavez chahiye hote hain: - Khatauni ki nakal (Bhulekh se nikali gayi) - Seller aur buyer ka Aadhaar aur PAN - Stamp duty ki rasid - Do gawah ka pehchan patra

Taaza sub-registrar offices ki list ke liye zila portal ya Stamp & Registration vibhag se confirm karein.

Bageshwar me district court kahan hai?
Bageshwar nagar me.
Muft kanooni salah kahan milegi?
Zila Vidhik Seva Pradhikaran (DLSA).

Bageshwar: Aapda Prabandhan aur Emergency Vyavastha

Bageshwar bhookamp zone aur pahadi dhalan ke karan aapda-prone zila hai. Zila Aapda Prabandhan Pradhikaran (DDMA) ka control room 24 ghante kaam karta hai aur toll-free number 1077 se aapda ki soochna di ja sakti hai.

Aapda Control Room (DDMA) aur Toll-Free Helpline

Zila Aapda Prabandhan Pradhikaran ka adhyaksh Zila Adhikari (DM) hota hai. Aapatkaal me 1077 (zila aapda control room), 112 (aapatkaleen sahayata) aur 108 (ambulance) numbers kaam karte hain.

SDRF / NDRF ke Thikane, Baadh Aashray aur Emergency Helipad

Uttarakhand me aapda rahat ke liye SDRF (State Disaster Response Force) aur NDRF (National Disaster Response Force) tainat hoti hain. Barsat ke mausam me bhooskhalan, baadal fatne (cloudburst) aur sadak band hone ki ghatnayein Bageshwar ke pahadi raaston par aam hain.

Pahadi zilon me barsat ke din (June se September) sabse zyada khatarnak hote hain. Yatra ya safar karne se pehle: 1. Zile ke aapda control room ya weather update dekh lein. 2. Pindari, Kafni aur Sundardhunga trek par barsat me jaane se bachein. 3. Sadak band hone par raste me rukne ke liye paas ke gaon ya Forest Rest House ka pata rakhein.

Shelter aur helipad ki exact sthiti zila aapda prabandhan vibhag se pata karein.

Bageshwar me aapda ka number kya hai?
1077 (zila control room) aur 112.
Baarish me Pindari trek safe hai?
Nahi, monsoon me bhooskhalan aur nadi ke uphaan ke karan jaana risky hai.

Bageshwar: Gaadi Registration (RTO) aur Parivahan Code

Bageshwar ke vahanon ka registration code UK-02 hai. Driving licence, vahan registration aur permit jaisi sevayein ARTO/Transport karyalay se milti hain.

Zile ka RTO/ARTO Code, Daftar aur Licence Sevayein

Bageshwar zile ka RTO code UK-02 maana jata hai. Is code se aapki gaadi ki number-plate se hi pata chal jata hai ki gaadi Bageshwar me register hui hai.

ARTO office ki mukhya sevayein: - Learner licence aur permanent driving licence - Naye vahan ka registration (RC) - Vahan ka transfer aur fitness - Commercial vahan ke permit (taxi, tempo, bus)

Online sevayein Parivahan portal par uplabdh hain. Office ke kaam ke ghante aur exact pata ke liye zila portal ya Transport vibhag ki website dekhein.

Pahadi Driving ke Niyam aur Green Cess / Eco-Tax

Pahadi raaston par gaadi chalane ke liye alag "hill endorsement" ki zaroorat aam taur par nahi hoti, par pahadi mod, dhalan aur barsat me anubhav ki kami khatarnak ho sakti hai. Bahari rajya ki gaadiyon par kuch jagah green cess lagta hai, jiske niyam badalte rehte hain.

Bageshwar ke pahadi raaston par safe driving ke niyam: - Chadhai par chadhne wali gaadi ko pehle raasta dena pahadi shishtachar hai. - Mod par horn bajana ya light dikhana mahatvapurn hai. - Barsat me raat ki drive se bachein. - Chaudi sadak par kinare se na chalein, kyunki khaai bahut gehri ho sakti hai.

Bageshwar ka RTO code kya hai?
UK-02.
Bageshwar me driving licence kahan banta hai?
ARTO karyalay, Bageshwar.
