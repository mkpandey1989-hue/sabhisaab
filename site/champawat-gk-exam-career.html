Seedha jawab: Champawat aur Pithoragarh zile ke yuvaon ke liye Agniveer bharti ARO Pithoragarh karta hai, aur rally Banbasa Military Station (Champawat zila) me hota hai. Pehle online Common Entrance Exam (CEE) hota hai, phir CEE-shortlisted yuvaon ke liye rally. Registration sirf official site joinindianarmy.nic.in par hoti hai.

Taaza suchna (Nov 2026): Ek local khabar-portal ke anusar Banbasa me Agniveer rally ki tithi 23 se 29 November 2026 batayi gayi, jisme lagbhag 4,492 CEE-qualified yuvaon ke shamil hone ki baat thi. Yeh khabar sarkari notification nahi hai. Admit card aur ARO Pithoragarh ke official notice se taareekh aur samay confirm karein.

Kya mat karein (bahut zaroori): - Bharti bilkul muft hai. Sena ki bharti me kisi ko paise dene ki zaroorat nahi. Kisi dalal ya “bharti karwane wale” ko paise na dein. Aisi shikayat police (112) ya sena ke bharti karyalay ko karein. - Photoshop ya nakli dastavez na banwayein. Pakde jane par jeevan bhar ki rok lag sakti hai. - Steroid ya anjaan supplement na lein. Medical me reject hone ka khatra aur swasthya nuksan hai.

Physical taiyari ki aam, surakshit salah (kisi sarkari mapdand ka dawa nahi): 1. Garam karke shuru karein (warm-up), aur dheere-dheere doori badhayein. 2. Pahadi sadak par daudne me savdhani: Subah ke andhere me vahan wali sadak par akele na daudein. Sambhav ho to maidan ya kam traffic wale raaste par daudein (Champawat: Kaise pahunchein, kahan rukein). 3. Paani aur poshan: Roz ka khana santulit rakhein. Pahadi anaj (mandua, bhatt, gahat; Champawat: Pahadi khana aur mithai) poshan ka achha strot ho sakte hain. 4. Aaram: Tezi se mehnat ke baad neend aur aaram zaroori hai. 5. Mapdand: Height, chest, daud aur anya shartein har saal ke official notification me dekhein.

Champawat: Sabse Zyada Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQ)

Champawat ke yuvaon ke liye Agniveer bharti kaun karta hai?
ARO Pithoragarh, jo Pithoragarh aur Champawat zilon ke liye rally karta hai. Rally Banbasa Military Station (Champawat zila) me hota hai.
Agniveer bharti ke liye registration kahan hota hai?
Sirf official site joinindianarmy.nic.in par. Pehle online CEE hota hai, phir shortlisted yuvaon ke liye rally.
Banbasa Agniveer rally 2026 ki tithi kya batayi gayi?
Ek local khabar-portal ke anusar 23 se 29 November 2026. Yeh sarkari notification nahi hai, isliye admit card aur ARO Pithoragarh ke notice se confirm karein.
Kya sena ki bharti me paise dene padte hain?
Nahi. Bharti muft hai. Kisi dalal ko paise na dein, aur shikayat police (112) ko karein.
UKSSSC ki official website kaun si hai?
sssc.uk.gov.in. Purane paper, syllabus aur notification wahin se dekhein.
“Mukhyamantri Abhyuday Yojana” kya Uttarakhand ki hai?
Nahi. Yeh Uttar Pradesh ki yojana hai. Uttarakhand ki apni “Mukhyamantri Yuva Bhavishya Nirman Yojana” free online coaching deti hai.
“Sewayojan” portal kya Uttarakhand ka hai?
Nahi. Sewayojan Uttar Pradesh ka portal hai. Uttarakhand ka rojgar portal rojgar.uk.gov.in hai.
Rojgar karyalay me panjikaran kaise karein?
eservices.uk.gov.in par khud, CSC se ya Rojgar Karyalay se. Sthayi niwas praman-patra jama karna padta hai.
Champawat ka Zila Rojgar Karyalay kahan hai?
Champawat, PIN 262523 (Rojgar vibhag ki suchi ke anusar).
Champawat me kitne ITI hain?
3: Tanakpur, Champawat aur Khetikhan (IndiaStat). ITI Champawat (1984) me Wireman aur Sewing Technology, 20-20 seat.
Champawat me Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya kahan hai?
Latoli (Latauli), Champawat. CBSE affiliation number 3540007, school code 84059.
Kya Champawat me Kendriya Vidyalaya hai?
Zila vikas suchi me Lohaghat me KV ka nirman chalu bataya gaya hai, aur ek school-suchi me kai KV-naam darj hain (KV SSB Champawat, KV Lohaghat, KV No. 2 Banbasa). Ek-ek ki pushti school ki official site se karein.
Scholarship ka aavedan kahan karein?
National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) aur rajya ke scholarship portal par. 2026-27 ki zyadatar antim tithi 30 September 2026 thi (ek guide ke anusar), jo beet chuki hai.
