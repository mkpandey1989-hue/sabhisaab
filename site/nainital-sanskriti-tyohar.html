Nainital ke log kaun hain, kaun si boli bolte hain, unke ghar kaise hote hain, kya pehente hain, aur unke tyohar, geet aur mele kaise hain. Yeh hissa Kumaoni sanskriti ki asli tasveer hai, jisme Nainital ki apni pehchan bhi hai: pahadi aur maidani, Kumaoni, Hindi, Urdu aur Punjabi, sab ek hi zile me.

Ek zaroori baat: Uttarakhand ki sanskriti ek jaisi nahi hai. Garhwal aur Kumaon ki kai parampara alag hain.

Nainital: Aabadi, Samuday aur Anusuchit Janjati

Census ke Aankde: Aabadi, Ling Anupat, Saksharta aur Gaon-Shahar

Census 2011 ke anusar Nainital zile ki aabadi 9,54,605 thi, sex ratio 934 mahilayein prati 1,000 purush, aur saksharta dar lagbhag 83.9%. Pichhle dashak me aabadi 25.1% badhi, jo Uttarakhand ke tezi se badhte zilon me se ek hai.

Aabadi ka dohra chehra: Nainital zile me aabadi ka bada hissa Haldwani, Kathgodam, Lalkuan, Ramnagar aur Kaladhungi jaise maidani aur shahri ilakon me rehta hai, jabki pahadi gaon me aabadi kam hui hai ya ghat rahi hai. Pahadi gaon se mard roti-rozgar ke liye shehron aur bahar jate hain, jiske kaaran kai gaon me mahilayein, bujurg aur bachche hi rehte hain (dekhein Aabaad aur Gair-Aabaad Gaon). Isiliye pahadi gaon ki asli sex ratio shahri ilakon se kaafi alag ho sakti hai, jabki zile ka kul aankda 934 hai.

Note: Ye Census 2011 ke aankde hain. Agli Census ke naye aankde aane tak ye hi sabse taaza sarkari aankde hain. Pichhle 15 saalon me aabadi kaafi badli hai, isliye aaj ka asli aankda alag ho sakta hai.

Nainital: Samaj ki Banawat, Upnaam aur Gotra

Nainital ke Mool Upnaam (Surname)

Kumaon me surname se aksar parivar ka ilaaka aur parampara ka andaaza lagta hai. Brahmin, Rajput aur Shilpkar (hunar wale) samudayon ke kai jaane-pehchane surname Nainital me bhi milte hain. Surname se kisi vyakti ke gun ya yogyata ka andaaza lagana galat hai.

Ye soochi sirf jaankari ke liye hai. Sabhi surname har ilake me nahi milte aur koi bhi soochi poori nahi hoti.

Samajik savdhani: Bharat ke Samvidhan ke anusar jaati ke aadhar par chhua-chhoot ya bhedbhav kanooni roop se ghair-kanooni hai (Anuchhed 17 aur SC/ST Atrocities Act). Nainital ke samaj me bhi samay ke saath ye bhedbhav kam hua hai, lekin gaon-gaon me chunauti abhi baki hai.

Mool Gaon (Mool Niwas / Thaat) ki Pehchan aur Vivah ki Paramparayein

Pahadi parivaar apne “Thaat” ya mool gaon se bahut jude rehte hain, jahan unke kuldevta aur purvaj ka sthan hota hai. Shaadi me aam taur par ek hi gotra (sagotra) me vivah nahi kiya jata, aur kai parivaar apne gaon ke aas-paas ya kisi doosri ghati me rishta dhoondte hain.

  • Thaat / Mool niwas: Parivar ka purana gaon. Palayan ke baad bhi log shaadi, jagar aur kuldevta ki pooja ke liye wahin lautte hain.
  • Kuldevta: Har parivaar ka apna devta hota hai, jiski pooja ghar ke naye tyohar aur shubh kaam ke samay ki jaati hai (Nainital: Puranon ka Bhugol: Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand aur Nainital: Pragaitihasik Kaal, Shila-Chitra aur Shailashray).
  • Sagotra vivah: Paramparik Hindu niyam ke anusar sagotra vivah varjit maana jata hai.
  • Naye samay me: Shehri ilakon me jaati aur kshetra ki seema se bahar shaadiyan badh rahi hain. Ye parivaar-parivaar par nirbhar karta hai.

Nainital: Bhasha, Up-boliyan aur Roz ke Shabd

Nainital ki Boli

Nainital zile me Kumaoni sabse zyada boli jane wali bhasha hai (~48.5%), uske baad Hindi (~38.7%). Haldwani-Lalkuan jaise Terai shehron me Urdu aur Punjabi bhi bahut boli jati hain. Bhasha-vigyaniyon ke anusar Kumaoni ki kai upa-boliyan hain, aur Nainital ke aas-paas Madhya Kumaoni ka prabhav zyada maana jata hai.

  • Kumaoni: Madhya Himalaya ki ek pramukh bhasha, jiski alag-alag ilakon me alag boli hoti hai. Kumaoni ki aam gini jane wali upa-boliyon me Kumaiya, Soryali, Askoti, Johari, Danpuriya, Gangoli, Pachhai, Chaugarkhyali, Khasparjiya aur Rau-Chaubhainsi shamil hain.
  • Nainital kshetra: Zile ke alag-alag hisson me Madhya Kumaoni (Majh Kumaiya) aur uske paas ki boli ka prayog hota hai. Gaon-gaon ke beech shabd aur uchcharan me thoda-thoda farak mil jata hai.
  • Hindi aur Urdu: Shahri, vyapaar aur sarkari kaam ki bhasha. Haldwani ke bazaar aur Terai kshetra me Urdu aur Punjabi ka asar zyada hai, jiske peeche vibhajan ke baad basane aur vyapar ka itihas hai.
  • Buksa: Ramnagar block ke kuch gaon me Buksa boli jati hai.
  • Garhwali, Nepali, Bhojpuri: Chhote samudayon ki bhashayein.

Kumaoni ki sthiti: Kumaoni ko Uttarakhand sarkar ne bhasha ke roop me protsahan dene ki baat ki hai, par aaj bhi ye 8vi Anusuchi ki bhasha nahi hai. Naye bachchon me iska prayog kam ho raha hai, isliye gaon-gaon me Kumaoni geet, kahani aur shabdkosh banane ke prayas ho rahe hain.

Nainital: Vernacular Pahadi Architecture (Pahadi Ghar)

Koti Banal Shaili, Bakhli, Pathal ki Chhat aur Devdar ki Nakkashi (Kholi)

Koti Banal shaili Garhwal (Uttarkashi aur Yamuna ghati) ki jaani-maani bhukamp-rodhi imarat parampara hai; Nainital me nahi. Kumaon ke Nainital kshetra me pathar aur lakdi ke ghar, pathal (slate) ki chhat aur lakdi par naqqashi wale darwaze (Kholi) milte hain.

  • Koti Banal: Lakdi ki patti aur pathar ko jodkar banne wali purani shaili, jo Garhwal ke Uttarkashi aur aas-paas ke kshetra se judi mani jati hai. Ise Nainital ki shaili ke roop me na batayein.
  • Pathal ki chhat: Pahadi dhalaan wali chhat par pathar ke patle slate laga kar chhat banai jati thi. Ye barish aur barf se ghar ko bachate the. Aaj teen ki (tin) chaadar aur RCC ka chalan badh gaya hai.
  • Bakhli: Pahadi gaon me ek lambi pankti me jude ghar jinka ek hi lamba aangan hota hai. Kumaon ke kai gaon me ye shaili dikhti hai.
  • Kholi: Ghar ka mukhya pravesh dwar, jis par lakdi ki naqqashi hoti hai. Kumaon me “Kholi ka Ganesh” ki parampara mashhoor hai, jahan darwaze ke upar Ganesh ki murti ya chitra banaye jate hain.
  • Lakdi aur mitti: Purane gharon me deodar, chir aur anya lakdi ke saath mitti aur gobar ka lepan hota tha, jisse ghar garmi me thanda aur sardi me garm rehta tha.

Nainital shehar ki alag pehchan: Nainital me angrezi kaal ki imaratein (church, Raj Bhawan, clubs) pahadi ghar se alag hain. Unki jaankari Nainital: Pramukh Mandir, Dham, Gurudwara aur Math aur Nainital: Isht Devta, Gram Devta aur Jagar mein Devta Avtaran me hai.

Pahadi Ghar ki Banawat: Goth, Chaja, Tibari, Chakh aur Khalian

Pahadi ghar me neeche ke tale par pashu (goth) rakhe jate the, jinki garmi se upar ka tala garm rehta tha. Upar ke tale par rehne ke kamre aur lakdi ki naqqashi wali baithak (Tibari) hoti thi, aur saamne pathar se bana aangan hota tha.

  • Goth / Obra: Neeche ka tala jahan gaay-bhains-bakri rakhe jate the. Pashu ki garmi se upar ke kamre sardiyon me garm rehte the.
  • Tibari: Lakdi ki teen mehrabon wali naqqashidaar baithak ya baramda, jahan parivaar baithta hai (Kumaon me ye parampara aam hai).
  • Chaja / Chakh: Ghar ke upar ki chhajja, baramda ya baithne ka sthan. Naam aur banawat ilaaka-wise badal jati hai.
  • Aangan (Khlian / Khalihan): Pathar se bana saamne ka aangan jahan anaj sukhaya jata hai, tyohar manaye jate hain aur bachche khelte hain.
  • Rasoi: Chulha aur dhuaan wali chhat (Nainital: Pahadi Kitchen Architecture & Utensils).

Aaj ke ghar: Kai purane pahadi ghar khaali ho chuke hain. Kuch jagah unhe homestay me badla ja raha hai, jisse parampara bhi bachti hai aur parivar ko aay bhi hoti hai (Nainital: Khaali Gaon aur Virasat ki Sair aur Nainital: Palayan Vapasi).

Bhukamp aur pahadi ghar: Pahadi dhalaan par ghar banate samay mitti, dhalaan aur bhukamp ki surakshit shaili ki jaanch zaroori hai (Nainital: Bhoo-vigyan, Mitti ke Prakar aur Bhookamp Zone aur Nainital: Building Bylaws, Naksha Manzoori aur Bhookamp Niyam).

Nainital: Paramparik Pehnawa aur Kapde

Mahilaon ka Pehnava: Rangwali Pichhora, Ghaghri-Angra, Lava/Gati, Choli aur Dhatu

Kumaoni mahilaon ka sabse khaas shubh vastra Rangwali Pichhora hai: peele-narangi rang ka dupatta jis par laal bindiyan aur swastik jaise shubh chinh bante hain. Ye shaadi, naamkaran aur pooja jaise shubh avsar par pehna jata hai.

  • Rangwali Pichhora: Peele (ya kesariya) rang ka dupatta, jis par laal bindiyan, swastik, shankh aur anya shubh chinh chhape ya haath se bane hote hain. Sone-chandi ki jhalar ya gota kinare par lagti hai. Kumaon me ise shubh aur saubhagya ka pratik maana jata hai.
  • Ghaghri-Angra: Purane samay ki mahilaon ki paramparik poshak: lamba ghaghra aur angra (choli). Aaj ye roz pehenne me kam, tyohar aur lok-nrityon me zyada dikhta hai.
  • Lava / Gati: Sardi ke liye oon ki chadar jo kamar par suga ya patke se baandhi jati hai. Pahadi dhalaan par kaam karne me bhi aaraam deti hai.
  • Choli aur Dhatu: Choli upar ka vastra, aur Dhatu (sir ka dupatta) paramparik pehnawa hai.
  • Aaj kal: Roz ki zindagi me saree, suit aur jeans ka chalan hai. Pichhora aur ghaghri-angra mukhya roop se tyohar aur shaadi ke liye bache hain.

Men’s Wear: Mirjai, Sadri, Churidar Pajama, Woolen Coat & Pahadi Topi

Kumaoni purush paramparik roop se kurta-churidar, mirjai, sadri (waistcoat), oon ka coat aur pahadi topi pehente the. Sardi me oon ke kapde aur topi sir aur kaan ko thand se bachate hain.

  • Mirjai aur Sadri: Kurte ke upar pehne jane wali sardi ki jacket aur waistcoat.
  • Churidar pajama: Pahadi chadhai me chalne ke liye pairon ke paas tang pajama.
  • Oon ka coat aur shawl: Sardiyon me oon ki garmi ke liye.
  • Pahadi topi: Sir ke liye topi, jise aaj bhi tyohar aur sarkari avsar par pehna jata hai. Uttarakhand me iska ek alag ahmiyat hai.

Oon ke vyapar ki parampara: Kumaon ke Bhotia samuday ka oon ka vyapar purana hai. Nainital ke paas Bhowali, Haldwani aur Almora ke bazaar me oon aur shawl milte hain. Asli pahadi oon aur mashin ke bane kapde me farak karne ke liye bharosemand dukan se hi kharidein.

Nainital: Anushthan ki Lok-Kala: Aipan, Jyunti aur Lakdi ka Kaam

Aipan ke Namoone, Geru-Biswar ki Vidhi aur Jyunti/Matrika Chitra

Aipan Kumaon ki paramparik rangoli jaisi lok-kala hai, jo pooja aur tyohar par zameen, deewar ya chowki par banai jati hai. Isme lal-bhuri geru mitti ki ek parat ke upar chaawal ke ghole (biswar) se safed aakritiyan banai jati hain, jo ungliyon se khinchi jati hain.

Aipan kaise banta hai:

  • Geru (laal mitti): Pahadi laal mitti ko paani me ghol kar zameen ya chowki par lepa jata hai. Ye laal-bhuri parat aipan ka aadhar hai.
  • Biswar (chaawal ka ghol): Bhige chaawal ko pees kar ya chaawal ke aate ko paani me ghol kar safed ghol banaya jata hai.
  • Ungliyon se khinchna: Biswar ko aam taur par teen ungliyon (angootha-tarjani aur beech wali ungli) ki madad se khinch kar bel-boote aur aakritiyan banai jati hain.
  • Shubh chinh: Swastik, shankh, kamal, surya, chand aur devi-devta ke peeth.

Nainital aur Almora me aise karigar milte hain. Agar aap sikhna chahein to sthaniya mahila samooh, Kala Kendra ya Aipan workshops se sampark karein.

Aipan se jude shabd ka matlab: Aipan shabd “Alpana” ka Kumaoni roop maana jata hai. Alag ilake ke log alag naam bhi lete hain.

Nainital: Lok-Sangeet, Lok-Gaathayein aur Gayan Parampara

Jagar, Pawada/Bhadau, Khuded Geet, Basanti, Chhopati, Chaiti, Nyoli, Chanchari, Jhoda & Bair

Kumaoni lok-sangeet me Jagar (devta ko jagane ka geet), Bair aur Chhapeli (naach-geet ki jugalbandi), Nyoli (virah aur prem ke geet), Jhoda aur Chanchari (samuhik nritya-geet) aur Malushahi jaise lambe katha-geet mashhoor hain.

  • Jagar: Devta ko “jagane” ki ek ritual-gayan parampara. Jagariya (gayak) hurka (damroo jaisa vadya) aur thali ke saath devta ki gaathaayein gate hain. Golu Devta, Nanda Devi jaise devta ke jagar mashhoor hain (Nainital: Puranon ka Bhugol: Kedarkhand aur Manaskhand aur Nainital: Pragaitihasik Kaal, Shila-Chitra aur Shailashray).
  • Malushahi: Kumaon ki mashhoor prem-katha ka lambi gayan parampara.
  • Bair: Do gayakon ke beech sawal-jawab wali kavya jugalbandi, jisme chaturai aur hasya-vyang hota hai.
  • Chhapeli: Prem aur sringaar ka naach-geet, aam taur par jodi me gaya jata hai.
  • Nyoli: Virah aur prem ke bhavuk geet.
  • Jhoda aur Chanchari: Samuhik naach ke saath gaye jane wale geet (Nainital: Paramparik Lok Nritya).
  • Basanti aur Chaiti: Basant aur Chaitra mahine ke mausmi geet.
  • Khuded geet: Mayke ki yaad me shaadi-shuda mahilaon ke geet (zyadatar Garhwal me prachalit).
  • Pawada / Bhadau: Veer yoddhaon ki gaathaon wale geet (zyadatar Garhwal me prachalit).

Nainital ki Kumaoni Holi: Nainital aur Almora ki Holi apni alag pehchan rakhti hai. Isme Baithki Holi (baith kar shastriya raag me gayi jati hai, aam taur par Pausha ke maheene se shuru), Khari Holi (khade hokar gaon me naach ke saath) aur Mahila Holi (mahilaon ki Holi) hoti hai (Nainital: Mausami Tyohar).

Note: Ye sabhi parampara Kumaon aur Garhwal me alag-alag roop me milti hain.

Nainital: Paramparik Vadya Yantra aur Bajgi/Auji Samuday

Vadya Yantra: Dhol-Damau, Ransingha, Bhankora, Algoza, Hudka aur Masakbeen

Dhol aur Damau Uttarakhand ke sabse mukhya paramparik vadya hain, jo shaadi, devta ki pooja aur har tyohar me bajte hain. Inhe bajane ki paramparik zimmedari Bajgi (ya Auji) samuday ki rahi hai, jinke paas dhol ke taalon ka lok-gyan (“Dhol Sagar”) chala aa raha hai.

Dhol Sagar kya hai?
Dhol Sagar bajgi aur auji samuday ki mukhya vidya hai, jisme dhol ke kai sau taal aur har sanskar, devta aur mauke ka alag taal maana jata hai. Kai bujurg kehte hain ki isme 84 taal hain, par ye aankda alag-alag vidwan alag batate hain. Ye vidya zyadatar mukh se sikhai jati hai, isliye iske jaankar kam hote ja rahe hain.

Bajgi samuday ka mahatva: Bajgi samuday ne sadiyon se Uttarakhand ke sanskritik jeevan ko apni dhun di hai. Aaj ke samay me unko samman, sahi mehnataana aur naye avsar dene ki baat ho rahi hai. Bhedbhav ke virudh kanoon hai (Nainital: Samaj ki Banawat, Upnaam aur Gotra).

Nainital: Pahadi Life-Cycle Rituals (Sanskars) & Weddings

Namkaran, Janeu, Pahadi Wedding Customs & Funeral Traditions

Kumaoni samaj me janm se mrityu tak ke sanskar parivar aur purohit ke saath manaye jate hain. Shaadi me Dhuli Arghya (dulhe ke pair dhona), Kanyadan, Pichhora aur mangal geet (“Shakunakhar”) jaisi parampara khaas hain.

  • Namkaran: Shishu ka naam rakhne ka sanskar, aam taur par janm ke kuch din baad (aksar 11vein din) kiya jata hai. Parivaar aur purohit milkar naam tay karte hain.
  • Annaprashan aur Mundan: Pehla anaj khilane aur pehle baal utarne ke sanskar.
  • Janeu (Upanayan): Kumaoni Brahmin aur Rajput parivaron me ladkon ka janeu sanskar bada utsav hota hai.
  • Vivah (shaadi): Kumaoni shaadi ki kuch jaani-maani parampara:
  • Ganesh Puja: Shubh kaam ki shuruaat Ganesh pooja se.
  • Suwal Pathai: Shaadi se pehle ki ek ritual jo aam taur par ghar ki mahilayein karti hain; iska sahi kram aur arth parivar aur ilake ke hisaab se alag ho sakta hai.
  • Dhuli Arghya: Barat ke aane par godhuli (shaam ke samay) dulhe ke pair dhona aur uska swagat.
  • Pichhora aur mangal geet: Dulhan ko Pichhora (Nainital: Paramparik Pehnawa aur Kapde) pehna kar mahilayein Shakunakhar jaise mangal geet gaati hain.
  • Kanyadan aur Phere: Vaidik rasmein purohit ki upasthiti me.
  • Bidaai: Dulhan ki vidai.
  • Antim sanskar: Hindu paramparik roop se cremation hota hai, aur Haldwani-Kathgodam ke log aam taur par Gaula nadi ke kinare Chitrashila Ghat ka istemal karte hain (Nainital: Pavitra Sangam). Iske baad aam taur par 13 din tak sheel-parampara aur Shraddha ke karm hote hain. Muslim, Sikh aur anya dharm ke log apne-apne dharm ke anusar sanskar karte hain.

Note: Rasmon ke naam, kram aur arth parivar, jaati aur ilake ke hisaab se badal jate hain. Apne parivar ke purohit se hamesha apni parampara ke baare me pooch lein.

Nainital: Mausami Tyohar

Phool Dei, Harela, Bhitauli, Ghughutiya/Uttarayan, Khatarua, Egaas & Bagwal

Kumaon ke tyohar mausam aur kheti se jude hain: Phool Dei (basant me phoolon se swagat), Harela (hariyali ka tyohar), Bhitauli (behen-betiyon ko uphar), Ghughutiya (Makar Sankranti par kauvon ko khilana) aur Kumaoni Holi. Nainital me Nanda Devi Mela aur Naina Devi ke mele bhi bade hote hain.

Nainital ke mele aur utsav:

  • Nanda Devi Mela: Nainital me Nanda Devi ke naam par bhadrapad ke maheene me (aam taur par September me) bada mela lagta hai, jisme devi ki jhanki aur sanskritik karyakram hote hain.
  • Naina Devi Mela: Naina Devi mandir me Navratri ke avsar par bheed aur mela.
  • Kainchi Dham Mela: Har saal 15 June ko Neem Karoli Baba ke aashram me bada utsav (Nainital: Zile ki Seemayein aur Nainital: Panwar & Chand Kingdoms).
  • Garjiya Devi Mela: Kartik Purnima par Ramnagar ke paas Kosi nadi ke kinare (Nainital: Pavitra Sangam).
  • Sharadotsav: Nainital me sharad ritu me hone wala sanskritik utsav; har saal ki tareekh aur karyakram alag hote hain.

Note: Tareekhein Hindu panchang aur Sankranti ke hisaab se har saal badalti hain. Kisi bhi tyohar ke sahi din ke liye aapke parivar ke purohit ya panchang se pushti karein.

Nainital: Historic Melas, Jatras & Doli Processions

Saalana Mele, Nanda Raj Jat aur Devta Doli Yatra

Kumaon ke mashhoor mele Uttarayani (Bageshwar), Jauljibi aur Thal (Pithoragarh), Syalde-Bikhauti (Dwarahat, Almora) aur Nanda Devi Mela (Almora aur Nainital) hain. Nanda Raj Jat 12 saal me ek baar hone wali lambi paidal yatra hai, jo Chamoli zile se hoti hai.

Nanda Raj Jat: Ye Nanda Devi ki lambi paidal yatra hai, jo har 12 saal me Chamoli ke Nauti se shuru hokar Himalaya ke raste Homkund tak jati hai. Is yatra ka netritva ek chaar sing wale mendhe (Chausingha Khadu) karte hain, jiska janm aam taur par yatra ke saal ke aas-paas maana jata hai. Iski agli tareekh aur nirdharit karyakram ke liye Nanda Devi Raj Jat Samiti ya Uttarakhand sarkar ke official sandesh se hi pushti karein. Ye yatra Nainital zile se seedhi nahi guzarti.

Devta Doli yatra: Kumaon aur Garhwal ke kai gaon me devta ki doli (paalki) gaon-gaon ghumti hai, jisme dhol-damau aur bhakton ki bheed hoti hai. Har gaon ki tareekh alag hoti hai, isliye apne gaon ke mandir samiti se jaankari lein.

Mele me jaane ke liye tips:

  • Bheed me bachchon aur bujurgon ka dhyan rakhein.
  • Pahadi mele me parking aur ghar wapsi ka samay pehle se soch lein.
  • Mansoon ke samay (Jul-Sep) pahadi mele me bhuskhalan ka jokhim hota hai; safar se pehle mausam alert dekhein (Nainital: Aapda Prabandhan aur Emergency Vyavastha).
  • Mele me khane-peene ki cheezein saaf jagah se hi lein.

Note: Mele ki tareekh, sthan aur vyavastha har saal badalte hain. Ghoomne ka plan banane se pehle District Tourism Office ya mela samiti se taaza jaankari lein.
