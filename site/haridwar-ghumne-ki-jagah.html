Haridwar khud ek tirth-nagri hai, adventure-hub nahi: rafting, bungee, camping Rishikesh me hoti hai (Haridwar se lagbhag 24 km), paragliding aur skiing doosre jilon me. Is hisse mein har hisse me pehle "Haridwar me kya hai", phir "Haridwar se kahan jaya jata hai" likha gaya hai. Doori ke aankde alag-alag sources me alag milte hain.

Haridwar: Kam Jaane-Pehchane aur Chhupe Gaon

Haridwar ke Offbeat Sthal: Sureshwari Devi, Neel Dhara Subah ki Birding, Piran Kaliyar ke Gaon, Kangri aur Gateway ke Walk-in Villages

Seedha jawab: Haridwar me offbeat ka matlab bheed se door sthal hai: subah ki Neel Dhara birding, Jhilmil Jheel ke daldali ghaas ke maidan, Rajaji ke jungle ke andar Sureshwari Devi, Roorkee ki Ganga-nahar ki purani patriyan aur gramin Haridwar ke gaon. Walk-in Pahadi gaon (jaise Nag Tibba, Pantwari ke raste) Haridwar se aage Dehradun-Tehri ke kshetra me hain.

Offbeat Haridwar:

  • Neel Dhara ki subah: Bheed se pehle (6-8 baje) pakshi dekhne ke liye shant jagah.
  • Jhilmil Jheel: Barasingha (swamp deer) ke liye jani jane wali daldali ghaas bhoomi; sthaniya guide ke saath.
  • Sureshwari Devi (Rajaji): Jungle ke beech mandir; jungle niyam aur permission zaroori.
  • Kankhal ki galiyan: Purane ashram, ghat aur Ganga-kinare ki shaanti.
  • Gramin Haridwar: Gannaa belt me gur ke kolhu (sardiyon me) dekhne ka anubhav.
  • Roorkee ke Ganga nahar kinare: Subah ki sair aur nahar-engineering ke nazaare.

Gateway ke walk-in gaon (Haridwar se aage, jile wale pages me vistar se): Pantwari (Nag Tibba), Sankri (Kedarkantha/Har Ki Dun), Dodital ke raste ke gaon aur Ghangaria (Valley of Flowers).

Chetavni: Jungle aur nadi-kinare ke offbeat sthal par akele ya raat ko na jayein; hathi aur tendue ka khatra rehta hai.

Haridwar ki Heritage Walks (Mayapuri, Kankhal, Roorkee Engineering) aur Pahadi Ghost Villages ki Pehchan

Seedha jawab: Haridwar me ghost village ki samasya nahi hai (ye Pauri, Almora jaise pahadi jilon me hai). Haridwar me heritage walk ke teen vikalp hain: Mayapuri walk (Har Ki Pauri se Kushavart aur Maya Devi), Kankhal ashram walk, aur Roorkee ki British-kaleen engineering heritage walk.

Palyan (palayan) kya hai?
Pahadi jilon se rozgar, shiksha aur suvidha ke liye logon ka gaon chhodkar jana. Uttarakhand sarkar ne 2017 me Rural Development and Migration Commission banaya tha. Kuch report me 1,700 se zyada gaon "khali" ya "ghost" batayi gayi hain; sahi aankda commission ki report se dekhein. Kuch gaon me Homestay ya Eco-tourism ke zariye punarjeevan ka prayas hota hai (Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali Paryatan Swarozgar Yojana jaisi yojanayein).

Haridwar: Parvatarohan, Chattan Chadhai aur Bouldering

Mountaineering Training aur Permits (Haridwar Gateway), Rock Climbing ke Vikalp

Seedha jawab: Haridwar me koi mountaineering peak nahi hai. Mountaineering ke liye log Haridwar se Uttarkashi (NIM) ya anya base jaate hain. Chotiyon ke liye Indian Mountaineering Foundation (IMF) ki permission aur Van Vibhag ke permit chahiye. Haridwar me prasiddh natural rock-climbing crag nahi hai.

Mukhya jaankari:

  • Training: Nehru Institute of Mountaineering (NIM), Uttarkashi basic aur advanced courses ke liye jana jata hai.
  • IMF permit: Zyadatar Himalayan peaks ke liye expedition permission.
  • Van Vibhag permit: Sanrakshit kshetron (National Park, Biosphere Reserve) me pravesh aur camping ke liye.
  • Rock climbing / bouldering: Rishikesh aur Dehradun ke aas-paas kai adventure operator kritrim climbing wall aur prakritik chattanon par climbing karwate hain; sthiti aur suraksha ke liye registered operator hi chunein.
  • Porter/HAP rate: Rate alag-alag hote hain; likhit tay karein.

Chhote Sawal (Quick FAQ)

Mountaineering course kahan karein?
NIM Uttarkashi me.
Kya Haridwar me rock climbing hoti hai?
Prasiddh natural crag nahi; Rishikesh/Dehradun me vikalp.

Haridwar: Sardi ke Khel: Skiing, Snowboarding aur Barf mein Trek

Haridwar me Barf Nahi; Winter Sports ke liye Auli aur Snow Treks (Gateway)

Auli (Chamoli): Skiing ke liye prasiddh; Haridwar se sadak marg lagbhag 270-290 km. Skiing course aur equipment ke liye GMVN ya adhikrit sansthan se sampark karein. Joshimath-Auli marg aur kshetra ki sthiti (bhu-dhansav ki chinta) ke liye taaza advisory dekhein.

Snow trekking (Dec-Mar): Kedarkantha, Dayara Bugyal, Chopta-Tungnath aur kai chhote trek.

Safety: Barf-baari ke samay sadak band ho sakti hai; yatra se pehle road status dekhein.

Haridwar: Paani ke Khel: Rafting, Kayaking aur Machhli Pakadna

Rishikesh Rafting (Haridwar se 24 km): Season, Stretches, Grades, Rates aur Safety + Mahseer Angling ki Sthiti

Seedha jawab: Rafting Haridwar me nahi, Rishikesh (lagbhag 24 km) me hoti hai. Season mid-September se 30 June tak chalta hai (July-August monsoon me band). 2026-27 ka season 23-24 September 2026 se shuru hua, aur kabhi-kabhi baarish ke kaaran rafting kuch din ke liye pause ho jati hai. Mukhya stretch Brahmpuri (9 km), Shivpuri (16 km), Marine Drive (24-26 km) aur Kaudiyala (35-36 km) hain.

Rishikesh ke rafting stretches (sources ke anusar):

StretchLagbhag dooriGradeKiske liye
Brahmpuri9 kmI-IIChhote bachche/pehli baar
Shivpuri16 kmII-III+Pehli baar ke liye sabse lokpriya
Marine Drive24-26 kmIII-III+Fit vayask
Kaudiyala35-36 kmIII-IVAnubhavi, poora din

Rate (2026-27, operator/portal sources ke anusar; official dar UTDB se dekhein): lagbhag ₹1,000-1,500 (Shivpuri), ₹2,000 (Marine Drive), ₹3,000-3,500 (Kaudiyala) prati vyakti. Ye dar badal sakte hain; rafting ke liye registered operator hi chunein.

Niyam aur safety (sanklit):

  • Rafting firms Uttarakhand River Rafting Rules, 2014 ke antargat licensed hone chahiye; guide registered aur prashikshit.
  • Aam taur par minimum umra 14 saal, adhikatam 60 (operator sources); sahi niyam operator se puchhein.
  • Sharab pee kar, garbhavastha me ya band season me rafting na karein.
  • Life jacket aur helmet zaroori; guide ki baat maanein.
  • Baarish se jal-star badhne par prashasan rafting rok deta hai.

Camping ka NGT niyam: National Green Tribunal ne 2017 me Rishikesh-Kaudiyala kshetra me beach camping par se shart-sahit rok hata di: 25 chune hue sthalon par hi camping, aur nadi se 100 meter ke andar nahi. Isliye "beach camping" ke naam par bina license ke camp na lein.

Mahseer angling (machhli-shikar) ki sthiti: Golden Mahseer Uttarakhand ki rajya-machhli hai. Jungle-kshetra ki nadiyon me angling par Van Vibhag ne pehle pratibandh ka irada jataya tha, aur Ganga ke dharmik kshetra (Haridwar ghat) me machhli pakadna anuchit/niyam-viruddh mana jata hai.

Haridwar: Hawai Khel, Paragliding, MTB aur Camping

Haridwar me Aero Sports Nahi; Rishikesh ke Bungee/Flying Fox aur Doosre Jilon ka Paragliding

Seedha jawab: Haridwar me paragliding ya aero sports ka manya site nahi hai. Rishikesh me bungee jumping, flying fox aur giant swing jaisi gatividhiyan hoti hain. Paragliding ke liye Uttarakhand me Naukuchiatal (Nainital) jaise sthal jaane jaate hain. Rajaji me cycling ya MTB ke liye Van Vibhag ki anumati zaroori hai.

Mukhya jaankari:

  • Rishikesh ki adventure gatividhiyan: Bungee jumping, flying fox, giant swing; sirf licensed operator se aur suraksha niyam ke saath.
  • Paragliding: Alag jilon me; sthiti aur mausam ke hisaab se; taaza operator ke liye Uttarakhand Tourism se dekhein.
  • MTB / Cycling: Rajaji ya Shivalik ke jungle me bina permission ke cycling nahi; sirf aadhikarik routes par.
  • Camping: Ganga kinare ke liye NGT niyam (Haridwar: Paani ke Khel: Rafting, Kayaking aur Machhli Pakadna); jungle me camping ke liye Van Vibhag ki anumati.

Drone ka niyam: Mela-kshetra, mandir aur jungle ke upar drone udane ke liye Drone Rules 2021 aur sthaniya prashasan ki anumati chahiye; bina anumati udana apradh ho sakta hai.

Haridwar: Mahine-dar-Mahine Yatra Guide (January se December)

Mahine-wise Haridwar: Kya Khula Hai, Mausam, Mele, Rafting/Safari Status aur Packing Checklist

Seedha jawab: Haridwar jane ka sabse achha samay October se March hai. Ardh Kumbh 2027 ke karan January se April 2027 me bhari bheed hogi. Rafting (Rishikesh) Sept se June tak, Rajaji safari mid-November se mid-June tak chalti hai. Monsoon (July-August) me rafting band aur Kanwar Mela ka traffic hota hai.

Char Dham yatra aur Haridwar: Char Dham ke kapat aam taur par Akshaya Tritiya ke aas-paas (April-May) khulte hain aur Diwali/Bhai Dooj ke baad (Oct-Nov) band hote hain. Registration zaroori hai; taaza tithiyan sarkar ke official portal se dekhein.

Packing checklist (har mausam ke liye):

  • Sabhi mausam: ID, mobile charger, sunscreen, comfortable chappal/jute, paani ki bottle.
  • Sardi: Sweater/jacket, muffler, moze.
  • Garmi: Cotton kapde, topi, chashma, ORS.
  • Barsat: Raincoat, waterproof bag.
  • Mandir ke liye: Halke, sammanjanak kapde; chamde ki cheezein na le jayein (Chandi Devi me niyam).
  • Rajaji safari: Hare/mitti ke rang ke kapde, binocular, tez khushboo wale perfume se bachein.
