CTC aur in-hand ka farak

Offer letter me ek bada number likha hota hai — CTC, yaani Cost To Company. Naam hi bata deta hai: ye wo kharcha hai jo company par aata hai, na ki wo paisa jo aap par aata hai.

CTC ke andar teen tarah ki cheezein hoti hain:

  • Jo har mahine account me aata hai — basic, HRA, special allowance, conveyance
  • Jo katta hai — aapka PF hissa, professional tax, TDS
  • Jo aapko dikhta hi nahi — employer ka PF hissa, gratuity ka provision, group insurance

Teesri category hi sabse badi confusion hai. Ye paisa CTC me juda hai par aapke haath kabhi nahi aata (gratuity 5 saal baad, PF retirement par).

Apna number nikalne ke liye CTC to In-Hand Calculator use kar lein.

Salary slip ka har hissa

Basic Salary

Ye sabse zaroori line hai kyunki baaki sab isi par tay hota hai — PF, gratuity, HRA ki limit, sab basic ke percentage me chalte hain. Aam taur par basic CTC ka 40-50% rakha jaata hai.

Kam basic ka matlab: haath me abhi zyada paisa, par PF aur gratuity kam. Zyada basic ka matlab ulta. Dono me se koi "behtar" nahi hai — ye aapki umar aur zaroorat par nirbhar karta hai.

HRA (House Rent Allowance)

Agar aap kiraye par rehte hain to HRA par tax chhoot milti hai — par sirf purane tax regime me. Chhoot in teenon me se sabse kam wali hoti hai:

  • Asli mila hua HRA
  • Metro me basic ka 50%, non-metro me 40%
  • Diya gaya kiraya minus basic ka 10%

Apna exemption HRA Calculator me nikal sakte hain.

Special Allowance

Ye "bacha hua" hissa hai — CTC me se basic, HRA aur baaki nikaalne ke baad jo bachta hai. Ispar poora tax lagta hai, koi chhoot nahi. Zyadatar logon ki salary ka bada hissa yahin hota hai.

PF (Provident Fund)

Slip me aksar sirf aapka hissa dikhta hai (basic ka 12%). Employer bhi utna hi daalta hai, par wo CTC me juda hota hai aur slip me alag se dikh bhi sakta hai, nahi bhi.

Poora hisaab EPF guide me hai, aur maturity ka andaza EPF Calculator me.

Professional Tax

Ye rajya ka tax hai, kendra ka nahi. Kuch rajyon me lagta hai, kuch me bilkul nahi. Rakam chhoti hoti hai — aam taur par mahine ka do sau rupaye ke aas-paas, aur rajya ke hisaab se badalti hai.

TDS

Ye aapke saal bhar ke anumaanit tax ka mahine wala hissa hai. Company saal ki shuruaat me aapse investment declaration leti hai aur usi hisaab se har mahine kaat leti hai.

Yahin sabse badi galti hoti hai: log declaration daal dete hain par saal ke aakhir me proof nahi dete. Tab company saara TDS aakhri teen mahinon me kaat leti hai, aur January-March ki salary achanak bahut kam aa jaati hai. Detail TDS guide me hai.

Offer letter me ye 4 sawaal zaroor poochhein

  1. Basic kitna hai? Sirf CTC mat poochhiye. Basic se PF aur gratuity dono tay hote hain.
  2. Variable pay kitna hai? Kai companies CTC me 10-20% bonus jodti hain jo performance par milta hai. Wo garanti nahi hai.
  3. Joining bonus CTC me juda hai? Agar haan, to wo sirf pehle saal ka number hai. Doosre saal CTC apne aap kam ho jaayegi.
  4. Notice period aur bond? Ye salary ka hissa nahi hai, par baad me sabse mehnga padta hai.

Hike ka asli matlab

"30% hike" sunne me bada lagta hai, par ye CTC par hota hai. Agar naye offer me variable pay ka hissa zyada hai, to in-hand me badhotri bahut kam ho sakti hai.

Sahi tulna ka tarika: purani in-hand vs nayi in-hand. Do CTC ki tulna kabhi mat kijiye. Percentage ka hisaab Salary Hike Calculator se, aur naya in-hand CTC Calculator se nikaal kar dono side-by-side rakh lijiye.

Form 16 aur salary slip ka rishta

Saal khatam hone par company Form 16 deti hai. Ye asal me aapki 12 salary slips ka jod hai, do hisson me:

  • Part A — kitna TDS kata aur sarkar ke paas jama hua. Ye seedha income tax portal se banta hai.
  • Part B — salary ka poora baantwara, exemption, deduction aur final tax.

ITR bharne se pehle ek kaam zaroor kijiye: Form 16 ka TDS number aur income tax portal par dikhne wala Form 26AS / AIS milaayein. Agar company ne TDS kaat kar jama nahi kiya, to wo yahin pakda jaata hai. Ye aam nahi hai par hota hai, aur baad me theek karwana lamba kaam hota hai.

Naukri badalte waqt salary ka hisaab

Beech saal me naukri badli to do Form 16 aayenge. Dono ki income jodni padti hai, aur yahin sabse aam galti hoti hai.

Dono companies apne-apne hisaab se TDS kaatti hain, aur dono maan kar chalti hain ki ye aapki poori saal ki income hai. Natija — dono ne kam TDS kaata, aur ITR bharte waqt achanak bada tax bharna pad jaata hai.

Bachne ka tarika: nayi company ko purani company ki salary aur kata hua TDS bata dijiye. Iske liye Form 12B hota hai. Tab nayi company sahi hisaab se TDS kaategi aur saal ke aakhir me jhatka nahi lagega.

Notice period aur full & final

Naukri chhodte waqt aakhri salary alag tarah se banti hai. Usme ye sab jud/kat sakta hai:

  • Bachi hui chhutti ka paisa (leave encashment)
  • Gratuity, agar 5 saal poore hue hain
  • Notice period poora na karne par uski katauti
  • Company ka koi samaan ya advance baaki ho to uski katauti

Full & final ka statement likhit me maangiye aur har line ka matlab poochhiye. Ye wo document hai jo baad me sabse zyada kaam aata hai, aur log ise sabse kam dhyaan se dekhte hain. Gratuity ka apna hisaab Gratuity Calculator me nikal sakte hain.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

CTC me gratuity kyun jodi jaati hai jab wo 5 saal baad milti hai?

Company ke liye ye ek annual kharcha hai, isliye wo CTC me jodti hai. Par aapke liye ye tabhi paisa banta hai jab aap 5 saal poore karein. 5 saal se pehle chhodne par aam taur par nahi milti.

Basic zyada rakhwana chahiye ya kam?

Jawan umar aur retirement door hai to zyada basic behtar — PF aur gratuity dono badhte hain. Abhi mahine ka paisa zyada chahiye to kam basic. Har kisi ke liye ek jawab nahi hai.

New regime me HRA chhoot milti hai?

Nahi, self-occupied ya kiraye wali HRA chhoot new regime me nahi milti. Isliye kiraya dene wale logon ke liye purana regime kabhi-kabhi behtar baith jaata hai — dono ka hisaab laga kar dekhna chahiye.

TDS zyada kat gaya to?

ITR bharte waqt refund mil jaata hai. Par saal bhar aapka paisa fasa rehta hai, isliye samay par investment proof dena behtar hai.

Professional tax har rajya me lagta hai?

Nahi. Ye rajya ka tax hai — kuch rajyon me hai, kuch me bilkul nahi. Rakam bhi rajya ke hisaab se alag hoti hai.
