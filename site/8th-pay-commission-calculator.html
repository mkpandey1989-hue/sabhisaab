Fitment factor asal me hai kya

Ye ek guna karne wala number hai, aur bas itna hi. Aapka abhi ka basic pay lijiye, use fitment factor se guna kar dijiye — jo aaya wo naya basic pay hai.

7th Pay Commission me ye number 2.57 tha. Isi ek number ne kam se kam basic pay ko 7,000 se seedha 18,000 pahuncha diya tha. Poora pay matrix, har level, har grade — sab isi ek number par tika hota hai.

Isi liye ye poore Pay Commission ka sabse bada sawaal hai. Baaki sab — HRA, TA, pension formula — baad me tay hota hai. Pehle ye number tay hota hai, phir usi ke aas-paas baaki cheezein banti hain.

Ek baat jo aksar log samajh nahi paate: fitment factor sirf basic par lagta hai, poori in-hand salary par nahi. Aapki salary slip me jo HRA, TA, ya doosre allowance dikhte hain, wo naye basic ke hisaab se alag se dobara ginne padte hain.

Sarkar ne khud kaha hai: 10 August 2026 ko Lok Sabha me poochha gaya tha ki kya lagoo hone ki tareekh aur fitment factor tay ho gaye hain. Vitt Mantralaya ka jawab tha — Aayog ne abhi report hi nahi di, isliye koi faisla hua hi nahi hai. Isse zyada saaf jawab nahi ho sakta.

Phir bhi 1 January 2026 baar-baar kyun sunai deta hai? Kyunki 7th CPC 31 December 2025 ko khatam ho gaya — to naye pay ki gin ti usi agle din se hoti hai. Wo reference date hai, bhugtan ki tareekh nahi.

Wo baat jo koi nahi batata — DA zero ho jaata hai

Ye sabse badi galatfehmi hai, aur isi par log sabse zyada nirash hote hain.

Aap abhi jo DA har mahine paate hain, wo naye pay ke saath gayab ho jaata hai — kyunki Aayog use naye basic me hi jod deta hai. Kagaz par basic bahut bada dikhta hai, par uska ek bada hissa wahi DA hota hai jo aap pehle se paa rahe the.

Ek misaal se samajhiye. Maan lijiye basic ₹18,000 aur DA 58%:

Abhi2.57 lagne ke baad
Basic₹18,000₹46,260
DA₹10,440 (58%)₹0 (reset)
Basic + DA₹28,440₹46,260
Asli badhotari+63%, na ki +157%
Yahi wo jagah hai jahan "157% badhotari" wali surkhiyan galat sabit hoti hain. Basic sach me 157% badhta hai, par DA khatam ho jaane ke baad haath me aane wali rakam sirf 63% badhti hai. Upar wala calculator dono number dikhata hai — isiliye usme DA ka khaana rakha gaya hai.

Achhi baat ye hai ki naya basic zyada hone se aage ka har 1% DA pehle se zyada rupaye deta hai. Aur HRA, gratuity, pension — sab basic par tay hote hain, to unka faayda saath me milta hai.

Yaani 7th CPC me reference date ke baad 7 mahine lage the pehli badhi salary aane me. 8th CPC me ye antar aur bada ho sakta hai, kyunki report hi 2027 me aayegi. Jitna lamba antar, utna bada arrear.

Ek asli salaah: arrear ek saath aane wali badi rakam hoti hai, aur usi saal ki income me judti hai — isliye us saal ka tax slab upar ja sakta hai. Aayakar Adhiniyam ki Section 89(1) me is par raahat ka intezaam hai, par uske liye Form 10E bharna padta hai. Bahut se log ye form bharte hi nahi aur zaroorat se zyada tax de dete hain.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

8th Pay Commission ka fitment factor kitna hoga?

Abhi tay nahi hua. 10 August 2026 ko Lok Sabha me sarkar ne saaf kaha ki Aayog ne apni report hi nahi di, isliye na fitment factor tay hua hai na lagoo hone ki tareekh. Alag-alag jagah 1.83 se 3.83 tak ke anumaan chal rahe hain — unions 3.68 maang rahe hain. Jo site pakka number bataye, wo anumaan ko sach bata rahi hai.

8th Pay Commission kab lagoo hoga?

Aayog 3 November 2025 ko bana tha aur use 18 mahine me report deni hai — yaani lagbhag May 2027 tak. 7th CPC me report ke baad Cabinet ko 7 mahine lage the. Usi hisaab se pahli badhi hui salary 2027 ke aakhir tak aa sakti hai, aur 1 January 2026 se arrear milne ki ummeed hai.

Fitment factor ka matlab kya hota hai?

Ye ek guna karne wala number hai. Aapke abhi ke basic pay ko isse guna karke naya basic pay banta hai. 7th CPC me ye 2.57 tha — isi se kam se kam basic 7,000 se badhkar 18,000 hua tha.

Kya DA alag se milta rahega?

Naya pay lagoo hone ke baad DA zero par reset ho jaata hai, kyunki purana DA naye basic me hi samaa jaata hai. Uske baad DA phir se shunya se badhna shuru hota hai.

Arrear milega ya nahi?

Agar sarkar 1 January 2026 se pichhli tareekh se lagoo karti hai, to us din se lekar asli bhugtan tak ke mahine ka antar arrear ke roop me ek saath milta hai. 7th CPC me 7 mahine ka arrear mila tha. Par ye sarkar ki aakhri adhisoochna par nirbhar hai.

Pension par kya asar padega?

Pension basic pay par tay hoti hai, isliye wahi fitment factor pension par bhi lagta hai. Isi liye 68 lakh se zyada pensioners bhi is faisle ka intezaar kar rahe hain.
