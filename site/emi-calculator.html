EMI Calculator

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Free · Bina login · Aakhri update: August 2026
🟢 WhatsApp par share

Loan lene se pehle sabse zaroori sawaal — har mahine kitna dena padega? Ye EMI calculator loan rakam, byaj dar aur saal ke hisaab se aapki monthly kist, kul byaj aur total payment turant nikaal deta hai. Home loan, car loan, personal loan — sab par kaam karta hai.

EMI ka formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n / [(1+r)n − 1]
P = loan rakam, r = mahine ka byaj (saalana ÷ 12 ÷ 100), n = kul mahine.

Shuru ke saalon me EMI ka bada hissa byaj me jaata hai aur mool rakam bahut dheere ghatti hai. Ye baat zyadatar log nahi jaante — 20 saal ke home loan me pehle 5 saal me mool rakam mushkil se 15-20% hi kam hoti hai.

Kitni EMI safe hai?

Aam salah ye hai ki aapki saari EMI milkar monthly income ke 40% se kam rehni chahiye. Isse zyada ho to ghar ka baaki kharcha aur bachat dono dab jaate hain, aur ek mahina salary late hui to dikkat ho jaati hai. Bank apna faisla lete waqt yahi ratio (DTI) dekhte hain.

Byaj kam karne ke 4 tarike

  • Loan ka saal kam rakho: 20 saal ki jagah 15 saal karne se EMI thodi badhti hai par kul byaj bahut kam ho jaata hai.
  • Prepayment karo: bonus ya extra paisa aaye to mool rakam me daalein — seedha byaj bachta hai.
  • Credit score sudhaaro: achha score sasti dar dilata hai. 0.5% ka farak bhi lakho ka hota hai.
  • Dar ki tulna karo: alag-alag bank ki dar alag hoti hai, ek jagah haan mat kar dena.

Calculator kaise istemaal karein

  1. Loan ki rakam daalein (ghar ki keemat nahi — down payment ghata kar).
  2. Bank ki saalana byaj dar daalein.
  3. Kitne saal me chukana hai, wo daalein.
  4. Result me EMI, kul byaj aur total payment dikh jaayega.

Teen asli misaal — number ke saath

Misaal 1 — home loan. ₹30 lakh ka loan, 8.5% dar, 20 saal. EMI lagbhag ₹26,000 banti hai. 20 saal me aap kul kareeb ₹62 lakh chukayenge — yaani ₹32 lakh sirf byaj. Loan se zyada byaj — ye baat zyadatar log EMI dekhte waqt nahi sochte.

Misaal 2 — samay kam karne ka asar. Wahi ₹30 lakh, wahi 8.5%, par 15 saal ke liye. EMI badh kar lagbhag ₹29,500 ho jaati hai — sirf ₹3,500 zyada. Par kul byaj ghat kar kareeb ₹23 lakh reh jaata hai. Mahine ke ₹3,500 zyada dekar aapne ₹9 lakh bacha liye.

Misaal 3 — personal loan. ₹3 lakh, 14% dar, 3 saal. EMI lagbhag ₹10,250, kul byaj kareeb ₹69,000. Chhoti rakam par bhi oonchi dar se byaj tezi se badhta hai — isliye personal loan hamesha jitni jaldi ho sake chukaana behtar hai.

Log yahan galti kya karte hain

  1. Sirf EMI dekhna, kul byaj nahi. Bank aapko "sirf ₹26,000 mahina" bata kar loan bech dega. Aap poochein ki 20 saal me kul kitna dena hoga — jawab sunkar tasveer badal jaayegi.
  2. Lamba tenure chun lena kyunki EMI kam dikhti hai. 20 ki jagah 25 saal karne se EMI thodi kam hoti hai par kul byaj lakho zyada. Agar EMI budget me aa rahi hai, to hamesha kam saal chunein.
  3. Processing fee aur doosre kharche bhool jaana. Loan ki rakam ke alawa processing fee, legal aur technical valuation, stamp duty, insurance — ye ghar ki keemat ka 6-10% tak ho sakte hain aur loan me shaamil nahi hote.
  4. Aakhir me prepayment karna. Shuru ke saalon me EMI ka bada hissa byaj me jaata hai. Isliye prepayment jitni jaldi ho, utna faayda. Aakhri 5 saal me kiya gaya prepayment lagbhag bekaar hota hai.
  5. Prepayment ke baad EMI kam karwana. Do vikalp hote hain — EMI kam karwayein ya tenure. Tenure kam karwana aam taur par zyada paisa bachata hai.

Bharat me EMI se judi khaas baatein

  • Floating rate par prepayment charge nahi: vyaktigat floating-rate loan par bank aam taur par foreclosure ya prepayment charge nahi le sakte. Fixed rate par le sakte hain — isliye agreement dhyan se padhein.
  • 40% ka niyam: aapki saari EMI milkar monthly income ke 40% se kam honi chahiye. Bank bhi yahi ratio (DTI) dekhte hain. Isse zyada hone par bachat khatam ho jaati hai aur emergency fund ban hi nahi paata.
  • Ek missed EMI ka asar: penalty aur bounce charge to lagta hi hai, par sabse bada nuksan credit score ka hota hai — jiska asar mahino tak rehta hai aur agla loan mehnga pad jaata hai.
  • Home loan par tax chhoot: Old Regime me mool rakam aur byaj dono par alag-alag dhaaraon me chhoot milti hai. New Regime me niyam alag hain — apne case ke liye CA se confirm karein.

Saal me ek extra EMI: ye sabse aasaan trick hai. Bonus ya extra income se saal me ek atirikt EMI dene se 20 saal ka loan aam taur par 2-3 saal pehle khatam ho jaata hai, aur lakho ka byaj bach jaata hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

EMI me byaj kaise kam hota hai?

Loan ka saal kam karke, prepayment karke, ya kam byaj dar wala bank chunkar. Prepayment sabse asardaar hai.

EMI income ka kitna hona chahiye?

Aam salah: saari EMI milkar monthly income ke 40% se kam. Isse zyada par bachat aur kharche dab jaate hain.

Floating aur fixed rate me kya farak?

Fixed me dar poore loan me wahi rehti hai. Floating market ke saath ghatti-badhti hai — aam taur par shuruaat me sasti hoti hai.

Kya calculator ka EMI final hota hai?

Ye anuman hai. Bank processing fee, insurance aur apne niyam jodta hai, isliye asli EMI thodi alag ho sakti hai.

Loan jaldi chukane ka fayda?

Jitna jaldi mool rakam ghategi, utna byaj bachega. Par prepayment charge ke baare me bank se pehle pooch lein.
