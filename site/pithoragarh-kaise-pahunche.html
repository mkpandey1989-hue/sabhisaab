Pithoragarh: Hawai aur Helicopter Seva (UDAN aur Charter)

Nazdeeki Airport, Helipad aur Udaan Seva

Pithoragarh ka apna hawai adda Naini-Saini Airport hai, jo shehar se lagbhag 4.5 km door hai. Isse Dehradun ke liye udaan seva shuru hui hai. (Flight ke din, samay aur kiraya airline se pushti karein.)

Taza jaankari (news reports ke anusar):

  • Naini-Saini Airport par vyavasayik udaan January 2024 me kuch saal ke baad dobara shuru hui thi.
  • 26 March 2026 ko Mukhyamantri ne Dehradun-Pithoragarh-Dehradun udaan seva ka udghatan kiya, jisse safar ka samay lagbhag 1 ghanta bataya gaya.
  • Isi mauke par Airports Authority of India (AAI) aur rajya sarkar ke beech Naini Saini airport ke adhigrahan ke liye ek MoU sign hone aur airport ke vistar (lagbhag Rs 450 crore) ki baat bhi saamne aayi.
  • AAI ke aankdon ke anusar April 2025 se March 2026 me is airport ne lagbhag 17,701 yatri sanbhale.

Doosre nikat ke hawai adde: Pantnagar (Udham Singh Nagar) aur Dehradun. Delhi se Pithoragarh aane ke liye train ya sadak ka vikalp bhi hai.

Helicopter seva: Kuch hisson me helicopter seva (jaise Pithoragarh se Gunji ya anya jagah) kabhi-kabhi sarkari yojna ya yatra ke samay chalayi jati hai. (Seva, kiraya aur booking process Uttarakhand Civil Aviation Development Authority (UCADA) ya airline se pushti karein.)

Pithoragarh: Railway Station aur Aane Wali Rail Pariyojanayein

Kya Pithoragarh mein Railway Station Hai?

Pithoragarh zile me abhi koi sidha rail station nahi hai; sabse nikat ke mukhya railhead Tanakpur (Champawat zila) aur Kathgodam (Nainital zila) hain. (Train ka naam, number aur samay IRCTC se pushti karein.)

Future rail: Tanakpur-Bageshwar jaisi kuch rail pariyojnayein charcha me rahi hain.

Pithoragarh: Sadak, Highway, Pul aur Seema Sadak (BRO)

Mukhya Sadak aur Seemant Sadak

Pithoragarh tak pahunchne ka sabse zyada istemal hone wala raasta Tanakpur-Champawat-Pithoragarh marg hai, aur Pithoragarh se Dharchula-Tawaghat tak ek mukhya rashtriya rajmarg wali sadak jati hai. (NH ka exact number aur naam badal sakta hai; NH-9 ya puraane NH-125 ke roop me bhi kai jagah milta hai, pushti karein.)

Mukhya raste (aam taur par):

  • Delhi / Bareilly / Haldwani se Tanakpur ya Champawat hokar Pithoragarh.
  • Almora ya Bageshwar ki taraf se Pithoragarh (pahadi raasta, zyada mode).
  • Pithoragarh se Dharchula, Tawaghat aur aage Gunji-Lipulekh.
  • Pithoragarh se Munsiari (Thal-Munsiari ya Didihat raste se).

Seemant sadak (BRO): Border Roads Organisation (BRO) ne Dharchula-Tawaghat-Gunji-Lipulekh kshetra me sadak banayi hai, jisse Kailash Mansarovar Yatra aur seemant ke gaon tak pahunch asaan hui hai. (Sadak ki lambai, taza sthiti aur sthayi band hone wali jagahon ke bare me BRO ya zila prashasan se pushti karein.)

Aam dikkat: Barsat me Tawaghat-Dharchula ke raste par bhooskhalan, aur Pithoragarh-Munsiari raste par band hone wali jagah. Hamesha yatra se pehle sadak sthiti jaanch lein (Aapda Control Room: 1077, pushti karein).

Pithoragarh: Bus Seva, Shared Taxi aur Sthaniya Yatayat

Bus aur Taxi

Pithoragarh aur aas-paas ke kasbon ke beech Uttarakhand Parivahan Nigam (UTC) ki bus, KMOU (Kumaon Motor Owners Union) ki bus aur sanjhi jeep (shared taxi) chalti hain.

Sanjhi jeep ke tips:

  • Subah ki pehli jeep aksar kam bheed wali hoti hai aur dopahar ke baad kam gaadiyan milti hain.
  • Seat ke hisab se kiraya lagta hai; poori gaadi ya “reserve” lene ka daam alag hota hai.
  • Bhari saman ya bachchon ke saath aage ki seat ya poori gaadi lena behtar hai.

Taxi union: Pithoragarh shehar aur Munsiari, Dharchula jaise kasbon me taxi union ke counter hote hain. Kiraya pehle se tay kar lein aur union ki parchi ya receipt le lein.

Pithoragarh: Petrol Pump, EV Charging aur Mechanic

Petrol, EV aur Mechanic

Unche aur seemant ilake me petrol pump kam hain, isliye lambi yatra ke liye pehle se tanki bhar lena zaroori hai. Dharchula ke baad aage ke ilake me pump milna mushkil ho sakta hai. (Kaun sa pump abhi chalu hai aur “last petrol pump” kahan hai, ye sthaniya logon se pakka puchh lein.)

EV charging: Pithoragarh shehar aur kuch kasbon me EV charging point ki jaankari hai, par seemant ilake me ye kam ya na ke barabar mil sakta hai. (EV se pahad ki lambi yatra karne se pehle raste ke charging point pushti karein.)

Mechanic aur puncture: Pithoragarh, Jauljibi, Didihat, Dharchula jaise bade kasbon me mechanic aur tyre puncture ki dukaan mil jati hain. Unchai wale ilake me kam milti hain, isliye extra tyre, jack aur basic toolkit saath rakhein.

Pithoragarh: Rukne ki Jagah: KMVN, Hotel aur Resort

Sarkari aur Nijee Rukne ki Jagah

Pithoragarh zile me rukne ke liye KMVN (Kumaon Mandal Vikas Nigam) ke tourist rest house, PWD ke inspection house, Van Vibhag ke forest rest house, nijee hotel aur resort milte hain.

Kiraya: Season (May-June, October) me kiraya badh sakta hai, aur Kailash Yatra ke samay Dharchula-Pithoragarh me kamre bahut jaldi bhar jate hain. Pehle se booking karein.

Pithoragarh: Registered Pahadi Homestays, Hostels & Ashrams

Homestay aur Dharamshala

Homestay pahadi gaon ka asli anubhav deta hai: ghar ka khana, gaon ki kahaniyan aur kam bheed. Uttarakhand sarkar ki Deen Dayal Upadhyay Griha Awas (Homestay) Yojana ke tahat kai homestay panjikrit hain. (Yojna ki sthiti aur panjikrit homestay ki list Paryatan Vibhag ki website se pushti karein.)

Homestay chunne ke tips:

  • Panjikrit (registered) homestay ko prathmikta dein.
  • Pehle se phone par baat kar lein, khaaskar seemant ilake me.
  • Network na hone ki sthiti me pehle se confirm karna zaroori hai.
  • Gaon ki sanskriti ka samman karein.

Dharamshala aur ashram: Dharchula, Gunji aur mandir ke paas kuch dharamshala aur ashram mil jate hain, jahan simple vyavastha hoti hai. (Sthiti aur niyam sthaniya sampark se pushti karein.)

Pithoragarh: Mobile Network aur Internet: Asli Sthiti

Kahan Network Chalta Hai

Pithoragarh shehar aur bade kasbon me Jio, Airtel aur BSNL ka network aam taur par milta hai, par Johar, Darma, Vyans ghati aur unche gaon me network bahut kam ya bilkul nahi hota. (Kaun sa operator kahan chalta hai ye badalta rehta hai; yatra se pehle sthaniya logon se poochhein.)

Yatra me kya karein:

  • Offline map pehle se download kar lein.
  • Emergency contact parivaar ko likh kar dein.
  • Seemant ilake me BSNL ya Satellite phone ka vikalp sthaniya agency se pata karein. (Satellite phone ke niyam sakht hote hain; sirf kanooni tareeke se hi lein.)

Fiber Broadband aur Workation

Pithoragarh shehar aur kuch kasbon me fiber broadband mil jata hai, par chhote gaon me internet ki speed kam ya rukti hui ho sakti hai. Workation ke liye jaane se pehle homestay se internet ki asli speed aur backup ke baare me zaroor puchhein (Pithoragarh: Basna, workation, retirement, Pithoragarh mein Workation ke liye Achhe Gaon).

Pithoragarh: Bijli, Solar aur Sardi mein Paani ki Sthiti

Bijli aur Paani

Pithoragarh zile me bijli UPCL (Uttarakhand Power Corporation Limited) dwara di jati hai, aur sardi me barf ya barsat me bijli kat sakti hai, jo kuch gaon me kai din tak rah sakti hai. (Reliability sthaan par depend karti hai.)

Sujhav: Gaon ya homestay me rehne ke liye power backup (inverter ya solar) aur power bank saath rakhein. Unche ilake me pani sardi me jam sakta hai, isliye plumber ya local se pehle puchh lein.

Pithoragarh: Inner Line Permit (ILP), Van Anumati aur Suraksha Niyam

Seemant Ilake me Jaane ki Anumati

Seemant ke kuch ilake, jaise Dharchula ke aage Gunji-Kuti-Nabhidhang (Adi Kailash-Om Parvat), Milam, Martoli aur Darma ghati ke kuch hisson me jane ke liye Inner Line Permit (ILP) ya sambandhit anumati lagti hai. (Kaun si jagah ke liye kaun si anumati chahiye, ye zila prashasan aur sambandhit adhikari se pushti karein.)

Aam taur par kaise milti hai:

  • Bharatiya yatri: Zila Magistrate ya SDM karyalay (Pithoragarh/Dharchula) ya online portal se ILP lena padta hai. Online portal ka naam aur link sarkari site se pushti karein.
  • Videshi yatri: Seema ke paas ke ilake ke liye alag Protected Area/Restricted Area anumati lagti hai, jo Grih Mantralay se judi prakriya se milti hai. (Videshi ke liye niyam sakht hain; Videsh Mantralay aur Grih Mantralay se pakka pushti karein.)

Zaroori dastavez (aam taur par): Photo ID (Aadhar ya passport), passport size photo aur medical certificate (high altitude ke liye). (Seemant ke ilake me kai jagah health check aur acclimatization anivarya ho sakte hain.)

Surakshit Yatra ke Niyam

  • Drone ka istemal na karein: Seema ke paas drone par pratibandh ho sakta hai.
  • Seema ya fauji ilake ki tasveer na khinchein jahan mana ho.
  • Local guide aur registered agent ke saath hi jayein.
  • Khali bukhaar, AMS lakshan hone par aage na badhein.
  • Jungli janwar aur bugyal ke niyam (Pithoragarh: Mausam, jungle aur jeev-jantu).
  • Network na hone ki sthiti me emergency plan pehle se banayein.

Pithoragarh: Kaise pahunchein, kahan rukein: Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Pithoragarh kaise pahunchein?
Sadak se Tanakpur ya Haldwani hokar, aur 26 March 2026 se Dehradun se udaan seva bhi shuru hui hai (flight ke din). Sabse nikat ke railhead Tanakpur aur Kathgodam hain.
Kya Pithoragarh me mobile network chalta hai?
Shehar aur bade kasbon me aam taur par chalta hai, par Johar, Darma, Vyans ghati me bahut kam ya nahi. Offline map saath rakhein.
Pithoragarh ka hawai adda kaun sa hai?
Naini-Saini Airport, jo shehar se lagbhag 4.5 km door hai; flight ke din aur samay airline se pakka karein.
Pithoragarh ka nazdeeki railway station kaun sa hai?
Zile mein seedha rail station nahi; nazdeeki railhead Tanakpur (lagbhag 150 km) aur Kathgodam hain.
Pithoragarh ke seemant ilakon mein jaane ke liye permit lagta hai?
Haan, Gunji-Kuti-Nabhidhang (Adi Kailash-Om Parvat), Milam, Martoli aur Darma ghati ke kuch hisson ke liye Inner Line Permit ya sambandhit anumati lagti hai.
