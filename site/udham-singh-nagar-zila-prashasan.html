Executive Summary & District Identity: Udham Singh Nagar Kya Hai?

Zile ki Pehchan: Ek Nazar Mein

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar Uttarakhand ke Kumaon mandal ka Terai zila hai, jiska mukhyalay Rudrapur hai. Census 2011 me iski abadi 16,48,902 thi, jo Haridwar aur Dehradun ke baad Uttarakhand me teesri sabse zyada hai. Isse "Anna-Bhandar", "Gateway of Kumaon" aur "Mini India" kaha jata hai.

Udham Singh Nagar (log chhote me USN bhi likhte hain) Uttarakhand ke dakshin-purvi hisse me, Uttar Pradesh aur Nepal ki seema ke paas phaila hua hai. Yeh pahadi nahi, balki Terai-Bhabar ka maidani ilaka hai. Yahan ki zameen itni upjau hai ki Uttarakhand ke bahut bade hisse ka anaj, ganna aur dudh yahin se aata hai. Isi liye zila sarkari kagzon aur aam bol-chaal dono me "Food Bowl of Uttarakhand" kehlata hai.

"Mini India" naam isliye pada kyunki yahan ek hi zile me Kumaoni aur Garhwali Pahadi, Punjabi Sikh, Bengali, Tharu, Bhuksa, Purvanchali, Muslim aur Marwari-Vyapari samaj sab saath rehte hain. Census 2011 ke hisab se yahan Hindu lagbhag 66.98%, Muslim lagbhag 22.58% aur Sikh lagbhag 9.87% hain, aur Hindi ke baad Punjabi sabse zyada boli jaane wali bhasha hai (lagbhag 10.09%).

Etymology: Zile ke Naam ka Itihas

Seedha jawab: Zile ka naam shaheed Udham Singh ke naam par rakha gaya, jinhone 13 March 1940 ko London me Michael O'Dwyer ko goli maari thi. Yeh naam 1995 me zila banne par diya gaya. Isse pehle yeh poora ilaka "Terai" kehlata tha aur Nainital zile ka hissa tha.

Shaheed Udham Singh ka janm 26 December 1899 ko Punjab ke Sunam me hua tha. Wo 1919 ke Jallianwala Bagh kaand ke gawah the aur barson baad unhone London ke Caxton Hall me us ghatna ka badla liya. 31 July 1940 ko unhe phansi di gayi. Unhone khud ko "Ram Mohammad Singh Azad" naam se bhi pehchaana, jo Hindu, Muslim aur Sikh ekta ka sandesh tha. Isi bhavna ke kaaran Terai jaise multi-community ilaake ke zile ke liye yeh naam bahut upyukt maana gaya.

"Terai" shabd ka matlab hai pahad ki talhati ka geela maidani ilaka. Pehle yeh jagah malaria wale ghane jungle ke liye jaani jaati thi, jise "Mar-Bhabar" tak kaha gaya. Is ilaake ke shehron ke naam bhi apni kahani rakhte hain:

  • Rudrapur: Kumaon ke Chand raja Rudra Chand ke naam par, jinka shasan 16vi sadi me tha.
  • Kashipur: Lokmanyata ke anusar Chand shasan ke ek adhikari Kashinath Adhikari ke naam par basa (kuch itihaskar iske alag mool bhi batate hain). Prachin kaal me yeh ilaka "Govishan" ke naam se jaana jata tha.
  • Pantnagar: Uttarakhand ke ashilp-purush aur UP ke pehle mukhyamantri Pandit Govind Ballabh Pant ke naam par.
  • Nanakmatta: Guru Nanak Dev ji ki yatra se jude sthaan ke naam par.

Baaki kasbon (jaise Sitarganj, Khatima, Kichha, Gadarpur, Bazpur, Jaspur) ke naam ke peeche alag-alag lok-kathayein hain.

Udham Singh Nagar: Collectorate aur Rajasv Prashasan

Zila Mukhyalay, Vikas Bhawan aur Collectorate Kahan Hai

Seedha jawab: Zile ka mukhyalay Rudrapur hai, jahan Collectorate (Kalektret) pin code 263153 par sthit hai. Zile ke sabse bade adhikari Zila Adhikari (DM) hain. Rudrapur ka Collectorate parisar hi zile ke zyadatar sarkari karyalayon ka kendra hai.

Zile ka sabse upar ka prashasnik pad Zila Adhikari (District Magistrate) ka hota hai, jo IAS adhikari hote hain. DM ke saath Apar Zila Adhikari, Mukhya Vikas Adhikari (CDO), Zila Vikas Adhikari, Sub Divisional Magistrate (SDM) aur tehsildar milkar zile ka rajasv aur kanoon-vyavastha ka kaam sambhalte hain. Sarkari numbers aur adhikariyon ke naam badalte rehte hain, isliye latest sampark ke liye zile ki official website usnagar.nic.in dekhein.

Practical tip: Kisi bhi sarkari kaam ke liye pehle tehsil jaayein. Zyadatar kaam (jaati, niwas, aay, khatauni nakal) tehsil ya online e-District portal par ho jaate hain. Collectorate sirf tab jaana padta hai jab kaam DM star ka ho (jaise arms license ya bade bhumi-vivaad).

Patwari Chowki: Rajasv Police aur Niyamit Police

Seedha jawab: Uttarakhand ke pahadi ilakon me kuch jagah "Revenue Police" (Patwari-Lekhpal) vyavastha chalti thi, par Udham Singh Nagar ke maidani ilake me niyamit civil police (Kotwali aur Thana) hi kanoon-vyavastha sambhalti hai. Yahan patwari sirf rajasv ke kaam dekhte hain.

Revenue police ka matlab hai ki gaon me chor-dakaiti ya aparadh ki shuruaati jaanch patwari karte the, jo police ka kaam bhi karte the. Yeh vyavastha pahadi zilon me lambe samay tak rahi. Terai ke zilon me aabadi aur aparadh ki prakriti ke kaaran regular police system hi raha. Isliye USN me patwari ka kaam khatauni, khasra, zameen ki naap-jokh, fasal-nirikshan aur bhumi-vivaad ki report tak seemit hai.

Patwari se kya kaam karwaya ja sakta hai?
Khatauni ki nakal, zameen ki paimaish, uttaradhikari (warasat) darj karwana, fasal-nuksan ka survey, aur pattay-bhumi se jude prapatra.

Udham Singh Nagar: Gaon aur Panchayati Raj Vyavastha

Vikas Khand (Development Blocks) ki Poori Suchi

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar me 7 vikas khand hain: Bazpur, Gadarpur, Jaspur, Kashipur, Khatima, Rudrapur aur Sitarganj. Gram vikas ke zyadatar kaam, jaise MGNREGA, awas yojana aur panchayat nidhi, inhi blocks ke maadhyam se hote hain.

Har vikas khand ka mukhya adhikari Khand Vikas Adhikari (BDO) hota hai. BDO ke karyalay se gram panchayat ko yojanaon ka paisa, safai aur peyjal ke kaam, aur gram sachivon (VDO/VPDO) ka samanvay milta hai. Gaon ke logon ke liye BDO ka karyalay wahi jagah hai jahan MGNREGA job card, Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana, widow/old-age pension aur gram vikas ki shikayatein pahunchti hain.

Dhyan dein: Kichha ek tehsil aur nagar palika hai, par vikas khand ke roop me alag se suchibaddh nahi hai. Kichha ke gramin kshetra paas ke blocks me aate hain. Exact block mapping ke liye block office ya Panchayati Raj vibhag se confirm karein.

Aabaad aur Gair-Aabaad (Khaali) Gaon

Seedha jawab: Alag sarkari sources me zile ke gaon ki sankhya 656 se 711 ke beech milti hai, kyunki sabki ginti ki taareekh aur parisiman alag hai. Pahadi zilon jaisa "ghost village" ka dar yahan nahi hai, kyunki Terai me palayan ulta hai, yahan log basne aate hain.

Census 2011 aur zile ke alag-alag vibhagon ke aankde thoda bhinn hain. Udaharan ke liye, ek sarkari audyogik profile me 656 gaon, CGWB report me 669, aur Census-aadharit sites par 674 se 688 tak darj hain.

Udham Singh Nagar ki ek khaas baat yeh hai ki yahan palayan ka ulta pravaah hai. Pahad ke zilon se Almora, Pithoragarh, Champawat, Bageshwar aur Chamoli ke parivar roji-roti aur bacchon ki padhai ke liye Rudrapur, Haldwani aur Kashipur me aakar basne lage hain. Isi wajah se Pahadi aabadi yahan lagatar badh rahi hai.

Nagar Panchayats & Cantonment Boards

Seedha jawab: Zile me Dineshpur, Shaktigarh, Sultanpur Patti, Garhinegi (Kashipur kshetra) aur Nanakmatta jaise bade gramin-kasbe Nagar Panchayat star ke shehri nikay ke roop me jaane jaate hain. Udham Singh Nagar me koi Cantonment Board (Chhavni Parishad) nahi hai.

Nagar Panchayat un chhote shehron ke liye hota hai jo gaon se shehar ban rahe hain. Isme abadi, vyapaarik gatividhi aur ghanatva ka ek nirdharit starr hona zaroori hai. Cantonment Board sirf un jagahon par hota hai jahan sena ka chhavni kshetra ho (jaise Ranikhet, Lansdowne ya Dehradun). Udham Singh Nagar ek civil, krishi aur audyogik zila hai, isliye yahan cantonment nahi hai.

Garhinegi Nagar Panchayat ke chunav ka nateeja June 2026 me ghoshit hua, jisme nirdaliy pratyashi Abhishek Sukhija adhyaksh bane. Aise nateeje badalte rehte hain, isliye "vartaman adhyaksh" jaise sawal ke liye hamesha taaza khabar dekhein.

Udham Singh Nagar: Rajneetik aur Chunavi Parichay

Lok Sabha Seat aur Maujooda Saansad

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar ke saare 9 Vidhan Sabha kshetra Nainital-Udhamsingh Nagar Lok Sabha seat me aate hain. Is seat me Nainital zile ke 5 kshetra milakar kul 14 Vidhan Sabha segments hain. 2024 ke chunav me yahan se BJP ke Ajay Bhatt sansad chune gaye.

Nainital-Udhamsingh Nagar seat 2009 ke parisiman ke baad bani, aur Uttarakhand ki paanch Lok Sabha seats me se ek hai. Isme zile ke Jaspur, Kashipur, Bajpur, Gadarpur, Rudrapur, Kichha, Sitarganj, Nanakmatta aur Khatima ke saath Nainital zile ke Lalkuan, Bhimtal, Nainital, Haldwani aur Kaladhungi shamil hain. Is seat par matdataon ki sankhya 20 lakh se zyada hai.

Udham Singh Nagar se Shuru Hue Pramukh Rajnaitik Andolan

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar ka sabse yaadgaar rajnitik adhyay 1994 ka Uttarakhand Rajya Andolan hai, jisme 1 September 1994 ko Khatima me firing ki ghatna hui. Iske alawa Terai me bhumi-adhikar, Bengali punarvas aur Tharu zameen ke mudde bhi lambe samay tak rajnitik charcha me rahe.

  • Uttarakhand Andolan (1994): Khatima Goli Kand (1 September 1994) alag rajya ki ladai ke sabse peedadayak adhyayon me gina jata hai. Is kaand ke baad Mussoorie, Muzaffarnagar (Rampur Tiraha) jaise sthanon par bhi hinsa hui. Mrit andolankariyon ki sankhya alag-alag sources me alag likhi gayi hai, isliye exact aankda sarkari ya Uttarakhand Andolan ke sammanit sources se hi lein.
  • Bhumi aur Pattay ke mudde: Terai ki zameen par Tharu, Buksa, Punjabi aur Bengali samajon ka haq, pattay (lease) aur zameen ke record ka mudda kai bar vidhan sabha tak pahuncha.
  • Chini mill aur Kisan andolan: Ganne ke bhugtan aur MSP ko lekar kisan sangathan yahan sakriya rahe hain.
  • Rajya ki rajneeti me bhumika: Khatima seat se Uttarakhand ke mukhyamantri Pushkar Singh Dhami ne vidhayak ke roop me kai bar pratinidhitva kiya, haalaanki 2022 me yahan se Congress ke Bhuwan Chandra Kapri jeete.

Udham Singh Nagar: Police, Suraksha aur Seema Bal

Zile ki Police Vyavastha: Kotwali, Thane aur Chowkiyan

Seedha jawab: Zile ki police ka netritva Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) karte hain. Rudrapur, Kichha, Kashipur, Khatima, Sitarganj jaise shehron me Kotwali aur Thana hain. Emergency ke liye 112 (ERSS) number poore Uttarakhand me kaam karta hai.

SSP ke neeche Apar Superintendent of Police (SP), Circle Officer (CO), Inspector aur Sub-Inspector hote hain. Zile ke prasiddh Kotwali aur Thana kshetron me Rudrapur, ITI (Pantnagar), Transit Camp, Kichha, Kashipur, Jaspur, Bazpur, Gadarpur, Sitarganj, Khatima, Nanakmatta aur Dineshpur aate hain (khabron me inke naam bar-bar aate hain).

Zaroori helpline (poore Uttarakhand ke liye):

  • 112: Police, fire aur medical ke liye ek emergency number (ERSS)
  • 108: Ambulance
  • 1930: Cyber fraud ki shikayat
  • 181: Mahila helpline
  • 1098: Child helpline
  • 1905: Mukhyamantri Helpline

FIR kaise karein? Nazdiki thana ya Kotwali jaakar likhit shikayat dein. Agar thana FIR darj na kare, to SP ya SSP karyalay me likhit arzi dein. Online shikayat ke liye Uttarakhand Police ki official website ka "Citizen Services" section istemal karein.

Fauj, ITBP aur SSB ke Camp

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar me Garhwal Rifles ya Kumaon Regiment jaisa koi regiment centre nahi hai, kyunki yeh maidani zila hai. Par yahan bahut bade paimane par purv-sainik (ex-servicemen) rehte hain, jinhe Terai ki zameen aazadi ke baad bataur punarvas di gayi thi.

Aazadi ke baad Terai me ex-servicemen colonies basayi gayi aur kai sainik parivar yahan bas gaye. Aaj bhi Rudrapur, Kichha aur Sitarganj me ex-servicemen ki bhari sankhya hai, aur ve Zila Sainik Kalyan evam Punarvas Karyalay se jude hain. Nepal seema ke paas Khatima aur Banbasa (Champawat) kshetra me seema suraksha bal (SSB) ki upasthiti seema-kshetra ki suraksha me rehti hai.

Udham Singh Nagar: Nyayalay aur Kanooni Vyavastha

Zila Nyayalay, Parivar Nyayalay aur Muft Kanooni Sahayata

Seedha jawab: Zile ka Jila evam Satra Nyayalay (District and Sessions Court) Rudrapur me hai. Kashipur, Khatima, Sitarganj, Kichha, Jaspur aur Bazpur jaise shehron me bhi nyayalay hain. Muft kanooni madad ke liye Zila Vidhik Seva Pradhikaran (DLSA) hai.

Aam logon ke liye sabse kaam ki cheez muft kanooni sahayata hai. Zila Vidhik Seva Pradhikaran (DLSA) aur tehsil Vidhik Seva Samiti garib, mahila, anusuchit jati-janjati, bachon aur majdooron ko muft vakil ki suvidha deti hain. Is ke liye aay ki seema jaise niyam lagte hain, jo samay-samay par badalte hain. Lok Adalat me cheque bounce, bijli bill aur choti zameen ke vivaad bina lambe mukadme ke nipta liye jaate hain.

Udham Singh Nagar mein Sub-Registrar (Zameen Registry) Daftar

Seedha jawab: Zameen ki registry (sale deed) Sub-Registrar karyalay me hoti hai, jo zile ke pramukh tehsil shehron me sthit hain. Registry ke liye stamp duty aur registration fee deni hoti hai, aur rate Tehsil ke circle rate ke aadhar par lagte hain.

Registry karte samay yeh dastavez aam taur par chahiye hote hain: bechne wale ka khatauni/khasra ka prapatra, aadhar aur pan, gawahon ki pehchan, naksha ya chauhaddi, aur "No Dues" certificate. Registry ke baad khatauni me naam chadhwane ke liye (daakhil-kharij ya mutation) tehsil me alag se aavedan dena padta hai. Is poori prakriya ki detail Udham Singh Nagar: Sarkari seva aur portal me di gayi hai.

Udham Singh Nagar: Aapda Prabandhan aur Emergency Vyavastha

DDMA Control Room aur Muft Helpline

Seedha jawab: Zile me aapda prabandhan ka zimma Zila Aapda Prabandhan Pradhikaran (DDMA) ke paas hai, jiske adhyaksh Zila Adhikari hote hain. Aapda ki sthiti me rajya ka Emergency Operation Centre number 1070 aur zile ka control room number 1077 kaam karta hai.

Udham Singh Nagar ke liye sabse bada khatra bahar ka hai. Yahan pahad jaisa bhuskhalan nahi, par baadh sabse badi aapda hai. Sharda, Kosi, Dhela, Deoha, Gaula, Baur aur Bhakra jaisi nadiyan aur Nanak Sagar jaisa bada jalashay barish ke mausam me khatara bana sakte hain. Kuch varshon me Terai ke kai gaon me paani bhar jane ki ghatnayein hui hain, isliye monsoon ke samay (June se September) DDMA ki chetavni par dhyan dena zaroori hai.

Barish aur baadh me kya karein?

  • Nadi-kinare ke gaon wale chetavni milte hi oonche sthan ya raahat shivir me jaayein.
  • 1077 aur 112 par madad maangein.
  • Bijli ke khambhon aur tooti taaron se door rahein.
  • Baadh ke paani me gaadi na chalayein.

SDRF / NDRF ki Tainaati, Baadh Aashray aur Emergency Helipad

Seedha jawab: Baadh aur aapda ke samay SDRF aur zila prashasan ki teamen raahat kaam karti hain. Zile me raahat shivir aam taur par school, panchayat bhawan aur sarkari imaratein banai jaati hain. Pantnagar hawai adda emergency evacuation me upyogi sabit ho sakta hai.

Aapda ke samay jin sthanon ko raahat shivir banaya jata hai wo har baar prashasan tay karta hai, aur unki suchi DDMA ke notice me aati hai. Pantnagar airport zile ka mukhya hawai adda hai, jahan se Delhi aur anya shehron ke liye udan sewa chalti hai. Emergency helipad ki sthiti badalti rehti hai, isliye sarkari suchi ke liye DDMA se sampark karein.

Zile ka RTO Code, Daftar aur Licence Sevayein

Karyalay ke samay aur phone number badalte rehte hain, isliye official portal par dekhna sahi rahta hai.

Pahadi Driving ke Niyam aur Green Cess / Eco-Tax

Seedha jawab: Udham Singh Nagar maidani zila hai, isliye yahan pahadi driving ke liye alag endorsement ki zaroorat nahi padti. Par Uttarakhand me bahar ki gaadiyon par Green Cess ya eco-tax ke niyam lag sakte hain, jinki dar aur shartein parivahan vibhag ke notification se tay hoti hain.

Agar aap Udham Singh Nagar se Nainital, Almora, Champawat ya Pithoragarh jaisi pahadi jagahon par gaadi le jaate hain, to pahadi sadakon par chadhai-utrai, ghumavdar mod aur barish me bhuskhalan ka dhyan rakhein. Lambi chadhai se pehle brake, tyres, coolant aur engine oil ki jaanch zaroor karein. Bahari rajya ki gaadiyon ke liye Green Cess ki taaza dar aur chhoot ki shartein sirf official notification se maanein.
