Mehngai paise ko kaise khaati hai

Mehngai (inflation) ka matlab hai cheezein har saal mehngi hoti jaati hain. Iska ulta asar ye hai ki aapke paise ki kharch-shakti ghatti jaati hai. Aaj ₹100 me jo milta hai, 10 saal baad ussе kam milega. Isliye paise ko sirf rakhna nahi, badhana zaroori hai — kam se kam mehngai se tez.

Future me chahiye = Aaj ki rakam × (1 + mehngai)^saal
Asli keemat = Aaj ki rakam ÷ (1 + mehngai)^saal

Ek misaal

Aaj ₹1 lakh, mehngai 6%, 10 saal. 10 saal baad usi ₹1 lakh ki asli keemat sirf ~₹55,800 reh jayegi. Aur aaj jo cheez ₹1 lakh me aati hai, wo tab ~₹1.79 lakh me aayegi. Yaani paisa wahi, par khareedne ki taakat aadhi. Ye dikhata hai ki paise ko sirf FD/bachat me rakhna kaafi nahi.

Isse kya seekhein

  • Sirf bachat kaafi nahi — paisa aisi jagah lagayein jo mehngai se tez badhe.
  • FD ka asli return dekhein — 7% FD par 6% mehngai me asli badhotri sirf ~1%.
  • Lambe goal me mehngai jodein — bachche ki padhai ya retirement ka future kharcha aaj se kaafi zyada hoga.
  • Cash rakhna nuksan — ghar me para paisa har saal keemat khota hai.

Mehngai ko kaise haraayein

Mehngai ko harane ke liye paisa aisi jagah chahiye jahan return mehngai se zyada ho. Lambe samay ke liye SIP aur equity aam taur par mehngai se tez badhte hain. Surakshit hisse ke liye PPF theek hai. Asli return (return minus mehngai) samajhne ke liye ye rule yaad rakhein — jo dikhta hai wo asli badhotri nahi hoti. FD vs mutual fund ki tulna yahan hai.

Aapki apni mehngai, sarkari aankde se alag hoti hai

Khabar me mehngai dar 5-6% bataayi jaati hai, par ghar ka kharcha usse tez badhta mehsoos hota hai. Dono baatein sahi ho sakti hain.

Sarkari aankda ek badi list par ausat hota hai jisme kapda, joota, transport, sab kuch shamil hai. Aapki apni mehngai us par tiki hai ki aapka paisa kis cheez me jaata hai:

  • School fees aur coaching — aam taur par ausat mehngai se kaafi tez badhti hai. Do-teen bacche padh rahe hain to aapki asli mehngai bahut upar hai.
  • Ilaaj aur dawaiyan — yahan bhi badhotari tez rehti hai, aur budhape me kharcha aur badhta hai.
  • Makan ka kiraya — sheher par nirbhar, par bade shehron me tezi se badhta hai.
  • Electronics — yahan aksar keematein girti bhi hain.
Isi liye is calculator me 6% ka default number aankh band karke mat maaniye. Agar aapke kharche ka bada hissa fees aur ilaaj me jaata hai, to 8-9% daalkar dekhiye — jawab kaafi alag aayega, aur wahi aapke liye zyada sach hai.

Retirement ka lakshya isi wajah se badla hua dikhta hai

Mehngai ka sabse bada asar retirement par padta hai, kyunki wahan samay sabse lamba hota hai.

Aaj ke ₹40,000 mahine me ghar chal jaata hai. 25 saal baad, 6% mehngai par, usi jeevan shaili ke liye lagbhag ₹1.7 lakh mahina chahiye hoga. Ye number pehli baar dekhne par asambhav lagta hai — par yahi asli lakshya hai.

Aur retirement ke baad mehngai rukti nahi. 60 saal par jo rakam kaafi lag rahi thi, 75 saal tak wo aadhi keemat ki reh jaati hai. Isi liye retirement corpus ka poora paisa aisi jagah rakhna jahan return mehngai se kam ho, dheere-dheere gareebi ki taraf le jaata hai.

Isse do saaf sabak nikalte hain:

  • Sirf "surakshit" jagah kaafi nahi. Jahan return mehngai ke aas-paas ho, wahan paisa badhta dikhta hai par kharidne ki taakat wahi rehti hai. Ek hissa aisi jagah bhi hona chahiye jo lambe samay me mehngai se aage nikle.
  • Nivesh har saal badhaiye. Jo SIP aaj ₹5,000 ki hai, wo 10 saal baad utni badi nahi rahegi. Har saal 10% badhana mehngai ka seedha jawab hai — iska hisaab Step-up SIP Calculator me dekh sakte hain.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Mehngai se paise ka kya hota hai?

Har saal cheezein mehngi hoti hain, isliye paise ki kharch-shakti ghatti hai. Aaj ke ₹1 lakh 10 saal baad kam value ke ho jaate hain.

Kitni mehngai maanein?

India me aam taur par 5-7% saalana. Lambe goal ka hisaab lagate waqt 6% ke aas-paas maanna theek rehta hai.

FD mehngai se bachati hai?

Aksar bahut kam. 7% FD par 6% mehngai me asli badhotri sirf ~1%. Isliye poora paisa FD me rakhna lambe samay me nuksan.

Mehngai kaise haraayein?

Paisa aisi jagah lagayein jo mehngai se tez badhe — lambe samay ke liye SIP/equity. Surakshit hisse ke liye PPF.

Calculator me kitni mehngai dar daalein?

Ye aapke kharche par nirbhar hai. Aam kharche ke liye 6% theek hai, par agar bada hissa school fees aur ilaaj me jaata hai to 8-9% daalkar dekhiye — wo aapke liye zyada sach hoga.

Retirement ke baad bhi mehngai ka asar rehta hai?

Haan, aur wahi sabse khatarnak hai. 60 saal par jo rakam kaafi lagti hai, 75 saal tak uski keemat kaafi ghat chuki hoti hai. Isliye poora paisa sirf ek hi jagah rakhna theek nahi.
