Dehradun: Pahadi Land Measurement: Nali, Mutthi & Bigha Calculator

Uttarakhand me zameen aksar nali aur mutthi me naapi jati hai. 1 nali = 2,160 square feet (lagbhag 200.67 square meter) hota hai, aur 1 nali me 16 mutthi hoti hain. Dehradun ke Doon ghati wale hisse me bigha-biswa, aur Chakrata-Kalsi ke pahadi gaon me nali-mutthi zyada sunai dete hain.

Sahi Hisaab: 1 Nali = 2,160 Sq. Feet = 240 Gaj = 200.67 Varg Meter

1 nali = 2,160 sq ft = 240 gaj (sq yard) = lagbhag 200.67 sq meter. Ek acre me lagbhag 20 nali aate hain, aur ek hectare me lagbhag 49.83 nali.

Rajya me zameen ka sarkari record aam taur par hectare, are aur square meter me hota hai, par gaon ki khareed-bikri me nali ka chalan aaj bhi hai. Isliye registry ya sauda karne se pehle nali ko metric me badalkar record se match karna zaroori hai.

Mutthi Kya Hai? (1 Nali = 16 Mutthi; 1 Mutthi = 135 Sq. Feet = 15 Gaj)

1 mutthi = 135 sq ft = 15 gaj = lagbhag 12.54 sq meter. 16 mutthi milkar 1 nali banti hain. “Mutthi” shabd ka matlab hai muthi bhar, yaani itni zameen jisme ek mutthi beej boya ja sake.

Mutthi ka aitihasik matlab pahadi kheti se juda hai. Maana jata hai ki kheti ka naap is baat se aya ki ek khet me kitna beej lagta hai (beej ki matra se zameen ka aakaar). Yeh ek paramparik maanyata hai, ise sarkari parimaan ki tarah nahi, lok-parampara ki tarah likhein.

Dehradun: Pahadi Land Classification & Revenue Vocabulary

Pahadi zameen ko uski sinchai (paani ki suvidha) aur dhaalan ke hisaab se alag naam diye jate hain. Talaon sinchit ghati ki zameen hai, upraon sookhi terrace wali zameen hai. Yeh shabd Garhwal-Kumaon ke revenue record me aam hain, aur Dehradun ke Chakrata-Kalsi jaise pahadi ilake me bhi sthaniya roop me sunai de sakte hain.

Talaon, Upraon Avval/Doyam & Ijran/Katil

Talaon wo zameen hai jo nadi ya naale ke paas ghati me hoti hai aur jisme sinchai ho sake. Upraon wo terrace wali sookhi zameen hai jo barish par nirbhar rehti hai. Ijran/Katil wo dhaalu zameen hai jo kabhi-kabhi hi jotne layak hoti hai.

Doon ghati ke maidani gaon me zameen ka vargikaran alag hai: sinchit (nahar/tubewell se), asinchit aur baagh (litchi, aam). Neeche Dehradun: Bagwani, Phalon ke Kshetra aur Nakdi Fasalein me dekhein.

Naap, Benaap aur Kesar-e-Hind Zameen ka Matlab

Nap zameen wo hai jiska survey aur naap sarkari record me hua hai. Benap zameen wo hai jiska poora naap nahi hua. Kesar-e-Hind zameen British kaal ki ek khas shreni hai, jisse jude niyam rajya me alag se dekhe jate hain.

Agar aapke paas aisi zameen hai to Tehsildar ya vakil se baat karein.

Dehradun mein Zameen Khareedne ke Kanoon aur Section 143

Dehradun un 11 zilon me hai jahan 2025 ke naye Bhu Kanoon ke baad rajya se bahar ke log kheti aur baagwani ki zameen nahi khareed sakte. Yeh ban Haridwar aur Udham Singh Nagar par lagu nahi hai. Yeh niyam nagar nigam/nikay seema ke bahar ki zameen par lagu hota hai.

Bahari Logon ke Liye Zameen Kharidne ke Niyam

February 2025 me Uttarakhand Vidhan Sabha ne “Uttarakhand (Uttar Pradesh Zamindari Vinash Evam Bhumi Vyavastha Adhiniyam, 1950) Sanshodhan Vidheyak, 2025” paas kiya. Isse rajya ke 13 me se 11 zilon (Dehradun sahit) me bahari rajya ke logon ke liye kheti aur baagwani ki zameen kharidna band ho gaya. Haridwar aur Udham Singh Nagar isme shamil nahi hain.

Dhara 143 (Kheti se Gair-Kheti Zameen) aur Circle Rate

Section 143 ke tahat krishi zameen ko gair-krishi (non-agricultural) ghoshit karwaya jata hai. Ghar, dukan ya vyavsayik nirman se pehle yeh zaroori hota hai. Isme Tehsil/SDM karyalay me avedan, jaanch aur shulk ki prakriya hoti hai.

Section 143 kya hai (saral):

  • Agar aapki zameen krishi bhoomi ke roop me record me hai aur aap usme ghar ya vyavsay banana chahte hain, to use pehle gair-krishi ghoshit karwana padta hai.
  • Yeh kaam Tehsil ya SDM star par hota hai. Avedan me khatauni, naksha, shapath-patra aur anya kagaz lagte hain.
  • Zameen ki sthiti (master plan, green belt, nadi-kinara, forest kshetra) dekhkar anumati milti hai ya nahi milti.
  • 2025 ke sanshodhan ke baad kuch dharaon aur prakriya me badlav hue hain. Exact steps aur shulk ke liye tehsil karyalay ya portal se dekhein.

Circle rate (sarkari minimum dar): Registry ki stamp duty circle rate par ginti jati hai. Dehradun ke alag-alag ilake (jaise Rajpur Road, Cantt, Sahaspur, Vikasnagar) me circle rate alag hota hai. Taaza rate Stamp & Registration Vibhag ya Sub-Registrar karyalay se hi lein.

Zameen kharidne se pehle checklist:

  • Khatauni aur khasra bhulekh portal par dekhein.
  • Zameen krishi hai ya awasiya, record me dekhein.
  • Naksha se plot ki seema aur raasta match karein.
  • Nadi, naala ya jungle ke paas ki zameen ke liye pehle baadh-kshetra aur jungle-seema check karein (Dehradun: Paryavaran ki Chunautiyan aur Insaan-Jaanwar Sangharsh me Sahastradhara ki ghatna dekhein).
  • Registry sirf Sub-Registrar karyalay me hoti hai (Dehradun mein Sub-Registrar Daftar).
  • Vakil se title (maalikana haq) ki jaanch karwayein.
Section 143 kya hota hai?
Krishi zameen ko gair-krishi ghoshit karwane ka kanooni prakriya.
Section 143 kahan karwate hain?
Tehsil ya SDM karyalay me.
Circle rate kya hota hai?
Registry ke liye sarkar ki tay ki minimum dar.

Dehradun: Van Panchayat aur Samudayik Van Adhikar

Van Panchayat Uttarakhand ki ek khas samudayik jungle prabandhan vyavastha hai, jo 1931 ke Van Panchayat niyam se shuru hui. Isme gaon ke log milkar apne gaon ke jungle ka prabandhan karte hain, aur ghaas-lakdi ke adhikar tay karte hain.

Van Panchayat Kaise Kaam Karti Hai: Ghaas-Lakdi ke Haq aur Sarpanch

Van Panchayat me gaon ke log ek Sarpanch aur kamety chunte hain jo gaon ke jungle ka dekhbhal karti hai. Ghaas, chara aur sukhi lakdi ke adhikar gaon walon ko milte hain, par jungle se ped kaatna aam taur par manaa hai. Ghaas kaatne ki tithiyan gaon ki van panchayat tay karti hai.

Dehradun me: Van Panchayat ki vyavastha mukhya roop se Chakrata, Kalsi, Tyuni jaise pahadi ilake me hai. Dehradun zile me exact Van Panchayat ki ginti ke liye Forest Dept./Panchayati Raj ki list dekhein. Rajya me kul Van Panchayat ki sankhya lagbhag 12,000 ke aas-paas batai jati hai.

Ghaas kaatne ki tithi (danda khulna): Kai gaon me kuch tithi tak ghaas kaatna band rehta hai taaki ghaas badh sake, phir tithi khulne par sab milkar kaat sakte hain. Is parampara ka sahi naam aur tithi har gaon me alag hoti hai. Dehradun ke liye koi ek tithi nahi hai.

Dehradun: Paramparik Takneek: Gharat, Guhl aur Seedhi-daar Kheti

Pahadon me paramparik paani-chakki (gharat), nali-naharein (guhl/kul) aur patthar ki deewar wale terrace kheti ka ek puri swadeshi engineering system raha hai. Dehradun ke Jaunsar-Bawar me bhi paramparik paani-chakki aur terrace kheti dekhi jati hai, jabki Doon ghati me nahar-aadharit sinchai ki parampara rahi hai.

Gharat / Panchakki: Gadhere ke Paani se Chalne Wali Chakki

Gharat ek paramparik paani se chalne wali chakki hai jisme nadi-naale ka paani lakdi ke turbine (bhirnd) ko ghumata hai aur patthar ke paat anaj peesta hai. Kaha jata hai ki iska aata dheere-dheere peesa hone ke karan thanda peesa hua aata hai.

  • Kaise kaam karti hai: Naale ka paani ek nali se girta hai, jisse lakdi ka pahiya (turbine) ghumta hai. Wahi ghoomna upar ke patthar ke paat ko ghumata hai.
  • Aate ki visheshta: Dheere peesne ke karan garmi kam banti hai, jisse aate ka swad aur poshan bana rehta hai, aisa lok-mat hai. Iske vaigyanik praman ke liye alag adhyayan dekhne honge.
  • Aaj ki sthiti: Bahut se gharat band ho gaye hain, kuch ko bijli utpadan ke liye “micro-hydro” me badla gaya hai.

Dehradun me gharat ki exact ginti aur kahan chal rahe hain, iske liye gaon-level survey chahiye (khaaskar Jaunsar-Bawar me).

Gharat kya hota hai?
Paani se chalne wali paramparik aata chakki.
Panchakki kya hai?
Paani se chalne wali chakki ka ek aur naam, jo kai jagah gharat ke liye bhi bola jata hai.
Gharat ka aata kyun achha mana jata hai?
Dheere peesne se garmi kam hoti hai (paramparik manyata).

Paramparik Sinchai Nahar (Guhl/Kul) aur Pathar ki Seedhi-daar Deewarein (Bhida/Pushta)

Pahadon me guhl ya kul paani ko door se khet tak le jane wali chhoti naharein hain. Terrace kheti me khet seedhi dhaalan par patthar ki sukhi deewar (bina cement ke) se banaye jate hain, jisse mitti bahne se rukti hai.

  • Guhl / Kul (sinchai ki nahar): Nadi ya jharne se paani door tak khet me le jaya jata hai. Sinchai ka samay aur baari gaon ki samiti tay karti hai.
  • Dry-stone terrace: Dhalaan par patthar ki deewar se samatal khet banaye jate hain (jinhe sthaniya bhasha me alag naam milte hain, jaise pushta ya bhida) (Dehradun ke liye sthaniya naam).
  • Doon ghati me nahar: Dehradun shehar ke purane ilake me kai nahar thin (jaise Rajpur nahar), jo British kaal ya usse pehle bani batayi jati hain. Ye nahar sinchai ke saath shehar ki jal-vyavastha ka hissa thin. Aaj ke liye in nahron ki sthiti dekhna zaroori hai.
Guhl ya kul kya hota hai?
Pahadi paramparik sinchai nahar.
Dry-stone terrace kya hota hai?
Bina cement ke patthar ki deewar wale seedhi khet.
Terrace kheti kyun zaroori hai?
Dhalaan par mitti ko bahne se rokne ke liye.

Dehradun: Desi Mota Anaj aur Fasalein (Barahnaja)

Barahnaja ek paramparik “12 anaj” ki mishrit kheti hai, jo mukhya roop se Garhwal ke pahadi gaon me hoti thi. Dehradun ke Jaunsar-Bawar me mandua, jhangora, chaulai (marsha), kauni jaise anaj ki parampara hai, jabki Doon ghati me dhan, gehun aur ganna mukhya fasal hain.

Paramparik ‘Barahnaja’: 12 Anaj ki Mili-Juli Kheti

Barahnaja ek aisi mishrit kheti hai jisme ek hi khet me kai tarah ke anaj (jaise mandua, jhangora, rajma, gahat, bhatt, tor aadi) saath boye jate hain. Isse mitti ki upjau bani rehti hai, aur ek fasal kharab hone par dusri bachi rehti hai.

  • Faayde: Mitti me nitrogen aur poshan bana rehta hai, keeton ka asar bante-bante kam rehta hai, aur parivar ko saal bhar alag-alag anaj milte hain.
  • Dehradun ki sthiti: Dehradun ke Jaunsar-Bawar ke gaon me mishrit kheti ki parampara hai. Doon ghati me ab zyadatar ek-fasal (dhan-gehun) kheti hoti hai. Barahnaja ka naam aur 12 anaj ki list Garhwal ke anya ilakon me zyada prachalit hai (Dehradun ke liye sthaniya naam).
Barahnaja kya hai?
12 anaj ki mishrit paramparik kheti.

Mitti ki upjau aur jokhim dono sambhalna.

Mandua, Jhangora, Kauni, Cheena, Ogal & Phaphar (Buckwheat)

Mandua (finger millet), jhangora (barnyard millet), kauni (foxtail millet), cheena (proso millet) aur phaphar/ogal (buckwheat) Garhwal ke paramparik anaj hain. CGWB ki ek report me Dehradun ke liye mandua ka kshetrafal lagbhag 3,123 hectare dikhaya gaya hai (purana aankda).

Red Rice (Lal Chawal / Ukhad Dhan), Satti & Pahadi Wheat

Pahadi lal chawal aur ukhad dhan ke liye Garhwal-Kumaon jane jate hain. Dehradun ki sabse badi chawal ki pehchan “Dehradooni Basmati” hai, jo apni khushboo ke liye prasiddh rahi aur ab ise phir se zinda karne ki mehnat chal rahi hai.

Dehradun Basmati: ek zinda kahani (July 2026 tak ki khabar):

  • Pehchan: Dehradooni Basmati Doon ghati ki paramparik basmati kisim hai, jo apni khushboo ke liye jani jati hai.
  • Giravat: Ek samay Doon basmati lagbhag 550 hectare me hoti thi, jo ghatkar 150-200 hectare reh gayi (mukhya krishi adhikari ke anusar, Amar Ujala, 26 July 2026).
  • Punarjeevan prayas (2025): Zila prashasan, Krishi Vibhag aur kisanon ne milkar 65 hectare me 100 se zyada kisanon ke saath trial kheti ki. REAP pariyojana ke zariye 200 quintal se zyada dhan 65 rupaye prati kilo ke hisaab se kharida gaya aur 13 lakh rupaye se zyada seedhe kisanon ke khate me gaye (Mukhya Vikas Adhikari ke anusar).
  • 2026: Is saal lagbhag 75 hectare me bawai shuru hui, lagbhag 160 kisan jude hain. Sahaspur aur Vikasnagar ke saath ab Raipur aur Doiwala ke kisan bhi ruchi dikha rahe hain.
  • Aage ki yojna: Vikasnagar me seed bank aur vaigyanik testing suvidha ki taiyari ki ja rahi hai.
  • Kyun ghati: Zameen par makaan banna, mausam badalna, bazaar me kam daam aur keeton ka prakop, ye sab karan batate jate hain (ek 2024 journal adhyayan me bhi aisa hi zikr hai).

Satti aur pahadi gehun: Pahadi gaon me gehun aur satti ke paramparik prakar ki jaankari Dehradun ke liye seemit hai.

Dehradun Basmati kya hai?
Doon ghati ki paramparik khushboodar basmati.
Dehradun me basmati kitne hectare me hoti thi?
Ek samay lagbhag 550 ha, ab 150-200 ha (2026 ki khabar).
Dehradun Basmati ke liye prashasan kya kar raha hai?
2026 me 75 ha me kheti, seed bank aur testing ki yojna.

Dehradun: Pahadi Pulses (Dals), Oilseeds & Spices

Pahadi daalon me gahat (kulath), bhatt (kaali soybean), tor aur rajma prasiddh hain. Chakrata ka rajma sthaniya roop se bahut prasiddh mana jata hai. Til, bhangjeera, sarson aur jambu jaise masale bhi pahadi rasoi ka hissa hain.

Pahadi Dalein: Gahat, Bhatt, Tor, Naurangi, Lobia aur Chakrata ka Rajma

Chakrata aur Jaunsar-Bawar ka rajma pahadi rajma ke naam se jana jata hai. Gahat (horse gram) aur bhatt ki daalein bhi Garhwal ki paramparik rasoi ka hissa hain.

Sthaniya jalvayu aur mitti me ugne wala rajma, apne swad aur rang ke liye.

Bhangjeera, Til, Laahi, Jakhiya, Jambu/Faran, Gandhraini & Chhipi

Bhangjeera (perilla seeds), jakhiya (jungli sarson ka tadka), jambu/faran (Allium stracheyi) aur gandhraini pahadi masalon ka bahut khaas hissa hain. Jakhiya aur jambu ka tadka Uttarakhand ki pehchan hai, par yeh mukhya roop se oonche Garhwal-Kumaon me zyada milte hain.

  • Bhangjeera: Chutney aur masale ke roop me, pahadi gaon me.
  • Jakhiya: Daal-sabzi ke tadke me, pahadi swad ke liye.
  • Jambu (Faran): Himalayan ooncho ke ilake me milne wala sukhaya hua pyaaz-jaisa masala, jo seemit jagah par milta hai.
  • Til, laahi (sarson): Pahadi aur Doon, dono me ugaye jate hain.

Dehradun ke bazar (Paltan Bazaar, Hanuman Chowk ke aas-paas) me pahadi masale milne ki baat aati hai, par kahan kya milta hai, iska verified record kam hai.

Jakhiya kya hai?
Jungli sarson jaisa tadke ka masala.
Bhangjeera kya hai?
Perilla seeds, chutney me upyogi.
Jambu / faran kya hai?
Pahadi sukha masala (Allium stracheyi).

Pahadi Potato, Ginger, Turmeric, Large Cardamom & High-Altitude Pea/Cabbage

Chakrata kshetra aloo, matar aur gobhi jaisi sabziyon ke liye jana jata hai. CGWB ki report me Dehradun ke liye aaloo ka kshetrafal lagbhag 1,562 hectare dikhaya gaya hai. Ginger aur turmeric chhote pairmaane par hote hain, aur badi elaichi Dehradun ka fasal nahi hai.

  • Aloo: Chakrata aur Kalsi ke oonche gaon me, madhyam kshetra me. Aankda lagbhag 1,562 ha (CGWB, purana).
  • Matar, gobhi: Pahadi kheti me mausmi sabziyan jo Dehradun ki mandi tak aati hain.
  • Adrak (ginger), haldi (turmeric): Dehradun ke neeche ke ilake me chhoti kheti.
  • Badi elaichi: Dehradun me nahi hoti; Sikkim jaise rajya me hoti hai.
  • Doon ghati ki sabzi: Shehar ke aas-paas sabzi ki kheti (palak, methi, mooli aadi) rozmarra ki mandi ke liye hoti hai.
Chakrata ka aloo kaisa hota hai?
Pahadi aloo, madhyam kshetra me ugne wali sabzi.
Kya Dehradun me badi elaichi hoti hai?
Nahi.

Dehradun: Phoolon ki Kheti, Mushroom, Madhumakkhi Palan aur Chai Bagaan

Dehradun ka tea garden ka itihaas purana hai: Doon ghati me 19vi sadi se chai ke baagan the, jinme se kai ab ghar-colony me badal gaye. Phool, mushroom aur madhumakkhi palan me bhi Dehradun ke aas-paas chhote lekin tezi se badhte udyog hain.
