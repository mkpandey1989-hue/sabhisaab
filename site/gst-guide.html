GST Kya Hai Aur Kaise Nikaalein?

Lekhak: Manoj Kumar Pandey · Aakhri update: August 2026

GST yaani Goods and Services Tax, Bharat me lagne wala ek hi (single) indirect tax hai jo saamaan aur sevaon (goods & services) par lagta hai. Pehle alag-alag tax (VAT, service tax, etc.) hote the, jinhe mila kar 2017 me GST laaya gaya — isi liye ise "One Nation, One Tax" bhi kehte hain. Is page par hum samjhenge ki GST kaise nikalti hai, aur neeche free GST calculator se aap turant kisi bhi rakam par GST jod ya hata sakte hain.

GST ke mukhya slab

Alag-alag cheezon par alag GST dar lagti hai. Mukhya slab ye hain:

  • 5% — zaroorat ki cheezein (jaise kuch khaane-peene ki vastuein, transport).
  • 12% — kuch processed food, mobile, etc.
  • 18% — sabse aam slab (zyadatar services, electronics, etc.).
  • 28% — luxury aur "sin goods" (jaise AC, badi gaadi, tambaku).

Kuch zaroori cheezein (jaise anaj, doodh) GST-mukt (0%) bhi hoti hain.

GST jodna (add) aur hatana (remove)

Do tarah ke hisaab hote hain:

GST jodna: Agar kisi cheez ka daam ₹1,000 hai aur GST 18% hai, to GST = ₹180, aur total = ₹1,180.

GST hatana: Agar aapko ₹1,180 (GST samet) me se asli daam nikalna hai, to base = 1180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000, aur GST = ₹180. Ye tab kaam aata jab bill par sirf final rakam likhi ho aur aapko andar ka GST jaanna ho.

Sab Hisaab ka GST calculator dono karta hai — bas "jodo" ya "hatao" chunein.

CGST aur SGST kya hai?

Jab aap apne hi rajya me kharidte hain, to GST do hisson me batta hai — CGST (Central) aur SGST (State), dono aadha-aadha. Jaise 18% GST me 9% CGST + 9% SGST. Doosre rajya se kharidne par IGST (Integrated) lagta hai. Aam grahak ke liye kul rakam wahi rehti — sirf sarkar ke beech batwara alag hota hai.

GST Calculator kaise use karein?

  2. GST % chunein (5, 12, 18 ya 28).
  3. Jodo ya Hatao chunein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Kya har cheez par GST lagta hai?

Nahi. Kuch zaroori cheezein (anaj, taaza sabzi, doodh) GST-mukt hain.

GST kaun bharta hai?

Aakhir me grahak (customer) bharta hai, par ise business collect karke sarkar ko jama karta hai.

GST dar badal sakti hai?

Haan, GST Council samay-samay par slab badal sakti. Isliye badi rakam ke liye current dar confirm kar lein.

GST inclusive aur exclusive me kya farak hai?

Exclusive matlab daam me GST joda jaana hai. Inclusive matlab GST daam ke andar hi hai — usme se GST nikaalne ka formula alag hota hai.

Chhote karobar ko GST registration zaroori hai?

Ek tay saalana bikri ke baad zaroori ho jaata hai, aur sewa aur saman ke liye ye seema alag hoti hai. Online bechne par niyam aur bhi alag hain — CA se confirm kar lijiye.

Inclusive aur exclusive — sabse aam galti

Ye do bilkul alag hisaab hain aur yahin sabse zyada galti hoti hai.

GST jodna (exclusive): GST = rakam × rate ÷ 100
GST hatana (inclusive): base = rakam × 100 ÷ (100 + rate)

Misaal: ₹1,000 par 18% jodna hai to GST ₹180, total ₹1,180. Par agar ₹1,000 me GST pehle se juda hai, to base ₹847 aur GST ₹153 nikalta hai — ₹180 nahi. Seedha 18% ghata dena galat jawab deta hai, aur bill banate waqt ye galti mehngi padti hai.

Input Tax Credit — GST ka asli fayda

Agar aapka GST registration hai, to jo GST aapne kharidne par diya hai wo aapke bechne par jama karne wale GST me se ghata sakte hain. Isi ko Input Tax Credit (ITC) kehte hain.

Misaal: aapne maal kharida aur ₹9,000 GST diya, phir becha aur ₹15,000 GST vasoola. Aapko sarkar ko sirf ₹6,000 dena hoga, ₹15,000 nahi. Isi liye GST wale vyapari GST wale supplier se hi kharidna pasand karte hain — bina bill ke saude me ITC nahi milta.

Kis dhandhe ko GST number lena hi padta hai

Har dhandhe ko GST number nahi chahiye. Aam taur par ye seemaayein maani jaati hain:

KaamSeema (aam taur par)
Saaman bechna (zyadatar rajya)Rs 40 lakh saalana
Saaman — kuch vishesh rajyaRs 20 lakh
Seva (service)Rs 20 lakh
Doosre rajya me bechnaSeema chahe jo ho, zaroori
Online platform par bechnaZaroori
Do cheezein jo chhote dhandhe ko mehngi padti hain: pehli — GST number lene ke baad bikri ho ya na ho, return bharni padti hai; na bharne par roz ka late fee lagta hai. Doosri — bina GST number ke aap apne kharche ka input credit nahi le sakte, jo B2B kaam me nuksan hai. Isliye faisla sirf seema dekhkar nahi, apne grahak dekhkar kijiye — dukaan ke aam grahak hain to registration ki jaldi nahi, par company ko bechna hai to lagbhag zaroori hai. Seemaayein badalti rehti hain, cbic-gst.gov.in par confirm kijiye. Hum CA nahi hain.
ITC ke liye shartein: supplier ne apna return bhara ho, aapke paas sahi tax invoice ho, aur maal/seva sach me mili ho. Supplier ne return nahi bhara to aapka credit atak jaata hai — isliye supplier chunte waqt savdhaan rahein.

Registration kab zaroori hota hai

Ek tay turnover paar karne par GST registration zaroori ho jaata hai (saaman aur seva ke liye limit alag hoti hai, aur kuch rajyon me kam). Kuch mamlon me turnover chahe kitna bhi ho, registration zaroori hota hai — jaise doosre rajya me saaman bechna, ya online platform par bechna.

Chhote vyapariyon ke liye Composition Scheme bhi hota hai — kam dar par flat tax, kam kagzi kaam, par ITC nahi milta aur doosre rajya me bech nahi sakte. Apne dhandhe ke liye kya theek hai, ye CA se pooch lena behtar hai.

Return aur bill ki baatein

  • Tax invoice par GSTIN, HSN/SAC code, rate aur GST ka batwara saaf likha hona chahiye
  • CGST + SGST ek hi rajya me, IGST doosre rajya me — customer ke liye total wahi rehta hai
  • Return time par bharein — deri par late fee aur byaj lagta hai, aur aapke khareedar ka ITC bhi atak jaata hai
  • Bina registration ke GST charge karna galat hai — ispar penalty lagti hai

GST ke rate aur limit samay-samay par badalte rehte hain. Bade faisle se pehle gst.gov.in par ya apne CA se taaza jaankari zaroor confirm karein.
